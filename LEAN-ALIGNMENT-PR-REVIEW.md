# Review of the Blueprint–Lean alignment PRs #210–#212

Three pull requests by littlebin530, opened on 5 November 2026, rewrite the AI-written layer 2 of chapters 2–4 so that it follows the Lean formalisation (slopmaster2 `08fcdf1cca`), and add Lean names and dependency arrows:

- #210, Chapter 2: The pro-étale site
- #211, Chapter 3: Constructible complexes
- #212, Chapter 4: Proper base change

This report describes every change and what it means for the blueprint. It covers 503 files. Each of the 321 rewritten or new texts was read against the readable tex, its layer-2 clause and the cited Lean. The PRs were compared with `main` at their base (`13eeb096a`) and at today's head (`dfce30bbc`).

## Summary

**Most of the work is sound, but the PRs should not be merged as they stand.**

What holds up:
- 198 of the 321 texts raise no issue, and 106 raise only minor ones.
- As the descriptions say, the text of the human-written declarations is untouched.
- `blueprint check` passes on all three PRs, and every link and formula resolves.

What stops a merge:
- **The edits will be overwritten.** They are made in the generated markdown, while `blueprint-layer-2.tex` is unchanged. The next `scripts/retex.py` run would put back the old texts and delete the two new clauses. To last, the changes have to go into the layer-2 tex.
- **17 items drop what the declaration asserts** (12 in #210, 2 in #211, 3 in #212). They rewrite layer 2 to the narrower theorem Lean proves, so it no longer states the human declaration. Content is deleted or replaced, mostly without a notice, and one clause becomes a tautology.
- **#210 conflicts with `main`.** The clash is in the Tate-twist clause that #203 corrected today. #211 and #212 merge cleanly.

## Findings that need a decision

### Content dropped from layer 2 (17 major items)

In each item the clause used to restate (part of) the human declaration. It now states only what Lean proves, so nothing in layer 2 carries the declaration's claim any more. The parent declaration's own text is unchanged.

- `proposition-topos-functoriality--1` (#210): subcanonicality and the claim that the category of sheaves is a Grothendieck topos are dropped silently, though later items use representable sheaves.
- `proposition-comparison-morphism-inverse-image--2` (#210): the formula ν\*F(V) = Γ(V_ét, h\*F) is deleted; the PR calls it "unsupported".
- `proposition-comparison-immersions--3` (#210): the comparison of localization triangles is deleted rather than kept under its bypass notice, and the derived direct-image isomorphism is handed to a clause that no longer states it.
- `corollary-continuity-cohomology--1`, `--2` (#210): narrowed from bounded-below complexes of R-modules to single abelian sheaves, with no notice.
- `proposition-locally-constant-sheaves-finite-presentation--1`, `--2`, `--3` (#210): restricted to a finite coefficient ring. The chapter allows any discrete ring, and `theorem-comparison-constructible-sheaves` uses the proposition for Noetherian Λ.
- `lemma-inverse-image-coefficient-sheaves--2` (#210): the pro-étale Tate-twist comparison is replaced by an étale statement for torsion coefficients, proved from "the Lean construction" of the twist, which is not the blueprint's definition. This is also the file that conflicts with #203.
- `lemma-reduction-derived-nakayama--2` (statement and proof), `--3` (#210): "derived complete" is replaced by "every map from a Z with Z₁ = 0 is zero", which makes `--2` a tautology (take Z = K). It is not linked to `definition-derived-adic-completeness`. The tagged Lean theorem is equally trivial, since Lean defines completeness that way.
- `proposition-ctf-equals-bc--1` (#211): the only clause of the declaration no longer states D^b_ctf = D^b_c for ℓ-adic coefficients. It now states a helper about Tor amplitude, though Lean has the full statement (`localizingClosure_ctf_equals_bc`).
- `proposition-integral-constructibility-criterion--1` (#211): condition (3), the local model O_{E,S} ⊗ P^• on strata, is dropped. The proposition's own proof and `lemma-integral-models`(3) use (2) ⇔ (3).
- `proposition-proper-direct-image-finite-coefficients--1` (#212): the D^b_ctf statement and the Tor-amplitude bound [a, b+d] are deleted, replaced by an étale D^b_c statement over a Noetherian base.
- `proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--1` (#212): for finite rings A, D^b_ctf becomes D^b_c, with an explicit "no Tor bound asserted". For finite rings that is a real loss.
- `proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--9` (#212): the projection formula for finite admissible rings such as ℤ/ℓⁿ is narrowed to finite fields, and the finite-ring case is stated nowhere.

### How the PRs are made

- **Generated files edited, tex untouched.** The 321 texts and the two new clauses (`lemma-constructible-sheaves--8`, `proposition-proper-base-change-etale-site--2`) exist only in `blueprint/`. The new clauses have no `bp-r…` label in `blueprint-layer-2.tex`, so a retex run deletes them.
- **Lean names.** The PRs add 320 Lean tags, 253 distinct names, and remove 92. A source scan at `08fcdf1cca` finds 233 of the 253. Most of the rest are instance or generated names that a text scan can't see, so a real `check --lean` is still needed. Two names need attention:
  - `_private.…ComparisonCore.comparison_counit_plus` on `lemma-comparison-direct-images-compatibilities--2` is the internal name of a private declaration, which is not stable.
  - `…boundedConstructible_rightDerivedLowerStar_obj_of_ringChar_ne` on `proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes` and its `--4` exists only after the pinned Lean revision `f3cad3b`. `check --lean` fails until the pin moves to `08fcdf1` or later, 386 commits ahead.
- **Arrows.** The PRs add 1,186 `uses` arrows and remove 238.
  - 383 of the added arrows point to definitions and 346 to layer-2 clauses.
  - About 220 go from a proof to an item that comes later in the order.
  - They follow Lean dependencies. Compare that with the arrow rule wanted for declarations: #192 drew arrows from tex citations, without definition arrows, while #200, still a draft, draws them from Lean.
- **The descriptions don't match the files.**
  - The descriptions list Lean tags for many proofs, but none of the 149 new or rewritten proof files has a `lean` field.
  - In #212, some proofs the description says were moved into proof files are inline "*Proof.*" paragraphs inside statement nodes, so their arrows and Lean links are not recorded.
- **Lean leaking into the text.**
  - Phrases like "Lean packages…", "sheafification instances", "Roos augmentation", "w-strict localization", "normalized quotient maps", and hypotheses copied from Lean typeclasses (Grothendieck abelian categories, universe conditions).
  - #211 also redefines `\Dbc` locally as "locally finitely presented on strata" and introduces D^b_fp with no node behind it.

### Patterns rated minor

- **Bypass notices.** 36 clauses carry one: "bypassed by the Lean route", followed by the tex statement word for word. That is consistent and harmless, except in the major items above, where the statement itself was removed.
- **Narrowed to Lean's intermediate theorem.** Clauses that only repeated their parent now state the step Lean proves. Examples: abelian sheaves instead of sheaves of sets, algebraically closed Ω, torsion coefficients, étale instead of pro-étale. The parent still makes the full claim, but layer 2 no longer restates it.
- **Generalized to Lean's setting.** Grothendieck abelian categories, unbounded complexes. These are mostly fine.
- **Small slips.**
  - `proof/proposition-exact-products--1` cites part (2) where it means part (1).
  - `proof/proposition-w-local-components--1` assumes generalizations of a closed set are closed.
  - In #211, a few circular or unsupported proof steps, e.g. a "needed strengthening" of `lemma-continuity-ext-out-constructible-complex` that no node states.
