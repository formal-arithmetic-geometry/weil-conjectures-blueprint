title = "A finite module killed elementwise by a multiplicative set is killed by one element of it"

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $S\subseteq A$ a multiplicative subset and $M$ a finitely generated $A$-module such that $S^{-1}M=0$, i.e. for every $m\in M$ there is $t\in S$ with $tm=0$. Then there is $s\in S$ with $sM=0$ (that is, $sm=0$ for all $m\in M$).
