For each $k\in K$ choose $u_k:i\to j_k$ with $G(u_k)(s|_{V_k})=0$. Since $K$ is finite and $I$ is filtered, there are an object $j$ and morphisms $v_k:j_k\to j$ such that all composites $v_k\circ u_k:i\to j$ are equal to one morphism $u$. As the transition maps of the diagram commute with restriction, $G(u)(s)|_{V_k}=G(v_k)\bigl(G(u_k)(s|_{V_k})\bigr)=0$ for every $k$. By separatedness at $j$, $G(u)(s)=0$.
