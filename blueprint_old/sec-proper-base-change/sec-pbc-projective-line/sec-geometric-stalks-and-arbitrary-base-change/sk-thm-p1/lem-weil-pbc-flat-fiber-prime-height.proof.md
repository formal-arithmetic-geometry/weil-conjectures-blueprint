*Step 1.* The prime $\mathfrak q$ lies over $\mathfrak p$. The induced map $R_{\mathfrak p}\to S_{\mathfrak q}$ is a flat local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings, so it satisfies going down.

*Step 2.* For a flat local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings with going down, $\dim S_{\mathfrak q}=\dim R_{\mathfrak p}+\dim S_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak pS_{\mathfrak q}$. Here $\dim R_{\mathfrak p}=\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak p$ and $\dim S_{\mathfrak q}=\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak q$.

*Step 3.* The ideal $\mathfrak pS_{\mathfrak q}$ is proper since the map is local, and $S_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak pS_{\mathfrak q}$ is identified with the localization $C_Q$ of the fibre ring $C=\kappa(\mathfrak p)\otimes_RS$ at $Q$. Its dimension is $\operatorname{ht}Q$. Substituting into Step 2 gives $\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak q=\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak p+\operatorname{ht}Q$.
