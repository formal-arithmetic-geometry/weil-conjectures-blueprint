The source is $R\Gamma(X-D_2,j'_!N)=R\Gamma(X,M)$. The map factors as the
restriction to $R\Gamma(D_2,M|_{D_2})$, followed by the
restriction to the stalk $M_w=R\Gamma(X_{(w)}-D_2,j'_!N)$ (by the stalk formula), followed
by the restriction to the displayed target. All these maps are restrictions of
the same complex, so the composite is the given map.
If $w\ne z$, the middle map is zero by Lemma [sk-lem-tf-transverse-boundary].
If $w=z$, the intermediate object $M_z$ is zero.
