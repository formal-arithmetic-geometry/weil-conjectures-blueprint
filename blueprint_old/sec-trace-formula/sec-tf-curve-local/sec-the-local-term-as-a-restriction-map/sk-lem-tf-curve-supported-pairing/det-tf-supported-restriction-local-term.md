In the situation of [det-tf-supported-restriction], let
$\mathrm{ev}_K:b^*P\to\omega_X|_T$ be the evaluation map. For every
$v:(\Phi_X^m)^*K\to K$, the local term $\operatorname{LT}_y(v)$ at $y$ of the graph
correspondence $(\operatorname{id}_X,\Phi_X^m)$ with coefficient $v$
([def-trf-local-term]) equals

$$
 \int_y\mathrm{ev}_K\bigl(\rho_{a,b}[v]\bigr)\in A,
$$

where $R\Gamma_y(X,\omega_X)\simeq R\Gamma(y,\omega_y)$ and $\int_y:H^0(y,\omega_y)\to A$ is
integration.
