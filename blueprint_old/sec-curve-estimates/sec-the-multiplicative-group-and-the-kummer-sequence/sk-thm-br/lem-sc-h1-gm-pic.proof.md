This is part of [thm-sc-hilbert-90] for the scheme $X$: the group of
isomorphism classes of invertible $\mathcal O_X$-modules under
$\otimes$ appearing there is $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$ of
[def-sc-picard-group] (same set, same group law), and the map of the
statement is the composite of the two isomorphisms of groups of
[thm-sc-hilbert-90], which sends $\mathcal L$ to
$[\underline{\operatorname{Isom}}(\mathcal O_{X_{\Et}},\mathcal L_{\Et})]$.
(*Stacks Project, Lemma 21.6.1, Tag [040E](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/040E)*,
in Section 21.6: for a (locally) ringed site,
$H^1(\mathcal C,\mathcal O^*)=\operatorname{Pic}(\mathcal O)$.)
