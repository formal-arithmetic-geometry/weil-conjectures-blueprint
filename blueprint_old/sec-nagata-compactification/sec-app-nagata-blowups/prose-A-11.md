The blow-up is not uniquely determined, but on any such modification
with $U$ schematically dense the graph of the extension is forced to be
the closure of the original section. Hence the identifications obtained
on common admissible refinements agree by
Lemma [sk-lem-nagata-sketch-closure].

In the gluing argument below, a compactification of an open $V$ of $M$
is related to $M$ itself by a proper map defined on a larger open of
the compactification.
