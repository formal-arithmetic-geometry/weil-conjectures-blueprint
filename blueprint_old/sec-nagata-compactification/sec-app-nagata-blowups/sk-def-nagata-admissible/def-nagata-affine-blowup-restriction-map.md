Let $\varphi\colon A\to B$ be a ring homomorphism and let $J\subseteq B$
be the ideal generated by $\varphi(I)$. Define

$$
r_\varphi\colon
\operatorname{Bl}_{J}(\operatorname{Spec}B)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Bl}_I(\operatorname{Spec}A)
$$

to be the morphism of $\operatorname{Proj}$ schemes induced by the graded
ring homomorphism $\rho_\varphi\colon\mathcal R_A(I)\to\mathcal R_B(J)$ of
[def-nagata-rees-localization-map]. For the localization $A\to A_s$ and
$J=IA_s$ we write $r_s$.
