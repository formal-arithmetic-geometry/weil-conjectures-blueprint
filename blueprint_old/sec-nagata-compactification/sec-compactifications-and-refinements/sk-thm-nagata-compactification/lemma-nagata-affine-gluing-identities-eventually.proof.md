By the choice of $\lambda_{\mathrm{glue}}(S)$,
$A^{\mathrm{coeff}}_a\subseteq A_{\lambda_{\mathrm{glue}},a}\subseteq
A_{\lambda,a}$. The four gluing identities are part of the definition of a
valid stage, so they hold at $\lambda$.
