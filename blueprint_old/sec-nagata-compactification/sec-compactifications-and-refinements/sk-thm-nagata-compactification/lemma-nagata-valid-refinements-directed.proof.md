The selected valid stage $\lambda_{\mathrm{glue}}(S)$ is one object. For two
valid stages, the generated subrings are finitely generated and contain the
basic-open elements; localization commutes with the generated subring, so
$(A_{\lambda,a}+A_{\mu,a})[1/f_{abr}]
=A_{\lambda,a}[1/f_{abr}]+A_{\mu,a}[1/f_{abr}]
=A_{\lambda,b}[1/g_{abr}]+A_{\mu,b}[1/g_{abr}]$, and the unit conditions
are inherited from either stage because the localizations only grow. Hence
the chartwise sum is valid and lies above both inputs.

Inclusion of coefficient rings induces the maps on every chart; they carry
the overlaps $\bigcup_rD(f_{abr})$ into each other and are compatible with
the overlap maps, which are determined by their compatibility with the maps
from $U_a\cap U_b$, so they glue. The identity and composition laws hold
chartwise and therefore globally.
