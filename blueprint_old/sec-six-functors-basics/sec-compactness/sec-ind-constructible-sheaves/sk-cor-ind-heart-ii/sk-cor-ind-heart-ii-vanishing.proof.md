The diagram $(D_\alpha)$ is a filtered diagram of complexes concentrated in degree $0$, so all vanish
below degree $0$; its colimit in $\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, viewed in degree $0$, is $B$ (filtered
colimits of sheaves are exact, so the termwise colimit represents the colimit in the derived category
as well). Part (1) of [sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity] applies to $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)\subset\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$
and gives the isomorphism $\varinjlim_\alpha\Hom(C,D_\alpha[q])\cong\Hom(C,B[q])$. For $q>d_C$, every
$\Ext^q(C,D_\alpha)$ vanishes by [sk-lem-uniform-constructible-ext], hence so does the colimit.
