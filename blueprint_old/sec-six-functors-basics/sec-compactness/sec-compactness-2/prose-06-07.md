We now have both ingredients: $\Ext^q(C,-)$ vanishes above $d_C$ and
commutes with direct sums on ind-constructible sheaves
(Corollary [sk-cor-ind-heart-ii]). Because of the uniform bound $d_C$,
maps from a constructible sheaf $C$ into an unbounded complex depend only
on finitely many of its cohomology sheaves, namely those in degrees
$[-d_C,0]$. This is the step that fails in the example over $\mathbf R$
in the introduction to this section, where $\Ext^n(\mathbf F_2,\mathbf F_2)\neq0$
for all $n$. Once the target is cut down to a window of fixed length,
uniformly for a whole family, compactness follows from the case of
sheaves by induction on the length of the window.
