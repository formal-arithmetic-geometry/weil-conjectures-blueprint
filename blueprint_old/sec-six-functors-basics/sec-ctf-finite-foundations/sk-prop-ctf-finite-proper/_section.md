Let $p:X\to Y$ be proper. Then $Rp_*$ maps $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ into $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,A)$,
and it maps $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A))$ into $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,A))$ and preserves
arbitrary direct sums there. More precisely, if $C\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ has Tor
amplitude in $[a,b]$ and $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(X_{\bar y})\leq d$ for all
geometric points $\bar y$ of $Y$, then $Rp_*C$ has Tor amplitude in $[a,b+d]$.
