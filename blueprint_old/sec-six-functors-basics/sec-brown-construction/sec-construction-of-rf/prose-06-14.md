Consider a commutative square of schemes with $p:X\to Y$,
$p':X'\to Y'$, $g:Y'\to Y$, $g':X'\to X$ and
$p\circ g'=g\circ p'$. Its base change map is the map

$$
g^*Rp_*\longrightarrow Rp'_*g'^*
$$

of functors $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D(Y'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ obtained by
applying the unit of $p'^*\dashv Rp'_*$, the isomorphism
$p'^*g^*\simeq g'^*p^*$, and the counit of $p^*\dashv Rp_*$. By
Lemma [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients], these inverse images are the exact
inverse images of sheaves, so the adjunctions are the usual ones between
the unbounded derived categories. We show next that for a cartesian square
with $p$ proper, this map is an isomorphism on $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$.
