This is classical: the polynomial ring is standard smooth over the base (Stacks Project, Algebra, Definition 10.137.5 and Lemma 10.137.6, [Tag 00T6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00T6), [Tag 00T7](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00T7)), the two notions of smoothness agree (Lemma 37.11.7, [Tag 02H6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02H6)), and the module of differentials is finite locally free of rank n, which by Lemma 29.35.12 ([Tag 02G1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02G1)) computes the relative dimension. No separate proof is given.
