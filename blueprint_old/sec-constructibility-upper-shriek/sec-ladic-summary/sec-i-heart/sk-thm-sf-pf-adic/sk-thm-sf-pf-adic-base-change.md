For a cartesian square with $p:X\to Y$ proper, $g:Y'\to Y$, $p':X'\to Y'$ and
$g':X'\to X$, the base change map $g^*Rp_*\to Rp'_*g'^*$ is an isomorphism of
functors $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))\to\Loc(\Dbc(Y',\mathcal O_E))$.
