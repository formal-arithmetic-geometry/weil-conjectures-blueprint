The functors preserve bounded constructible complexes by the cited preservation statements.
Since the inclusions $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(-,\Lambda)\subset D((-)_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ are
fully faithful, the units, counits and composition isomorphisms of the ambient functors,
evaluated at bounded constructible complexes, are morphisms of
$D_{\mathrm{cons}}(-,\Lambda)$, and the triangle identities and coherence conditions hold there
because they hold in the ambient categories.
