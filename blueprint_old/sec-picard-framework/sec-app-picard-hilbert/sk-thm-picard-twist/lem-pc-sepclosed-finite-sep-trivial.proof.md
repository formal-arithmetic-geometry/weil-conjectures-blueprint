Let $\alpha\in K$. It is separable algebraic over $k$; let $m_\alpha\in k[T]$
be its minimal polynomial, which is irreducible, nonconstant and
separable. By separable closedness $m_\alpha$ has a root in $k$, so being
irreducible it has degree $1$. Therefore $\alpha\in k$. As $\alpha$ was
arbitrary, $K=k$. (For finite $K/k$ one may also argue with a primitive
element $K=k[\alpha]$.)
