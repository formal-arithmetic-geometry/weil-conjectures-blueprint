The local regularity facts needed here are [lem-sc-smooth-regular] (a smooth curve over a field has
regular, hence domain, local rings), [lem-sc-regular-dim1-dvr] (a
regular local ring of dimension $1$ is a discrete valuation ring) and
[lem-sc-point-cartier] (a closed point of a smooth connected curve is an
effective Cartier divisor with ideal generated by one non-zero-divisor).
They apply in our situation because a
smooth projective curve over a
field is in particular a *smooth connected curve* in the sense of
[def-sc-curves] (nonempty, connected, separated, of finite type, of dimension
one; see [rem-pc-relation-sc]), and every base change $X_K$ is
again such a curve ([lem-sc-base-change-curve] and
[prop-pc-xk-integral]).
