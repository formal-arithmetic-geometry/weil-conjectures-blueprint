By flat base change for the quasi-coherent module $\mathcal{O}_X$
along the flat $k$-algebra $K$ ([lem-pc-flat-base-change-cohom]),

$$
H^1(X_K,\mathcal{O}_{X_K})\cong H^1(X,\mathcal{O}_X)\otimes_kK,
$$

whence $\dim_KH^1(X_K,\mathcal{O}_{X_K})=\dim_kH^1(X,\mathcal{O}_X)$: a
$k$-basis of a $k$-vector space $V$ gives a $K$-basis of $V\otimes_kK$.
