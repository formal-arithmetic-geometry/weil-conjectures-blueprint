The map $a/t\mapsto f^\#(a)/t$ defines the first morphism.
If $I'=I\mathcal O_{X'}$, the natural map from the base change of the
Rees algebra onto $\mathcal O_{X'}[t,I'/t]$ is surjective, and it is
injective if $f$ is flat. On the associated graded algebras, the
generators of $\operatorname{gr}_I$ of positive degree generate the
ideal of elements of positive degree of $\operatorname{gr}_{I'}$. The
quotient by them is $\mathcal O_{Z'}$, and this proves the assertion
about the zero section. If $f$ is proper, then $\widetilde f$ is the
closed immersion followed by the base change of $f$, which is proper.
If $f$ is smooth, it is flat, so $\widetilde f$ is the base change
$X'\times_X\mathcal D_ZX\to\mathcal D_ZX$ of $f$, which is smooth.
