Let $f\colon X\to Y$ be separated of finite type, $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ a listed map, and $K\in\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$. Let
$\beta_{f,K}\colon(f^!K)_{\Lambda'}\to f^!(K_{\Lambda'})$ be the transpose under $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ of
$Rf_!((f^!K)_{\Lambda'})\simeq(Rf_!f^!K)_{\Lambda'}\xrightarrow{(\epsilon_K)_{\Lambda'}}K_{\Lambda'}$. Then $\beta_{f,K}$ is an
isomorphism, and the $\beta$ are compatible with composition and open restriction.
