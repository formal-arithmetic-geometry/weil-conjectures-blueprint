*Isomorphism.* $j_!K\in\Dctf(\overline X,\Lambda)$; the first arrow is the direct-image comparison for the proper
$p$, an isomorphism by [lem-coef-finite-direct-image] (finite rings) or
[det-sfb-coefficient-comparisons-direct-image]; the second is the extension-by-zero identification
([def-sfb-derived-open-immersion-shriek-iso]). For finite coefficients see also
[lem-fix-compact-support-coefficient-change].

*Independence.* For a refinement $h\colon Z'\to Z$ of compactifications, the refinement map
$e_h\colon j_!K\to Rh_*j'_!K$ is characterized by restricting to the identity on $X$. The comparison maps
for $j_!$, $j'_!$ and $Rh_*$ are formed from the same pullback identifications and adjunction maps, so the
two composites $(j_!K)_{\Lambda'}\to Rh_*j'_!(K_{\Lambda'})$ (compare then refine, refine then compare) both restrict
to the identity on $X$; by the adjunction $j_!\dashv j^*$ they are equal. Applying $Rp_*$ and using
compatibility of the direct-image comparison with composition $R(ph)_*=Rp_*Rh_*$ gives independence.

*Composition.* The composition isomorphism of $Rf_!$ is built from proper direct images, extensions by zero
and interchange maps characterized by restriction to an open subscheme; each of these commutes with the
comparison maps by the same argument.
