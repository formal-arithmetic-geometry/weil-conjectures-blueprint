Each level $Rf_{!}C_n$ lies in $\Dctf(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^n)$ by
[sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability]. For uniformity, compactly supported base change and
the projection formula compute $(Rf_{!}C_n)_{\bar y}\otimes^{\mathbf L}N$ as
$R\Gamma_c(X_{\bar y},C_n|_{X_{\bar y}}\otimes^{\mathbf L}N)$, and $R\Gamma_c$ on
fibres of dimension $\leq d$ has amplitude $[0,2d]$ ([Stacks, Tag 0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10)); so a
common Tor interval $[a,b]$ of the $C_n$ gives the common interval $[a,b+2d]$.
