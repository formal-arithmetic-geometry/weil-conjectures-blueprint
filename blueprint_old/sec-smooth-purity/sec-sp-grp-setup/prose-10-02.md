Smooth purity is stated for six kinds of coefficients at once, and the
proof moves between them: the geometry is done with finite rings, the
case $\mathcal O_E$ is a limit of finite levels, and the fields are
obtained by inverting $\ell$. Before stating the theorem we therefore
fix what $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ means for each coefficient ring and record the two
facts about integral coefficients that make the passage to the limit
possible. Proposition [sk-prop-sp-integral-towers] says that the
reductions of an integral complex are bounded uniformly in $n$, and
that a derived complete complex is constructible as soon as
its reduction modulo $\ell$ is. Both are used for towers in
Section [sec-sp-grp-ladic-summary]. The theorem itself and the definition of the
relative dualizing complex $\omega_f$ follow, together with the reason
why $\omega_f$ determines $f^!$ for smooth $f$.
