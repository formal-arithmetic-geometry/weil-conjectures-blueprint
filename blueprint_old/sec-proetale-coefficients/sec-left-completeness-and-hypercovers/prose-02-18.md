An unbounded complex on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ is determined by its
bounded-below truncations, and its cohomology is computed from theirs
by a derived limit. On the étale site of a scheme of infinite
cohomological dimension this can fail. On $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ it holds because
products are exact, so that the Milnor sequence applies.

Left completeness (Proposition [sk-prop-pe-left-complete]) lets us treat an
unbounded complex as the derived limit of bounded below ones, to which
injective resolutions and the comparison theorems apply; this is how
the unbounded comparison of Proposition [sk-prop-pe-unbounded-classical] is
proved. The second result of the subsection gives an explicit model for
cohomology of bounded below complexes. On a quasi-compact quasi-separated
object we choose a hypercover all of whose terms are affine weakly
contractible. Since these terms have no higher cohomology, the
hypercover spectral sequence collapses, and $R\Gamma(U,K)$ is the total
complex of the sections $K^b(W_r)$
(Proposition [sk-prop-pe-hypercover]). In each total degree only finitely
many terms occur, and evaluation on each $W_r$ commutes with filtered
colimits, so cohomology commutes with filtered colimits of uniformly
bounded below complexes (Corollary [sk-cor-pe-bounded-continuity]). This
is the pro-étale form of the continuity of étale cohomology for
qcqs schemes. Later it shows that cohomology and direct images commute
with inverting $\ell$ and with extension of scalars to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, the
colimit over the finite extensions $E'$ of $E$.
