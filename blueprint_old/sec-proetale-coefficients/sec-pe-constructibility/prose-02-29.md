title = "Finite local systems, constructibility and finite Tor amplitude"

The six operations are studied on constructible complexes. We show
that constructible pro-étale sheaves are classical, so that they
correspond to constructible étale sheaves, and that the comparison
preserves finite Tor amplitude. We use the following definitions.
A sheaf of modules on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ is locally constant if it is trivialized by
a pro-étale covering, and it is of finite presentation if its constant
models are finitely presented $\Lambda$-modules. For Noetherian
$\Lambda$, a sheaf is constructible if, locally on $X$, there is a finite
partition into constructible locally closed strata such that it is locally
constant of finite type on each stratum. For a discrete ring this agrees
with the definition used in Section [sec-n-constructible]. These conditions refer
only to pro-étale covers, and the main step is to descend torsors from
pro-étale to étale covers.

A priori there could be more pro-étale local systems than étale
ones, since the covers are larger. For finite groups this does not
happen, by a finiteness argument: a torsor under a finite group is
described by a cocycle on one pro-étale affine cover, and since that
cover is a limit of étale covers and the group is classical, the
cocycle is defined at a finite stage. For $\Zl$ the situation is
different, and this is the purpose of the site. Over
$\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$ a rank-one $\Zl$-local system on which
Frobenius acts by a unit $u\in\Zl^\times$ of infinite order is
trivialized by a pro-étale cover but by no étale one. The comparison
proved here therefore concerns only finite coefficients, and $\ell$-adic
local systems are reached through their reductions. The result used
later is Theorem [sk-thm-pe-constructible-comparison] with its
Corollary [sk-cor-pe-finite-tor-comparison]: for a finite ring $A$, the
category $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ on the pro-étale site is the classical one.
