Let $U$ be an object of $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$.
Cover $X$ by affine opens and cover their inverse images in $U$ by
affine opens. On such a chart the map is a weakly étale ring map $R\to B$.
By Theorem [sk-thm-pe-indetale-refinement] there is a single faithfully flat
cover $\operatorname{Spec}(B')\to\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ such that
$\operatorname{Spec}(B')$ is a limit of
affine schemes étale over $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, hence over
$X$. The equivalence of categories of sheaves follows from the comparison
lemma for sites. This is [bhatt-scholze15, Lemma 4.2.4].
