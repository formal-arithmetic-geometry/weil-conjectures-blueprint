By the theorem applied to $U$, the left side is $R\Gamma(U_{\acute{e}t},K|_U)$.
For a sheaf, continuity of étale cohomology under affine limits
[Stacks, Tag 03Q6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03Q6) identifies this with the colimit in each degree. For a
bounded-below complex, only finitely many terms of the hypercohomology
spectral sequence contribute to each total degree, and filtered colimits
are exact.
