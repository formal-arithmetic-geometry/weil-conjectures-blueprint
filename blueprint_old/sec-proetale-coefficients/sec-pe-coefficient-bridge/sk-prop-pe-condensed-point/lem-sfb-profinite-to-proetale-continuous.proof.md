By [lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-finite-limits], $u$ is a finite-limit
preserving functor between finitely complete categories. Hence it is
representably flat, and so it preserves compatible families. By
[lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-cover-preserving] it is cover preserving. A
cover preserving functor that preserves compatible families is continuous.
