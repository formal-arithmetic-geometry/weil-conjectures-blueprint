Restriction along $u$ commutes with the objectwise symmetry and with
sheafification, so $p_*$ is lax braided. The compatibility of $p^*$ with the
symmetries is the mate of this compatibility under the adjunction
[def-sfb-point-adjunction]. This gives the identity after composing with the
inverse tensor comparison.
