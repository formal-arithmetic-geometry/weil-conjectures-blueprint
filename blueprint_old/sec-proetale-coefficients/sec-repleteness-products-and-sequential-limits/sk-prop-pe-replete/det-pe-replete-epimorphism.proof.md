By [det-pe-replete-definition] it suffices to show that $p_m$ is locally surjective. Let
$U$ be an object and $y\in F_m(U)$. By [det-pe-wcb-cover] there is a covering
$\{W_k\to U\}$ by affines $W_k=\operatorname{Spec}D_k$ with $D_k$ w-contractible; these are
weakly contractible ([det-pe-weak-contractible-equivalences]). By
[det-pe-replete-on-contractible] each $y|_{W_k}$ is the image of some
$x_k\in(\varprojlim_nF_n)(W_k)$. So $y$ is locally in the image of $p_m$.
