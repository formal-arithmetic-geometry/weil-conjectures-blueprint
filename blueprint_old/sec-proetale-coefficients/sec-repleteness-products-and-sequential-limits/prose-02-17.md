More generally, the $R^1\varprojlim$ term vanishes if, on every affine
weakly contractible $W$, the tower of section modules satisfies the
Mittag–Leffler condition. To see this, recall from
Corollary [sk-cor-pe-sections] that evaluation on $W$ preserves cokernels.
Hence the cokernel of $1-T$ evaluated on $W$ is the cokernel of $1-T$ on
the tower $(M_n(W))$, which vanishes by the Mittag–Leffler lemma
[Stacks, Tag 07KV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07KV). Since evaluation on these $W$ detects vanishing, the
$R^1\varprojlim$ term vanishes.
