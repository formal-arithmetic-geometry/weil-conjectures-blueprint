Kernels and cokernels of module objects are computed on the underlying objects, both for
$\mathcal R$- and for $\mathcal S$-modules, so $\operatorname{Res}_\varphi$ preserves finite
limits and finite colimits, i.e. it is exact [det-def-sfb-change-of-scalars]. The right
derived functor of an exact functor is the termwise functor
[det-def-sfb-derived-categories].
