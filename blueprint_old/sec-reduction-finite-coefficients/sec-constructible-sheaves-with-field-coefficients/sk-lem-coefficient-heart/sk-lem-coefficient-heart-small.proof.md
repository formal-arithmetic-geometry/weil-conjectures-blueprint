Let $F$ be constructible. Choose a finite stratification $X=\coprod_aS_a$ with $F|_{S_a}$ lisse
([det-lem-coefficient-heart-closure]), and for each $a$ a finite cover by quasi-compact
objects $U_{a,k}\in(S_a)_{\mathrm{proét}}$ with $F|_{U_{a,k}}\cong\Lambda^{r_{a,k}}$ ($S_a$ is
quasi-compact, and covers have finite quasi-compact refinements). Then $F|_{S_a}$ is
determined up to isomorphism by the descent datum: the transition matrices in
$\GL_{r}(\Lambda(U_{a,k}\times_{S_a}U_{a,k'}))$. Moreover $F$ is determined up to isomorphism
by the $F|_{S_a}$ together with the gluing data, by induction on the number of strata: for an
open stratum $u$ with closed complement $i$, $F$ is the extension of $i_*i^*F$ by $u_!u^*F$
classified by an element of $\operatorname{Ext}^1(i_*i^*F,u_!u^*F)$, a set.

The finite stratifications of $X$ form a set; the quasi-compact objects of
$(S_a)_{\mathrm{proét}}$ lie in the fixed universe, so finite families of them form a set;
the transition matrices form a set; and the Ext groups are sets. Hence the isomorphism
classes of such data, and so the isomorphism classes of constructible sheaves, form a set.
