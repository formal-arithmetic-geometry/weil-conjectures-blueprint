The exponential definition and the closed-point product are
[def-weil-point-counts-zeta], [prop-euler-product]; the latter also gives
$Z(X_0,T)\in1+T\mathbf Z[[T]]$. For separated $X_0$, rationality over
$\mathbf Q$ is part of [sk-cor-tf-point-counting]; a general $X_0$ of finite
type is a finite disjoint union of separated locally closed subschemes, over
which the point counts add, so $Z(X_0,T)$ is the product of their zeta
series and again lies in $\mathbf Q(T)$ ([det-thm-rationality]). Finally
[prop-weil-integral-rational-series] gives $A,B\in\mathbf Z[T]$ with
$B(0)=1$ and $BZ=A$, so the two asserted forms hold.
