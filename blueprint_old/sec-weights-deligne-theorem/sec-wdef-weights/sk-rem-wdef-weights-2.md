1. A mixed sheaf has weights at most $w$ if and only if it is
   punctually of weights $\le w$. This condition alone does not make a
   sheaf mixed.
2. Weights depend only on the characteristic polynomials of
   Frobenius; for instance, a Jordan block and its semisimplification
   have the same weights.
3. At a point over $k_m=\mathbf F_{q^m}$ lying above $x$, with
   $\deg(x)\mid m$, the operator is $\operatorname{Frob}_x^{m/\deg(x)}$, and an eigenvalue of
   weight $w$ has modulus $q^{mw/2}$.
