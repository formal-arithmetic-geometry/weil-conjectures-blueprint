Let $\mathcal G$ be a nonzero semisimple $\iota$-real lisse Weil sheaf
([def-wf-iota-real]) on a smooth geometrically connected curve over
$\mathbf F_q$. Group its irreducible summands by determinant weight:
$\mathcal G=\bigoplus_{i=1}^t\mathcal G_i$, where $\mathcal G_i$ is the sum of
the summands of determinant weight $\gamma_i$ and
$\gamma_1>\cdots>\gamma_t$. Then each $\mathcal G_i$ is punctually
$\iota$-pure of weight $\gamma_i$ and $\iota$-real.
