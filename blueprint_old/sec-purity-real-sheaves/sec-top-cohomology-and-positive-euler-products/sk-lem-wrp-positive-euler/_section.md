If $\mathcal G$ is $\iota$-real and $m\ge1$, every local inverse Euler
factor of $\mathcal G^{\otimes 2m}$ has nonnegative real coefficients
after applying $\iota$, and constant coefficient one.
Let

$$
L(t)=\prod_j f_j(t),\qquad f_j\in1+t\mathbf R_{\ge0}[[t]],
$$

be a coefficientwise locally finite product. If $L$ is the Taylor
series of a function holomorphic on $|t|<R$, then every $f_j$ has
radius of convergence at least $R$, and if $f_j=P_j^{-1}$ with
$P_j(0)=1$, then $P_j$ has no zero in that disc.

*Complement.* Radii of formal series and locally finite products are taken in
the sense of [d-real-series].
