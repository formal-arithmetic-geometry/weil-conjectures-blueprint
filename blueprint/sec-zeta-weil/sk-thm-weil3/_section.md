Every reciprocal root $\alpha$ of $P_i$ is an algebraic integer with
$|\tau(\alpha)|=q^{i/2}$ for every embedding
$\tau:\Q(\alpha)\hookrightarrow\C$; equivalently
$|\tau(\beta)|=q^{-i/2}$ for every ordinary root.
