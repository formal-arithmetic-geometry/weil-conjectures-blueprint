For $u\in\mathbf Z$ and $K\in\mathrm{WeilD}(X_0,E)$, the predicate
$\mathrm{WtGe}(K,u)$ holds if every weight of an eigenvalue of the
Frobenius of $\varphi_K$ is $\geq u$; $\mathrm{WtGt}(K,u)$ is the
strict version.
