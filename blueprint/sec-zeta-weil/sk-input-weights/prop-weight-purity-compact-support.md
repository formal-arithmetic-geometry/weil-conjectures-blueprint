In the situation of [prop-weight-purity], for every
$0\leq i\leq2d$ the
Frobenius module
$H_c^i(X,\Qlb)$ is $\iota$-mixed of weights at most $i$.

## Proof

The cohomology groups and the weight predicates are those of the situation
of [prop-weight-purity], and that situation is the smooth-proper
purity interface of [thm-weil-deligne-purity]: clause (2) of that
interface states that $H_c^i(X,\Qlb)$ is $\iota$-mixed of weights at
most $i$ in the sense of [def-weil-frobenius-module-weights].
