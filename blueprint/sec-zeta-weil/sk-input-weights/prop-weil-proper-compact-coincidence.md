In the notation of
[def-weil-ordinary-compact-cohomology], if $Y$ is
proper then the
compact-support direct image and the ordinary direct image agree,

$$
Ra_{Y!}\cong Ra_{Y*},
$$

so that $H_c^i(Y,K)\cong H^i(Y,K)$ for every $K$.
