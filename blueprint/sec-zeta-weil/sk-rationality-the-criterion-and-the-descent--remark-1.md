*Why the descent is needed.* The determinant formula of
[sk-cor-tf-point-counting] is produced by one chosen $\ell$, so it only
exhibits $Z\in\Ql(T)$. The descent to $\Q(T)$ does not compare the different
$\ell$; it uses only that the coefficients $z_n$ are rational integers, so
that a rank bound over $\Ql$ propagates to $\Q$ ([sk-prop-criterion]). The
comparison of the realizations (that $P_i$ is independent of $\ell$) is a
separate statement, [sk-thm-weil-final].
