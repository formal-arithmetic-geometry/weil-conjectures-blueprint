Let $K$ be a field and
$V\subseteq K^{\mathbf Z_{\geq0}}$ a
finite-dimensional
vector subspace.
There is a finite set of indices
$J\subseteq\mathbf Z_{\geq0}$ such that
$v\mapsto(v_j)_{j\in J}$ is injective on $V$.
