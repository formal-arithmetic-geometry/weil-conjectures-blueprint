In the situation of [prop-galois-weight-strata], for every integer
$i$ with $0\leq i\leq2d$ the normalized stratum polynomial

$$
Q_i(T)=\prod_{\alpha\in R_i}(1-\alpha T)
$$

over the $q$-Weil numbers of weight $i$
lies in $\mathbf Z[T]$.
