In the situation of [prop-rational-descent], the point-count zeta
series has a unique
normalized reduced
integral presentation: there are unique $A,B\in\mathbf Z[T]$ with
$A(0)=B(0)=1$, $\gcd(A,B)=1$ in $\mathbf Q[T]$, and
$Z(X_0,T)=A(T)/B(T)$.
