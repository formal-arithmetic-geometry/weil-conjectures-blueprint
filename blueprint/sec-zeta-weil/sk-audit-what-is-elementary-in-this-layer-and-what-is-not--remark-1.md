*What is elementary in this layer.* Once the trace formula
([sk-cor-tf-point-counting]), Poincaré duality ([sk-cor-tf-functional-equation])
and the weight estimate ([sk-input-weights]) are available, the passage to
the Weil conjectures is elementary apart from four points: the alternating
middle pairing (for the parity of $d\chi$), the two endpoint computations
in degrees $0$ and $2d$, the extension of an embedding of a number field to
an isomorphism $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\cong\mathbf C$, and the
intrinsic-weight/integrality step that produces the integral factors. The
remaining steps are formal power-series and linear algebra
([sk-prop-criterion], [sk-lem-wweil-integer-rationality]), which is why this
layer can be stated over any coefficient theory in which the three inputs
hold.
