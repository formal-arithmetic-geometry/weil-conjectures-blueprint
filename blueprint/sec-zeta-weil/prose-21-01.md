The main results of this section are the purity of the cohomology of
a smooth proper scheme over $\mathbf F_q$
(Corollary [sk-input-weights]) and the Weil conjectures
(Theorem [sk-thm-weil-final]). For purity, we obtain an upper
bound for each Frobenius eigenvalue from the weight bound for $Rf_!$
(Theorem [sk-cor-dl-general-complex]). By Frobenius-equivariant
Poincaré duality, the same bound for the dual sheaf is a lower
bound. To show that the characteristic polynomials of Frobenius on
cohomology have integer coefficients and do not depend on $\ell$, we
recover them from the zeta function, which does not involve $\ell$.
Since its Taylor coefficients are integers, it has a unique
reduced fraction of integral polynomials with constant coefficients
one, and purity separates the factors of this fraction by cohomological
degree.
