By Corollary [sk-cor-tf-point-counting],
$Z(X_0,T)\in\mathbf Q(T)\cap(1+T\mathbf Z[[T]])$ for every
separated $X_0$ of finite type over $\mathbf F_q$. Hence
Lemma [sk-lem-wweil-integer-rationality] applies, and we write
$Z(X_0,T)=A/B$ for the resulting fraction, with $A$ and $B$ coprime in
$\mathbf Z[T]$ and $A(0)=B(0)=1$.
