In the situation of [prop-weight-purity], for every
$0\leq i\leq2d$ every
reciprocal root
$\alpha\in\Qlb$ of the polynomial $P_{i,\ell}$ of the
smooth-proper
cohomological family satisfies

$$
|\iota(\alpha)|=q^{i/2}.
$$
