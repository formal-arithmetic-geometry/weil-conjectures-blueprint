On every separated finite-type $k_0$-scheme $X_0$, with $X=X_0\times_{k_0}k$,
the constant Weil sheaf $\QbarX=p_X^*\Qlb$
([det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients]) with its canonical Weil structure
([sk-def-sf-weil-complexes]) is punctually $\iota$-pure of weight zero.
