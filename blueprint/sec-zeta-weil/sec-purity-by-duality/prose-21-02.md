title = "Purity by duality"

For a proper map, $Rf_!=Rf_*$, and the upper bound of
Theorem [sk-thm-dl-general-sheaf] applies to ordinary cohomology. If
the map is also smooth, Poincaré duality on each fibre pairs
$H^i(X_{\bar y},F)$ perfectly with $H^{2d-i}(X_{\bar y},F^\vee(d))$, and
the eigenvalues of the second group are the inverses of those of the
first. The second group has weights at most $(-w-2d)+(2d-i)=-w-i$,
and this upper bound becomes the lower bound $w+i$ for the first. Both hypotheses are needed. Without
properness the example $\mathbf G_m$ shows that $H^1_c$ can have weight
$0$ instead of $1$, and without smoothness duality fails: the nodal
cubic is proper, and its $H^1$ is a line on which Frobenius acts by
$\pm1$, of weight $0$. The relative statement costs nothing extra,
because the bound is checked at one closed point $y$ at a time, on the
smooth proper fibre over $\kappa(y)$. Corollary [sk-input-weights]
is the case of a point as base and the constant sheaf, and it is the
form in which purity enters the proof of
Theorem [sk-thm-weil-final].
