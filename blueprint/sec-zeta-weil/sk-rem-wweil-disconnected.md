The theorem [sk-thm-weil-final] extends to every smooth proper $X_0$ of pure
dimension $d$. Poincaré duality holds without connectedness: the trace
$H^{2d}(X,\Ql)\to\Ql(-d)$ is the sum of the traces of the connected
components, and the cup product pairing is still perfect and compatible
with Frobenius, so (1), (2), (4) and $b_i=b_{2d-i}$ hold with the same
proofs. Alternatively one reduces to the geometrically connected case:
each connected component of $X_0$ is geometrically connected over its
field of constants $\mathbf F_{q^r}$, and
$Z(X_0/\mathbf F_q,T)=Z(X_0/\mathbf F_{q^r},T^r)$. Only the endpoint
factors change: a Frobenius orbit of length $r$ on the components of $X$
contributes $1-T^r$ to $P_0$ and $1-q^{dr}T^r$ to $P_{2d}$.
