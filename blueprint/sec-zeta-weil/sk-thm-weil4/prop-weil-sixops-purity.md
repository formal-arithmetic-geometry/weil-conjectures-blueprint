In the setting of [prop-functor-package], assume $f$ is
exceptional-adjoint admissible. If $f$ is smooth and each geometric
fiber has dimension $r\in\mathbf Z_{\geq0}$, and
$L\in D^b_{ctf}(Y,\Lambda)$, then

$$
Rf^!L\xrightarrow{\sim}Lf^*L(r)[2r]
$$

for the coefficient-twist convention of [def-coefficient-twists-detailed].

## Proof

The assertion is item (6) of [prop-functor-package] of the terminal assembly, on exactly the domains stated there.
