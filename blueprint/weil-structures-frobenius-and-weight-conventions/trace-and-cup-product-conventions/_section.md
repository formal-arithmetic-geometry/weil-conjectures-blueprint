The Poincare duality data, fixed as a convention rather than proved here: an
orientation isomorphism $\eta_X:Ra_X^!\Ql\xrightarrow{\sim}(\Ql)_X(d)[2d]$
for the smooth structure morphism, the trace map it induces, the cup product,
and the resulting pairing $\langle-,-\rangle_i$ in complementary degrees
[def-weil-poincare-trace-cup]. The definition assumes the structure morphism
is exceptional-adjoint admissible; that is an assumption here, not a
consequence.
