The local operator $F_y$ attached to a closed point
[def-weil-local-frobenius-trace] is built by iterating the Weil isomorphism
$\deg(y)$ times; it depends on a chosen embedding
$\kappa(y)\hookrightarrow k$, and only its conjugacy class does not
[lem-weil-local-frobenius-conjugacy-class]. The section then fixes the family
of cohomology spaces with Frobenius that the conjectures are about
[def-weil-smooth-proper-cohomological-family] and the root conventions used
for their characteristic polynomials
[def-weil-ordinary-reciprocal-roots].
