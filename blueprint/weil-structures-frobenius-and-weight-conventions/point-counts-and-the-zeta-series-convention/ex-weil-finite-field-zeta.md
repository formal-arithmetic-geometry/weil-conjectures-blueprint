For the finite-field datum $p,a,q,k_0,k$, where $q=p^a$, fix an indeterminate
$T$ and put $Y_0=\Spec\Fq$. The point-count and zeta definitions
[def-weil-point-counts-zeta] give $N_n(Y_0)=1$, $Z(Y_0,T)=(1-T)^{-1}$, and
one closed point of degree one; the corresponding closed-point product is

$$
 Z(Y_0,T)=(1-T)^{-1}.
$$
