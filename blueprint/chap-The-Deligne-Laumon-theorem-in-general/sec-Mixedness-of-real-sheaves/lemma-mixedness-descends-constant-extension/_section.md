Let $X_0$ be a separated scheme of finite type over $k_0$, let
$g:X'_0=X_0\otimes_{k_0}k_m\to X_0$ be a finite constant extension, and
let $G$ be a constructible Weil sheaf on $X_0$. If $g^*G$ is mixed,
then $G$ is mixed.
