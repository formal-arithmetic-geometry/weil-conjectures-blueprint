Let $X_0$ be a quasi-separated scheme of finite type over $k_0=\Fq$,
let $j:U_0\to X_0$ be a quasi-compact open immersion, and let
$i:Z_0\to X_0$ be a closed immersion whose image is the complement
of the image of $j$. Fix a prime $\ell\nmid q$ and
$\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
For a constructible Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf $G$ on
$X_0$ and $w\in\mathbf R$, if $j^*G$ and $i^*G$ are
$\iota$-mixed of weights at most $w$, then $G$ is
$\iota$-mixed of weights at most $w$. In particular, if $j^*G$ and
$i^*G$ are $\iota$-mixed, then so is $G$.
