Let $Y_0$ be separated of finite type over $k_0=\Fq$, and let
$p:\mathbf A^1_{Y_0}\to Y_0$ be the projection. Fix a prime
$\ell\nmid q$ and an embedding $\iota:\Qbarl\hookrightarrow\C$.
If $G$ is a constructible Weil $\Qbarl$-sheaf on
$\mathbf A^1_{Y_0}$, mixed of weights at most $w\in\R$, then
$R^ip_!G$ is mixed of weights at most $w+i$ for every $i\geq0$.
All weights are taken with respect to $\iota$.
