Let $X_0$ be a scheme locally of finite type over $\Fq$, fix a prime
$\ell\nmid q$ and an embedding
$\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$, and let
$u\in\mathbf R$. Let
$F'\xrightarrow{a}F\xrightarrow{b}F''$ be exact at $F$ in the abelian
category of constructible Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaves on
$X_0$. If $F'$ and $F''$ are $\iota$-mixed of weights at most $u$, then
so is $F$.
