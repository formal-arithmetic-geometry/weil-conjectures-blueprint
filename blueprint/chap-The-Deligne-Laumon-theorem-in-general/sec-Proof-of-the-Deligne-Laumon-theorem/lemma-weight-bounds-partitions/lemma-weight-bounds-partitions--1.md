Let $j:U_0\to X_0$ be an open immersion with closed complement
$i:Z_0\to X_0$. If $R^q(fj)_!j^*K$ and $R^q(fi)_!i^*K$ are mixed of
weights at most $w+q$ for every $q$, then so is $R^qf_!K$.
