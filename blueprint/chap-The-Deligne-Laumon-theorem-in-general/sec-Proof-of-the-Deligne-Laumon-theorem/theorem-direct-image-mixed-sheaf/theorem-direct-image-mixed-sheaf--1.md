Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell$ be a prime not dividing $q$, and fix an embedding
$\iota:\Qbarl\hookrightarrow\C$. Let $f:X_0\to Y_0$ be a separated
morphism of finite type over $k_0$, with $Y_0$ separated of finite type
over $k_0$. For every $w\in\R$ and every constructible Weil
$\Qbarl$-sheaf $F$ on $X_0$ which is $\iota$-mixed of weights at most
$w$, the sheaf $R^if_!F$ is $\iota$-mixed of weights at most $w+i$
for every $i\geq0$.
