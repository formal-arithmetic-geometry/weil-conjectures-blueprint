Let $h:U_0\to Y_0$ be smooth separated of relative dimension one,
with affine geometric fibres, and let $F$ be a lisse Weil sheaf on
$U_0$, pure of weight $w$. Then $R^ih_!F$ is mixed of weights at most
$w+i$.
