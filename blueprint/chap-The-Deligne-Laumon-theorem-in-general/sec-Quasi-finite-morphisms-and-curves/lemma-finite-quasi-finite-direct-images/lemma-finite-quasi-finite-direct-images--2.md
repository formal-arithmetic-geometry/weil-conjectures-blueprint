Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k_0)$ be prime, and fix
$\iota:\Qbarl\hookrightarrow\C$. Let $g:Z_0\to Y_0$ be a separated
morphism of finite type between schemes of finite type over $k_0$,
with $Y_0$ quasi-separated. Suppose that for every $v\in\R$ and every
constructible Weil sheaf $F$ mixed of weights at most $v$, the sheaf
$R^rg_!F$ is mixed of weights at most $v+r$ for all $r\geq0$.
Then

$$
Rg_!D_{\leq w}(Z_0)\subset D_{\leq w}(Y_0)
\qquad(w\in\R).
$$

In particular, this inclusion holds when $g$ is quasi-finite.
