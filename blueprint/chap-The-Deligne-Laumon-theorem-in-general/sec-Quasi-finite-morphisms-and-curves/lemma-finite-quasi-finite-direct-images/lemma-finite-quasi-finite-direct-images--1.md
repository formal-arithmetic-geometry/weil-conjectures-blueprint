Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k_0)$ be prime, and fix
$\iota:\Qbarl\hookrightarrow\C$. Let $g:Z_0\to Y_0$ be a separated,
quasi-finite morphism of schemes of finite type over $k_0$, with $Y_0$
quasi-separated. For every constructible Weil sheaf $F$ on $Z_0$,

$$
R^rg_!F=0\qquad(r>0).
$$

If $F$ is mixed of weights at most $w\in\R$, then $Rg_!F$, with $F$
placed in degree zero, belongs to $D_{\leq w}(Y_0)$. In particular,
$R^0g_!F$ is mixed of weights at most $w$.
