Let $\Pi$ be profinite and let $\rho:\Pi\to\GL_E(V)$
be continuous and absolutely irreducible, with $\dim_E V=n$.
There are a $\Pi$-stable lattice $L\subset V$ and an integer $N$
such that every subgroup $\Pi'\subseteq\Pi$ with the same image as
$\Pi$ in $\GL(L/\pi_E^NL)$ acts absolutely irreducibly
on $V$.
