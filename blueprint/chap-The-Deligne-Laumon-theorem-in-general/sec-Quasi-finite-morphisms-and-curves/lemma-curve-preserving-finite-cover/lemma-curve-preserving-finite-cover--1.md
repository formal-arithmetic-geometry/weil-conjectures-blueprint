Let $X$ be a smooth connected affine scheme over
$\overline{\mathbf F}_q$, of dimension at least two. Let $T\to X$
be a connected finite étale cover and let $S\subset X$ be a nonempty
finite set of closed geometric points. There is a smooth connected closed
affine curve $C\subset X$ containing $S$, with $T\times_XC$ connected.
The curve and its inclusion are defined over a finite field.
