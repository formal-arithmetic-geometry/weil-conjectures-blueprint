Let $X_0$ be a smooth geometrically connected affine scheme over
$\mathbf F_q$, of relative dimension $d\ge2$, and put
$X=X_0\times_{\mathbf F_q}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$. Let $F$ be the
geometric Galois Frobenius of $X$. Let $T\to X$ be a finite étale
morphism with $T$ connected, and let $S$ be a nonempty finite set of
$\overline{\mathbf F}_q$-points of $X$.

There is a closed immersion $i:C\hookrightarrow X$, where $C$ is
affine, connected and smooth of relative dimension one over
$\overline{\mathbf F}_q$, such that every point of $S$ factors through
$i$ and $T\times_XC$ is connected. Moreover, there are an integer
$m>0$ and an automorphism of schemes $\varphi:C\xrightarrow{\sim}C$
such that
$$
i\circ\varphi=F^m\circ i.
$$
