For a lisse sheaf on $X$ the finite quotient corresponds to a
connected finite Galois cover $T\to X$. If $C\subset X$ is a curve with
$T\times_XC$ connected, then the fundamental group of $C$ surjects onto
the Galois group of $T$. What we need, then, are curves through given
points whose inverse image in a given connected cover stays connected.
