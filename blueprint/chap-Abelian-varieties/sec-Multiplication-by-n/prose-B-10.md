For a [symmetric](@definition-abelian-variety) ample $\mathcal N$, the degree of [$[n]$](@definition-abelian-variety) can be read off from
the growth of $\chi(A,\mathcal N^{\otimes m})$, since pulling back by
[$[n]$](@definition-abelian-variety) multiplies $m$ by $n^2$ and $\chi$ grows like $m^g$.
