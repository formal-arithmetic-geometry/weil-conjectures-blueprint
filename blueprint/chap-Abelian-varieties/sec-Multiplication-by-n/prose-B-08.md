We use three facts: commutativity, which follows from [rigidity](@lemma-rigidity); a
[symmetric](@definition-abelian-variety) ample sheaf, which follows from projectivity; and the formula
for $[n]^*\mathcal L$, which follows from the [cube](@theorem-theorem-cube). [Rigidity](@lemma-rigidity) and the [cube](@theorem-theorem-cube) require $A$ to be geometrically integral,
which we check together with commutativity.
