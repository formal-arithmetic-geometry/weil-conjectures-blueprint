We use three facts: commutativity, which follows from rigidity; a
symmetric ample sheaf, which follows from projectivity; and the formula
for $[n]^*\mathcal L$, which follows from the cube. Rigidity and the cube require $A$ to be geometrically integral,
which we check together with commutativity.
