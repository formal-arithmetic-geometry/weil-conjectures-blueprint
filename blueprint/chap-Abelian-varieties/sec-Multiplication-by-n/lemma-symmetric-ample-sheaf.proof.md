Every group scheme of finite type over a field is quasi-projective
[Stacks, [0BF7](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BF7)]. A quasi-projective
proper scheme is projective [Stacks, [0B45](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B45)]. If $\mathcal L$ is ample, then
so is $[-1]^*\mathcal L$, since $[-1]$ is an automorphism, and the
tensor product $\mathcal N=\mathcal L\otimes[-1]^*\mathcal L$ of two ample
sheaves is ample. It is symmetric because $[-1]$ is an involution, which
interchanges the two factors.
