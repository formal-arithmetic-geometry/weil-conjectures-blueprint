Every group scheme of finite type over a field is quasi-projective
[Stacks, [0BF7](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BF7)]. A quasi-projective
proper scheme is projective [Stacks, [0B45](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B45)]. If $\mathcal L$ is ample, then
so is $\href{@definition-abelian-variety}{[-1]^*}\mathcal L$, since [$[-1]$](@definition-abelian-variety) is an automorphism, and the
tensor product $\mathcal N=\mathcal L\otimes\href{@definition-abelian-variety}{[-1]^*}\mathcal L$ of two ample
sheaves is ample. It is [symmetric](@definition-abelian-variety) because [$[-1]$](@definition-abelian-variety) is an involution, which
interchanges the two factors.
