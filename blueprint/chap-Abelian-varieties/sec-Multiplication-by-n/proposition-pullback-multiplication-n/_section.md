Let $A$ be an [abelian variety](@definition-abelian-variety) over $k$ and let $\mathcal L$ be an
invertible sheaf on $A$. For every integer $n\geq0$,

$$
\href{@definition-abelian-variety}{[n]^*}\mathcal L\simeq
\mathcal L^{\otimes n(n+1)/2}\otimes
(\href{@definition-abelian-variety}{[-1]^*}\mathcal L)^{\otimes n(n-1)/2}.
$$

In particular $\href{@definition-abelian-variety}{[n]^*}\mathcal N\simeq\mathcal N^{\otimes n^2}$ if
$\mathcal N$ is [symmetric](@definition-abelian-variety).
