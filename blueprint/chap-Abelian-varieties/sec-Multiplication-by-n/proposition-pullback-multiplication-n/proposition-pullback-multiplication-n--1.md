Let $A$ be an abelian variety over $k$ and let $\mathcal L$ be an
invertible sheaf on $A$. For every integer $n\geq0$,

$$
[n]^*\mathcal L\simeq
\mathcal L^{\otimes n(n+1)/2}\otimes
([-1]^*\mathcal L)^{\otimes n(n-1)/2}.
$$
