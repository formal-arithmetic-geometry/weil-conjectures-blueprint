Let $A$ be an abelian variety over $k$ and let $\mathcal N$ be a
symmetric invertible sheaf on $A$, so that
$[-1]^*\mathcal N\simeq\mathcal N$. For every integer $n\geq0$,

$$
[n]^*\mathcal N\simeq\mathcal N^{\otimes n^2}.
$$
