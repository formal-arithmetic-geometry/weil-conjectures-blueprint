Let $A$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$ over $k$. For every
$n>0$, the morphism $[n]$ is surjective and finite, and its kernel
$A[n]$ is finite over $k$, with

$$
\dim_k\Gamma(A[n],\mathcal O_{A[n]})=n^{2g}.
$$
