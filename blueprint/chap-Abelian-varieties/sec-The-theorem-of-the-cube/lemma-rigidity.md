Let $X$ be a proper geometrically integral $k$-scheme, let $Y$ be a
$k$-scheme of finite type, let $Z$ be a $k$-scheme, and let
$h:X\times Y\to Z$ be a morphism. If $h$ maps $X\times y_0$ to a single
point for some $y_0\in Y$, then there are an open neighbourhood $V$ of
$y_0$ and a morphism $g:V\to Z$ with $h=g\circ\operatorname{pr}_2$ on
$X\times V$.
