Let $P$ and $Q$ be proper geometrically integral $k$-schemes with points
$p_0\in P(k)$ and $q_0\in Q(k)$, let $T$ be a $k$-scheme,
and let $\mathcal L$ be an invertible sheaf on $P\times Q\times T$ whose
restrictions to $p_0\times Q\times T$ and to $P\times q_0\times T$ are
trivial. Write $f:P\times Q\times T\to T$ for the projection. If
$\mathcal L_t\simeq\mathcal O_{P\times Q\times\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(t)}$
for a point $t\in T$, then there are an open neighbourhood $U$ of $t$
and a section
$$
\sigma\in H^0(f^{-1}(U),\mathcal L)
$$
which freely generates $\mathcal L|_{f^{-1}(U)}$. Thus multiplication
by $\sigma$ gives an isomorphism
$\mathcal O_{f^{-1}(U)}\simeq\mathcal L|_{f^{-1}(U)}$.
