Let $P$ and $Q$ be proper geometrically integral $k$-schemes with points
$p_0\in P(k)$ and $q_0\in Q(k)$, let $T$ be a $k$-scheme of finite type,
and let $\mathcal L$ be an invertible sheaf on $P\times Q\times T$ whose
restrictions to $p_0\times Q\times T$ and to $P\times q_0\times T$ are
trivial. If $\mathcal L$ is trivial on the fibre over a point $t\in T$,
then $\mathcal L$ is trivial on $P\times Q\times U$ for an open
neighbourhood $U$ of $t$.
