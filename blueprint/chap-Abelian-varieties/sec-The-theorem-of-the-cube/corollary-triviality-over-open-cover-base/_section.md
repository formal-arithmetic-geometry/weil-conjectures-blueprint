order = 1472
uses = ["lemma-triviality-fibres"]

With the notation of Lemma [lemma-triviality-fibres], suppose that
$\mathcal L$ is trivial on $P\times U_\alpha$ for an open cover
$(U_\alpha)$ of $T$. Then $f_*\mathcal L$ is invertible and the
evaluation map $f^*f_*\mathcal L\to\mathcal L$ is an isomorphism.
