Lean bypasses the restriction isomorphism of
[lemma-h-1-structure-sheaf-product] and uses the following vanishing
statement.

Let $K$ be a field, let $P$ and $Q$ be proper geometrically integral
$K$-schemes, and let $p_0\in P(K)$ and $q_0\in Q(K)$. Let $\mathcal J$
be the ideal sheaf of the union of the two slices
$p_0\times Q$ and $P\times q_0$ in $P\times_K Q$. Then

$$
H^1(P\times_K Q,\mathcal J)=0.
$$
