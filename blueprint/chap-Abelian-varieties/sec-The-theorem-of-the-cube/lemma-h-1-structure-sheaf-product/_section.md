title = "$H^1$ of the structure sheaf of a product"
order = 1481
+++
Let $K$ be a field, let $P$ and $Q$ be proper $K$-schemes with
$H^0(P,\mathcal O_P)=H^0(Q,\mathcal O_Q)=K$, and let $p_0\in P(K)$ and
$q_0\in Q(K)$. Restriction to the slices $P\times q_0$ and $p_0\times Q$
is an isomorphism

$$
H^1(P\times Q,\mathcal O)\longrightarrow
H^1(P,\mathcal O)\oplus H^1(Q,\mathcal O).
$$
