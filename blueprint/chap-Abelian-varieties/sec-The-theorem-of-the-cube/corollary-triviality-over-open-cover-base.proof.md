Both assertions are local on $T$, so we may replace $T$ by one of the
$U_\alpha$ and assume that $\mathcal L\simeq\mathcal O_{P\times T}$.
Since $H^0(P,\mathcal O_P)=k$ [Stacks, [0BUG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BUG)], and $T$ is flat over $k$,
flat base change gives $f_*\mathcal O_{P\times T}=\mathcal O_T$. Hence
the chosen trivialization identifies $f_*\mathcal L$ with the
invertible sheaf $\mathcal O_T$. Under the same identification the
evaluation map $f^*f_*\mathcal L\to\mathcal L$ becomes the identity of
$\mathcal O_{P\times T}$.
