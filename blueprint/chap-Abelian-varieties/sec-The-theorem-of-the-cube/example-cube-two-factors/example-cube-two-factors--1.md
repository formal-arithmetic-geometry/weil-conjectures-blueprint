Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over an algebraically closed field, with
origin $O$, and let $\Delta\subset E\times E$ be the diagonal. On
$E\times E$ put

$$
\mathcal L=\mathcal O_{E\times E}\bigl(\Delta-E\times\{O\}-\{O\}\times E\bigr).
$$

Consider the slice $E\times\{t\}$. The diagonal meets it in the point
$t$, and the divisor $\{O\}\times E$ meets it in the point $O$. The
divisor $E\times\{O\}$ is pulled back from the second factor, so it
contributes the trivial sheaf for every $t$.
