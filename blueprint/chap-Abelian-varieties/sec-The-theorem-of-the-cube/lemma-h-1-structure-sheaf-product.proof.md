By the Künneth formula [Stacks, [0BED](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BED)], and since $H^0$ of $\mathcal O$ is
$K$ on both factors, the source is
$\operatorname{pr}_1^*H^1(P,\mathcal O)\oplus
\operatorname{pr}_2^*H^1(Q,\mathcal O)$.
Restriction to $P\times q_0$ is the identity on the first summand. It
kills the second, because on the second summand it factors through
$H^1$ of the point $q_0$. Restriction to $p_0\times Q$ does the
opposite.
