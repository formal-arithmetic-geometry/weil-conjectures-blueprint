Let $f:X\to T$ be proper, flat and locally of finite presentation,
with geometrically integral fibres, and let $\mathcal M$ and
$\mathcal N$ be invertible sheaves on $X$. Then

$$
\{t\in T\mid\mathcal M_t\simeq\mathcal N_t\}
$$

is closed in $T$. Taking $X=P\times_k T$, $\mathcal M=\mathcal L$
and $\mathcal N=\mathcal O_X$, the set of $t\in T$ such that $\mathcal L_t$ is trivial is closed.
