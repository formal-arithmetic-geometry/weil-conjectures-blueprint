Let $P$ be a proper geometrically integral $k$-scheme, let $T$ be a
$k$-scheme of finite type, let $f:P\times T\to T$ be the projection,
and let $\mathcal L$ be an invertible sheaf on $P\times T$. For $t\in T$
write $P_t=P\times_k\kappa(t)$ and $\mathcal L_t=\mathcal L|_{P_t}$.

The clauses below are stated more generally, for a proper morphism $f:X\to T$ with
geometrically integral fibres. There we write $X_t=X\times_T\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(t)$,
$\mathcal F_t=\mathcal F|_{X_t}$ and
$h^0(X_t,\mathcal F_t)=\dim_{\kappa(t)}H^0(X_t,\mathcal F_t)$.
For closedness we also require $f$ to be flat and locally of finite
presentation. These hypotheses hold for the projection $P\times_k T\to T$,
even without the finite type assumption on $T$.
