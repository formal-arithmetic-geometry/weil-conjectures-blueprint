This quotient identification is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main
theorem: the available Tate-module rank theorem does not identify the
kernel of the canonical projection.

Let $A$ be an abelian variety over $k$, let $\ell$ be a prime invertible
in $k$, and let $n\geq0$.
The canonical projection induces an isomorphism
$T_\ell A/\ell^nT_\ell A\simeq A[\ell^n](\bar k)$.
