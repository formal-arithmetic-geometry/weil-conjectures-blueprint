Let $A$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$ over an algebraically
closed field $k$ of characteristic $p$, and let $\ell$ be a prime
with $\ell\ne p$. There is a noncanonical isomorphism of
$\mathbf Z_\ell$-modules

$$
T_\ell A\simeq\mathbf Z_\ell^{2g}.
$$
