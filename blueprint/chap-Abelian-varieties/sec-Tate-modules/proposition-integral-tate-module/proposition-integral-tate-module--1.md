Let $A$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$ over an algebraically
closed field $k$ of characteristic $p$. For every integer $m>0$ with
$p\nmid m$, there is a noncanonical isomorphism of abelian groups

$$
A(k)[m]\simeq(\mathbf Z/m)^{2g}.
$$

Here $A(k)[m]$ denotes the subgroup of $A(k)$ killed by $m$.
Taking $m=\ell^n$ gives the finite-level assertion for every prime
$\ell\ne p$ and every $n\geq0$.
