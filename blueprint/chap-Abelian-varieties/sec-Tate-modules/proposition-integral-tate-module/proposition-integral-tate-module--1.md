Let $A$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$ over $k$, and let $\ell$
be a prime invertible in $k$. There are noncanonical isomorphisms

$$
A[\ell^n](\bar k)\simeq(\mathbf Z/\ell^n)^{2g}.
$$
