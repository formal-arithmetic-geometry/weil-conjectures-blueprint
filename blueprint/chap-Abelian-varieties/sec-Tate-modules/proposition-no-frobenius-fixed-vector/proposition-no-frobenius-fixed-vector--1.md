Let $A_0/\mathbf F_q$ be an abelian variety, let
$A=A_0\times_{\mathbf F_q}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$, and let $F$
be geometric Frobenius on $T_\ell A$. For every $m>0$,

$$
(T_\ell A)^{F^m}=0.
$$
