Over a finite field, Frobenius acts on $T_\ell A$, and by the last
proposition this action is compatible with its action on each
$A[\ell^n](\bar k)$. No power of Frobenius has a nonzero fixed vector.
For the Jacobian of a curve, this is the input to
Proposition [proposition-no-fixed-class-degree-one].
