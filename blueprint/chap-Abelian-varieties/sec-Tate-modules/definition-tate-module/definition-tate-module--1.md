Let $A$ be an abelian variety over $k$ and let $\ell$ be a prime
invertible in $k$. The *Tate module* of $A$ is

$$
T_\ell A=\varprojlim_n A[\ell^n](\bar k),
$$

the limit taken along the maps $[\ell]:A[\ell^{n+1}](\bar k)\to
A[\ell^n](\bar k)$,
