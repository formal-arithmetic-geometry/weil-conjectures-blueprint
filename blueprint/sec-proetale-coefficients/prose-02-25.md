Proper base change, proved on the étale site in
Section “Proper base change”, and the direct images used to define
$Rf_!$ pass to the pro-étale site through the comparison of direct images
below. For direct images the comparison needs a hypothesis on $g$. The
comparison map
$\nu_Y^*Rg_{\mathrm{\acute et},*}\to Rg_{\mathrm{pro\acute et},*}\nu_X^*$ of functors
$D^+(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},\Lambda)\to D^+(Y_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda)$ is defined as the
adjoint of the composite of
$g_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}}^*\nu_Y^*Rg_{\mathrm{\acute et},*}\simeq\nu_X^*g_{\mathrm{\acute et}}^*Rg_{\mathrm{\acute et},*}$ with
$\nu_X^*$ applied to the counit of the adjunction $g_{\mathrm{\acute et}}^*\dashv Rg_{\mathrm{\acute et},*}$.
