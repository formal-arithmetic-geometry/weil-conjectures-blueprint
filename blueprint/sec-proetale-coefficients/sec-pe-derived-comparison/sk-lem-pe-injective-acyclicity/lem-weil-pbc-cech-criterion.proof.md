*(1).* Exactness is a local assertion. Locally on a test object $T\to U$, the
sieve generated by $f$ is covering, so after passing to a covering of $T$ the
map $T\to U$ lifts to $V$. Inserting that lift as first coordinate defines a
contracting homotopy $h$ of the augmented complex of free abelian presheaves
evaluated at $T$: the alternating-face differential satisfies
$\partial h+h\partial=1$, since adjacent inserted faces cancel. A local chain
has finite support, so one common covering suffices for all its generators;
the same construction in degree zero shows that $\mathbb Z[V]\to\mathbb Z[U]$
is an epimorphism. Sheafification is exact, so the complex of free abelian
sheaves is exact. By the free-sheaf adjunction
$\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb Z[W],I)=I(W)$, applying
$\operatorname{Hom}(-,I)$ gives the augmented Cech complex of
[def-weil-pbc-cover-cech] along $f$, which is exact because $I$ is injective.

*(2).* We prove by strong induction on $p\geq0$ that every
$\xi\in H^{p+1}(U,F)=\operatorname{Ext}^{p+1}(\mathbb Z[U],F)$ vanishes, for all
$P$-objects $U$. A positive-degree class vanishes locally: there is a covering
sieve $R$ of $U$ on whose members $\xi$ restricts to zero (realize $\xi$ by an
injective resolution; a cocycle is locally a coboundary). Choose $f\colon V\to
U$ as in the hypothesis; then $\xi$ restricts to zero along $f$. By (1) the
complex $\mathbb Z[V_\bullet]$ of the Cech nerve is a resolution of
$\mathbb Z[U]$. The dimension-shifting lemma for $\operatorname{Ext}$ along a
resolution shows that $\xi=0$ provided $\xi$ restricts to zero along the
augmentation, every positive-degree Cech cocycle of $F$ along $f$ is a
coboundary, and $\operatorname{Ext}^{q}(\mathbb Z[V_n],F)=0$ for $0<q\leq p$.
The first two conditions hold by the choice of $f$, and the third by the
induction hypothesis, since every $V_n$ is a $P$-object. This is Cartan's
criterion, compare [Stacks Project, Tag 03F9](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03F9).
