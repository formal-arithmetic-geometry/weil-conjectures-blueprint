For an affine scheme [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions]
$X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, its *patch topology* (constructible topology) is
generated by the quasi-compact open subsets and their complements;
equivalently, by the subsets $D(f)$ and $V(f)$, for $f\in A$. A subset is
*constructible* if it is a finite Boolean combination of quasi-compact open
subsets, equivalently of the subsets $D(f)$. A spectral space $T$ is
*w-local* if its set $T^c$ of closed points is closed and every point of $T$
specializes to exactly one closed point. We call $X$ *w-local* if its
underlying spectral space is w-local.
