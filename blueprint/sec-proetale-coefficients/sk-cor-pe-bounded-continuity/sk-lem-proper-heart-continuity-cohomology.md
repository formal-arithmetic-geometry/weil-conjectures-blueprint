+++
id = "det-proper-heart-continuity-cohomology"
kind = "lemma"
title = "Pro-étale cohomology of a qcqs scheme commutes with filtered colimits"
uses = ["det-proper-heart-continuity-sections", "sk-prop-pe-hypercover"]
+++
Let $Z$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme and $A$ a sheaf of rings on $Z_{\mathrm{proét}}$. For every
$q$ and every filtered diagram (or family) $(M_i)$ of $A$-modules, the natural maps
$\varinjlim_iH^q(Z,M_i)\to H^q(Z,\varinjlim_iM_i)$ and $\bigoplus_iH^q(Z,M_i)\to H^q(Z,\bigoplus_iM_i)$ are
isomorphisms.
