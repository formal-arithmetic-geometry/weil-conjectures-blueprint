uses = ["sk-cor-pe-wc-sections", "sk-cor-pe-sections"]

Let $W$ be an affine weakly contractible object of a pro-étale site and $A$ a sheaf of rings. The functor
$M\mapsto\Gamma(W,M)$ from sheaves of $A$-modules to abelian groups is exact and preserves all colimits; in
particular filtered colimits and direct sums.
