Let $X$ be a geometric point and let $S=\mathbf Z_\ell$ be regarded as
a profinite object of $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$. The completed coefficient sheaf
$\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}$ has sections
$\operatorname{Cont}(S,\mathbf Z_\ell)$ on $S$, among them the
identity function. The pullback $\nu_X^*$ from $X_{\acute{e}t}$ of the
constant sheaf with value the *discrete* abelian group
$\mathbf Z_\ell$ has only locally
constant functions as sections, and these have finite image on the
compact space $S$. Hence the identity function does not come from any
finite stage of the profinite presentation of $S$, and
$\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}$ is not classical. Its reductions
$\mathbf Z/\ell^n$, on the other hand, are classical. Thus étale arguments apply at each
finite level, while the coefficient sheaf itself is defined once on
the pro-étale site.
