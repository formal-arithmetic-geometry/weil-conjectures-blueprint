All identities below are checked after composing with the monomorphism
$\Hom_{\mathcal R}(\mathcal S,N)\to\underline{\Hom}(\mathcal S,N)$ and passing to adjoint
maps $\mathcal S\otimes Y\to N$. In elementwise notation for these maps, the equalizer
condition on $f$ reads $f(\varphi(r)t)=r\cdot f(t)$.
- The action lands in the equalizer: $t\mapsto f(ts)$ satisfies
  $f(\varphi(r)ts)=r\cdot f(ts)$, which is the condition on $f$ at $ts$, using
  associativity of $\mu_{\mathcal S}$.
- The unit and associativity laws of the action follow from those of $\mu_{\mathcal S}$.
- For an $\mathcal R$-linear $g\colon N\to N'$, postcomposition with $g$ preserves the
  equalizer condition and commutes with the $\mathcal S$-action.
