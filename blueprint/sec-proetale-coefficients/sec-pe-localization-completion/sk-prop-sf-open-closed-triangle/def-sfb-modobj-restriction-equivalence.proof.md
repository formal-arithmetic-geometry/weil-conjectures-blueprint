The inverse $\varphi^{-1}$ is again a morphism of monoid objects. Restriction of scalars
([def-sfb-modobj-restriction]) is the identity on underlying objects and morphisms, and
satisfies $\operatorname{Res}_{\mathrm{id}}=\mathrm{id}$ and
$\operatorname{Res}_\varphi\circ\operatorname{Res}_\psi=\operatorname{Res}_{\psi\varphi}$.
Indeed, both sides of the second identity give $N$ the action
$\gamma_N((\psi\varphi)\otimes N)$. Hence
$\operatorname{Res}_{\varphi^{-1}}\circ\operatorname{Res}_\varphi
=\operatorname{Res}_{\varphi\varphi^{-1}}=\mathrm{id}$, and similarly in the other order.
The argument uses only that $\varphi$ is an isomorphism of monoid objects.
