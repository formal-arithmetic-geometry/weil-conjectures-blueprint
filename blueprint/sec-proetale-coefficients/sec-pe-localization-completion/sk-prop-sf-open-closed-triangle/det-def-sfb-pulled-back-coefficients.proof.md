[det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients-definition]. That it is a ring object, that
$\Lambda\mapsto\Lambda_X$ is a functor on ring objects, and that $\Lambda_X$ is
commutative when $\Lambda$ is, is [det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients-ring].
