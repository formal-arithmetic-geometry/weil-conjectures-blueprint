The derived tensor product $-\otimes^{\mathrm L}-$ of [def-weil-derived-tensor],
together with the lax monoidal structure map of the localization functor, is the left
derived functor in two variables of the tensor product of complexes of pro-étale sheaves,
with respect to the quasi-isomorphisms in each variable.
