Identities are morphisms of ring objects. If $f\colon R\to R'$ and $g\colon R'\to R''$ are
morphisms of ring objects, then
$gf\mu=g\mu'(f\otimes f)=\mu''(g\otimes g)(f\otimes f)=\mu''(gf\otimes gf)$ by functoriality of
$\otimes$, and $gf\eta=g\eta'=\eta''$. So composites are morphisms, composition is associative and
unital because it is so in $\mathcal C$, and the forgetful functor is faithful because a
morphism of ring objects is a morphism of $\mathcal C$ satisfying conditions.
