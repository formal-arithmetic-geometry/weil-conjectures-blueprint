By [det-prop-sfb-module-category], $\Mod_R(\mathcal C)$ is a closed symmetric monoidal
Grothendieck abelian category for $\otimes_R$, with functorial flat resolutions, so
[lem-sfb-derived-monoidal] applies. For $\mathcal C=\Sh(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Ab)$, the ring
object $R=\Lambda_X$ of [det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients] is commutative.
