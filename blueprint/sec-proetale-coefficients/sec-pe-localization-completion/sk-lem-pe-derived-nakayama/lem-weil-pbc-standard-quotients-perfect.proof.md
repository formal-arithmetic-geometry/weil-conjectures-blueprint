Multiplication by $\varpi^n$ is injective because $\varpi$, hence $\varpi^n$,
is a nonzerodivisor; its image is the ideal $\varpi^nR$, which is the kernel of
the quotient map, and the quotient map is surjective. The displayed complex is
therefore a resolution of the quotient by finite free modules, which is what
perfectness means. A uniformizer of the discrete valuation ring
$\mathcal O_E$ is a nonzerodivisor, giving the special cases.
