title = "Cohomology of the truncation tower"

Let $K$ be a complex of $\mathcal R$-modules on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ and let
$\tau_{\geq-n}K$ be its canonical truncations, with the quotient maps
$K\to\tau_{\geq-n}K$ and $\tau_{\geq-(n+1)}K\to\tau_{\geq-n}K$. Fix $q\in\mathbf Z$.

1. $H^q(\tau_{\geq-n}K)=0$ for $n<-q$, and for $n\geq-q$ the map
   $H^q(K)\to H^q(\tau_{\geq-n}K)$ is an isomorphism; the transition maps
   $H^q(\tau_{\geq-(n+1)}K)\to H^q(\tau_{\geq-n}K)$ are identities (under these
   isomorphisms) for $n\geq-q$ and maps to $0$ for $n<-q$. In particular they are all
   epimorphisms.
2. $R^1\varprojlim_nH^q(\tau_{\geq-n}K)=0$ and
   $H^q(K)\to\varprojlim_nH^q(\tau_{\geq-n}K)$ is an isomorphism.
