For every complex $K$ of $\mathcal R$-modules on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$, the map of
complexes

$$
\iota\colon K\to R\varprojlim_n\tau_{\geq-n}K=\operatorname{Cone}\Bigl(\prod_n\tau_{\geq-n}K
\xrightarrow{1-T}\prod_n\tau_{\geq-n}K\Bigr)[-1],\qquad k\mapsto\bigl((\bar k_n)_n,0\bigr),
$$

where $\bar k_n$ is the image of $k$ in $\tau_{\geq-n}K$, is a quasi-isomorphism.
