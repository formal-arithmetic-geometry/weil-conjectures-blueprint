Represent $K$ by a complex and form its canonical truncation tower, with maps
$K\to\tau_{\geq-n}K$ and $\tau_{\geq-(n+1)}K\to\tau_{\geq-n}K$. For fixed $q$, the sheaves
$H^q(\tau_{\geq-n}K)$ and $H^{q-1}(\tau_{\geq-n}K)$ are eventually constant, with identity
transition maps, equal to the corresponding cohomology sheaves of $K$
([det-pe-left-complete-truncation-tower]). In the Milnor sequence of [sk-prop-pe-milnor]
the $R^1$ term therefore vanishes and the limit term is $H^q(K)$. The natural map induces
this identification in every degree, so it is an isomorphism
([det-pe-left-complete-iso]). This is Stacks, Tag 0947(2).
