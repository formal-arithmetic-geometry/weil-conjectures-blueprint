A finitely presented étale $\Omega$-algebra is a finite product of copies of
$\Omega$, so filtered colimits of such algebras correspond, under $\Spec$, to
inverse limits of finite sets ([det-pe-condensed-point-basis]). By the affine
pro-étale basis theorem ([sk-prop-pe-affine-basis]) sheaves are determined on
this basis. Faithfully flat covering maps become surjections of profinite sets,
and quasi-compactness gives the finite-cover condition
([det-pe-condensed-point-topology]). This proves the equivalence of sites. The
description of the coefficient sheaves is
[det-pe-condensed-point-coefficients].
