Let $\Omega$ be a separably closed field. The affine pro-étale basis of
$\Spec\Omega$ is equivalent to the category of profinite sets, with finite
jointly surjective families as covers. Its topos is consequently the topos of
condensed sets. A discrete scalar ring $A$ gives the classical constant sheaf,
while a profinite or locally profinite coefficient ring gives its sheaf of
continuous functions on these profinite test objects.
