Let $S$ be a profinite set and let $LC(S,\Z)$ be the ring of locally constant
functions $S\to\Z$. Then the morphism $\Spec LC(S,\Z)\to\Spec\Z$ is weakly
étale ([def-weil-scheme-morphism-properties]).
