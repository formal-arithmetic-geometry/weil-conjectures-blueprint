Every presheaf $F$ of abelian groups on $*_{\mathrm{proét}}$ has a left Kan
extension $\operatorname{Lan}_{u^{\mathrm{op}}}F$ along the opposite of the
functor $u$ of [def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale].
