Let $F$ be a sheaf on $*_{\mathrm{proét}}$ and $G$ a sheaf on
$(\Spec\Z)_{\mathrm{proét}}$. Sheafification is left adjoint to the inclusion
of sheaves into presheaves, and $\operatorname{Lan}_{u^{\mathrm{op}}}$ is left
adjoint to restriction along $u^{\mathrm{op}}$. Hence
$$
\Hom(p^*F,G)=\Hom(\operatorname{Lan}_{u^{\mathrm{op}}}F,G)=\Hom(F,G\circ u^{\mathrm{op}})=\Hom(F,p_*G),
$$
naturally in $F$ and $G$.
