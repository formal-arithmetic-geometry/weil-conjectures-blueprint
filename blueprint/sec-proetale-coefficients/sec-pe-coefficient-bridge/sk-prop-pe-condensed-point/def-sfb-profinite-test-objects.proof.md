Weakly étale morphisms are stable under base change. So base change along
$\pi_X$ is a functor $(\Spec\Z)_{\mathrm{proét}}\to X_{\mathrm{proét}}$
([def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]), and $u_X$ is a composite of two functors.

For the formula, $\Spec LC(S,\Z)=\lim_i\coprod_{S_i}\Spec\Z$ is a cofiltered
limit along affine transition morphisms. Fibre products commute with such
limits and with finite coproducts, which gives the formula.
