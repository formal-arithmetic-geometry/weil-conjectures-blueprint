The value of the pointwise left Kan extension at $V$ is the colimit of
$F(S)$ over the comma category of pairs $(S,\ V\to u(S))$. Both sites are
essentially small with respect to the universe in which the abelian groups
take values. So these comma categories are essentially small, and the
colimits exist because $\Ab$ is cocomplete.
