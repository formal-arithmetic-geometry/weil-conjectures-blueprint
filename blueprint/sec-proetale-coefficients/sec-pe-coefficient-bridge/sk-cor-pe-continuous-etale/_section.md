In Proposition [sk-prop-pe-continuous-cohomology], suppose that
$F_n=\nu_X^*F_{n,\acute{e}t}$ for an inverse system $(F_{n,\acute{e}t})$ of abelian
sheaves on $X_{\acute{e}t}$ with epimorphic transition maps. Then
$R\Gamma(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},F)$ is the continuous étale cohomology of
$(F_{n,\acute{e}t})$: the right derived functor of
$(G_n)\mapsto\Gamma(X_{\acute{e}t},\varprojlim_nG_n)$ on the abelian category
of inverse systems, evaluated at $(F_{n,\acute{e}t})$.
