The maps are isomorphisms by assumption. Each of the remaining identities is checked after
transposing along $P\dashv S$
([def-sfb-adjunction-has-projection-formula]). Naturality in $L$ and in $M$ becomes
naturality of $\mu^S$ and of the unit. The unit compatibility becomes unitality of $\mu^S$
together with a triangle identity. The associativity compatibility becomes the
associativity axiom of $\mu^S$ together with naturality.
