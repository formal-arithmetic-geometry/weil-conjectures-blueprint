title = "Cohomology on non-contractible objects"
order = 979
+++
Cohomology on other objects need not vanish, since such an object is in general not
weakly contractible. For instance, a fibre product of two such affines
need not be weakly contractible, so the Čech complex of a cover by
them does not in general compute cohomology.
