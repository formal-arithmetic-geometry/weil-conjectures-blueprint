By [sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point], $p_X^*$ is strong monoidal. A (lax)
monoidal functor sends monoid objects to monoid objects, with the unit and
multiplication described above, and morphisms of monoid objects to morphisms
of monoid objects. The associativity and unit axioms follow from the
coherence of the monoidal structure maps. This gives the functor on ring
objects.

Also by [sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point], $p_X^*$ is braided. Suppose
$\Lambda$ is commutative, i.e. its multiplication is invariant under the
symmetry of $\Lambda\otimes\Lambda$. Then the multiplication of $p_X^*\Lambda$
is invariant under the symmetry of $p_X^*\Lambda\otimes p_X^*\Lambda$, because
$p_X^*$ carries one symmetry to the other.
