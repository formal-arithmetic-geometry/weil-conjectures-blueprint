A strong monoidal functor is in particular lax monoidal, with structure maps $\delta_{F,G}$ and
$\epsilon$ satisfying the associativity and unit coherences. The multiplication and unit of
$\Lambda_X$ are exactly $\mu_{FR}$ and $\eta_{FR}$ of [det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport] for
$F=p_X^*$ and $R=\Lambda$. So 1 and 2 are part 1 of [det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport]
(functoriality of $\varphi\mapsto p_X^*\varphi$ is functoriality of $p_X^*$). Since $p_X^*$ is
braided ([def-sfb-upper-star-from-point]), i.e. carries the symmetry of $\Lambda\otimes\Lambda$ to
that of $\Lambda_X\otimes\Lambda_X$ under $\delta$, part 2 of [det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport]
gives 3.
