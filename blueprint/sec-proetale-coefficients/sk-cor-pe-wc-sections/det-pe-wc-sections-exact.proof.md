Kernels of sheaves of modules are computed sectionwise, so $\Gamma(U,-)$ is left exact. Let
$F\to G$ be an epimorphism of sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules. It is locally surjective, i.e. the
underlying map of sheaves of sets is an epimorphism (cokernels of sheaves of modules are
sheafified presheaf cokernels, so surjectivity in either sense means local surjectivity on
sections). By [det-pe-wcb-projective], $F(U)\to G(U)$ is surjective. A left exact functor
preserving epimorphisms is exact.
