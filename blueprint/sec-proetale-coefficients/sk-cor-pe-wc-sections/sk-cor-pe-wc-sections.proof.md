Sections preserve kernels, and projectivity gives surjectivity on sections for every
epimorphism ([det-pe-wc-sections-exact]). The sections functor is therefore exact, carries
acyclic complexes to acyclic complexes, and agrees with its derived functor, including for
unbounded complexes; in particular the higher cohomology of $U$ vanishes
([det-pe-wc-sections-derived]).
