Let $U$ and $\mathcal R$ be as in [det-pe-wc-sections-exact], and let $K$ be an arbitrary
(possibly unbounded) complex of $\mathcal R$-modules on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$. Then the
natural map $K(U)\to R\Gamma(U,K)$ is an isomorphism in the derived category of
$\mathcal R(U)$-modules, where $K(U)$ is the complex of sections computed term by term. In
particular $H^q(U,F)=0$ for every sheaf of modules $F$ and every $q>0$.
