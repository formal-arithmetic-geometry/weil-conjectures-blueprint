title = "Sections over a w-contractible affine are exact"
uses = ["det-pe-wcb-projective"]
+++
Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}D$ with $D$ w-contractible be an object of $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$,
and let $\mathcal R$ be a sheaf of rings on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$. The functor
$\Gamma(U,-)\colon F\mapsto F(U)$ from abelian sheaves (or from sheaves of
$\mathcal R$-modules) to abelian groups (or $\mathcal R(U)$-modules) is exact.
