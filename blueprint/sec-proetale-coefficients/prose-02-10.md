We now combine the two theorems just proved: the refinement theorem
replaces an arbitrary object of $X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}}$ by pro-étale affines, and
the existence of w-contractible covers replaces those by affines over which
taking sections is exact. Hence, to compute with sheaves on $X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}}$,
it suffices to evaluate on such affines.
