The sheaf property of $\underline T$, and the ring object structure on it, are
[def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring]. The sheaf condition reduces to descent of
continuous maps along a surjection of profinite sets, which is a quotient map. Only the
topology of $T$ enters. In particular, no completeness, Noetherianity or limit
presentation of $T$ is used: $\underline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ is defined by its sections and
not as $\underline{\mathbf Z}_\ell[1/\ell]$, and $\underline{\mathbf Z}_\ell$ is not
defined as $\lim_n\underline{\mathbf Z/\ell^n}$. The morphisms $\varphi_*$ and their
functoriality are [def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map]. The four $\ell$-adic
instances, with their inclusions, are [def-sfb-padic-int-condensed-ring],
[def-sfb-padic-condensed-ring], [def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring] and
[def-sfb-padic-integers-condensed-ring]. All these rings are commutative, so the ring
objects are commutative.
