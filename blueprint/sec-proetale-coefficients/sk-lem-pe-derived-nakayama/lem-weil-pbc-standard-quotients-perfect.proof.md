A uniformizer is a non-zero-divisor in the discrete valuation ring
$\mathcal O_E$, so multiplication by $\pi_E^n$ is injective with cokernel
$\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$. The displayed complex is therefore a resolution of the
quotient by finite free modules, which is what perfectness means.
