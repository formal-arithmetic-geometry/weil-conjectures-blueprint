title = "Perfectness of the standard quotients"
order = 1322

Let $E/\Ql$ be finite, with ring of integers $\mathcal O_E$ and uniformizer
$\pi_E$, and let $n\geq1$. The quotient $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$ is a perfect
$\mathcal O_E$-module, represented by the two-term complex of finite free
modules

$$
 [\mathcal O_E\xrightarrow{\pi_E^n}\mathcal O_E]
$$

in degrees $-1$ and $0$. In particular, for $E=\Ql$ and $\pi_E=\ell$, the
quotient $\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z$ is perfect over $\Zl$. This is the
algebraic input for the sheaf resolution of
$(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_X$ in [det-pe-derived-nakayama-resolution].
