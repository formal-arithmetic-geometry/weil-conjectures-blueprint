An adjunction between a strong monoidal left adjoint and a lax monoidal right adjoint is
monoidal exactly when the oplax structure of the left adjoint is the mate of the lax
structure of the right adjoint. This holds by the definition of the structure in
[def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal].
