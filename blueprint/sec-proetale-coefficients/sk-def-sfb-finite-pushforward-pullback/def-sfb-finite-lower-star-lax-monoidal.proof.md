On presheaves, restriction along base change is strictly monoidal for the objectwise
tensor product. The sheaf tensor product is the sheafification of the objectwise one
([sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor]). The map $g_*F\otimes g_*G\to g_*(F\otimes G)$ is therefore
induced, by the universal property of sheafification, from the restriction of the
sheafification map $F\otimes_{\mathrm{pre}}G\to F\otimes G$. The unit map is obtained in
the same way from the constant presheaf with value $\mathbf 1$. The associativity, unit and
symmetry conditions hold objectwise, hence after sheafification.
