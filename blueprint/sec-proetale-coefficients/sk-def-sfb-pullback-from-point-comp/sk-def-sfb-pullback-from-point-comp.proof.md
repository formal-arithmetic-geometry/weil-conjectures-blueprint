The isomorphism is [def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso], and its
monoidality is [lem-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-monoidal].

On test objects the isomorphism is the canonical one. Under the adjunctions,
it corresponds to an isomorphism of right adjoints
$$
p_*\pi_{Y*}f_*\cong p_*\pi_{X*}.
$$
At a sheaf $G$ on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ and a profinite set $S$, this is the
map
$$
G\bigl(Y[S]\times_YX\bigr)\cong G(X[S])
$$
induced by the canonical isomorphism $X\times_YY[S]\cong X[S]$ of iterated
fibre products.
