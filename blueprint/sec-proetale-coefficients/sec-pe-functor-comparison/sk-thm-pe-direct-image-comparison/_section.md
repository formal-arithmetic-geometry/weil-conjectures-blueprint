If $g$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated, the canonical natural
transformation

$$
\nu_Y^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}K\longrightarrow
Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}\nu_X^*K
\tag{2.9}
$$

is an isomorphism for $K\in D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$. Consequently
$Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}$ carries bounded-below classical complexes to classical
complexes. The isomorphism is compatible with units, counits and
composition.
