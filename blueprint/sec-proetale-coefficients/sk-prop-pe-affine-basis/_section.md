Every object of $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ [sk-def-pe-site] has a pro-étale covering by pro-étale
affines over $X$ [sk-def-pe-morphisms]. For affine $X$ these form a full subsite whose
category of sheaves is equivalent to $\Sh(X_{\mathrm{proét}})$.
