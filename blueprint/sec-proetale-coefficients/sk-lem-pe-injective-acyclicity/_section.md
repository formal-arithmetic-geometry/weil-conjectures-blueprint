If $I$ is an injective sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules on $X_{\Et}$, then

$$
R^q\nu_{X,*}\nu_X^*I=0\qquad(q>0),
$$

where $\nu_{X,*}\colon\Mod(X_{\Proet},\Lambda)\to\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$ is the
direct image of the comparison morphism.
