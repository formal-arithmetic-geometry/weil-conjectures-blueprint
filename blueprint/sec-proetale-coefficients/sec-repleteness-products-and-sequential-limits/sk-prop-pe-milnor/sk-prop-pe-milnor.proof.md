The formulas follow from the long exact cohomology sequence of
(2.1), since by Proposition [sk-prop-pe-products] the
cohomology of each product term is the product of cohomology sheaves.
If the transition maps are epimorphisms, they are surjective on sections
over every weakly contractible $W$, and so is $1-T$: given $(a_n)$, choose $m_0$ arbitrarily and
then, recursively, $m_{n+1}$ with $t_n(m_{n+1})=m_n-a_n$. By
Corollary [sk-cor-pe-sections], $1-T$ is then an epimorphism of sheaves, so
$R^1\varprojlim_nM_n=0$ and the derived limit is $\varprojlim_nM_n$. See
[Stacks, Tag 0947](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0947).
