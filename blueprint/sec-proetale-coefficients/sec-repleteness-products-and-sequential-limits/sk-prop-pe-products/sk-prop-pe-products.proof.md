On a weakly contractible $W$, a product of short exact sequences
evaluates to a product of short exact sequences of
$\mathcal R(W)$-modules. The latter is exact, because by the axiom of
choice we may lift a tuple coordinate by coordinate. By
Corollary [sk-cor-pe-sections], exactness of sheaves can be checked on such
$W$.

The derived product is
the termwise product of K-injective representatives (complexes $I$ such
that maps from acyclic complexes to $I$ are null-homotopic, the unbounded
analogue of bounded-below injective complexes). Since products are exact,
a product of quasi-isomorphisms is a quasi-isomorphism, so we may replace
these by any quasi-isomorphic representatives. Hence termwise
products compute the product and commute with cohomology;
see [Stacks, Tag 0947](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0947), [Stacks, Tag 08TB](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/08TB).
