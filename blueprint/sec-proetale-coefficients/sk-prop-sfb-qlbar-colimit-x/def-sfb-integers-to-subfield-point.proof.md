The condensed ring construction is functorial in continuous ring maps
[det-def-sfb-condensed-ring]. Forgetting from sheaves of rings to sheaves of abelian groups
sends morphisms of sheaves of rings to morphisms of ring objects, because the
multiplication of the ring object is induced by the sectionwise multiplication
[det-def-sfb-ring-object].
