The colimit statement (2) of Corollary [sk-cor-pe-sections] is special to
modules. For coproducts of *sheaves of sets* it is in general
false, since on a disconnected $W$ a section of $F\amalg G$ can lie in
different summands on different components. Thus for disconnected $W$,
evaluation at $W$ is in general not the inverse image functor of a
point of the topos. What we use below is only exactness and joint
conservativity on modules, as in Corollary [sk-cor-pe-sections]; see
[Stacks, Tag 0991](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0991).
