By [thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-refinement] there is a faithfully flat ind-étale $E\to E'$ with
$D\to E'$ ind-étale. The composite $D\to E\to E'$ is faithfully flat, as a composite of
faithfully flat maps. By [det-pe-wc-rings-retraction] there is a $D$-algebra retraction
$\rho\colon E'\to D$. Then $E\to E'\xrightarrow{\rho}D$ is a $D$-algebra map.
