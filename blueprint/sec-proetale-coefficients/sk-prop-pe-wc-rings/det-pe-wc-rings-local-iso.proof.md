An ind-étale map is weakly étale by [lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-stability]. The base change
$R_{\mathfrak m}\to B\otimes_RR_{\mathfrak m}$ is weakly étale. The localization
$B\otimes_RR_{\mathfrak m}\to B_{\mathfrak n}$ is a filtered colimit of principal
localizations, hence ind-Zariski and in particular weakly étale. By stability under
composition ([lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-stability]) the map $R_{\mathfrak m}\to
B_{\mathfrak n}$ is weakly étale, and it is a local homomorphism because $\mathfrak n$ lies
over $\mathfrak m$. Olivier's theorem [thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-strict-local] says that a
weakly étale local homomorphism out of a strictly henselian local ring is an isomorphism.
