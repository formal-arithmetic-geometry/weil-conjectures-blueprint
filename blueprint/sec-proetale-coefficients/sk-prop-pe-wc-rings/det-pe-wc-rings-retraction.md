Let $R$ be w-contractible [det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible] and $R\to B$ faithfully flat
and ind-étale. Let $B'$ be as in [det-pe-wc-rings-w-local-refinement] and
$T=(\operatorname{Spec}B')^c$. Then there is a closed subset $T'\subset T$ mapping
homeomorphically onto $(\operatorname{Spec}R)^c$, and for any such $T'$ the composite
$R\to B'\to B'':=B'_{[T']}$ is an isomorphism. In particular
$B\to B'\to B''\cong R$ is an $R$-algebra retraction of $R\to B$.
