title = "Local rings of a weakly étale algebra over a strictly henselian point"
uses = ["thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-strict-local", "lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-stability"]
+++
Let $R\to B$ be weakly étale (for instance ind-étale), let $\mathfrak m\subset R$ be a
maximal ideal with $R_{\mathfrak m}$ strictly henselian, and let $\mathfrak n\subset B$ be a
prime with $\mathfrak n\cap R=\mathfrak m$. Then $R_{\mathfrak m}\to B_{\mathfrak n}$ is an
isomorphism.
