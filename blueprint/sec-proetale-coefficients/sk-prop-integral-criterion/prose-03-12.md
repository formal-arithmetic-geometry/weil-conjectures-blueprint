Lemma [sk-lem-integral-models] compares $\Dctf(X,\mathcal O_E)$ with
the classical category of constructible $\mathcal O_E$-complexes
([Stacks, Tag 09C1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09C1)), which is defined with modules over the constant sheaf with
value the discrete ring $\mathcal O_E$.

For the integral and rational rings the finite Tor amplitude condition is
automatic.
