For modules, sections over a quasi-compact weakly contractible $W$ preserve all colimits
(by the corollary on a conservative family preserving all module colimits). The corresponding assertion for coproducts of *sheaves of sets* is
generally false: on a disconnected $W$, a section of $F\amalg G$ can choose different
summands on different components. Thus evaluation need not be the inverse image of a point
of the topos. It nevertheless gives the conservative exact module functors needed below;
see Stacks, Tag 0991.
