title = "The comparison unit is an isomorphism"
uses = ["def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism", "lem-weil-pbc-comparison-sections", "sk-prop-pe-comparison-morphism"]
+++
Let $X$ be a scheme. For every sheaf of sets $F$ on $X_{\Et}$ the unit
$\eta_F\colon F\to\nu_{X,*}\nu_X^*F$ is an isomorphism. Consequently
$\nu_X^*\colon\Sh(X_{\Et})\to\Sh(X_{\Proet})$ is fully faithful: for sheaves
$F,F'$ on $X_{\Et}$ the map
$\Hom(F,F')\to\Hom(\nu_X^*F,\nu_X^*F')$, $\varphi\mapsto\nu_X^*\varphi$, is
bijective.
