The functor $\nu_X^*$ is fully faithful. Its essential image is exactly the
classical sheaves of [sk-def-pe-classical-definition]. For modules it is an
exact equivalence between $\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$ and the full subcategory of
classical $\Lambda_X$-module sheaves on $X_{\Proet}$.
