For a bounded-below complex $K$ on $X_{\mathrm{\acute et}}$, the pro-étale
cohomology of $\nu_X^*K$ is the étale cohomology of $K$; bounds on
cohomological dimension are needed only for unbounded complexes (Section “Unbounded complexes with finite coefficients”). The proof
uses the following acyclicity statement for the direct image
$\nu_{X,*}:\Mod(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda)\to\Mod(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},\Lambda)$.
