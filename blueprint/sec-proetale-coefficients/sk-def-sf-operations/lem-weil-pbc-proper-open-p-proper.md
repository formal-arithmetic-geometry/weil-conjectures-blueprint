Let $u:T_1\to T_2$ be
proper and let
$j_2:X\hookrightarrow T_2$ be an open immersion. For the fibre
product $V=T_1\times_{T_2}X$ with projection $p:V\to X$ of
[def-weil-pbc-proper-open-comparison], the morphism $p$ is
proper and therefore
separated.
