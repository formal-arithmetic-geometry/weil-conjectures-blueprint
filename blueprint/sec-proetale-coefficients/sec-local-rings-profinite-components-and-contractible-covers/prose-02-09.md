So a w-strictly local ring is a family of strictly henselian local rings
indexed by the profinite set of its components, and a w-contractible ring
is such a family indexed by an extremally disconnected profinite set.

We use extremally disconnected spaces through their projectivity. In
Proposition [sk-prop-pe-wc-rings] it splits a surjection of component
spaces, and in Theorem [sk-thm-pe-wc-cover] we cover a profinite component
space by an extremally disconnected one.
