Let $K\in D^+(X_{\mathrm{ét}},\Lambda)$, and let $U=\varprojlim_iU_i$ be a
pro-étale affine with $U_i$ affine and étale over $X$. Then there is
an isomorphism

$$
 R\Gamma(U_{\mathrm{proét}},(\nu_X^*K)|_U)
   \simeq \varinjlim_i R\Gamma((U_i)_{\mathrm{ét}},K|_{U_i}).
$$
