We apply the statement on global sections of
[sk-prop-sf-comparison] to $U$ and then use continuity of
étale cohomology under affine limits (Stacks, Tag 03Q6). This proves the
formula in each degree for a sheaf. For a bounded-below complex, only
finitely many terms of the hypercohomology spectral sequence contribute
to each total degree, and filtered colimits are exact.
