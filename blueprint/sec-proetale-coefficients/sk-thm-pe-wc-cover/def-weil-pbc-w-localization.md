For a ring $A$, a finite subset $E\subset A$ and a decomposition
$E=E'\sqcup E''$, put

$$
 Z(E',E'')=D\Bigl(\prod_{f\in E'}f\Bigr)\cap V(E'')\subset\operatorname{Spec}A .
$$

For fixed $E$ these constructible [def-weil-pbc-w-local] strata partition
$\operatorname{Spec}A$. Let $A_{Z(E',E'')}$ be the localization of $A$ at the
multiplicative set of elements lying in no prime of $Z(E',E'')$, i.e. of the
elements that become units in $(A/(E''))_{\prod_{f\in E'}f}$, and put

$$
 P_E=\prod_{E=E'\sqcup E''}A_{Z(E',E'')} .
$$

If $E\subset F$, every stratum for $F$ lies in a stratum for $E$, and the
localization maps between the factors give an $A$-algebra map $P_E\to P_F$.
Define

$$
 A^w=\varinjlim_{E}P_E ,
$$

the filtered colimit over the finite subsets of $A$ ordered by inclusion. Let
$I_A\subset A^w$ be the ideal generated by the images of the kernels of the
maps $A_{Z(E',E'')}\to(A/(E''))_{\prod_{f\in E'}f}$.
