title = "Cohomology commutes with direct sums"
order = 1000

In particular, $H^q(U,-)$ commutes with direct sums of sheaves of
modules. For direct sums of unbounded complexes the common lower bound
is replaced by uniform bounds on cohomological dimension
(Proposition [sk-prop-pe-unbounded-classical]).
