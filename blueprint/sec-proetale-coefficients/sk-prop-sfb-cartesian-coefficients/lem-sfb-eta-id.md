Let $\Lambda$ be a condensed ring and $X$ a scheme. Then
$\eta_{\mathrm{id}_X}\colon\Lambda_X\to(\mathrm{id}_X)_*\Lambda_X$
([def-sfb-eta]) is the inverse of the identity isomorphism
$(\mathrm{id}_X)_*\cong\mathrm{id}$ of the pushforward of
[def-sfb-finite-lower-star] at $\Lambda_X$, given by restriction along the canonical
isomorphisms $V\times_XX\cong V$.
