For finite extensions $E\le F$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ inside $\Qlb$ and every scheme $X$, the
extension of scalars $F_X\otimes_{E_X}-\colon\Mod_{E_X}\to\Mod_{F_X}$ along
$E_X\to F_X$ ([def-sfb-subfield-coefficient-map], [det-def-sfb-change-of-scalars]) preserves
finite limits; that is, $F_X$ is flat over $E_X$.
