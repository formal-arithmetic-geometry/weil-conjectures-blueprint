The coefficient change $a_X$ is the morphism of ring objects of [def-sfb-coefficient-map]; its
functoriality in $a$ is [lem-sfb-coefficient-map-id] and [lem-sfb-coefficient-map-comp]. Since
$\Lambda_X$ and $\Lambda'_X$ are commutative ring objects (the inverse image $p_X^*$ being
strong monoidal and braided), $\operatorname{Ext}_{a_X}$ is defined, and $T^X$ with its
adjunction $T^X\dashv D(\operatorname{Res}_{a_X})$ is the derived extension of scalars of
[det-def-sfb-derived-extension-of-scalars] along $a_X$. The commutativity of the first square is
[lem-sfb-coefficient-pullback-square], that of the second is
[lem-sfb-coefficient-pushforward-square].
