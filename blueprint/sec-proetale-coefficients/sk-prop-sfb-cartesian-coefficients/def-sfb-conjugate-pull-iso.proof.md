Composites of adjunctions are adjunctions, so $E_1U_1\dashv L_1\mathrm{Res}_1$ and
$U_2E_2\dashv\mathrm{Res}_2L_2$. For two adjunctions $F\dashv G$, $F'\dashv G'$ between the same
categories, conjugation $\tau\mapsto(F'\xrightarrow{F'\eta}F'GF\xrightarrow{F'\tau F}F'G'F
\xrightarrow{\varepsilon'F}F)$ is a bijection $\operatorname{Nat}(G,G')\cong
\operatorname{Nat}(F',F)$ compatible with composition and identities, hence it sends
isomorphisms to isomorphisms. Apply it to $e$.
