uses = ["prop-weil-pbc-comparison-direct-image", "det-pe-classical-derived-equivalence"]

Let $g\colon X\to Y$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated. If
$K\in D^+(X_{\Proet},\Lambda)$ has classical cohomology sheaves, then
$Rg_{\Proet,*}K\in D^+(Y_{\Proet},\Lambda)$ has classical cohomology sheaves, and
$Rg_{\Proet,*}K\cong\nu_Y^*Rg_{\Et,*}R\nu_{X,*}K$.
