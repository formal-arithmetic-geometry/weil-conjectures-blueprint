Let $F$ be a locally constant $\Lambda_X$-module sheaf of finite presentation on
$X_{\Proet}$. Then there is a $\Lambda$-module sheaf $F_0$ on $X_{\Et}$, étale
locally isomorphic to constant sheaves of finitely presented modules, with
$\nu_X^*F_0\cong F$. Consequently $F$ is classical, has a trivializing étale
covering, and $\nu_X^*$ restricts to an equivalence between étale local systems
of finite presentation and pro-étale ones, with quasi-inverse $\nu_{X,*}$. If
$\Lambda=A$ is a finite ring, every locally constant sheaf of $A$-modules with
finite models is of finite presentation.
