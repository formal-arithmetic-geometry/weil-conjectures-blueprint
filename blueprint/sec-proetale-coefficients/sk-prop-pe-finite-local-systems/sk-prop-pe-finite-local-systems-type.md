title = "Decomposition by the isomorphism type of the model"
uses = ["det-pe-finite-local-systems-hom", "det-pe-classical-sheaves-unit"]
+++
Let $F$ be a locally constant $\Lambda_X$-module sheaf of finite presentation on
$X_{\Proet}$, and fix a set $R$ of representatives of the isomorphism classes of
finitely presented $\Lambda$-modules. Then $X=\coprod_{M\in R}X_M$ is a disjoint
union of open and closed subschemes such that, for each $M$, the restriction
$F|_{X_M}$ is pro-étale locally isomorphic to $\underline M$.
