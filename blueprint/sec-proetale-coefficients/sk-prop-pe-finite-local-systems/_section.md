A locally constant module sheaf on $X_{\Proet}$ is one trivialized by a pro-étale
covering; it is of finite presentation if its constant models are finitely
presented $\Lambda$-modules.

Every locally constant pro-étale $\Lambda_X$-module sheaf of finite presentation
is classical. Thus it has a trivializing étale covering, and $\nu_X^*$
identifies the étale local systems of finite presentation with these pro-étale
ones. For a finite ring $A$ this includes every finite local system of
$A$-modules.
