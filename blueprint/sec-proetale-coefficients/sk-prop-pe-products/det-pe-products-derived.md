id = "det-pe-products-derived"
kind = "lemma"
title = "Products in the derived category are termwise"
uses = ["det-pe-products-exact"]
+++
Let $\mathcal R$ be a sheaf of rings on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ and let $(K_i)_{i\in I}$ be a
family of complexes of $\mathcal R$-modules. Then the termwise product complex
$\prod_iK_i$ is a product of the $K_i$ in $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal R)$, and
$H^p(\prod_iK_i)\cong\prod_iH^p(K_i)$ for every $p$. If $K_i\to K'_i$ are
quasi-isomorphisms, so is $\prod_iK_i\to\prod_iK'_i$.
