Arbitrary set-indexed products are exact in $\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal R)$. Products
in $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal R)$ are computed by taking termwise products of
representative complexes, and their cohomology sheaves are the products of the cohomology
sheaves.
