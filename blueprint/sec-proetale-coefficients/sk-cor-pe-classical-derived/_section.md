Write $D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(X_{\Proet},\Lambda)$ for the full subcategory of
$D^+(X_{\Proet},\Lambda)$ of classical complexes
([sk-def-pe-classical-definition]).

Classical module sheaves form a weak Serre subcategory, closed under kernels,
cokernels and extensions. There is an equivalence

$$
D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(X_{\Proet},\Lambda)\simeq D^+(X_{\Et},\Lambda),
$$

with inverse functors $R\nu_{X,*}$ and $\nu_X^*$.
