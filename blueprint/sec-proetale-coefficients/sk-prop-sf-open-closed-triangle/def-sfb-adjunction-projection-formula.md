Let $P\dashv S$ be an adjunction with $S$ strong monoidal that satisfies the projection
formula ([def-sfb-adjunction-has-projection-formula]). Then the projection formula maps
form a projection formula for $P$ relative to $S$ in the sense of
[def-sfb-projection-formula].
