Let $\mathcal C$ be a monoidal category and $\mathcal R$ a monoid object of $\mathcal C$.
The forgetful functor $\Mod_{\mathcal R}(\mathcal C)\to\mathcal C$ of
[det-def-sfb-module-objects-definition] is faithful.
