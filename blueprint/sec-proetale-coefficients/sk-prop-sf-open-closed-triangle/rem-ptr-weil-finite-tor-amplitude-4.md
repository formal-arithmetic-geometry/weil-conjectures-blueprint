In the notation of [def-weil-finite-tor-amplitude], the equality
$D^b_{ctf}(Y,\Lambda)=D^b_c(Y,\Lambda)$ at the field coefficients $\Lambda=E,\Qlb$
can also be seen directly: every bounded constructible complex at a field
coefficient has finite Tor amplitude, [lem-integral-ctf-equals-bc].
