Let $I\subset A$ be an ideal and $\overline C$ an ind-etale $A/I$-algebra. For
the lift $C=H_A(\overline C)$ of [def-exc-ind-etale-closed-lift], the
reduction $C/IC\to\overline C$ is an isomorphism.
