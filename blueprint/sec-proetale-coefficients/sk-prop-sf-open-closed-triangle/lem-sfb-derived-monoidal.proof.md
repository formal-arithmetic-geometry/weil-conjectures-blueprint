Since $\mathcal C$ is closed, $X\otimes-$ and $-\otimes X$ are left adjoints, so they
preserve all colimits. In particular they preserve the countable coproducts and the
exact $\mathbf Z$-indexed colimits used to build K-flat resolutions
[det-def-sfb-derived-categories]. So the K-flat complexes form a left derivability
structure for the quasi-isomorphisms of $K(\mathcal C)$. They are stable under the
tensor product: if $P,P'$ are K-flat, then $(P\otimes P')\otimes-=P\otimes(P'\otimes-)$
preserves quasi-isomorphisms, and similarly on the right. They also contain the unit.
A quasi-isomorphism between K-flat complexes stays a quasi-isomorphism after tensoring
with a K-flat complex. So the tensor product of complexes, restricted to K-flat
complexes, descends to $D(\mathcal C)$; this is $-\otimes^{\mathrm L}-$. It is the
two-variable left derived functor, and the iterated tensor products are the left
derived functors of the iterated tensor products of complexes, in three and four
variables. The constraints of $K(\mathcal C)$ restrict to K-flat complexes and descend,
and the coherence identities descend with them. The resolution maps make the
localization functor lax monoidal.
