The subcategory is [def-sfb-bounded-constructible-complexes]. Its description through
cohomology is [lem-sfb-bounded-constructible-iff], and for the listed coefficient rings
$\Lambda_X$ it is triangulated on a topologically Noetherian $X$ by
[prop-sfb-bounded-constructible-triangulated]. For a general $\mathcal R$, the cone of
multiplication by $x$ on the constant sheaf of
$k[x,y_1,y_2,\dots]/(xy_1,xy_2,\dots)$ on $\operatorname{Spec}\bar k$ is not bounded
constructible, as shown in the proof of [prop-sfb-bounded-constructible-triangulated].
