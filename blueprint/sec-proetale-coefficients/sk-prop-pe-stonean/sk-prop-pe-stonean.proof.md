*Topological input and construction.* The projectivity equivalence is the compact
Hausdorff projectivity theorem (Gleason; Stacks, Tag 08YN), proved in
[det-pe-stonean-projective]. One proof chooses a minimal closed subspace of a compact
source that still surjects onto $S$; extremal disconnectedness forces that irreducible
surjection to be a homeomorphism.

For the cover, take the underlying set $T^{\mathrm{disc}}$ with the discrete topology, and
let $S=\beta(T^{\mathrm{disc}})$ be its Stone–Čech compactification. For a discrete space
this compactification is extremally disconnected, and the map to $T$ extends uniquely and
surjectively (Stacks, Tag 090D); see [det-pe-stonean-cover]. Only this discrete-space case
is used here.
