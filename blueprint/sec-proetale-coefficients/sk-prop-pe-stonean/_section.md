For a compact Hausdorff space $S$, *extremal disconnectedness* means that the closure of
every open subset is open. It is equivalent to projectivity with respect to continuous
surjections of compact Hausdorff spaces. Every compact Hausdorff space $T$, in particular
every profinite space, admits a continuous surjection $S\to T$ with $S$ compact Hausdorff
and extremally disconnected; such $S$ is also profinite.
