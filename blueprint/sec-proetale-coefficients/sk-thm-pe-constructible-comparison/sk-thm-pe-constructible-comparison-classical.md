Let $\Lambda$ be a discrete Noetherian ring, $X$ a quasi-compact and
quasi-separated scheme, and $F$ a $\Lambda_X$-module sheaf on $X_{\Proet}$ for which
there is a partition $X=Z_1\sqcup\dots\sqcup Z_n$ into constructible locally
closed subsets such that each restriction $F|_{Z_k}$ to $(Z_k)_{\Proet}$ (reduced
structure) is locally constant of finite type. Then $F$ is classical. The same
holds if such a partition exists only locally on $X$.
