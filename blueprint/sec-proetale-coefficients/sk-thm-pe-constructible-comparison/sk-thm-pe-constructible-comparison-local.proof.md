The inverse image $\nu^*$ commutes with restriction to open subschemes, which
gives the converse. For the first statement, write
$\alpha_a\colon\nu_{X_a}^*F_a\cong F|_{X_a}$ with $F_a$ a $\Lambda$-module sheaf on
$(X_a)_{\Et}$ ([det-pe-classical-sheaves-modules]). On $X_{ab}=X_a\cap X_b$ the
isomorphism $\alpha_b^{-1}\alpha_a$ is $\nu^*\varphi_{ab}$ for a unique linear
isomorphism $\varphi_{ab}\colon F_a|_{X_{ab}}\to F_b|_{X_{ab}}$, by full
faithfulness of $\nu_{X_{ab}}^*$ on module sheaves; uniqueness gives the cocycle
condition $\varphi_{bc}\varphi_{ab}=\varphi_{ac}$. Étale sheaves glue along Zariski
coverings, so the $F_a$ glue to an étale module sheaf $F_0$, and the $\alpha_a$,
which agree on overlaps by construction, glue to an isomorphism
$\nu_X^*F_0\cong F$. Hence $F$ is classical.
