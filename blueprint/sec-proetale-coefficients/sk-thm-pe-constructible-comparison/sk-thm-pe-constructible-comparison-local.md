kind = "lemma"
title = "Classicality is Zariski local"
uses = ["det-pe-classical-sheaves-unit", "det-pe-classical-sheaves-modules"]
+++
Let $X$ be a scheme, $\Lambda$ a discrete commutative ring and $F$ a
$\Lambda_X$-module sheaf on $X_{\Proet}$. If $X=\bigcup_aX_a$ is an open covering
such that each restriction $F|_{X_a}$ is classical on $(X_a)_{\Proet}$, then $F$ is
classical. Conversely the restriction of a classical sheaf to an open subscheme
is classical.
