Let $X$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated and $\Lambda$ discrete Noetherian.

1. $\nu_X^*$ restricts to an equivalence from the category of constructible
   étale sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on $X$ to the category
   $\operatorname{Cons}(X,\Lambda)$ of constructible $\Lambda_X$-module sheaves
   on $X_{\Proet}$, with quasi-inverse $\nu_{X,*}$.
2. $\nu_X^*$ restricts to an equivalence of triangulated categories
   $D^b_c(X_{\Et},\Lambda)\to\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$, with quasi-inverse $R\nu_{X,*}$.
