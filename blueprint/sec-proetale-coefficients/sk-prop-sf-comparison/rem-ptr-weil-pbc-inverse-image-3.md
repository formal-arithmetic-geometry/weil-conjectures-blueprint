In the notation of [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], the following holds. That
$u^{-1}$ is exact, and that it therefore acts termwise on complexes, is
[prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-exact]; it induces the derived adjunction
$u^{-1}\dashv Ru_*$ on the derived categories
[def-weil-pbc-derived-categories] $\Dplus$.
