Weakly étale morphisms [sk-def-pe-morphisms] are stable under base change and
composition, and the property is local on source and target for the Zariski topology.
Every $X$-morphism between two weakly étale $X$-schemes is weakly étale. Affine pro-étale
morphisms are stable under affine base change and composition.
