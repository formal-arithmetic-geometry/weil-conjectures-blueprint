**Content.** The pro-étale site of a scheme and the coefficient categories built on it, as in
§2–§3 of the notes on condensed coefficients and six functors. Weakly étale, ind-étale and
pro-étale morphisms define the small pro-étale site ([sk-def-pe-site]), whose topos has enough
weakly contractible affine objects ([sk-thm-pe-weakly-contractible-basis]). Consequently it is
replete, products and sequential limits are exact, and unbounded complexes are left complete.
The comparison morphism with the étale site identifies classical sheaves, bounded-below
cohomology, direct images, constructible complexes and complexes of finite Tor amplitude on the
two sites ([sk-thm-pe-classical-sheaves], [sk-thm-pe-constructible-comparison]). The pro-étale
geometric point computes continuous Galois cohomology, and the open–closed operations and
derived completion prepare the adic coefficients.

The second part fixes the coefficient rings $A$, $\Zl$, $\cO_E$, $\Ql$, $E$, $\Qlb$ as condensed
rings, compares them with completed coefficients, and defines Tate twists, rationalization,
constructible complexes $\Dbc$ and $\Dctf$ ([sk-def-sfb-constructible]) and the localizing
closure $\Loc(\Dctf)$ on which the exceptional inverse image is constructed in
§[sec-six-functors-basics].
