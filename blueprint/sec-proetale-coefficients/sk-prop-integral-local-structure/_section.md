Let $X$ be Noetherian and let $K\in D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\cO_E)$ be derived complete with
$K_1\in\Dbc(X,\mathbf F_E)$. Then there is a finite stratification of $X$ such that on each
stratum $S$, pro-étale locally,

$$
K|_S\simeq\cO_{E,S}\otimes_{\cO_E}P^\bullet
$$

for a bounded complex $P^\bullet$ of finite free $\cO_E$-modules.
