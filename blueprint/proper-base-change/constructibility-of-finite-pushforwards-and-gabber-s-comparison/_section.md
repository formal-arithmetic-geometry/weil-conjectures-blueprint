Every constructible torsion sheaf on a quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme
embeds into a finite direct sum of finite pushforwards of constant sheaves
[lem-weil-pbc-constructible-finite-pushforward]. The route is: finite
pushforward preserves constructibility on a Noetherian base
[lem-weil-pbc-finite-pushforward-constructible-noetherian], the embedding
exists there [lem-weil-pbc-constructible-finite-pushforward-noetherian], and
it descends along a Noetherian approximation
[lem-weil-pbc-constructible-finite-pushforward-approximation]. The section
closes with Gabber's affine comparison for henselian pairs
[prop-weil-pbc-gabber-affine-comparison-detailed], recorded as an input, and
the acyclicity bound it yields
[lem-weil-pbc-injective-restriction-acyclicity].
