The Segre construction of the previous section is carried out over a
noetherian affine base; spreading out [lem-weil-pbc-chow-limit-descent]
reduces the general locally finitely presented case to it, giving Chow's
theorem in the form used here [thm-weil-pbc-chow]. The graph of the two maps
it supplies is the closed embedding over the source
[lem-weil-pbc-projective-embedding], which is what the reduction to the
projective line consumes.
