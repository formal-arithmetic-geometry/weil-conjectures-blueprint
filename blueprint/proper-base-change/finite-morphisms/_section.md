The base case of the whole chapter. For a finite morphism the direct image is
computed on geometric stalks by a finite product over the geometric fibre
[lem-weil-pbc-finite-stalk-formula], hence is exact
[lem-weil-pbc-finite-direct-image-exact], and the exchange morphism of a
cartesian square is an isomorphism [lem-weil-pbc-finite-exchange]. Since
finiteness is stable under base change, this gives $\mathrm{PBC}$ for every
finite morphism [prop-weil-pbc-finite-morphisms].
