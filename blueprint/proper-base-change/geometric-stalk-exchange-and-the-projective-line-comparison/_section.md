Where the two halves meet, under the subsection heading *Stalkwise
projective-line base change* in the source. On a geometric stalk the exchange
morphism of $\mathbf P^1_S\to S$ is a pullback map between proper curves over
separably closed fields [lem-weil-pbc-exchange-geometric-stalk], which the
previous section proved to be an isomorphism; conservativity of geometric
stalks turns that into $\mathrm{PBC}$ for the projective line over an
arbitrary base [prop-weil-pbc-projective-line-detailed].
