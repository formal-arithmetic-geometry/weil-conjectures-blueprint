Outline module 5: finite morphisms, the projective line, projective space, and
the proper case. The ambient language — the scheme conventions, the etale site
and its sheaves, geometric stalks, inverse and direct image, the underived and
derived exchange morphisms, the property $\mathrm{PBC}$ and the injective
acyclicity predicate $\mathrm{IA}$ — is the one fixed in the ambient
foundations chapter and is used here without restating it. The remaining
foundational inputs retain their stated status.

The sections are ordered as the proof of this chapter assembles its
statements: a section stands after the material it uses, so that no section
refers to material introduced later in the chapter.

The spine is: finite morphisms satisfy proper base change
[prop-weil-pbc-finite-morphisms]; the property is stable under composition
[prop-weil-pbc-composition-stability] and cancels along a proper surjection
[prop-weil-pbc-cancellation]; Chow's lemma [thm-weil-pbc-chow] presents a
proper morphism through projective space; the projective line over an
arbitrary base satisfies the property
[prop-weil-pbc-projective-line-detailed], by way of the strictly henselian
comparison and the eight curve assertions; symmetric-product coordinates
reduce projective space to products of lines
[lem-weil-pbc-reduction-projective-space]; and the two reductions combine into
the prime-coefficient theorem [thm-weil-pbc-prime-coefficient], the assembly
for arbitrary torsion sheaves [prop-weil-pbc-torsion-assembly] and the
bounded-below derived form [thm-weil-pbc-bounded-below].

This chapter is the import of section 5 of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex`. A
handful of its identifiers already name objects imported earlier from the
sketch sources; those six carry the suffix `-detailed` here so that the two
readings coexist.
