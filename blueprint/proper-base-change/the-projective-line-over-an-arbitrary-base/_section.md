under separably closed extensions* in the source. The geometric stalks of the
exchange morphism for $\mathbf P^1_S\to S$ are pullback maps between proper
curves over separably closed fields, so the whole of the remaining argument is
about such curves; this section fixes the pair $(C,F)$

[def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion], [def-weil-pbc-primary-torsion], and the
