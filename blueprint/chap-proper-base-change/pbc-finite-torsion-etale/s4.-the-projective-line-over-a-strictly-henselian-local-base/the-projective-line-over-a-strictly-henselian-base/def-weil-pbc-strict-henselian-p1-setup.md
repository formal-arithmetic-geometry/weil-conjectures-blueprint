Let $(A,\mathfrak m)$ be strictly henselian local, put $S=\operatorname{Spec}A$,
$s=\operatorname{Spec}(A/\mathfrak m)$, $X=\mathbf P^1_S$, and $X_0=\mathbf
P^1_s$. For an etale sheaf $F$ on $X$, write $F_0$ for its inverse image on
$X_0$ and let

$$
 \operatorname{res}_F:\Gamma(X,F)\longrightarrow\Gamma(X_0,F_0)
$$

be restriction.
