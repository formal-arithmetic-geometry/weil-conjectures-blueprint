Under the hypotheses of [lem-weil-pbc-finite-stage-killing], a cocycle
representing $c$ factors, after a quasi-compact etale neighborhood, through a
monomorphism $C\hookrightarrow F$ with $C$ a constructible torsion sheaf.

## Proof

By [lem-weil-pbc-finite-stage-killing], $c$ already vanishes after pullback to
one finite, finitely presented cover $T_{\lambda_0}\to T$; hence a cocycle
representing $c$ and a null-homotopy for its pullback involve only finitely
many sections of $F$ over a quasi-compact etale neighbourhood of $(T,T_0)$
together with finitely many relations among them, all of finite order (the
sheaf $F$ is torsion). The subsheaf $C\hookrightarrow F$ generated by these
finitely many sections is constructible: it is a quotient of a finite direct
sum of (suitably pushed-forward) constant $\mathbf Z/n$-sheaves on a
quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme, and finitely generated subsheaves of
torsion sheaves on such schemes are constructible. The cocycle factors through
the monomorphism $C\hookrightarrow F$ by construction, and the etale
neighbourhood may be taken quasi-compact.
