Proper base change on the small etale site with finite torsion
coefficients. S0 fixes the exchange map and reduces the base-change property
to injective acyclicity; S1--S5 prove it for finite morphisms, stability under
composition and proper-surjective cancellation, the reduction to
$\mathbf P^1$, and the projective line over a strictly henselian and then an
arbitrary base; S6 assembles torsion sheaves and S7 passes to bounded-below
complexes.
