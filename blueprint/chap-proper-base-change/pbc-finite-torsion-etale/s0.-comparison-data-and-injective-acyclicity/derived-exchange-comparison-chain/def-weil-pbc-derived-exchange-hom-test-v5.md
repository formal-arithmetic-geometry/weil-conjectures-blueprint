For complexes $A,B$, let $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}^r(A,B)$ be the
degree-$r$ graded maps, with differential $d(h)=d_Bh-(-1)^rh d_A$.
A complex $B$ is K-injective when this Hom complex is acyclic for every
acyclic $A$.
