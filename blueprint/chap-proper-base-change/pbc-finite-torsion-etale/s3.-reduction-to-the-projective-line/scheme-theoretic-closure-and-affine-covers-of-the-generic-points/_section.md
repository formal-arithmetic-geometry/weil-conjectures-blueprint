The auxiliary statements of Chow's lemma, under the subsection heading
*Chow's lemma* in the source. A quasi-separated scheme with finitely many
irreducible components admits affine opens through any prescribed finite set
of generic points [lem-weil-pbc-chow-affine-open-around-generics], hence a
finite affine cover whose intersection is dense
[lem-weil-pbc-chow-generic-cover]; passing to the scheme-theoretic closure of
that intersection [lem-weil-pbc-chow-dense-closure-reduction] makes it
scheme-theoretically dense. Each member of the cover then sits in a projective
space [lem-weil-pbc-chow-affine-immersion-projective] with a proper closed
model [lem-weil-pbc-chow-closed-model-proper].
