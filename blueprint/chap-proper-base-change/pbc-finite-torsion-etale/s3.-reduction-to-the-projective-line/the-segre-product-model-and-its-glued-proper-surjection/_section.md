The construction of the Chow modification. The Segre embedding
[def-chow-segre] makes a product of projective spaces
projective [lem-chow-segre]; the scheme-theoretic
image of the diagonal of the charts inside the product of their closed models
is the diagonal model [lem-chow-graph]; its projections
glue over the cover [lem-chow-glue] into a morphism
$\pi$ whose fibres over the charts are the pieces
[lem-chow-preimage], and $\pi$ is a proper surjection
that is an isomorphism over the dense open
[thm-chow-lemma], with the projective
factorization [cor-chow-lemma-proper].
