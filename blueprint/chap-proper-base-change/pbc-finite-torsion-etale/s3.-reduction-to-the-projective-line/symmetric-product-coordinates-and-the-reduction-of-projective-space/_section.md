The elementary symmetric functions of $n$ points of $\mathbf P^1$ define a
finite surjection $(\mathbf P^1_S)^n\to\mathbf P^n_S$
[def-weil-pbc-symmetric-product-morphism],
[lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-product-finite],
[lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-product-surjective]. Composition carries
$\mathrm{PBC}$ from the line to the product and cancellation along that
surjection carries it to projective space
[lem-weil-pbc-reduction-projective-space]; with Chow's lemma and target
locality this gives the general reduction
[prop-weil-pbc-reduction-to-projective-line].
