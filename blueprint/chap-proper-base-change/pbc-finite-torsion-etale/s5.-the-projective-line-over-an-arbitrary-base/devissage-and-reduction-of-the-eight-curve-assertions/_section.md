proper curve [prop-weil-pbc-algebraically-closed-extension], and its separably
closed form [prop-weil-pbc-proper-curve-sepclosed-invariance]. The eight assertions

zero-dimensional statements [lem-weil-pbc-zero-dimensional-cohomology],

out of the localization.
