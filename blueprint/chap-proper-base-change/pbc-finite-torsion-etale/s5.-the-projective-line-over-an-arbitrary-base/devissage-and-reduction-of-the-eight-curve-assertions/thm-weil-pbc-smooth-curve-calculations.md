For $k$ algebraically closed and $C$ a smooth separated finite-type $k$-scheme
of pure dimension one, the assertions of
[def-weil-pbc-eight-curve-assertions-detailed] hold for the constant sheaves
with the $r\neq\operatorname{char}k$ and $\operatorname{char}k=p>0$ cases, the
affine and proper refinements of $\mathcal C_2,\mathcal C_5,\mathcal C_7$, and
the pullback and restriction maps fixed there, exactly as recorded in the main
theorem of the Jacobian chapter.

**Status.** The statement is the theorem `thm:sc-main` of the Jacobian
chapter, which records it with the explicit cohomology computations; it is not
repeated here. The forward reference is genuine: that theorem lives in a
chapter compiled after this one.

## Proof

The statement is the main theorem of the Jacobian chapter: part (a) of that
theorem gives $\mathcal C_1,\mathcal C_4,\mathcal C_6,\mathcal C_8$ for
$r\neq\operatorname{char}k$, with $\mathcal C_2$ for $C$ affine and $\mathcal
C_5,\mathcal C_7$ for $C$ proper; part (b) gives $\mathcal C_1,\mathcal
C_3,\mathcal C_8$ for $\operatorname{char}k=p>0$, with the same affine and
proper refinements; part (c) identifies the maps of $\mathcal C_6$, $\mathcal
C_7$, and $\mathcal C_8$ with the pullback and restriction maps of
[def-weil-pbc-eight-curve-assertions-detailed].
