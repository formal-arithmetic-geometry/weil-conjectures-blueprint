The two reductions combine: the projective line satisfies $\mathrm{PBC}$ and
that implies it for every proper morphism locally of finite presentation, so
the exchange morphism is an isomorphism for $\mathbf F_r$-coefficients
[thm-weil-pbc-prime-coefficient], and devissage over the prime factors plus a
filtered colimit extends this to arbitrary torsion sheaves
[prop-weil-pbc-torsion-assembly].
