If $I$ is a direct summand of $M$ and $R^qh_*M=0$ for $q>0$, then
$R^qh_*I=0$ for $q>0$. This remains true after arbitrary base change.

## Proof

The higher direct image functors are additive, and additive functors preserve
retracts, so the direct summand structure on $I$ is preserved by $R^qh_*$; the
asserted vanishing follows from the vanishing on $M$. The argument is
insensitive to base change.
