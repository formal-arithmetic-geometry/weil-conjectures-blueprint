**Status.** Ordinary proper base change is used here only as the proper part of the raw compact-support comparison. Densification, open exchange, refinement independence, and all four displayed coherence diagrams are additional inputs.

**Formalization note.** The *geometric* inputs of § A43 are now formalized: existence of a
dense compactification of a separated finite-type morphism, refinements together with their
properness and their uniqueness out of a dense model, common dense refinements of two
compactifications, and flat base change of a dense compactification. What remains open in
§ A43 is the derived-categorical layer: the refinement comparison $\rho$ of
(BA43-RHO) being an isomorphism, the resulting $\kappa$ system and its
independence, the open–proper exchange (BA43-OPX), and densification after a
non-flat base change. Finite presentation of the constructed compactifications is also not
yet formalized.
