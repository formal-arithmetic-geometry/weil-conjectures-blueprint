Proper base change: for a proper finitely presented $f$ and a Cartesian
square, the exchange map $Lg^*Rf_*K\to Rf'_*Lg'^*K$ is an isomorphism, first
for finite torsion coefficients on the etale site and then for the adic and
$\Qlb$ coefficients of the Weil proof. The chapter is the S0--S12 proof,
in two parts, preceded by its one geometric input from outside the etale
theory.

**[chap-chow-lemma]**: a proper $S$-scheme is dominated by a projective one
[cor-chow-lemma-proper]. Only S3, the reduction to the projective line, cites
it; it depends on nothing in this chapter, only on the projective-space
results of [nagata-compactification], so it stands first.

**[curve-estimates]**: the eight assertions $\mathcal C_1,\dots,\mathcal C_8$ on
a curve and their proof for smooth curves [thm-sc-main], from the curve part
of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex`. Only the S5 devissage cites it; it rests on
[curves], [abelian-varieties] and [picard-schemes], not on this chapter.

**Formalized route.** The formalized statements of this chapter --- the exchange
map of S0 ([def-weil-pbc-cartesian-square], [def-weil-pbc-underived-exchange],
[def-weil-pbc-derived-exchange] with [lemma-weil-pbc-derived-exchange-replacement]
and [lemma-weil-pbc-derived-exchange-coherence], and [def-weil-pbc-property]),
the finite-morphism case of S1, the Chow embedding of S3 and the
universal-homeomorphism statements of S5 --- cite only formalized statements:
their `uses` follow the dependencies of the Lean proofs. Where the earlier
sketch had them cite unformalized lemmas the Lean proofs do not need (a
three-lemma comparison construction for the derived exchange map, the
acyclicity criterion for the finite case, the Chow factorization for the
embedding, a descent datum for the homeomorphism coverings), those edges were
dropped; the criterion and the factorization remain, for the proof of the
theorem itself.
