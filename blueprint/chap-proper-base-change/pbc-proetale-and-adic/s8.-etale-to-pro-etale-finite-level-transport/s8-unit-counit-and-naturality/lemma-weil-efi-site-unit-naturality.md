For $\alpha:F\to F'$ on $X_{\mathrm{et}}$,

$$
(\epsilon_{X,*}\epsilon_X^{-1}\alpha)\eta^X_F
=\eta^X_{F'}\alpha.
$$

## Proof

At an etale object $U$, each route sends $s\in F(U)$ to
$(1_{\iota_XU},\alpha_U(s))$.
