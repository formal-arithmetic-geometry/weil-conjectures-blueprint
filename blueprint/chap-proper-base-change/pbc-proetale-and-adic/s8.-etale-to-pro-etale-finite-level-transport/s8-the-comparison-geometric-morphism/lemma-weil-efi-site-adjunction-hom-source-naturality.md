For $u:F'\to F$ and $\alpha:\epsilon_X^{-1}F\to G$,

$$
\Phi(\alpha\,\epsilon_X^{-1}u)=\Phi(\alpha)u.
$$

## Proof

At $U$, each side sends $s\in F'(U)$ to
$\alpha_{\iota_XU}[(1_{\iota_XU},u_U(s))]$.
