For every $\beta:F\to\epsilon_{X,*}G$,

$$
\Phi(\Psi(\beta))=\beta.
$$

## Proof

At an etale object $U$, the left side sends $s$ to the evaluation of
$(1_{\iota_XU},s)$, namely
$G(1_{\iota_XU})(\beta_U(s))=\beta_U(s)$.
