For $A$-module sheaves $F$ on $X_{\mathrm{et}}$ and $G$ on
$X_{\mathrm{proet}}$, send
$\alpha:\epsilon_X^{-1}F\to G$ to the map $\Phi(\alpha):F\to\epsilon_{X,*}G$
whose component at $U$ is

$$
s\longmapsto
\alpha_{\iota_XU}\bigl[(1_{\iota_XU},s)\bigr].
$$
