For $v:G\to G'$ and $\alpha:\epsilon_X^{-1}F\to G$,

$$
\Phi(v\alpha)=\epsilon_{X,*}(v)\Phi(\alpha).
$$

## Proof

At $U$, each side sends $s\in F(U)$ to
$v_{\iota_XU}(\alpha_{\iota_XU}[(1_{\iota_XU},s)])$.
