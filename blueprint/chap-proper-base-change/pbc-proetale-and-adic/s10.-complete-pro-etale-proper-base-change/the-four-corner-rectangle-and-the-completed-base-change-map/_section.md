The complete pro-etale rectangle. Four objects of $D(Y'_{\Proet},P_n)$ are
attached to a cartesian square with proper $f$ and a derived $I$-complete
$K$ [def-weil-pbc-four-corners], joined by two projection-formula
isomorphisms and two exchange maps [def-weil-pbc-four-arrows]; the resulting
square commutes [lem-weil-pbc-finite-level-rectangle], is compatible with
the coefficient tower [lem-weil-pbc-tower-compatibility], and therefore
survives $\Rlim$ [lem-weil-pbc-rectangle-derived-limit]. The completed
base-change map is then the ordered composite through the limit of the
$B$-corner [def-weil-pbc-completed-base-change-map]; its level-$n$
reduction is the finite-level exchange map
[lemma-weil-pbc-completed-map-reduction], it is coherent
[lemma-weil-pbc-completed-exchange-coherence], and it is an isomorphism
whenever every $K_n$ carries a compatible etale-origin witness
[thm-weil-pbc-complete-proetale],
`prop-weil-pbc-complete-proetale-coherence`.
