For $b\geq a$, let

$$
\nu_{b,a,K}:
K_b\otimes^L_{P_b}P_a\longrightarrow K_a
$$

be the derived-tensor associator followed by the multiplication isomorphism
$P_b\otimes^L_{P_b}P_a\simeq P_a$.
