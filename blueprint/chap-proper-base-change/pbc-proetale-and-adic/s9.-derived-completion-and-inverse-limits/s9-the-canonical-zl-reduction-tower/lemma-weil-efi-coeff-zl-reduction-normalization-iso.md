The map $\nu_{b,a,K}$ is an isomorphism.

## Proof

The derived associator is an isomorphism. The second factor is the standard
derived unitor
$P_b\otimes^L_{P_b}P_a\simeq P_a$; composing the two isomorphisms gives the
claim.
