The displayed Hom differential satisfies $d^2=0$.

## Proof

For homogeneous $h$ of degree $r$, expanding $d(dh)$ gives
$d_B^2h-(-1)^rd_Bhd_A-(-1)^{r+1}d_Bhd_A-hd_A^2=0$.
