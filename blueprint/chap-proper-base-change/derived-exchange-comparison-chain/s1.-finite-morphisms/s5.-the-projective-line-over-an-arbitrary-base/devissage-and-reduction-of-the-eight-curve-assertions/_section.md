The proof of assertion $\mathcal C_7$ for an arbitrary torsion sheaf on a
proper curve [prop-weil-pbc-algebraically-closed-extension], and its separably
closed form [prop-weil-pbc-separably-closed-extension]. The eight assertions
are closed under extensions [lem-weil-pbc-eight-curve-devissage] and under
filtered colimits [lem-weil-pbc-curve-filtered-cohomology], which reduces to
constructible $\mathbf F_r$-coefficients
[lem-weil-pbc-curve-constructible-reduction]; localizing at a dense open
[lem-weil-pbc-curve-dense-open-localization] and passing to the normalization
[prop-weil-pbc-normalization-comparison] reduces to the smooth case, which is
quoted from the Jacobian chapter [thm-weil-pbc-smooth-curve-calculations]. The
zero-dimensional statements [lem-weil-pbc-zero-dimensional-cohomology],
[lem-weil-pbc-zero-dimensional-field-extension] handle the strata that fall
out of the localization.
