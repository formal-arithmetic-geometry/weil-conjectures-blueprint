For $g:Y'\to Y$ define

$$
Lg^*_{\mathrm{comp}}K:=
R\varprojlim_n\bigl(Lg^*K\otimes^L_\Lambda\Lambda/I^n\bigr).
$$

The comparison from ordinary pullback is the completion map of $Lg^*K$.
