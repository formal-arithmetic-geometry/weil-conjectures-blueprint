For $A,B$ on the same ringed pro-etale topos, define

$$
A\widehat\otimes^L_\Lambda B:=
R\varprojlim_n(A\otimes^L_\Lambda B\otimes^L_\Lambda P_n).
$$

Thus $A\widehat\otimes^L\Lambda$ is the derived $I$-completion of $A$.
