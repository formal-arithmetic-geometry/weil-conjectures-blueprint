The construction of the Chow modification. The Segre embedding
[def-weil-pbc-chow-segre-embedding] makes a product of projective spaces
projective [lem-weil-pbc-chow-segre-closed-immersion]; the scheme-theoretic
image of the diagonal of the charts inside the product of their closed models
is the diagonal model [lem-weil-pbc-chow-product-image-model]; its projections
glue over the cover [lem-weil-pbc-chow-projection-gluing] into a morphism
$\pi$ whose fibres over the charts are the pieces
[lem-weil-pbc-chow-fibres-proper-over-cover], and $\pi$ is a proper surjection
that is an isomorphism over the dense open
[lem-weil-pbc-chow-proper-surjective-isomorphism], with the projective
factorization [prop-weil-pbc-chow-closed-model-factorization].
