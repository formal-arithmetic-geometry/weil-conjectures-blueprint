For an etale arrow $b:U'\to U$, restriction carries
$\eta^X_{F,U}(s)$ to $\eta^X_{F,U'}(F(b)s)$.

## Proof

Restriction carries $(1_{\iota_XU},s)$ to $(\iota_Xb,s)$. The
left-Kan-extension relation identifies this representative with
$(1_{\iota_XU'},F(b)s)$.
