For an etale object $U\to X$ and a sieve $R\subset h_U$, let
$\iota_{X,!}^{\mathrm{sieve}}R$ be the sieve on $\iota_XU$ generated by the
arrows $\iota_XV\to\iota_XU$ with $V\to U$ in $R$.
