Let $u:T'\to T$ be finite and $F$ an etale sheaf on $T$. For an etale
$V\to T$ and $s\in F(V)$, define

$$
u_V^\sharp(s)\in(u^{-1}F)(V\times_TT')
$$

to be the image of $s$ under the unit from the presheaf inverse image to its
sheafification.
