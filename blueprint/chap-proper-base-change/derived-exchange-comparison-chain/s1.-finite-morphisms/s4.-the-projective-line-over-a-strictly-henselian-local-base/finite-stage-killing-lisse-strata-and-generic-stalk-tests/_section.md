Preparatory material for Gabber's affine comparison, under that subsection
heading in the source. A class killed on an integral strictly local cover is
already killed at a finite stage [lem-weil-pbc-finite-stage-killing], hence is
carried by a constructible subsheaf [lem-weil-pbc-constructible-approximation].
The remaining lemmas are the tools for testing monomorphisms of constructible
sheaves at finitely many generic geometric stalks
[lem-weil-pbc-constructible-noetherian-finite-selection], via irreducible
lisse strata [lem-weil-pbc-irreducible-lisse-strata] and the generic-stalk
criterion [lem-weil-pbc-lisse-generic-kernel].
