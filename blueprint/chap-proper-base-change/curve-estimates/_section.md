The smooth-curve cohomology estimates: the eight assertions
$\mathcal C_1,\dots,\mathcal C_8$ on a curve and their proof for smooth curves
[thm-sc-main], which the devissage of S5
([devissage-and-reduction-of-the-eight-curve-assertions]) quotes for smooth
curves. Like [chap-chow-lemma], it is an input from outside the etale theory:
it cites nothing else in this chapter, only [foundations], [curves],
[abelian-varieties] and [picard-schemes], so it stands before S0.

The first two sections, the curve setting and the statement of the
assertions, were filed under S5; they sit here with the estimates that prove
them. The remaining sections are chapter *The Jacobian and the smooth-curve
cohomology estimates* of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex`, without its first
section, which is in [picard-schemes]
([the-jacobian-the-theorem-of-the-base-and-picard-groups-of-curves]).
