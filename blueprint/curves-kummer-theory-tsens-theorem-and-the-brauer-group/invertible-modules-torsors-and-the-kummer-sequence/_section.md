The identification $H^1(C_{\Et},\mathbf G_m)=\operatorname{Pic}(C)$, proved
rather than cited. Duals make $\operatorname{Pic}$ a group
[lem-sc-invertible-dual], [lem-sc-pic-group]; $H^1$ with coefficients in an
abelian sheaf classifies torsors, through $\operatorname{Ext}^1$ against
$\mathbf Z_C$ [lem-sc-ext-torsor], [lem-sc-torsor-h1]; line bundles are
$\mathbf G_m$-torsors [lem-sc-line-bundle-torsor]; composing gives the
identification [lem-sc-h1-gm-pic]. The Kummer sequence is exact
[lem-sc-kummer-exact], a primitive root of unity exists and is stable under
extension of algebraically closed fields [lem-sc-primitive-root]. The
Čech-to-cohomology spectral sequence [lem-sc-cech-spectral] and the
finiteness of zero-dimensional closed subsets [lem-sc-dim0-finite] are the
two general tools the later sections draw on.
