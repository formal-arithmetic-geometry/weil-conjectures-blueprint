Let $C$ be a curve [def-sc-curves] over algebraically closed $k$ and let
$n$ be prime to $\operatorname{char}k$. The Kummer sequence of
[def-sc-kummer-sequence] is exact.

## Proof

The kernel of $t\mapsto t^n$ is $\mu_n$ by definition. For surjectivity:
let $U\to C$ be etale and $t\in\mathcal O_U(U)^*$. The scheme
$V:=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_U[x]/(x^n-t)\to U$ is a covering of $U$ in
the etale topology on which $t$ has an $n$-th root: indeed $V\to U$ is
finite flat (free of rank $n$, the polynomial being monic); it is
surjective (the fibre over every maximal ideal
$\mathfrak m\subset\mathcal O_U(U)$ is a $k(\mathfrak m)$-algebra of
dimension $n\geq1$, nonzero); and it is unramified: in
$B:=\mathcal O_U(U)[x]/(x^n-t)$ the ideal generated by the derivative
$nx^{n-1}$ is the unit ideal, because
$n\in k^*\subset\mathcal O_U(U)^*$ (as $n$ is prime to
$\operatorname{char}k$) and $t$ is a unit, so that
$B/(nx^{n-1})\cong\mathcal O_U(U)/(t)=0$ (from $x^{n-1}=0$ one gets
$t=x^n=x\cdot x^{n-1}=0$). The module of relative differentials is
$$
 \Omega_{V/U}=\mathcal O_U[x]/(x^n-t,\,nx^{n-1})=0
$$
(for a relative complete intersection
$\operatorname{Spec}A[x]/(f)\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ one has
$\Omega_{V/U}=A[x]/(f,f')$, where $f'=df/dx$), so $V\to U$ is unramified. A
morphism is etale if and only if it is locally of finite presentation,
flat, and unramified (the standard characterization of etale morphisms;
equivalently, etale is the class of morphisms which are formally etale and
locally of finite presentation), so $V\to U$ is finite etale and
surjective, i.e. an etale covering; therefore $t\mapsto t^n$ is surjective
as a morphism of etale sheaves, and the Kummer sequence
[def-sc-kummer-sequence] is exact.
