Let $C$ be a curve [def-sc-curves] over $k$ and let $n$ be prime to
$\operatorname{char}k$. The *Kummer sequence* is the sequence of etale
sheaves on $C_{\Et}$
$$
 1\longrightarrow\mu_n\longrightarrow\mathbf G_m
 \xrightarrow{\ t\mapsto t^n\ }\mathbf G_m\longrightarrow1.
$$
