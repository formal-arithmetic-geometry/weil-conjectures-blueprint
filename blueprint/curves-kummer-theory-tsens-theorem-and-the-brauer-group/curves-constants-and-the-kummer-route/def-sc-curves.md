Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field. A *curve over $k$* is a nonempty
*connected* (as a topological space) separated scheme of finite type over
$k$ of dimension one; it is *smooth* if it is smooth over $k$, *proper* if
it is proper over $k$, and *affine* if it is an affine scheme. $\mathcal C$
denotes the category of smooth separated curves over $k$.

Here $k$ is required to be algebraically closed and curves have pure
dimension one. Zero-dimensional schemes in the base-change interfaces are
treated separately by their point cohomology. The Picard chapter also uses
curves over arbitrary fields; its smooth curves over algebraically closed
fields satisfy this definition.
