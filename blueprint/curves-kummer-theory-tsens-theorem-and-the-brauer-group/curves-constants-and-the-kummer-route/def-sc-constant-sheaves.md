Let $C$ be a curve [def-sc-curves] and let $n\geq1$. The *constant etale
sheaf* $\mathbf F_n$ on $C_{\Et}$ is the sheaf
$\underline{\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z}$ associated to the constant presheaf
$U\mapsto\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$; its geometric stalks are
$\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$.
