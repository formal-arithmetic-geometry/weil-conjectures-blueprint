The smooth-curve calculations, serving the open interface
`thm:weil-pbc-smooth-curve-calculations` of the proper-base-change chapter
(OBL-PBC-CURVES). The route: identify $\mathbf F_n$ with $\mu_n$ through a
primitive $n$-th root of unity; the Kummer sequence; $H^1(C,\mathbf
G_m)=\operatorname{Pic}(C)$; the structure of $\operatorname{Pic}$ for
proper and affine smooth curves; Brauer group zero; cohomological-dimension
bounds including Artin--Schreier for the $p$-primary part; then the
assertions C1--C8 of `def:weil-pbc-eight-curve-assertions` for the constant
sheaves.

Two splits of the source have already happened and are visible here. The
divisor, degree, Riemann--Roch and duality material moved to the next
chapter, together with the divisor, Picard-functor, local-ring and
Kummer-sequence vocabulary those items need and this one does not; the
algebraic-group material (45 statements: group schemes and their subgroup
schemes, translations and characters; abelian varieties, isogenies,
multiplication by $n$ and $n$-torsion; the theorem of the cube and
rigidity) moved to the group-schemes and abelian-varieties chapters.
Nothing here cites a label of either. Three further statements
(`lem:sc-pic-proper-ntorsion`, `lem:sc-pic-affine`, `lem:sc-ga-cohomology`)
were moved to the Jacobian chapter and appear in the source only as
placeholder comments; they are not imported here.

Section order follows the source: each section stands after the material it
uses. The source records that this cut the chapter's forward references
from one to one, exchanging its sections 2–5.
