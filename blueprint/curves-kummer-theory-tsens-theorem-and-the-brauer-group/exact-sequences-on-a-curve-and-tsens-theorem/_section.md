Tsen's theorem and the two exact sequences the cohomological-dimension
bounds run on. $\mathbf Z$ has no higher cohomology on a point over an
algebraically closed field [lem-sc-point-z-cohomology]. The $C_1$ property
passes to finite extensions [lem-sc-c1-finite-extension] and kills the
Brauer group [lem-sc-c1-brauer-trivial], which with $k(t)$ being $C_1$
gives $\operatorname{Br}(k(C))=0$ [lem-sc-tsen]. In characteristic $p$ the
Artin--Schreier sequence [lem-sc-as-sequence] handles the $p$-primary part,
and every constructible torsion sheaf on a curve has a finite filtration by
$j_!L$'s and skyscrapers [lem-sc-standard-resolution].
