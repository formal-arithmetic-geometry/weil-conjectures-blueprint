title = "Reduction to a field"

If $\mathrm{BC}(g,n,q-1)$ holds but $\mathrm{BC}(g,n,q)$ fails, then
some failure occurs for an affine base change $T\to Y$, direct image along a
morphism $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to T$ with $\Omega$ a field, and a sheaf of
$\mathbf Z/n$-modules on $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$.
