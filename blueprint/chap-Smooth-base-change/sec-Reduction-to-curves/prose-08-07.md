Recall that $\mathrm{BC}(g,n,r)$ asserts base change in degrees $\leq r$,
for $\mathbf Z/n$-coefficients, along every base change $Y'\times_YT\to T$
of $g$. Assume now that $q\geq1$ and that $\mathrm{BC}(g,n,q-1)$ holds. Under
this assumption we show that if $\mathrm{BC}(g,n,q)$ fails, it also fails in a special form:
with constant coefficients and with direct image from the spectrum of an
algebraically closed field
(Lemma [lemma-reduction-curve-over-normal-base]). The next four lemmas,
all on the étale sites, carry out this reduction step by step.

We argue by contradiction rather than by dévissage, because a nonzero
cokernel of the base change map can be transported along an embedding of
coefficient sheaves $M\hookrightarrow N$, whereas an isomorphism cannot.
