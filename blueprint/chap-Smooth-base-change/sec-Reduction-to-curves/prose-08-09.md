The field $\Omega$ produced by this lemma is arbitrary, and the base $T$
is still an arbitrary affine scheme. To bring them into the special
situation of Lemma [lemma-reduction-curve-over-normal-base] we want to replace $\Omega$
by its algebraic closure and $T$ by a normal scheme. Both replacements are
integral morphisms, and the next lemma shows that a failure survives them.
