All other cases follow from the case of torsion sheaves, which is the
next theorem. Its proof passes through base schemes of infinite type over $k$,
such as spectra of fields and of integral closures. The comparison
results of Section [chap-The-pro-etale-site] hold over arbitrary
schemes for bounded below complexes, so they apply to these bases as
well.
