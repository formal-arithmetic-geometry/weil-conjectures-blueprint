The theorem on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ and the Künneth formula concern complexes, and
the integral case of the theorem on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ is reduced to complexes with
finite coefficients. So we need smooth base change for complexes of torsion
sheaves. Bounded below complexes suffice, since objects of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ are
bounded.
