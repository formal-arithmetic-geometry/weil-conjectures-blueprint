For $f=j:\mathbf G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$ and $M=A$, the lemma
says that the stalk of $R(j\times\operatorname{id}_T)_*A$ at a point
$(0,t)$ has the same cohomology as the stalk of $Rj_*A$ at the origin,
namely $A$ in degree $0$ and $A(-1)$ in degree $1$, whatever $T$ is.
The Künneth formula allows an arbitrary constructible second factor
in place of the constant one, and we first define the map it concerns.
