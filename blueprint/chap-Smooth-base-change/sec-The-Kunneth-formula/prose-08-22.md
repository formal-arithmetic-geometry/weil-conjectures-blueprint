Over a general finite ring the truncations of a complex in [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) need
not have [finite Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves), so the argument by truncation does not apply
directly. Instead we reduce to the residue fields and use derived Nakayama.
