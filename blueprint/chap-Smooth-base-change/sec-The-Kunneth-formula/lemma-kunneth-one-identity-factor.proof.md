We show that the cone $N$ of (8.4) vanishes
modulo every maximal ideal of $A$, and conclude by derived Nakayama on
stalks. The source and target of (8.4) are in
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y\times T,A)$ by Proposition [proposition-six-operations-dctf], so every
geometric stalk of $N$ is a perfect $A$-complex. Let
$\mathfrak m\subset A$ be a maximal ideal and put $\kappa=A/\mathfrak m$. The
change-of-coefficients maps of
Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients] for pullback, tensor product and
ordinary direct image respect the counit defining the
Künneth map. Hence they identify the reduction
$(8.4)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_A \kappa$ with the map
(8.4) for $M\otimes^{\mathbf L}_A \kappa$ and
$Q\otimes^{\mathbf L}_A \kappa$. The latter is an isomorphism by
Lemma [lemma-kunneth-field-coefficients]. This means that
$N\otimes^{\mathbf L}_A \kappa=0$ for every maximal ideal. The Artinian ring $A$
is a product of local rings. At a geometric point $\bar z$, derived
Nakayama for the perfect complex $N_{\bar z}$ therefore shows that
$N_{\bar z}=0$. Since $N$ lies in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y\times T,A)$, it comes from a
complex on the small étale site by
Theorem [theorem-comparison-constructible-sheaves]. There vanishing is
detected on geometric stalks, so $N=0$.
