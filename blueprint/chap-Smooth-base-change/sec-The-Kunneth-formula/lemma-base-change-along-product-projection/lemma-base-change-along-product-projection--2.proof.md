For $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, both sides lie in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$, and the base change map is compatible with reduction modulo $\pi_E^n$ (Lemma [lemma-change-coefficients]). Its reduction is the base change map for $M\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, an isomorphism by clause [lemma-base-change-along-product-projection--1]. A map in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\mathcal O_E)$ whose reduction modulo $\pi_E$ is an isomorphism is an isomorphism, by derived Nakayama. The cases $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ follow by inverting $\ell$ and extending scalars, which commute with $p_Y^*$, $p_X^*$ and the direct images (Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients]).
