For $\Lambda\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ and $M\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, the base change map $p_Y^*Rf_*M\to R(f\times\operatorname{id}_T)_*p_X^*M$ is an isomorphism.
