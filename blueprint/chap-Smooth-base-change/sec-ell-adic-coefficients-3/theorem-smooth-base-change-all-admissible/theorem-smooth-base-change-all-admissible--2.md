For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$, the map

$$
g^*_{\mathcal O_E}Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E}K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf'_{*,\mathcal O_E}g'^*_{\mathcal O_E}K
$$

is an isomorphism.
