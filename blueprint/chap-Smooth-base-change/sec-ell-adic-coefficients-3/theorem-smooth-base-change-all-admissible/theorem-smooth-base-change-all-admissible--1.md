For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$, the map

$$
g^*_{A}Rf_{*,A}K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf'_{*,A}g'^*_{A}K
\tag{8.2}
$$

is an isomorphism.
