For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$, the map

$$
g^*_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}Rf_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf'_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}g'^*_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}K
$$

is an isomorphism.
