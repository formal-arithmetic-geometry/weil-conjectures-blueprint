The inverse image $\nu_X^*$ is exact and its values are classical, so the
cohomology sheaves $H^q(K)\simeq\nu_X^*H^q(K_{\acute{e}t})$ are classical and
vanish for all but finitely many $q$. Thus $K$ lies in
$D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$. Since $N$ is invertible on $Y$,
smooth base change for classical torsion complexes
(Theorem [theorem-smooth-base-change-classical-torsion-2], with
$\Lambda=A$ and $n=N$) shows that (8.2) is an
isomorphism. Neither finite Tor amplitude nor a finite type hypothesis
enters.

For the last assertion, let $A$ be finite and killed by a power of $\ell$,
which is invertible on $Y$, and let $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$. Then $K$ is
bounded with constructible cohomology, $X$ is of finite type over $k$,
hence quasi-compact and quasi-separated, $f$ is a morphism of schemes of
finite type over $k$, hence quasi-compact and quasi-separated, and $A$
is Noetherian. By
Theorem [theorem-comparison-constructible-sheaves]
(clause [theorem-comparison-constructible-sheaves--3]) there are a $K_{\acute{e}t}\in\Dbc(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$ and an
isomorphism $\nu_X^*K_{\acute{e}t}\simeq K$, so the first part applies.
