For integral and rational coefficients we have $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}=\Dbc$, so in those cases
the theorem concerns all bounded constructible complexes. The isomorphisms
(8.2) respect composition of cartesian squares
and change of coefficients, because the base change map itself does
(Lemmas [lemma-naturality-pasting-comparison]
and [lemma-change-coefficients]).
