The simplest case already shows how the proof goes. Take $C=\mathbf G_{m,S}$
with coordinate $x$. Then $H^1(\mathbf G_{m,\Omega},\mathbf Z/m)$ is cyclic
of order $m$, generated by the class of the Kummer cover $y^m=x$. We view
this cover as a $\mathbf Z/m$-torsor through a primitive $m$-th root of
unity, which lies in $R$ because $R$ is normal and $L$ is algebraically
closed. The cover is already defined over $\mathbf G_{m,S}$, and it is
finite étale there because $m$ is invertible on $S$. The class becomes
trivial after pullback to it. The proof below produces such a cover for an
arbitrary class.
