Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative ring, and let $g:Y'\to Y$ be flat and
locally of finite presentation with geometrically reduced fibres. For
every cartesian square (8.1) with $f$ qcqs and every sheaf of
$\Lambda$-modules $F_{\acute{e}t}$ on $X_{\acute{e}t}$, the degree-zero map
induced by [definition-derived-base-change-map],

$$
H^0\bigl(g^{-1}Rf_*(F_{\acute{e}t}[0])\bigr)
\longrightarrow
H^0\bigl(Rf'_*g'^{-1}(F_{\acute{e}t}[0])\bigr),
$$

is an isomorphism. There is no torsion hypothesis on $F_{\acute{e}t}$.
