In degree zero the base change map can be computed on sections, even for
sheaves of sets. Sections of $f_*F$ over $V$ are sections of $F$ over
$V\times_YX$, and the question is whether pulling back to a $Y'$-scheme $U$
over $V$ creates new sections. It does not when $U\to V$ is flat,
quasi-compact and quasi-separated with geometrically connected fibres,
because pullback along such a map does not change sections of the pullback
of an étale sheaf. Moreover, a flat $g$ locally of finite presentation with
geometrically reduced fibres factors locally into such a map followed by an
étale one, and in this way Lemma [lemma-degree-zero-calculation] proves
the isomorphism for every such $g$.
