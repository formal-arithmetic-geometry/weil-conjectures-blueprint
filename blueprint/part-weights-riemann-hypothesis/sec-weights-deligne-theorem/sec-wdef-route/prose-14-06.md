Once weights are defined for complexes, Deligne's theorem becomes the
statement that $Rf_!$ preserves a class of complexes. The shift by $i$
in Definition [sk-d-real-weights] makes this statement uniform in the
degree. For the structure morphism of $X_0$, one condition on
$R\Gamma_c(X,K)$ bounds all the groups $H^n_c(X,K)$ at once, and
replacing $K$ by $K[r]$ only moves the bound by $r$
(Proposition [sk-r-real-linear]). The theorem is proved in
Section [sec-deligne-laumon-general]; the sections before it work on a single
curve, and the purity of the cohomology of a smooth proper variety is
deduced from it in Corollary [sk-input-weights].

For a separated $k_0$-scheme of finite type $X_0$, we write
$D_{\leq w}(X_0)$ for the category of bounded constructible Weil
complexes $K$ on $X_0$ of weights at most $w$ in the sense of
Definition [sk-d-real-weights], that is, such that $\mathcal H^i(K)$ is
mixed of weights at most $w+i$ for every $i$.
