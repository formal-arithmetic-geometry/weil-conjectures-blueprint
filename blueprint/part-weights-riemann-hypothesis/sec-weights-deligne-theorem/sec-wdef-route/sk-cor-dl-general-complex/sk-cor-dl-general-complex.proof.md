Apply $Rf_!$ to the ordinary truncation tower of the underlying
geometric complex $K$. Since this tower is bounded and functorial, we
obtain the spectral sequence

$$
E_2^{a,b}=R^af_!\mathcal H^b(K)
\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathcal H^{a+b}(Rf_!K).
$$

By naturality in $K$ and the Frobenius compatibility of $Rf_!$, its
exact couple, its differentials and its finite abutment filtration
carry compatible Frobenius actions. By
Theorem [sk-thm-dl-general-sheaf], $E_2^{a,b}$ is mixed of
weights at most $w+a+b$, and every $E_\infty^{a,b}$ is a subquotient
of this term. Therefore the finite filtration of
$\mathcal H^n(Rf_!K)$ has mixed quotients of weights at most $w+n$,
and hence $\mathcal H^n(Rf_!K)$ is mixed of weights at most $w+n$.
For the last assertion, we apply this to the structure map of $X_0$.
Its direct image has as geometric fibre the compactly supported
cohomology $H_c^n(X,K)$, with its Frobenius operator.
