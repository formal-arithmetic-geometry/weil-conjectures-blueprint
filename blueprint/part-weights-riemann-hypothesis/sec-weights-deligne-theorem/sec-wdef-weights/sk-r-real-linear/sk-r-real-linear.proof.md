By Lemma [sk-p-linear-filtration], the eigenvalues on a
subobject or a quotient occur among those of the whole. For the subquotient
assertions, we intersect a mixed filtration with a subobject, or take
its images in a quotient. To filter an extension, we first filter its
subobject and then take the inverse images of a filtration of its
quotient. Since the coefficients form a field, the tensor product of
two filtrations has as associated graded the tensor product of the two
associated gradeds, and Lemma [sk-p-linear-filtration] describes
the eigenvalues of tensor products and duals. At a closed point of an
inverse image, the Frobenius is a positive power of the Frobenius at
the image point, and the residue field degree is multiplied by the same
integer, which proves the assertion on inverse images. Finally,
$\mathcal H^i(K[r])=\mathcal H^{i+r}(K)$, and the local eigenvalue of
$\mathcal B_b$ at $x$ is $b^{\deg(x)}$. For the Tate sheaf, recall that
arithmetic Frobenius acts on roots of unity by raising to the $q$th
power, and geometric Frobenius is its inverse.
