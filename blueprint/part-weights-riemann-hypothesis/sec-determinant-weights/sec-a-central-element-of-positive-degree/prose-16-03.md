To compute determinant weights, we construct from the monodromy groups
a central element of positive degree
(Proposition [sk-p-central]). It acts by a scalar
on each simple factor, and this scalar determines the determinant
weight of the factor. We construct the element for semisimple
representations, and then use stable filtrations to obtain the
formulas for arbitrary representations.

The degree has to be positive. An element of $H$ that is central in
$G$ also acts by a scalar on each simple factor $Q$, but its
determinant on $Q$ is a root of unity, as for every element of $H$,
so under $\iota$ the scalar has absolute value one and records no
weight. An element
$z=h\rho(s)^N$ with $N>0$ contains $N$ factors of Frobenius, and its
scalar $c_Q$ on $Q$ satisfies
$\lambda_\iota(c_Q)=N\operatorname{dw}_\iota(Q)$
(Lemma [sk-p-readout]).

The central element is produced by Lie theory, which applies to
algebraic groups and not to the abstract images of $\pi_1(U)$ and
$W(U_0)$. For $\mathcal B_b$ with $b$ not a root of unity, the image of
$W(U_0)$ in $\GL_1$ is the infinite cyclic group generated by $b$, which
is not an algebraic subgroup; its Zariski closure is all of $\GL_1$,
since the proper closed subgroups of $\mathbf G_m$ are finite. Passing
to the closure loses nothing that we need, because a subspace of $V$ is
stable under a group of matrices if and only if it is stable under its
Zariski closure, stabilizers being closed. The closures are what lets
us read determinant weights off a central element: they are the input of
Lemma [sk-p-product] and
Proposition [sk-p-central].

Let $\rho:W(U_0)\to\GL(V)$ be a Weil representation. Its *geometric*
and *arithmetic monodromy groups* are the Zariski closures
$H$ of $\rho(\pi_1(U))$ and $G$ of $\rho(W(U_0))$ in $\GL(V)$.
We keep the letters $H$ and $G$ for these two groups in the rest of
this section.
