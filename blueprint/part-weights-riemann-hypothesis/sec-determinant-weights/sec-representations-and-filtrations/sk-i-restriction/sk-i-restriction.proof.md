It suffices to consider a simple $\Gamma$-module $V$. A nonzero
$N$-submodule $A$ of least dimension is simple, and since $N$ is
normal, each translate $gA$ is again a simple $N$-submodule. The sum
of all these translates is a nonzero $\Gamma$-submodule, hence equals
$V$. It remains to see that a finite sum of simple submodules is
semisimple. The intersection of a simple submodule with the sum of
those already chosen is either zero or the whole submodule. Adding it
in the first case and omitting it in the second, we obtain a
direct-sum decomposition after finitely many steps.
