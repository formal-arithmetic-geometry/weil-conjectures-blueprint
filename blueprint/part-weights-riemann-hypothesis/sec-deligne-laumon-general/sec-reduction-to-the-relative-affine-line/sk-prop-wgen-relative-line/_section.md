Let $p:\mathbf A^1_{Y_0}\to Y_0$ be the projection, and let $G$ be a
constructible Weil sheaf on $\mathbf A^1_{Y_0}$, mixed of weights at
most $w$. Then $R^ip_!G$ is mixed of weights at most $w+i$ for every
$i$.
