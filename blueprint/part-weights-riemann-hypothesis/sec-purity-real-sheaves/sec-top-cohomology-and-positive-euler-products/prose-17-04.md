The positivity in the next
lemma is the $\ell$-adic form of Rankin's trick: the even powers of a
real trace are nonnegative, so the Euler product of
$\mathcal G^{\otimes2m}$ has nonnegative coefficients, and then each
local factor converges wherever the whole product does.
