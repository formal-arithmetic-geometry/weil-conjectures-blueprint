The open curve $U_0$ is again smooth, geometrically connected and
affine, and Theorem [sk-thm-wrp-l2-dictionary] on $U_0$ gives
$\nu(\mathcal G)=w$. Extension by zero only adds zero traces, so
$\nu(j_!\mathcal G)=w$. The trace functions of $j_*$
and of the extension by zero differ only at the points of
$X_0-U_0$, by the trace function of the boundary stalks. By
Proposition [sk-p-real-semi] these stalks have weights at most
$w$, so by Lemma [sk-lem-wrp-l2-growth], applied to the complex supported
on $X_0-U_0$, the squared sum of the difference is at most
$Cq^{nw}$. Since $\nu(j_!\mathcal G)=w>w-1$,
the last assertion of Lemma [sk-lem-wrp-l2-growth] shows that
$\nu(j_*\mathcal G)$ is still $w$.
