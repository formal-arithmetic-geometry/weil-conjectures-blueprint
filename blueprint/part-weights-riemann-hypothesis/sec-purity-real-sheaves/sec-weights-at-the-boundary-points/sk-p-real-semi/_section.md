Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow X_0$ be a dense open of a smooth
geometrically connected curve. If a lisse Weil sheaf $\mathcal F$ on
$U_0$ is punctually of weights $\le a$, so is $j_*\mathcal F$ on
$X_0$.
