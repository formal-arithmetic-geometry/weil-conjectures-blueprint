Let $p_1:X\times_SY\to X$ and $p_2:X\times_SY\to Y$ be the projections,
so that $K\boxtimes_SL=p_1^*K\otimes p_2^*L$. By proper base change, $Rp_{1!}p_2^*L\simeq f^*Rg_!L$. Applying the
projection formula first to $p_1$ and then to $f$, we get

$$
Rh_!(p_1^*K\otimes p_2^*L)
\simeq Rf_!(K\otimes f^*Rg_!L)
\simeq Rf_!K\otimes Rg_!L.
$$

This isomorphism is natural and compatible with Weil structures,
because base change and the projection formula are. For smooth maps,
the compatibility with traces follows from the composition law for
the counits.
