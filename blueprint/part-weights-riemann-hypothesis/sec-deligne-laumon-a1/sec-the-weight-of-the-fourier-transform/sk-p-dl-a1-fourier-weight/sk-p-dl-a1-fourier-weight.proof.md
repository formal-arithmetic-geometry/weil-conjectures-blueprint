Let $v$ be the weight of $M'$ given by
Proposition [sk-p-dl-a1-some-weight]. Shifts do not change the
squared absolute values of traces, and for a nonzero pure lisse sheaf of
weight $u$ on a dense open of the line, its direct image $j_*$ has
$\nu=u$ (Corollary [sk-cor-wrp-l2-real-blocks]). Applying this
to $M'$ and to $M$, and using the Plancherel formula
(Corollary [sk-cor-plancherel]), we get

$$
v=\nu(j'_*M')
=\nu(\operatorname{FT}_\psi((j_*M)[1]))
=\nu((j_*M)[1])+1=w+1.
$$
