Since $j_*$ is left exact, it suffices to show that the connecting map
$\delta:j_*F_2\to R^1j_*F_1$, which is Frobenius-equivariant, vanishes.
It is zero on $U$, so we look at a point of $C\setminus U$. There the stalk of
the source has weights at most $w$ by
Proposition [sk-p-real-semi]. We apply the same result to
the dual $F_1^\vee$, which is pure of weight $-w$. Since
Lemma [sk-i-dl-a1-local] identifies the stalk of the target
with the dual of the stalk of $j_*F_1^\vee$ twisted by $(-1)$, we see
that the target has weights at least $w+2$. The image of $\delta$ is a
quotient of the source and a submodule of the target, so its
eigenvalues would have weights both at most $w$ and at least $w+2$.
Hence $\delta$ vanishes at each point of $C\setminus U$, and $\delta=0$.
