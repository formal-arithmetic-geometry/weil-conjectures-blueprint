Let $\mathcal V$ be a nonzero lisse Weil sheaf of rank $r$ on a smooth
geometrically connected curve. If the irreducible constituents
$\mathcal V_1,\dots,\mathcal V_n$ of $\mathcal V$ are pure of weights
$w_1,\dots,w_n$, then
$\operatorname{dw}_\iota(\mathcal V)=\sum_a\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal V_a)w_a/r$.
In particular a sheaf pure of weight $w$ has determinant weight $w$.
