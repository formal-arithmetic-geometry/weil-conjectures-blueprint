The determinant is a rank-one lisse Weil sheaf defined over a finite
extension of $E$, and so Theorem [sk-p-real-rankone] and
Corollary [sk-cor-wrp-rankone-pure] apply to it. A finite extension of
scalars changes neither its character nor its absolute value under the
fixed embedding. The weight of $\det\mathcal V$ is given by the
formula of Corollary [sk-cor-wrp-rankone-pure], and dividing it by $r$ we
obtain $\operatorname{dw}_\iota(\mathcal V)$. Finally, a
finite-dimensional representation has a composition series, and each
factor is again lisse and defined over a finite extension of $\Ql$,
because after enlarging $E$ the invariant subspaces and quotients have
bases defined over $E$. Hence the same argument applies to all the
constituents in the statement.
