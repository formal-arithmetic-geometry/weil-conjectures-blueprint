By Theorem [sk-thm-nagata-compactification], $U_0$ is an open subscheme of a
proper $k_0$-scheme. We take the reduced closure of $U_0$ in it and
normalize this closure in $k_0(U_0)$. Since a scheme of finite type
over a field is Nagata, the normalization is finite
[Stacks, Tag 035B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/035B), [Stacks, Tag 035S](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/035S), as in the normalization arguments of
Appendix [sec-curve-estimates], and it is an isomorphism over the normal
open $U_0$. The resulting proper normal curve is regular, because a
one-dimensional noetherian normal local domain is a discrete valuation
ring. Hence it is smooth over the perfect field $k_0$
[Stacks, Tag 0B8Y](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B8Y). Its boundary is zero-dimensional and proper over $k_0$,
hence finite, and its reduced structure is étale because $k_0$ is
perfect. After base change to $k$, the curve $U$ is dense and
connected, and therefore its closure $C$ is connected. Finally, we
need $C_0$ to be projective in order to construct the Jacobian, and a
proper scheme of dimension one over a field is projective
[Stacks, Tag 0A26](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A26); concretely, a divisor of degree at least $2g+1$, where
$g$ is the genus, is very ample by Riemann–Roch.
