Use the compactification $f=pj$ in the source proof. The composition identification gives $Rf_{*,A}=Rp_{*,A}Rj_{*,A}$. For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$, the open-immersion result [proposition-open-direct-image-preserves-finite] places $Rj_{*,A}K$ in the constructible category of the compactification. Clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--1] then places its proper direct image in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$. This is $Rf_{*,A}K$.
