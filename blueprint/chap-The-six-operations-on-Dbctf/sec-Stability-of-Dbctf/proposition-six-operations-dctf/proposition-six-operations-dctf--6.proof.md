Choose a compactification $f=pj$. By construction, $Rf_{!,A}=Rp_{*,A}j_{!,A}$. Extension by zero is exact and preserves finite Tor amplitude and constructibility, by Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-extension-zero]; thus it sends $K$ into the constructible category on the compactification. Apply the proper-image preservation clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--1] to $j_{!,A}K$. The resulting object is $Rf_{!,A}K$ and lies in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$.
