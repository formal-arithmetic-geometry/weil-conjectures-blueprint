Use the compactification $f=pj$ in the source proof. The composition identification gives $Rf_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}=Rp_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}Rj_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$. For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$, the open-immersion result [proposition-direct-image-along-open-immersion--3] places $Rj_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}K$ in the constructible category of the compactification. Clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--4] then places its proper direct image in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$. This is $Rf_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}K$.
