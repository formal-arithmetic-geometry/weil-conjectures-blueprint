Use the compactification $f=pj$ in the source proof. The composition identification gives $Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E}=Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E}Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E}$. For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$, the open-immersion result [proposition-direct-image-along-open-immersion--1] places $Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E}K$ in the constructible category of the compactification. Clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--2] then places its proper direct image in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$. This is $Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E}K$.
