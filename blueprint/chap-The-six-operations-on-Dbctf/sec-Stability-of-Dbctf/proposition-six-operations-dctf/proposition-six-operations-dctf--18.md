For every admissible $\Lambda$, and $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, the adjunction $-\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Lambda_X}K\dashv\cHom_{\Lambda_X}(K,-)$ on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ is the restriction of the ambient adjunction on $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$.
