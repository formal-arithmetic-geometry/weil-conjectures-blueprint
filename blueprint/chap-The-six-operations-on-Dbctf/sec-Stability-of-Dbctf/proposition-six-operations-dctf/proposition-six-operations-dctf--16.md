For every admissible $\Lambda$, the adjunction $f^*_\Lambda\dashv Rf_{*,\Lambda}$ on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ is the restriction of the ambient adjunction on $D(-_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$.
