Pull back the finite stratification for $K$; the inverse images of its strata form a finite stratification of $X$. On a stratum, pullback of a locally perfect model is again locally perfect, with the same finite bounds. For finite coefficients this follows from the bounded flat constructible models of Lemma [lemma-flat-models-generators]. For $\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ use Lemma [lemma-inverse-image-tensor-product]: the finitely generated modules on a stratum have finite free resolutions over a DVR or a field. Hence boundedness, constructibility and finite Tor amplitude are preserved.
