The ambient adjunction is $Rf_{!,\Lambda}\dashv f^!_\Lambda$. The preservation clauses [proposition-six-operations-dctf--6]–[proposition-six-operations-dctf--13] show that both functors carry the indicated constructible subcategories into one another. Since these subcategories are full, the ambient adjunction bijections restrict to their Hom sets. They remain natural, and their units and counits are the ambient ones, so their triangle identities are unchanged.
