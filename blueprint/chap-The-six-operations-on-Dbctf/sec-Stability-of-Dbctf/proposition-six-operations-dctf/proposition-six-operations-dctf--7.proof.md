Choose a compactification $f=pj$. By construction, $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E}=Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E}j_{!,\mathcal O_E}$. Extension by zero is exact and preserves finite Tor amplitude and constructibility, by Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-extension-zero]; thus it sends $K$ into the constructible category on the compactification. Apply the proper-image preservation clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--2] to $j_{!,\mathcal O_E}K$. The resulting object is $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E}K$ and lies in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$.
