Choose a common refinement of the finite stratifications of $K$ and $L$. On each stratum their locally perfect models are bounded complexes of finite projective modules, so their derived tensor is represented by the bounded tensor complex of those models. Its terms are finite projective. For finite coefficients one may use the bounded flat models of Lemma [lemma-flat-models-generators]; for the other coefficients use Lemma [lemma-inverse-image-tensor-product]. A finite stratification gives common finite cohomological and Tor bounds, proving the result.
