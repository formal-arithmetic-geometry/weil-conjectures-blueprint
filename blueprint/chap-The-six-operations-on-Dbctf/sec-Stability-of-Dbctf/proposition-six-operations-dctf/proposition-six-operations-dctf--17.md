For every admissible $\Lambda$, the adjunction $Rf_{!,\Lambda}\dashv f^!_\Lambda$ on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ is the restriction of the ambient adjunction on $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda))$.
