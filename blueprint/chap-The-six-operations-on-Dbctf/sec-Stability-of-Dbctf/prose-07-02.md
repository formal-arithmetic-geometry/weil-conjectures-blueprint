Pullback and tensor product are local, and they preserve the description
of a complex of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ on the strata of a stratification as
$\Lambda_S\otimes P$ with $P$ perfect. The direct images and internal Hom
are not local. For the direct images a compactification $f=pj$ helps.
Since $Rf_*=Rp_*Rj_*$, it suffices to know proper direct image and direct
image along an open immersion. Internal Hom hides both of these
operations and also $f^!$. For an open immersion $j$ we have
$\cHom(j_!\Lambda,K)=Rj_*j^*K$, and for a closed immersion $i$ we have
the formula $\cHom(i_*\Lambda,K)\simeq i_*i^!K$ recalled at the beginning
of this chapter. If $a$ is the locally closed immersion of a
stratum and $P$ is a perfect complex on it, the formula

$$
\cHom(a_!P,K)\simeq Ra_*\bigl(P^\vee\otimes^{\mathbf L}a^!K\bigr)
$$

combines the two. Every constructible complex is built from objects
$a_!P$ by finitely many cones. By this formula, internal Hom is therefore
expressed through $Ra_*$, $a^!$ and tensor products. This is one reason we
needed $f^!$ on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ first. The result is Proposition [proposition-six-operations-dctf].
