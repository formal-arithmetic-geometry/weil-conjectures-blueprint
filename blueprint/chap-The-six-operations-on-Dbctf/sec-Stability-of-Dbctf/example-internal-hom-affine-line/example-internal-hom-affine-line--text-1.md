*Shared context.* Over an algebraically closed $k$, let $j:U=\mathbf G_{m,k}\hookrightarrow X=\mathbf A^1_k$ and let $i$ be the inclusion of the origin. The localization sequence $0\to j_!\Lambda_U\to\Lambda_X\to i_*\Lambda\to0$ shows that $\Lambda_X$ is built from the open and closed pieces. The following are the separate calculations in Example [example-closed-support]; the open piece uses nonproper direct image, and the closed piece uses exceptional inverse image.
