Use Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients] and the defining adjunction formulas for the base-change maps.

In Lean, the exceptional base-change map is a mate of compact-support base change. Its compatibility with each coefficient change is proved using the corresponding comparison isomorphisms and the identity for transporting mates. These individual identities do not state the general assertion for arbitrary expressions in the six operations.
