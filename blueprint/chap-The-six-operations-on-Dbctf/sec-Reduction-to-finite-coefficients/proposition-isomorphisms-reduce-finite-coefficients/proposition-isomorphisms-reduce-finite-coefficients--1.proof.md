The coefficient comparisons for the six operations are those of Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients]. That proposition identifies their units, counits and composition maps after scalar extension. Associativity identifies the tensor comparisons as well. Thus any comparison obtained from the specified units, counits, composition and tensor maps commutes with scalar extension: apply these equalities to each constituent map and then compose them. This verifies the compatibility input without assuming that the comparison itself is an isomorphism.
