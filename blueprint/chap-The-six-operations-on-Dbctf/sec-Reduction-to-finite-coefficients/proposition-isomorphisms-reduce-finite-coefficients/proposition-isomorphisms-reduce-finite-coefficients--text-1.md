*Shared context.* For every admissible $\Lambda$, let $F_\Lambda,G_\Lambda:D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ be exact functors, and let $\theta_\Lambda:F_\Lambda\to G_\Lambda$ be a natural transformation of exact functors. For clauses 2–4 assume the scalar-extension identifications for $F$ and $G$ in the reference, carrying $\theta_\Lambda(K)\otimes\Lambda'$ to $\theta_{\Lambda'}(K\otimes\Lambda')$, and assume $\theta_A(K)$ is an isomorphism for every finite admissible $A$ and every $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$. The finite case is this hypothesis; clauses 2–4 are the three successive conclusions.
