The mainline Lean proof bypasses this general assertion, proving compatibility for the particular maps to which it applies reduction.

The scalar-compatibility hypothesis of [proposition-isomorphisms-reduce-finite-coefficients] holds whenever $F,G,\theta$ are built, covariantly in $K$, from the six operations, their units and counits, and base-change maps.
