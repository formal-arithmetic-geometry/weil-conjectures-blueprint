Many later theorems say that a canonical map is an isomorphism. We prove
them with finite coefficients and extend them to all admissible rings in
three steps. For $\mathcal O_E$ we reduce modulo $\pi_E$ and use derived
Nakayama. For $E$ we invert $\ell$ on integral models. For $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we
descend to a finite extension. We state the argument once.

The integral step needs derived completeness, since $E$ itself is a
nonzero $\mathcal O_E$-module with $E/\pi_E E=0$. The proposition only
asks that $F$, $G$ and $\theta$ commute with change of coefficients. By
Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients], this
holds for everything built from the six operations.
