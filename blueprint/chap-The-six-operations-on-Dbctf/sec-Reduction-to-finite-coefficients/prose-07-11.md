After a rewriting, the proposition also applies to some contravariant
constructions. For instance, the double-dual map $K\to\mathbb D\mathbb DK$
is a natural transformation of covariant exact functors. What does not
reduce in this way is the construction of an adjoint or of a map. For this
reason the cycle class of the diagonal and the adjunction
$Rf_!\dashv f^*(d)[2d]$ for smooth $f$ must be built as single morphisms
with $\mathcal O_E$-coefficients
(Sections [sec-ell-adic-coefficients-2] and [sec-ell-adic-coefficients-4]). Likewise, a
trace with values in $\Lambda$ is not a map of complexes.
