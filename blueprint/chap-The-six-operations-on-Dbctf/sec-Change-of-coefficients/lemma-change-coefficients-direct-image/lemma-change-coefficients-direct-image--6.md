If $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ is a finite composite of the preceding kinds of maps, then $(Rf_{*,\Lambda}K)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow Rf_{*,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$ is an isomorphism in $D(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
