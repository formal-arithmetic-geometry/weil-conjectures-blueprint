Let $E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ be a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$, let $f:X\to Y$ be a quasi-compact and quasi-separated morphism of schemes, and let $K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$ be bounded below. Then

$$
(Rf_*K)_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}\longrightarrow Rf_*(K_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell})
$$

is an isomorphism. The coefficient sheaf $\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}$ is the filtered colimit of $F_X$ over finite subextensions $F\supset E$ of $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
