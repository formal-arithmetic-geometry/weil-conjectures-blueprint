The direct-image comparison is the adjoint transpose of the scalar extension of the counit. Associativity of scalar extension and the triangle identities identify the comparison for a composite coefficient map with the composite of the successive comparisons. Each elementary comparison is an isomorphism by clauses [lemma-change-coefficients-direct-image--1]–[lemma-change-coefficients-direct-image--5]; their composite is therefore an isomorphism.
