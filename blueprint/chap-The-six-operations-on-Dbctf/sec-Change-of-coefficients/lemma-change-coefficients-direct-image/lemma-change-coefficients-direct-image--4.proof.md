Write $K_E$ as the filtered colimit of $K\xrightarrow{\ell}K\xrightarrow{\ell}\cdots$. All its terms share the lower bound of $K$. Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology] therefore identifies $Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E}$ of this colimit with the colimit of $Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E}K$. The latter is $(Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E}K)_E$. The identifications come from the same counit that defines the comparison, so the resulting isomorphism is the specified coefficient-comparison map.
