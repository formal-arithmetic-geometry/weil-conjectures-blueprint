Let $E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ be a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$, let $f:X\to Y$ be a quasi-compact and quasi-separated morphism of schemes, and let $K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$ be bounded below. Then
$$
(Rf_*K)_E\longrightarrow Rf_*(K_E)
$$
is an isomorphism in $D(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$.
