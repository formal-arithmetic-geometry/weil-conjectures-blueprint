If $(\Lambda,\Lambda')=(\mathcal O_E,\mathcal O_F)$ or $(E,F)$ for a finite extension $F/E$, then $(Rf_{*,\Lambda}K)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow Rf_{*,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$ is an isomorphism in $D(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
