Let $E\subset F\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ be finite extensions of $\mathbf Q_\ell$. For every morphism $f:X\to Y$ of schemes, the comparison
$$
(Rf_{*,E}K)_F\longrightarrow Rf_{*,F}(K_F)
$$
is an isomorphism, naturally in every $K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$. No boundedness or constructibility hypothesis is required.

The integral clause $\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_F$ of the human statement is not asserted by this specialized Lean declaration.
