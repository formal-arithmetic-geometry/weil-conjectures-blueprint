Choose a bounded below injective representative $I$ of $K$. At every finite stage $F\supset E$, the basis argument in [lemma-change-coefficients-direct-image--3] shows that the underived comparison is an isomorphism and that the underlying sheaf of $F_X\otimes_{E_X}I^j$ is injective, hence $f_*$-acyclic.

Scalar extension to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ is the filtered colimit of these finite scalar extensions. The comparisons are compatible with the transition maps. Continuity [corollary-continuity-cohomology] therefore makes the underived comparison an isomorphism on each $I^j$ and makes $\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}\otimes_{E_X}I^j$ $f_*$-acyclic. Exactness of scalar extension and the common lower bound let us compute both derived pushforwards on these complexes. The factorization of the derived comparison through the underived one and the derived-functor units and counits gives the result.
