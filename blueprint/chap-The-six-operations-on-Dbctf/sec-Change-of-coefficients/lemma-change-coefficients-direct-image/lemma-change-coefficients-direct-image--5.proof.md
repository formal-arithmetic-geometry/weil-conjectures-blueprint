Express $K_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$ as the filtered colimit of $K_F$ over finite extensions $F/E$ inside $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. Clause [lemma-change-coefficients-direct-image--3] proves the comparison at each finite extension. Those systems have a common lower bound, and Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology] permits the colimit to pass through direct image. Taking the colimit of the finite-extension comparisons yields the asserted comparison isomorphism.
