If $\Lambda=A$, $\Lambda'=B$ for a homomorphism of finite admissible rings, then $(Rf_{*,\Lambda}K)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow Rf_{*,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$ is an isomorphism in $D(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
