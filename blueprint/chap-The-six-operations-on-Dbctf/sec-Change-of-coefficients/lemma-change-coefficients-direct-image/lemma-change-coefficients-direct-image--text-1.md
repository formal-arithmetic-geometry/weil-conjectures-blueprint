*Context for the details.* The finite-ring clause retains the context of the human statement: $f:X\to Y$ is a morphism of schemes of finite type over $k$, and $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$. The remaining clauses state their own, more general, hypotheses on $f$ and $K$. Throughout, $\ell$ is prime, coefficient sheaves are pulled back from the corresponding condensed rings, and the comparison is the one of Definition [definition-change-of-coefficients-maps](4).
