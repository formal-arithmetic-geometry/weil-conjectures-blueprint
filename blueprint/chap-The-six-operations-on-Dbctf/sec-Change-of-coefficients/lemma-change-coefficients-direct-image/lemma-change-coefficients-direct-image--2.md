Let $E$ be a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ with a fixed embedding into $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, and put $\mathbf F_E=\mathcal O_E/\mathfrak m_E$. For every morphism $f:X\to Y$ of schemes and every bounded below $K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$, the comparison
$$
(Rf_*K)_{\mathbf F_E}\longrightarrow Rf_*(K_{\mathbf F_E})
$$
is an isomorphism. Here scalar extension is derived, and the comparison is the mate of the derived pullback comparison, as in Definition [definition-change-of-coefficients-maps](4).
