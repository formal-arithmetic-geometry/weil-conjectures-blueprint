If $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$ and $\Lambda'=A$ for a continuous map to a finite admissible ring (in particular $A=\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$, where $\varpi=\pi_E$), then $(Rf_{*,\Lambda}K)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow Rf_{*,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$ is an isomorphism in $D(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
