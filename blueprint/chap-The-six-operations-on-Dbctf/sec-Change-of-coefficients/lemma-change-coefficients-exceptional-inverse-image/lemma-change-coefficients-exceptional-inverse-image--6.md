Let $E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ be finite over $\mathbf Q_\ell$,
and take $\Lambda=E$ and $\Lambda'=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. For every
$K\in\Dbc(Y,E)$ the comparison

$$
\beta_{f,K}:(f^!_E K)_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}\longrightarrow
f^!_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}(K_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell})
$$

is an isomorphism in $\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$, natural in $K$.
It is the adjoint transpose of the inverse of the compactly supported
comparison for the chosen compactification of $f$.
