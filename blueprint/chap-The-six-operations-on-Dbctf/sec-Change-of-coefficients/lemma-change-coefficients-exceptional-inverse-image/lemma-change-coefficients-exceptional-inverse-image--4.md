Assume $\Lambda=E$ and $\Lambda'=F$ for a finite extension $F/E$. The comparison

$$
\beta_{f,K}:(f^!_\Lambda K)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow f^!_{\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})
$$

is an isomorphism in $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
