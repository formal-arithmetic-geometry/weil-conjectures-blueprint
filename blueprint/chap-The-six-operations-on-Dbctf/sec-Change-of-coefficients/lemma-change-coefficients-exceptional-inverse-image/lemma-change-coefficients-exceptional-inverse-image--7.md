This assertion for arbitrary composites of the listed coefficient maps is bypassed by the Lean proof; its applications compose the particular comparisons established in [lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--5] and [lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--6].

Assume $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ is a finite composite of the maps in the original list of coefficient changes. For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$, the comparison

$$
\beta_{f,K}:(f^!_\Lambda K)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow f^!_{\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})
$$

is an isomorphism in $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
