This integral finite-extension comparison is bypassed by the Lean proof; finite extensions of coefficient fields are treated directly in [lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--4].

Assume $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$ and $\Lambda'=\mathcal O_F$ for a finite extension $F/E$. For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$, the comparison

$$
\beta_{f,K}:(f^!_\Lambda K)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow f^!_{\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})
$$

is an isomorphism in $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
