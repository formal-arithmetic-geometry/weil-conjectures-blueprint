For coefficient maps $\Lambda\to\Lambda'\to\Lambda''$, take the adjoint transpose of each exceptional comparison. Both the comparison for the composite and the composite of comparisons transpose to the scalar extension of the same counit, using the transitivity of the $Rf_!$ comparison. The adjunction bijection makes the two maps equal. Each elementary map is an isomorphism by clauses [lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--1]–[lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--6], so any finite composite is an isomorphism.
