*Shared context.* For the Lean comparisons in
[lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--2],
[lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--4],
[lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--5] and
[lemma-change-coefficients-exceptional-inverse-image--6], let $\ell$ be a
prime, let $k$ be a field of prime characteristic $p\ne\ell$, and let
$f:X\to Y$ be a morphism of separated schemes of finite type over $k$.
Write $\Dbc$ for bounded constructible complexes, as in the Lean
coefficient categories; the declarations impose no separate finite
Tor-dimension hypothesis. The coefficient fields are finite subextensions
of $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell/\mathbf Q_\ell$. Each comparison is obtained
by adjunction from a direct-image or compactly supported comparison.
The reduction case is read on the étale site, and the finite extension of coefficient fields
case assumes that $f$ is proper. The other three details retain
the original coefficient cases and are marked as bypassed by Lean.
