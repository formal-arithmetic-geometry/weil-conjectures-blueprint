*Shared context.* Let $f:X\to Y$ be separated and of finite type, and let $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$. In each case use the specified map $\beta_{f,K}$ of (7.1). The geometric factorization in the original proof is common to all cases; the coefficient-dependent construction of the diagonal class and trace is recorded separately.
