*Shared context.* Let $X=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$, with coefficient map $\mathbf Z/\ell^2\to\mathbf F_\ell$. Let $K$ be the lisse sheaf $\mathbf Z/\ell^2$ on which Frobenius, the topological generator of $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbf F}_q/\mathbf F_q)\simeq\widehat{\mathbf Z}$, acts as multiplication by $1+\ell$.
