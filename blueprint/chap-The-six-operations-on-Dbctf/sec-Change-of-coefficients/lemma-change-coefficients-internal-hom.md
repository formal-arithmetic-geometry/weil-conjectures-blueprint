For each map $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ listed above and all
$K,L\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$, the map
$\cHom(K,L)_{\Lambda'}\to\cHom(K_{\Lambda'},L_{\Lambda'})$ of
Definition [definition-change-of-coefficients-maps](6) is an
isomorphism.
