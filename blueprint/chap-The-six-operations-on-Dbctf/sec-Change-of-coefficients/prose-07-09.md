In practice the proposition is used as follows. To prove a statement about
finitely many objects and morphisms of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$, we may assume that
they are extensions of scalars of objects and morphisms of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,E)$ for
a finite extension $E$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$. Moreover, two morphisms over $E$ that
agree over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ already agree over a finite extension of $E$.
