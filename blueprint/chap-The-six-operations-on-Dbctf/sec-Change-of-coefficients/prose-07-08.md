Without the condition on Tor amplitude, change of coefficients would not
preserve the categories. For instance, let $X$ be a geometric point. The
sheaf $\mathbf F_\ell$ belongs to $\Dbc(X,\mathbf Z/\ell^2)$ but not to
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathbf Z/\ell^2)$. Its extension of scalars
$\mathbf F_\ell\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathbf Z/\ell^2}\mathbf F_\ell$ is
unbounded.

For coefficients in $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$,
Lemma [lemma-descent-finite-coefficient-field] expresses $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$
in terms of the categories $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,E)$ for finite $E$. As a statement about
