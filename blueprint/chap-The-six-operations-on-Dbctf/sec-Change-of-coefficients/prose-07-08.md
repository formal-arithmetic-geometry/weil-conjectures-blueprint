Without the condition on [Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves), change of coefficients would not
preserve the categories. For instance, let $X$ be a geometric point. The
sheaf $\mathbf F_\ell$ belongs to [$\Dbc(X,\mathbf Z/\ell^2)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) but not to
[$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathbf Z/\ell^2)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves). Its extension of scalars
$\mathbf F_\ell\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathbf Z/\ell^2}\mathbf F_\ell$ is
unbounded.

For coefficients in $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$,
Lemma [lemma-descent-finite-coefficient-field] expresses [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves)
in terms of the categories [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,E)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) for finite $E$. As a statement about
ordinary categories, it reads as follows.
