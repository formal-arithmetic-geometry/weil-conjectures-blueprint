Extension of scalars identifies

$$
D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)\simeq\mathop{\text{2-colim}}_{E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E).
$$

The notation on the right has exactly the ordinary-category meaning specified by the reference.
