Apply Lemma [lemma-descent-finite-coefficient-field](1) with $r=0$, using fullness of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$. Continuity of Ext out of a constructible complex identifies the filtered colimit of Hom groups with the Hom group over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. Surjectivity gives descent of each morphism, and injectivity gives equality at some later finite stage whenever two morphisms become equal over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
