Suppose that the underlying topological space of $X$ is noetherian, and
use the categories and scalar extensions of
[proposition-dctf-x-qbar-2-colimit--1]. Every object of
$\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ is isomorphic to
$K_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$ for some finite subextension
$E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ and some
$K\in\Dbc(X,E)$.
