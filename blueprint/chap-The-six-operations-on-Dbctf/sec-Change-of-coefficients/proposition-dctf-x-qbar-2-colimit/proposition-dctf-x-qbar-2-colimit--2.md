Suppose that the underlying topological space of $X$ is noetherian, and
use the scalar extensions of [proposition-dctf-x-qbar-2-colimit--1].
Every object of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ is
isomorphic to $K_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$ for some finite
subextension $E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ and
some $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,E)$.
