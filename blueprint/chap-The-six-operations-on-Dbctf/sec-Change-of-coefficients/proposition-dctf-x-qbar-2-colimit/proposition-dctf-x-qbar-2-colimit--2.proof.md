Use Lemma [lemma-descent-finite-coefficient-field](2). Its construction descends the finitely many lisse pieces and their transition matrices to a common finite field. It then descends the finitely many connecting morphisms in a Postnikov tower, enlarging that field finitely many times. The resulting complex extends to the prescribed object, with its constructibility and Tor bound unchanged.
