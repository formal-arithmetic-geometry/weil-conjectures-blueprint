For $K,L\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$, extension of scalars induces the bijection

$$
\varinjlim_{F\supset E}\Hom_{D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},F)}(K_F,L_F)
\xrightarrow{\sim}\Hom_{D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)}(K_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell},L_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}).
$$
