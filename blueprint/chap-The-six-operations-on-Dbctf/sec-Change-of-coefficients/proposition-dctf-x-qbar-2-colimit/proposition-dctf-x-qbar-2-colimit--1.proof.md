Extension of scalars preserves bounded constructibility, by the bounded
constructible assertion of [lemma-extension-scalars-preserves-dctf].
Thus the derived scalar-extension functors restrict to the full
subcategories in the statement. Transitivity of extension of scalars
gives $\Phi_F\circ T_{E,F}\simeq\Phi_E$ on the ambient derived categories.
Since the inclusions of the bounded constructible subcategories are
fully faithful, these isomorphisms restrict to them. These are the data
of the cocone.
