Consider the ordinary category whose objects are pairs $(E,K)$ and whose Hom sets are filtered colimits of the Hom sets after finite coefficient extension. Lemma [lemma-descent-finite-coefficient-field](1), in degree zero, identifies those colimits bijectively with the Hom sets after extension to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. Its part (2) makes every target object isomorphic to one represented by a pair $(E,K)$. Thus the comparison is fully faithful and essentially surjective, and is an equivalence of ordinary categories.
