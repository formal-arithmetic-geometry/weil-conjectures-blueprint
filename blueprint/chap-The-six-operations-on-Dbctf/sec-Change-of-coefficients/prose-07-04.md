Before stating the result we fix the comparison maps. Items (1)–(3)
below, for $f^*$, $j_!$ and $\otimes^{\mathbf L}$, are identifications of
two composites of left adjoints. Items (4) and (6), for $Rf_*$ and
$\cHom$, are adjoint transposes of the inverse of (1), respectively (3),
composed with the extension of scalars of the counit, and item (5)
combines (4) for $p$ with (2).
