For $\mathcal O_E\to E$, choose a bounded below injective resolution. The underived comparison is invertible, and scalar extension of each injective is acyclic for direct image; the comparison on the resolution therefore computes the derived comparison. For $E\to F$, a finite basis identifies both functors with finite direct sums of the original direct image. For $E\to\Qbarl$, use the filtered union of finite extensions, the finite-extension comparison, and acyclicity after scalar extension.

For the residue field, choose an acyclic resolution on which a uniformizer acts injectively, and use its short exact reduction sequences to compare the two derived functors. For reduction modulo $\ell^n$, use the natural reduction triangles: pullback is compatible with their unit and connecting morphism. Taking mates gives the direct-image comparison, and the reduction-triangle criterion proves it invertible; restrict to bounded constructible complexes.
