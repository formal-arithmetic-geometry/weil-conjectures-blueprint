Under the mate-square hypotheses of [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--20], the counits of $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ satisfy

$$
\varepsilon'_{T_YN}\circ Rf'_!(c^!(N))\circ c_!(f^!N)=T_Y(\varepsilon_N).
$$

By [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--20] these hypotheses hold for the compactly supported comparison of Definition [definition-change-of-coefficients-maps](5) and the exceptional comparison $\beta$ on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$, so scalar extension of the counit of $Rf_{!,\Lambda}\dashv f^!_\Lambda$ is the counit of the same adjunction over $\Lambda'$.
