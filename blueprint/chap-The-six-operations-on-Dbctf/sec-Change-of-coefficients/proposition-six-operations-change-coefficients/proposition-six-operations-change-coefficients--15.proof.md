Take adjoint transposes under the pullback–direct-image adjunction. The comparison for $gf$ is defined using its counit, which is the composite of the counits for $f$ and $g$. Transposing the route through the two separate comparisons gives that same composite. Naturality and the triangle identities identify the transposes, so the two routes agree.
