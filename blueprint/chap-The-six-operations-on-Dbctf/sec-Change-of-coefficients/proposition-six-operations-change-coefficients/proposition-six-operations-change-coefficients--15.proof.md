Compose the mate squares for $f$ and $g$. The pullback composition isomorphisms are compatible with coefficient change by hypothesis. Passing to their conjugates under the composite adjunctions gives compatibility of the direct-image composition isomorphisms. This is `NatTrans.SquareCompatible.conjugateEquiv` applied to the composed mate squares.
