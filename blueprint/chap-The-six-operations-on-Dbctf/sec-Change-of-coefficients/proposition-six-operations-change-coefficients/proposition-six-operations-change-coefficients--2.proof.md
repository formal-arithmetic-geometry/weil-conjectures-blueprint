Write pullback on modules as pullback of the underlying sheaf followed by extension of scalars along the coefficient isomorphism. Use transitivity of derived extension of scalars, and derive the underived pullback comparison. Pullback is exact and preserves K-flat complexes, so the two composite derived functors are identified with the two sides of the displayed isomorphism.
