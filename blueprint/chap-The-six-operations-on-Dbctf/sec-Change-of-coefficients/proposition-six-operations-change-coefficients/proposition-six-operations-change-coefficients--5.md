With the scheme and coefficient hypotheses of [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--4], for $\mathcal O_E\to E$ and $E\to\Qbarl$ the mate of the inverse compact-support comparison is an isomorphism on bounded constructible complexes:
$$
(f^!_{\Lambda}K)_{\Lambda'}\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_{\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'}).
$$
For $\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$, the inverse compact-support comparison for the chosen compactification has an invertible mate
$$
\mathrm{red}_X(f^!_{\mathcal O_E}K)\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_{\mathcal O_E/\ell^n}(\mathrm{red}_YK)
$$
in the étale derived category.
