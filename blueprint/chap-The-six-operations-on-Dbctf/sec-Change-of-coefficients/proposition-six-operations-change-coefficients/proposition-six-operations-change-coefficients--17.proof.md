The mate of a pasted square is the paste of the mates, after transport through the conjugate composition isomorphisms. Apply `CategoryTheory.isIso_mateEquiv_vComp_whiskerLeft_whiskerRight`: the two constituent mates and all transport isomorphisms are invertible, so the resulting mate is invertible.
