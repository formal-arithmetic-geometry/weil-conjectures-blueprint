Choose a compactification. The $Rf_!$ comparison is the composite of the proper direct-image comparison and the extension-by-zero comparison. Both are transitive: the former by adjunction and the latter because the right adjoints are restrictions of scalars followed by restriction to the open. Their composite is therefore transitive. Independence of compactification gives the statement for $f$. This proves the asserted equality for the comparison of clause [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--4].
