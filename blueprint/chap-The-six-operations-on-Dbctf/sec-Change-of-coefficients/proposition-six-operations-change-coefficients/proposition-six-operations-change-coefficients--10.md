This clause is bypassed by the Lean route: the mainline constructs compact-support comparisons along compactifications and uses direct-image coefficient cocycles, without a general compact-support coefficient-transitivity theorem.

For $\Lambda\to\Lambda'\to\Lambda''$, the compactly supported direct image comparison of clause [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--4] for $\Lambda\to\Lambda''$ equals the composite of the comparisons for the two maps, under associativity of scalar extension.
