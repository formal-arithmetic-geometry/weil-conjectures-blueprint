Let $E\subset F\subset\Qbarl$ be finite over $\Qell$, and let $X$ be any scheme. The transitivity isomorphism
$$
T_{F,\Qbarl}\,T_{E,F}\xrightarrow{\sim}T_{E,\Qbarl}
$$
on the unbounded derived categories of condensed coefficient modules is monoidal. In particular it identifies the tensor comparison for $E\to\Qbarl$ with the composite tensor comparison for $E\to F\to\Qbarl$, and is compatible with the tensor unit.
