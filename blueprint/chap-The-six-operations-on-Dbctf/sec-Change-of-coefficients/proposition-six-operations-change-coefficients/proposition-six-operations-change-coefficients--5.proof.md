For $\mathcal O_E\to E$, scalar extension on bounded constructible complexes is a rationalization on both schemes. The rationalization theorem for mates therefore makes the transpose of the compact-support isomorphism invertible.

For $E\to\Qbarl$, reduce along a compactification to the proper part and the open part. The open part follows from full faithfulness of extension by zero. For the proper part, use the filtered $2$-colimit of finite coefficient fields, the cocycle for direct-image comparisons from [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--9], and invertibility of the mates between finite subextensions.

For reduction modulo $\ell^n$, the transposes for the open and proper parts are invertible. The composition result [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--17] makes the mate for the chosen compactification invertible.
