The right-adjoint comparison is defined as the transpose of the scalar extension of the counit, after applying the left-adjoint comparison. For the two-step extension, associativity identifies the left-adjoint comparison with the one-step comparison. The two candidate right-adjoint maps thus have the same transpose, by the counit and triangle identities, and are equal. This proves the asserted equality for the comparison of clause [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--5].
