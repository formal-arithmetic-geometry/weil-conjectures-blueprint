Let $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ be any morphism of commutative condensed rings and $X$ any scheme. For all $K,L\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, the tensor comparison is a natural isomorphism

$$
(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Lambda_X}L)_{\Lambda'}\xrightarrow{\sim}K_{\Lambda'}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Lambda'_X}L_{\Lambda'}.
$$
