Restriction of scalars commutes with direct image, and its comparisons for $a,b,c$ compose: on underlying sheaves the maps are identities. Right derivation preserves this equality, giving `derivedComapLowerStarIso_hom_app_comp`. The transitivity isomorphisms for extension of scalars are conjugates of those for restriction of scalars. Apply `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.pushHomOfComapIso_app_eq_comp` to obtain the displayed formula.
