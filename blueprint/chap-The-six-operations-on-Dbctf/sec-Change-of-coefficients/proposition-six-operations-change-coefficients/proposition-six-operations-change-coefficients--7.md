The internal-Hom comparison
$$
\cHom_{\Lambda_X}(K,L)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow\cHom_{\Lambda'_X}(K_{\Lambda'},L_{\Lambda'})
$$
is an isomorphism in the following settings.

For $\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$, it is invertible for every scheme $X$, every $n\geq 0$, every field $E$ embedded in $\Qbarl$, and all unbounded complexes $K,L$ of $\mathcal O_{E,X}$-modules.

For $E\to F$ with $E\subset F\subset\Qbarl$ finite over $\Qell$, it is invertible on all unbounded complexes on any scheme.

For $\mathcal O_E\to E$, $E\to\Qbarl$, and the composite $\mathcal O_E\to E\to\Qbarl$, it is invertible on bounded constructible complexes when $E\subset\Qbarl$ is finite over $\Qell$ and $X$ is separated of finite type over a field of characteristic $p\ne\ell$.
