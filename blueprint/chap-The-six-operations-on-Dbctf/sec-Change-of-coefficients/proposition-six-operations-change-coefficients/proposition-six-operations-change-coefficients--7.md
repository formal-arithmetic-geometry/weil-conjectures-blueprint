For $K,L\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, the comparison

$$
\cHom_{\Lambda_X}(K,L)_{\Lambda'}\xrightarrow{\sim}\cHom_{\Lambda'_X}(K_{\Lambda'},L_{\Lambda'})
$$

is an isomorphism in $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
