For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, the comparison

$$
(Rf_{*,\Lambda}K)_{\Lambda'}\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf_{*,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})
$$

is an isomorphism in $D(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda')$.
