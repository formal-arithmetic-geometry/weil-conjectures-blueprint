Let $E\subset F\subset\Qbarl$ be finite extensions of $\Qell$. The direct-image comparison
$$
(Rf_{*,\Lambda}K)_{\Lambda'}\longrightarrow Rf_{*,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})
$$
is an isomorphism in the following cases:

- $\mathcal O_E\to E$, for a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism $f$ of schemes and a bounded below complex $K$;
- $E\to F$, for any morphism of schemes and any unbounded complex $K$;
- $E\to\Qbarl$, for a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism and a bounded below complex;
- $\mathcal O_E\to\mathbb F_E$, for any morphism of schemes and a bounded below complex;
- $\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$, for $n\geq 0$, a morphism of separated schemes of finite type over a field of characteristic $p\ne\ell$, and a bounded constructible complex.
