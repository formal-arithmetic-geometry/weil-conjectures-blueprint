Let $X$ be a scheme and $R\to S$ a morphism of commutative ring objects in abelian sheaves on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$. If extension of scalars on modules is exact, its left derived functor sends $\Dbc(X,R)$ into $\Dbc(X,S)$.

Let $E\subset\Qbarl$ be finite over $\Qell$. For every $n\geq 0$, derived extension of scalars along $\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$ preserves bounded constructibility when the underlying topological space of $X$ is Noetherian. Derived extension of scalars along $\mathcal O_E\to\mathbb F_E$, where $\mathbb F_E$ is the residue field, has the same property when $X$ is Noetherian.
