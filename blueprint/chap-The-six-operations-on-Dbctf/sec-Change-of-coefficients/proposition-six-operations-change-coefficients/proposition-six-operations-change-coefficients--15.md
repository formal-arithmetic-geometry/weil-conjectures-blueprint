For composable $f,g$ with the standing applicability conditions, the coefficient comparison commutes with the composition isomorphism $R(gf)_*\simeq Rg_*Rf_*$: after scalar extension, the route through the comparison for $gf$ equals the route through the comparisons for $f$ and $g$.
