Let $\Lambda,\Lambda'$ be commutative condensed rings and let $\ell$ be prime. On separated schemes of finite type over a field of characteristic $p\ne\ell$, suppose given coefficient-change data consisting of functors $T_X:\Dbc(X,\Lambda)\to\Dbc(X,\Lambda')$, pullback comparison isomorphisms compatible with composition, open extension-by-zero comparison isomorphisms mated with pullback, and proper direct-image comparison isomorphisms mated with pullback. The direct-image adjunctions and their composition isomorphisms are those of the given pushforward data.

For composable proper morphisms $f,g$, the coefficient comparisons commute with $Rg_*Rf_*\simeq R(gf)_*$.
