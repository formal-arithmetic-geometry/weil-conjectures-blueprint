*Shared context.* In the details below, $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ denotes the bounded constructible category of modules over the condensed coefficient ring on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ used in Lean. This notation makes no assertion of finite Tor amplitude. Write $T_a^XK=K_{\Lambda'}$ for derived extension of scalars along $a:\Lambda\to\Lambda'$.

The prime $\ell$ is fixed in the arithmetic coefficient cases, $E\subset\Qbarl$ is finite over $\Qell$ unless specified otherwise, and $\mathbb F_E$ denotes its residue field. Statements involving separated schemes of finite type over $k$ assume that $k$ has positive characteristic different from $\ell$. General statements explicitly allowing arbitrary schemes or condensed coefficient rings have no such restriction. Reduction followed by the étale reading is denoted by $\mathrm{red}_X$.
