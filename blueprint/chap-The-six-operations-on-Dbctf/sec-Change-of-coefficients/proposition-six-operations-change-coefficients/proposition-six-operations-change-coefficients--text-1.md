*Shared context.* Let $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ be a coefficient map listed in this subsection. All schemes are of finite type over $k$. The morphism $f:X\to Y$ is separated for $Rf_!$ and $f^!$. Comparisons are those of Definition [definition-change-of-coefficients-maps] and (7.1); no new comparison is chosen. All arguments of these restricted operations lie in the indicated $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ categories. In the tensor–Hom adjunction clauses, fix $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$.
