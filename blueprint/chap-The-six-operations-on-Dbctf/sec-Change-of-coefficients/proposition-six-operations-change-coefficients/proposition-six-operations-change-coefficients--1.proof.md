For exact extension of scalars, finite presentations remain finite presentations. Pullback commutes with extension of scalars, so the same stratification witnesses constructibility. Exactness identifies the new cohomology objects with the scalar extensions of the old ones and preserves their bounds.

For reduction modulo $\ell^n$, use the distinguished triangle
$$
K\xrightarrow{\ell^n}K\longrightarrow K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E}\mathcal O_E/\ell^n\longrightarrow K[1]
$$
after restriction of scalars. Bounded constructible complexes are closed under this triangle, and constructibility descends along the quotient map. For the residue field, use the corresponding triangle for a uniformizer and descend constructibility along $\mathcal O_E\to\mathbb F_E$.
