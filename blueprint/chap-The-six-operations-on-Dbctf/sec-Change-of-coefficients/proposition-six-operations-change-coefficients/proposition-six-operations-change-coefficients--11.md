This clause is bypassed by the Lean route: the mainline proves invertibility of the exceptional comparisons by rationalization, finite-field descent and reduction, without a general coefficient-transitivity theorem for them.

For $\Lambda\to\Lambda'\to\Lambda''$, the exceptional inverse image comparison of clause [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--5] for $\Lambda\to\Lambda''$ equals the composite of the comparisons for the two maps, under associativity of scalar extension.
