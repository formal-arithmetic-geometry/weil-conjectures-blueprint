The geometric composition isomorphism for $Rf_!$ is constructed from $R(qr)_*\simeq Rq_*Rr_*$ and the interchange of an open extension by zero and a proper direct image. Compatibility for the proper-image part is clause [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--15]. The interchange is a map out of an extension by zero, hence is determined by restriction to the open subscheme; there both comparisons are the same pullback identification. Thus every map used to construct composition commutes with scalar extension, proving the claim.
