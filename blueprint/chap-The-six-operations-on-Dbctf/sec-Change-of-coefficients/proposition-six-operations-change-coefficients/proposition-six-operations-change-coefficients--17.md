In the setting of [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--16], assume that the mates of $\gamma_f^{-1}$ and $\gamma_g^{-1}$ under the adjunctions $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ and $Rg_!\dashv g^!$ are invertible. Then the mate of $\gamma_h^{-1}$ under $Rh_!\dashv h^!$ is invertible, where $\gamma_h$ is the composite comparison constructed there.
