Apply Lemma [lemma-change-coefficients-compact-support] to the objects and morphism in the statement. Its map is the same comparison from Definition [definition-change-of-coefficients-maps], and all its hypotheses are part of the shared context. It is therefore an isomorphism in the indicated target derived category. Both sides belong to its constructible subcategory by Proposition [proposition-six-operations-dctf] and Lemma [lemma-extension-scalars-preserves-dctf], so it is also the required isomorphism there.
