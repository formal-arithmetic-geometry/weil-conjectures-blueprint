For each of the two flat coefficient changes, choose a compactification $f=pj$. Compose the comparison for the proper direct image with the scalar-extension isomorphism for extension by zero along $j$. For $\mathcal O_E\to E$, the bounded constructible direct-image comparison is invertible because scalar extension is a rationalization. For $E\to\Qbarl$, use the bounded below direct-image comparison of [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--3].

For reduction, the open-immersion comparison is the transpose of the pullback comparison; its invertibility is checked on the open and on the closed complement. The proper comparison comes from the reduction comparison for direct image and the étale reading. Compose these two isomorphisms along $c$, as in [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients--16].
