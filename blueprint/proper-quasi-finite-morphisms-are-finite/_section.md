A decomposition of Stacks Project, Cohomology of Schemes, Section 30.21
([Tag 02OF](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02OF)), Lemmas 30.21.1
([Tag 02OG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02OG)) and 30.21.2 ([Tag
02OH](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02OH)), in the formulation the
curve-calculation chapter uses: *proper and quasi-finite implies finite*.
That chapter currently quotes the Stacks citation as an input; this chapter
supplies its proof [cor-pqf-proper-quasifinite-finite].

Conventions of the source: labels `pqf-*`; every Stacks Project citation
carries a hyperlink; the quasi-coherent tools (coherent modules,
$\mathcal O_X$-module cohomology, Serre's criterion, the theorem on formal
functions) are recorded as classical inputs with citations, exactly as in
the reference decomposition of Section 30.21, and the logical chains of the
two theorems are decomposed in full.

Six statements remain without a proof and are recorded classical inputs in
the source's ledger, OBL-PQF-01..06: upper semicontinuity of the fibre
dimension [pqf-lem-semicontinuity-fibre-dimension], integral = affine +
universally closed [pqf-lem-integral-univclosed] (converse direction only),
affineness local on the target [pqf-lem-affine-local-target], Serre's
criterion [pqf-lem-serre-criterion], affine vanishing
[pqf-lem-affine-vanishing], and openness of the quasi-finite locus
[pqf-lem-qf-locus-open]. Their decomposition needs reference-grade
sub-lemmas and is planned as separate work.

The sections are ordered as the proof assembles its statements: a section
stands after the material it uses, so that no section refers to material
introduced later in the chapter. Measured for this chapter by the source:
six forward references before this ordering, two after. Sections 4 and 5
were exchanged to achieve it.
