What properness gives [pqf-lem-proper-basic], [pqf-lem-proper-closed-map],
[pqf-lem-semicontinuity-fibre-dimension], and the properness of a
scheme-theoretic image [pqf-lem-image-proper]. Quasi-finiteness is finite
type plus finite fibres [pqf-lem-qf-finitefibres], and a finite morphism is
quasi-finite [pqf-lem-finite-qf]. On the algebra side, lying over
[pqf-lem-lying-over], incomparability [pqf-lem-incomparability] and going
up [pqf-lem-going-up] for an integral extension give one direction of
"integral = affine + universally closed"
[pqf-lem-integral-univclosed]; finite implies integral
[pqf-lem-finite-integral-lft] and integral plus locally of finite type
implies finite [pqf-lem-integral-lft-finite].
