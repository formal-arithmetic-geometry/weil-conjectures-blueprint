The five elementary inputs used by the immersion-descent interface
[thm-pqf-immersion-descent]. They are placed after the interface they
serve so that no earlier statement and no earlier number of the chapter
moves. The first two are the scheme-theoretic forms of the two limit
lemmas that the proof of Zariski's main theorem uses at this point, Stacks
Project, Limits of Schemes, Lemma 32.4.11 ([Tag
01Z4](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01Z4))
[pqf-lem-limit-qc-open-descends] and Lemma 32.4.16 ([Tag
081B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/081B))
[pqf-lem-limit-immersion-descends], with the limit description of the opens
of Lemma 32.2.2 ([Tag 01YX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01YX)) as
clause (3) of the first; the third is the basic fact about quasi-separated
schemes that both use [pqf-lem-qs-intersection-qc]; and the last two are
the local Noetherian inputs of the finitely presented clause of the
interface [pqf-lem-affine-open-locnoeth], [pqf-lem-finite-fp-locnoeth].
