Let $X$ be a scheme. Assume that $X$ is quasi-compact, that $X$ is
quasi-separated, and that $\mathrm H^1(X,\mathcal I)=0$ for every
quasi-coherent sheaf of ideals $\mathcal I\subset\mathcal O_X$ of finite
type. Then $X$ is affine.

*Stacks Project, Cohomology of Schemes, Lemma 30.3.2, [Tag
01XG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XG).*

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks
Project, Cohomology of Schemes, Lemma 30.3.2 ([Tag
01XG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XG)): Serre's criterion, that
a quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme is affine as soon as the first
cohomology of every quasi-coherent sheaf of finite type ideals vanishes. No
separate proof is given.
