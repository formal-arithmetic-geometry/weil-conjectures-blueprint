The cohomological toolkit. The Leray spectral sequence
[pqf-lem-leray] with its low-degree exact sequence
[pqf-lem-leray-lowdegree]; affineness is local on the target
[pqf-lem-affine-local-target]; finite type over a locally Noetherian base
stays locally Noetherian [pqf-lem-locnoeth-lft], where coherent means
quasi-coherent of finite type [pqf-lem-coherent-ft-locnoeth]. The key
input is the vanishing of the higher direct images over a finite fibre,
via the theorem on formal functions [pqf-lem-fff-vanishing]. The rest is
bookkeeping: Serre's criterion [pqf-lem-serre-criterion], localization of
an associated sheaf [pqf-lem-associated-sections-localize], quasi-coherence
of a pushforward [pqf-lem-pushforward-qcoh], vanishing on affines
[pqf-lem-affine-vanishing], and the stalk criterion
[pqf-lem-sheaf-zero-stalks].
