The two theorems of Stacks Section 30.21 and the factorization theorem
that uses them. Over a locally Noetherian base, finite is the same as
proper with finite fibres [thm-pqf-finite-iff-proper-finite-fibres] — the
proof runs Serre's criterion against the formal-functions vanishing —
whence the statement the curve-calculation chapter needs, that a proper
quasi-finite morphism is finite [cor-pqf-proper-quasifinite-finite], and
its spreading-out form [thm-pqf-finiteness-spreads], which uses the
elementary quasi-finiteness bookkeeping
[pqf-lem-locqf-fibre-discrete], [pqf-lem-qf-locus-open],
[pqf-lem-locqf-restrict], [pqf-lem-qf-locqf-qc]. Zariski's main theorem
[thm-pqf-zmt-factorization] factors a separated quasi-finite morphism of
finite type as a quasi-compact open immersion followed by a finite
morphism; its missing step is isolated as the interface
[thm-pqf-immersion-descent], which is proved here from the two limit
lemmas of the next section.
