Let $X$ be a scheme, $\Lambda$ a commutative condensed ring, and
$K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$. Let $a\leq b$ and $c\leq d$
be integers. Suppose that $H^q(K)=0$ for $q\notin[a,b]$ and that, for
every $q\in[a,b]$, the sheaf $H^q(K)$ placed in degree zero has Tor
amplitude in $[c,d]$. Then $K$ has Tor amplitude in

$$
[a+c,b+d].
$$

No constructibility or finiteness assumption on $X$ is required.
