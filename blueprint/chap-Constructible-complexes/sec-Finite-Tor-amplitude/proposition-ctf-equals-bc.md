For $\Lambda=\mathbf Z_\ell,\mathcal O_E,\mathbf Q_\ell,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, we have

$$
D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)=\Dbc(X,\Lambda).
$$
