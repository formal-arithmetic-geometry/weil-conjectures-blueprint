For a finite ring that is not regular the condition is needed, as the
$\mathbf Z/\ell^2$-module $\mathbf F_\ell$ of Section [sec-Constructibility]
shows. Moreover, finite Tor amplitude is a condition on the whole complex, not on its
cohomology sheaves. The category
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ is thick, but in general it is not stable under standard truncations.
For example, for $A=\mathbf Z/\ell^2$ the complex $[A\xrightarrow{\ell}A]$ is
perfect, whereas its nonzero cohomology modules are $\mathbf F_\ell$,
which are not perfect. For this reason, the compactness proofs for
finite $A$ work with bounded flat constructible models, and standard
truncations are taken in $\Dbc$. The models exist by the next lemma.
