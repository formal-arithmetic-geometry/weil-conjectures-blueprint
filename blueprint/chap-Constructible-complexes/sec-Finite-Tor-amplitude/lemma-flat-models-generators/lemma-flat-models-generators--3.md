The generation statement below is bypassed by the mainline Lean proof.

$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ is generated,
using
shifts, finite extensions and direct summands, by sheaves $a_!L$, where
$a:S\hookrightarrow X$ is locally closed and $L$ is locally of the form
$\underline M$ with $M$ a finite projective $A$-module.

Lean uses generic duality for finite coefficients and a separate
constructible dévissage for $\Qbarl$-coefficients. The latter uses extensions
by zero of dualizable complexes on affine strata; it does not supply the
finite-coefficient generation statement above.
