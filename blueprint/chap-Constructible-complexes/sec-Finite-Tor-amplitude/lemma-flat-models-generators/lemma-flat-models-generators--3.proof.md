Use clause [lemma-flat-models-generators--1] to choose a bounded flat constructible model and clause [lemma-flat-models-generators--2] to stratify its finitely many terms simultaneously. The localization exact sequences express each term as a finite extension of the sheaves $a_!L$ from its strata. The stupid filtration expresses the complex as finite extensions of shifts of those terms. These operations preserve finite Tor-amplitude; allowing direct summands gives the stated generation.
