By Corollary [corollary-finite-tor-amplitude-under-comparison], write $C=\nu_X^*C_{\acute{e}t}$ with $C_{\acute{e}t}\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$. The bounded flat constructible model exists there by [Stacks, [0F4M](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F4M)]. The comparison inverse image is exact and preserves flatness and constructibility, so pulling that model back gives the asserted model on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$.
