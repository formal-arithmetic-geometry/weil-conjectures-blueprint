Let $A$ be a finite commutative ring and $F$ a finite locally constant flat
$A$-sheaf on $X_{\acute{e}t}$. If $X$ is nonempty and
$F\simeq\underline M$, then $M$ is a finite projective $A$-module,
or equivalently a dualizable object of the category of $A$-modules.

For arbitrary $X$, the internal dual
$F^\vee=\mathcal{H}om_A(F,A_X)$ is a left dual of $F$, with the usual
evaluation map, and $F^\vee$ is again finite locally constant.
