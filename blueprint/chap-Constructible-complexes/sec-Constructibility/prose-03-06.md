Part (5) is special to the admissible coefficients. For a general condensed ring, kernels of maps between [constructible
sheaves](@definition-constructible-sheaves) can fail to be [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves), and then [$\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) is not
triangulated.
