In (4) each $L_0$ satisfies the condition of
Definition [definition-constructible-sheaves](1), so it lies in
$\Cons(S,\mathcal O_E)$. By (3) the objects $a_!L_0$ then lie in
$\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$. Their reductions modulo $\pi_E^n$ are
bounded constructible complexes with coefficients in
$\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$, which are pulled back from $X_{\acute{e}t}$
(Corollary [corollary-finite-tor-amplitude-under-comparison]).
