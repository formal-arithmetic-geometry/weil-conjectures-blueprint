On $\mathbf A^1$, part (4) is visible in the running example. There the constant
sheaf $E_{\mathbf A^1}$ is the cone of a map $i_*E[-1]\to j_!E_{\mathbf G_m}$,
and up to shift both terms are of the form $(a_!L_0)[1/\ell]$ with $L_0$ the constant
lattice $\mathcal O_E$ on the stratum.
