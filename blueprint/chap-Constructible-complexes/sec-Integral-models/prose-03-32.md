On $\mathbf A^1$, part (4) is visible in the running example. There the constant
sheaf [$E_{\mathbf A^1}$](@lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients) is the cone of a map $i_*E[-1]\to j_!\href{@lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients}{E_{\mathbf G_m}}$,
and up to shift both terms are of the form $(a_!L_0)[1/\ell]$ with $L_0$ the constant
[lattice](@definition-lattice) $\mathcal O_E$ on the stratum.
