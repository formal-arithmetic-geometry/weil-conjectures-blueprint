Identify the continuous integral coefficient sheaf with its completed
constant-coefficient presentation, keeping the scalar map and residue
projection. The scalar map is flat; the kernel of the first residue map
is a finite type ideal, and extending it gives the same quotient on both
sides. The equivalence for complete complexes under such a flat map gives
the result in the small ringed-site presentation.

The exact equivalences between module objects, sectionwise modules and the
small-site presentation commute with scalar restriction. They also identify
the local completeness predicates. Restrict these equivalences to complete
objects and transport the preceding equivalence back to the original
categories. Full faithfulness transports the natural isomorphism with the
inclusion functors. Preservation and reflection of completeness give the
assertion for every $K$.
