Essential surjectivity of the finite-coefficient descent cocone gives a
finite subextension $E$ and a bounded constructible $E$-complex $K_E$
whose scalar extension is $K$. The localization functor of
[proposition-inverting-ell] is essentially surjective, so choose an
integral bounded constructible complex $K_0$ and an isomorphism
$E_X\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}K_0\simeq K_E$.
This is the integral-model existence supplied by
[lemma-integral-models--6] in the construction of rationalization.
Apply scalar extension to this isomorphism and compose it with the descent
isomorphism. No decomposition into the original generators is used.
