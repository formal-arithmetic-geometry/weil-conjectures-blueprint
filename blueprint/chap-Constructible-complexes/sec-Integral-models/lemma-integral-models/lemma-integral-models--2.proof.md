Transport $K$ to the completed constant-coefficient presentation. This exact
transport preserves boundedness and identifies finite constant local models
of the cohomology sheaves. The cohomological adic criterion identifies these
conditions with derived completeness and a constructible étale first
reduction of finite Tor amplitude; its reverse direction uses the
stratified perfect models of
[proposition-local-structure-integral-constructible-complexes].

The completion comparison [lemma-integral-models--1] identifies the
completeness conditions. The normalized finite-reduction comparison
identifies the two first reductions, including their scalar actions.
The pro-étale/étale comparison then identifies the residue conditions,
including finite Tor amplitude. This proves the membership criterion.
Finally restrict the equivalence on complete complexes to these full
subcategories: every object on either side is complete, and the membership
criterion identifies the selected objects. The natural isomorphism with
restriction is inherited from the complete-object equivalence.
