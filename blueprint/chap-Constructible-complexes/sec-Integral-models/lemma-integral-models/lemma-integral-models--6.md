Every object of $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$ is obtained by finitely many cones and shifts from $(a_!L_0)[1/\ell]$, where $a:S\hookrightarrow X$ is locally closed and $L_0$ is a lisse $\mathcal O_{E,S}$-module locally free of finite rank.
