Pull back a finite stratification and its pro-étale covers. Pullback
preserves finite presentations, and the coefficient isomorphism of
[lemma-inverse-image-coefficient-sheaves] identifies the pulled-back ring
with the coefficient ring on the source. Thus pullback preserves
constructible module objects.

Since this pullback is exact, its derived functor commutes with cohomology
and preserves the same cohomological bounds. This proves the assertion.
For integral coefficients, the comparison between stratified local finite
presentation and finite constant local models identifies the two bounded
constructible categories on Noetherian schemes.
