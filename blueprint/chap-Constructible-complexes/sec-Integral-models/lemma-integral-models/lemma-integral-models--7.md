Every object of $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ is obtained by finitely many cones and shifts from $\QbarX\otimes_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}a_!L_0$, with $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ finite and $a,L_0$ as in the preceding clause.
