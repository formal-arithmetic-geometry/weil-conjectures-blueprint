For $j_!$, add the closed complement as a zero stratum to a stratification
of $U$. On each original stratum restriction recovers the original sheaf;
on the complement it is zero. Thus extension by zero preserves
constructible module objects. Exactness identifies the cohomology of
$j_!K$ with $j_!H^q(K)$ and preserves the cohomological bounds.

For $Ri_*$, its restriction to the open complement vanishes, while its
restriction to $Z$ is the original complex by the counit isomorphism.
The open--closed localization criterion for bounded constructibility
therefore gives the second assertion. Compose the two functors for
$a=i\circ j$ and use the integral comparison of constructibility
predicates from [lemma-integral-models--3].
