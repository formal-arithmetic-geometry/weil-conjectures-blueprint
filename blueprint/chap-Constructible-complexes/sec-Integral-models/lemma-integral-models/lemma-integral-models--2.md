Let $X$ be Noetherian and let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be finite. An object
$K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_{E,X})$ belongs to
$\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ if and only if its restriction to the constant
sheaf $\mathcal O_E$ belongs to $\Dc(X,\mathcal O_E)$. Here the latter
condition means derived completeness, constructible cohomology of the
derived first reduction, and finite Tor amplitude of that reduction.

Restriction induces an equivalence
$$
\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)\simeq\Dc(X,\mathcal O_E),
$$
compatible, up to natural isomorphism, with the inclusions into the ambient
derived categories and restriction of scalars.
