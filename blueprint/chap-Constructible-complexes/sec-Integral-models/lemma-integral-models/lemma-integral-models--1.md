Let $X$ be Noetherian and let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be finite. Write $R$ for the
constant sheaf $\mathcal O_E$, $B=\mathcal O_{E,X}$ and
$\Res:D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},B)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},R)$
for restriction along $R\to B$. For every complex $K$, $K$ is derived
$\pi_E$-complete if and only if $\Res K$ is derived $\pi_E$-complete.
Moreover, restriction induces an equivalence of the full subcategories
of complete complexes. The composite of this equivalence with the target
inclusion is naturally isomorphic to restriction composed with the source
inclusion. No boundedness or constructibility is assumed.
