We pass from statements with coefficients $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$ to
statements with coefficients $E$ through integral lattices. A lisse
$E_X$-module may fail to have a lattice on all of $X$, but it has one on
each stratum of a finite stratification
(Lemma [lemma-lattices-after-finite-stratification]), and this is enough for
dévissage arguments. In particular, from the stratified statement we obtain the integral
generators of Lemma [lemma-integral-models](4).
Lemma [lemma-lattices-after-finite-stratification] is also the
first step in the proofs of the uniform Ext bound and of the degree bound for
proper direct image in Chapter [chap-Construction-of-f]. On a
connected normal scheme, by contrast, there is a global lattice, and lisse $E$-sheaves are
representations of $\pi_1(X,\bar x)$; we prove this where it is used, in
Proposition [proposition-lattices-representations-normal-scheme].

On the nodal curve $X$ of Example [example-lattices-nodal-curve], the
sheaf $L$ has no global lattice, and one stratification step
repairs this. On the open stratum
$X\smallsetminus\{\text{node}\}\simeq\mathbf G_m$ the torsor is
trivial, so $L$ is constant there and has a lattice. The node, with its
reduced structure, is the spectrum of a field, which is normal, and hence the
restriction of $L$ to the node has a lattice as well.
