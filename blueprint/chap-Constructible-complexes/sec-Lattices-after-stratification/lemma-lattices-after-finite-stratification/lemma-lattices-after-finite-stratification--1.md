Let $L$ be a lisse $E_X$-module. There is a finite stratification of $X$
by locally closed subschemes $S$ such that each $L|_S$ contains a lisse
$\mathcal O_{E,S}$-submodule $L_0$ with $L_0[1/\ell]=L|_S$.
