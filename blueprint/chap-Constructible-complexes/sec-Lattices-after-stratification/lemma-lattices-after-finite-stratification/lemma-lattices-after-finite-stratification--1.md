Let $\ell$ be a prime, let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be a finite extension,
and let $X$ be a topologically Noetherian scheme. Let $L$ be an $E_X$-module
which is pro-étale locally free of finite rank. There is a finite
stratification of $X$ by locally closed subschemes $j_s:S_s\hookrightarrow X$
and, for every $s$, an $\mathcal O_{E,S_s}$-module $L_{0,s}$ which is
pro-étale locally free of finite rank, together with an isomorphism

$$
E_{S_s}\otimes_{\mathcal O_{E,S_s}}L_{0,s}\simeq j_s^*L.
$$
