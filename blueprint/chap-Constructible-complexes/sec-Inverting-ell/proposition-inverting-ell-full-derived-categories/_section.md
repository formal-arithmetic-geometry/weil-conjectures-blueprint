Extension of scalars $\href{@lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients}{E_X}\otimes_{\href{@lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients}{\mathcal O_{E,X}}}-:
D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\mathcal O_E)\to D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},E)$ identifies
$D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},E)$ with the Verdier quotient of $D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\mathcal O_E)$
by the complexes $K$ with $H^q(K)[1/\ell]=0$ for all $q$.
