Extension of scalars $E_X\otimes_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}-:
D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$ identifies
$D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$ with the Verdier quotient of $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$
by the complexes $K$ with $H^q(K)[1/\ell]=0$ for all $q$.
