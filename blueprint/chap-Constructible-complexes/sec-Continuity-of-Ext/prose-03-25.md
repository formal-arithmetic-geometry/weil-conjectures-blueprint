Formula (3.1) expresses Hom out of $a_!L$ through
cohomology on the stratum $S$ and the functors $i^*$, $u^*$ and $Rj_*$.
Each of these commutes with filtered colimits of uniformly bounded below
complexes, and from this we deduce the continuity of Ext.
