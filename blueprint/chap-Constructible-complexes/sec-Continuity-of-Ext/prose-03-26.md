Applied to sheaves, which are complexes in degree $0$, the lemma says that
for $M\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$, $\Lambda=E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, the functors
$\Ext^n(M,-)$ commute with filtered colimits of $\Lambda_X$-modules.
