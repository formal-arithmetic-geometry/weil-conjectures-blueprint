Let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be finite and let $L$ be a [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) $E_X$-module. The sheaf on
[$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$](@definition-small-pro-etale-site) of [lattices](@definition-lattice) in $L$ is represented
by a separated surjective étale $X$-scheme, with a universal [lattice](@definition-lattice).
In particular, $L$ admits a [lattice](@definition-lattice) étale locally on $X$.
