Let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be finite and let $L$ be a lisse $E_X$-module. The sheaf on
$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ of lattices in $L$ is represented
by a separated surjective étale $X$-scheme, with a universal lattice.
