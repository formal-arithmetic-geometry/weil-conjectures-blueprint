Use the abelian structure of [lemma-constructible-sheaves--4]. Kernels
and cokernels in the full subcategory are computed in the ambient
category by [lemma-constructible-sheaves--1] and
[lemma-constructible-sheaves--2]. The additive inclusion therefore
preserves kernels and cokernels, and hence finite limits and finite
colimits.
