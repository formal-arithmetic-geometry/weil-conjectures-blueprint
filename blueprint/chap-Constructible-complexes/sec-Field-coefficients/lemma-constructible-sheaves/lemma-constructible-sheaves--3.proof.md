Take a common stratification for $F$ and $G$ and restrict the exact
sequence to a stratum. After a common pro-étale cover, both outer terms
are finite free, by [lemma-constructible-sheaves--8]. Lift a basis of the
quotient after a further cover. This splits the sequence, so its middle
term is a direct sum of two finite free modules. It is therefore lisse
on the stratum. Exact restriction and the common stratification prove
that $M$ is constructible.
