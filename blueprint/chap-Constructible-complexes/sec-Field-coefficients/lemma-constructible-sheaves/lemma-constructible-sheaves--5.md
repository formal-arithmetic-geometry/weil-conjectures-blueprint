Let $X$ be a Noetherian scheme and let $\Lambda=E$ or
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, where $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ is finite and the
coefficients are regarded as condensed rings.
The inclusion

$$
\Cons(X,\Lambda)\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Mod}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)
$$

preserves finite limits and finite colimits. In particular it is exact.
