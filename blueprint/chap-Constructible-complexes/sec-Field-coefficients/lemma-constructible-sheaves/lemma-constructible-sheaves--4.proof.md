Constructibility is invariant under isomorphism, and the zero sheaf has
a rank-zero presentation on the identity stratification. The closure
properties [lemma-constructible-sheaves--1], [lemma-constructible-sheaves--2]
and [lemma-constructible-sheaves--3] give kernels, cokernels and finite
biproducts in the full subcategory; for biproducts use split extensions.
The abelian structure is the one induced from the ambient abelian
category by these closure properties.
