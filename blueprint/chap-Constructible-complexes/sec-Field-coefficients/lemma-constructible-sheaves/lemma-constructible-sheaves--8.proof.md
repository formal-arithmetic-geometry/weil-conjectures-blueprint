For $\Lambda=E$, realize a local presentation matrix as a matrix of
continuous $E$-valued functions. Its cokernel has fibre dimension equal
to the number of rows minus the pointwise rank. Geometric fibres show
that this dimension agrees on overlaps of presentation covers. Flatness
of the covering maps lifts generalizations, and continuous functions to
the Hausdorff field $E$ agree at comparable points. The fibre dimension
therefore descends to a function constant on specializations. The
finitely many irreducible components and their generic points make its
level sets open and closed.

On each rank level, cover by the nonvanishing loci of maximal minors.
Invert a chosen minor and apply row and column operations to reduce the
matrix to an identity block and its Schur complement. Constant
pointwise rank forces the Schur complement to vanish, so the cokernel
is finite free. Rank zero is treated by the zero matrix, and composing
the covers proves the assertion.

For $\Lambda=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, first refine the presentation
covers so that the finitely many matrix entries are continuous functions
with values in a common finite extension $E$. Fibre dimensions are
unchanged by extending $E$ to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. The same rank
and minor argument gives finite free modules over the actual
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ coefficient sheaf. Compose these refinements
with the original presentation cover.
