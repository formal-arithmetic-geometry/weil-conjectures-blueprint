Refine the stratifications of $F$ and $G$ by their fibre products.
On each stratum their restrictions are locally finitely presented.
By [lemma-constructible-sheaves--8], a common pro-étale cover makes them
finite free. The restricted morphism is then a map between finite free
modules, so its cokernel has a finite presentation. The same lemma makes
this cokernel locally free of finite rank. Exact restriction identifies
it with the restriction of $\coker f$, and the common stratification
proves constructibility.
