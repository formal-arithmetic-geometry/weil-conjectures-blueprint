Let $X$ be a Noetherian scheme and let $\Lambda=E$ or
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, where $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ is finite and the
coefficients are regarded as condensed rings.
Fix a universe $u$ containing $X$ and the profinite test objects, and take
coefficient sheaves with values in the next universe $u+1$.
Then $\Cons(X,\Lambda)$ is essentially small in universe $u+1$.
