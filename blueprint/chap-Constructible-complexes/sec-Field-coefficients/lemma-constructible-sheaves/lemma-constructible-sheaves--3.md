Let $X$ be a Noetherian scheme and let $\Lambda=E$ or
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, where $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ is finite and the
coefficients are regarded as condensed rings.
If

$$
0\longrightarrow F\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow0
$$

is exact in $\operatorname{Mod}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ and
$F,G$ are constructible, then $M$ is constructible.
