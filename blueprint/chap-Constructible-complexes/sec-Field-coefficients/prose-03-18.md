Until the end of this subsection, $\Lambda=E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, so that
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}=\Dbc$ and we can argue with constructible cohomology sheaves
and standard truncations. Recall (after
Definition [definition-lisse-sheaves]) that a $\Lambda_X$-module is
constructible exactly when it is lisse, that is locally free of
finite rank for the pro-étale topology, on each stratum of a finite
stratification of $X$. By dévissage we reduce statements about
$\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ to the simplest constructible sheaves, namely lisse sheaves
extended by zero from a stratum, the sheaves $a_!L$ of
Section [sec-Derived-functors]. The dévissage first splits a complex into its
cohomology sheaves by truncation, and then filters each constructible sheaf by
strata.

For this dévissage we need constructible sheaves to form an
abelian category (Lemma [lemma-constructible-sheaves]). In the proof we
reduce a morphism of lisse $E$-sheaves to a morphism of integral lattices.
For $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we first descend the morphism to a finite $E$. In the next two
lemmas we construct the lattices locally and carry out the descent.
