Every object of $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is obtained by finitely many cones from $a_!L[n]$, where $a:S\hookrightarrow X$ is locally closed, $L$ is lisse over $\Lambda_S$, and $n\in\mathbf Z$.
