Let $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$ and let $L$ be the sheaf on the nodal curve $X$ from the start of
this chapter, obtained from $E_Y$ on the chain of lines $Y\to X$ by letting
the generator of $\mathbf Z$ act by $\ell$. Over $Y$ the lattices in
$L|_Y=E_Y$ are the constant lattices $\ell^m\mathbf Z_\ell$, so over $Y$ the scheme of
lattices is $\coprod_{m\in\mathbf Z}Y$. The generator of $\mathbf Z$ moves the
copy indexed by $m$ to the copy indexed by $m+1$ while translating $Y$. Hence the
scheme of lattices over $X$, which is the quotient by $\mathbf Z$, is a single
copy of $Y$. It is surjective and étale over $X$, as the lemma says, but it
has no section. Indeed, a section would trivialize the $\mathbf Z$-torsor $Y\to X$,
whereas $Y$ is connected and $\coprod_{\mathbf Z}X$ is not. This is
the absence of a global lattice seen geometrically, and the universal
lattice lives on $Y$, where $L$ becomes constant.
