Let $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$ and let $L$ be the sheaf on the nodal curve $X$ from the start of
this section, obtained from $E_Y$ on the chain of lines $Y\to X$ by letting
the generator of $\mathbf Z$ act by $\ell$. Over $Y$ the lattices in
$L|_Y=E_Y$ are the constant lattices $\ell^m\mathbf Z_\ell$, so over $Y$ the scheme of
lattices is $\coprod_{m\in\mathbf Z}Y$. The generator of $\mathbf Z$ moves the
copy indexed by $m$ to the copy indexed by $m+1$ while translating $Y$.
