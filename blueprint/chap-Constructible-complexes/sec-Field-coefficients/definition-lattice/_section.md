Let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be finite and let $L$ be a [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) [$E_X$](@lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients)-module. A
*lattice* in $L$ is a [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) [$\mathcal O_{E,X}$](@lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients)-submodule $M\subset L$
with $M[1/\ell]=L$.
