If $X$ is a curve, part (3) amounts to an exact sequence
$0\to j_!L\to F\to i_*i^*F\to0$, where $j$ is the inclusion of a dense
open subset on which $F$ is lisse, $L=j^*F$, and $i$ is the inclusion of
the finitely many closed points of the complement.
