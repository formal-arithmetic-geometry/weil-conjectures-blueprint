Extension of scalars commutes with pullback of coefficient sheaves, sends
$\Lambda_U\otimes_{\Lambda(*)}P$ to
$\Lambda'_U\otimes_{\Lambda'(*)}(\Lambda'(*)\otimes_{\Lambda(*)}P)$,
and so preserves finitely presented local models on the strata of a
stratification. Being exact, it commutes with cohomology sheaves and
preserves bounds.
