Let $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ be a map of commutative condensed rings for which
extension of scalars $\Lambda'_X\otimes_{\Lambda_X}-$ is exact, for instance
$E\to E'$ or $E\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ for finite extensions $E'\supset E$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ in
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, or $\mathcal O_E\to E$. Then extension of scalars sends
$\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ into $\Dbc(X,\Lambda')$. In particular
$C_{E'}\in\Dbc(X,E')$ and $C_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}\in\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ for $C\in\Dbc(X,E)$.
