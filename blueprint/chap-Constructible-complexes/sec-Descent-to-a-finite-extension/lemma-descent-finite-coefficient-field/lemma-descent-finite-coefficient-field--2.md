Every object of $\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ is isomorphic to $K_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$ for
some $K\in\Dbc(X,E)$ and some finite $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$.
