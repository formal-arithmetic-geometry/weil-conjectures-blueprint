On a stratum where the cohomology of $K_1$ is locally constant, the filtration triangles in clause [lemma-bounds-finite-reductions--1] express each $K_n$ as successive extensions of copies of $K_1$. Locally constant finite sheaves are closed under kernels, cokernels and extensions on that stratum. Induction and the long cohomology sequence give local constancy of all cohomology sheaves of $K_n$ there.

*Source:* the proof of Lemma [lemma-bounds-finite-reductions].
