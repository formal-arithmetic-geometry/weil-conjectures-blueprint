*Shared context.* Let $X$ be Noetherian and $K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$ derived complete, with $K_1\in\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathbf F_E)$. Choose the interval $[a,b]$ containing the cohomology of $K_1$ as in the reference. Write $\varpi=\pi_E$ and use the same reductions $K_n$.
