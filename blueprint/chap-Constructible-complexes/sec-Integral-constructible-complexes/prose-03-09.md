The second fact is used in the proof of
Lemma [lemma-integral-model-weakly-contractible] for the internal Hom
complex $(P^\bullet)^\vee\otimes P^\bullet$, whose $H^0$ at level $n$
consists of the maps $P^\bullet/\pi_E^n\to P^\bullet/\pi_E^n$ in the derived
category. There it says that a map at level $n$ which lifts far enough lifts
to all levels, and how far is enough depends only on the torsion of the
cohomology.
