$$
\bigl[\mathbf F_E\xrightarrow{\,0\,}\mathbf F_E\bigr]=\mathbf F_E\oplus\mathbf F_E[1].
$$

since $\pi_E^e$ becomes zero modulo $\pi_E$. For
$P=\mathcal O_E^r\oplus\bigoplus_{i=1}^s\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^{e_i}$ the
reduction $K_1$ has $H^0=\mathbf F_E^{r+s}$ and $H^{-1}=\mathbf F_E^s$.
