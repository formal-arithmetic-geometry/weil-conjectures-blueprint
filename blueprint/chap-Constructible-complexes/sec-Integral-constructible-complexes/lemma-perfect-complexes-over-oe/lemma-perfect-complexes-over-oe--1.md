For $n\ge1$ let $M_n\in D(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$ be perfect, with
maps $M_{n+1}\to M_n$ inducing isomorphisms
$M_{n+1}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^{n+1}}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n\simeq M_n$.
Then there is a bounded complex $P^\bullet$ of finite free
$\mathcal O_E$-modules with isomorphisms $P^\bullet/\pi_E^n\simeq M_n$
in $D(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, compatible with the transition maps.
