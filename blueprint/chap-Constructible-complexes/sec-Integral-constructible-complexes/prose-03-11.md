The bound $m\geq n+e$ cannot be improved. Take
$Q^\bullet=[\mathcal O_E\xrightarrow{\pi_E^e}\mathcal O_E]$ in degrees
$0,1$, so that $H^0(Q^\bullet)=0$ and $H^1(Q^\bullet)=\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^e$.
For $m\geq e$ the group $H^0(Q^\bullet/\pi_E^m)$ is $\pi_E^{m-e}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^m$,
and its image at level $n$ is generated by $\pi_E^{m-e}$ modulo $\pi_E^n$.
This image is zero exactly when $m\geq n+e$, in agreement with the image of
$H^0(Q^\bullet)=0$.

At a single finite level the local structure is a statement about the
étale site: a perfect complex with locally constant cohomology is
locally constant. On a connected scheme its local model is moreover the same
at every point.
