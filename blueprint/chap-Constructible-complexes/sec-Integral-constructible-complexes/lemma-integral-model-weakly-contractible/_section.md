Let $W$ be an affine weakly contractible scheme, let
$K\in D(W_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$ be derived complete, and let $P^\bullet$
be a bounded complex of finite free $\mathcal O_E$-modules. Suppose that
for every $n\geq1$ there is an isomorphism
$(P^\bullet/\pi_E^n)_W\simeq K_n$ in $D(W_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$.
Then $K\simeq\mathcal O_{E,W}\otimes_{\mathcal O_E}P^\bullet$.
