Let $W$ be an affine [weakly contractible](@definition-weak-contractibility) scheme, let
$K\in D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{W_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\mathcal O_E)$ be [derived complete](@definition-derived-adic-completeness), and let $P^\bullet$
be a bounded complex of finite free $\mathcal O_E$-modules. Suppose that
for every $n\geq1$ there is an isomorphism
$(P^\bullet/\pi_E^n)_W\simeq K_n$ in $D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{W_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$.
Then $K\simeq\href{@lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients}{\mathcal O_{E,W}}\otimes_{\mathcal O_E}P^\bullet$.
