title = "Locally constant perfect complexes"

Let $S$ be a connected Noetherian scheme, let $A$ be a finite local ring,
and let $L\in D^b(S_{\acute{e}t},A)$ have finite Tor amplitude and cohomology
sheaves that are locally constant with finite stalks. Let $\bar s$ be a
geometric point of $S$ and let $M$ be a bounded complex of finite free
$A$-modules with $M\simeq L_{\bar s}$ in $D(A)$. Then $L$ is étale
locally on $S$ isomorphic to the constant complex $\underline M$.
