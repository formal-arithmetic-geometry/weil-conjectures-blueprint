title = "Images of cohomology at finite levels"
order = 234
+++
Let $Q^\bullet$ be a bounded complex of finite free
$\mathcal O_E$-modules, and let $\pi_E^e$ kill the torsion of
$H^{q+1}(Q^\bullet)$. Then for $m\ge n+e$

$$
\operatorname{im}\bigl(H^q(Q^\bullet/\pi_E^m)\to
H^q(Q^\bullet/\pi_E^n)\bigr)
=\operatorname{im}\bigl(H^q(Q^\bullet)\to
H^q(Q^\bullet/\pi_E^n)\bigr).
$$
