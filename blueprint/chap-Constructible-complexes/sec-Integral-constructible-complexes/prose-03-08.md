To make the local trivializations of the $K_n$ compatible, we need two facts about
complexes over $\mathcal O_E$:
a compatible system of perfect complexes over the rings
$\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$ comes from a single bounded finite free complex, and
the images of the cohomology at finite levels stabilize at a uniform rate.
