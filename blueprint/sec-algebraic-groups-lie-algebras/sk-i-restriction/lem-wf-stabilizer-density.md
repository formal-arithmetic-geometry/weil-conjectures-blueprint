Let $S\subseteq V$ be a vector subspace. The stabilizer
$\operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{GL}(V)}(S)$ is Zariski closed.
Consequently $S$ is $\Pi$-stable if and only if it is
$H_{\mathrm{geom}}$-stable.
