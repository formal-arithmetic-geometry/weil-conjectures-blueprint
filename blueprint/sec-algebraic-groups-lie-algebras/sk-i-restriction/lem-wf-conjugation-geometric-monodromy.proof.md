The subgroup $P$ is the kernel of the degree homomorphism of
[d-monodromy], hence normal in $W$; since $\rho$ is a homomorphism, the set
$\rho(P)$ is carried onto $\rho(sPs^{-1})=\rho(P)$ by conjugation with
$F$. Conjugation by the matrix $F$ is an automorphism of the algebraic
group $\operatorname{GL}_n$; on coordinate rings it carries the ideal of
functions vanishing on $\rho(P)$ onto itself, so it carries the closure
$H=\overline{\rho(P)}$ of [d-monodromy] onto itself. The set $H$ is a
subgroup by [lem-wf-zariski-closure-subgroup], and conjugation is a group
homomorphism, so $\alpha$ is an automorphism of the group $H$; as the
restriction of an automorphism of $\operatorname{GL}_n$ preserving $H$,
it is an automorphism of algebraic groups.
