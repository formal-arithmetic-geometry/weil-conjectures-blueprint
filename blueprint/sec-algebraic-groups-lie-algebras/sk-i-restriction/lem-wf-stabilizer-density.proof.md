Choose a basis whose first vectors span $S$. The condition that a
matrix preserve $S$ says that each lower-left matrix entry is zero,
which is a finite list of polynomial equations. The stabilizer is
therefore closed. If $S$ is $\Gamma$-stable, this closed stabilizer
contains $\Gamma$, hence contains its Zariski closure; the converse is
immediate. For a represented degree datum apply this to
$\Gamma=\rho(P)$ and $\Gamma=\rho(W)$, whose closures are $H$ and $G$ by
[d-monodromy].
