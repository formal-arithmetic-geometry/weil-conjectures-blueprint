This is Engel's theorem in its matrix form [humphreys72, §3.3]. The
first assertion is proved by induction on $\dim\mathfrak n$, using that
a maximal proper subalgebra is an ideal of codimension one; the
triangular basis follows by induction on $\dim M$.
