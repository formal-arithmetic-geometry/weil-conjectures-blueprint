For a composition series
$V_\bullet$, the *semisimplification* is

$$
V^{\mathrm{ss}}:=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{t}V_i/V_{i-1}.
$$

Its isomorphism class does not depend on the chosen composition series,
and the multiset of its simple summands is the multiset of composition
factors (Jordan–Hölder).
