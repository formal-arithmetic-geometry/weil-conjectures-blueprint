For every $m\in\mathbf Z$ and every closed point $x$ of $X_0$, the
geometric Frobenius $\varphi_{E(m)}$ on $E(m)_{\bar x}$ is
$Q_x^{-m}\mathrm{id}$, where $Q_x=|\kappa(x)|$.
