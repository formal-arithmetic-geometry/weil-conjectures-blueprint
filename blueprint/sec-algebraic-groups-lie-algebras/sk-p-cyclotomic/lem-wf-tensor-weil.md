The construction of [def-wf-tensor-product] restricts to an exact
bifunctor

$$
-\otimes^L_E-:\mathrm{WeilD}(X_0,E)\times\mathrm{WeilD}(X_0,E)
\longrightarrow\mathrm{WeilD}(X_0,E).
$$
