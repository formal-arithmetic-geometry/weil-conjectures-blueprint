On $\mu_{\ell^n}$ the geometric Frobenius, i.e. the Weil structure $\tau_1$ of
$\Lambda_X(1)$ given by the strict arrow of [lem-wf-tate-twist-compatibility], acts by raising to the
inverse $q$-th power, hence on the stalk of $\cO_{E,X}(1)$ at $\bar x$, the free rank-one module
$\mathbf Z_\ell(1)\otimes_{\mathbf Z_\ell}\cO_E$ with
$\mathbf Z_\ell(1)=\varprojlim_n\mu_{\ell^n}(k)$, the local Frobenius at $x$ is
multiplication by $Q_x^{-1}$; inverting $\ell$, passing to
$\Qlb$, and the tensor/dual constructions of [def-wf-tate-twist] then give
$Q_x^{-m}$ on $\Lambda_X(m)_{\bar x}$.
