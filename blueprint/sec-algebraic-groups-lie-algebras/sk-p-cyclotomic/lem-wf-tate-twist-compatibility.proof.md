For finite coefficients $A$ the sheaf $A_X(1)=\mu_{\ell^r,X}\otimes A_X$ of
[sk-def-sp-tate-twist] receives the canonical isomorphism
$\Phi_X^*\mu_{\ell^r,X}\cong\mu_{\ell^r,X}$, and the $\ell$-power maps are
$\Phi_X$-equivariant. Since $\Phi_X$ is an automorphism of $X$, the pullback
$\Phi_X^*$ is an equivalence of pro-étale topoi and commutes with the inverse limit
$\cO_{E,X}(1)=\varprojlim_n(\cO_E/\ell^n)_X(1)$, with inverting $\ell$ and with the
colimit over $E$; this gives the strict arrow on $\Lambda_X(1)$. The induced arrows
for tensor products and duals give the cases $m>0$ and $m<0$ by
[lem-wf-tensor-weil] and [lem-wf-internal-hom-weil]. For a finite extension
$F/E$ one has $F_X(m)=F_X\otimes_{E_X}E_X(m)$ by construction, compatibly with the
Weil structures, so $(-)(m)$ commutes with finite scalar extension; functoriality in
$m$ follows from the same constructions being natural.
