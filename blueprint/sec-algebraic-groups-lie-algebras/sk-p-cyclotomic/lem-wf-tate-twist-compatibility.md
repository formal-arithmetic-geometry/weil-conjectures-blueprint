For every $m\in\mathbf Z$, the functorial pullback of roots of unity
gives a strict arrow
$\Phi_X^*E(1)\stackrel{\sim}{\to}E(1)$, and the Tate twist functor
$(-)(m)$ of [def-wf-tate-twist] commutes with finite scalar
extension.
