If $\operatorname{rad}\mathfrak l=0$, the Killing form
$B(x,y)=\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}x\operatorname{ad}y)$
is nondegenerate and $[\mathfrak l,\mathfrak l]=\mathfrak l$.
