In the notation of [def-wf-degree-components], the following holds. The matrix image
of $G^{[m]}$ under the map $(h,m)\mapsto h\rho(s)^m$ of
[def-wf-arithmetic-monodromy-extension] is computed in
[lem-wf-degree-component-image], and the products and inverses of the
components $G^{[m]}$ are computed in
[lem-wf-degree-components-grading].
