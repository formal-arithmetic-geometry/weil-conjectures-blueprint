For a geometrically connected separated finite-type $k_0$-scheme
$X_0$ with $X=X_0\times_{k_0}k$, the *Weil group*
$W(X_0)$ appearing in the Weil fundamental representation of
[def-wf-fundamental-representation] is the inverse image of the infinite cyclic subgroup
generated by the geometric Frobenius $\Phi_X$ of
[def-tw-frobenius-action] inside the arithmetic
fundamental group, and the
*degree map* is the resulting projection
$\deg:W(X_0)\to\mathbf Z$, which records the exponent of $\Phi_X$.
