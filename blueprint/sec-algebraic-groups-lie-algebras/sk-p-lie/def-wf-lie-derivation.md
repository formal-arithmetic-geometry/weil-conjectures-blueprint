A *derivation* of a Lie algebra
$\mathfrak k$ is a linear map $D:\mathfrak k\to\mathfrak k$ satisfying

$$
D([X,Y])=[D(X),Y]+[X,D(Y)].
$$

It is *inner* if $D=\operatorname{ad}(Z)=[Z,-]$ for some
$Z\in\mathfrak k$.
