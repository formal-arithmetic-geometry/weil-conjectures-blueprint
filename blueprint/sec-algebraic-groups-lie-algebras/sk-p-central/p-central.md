Every semisimple represented degree datum ([d-monodromy]) over an
algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic zero
admits a positive central pair ([d-central]) $(N,z)$, that is $N>0$ and
$z\in Z(G)(k)\cap H(k)F^N$.
