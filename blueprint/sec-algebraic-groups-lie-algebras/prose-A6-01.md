Lemma [sk-p-product] says that the identity component
$G^\circ$ of the arithmetic monodromy group is generated by
$Z(G^\circ)^\circ$ and the identity component $H^\circ$ of the
geometric monodromy group. Its proof studies $G^\circ$ through the Lie
algebra $\mathfrak g$ of $G^\circ$. It uses three
facts: the group and $\mathfrak g$ have the same
stable subspaces, the center of $\mathfrak g$ is the Lie algebra of the
center, and $\mathfrak g=Z(\mathfrak g)+[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]$ when
$\mathfrak g$ has a faithful semisimple representation.
The first two hold because in characteristic zero every algebraic group
is smooth (Cartier's theorem), so that a closed subgroup of a connected
group with the same Lie algebra is the whole group. For the third we show that the radical of
$\mathfrak g$ acts on each simple summand by scalars, hence is
central, and the quotient by the center has nondegenerate Killing form
by Cartan's criterion, hence equals its derived algebra.
