Suppose $\mathfrak a\subseteq\operatorname{End}(M)$ satisfies

$$
\operatorname{tr}(XY)=0
\qquad(X\in[\mathfrak a,\mathfrak a],\ Y\in\mathfrak a).
$$

Then $\mathfrak a$ is solvable.
