The radical $\rad\mathfrak l$ of a Lie algebra $\mathfrak l$ is the sum of
all its solvable ideals. If $\rad\mathfrak l=0$, the Killing form
$B(x,y)=\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}x\operatorname{ad}y)$ is
nondegenerate and $[\mathfrak l,\mathfrak l]=\mathfrak l$.
