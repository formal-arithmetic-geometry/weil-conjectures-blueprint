Constructibility of the direct image of a finite-type morphism
[thm-weil-direct-image-constructibility], the Milnor triangle for towers
[prop-weil-milnor-triangle], and the stability of the finiteness conditions
under twists [prop-weil-twist-preserves]. The derived operations are
well defined [prop-weil-derived-operations-well-defined], including derived
extension by zero [prop-weil-derived-extension-by-zero]; coefficient passage
for proper support [prop-compact-support-coefficient-passage-detailed] and the
ambient exceptional right adjoint
[prop-ambient-exceptional-adjoint-detailed] follow.

The proper-base-change side runs from the geometric-fibre form
[prop-proper-fibers] and the finite-torsion exchange isomorphism
[prop-weil-finite-proper-base-change-exchange] through the boundedness
[prop-weil-proper-direct-image-boundedness] and constructibility
[prop-weil-proper-direct-image-constructibility],
[lem-weil-proper-direct-image-cohomology-sheaf] of the proper derived direct
image, its coherence [prop-weil-proper-base-change-coherence], to the adic,
rational and algebraically closed passage [prop-coefficient-passage] and the
resulting functor on the constructible categories
[prop-weil-direct-image-constructible-landing].
