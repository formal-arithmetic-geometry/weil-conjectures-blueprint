For $\mathfrak{sl}_2$, with basis $e,h,f$, the brackets $[h,e]=2e$,
$[h,f]=-2f$ and $[e,f]=h$ already show that $[\mathfrak{sl}_2,\mathfrak{sl}_2]=\mathfrak{sl}_2$.
The lemma gives the second half of the next proposition, once we know
that the quotient by the center has zero radical.
