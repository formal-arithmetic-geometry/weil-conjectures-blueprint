Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero, let $\mathfrak l$ be a
finite-dimensional Lie algebra over $k$, and let $M$ be a faithful
finite-dimensional semisimple representation of $\mathfrak l$. Assume
that each $x\in\mathfrak l$ acts on $M$ by a triangularizable
endomorphism. Then

$$
\operatorname{rad}\mathfrak l=Z(\mathfrak l).
$$

Here semisimplicity means that every $\mathfrak l$-stable subspace of
$M$ has a $\mathfrak l$-stable complement. The triangularizability
condition is automatic over $k=\Omega$.
