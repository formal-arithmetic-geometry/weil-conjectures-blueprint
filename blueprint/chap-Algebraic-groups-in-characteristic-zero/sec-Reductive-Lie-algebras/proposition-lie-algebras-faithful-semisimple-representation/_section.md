If $\mathfrak l$ has a faithful semisimple representation, then

$$
\operatorname{rad}\mathfrak l=Z(\mathfrak l),
\qquad
\mathfrak l=Z(\mathfrak l)+[\mathfrak l,\mathfrak l].
$$
