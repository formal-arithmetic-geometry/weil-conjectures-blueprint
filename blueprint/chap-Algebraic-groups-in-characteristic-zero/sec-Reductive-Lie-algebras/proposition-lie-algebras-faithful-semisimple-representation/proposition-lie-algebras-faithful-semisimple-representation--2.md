Let $k$ be a field and let $\mathfrak l$ be a finite-dimensional Lie
algebra over $k$ such that $\operatorname{rad}\mathfrak l=Z(\mathfrak l)$.
Then

$$
\operatorname{rad}\bigl(\mathfrak l/Z(\mathfrak l)\bigr)=0.
$$

If $k$ has characteristic zero, then also

$$
\mathfrak l=Z(\mathfrak l)+[\mathfrak l,\mathfrak l].
$$
