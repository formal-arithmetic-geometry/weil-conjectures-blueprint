Let $\mathfrak n\subseteq\operatorname{End}(M)$ be a Lie algebra
consisting of nilpotent endomorphisms. If $M\ne0$, then the common
kernel of $\mathfrak n$ is nonzero. There is a basis of $M$ in which all
elements of $\mathfrak n$ are strictly upper triangular. In
particular, $\mathfrak n$ is nilpotent and solvable.
