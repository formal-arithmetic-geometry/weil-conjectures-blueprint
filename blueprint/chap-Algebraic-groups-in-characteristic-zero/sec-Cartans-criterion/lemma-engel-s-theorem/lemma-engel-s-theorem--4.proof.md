By clause [lemma-engel-s-theorem--3], the lower central series of $\mathfrak n$ terminates. Each successive derived commutator lies in a later term of that lower central series (or use $[N_r,N_s]\subseteq N_{r+s}$ from its proof). Hence the derived series also terminates, and $\mathfrak n$ is solvable.
