In the basis of clause [lemma-engel-s-theorem--2], the Lie algebra lies in the strictly upper triangular matrices. Let $N_r$ consist of matrices supported at least $r$ places above the diagonal. Matrix multiplication gives $[N_r,N_s]\subseteq N_{r+s}$. The $r$th term of the lower central series lies in $N_r$, and $N_{\dim M}=0$. Thus the lower central series terminates and $\mathfrak n$ is nilpotent.
