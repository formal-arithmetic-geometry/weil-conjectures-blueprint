Apply the matrix form of Engel's theorem [humphreys72, §3.3], which is the external input explicitly quoted in the reference. Its hypothesis is that every element of the Lie subalgebra of $\operatorname{End}(M)$ acts nilpotently. For $M\ne0$ it gives a nonzero vector annihilated by all these operators, that is, a nonzero common kernel.
