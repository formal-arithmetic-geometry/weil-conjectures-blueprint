Induct on $\dim M$. The zero-dimensional case has the empty basis. Otherwise clause [lemma-engel-s-theorem--1] gives a nonzero common kernel vector $v$. The induced operators on $M/\Omega v$ remain nilpotent, so their Lie algebra has a strictly triangular basis by induction. Lift that basis and place $v$ first. All operators are then strictly upper triangular in the resulting basis of $M$.
