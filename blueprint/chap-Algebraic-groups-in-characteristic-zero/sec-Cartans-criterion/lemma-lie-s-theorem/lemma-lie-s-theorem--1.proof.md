This is Lie's theorem over the algebraically closed characteristic-zero field $\Omega$ fixed in this appendix, in the form [humphreys72, §4.1] quoted in the reference. The representation is finite dimensional and nonzero, and the Lie algebra is solvable, so that theorem supplies a one-dimensional invariant subspace $\Omega v$. Every $a$ acts on it by a scalar $\lambda(a)$, and linearity of the action makes $\lambda$ a linear functional. This gives $av=\lambda(a)v$ for all $a$.
