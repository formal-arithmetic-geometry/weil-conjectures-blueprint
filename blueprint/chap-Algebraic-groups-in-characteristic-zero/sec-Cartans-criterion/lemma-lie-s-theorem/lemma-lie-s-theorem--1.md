Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero, let $\mathfrak l$ be a
solvable Lie algebra over $k$, and let $M$ be a nonzero
finite-dimensional representation. Suppose that each $a\in\mathfrak l$
acts by an endomorphism whose generalized eigenspaces over $k$ span
$M$. Then there are $v\ne0$ and a $k$-linear functional
$\lambda:\mathfrak l\to k$ such that

$$
av=\lambda(a)v\quad(a\in\mathfrak l),
\qquad
\lambda\bigl([\mathfrak l,\mathfrak l]\bigr)=0.
$$

No finite-dimensionality assumption on $\mathfrak l$ is needed.
The hypothesis on the action is automatic for $k=\Omega$.
