Let $\mathfrak l$ be a solvable Lie algebra over $\Omega$, and let $M$ be
a nonzero finite-dimensional representation of $\mathfrak l$. Then there are $v\ne0$ and a
linear functional $\lambda:\mathfrak l\to\Omega$ such that

$$
av=\lambda(a)v\quad(a\in\mathfrak l).
$$

In particular $\lambda([\mathfrak l,\mathfrak l])=0$, since $[a,b]$ acts on $v$
by $\lambda(a)\lambda(b)-\lambda(b)\lambda(a)=0$.
