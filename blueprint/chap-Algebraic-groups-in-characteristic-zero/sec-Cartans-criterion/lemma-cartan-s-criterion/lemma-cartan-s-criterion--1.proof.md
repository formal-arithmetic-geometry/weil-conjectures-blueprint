Apply the linear form of Cartan's solvability criterion [humphreys72, §4.3], explicitly quoted in the reference. It applies over the characteristic-zero field of this appendix to the finite-dimensional representation $\mathfrak a\subseteq\operatorname{End}(M)$. Its trace hypothesis is exactly $\operatorname{tr}(XY)=0$ for $X\in[\mathfrak a,\mathfrak a]$ and $Y\in\mathfrak a$. The conclusion is that $\mathfrak a$ is solvable.
