See [humphreys72, §4.1]. By induction one finds a common
eigenvector for an ideal of codimension one, and then shows by a trace
computation, which we redo in the proof of
Proposition [proposition-lie-algebras-faithful-semisimple-representation], that the corresponding
eigenspace is stable under the remaining direction.
