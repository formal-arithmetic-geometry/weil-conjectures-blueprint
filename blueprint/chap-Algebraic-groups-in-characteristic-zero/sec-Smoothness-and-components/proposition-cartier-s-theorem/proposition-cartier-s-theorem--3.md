Let $I\subseteq\Omega[\GL_n]$ be a Hopf ideal, with $n$ finite, and put
$G=V(I)$. Let $G^\circ$ denote its identity component with the reduced
subscheme structure. Viewing the Lie algebras as subalgebras of
$\mathfrak{gl}_n(\Omega)$ by means of dual-number points, we have

$$
\operatorname{Lie}(G^\circ)=\operatorname{Lie}(G).
$$

This is the closed matrix subgroup case used in the Lean proof.
