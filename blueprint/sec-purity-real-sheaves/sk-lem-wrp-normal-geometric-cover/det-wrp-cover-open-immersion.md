kind = "lemma"
title = "An étale component is open in the normalization"
order = 3785
+++
Let $X$ be a nonempty connected normal scheme of finite type over a field,
and let $Y_1\to X$ be a separated étale morphism from a nonempty connected,
hence integral and normal, scheme $Y_1$. Its generic fibre is
$\operatorname{Spec}L$ for a finite separable extension $L/k(X)$; let $N$ be
the normalization of $X$ in $L$. Then $N$ is finite over $X$, and
$Y_1\to X$ factors through an open immersion $f:Y_1\to N$.
