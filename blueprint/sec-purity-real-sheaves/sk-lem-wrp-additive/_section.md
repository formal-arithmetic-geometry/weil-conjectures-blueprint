In the situation of [sk-d-real-weil], with $U_0/k_0$ a smooth geometrically
connected curve, $P=\pi_1(U)$ and $s$ a degree-one lift, every continuous
additive homomorphism $a:P\to E$ satisfying $a(s^{-1}gs)=a(g)$ is zero.
