The stalk of $j_*\mathcal F$ is the group of sections over the
punctured spectrum of the strict henselization at $\bar s$. Finite
locally constant sheaves there correspond to finite continuous
representations of the inertia group ([sk-lem-finite]), and their sections
are the invariant vectors. Applying this to the finite reductions of a stable
lattice ([sk-p-real-integral]) and passing to the inverse limit, we obtain
the formula with integral coefficients. Rationalization commutes with taking
invariants, since a power of the uniformizer carries an invariant
rational vector into the lattice. A finite extension of scalars also
commutes with invariants, because in a finite-dimensional space an
intersection of kernels is the intersection of finitely many of them.
This gives the formula with rational coefficients, after finite
extension of scalars, and hence also over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$
([lem-wf-boundary-stalk-invariants]). All these maps commute with the action
of the decomposition group, which proves Frobenius equivariance. If
$\mathcal F$ extends to a lisse sheaf on $X_0$, the local inertia acts
trivially, and so the adjunction map is an isomorphism on every geometric
stalk ([lem-a1-lisse-middle-extension]).

*Complement.* Constructibility holds because $j_*\mathcal F$ is lisse on
$U_0$ and has finite-dimensional stalks at the finitely many missing points;
for lisse $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaves it also follows from the
stability of $\Dbc$ under $Rj_*$ ([sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability]).
