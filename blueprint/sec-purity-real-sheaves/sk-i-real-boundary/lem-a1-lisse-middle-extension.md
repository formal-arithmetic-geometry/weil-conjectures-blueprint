If $\mathcal F$ is lisse on a smooth curve $C_0$ and
$j:U_0\hookrightarrow C_0$ is dense, then the adjunction map

$$
\mathcal F\xrightarrow{\sim}j_*j^*\mathcal F
$$

is an isomorphism.
