If $\mathcal G$ is $\iota$-real ([sk-def-wrp-real-definition]) and $k\ge1$,
every local inverse Euler factor of $\mathcal G^{\otimes 2k}$ has nonnegative
real coefficients after applying $\iota$, and constant coefficient one.
For a coefficientwise locally finite product

$$
 L(t)=\prod_j f_j(t),\qquad f_j\in1+t\mathbf R_{\ge0}[[t]],
$$

if $L$ is the Taylor series of a function holomorphic on $|t|<R$, every
$f_j$ has convergence radius at least $R$. If $f_j=P_j^{-1}$ with
$P_j(0)=1$, its denominator has no zero in that disc.

*Complement.* Radii of formal series and locally finite products are taken in
the sense of [d-real-series].
