For a Weil representation $\rho:W(C_0)\to\mathrm{GL}(V)$ over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ with
$\gamma$ of degree one, the degree sequence makes $(W(C_0),\deg,\gamma,\rho)$ a
represented degree datum with $P=\pi_1(C)$, and its monodromy data
$H=\overline{\rho(\pi_1(C))}$, $G=\overline{\rho(W(C_0))}$, $F=\rho(\gamma)$ are
those of [d-monodromy].
