A smooth geometrically connected curve $U_0/k_0$ has a smooth proper
geometrically connected compactification $j_0:U_0\hookrightarrow C_0$.
The reduced boundary $D_0$ is finite étale over $k_0$.

*Complement.* The curve $C_0$ is projective
([lem-wf-curve-normal-smooth-compactification]).
