Let $U_0$ be a smooth geometrically connected affine curve and let
$\mathcal F$ be a lisse Weil sheaf
on $U_0$. Then, in the
compactly supported
cohomology of [def-weil-ordinary-compact-cohomology],
$H_c^0(U,\mathcal F)=0$.
