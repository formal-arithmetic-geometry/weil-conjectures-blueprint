Let $X$ be a nonempty connected normal scheme of finite type over a
field, and fix a geometric point $\bar x$. Taking the fibre at $\bar x$
gives an equivalence between lisse $\QbarX$-modules and
finite-dimensional continuous $\Qlb$-representations of the profinite
group $\pi_1(X,\bar x)$.
