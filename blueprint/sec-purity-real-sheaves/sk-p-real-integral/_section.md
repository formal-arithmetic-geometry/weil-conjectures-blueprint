*Proposition (sketch).*

Keep the notation of [sk-i-real-sheaves], and let $U_0\subseteq C_0$ be a
nonempty open subscheme with base change $U$ and geometric fundamental group
$\pi_1(U)$, acting through the restriction map $W(U_0)\to W(C_0)$ of Weil
groups. An $\mathcal O_E$-lattice in a $\Qlb$-vector space $V$ means a free
$\mathcal O_E$-submodule of $V$ of rank $\dim_{\Qlb}V$ that spans $V$ over
$\Qlb$.

Let $(V,\rho)$ be a lisse Weil sheaf on $C_0$, i.e. a lisse representation
of $W(C_0)$ on a finite-dimensional $\Qlb$-vector space $V$. Then there are
a finite extension $E/\Ql$ inside $\Qlb$ and an $\mathcal O_E$-lattice
$L\subset V$ with

$$
\rho(\pi_1(U))L\subseteq L,
$$

such that the image of $\pi_1(U)$ in $\operatorname{Aut}(L/\mathfrak m_E^nL)$ is
finite for every $n$.

## Proof

The restriction map $W(U_0)\to W(C_0)$ is continuous and preserves degrees,
so it carries $\pi_1(U)$ continuously into $P$. Fix a basis $b_1,\dots,b_n$
of $V$ and write $\sigma(g)\in\GL_n(\Qlb)$ for the matrix of $\rho(g)$,
$g\in\pi_1(U)$. Since $\rho$ is lisse, the coefficients of $\sigma$ are
continuous functions on the profinite, hence compact Hausdorff, group
$\pi_1(U)$.

*The coefficients lie in a finite extension.* There are only finitely many
extensions of $\Ql$ of each degree inside $\Qlb$, so $\Qlb$ is the union of
an increasing sequence $E_1\subseteq E_2\subseteq\cdots$ of finite
extensions of $\Ql$, each complete and hence closed in $\Qlb$. The sets
$H_m=\sigma^{-1}(\GL_n(E_m))$ are closed subgroups of $\pi_1(U)$ whose union
is $\pi_1(U)$. By the Baire category theorem some $H_m$ has nonempty
interior; being a subgroup, it is then open, hence of finite index. Let
$E$ be the finite extension of $E_m$ generated by the coefficients of
$\sigma(g_1),\dots,\sigma(g_r)$ for coset representatives $g_i$ of $H_m$.
Then $\sigma(g)\in\GL_n(E)$ for every $g\in\pi_1(U)$.

*A stable lattice.* Each coefficient of $\sigma$ is a continuous function
on a compact space, hence bounded, so there is $a\ge0$ with
$\ell^a\sigma(g)$ integral over $\mathcal O_E$ for all $g$. Let
$L_0=\bigoplus_i\mathcal O_Eb_i$ and let $L$ be the $\mathcal O_E$-span of
$\bigcup_{g\in\pi_1(U)}\rho(g)L_0$. Then $L_0\subseteq L\subseteq\ell^{-a}L_0$,
so $L$ is a finitely generated torsion-free, hence free, module over the
discrete valuation ring $\mathcal O_E$, of rank $n=\dim_{\Qlb}V$, and it
spans $V$ over $\Qlb$ because it contains the basis $b_i$. It is stable
under $\rho(\pi_1(U))$ because $\rho(h)\rho(g)L_0=\rho(hg)L_0$.

*Finite images.* Since $L$ is stable, each $g\in\pi_1(U)$ induces a map of
the finite set $L/\mathfrak m_E^nL$ to itself; there are only finitely many
such maps, so the image of $\pi_1(U)$ in
$\operatorname{Aut}(L/\mathfrak m_E^nL)$ is finite for every $n$.
