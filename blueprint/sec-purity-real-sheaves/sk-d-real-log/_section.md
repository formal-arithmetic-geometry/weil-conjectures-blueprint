For a finite coefficient model $E/\Ql$, normalize
$v_\ell(\ell)=1$, put $U_E=1+\ell^2\mathcal O_E$, and define

$$
\log(1+x)=\sum_{n\ge1}(-1)^{n+1}x^n/n\qquad
(x\in\ell^2\mathcal O_E).
$$
