Let $A\to B$ be integral, let
$\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak p'$ be primes of $A$, and let
$\mathfrak q\subset B$ lie over $\mathfrak p$. There is a prime
$\mathfrak q'\supseteq\mathfrak q$ lying over $\mathfrak p'$.
