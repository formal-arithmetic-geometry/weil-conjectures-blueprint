For a smooth affine curve $U_0/k_0$ there is a constant $D$ such that
$\#U_0(k_n)\le Dq^n$ for all $n\ge1$. For a finite $k_0$-scheme the
number of $k_n$-points is bounded independently of $n$.

*Complement.* The input is one-dimensional Noether normalization: a nonzero
finitely generated algebra of Krull dimension one over a field $K$ is finite
over a polynomial subring $K[T]$ ([det-i-real-normalization]).
