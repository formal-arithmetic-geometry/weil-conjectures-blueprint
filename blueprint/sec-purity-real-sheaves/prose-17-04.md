Let $j$ be the inclusion of a dense open in a smooth curve. For a
lisse sheaf $\mathcal F$ on the open, the ordinary direct image
$j_*\mathcal F$ is called the *middle extension* of $\mathcal F$.
We show in Proposition [sk-p-real-semi] that if $\mathcal F$ is
punctually of weights $\le a$, then so is $j_*\mathcal F$, also at the
points of the complement (the boundary points).
In Section “Weights of the cohomology of curves” we use it to bound the weights of
the boundary terms in the Fourier transform of a middle extension.
