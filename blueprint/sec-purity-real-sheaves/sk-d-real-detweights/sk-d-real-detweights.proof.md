The determinant is a rank-one lisse Weil sheaf defined over a finite
extension of $E$, so [sk-p-real-rankone] and [sk-cor-wrp-rankone-pure] apply
to it ([lem-wf-determinantal-weight-exists]). A finite extension of
scalars changes neither its character nor its absolute value under the
fixed embedding ([lem-wf-determinantal-weight-coefficient-field]). The
formula for the weight of $\det\mathcal V$ is the rank-one formula, and
dividing by $r$ gives $\operatorname{dw}_\iota(\mathcal V)$
([def-wf-determinantal-weight]). Finally, a finite-dimensional
representation has a composition series, and each factor is again
lisse and defined over a finite extension of $\Ql$, because after
enlarging $E$ the invariant subspaces and quotients have bases defined
over $E$. Hence the same argument applies to all the constituents in
the statement.
