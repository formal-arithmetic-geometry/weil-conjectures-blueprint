The positivity we want is $t^{2m}\ge0$ for the traces $t$ of all powers
$\operatorname{Frob}_x^n$ on $\mathcal G$, and this needs all these
traces to be real. A condition on $\operatorname{Frob}_x$ alone is not
enough. If the eigenvalues at $x$ are $i$ and $1-i$, the trace is $1$,
but the trace of the square is $-1+(1-i)^2=-1-2i$. Requiring all the
power sums to be real amounts, by the Newton identities, to requiring
the characteristic polynomial to be real, and this is the condition
below. It is stable under the operations the argument uses: tensor and
exterior powers, duals, restriction to an open, and extension by zero.
The basic examples are the constant sheaf, its Tate twists and Kummer
sheaves of quadratic characters, and Proposition [sk-prop-wrp-real-envelope]
makes every pure sheaf a summand of a real one after a twist. The
condition is used in Lemma [sk-lem-wrp-positive-euler] and through it in
Theorem [sk-p-real-purity].
