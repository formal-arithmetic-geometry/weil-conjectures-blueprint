To compute determinant weights, we construct from the monodromy groups
a central element of positive degree
(Proposition [sk-p-central]). It acts by a scalar
on each simple factor, and this scalar determines the determinant
weight of the factor. We construct the element for semisimple
representations, and then use stable filtrations to obtain the
formulas for arbitrary representations.
