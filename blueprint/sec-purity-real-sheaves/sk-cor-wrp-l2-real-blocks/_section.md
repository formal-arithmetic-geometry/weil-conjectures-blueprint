For a nonzero real lisse sheaf on a smooth geometrically connected
affine curve, $\nu$ is the largest determinant weight of its
constituents. If a nonzero pure lisse sheaf of weight $w$ is extended
by zero, or by the direct image $j_*$ of sheaves, across finitely many
points of a smooth affine curve, its $L^2$ growth parameter is still
$w$.
