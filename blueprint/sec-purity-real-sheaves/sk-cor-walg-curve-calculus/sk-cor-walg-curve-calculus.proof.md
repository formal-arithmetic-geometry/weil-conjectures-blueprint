The simple factors of the given sheaves and of their tensor or
exterior constructions are lisse Weil sheaves on the same curve.
By [sk-d-real-detweights] their determinants have finite
geometric image, so both propositions apply. Iterating the tensor
formula, we obtain the assertion for tensor powers
([cor-mg-det-weight-finite-tensor]); the exterior formula is
[cor-mg-det-weight-exterior]. For duals, note that
$\det(F\mid A^\vee)=\det(F\mid A)^{-1}$, and that duality carries
a composition series to one whose simple factors are the duals.
