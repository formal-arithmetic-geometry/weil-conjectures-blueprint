For lisse Weil sheaves on a smooth geometrically connected curve, the
tensor and exterior formulas [sk-p-tensor] and [sk-p-exterior] hold without
further hypotheses on determinants. The determinant weights of the simple
factors of a tensor power are sums of determinant weights of the simple
factors of the given sheaf, with repetition allowed.
