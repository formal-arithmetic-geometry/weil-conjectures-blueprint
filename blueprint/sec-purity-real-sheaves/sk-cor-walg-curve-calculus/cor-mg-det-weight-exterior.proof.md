The factors $A_a$ are those of the chosen series of
[def-mg-weil-composition-series], counted as in
[def-mg-determinantal-weight-multiset], and $\gamma_a$ is their
determinantal weight in the sense of [def-wf-determinantal-weight]. By [lem-rp-determinant-weights-agree], every
$A_a$ and every Jordan–Holder constituent of
$\bigwedge^r\mathcal F_0$ has finite geometric determinant, and for
each of them $\dw_\iota$ equals $\dw_{\lambda_\iota}$. Hence
[prop-ag-det-weight-exterior], applied with $\lambda=\lambda_\iota$ to the Weil
representation of $\mathcal F_0$, a Weil representation of the degree
datum $(W(X_0),\deg,s)$ of [def-wf-geometric-monodromy-group], gives the
assertion and its converse.
