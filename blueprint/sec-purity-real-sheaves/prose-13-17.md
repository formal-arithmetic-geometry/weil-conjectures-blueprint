Part “Weights and the Riemann hypothesis” proves Weil's axiom (d), purity, and with it the
Riemann hypothesis and the independence of $\ell$. The argument is
Deligne's theorem on weights in Laumon's form: $Rf_!$ does not increase
weights. Section “Weights: definitions and the main theorem” states the theorem and explains why it
holds; the following sections prove it on curves, by Rankin's positivity
argument and the Fourier transform, and then in general by fibring in
curves.
