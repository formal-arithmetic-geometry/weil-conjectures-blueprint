Rankin's argument bounds the local eigenvalues of a real sheaf by the
weights of $H^2_c$ of its tensor powers, and $H^2_c$ only sees the
simple factors on which geometric monodromy acts trivially. To use
the bound we must therefore know the weights of the simple factors of
tensor and exterior powers. The main result of this section,
Proposition [sk-prop-walg-determinant-weights], expresses the determinant
weights of these factors in terms of those of the factors of the original
representation.

The facts on algebraic groups that we use, namely the relation between
closed subgroups, their identity components and their Lie algebras, and
the structure of a Lie algebra with a faithful semisimple
representation, are proved in Appendix “Algebraic groups and Lie algebras in characteristic zero”.

We need two elementary facts. Semisimplicity is preserved by
restriction to a normal subgroup, which lets us apply Lie theory to the
identity component of the monodromy group below. The composition
factors of tensor and exterior powers are computed from those of the
given representations, which reduces the formulas to semisimple
representations.
