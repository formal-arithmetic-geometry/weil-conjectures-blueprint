Let $X$ be of finite type over $k$ and let $F$ be a
constructible
$\Lambda$-sheaf on $X_{{\mathrm{et}}}$. Then there is an open
subscheme $U\hookrightarrow X$ with
$\dim(X\setminus U)<\dim X$ on which the restriction $F|_U$ is
lisse.
