If $\mathcal G_0$ is an $\iota$-real lisse Weil sheaf, then
$\bigwedge^r\mathcal G_0$ (the exterior power of
[def-real-weil-representation-operations]) is $\iota$-real for every
$0\le r\le\rank(\mathcal G_0)$.
