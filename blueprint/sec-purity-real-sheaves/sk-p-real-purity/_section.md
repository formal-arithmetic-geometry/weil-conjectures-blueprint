Let $C_0/\Fq$ be a smooth geometrically connected
curve and $\mathcal F$ an $\iota$-real
lisse Weil sheaf.
Every irreducible constituent $V$ of
$\mathcal F$ is punctually $\iota$-pure of weight
$\dw_\iota(V)$.
