Let $U_0/\mathbf F_q$ be a smooth geometrically connected curve, let
$\mathcal F$ be a lisse Weil sheaf, and let $V$ be its geometric generic
fibre, with geometric fundamental group $P$ ([sk-d-real-weil]). There are
canonical Frobenius-equivariant identifications

$$
 H^0(U,\mathcal F)=V^P,\qquad
 H_c^2(U,\mathcal F)=V_P(-1).
$$

There is no compactly supported cohomology outside degrees $0,1,2$.
If $U_0$ is affine, $H_c^0(U,\mathcal F)=0$.
If $\mathcal F$ has a punctual bound at most $a$ ([sk-d-real-weights]), the
weights of $H^0$ are at most $a$ and those of $H_c^2$ at most $a+2$. If
$\mathcal F$ is pure of weight $a$, the two groups, when nonzero, are pure
of weights $a$ and $a+2$, respectively.

*Complement.* The identification $H^0(U,\mathcal F|_U)=V^{\pi_1(U)}$ holds for
every nonempty open $U_0$ of a curve, compatibly with the residual action of
$F=\rho(s)$ ([p-real-sections]).
