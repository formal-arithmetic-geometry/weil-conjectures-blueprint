Apply [sk-p-real-semi] to the sheaf and to its dual, which is pure of
weight $-a$ ([sk-r-real-linear]). Since the inertia acts trivially,
the stalk of the extended dual is the dual of the stalk of the
extension. Thus every eigenvalue $\alpha$ satisfies
$|\iota\alpha|^2\le q_s^a$ and $|\iota\alpha^{-1}|^2\le q_s^{-a}$, and
hence equality holds. For a sheaf that is already lisse on the curve,
the inertia is trivial, and [sk-i-real-boundary] identifies
the stalk of $j_*$ with the original stalk. It remains to apply the
first assertion at each of the finitely many missing points
([cor-wf-unramified-boundary-purity]).
