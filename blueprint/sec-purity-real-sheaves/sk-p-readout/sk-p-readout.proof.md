The determinant of $Q$ is an algebraic character of $H$. Its image on the
dense subgroup $\rho(\pi_1(U))$ is finite, and hence its image on all of
$H$ lies in the same finite group of roots of unity. Taking determinants in
$z=h\rho(s)^N$, we find

$$
c_Q^{\dim Q}=\det(h\mid Q)\det(s\mid Q)^N.
$$

Now apply $\lambda_\iota$, which vanishes on the first factor, and divide
by $N\dim Q$. The same computation proves the complement, since a real
weight character kills elements of finite order: if $a^e=1$ with $e>0$, then $e\lambda(a)=\lambda(a^e)=0$.
