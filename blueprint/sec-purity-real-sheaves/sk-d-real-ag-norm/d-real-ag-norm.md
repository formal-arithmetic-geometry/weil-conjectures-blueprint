For the monodromy group $G$, choose representatives
$g_1,\ldots,g_r$ of the left cosets of $G^\circ$ in $G$
and $c\in Z(G^\circ)$, define

$$
\mathcal N_G(c)=\prod_{i=1}^r g_i c g_i^{-1}
$$

in the displayed order. If also $c\in HF^m$ with $m\ge1$, set
$N=mr$ and $z=\mathcal N_G(c)$.
