The series $\varphi_K(t)=\sum_{n\ge1}S_n(K)t^{n-1}$ of
[sk-def-wrp-l2-definition] has radius $q^{-\nu(K)-1}$, with the usual
conventions at infinity. If a lisse sheaf has a punctual bound at most $a$
([sk-d-real-weights]), then $\nu\le a$. A complex supported at finitely many
points, all of whose stalk eigenvalues have modulus at most $q_x^{a/2}$,
satisfies $S_n\le Cq^{na}$ and $\nu\le a-1$. Tensoring by $\mathcal B_b$ adds
$\lambda_\iota(b)$ to $\nu$.

If $t_{K,n}-t_{L,n}$ is supported at finitely many points and its energy
is at most $Cq^{na}$, then

$$
 \max(\nu(K),\nu(L))>a-1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\nu(K)=\nu(L).
$$
