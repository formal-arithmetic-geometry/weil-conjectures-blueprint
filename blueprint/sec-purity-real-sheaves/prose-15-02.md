We now deduce Theorem [sk-p-real-rankone]. A logarithm turns a
power of a rank-one character of the geometric fundamental group
$\pi_1(U)$ into an additive character that
is invariant under Frobenius, and by
Proposition [sk-prop-wrp-h1-no-fixed] such a character vanishes. In this
subsection $U_0/k_0$ is a smooth geometrically connected curve, and
$s\in W(U_0)$ is a lift of degree one, as in
Convention [sk-d-real-weil].
