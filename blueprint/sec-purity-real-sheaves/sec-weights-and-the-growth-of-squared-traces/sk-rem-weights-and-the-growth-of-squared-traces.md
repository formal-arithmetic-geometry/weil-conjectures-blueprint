1. A cohomological shift multiplies the trace function by a sign,
   and hence leaves $\nu(K)$ unchanged.
2. On $\mathbf A^1$, the sheaf $\mathcal B_b$ with $|\iota(b)|=1$
   has trace of absolute value one at every point, hence $S_n=q^n$ and
   $\nu=0$, which is its weight. For $b=1$ this is the constant sheaf.
