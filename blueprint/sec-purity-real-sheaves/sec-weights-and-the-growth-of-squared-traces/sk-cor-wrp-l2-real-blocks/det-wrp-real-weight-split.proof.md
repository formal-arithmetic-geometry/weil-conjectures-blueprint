By [thm-rp-real-lisse-purity-affine] every irreducible summand of
determinant weight $\gamma_i$ is punctually pure of weight $\gamma_i$, hence
so is $\mathcal G_i$. At a closed point $x$, the eigenvalues of
$\mathcal G_i$ are exactly the eigenvalues of $\mathcal G$ of absolute value
$q_x^{\gamma_i/2}$ after applying $\iota$, counted with multiplicity, since
the $\gamma_i$ are distinct. Complex conjugation preserves the multiset of
eigenvalues of $\mathcal G$ ([def-wf-iota-real]) and preserves absolute
values, so it preserves the multiset of each $\mathcal G_i$, which is
therefore $\iota$-real.
