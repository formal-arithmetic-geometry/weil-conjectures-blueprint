Choose a closed point $x$, of degree $d$, at which the stalk is
nonzero, and a Frobenius eigenvalue $\alpha$ at $x$. Let $b\in \overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$
be a $d$th root of $\alpha^{-1}$. By purity,
$|\iota(b)|=|\iota(\alpha)|^{-1/d}=q^{-w/2}$, and hence $\mathcal F^0$
is pure of weight zero by Proposition [sk-r-real-linear]. Its local
eigenvalues $\beta_j$ have absolute value one after applying $\iota$,
the local eigenvalues of its dual are the $\beta_j^{-1}$, and
$\iota(\beta_j^{-1})=\overline{\iota(\beta_j)}$. Thus the union of the
two multisets is stable under complex conjugation. This proves that
$\mathcal E$ is real and pure. Moreover, at a point $x$ over $k_n$ the
eigenvalues of $\operatorname{Frob}_{x,n}$ on $(\mathcal F^0)^\vee$ are
the inverses of those on $\mathcal F^0$, hence after $\iota$ their
complex conjugates. So $\iota$ of the trace of $(\mathcal F^0)^\vee$
is the complex conjugate of $\iota$ of the trace of $\mathcal F^0$,
and the trace of $\mathcal E$ is twice its real part.
