By (1), a shift changes the weight of a complex in the sense of
Definition [sk-d-real-weights] but not $\nu$. Moreover, $\nu$ does not
determine the sheaf: by (2), the sheaves $\mathcal B_b$ on
$\mathbf A^1$ with $|\iota(b)|=1$ are pairwise non-isomorphic for
distinct $b$, but all have $\nu=0$.

To compute $\nu$ we need upper bounds for lisse sheaves and for
complexes with finite support, the effect of twisting by
$\mathcal B_b$, and the fact that a correction at finitely many points
of small weight does not change $\nu$. The last fact is how the
boundary terms are discarded in Corollary [sk-cor-wrp-l2-real-blocks].
