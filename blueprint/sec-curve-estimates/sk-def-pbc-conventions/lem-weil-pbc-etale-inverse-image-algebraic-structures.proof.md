**Step 1.** *Continuity of base change and the direct image on sheaves of sets.* Let $b:V_{\Et}\to U_{\Et}$, $W\mapsto U\times_VW$, be the base-change functor; it is a functor, $V_{\Et}$ has finite limits (final object $\mathrm{id}_V$, fibre products the scheme fibre products) and $b$ commutes with them: $b(V)=U\times_VV\cong U$ is final in $U_{\Et}$ and $U\times_V(W_1\times_WW_2)\to(U\times_VW_1)\times_{U\times_VW}(U\times_VW_2)$ is an isomorphism [lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-finite-limits] (clauses 1-3). By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-continuous] $b$ sends etale coverings $\{W_i\to W\}$ to etale coverings $\{U\times_VW_i\to U\times_VW\}$, is continuous, and for every sheaf of sets $\mathcal F$ on $U_{\Et}$ the presheaf $u_*\mathcal F=\mathcal F\circ b$ is a sheaf on $V_{\Et}$. So $u_*:\Sh(U_{\Et})\to\Sh(V_{\Et})$ is a functor ($u_*\varphi=\varphi\circ b$ on morphisms), and $\iota_Vu_*=b^p\iota_U$ where $\iota_T:\Sh(T_{\Et})\to\mathrm{PSh}(T_{\Et})$ is the inclusion of sheaves of sets into presheaves of sets and $b^p\mathcal P=\mathcal P\circ b$; the same formula defines $u_*$ on sheaves of abelian groups and of commutative rings (sectionwise structures of $\mathcal F(U\times_VW)$), whose sheaf condition is that of the underlying presheaf of sets.

**Step 2.** *(1) The adjunction on sheaves of sets.* Let $b_p:\mathrm{PSh}(V_{\Et})\to\mathrm{PSh}(U_{\Et})$ be the presheaf pullback of presheaves of sets along $b$: $b_p\mathcal P(U')=\varinjlim\mathcal P(W)$, the colimit over $\mathcal I_{U'}^{\mathrm{op}}$, $\mathcal I_{U'}$ the category of pairs $(W/V,\ \phi:U'\to U\times_VW$ over $U)$; it is left adjoint to $b^p$ [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-pullback-sets-left-exact] (clause 1). Let $\#$ be the sheafification of presheaves of sets on $U_{\Et}$, left adjoint to $\iota_U$ [def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. By definition $u^{-1}\mathcal G=(b_p\iota_V\mathcal G)^\#$, a functor $\Sh(V_{\Et})\to\Sh(U_{\Et})$ as a composite of functors. For $\mathcal G\in\Sh(V_{\Et})$ and $\mathcal F\in\Sh(U_{\Et})$ we have, naturally in both, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\Sh(U_{\Et})}((b_p\iota_V\mathcal G)^\#,\mathcal F)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{PSh}(U_{\Et})}(b_p\iota_V\mathcal G,\iota_U\mathcal F)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{PSh}(V_{\Et})}(\iota_V\mathcal G,b^p\iota_U\mathcal F)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\Sh(V_{\Et})}(\mathcal G,u_*\mathcal F)$, the last equality because $\iota_V$ is fully faithful and $b^p\iota_U\mathcal F=\iota_Vu_*\mathcal F$ (step 1). Hence $u^{-1}\dashv u_*$ on sheaves of sets; the unit $\eta_{\mathcal G}:\mathcal G\to u_*u^{-1}\mathcal G$ is, at $W\in V_{\Et}$, the composite of the coprojection $\mathcal G(W)\to b_p\mathcal G(U\times_VW)$ at the pair $(W,\mathrm{id}_{U\times_VW})$ (the unit of $b_p\dashv b^p$) with the sheafification map $b_p\mathcal G(U\times_VW)\to(b_p\mathcal G)^\#(U\times_VW)$.

**Step 3.** *(1) Commuting with finite limits.* By step 1 the category $V_{\Et}$ has a final object $V$ with $b(V)$ final in $U_{\Et}$, has fibre products, and $b$ commutes with them [lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-finite-limits] (clauses 2, 3). Hence by [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-pullback-sets-left-exact] (clause 2) every index category $\mathcal I_{U'}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is filtered (nonempty: $U'\to U=b(V)$; connected: two pairs $(W_1,\phi_1)$, $(W_2,\phi_2)$ both receive a map from $(W_1\times_VW_2,(\phi_1,\phi_2))$ since $b(W_1\times_VW_2)=b(W_1)\times_Ub(W_2)$; equalizers through fibre products) and $b_p$ commutes with finite limits, filtered colimits of sets commuting with finite limits and finite limits of presheaves being sectionwise. The inclusion $\iota_V$ commutes with finite limits [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise] (clause 2, with values in sets), and the sheafification $\#$ of presheaves of sets on $U_{\Et}$ commutes with finite limits [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-sets-exact]. A composite of functors commuting with finite limits commutes with finite limits, so $u^{-1}=\#\circ b_p\circ\iota_V$ does; in particular $u^{-1}(*)$ is a final sheaf, i.e. $u^{-1}(*)=*$, and the map $u^{-1}(\mathcal G_1\times\mathcal G_2)\to u^{-1}\mathcal G_1\times u^{-1}\mathcal G_2$ induced by $u^{-1}$ of the projections is an isomorphism. (The sites are taken, as in the source, with set-sized underlying categories, Stacks, Topologies, Definition 021B; the lemma on $b_p$ needs this smallness.) Being a left adjoint, $u^{-1}$ also commutes with all colimits, so $u^{-1}$ is exact and $b$ defines a morphism of sites $U_{\Et}\to V_{\Et}$ with $f^{-1}=u^{-1}$, $f_*=u_*$.

**Step 4.** *(2) Sheaves of abelian groups and of commutative rings.* By [lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-algebraic-structures-objects], applied on $V_{\Et}$ and on $U_{\Et}$, sheaves of abelian groups (resp. commutative rings) are, compatibly with the forgetful functors, the abelian group objects $(\mathcal F,+,0,i)$ (resp. commutative ring objects $(\mathcal F,+,0,i,\cdot,1)$) of the categories of sheaves of sets, and morphisms are the morphisms of sheaves of sets commuting with the structure maps. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-lifts-abelian-group-objects] to $L=u^{-1}:\Sh(V_{\Et})\to\Sh(U_{\Et})$, $R=u_*$, which is legitimate by step 2 ($u^{-1}\dashv u_*$), step 3 ($u^{-1}$ preserves the final object and binary products) and [lem-weil-pbc-right-adjoint-preserves-limits] ($u_*$ preserves them). For commutative ring objects the same transport works: the ring axioms are equalities between morphisms out of finite products built from the structure maps and the projections, and $u^{-1}$ carries them to the same equalities for the transported structure maps since it preserves finite products; a morphism $lpha:u^{-1}\mathcal G\to\mathcal F$ respects $\cdot$ and $1$ iff its adjoint does, by naturality of the unit and counit at $\cdot$ and $1$ and the triangle identities. It gives: for a sheaf of abelian groups (rings) $\mathcal G$ on $V_{\Et}$ the sheaf of sets $u^{-1}\mathcal G$ with structure maps $u^{-1}(+)\circ\lambda^{-1}$, $u^{-1}(0)$, $u^{-1}(i)$ (and $u^{-1}(\cdot)\circ\lambda^{-1}$, $u^{-1}(1)$), $\lambda$ the comparison isomorphism of step 3, is an abelian group (commutative ring) object, i.e. a sheaf of abelian groups (rings) on $U_{\Et}$; this is a functor commuting with the forgetful functors; it is left adjoint to the lift of $u_*$, the adjunction bijection being the restriction of that of step 2 (a map $u^{-1}\mathcal G\to\mathcal F$ respects the structures iff its adjoint does), and the unit and counit are those of step 2. Finally the lift of $u_*$ is the direct image $u_*$ on sheaves of abelian groups (rings) of step 1: the comparison $u_*(\mathcal F\times\mathcal F)\cong u_*\mathcal F\times u_*\mathcal F$ is the identity of $W\mapsto\mathcal F(U\times_VW)\times\mathcal F(U\times_VW)$, so the transported structure maps $u_*(+)$, $u_*(0)$, $u_*(i)$ (and $u_*(\cdot)$, $u_*(1)$) are, over $W$, the operations of the group (ring) $\mathcal F(U\times_VW)$; that is, the sectionwise structure of $(u_*\mathcal F)(W)=\mathcal F(U\times_VW)$ of [def-weil-pbc-direct-image].

**Step 5.** *(3) Agreement with the abelian inverse image.* By [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] the inverse image $u^{-1}_{\mathrm{ab}}:\Ab(V_{\Et})\to\Ab(U_{\Et})$ between the categories of abelian sheaves [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves] is a left adjoint of the direct image $u_*:\Ab(U_{\Et})\to\Ab(V_{\Et})$ of [def-weil-pbc-direct-image], with unit $\eta_u$. By step 4 the functor $u^{-1}$ of (2) on sheaves of abelian groups is a left adjoint of the same functor $u_*$ (same objects $W\mapsto F(U\times_VW)$ with sectionwise structures, same action on morphisms), with unit $\eta$, the unit of (1) on underlying sheaves of sets. By [lem-weil-pbc-conjugate-iso-unit-equation] (case $R_1=R_2=u_*$, $\delta=\mathrm{id}$) there is a unique natural isomorphism $c:u^{-1}\xrightarrow{\sim}u^{-1}_{\mathrm{ab}}$ of functors $\Ab(V_{\Et})\to\Ab(U_{\Et})$ with $u_*(c_G)\circ\eta_G=\eta_{u,G}$ for every $G$; consequently the two adjunction bijections correspond under $c$: for $\alpha:u^{-1}_{\mathrm{ab}}G\to F$ the adjoint $u_*(\alpha)\circ\eta_{u,G}$ equals $u_*(\alpha\circ c_G)\circ\eta_G$, the adjoint of $\alpha\circ c_G$. Applying the forgetful functor to sheaves of sets [lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-algebraic-structures-objects], $c_G$ is an isomorphism of sheaves of sets from the set-theoretic inverse image $u^{-1}$ of the underlying sheaf of sets of $G$ (step 4) to the underlying sheaf of sets of $u^{-1}_{\mathrm{ab}}G$, and through it $\eta_u$ is, on underlying sheaves of sets, the unit of (1).
