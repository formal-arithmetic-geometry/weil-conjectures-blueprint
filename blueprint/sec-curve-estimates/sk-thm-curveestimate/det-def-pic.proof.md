(P1): the identification of $H^1(C_{\Et},\mathbb G_m)$ with the group of isomorphism
classes of invertible modules is [lem-sc-h1-gm-pic], and its factorization through
$H^1(C_{\mathrm{Zar}},\mathcal O_C^\times)$ is [thm-sc-hilbert-90]. For $C$ integral and
regular, hence smooth over the algebraically closed (so perfect) field $k$, the
identification with $\mathrm{Div}(C)/\mathrm{Princ}(C)$ is
[lem-sc-divisor-class-curve].

(P2): over the algebraically closed $k$ every closed point has $[\kappa(p):k]=1$. For $C$
smooth, proper and connected, the degree of [def-sc-pic0] satisfies
$\deg[\mathcal O(D)]=\deg D$, kills principal divisors and is a homomorphism
$\Pic(C)\to\Z$ ([def-sc-pic0]); it is surjective by [lem-sc-pic-proper-decomposition].
