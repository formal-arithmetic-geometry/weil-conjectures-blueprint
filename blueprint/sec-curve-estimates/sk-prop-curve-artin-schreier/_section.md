Let $C$ be a smooth proper connected curve of genus $g$ over an algebraically
closed field of characteristic $p>0$. Then

$$
H^0(C,\mathbf F_p)=\mathbf F_p,\qquad
H^1(C,\mathbf F_p)=
\ker\bigl(F-1:H^1(C,\cO_C)\to H^1(C,\cO_C)\bigr),
$$

and $H^q(C,\mathbf F_p)=0$ for $q>1$. The group $H^1(C,\mathbf F_p)$ is
finite, of $\mathbf F_p$-dimension at most $g$, and its pullback map is an
isomorphism under algebraically closed extension.
