Let $C$ be a curve over a field $k$ of
characteristic $p>0$. The sequence of etale sheaves on
$C_{\Et}$

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbf F_p\longrightarrow\mathbf G_a
\xrightarrow{\ \wp\ }\mathbf G_a\longrightarrow0,
\qquad \wp(x)=x^p-x,
$$

is exact.
