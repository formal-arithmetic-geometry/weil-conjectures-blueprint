Let $v:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ be an additive functor between abelian categories, $\mathcal A$ having enough injectives (for example the left exact functor $u_*:\Ab(U_{\Et})\to\Ab(V_{\Et})$ for a morphism of schemes $u:U\to V$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image], $\Ab(U_{\Et})$ being a Grothendieck abelian category). Every $K\in D^+(\mathcal A)$ is isomorphic to a bounded below complex of injectives $I(K)$, unique up to homotopy equivalence (such a complex is K-injective [def-sp-kinjective-bridge], and bounded below complexes of injectives up to homotopy form a category equivalent to $D^+(\mathcal A)$); set $RvK=v(I(K))\in D^+(\mathcal B)$, on morphisms through the homotopy classes of the corresponding maps of complexes of injectives. Let $\sigma_K:Rv(K[1 ])\xrightarrow{\sim}(RvK)[1 ]$ be the isomorphism $v(I(K[1 ]))\cong v(I(K)[1 ])=v(I(K))[1 ]$ given by the unique-up-to-homotopy comparison of the two bounded below complexes of injectives $I(K[1 ])$ and $I(K)[1 ]$ representing $K[1 ]$. Then $(Rv,\sigma)$ is a triangulated functor $D^+(\mathcal A)\to D^+(\mathcal B)$ [def-weil-triangulated-category]: $\sigma$ is natural in $K$, and for every distinguished triangle $K_1\xrightarrow{a}K_2\xrightarrow{b}K_3\xrightarrow{c}K_1[1 ]$ of $D^+(\mathcal A)$ the triangle $RvK_1\xrightarrow{Rv\,a}RvK_2\xrightarrow{Rv\,b}RvK_3\xrightarrow{\sigma_{K_1}\circ Rv\,c}(RvK_1)[1 ]$ is distinguished in $D^+(\mathcal B)$. In particular $Ru_*:\Dplus(U_{\Et})\to\Dplus(V_{\Et})$ [def-weil-pbc-derived-categories] is triangulated with this shift isomorphism.
