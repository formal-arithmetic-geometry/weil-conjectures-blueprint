A local section of $g^{-1}f_*F$ cannot be represented by a section on
$V_i\times_{Y'}Y$: the square only supplies $g:Y'\to Y$, not a map
$Y\to Y'$, so that fibre product is not defined. The categorical
unit–Cartesian-identification–counit construction above is used instead.
