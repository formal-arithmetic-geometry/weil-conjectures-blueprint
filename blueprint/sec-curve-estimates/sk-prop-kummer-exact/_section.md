Let $C$ be a scheme and $n\ge1$ invertible on $C$ (e.g. $C$ as in
[sk-def-curve] over $k$ with $n$ prime to $\operatorname{char}k$).
Then the sequence of [sk-def-kummer] is an exact sequence of
sheaves on $C_{\Et}$.
