Quasi-coherent cohomology on an affine scheme vanishes in positive degrees,
so the long exact Artin–Schreier sequence on $U_{\mathrm{ét}}$ gives both
claims. [sk-prop-sf-comparison] transports them to $U_{\mathrm{proét}}$.
