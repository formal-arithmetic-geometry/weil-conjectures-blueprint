If $U$ is affine over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p$,
then, for the classical constant sheaf $\mathbf F_p$,

$$
H^1(U,\mathbf F_p)=
\Gamma(U,\cO_U)/(F-1)\Gamma(U,\cO_U),\qquad
H^q(U,\mathbf F_p)=0\quad(q>1).
$$
