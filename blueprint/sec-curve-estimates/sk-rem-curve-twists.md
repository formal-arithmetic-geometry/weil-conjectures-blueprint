The canonical formula is $H^2(C,\mu_n)=\Z/n$, with $\mu_n=(\Z/n)(1)$.
Consequently

$$
H^2(C,\Z/n)=(\Z/n)(-1).
$$

A primitive $n$th root of unity trivializes this twist; the identifications
above are functorial without such a choice. For $C=\PP^1_k$ the Jacobian is
zero, so the cohomology with constant coefficients is concentrated in
degrees zero and two, and the Kummer class of $\cO(1)$ maps to $1$ in
$H^2(\PP^1_k,\mu_n)$. This is the normalization of the geometric fibre used
in the section on proper base change for the projective line.
