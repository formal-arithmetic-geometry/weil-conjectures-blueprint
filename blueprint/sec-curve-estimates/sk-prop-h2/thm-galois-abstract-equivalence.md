Let $(\mathcal C,F)$ be a Galois category and
$G=\operatorname{Aut}(F)$. Then $\mathcal C\to \text{finite-}G\text{-}\mathbf{Sets}$, $X\mapsto F(X)$ with the induced
$G$-action, is an equivalence of categories.
