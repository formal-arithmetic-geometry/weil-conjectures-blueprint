[sk-prop-kummer-exact] (with
$H^1(C,\mathbb G_m)=\Pic(C)$ by [sk-def-pic] and the last term
$H^2(C,\mathbb G_m)=\Br(C)=0$ by [sk-thm-br], $C$ being smooth
proper) the displayed quotient is the cokernel of
$n:\Pic(C)\to\Pic(C)$.
