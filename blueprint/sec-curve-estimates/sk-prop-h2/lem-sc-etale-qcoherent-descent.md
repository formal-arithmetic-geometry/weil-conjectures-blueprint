Let $X$ be a scheme and $\mathcal F$ a quasi-coherent
$\mathcal O_X$-module. Then the presheaf on the
small etale site of $X$ that
sends $u:U\to X$ to $\Gamma(U,u^*\mathcal F)$, with the pullback
restrictions, is a sheaf.
(*Classical input: Stacks Project, Descent, Section 35.8, Tag
[03DR](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03DR).*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Descent, Section 35.8, Tag [03DR](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03DR): quasi-coherent modules descend along the etale topology, equivalently the presheaf of sections of the pullback is a sheaf. No separate proof is given.
