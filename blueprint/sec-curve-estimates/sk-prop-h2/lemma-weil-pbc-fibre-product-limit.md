If $f:X\to S$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated
[def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions], then

$$
 X\times_SS_{(\bar s)}\xrightarrow{\sim}
 \varprojlim_{(V,\bar v)}X\times_SV.
$$
