Let $k$ be algebraically closed and let $C$ be an *affine* curve
over $k$ in the sense of [sk-def-curve] (separated, of finite type,
of dimension $\le1$). Then for *every* torsion abelian sheaf
$F$ on $C_{\Et}$

$$
H^q(C_{\Et},F)=0\qquad(q>1).
$$

Equivalently, the étale cohomological dimension of an affine curve
over an algebraically closed field is $\le1$.
