Let $C/k$ be a smooth integral affine
connected curve over algebraically closed $k$
and $\eta:\operatorname{Spec}k(C)\to C$ its
generic point.
For every torsion etale sheaf
$M$ on $\operatorname{Spec}k(C)$, one has
$H^2(C,\eta_*M)=0$, with
etale cohomology.
