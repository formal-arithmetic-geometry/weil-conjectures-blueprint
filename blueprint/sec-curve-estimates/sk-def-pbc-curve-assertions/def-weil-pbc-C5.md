For every curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting] $(C,F)$ with $C$
proper over $k$ and $F$ constructible [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion]:
for every integer $q\geq0$, $H^q(C_{\Et},F)$ is a finite abelian group.
