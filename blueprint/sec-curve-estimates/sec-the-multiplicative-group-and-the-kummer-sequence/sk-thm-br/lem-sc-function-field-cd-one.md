Let $k$ be algebraically closed, $C/k$ an integral
curve, and $L$ an algebraic extension
of its function field $k(C)$.
For every torsion abelian etale sheaf
$M$ on $\operatorname{Spec}L$, its
cohomology vanishes
in degrees $q\geq2$.
