Let $X$ be an affine scheme and $\mathcal F$ a quasi-coherent module.
Then $H^q(X_{\mathrm{et}},\mathcal F_{\mathrm{et}})=0$ for $q>0$,
with the associated sheaf of
Definition [def-sc-etale-qcoherent-module].
