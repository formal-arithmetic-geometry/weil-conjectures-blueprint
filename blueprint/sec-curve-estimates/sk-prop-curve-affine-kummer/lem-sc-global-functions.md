Let $k$ be algebraically closed and let $X$ be a
proper, reduced and connected scheme over $k$ (for instance a
proper smooth connected curve). Then the structure map
$k\to H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)$ is bijective:

$$
H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=k.
$$
