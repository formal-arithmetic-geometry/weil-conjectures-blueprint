Let $X$ be a scheme and $G$ an abelian sheaf on $X_{\Et}$.
There is a canonical bijection

$$
\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathrm{Ab}(X_{\Et})}(\mathbf Z_X,G)
\cong\{\text{isomorphism classes of }G\text{-torsors}\}.
$$
