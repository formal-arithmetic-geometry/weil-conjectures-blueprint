For a scheme $X$ (for instance $C$ as in [sk-def-curve]), the
*(cohomological) Brauer group* of $X$ is
$\Br(X)=H^2(X_{\Et},\mathbb G_m)$, where $\mathbb G_m$ is the sheaf of
units of the structure sheaf on the small étale site.
