For $C$ as in [def-sc-curves], the *Brauer group* of $C$ is
$\Br(C)=H^2(C_{\Et},\mathbb G_m)$, the cohomology of the sheaf
$\mathbb G_m$ of [def-sc-gm-torsor] on the small etale site
$C_{\Et}$ ([def-weil-pbc-etale-site]).
