Quasi-coherent cohomology on an affine scheme vanishes in positive
degrees, and hence both claims follow from the long exact sequence
associated to the Artin–Schreier sequence on $U_{\acute{e}t}$. By
Theorem [sk-prop-sf-comparison] they carry over to $U_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$.
