Let $u:X_0\to X$ be universally injective and surjective (for instance a
universal homeomorphism), and let $U$ be an etale $X$-scheme. A sieve $S$ on
$U$ in the small etale site of $X$ is a covering sieve (the etale morphisms
$U_i\to U$ in $S$ are jointly surjective) if and only if the sieve on
$U\times_XX_0$ generated by the base changes $U_i\times_XX_0\to U\times_XX_0$
is a covering sieve in the small etale site of $X_0$.
