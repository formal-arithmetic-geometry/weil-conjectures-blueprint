Let $j\colon U\hookrightarrow C$ be a dense open in a reduced curve and
$i\colon Z\hookrightarrow C$ its finite complementary closed subscheme. For a
classical constructible sheaf $F$ there is an exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow j_!j^*F\longrightarrow F\longrightarrow i_*i^*F\longrightarrow0.
$$

Consequently the assertions $(C_1)$–$(C_8)$ of the theorem on cohomology of
curves ([sk-def-pbc-curve-assertions]) for $F$ and for $j_!j^*F$ are
equivalent, each within its stated scope.
