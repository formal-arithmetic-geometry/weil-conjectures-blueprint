Let $U$ be connected and $G$ a finite classical local system of
$\mathbf F_r$-vector spaces. There is a finite étale cover $v\colon V\to U$
of degree $d$ prime to $r$ such that $v^*G$ has a finite filtration with
successive quotients the constant sheaf $\mathbf F_r$. Moreover $G$ is a
direct summand of $v_*v^*G$.
