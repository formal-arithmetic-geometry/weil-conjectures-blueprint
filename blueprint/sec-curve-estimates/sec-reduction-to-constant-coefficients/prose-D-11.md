It remains to pass from constant coefficients to local systems. A local
system of finite $\mathbf F_\ell$-vector spaces has finite monodromy, and it
becomes a successive extension of constant sheaves on the cover defined
by an $\ell$-Sylow subgroup of the monodromy group. Trivializing the
local system on the full cover of its monodromy would also make it
constant, but that cover can have degree divisible by $\ell$, and then
the local system need not be a direct summand of the pushforward of its
pullback. For the double cover $v:y^2=x$ of $\mathbf G_m$, in
characteristic different from two, and $\ell=2$, the composite of the
unit $\mathbf F_2\to v_*\mathbf F_2$ and the trace is multiplication by
$2$, which is zero. In fact $\mathbf F_2$ is not a direct summand of
$v_*\mathbf F_2$ at all, because the monodromy representation of
$v_*\mathbf F_2$ is the regular representation $\mathbf F_2[\mathbf Z/2]$,
which is indecomposable. With a cover of degree prime to $\ell$, dividing the trace by the
degree splits the unit (Lemma [sk-lem-curve-sylow-trace]), and we only
need the vanishing, finiteness and invariance statements for the pullback,
which is unipotent and so is built from constant sheaves by extensions.
