The action factors through the image $\bar H$ of $H$ in the finite group
$\operatorname{GL}(V)$, which is a finite $\ell$-group. If $V\neq0$, the
nontrivial $\bar H$-orbits on the finite set $V$ have cardinality a positive
power of $\ell$, so $\#V^H\equiv\#V\equiv0\pmod\ell$; as $0\in V^H$, this
forces $V^H\neq0$. Build the flag inductively: given $F_i$ with $i<n$, the
quotient $V/F_i$ is a nonzero representation of $H$, so it has a nonzero
invariant vector; let $F_{i+1}$ be the preimage of the line it spans. Then
$F_{i+1}$ is $H$-stable of dimension $i+1$ and $H$ acts trivially on
$F_{i+1}/F_i$.
