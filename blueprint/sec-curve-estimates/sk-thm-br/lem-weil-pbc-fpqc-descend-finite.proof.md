Finiteness is Zariski local on the target, stable under base change, and descends along surjective flat quasi-compact morphisms (Stacks, Descent, Lemma 02LA: a morphism is finite if and only if it is affine, universally closed and locally of finite type, and each of these three properties descends). Hence the fpqc family descent lemma [lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-family-descent-of-single-morphism-descent], applied to the covering sieve generated by $\{g_i\}$ and the cartesian squares formed by the fibre products $X\times_SS_i$, shows that $f$ is finite.
