A morphism of schemes $f:X\to Y$ whose geometric fibres
$X\times_Y\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$ (over all geometric points
$\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$, $\Omega$ algebraically closed) are
all nonempty is surjective.
