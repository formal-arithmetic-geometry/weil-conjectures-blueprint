A scheme $C$ over a field $k$ (for instance a curve) is
*quasi-projective* if it admits an open immersion over $k$ into a
closed subscheme of some projective space $\mathbf P^N_k$, equivalently
a locally closed immersion $C\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N_k$ over $k$.
