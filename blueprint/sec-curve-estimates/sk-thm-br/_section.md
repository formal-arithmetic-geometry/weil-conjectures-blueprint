*Theorem (sketch).*

For $C$ smooth proper over algebraically closed $k$: $\Br(C)=0$.

## Proof

$\Br(C)\hookrightarrow\Br(k(C))=0$ by [sk-prop-br-inj] and
[sk-prop-tsen].
