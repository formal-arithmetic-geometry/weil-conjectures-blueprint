Let $k$ be algebraically closed, let $C$ be an integral curve of dimension
one over $k$ with function field $K=k(C)$, and let $L$ be an algebraic
extension of $K$. Then

$$
H^q(\operatorname{Spec}L_{\mathrm{ét}},\mathbf G_m)=0\qquad(q\geq1).
$$

In particular this holds for $L=K$ and for the fraction field of every
strict henselization of a local ring of $C$.
