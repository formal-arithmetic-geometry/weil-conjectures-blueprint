Let $k$ be algebraically closed of characteristic $p>0$ and let $C$ be a
smooth separated finite-type scheme of dimension at most one (not
necessarily connected). Then the constant sheaf $\underline{\mathbf F_p}$
satisfies all eight assertions $\mathcal C_1,\dots,\mathcal C_8$ of
[def-weil-pbc-eight-curve-assertions]; $\mathcal C_4$ and $\mathcal C_6$
hold because their hypothesis (torsion prime to $p$) forces the sheaf to
vanish.
