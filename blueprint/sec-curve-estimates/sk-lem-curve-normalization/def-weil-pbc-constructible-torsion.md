Let $(C,F)$ be a curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting]. The
torsion sheaf $F$ is *constructible* if $C$ has a finite partition by locally
closed constructible subschemes on each of which its restriction is locally
constant with finite stalks.
