Let $C$ be a reduced curve of finite type over an algebraically closed field
and $a\colon C^{\mathrm{nor}}\to C$ its normalization. For a finite abelian
group $M$, the natural map

$$
\underline M_C\longrightarrow a_*\underline M_{C^{\mathrm{nor}}}
$$

is injective with cokernel supported on finitely many points; at a point with
$b$ geometric branches its cokernel is $M^b/\Delta M$. These cokernels and
the resulting exact sequences commute with algebraically closed field
extension.
