For every curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting] $(C,F)$ with $F$
constructible [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion] and prime to
$\operatorname{char}k$ [def-weil-pbc-primary-torsion]: for every integer
$q\geq0$, $H^q(C_{\Et},F)$ is a finite abelian group.
