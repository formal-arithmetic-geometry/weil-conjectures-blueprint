Let $\pi:T'\to T$ be an
integral morphism of
schemes. Then the
direct image
$\pi_*:\Ab(T'_{\Et})\to\Ab(T_{\Et})$ is exact; consequently
$R^q\pi_*=0$ for $q>0$ and $R\pi_*=\pi_*$ on $\Dplus(T'_{\Et})$.
