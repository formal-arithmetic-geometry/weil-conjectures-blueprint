Let $g:Y'\to Y$ be a morphism, $n$ an integer invertible on $Y$, and $q\geq1$. Consider data
consisting of a scheme $T$ over $Y$, a qcqs direct-image morphism $Z\to T$ and a sheaf of
$\mathbf Z/n$-modules $M$ on $Z_{\mathrm{\acute et}}$, with the square formed by the base change
$Y'\times_YT\to T$ and the direct image along $Z\to T$. Recall that $\mathrm{BC}(g,n,r)$ means
that for all such data the degree-$i$ exchange maps are isomorphisms for $0\leq i\leq r$, and
that a *failure* in degree $q$ is a datum whose degree-$q$ exchange map is not an isomorphism.
Assume $\mathrm{BC}(g,n,q-1)$. A failure of base change in degree $q$
persists after an integral surjective change of the source of the
direct-image morphism. It also persists after an integral change of
the base through which the direct-image morphism factors.
