For every admissible coefficient ring, smooth base change takes the
following form. Smooth base change for torsion complexes
(Theorem [sk-thm-sbc-derived]) gives it for finite rings, and
Proposition [sk-prop-reduction-to-finite] gives the other rings.

This is the first application of the reduction principle of
Section [sec-n-reduction-finite], and it shows why the pro-étale formalism is
convenient. The base change map for $\mathcal O_E$-coefficients is a single
map of complexes, defined by the same adjunction as for finite rings, and
by Lemma [sk-lem-sbc-exchange-compatibility] its reduction modulo $\pi_E$ is the
base change map for $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$. Derived Nakayama then turns the
torsion theorem into the integral one, without inverse systems
$(K_n)$ and without checking compatibilities level by level. The rational
cases follow by inverting $\ell$ and by descent to a finite extension.
