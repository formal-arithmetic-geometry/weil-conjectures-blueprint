title = "Smooth base change for $\\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\\Qlb$ from finite coefficients"

Consider a cartesian square with $g'\colon X'\to X$, $f'\colon X'\to Y'$, $f\colon X\to Y$ and
$g\colon Y'\to Y$ of schemes of finite type over $k$, with $g$ smooth, and its base change map
$\operatorname{Ex}_{g,f}(K)\colon g^*Rf_*K\to Rf'_*g'^*K$ ([sk-lem-sbc-exchange-compatibility]).
Assume that $\operatorname{Ex}_{g,f}(K)$ is an isomorphism for every finite admissible ring $A$ and
every $K\in\Dctf(X,A)$ ([sk-def-sfb-constructible]). Then $\operatorname{Ex}_{g,f}(K)$ is an
isomorphism for $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\Qlb$ (in particular $\Zl$ and $\Ql$) and every
$K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$, and for $\Qlb$ the isomorphism does not depend on the choice of a model over
a finite coefficient field.
