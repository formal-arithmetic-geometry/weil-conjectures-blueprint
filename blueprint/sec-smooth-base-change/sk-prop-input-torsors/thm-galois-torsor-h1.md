In the situation of [def-galois-categories], let $M$ be a finite
abelian group. Then the construction below is a canonical bijection

$$
\operatorname{Tor}^1(Y,M)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H^1(Y_{\Et},\underline M),
$$

natural in $(Y,M)$.
