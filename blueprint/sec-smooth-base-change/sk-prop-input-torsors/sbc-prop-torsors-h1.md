For a finite abelian group $M$ and any scheme $Y$, the pointed set of
isomorphism classes of $M$-torsors of
[def-weil-pbc-finite-torsors] is canonically
$\operatorname{Tor}^1(Y,M)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^1(Y_{\Et},\underline M)$.
(*the finite-etale chapter, [thm-galois-torsor-h1].*)

This is the assertion of [thm-galois-torsor-h1] of the finite-etale chapter, restated for use below.
