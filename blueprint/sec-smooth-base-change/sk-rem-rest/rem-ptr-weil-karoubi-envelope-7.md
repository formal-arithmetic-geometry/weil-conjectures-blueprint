In the notation of [def-weil-karoubi-envelope], the
blueprint supplies the envelope with objects $(K,e)$, the morphism
condition $u=due$, the inclusion $K\mapsto(K,\operatorname{id}_K)$, its full
faithfulness ([lem-weil-karoubi-envelope-fully-faithful]) and its idempotent
completeness ([lem-weil-karoubi-envelope-idempotent-complete]), which are the
two inputs of the bounded-t-structure argument of this subsection.
