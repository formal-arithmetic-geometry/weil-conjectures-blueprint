This is classical, Stacks Project, More on Morphisms, Lemma 37.38.8 ([Tag 0EY4](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EY4)): a smooth morphism becomes, after an etale cover of its source, a smooth morphism of affine schemes with a section and geometrically connected fibres over an etale base. No separate proof is given.
