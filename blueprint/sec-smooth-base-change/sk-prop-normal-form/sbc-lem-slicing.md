Let $f:X\to S$ be a
smooth morphism of schemes. Then
there exists an etale covering $\{U_i\to X\}_{i\in I}$ such that
$U_i\to S$ factors as $U_i\to V_i\to S$ where $V_i\to S$ is etale and
$U_i\to V_i$ is a smooth morphism of affine schemes, which has a
section, and has geometrically
connected fibres.
(*Stacks Project, More on Morphisms, [Tag `0EY4`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EY4) = Lemma
37.38.8.*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, More on Morphisms, Lemma 37.38.8 ([Tag 0EY4](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EY4)): a smooth morphism becomes, after an etale cover of its source, a smooth morphism of affine schemes with a section and geometrically connected fibres over an etale base. No separate proof is given.
