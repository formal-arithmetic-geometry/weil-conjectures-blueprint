A square Jacobian presentation of an $R$-algebra $B$ is a specified
isomorphism
$$
 B=R[x_1,\ldots,x_m]/(f_1,\ldots,f_m)
$$
such that $\Delta=\det(\partial f_i/\partial x_j)$ has invertible image in
$B$.
