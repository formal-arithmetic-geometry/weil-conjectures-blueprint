A morphism of schemes $f:X\to Y$ is *smooth* if it is locally of
finite presentation and formally smooth, the latter meaning that every
morphism $T\to X$ over $Y$ from a closed subscheme $T\subset T'$
defined by a nilpotent ideal sheaf extends, Zariski locally on $T'$,
to a morphism $T'\to X$ over $Y$
([Stacks Project,
Definition 37.11.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02H0)); by
[Lemma 37.11.7](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02H6) this is
the notion of a smooth morphism of
[Stacks Project,
Section 29.35](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01V4). The description of
