Let $f:X\to Y$ be a smooth
morphism of schemes. Then $f$ has
geometrically reduced
fibres.
(*Stacks Project: the fibres are smooth over their residue fields
by base change,
[Lemma 29.35.5](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01VB), and a
scheme smooth over a field is geometrically reduced,
[Lemma 33.25.4](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/056T).*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Lemmas 29.35.5 and 33.25.4 ([Tag 01VB](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01VB), [Tag 056T](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/056T)): the fibres of a smooth morphism are smooth over their residue fields by base change, and a scheme smooth over a field is geometrically reduced. No separate proof is given.
