Let $f:X\to Y$ be a smooth
morphism of schemes. Then $f$ has
geometrically reduced
fibres.

## Proof

This is classical, Stacks Project, Lemmas 29.35.5 and 33.25.4 ([Tag 01VB](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01VB), [Tag 056T](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/056T)): the fibres of a smooth morphism are smooth over their residue fields by base change, and a scheme smooth over a field is geometrically reduced. No separate proof is given.
