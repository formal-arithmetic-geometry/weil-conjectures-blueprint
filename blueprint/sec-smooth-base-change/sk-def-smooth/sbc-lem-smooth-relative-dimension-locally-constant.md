Let $f:X\to Y$ be a smooth
morphism of schemes. Then the function
$x\mapsto\dim_x\bigl(X_{f(x)}\bigr)$ is locally constant on $X$.
(*Stacks Project,
[Lemma 29.35.12](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02G1):
$\Omega_{X/Y}$ is finite locally free and
$\operatorname{rank}_x(\Omega_{X/Y})=\dim_x(X_{f(x)})$.*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Lemma 29.35.12 ([Tag 02G1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02G1)): for a smooth morphism the module of differentials is finite locally free and its rank at a point is the dimension of the fibre there, whence the local constancy. No separate proof is given.
