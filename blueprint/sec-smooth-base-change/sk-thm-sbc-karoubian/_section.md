For a scheme $T$ and a finite extension $E/\Ql$, the bounded constructible
categories $\Dbc(T_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ and $\Dbc(T,E)$ carry bounded
t-structures, and both are idempotent complete.

1. *Integral.* The truncations of the adic tower are computed levelwise
([prop-weil-pbc-levelwise-truncations]); they define the standard bounded
t-structure on the integral category
([thm-weil-pbc-integral-truncations], [prop-weil-pbc-integral-tstructure]).
1. *Rational.* Localization at $\varpi$ carries it to a bounded t-structure on
$\Dbc(T,E)$ for which the localization functor is t-exact
([prop-weil-localized-tstructure]).
1. *Karoubian.* A triangulated category with a bounded t-structure is
idempotent complete ([thm-weil-karoubian-bounded-t], through the Karoubi
envelope lemmas), so $\Dbc(T,E)\simeq\Kar(\Dbc(T,E))$
([thm-weil-pbc-finite-stage-karoubian]).

The heart is abelian ([prop-weil-heart-abelian]), and short exact sequences in
it are distinguished triangles ([lem-weil-heart-short-exact-triangles]).
