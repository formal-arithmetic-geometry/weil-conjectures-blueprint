*(i) The two lanes contradict (this is the whole proof):* a
first failure in degree $q$ produces a universal curve-test datum
with $R^qh_*(\Z/d\Z)\neq0$ ([sbc-lem-bc-universal-test], from the
counterexample chain anchored at the minimal-counterexample lemma
`0F06`), while the curve-test lane ([sbc-thm-universal-test-vanishing])
makes it zero. *(ii) The curve-estimate seam:*
[thm-sc-constant-invariance] (the algebraically closed
field-extension invariance) is the same input that computes the
closed fibre of $\mathbb P^1$ in proper base change; it is used in
[sbc-thm-universal-test-vanishing] at $q=1$ only, together with the torsor
translation [thm-galois-torsor-h1] and the spreading theorem
[sbc-thm-spreading-finite-etale-galois] (tame extension), the deepest of
the lane's inputs and the one the blueprint carries only in
sketch.
*(iii) This is Stacks' second, shorter proof:* the
counterexample lemmas are Stacks `0F06`, `0F07`,
`0F08`, and the theorem is `0EYU`; the blueprint's
definitions (base-change map, smooth, allowed coefficients)
are the ones its own proof actually uses. *(iv) Local
acyclicity/Milnor tubes are later:* they are defined in the
subsequent local-acyclicity section of the chapter, and the
*smooth morphisms are universally locally acyclic* theorem
there is derived from the base-change theorem (via base change for
open immersions under local acyclicity), not the other way around.
