The sheaf argument also allows torsion sections of varying orders,
provided their prime divisors are invertible on $Y$: filtered
continuity of cohomology reduces to sections killed by a single such
integer, and the spectral-sequence argument extends to bounded-below
classical abelian complexes with this property. For a finite
admissible ring $A$, Theorem [sk-thm-sbc-derived] applies with $n=\ell^r$,
where $\ell^rA=0$. The theorem is an isomorphism of bounded-below
complexes; that both sides lie in $\Dctf$ when $K$ does is
Proposition [sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability].
