In the Cartesian square of [sk-thm-smooth-base-change], with $f$ smooth and
$g$ quasi-compact and quasi-separated, let $\mathcal P$ be the set of primes
invertible on the base. The base-change map of [sk-def-bc] is an isomorphism

$$
Lf^*Rg_*K\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Rh_*Le^*K
$$

for bounded constructible $K$ ([sbc-thm-bounded-derived-smooth-base-change]),
for bounded $\mathcal P$-torsion $K$ ([sbc-thm-bounded-allowable-torsion]), and
for every $K\in D^+_{\mathcal P\text{-tor}}(T_{\Et})$
([sbc-thm-bounded-below-derived-smooth-base-change]). Each is reduced to the
degreewise statement of [sk-thm-smooth-base-change] by a finite-range
comparison and a truncation criterion.
