title = "From sheaves to bounded below complexes"

The theorem on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ concerns complexes, and its integral case is
reduced to complexes with finite coefficients. We therefore pass from
sheaves to bounded below complexes first. For a bounded below complex the
passage is a spectral sequence argument: the base change map induces a map
between the hypercohomology spectral sequences
$E_2^{p,q}=R^pf_*H^q(K)$ of the two sides, which is an isomorphism on $E_2$
by the theorem for sheaves. Boundedness below is what makes the spectral
sequences converge, since in each total degree only finitely many terms
contribute. Since objects of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ are bounded, this is all we need.
