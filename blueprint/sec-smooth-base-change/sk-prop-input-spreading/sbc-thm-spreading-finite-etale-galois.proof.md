This is classical, Stacks Project, Fundamental Groups of Schemes, Lemma 58.31.9 ([Tag 0EZJ](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EZJ)): a finite etale Galois cover of the generic fibre whose group has order invertible on the base spreads out to a finite etale Galois cover of the whole smooth family with geometrically connected fibres. No separate proof is given.
