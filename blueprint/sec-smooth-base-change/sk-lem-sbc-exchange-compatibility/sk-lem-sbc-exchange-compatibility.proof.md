The adjoint transpose defining the map is a counit preceded by the
composition isomorphism for inverse images. The counit for a
composite adjunction is the composite of the two counits, so taking
adjoint transposes proves pasting, in either order of composing the
two directions of a rectangle. The triangular identities prove the
identity assertion; this is also Lemma [sk-lem-pbc-exchange-pasting].
The site comparisons of [sk-thm-pe-direct-image-comparison]
identify the inverse-image composition maps and the units and counits,
hence the exchange morphisms built from them. The same argument with
Proposition [sk-prop-scalar-six] proves the coefficient assertion.
