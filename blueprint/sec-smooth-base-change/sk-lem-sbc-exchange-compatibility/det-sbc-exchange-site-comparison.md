In a cartesian square $X'\xrightarrow{u}X$, $f':X'\to Y'$, $f:X\to Y$, $g:Y'\to Y$ with $f$
and $f'$ quasi-compact and quasi-separated, let $\Lambda$ be a discrete ring and
$K\in D^+(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},\Lambda)$. Under the isomorphisms
$\nu_{Y'}^*g_{\mathrm{\acute et}}^{-1}\simeq g^*\nu_Y^*$ and
$\nu_X^*u_{\mathrm{\acute et}}^{-1}\simeq u^*\nu_X^*$ of [lem-weil-pbc-comparison-pullback] and
$\nu_Y^*Rf_{\mathrm{\acute et},*}\simeq Rf_*\nu_X^*$,
$\nu_{Y'}^*Rf'_{\mathrm{\acute et},*}\simeq Rf'_*\nu_{X'}^*$ of
[prop-weil-pbc-comparison-direct-image], the exchange map
$\operatorname{Ex}_{g,f}(\nu_X^*K)$ of [det-sbc-exchange-formal] is identified with
$\nu_{Y'}^*$ of the étale exchange map
$g^{-1}Rf_{\mathrm{\acute et},*}K\to Rf'_{\mathrm{\acute et},*}u^{-1}K$.
