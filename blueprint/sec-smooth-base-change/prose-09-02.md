With the base change map and its compatibilities in hand, we state the case
of torsion sheaves, from which all others follow. The proof below passes through base schemes of infinite type over $k$,
such as spectra of fields and of integral closures. The comparison
results of Section “The pro-étale site and its comparison theorems” hold over arbitrary
schemes for bounded below complexes, so they apply to these bases as
well.
