The bound itself is defined in [def-sc-etale-torsion-cd]. The curve estimates
give it on an arbitrary algebraic curve [lem-sc-arbitrary-curve-cd]; two
formal lemmas let it pass to an affine limit [lem-sc-cd-affine-limit] and
descend from a geometric fibre [lem-sc-cd-ground-field], whence the bound for
one-variable fields [lem-sc-one-variable-field-cd]. With the support lemma
[lem-sc-support-dimension-union] these assemble into the affine bound
[thm-sc-affine-torsion-cd]. Finally $\operatorname{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(k)\le1$
for a finite field [lem-sc-finite-field-cd], and the affine bound yields a
uniform vanishing range for $R^qj_*$ along a quasi-compact open immersion
[thm-sc-open-uniform-cd].
