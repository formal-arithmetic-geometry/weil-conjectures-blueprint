The last chapter of outline module 4: the curve estimates are turned into
cohomological-dimension bounds for arbitrary finite-type schemes. These are
inputs to smooth base change and to direct-image finiteness.

The first part
[torsion-cohomological-dimension-and-open-complements] establishes the affine
bound $\operatorname{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(X)\le\dim X+c$ over a field of
torsion cohomological dimension $\le c$, together with a uniform vanishing
bound for $R^qj_*$ along a quasi-compact open immersion. The second part
[from-the-affine-bound-to-arbitrary-schemes] removes the affineness
hypothesis by a Zariski-cohomology argument.
