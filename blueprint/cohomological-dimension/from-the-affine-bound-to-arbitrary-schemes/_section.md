The affine bound is spread over a general finite-type scheme by comparing
the étale and Zariski sites. The Zariski site and its cohomology are fixed in
[def-sc-zariski-site] and [def-sc-zariski-cohomology]; flasque sheaves
[def-sc-zariski-flasque] are acyclic [lem-sc-zariski-flasque-acyclic], and
with the filtered-colimit lemma [lem-sc-zariski-filtered-cohomology] this
gives the Zariski dimension bound [lem-sc-zariski-dimension-bound]. Feeding
both bounds into the Leray spectral sequence of $\epsilon:X_{\rm et}\to
X_{\rm Zar}$ yields $\operatorname{cd}_{\rm tor}(X)\le2d+c$
[thm-sc-finite-type-torsion-cd]. A closing remark lists the standard inputs
[rem-sc-cd-standard-inputs].
