For an abelian sheaf $F$ on the small Zariski site [def-sc-zariski-site] of
$X$, let $H^q(X_{\rm Zar},F)$ be the $q$-th right derived functor of
$F\mapsto F(X)$. Equivalently take the cohomology of the section complex of
an injective resolution, using the ordinary resolution convention.
