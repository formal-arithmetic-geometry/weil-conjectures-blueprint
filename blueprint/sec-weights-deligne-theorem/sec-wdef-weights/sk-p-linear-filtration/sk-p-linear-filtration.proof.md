In a basis adapted to the filtration, the matrix of $u$ is block upper
triangular, so its characteristic polynomial is the product of those
of the diagonal blocks. Over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we may also choose bases in which
$u$ and $v$ are upper triangular. In the induced tensor basis and in
the ordered wedge basis, the resulting matrices are upper triangular
with the stated diagonal entries. The matrix on the dual is the
inverse transpose.
