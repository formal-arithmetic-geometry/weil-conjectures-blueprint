Since $\iota$ is a ring homomorphism and the complex modulus is
multiplicative, the absolute value $|\cdot|_\iota$ of [def-tw-eigenvalue]
is multiplicative:
$|\iota(\alpha\beta)|=|\iota(\alpha)||\iota(\beta)|=q^{w_1/2}q^{w_2/2}=q^{(w_1+w_2)/2}$,
and, for $\alpha\neq0$,
$|\iota(\alpha^{-1})|=|\iota(\alpha)|^{-1}=q^{-w_1/2}$.
