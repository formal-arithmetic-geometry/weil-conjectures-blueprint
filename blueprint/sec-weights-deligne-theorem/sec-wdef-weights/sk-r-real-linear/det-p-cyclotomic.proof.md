By [d-ab-field], arithmetic Frobenius sends each root of unity to
its $q$th power. This is precisely multiplication by $q$ in
[det-d-ab-cyclotomic]. Since $\ell\nmid q$, the inverse of $q$ modulo
each $\ell^n$ exists; uniqueness makes these inverses compatible under
reduction. They define $q^{-1}$ in the ring of [def-tate-module-abelian-group].
The inverse automorphism is therefore multiplication by $q^{-1}$.
