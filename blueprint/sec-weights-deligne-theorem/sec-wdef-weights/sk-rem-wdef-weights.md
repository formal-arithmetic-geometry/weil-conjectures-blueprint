1. Every sheaf defined over $k_0$ is a Weil sheaf.
2. In the description of lisse Weil sheaves by representations
   (Convention [sk-d-real-weil]), the Weil isomorphism is
   transport by $s$, and conversely the Weil isomorphism determines the
   action of $s$.
3. This correspondence commutes with finite extension of scalars and
   with inverse image between normal schemes, as one sees by passing to
   the finite reductions.
