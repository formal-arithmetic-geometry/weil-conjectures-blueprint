Let $\Gamma$ be a subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}(V)$ over
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, and let $H$ be its Zariski closure
inside $\operatorname{GL}(V)$. Then $H$ is a closed algebraic
subgroup.
