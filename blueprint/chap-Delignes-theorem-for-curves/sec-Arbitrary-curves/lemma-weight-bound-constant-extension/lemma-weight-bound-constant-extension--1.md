Let $X_0$ be a separated scheme of finite type over $k_0$, let $F$ be a
constructible Weil sheaf on $X_0$, let $k_m\supset k_0$ be the
extension of degree $m$, and let $u\in\mathbf R$. The group
$H^r_c(X,F)$ is the same for $X_0$ and for $X_0\otimes_{k_0}k_m$, and
the geometric Frobenius of $k_m$ acts on it as the $m$th power of that
of $k_0$. Every eigenvalue $\alpha$ of the Frobenius of $k_0$ on
$H^r_c(X,F)$ satisfies $|\iota\alpha|\le q^{u/2}$ if and only if every
eigenvalue $\beta$ of the Frobenius of $k_m$ satisfies
$|\iota\beta|\le q^{mu/2}$.
