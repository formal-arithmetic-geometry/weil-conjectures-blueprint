For example, take for $C_0$ the affine curve $y^2=x^3-x$ over
$\mathbf F_3$, the complement of the point at infinity of the
projective curve. There $g=1$, the point $s=\infty$ is rational, and
$m=2$ satisfies $(m-1)\deg(s)>0$. The function $x$ has a single pole,
of order two, which is prime to $3$. The map is the double cover
$(x,y)\mapsto x$ of the line. Its extension to the projective curve is
branched over $0$, $\pm1$ and $\infty$.

With both lemmas the bound on the line extends to every affine curve.
