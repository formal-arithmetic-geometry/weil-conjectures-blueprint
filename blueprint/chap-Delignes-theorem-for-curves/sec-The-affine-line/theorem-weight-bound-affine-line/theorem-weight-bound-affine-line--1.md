Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1_{k_0}$ be a dense open, and let
$F$ be a lisse sheaf on $U_0$, pure of weight $w$ and unramified at
infinity. Then $H_c^1(\mathbf A^1,j_*F)$ has weights at most $w+1$.
