Put $B=j_*M/j_!M$. There are sheaves $G$ and $B'$ on the dual line such
that $\operatorname{FT}_\psi(j_!M)=G$, $\operatorname{FT}_\psi(B)=B'[1]$,
and the sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow B'\longrightarrow G
\longrightarrow j'_*M'\longrightarrow0
$$

is exact.
