The six operations: exceptional right adjoints and constructible restrictions,
the six-functor interfaces, Verdier duality, the formal core and projection
formula, finite-Tor models, ambient comparison and admissibility, and the
biduality exports, from Part 5 of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex` --- as far as
they come before [smooth-purity]. Smooth purity rests on them; the results of
the same chapters that rest on smooth purity are in [verdier-biduality], and
[poincare-duality] follows both.

The compactification comparison of chapter 10, which this part used to open
with, is now in [compact-support-operations]: the smooth and compact-support
base change blocks cite it, and they stand before this part.
