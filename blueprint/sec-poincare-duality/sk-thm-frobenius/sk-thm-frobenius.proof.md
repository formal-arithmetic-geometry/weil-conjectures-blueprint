Mixed-support cup product and evaluation are natural for Frobenius pullback.
Smooth purity and the normalized counit are equivariant by the section on
smooth purity. Their composite $\beta_K$ is therefore equivariant. Geometric
Frobenius acts on $\Lambda(d)$ by $q^{-d}$ and on $\Lambda(-d)$ by $q^d$;
removing the twist gives the displayed similitude identity.

At a field stage, Corollary [sk-thm-derived-duality] identifies the dual
cohomology space with the vector-space dual of compactly supported cohomology.
If $F_c$ is the latter Frobenius, the untwisted identity identifies Frobenius on
the former with $q^d(F_c^{-1})^{\mathsf t}$. A transpose has the same
characteristic polynomial, so this proves the eigenvalue statement with
algebraic multiplicities. For integral coefficients and arbitrary finite rings
the statement is the equivariance of the derived pairing.
