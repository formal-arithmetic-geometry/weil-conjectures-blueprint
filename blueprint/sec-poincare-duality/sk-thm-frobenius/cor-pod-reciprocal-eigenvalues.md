In the Poincare duality conventions of this chapter (the fixed setting), let $(K,\varphi_K)$ be a strict Weil complex with $K$ perfect, at a *field* coefficient stage $\Lambda \in \{ E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell }\}$ (the hypothesis of [thm-pod-degreewise-perfectness-over-a-field]; the statement fails over $\mathcal O_E$, where the degreewise pairing need not be perfect). Relative to the perfect pairing of [thm-pod-degreewise-perfectness-over-a-field], the operator $F_{K^\vee }$ on $H^{2d-i}(X,K^\vee )$ is conjugate to

$$
q^d\, \bigl(F_{c,K}^{-1}\bigr)^{\mathsf t}.
$$

Thus an eigenvalue $\alpha$ of $F_{c,K}$ on $H_c^i(X,K)$ with algebraic multiplicity $m$ corresponds, with the same multiplicity, to the eigenvalue $q^d/\alpha$ of $F_{K^\vee }$ on $H^{2d-i}(X,K^\vee )$. If $X_0$ is proper, the first space is $H^i(X,K)$.
