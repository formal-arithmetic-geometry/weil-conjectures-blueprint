*Definition (sketch).*

In the setting of [sk-def-set], an *orientation* is, for every
smooth separated finite-type $f:X\to Y$ of pure relative dimension $d$,
an isomorphism

$$
\gamma_{f,\Lambda_Y}:Lf^*\Lambda_Y(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Rf^!\Lambda_Y,
$$

natural in $Y$, compatible with composition, étale base change, finite
reduction, scalar extension and the pro-étale realization; for
$f=a_X$ it reads $\omega_{X,\Lambda}\cong\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$.
(Blueprint 13, def:sf-orientation.)

Uses: [sk-def-ops], [sk-def-set]
