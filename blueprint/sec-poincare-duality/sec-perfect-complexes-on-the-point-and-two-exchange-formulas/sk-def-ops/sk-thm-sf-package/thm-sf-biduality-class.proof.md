The finite-stage anti-equivalence is [thm-fix-general-biduality],
with its independent bound from
[lem-weil-verdier-preservation-strata]. For $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, the
evaluation map $K\to D_XD_XK$ is a map in $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, whose
objects are derived complete; its reduction modulo $\pi_E$ is the finite
evaluation map for $K_1$, because duality commutes with reduction
([sk-prop-scalar-six]), so it is an isomorphism, and derived Nakayama shows
the cone vanishes ([thm-coef-integral-verdier]). The Tor bounds follow from
the finite ones for the reductions, whose Tor interval is uniform by
[lem-coef-integral-residue-tor]. For $E$, objects are obtained by cones and
shifts from integral generators ([sk-lem-integral-models]), and duality
commutes with $-\otimes_{\mathcal O_E}E$; for $\Qlb$, objects and morphisms
descend to a finite $E$ ([sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit]) and duality commutes
with extension of scalars. These reductions are carried out in
[det-pd-biduality-coefficients], and they apply to the evaluation map
itself, so the resulting isomorphism is the stated double-dual map for every
admissible $\Lambda$ ([sk-thm-pd-verdier-duality]).
