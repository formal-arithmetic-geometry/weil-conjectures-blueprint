Let $\Lambda=A_{E,n}$, let $M\in D^b_{ctf}(Y,\Lambda)$ be a
bounded constructible
complex of finite Tor amplitude
on the coefficient site,
and let $\bar y\to Y$ be a
geometric point.
Then $M_{\bar y}\in D(\Lambda)$ is quasi-isomorphic to a bounded
complex of finite free $\Lambda$-modules. The same conclusion
holds on the small etale site with the corresponding constructibility
and derived-tensor
Tor-amplitude conditions.
Separately, let $\Lambda\in\{E,\Qlb\}$, regarded as a condensed ring, and
$K\in\Dbc(Y,\Lambda)$ an object of the condensed bounded constructible
category ([det-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]). Its geometric stalk
$\operatorname{Fib}(\bar y^*K)\in D^b_{\mathrm{fd}}(\Lambda)$
([def-weil-finite-coefficient-point]) is quasi-isomorphic to a bounded
complex of finite-dimensional vector spaces over the field $\Lambda$, hence
perfect.
