In the setting of [def-sf-datum], let $A$ be a finite admissible coefficient
ring and let $K,L$ be the pro-étale
realizations of bounded constructible finite-Tor complexes of
$A$-modules on the small étale sites,
with étale-origin witnesses or with witnesses supplied by the site
comparison. Then $\operatorname{PF}_{f,K,L}$ is an
isomorphism on the pro-étale site. For $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E,E,\Qlb$ the
projection formula holds directly on $X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}}$ by
[thm-sf-pf-adic] and [thm-sf-pf-field]; it is not obtained by transport from
the étale site.
