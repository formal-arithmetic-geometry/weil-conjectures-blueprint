The exchange maps $\mathrm{Ex}_*$ and $\mathrm{Ex}_!$ of [fix-lem-exchanges] are
isomorphisms for automorphism squares, and their pasting identities give the
iterate compatibility, exactly as in [lem-fix-weil-operations]; nothing there
uses that $\Lambda$ is a field. Tensor, evaluation and the projection maps of
[def-exchange-projection] are equivariant by naturality. The smooth trace is
equivariant by [thm-pur-weil-equivariance-of-purity-and-trace], and the pairing
is the composite of these maps, hence equivariant with the prescribed Tate
action. The argument works directly at the finite or integral level, so no
integral lift of an arbitrary finite-coefficient Weil complex is needed.
