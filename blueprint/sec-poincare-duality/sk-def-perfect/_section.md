*Definition (sketch).*

In the setting of [sk-def-ops], a *perfect point complex* is a
bounded complex of finite projective $\Lambda$-modules; over a field
this is a bounded complex with finite-dimensional cohomology. An object
$K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ is *perfect over $\Lambda_X$* if it is
locally quasi-isomorphic to a bounded complex of finite projective
$\Lambda_X$-modules. (Blueprint 15,
def:pod-perfect-point-complex, def:pod-perfect-complex-on-x.)
