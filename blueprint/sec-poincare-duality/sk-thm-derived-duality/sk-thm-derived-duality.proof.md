A bounded finite-dimensional field complex splits in the derived category as
the sum of its cohomology spaces. Thus
$H^{-i}(R\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(P,\Lambda))=\Hom_\Lambda(H^i(P),\Lambda)$.
Apply this to the canonical isomorphism

$$
R\Gamma(X,K^\vee(d))[2d]
\xrightarrow{\sim}
R\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(R\Gamma_c(X,K),\Lambda)
$$

of [sk-thm-sf-identification].
