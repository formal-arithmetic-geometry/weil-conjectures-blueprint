Finite exceptional preservation and the geometric duality bound are
[thm-sf-preservation-class], [lem-weil-verdier-preservation-strata].
Finite biduality and its boundary exchange are
[thm-fix-general-biduality], [cor-weil-boundary-duality-discharge].
The coefficient comparisons needed to pass further are now proved:
[thm-coef-finite-hom], [cor-coef-finite-verdier], [lem-coef-finite-exceptional]
at finite levels, and
[lem-coef-integral-hom-reduction], [thm-coef-integral-verdier]
for integral coefficients. For every admissible condensed $\Lambda$ the
exceptional inverse image is the right adjoint of $Rf_!$ on $\Dctf$ given by
[sk-thm-existence], biduality is [sk-thm-pd-verdier-duality], and the
comparisons under reduction and extension of scalars, including
[lem-coef-integral-finite-extension], are [sk-prop-scalar-six]. No
additional local-model hypothesis enters. Geometric base-change clauses in
[def-trf-admissibility], [def-pod-conventions] retain the hypotheses
of their respective exchange theorems. The unrestricted unbounded
pro-etale adjunction is outside the conclusions here, as specified
in [rem-sf-class-adjoint-residue].
