Let $A$ be a finite coefficient ring, $k_0$ a field of characteristic different
from $\ell$, $k'/k_0$ a separable algebraic extension, $X$ a separated
finite-type $k_0$-scheme, and $g\colon X'=X\otimes_{k_0}k'\to X$ the projection.
For $K,L\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$, pullback along $g$ commutes
with direct images, closed exceptional restriction and $R\mathcal Hom(K,L)$, and
$g^*\omega_X\simeq\omega_{X'}$, all compatibly with evaluation. Hence $g^*$
carries the double-dual morphism $\delta_K$ of [lem-weil-duality-pairing] to
$\delta_{g^*K}$.
