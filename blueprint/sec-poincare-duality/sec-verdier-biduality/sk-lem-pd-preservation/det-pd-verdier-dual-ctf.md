title = "The Verdier dual of a $\\Dctf$ complex lies in $\\Dctf$"

Let $\Lambda$ be an admissible coefficient ring and let $X$ be separated of
finite type over $k$. For every $K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ the Verdier dual
$\mathbb D_XK=\cHom_{\Lambda_X}(K,\omega_{X,\Lambda})$ lies in
$\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. Consequently $\mathbb D_X$ restricts to an exact functor
$\Dctf(X,\Lambda)^{\mathrm{op}}\to\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
