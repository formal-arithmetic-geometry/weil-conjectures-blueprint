Perfectness is Corollary [sk-thm-derived-duality] for $K=\Lambda_X$
combined with Theorem [sk-cor-trace]. Graded commutativity of the
cup product gives $\langle b,a\rangle=(-1)^{i(2d-i)}\langle a,b\rangle =(-1)^i\langle a,b\rangle$.
