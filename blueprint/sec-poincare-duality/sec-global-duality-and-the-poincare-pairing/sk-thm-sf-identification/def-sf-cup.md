In the setting of [def-sf-datum], let $\Lambda$ be an admissible coefficient
ring, regarded as a condensed ring, and let
$K,L\in D^b_{ctf}(X,\Lambda)\subseteq D(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda)$.
The *mixed-support cup product* is the composite

$$
\smile_c:R\Gamma_c(X,K)\otimes^L_\Lambda R\Gamma(X,L)\longrightarrow
R\Gamma_c\bigl(X,K\otimes^L_{\Lambda_X}L\bigr)
$$

of the projection transformation for $Ra_{X!}$ applied to the pair $(K,L)$
and the map induced by the counit $La_X^*Ra_{X*}L\to L$, formed directly in
$D(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda)$ for every admissible $\Lambda$. It is
associative, unital and graded-commutative.
