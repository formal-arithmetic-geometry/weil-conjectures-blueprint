Duality uses $a_X^!$, internal Hom and evaluation; the pairings add only tensor
product, the two direct images and the exceptional counit.
Proposition [sk-prop-scalar-six] compares all these maps, together with their
units, counits and evaluations. It therefore compares the adjoints defining
duality and the double-dual morphism. Residue reductions and derived
completeness give the integral theory; localization and finite integral models
give finite coefficient fields; descent to a finite coefficient field
([sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit]) and filtered scalar
extension give $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$; the last passage is a filtered colimit
over finite extensions. Naturality for Frobenius proves the equivariance
assertion.
