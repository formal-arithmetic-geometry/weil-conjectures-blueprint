Each of these objects and maps is assembled from operations that are already
compared. Duality uses $a_X^!$, internal Hom and evaluation, and the pairings use
in addition only tensor products, the two direct images and the exceptional
counit. Proposition [sk-prop-scalar-six] compares all these operations, together
with their units, counits and evaluations, for every listed coefficient map.
Hence it also compares the maps obtained from them by adjunction, namely the
duality isomorphisms and the double-dual morphism. The Poincaré pairing and the
top trace involve in addition the purity isomorphism and the smooth trace, and
these are compatible with change of coefficients for every admissible $\Lambda$
by Theorem [sk-thm-purity-transport]. All comparisons are natural for pullback
by Frobenius, which proves the equivariance assertion.
