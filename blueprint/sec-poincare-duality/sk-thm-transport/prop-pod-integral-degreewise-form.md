In the Poincare duality conventions of this chapter (the fixed setting), let $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X,\mathcal O_E)$ be perfect over $(\mathcal O_E)_X$ (the hypothesis of [thm-pod-derived-poincare-duality] at $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$) and put $K_n=K\otimes ^L_{\mathcal O_E}A_{E,n}$. Derived perfectness at the $\mathcal O_E$-stage yields the universal-coefficient exact sequences for the cohomology modules

$$
0\longrightarrow H^i_c(X,K)\otimes _{\mathcal O_E}A_{E,n} \longrightarrow H^i_c(X,K_n) \longrightarrow \operatorname {Tor}_1^{\mathcal O_E}\bigl(H^{i+1}_c(X,K),A_{E,n}\bigr) \longrightarrow 0,
$$

and the corresponding sequences for the second variable of the pairing and for the adjoint maps. If the relevant cohomology modules are finite projective over $\mathcal O_E$, the Tor terms vanish and the degreewise maps are perfect $\mathcal O_E$-module pairings.
