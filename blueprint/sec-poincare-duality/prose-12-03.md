Let $f$ be obtained from a smooth morphism $f_0:X_0\to Y_0$ over
$k_0=\mathbf F_q$ by extension to $k$, and let $\operatorname{Fr}_X$,
$\operatorname{Fr}_Y$ be the geometric Frobenius maps above. The isomorphism
$\gamma_f$ of the formula in Theorem [sk-thm-purity-transport] and the trace map commute with pullback by Frobenius as natural
transformations, before any Weil structure is chosen.
