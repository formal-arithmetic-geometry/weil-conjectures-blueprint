In the notation of [def-pod-conventions], the following holds. Of that hypothesis the
ambient exceptional right adjoint datum is not part, and it is false in
general: on the categories $D^b_{ctf}(X,\Lambda)$ used below the right
adjoint of $Ra_{X!}$ is the constructible exceptional inverse image,
which at a finite coefficient ring is the adjoint relative to the
bounded-below objects of étale origin of [thm-sf-ambient-adjoint-class]
and for the condensed coefficient rings is given by [sk-thm-existence];
and for the smooth morphism
$a_X$ the two preservation clauses are [cor-pur-smooth-admissibility],
with the constants $c_{a_X}^-=c_{a_X}^+=-2d$ of a morphism smooth of pure
relative dimension $d$, hence independent of the admissible coefficient ring; in the
general case the corresponding constants are those of
[thm-sf-preservation-class].
