Finiteness and constructibility of direct images. The first six sections are
the finiteness chain the source files inside its smooth-base-change chapter
(finite, open, proper and projective direct images, the reduction to the
projective line and to curve families); they need only the set-up of
[smooth-base-change]. The rest is chapter *Finiteness and constructibility of
direct images* of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex`: the compact-support
amplitude bound, generic finiteness, the finite-type direct-image statements,
and compact-support constructibility.
