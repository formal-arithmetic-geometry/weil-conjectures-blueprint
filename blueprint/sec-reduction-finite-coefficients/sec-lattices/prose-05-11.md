On a connected normal scheme a lattice exists globally, and lisse sheaves are
the same as continuous representations of the fundamental group. The proof
below shows that the étale $X$-scheme representing lattices in $L$ has a component that is finite étale
and surjective, and then argues as in Lemma [sk-lem-stratified-lattice].
This description is used for the weights of lisse sheaves on curves
(Section [sec-rank-one-curves]).
