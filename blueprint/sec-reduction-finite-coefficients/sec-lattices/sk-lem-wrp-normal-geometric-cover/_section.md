Let $X$ be a nonempty connected normal scheme of finite type over a
field. Let $p:Y\to X$ be separated and étale. Suppose that for every
valuation ring $V$, with fraction field $K$, every commutative diagram

$$
\begin{CD}
\operatorname{Spec}K @>>> Y\\
@VVV @VVpV\\
\operatorname{Spec}V @>>> X
\end{CD}
$$

has a lift $\operatorname{Spec}V\to Y$. Then every connected component
of $Y$ is finite étale and surjective over $X$.
