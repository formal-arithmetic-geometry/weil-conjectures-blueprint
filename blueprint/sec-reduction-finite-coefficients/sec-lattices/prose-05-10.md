In the proof we first find a lattice after a surjective étale base change
and then push it down by the trace. For the first step we use that the
lattices in $E^r$ form a discrete set on which $\GL_r(E)$ acts continuously,
so that the sheaf of lattices in $L$ is an étale $X$-scheme. The second
step requires the base change to be finite étale, and this holds on the
strata of a finite stratification.
