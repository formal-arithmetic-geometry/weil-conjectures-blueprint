By the proof of [lem-sfb-subfield-flat], a basis of $F$ over $E$ gives $F_X\cong E_X^n$ and
$F_Y\cong E_Y^n$, compatibly with $\eta_f$ (both come from the same isomorphism on the point).
After restriction of scalars to $E$, which is conservative, both sides of (Push) become
$(Rf_*K)^n$ and the comparison becomes the canonical map
$\bigoplus^nRf_*K\to Rf_*\bigoplus^nK$, an isomorphism since $Rf_*$ is additive.
