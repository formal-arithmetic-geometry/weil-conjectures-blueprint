For a prime $\ell$, a scheme $X$, finite extensions $E\le F$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ inside $\Qlb$ and
a bounded constructible complex $K$ of $E_X$-modules ([det-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]), the
complex $T_{E,F}K$ ([def-sfb-derived-extend-subfield]) is a bounded constructible complex of
$F_X$-modules.
