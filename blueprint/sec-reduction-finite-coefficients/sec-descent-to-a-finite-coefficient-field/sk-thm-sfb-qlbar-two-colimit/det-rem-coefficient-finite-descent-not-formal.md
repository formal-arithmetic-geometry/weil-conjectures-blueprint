Neither part of [sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit] is a formal consequence of
$\QbarX=\varinjlim_EE_X$ ([sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x]). The proof needs the continuity of
Ext out of a constructible complex ([sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity]), whose hypotheses
are a common lower bound of the complexes and a constructible source, and the dévissage of
constructible sheaves ([sk-lem-coefficient-heart]); both properties fail on the unbounded
derived categories $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},-)$.
