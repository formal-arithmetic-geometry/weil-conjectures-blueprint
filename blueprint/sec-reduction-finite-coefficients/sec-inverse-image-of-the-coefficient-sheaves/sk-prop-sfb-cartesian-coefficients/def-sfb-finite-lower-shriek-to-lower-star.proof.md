The only point is the invertibility of the counit of $i^*\dashv i_*$, which is shown in
the proof of [lem-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero-fully-faithful]: for $T$ over $X$,
$(i^*i_*G)(T)=G(T\times_{\bar X}X)\cong G(T)$ since $i$ is a monomorphism.
