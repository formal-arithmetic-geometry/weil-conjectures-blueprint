$\operatorname{Ext}_\varphi$ preserves K-flat quasi-isomorphisms
[lem-sfb-extend-scalars-kflat]. $\Mod_{\mathcal R}$ satisfies the hypotheses for left
derived functors, and $\Mod_{\mathcal S}$ is Grothendieck abelian, hence has K-injective
resolutions [det-prop-sfb-module-category]. So the derived adjunction of
[det-def-sfb-derived-categories] applies to
$\operatorname{Ext}_\varphi\dashv\operatorname{Res}_\varphi$.
