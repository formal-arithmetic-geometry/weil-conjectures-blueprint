Every base change argument below compares a module over $\Lambda_Y$ with
its pullback to $X$, and for this we want the pullback of a
$\Lambda_Y$-module to be its inverse image as a sheaf, with no tensor
product. For coherent sheaves this fails, since
$f^*M=\mathcal O_X\otimes_{f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y}f^{-1}M$ and
$f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y\ne\mathcal O_X$ in general. For our coefficient sheaves
the two notions agree. The second part of the next lemma is used when we
extend scalars to a finite extension $E'/E$, for instance in the proof of
Proposition [sk-thm-sfb-proper-base-change], where it makes extension of
scalars a finite direct sum.

Since all coefficient sheaves are pulled back from the point
(Section [sec-n-coefficients]), they are compatible with inverse image, and so are
their Tate twists.
