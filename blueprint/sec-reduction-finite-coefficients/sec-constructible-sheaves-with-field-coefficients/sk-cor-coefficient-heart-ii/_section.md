Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. Then $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is a triangulated
subcategory of $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, and every object of $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$
is obtained by finitely many cones from objects $a_!L[n]$, where
$a:S\hookrightarrow X$ is a locally closed immersion, $L$ is a lisse
$\Lambda_S$-module and $n\in\mathbf Z$.
