Let $E/\Ql$ be finite and let $L$ be a lisse $E_X$-module. A
*lattice* in $L$ is a lisse $\mathcal O_{E,X}$-submodule $M\subset L$
with $M[1/\ell]=L$. The sheaf on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ of lattices in $L$ is represented
by a separated surjective étale $X$-scheme, with a universal lattice.
In particular, $L$ admits a lattice étale locally on $X$.
