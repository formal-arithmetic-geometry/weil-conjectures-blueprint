Let $\{U_k\to X\}$ be a covering with $L|_{U_k}\cong E_{U_k}^{\oplus\rho_k}$, where
$\rho_k\colon|U_k|\to\mathbf Z_{\ge0}$ is locally constant (on a connected open-closed piece the
rank of a free module is well defined since $E_X(W)\ne0$ for $W\ne\emptyset$; over a field-valued
stalk it is the dimension). On $U_k\times_XU_{k'}$ the two pullbacks of $\rho_k$, $\rho_{k'}$
agree, because the transition isomorphisms preserve the rank. Let $U=\coprod_kU_k\to X$
(a pro-étale, hence fpqc, covering). The set $\{\rho=r\}\subseteq U$ is open and closed and
saturated for the equivalence relation $U\times_XU\rightrightarrows U$, so it is the preimage
of a subset $X_r\subseteq|X|$; since $U\to X$ is an fpqc covering, a subset of $|X|$ whose preimage in $|U|$ is open is
itself open (fpqc descent of open subsets: an fpqc covering is submersive), so $X_r$ and its
complement are open.
If $X$ is quasi-compact, finitely many $U_k$ (quasi-compact) suffice, and $\rho$ takes
finitely many values.
