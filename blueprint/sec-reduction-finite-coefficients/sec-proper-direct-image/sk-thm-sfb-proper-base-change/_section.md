Let $p:X\to Y$ be proper and let $\Lambda$ be admissible. Then
$Rp_*:D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ maps
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ into $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$, and on these categories the
canonical proper base change and projection formula maps are
isomorphisms. Explicitly, for a cartesian square with $g:Y'\to Y$,
$g':X'\to X$ and $p':X'\to Y'$, the base change map
$g^*Rp_*\to Rp'_*g'^*$ is an isomorphism of functors
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)\to D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y',\Lambda)$. The projection formula map
$Rp_*K\otimes^{\mathbf L}M\to Rp_*(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}p^*M)$ is an
isomorphism for $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ and
$M\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$.
