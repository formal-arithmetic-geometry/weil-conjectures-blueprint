The square formed by $f$, $\bar y$ and the projections from $X_{\bar y}$ is
cartesian. Its four schemes are separated and of finite type over $k$:
$\operatorname{Spec}k$ trivially, and $X_{\bar y}$ because it is proper over
$\operatorname{Spec}k$. So the claim is
[det-thm-sfb-proper-base-change-rational] (3) for $\Lambda=E$ and this square.
