title = "Elimination of indeterminacy with a dense preserved open"
uses = ["lemma-nagata-elimination-indeterminacy", "def-nagata-elimination-indeterminacy"]
+++
Let $T$ be qcqs, $U\subseteq T$ a quasi-compact open, and $q:W\to T$
separated of finite type with $q^{-1}(U)\to U$ an isomorphism. Then there is
a $U$-admissible blow-up $b:T'\to T$ such that

1. $b^{-1}(U)$ is scheme-theoretically dense in $T'$, and
1. the strict transform $W'\to T'$ of $W$ is an open immersion.

Moreover the blow-up can be taken to factor as
$T'\to T_1\to T$ where $T_1=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I_0}(T)$ for a
finite-type ideal $\mathcal I_0$ with $V(\mathcal I_0)=T\setminus U$.
