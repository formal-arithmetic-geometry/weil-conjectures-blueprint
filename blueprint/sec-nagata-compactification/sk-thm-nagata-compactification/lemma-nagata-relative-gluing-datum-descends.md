Every chart, overlap open, and overlap map in the constructed raw datum
base changes to its named counterpart in $\mathsf R_X$: the base change of
$\mathsf R_{X,\lambda_{\mathrm{raw}}}^{\mathrm{raw}}$ to $S$ has overlaps
$\ell_a^{-1}(W_b)$, and its overlap maps are compatible with the open
immersions $\ell_a$.
