For a subring $A\subseteq\Gamma(S,U)$, a section $s\in\Gamma(S,U)$ and an
open $W\subseteq D(s)$, write $A[1/s]\subseteq\Gamma(S,W)$ for the subring
generated by the restrictions of the elements of $A$ and the inverse of the
restriction of $s$.

Let $\mathsf G_S$ be a finite affine gluing presentation. For each chart
$a$, the *witnesses* of $a$ are the following finitely many elements of
$\Gamma(S,U_a)$: the basic-open elements of $a$; coefficients $c_r$ with
$\sum_r c_rf_{aar}=1$; and numerators of the coefficients exhibiting that
the restrictions of the $f_{abq}$ generate the unit ideal of
$\Gamma(S,D(g_{bar}))$ and that the restrictions of the $f_{bcq}$ generate
the unit ideal of $\Gamma(S,D(f_{abr}f_{acs}))$. Let
$A^{\mathrm{coeff}}_a\subseteq\Gamma(S,U_a)$ be the subring generated by
the witnesses of $a$ together with, for all $b$, $r$ and every basic-open
element $y$ of the chart $b$, a numerator $n\in\Gamma(S,U_a)$ with
$y|_{D(f_{abr})}=n/f_{abr}^k$. Define the *coefficient stage*
$\lambda_{\mathrm{coeff}}(S)$ to be the family
$(A^{\mathrm{coeff}}_a)_a$.
