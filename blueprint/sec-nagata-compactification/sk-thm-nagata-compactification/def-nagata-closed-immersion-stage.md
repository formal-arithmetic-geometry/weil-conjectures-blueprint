Let $g_\lambda\colon Z_\lambda\to Y_\lambda$ be a morphism of finite
presentation between $S_\lambda$-schemes, with $Y_\lambda$ quasi-compact
and quasi-separated, and assume its base change to $S$ is a closed
immersion. Define $\mu_{\mathrm{cl}}(g)\geq\lambda$ to be the index of a
chosen witness supplied by
[lemma-nagata-closed-immersion-stage-exists].
