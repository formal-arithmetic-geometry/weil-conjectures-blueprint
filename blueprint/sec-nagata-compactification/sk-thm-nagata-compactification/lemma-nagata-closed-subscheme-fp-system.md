Every $X_\alpha\hookrightarrow Y$ is of finite presentation and all
transitions are closed immersions. If $Y$ is quasi-compact and
quasi-separated, then every section of $\mathcal I$ over an affine open of
$Y$ lies in some $\mathcal I_\alpha$, and the canonical map
$X\to\varprojlim_\alpha X_\alpha$ is an isomorphism.
