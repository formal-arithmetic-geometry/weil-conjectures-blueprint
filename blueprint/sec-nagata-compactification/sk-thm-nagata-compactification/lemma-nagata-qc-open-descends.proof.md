These are the defining properties of the selected stage and open in
[def-nagata-qc-open-descent], whose existence is shown there: a finite
union of lifted basic opens at a common stage is quasi-compact, and base
change sends each $D(f_{ak,\lambda_W})$ to $D(f_{ak})$, so the inverse image
of the union is $W$.
