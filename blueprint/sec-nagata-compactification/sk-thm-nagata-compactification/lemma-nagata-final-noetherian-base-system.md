In the system of
[def-nagata-final-noetherian-base-system], every $S_\lambda$ is
Noetherian, quasi-compact and locally of finite type over $\mathbf Z$,
every transition map is affine, and the projections
$\pi_\lambda\colon S\to S_\lambda$ form a limit cone, so
$S=\varprojlim_\lambda S_\lambda$.
