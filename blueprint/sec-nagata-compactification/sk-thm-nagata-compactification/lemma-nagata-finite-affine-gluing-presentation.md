For a finite affine gluing presentation $\mathsf G_S$, every overlap
$U_a\cap U_b$ is quasi-compact and is the union of the finitely many basic
opens $D(f_{abr})$, there are finitely many charts and overlap basic opens
(so all gluing identities form finite lists), and gluing the charts along
their overlaps recovers $S$: the canonical map from the glued scheme to $S$
is an isomorphism.
