Every quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme $S$ admits a Noetherian
approximation in the sense of [def-nagata-noetherian-inverse-system]:
$S$ is the limit of a cofiltered system of schemes of finite type over
$\mathbf Z$ with affine transition morphisms.
