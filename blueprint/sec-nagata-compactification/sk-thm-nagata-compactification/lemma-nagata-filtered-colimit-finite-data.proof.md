Every element of a filtered colimit of rings comes from some stage, and
filteredness (for a directed system,
[lemma-nagata-directed-finite-upper-bound]) moves finitely many
representatives to one common stage $i_0$. The finitely many differences
$P_l(y)-Q_l(y)$ vanish in $A$, and an element of a stage that vanishes in
a filtered colimit vanishes at a later stage; filteredness again gives one
stage $i$ past $i_0$ at which all of them vanish. Ring homomorphisms
preserve polynomial identities with integer coefficients, so the
equalities persist after every transition $A_i\to A_j$.
