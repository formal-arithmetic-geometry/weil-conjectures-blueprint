The constructed morphism $p^{\mathrm{cl}}_f\colon\overline X^{\mathrm{cl}}_f\to S$ is proper.

## Proof

The ambient scheme $\overline X^{\mathrm{cl}}_f$ is closed in the proper ambient scheme
of $C_f^{\mathrm{fp}}$. A closed immersion followed by a proper morphism is
proper; this is the properness part of the closed-subscheme construction.
