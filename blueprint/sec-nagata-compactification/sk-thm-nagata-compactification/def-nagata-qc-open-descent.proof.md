We show that such a stage and open exist. The base-changed system has
affine transition morphisms and limit $Y$. By quasi-compactness, $W$ is
covered by finitely many affine opens of $Y$, each contained in the inverse
image of an affine open $V_a$ of $Y_{\lambda_0}$. The inverse images of
$V_a$ in the later stages are affine, and the ring of their inverse image
in $Y$ is the filtered colimit of their rings, so $W$ is a finite union of
basic opens $D(f_{ak})$ with $f_{ak}$ coming from some stage
$\lambda_{ak}$. At a common stage $\lambda_W$ above $\lambda_0$ and all
$\lambda_{ak}$ (for instance
$u(\{\lambda_0\}\cup\{\lambda_{ak}\}_{a,k})$ for a directed index set), the
finite union $\bigcup_{a,k}D(f_{ak,\lambda_W})$ is a quasi-compact open of
$Y_{\lambda_W}$ whose inverse image in $Y$ is $W$.
