For $Y\to S$ as in [def-nagata-final-descended-model], the morphism
$Y_{\lambda_Y}\to S_{\lambda_Y}$ is separated and of
finite presentation, $S_{\lambda_Y}$ is Noetherian, and $\epsilon_Y$ is an
isomorphism over $S$.
