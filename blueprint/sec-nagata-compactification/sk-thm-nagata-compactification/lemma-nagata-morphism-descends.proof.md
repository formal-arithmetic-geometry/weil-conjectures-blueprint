This is the defining property of the selected morphism, and a morphism with
this property exists by the construction in the proof of
[def-nagata-morphism-descent]: the inverse-image opens pull back to the
original opens by [lemma-nagata-qc-open-descends]; on each affine piece,
the descended ring map pulls back to the original map by
[lemma-nagata-affine-fp-descends]; the selected equality stages make
those local maps agree on all overlaps, so their gluing pulls back to the
gluing of the original local maps, namely $\varphi$.
