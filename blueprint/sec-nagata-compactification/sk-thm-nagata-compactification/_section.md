Let $S$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated. Every separated
morphism $f:X\to S$ of finite type admits a factorization

$$
\boxed{X\xrightarrow{\ j\ }\overline X\xrightarrow{\ p\ }S,
\qquad f=p\circ j,}
$$

with $j$ an open immersion and $p$ proper.
