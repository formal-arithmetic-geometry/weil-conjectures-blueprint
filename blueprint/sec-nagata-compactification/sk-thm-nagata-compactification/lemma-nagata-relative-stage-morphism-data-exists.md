Let $S$ be qcqs and let $X\to S$ be locally of finite type with $X$
quasi-compact and quasi-separated. For any choice of the Noetherian inverse
systems $\mathsf N(X)$ and $\mathsf N(S)$, a finite-stage datum for
$X\to S$ ([def-nagata-relative-stage-morphism-data]) exists.
