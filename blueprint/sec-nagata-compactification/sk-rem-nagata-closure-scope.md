The same normalization proof as in [sk-lem-nagata-density-normalization]
works for qcqs $\overline X$ whenever $\overline X\setminus Z$ is
quasi-compact. For an arbitrary non-Noetherian compactification this
condition can fail, and then the open–closed proposition
[sk-prop-sf-open-closed-triangle] gives no exactness statement for the closed
pro-étale pushforward. The schematic closure and dense refinements exist in
the generality of [sk-thm-nagata-compactification].
