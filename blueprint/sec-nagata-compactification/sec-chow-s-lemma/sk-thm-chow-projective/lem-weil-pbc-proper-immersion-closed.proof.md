Let $f:U\to Y$ be universally closed, a homeomorphism onto its image and
surjective on stalks (an immersion has the last two properties). Since $f$
is universally closed it is a closed map, so $f(U)$ is closed in $Y$. Thus
$f$ is a homeomorphism onto a closed subset, and $f^\sharp$ is surjective on
stalks; this is the definition of a closed immersion. A proper morphism is
universally closed, and its image is closed by
[lem-weil-pbc-proper-closed-image].
