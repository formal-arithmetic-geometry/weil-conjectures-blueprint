In the situation of [det-nagata-density-normalization-sheaves], for every
$K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute{e}t}},\Lambda_X)$ there is a canonical
isomorphism in $D(S_{\mathrm{pro\acute{e}t}},\Lambda_S)$

$$
Rp_*j_!K\simeq R(p\circ i)_*j_{Z,!}K .
$$
