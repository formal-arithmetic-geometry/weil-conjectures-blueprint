Let $S$ be Noetherian and let $X\to S$ be separated and of finite type.
Then $X\to S$ is compactifiable: the recursively constructed factorization
$\mathsf C_{\mathrm{Noeth}}(X/S)$ is a compactification of $X\to S$.
