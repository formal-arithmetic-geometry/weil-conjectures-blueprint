Let $S$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated and let $X\to S$ be separated
and of finite type. The constructed morphism $X^{\mathrm{fp}}\to S$ is separated and of
finite presentation, and the constructed map
$i^{\mathrm{fp}}_{X/S}\colon X\to X^{\mathrm{fp}}$ is a closed immersion
over $S$.
