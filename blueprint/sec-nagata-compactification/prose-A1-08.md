This is [Stacks, Tag 081S](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/081S). Its main ingredient is flattening by blow-up
[Stacks, Tag 081R](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/081R), which says the following. If $W\to T$ is of finite type and quasi-separated,
and flat and locally of finite presentation over $U$, then its strict
transform under a suitable $U$-admissible blow-up is flat and of
finite presentation. If we first apply
Lemma [sk-lem-nagata-boundary-divisor], then this strict transform
contains the inverse image of $U$ as a schematically dense open and is
the identity there, and hence it is an open immersion.

A different proof, due to Deligne, resolves partial sections by
blow-ups; see (Con, Theorem 2.4).

In the gluing argument below, a compactification of an open $V$ of $M$
is related to $M$ itself by a proper map defined on a larger open of
the compactification.
