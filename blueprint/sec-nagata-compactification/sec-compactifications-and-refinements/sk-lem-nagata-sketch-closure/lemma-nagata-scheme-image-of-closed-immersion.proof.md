Let $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow Y$ be a closed immersion. Then
$i_{\mathrm{im}}\circ i^{\mathrm{im}}=i$ is a closed immersion, so
$i^{\mathrm{im}}$ is a closed immersion. Its range is closed and, by
[lemma-nagata-scheme-image-ideal-qc] ($i$ is quasi-compact), dense in
$\operatorname{SIm}(i)$; hence $i^{\mathrm{im}}$ is a homeomorphism. On
stalks it is surjective, being a closed immersion, and injective, because
$\mathcal O_{\operatorname{SIm}(i)}\to i^{\mathrm{im}}_*\mathcal O_Z$ is
injective on stalks by [lemma-nagata-scheme-image-ideal-qc]. So
$i^{\mathrm{im}}$ is an isomorphism, and it is compatible with the maps to
$Y$ by the factorization $i_{\mathrm{im}}\circ i^{\mathrm{im}}=i$.
