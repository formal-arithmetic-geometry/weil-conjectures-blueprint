The ideal of the image is
$\ker(\mathcal O_T\to i_*\mathcal O_U)$, which is quasi-coherent
because $i$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated. For the same reason
its formation commutes with restriction to opens of $T$, and on an open
where the immersion is closed, the image is therefore $U$ itself.
Hence $U$ is open in its image. By construction, a section of
$\mathcal O_{\overline U}$ vanishing on $U$ is zero, which is what
schematic density means. For these statements see
[Stacks, Tag 01R8](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01R8), [Stacks, Tag 01RG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01RG).
To prove uniqueness, note that the equalizer of the two maps is a closed
subscheme of $T$, since it is the inverse image of the diagonal of $Y/S$.
Its ideal restricts to zero on $U$, and hence is zero because $U$ is
scheme-theoretically dense in $T$.
