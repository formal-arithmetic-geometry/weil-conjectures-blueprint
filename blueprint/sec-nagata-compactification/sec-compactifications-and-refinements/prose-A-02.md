To show that $Rp_*j_!$ does not depend on the compactification, we must
compare two of them, and in general neither maps to the other. The plane
$\mathbf A^2$ is open in $\mathbf P^2$ and in
$\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1$, but the identity of $\mathbf A^2$ extends
to a morphism in neither direction: the map
$(x:y:z)\mapsto((x:z),(y:z))$ is undefined at $(1:0:0)$ and $(0:1:0)$,
and its inverse is undefined at $(\infty,\infty)$. What exists is a third
compactification mapping to both, here the closure of $\mathbf A^2$ in
$\mathbf P^2\times\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1$. We therefore compare
along refinements; the condition $h^{-1}(X)=X$ is what lets the proof of
Proposition [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence] compute $Rh_*j'_!$ by
restriction to $X$. Density makes refinements unique: $X=\operatorname{Spec}k$
is open in the non-dense compactification $\operatorname{Spec}k\sqcup\mathbf P^1_k$,
and every automorphism of $\mathbf P^1_k$ gives a refinement of it to
itself. Proposition [sk-prop-nagata-sketch-comp-category] proves that dense
compactifications and their refinements form a cofiltered category.
