In the situation of the base-changed system $Y_\lambda$ with limit $Y$, let
$W_{1,\lambda_0},W_{2,\lambda_0}\subseteq Y_{\lambda_0}$ be quasi-compact
opens whose inverse images in $Y$ agree. Define
$\lambda_{W_1=W_2}\geq\lambda_0$ to be a selected stage such that the
inverse images of $W_{1,\lambda_0}$ and $W_{2,\lambda_0}$ in $Y_\lambda$
agree for every $\lambda\geq\lambda_{W_1=W_2}$.
