This is the defining property of the stage selected in
[def-nagata-qc-open-equality-stage]: chartwise, equality of finite unions
of basic opens amounts to two finite lists of radical containments, each
witnessed by a power identity that holds at its witness stage and at every
later stage, so above a common upper bound both inclusions hold on every
affine chart.
