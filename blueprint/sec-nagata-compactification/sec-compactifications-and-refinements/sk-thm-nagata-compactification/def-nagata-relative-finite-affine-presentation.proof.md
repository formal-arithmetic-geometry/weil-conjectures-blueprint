Fix any index $\lambda_0$. The affine opens of $X$ lying over some affine
open of $S_{\lambda_0}$ form a basis of $X$, so quasi-compactness gives
finitely many of them covering $X$. The overlaps and their identifications
are restrictions of the identity of $X$, so the identity, inverse, and
cocycle equations hold. If $X$ is moreover quasi-separated, every overlap
$W_a\cap W_b$ is quasi-compact.
