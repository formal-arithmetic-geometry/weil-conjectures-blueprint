Each $D(f_{abr})$ is a basic open of the affine $U_a$, hence quasi-compact,
and $U_a\cap U_b$ is the finite union of them. The index sets of charts and
of overlap basic opens are finite by definition, so all recorded equations
form a finite list. The recovery assertion is the ordinary gluing theorem
applied to the open cover $(U_a)_a$ of $S$.
