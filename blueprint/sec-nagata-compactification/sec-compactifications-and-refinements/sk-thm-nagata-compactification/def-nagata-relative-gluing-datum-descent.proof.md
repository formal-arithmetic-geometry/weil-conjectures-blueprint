Each affine chart $W_a$ is of finite presentation over the inverse image of
the affine open $V_a$ in $S$, whose ring is the filtered colimit of the
rings of its inverse images at later stages, so it descends to an affine
scheme of finite presentation at some stage; cofilteredness gives one
common index $\lambda_1$ for the finitely many charts. Since $X$ is
quasi-separated, the overlaps $\ell_a^{-1}(W_b)$ are quasi-compact, so they
descend. Because every overlap map takes values in the fixed scheme $Y_b$,
the datum can be transported along every transition morphism; functoriality
of base change supplies the comparison maps and coherence, so the raw datum
over $S_{\lambda_{\mathrm{raw}}}$ is well defined.
