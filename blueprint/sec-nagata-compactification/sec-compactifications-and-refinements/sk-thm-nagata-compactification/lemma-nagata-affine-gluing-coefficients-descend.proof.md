The generating set of $A^{\mathrm{coeff}}_a$ is finite and contains the
witnesses of $a$, among them the basic-open elements. For a basic-open
element $y$ of the chart $b$, the generating set contains a numerator $n$
with $y|_{D(f_{abr})}=n/f_{abr}^k$; since $n$ and $f_{abr}$ lie in
$A^{\mathrm{coeff}}_a$, this restriction lies in
$A^{\mathrm{coeff}}_a[1/f_{abr}]$.
