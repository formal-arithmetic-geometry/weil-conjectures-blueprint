We show that such a stage exists. On a finite affine cover of
$Y_{\lambda_0}$, choose finite basic-open presentations of both opens.
Equality of the two inverse images in $Y$ amounts, chartwise, to finitely
many radical containments in the colimit rings, each witnessed by a power
identity involving finitely many coefficients. By
[lemma-nagata-filtered-colimit-finite-data] each identity holds at some
stage and at every later stage; a common upper bound of these finitely many
witness stages (for instance the selected upper bound
$u(\cdot)$ of [def-nagata-directed-finite-upper-bound] for a directed
index set) imposes both inclusions on every affine chart, hence equality of
the inverse images at every later stage.
