This is the defining property of the stage selected in
[def-nagata-morphism-equality-stage]. Concretely, above that stage the two
maps have equal inverse images of every target chart
([lemma-nagata-qc-open-eq-eventually]); on a finite affine cover of each
common inverse image, their ring maps agree on a finite generating set and
hence agree. The local equalities glue to the asserted global equality and
persist at later stages.
