All morphism-theoretic notions used below are the standard ones: *quasi-compact*, *quasi-separated*,
*separated*, *locally of finite type* and *of finite
type*, *locally of finite presentation* and *of finite
presentation*, *surjective*, *open immersion*,
*closed immersion*, *immersion*, *universally closed*,
*proper*, *finite*, *integral*, *flat*,
*unramified*, *etale*, *geometric fibre* and
*geometric point* (see
Definition [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions]).
One further notion is fixed here: *projective*
([def-pc-projective-morphism]).
