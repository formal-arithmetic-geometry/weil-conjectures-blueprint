Let $T$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated and $U\subseteq T$ a
quasi-compact open. There is a $U$-admissible blow-up $T'\to T$
([sk-def-nagata-admissible]) such that its inverse image of $U$ is
schematically dense in $T'$ and its complement is the support of an
effective Cartier divisor.
