*Admissibility.* $\mathcal I$ is of finite type and $V(\mathcal I)\cap U=
\varnothing$, so $\mathcal I|_U=\mathcal O_U$ and $b$ is $U$-admissible
([def-nagata-admissible-blowup]).

*Cartier boundary.* By [thm-nagata-blowup-principalizes-center] the
extended ideal $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'}$ is locally generated by a single
non-zero-divisor; on a standard chart $\operatorname{Spec} R[I/a]$ (with
$I=(a_1,\dots,a_r)$ and $a=a_i$) it is generated by $a$. So it defines an
effective Cartier divisor $E$. The support of $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'}$
is $b^{-1}(V(\mathcal I))=b^{-1}(T\setminus U)=T'\setminus b^{-1}(U)$.

*Density.* On a chart where $E$ is cut out by the non-zero-divisor $a$, the
restriction map from the chart ring $B$ to the complement of $E$ is the
localization $B\to B_a$, which is injective since $a$ is a
non-zero-divisor. Hence the complement $b^{-1}(U)$ of $E$ is
scheme-theoretically dense; this is also the last assertion of
[lemma-nagata-conrad-dense-in-admissible-blowup] with $V(\mathcal I)=
T\setminus U$.

*Persistence.* Let $c:T''\to T'$ be a $b^{-1}(U)$-admissible blow-up. By
[lemma-nagata-cartier-total-transform-blowup], the pullback of the
invertible ideal $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'}$ to $T''$ is invertible: locally,
if $t$ generates it, multiplication by $t$ is injective on each power of the
blow-up ideal (a submodule of the structure sheaf), hence on the Rees algebra
and on its degree-zero localized charts. Its support is
$c^{-1}(T'\setminus b^{-1}(U))=T''\setminus(bc)^{-1}(U)$. Scheme-theoretic
density of $(bc)^{-1}(U)$ follows from the chart argument above applied to
the generator $t$, or from [lemma-nagata-admissible-blowup-preserves-density]
since $b^{-1}(U)$ is quasi-compact and scheme-theoretically dense in $T'$.
