Let $\mathcal L\subseteq\mathcal O_T$ be an invertible ideal and let
$T'=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal K}(T)\to T$ be the blow-up of $T$ along an
arbitrary quasi-coherent ideal $\mathcal K$. Then the extended ideal
$\mathcal L\mathcal O_{T'}$ is again invertible, i.e. locally generated by
a non-zero-divisor.
