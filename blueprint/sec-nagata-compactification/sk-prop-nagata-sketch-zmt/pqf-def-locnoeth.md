A scheme $X$ is *locally Noetherian* if it admits an affine open
covering $X=\bigcup_iU_i$ with $\mathcal O_X(U_i)$ Noetherian for all
$i$.

Open subschemes of locally Noetherian schemes are locally Noetherian. A
locally Noetherian morphism situation is one where the target is locally
Noetherian.
