title = "Projective compactification of an affine piece"
uses = ["def-nagata-projective-space-base-change", "def-nagata-affine-compactification"]

Let $S$ be Noetherian, let $U$ be an affine scheme with a morphism $U\to S$
of finite type, and let $\iota:U\to\mathbf A^n_S$ be an immersion over $S$
(as supplied by [def-nagata-quasi-affine-immersion-affine-space]). Let
$\overline U\subseteq\mathbf P^n_S$ be the scheme-theoretic image of the
composite immersion $U\xrightarrow{\iota}\mathbf A^n_S\hookrightarrow
\mathbf P^n_S$. Then $\overline U$ is a closed subscheme of
$\mathbf P^n_S$, so $\overline U\to S$ is projective and in particular
proper, and $U\to\overline U$ is a quasi-compact, scheme-theoretically dense
open immersion. Thus $U\to\overline U\to S$ is a dense projective
compactification of $U\to S$.
