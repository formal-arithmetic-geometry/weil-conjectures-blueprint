Under the hypotheses of
[lemma-nagata-dense-affine-cover-exists], choose one witness and denote
its finite family and its total intersection by

$$
\mathcal U_X=(U_1,\ldots,U_n),
\qquad D_X=\bigcap_{i=1}^nU_i.
$$
