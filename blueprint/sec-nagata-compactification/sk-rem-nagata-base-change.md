For every $S'\to S$, the base change of a compactification is an open
immersion followed by a proper morphism, and this suffices to define the
compactly supported direct image of $X\times_SS'\to S'$. When the
base-changed ambient scheme is Noetherian, as for schemes of finite type over
$k$, [sk-lem-nagata-density-normalization] identifies it with the one defined
by the schematic closure. Schematic density and schematic closure commute
with flat base change, but density can be lost under an arbitrary base
change. In that case the schematic closure of $X\times_SS'$ in
$\overline X\times_SS'$ is a dense compactification, and a base-changed
common refinement can be replaced by its closure in the same way. The
comparisons in the main text are made on a further common refinement, where
they are canonical by uniqueness.
