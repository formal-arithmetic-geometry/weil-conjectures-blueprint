Since $I^n=(a^n)$, every homogeneous element of degree $n$ of the Rees
algebra is $r(at)^n$ for some $r\in A$. In particular every element of
the irrelevant ideal lies in the ideal generated by $at$, so the
standard-open cover lemma makes $D_+(at)$ the whole Proj.

A degree-zero fraction at $at$ has the form $r(at)^n/(at)^n$ and is the
image of $r\in A$, so the canonical map is surjective. If $r\in A$ maps to
zero, then $(at)^kr=0$ in the Rees algebra for some $k$, i.e. $a^kr=0$ in
$A$; since $a$ is a non-zero-divisor, $r=0$. Hence the canonical map is
bijective.

The structure morphism restricted to $D_+(at)$ is $\operatorname{Spec}$ of
this bijection, hence an isomorphism, and $D_+(at)$ is the whole Proj.
