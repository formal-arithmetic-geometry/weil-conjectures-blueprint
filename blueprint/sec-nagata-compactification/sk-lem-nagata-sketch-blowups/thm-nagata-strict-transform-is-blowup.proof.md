Write $U=X\setminus V(\mathcal I)$ and let
$\Omega\subseteq Y\times_XX'$ be the open locus over $U$, whose
scheme-theoretic closure is the strict transform
([def-nagata-strict-transform]). Both $\psi_{f,\mathcal I}$ and the
inclusion of $\Omega$ are cut out by quasi-coherent kernels, and it
suffices to identify the two kernels. The blow-up of
$\mathcal I\mathcal O_Y$ is an isomorphism over the preimage of $U$
([thm-nagata-global-blowup-complement]), which identifies $\Omega$
with the preimage $\Omega'$ of $f^{-1}(U)$ in
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I\mathcal O_Y}(Y)$, compatibly with
$\psi_{f,\mathcal I}$ — the two composites into $X'$ agree by the
uniqueness part of [thm-nagata-blowup-universal-property], since both
lie over $U$ where the pulled-back ideal is the unit ideal. The
exceptional ideal is invertible, i.e. locally generated by a
nonzerodivisor, so $\Omega'$ is schematically dense in
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I\mathcal O_Y}(Y)$ (the blow-up part of
[lemma-nagata-admissible-blowup-preserves-density]). Hence
precomposing $\psi_{f,\mathcal I}$ with the schematically dense open
$\Omega'\cong\Omega$ does not change the kernel, and the kernel of
$\psi_{f,\mathcal I}$ equals the kernel of the inclusion of $\Omega$.
Since $\psi_{f,\mathcal I}$ is a closed immersion
([def-nagata-strict-transform-blowup-comparison]), it factors as an
isomorphism onto the strict transform, and the stated compatibilities
hold by construction.
