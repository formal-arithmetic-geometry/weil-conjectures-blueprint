If $\mathcal L$ is an invertible ideal on $Y$, the structure morphism

$$
\operatorname{Proj}_Y\Bigl(\bigoplus_{n\geq0}\mathcal L^n\Bigr)
\longrightarrow Y
$$

of the relative Proj of the Rees algebra of $\mathcal L$ is an
isomorphism.
