A quasi-coherent ideal $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ is an
*effective Cartier ideal* if every point of $X$ has an affine open
neighbourhood $U$ such that the ideal $\mathcal I(U)\subseteq\mathcal O_X(U)$
is generated by a single non-zero-divisor of $\mathcal O_X(U)$.
