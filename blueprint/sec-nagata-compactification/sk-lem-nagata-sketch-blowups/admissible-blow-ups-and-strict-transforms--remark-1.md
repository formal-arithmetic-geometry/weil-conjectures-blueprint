The strict transform can be handled directly through this
scheme-theoretic-closure definition: the identification over the preserved
open ([lemma-nagata-strict-transform-over-complement]) follows from flat
base change of scheme-theoretic images along a quasi-compact flat
monomorphism, without the torsion-quotient chart model. Likewise the
identification of the strict transform with the blow-up of the extended
ideal ([thm-nagata-strict-transform-is-blowup], Stacks Tag 080E) can be
obtained by the universal-property route recorded in
[def-nagata-strict-transform-blowup-comparison] and in the proof of
[thm-nagata-strict-transform-is-blowup] — a comparison morphism into the
fibre product which is a closed immersion by a chart-wise surjectivity
computation ([lemma-nagata-strict-chart-map-surjective]), together with
schematic density of the exceptional complement — rather than by gluing the
chart-ring isomorphisms of [def-nagata-strict-transform-chart-ring]
through [prop-nagata-strict-transform-closed-fiber-product].
