The principalization of the center can be proved in its effective-Cartier
form directly from [lemma-nagata-chart-extended-ideal-principal] and
[lemma-nagata-chart-generator-regular]: being effective Cartier is a
local condition, so neither the gluing step through
[lemma-nagata-chart-generators-associated] nor the invertible-sheaf
half ([def-nagata-invertible-ideal],
[lemma-nagata-effective-cartier-is-invertible],
[lemma-nagata-invertible-ideal-is-effective-cartier]) is needed. This
argument requires no finite-type hypothesis on the center.
