The product of two invertible ideal sheaves is invertible.

## Proof

Let $\mathcal L_1,\mathcal L_2$ be invertible ideals on $X$ and let $x\in X$.
By [def-nagata-invertible-ideal] there are affine opens $V_1,V_2\ni x$ with
$\mathcal L_i(V_i)$ generated by a non-zero-divisor. Choose an affine open
$W\ni x$ which is a basic open of both $V_1$ and $V_2$. Restriction to a
basic open is a localization, which is flat and hence preserves
non-zero-divisors, so $\mathcal L_1(W)=(a)$ and $\mathcal L_2(W)=(b)$ with
$a,b$ non-zero-divisors of $\mathcal O_X(W)$. On the affine $W$ the product
ideal is $(\mathcal L_1\mathcal L_2)(W)=\mathcal L_1(W)\mathcal L_2(W)=(ab)$,
and $ab$ is a non-zero-divisor because multiplication by $a$ and by $b$ are
injective. Hence $\mathcal L_1\mathcal L_2$ is invertible.
