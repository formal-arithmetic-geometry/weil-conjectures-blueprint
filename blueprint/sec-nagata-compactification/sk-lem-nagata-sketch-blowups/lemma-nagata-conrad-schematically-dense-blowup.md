Let $X$ be qcqs and let $U\subseteq X$ be a quasi-compact open. There is a
$U$-admissible blow-up $X_1\to X$ in which the inverse image of $U$ is
scheme-theoretically dense. More generally, for every $U$-admissible
blow-up $\widetilde X\to X$ there are a $U$-admissible blow-up $X_1\to X$
in which the inverse image of $U$ is scheme-theoretically dense and a
morphism $X_1\to\widetilde X$ over $X$.
