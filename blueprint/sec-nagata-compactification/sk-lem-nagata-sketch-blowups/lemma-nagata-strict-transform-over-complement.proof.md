Write $p_1\colon Y\times_XX'\to Y$ and $p_2\colon Y\times_XX'\to X'$ for
the projections. By definition $\Omega=p_2^{-1}(\pi^{-1}(U))$. Restricting
a morphism to the preimage of an open gives a fibre square, so $\Omega$
with the restriction $p_2|_\Omega\colon\Omega\to\pi^{-1}(U)$ is a fibre
product of $p_2$ and the open immersion $\pi^{-1}(U)\hookrightarrow X'$.
Pasting this square with the fibre square defining $Y\times_XX'$ shows that
$\Omega$, with $p_1|_\Omega$ and $p_2|_\Omega$, is a fibre product of $f$
and the composite $\pi^{-1}(U)\hookrightarrow X'\to X$, which is
$\pi^{-1}(U)\xrightarrow{\sim}U\hookrightarrow X$. Replacing
$\pi^{-1}(U)$ by $U$ along the isomorphism $\pi|_{\pi^{-1}(U)}$ gives the
asserted fibre square.
