Let $A$ be a ring and let $I=(a)\subseteq A$ be the ideal generated by a
non-zero-divisor $a$, so that $\widetilde I$ is an invertible ideal on
$V=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$. In the Rees algebra
$\bigoplus_{n\geq0}I^n$, let $at$ denote $a$ placed in degree one. Then the
standard open $D_+(at)$ is all of
$\operatorname{Proj}(\bigoplus_{n\geq0}I^n)$, and the canonical map from
$A$ to the degree-zero homogeneous localization of
$\bigoplus_{n\geq0}I^n$ at $at$ is bijective. Consequently the structure
morphism

$$
\operatorname{Proj}\Bigl(\bigoplus_{n\geq0}I^n\Bigr)\longrightarrow V
$$

is an isomorphism.
