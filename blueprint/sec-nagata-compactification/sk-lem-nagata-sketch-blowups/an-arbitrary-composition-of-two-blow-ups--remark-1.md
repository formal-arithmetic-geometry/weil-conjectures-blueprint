Stacks Tag 0803 can be recast purely in ideal-sheaf terms: $Z'$ is replaced
by a finite-type ideal sheaf $\mathcal J$ on the blow-up, and the conclusion
becomes the inverse-image equality
$\mathcal L\cdot\mathcal O_{X'}=\mathcal I_E^d\cdot\mathcal J$ for a
finite-type $\mathcal L\subseteq\mathcal I^d$. No relative-Proj quotient is
formed, and the support bound is recovered afterwards from this equality.
