Let $A\to B$ be a ring map, let $I\subseteq A$, and let $a\in I$.
Define

$$
C_a(A,B,I)=B\otimes_A A[I/a].
$$

We regard $a$ as an element of $C_a(A,B,I)$ through the structural map
$A\to C_a(A,B,I)$.
