Let $\Omega\subseteq Y\times_XX'$ be the open part lying over
$X\setminus V(\mathcal I)$, so that $Y^{\mathrm{st}}$ is the
scheme-theoretic image of $\Omega\hookrightarrow Y\times_XX'$. Fix a chart
datum $c=(U,V,a)$ and put $C=C_a(A,B,I)$. The preimage of $V(\mathcal I)$
in $\operatorname{Spec}(C)$ is cut out by $IC$, which is generated by $a$
because $I\,A[I/a]=(a)$ ([lemma-nagata-chart-extended-ideal-principal]).
Hence $\iota_c^{-1}(\Omega)=D(a)=\operatorname{Spec}(C_a)$, which is affine.
The chart images cover $Y\times_XX'$ (the $\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ cover
$Y$ over the $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$, and the $a$-charts cover the
blow-up of each $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$), so
$\Omega\hookrightarrow Y\times_XX'$ is quasi-compact and the ideal of its
scheme-theoretic image is computed chart by chart and commutes with
restriction to the open charts ([lemma-nagata-scheme-image-ideal-qc]). On
the chart it is the kernel of $C\to C_a$, which is $\Gamma_{(a)}(C)$ by
[lemma-nagata-power-torsion-kernel-localization]. This proves the
chartwise statement.

Let $\mathcal J^{\mathrm{st}}$ be the ideal of $Y^{\mathrm{st}}$. By the
chartwise statement $\mathcal J^{\mathrm{st}}$ satisfies the condition in
the definition of the chart model, so
$\mathcal J^{\mathrm{st}}\subseteq\mathcal J^{\mathrm{st}}_{\mathrm{chart}}$.
Conversely, the restriction of $\mathcal J^{\mathrm{st}}_{\mathrm{chart}}$
to every chart is contained in $\Gamma_{(a)}(C)$, which is the restriction
of $\mathcal J^{\mathrm{st}}$; since the charts cover $Y\times_XX'$ and
inclusions of quasi-coherent ideals can be checked on an open cover,
$\mathcal J^{\mathrm{st}}_{\mathrm{chart}}\subseteq\mathcal J^{\mathrm{st}}$.
Hence the two ideals are equal, and the last assertion is the chartwise
statement once more.
