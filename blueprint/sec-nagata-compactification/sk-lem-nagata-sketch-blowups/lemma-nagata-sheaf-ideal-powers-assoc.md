For a quasi-coherent ideal $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$, the canonical
maps $\mathcal I^m\otimes\mathcal I^n\to\mathcal I^{m+n}$ are associative
and have $\mathcal I^0=\mathcal O_X$ as a two-sided unit.
