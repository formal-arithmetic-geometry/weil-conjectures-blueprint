We prove the module versions; the ideal versions are the case
$\mathcal F=\mathcal O_X$, where all submodules are ideals. Let
$j\colon W\hookrightarrow X$ be the inclusion; it is quasi-compact because
$W$ is quasi-compact and $X$ is quasi-separated, so direct images along
$j$ (and along the inclusions of the quasi-compact opens below) of
quasi-coherent modules are quasi-coherent.

*Second assertion.* Let $\mathcal H\subseteq\mathcal F$ be quasi-coherent
with $\mathcal G:=\mathcal H|_W$ of finite type. Choose affine opens
$V_1,\ldots,V_n$ with $X=W\cup V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_n$. Inductively suppose
that a finite type quasi-coherent submodule
$\mathcal G_r\subseteq\mathcal H|_{W_r}$ has been constructed on
$W_r=W\cup V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_r$ which restricts to $\mathcal G$ on $W$
(start with $\mathcal G_0=\mathcal G$ on $W_0=W$). Put
$V_{r+1}=\operatorname{Spec}(R)$. On $V_{r+1}$ take the inverse image
$\mathcal K$ of the direct image of
$\mathcal G_r|_{W_r\cap V_{r+1}}$ under the restriction map from
$\mathcal H|_{V_{r+1}}$. The same quasi-compact-open direct-image argument
shows that $\mathcal K$ is quasi-coherent, and by construction
$\mathcal K\subseteq\mathcal H|_{V_{r+1}}$ and its restriction to
$W_r\cap V_{r+1}$ is $\mathcal G_r$. Write
$\mathcal K=\widetilde N$. The intersection is quasi-compact. Cover it by
finitely many basic opens $D(f_i)$ on which $\mathcal G_r$ is generated by
finitely many sections. Because $\mathcal G_r\subseteq\widetilde N$ there,
after shrinking the basic opens if necessary and clearing denominators, those
generators are represented by finitely many elements $x_{ij}\in N$. Let
$N'\subseteq N$ be generated by all $x_{ij}$. Enlarging the denominator
powers when clearing them ensures
$\widetilde{N'}|_{D(f_i)}=\mathcal G_r|_{D(f_i)}$: one inclusion holds by
construction, and the other is checked on the chosen finite generators.
Hence $\widetilde{N'}\subseteq\mathcal H|_{V_{r+1}}$ and $\mathcal G_r$
agree on the whole intersection and glue to a finite type submodule
$\mathcal G_{r+1}\subseteq\mathcal H|_{W_{r+1}}$ restricting to
$\mathcal G$ on $W$. Finite induction gives a finite type quasi-coherent
submodule $\mathcal G_n\subseteq\mathcal H$ on $X$ with
$\mathcal G_n|_W=\mathcal H|_W$.

*First assertion.* Given a finite type quasi-coherent
$\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F|_W$, let $\mathcal H$ be the inverse image of
$j_*\mathcal G$ under $\mathcal F\to j_*j^*\mathcal F$. It is
quasi-coherent, being a kernel of a map of quasi-coherent modules, and
$\mathcal H|_W=\mathcal G$ is of finite type. The second assertion gives a
finite type quasi-coherent submodule of $\mathcal H\subseteq\mathcal F$
restricting to $\mathcal G$ on $W$.
