For $x\in I$, the identity $x=a(x/a)$ gives
$IA[I/a]\subseteq(a)$. Since $a\in I$ and $1\in A[I/a]$, the element
$a=a\cdot1$ belongs to $IA[I/a]$, so every multiple of $a$ belongs to that
ideal and $(a)\subseteq IA[I/a]$.
