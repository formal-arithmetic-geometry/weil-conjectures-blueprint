Let $X$ be a scheme and let $\mathcal I,\mathcal J$ be quasi-coherent ideals
on $X$. If $\mathcal I\mathcal J$ is invertible (locally generated by a
non-zero-divisor), then $\mathcal I$ and $\mathcal J$ are invertible.
