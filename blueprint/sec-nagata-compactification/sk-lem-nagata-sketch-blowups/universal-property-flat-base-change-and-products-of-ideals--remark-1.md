The universal property can be proved under the Stacks-style hypothesis that
the inverse image ideal sheaf of the center is effective Cartier, entirely
chart-locally: existence is glued from the universal property of the chart
ring $A[\mathcal I/a]$ on affine probes, and uniqueness follows from
extensionality for homomorphisms out of the chart ring together with the
localization on chart overlaps. Along this route the graded-map and
tensor-power scaffolding ([thm-nagata-blowup-universal-property],
[lemma-nagata-invertible-ideal-tensor-power],
[lemma-nagata-relative-proj-invertible-tensor-powers]) is not needed.
