Let $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be a quasi-coherent ideal. If
$g\colon Y\to X$ is flat, then the image of $g^*(\mathcal I^n)$ in
$\mathcal O_Y$ is $(\mathcal I\mathcal O_Y)^n$ for every $n$.
