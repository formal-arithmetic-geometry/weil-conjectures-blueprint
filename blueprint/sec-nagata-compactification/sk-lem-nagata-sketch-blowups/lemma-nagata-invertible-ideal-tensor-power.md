If $\mathcal L\subseteq\mathcal O_Y$ is an invertible ideal, then for every
$n\geq0$ the power $\mathcal L^n$ is an invertible ideal.

## Proof

Let $y\in Y$. By [def-nagata-invertible-ideal] there is an affine open
$V\ni y$ with $\mathcal L(V)=(a)$ for a non-zero-divisor
$a\in\mathcal O_Y(V)$. Powers of quasi-coherent ideals are computed on
affine opens by taking powers of ideals, so
$\mathcal L^n(V)=\mathcal L(V)^n=(a^n)$, and $a^n$ is again a
non-zero-divisor. Thus $\mathcal L^n$ is generated near every point by a
single non-zero-divisor, i.e. it is invertible. (For $n=0$,
$\mathcal L^0=\mathcal O_Y$ is generated by $1$.)

Moreover, the multiplication map $\mathcal L^{\otimes n}\to\mathcal L^n$ is
an isomorphism: on such a $V$ the source is free with basis
$a^{\otimes n}$, the target is free with basis $a^n$ (because $a^n$ is a
non-zero-divisor), and multiplication sends the first basis to the second;
an isomorphism on an open cover is an isomorphism of sheaves.
