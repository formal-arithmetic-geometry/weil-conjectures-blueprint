If $\mathcal I$ is a quasi-coherent ideal on $X$, then on
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)$ the extended ideal
$\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)}$ is invertible:
it is locally generated by a single non-zero-divisor, and so defines an
effective Cartier divisor.
