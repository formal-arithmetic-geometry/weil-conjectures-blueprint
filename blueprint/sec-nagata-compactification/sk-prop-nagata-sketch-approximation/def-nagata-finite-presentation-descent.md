Define

$$
X^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}}
$$

by gluing the valid relative datum
$\mathsf R_{X,\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}}$. Define

$$
\epsilon_X\colon
X^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}}
\times_{S_{\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}}}S
\longrightarrow X
$$

by gluing the chartwise base-change identifications.
