Let $T$ be qcqs, $U\subseteq T$ a dense quasi-compact open, and $Y\to S$
proper. Every $S$-map $a:U\to Y$ extends to an $S$-map $T'\to Y$ after a
$U$-admissible blow-up $T'\to T$ ([sk-def-nagata-admissible]).
