The constructed factorization $C_{S'}$ is a compactification of
$X\times_SS'\to S'$. Consequently, if $f\colon X\to S$ is compactifiable,
then so is every base change $u\colon X'\to S'$ of $f$ along a morphism
$S'\to S$.
