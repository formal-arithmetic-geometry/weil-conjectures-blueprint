Let $S$ be a qcqs scheme, let $s_i\colon X_i\to S$ ($i=1,2$) be separated
and of finite type, and let $j_i\colon U\hookrightarrow X_i$ be
quasi-compact open immersions with $s_1\circ j_1=s_2\circ j_2$. Then a
common modification of $X_1$ and $X_2$ along $U$ exists.
