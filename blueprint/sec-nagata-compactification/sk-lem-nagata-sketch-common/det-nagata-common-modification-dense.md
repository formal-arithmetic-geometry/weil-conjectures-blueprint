kind = "lemma"
title = "Density of the common open in a common modification"
uses = ["def-nagata-common-modification"]
+++
Let $S$ be qcqs, let $T_1,T_2$ be separated finite-type $S$-schemes with
quasi-compact open immersions $j_i:U\hookrightarrow T_i$ over $S$, and let
$(\pi_i:T_i'\to T_i,\ w:T\to S,\ k_i:T_i'\to T,\ u_i:U\to T_i')$ be a common
modification of $T_1,T_2$ along $U$ ([def-nagata-common-modification]); put
$j=k_1u_1=k_2u_2:U\to T$.

1. If $j_i(U)$ is topologically dense in $T_i$ for $i=1,2$, then $j(U)$ is
   dense in $T$.
1. If $j_i(U)$ is scheme-theoretically dense in $T_i$ for $i=1,2$, then
   $j(U)$ is scheme-theoretically dense in $T$.
