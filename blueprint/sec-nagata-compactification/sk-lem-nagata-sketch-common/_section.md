Let $S$ be qcqs and $U\subseteq T_i$ ($i=1,2$) be quasi-compact open
immersions between separated finite-type $S$-schemes. There are
$U$-admissible blow-ups $T_i'\to T_i$ ([sk-def-nagata-admissible]) and open
immersions $T_i'\to T$ agreeing on $U$, with $T$ separated and of finite
type over $S$. If $U$ is dense in both $T_i$, it is dense in $T$.
