Let $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be a quasi-coherent ideal with support
$Z=V(\mathcal I)$. For every affine open $V\subseteq X$ disjoint from $Z$,
$\Gamma(V,\mathcal I)=\Gamma(V,\mathcal O_X)$.
