Coefficientwise maps compose like the underlying ring homomorphisms:
applying $\varphi$ and then $\psi$ to every coefficient is applying
$\psi\varphi$. The composite $A\to A_s\to(A_s)_t\to A_{st}$ is the
localization map $A\to A_{st}$, so the composite of the three
coefficientwise maps is $\rho_{st}$; both send a homogeneous coefficient
$x\in I^n$ to $x/1$. The morphisms of affine blow-ups are induced by
these graded homomorphisms on Proj, contravariantly and compatibly with
composition, which gives the second statement.
