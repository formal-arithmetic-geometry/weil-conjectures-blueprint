Let $A$ be a ring, $I\subseteq A$ an ideal and $a\in A$ (in the blow-up
charts, $a\in I$). Define $A[I/a]$ to be the $A$-subalgebra of $A_a$
generated by the fractions $x/a$ with $x\in I$.
