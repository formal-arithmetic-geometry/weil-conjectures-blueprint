Let $\mathcal I$ be a $U$-admissible blow-up center on $X$ and let
$\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)\to X$ be the associated
$U$-admissible blow-up. Then the restriction
$\pi^{-1}(U)\to U$ of $\pi$ is an isomorphism.
