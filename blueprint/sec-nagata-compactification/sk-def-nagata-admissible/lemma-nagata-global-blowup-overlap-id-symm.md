Let $U$ be an affine open contained in the affine opens $V_i$ and $V_j$.
The overlap isomorphisms of [def-nagata-global-blowup-overlap-basic-map]
satisfy $\omega_{ii,U}=\mathrm{id}$ and
$\omega_{ij,U}^{-1}=\omega_{ji,U}$.
