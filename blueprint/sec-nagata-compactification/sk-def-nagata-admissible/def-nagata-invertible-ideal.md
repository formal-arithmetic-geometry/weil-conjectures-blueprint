A quasi-coherent ideal $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ is
*invertible* if every point of $X$ has an affine open neighbourhood $U$ such
that the ideal $\mathcal I(U)\subseteq\mathcal O_X(U)$ is generated by a
single non-zero-divisor of $\mathcal O_X(U)$. Such an ideal is also called
an *effective Cartier ideal*; its vanishing locus is an effective Cartier
divisor.
