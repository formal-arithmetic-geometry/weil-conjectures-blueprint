After the canonical identification $A_1\cong A$, the Rees localization map
at $1$ is the identity: the composite of
$\rho_1\colon\mathcal R_A(I)\to\mathcal R_{A_1}(IA_1)$ with the
coefficientwise map induced by $A_1\cong A$ is the identity of
$\mathcal R_A(I)$. Consequently the same holds for the induced morphisms of
affine blow-ups.
