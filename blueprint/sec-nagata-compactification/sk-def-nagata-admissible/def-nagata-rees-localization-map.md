Let $\varphi\colon A\to B$ be a ring homomorphism and let $J\subseteq B$ be
the ideal generated by $\varphi(I)$. Define the graded ring homomorphism

$$
\rho_\varphi\colon \mathcal R_A(I)
\longrightarrow\mathcal R_B(J)
$$

by applying $\varphi$ coefficientwise: a coefficient $x\in I^n$ of $t^n$ is
sent to $\varphi(x)\in J^n$. For the localization $A\to A_s$ and
$J=IA_s$ we write $\rho_s$.
