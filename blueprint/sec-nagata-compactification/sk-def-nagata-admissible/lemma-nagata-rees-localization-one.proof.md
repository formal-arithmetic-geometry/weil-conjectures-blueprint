Coefficientwise maps compose like the underlying ring homomorphisms, and
the composite $A\to A_1\xrightarrow{\sim}A$ is the identity. Hence the
composite of the two coefficientwise maps sends each homogeneous monomial
$at^n$ to itself, so it is the identity by
[lemma-nagata-rees-sum-components] and ring-homomorphism extensionality.
The morphisms of affine blow-ups are induced on Proj compatibly with
composition and identities, which gives the second statement.
