Let $f\in R_d$ with $d>0$. A homogeneous prime $\mathfrak p$ with
$f\notin\mathfrak p$ determines the prime
$\mathfrak p R_f\cap R_{(f),0}$ of $R_{(f),0}$; conversely a prime
$\mathfrak q$ of $R_{(f),0}$ determines the homogeneous prime generated by
the homogeneous $x\in R_n$ ($n\ge0$) with $x^d/f^n\in\mathfrak q$. These
constructions are mutually inverse homeomorphisms
$D_+(f)\cong\operatorname{Spec}(R_{(f),0})$. The ring of sections of the
structure sheaf of $\operatorname{Proj}(R)$ over $D_+(f)$ is $R_{(f),0}$,
and at corresponding points both structure sheaves have the same stalk,
the degree-zero homogeneous localization at $\mathfrak p$; so the canonical morphism $D_+(f)\to\operatorname{Spec}(R_{(f),0})$ is an
isomorphism of schemes.

For the cover: a point of $\operatorname{Proj}(R)$ is a homogeneous prime
$\mathfrak p$ not containing $R_+$. If $\mathfrak p$ contained every $f_i$,
it would contain the ideal they generate, hence $R_+$, which is
impossible. So $\mathfrak p\in D_+(f_i)$ for some $i$.
