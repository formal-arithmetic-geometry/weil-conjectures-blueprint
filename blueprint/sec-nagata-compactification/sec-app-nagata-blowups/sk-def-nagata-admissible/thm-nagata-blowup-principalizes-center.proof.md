Invertibility is a condition at each point: every point must have an affine
open neighbourhood on which the ideal is generated by one non-zero-divisor
([def-nagata-invertible-ideal]). Let $\xi\in\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)$
and choose an affine open $V=\operatorname{Spec}(A)\subseteq X$ containing
its image; put $I=\mathcal I(V)$. By
[prop-nagata-global-blowup-cover-independent], $b^{-1}(V)$ is identified
over $V$ with the affine blow-up
$\operatorname{Bl}_I(\operatorname{Spec}A)=\operatorname{Proj}(\mathcal R_A(I))$.
The standard opens $D_+(at)$, $a\in I$, cover it
([lemma-nagata-rees-generators-cover-proj]); no finite generation of $I$
is needed for this, since all elements of $I$ are allowed. So $\xi$ lies in
some $D_+(at)\cong\operatorname{Spec}(A[I/a])$
([prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart]). On this affine chart the extended
ideal has global sections $I\,A[I/a]$
([lemma-nagata-extended-ideal-affine-chart]), which equals the principal
ideal $(a)$ by [lemma-nagata-chart-extended-ideal-principal], and $a$ is
a non-zero-divisor of $A[I/a]$ by [lemma-nagata-chart-generator-regular].
Hence $\xi$ has an affine open neighbourhood on which
$\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)}$ is generated by a
single non-zero-divisor, which is the invertibility condition. (On overlaps
of two charts the generators $a$ and $c$ differ by a unit, by
[lemma-nagata-chart-generators-associated], as they must.)
