Every element of $\mathcal R_A(I)_{(at),0}$ is a fraction $xt^n/(at)^n$
with $x\in I^n$, and $x/a^n$ lies in $A[I/a]$ because $x$ is a sum of
products of $n$ elements of $I$. If $xt^n/(at)^n=yt^m/(at)^m$, then
$(at)^k\bigl((at)^mxt^n-(at)^nyt^m\bigr)=0$ in $\mathcal R_A(I)$ for some
$k$, that is $a^k(a^mx-a^ny)=0$ in $A$, so $x/a^n=y/a^m$ in $A_a$; this is
the equality relation of the localization away from $a$, with no
cancellation of $a$, which may be a zero divisor. Hence $\theta_a$ is well
defined. Addition and multiplication of fractions with a common
denominator power are compatible with the displayed formula, and
$\theta_a(1)=1$, so $\theta_a$ is a ring homomorphism.
