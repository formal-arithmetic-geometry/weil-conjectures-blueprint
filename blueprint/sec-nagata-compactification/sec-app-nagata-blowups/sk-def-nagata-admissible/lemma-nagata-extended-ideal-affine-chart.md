Let $\mathcal J$ be a quasi-coherent ideal on a scheme $X$, let
$U=\operatorname{Spec}(A)\subseteq X$ be an affine open, let
$\psi\colon A\to R$ be a ring map, and let
$g\colon\operatorname{Spec}(R)\to U\subseteq X$ be the induced morphism.
Then the ideal of global sections of the extended ideal
$\mathcal J\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}(R)}$ is the extension
$\mathcal J(U)R$ of $\mathcal J(U)$ along $\psi$. In particular, on an affine
chart $\operatorname{Spec}(A[I/a])$ of the affine blow-up, the
extended ideal of $I$ is the ideal generated by the image of $I$ in
$A[I/a]$.
