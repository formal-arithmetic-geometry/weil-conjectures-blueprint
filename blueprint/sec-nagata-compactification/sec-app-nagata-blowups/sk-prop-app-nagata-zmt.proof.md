By Zariski's Main Theorem in the form [Stacks, Tag 02LR](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02LR), locally on $T$
the canonical map from $Z$ to the normalization $T_0$ of $T$ in $Z$ is
an open immersion, and $T_0\to T$ is affine. These factorizations agree
on overlaps and glue to the stated factorization. Over an affine open of $T$ the scheme $T_0$ is
affine, so $Z$ is a quasi-compact open of an affine scheme there; this
is what it means for $Z\to T$ to be quasi-affine.
