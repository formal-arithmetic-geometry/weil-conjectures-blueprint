Since $Y\to S$ is separated, the graph is always closed in
$U\times_SY$; the condition is about its behaviour over $X\setminus U$.
Every map $X\to Y$ is a quasi-domination with domain $X$. Conversely,
if $Y\to S$ is proper, every quasi-domination has domain $X$, because
the projection of the closed graph to $X$ is closed with image the
dense open $U$.
