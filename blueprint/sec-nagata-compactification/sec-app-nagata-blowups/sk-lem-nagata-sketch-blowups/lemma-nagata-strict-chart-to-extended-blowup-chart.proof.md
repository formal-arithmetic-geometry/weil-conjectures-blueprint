The structure map $B\to B_b$ and the composite
$A[I/a]\subseteq A_a\to B_b$ are $A$-algebra homomorphisms into a
commutative ring, so the universal property of
$B\otimes_AA[I/a]$ gives the unique $B$-algebra homomorphism with the
displayed formula. For the image: $B[J/b]$ is a $B$-subalgebra of $B_b$,
so it suffices to treat pure tensors $1\otimes c$. Every $c\in A[I/a]$ is
a fraction $x/a^n$ with $x\in I^n$, and its image is $\overline x/b^n$
with $\overline x\in I^nB=J^n$, which lies in $B[J/b]$. Sums are handled by
additivity. On chart generators the map is
$y\otimes(x/a)\mapsto y\,\overline x/b$, which is the defining formula of
$\sigma_a$.
