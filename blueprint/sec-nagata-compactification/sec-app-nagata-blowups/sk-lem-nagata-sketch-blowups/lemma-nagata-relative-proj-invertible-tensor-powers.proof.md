Being an isomorphism is local on the target. Cover $Y$ by affine opens
$V=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ on which $\mathcal L(V)=(a)$ for a
non-zero-divisor $a$. Over such a $V$ the relative Proj is
$\operatorname{Proj}(\bigoplus_{n\geq0}\mathcal L(V)^n)$ with its
structure morphism to $V$, which is an isomorphism by
[lemma-nagata-relative-proj-invertible-tensor-powers-affine]. Hence the
structure morphism is an isomorphism over every member of an open cover of
$Y$, and therefore an isomorphism.
