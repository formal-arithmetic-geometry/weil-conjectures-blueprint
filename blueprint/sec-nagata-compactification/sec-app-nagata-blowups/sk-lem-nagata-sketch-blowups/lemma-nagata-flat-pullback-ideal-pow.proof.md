The image is generated by the elements $bx$ with $x\in I^n$ and
$b\in B$, which is exactly $(IB)^n$: expand products of generators for one
inclusion and write elements of $(IB)^n$ as finite sums of $B$-multiples of
images of products of $n$ elements of $I$ for the other. If $B$ is flat
over $A$, tensoring the injection $I^n\to A$ with $B$ gives an injection
$B\otimes_A I^n\to B\otimes_A A=B$, which is the map in question.
