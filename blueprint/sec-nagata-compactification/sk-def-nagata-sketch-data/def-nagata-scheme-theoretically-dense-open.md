For a quasi-compact open immersion $j\colon U\hookrightarrow X$, say that
$U$ is scheme-theoretically dense in $X$ if the sheaf morphism

$$
\mathcal O_X\longrightarrow j_*\mathcal O_U
$$

is injective.
