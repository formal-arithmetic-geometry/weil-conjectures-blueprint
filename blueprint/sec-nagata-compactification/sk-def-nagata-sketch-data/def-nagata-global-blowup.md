Let $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)$ be the scheme obtained by gluing the
local models along the datum of [def-nagata-global-blowup-glue-data], that
is, the colimit of the diagram $V\mapsto\mathsf B_V(\mathcal I)$ over the
affine opens $V$ of $X$.
