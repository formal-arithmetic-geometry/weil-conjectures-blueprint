If $I$ is finitely generated, then $\mathcal R_A(I)$ is of finite type as an
algebra over its degree-zero piece $\mathcal R_A(I)_0$.

## Proof

A homogeneous coefficient in $I^n$ is a linear combination of degree-$n$
monomials in the $f_i$. Hence every homogeneous Rees element belongs to the
algebra generated by the $f_it$, and
[lemma-nagata-rees-sum-components] treats an arbitrary element.
Transport finite generation along
[lemma-nagata-rees-degree-zero].
