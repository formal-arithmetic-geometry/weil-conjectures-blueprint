Define

$$
\rho_s\colon A_s\otimes_A\mathcal R_A(I)
\longrightarrow\mathcal R_{A_s}(IA_s)
$$

degreewise by sending a coefficient $x\in I^n$ to $x/1\in(IA_s)^n$.
