Let $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be a quasi-coherent ideal and let
$U=X\setminus V(\mathcal I)$. The restriction

$$
b_{X,\mathcal I}^{-1}(U)\longrightarrow U
$$

of the blow-up morphism is an isomorphism.
