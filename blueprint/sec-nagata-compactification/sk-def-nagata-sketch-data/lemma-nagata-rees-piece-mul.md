The pieces $\mathcal R_A(I)_n$ form a graded monoid:
$1\in\mathcal R_A(I)_0$ and

$$
\mathcal R_A(I)_m\cdot\mathcal R_A(I)_n
\subseteq\mathcal R_A(I)_{m+n}.
$$
