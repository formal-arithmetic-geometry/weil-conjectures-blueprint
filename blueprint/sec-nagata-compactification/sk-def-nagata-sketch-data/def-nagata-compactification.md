Let $f\colon X\to S$ be a morphism of schemes. A *compactification*
of $f$ consists of a scheme $\overline X$, morphisms

$$
X\xrightarrow{j}\overline X\xrightarrow{p}S,
$$

and an equality $j;p=f$, such that $j$ is an open immersion and
$p$ is proper.
