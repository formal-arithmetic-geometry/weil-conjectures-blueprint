Let $X$ be qcqs, let $U\subseteq X$ be a quasi-compact open, and let
$T_1,T_2\subseteq U$ be disjoint closed subsets of $U$ whose complements in
$U$ are quasi-compact. Then there is a $U$-admissible blow-up
$\pi\colon X'\to X$ such that the closures of $\pi^{-1}(T_1)$ and
$\pi^{-1}(T_2)$ in $X'$ are disjoint.
