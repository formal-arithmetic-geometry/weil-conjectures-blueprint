In a quasi-separated space every quasi-compact open is retrocompact, so
its complement is a constructible closed subset. The choice of $W$ gives $T\subseteq T_c$, the choice of
$W'$ gives $T'\subseteq T'_c$, and $V\setminus W\subseteq W'$ gives
$T_c\cap T'_c=\varnothing$.
