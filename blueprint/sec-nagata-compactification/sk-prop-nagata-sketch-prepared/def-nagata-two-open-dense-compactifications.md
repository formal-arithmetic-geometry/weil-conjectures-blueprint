Let $C_i^0$ be a chosen compactification of the compactifiable morphism
$U_i\to S$. Define

$$
C_i=\mathsf{Dense}(C_i^0)
=\bigl(U_i\hookrightarrow X_i\longrightarrow S\bigr).
$$
