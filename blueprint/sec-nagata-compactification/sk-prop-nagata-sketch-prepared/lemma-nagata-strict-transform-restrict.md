Let $X'\to X$ be the blow-up of a center $C\hookrightarrow X$ and let
$f\colon Y\to X$ be a morphism. Over $X\setminus C$, the strict transform of
$f$ is canonically the ordinary pullback $Y\times_X(X\setminus C)$.
This identification is compatible with the projection to the original
scheme.
