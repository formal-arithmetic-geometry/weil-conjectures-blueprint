This is [Stacks, Tag 0F41](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F41), and we give the proof in
Section “Proof of Nagata's theorem”. Proper base change over an
arbitrary qcqs base uses the theorem in this generality, whereas for
schemes of finite type over $k$ the Noetherian case of the proof suffices.

Density is a condition on structure sheaves: a nilpotent section
supported on the boundary is invisible on $X$ but obstructs schematic
density. With this condition, morphisms between dense compactifications are unique and
common refinements exist (Proposition [sk-prop-nagata-sketch-comp-category]), and
passing to a dense compactification does not change the direct image
with proper support (Lemma [sk-lem-nagata-density-normalization]).
