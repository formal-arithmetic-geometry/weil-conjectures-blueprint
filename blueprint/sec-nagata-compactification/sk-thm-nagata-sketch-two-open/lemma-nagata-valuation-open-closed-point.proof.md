Choose a basic open $D(a)$ with $\mathfrak m_A\in D(a)\subseteq O$.
Then $a\notin\mathfrak m_A$, hence $a$ is a unit of the local ring $A$ and
$D(a)=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$.
