Let $A$ be a valuation ring with fraction field $K$. Every commutative
diagram whose generic map
$\operatorname{Spec}(K)\to W$ factors through $U_1\cap U_2$ has a unique
lift $\operatorname{Spec}(A)\to W$ over $S$.
