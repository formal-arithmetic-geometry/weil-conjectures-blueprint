Let $A$ be a local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_A$ and let
$O\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ be open. Then the closed point
$\mathfrak m_A$ belongs to $O$ if and only if $O=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$.
