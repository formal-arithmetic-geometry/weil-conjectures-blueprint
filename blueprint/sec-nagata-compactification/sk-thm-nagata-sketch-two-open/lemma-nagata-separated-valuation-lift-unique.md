Let $f\colon Y\to S$ be separated, let $A$ be a valuation ring with
fraction field $K$, and let $h_1,h_2\colon\operatorname{Spec}(A)\to Y$ be
$S$-morphisms whose restrictions to $\operatorname{Spec}(K)$ coincide.
Then $h_1=h_2$.
