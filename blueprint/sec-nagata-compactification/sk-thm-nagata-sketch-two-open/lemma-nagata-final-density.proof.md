By [lemma-nagata-two-open-density-transport] the induced open immersion
$j_W\colon U\to W$ has dense image. The map $U_1\cap U_2\to W$ is the
composite of the inclusion $U_1\cap U_2\subseteq U$, which has dense image
by hypothesis, with $j_W$. By continuity of $j_W$, the closure of the image
of $U_1\cap U_2$ contains $j_W(U)$, whose closure is $W$; hence the
composite has dense image.
