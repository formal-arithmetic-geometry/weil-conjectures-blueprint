For $i=1,2$ the open immersion $U\hookrightarrow W_i$ has dense image.
Moreover, let $W$ be a common modification of two Noetherian schemes $W_1$
and $W_2$ along a common open $U$, with $U$-admissible blow-ups
$W'_i\to W_i$ and open immersions $W'_i\hookrightarrow W$. If $U$ is dense
in $W_1$ and in $W_2$, then the induced open immersion $U\to W$ has dense
image. In particular, for the final common modification $W$ of the
intermediate schemes $W_1,W_2$ (over a Noetherian base $S$, with $U\to S$
separated, locally of finite type and quasi-compact), the map
$j_W\colon U\to W$ has dense image.
