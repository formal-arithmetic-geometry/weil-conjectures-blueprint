Let a point in the image of $U_i$ specialize in $U\times_SX_i$ to a point
$(u,x)$. There is a point $v\in V_i$ with
$\psi_i(v)=u$ and image $x$ in $X_i$. If $u\notin U_i$, then
$v\in Z_{i,j}$ and $x\in\overline Z_{i,j}$.
