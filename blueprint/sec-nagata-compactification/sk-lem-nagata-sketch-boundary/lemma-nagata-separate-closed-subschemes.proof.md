Put $\mathcal I=\mathcal I_1+\mathcal I_2$, a finite type quasi-coherent
ideal, $b\colon X'=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)\to X$, and
$\Omega=X\setminus V(\mathcal I)$. By [def-nagata-strict-transform] and
[def-nagata-strict-transform], the strict
transform $\widetilde Z_i$ of $Z_i$ is the scheme-theoretic closure in
$Z_i\times_XX'$ of the open part $Z_i\times_X\Omega$. Since $X$ is
quasi-compact and $\mathcal I$ is of finite type, $\Omega$ is quasi-compact;
since $X$ is quasi-separated, the inclusion $\Omega\hookrightarrow X$ is
quasi-compact, and so is its base change
$Z_i\times_X\Omega\hookrightarrow Z_i\times_XX'$. For a quasi-compact
morphism the underlying set of the scheme-theoretic image is the closure of
the image (Stacks Project, Tag 01R8). Hence the underlying set of
$\widetilde Z_i$ is the closure of $Z_i\times_X\Omega$ in $Z_i\times_XX'$.
We regard $\widetilde Z_i$ as a subset of $X'$ via the closed immersion
$Z_i\times_XX'\hookrightarrow X'$; it suffices to show that these two
subsets of $X'$ are disjoint.

**Step 1: a cover of $X'$ by charts of two kinds.** Let
$V=\operatorname{Spec}(A)\subseteq X$ be an affine open and put
$I=\mathcal I(V)=\mathcal I_1(V)+\mathcal I_2(V)$. By
[prop-nagata-global-blowup-cover-independent], $b^{-1}(V)$ is identified
with $\operatorname{Proj}(\mathcal R_A(I))$, and by
[lemma-nagata-rees-generators-cover-proj] it is covered by the standard opens
$D_+(at)$, $a\in I$, where $at$ is the degree-one element of
[lemma-nagata-rees-degree-one-element]. Write $a=a_1+a_2$ with
$a_k\in\mathcal I_k(V)$. Then $at=a_1t+a_2t$, and a homogeneous prime not
containing a sum does not contain one of the summands, so
$D_+(at)\subseteq D_+(a_1t)\cup D_+(a_2t)$ (cf.
[prop-nagata-proj-standard-open]). Hence $X'$ is covered by the opens
$D_+(ct)\cong\operatorname{Spec}(A[I/c])$
([prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart]), where $V=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ runs
over the affine opens of $X$ and $c$ runs over
$\mathcal I_1(V)\cup\mathcal I_2(V)$.

**Step 2: $\widetilde Z_k$ misses the charts at sections of $\mathcal I_k$.**
Let $c\in\mathcal I_k(V)$ and let $D=D_+(ct)\subseteq X'$ be the
corresponding chart. By [lemma-nagata-extended-ideal-affine-chart] and
[lemma-nagata-chart-extended-ideal-principal], the extended ideal
$\mathcal I\mathcal O_{X'}$ restricted to $D$ is generated by $c$. Suppose a
point of $\widetilde Z_k$ lies in $D$. Its preimage in $Z_k\times_XX'$ lies in
the closure of $Z_k\times_X\Omega$ and in the open preimage of $D$, so this
open preimage contains a point $w$ of $Z_k\times_X\Omega$. Let $y\in D$ and
$x=b(y)\in X$ be the images of $w$. Then $x\in Z_k$, so every section of
$\mathcal I_k$ vanishes at $x$; in particular $c$ vanishes at $y$, so the
stalk $(\mathcal I\mathcal O_{X'})_y=(c)$ lies in the maximal ideal of
$\mathcal O_{X',y}$. On the other hand $x\in\Omega$, so
$\mathcal I_x=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $(\mathcal I\mathcal O_{X'})_y$ is the
unit ideal. This contradiction shows $\widetilde Z_k\cap D=\varnothing$.

**Step 3: conclusion.** By Step 1 every point of $X'$ lies in a chart
$D_+(ct)$ with $c$ a section of $\mathcal I_1$ or of $\mathcal I_2$. By
Step 2 it lies outside $\widetilde Z_1$ in the first case and outside
$\widetilde Z_2$ in the second. Hence $\widetilde Z_1\cap\widetilde
Z_2=\varnothing$.

For the last assertion, if $Z_1\cap Z_2\cap U=\varnothing$ then
$\mathsf{SepBl}_U(Z_1,Z_2)$ is by [def-nagata-separate-closed-subschemes]
exactly the blow-up $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I_1+\mathcal I_2}(X)\to X$,
which is $U$-admissible; the strict transforms in it are the ones just shown
to be disjoint.
