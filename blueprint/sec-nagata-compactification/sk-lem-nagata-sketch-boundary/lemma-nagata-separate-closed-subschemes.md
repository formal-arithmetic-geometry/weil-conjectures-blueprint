Let $X$ be a qcqs scheme and let $\mathcal I_1,\mathcal I_2$ be finite type
quasi-coherent ideals of $\mathcal O_X$, with $Z_i=V(\mathcal I_i)$. Then
the strict transforms of $Z_1$ and $Z_2$ in the blow-up
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I_1+\mathcal I_2}(X)\to X$ are disjoint. In
particular, if $U\subseteq X$ is an open subscheme with
$Z_1\cap Z_2\cap U=\varnothing$, the strict transforms of
$Z_1$ and $Z_2$ in the $U$-admissible blow-up
$\mathsf{SepBl}_U(Z_1,Z_2)\to X$ are disjoint.
