Suppose moreover that $V$ is quasi-separated (for instance a spectral
space). Then the constructed subsets $T_c,T'_c$ are constructible and
closed in $V$, and they satisfy

$$
T\subseteq T_c,\qquad T'\subseteq T'_c,\qquad
T_c\cap T'_c=\varnothing.
$$
