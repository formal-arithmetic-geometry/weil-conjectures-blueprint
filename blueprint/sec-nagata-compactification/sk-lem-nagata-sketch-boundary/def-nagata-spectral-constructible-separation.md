Let $T,T'\subseteq V$ be disjoint closed subsets of a spectral space.
Select quasi-compact opens $(W,W')$ from
[lemma-nagata-spectral-constructible-separation-exists] and define

$$
T_c=V\setminus W,
\qquad
T'_c=V\setminus W'.
$$
