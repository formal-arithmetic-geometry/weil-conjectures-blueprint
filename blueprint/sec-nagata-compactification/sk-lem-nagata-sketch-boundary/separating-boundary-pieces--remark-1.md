The formal proof
(`separationBlowup_disjoint_strictTransforms`) blows up the sum
$\mathcal I_1+\mathcal I_2$ directly on $X$
(`separationBlowup`) and proves disjointness of the strict
transforms chart-locally, using the chart inclusion
$D_+(b_1+b_2)\subseteq D_+(b_1)\cup D_+(b_2)$ for degree-one sections
$b_i$ coming from $\mathcal I_i$. The Tag 080P preparation inside
$Z=V(\mathcal I_1\mathcal I_2)$
([lemma-nagata-separate-closed-data-exists]) and
[lemma-nagata-finite-type-nil-ideal-nilpotent] are not formalized.
