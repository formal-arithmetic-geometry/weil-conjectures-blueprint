The subset $\operatorname{IsoOpen}(q)$ is open,
$q^{-1}(\operatorname{IsoOpen}(q))\to\operatorname{IsoOpen}(q)$ is an
isomorphism, and every open over which $q$ is an isomorphism is contained in
it.
