Let $q:P\to T$ be proper between qcqs schemes, let $V\subseteq T$ be
quasi-compact open, and let $F\subseteq V$ be closed. Suppose $q$ is an
isomorphism over a neighbourhood of $F$ in $V$. After a $V$-admissible
blow-up ([sk-def-nagata-admissible]) the strict transform of $q$ is an
isomorphism over a neighbourhood of the closure of $F$.
