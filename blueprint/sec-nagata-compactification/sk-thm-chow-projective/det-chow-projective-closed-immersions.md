id = "det-chow-projective-closed-immersions"
kind = "lemma"
title = "Closedness of the Chow immersions for a proper morphism"
uses = ["det-chow-projective-qcqs-chow"]
+++
Let $S$ be qcqs, let $f:X\to S$ be proper, and let $\pi:\widetilde X\to X$
(proper, surjective) and $i_S:\widetilde X\to\mathbf P^n_S$ (an immersion
with $q_Si_S=f\pi$) be as in [det-chow-projective-qcqs-chow]. Then:

1. $f\pi$ is proper and $i_S$ is a closed immersion, so $f\pi$ is projective;
1. $i_X=(\pi,i_S):\widetilde X\to X\times_S\mathbf P^n_S=\mathbf P^n_X$ is a
   closed immersion, and since its composite with the projection
   $\mathbf P^n_X\to X$ is $\pi$, the morphism $\pi$ is projective.
