*Definition (sketch).*

A morphism $f:X\to Y$ is *proper* if it is separated, of finite
type and universally closed; *finite* if it is affine and
$f_*\mathcal O_X$ is a finite $\mathcal O_Y$-algebra; an
*immersion* is a composite of an open and a closed immersion;
*surjective* if surjective on underlying spaces; étale if
locally of finite presentation, flat and unramified; and a scheme is
*affine* if it is $\Spec$ of a commutative ring.
