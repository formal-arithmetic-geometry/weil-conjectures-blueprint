A *scheme* is a locally ringed space which is locally isomorphic to
the spectrum $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ of a commutative ring $A$; $\mathit{Sch}$ denotes
the category of schemes, and a *morphism of schemes* is a morphism
in $\mathit{Sch}$. A scheme is *affine* if it is isomorphic to
$\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ for a commutative ring $A$; an *open subscheme* of $X$
is a subscheme whose underlying topological space is open in $X$.
