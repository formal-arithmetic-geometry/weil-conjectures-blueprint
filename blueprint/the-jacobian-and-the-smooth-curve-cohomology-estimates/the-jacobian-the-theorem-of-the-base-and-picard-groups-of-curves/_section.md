The theorem of the base [thm-sc-theorem-of-the-base] decomposes
$\operatorname{Pic}_{C/k}$ into smooth proper $g$-dimensional components;
the tangent space at the identity is $H^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$
[lem-sc-pic-tangent-space]. Together these produce the Jacobian over a
separably closed field [thm-sc-jacobian-representability]. The general-field
vocabulary is fixed in [def-sc-general-curve] and [def-sc-jacobian], with
uniqueness [lem-sc-jacobian-unique], base-change invariance of the genus
[lem-sc-genus-invariant], and an explicit statement of what is and is not
proved here [rem-sc-jacobian-scope].

The last three statements compute Picard groups: $n$-torsion and
divisibility on a proper curve [lem-sc-pic-proper-ntorsion], a purely
group-theoretic torsion sequence [lem-sc-divisible-quotient-torsion], and
the quotient description on an affine curve [lem-sc-pic-affine].
