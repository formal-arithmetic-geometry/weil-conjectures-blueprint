The eight assertions $\mathcal C_1,\dots,\mathcal C_8$ fixed in the
proper-base-change chapter, verified one by one for the constant sheaf
$\mathbf F_r$ on a smooth curve over an algebraically closed field:
vanishing above degree two [lem-sc-C1], vanishing on affine curves
[lem-sc-C2], the $p$-primary case [lem-sc-C3], finiteness away from the
characteristic [lem-sc-C4] and on proper curves [lem-sc-C5], invariance
under algebraically closed extension [lem-sc-C6] and its proper-curve form
[lem-sc-C7], and surjectivity of the degree-two restriction map
[lem-sc-C8]. The section closes with the assembled statement
[thm-sc-main].
