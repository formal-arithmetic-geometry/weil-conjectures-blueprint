Normalization in a finite extension [def-fin-normalization-extension] is
finite [lem-fin-normalization-extension-finite], which produces an affine
curve model for a finite extension of the function field
[lem-sc-finite-extension-affine-model]. A trace argument then kills $H^2$ of
a generic pushforward on an affine curve [lem-sc-affine-generic-second],
giving $\operatorname{cd}\le1$ in the affine case
[lem-sc-affine-cohomological-dimension]; the characteristic-$p$ counterpart
is [lem-sc-artin-schreier-dimension].

The Kummer computations follow: the full answer on a proper curve
[thm-sc-proper-cohomology], the finiteness of units modulo powers
[lem-sc-affine-unit-quotient] and the answer on an affine curve
[thm-sc-affine-cohomology], and the invariance of all of this under
extension of the algebraically closed ground field
[thm-sc-constant-invariance].
