Continuous cohomology of a profinite group is set up in
[def-exc-continuous-cohomology], and identified with étale cohomology of a
field through the Galois dictionary [lem-exc-field-discrete-modules]. A
Sylow filtration [lem-sc-sylow-module-filtration] reduces a vanishing
statement to trivial $\mathbf F_\ell$-coefficients, giving the degree-two
criterion [lem-sc-group-cd-one]. Tsen's theorem and Artin–Schreier then
give $\operatorname{cd}\le1$ for algebraic extensions of a curve function
field [lem-sc-function-field-cd-one].

On the curve itself: a sheaf with zero generic restriction is acyclic
[lem-sc-zero-generic-acyclic], so in degrees $\ge2$ cohomology may be
computed after pushing forward from the generic point
[lem-sc-generic-cohomology-comparison]; hence $\operatorname{cd}\le2$ for a
smooth curve [lem-sc-cohomological-dimension]. The section closes with the
Kummer computations: the generic residue sheaf
[lem-sc-generic-kummer-residues], second cohomology in terms of divisors
[lem-sc-second-kummer-divisors], and the affine constant case
[lem-sc-affine-constant-second].
