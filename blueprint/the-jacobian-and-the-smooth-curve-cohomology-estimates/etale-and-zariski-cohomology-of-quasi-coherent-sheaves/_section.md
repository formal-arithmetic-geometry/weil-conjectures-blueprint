A quasi-coherent module defines a sheaf on the small étale site
[def-sc-etale-qcoherent-module], because quasi-coherent modules descend
[lem-sc-etale-qcoherent-descent]. On an affine scheme that sheaf is acyclic
[lem-sc-etale-affine-qcoherent], and a Čech comparison then shows that étale
and Zariski cohomology agree [lem-sc-ga-cohomology] — in particular
$H^q(C_{\mathrm{et}},\mathbf G_a)=H^q(C,\mathcal O_C)$, which vanishes for
$q\geq2$ on a smooth curve.
