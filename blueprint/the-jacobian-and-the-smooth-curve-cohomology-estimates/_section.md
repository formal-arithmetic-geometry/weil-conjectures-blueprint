The Jacobian material of the smooth-curve calculations: the statements whose
proofs depend on the representability of the Picard scheme, collected after
the Picard chapter so that every reference points backwards. Conventions and
labels follow the curve-calculation chapter (`sc-*`); no statement of that
chapter is repeated here.

The chapter has five parts. First the Jacobian itself
[the-jacobian-the-theorem-of-the-base-and-picard-groups-of-curves]: the
theorem of the base, the tangent space of $\operatorname{Pic}$, the
existence of $J_C$ over a separably closed field, and the consequences for
the Picard groups of proper and affine curves. Then a short section on
étale versus Zariski cohomology of quasi-coherent sheaves
[etale-and-zariski-cohomology-of-quasi-coherent-sheaves]. Then continuous
group cohomology and the cohomological dimension of curve function fields
[continuous-group-cohomology-and-the-cohomological-dimension-of-curve-function-fields],
followed by the finite-extension and affine-model inputs to the Kummer
computations
[finite-extensions-affine-models-and-the-kummer-computations-on-curves].
The chapter closes with the eight conditions C1–C8 and the main
smooth-curve estimate [the-c1-c8-vanishing-and-finiteness-conditions].

*Note on dependencies.* This chapter sits on top of the Picard chapter and
of the curve-calculation and abelian-variety chapters. References to those
chapters' identifiers are recorded as `Unresolved dependencies` lines
rather than as edges, since they are not yet on `main`.
