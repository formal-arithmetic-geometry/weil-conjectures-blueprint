We construct $f^!$ by Brown representability, as outlined at the beginning
of this section, without writing it down. Brown representability needs a
category with arbitrary direct sums, and $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ is not closed
under them. We therefore enlarge it to the smallest category that has them
and show that $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ compactly generates it.
