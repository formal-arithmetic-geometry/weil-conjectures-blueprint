Let $\Lambda\in\{A,\mathbf Z_\ell,\mathcal O_E,\mathbf Q_\ell,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ be an admissible
coefficient ring and $f:X\to Y$ a morphism of separated schemes of
finite type over $k$.

1. The objects of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ are compact in
   $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$ and a small skeleton compactly generates it.
2. Compactifications define an exact functor
   $Rf_!:\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda))$, preserving
   direct sums and sending $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ into $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$.
3. The functor $Rf_!$ has an exact right adjoint
   $f^!:\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda))\to\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$, obtained from
   Brown representability.
4. This right adjoint sends $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$ into
   $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$, so its adjunction restricts to $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$.
