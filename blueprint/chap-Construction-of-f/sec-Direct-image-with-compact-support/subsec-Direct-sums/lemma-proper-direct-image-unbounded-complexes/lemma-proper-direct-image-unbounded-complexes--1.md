This statement is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem, which
uses preservation of bounded constructible complexes by direct image.
The assertion for unbounded complexes is retained here:

$Rp_*$ maps $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ into $\Dic(Y,\Lambda)$, with
$Rp_*\Dic^{\leq b}(X,\Lambda)\subset\Dic^{\leq b+2d+1}(Y,\Lambda)$ and
$Rp_*\Dic^{\geq a}(X,\Lambda)\subset\Dic^{\geq a}(Y,\Lambda)$.
