Compute $R^qp_*$ on the weakly contractible affine basis used in clause [lemma-uniform-bound-proper-direct-image--1]. Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology] makes these cohomology functors commute with filtered colimits. Every direct sum is the filtered colimit of its finite subsums, and cohomology is additive on finite sums. The direct-sum comparison is therefore an isomorphism on that basis and hence an isomorphism of sheaves.
