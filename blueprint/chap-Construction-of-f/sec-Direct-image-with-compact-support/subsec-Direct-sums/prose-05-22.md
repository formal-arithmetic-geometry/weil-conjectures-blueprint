The bound $2d+1$ is not sharp. For example, for the structure map of a smooth projective curve over $\bar k$, where $d=1$, the lemma only says that
cohomology vanishes above degree $3$, while in fact it vanishes above
degree $2$. The extra degree does no harm, because the arguments
below only need a bound that does not depend on the sheaf. As in the
compactness argument, such a bound passes from sheaves to unbounded complexes.
