By a stratification we reduce the lemma to the extension
by zero of a lisse $\mathcal O_E$-lattice on a stratum. Its reductions
modulo $\pi_E^n$ are torsion sheaves, for which the classical bound $2d$ holds. In the passage to the limit over $n$, the $\varprojlim^1$ term raises the bound by one, to $2d+1$. The remaining steps, inverting $\ell$, extending scalars to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ and passing to ind-objects, are filtered colimits. By
Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology], the bound survives each of them.
