For $\mathcal O_E$ the rational arguments for compactness
(Theorem [theorem-compactness-rational-coefficients]) and for direct
sums (Lemma [lemma-proper-direct-image-unbounded-complexes]) go
through with one change. Integral [constructible sheaves](@definition-constructible-sheaves) are no
longer locally free on strata; for example,
$\href{@lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients}{\mathcal O_{E,X}}/\pi_E$ is [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves). But $\mathcal O_E$ is a
discrete valuation ring, so they have free resolutions of length one.
The estimates only use the resulting [Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves) $[-1,0]$.
