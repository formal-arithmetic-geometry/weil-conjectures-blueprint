By Proposition [proposition-local-structure-integral-constructible-complexes], a degree-zero constructible object has on a finite stratification a local model $\mathcal O_{E,S}\otimes P^\bullet$ with $P^\bullet$ bounded finite free. Flatness of $\mathcal O_{E,S}$ over $\mathcal O_E$ identifies its degree-zero cohomology with $\mathcal O_{E,S}\otimes H^0(P^\bullet)$. The latter module is finitely generated and gives the asserted model.
