For the finitely generated module $P$ in clause [lemma-structure-integral-constructible-complexes--2], the discrete valuation ring $\mathcal O_E$ gives a free resolution $0\to\mathcal O_E^r\to\mathcal O_E^s\to P\to0$. Tensoring this with the flat sheaf $\mathcal O_{E,S}$ gives a finite free resolution in degrees $[-1,0]$. Hence the object is dualizable with that Tor bound, and dualizing this resolution puts its dual in degrees $[0,1]$.
