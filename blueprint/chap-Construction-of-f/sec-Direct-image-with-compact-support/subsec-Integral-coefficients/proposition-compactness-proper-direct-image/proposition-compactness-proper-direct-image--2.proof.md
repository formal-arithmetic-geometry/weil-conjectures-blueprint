The continuity and bounded-cohomological-dimension inputs are established in clause [proposition-compactness-proper-direct-image--1]. Apply the finite-window argument of Lemma [lemma-proper-direct-image-unbounded-complexes], with integral coefficients and the bound below.

The same count bounds $Rp_*$. For fibres of
dimension $\le d$, the torsion bound [Stacks, [095U](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/095U)] gives $2d$, and the
derived limit adds one. Hence $Rp_*$ of a constructible sheaf lies in
$[0,2d+1]$.
