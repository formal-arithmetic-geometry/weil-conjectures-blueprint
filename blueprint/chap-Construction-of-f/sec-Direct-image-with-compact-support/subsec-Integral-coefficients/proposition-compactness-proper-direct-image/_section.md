Every object of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathcal O_E)=\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ is compact in
$\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathcal O_E))$, and for proper $p:X\to Y$ the functor
$Rp_*$ preserves direct sums of objects of $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathcal O_E))$.
