Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring, let $X$ be a scheme whose
underlying topological space is Noetherian, and let $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$
be a closed immersion. For $K\in\Dbc(Z,\Lambda)$ one has

$$
\mathbf Ri_*K\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda).
$$

In particular, for $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$ the functor $i_{*,\mathcal O_E}$
sends $\Dbc(Z_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$ into $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$.
