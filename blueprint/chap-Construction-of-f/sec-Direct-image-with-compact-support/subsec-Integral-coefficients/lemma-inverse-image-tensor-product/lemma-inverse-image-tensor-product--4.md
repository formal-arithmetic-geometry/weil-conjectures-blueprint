For a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$, the functor $i_{*,\mathcal O_E}$ sends $\Dbc(Z_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$ into $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$.
