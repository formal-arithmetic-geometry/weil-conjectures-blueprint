Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring, let the underlying space
of $X$ be Noetherian, and let $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion.
For $K\in\Dbc(Z,\Lambda)$, one has
$$
\mathbf Ri_*K\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda).
$$
In particular, this applies to $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$.
