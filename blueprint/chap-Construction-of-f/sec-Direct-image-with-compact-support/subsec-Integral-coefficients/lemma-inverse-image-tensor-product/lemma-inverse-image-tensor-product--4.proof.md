Closed direct image is exact and preserves limits by [Stacks, [09AA](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09AA), [09BM](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09BM)]. It therefore preserves the derived-completeness and reduction conditions of Lemma [lemma-integral-models](3). On the closed subscheme the models are the given ones, and on its open complement they vanish, proving constructibility.
