For $K,L\in\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$, the uncompleted tensor product $K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}L$ lies in $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$.
