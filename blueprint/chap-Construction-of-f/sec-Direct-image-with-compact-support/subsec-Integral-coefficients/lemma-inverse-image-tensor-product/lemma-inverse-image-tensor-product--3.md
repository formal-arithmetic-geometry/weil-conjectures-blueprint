Let $\ell$ be prime, let $k$ be a field of positive characteristic
$p\ne\ell$, and let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring.
For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ of separated schemes of
finite type over $k$, extension by zero, applied to complexes, sends
$\Dbc(U,\Lambda)$ into $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$. In particular, this holds for
$\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$. The open immersion is quasi-compact in this setting.
