Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring and $f:X\to Y$ a morphism of
schemes. Then
$$
\mathbf Lf^*: \Dbc(Y,\Lambda)\longrightarrow\Dbc(X,\Lambda).
$$
No Noetherian or finite type hypothesis is required. In particular, this
applies to $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$.
