Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring and $f:X\to Y$ a morphism of
schemes. Then $\mathbf Lf^*$ sends $\Dbc(Y,\Lambda)$ into $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$.
No Noetherian or finite type hypothesis is required. In particular, for
$\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$ the functor $f^*_{\mathcal O_E}$ sends
$\Dbc(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$ into $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$.
