For a local model $\mathcal O_{E,S}\otimes P$ with $P$ finitely generated, ordinary inverse image has the same complete local model as completed inverse image. The finite-free model and truncation description in Lemma [lemma-structure-integral-constructible-complexes] extends this comparison to bounded constructible complexes. Thus the completion map is an isomorphism on precisely the stated subcategory.
