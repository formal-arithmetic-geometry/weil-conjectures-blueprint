*Shared context.* In the details below, $\Lambda$ is a commutative condensed
ring. We use $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ for bounded complexes of $\Lambda_X$-modules
whose cohomology sheaves are pro-étale locally finitely presented on a
finite stratification. Local finite presentation means a presentation by
finite free modules over the coefficient sheaf; the matrix entries need
not come from $\Lambda(*)$.

For integral coefficients, let $\ell$ be prime and
$E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$,
with ring of integers $\mathcal O_E$. The geometric hypotheses used by the
individual Lean declarations are stated in each detail. Pullback is the
ordinary derived pullback, without completion.
