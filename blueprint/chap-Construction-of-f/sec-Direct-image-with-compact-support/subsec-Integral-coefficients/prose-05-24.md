For instance, $\mathcal O_{E,X}/\pi_E$ is the cokernel of
$\pi_E:\mathcal O_{E,X}\to\mathcal O_{E,X}$, so it has Tor amplitude
$[-1,0]$. Its dual $\cHom(\mathcal O_{E,X}/\pi_E,\mathcal O_{E,X})=
(\mathcal O_{E,X}/\pi_E)[-1]$ has Tor amplitude $[0,1]$, as in (3).

The dévissage in the proof of compactness also needs that the operations it
uses preserve $\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E)$.
