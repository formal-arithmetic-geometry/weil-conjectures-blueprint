On a space, a section with compact support is in particular a section,
and so compactly supported cohomology maps to ordinary cohomology. The algebraic counterpart is a map $Rf_!\to Rf_*$, which is an isomorphism for proper $f$, and we need it to be compatible with the composition maps
just constructed: in Section [sec-Conventions-2] this is one of the compatibilities that the comparison with the étale site has to respect.
