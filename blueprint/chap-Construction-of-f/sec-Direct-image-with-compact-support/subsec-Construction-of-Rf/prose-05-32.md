On a space, a section with compact support is in particular a section,
and so compactly supported cohomology maps to ordinary cohomology. The
algebraic counterpart is a map $Rf_!\to Rf_*$, which is an isomorphism
for proper $f$. We need it to be compatible with the composition maps
just constructed. In Section [sec-Conventions-2] this is one of the
compatibilities that the comparison with the étale site has to respect.
