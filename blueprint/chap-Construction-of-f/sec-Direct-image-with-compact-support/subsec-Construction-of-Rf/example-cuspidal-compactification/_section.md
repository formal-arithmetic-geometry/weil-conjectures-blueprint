Over $k$, let $C\subset\mathbf P^2$ be the cuspidal cubic $zy^2=x^3$,
with cusp $c=(0:0:1)$. Its normalization is the map $h:\mathbf P^1\to C$,
$(s:u)\mapsto(su^2:u^3:s^3)$. It sends $u=0$ to the
cusp and the affine line $u\neq0$ isomorphically onto $C\setminus\{c\}$.
So $X=\mathbf A^1$ has the two compactifications $\mathbf P^1$ and $C$.
Since $h^{-1}(C\setminus\{c\})=\mathbf A^1$, the map $h$ is a refinement. The refinement map $j_!\Lambda\to Rh_*j'_!\Lambda$ is the identity on $X$, and at the cusp both stalks vanish: for the right side, proper base change
shows that the stalk of
$Rh_*j'_!\Lambda$ at $c$ is the cohomology of $j'_!\Lambda$ on the single
point $h^{-1}(c)=\{\infty\}$, where $j'_!\Lambda$ is zero. So the
singularity of $C$ does not affect $R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,\Lambda)$.
