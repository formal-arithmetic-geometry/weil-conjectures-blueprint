For a cartesian square with $p$ proper, the base change map
$g^*Rp_*\to Rp'_*g'^*$ of (4.5) is a map of functors
$D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D(Y'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, and it is an
isomorphism on $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$.
