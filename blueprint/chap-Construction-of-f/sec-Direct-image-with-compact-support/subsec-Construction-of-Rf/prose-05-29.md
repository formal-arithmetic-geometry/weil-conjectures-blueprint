For a cartesian square with $p$ proper, the [base change map](@definition-pro-etale-base-change-map)
$g^*Rp_*\to Rp'_*g'^*$ of (4.5) is a map of functors
$D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\Lambda)\to D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{Y'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\Lambda)$. The lemma lets us pass
from [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves), where it is an isomorphism, to
[$\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$](@definition-localizing-subcategory).
