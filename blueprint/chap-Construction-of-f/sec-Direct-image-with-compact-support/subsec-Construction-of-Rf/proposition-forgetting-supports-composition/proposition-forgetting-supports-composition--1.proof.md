For $f=pj$, the inverse counit $K\to j^*Rj_*K$ has under $j_!\dashv j^*$ a unique adjoint $j_!K\to Rj_*K$. Applying $Rp_*$ and the composition comparison $Rp_*Rj_*\simeq Rf_*$ gives the displayed map. All three constructions are natural in $K$, so they define the required transformation.
