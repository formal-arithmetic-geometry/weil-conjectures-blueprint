When $f$ is proper choose $p=f$ and $j=\mathrm{id}$. The inverse counit in clause [proposition-forgetting-supports-composition--1] is then the identity, making $\eta_f$ an isomorphism. If both $f$ and $g$ are proper, substitute these isomorphisms into the commutative square of clause [proposition-forgetting-supports-composition--2]; it identifies the two composition maps.
