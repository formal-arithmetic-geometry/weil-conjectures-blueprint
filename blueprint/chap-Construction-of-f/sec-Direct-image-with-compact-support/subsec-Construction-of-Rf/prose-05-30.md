The comparisons between compactifications below are the proper direct
images of the unique maps out of $j_!K$ that restrict to the identity on
$X$. They are unique because, for an open immersion $j:X\to Z$, the functor
$j_!$ is left adjoint to $j^*$ on the pro-étale derived categories, so
that a map out of $j_!K$ is determined by its restriction to $X$.
