*Shared context.* Let $\Lambda$ be admissible and let $f:X\to Y$ be separated of finite type. For a compactification $f=p\circ j$, use the specified functor $Rf_{!,\Lambda}=Rp_{*,\Lambda}\circ j_{!,\Lambda}$ between the localizing categories of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ on the pro-étale sites.
