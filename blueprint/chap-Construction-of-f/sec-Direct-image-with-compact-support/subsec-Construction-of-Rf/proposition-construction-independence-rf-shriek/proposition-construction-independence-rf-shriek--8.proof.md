The refinement comparisons in the proof of clause [proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek--7] are characterized as the maps restricting to the identity on the open $X$. Hence the comparison for two successive refinements is their composite. Pass any three compactifications to a common further refinement. Cancelling the intervening refinement isomorphisms gives the cocycle equality for the three induced comparisons. This also shows independence of the chosen common refinement.
