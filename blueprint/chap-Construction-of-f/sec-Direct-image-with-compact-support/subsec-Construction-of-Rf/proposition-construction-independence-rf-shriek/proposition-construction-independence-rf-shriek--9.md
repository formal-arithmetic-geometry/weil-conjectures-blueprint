Let $\Lambda\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$. For two compactifications of $f$, the isomorphism of clause [proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek--7] between the corresponding functors $Rf_{!,\Lambda}$ restricts to an isomorphism between their restrictions $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$.
