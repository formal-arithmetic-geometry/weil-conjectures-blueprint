The proper [base change map](@definition-pro-etale-base-change-map) and the comparison maps between compactifications
are natural transformations between exact functors on [$\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda))$](@definition-localizing-subcategory)
that preserve direct sums. To show that such a transformation is an
isomorphism, it suffices to check this on [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves).
