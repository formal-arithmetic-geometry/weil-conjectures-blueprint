Let $\Lambda\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$. The canonical isomorphism $Rg_{!,\Lambda}Rf_{!,\Lambda}\simeq R(gf)_{!,\Lambda}$ of clause [proposition-composition-base-change-rf-shriek--1], on the localizing categories, restricts to an isomorphism of functors $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Z_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$.
