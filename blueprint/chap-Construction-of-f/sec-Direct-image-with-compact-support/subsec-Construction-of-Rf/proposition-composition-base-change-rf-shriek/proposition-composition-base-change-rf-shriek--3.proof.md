When one map is the identity, choose its compactification to be the identity. Every refinement or open–proper interchange map in the construction of clause [proposition-composition-base-change-rf-shriek--1] then restricts to an identity on the same open. Its adjoint characterization makes the resulting composite the identity comparison, proving the unit compatibility.
