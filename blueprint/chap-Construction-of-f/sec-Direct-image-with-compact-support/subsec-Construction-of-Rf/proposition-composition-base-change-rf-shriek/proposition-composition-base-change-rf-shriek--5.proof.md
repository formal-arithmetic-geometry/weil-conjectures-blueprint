Replace a compactification by a refinement and pull the refinement square back. The refinement comparison and the open–proper interchange are characterized by their restrictions to the open, where both proposed base-change composites coincide. Adjunction gives equality before proper direct image; proper base-change pasting gives equality afterwards. Passing to a common refinement proves independence for any two choices.
