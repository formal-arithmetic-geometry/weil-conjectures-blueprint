For a fixed compactification the comparison is the composite of the base-change maps for $j_!$ and for the proper map. Open inverse-image identities paste, and the proper maps paste by Lemma [lemma-naturality-pasting-comparison] (equivalently the exchange-map pasting proved above). Combining the two equalities gives the comparison for the pasted square. Clause [proposition-composition-base-change-rf-shriek--5] removes dependence on the compactifications used.
