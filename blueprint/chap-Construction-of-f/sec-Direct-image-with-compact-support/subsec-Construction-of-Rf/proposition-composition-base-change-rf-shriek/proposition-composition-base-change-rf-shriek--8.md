Let $\Lambda\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$. In the situation of clause [proposition-composition-base-change-rf-shriek--4], the isomorphism $v^*Rf_{!,\Lambda}\simeq Rf'_{!,\Lambda}u^*$ restricts to an isomorphism of functors $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$.
