Extension by zero is exact and preserves direct sums because it is left adjoint to restriction. It maps the constructible finite-Tor-amplitude generators to such generators: add the zero stratum on the complement, and use the stalkwise Tor bound (or Lemma [lemma-inverse-image-tensor-product] for integral coefficients). The objects whose images lie in the target localizing category therefore form a localizing subcategory containing all generators, hence the whole source.
