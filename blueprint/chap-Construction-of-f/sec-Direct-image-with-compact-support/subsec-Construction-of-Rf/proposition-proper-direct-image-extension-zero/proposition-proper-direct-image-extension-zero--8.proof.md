Adjoin the closed complement to a constructible stratification; the extended sheaf is zero on that new stratum. For finite coefficients, its stalks are either the old stalks or zero, preserving the Tor bound. For the other coefficients $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}=\Dbc$ in the established framework; for the integral case use Lemma [lemma-inverse-image-tensor-product]. Thus extension by zero has the asserted preservation property.
