For proper $p:X\to Y$, $Rp_{*,\Lambda}$ restricts to an exact functor $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda))\to\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda))$.
