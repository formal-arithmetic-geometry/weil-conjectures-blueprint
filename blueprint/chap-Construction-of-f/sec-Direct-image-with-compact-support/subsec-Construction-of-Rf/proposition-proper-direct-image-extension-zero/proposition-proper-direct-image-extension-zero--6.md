For an open immersion $j:U\to X$, $j_{!,\Lambda}$ restricts to an exact functor $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(U_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda))\to\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda))$.
