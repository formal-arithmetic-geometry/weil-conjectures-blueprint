1. By Nagata's theorem (Theorem [theorem-nagata-s-compactification-theorem]), every
   separated morphism of finite type $f:X\to Y$ with $Y$ qcqs has a
   compactification, that is, a factorization $f=p\circ j$ with
   $j:X\to\overline X$ a quasi-compact open immersion and
   $p:\overline X\to Y$ proper.
2. In comparisons we may replace $\overline X$ by the
   scheme-theoretic closure of $X$, so that $X$ is dense, and by
   Lemma [lemma-passing-schematic-closure] this does not change
   $Rp_*j_!$.
