In this subsection the coefficient ring is a finite admissible ring $A$.
Proper direct image preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ by
Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-finite-coefficients]. We
show that the constructible generators are compact and that proper
direct image preserves direct sums on the localizing categories. In both
cases we reduce to the classical étale theory, using bounded flat
constructible models and the fact that étale cohomology commutes with
direct sums of unbounded complexes.

Finite Tor amplitude is a property of the complex, not of its cohomology
sheaves: the cohomology sheaves of an object of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ can have infinite
Tor amplitude (Section [sec-Finite-Tor-amplitude]). The property can,
however, be read off from a flat model. This is why we work with the
bounded flat models of Lemma [lemma-flat-models-generators].
