In this section the coefficient ring is a finite admissible ring $A$.
Proper direct image preserves [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) by Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-finite-coefficients], and we now show that the [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) generators are compact and that proper
direct image preserves direct sums on the [localizing categories](@definition-localizing-subcategory). In both
cases we reduce to the classical étale theory, using bounded flat
[constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) models and the fact that étale cohomology commutes with
direct sums of unbounded complexes.

Finite [Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves) is a property of the complex, not of its cohomology
sheaves: the cohomology sheaves of an object of [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) can have infinite
[Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves) (Section [sec-Finite-Tor-amplitude]). The property can,
however, be read off from a flat model. This is why we work with the
bounded flat models of Lemma [lemma-flat-models-generators].
