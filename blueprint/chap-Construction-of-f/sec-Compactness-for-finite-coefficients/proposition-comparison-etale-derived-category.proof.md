There are two statements: $\nu_X^*$ identifies the two localizing
categories, and on the étale side the localizing category is
everything. The first follows formally from the comparison results for
the pro-étale site. For the second we exhibit compact generators of
$D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$ that lie in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$.

An exact fully faithful functor that preserves direct sums identifies
the localizing subcategories generated by corresponding generators.
Proposition [proposition-unbounded-comparison-finite-coefficients] shows that $\nu_X^*$ is such a
functor on unbounded complexes and that it is compatible with direct
images. On $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ it is the equivalence of
Corollary [corollary-finite-tor-amplitude-under-comparison]. Inverse images commute with $\nu^*$ because both composites are
inverse images along the same morphism of topoi.

For the equality with the whole étale derived category, let
$u:U\to X$ range over a basis of quasi-compact separated étale
objects. The free representable module sheaves $u_!A_U$ are flat
constructible, hence belong to $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$. We have
$R\!\operatorname{Hom}_X(u_!A_U,K)=R\Gamma(U,K)$. It follows that their shifts
detect zero objects of $D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$. Indeed, if $H^n(U,K)=0$ for all such $U$
and all $n$, then every cohomology sheaf of $K$ vanishes, being the sheafification
of $U\mapsto H^n(U,K)$. These objects are also compact in all of
$D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$. The reason is that $R\Gamma(U,-)$ commutes with
direct sums, since $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(U)<\infty$, as in the étale part of the
proof of Proposition [proposition-compactness-finite-coefficients]. A set of
compact objects whose shifts detect zero objects generates a
triangulated category with direct sums as a localizing subcategory.
Hence these objects generate the entire étale derived category.
