On unbounded étale derived categories, proper direct image preserves
arbitrary direct sums by [Stacks, [0F0D](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0D)]. Forgetting coefficients gives
the $A$-linear statement, and
Proposition [proposition-comparison-etale-derived-category] transports it to
the pro-étale localizing categories.

Since $Rp_*$ maps $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ into $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,A)$ by
Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-finite-coefficients], the objects
$K\in\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A))$ whose direct image belongs to
$\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,A))$ form a triangulated subcategory closed under direct
sums and containing $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$, that is, all of $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A))$.
