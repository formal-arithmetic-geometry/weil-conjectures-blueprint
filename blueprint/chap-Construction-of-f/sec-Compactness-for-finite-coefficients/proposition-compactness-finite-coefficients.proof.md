The plan is to prove compactness on the étale
site against arbitrary objects of $D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$, where the commutation of
cohomology with direct sums that we need is classical, and then transfer the
result to $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ by $\nu_X^*$.

Recall that compact objects are closed under shifts,
finite extensions and direct summands. Hence, by
Lemma [lemma-flat-models-generators], it suffices to treat $a_!L$. Factor $a=i\circ u$ with
$i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ closed and $u:S\hookrightarrow Z$ open, and let
$j:X\setminus Z\hookrightarrow X$ be the complementary open immersion.
The idea is to write $R\!\operatorname{Hom}(a_!L,-)$ as a composite of functors each of
which commutes with direct sums. Since $L$ has finite projective stalks, it is dualizable with dual
$L^\vee=\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(L,A_S)$, and the étale form of
Lemma [lemma-sections-support-closed-subscheme](2) shows that for $K\in D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$ we have

$$
R\!\operatorname{Hom}_X(a_!L,K)
=R\Gamma\bigl(S,L^\vee\otimes_A u^*R\underline\Gamma_ZK\bigr),
\qquad R\underline\Gamma_ZK=i^*\fib\bigl(K\to Rj_*j^*K\bigr),
$$

where $R\underline\Gamma_Z$ denotes sections with support in $Z$.
We claim that every functor on the right, viewed as a functor between étale
derived categories, preserves arbitrary direct sums. For $Rj_*$
and $R\Gamma(S,-)$, this is [Stacks, [0F11](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F11)], which says that a derived direct image
of finite cohomological dimension commutes with direct sums of unbounded
complexes. It applies to $A$-modules because derived direct
image and global sections commute with forgetting the coefficient ring
[Stacks, [0D6J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0D6J)]. The remaining functors are inverse images, tensor products
with finite projective sheaves, and a fibre. Inverse images are left
adjoints, tensor products commute with direct sums, and the fibre of a
map between functors that preserve direct sums again preserves them.
Hence $R\!\operatorname{Hom}_X(a_!L,-)$ commutes with direct sums. Taking $H^0$, we see
that $\Hom(a_!L,-)$ commutes with direct sums, that is, $a_!L$ is compact.
By Lemma [lemma-flat-models-generators] it follows that every object of
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$ is compact.

On the pro-étale site, Corollary [corollary-finite-tor-amplitude-under-comparison] and
Proposition [proposition-unbounded-comparison-finite-coefficients] show that the inverse image
$\nu_X^*:D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$ is exact, fully
faithful and preserves direct sums. Such a functor identifies the localizing
subcategories generated by $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ on the two sites. Compactness against
all étale complexes therefore implies compactness in $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A))$.

For the last assertion we have to show that there are no other compact
objects. Since constructibility and finite Tor amplitude are preserved by direct
summands, the category $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ is thick. It is essentially small,
because it is a full subcategory of the essentially small bounded constructible
category, and by definition a skeleton generates $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A))$. In a
triangulated category with direct sums generated by a set of compact
objects, the compact objects form the thick closure of that set
[Stacks, [09SM](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SM), [09SN](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SN)]. In our case this closure is $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$.
