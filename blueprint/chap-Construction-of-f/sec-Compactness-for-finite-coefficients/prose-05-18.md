Proper direct image already preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ by
Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-finite-coefficients].
Its extension to the localizing categories therefore only needs preservation of
arbitrary direct sums. By the comparison just proved, this reduces
to the unbounded étale statement.
