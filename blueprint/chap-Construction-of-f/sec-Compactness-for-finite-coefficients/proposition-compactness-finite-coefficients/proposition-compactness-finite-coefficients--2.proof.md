Use the compact generators established in clause [proposition-compactness-finite-coefficients--1].

For the last assertion we have to show that there are no other compact
objects. Since constructibility and finite Tor amplitude are preserved by direct
summands, the category $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ is thick. It is essentially small,
because it is a full subcategory of the essentially small bounded constructible
category, and by definition a skeleton generates $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A))$. In a
triangulated category with direct sums generated by a set of compact
objects, the compact objects form the thick closure of that set
[Stacks, [09SM](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SM), [09SN](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SN)]. In our case this closure is $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$.
