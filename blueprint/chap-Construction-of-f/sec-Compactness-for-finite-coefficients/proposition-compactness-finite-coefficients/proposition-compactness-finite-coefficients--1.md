Every object of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ is compact in $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A))$,
and a small skeleton of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ is a set of compact generators.
