The comparison functor is the inverse image $\nu_X^*$ of the morphism of ringed topoi. It is exact and preserves direct sums, as stated in Proposition [proposition-unbounded-comparison-finite-coefficients]; its restriction to the localizing categories in clause [proposition-comparison-etale-derived-category--2] therefore preserves the same sums.
