This identification of localizing categories is bypassed by the Lean route, which transports bounded constructible objects through their étale origins without identifying these localizing categories.

The functor $\nu_X^*$ identifies $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\acute{e}t},A))$ with $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A))$.
