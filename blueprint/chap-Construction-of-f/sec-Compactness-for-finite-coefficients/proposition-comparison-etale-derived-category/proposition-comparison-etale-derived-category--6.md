For any commutative ring $A$ and any open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$,
there is a natural isomorphism
$$
\nu_X^*j_{\acute{e}t,!}\simeq j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,!}\nu_U^*
$$
of functors $D(U_{\acute{e}t},A)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$,
induced by (2.10).
