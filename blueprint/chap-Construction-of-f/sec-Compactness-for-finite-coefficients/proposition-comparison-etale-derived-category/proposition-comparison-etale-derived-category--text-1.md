*Shared context.* Let $A$ be a finite admissible ring and $\nu_X^*:D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$ the comparison functor.

The first two details retain the localizing-category assertions of the proposition;
the Lean route bypasses them. The remaining details state the comparison results
used in Lean, in their more general setting of a commutative ring $A$ and schemes
with the indicated hypotheses. Direct-image comparison is stated for bounded below
complexes.
