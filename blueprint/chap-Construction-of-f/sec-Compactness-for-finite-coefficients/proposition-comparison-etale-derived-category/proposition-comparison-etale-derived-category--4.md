For every morphism $g:X\to Y$ of finite-type $k$-schemes and $K\in D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$, the map (2.9), $\nu_Y^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}K\to Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}\nu_X^*K$, is an isomorphism.
