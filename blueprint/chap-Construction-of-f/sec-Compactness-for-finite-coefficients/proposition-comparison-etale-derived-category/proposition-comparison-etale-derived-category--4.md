Let $A$ be a commutative ring and let $g:X\to Y$ be a quasi-compact and
quasi-separated morphism of schemes. For every $K\in D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$,
the canonical comparison map (2.9)
$$
\nu_Y^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}K\longrightarrow
Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}\nu_X^*K
$$
is an isomorphism, naturally in $K$.
