For any commutative ring $A$ and any morphism of schemes $g:X\to Y$,
there is a natural isomorphism
$$
g_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*\nu_Y^*\simeq\nu_X^*g_{\acute{e}t}^*
$$
of functors $D(Y_{\acute{e}t},A)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$.
