Inverse images compose compatibly with the change-of-site inverse image. Thus their natural comparison on sheaves is an isomorphism, and exactness gives the same statement on complexes. Under the equivalence of clause [proposition-comparison-etale-derived-category--2], it is the required inverse-image comparison.
