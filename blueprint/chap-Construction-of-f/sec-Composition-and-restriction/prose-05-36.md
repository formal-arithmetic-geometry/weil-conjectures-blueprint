Once we know that [$f^!$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image) preserves [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves)
(Chapter [chap-Constructibility-of-f]), these isomorphisms restrict to the
full subcategories [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves), and the same statements hold for
the adjunction between [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) and [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves).
