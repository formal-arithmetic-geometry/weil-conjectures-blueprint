For a finite commutative ring $\Lambda$ and a scheme $X$, there is a
specified isomorphism
$$
(\mathrm{id}_X)^!\simeq\mathrm{id}
$$
on $D(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},\Lambda)$. There is also a specified identity
isomorphism on $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Qbarl)$ for $X$ as in the
shared context.

These declarations identify the exceptional inverse image of the identity.
They do not state the compatibility of the composition comparisons with
this identification.
