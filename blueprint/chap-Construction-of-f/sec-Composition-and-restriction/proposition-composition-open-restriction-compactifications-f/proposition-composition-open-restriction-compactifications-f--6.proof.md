(4) On $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda))$, $j^*$ is right adjoint to $j_!$ and $p^!$
to $Rp_*=Rp_!$, so $j^*\circ p^!$ and $f^!$ are both right adjoint to
$Rp_*\circ j_!=Rf_!$, and they are isomorphic by uniqueness of right adjoints.
