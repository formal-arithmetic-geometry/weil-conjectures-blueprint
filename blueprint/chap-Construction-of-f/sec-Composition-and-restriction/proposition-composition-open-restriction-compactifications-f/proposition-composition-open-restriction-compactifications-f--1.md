Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring. For composable
$X\xrightarrow fY\xrightarrow gZ$, suppose that $f$, $g$, and $gf$ admit
compactifications and that $gf$ is quasi-compact. On the unbounded étale
derived categories there exists an isomorphism
$$
(gf)^!\simeq f^!g^!.
$$
This assertion does not specify a composition comparison or its coherence laws.

For separated schemes of finite type over $k$ as in the shared context,
there is a specified composition isomorphism on bounded constructible
$\Qbarl$-complexes. It is the conjugate of the specified comparison
$Rg_!Rf_!\simeq R(gf)_!$ under the exceptional adjunctions.
