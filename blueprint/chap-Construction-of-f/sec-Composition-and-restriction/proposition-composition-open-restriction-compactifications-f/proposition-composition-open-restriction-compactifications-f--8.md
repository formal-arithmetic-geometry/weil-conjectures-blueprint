The mainline bypasses a separate gluing statement for $f^!$ on open
coverings of $Y$ and uses open-base-change comparisons as needed.

For finite coefficients and a cartesian square satisfying
[proposition-composition-open-restriction-compactifications-f--5], it
identifies the restriction $u^*f^!K$ with $f_V^!v^*K$. This is the
comparison used when replacing the base by an open subscheme. The
statement that these local functors reconstruct $f^!$ for every
admissible coefficient ring is not a separate mainline declaration.
