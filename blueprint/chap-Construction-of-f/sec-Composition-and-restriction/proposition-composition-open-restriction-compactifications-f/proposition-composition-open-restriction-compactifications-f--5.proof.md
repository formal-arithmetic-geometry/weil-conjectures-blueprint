(3) Since $f\circ u=v\circ f_V$, parts (1) and (2) show that
$u^*f^!\simeq(fu)^!=(vf_V)^!\simeq f_V^!v^*$. For an open $j:U\to X$,
parts (1) and (2) also give $j^*f^!\simeq(fj)^!$. These two isomorphisms
show that $f^!$ is local on $X$ and on $Y$.
