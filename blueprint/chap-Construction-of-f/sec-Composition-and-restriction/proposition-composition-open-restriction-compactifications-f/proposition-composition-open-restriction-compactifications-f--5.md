Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring and consider a cartesian square
$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
X_V&\xrightarrow{u}&X\\
\downarrow f_V&&\downarrow f\\
V&\xrightarrow{v}&Y
\end{array}
$$
with $v$ an open immersion. Suppose that $f$ and $f_V$ are quasi-compact
and admit compactifications. The canonical comparison
$$
u^*f^!\longrightarrow f_V^!v^*
$$
is an isomorphism on the unbounded étale derived categories. Here the
pullbacks are exact, and the comparison is the mate of the inverse
compact-support base-change isomorphism.
