Let $v:V\to Y$ be an open immersion, $X_V=f^{-1}(V)$ with inclusion
$u:X_V\to X$, and $f_V:X_V\to V$ the restriction of $f$. Then
$u^*f^!\simeq f_V^!v^*$.
