Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring and let $j:U\to X$ be a
quasi-compact open immersion admitting a compactification. Then there is
an isomorphism
$$
j^!\simeq j^*
$$
on the unbounded étale derived categories, where $j^*$ is the exact
pullback applied to complexes.

For an open immersion between separated schemes of finite type over $k$
as in the shared context, the same comparison is specified on bounded
constructible complexes with coefficients $\mathcal O_E$ or $\Qbarl$.
