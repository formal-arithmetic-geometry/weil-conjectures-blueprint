*Shared context for the details.* For finite coefficients, $\Lambda$ is a finite
commutative ring and the functors act on the unbounded categories
$D(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},\Lambda)$ of étale sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules.
The schemes lie in a fixed universe, and the coefficient modules and sheaves
are taken in the next universe. Compactifications and quasi-compactness
assumptions are specified in each statement.

For $\ell$-adic coefficients, $\ell$ is prime and all schemes and morphisms
are over a field $k$ of characteristic $p>0$, with $p\ne\ell$; the schemes
are separated and of finite type over $k$. The functors act on
$\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ with the condensed coefficient ring
associated to $\Lambda$. Here $E$ is a finite extension of $\Qell$ embedded
in $\Qbarl$, and $\mathcal O_E$ is its ring of integers. These are the
settings of the mainline declarations; they do not supply the whole
statement on $\Loc(\Dctf(-,\Lambda))$ for every admissible $\Lambda$.
