Brown representability produces $f^!$ once the bounded constructible
complexes are known to be compact, that is, once $\Hom(C,-)$
commutes with direct sums for constructible $C$. The difficulty is that the
summands may have cohomology in degrees going off to $-\infty$, so that
$\Hom(C,-)$ sees all the groups $\Ext^n(C,-)$ at once. For instance, over
$\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf R$ these groups never stop, and we saw at the
beginning of this section that compactness fails there. By contrast, over a finite
field they stop, as the following example shows.
