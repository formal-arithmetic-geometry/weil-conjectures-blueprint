Brown representability produces $f^!$ once the bounded constructible
complexes are known to be compact generators. The point is that
$\Hom(C,-)$ commutes with direct sums for constructible $C$. The difficulty is that the
summands may have cohomology in degrees going off to $-\infty$. Then
$\Hom(C,-)$ sees all the groups $\Ext^n(C,-)$ at once. Over
$\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf R$ these groups never stop, and we saw at the
beginning of this section that compactness fails there. The following example
shows what happens over a finite field.
