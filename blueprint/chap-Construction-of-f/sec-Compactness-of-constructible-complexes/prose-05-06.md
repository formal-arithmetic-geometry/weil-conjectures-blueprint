The general argument rests on the same mechanism: a bound $d_C$, valid
for all constructible targets at once, beyond which $\Ext^q(C,-)$
vanishes. Until the end of Section [sec-Compactness-of-constructible-complexes], $\Lambda$ is $E$
or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, and we show that bounded constructible complexes are compact
in $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$. Since $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)=\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$,
these are the proposed compact generators. The advantage of working with $\Dbc$ is that we can use truncations
and the constructible heart, which is abelian by
Lemma [lemma-constructible-sheaves]. As for notation, $\Hom$ and
$\Ext$ without decoration are taken in $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, also when both arguments are
constructible sheaves, and a subscript indicates the coefficient ring where
it changes, as in the lattice arguments over $\mathcal O_E$.

By dévissage it suffices to show, for a constructible sheaf $C$ and a
family $(K_\lambda)$ of objects of $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$, that
$\bigoplus_\lambda\Hom(C,K_\lambda)\to\Hom(C,\bigoplus_\lambda K_\lambda)$
is bijective. Besides the uniform bound
$\Ext^q(C,N)=0$ for $q>d_C$ and all constructible $N$
(Lemma [lemma-uniform-ext-bound] below), we use that $\Ext^q(C,-)$
commutes with filtered colimits of complexes that vanish below a fixed
degree, in particular of sheaves
(Lemma [lemma-continuity-ext-out-constructible-complex], proved in
Section [sec-Continuity-of-Ext]).
