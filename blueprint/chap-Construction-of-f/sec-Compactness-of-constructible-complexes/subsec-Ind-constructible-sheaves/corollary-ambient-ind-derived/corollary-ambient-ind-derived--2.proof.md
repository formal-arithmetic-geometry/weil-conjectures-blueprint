Clause [corollary-ambient-ind-derived--1] shows that $\Dic(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is closed under triangles and direct sums. It contains $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ because constructible sheaves are ind-constructible. By the defining minimality of the localizing subcategory generated by these objects, it contains $\Loc(\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda))$.
