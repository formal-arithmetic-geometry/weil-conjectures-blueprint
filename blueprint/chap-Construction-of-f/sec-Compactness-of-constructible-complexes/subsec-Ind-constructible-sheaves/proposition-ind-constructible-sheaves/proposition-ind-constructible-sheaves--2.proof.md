By definition, realization sends a filtered diagram of constructible sheaves to its sheaf colimit. Its objects are therefore ind-constructible, and every ind-constructible sheaf has such a presentation. Together with full faithfulness from clause [proposition-ind-constructible-sheaves--1], this identifies the essential image exactly.
