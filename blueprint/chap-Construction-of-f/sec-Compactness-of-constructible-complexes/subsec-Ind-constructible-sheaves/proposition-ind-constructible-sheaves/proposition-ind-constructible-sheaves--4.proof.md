A finite direct sum of ind-constructible sheaves is a filtered colimit of finite direct sums of their constructible presentations, and these finite sums are constructible. An arbitrary direct sum is the filtered colimit of its finite subsums. Closure under filtered colimits from clause [proposition-ind-constructible-sheaves--3] proves the assertion.
