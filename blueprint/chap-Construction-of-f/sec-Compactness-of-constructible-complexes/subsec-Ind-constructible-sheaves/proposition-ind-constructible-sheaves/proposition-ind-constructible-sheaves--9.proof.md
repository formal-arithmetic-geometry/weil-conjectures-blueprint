By clauses [proposition-ind-constructible-sheaves--5] and [proposition-ind-constructible-sheaves--6], kernels and cokernels in the full ind-constructible subcategory are those in the ambient category of sheaves. An additive functor preserving these kernels and cokernels is exact. This applies to the inclusion.
