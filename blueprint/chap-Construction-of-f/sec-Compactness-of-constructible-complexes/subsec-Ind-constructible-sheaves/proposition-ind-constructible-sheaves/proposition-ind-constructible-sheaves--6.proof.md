The diagram construction in the proof of clause [proposition-ind-constructible-sheaves--5] writes a map $f$ as a filtered colimit of maps $v:C\to D$ between constructible sheaves, with both source and target colimits equal to those of $f$. Cokernels commute with these colimits, so $\operatorname{coker}f=\varinjlim\operatorname{coker}v$. The cokernels of $v$ are constructible, proving the claim.
