An *ind-constructible sheaf* on $X$ is a sheaf of $\Lambda_X$-modules
on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ that is a filtered colimit, computed in
$\operatorname{Mod}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, of constructible sheaves.
