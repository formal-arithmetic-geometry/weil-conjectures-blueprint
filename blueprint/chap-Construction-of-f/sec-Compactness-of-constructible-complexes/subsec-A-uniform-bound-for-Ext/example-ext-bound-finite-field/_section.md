The proof gives an explicit bound, which is far from sharp. For instance, for
$X=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$ and
$C=N=E$ we have $Z=S=X$. The complement of $Z$ is then empty, so
$R\underline\Gamma_Z$ is the identity. Moreover
$\operatorname{cd}_\ell(\mathbf F_q)=1$, and so the formula, read with
$\operatorname{cd}_\ell(j)=0$ for the empty open, gives
$d_C=0+1+2=3$, while $\Ext^q(E,E)$ vanishes already for $q>1$
(Example [example-compactness-finite-field]). The two wasted degrees do
no harm, because the arguments below only need some bound independent of $N$.
