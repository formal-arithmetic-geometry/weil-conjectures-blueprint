Let $\Lambda$ be admissible, let $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion
with open complement $j:V\hookrightarrow X$, and let $u:S\hookrightarrow Z$
be an open immersion. The functors
$R\underline\Gamma_Z:D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D(Z_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ and
$u^*R\underline\Gamma_Z:D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$
are left $t$-exact and commute with derived inverse limits of sequences,
and so does tensoring with a finite locally free $\Lambda_S$-module.
