Write $R\underline\Gamma_Z=i^*\fib(\mathrm{id}\to Rj_*j^*)$. Open restriction preserves products and is exact; $i^*$ preserves the required products by [Stacks, [09AA](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09AA)]; $Rj_*$ is a right adjoint and preserves products. All are exact as derived functors. Thus this fibre construction preserves products and fibres, and hence preserves the cone construction of derived inverse limits.
