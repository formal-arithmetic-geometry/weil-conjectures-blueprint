For every $C\in\Cons(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ there exists an integer $d_C\geq0$ such that $\Ext^q(C,N)=0$ for all $q>d_C$ and all $N\in\Cons(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$.
