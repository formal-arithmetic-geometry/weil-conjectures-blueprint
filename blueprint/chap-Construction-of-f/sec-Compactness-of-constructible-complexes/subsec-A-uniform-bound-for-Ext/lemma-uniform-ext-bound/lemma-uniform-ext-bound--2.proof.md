Descend the source's lisse sheaf alone to a finite extension $E_0$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$, and refine its strata so that it contains a lattice; this depends only on the source. Then descend the target's lisse sheaf to a finite extension $E\supset E_0$. The bound in the preceding clause depends only on the source strata and their cohomological dimensions, so it is uniform in the finite extensions $E'\supset E$ and passes to their colimit.
