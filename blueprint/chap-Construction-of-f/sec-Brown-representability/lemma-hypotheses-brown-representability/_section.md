For every admissible $\Lambda$, the category $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$
is locally small and triangulated, has arbitrary direct sums, and
has split idempotents. The thick category $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ is
essentially small, and a small skeleton is a set of compact generators
of $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$.
