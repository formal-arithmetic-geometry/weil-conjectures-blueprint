By clauses [lemma-hypotheses-brown-representability--2] and [lemma-hypotheses-brown-representability--3], this is a triangulated category with countable coproducts. The idempotent-splitting theorem [Stacks, [05QW](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05QW)] applies to precisely these hypotheses and shows that every idempotent splits.
