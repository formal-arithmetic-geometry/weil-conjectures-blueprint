For any object $N$, both proposed right adjoints represent the same functor $M\mapsto\Hom(Rf_!M,N)$. Yoneda gives a unique isomorphism that identifies the representing Hom bijections. Applying uniqueness to maps of $N$ makes these isomorphisms natural, and identifying the Hom bijections identifies the unit and counit. This is the stated adjunction-compatible uniqueness.
