Clause [theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image--1] constructs $f^!$ as a right adjoint to the exact triangulated functor $Rf_!$. The adjoint exactness theorem [Stacks, [0A8G](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A8G)], applied with this adjunction, gives its shift comparison and preservation of distinguished triangles. Hence $f^!$ is exact.
