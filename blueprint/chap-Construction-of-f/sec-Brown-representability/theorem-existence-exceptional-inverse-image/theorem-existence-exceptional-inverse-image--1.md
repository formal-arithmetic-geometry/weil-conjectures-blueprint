title = "Existence of the exceptional adjunction"

The coproduct-preserving functor $Rf_{!,\Lambda}$ has a right adjoint $f^!_\Lambda:\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda))\to\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda))$, with the adjunction bijections $\Hom(Rf_{!,\Lambda}M,N)\simeq\Hom(M,f^!_\Lambda N)$ for objects in the indicated categories.
