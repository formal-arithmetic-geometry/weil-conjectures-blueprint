The theorem of the cube [thm-sc-theorem-of-cube] and projectivity
[lem-sc-abelian-variety-projective], [lem-sc-ample-tensor] give the degree
of $[n]$ [lem-sc-isogeny-facts]. The rigidity lemma
[lem-sc-rigidity] — whose two topological steps
[lem-sc-constant-locus-closed], [lem-sc-finite-image-locus-open] are proved
here — makes abelian varieties commutative [lem-sc-av-commutative]. The
rest identifies the prime-to-$p$ torsion: $[n]$ is surjective on points
[lem-sc-finite-nonempty-point], [lem-sc-finite-flat-surj-points],
[lem-sc-isogeny-surjective]; a finite flat group scheme of order prime to
$p$ is etale [lem-sc-connected-finite-order], [lem-sc-fg-order-prime-etale],
so its points are $m$ in number and stable under extension of
algebraically closed fields [lem-sc-finite-etale-points]; and a divisible
abelian group with $\ell$-torsion of order $\ell^r$ has
$\ell^n$-torsion $(\mathbf Z/\ell^n)^r$
[lem-sc-divisible-group-structure], whence
$A[n](k)\cong(\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z)^{2g}$ [lem-sc-ntorsion-etale]. The
three $p$-linear lemmas [lem-sc-fixed-points-finite],
[lem-sc-lang-steinberg], [lem-sc-artin-schreier-k],
[lem-sc-frobenius-fixed-invariance] are the characteristic-$p$ toolkit
used downstream.
