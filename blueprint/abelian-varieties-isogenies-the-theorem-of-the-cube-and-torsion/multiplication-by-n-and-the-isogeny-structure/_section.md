The pullback of a symmetric line bundle under $[n]$ is the $n^2$-th tensor
power [lem-sc-mult-n-pullback]; the degree of a pullback along a finite
morphism multiplies by the degree [lem-sc-deg-pullback-finite]; an ample
module on a proper curve has positive degree
[lem-sc-ample-positive-degree]. Together these show that $[n]$ is a
surjective homomorphism with finite kernel [lem-sc-mult-n-isogeny], which
is what the degree computation of the previous section rests on.
