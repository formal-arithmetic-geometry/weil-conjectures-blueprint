The abelian-variety half of the source's algebraic-group extraction, split
off from the group-scheme chapter at a section boundary chosen so that no
item of that chapter refers to an item of this one. Statements, proofs,
labels and dependencies are unchanged by the split.

The chapter carries the abelian-variety material the curve estimates need:
the theorem of the cube, rigidity and commutativity, the degree of
multiplication by $n$, and the structure
$A[n](k)\cong(\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z)^{2g}$ of the prime-to-$p$ torsion.

Four statements appear in the source only as placeholder comments
recording that they moved to the Jacobian chapter
(`thm:sc-jacobian-representability`, `def:sc-jacobian`,
`lem:sc-pic-tangent-space`, `thm:sc-theorem-of-the-base`); they are not
imported here.
