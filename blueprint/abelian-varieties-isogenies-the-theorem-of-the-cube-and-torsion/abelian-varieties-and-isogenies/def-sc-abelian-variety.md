Let $k$ be a field. An *abelian variety* over $k$ is a group scheme $A$
over $k$ which is proper, smooth and connected over $k$. A group scheme
with these three properties is also called a *complete group variety*.

*Import note.* The source's sentence ends with a semicolon and the
definition then closes, so the body is slightly truncated; the clause above
is completed with a full stop and nothing else is added. That the two
classes coincide is [lem-sc-abelian-variety-characterization], as the
pointer remark [rem-ptr-sc-abelian-variety-1] records.
