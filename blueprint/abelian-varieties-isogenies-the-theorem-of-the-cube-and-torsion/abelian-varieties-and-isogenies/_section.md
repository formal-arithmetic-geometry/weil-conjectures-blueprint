Abelian varieties [def-sc-abelian-variety] are exactly the complete group
varieties [lem-sc-abelian-variety-characterization],
[rem-ptr-sc-abelian-variety-1] — the smoothness hypothesis cannot be
dropped, as $\alpha_p$ and $\mu_p$ show. Isogenies
[def-sc-isogeny] are finite flat, with degree the order of the kernel
[lem-sc-isogeny-degree], [rem-ptr-sc-isogeny-2]. Multiplication by $n$ and
the $n$-torsion subgroup scheme [def-sc-mult-n] are finite flat of rank
$n^{2g}$ [lem-sc-mult-n-finite-flat].
