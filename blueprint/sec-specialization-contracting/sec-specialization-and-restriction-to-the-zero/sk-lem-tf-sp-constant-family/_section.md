For a separated $k$-scheme $Y$ of finite type and
$L\in\Dbc(Y,A)$, the specialization map is an isomorphism

$$
\sigma_Y(L):L\xrightarrow{\sim}
R\Psi_{Y\times S}(L_\eta).
$$

It is natural in $L$ and compatible with the preceding base change maps
for proper and smooth morphisms.
