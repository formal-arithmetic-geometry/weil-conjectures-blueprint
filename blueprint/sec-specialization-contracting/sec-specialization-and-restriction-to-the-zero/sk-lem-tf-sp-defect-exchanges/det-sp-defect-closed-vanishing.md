title = "The defect kills complexes supported on the centre"

Let $X$ be a separated finite-type $k$-scheme, $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ a closed subscheme
and $j\colon X\setminus Z\hookrightarrow X$ the open complement, and let $C$ be the defect of
[det-sp-defect-definition]. Then $C_{Z,Z}=0$, $C_{X,Z}(i_*M)=0$ for every
$M\in D^b_c(Z_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$, and for every $L\in D^b_c(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$ the
map induced by $j_!j^*L\to L$ is an isomorphism

$$
C_{X,Z}(j_!j^*L)\xrightarrow{\sim}C_{X,Z}(L).
$$
