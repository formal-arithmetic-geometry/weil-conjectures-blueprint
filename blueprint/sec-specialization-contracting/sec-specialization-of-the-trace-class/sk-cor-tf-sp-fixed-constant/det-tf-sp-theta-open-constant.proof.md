By Nagata's theorem choose a proper compactification $j:B\hookrightarrow\overline B$.
Then $\omega_B=j^*\omega_{\overline B}$, and open base change identifies the constant-family
units $\sigma_B$ with the restrictions of $\sigma_{\overline B}$. The composition law
$\theta_B=j_s^!\theta_{\overline B}\,\beta_j(\omega_{\overline B_\eta})$, together with
$j^!=j^*$ and $\beta_j=\alpha_j^{-1}$ for the open immersion, identifies $\theta_B$ with
$j^*\theta_{\overline B}$. Restricting the identity
$\theta_{\overline B}\sigma_{\overline B}(\omega_{\overline B})=1$ of
[det-tf-sp-theta-proper-constant] gives $\theta_B\sigma_B(\omega_B)=1$, and naturality of
$\sigma_B$ gives $\theta_BS_B(z)\sigma_B(A)=z$ as in the proper case.

If only $F_{\mathrm{red}}=B\times S$, nilpotent invariance of the total family and of its
two fibres identifies the six operations, their units and counits, and nearby cycles with
those of the reduction, so the assertion for $B\times S$ applies.
