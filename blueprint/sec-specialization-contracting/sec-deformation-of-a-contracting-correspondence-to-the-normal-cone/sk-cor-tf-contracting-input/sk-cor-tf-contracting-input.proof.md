We apply Theorem [sk-thm-tf-contracting-local-term] with $Z=\{y\}$.
Then $C_Z$ is the reduced point $z$ and $F_{0,Z}=F_0$, so that $c_Z$
is the identity correspondence of the point, with coefficient
$K_{\bar y}$, and by Definition [sk-d-trace-class]
its local term is the categorical trace. If the first leg is the
identity, the base change map in the construction of $u_Z$ is the
identity, and hence the restricted morphism is the stalk map $u_{\bar y}$.
