Write $t_f:f^!M\otimes^{\mathbf L} f^*N\to f^!(M\otimes^{\mathbf L} N)$ for the
projection-formula mate of the exceptional counit. Then

$$
\beta_f \Psi_V(t_{f_\eta})\mu_V(1\otimes\alpha_f)
=f_s^!\mu_W\ t_{f_s}(\beta_f\otimes1).
\tag{E.8}
$$

The source is $\Psi_Vf_\eta^!M\otimes^{\mathbf L} f_s^*\Psi_WN$. Moreover

$$
\beta_{gf}=f_s^!\beta_g\ \beta_f(g_\eta^!),
\qquad\theta_V=f_s^!\theta_W\ \beta_f(\omega_{W_\eta}).
\tag{E.9}
$$

On $X\times X$, let
$\kappa_M:p_2^*\omega_X\otimes^{\mathbf L} p_1^*M\to p_1^!M$ be the
normalized map of external duality. Then

$$
\beta_{p_1}\Psi(\kappa_M)\mu
(\alpha_{p_2}\otimes\alpha_{p_1})
=\kappa_{\Psi_XM}(p_{2s}^*\theta_X\otimes1).
\tag{E.10}
$$

Finally, let $c:C\to X\times_SX$ be a correspondence with fixed scheme
$F$, and consider the cartesian square defining $F$, with closed immersion $i:F\hookrightarrow C$,
$d:F\to X$, and closed diagonal $\Delta:X\hookrightarrow X\times X$,
and let $\zeta:c^!R\Delta_*\xrightarrow\sim Ri_*d^!$ be the base change
isomorphism for exceptional inverse image and proper direct image. Then

$$
Ri_{s*}\beta_d\ \chi_i\ \Psi_C(\zeta_\eta)
=\zeta_s\ c_s^!(\chi_\Delta)\ \beta_c(R\Delta_{\eta*}).
\tag{E.11}
$$
