The results of the six-functor chapters of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex`
that need [smooth-purity]: Verdier biduality through its finite, integral,
rational and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ stages, the class of coefficients
preserved by the six operations, the biduality class, and the parts of the
formal core and of the finite-Tor toolbox that rest on them. Everything these
results need before purity is in [six-functors]. The two hubs are the
finite-stage site comparison [lem-weil-finite-duality-site-comparison], which
identifies the dualizing objects of the two sites by smooth purity, and the
preservation class [thm-sf-preservation-class], which uses the general
admissibility theorem of [smooth-purity].

Sections the source splits between the two blocks keep their title here, with
"(continued)" and an id ending in `-continued`.
