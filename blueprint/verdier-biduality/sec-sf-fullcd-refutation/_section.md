The residue that [rem-sf-fullcd-general-form] records and that
[open-sf-ambient-adjoint-input] and [open-sf-preservation-gaps] name
is a statement about the comparison morphism
def:weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism alone: a bound $M$, uniform in
the coefficient sheaf *and* over the pro-étale base changes, on the
vanishing degree of $R^q\nu_{V,*}$. This section adds three items and states
that this statement is *false*, and that the sharp bound on the
coefficient class the blueprint uses is $M=0$. No other statement, proof,
label or number of this chapter is changed by them; the affected remarks were
rewritten to cite them.
