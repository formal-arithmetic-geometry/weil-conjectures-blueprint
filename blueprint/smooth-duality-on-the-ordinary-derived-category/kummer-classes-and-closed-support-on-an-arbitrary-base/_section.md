The Kummer sequence is exact over any base on which $n$ is invertible
[lem-amb-kummer-any-base]; cohomology with support in a closed subscheme is
a relative section complex [lem-amb-closed-support-hom]; and an effective
Cartier divisor produces a supported Kummer class
[lem-amb-cartier-kummer-map], which after forgetting support is the first
Chern class of $\mathcal O_T(D)$. These are the classes that will provide
the trace maps.
