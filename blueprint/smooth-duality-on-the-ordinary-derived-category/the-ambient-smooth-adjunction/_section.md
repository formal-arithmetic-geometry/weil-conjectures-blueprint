We work with a finite commutative ring $A$, killed by an integer $n$
invertible on the schemes in question. Derived categories, inverse images
and tensor products have their ambient meanings, and the construction takes
place on the small étale sites. In particular it does *not* identify a right
adjoint on another site by restriction to constructible test objects.

Compactifiable morphisms [def-asd-compactifiable] carry a compact-support
candidate [def-amb-etale-compact-support]. Coproduct compatibility from a
finite cohomological dimension [lem-exc-direct-image-coproducts] gives it
for proper morphisms [lem-amb-proper-coproducts], together with the proper
projection formula [lem-amb-proper-coproducts-projection]. The comparison
maps are coherent [lem-amb-etale-compact-comparisons], so Brown
representability produces the right adjoint
[thm-amb-etale-exceptional-adjoint], which is étale-local
[lem-amb-etale-adjoint-locality].
