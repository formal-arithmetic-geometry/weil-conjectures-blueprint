The computation that replaces purity. $Rq_*A$ for the projective line splits
as $A\oplus A(-1)[-2]$ [thm-amb-p1-decomposition], which defines the trace
and the counit [def-amb-p1-trace]. Both integrals of the diagonal class are
the identity [lem-amb-p1-diagonal-integrals], and the two triangle
identities follow: $Rq_*\dashv q^*(1)[2]$ [thm-amb-p1-adjunction]. Factoring
affine space into affine lines gives $Rp_{d!}\dashv p_d^*(d)[2d]$
[cor-amb-affine-space-adjunction].
