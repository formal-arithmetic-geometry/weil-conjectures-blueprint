For a smooth separated compactifiable $f$ of pure relative dimension $d$,
$\omega_f=f^\times A_S$ is a shifted rank-one lisse sheaf and
$f^*K\otimes^L\omega_f\cong f^\times K$
[thm-asd-smooth-tensor] — the orientation is not chosen. The adjunction
internalizes [prop-asd-internal-adjunction], commutes with arbitrary base
change [prop-asd-smooth-base-change], and preserves bounded constructible
complexes of finite Tor amplitude [cor-amb-smooth-ctf-preservation].
