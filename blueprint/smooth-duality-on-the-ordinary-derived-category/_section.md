An independent replacement for the cyclic smooth-purity input. **All
categories in this chapter are ordinary derived categories of étale
sheaves**, and no constructibility of an exceptional inverse image is
assumed anywhere.

The chapter builds the ambient compact-support functor and its right adjoint
by Brown representability
[the-ambient-smooth-adjunction], assembles the Kummer and closed-support
input [kummer-classes-and-closed-support-on-an-arbitrary-base], computes the
projective line and affine space
[the-projective-line-and-affine-space-adjunctions], deduces cohomological
duality for smooth morphisms with its base change
[cohomological-duality-for-smooth-morphisms-and-its-base-change], and closes
with purity along the zero section of a vector bundle
[vector-bundles-and-their-ambient-purity].
