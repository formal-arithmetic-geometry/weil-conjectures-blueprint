Locally free modules [def-amb-finite-locally-free-module] give vector
bundles with a zero section [def-asd-vector-bundle-zero-section], the same
notion the purity chapter introduces under its own conventions
[rem-asd-vector-bundle-comparison]. The affine-space trace is an isomorphism
[lem-amb-affine-space-top-trace] and is invariant under linear coordinate
changes [lem-amb-linear-trace-invariance], so the traces glue to an
integration map along a bundle [def-amb-vector-integration]. Its adjoint is
the purity isomorphism [thm-amb-vector-bundle-purity] — obtained from the
projective-line diagonal, compact supports and ordinary adjunctions alone,
with no localized top Chern class and no general smooth purity.
