Two identifications that the proper-base-change chapter states as admitted
classical inputs are proved here. Gluing along open immersions
[lem-galois-glue] gives effective etale descent for finite etale morphisms
[lem-galois-descent]; with the etale-local triviality of a finite etale
cover [lem-fet-locally-trivial] this identifies $\mathrm{F\acute Et}(Y)$
with $\mathrm{FLC}(Y)$ [thm-galois-lc-etale]. A two-term flasque resolution
[lem-galois-flasque] then turns the lifting construction into the canonical
bijection between $M$-torsors and $H^1(Y_{\Et},\underline M)$
[thm-galois-torsor-h1].
