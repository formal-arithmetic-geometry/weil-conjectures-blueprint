$\mathrm{F\acute Et}(Y)$ has finite limits and colimits, with base change
exact [lem-fet-finite-limits]; for connected $Y$ the categorical connected
objects are the connected schemes and $F_{\bar y}$ reflects isomorphisms,
so $(\mathrm{F\acute Et}(Y),F_{\bar y})$ is a Galois category
[lem-fet-galois-category].
