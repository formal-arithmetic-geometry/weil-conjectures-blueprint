The fibre functor is an equivalence onto finite continuous
$\pi_1(Y,\bar y)$-sets [thm-galois-fundamental]. Galois covers
[def-galois-object] are characterised by their degree
[lem-galois-object-degree] and as the connected Galois objects of the
Galois category [lem-galois-object-categorical],
[rem-ptr-galois-object-2]; components of products of Galois covers are
Galois [lem-galois-products], so the Galois covers are cofinal and
pro-represent the fibre functor [cor-galois-cofinal].
