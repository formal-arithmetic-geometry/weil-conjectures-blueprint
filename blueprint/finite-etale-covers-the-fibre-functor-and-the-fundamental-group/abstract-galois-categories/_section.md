The abstract machinery, with $\mathcal C$ and $F$ axiomatised
[def-galois-category]. $\operatorname{Aut}(F)$ is profinite
[lem-galois-aut-profinite] and acts continuously
[lem-galois-action-continuous], [rem-ptr-galois-category-1]. The axioms
give the usual dictionary between $F$ and $\mathcal C$
[lem-galois-axioms]; the $S_n$-action on the $n$-fold fibre power produces
a Galois object over every connected object
[lem-galois-object-exists] — this is what replaces the Galois closure as a
black box — whence transitivity of $\operatorname{Aut}(F)$ on fibres
[lem-galois-transitive] and the equivalence with finite $G$-sets
[thm-galois-abstract-equivalence].
