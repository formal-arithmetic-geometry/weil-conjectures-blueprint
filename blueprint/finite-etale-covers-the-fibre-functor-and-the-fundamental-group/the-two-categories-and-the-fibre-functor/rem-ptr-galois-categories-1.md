In the notation of [def-galois-categories], the fibre $|P_{\bar y}|$ of a
finite etale $P\to Y$ is the set of commutative triangles
$\bar y\to P\to Y$ with the first arrow equal to the given $\bar y\to Y$,
and that this set is finite, $P\times_Y\bar y$ being a finite disjoint
union of copies of $\bar y$, is [lem-galois-geometric-fibre].
