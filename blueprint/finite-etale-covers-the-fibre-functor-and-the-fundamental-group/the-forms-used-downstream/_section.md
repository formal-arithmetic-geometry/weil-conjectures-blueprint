The three corollaries the rest of the blueprint invokes: connected finite
etale covers correspond to open subgroups of $\pi_1$, with degree the index
[cor-galois-open-subgroups]; for finite abelian $M$,
$H^1(Y_{\Et},\underline M)$ is the group of continuous homomorphisms
$\pi_1(Y,\bar y)\to M$ [cor-galois-abelian-h1]; and lisse sheaves are
continuous representations of $\pi_1$
[cor-galois-lisse-representations]. [rem-galois-uses] records exactly
which uncited invocations elsewhere in the blueprint these replace.
