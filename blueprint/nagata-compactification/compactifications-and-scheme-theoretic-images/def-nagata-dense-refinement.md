For a compactification $C=(X\xrightarrow{j}\overline X\to S)$, define
$\mathsf{Dense}(C)$ by replacing $\overline X$ with
$\operatorname{SIm}(j)$ and using $j^{\mathrm{im}}$ as its first map.
