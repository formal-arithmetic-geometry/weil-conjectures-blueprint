Throughout this section let $S$ be Noetherian, let $U\to S$ be separated and
of finite type, and fix an open cover $U=U_1\cup U_2$ such that
$U_1\cap U_2$ is dense in $U$. Assume each $U_i\to S$ is compactifiable.
