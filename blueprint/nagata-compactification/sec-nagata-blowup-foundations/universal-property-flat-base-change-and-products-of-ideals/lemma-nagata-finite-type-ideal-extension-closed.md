Let $X$ be qcqs, let $j\colon Z\to X$ be an affine morphism — in the
application a closed immersion —, let $V\subseteq X$ be a quasi-compact
open, and let $\mathcal H\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be a quasi-coherent ideal
whose pullback $\mathcal H\cdot\mathcal O_Z$ is of finite type and whose
vanishing locus $V(\mathcal H)$ is disjoint from $V$. There exists a finite
type quasi-coherent ideal $\mathcal J\subseteq\mathcal H$ with

$$
\mathcal J\cdot\mathcal O_Z=\mathcal H\cdot\mathcal O_Z
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
V(\mathcal J)\cap V=\emptyset .
$$

Applied to the preimage of a finite type ideal $\mathcal I$ on a closed
subscheme $Z\subseteq X$ under
$\mathcal O_X\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal O_Z$, this produces a finite type
ideal $\mathcal J$ on $X$ with
$\mathcal J\cdot\mathcal O_Z=\mathcal I$ and
$V(\mathcal J)\cap V=\emptyset$.

## Proof

Note first that [lemma-nagata-finite-type-ideal-extension-exists] alone
does not suffice: it prescribes the restriction to a quasi-compact
*open*, and the inclusion $\mathcal J\subseteq\mathcal H$ only gives
$\mathcal J\cdot\mathcal O_Z\subseteq\mathcal H\cdot\mathcal O_Z$. We
build $\mathcal J$ as a sum $\mathcal J_1+\mathcal J_2$ of two ideals, using
that $V(\mathcal J_1+\mathcal J_2)=V(\mathcal J_1)\cap V(\mathcal J_2)$.

For $\mathcal J_1$ apply
[lemma-nagata-finite-type-ideal-extension-exists] with $W=V$: since
$V(\mathcal H)\cap V=\emptyset$ we have $\mathcal H|_V=\mathcal O_V$,
which is of finite type, so we obtain a finite type
$\mathcal J_1\subseteq\mathcal H$ with $\mathcal J_1|_V=\mathcal O_V$,
that is $V(\mathcal J_1)\cap V=\emptyset$.

For $\mathcal J_2$ we ask only for a finite type
$\mathcal J_2\subseteq\mathcal H$ with
$\mathcal J_2\cdot\mathcal O_Z=\mathcal H\cdot\mathcal O_Z$. Cover $X$
by finitely many affine opens $W$. Since $j$ is affine, $j^{-1}(W)$ is
affine, and $(\mathcal H\cdot\mathcal O_Z)(j^{-1}(W))$ is the extension of
$\mathcal H(W)$ along $\mathcal O_X(W)\to\mathcal O_Z(j^{-1}(W))$; it is
finitely generated by hypothesis, so finitely many sections of
$\mathcal H(W)$ already generate it. Let $N_W\subseteq\mathcal H(W)$ be
the ideal they span. The quasi-coherent ideal $\widetilde{N_W}$ on $W$ is of
finite type, so [lemma-nagata-finite-type-ideal-extension-exists],
applied to the open $W$ and to the ambient ideal
$\iota_*\widetilde{N_W}\cap\mathcal H$, yields a finite type
$\mathcal G_W\subseteq\mathcal H$ on $X$ with $\mathcal G_W(W)=N_W$. Put
$\mathcal J_2=\sum_W\mathcal G_W$, a finite sum of finite type ideals
inside $\mathcal H$. Both $\mathcal J_2\cdot\mathcal O_Z$ and
$\mathcal H\cdot\mathcal O_Z$ may be compared on the affine cover
$\{j^{-1}(W)\}$ of $Z$, where they agree by the choice of $N_W$; hence they
are equal.

Finally $\mathcal J=\mathcal J_1+\mathcal J_2$ is of finite type and
contained in $\mathcal H$, so
$\mathcal J\cdot\mathcal O_Z\subseteq\mathcal H\cdot\mathcal O_Z$, while
$\mathcal J\supseteq\mathcal J_2$ gives the reverse inclusion; and
$V(\mathcal J)\subseteq V(\mathcal J_1)$ is disjoint from $V$.
