The Lean statement
(`nagataCompactification_of_locallyOfFinitePresentation`) asserts the
existence of a compactification for an arbitrary separated, quasi-compact
morphism of finite presentation over a quasi-compact quasi-separated base,
rather than for the finite-presentation envelope $X_f^{\mathrm{fp}}\to S$
specifically, and the intermediate choice-definitions of this section
([def-nagata-final-descended-model],
[def-nagata-final-noetherian-compactification],
[def-nagata-final-basechanged-compactification] and their spec lemmas)
are collapsed into its proof and are not separately formalized.
