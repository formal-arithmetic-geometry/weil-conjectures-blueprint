Since $Y\to S$ is separated, $\Gamma_f$ is automatically closed in
$U\times_SY$; the condition above is the stronger requirement that it stay
closed after enlarging $U$ to $X$. The subscheme $\Gamma_f$ maps
isomorphically to $U$, so it carries exactly the data of the pair $(U,f)$.
This is the closedness property that will be needed when separated $S$-schemes
are glued along isomorphic open subschemes.
