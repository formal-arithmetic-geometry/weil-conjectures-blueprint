This is the relative form of quasi-affineness, and it is the hypothesis Cases
1–3 below actually use. Over an affine base it says exactly that $Y$ is a
quasi-affine scheme, so the reduction in Case 1 to an absolutely quasi-affine
$Y$ loses nothing. Conversely a quasi-compact morphism whose source is a
quasi-affine scheme is quasi-affine, since a quasi-compact open subscheme of a
quasi-affine scheme is quasi-affine, and an affine morphism is quasi-affine.
