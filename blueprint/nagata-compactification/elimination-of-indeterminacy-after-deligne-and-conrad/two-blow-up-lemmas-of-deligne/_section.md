Note that $q$ is an isomorphism over $V$, since
$V(\mathcal I_1+\mathcal I_2)\subseteq V(\mathcal I_2)=T_2$ is disjoint from
$V$; so if $\mathcal I_1$ and $\mathcal I_2$ are of finite type then $q$ is a
$V$-admissible blow-up. The set-theoretic inclusion
$p^{-1}(T_1)\cap\overline Y\subseteq p^{-1}(T_2)$ is not enough for what
follows: it is the scheme-theoretic factorization in item (3) that produces the
function used in the proof.
