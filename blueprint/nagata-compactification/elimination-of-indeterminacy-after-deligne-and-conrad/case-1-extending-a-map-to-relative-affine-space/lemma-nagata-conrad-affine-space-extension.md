Let $X=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ be affine, let $U\subseteq X$ be a
quasi-compact open and let $f\colon U\to\mathbb A^n_X$ be an $X$-morphism.
There is a finite type quasi-coherent ideal $\mathcal I$ on $X$ with
$V(\mathcal I)\subseteq X\setminus U$, so that
$\widetilde X=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)\to X$ is a $U$-admissible
blow-up, together with an open subscheme $W\subseteq\widetilde X$ containing
the inverse image of $U$ and an $X$-morphism
$\widetilde f\colon W\to\mathbb A^n_X$ extending $f$, whose graph

$$
\Gamma_{\widetilde f}\hookrightarrow
\widetilde X\times_X\mathbb A^n_X
$$

is closed. That is, $(W,\widetilde f)$ is a quasi-domination of
$\widetilde X$ over $\mathbb A^n_X$ extending $f$.

No density hypothesis on $U$ is needed here; when $U$ is dense in $X$, the
open $W$ is automatically dense in $\widetilde X$ by
[lemma-nagata-conrad-dense-in-admissible-blowup].

## Proof

Since $U\hookrightarrow X$ is quasi-compact we may cover $U$ by finitely
many basic opens $\operatorname{Spec}(A_{u_i})$, $1\le i\le m$; then the
finitely generated ideal $(u_1,\ldots,u_m)$ cuts out $X\setminus U$. The
$X$-morphism $\operatorname{Spec}(A_{u_i})\to\mathbb A^n_X$ corresponds to
an $A$-algebra map

$$
A[t_0,\ldots,t_{n-1}]\to A_{u_i},\qquad t_j\mapsto g_{ij}/u_i^N
$$

with $g_{ij}\in A$ and $N$ large (one $N$ for all $i,j$). Agreement on the
overlaps $\operatorname{Spec}(A_{u_iu_{i'}})$ says that for some large $M$,
$(u_iu_{i'})^M(u_i^Ng_{i'j}-u_{i'}^Ng_{ij})=0$ in $A$. Replacing $g_{ij}$ by
$u_i^Mg_{ij}$ and $N$ by $N+M$ we may assume

$$
u_i^Ng_{i'j}=u_{i'}^Ng_{ij}\quad\text{for all }i,i',j,
$$

and we call this common element $g_{i,i',j}=g_{i',i,j}\in A$; note
$g_{i,i,j}=u_i^Ng_{ij}$.

Let $I\subseteq A$ be the ideal generated by all $u_i^N$ and all
$g_{i,i',j}$, and let $\mathcal I$ be the associated quasi-coherent ideal
sheaf; it is of finite type. Since $I\subseteq(u_1,\ldots,u_m)$ we get
$V(\mathcal I)\subseteq X\setminus U$, and $\mathcal I$ restricts to the unit
ideal on $U$ because $u_i^N$ is invertible on
$\operatorname{Spec}(A_{u_i})$; so $\widetilde X=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)\to X$ is a $U$-admissible blow-up.

Write $\mathbb P^n_X=\operatorname{Proj}(A[T_0,\ldots,T_n])$. The affine
charts $\operatorname{Spec}(A[I/u_i^N])$ and
$\operatorname{Spec}(A[I/g_{i,i',j}])$ cover $\widetilde X$ by
[prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart-cover]. Define $X$-morphisms on them
by

$$
\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(A[I/u_i^N]\bigr)\to
\mathbb A^n_X=D_+(T_n),\qquad
T_j/T_n\mapsto g_{i,i,j}/(u_i^N)^2,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(A[I/g_{i,i',j}]\bigr)\to D_+(T_j),\qquad
T_r/T_j\mapsto g_{i,i',r}/g_{i,i',j}\ (r\le n-1),\quad
T_n/T_j\mapsto u_i^Nu_{i'}^N/g_{i,i',j}.
$$

All displayed fractions lie in the relevant chart algebra because their
numerators lie in $I$. Repeated use of the relation
$u_i^Ng_{i'j}=u_{i'}^Ng_{ij}$ checks that these maps agree on overlaps, so
they glue to an $X$-morphism $\widetilde f\colon\widetilde X\to \mathbb P^n_X$. On $\operatorname{Spec}(A_{u_i})\subseteq U$ the first
formula reads $T_j/T_n\mapsto g_{ij}/u_i^N$, so $\widetilde f$ extends $f$.

For the last sentence: $\mathbb P^n_X\to X$ is separated, so the graph
$\Gamma_{\widetilde f}\subseteq\widetilde X\times_X\mathbb P^n_X$ is closed.
Taking $W=\widetilde f^{-1}(\mathbb A^n_X)$, which is an open subscheme of
$\widetilde X$ containing the inverse image of $U$, the intersection
$\Gamma_{\widetilde f|_W}=\Gamma_{\widetilde f}\cap(\widetilde X\times_X \mathbb A^n_X)$ is closed in $\widetilde X\times_X\mathbb A^n_X$, which is
the quasi-domination condition of
[def-nagata-conrad-quasi-domination].
