Let $Y=\varprojlim_\alpha Y_\alpha$ be a cofiltered limit of qcqs schemes with
affine transition maps. If $Y$ is separated, then there is an index
$\alpha_0$ such that $Y_\alpha$ is separated for every $\alpha\geq\alpha_0$.

## Proof

Choose a finite affine open cover $U_1,\dots,U_m$ of one stage
$Y_{\alpha_1}$ and let $U_{\alpha,k}$, $U_k$ denote the inverse images in
$Y_\alpha$ and in $Y$; these are affine because the transition maps are
affine. A scheme covered by affine opens whose pairwise intersections are
affine, and for which the sections over such an intersection are generated by
the sections of the two members, is separated; so two things must be
descended.
First, $U_{k_1}\cap U_{k_2}$ is affine because $Y$ is separated, hence
$U_{\alpha,k_1}\cap U_{\alpha,k_2}$ is affine for all $\alpha$ past some
stage by Stacks [Tag 01Z6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01Z6).
Second, the sections over $U_{\alpha,k_1}\cap U_{\alpha,k_2}$ are generated
over the sections of $U_{\alpha,k_1}$ by finitely many elements, because that
intersection is a quasi-compact open of the affine $U_{\alpha,k_1}$; and the
elements of $\Gamma(U_{k_1}\cap U_{k_2})$ that already come from a stage,
inside the subring generated there by the sections of the two members, form a
subring, which by separatedness of $Y$ is everything. So those finitely many
generators are reached at a common stage, and the generation statement then
persists at every later stage because the sections over the intersection at a
later stage are generated by those of the earlier stage together with those of
the corresponding member of the cover.
