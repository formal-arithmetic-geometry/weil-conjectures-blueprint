1. Algebraize the given ring map f as an R-algebra S and retain its weakly etale hypothesis. We use the w-local and w-strictly-local terminology of [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local] and [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], together with the equivalent spectral description in [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible]. The construction of the strict w-local cover is carried out in the same universe: its filtered-colimit flatness and size bookkeeping use `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, while the description of its component space uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-prime-spectrum], `lem-weil-pbc-quasicompact-components-clopen-intersection`, and [dag-lem-weil-pbc-w-local-closed-points-components-homeomorphism]. The restriction construction has the stated spectral range by `lem-weil-pbc-restriction-spectrum-range`. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local], [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-prime-spectrum], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-w-local-closed-points-components-homeomorphism], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, `lem-weil-pbc-quasicompact-components-clopen-intersection`, `lem-weil-pbc-restriction-spectrum-range`)
2. Put C = WStrictLocalization R and let J be the image in C of the closed-point ideal of WLocalization R. The strict-local construction gives R to C ind-etale and faithfully flat, and C is w-strictly local; in particular its maximal localizations are strictly henselian in the sense of `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`. The split-etale maximal-local criterion `lem-weil-pbc-split-etale-covers-maximal-local-criterion` is the input establishing the strict henselian conclusion. Every prime of R is the contraction of a point of V(J) in Spec C, as in the closed-point-surjectivity part of the construction. (uses `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`, `lem-weil-pbc-split-etale-covers-maximal-local-criterion`)
3. Form the base change D' = C tensor_R S. Base change preserves the weakly etale condition, so C to D' is weakly etale. It also gives S to D' ind-etale and faithfully flat, by the standard Mathlib base-change results RingHom.IndEtale.isStableUnderBaseChange and RingHom.FaithfullyFlat.isStableUnderBaseChange. These are the ind-etale and flat properties in the sense of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra])
4. Let K = J D' and let D = K.WLocalization, which is the refinement ring used by the Lean definition. The map D' to D is ind-Zariski and w-local. For a prime q' of D' over a maximal m of C, weak etaleness makes the residue-field extension algebraic: the fiber over a field is ind-etale, and residue separability is supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-weakly-etale-field-separable` and `lem-trf-ind-etale-residue-separable`. Thus the W-localization closed-point formula applies and identifies V(JD) with the closed points of Spec D. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], `lem-weil-pbc-weakly-etale-field-separable`, `lem-trf-ind-etale-residue-separable`)
5. If n is maximal in D, the preceding closed-point equality implies that J is contained in its contraction to C. That contraction is a closed point of Spec C, hence is maximal because C is w-local; this is precisely the closed-point/component interpretation from [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local], [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], and [dag-lem-weil-pbc-w-local-closed-points-components-homeomorphism]. Therefore every maximal ideal of D lies over a maximal ideal of C. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local], [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-w-local-closed-points-components-homeomorphism])
6. The map C to D is weakly etale by transitivity. At a prime of D, choose a maximal ideal above it. Its contraction is maximal by the previous step, so the corresponding localization of C is strictly henselian. The localized map is weakly etale (localization itself is weakly etale by `lem-weil-pbc-localization-ring-weakly-etale`) and local, hence is an isomorphism by the strict-local rigidity theorem `thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-strict-local`. The proof of that rigidity theorem uses the filtered-union argument `lem-kun-algebra-union` and the residue-field separability input `lem-weil-pbc-weakly-etale-field-separable`, together with the henselian terminology `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`. Consequently Algebra.WeaklyEtale.bijectiveOnStalks gives bijectivity on every stalk of C to D. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-localization-ring-weakly-etale`, `thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-strict-local`, `lem-kun-algebra-union`, `lem-weil-pbc-weakly-etale-field-separable`, `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`)
7. The contraction map Spec D to Spec C is w-local: by RingHom.isWLocal_iff_isMaximal_of_isMaximal it is enough to check maximal ideals, which was done above. Applying Algebra.BijectiveOnStalks.indZariski_of_isWLocal, the ind-Zariski-on-bijective-stalks result `lem-weil-pbc-ind-zariski-bijective-on-stalks`, shows that C to D is ind-Zariski. Since R to C is ind-etale, transitivity now gives that R to D is ind-etale, in the sense of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local], [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], `lem-weil-pbc-ind-zariski-bijective-on-stalks`)
8. The S-algebra structure on D is the composite S to D' to D. Since S to D' is ind-etale and D' to D is ind-Zariski (hence ind-etale), transitivity gives Algebra.IndEtale S D. The localization presentation remains in the common universe, and its flatness is the filtered-colimit flatness supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
9. It remains to prove that Spec D to Spec S is surjective. First, the closed-point construction for C supplies, for every p in Spec R, a point of V(J) over p; passing to C/J proves that Spec(C/J) to Spec R is surjective. The component and restriction descriptions used for this assertion are exactly those in `lem-weil-pbc-restriction-spectrum-range`, [dag-lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-prime-spectrum], and `lem-weil-pbc-quasicompact-components-clopen-intersection`. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-prime-spectrum], `lem-weil-pbc-restriction-spectrum-range`, `lem-weil-pbc-quasicompact-components-clopen-intersection`)
10. Base-changing that surjection along R to S gives a surjection Spec(S tensor_R (C/J)) to Spec S; in Lean this is the explicit lemma PrimeSpectrum.comap_surjective_tensorProduct_left. For a prime q of S, choose a prime Q0 of this tensor product above it and pull Q0 back along the quotient-and-symmetry map D' to S tensor_R (C/J). The ideal K maps to zero, so the resulting prime Q of D' lies in V(K) and still lies over q.
11. The W-localization spectral-range theorem, applied to K and the fact that V(K) consists of closed points (the residue-field algebraicity from `lem-trf-ind-etale-residue-separable` and `lem-weil-pbc-weakly-etale-field-separable` is what verifies this), lifts Q to a prime P of D. Since the S-algebra structure factors through D' and the D' prime contracts to q, P contracts to q. Thus Spec D to Spec S is surjective. (uses `lem-trf-ind-etale-residue-separable`, `lem-weil-pbc-weakly-etale-field-separable`)
12. The S-module D is flat by transitivity of flatness from S to D' and from D' to its W-localization. Flatness together with the just-proved surjectivity of Spec D to Spec S gives Module.FaithfullyFlat S D via Module.FaithfullyFlat.of_comap_surjective. Therefore D is ind-etale and faithfully flat over S, while R to D is ind-etale. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra])
13. Finally take T to be D and g to be the canonical map S to D. Forgetting the auxiliary algebra structures, the Lean declaration RingHom.WeaklyEtale.exists_indEtale_comp packages T, its commutative-ring instance, and g. The equivalences IndEtale.algebraMap_iff and faithfullyFlat_algebraMap_iff, together with IsScalarTower.algebraMap_eq, identify g with the required ring homomorphism and identify g.comp f with the R-algebra map R to D. Hence g is ind-etale and faithfully flat and g.comp f is ind-etale, as required.
