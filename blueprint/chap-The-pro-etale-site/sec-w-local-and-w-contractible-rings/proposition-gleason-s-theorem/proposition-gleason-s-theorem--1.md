A compact Hausdorff space $S$ is extremally disconnected if and only if
it is projective with respect to continuous surjections of compact
Hausdorff spaces: for every continuous surjection $T_1\to T_2$ of compact
Hausdorff spaces, every continuous map $S\to T_2$ lifts to a continuous map
$S\to T_1$.
