1. Regard the three geometric hypotheses on R as the structure [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible] of an IsWContractibleRing: R is w-local in the sense of [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], its maximal localizations are strictly henselian in the terminology of `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`, and its connected-component space is extremally disconnected. The quasi-compact spectral component and clopen-intersection facts used in this packaging are supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-quasicompact-components-clopen-intersection`. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local], `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`, `lem-weil-pbc-quasicompact-components-clopen-intersection`)
2. Let I := vanishingIdeal (closedPoints (PrimeSpectrum R)). By Mathlib's zeroLocus_vanishingIdeal_eq_closure and IsClosed.closure_eq, together with the closedness of closed points supplied by w-locality [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local], we have hI : zeroLocus I = closedPoints (PrimeSpectrum R). (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local])
3. Put S' := (I.map (algebraMap R S)).WLocalization. The W-localization is an ind-Zariski localization, hence its local maps are stalkwise bijections by `lem-weil-pbc-ind-zariski-bijective-on-stalks`; the localization maps are weakly etale by `lem-weil-pbc-localization-ring-weakly-etale`. Its filtered colimit presentation remains in the common universe and preserves the required flatness by `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. Since R to S is faithfully flat and ind-etale, the WLocalization theorem Ideal.WLocalization.faithfullyFlat_map_algebraMap gives faithful flatness of R to S'; the residue-field algebraicity needed there is the separable-algebraic residue result `lem-trf-ind-etale-residue-separable`. Algebra.IndEtale.trans then gives the ind-etale structure R to S'. (uses `lem-trf-ind-etale-residue-separable`, `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, `lem-weil-pbc-ind-zariski-bijective-on-stalks`, `lem-weil-pbc-localization-ring-weakly-etale`)
4. The WLocalization closed-point calculation Ideal.WLocalization.algebraMap_specComap_preimage_closedPoints_eq, applied with hI and the same residue-field separability input `lem-trf-ind-etale-residue-separable`, gives zeroLocus (I.map (algebraMap R S')) = closedPoints (PrimeSpectrum S'). Thus S' satisfies the hypotheses of IsWContractibleRing.exists_retraction_of_zeroLocus_map_eq_closedPoints. (uses `lem-trf-ind-etale-residue-separable`)
5. For any ind-etale faithfully flat R-algebra T that is w-local and satisfies the corresponding zero-locus equality, closed points of Spec T contract to closed points of Spec R: unfold mem_zeroLocus and use Ideal.mem_comap.mpr together with hI. Ind-etale maps are weakly etale, and Algebra.WeaklyEtale.bijectiveOnStalks applies as follows. Given a prime q of T, choose a maximal ideal n containing q with q.exists_le_maximal. The zero-locus hypothesis makes n.map's contraction contain I, so hI makes that contraction maximal; the w-strictly-local structure then makes its localization strictly henselian by IsWStrictlyLocalRing.isStrictlyHenselianLocalRing_localization and `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`. The stalk map at n is bijective by thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-strict-local, and localization from n down to q yields bijectivity on every stalk. (uses `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`, `lem-weil-pbc-localization-ring-weakly-etale`, `thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-strict-local`)
6. Now apply the auxiliary theorem `lem-weil-pbc-wlocal-isClosed-bijOn-retraction` to T. Faithful flatness makes the connected-components map Spec T to Spec R surjective, via PrimeSpectrum.comap_surjective_of_faithfullyFlat. Since the component space of R is extremally disconnected, CompactT2.ExtremallyDisconnected.projective supplies a continuous section rho. Its range is compact and therefore closed (isCompact_range), and the section makes the component map restricted to range rho bijective. The auxiliary theorem, using the stalk bijectivity from the preceding step and the closed-point contraction property, produces g : T ->+* R with g.comp (algebraMap R T) = RingHom.id R. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-wlocal-isClosed-bijOn-retraction`)
7. Apply the preceding construction to T = S' and the equality from step 4, obtaining g : S' ->+* R. The required map is f := g.comp (algebraMap S S'). The scalar-tower identity for the W-localization and the equation for g give f.comp (algebraMap R S) = RingHom.id R by RingHom.comp_assoc. This is exactly the retraction asserted for the original faithfully flat ind-etale map.
