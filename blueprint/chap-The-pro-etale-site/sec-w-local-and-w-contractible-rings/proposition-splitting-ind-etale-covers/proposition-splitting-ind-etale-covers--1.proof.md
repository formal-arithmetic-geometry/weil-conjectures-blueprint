Use the splitting theorem for w-contractible affine schemes in [Stacks, [097D](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/097D), [09AZ](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09AZ), [0982](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0982)], the imported input cited by the reference. In its ring formulation, a faithfully flat ind-étale algebra over a w-strictly local ring with extremally disconnected profinite component space admits an algebra retraction. The definition of w-contractibility supplies exactly those two hypotheses for $R$, and the given map $R\to B$ supplies the faithfully flat ind-étale hypothesis. The theorem therefore gives an $R$-algebra map $s:B\to R$ with $s\circ(R\to B)=\mathrm{id}_R$. The source outline below explains its component-space construction; the refinement and gluing assertion in that outline is supplied by this cited splitting theorem.
