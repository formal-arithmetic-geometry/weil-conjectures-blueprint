Let $R$ be w-contractible and let $I\subset R$ be an ideal such that
$V(I)$ is the set of closed points of $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$.
Let $R\to B$ be a faithfully flat ind-étale ring map. Suppose that $B$
is w-local and that $V(IB)$ is exactly the set of closed points of
$\operatorname{Spec}(B)$. Then $R\to B$ has an $R$-algebra retraction
$B\to R$.
