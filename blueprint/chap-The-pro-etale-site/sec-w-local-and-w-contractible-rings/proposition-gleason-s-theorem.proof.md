This is the projectivity theorem for compact Hausdorff spaces; see
[Stacks, [08YN](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/08YN)]. We sketch the implication from extremal disconnectedness
to projectivity, which is the direction used later. To lift a map
$S\to T_2$ along a surjection $T_1\to T_2$, one pulls the surjection back to
$S$ and chooses a minimal closed subspace of the fibre product that
still surjects onto $S$. Extremal disconnectedness forces this
irreducible surjection to be a homeomorphism, and its inverse gives the
lift.
