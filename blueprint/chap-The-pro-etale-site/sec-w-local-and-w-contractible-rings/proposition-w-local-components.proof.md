For (1), each component consists of the generalizations of its unique
closed point, and $T^c$ is compact. Part (2) follows, and (3) holds
because the spectrum of a strictly henselian local ring has one closed
point and one component. See [Stacks, [096A](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/096A), [0968](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0968), [0969](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0969)].
