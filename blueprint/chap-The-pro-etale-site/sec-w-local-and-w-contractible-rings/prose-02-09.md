So a [w-strictly local](@definition-w-local-w-strictly-local) ring is a family of strictly henselian local rings
indexed by the profinite set of its components, and a [w-contractible](@definition-w-local-w-strictly-local) ring
is such a family indexed by an [extremally disconnected](@definition-w-local-w-strictly-local) profinite set.
