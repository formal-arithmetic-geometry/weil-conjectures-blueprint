The image of $\operatorname{Spec}(R_{\mathfrak m})$ consists exactly of the primes contained in $\mathfrak m$, namely the generalizations of its closed point. By the w-local component description used in clause [proposition-w-local-components--1], this is precisely its connected component; see [Stacks, [0968](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0968)].
