1. The spectrality, closed-point, and w-local hypotheses are exactly the data packaged by `WLocalSpace X`: spectrality as in `def-weil-spectral-space`, the closed-point subset from `def-weil-closed-points`, and closedness together with uniqueness of closed specializations as in [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local] and [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal]. Let f be the canonical map `ConnectedComponents.mk ∘ ((↑) : closedPoints X → X)`.
2. Because `closedPoints X` is a closed subspace, `IsClosed.isClosedEmbedding_subtypeVal WLocalSpace.isClosed_closedPoints` gives a closed embedding into X and hence a `CompactSpace (closedPoints X)`. The spectral hypotheses also provide the compact, quasi-separated, and prespectral structure needed for `t2Space_connectedComponent`; using the clopen-intersection description of components from `lem-weil-pbc-quasicompact-components-clopen-intersection`, this gives `T2Space (ConnectedComponents X)`. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-quasicompact-components-clopen-intersection`)
3. Apply `isHomeomorph_iff_continuous_bijective`. The continuity part is `ConnectedComponents.continuous_coe.comp continuous_subtype_val`, so it remains to prove injectivity and surjectivity of f.
4. For injectivity, take closed points `⟨x,hx⟩` and `⟨y,hy⟩` with equal images under f. If x ≠ y, the totally separated closed-point subtype yields, by `exists_isClopen_of_totally_separated`, a clopen U₀ containing x and whose complement contains y. Let U be `generalizationHull (Subtype.val '' U₀)` and V be `generalizationHull (Subtype.val '' U₀ᶜ)`.
5. The closed embedding of the closed-point subtype has a closed-map operation, so the images of U₀ and U₀ᶜ are closed. Therefore `isClosed_generalizationHull_of_wLocalSpace` makes U and V closed. Every z in X has a closed specialization c by `exists_isClosed_specializes`; c lies in one of U₀ or U₀ᶜ, and `mem_generalizationHull_iff` then shows z ∈ U ∪ V.
6. U and V are disjoint. Indeed, if z belonged to both, `mem_generalizationHull_iff` would give closed points c and d, lying respectively in U₀ and U₀ᶜ, to which z specializes. `WLocalSpace.eq_of_specializes` forces c = d, contradicting membership in the clopen set and its complement. Thus U and V are complementary; since both are closed, U is clopen.
7. The points x and y lie in U and V respectively by `mem_generalizationHull_iff`. Since U is clopen, `IsClopen.connectedComponent_subset` puts the connected component of x inside U. Equality of the component images, rewritten with `ConnectedComponents.coe_eq_coe'.mp`, puts y in that same component, hence in U, contradicting y ∈ V and the disjointness. Therefore x = y, proving injectivity.
8. For surjectivity, let c ∈ `ConnectedComponents X`. Choose a representative x with `c.exists_rep`. By `exists_isClosed_specializes`, choose a closed point p with x ⤳ p. The closure of the singleton {p} is connected, and `isConnected_singleton.closure.subset_connectedComponent` together with `specializes_iff_mem_closure` shows that p lies in the connected component of x. Hence `connectedComponent_eq_iff_mem` and `ConnectedComponents.coe_eq_coe.mpr` identify f(⟨p, mem_closedPoints_iff.mpr hp⟩) with c.
9. The map f is continuous, injective, and surjective, so `isHomeomorph_iff_continuous_bijective` proves that the canonical map from closed points to connected components is a homeomorphism.
