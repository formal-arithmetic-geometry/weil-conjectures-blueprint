The Lean proof bypasses this description of components and works
directly with closed points and localizations at prime ideals.

If $R$ is w-local, the component of $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$
containing a closed point $\mathfrak m$ is the image of
$\operatorname{Spec}(R_{\mathfrak m})$.
