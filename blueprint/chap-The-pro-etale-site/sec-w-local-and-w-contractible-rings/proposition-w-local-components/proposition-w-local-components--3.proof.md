Clause [proposition-w-local-components--2] identifies the component with the local spectrum at its closed point. The w-strict locality condition makes this local ring strictly henselian, as in [Stacks, [0969](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0969)]. Thus the component has the asserted form.
