For the retained informal statement, every component contains a
unique closed point $\mathfrak m$ by
[proposition-w-local-components--1], and is identified with
$\operatorname{Spec}(R_{\mathfrak m})$ by
[proposition-w-local-components--2]. The ring $R_{\mathfrak m}$ is
strictly henselian by [definition-w-local-w-strictly-local--3].
Lean uses this last property as part of the w-strict locality
hypothesis, without identifying the component with this spectrum.
