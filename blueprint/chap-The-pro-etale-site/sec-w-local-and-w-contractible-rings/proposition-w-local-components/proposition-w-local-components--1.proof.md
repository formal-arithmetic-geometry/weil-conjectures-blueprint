In a w-local spectral space each connected component consists of the generalizations of its unique closed point. Hence the indicated map is bijective. The closed-point subspace is compact and the component space is Hausdorff (the profinite component-space description in [Stacks, [096A](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/096A)]), so the continuous bijection is a homeomorphism.
