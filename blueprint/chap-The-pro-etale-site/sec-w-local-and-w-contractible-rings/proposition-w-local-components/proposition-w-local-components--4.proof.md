Let $\mathfrak m$ be the maximal ideal of $R$. The spectrum of any
local ring is w-local: its only closed point is $\mathfrak m$, so
closed specializations are unique and the set of closed points is
closed. Every element outside $\mathfrak m$ is a unit, and hence
$R_{\mathfrak m}\cong R$. Strict henselianity transfers along this
isomorphism. Since every maximal ideal is $\mathfrak m$, this proves
that $R$ is w-strictly local.

Every prime specializes to $\mathfrak m$, so all points have the
same image in $\pi_0(\operatorname{Spec}(R))$. A space with at most
one point is extremally disconnected: the closure of an open subset
is either empty or the whole space. The definition
[definition-w-local-w-strictly-local--4] now gives w-contractibility.
