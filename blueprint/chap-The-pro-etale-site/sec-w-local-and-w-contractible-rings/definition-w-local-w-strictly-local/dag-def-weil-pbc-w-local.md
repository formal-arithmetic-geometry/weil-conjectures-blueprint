Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ an affine scheme `def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions` `def-weil-affine-spectrum`. For $f\in A$, put $D(f)=\{\mathfrak p\in X:f\notin\mathfrak p\}$ and $V(f)=X\setminus D(f)$. The *patch topology* on $X$ `def-weil-pbc-constructible-topology` is generated by the subsets $D(f)$ and $V(f)$ for $f\in A$. A subset is *constructible* here `def-weil-constructible-subset` if it is a finite Boolean combination of these subsets. We call $X$ *w-local* if its closed points `def-weil-closed-points` form a closed subset and every connected component has exactly one closed point. In this last sentence, closedness and connected components refer to the Zariski topology; a connected component is a maximal connected subset.
