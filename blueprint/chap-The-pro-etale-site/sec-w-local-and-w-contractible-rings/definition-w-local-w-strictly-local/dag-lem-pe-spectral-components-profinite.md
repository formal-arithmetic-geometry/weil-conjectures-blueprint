Let $T$ be a spectral topological space `def-weil-spectral-space`. Let $\pi_0(T)$ be the quotient of $T$ by the equivalence relation of belonging to the same connected component (a maximal connected subset), endowed with the quotient topology. Then $\pi_0(T)$ is compact, Hausdorff, and totally disconnected (every connected subset has at most one point); in particular it is a profinite space.
