Let $x\ne y$. Choose disjoint open neighbourhoods $U$ and $V$ of
$x$ and $y$. Extremal disconnectedness makes $\overline U$ open,
so $\overline U$ is clopen. Since $V$ is disjoint from $U$, it is
also disjoint from $\overline U$. Thus $\overline U$ and its
complement separate $x$ and $y$.

A totally separated space is totally disconnected. If the space is
also compact, it is therefore compact Hausdorff and totally
disconnected, which is the defining condition for a profinite space.
