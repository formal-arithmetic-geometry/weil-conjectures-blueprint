In an extremally disconnected compact Hausdorff space, the closure of every open set is clopen. By regularity, for two distinct points one can choose an open neighborhood of the first whose closure misses the second. Thus clopen sets separate points, and the space is totally disconnected and compact Hausdorff, hence profinite.
