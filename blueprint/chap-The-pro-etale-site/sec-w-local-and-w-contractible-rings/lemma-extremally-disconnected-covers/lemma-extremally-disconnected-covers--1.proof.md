Give the underlying set $T^\delta$ the discrete topology and take $S=\beta(T^\delta)$. By [Stacks, [090D](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/090D)], this Stone–Čech compactification is extremally disconnected. The identity map $T^\delta\to T$ extends continuously to $S\to T$ because $T$ is compact Hausdorff. Its image contains every point of $T$, so it is surjective.
