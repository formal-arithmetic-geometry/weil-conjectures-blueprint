To compute on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ we want the limits of étale affines to form a
basis (Proposition [proposition-pro-etale-affines-form-basis]), so that
étale sheaves can be evaluated on every object by a colimit. Objects of
$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ are only weakly étale, while the covers above are ind-étale,
and the next theorem bridges the two.
