The proposition would be of little use if [w-contractible](@definition-w-local-w-strictly-local) rings were rare.
The next theorem shows that every ring has a faithfully flat [ind-étale](@definition-ind-etale-pro-etale-weakly)
cover by one, so that, after a single refinement, every affine object of
[$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$](@definition-small-pro-etale-site) is replaced by one over which covers split.
