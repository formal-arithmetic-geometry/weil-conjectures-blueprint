To pass from a single stratum to arbitrary constructible sheaves, note
that for a finite ring $A$ the proposition applies to every finite local
system of $A$-modules. A constructible sheaf is built from such local
systems on the strata of a stratification, by extension by zero and
extensions. Both operations preserve classical sheaves. For extension by
zero this follows from the comparisons of Section [sec-Direct-images].
For extensions it is
Corollary [corollary-bounded-below-classical-complexes], which uses that
$R^1\nu_{X,*}$ vanishes on classical sheaves.
