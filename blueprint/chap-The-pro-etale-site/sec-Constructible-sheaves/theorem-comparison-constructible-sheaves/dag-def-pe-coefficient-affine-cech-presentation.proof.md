1. Fix the parameters X, U, m, V, f, the chosen SiteCechComplex S supplied by `def-weil-pbc-site-cech-complex`, and the small category J. Define CoefficientAffineCechPresentation as the Lean structure record whose fields are, for every j, a source-model base object, a source-model family object for each a, an arrow from each family object to the base, a covering proof for those arrows, and a chosen SiteCechComplex stage j. The remaining fields are transition arrows for every i to j, p, and tuple t; their identity and composition equations; the equation saying transitions commute with every index map; projections from S.W p t to each included stage; projection compatibility with transitions and with every index map; and the affine_stage and affine_vertex assertions. Thus the displayed data and equations are recorded directly as fields.
2. For each p and tuple t : Fin (p + 1) → Fin m, define P.nerve p t on J by sending j to (P.stage j).W p t and a : i ⟶ j to P.trans a p t. The fields P.trans_id and P.trans_comp are exactly the identity and composition laws, so this data is a functor J ⥤ Scheme.smallModelComparisonEtaleSite X. The field P.trans_index additionally records compatibility of every transition with every index map.
3. For the same p and t, define P.cone p t over P.nerve p t followed by the small-model inclusion with vertex S.W p t and j-leg P.projection p t j. For a : i ⟶ j, the required cone naturality is the projection-transition equation. In the Lean natural-transformation orientation its goal is reduced by Category.id_comp, and `(Category.id_comp _).trans (P.projection_trans a p t).symm` proves it.
4. The field P.projection_index makes these cone projections commute with every index map, while P.affine_stage and P.affine_vertex give absolute affineness for every stage tuple and for every cone vertex S.W p t. Together with the base, family, covering, stage, transition, and projection fields above, this is exactly the stated presentation structure.
5. Everything asserted here is either a field of the structure or the two definitions P.nerve and P.cone with their displayed functor and naturality proofs. The definition therefore records data and coherence equations only; it makes no existence, exactness, section, limit, or cohomology assertion.
