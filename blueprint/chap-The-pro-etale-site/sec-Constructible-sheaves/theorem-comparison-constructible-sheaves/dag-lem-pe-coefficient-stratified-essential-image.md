Let X be a scheme, A an arbitrary commutative ring in the coefficient universe, S a finite constructible locally closed scheme stratification, and F a fixed-scalar module sheaf on the coefficient-sized pro-etale site. Suppose each pullback along a stratum immersion is in the essential image of the corresponding fixed-scalar etale comparison inverse image. Then F itself is in that essential image. Neither finite local models nor Noetherian coefficients nor quasi-compactness of X are assumed. Stratification and pullbacks are as in `def-sfb-affine-locally-constructible-proetale-modules`, and comparison is `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism`.
