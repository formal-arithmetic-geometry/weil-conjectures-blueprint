1. Let J and K be the coefficient-sized etale and pro-etale topologies, let G be the small-model comparison inclusion, and let a be its continuous sheaf pullback adjunction. The continuity needed for this comparison is supplied by [dag-lem-route-small-model-comparison-inclusion-is-continuous]. The unit of a is invertible: apply NatTrans.isIso_iff_isIso_app, then reflect isomorphisms through the two dense restrictions AsSmall.up and AffineEtale.Spec using isIso_of_reflects_iso. On presheaves the unit factors, by Adjunction.map_restrictFullyFaithful_unit_app and Functor.whiskerLeft_comp, as the fully faithful opposite-Kan-extension unit followed by the pro-etale sheafification unit. The Kan-extension factor is invertible by full faithfulness. For each affine etale object Y, form the constant affine presentation P : AffinePresentation X PUnit (the presentation notion and its limit data are those of [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map]), with vertex the corresponding pro-etale affine object and identity diagram. The theorem coefficientComparison_presentation_toSheafify_isIso, namely [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-presentation-sheafification], makes the sheafification factor invertible at that vertex; hence it is invertible at every affine Y, and therefore the comparison unit is an isomorphism on every sheaf. (uses [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map], [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-presentation-sheafification], [dag-lem-route-small-model-comparison-inclusion-is-continuous])
2. The inclusion G preserves finite limits (the Lean proof unfolds smallModelComparisonInclusion and invokes infer_instance), so flat_of_preservesFiniteLimits gives RepresentablyFlat G. Together with the continuity from [dag-lem-route-small-model-comparison-inclusion-is-continuous], compatiblePreservingOfFlat and Functor.isContinuous_of_coverPreserving show that Over.post G is continuous on the slice over V; the cover part is obtained with CoverPreserving.of_isContinuous G J K and its overPost V instance. Thus the continuous direct images R = G.sheafPushforwardContinuous and Rv = (Over.post G).sheafPushforwardContinuous used below are defined. (uses [dag-lem-route-small-model-comparison-inclusion-is-continuous])
3. Both constant-sheaf functors are the sheafifications of constant presheaves as in [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], with values in the module-sheaf category of `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`. Since G preserves the terminal object, compose constantSheafAdj J D with the comparison adjunction a and identify its right adjoint with terminal evaluation using Functor.sheafPushforwardContinuousCompSheafSectionsObjIso. The left-adjoint uniqueness isomorphism leftAdjointUniq gives k : (comparison pullback)(constantSheaf J M) ≅ constantSheaf K M. Composing the invertible unit at the etale constant sheaf with R.mapIso k gives d : constantSheaf J M ≅ R(constantSheaf K M).
4. For any pro-etale sheaf H, define q_H : (R H).over V ≅ Rv(H.over (G.obj V)) by ObjectProperty.isoMk _ (Iso.refl _); this is the literal sectionwise precomposition identification. Apply the invertible unit from the first step to F and then q_(comparison pullback F), followed by the supplied chart isomorphism e. The resulting isomorphism has target Rv((constantSheaf K D M).over (G.obj V)); use the inverse of the pro-etale constant-restriction isomorphism and the inverse q for the constant sheaf to rewrite this as (R(constantSheaf K D M)).over V. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-site-constant-restriction`)
5. Finally apply the etale and pro-etale slice constant-restriction isomorphisms from `lem-weil-pbc-site-constant-restriction`. In the exact order of the Lean term, compose (J.overPullback D V).mapIso of the unit at F, q, Rv.mapIso e, Rv.mapIso of the inverse pro-etale restriction isomorphism, the inverse q for the constant pro-etale sheaf, the inverse of (J.overPullback D V).mapIso d, and the etale restriction isomorphism. This is an isomorphism F.over V ≅ constantSheaf (J.over V) M, hence supplies the required Nonempty witness. The construction uses the same module M throughout and makes no nonemptiness, finiteness, local-constancy, or Noetherian assumption. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-site-constant-restriction`)
