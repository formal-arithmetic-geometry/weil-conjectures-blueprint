1. Write J for the coefficient-sized pro-etale topology, F for smallModelComparisonInclusion, G_a for the corresponding sheafPushforwardContinuous of H_a, and P_a = F.op.lan.obj (G_a).obj. The continuity of F is exactly [dag-lem-route-small-model-comparison-inclusion-is-continuous], so these comparison functors and their sheaf adjunction are available. For each a, [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-locally-constant-counit] makes the comparison counit at H_a an isomorphism. Compose its underlying presheaf map with toSheafify J on P_a; this composite tau_a is locally surjective by Mathlib Presheaf.isLocallySurjective_toSheafify', Presheaf.isLocallySurjective_of_iso, and Presheaf.isLocallySurjective_comp. (uses [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-locally-constant-counit], [dag-lem-route-small-model-comparison-inclusion-is-continuous])
2. Let L be the coefficient-sized sigma type of triples (Y,a,y), where Y is a small-model etale object, a : K, and y is a section of H_a at F.obj Y; let V(Y,a,y)=Y. The third coordinate immediately supplies the required section on F.obj Y. To prove that these images cover, fix a pro-etale object T and use the assumed covering family U with J.transitive (hU T). For an arrow W to T lying over U_i, choose a and a section s of H_a on U_i from hsec, and restrict s along W to obtain (H a).obj.map g.op s.
3. The sieve imageSieve of tau_a at this restricted section covers W by Mathlib Presheaf.imageSieve_mem. If k : Z ⟶ W belongs to that image sieve, its witness is an element t of P_a at Z. The private pointwise Kan-extension argument inhabited_lan_source, using Mathlib Types.jointly_surjective_of_isColimit on the pointwise left-Kan colimit, gives an etale object Y, a map b : Z ⟶ F.obj Y, and y in (G_a).obj (op Y). By the definition of G_a this is a section of H_a on F.obj Y, so (Y,a,y) is in L and b shows that the domain Z of k maps to the image of this member of the family. Thus k belongs to the pullback along W ⟶ T of Sieve.ofObjects (fun j ↦ F.obj (V j)) T. Hence the image sieve is contained in that pullback sieve, and J.superset_covering transfers its covering property.
4. Applying J.transitive to the original cover and these pullback coverings proves CoversTop for the family of images F.obj (V j). The family and all its section data are coefficient-sized sigma types. No element of K is chosen independently of hsec; therefore the same transitivity argument also covers empty K, empty coefficient sets, and an empty base.
