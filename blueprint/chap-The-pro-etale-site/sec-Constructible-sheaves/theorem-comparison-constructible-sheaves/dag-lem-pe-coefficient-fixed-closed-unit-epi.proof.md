1. Work first on the original pro-etale sites with ModuleCat in max v (u+1). Transport sheafification from the coefficient-sized AsSmall models. The comma categories computing left Kan extension are small in that same universe, so these extensions exist. Define the original-site inverse/direct-image adjunction using these actual constructions. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`)
2. For a quotient immersion Spec(B/I) into Spec B, apply the closed-site adjunction v left adjoint to b with invertible unit. Opposites and presheaf precomposition make b.op precomposition fully faithful for modules in the independent coefficient universe. Restriction to affine pro-etale bases is an equivalence, so the original-site direct image is fully faithful. An arbitrary closed immersion into affine X is isomorphic to a quotient immersion, giving an invertible sheaf counit. The proof is the affine portion of UpperLowerStar/ClosedImmersion.lean, with its coefficient universe generalized in proved private helpers. (uses `lem-exc-closed-site-adjunction`, `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`)
3. Use the complete argument of finite quasi-compact module detection in the larger coefficient universe: if the inverse-image unit kills a section, local injectivity of sheafification and the filtered-colimit description of Kan extension give weakly etale neighborhoods where it vanishes. QuasiCompactCover.of_comp_factor combines these neighborhoods for a finite jointly surjective family into a pro-etale cover. Separatedness kills the section, and naturality of the units proves detection of zero morphisms. These steps are reproved as private lemmas, because the existing Lean detection declaration fixes the smaller universe. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], `lem-sfb-finite-qc-proetale-module-detection`, `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`)
4. The open inverse image of a closed direct image is zero: the site-level map/base-change adjunction identifies open inverse image with restriction, whose sections are evaluated on an empty base change. The empty covering forces those sections to be zero. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`)
5. Prove the original-site unit epic by cancellation. If unit(F) composed with phi is zero, its closed pullback is zero because the pulled-back unit is inverse to the invertible counit by a triangle identity. Its open pullback is zero because its source is zero by the preceding disjointness calculation. The closed immersion and complementary quasi-compact open are a finite jointly surjective quasi-compact family, so zero-morphism detection gives phi=0. The preadditive cancellation criterion proves epimorphy. (uses `lem-exc-closed-site-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-finite-qc-proetale-module-detection`)
6. Transport the original-site adjunction through the two AsSmall sheaf equivalences. Its unit is a composite of equivalence units, the equivalence image of the epic original unit, and the direct image of another equivalence unit; hence it is epic. Its right adjoint is definitionally the coefficient-sized pushforward. Uniqueness of left adjoints identifies this transported unit with the canonical fixed-site unit up to an invertible factor, proving the stated Epi instance. (uses `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`)
