1. Apply the Grothendieck abelian comparison-unit argument in the full coefficient universe. The affine Kan-extension and dense-subsite sheafification argument is already proved for this universe by additiveUnitIso in ConstructibleComparisonModuleExact. Conjugate its adjunction by the two AsSmall sheaf equivalences; full faithfulness and hence invertibility of the unit are preserved. The constant-ring pullback presentation and lem-weil-pbc-module-inverse-image-unit-comparison identify the forgotten canonical module unit with the abelian unit followed by an invertible comparison. The module forgetful functor reflects isomorphisms, so the canonical module unit is invertible. The module pullback is therefore full and faithful, and the unit of every adjunction with this fixed left adjoint is invertible. This works on every module, without using injectivity. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-comparison-grothendieck-unit`, `lem-pe-constructible-comparison-module-exact`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-inverse-image-unit-comparison`)
2. Choose an injective resolution of the module pullback of I. For each positive degree, its direct-image homology computes the right derived direct image. Forget the module structure, transport the etale model to the original etale site, and restrict to its affine etale dense subsite. All three functors are faithful and exact, so they reflect exactness of the degree short complex. At every affine etale object, lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-affine-sections and the injective-resolution derived-evaluation isomorphism show that the evaluated short complex is exact. Objectwise evaluation therefore proves exactness of the presheaf short complex. Exact sheafification preserves it, and the sheafification counit identifies this sheafified short complex with the restricted sheaf complex. Faithful exact restriction reflects exactness back to the module short complex, proving zero homology in every positive degree. (uses [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-affine-sections], `lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful`)
