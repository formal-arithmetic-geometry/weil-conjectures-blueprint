Let i:Z→X be a closed immersion of schemes in universe u, A a commutative ring in max v (u+1), and H a sheaf of A-modules on the coefficient-sized pro-etale site of Z. If H belongs to the essential image of the etale comparison inverse image on Z, then its direct image along the canonical coefficient-model base-change functor belongs to the essential image of comparison on X. The comparison and inverse images are those of `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism` and `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`.
