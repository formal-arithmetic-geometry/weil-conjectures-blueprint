Let $X$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated, and let $\Lambda$ be a
discrete commutative Noetherian ring. There is an equivalence from the
full subcategory of constructible étale sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules
to $\Cons(X,\Lambda)$ whose forward functor, after inclusion into all
pro-étale $\Lambda_X$-module sheaves, is naturally isomorphic to the
restriction of the canonical inverse image $\nu_X^*$.
