1. Work with the affine pro-etale category `AffineProEt X` of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], interpreted for arbitrary X by the inverse-limit definition [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. Its inclusion `AffineProEt.toProEt X` uses `proAffineEtale_le_weaklyEtale`. The algebraic input to this inclusion is that ind-etale algebras, defined by filtered etale presentations in [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], are weakly etale; its flatness argument uses stability of flat modules under same-universe filtered colimits `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. On this basis restrict the coefficient-sized Kan extension along `AffineProEt.toProEt X` followed by `AsSmall.up`. For a universe-u cofiltered affine etale diagram, `AffineProEt.isLimitMapConeToSchemeOfIsLimit` gives the underlying scheme limit, and `Scheme.preservesColimit_yoneda` gives the colimit of Hom sets into each etale X-scheme: the stages are quasi-compact and quasi-separated, transitions are affine, and the target is locally of finite presentation over X. Transport this colimit through `ProEt.forgetOpCompYonedaIso` and `uliftFunctor`, obtaining exactly the representables of the coefficient-sized model. The fully faithful Kan-extension colimit criterion of `CategoryTheory/Pro/PresheafColimit`, proved using `final_costructuredArrowLift_of_isFinitelyPresentable`, then proves relative filtered-colimit preservation of the restricted Kan extension. Only universe-u diagrams are used in this sheaf-condition argument. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
2. Restriction further to affine etale objects identifies the Kan extension with the given etale sheaf using the invertible unit `B.op.lanUnit.app G.obj`, where B is the coefficient-sized comparison inclusion. The restriction is a sheaf by continuity of `AsSmall.up` and `AffineEtale.Spec X`, using `Functor.op_comp_isSheaf_of_isSheaf`. Apply `Presheaf.isSheaf_of_generates_of_isRelativelyPresentable` to `AffineProEt.minimalPrecoverage X`: `AffineProEt.generates_minimalPrecoverage`, `AffineProEt.minimalPrecoverage_le_relativelyPresentable`, and `AffineProEt.minimalPrecoverage_le_finite` supply its generating, relatively presentable, and finite properties. Together with the preceding relative-colimit calculation and exactness of universe-u filtered colimits of the displayed coefficient-sized modules (obtained by `hasFilteredColimitsOfSize_of_univLE` and `AB5OfSize_of_univLE`), this proves that the restricted Kan extension is a sheaf on the affine basis [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis])
3. The affine-basis inclusion followed by `AsSmall.up` is a dense subsite of the coefficient-sized pro-etale model: use the affine cover-density and restriction equivalence of [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], compose the two covering-sieve equivalences by `Sieve.functorPushforward_comp`, and use `Functor.IsCoverDense.comp_iff_of_isCoverDense`. To provide sheafification on the affine basis, first use `GrothendieckTopology.hasSheafify_of_isGrothendieckAbelian` on its coefficient-sized `AsSmall` model. This construction uses local presentability of the Grothendieck abelian coefficient category `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, the sheaf-orthogonality universal property of the transfinite plus unit [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], and finite-limit preservation of its sheaf corestriction [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits]. Transport `HasSheafify` back along `AsSmall.equiv.hasSheafify`; the required module-valued limits exist by `hasLimitsOfSizeShrink`. Now `Functor.IsDenseSubsite.isIso_whiskerLeft_toSheafify`, applied to the sheaf just established, makes the actual `toSheafify` map invertible at every `AsSmall.up` of an affine pro-etale basis object. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
4. For the vertex choose a stage i and restrict the original cone, after forgetting to schemes, along `Over.forget i`. This functor is initial, so `Functor.Initial.isLimitWhiskerEquiv` preserves the limiting property. Full faithfulness of `Scheme.Spec`, together with the natural isomorphisms `Scheme.isoSpec`, identifies the global-sections cocone of this affine limit with a ring colimit. The structure maps from the ring of stage i are etale: their scheme maps have etale composites to X and `Etale.of_comp` shows they are etale, while `HasRingHomProperty.iff_of_isAffine` translates this to rings. Cone compatibility gives the required factorization of the stage-i-to-vertex ring map through each term. Thus this is a universe-(u+1) filtered etale presentation in the sense of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]. Apply `CommRingCat.ind_etale_small_index` [dag-lem-pe-ind-etale-small-index] to shrink that ring presentation to universe u. The equivalences `RingHom.IndEtale.iff_ind_etale` and `proAffineEtale_iff_appTop` then prove that the projection to stage i is pro-affine-etale. Compose with the affine etale structure map of stage i using `proAffineEtale.comp_mem` and `proAffineEtale.of_isAffine`. This proves that the vertex is a basis object in the sense of [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. The shrinking is used only for basis membership. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-pe-ind-etale-small-index], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms])
5. Each affine etale stage is also a basis object by `proAffineEtale.of_isAffine` [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. Apply the basis sheafification-unit result of step 3 to the vertex from step 4 and to each stage. The resulting components are exactly those at `op (AsSmall.up.obj P.cone.pt)` and at `op (AsSmall.up.obj (P.diagram.obj i))`, giving the required conjunction with the original universe-(u+1) presentation unchanged. (uses [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms])
