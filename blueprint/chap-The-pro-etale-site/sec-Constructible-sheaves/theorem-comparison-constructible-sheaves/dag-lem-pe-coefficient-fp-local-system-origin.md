Let X be a scheme and A a commutative ring in an independent coefficient universe. Let F be a fixed-scalar module sheaf on the equivalent coefficient-sized pro-etale site. Suppose that, over every object, a covering sieve locally identifies F with constant finitely presented A-modules. Then F is in the essential image of the canonical fixed-scalar etale/pro-etale comparison inverse image. No Noetherian hypothesis is imposed. The sites and comparison are those of `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism` and `def-sfb-affine-locally-constructible-proetale-modules`; finite presentation is `def-sc-finitely-presented-module`.
