Apply coefficientFpLocalSystem_etaleCover to the actual comparison inverse image. 2. Reflect the cover through the transported inclusion directly: push the model covering sieve down through AsSmall, extract a generating pro-etale covering presieve at the terminal object, and use its joint surjectivity. Factoring each of its members through an etale V_i shows the underlying etale family is jointly surjective. The etale covering criterion gives a covering sieve of these objects, and AsSmall transports it to the coefficient-sized etale site. This is the fully proved private helper comparison_coversTop_reflect, including the empty-family and empty-base cases. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism`, `def-weil-pbc-etale-site`)
3. Reflect each supplied constant chart on the single etale object V_i, preserving its module M_i. For an arbitrary etale site object W use Sieve.ofObjects V W, which is covering by the preceding step. Each arrow of this sieve has domain U admitting q:U→V_i. Restrict the reflected chart along q; pushforwardOverMapIso identifies the restricted F with F.over U, and pushforwardContinuousSheafificationCompatibility identifies the restricted constant sheaf with constant M_i. Finite presentation implies finite generation, giving precisely the required witnesses on this sieve. No nonempty-chart or nonzero-model choice is made.
