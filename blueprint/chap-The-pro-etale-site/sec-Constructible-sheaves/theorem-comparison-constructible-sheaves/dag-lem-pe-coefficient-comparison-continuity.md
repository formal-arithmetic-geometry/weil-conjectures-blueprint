Let X be a scheme and A any commutative ring in the coefficient universe. For an A-module sheaf G on the coefficient-sized etale site and any affine etale presentation P of [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map], the projection-restriction cocone of the canonical comparison inverse image of G is a colimit in A-modules. The presentation has index in universe u+1 when X is in universe u; coefficients have independent size. This is a statement about all comparison inverse images with no stratification or constructibility hypothesis. The sites and comparison are `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism`.
