1. Prove full faithfulness of the fixed-scalar comparison in ModuleCat of universe max v (u+1). Use the original affine-site API of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. For a cofiltered affine etale presentation, Scheme.preservesColimit_yoneda shows that the Hom functors into etale objects preserve its colimit; the pointwise left Kan extension therefore preserves that colimit. The relative-presentability sheaf criterion applies to the finite generating affine pro-etale precoverage, since filtered colimits of modules are exact. Thus the Kan extension of an etale sheaf is already a sheaf on the affine basis. Dense-subsite sheafification is invertible there. The comparison unit is the fully faithful Kan-extension unit followed by sheafification, hence invertible on affine etale objects and globally. Transport the adjunction through the AsSmall sheaf equivalences of `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism`, using uniqueness of left adjoints. The private helpers additivePreservesRelative, additiveIsSheaf, additiveUnitIso and smallModelModuleFullyFaithful implement the entire argument with independent coefficient size. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism`)
2. For an etale scheme morphism, construct the localization functors on both coefficient-sized models by composition of structure maps. The map/pullback adjunction `lem-weil-pbc-etale-localization-functor-adjoint`, applied also with the weakly-etale property, transports through AsSmall and induces restriction/direct-image adjunctions on module sheaves. Uniqueness of left adjoints identifies pro-etale restriction with the inverse image of `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`. Comparison direct image commutes literally with these restriction functors. Its mate with respect to the restriction adjunctions is the identity fibre-product comparison: the component calculation is pullback.lift_fst, after composing the two presheaf maps. The iterated-mates identity `lem-trf-iterated-square-mates` makes its mate under the comparison adjunctions invertible. The helpers proLocAdj, etLocAdj, comparisonRestrictionIso, comparisonRestrictionMate_isIso and proPullbackRestrictionIso give these specified maps and calculations. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-etale-localization-functor-adjoint`, `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`, `lem-trf-iterated-square-mates`)
3. On each covering open, transport the supplied essential-image witness along proPullbackRestrictionIso. Full faithfulness from Step 1 makes the comparison counit of this restricted sheaf invertible. The mate counit equation identifies its composite with the invertible direct-image restriction comparison with the invertible inverse-image mate followed by restriction of the global counit. Cancel this mate; restriction of the global counit is therefore invertible. This is the proved helper restrictedCounit_isIso.
4. The objects U_i cover the original pro-etale site: for every object T, its inverse-image opens T.hom inverse-image U_i cover T and map to U_i. The source mem_topology_of_openImmersion theorem makes the resulting sieve covering. Objects over U_i in the slice site identify with weakly-etale U_i-schemes by their structure morphisms; the identity underlying scheme gives the required isomorphism of evaluations. Thus `lem-sfb-sheaf-cover-local-isomorphism` applied after restriction along AsSmall.up proves that a morphism invertible on all these opens is invertible globally. Apply this to the comparison counit and take comparison direct image as the etale antecedent. Empty bases and empty covers are included in the same covering-sieve argument. The helper isIso_of_openRestrictions and the final theorem implement this gluing. (uses `lem-sfb-sheaf-cover-local-isomorphism`)
