By clause [theorem-comparison-constructible-sheaves--1], every constructible pro-étale sheaf is classical. The comparison is fully faithful by Theorem [theorem-classical-sheaves-essential-image] and preserves the finite stratifications and finite-presentation local systems defining constructibility. It is therefore an equivalence on these sheaf categories.
