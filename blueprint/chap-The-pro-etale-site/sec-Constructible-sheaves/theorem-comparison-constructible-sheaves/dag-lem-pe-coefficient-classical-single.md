For the standard derived categories of [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-data], the degree-zero embedding is fully faithful, its objects are bounded in the sense of [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-bounded-classical-descent], and its degree-zero cohomology is the original module. A module with invertible continuity maps as in [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map] has continuous cohomology after degree-zero embedding. Moreover a functor compatible with degree-zero embeddings is fully faithful if its derived functor induces bijections on morphisms between bounded objects.
