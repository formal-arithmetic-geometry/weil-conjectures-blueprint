For any scheme X, a Noetherian coefficient ring A of independent size and an intrinsic pro-etale module F on the coefficient-sized site of `def-sfb-affine-locally-constructible-proetale-modules`, suppose one global finite constructible stratification has finite locally constant pullback models on every stratum as in [dag-def-pe-etale-constructible-module]. Then every affine-presentation continuity map of F, with the convention of [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map], is invertible. No quasi-compactness of X is assumed.
