Let D be any functor on the standard derived intrinsic module categories whose factorization through complexes is the canonical comparison of [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-data]. Comparison induces bijections on all morphisms between source objects with bounded cohomology. Every target object with bounded cohomology and invertible continuity maps on each cohomology module, as in [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map], has a derived antecedent. No constructibility, Noetherianity, or qcqs assumption is imposed. Boundedness means vanishing outside an ordered finite integer interval.
