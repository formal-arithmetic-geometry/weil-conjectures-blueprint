For affine X and complementary closed i:Z→X and quasi-compact open j:U→X, let F be a coefficient-sized pro-etale sheaf of A-modules. If its open restriction j^*F is in the etale comparison essential image, the kernel of the canonical closed unit is in the etale comparison essential image over X. This is only the kernel part of localization; it asserts no epimorphy.
