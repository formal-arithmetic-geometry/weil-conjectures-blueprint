1. Put D := AsSmall.down. Transport the covering sieve through D by `CoverPreserving.of_isContinuous` and `Sieve.functorPushforward_ofArrows`. Regard the covered object B and its members A_a as objects of `X.AffineProEt`, using their proAffineEtale structure maps [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. Here we use the scheme-general implementation `Scheme.AffineProEt` of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]; over an affine base its objects are precisely the affine ind-etale objects of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]. Its inclusion into X.ProEt uses `proAffineEtale_le_weaklyEtale`; the flatness part of the underlying ind-etale implication uses `ModuleCat.flat_of_colimitPresentation` `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, since the etale algebra stages are flat. Transfer the cover to this affine site. The finite refinement result `Scheme.AffineProEt.exists_finite_jointly_surjective_of_mem_topology` [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] shows that the original family is jointly surjective: every arrow in the refinement factors through an original member. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
2. Form the finite affine disjoint union using `Scheme.AffineProEt.exists_surjective_factorization`, with the family reindexed by ULift (Fin m). Its map q to B is surjective, and the original members form a finite affine open cover of its source W. This construction uses the ind-etale ring interpretation of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]: the finite product of the member coordinate rings defines W. Apply `Scheme.AffineProEt.singleton_inf_le_relativelyPresentable` to q. The singleton index has exactly one member, so the resulting relative presentation gives compatible affine-etale presentations pW and pB over a small cofiltered category I in universe u, and a presentation morphism whose singleton stage arrows cover. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra])
3. Descend this finite affine open cover by `Scheme.OpenCover.exists_of_isCofiltered_of_finite`. Over the resulting index i, use `RelativeLimitPresentation.restrict` to present each member by pullbacks of its descended open, and compose `RelativeLimitPresentation.restrictHom` with the presentation morphism of q. The cone identities identify the induced limit maps with the original arrows. Convert these to etale-site presentations using `presentationDiagram`, `presentationCone`, and `isLimitPresentationCone`. A point of any base stage lifts through its singleton q-stage cover; its image at i lies in a descended open, and `Scheme.Pullback.exists_preimage_pullback` lifts this compatible pair to a member stage. Thus every resulting stage family covers, and the base and member cones are scheme limits with affine stages. This uses the scheme-general affine-site covering and pullback API recorded in [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], and requires no affineness of X. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis])
4. Use `systemOfPresentation` to construct all ordered intersections, transitions and cone legs, giving an `AffineEtaleCechSystem` in the sense of `def-cbc-affine-etale-cech-system`. Its transitions and cone legs commute with every index map. Apply `presentedNerveIsLimit` to the base and member scheme-limit cones to prove that each ordered intersection cone is a scheme limit; the affine base and member stages make every stage intersection affine. These are exactly the constructions and limit properties of `lem-cbc-cech-nerve-presentation-construction`. (uses `def-cbc-affine-etale-cech-system`, `lem-cbc-cech-nerve-presentation-construction`)
5. Select normalized stage complexes R_i using `SiteCechComplex.exists_of_covering` `def-weil-pbc-site-cech-complex`. Express each chosen intersection, both in R_i and in D(S), as a limiting wide-pullback cone using `WidePullbackCone.IsLimit.mk`; finite-limit preservation gives the mapped universal property. By `IsLimit.conePointUniqueUpToIso` and `Presheaf.nerveWideConeIsLimit`, obtain the projection-compatible isomorphisms `orderedIso` to the ordered intersections of `lem-cbc-cech-nerve-presentation-construction`. Projecting to each factor and using `Over.pi_left_hom_ext` proves that these isomorphisms respect every index map. Conjugate transitions and cone projections by these product isomorphisms. Their identities prove functoriality, naturality and compatibility with all index maps. Stage affineness transfers through `IsAffine.of_isIso`; `Scheme.isAffine_of_isLimit` proves affineness of the original ordered intersection, and its product isomorphism transfers this to the specified S.W. (uses `lem-cbc-cech-nerve-presentation-construction`)
6. Map the normalized stage complexes through AsSmall.up, which preserves their finite-limit universal properties and their coverings by `CoverPreserving.of_isContinuous`. Replace the small index I by J := AsSmall I in universe u+1. The equivalence L := AsSmall.down makes J small and cofiltered. These data give the structure [dag-def-pe-coefficient-affine-cech-presentation]. For each tuple, the product isomorphisms identify its stage diagram with the ordered-intersection diagram of `lem-cbc-cech-nerve-presentation-construction` whiskered by L. Use `isLimitOfReflects` through `Over.forget X` and `Functor.Initial.isLimitWhiskerEquiv` to retain its limit in schemes over X. Then `IsLimit.postcomposeHomEquiv` transports along the stage-diagram isomorphism, and `IsLimit.ofIsoLimit` with `Cone.ext` changes the vertex by its product isomorphism. The resulting cone is exactly the specified cone from S.W with the constructed projections. Repeated factors are covered by the arbitrary index-map argument; when m = 0 there are no tuples Fin (p+1) -> Fin m, so the intersection assertions are vacuous. (uses `lem-cbc-cech-nerve-presentation-construction`)
