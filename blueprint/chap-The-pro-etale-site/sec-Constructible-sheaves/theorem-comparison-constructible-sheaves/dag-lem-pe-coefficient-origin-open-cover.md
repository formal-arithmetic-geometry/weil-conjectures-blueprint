Let X be a scheme in universe u, A an arbitrary commutative ring in universe max v (u+1), and F a sheaf of A-modules on the coefficient-sized pro-etale site. Let U_i be an open cover indexed by a type in universe u. Suppose the fixed-scalar inverse image of F on each U_i lies in the essential image of the etale comparison inverse image there. Then F lies in the essential image of etale comparison over X. The sites and comparison are those of `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism`, and scheme inverse image is `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`, transported to the equivalent AsSmall sites.
