Suppose i^*F and j^*F are in the essential image of the corresponding etale comparison inverse images. Then F is in the essential image over X. Use the comparison of `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism` and inverse images of `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image` on their equivalent coefficient-sized sites.
