Let $X$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated, and let $\Lambda$ be a
discrete commutative Noetherian ring. Let

$$
\nu_X^*:D(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\longrightarrow
D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)
$$

be the functor obtained by applying the exact inverse image termwise to
complexes, so that $H^q(\nu_X^*K)\simeq\nu_X^*H^q(K)$ for every
$q\in\mathbf Z$. Then $\nu_X^*$ restricts to an equivalence
$D^b_c(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\xrightarrow{\sim}\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ of the
full bounded constructible subcategories.
