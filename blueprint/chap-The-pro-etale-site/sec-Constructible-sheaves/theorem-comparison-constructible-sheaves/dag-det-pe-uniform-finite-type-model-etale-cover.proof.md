1. Use the Grothendieck-module sheafification infrastructure used by the Lean module: Grothendieck abelian module categories are locally presentable by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, the transfinite plus construction preserves finite limits by [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], and its maps into sheaves are characterized by the orthogonality statement [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]. Thus the constant module sheaves, sheafHom, and the sheafification units used below exist with the required left-exactness.
2. For sheaves F and G on a site, form the presheaf whose value on U is the type of isomorphisms F.over U to G.over U, with restriction by overMapPullback. The Lean construction localIsom_isSheaf glues the homomorphisms and inverses in the sheafHom sheaves; separatedness and Sheaf.hom_ext prove the two inverse identities and uniqueness. Hence these slice isomorphisms form the sheaf localIsomSheaf F G, and restriction to a slice agrees with the corresponding localIsomSheaf on the localized site.
3. For finitely generated modules P and Q, the private map fixedConstantHom sends a constant P-to-Q linear map to the induced map of constant module sheaves. If two such maps agree as sheaf maps, apply Presheaf.equalizerSieve_mem to each element of a finite generating set from Module.Finite.fg_top, intersect the finitely many covering sieves with GrothendieckTopology.finset_inf_mem, and use LinearMap.ext_on; this is the local injectivity argument. Conversely, for a sheaf map between the constant sheaves, apply the finite-section lifting result `lem-weil-pbc-finite-constant-set-sections-cover` to the images of those generators. On a member not covered by the empty sieve, GrothendieckTopology.toSheafify_const_app_injective makes the constant-sheaf unit for Q injective; Submodule.span_le shows that the image of the whole linear map lies in its range, and LinearEquiv.ofInjective recovers a linear P-to-Q map. The helper fixedConstantHom_ext, using sheaf separatedness, identifies the induced map with the original one. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-finite-constant-set-sections-cover`)
4. Apply the preceding local lifting to both arrows of a slice isomorphism between constant P and Q, intersect the two image sieves, and then intersect the equalizer sieves for the two composite identities. The Lean helpers fixedConstantIsom_injective and fixedConstantIsom_surjective use these refinements, Iso.ext, and the hom and inverse identities to produce a local isomorphism induced by a unique element of P ≅ Q. Sheaf.isLocallyBijective_iff_isIso then identifies the resulting sheaf with the constant sheaf of the set of module isomorphisms; in particular, for the fixed model M, localIsomSheaf of two constant M-sheaves is constant Aut(M). The empty module is covered by the same argument with its empty generating set. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-finite-constant-set-sections-cover`)
5. Write the supplied pro-etale family as W_i to X and use proetalePrecoverage_le_precoverage_weaklyEtale to make each map an object U_i of X.ProEt. The generated sieve of the U_i covers X. The pullback model is transported across the weakly-etale slice localization `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`: the upper-star is the sheafified left Kan extension of [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], the lower-star is restriction along base change from [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], and their comparison is the adjunction `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. The finite-limit exactness [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact] supplies the exact sheaf transport used by the Lean construction. Together with the constant-sheaf restriction identification `lem-weil-pbc-site-constant-restriction`, this gives F.over U_i ≅ the constant M-sheaf on the slice whenever the hypothesis gives upperStar F ≅ constant M. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-weil-pbc-site-constant-restriction`)
6. Let I be the sheaf of slice isomorphisms from F to the constant M-sheaf on X. Restrict I along the continuous composition functor defining the set-valued restriction `def-a1-proetale-set-restriction`. The slice-localization equivalence, the restriction of constant sheaves, and the constant-isomorphism calculation above identify each restriction I|_{U_i} with the constant sheaf on M ≅ M. Thus I is locally constant on the pro-etale covering, and the transported model isomorphisms give a section of I on every U_i. (uses `def-a1-proetale-set-restriction`, `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`, `lem-weil-pbc-site-constant-restriction`)
7. Apply the locally-classical pro-etale-to-etale section theorem [dag-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-locally-classical-etale-cover] to I. Its locally constant conclusion makes I classical and, since it has sections on the pro-etale cover, yields an etale covering family Y_j to X with a section of I on each Y_j. By the definition of I, each section is an isomorphism on the corresponding slice; the Lean proof transports it through the localization equivalence and constantSheafOverIso to an isomorphism F.over T_j ≅ the constant M-sheaf on the localized pro-etale site. The sieve-to-family conversion uses ofArrows_mem_smallEtaleTopology_iff and Cover.mkOfCovers, and Cover.ulift reindexes the family into the required universe. Therefore the resulting etale Cover has the asserted constant model on every member, including the empty-scheme and zero-module cases. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-locally-classical-etale-cover`, `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`, `lem-weil-pbc-site-constant-restriction`)
