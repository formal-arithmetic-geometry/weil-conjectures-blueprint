1. Put w = max v (u+1) and work on the original small etale and pro-etale sites with values in Type w. For an etale sheaf G, the left Kan extension along toProEt preserves the required filtered colimits: on affine ind-etale objects, [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms] give a cofiltered presentation by affine etale stages, and the finite-presentation mapping argument is `Scheme.preservesColimit_yoneda`. The density argument is packaged by `CategoryTheory.CoefficientClassical.lan_preserves_colimit_of_representables`; `Presheaf.isSheaf_of_generates_of_isRelativelyPresentable` and `Functor.IsDenseSubsite.isIso_whiskerLeft_toSheafify` show that the Kan extension is already a sheaf on the affine basis [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] and its coefficient-sized ind-etale refinement [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], while `ProEtale.isColimitMapCoconeOfNatTrans` passes the affine section formula to the limit. The same-universe filtered-colimit framework `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe` keeps these diagrams in Type w. Consequently `proEtaleComparisonUnitSets_isIso` gives an invertible comparison unit, and `proEtaleComparisonSets_continuous` gives preservation of filtered colimits on affine ind-etale presentations. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
2. Transport a trivialization over each given pro-etale chart through `ProEt.weaklyEtale_localization` `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization` and the AsSmall sheaf equivalences. The resulting slice restriction is the continuous sheaf pushforward along base change, as in [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star]. Continuity of etale base change `lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-continuous` and the corresponding pro-etale comparison maps provide the comparison/pullback isomorphisms used in `comparisonCoefficientPullbackIso`, `restrictionInverseImageIso`, and `classicalRestrictionIso`. Constant sheaves are identified by composing the constant-sheaf adjunction with the comparison adjunction and applying `Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq`; this is the `comparisonConstantIso` construction for the constant sheaf from [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf]. Thus every member of the transported cover has a local etale presentation by a constant sheaf. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-continuous`, `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`)
3. Show that these local presentations imply continuity of the original sheaf. On an affine basis object, refine the cover by all affine ind-etale objects mapping to a chart; cocontinuity of the localization and the affine ind-etale basis [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], together with its coefficient-sized refinement [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], gives a covering refinement. For a filtered presentation, form the diagram of product-section sheaves. `finite_sections_lift` and `finite_sections_equal` move the finitely many representatives and compatibility equalities to one stage, and `finitePresieveSheafFilteredColimit` followed by `finiteGeneratedSheafFilteredColimit` proves that its pointwise filtered colimit is a sheaf. `productContinuity_of_cover` makes the comparison from this colimit to the cocone point an isomorphism on the refined cover; `continuity_over_basis_object` then gives the section colimit at the affine terminal object, and `affine_continuity` and `global_continuity` extend this to arbitrary bases by the cofinal over-category. The ind-etale presentations and their coefficient-universe bookkeeping are those of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
4. Apply `proEtale_locallyClassical_continuity` to the transported cover and then `proEtaleComparisonCounitSets_isIso_of_continuous`: the latter tests the counit on the dense affine ind-etale subsite, presents each basis object as a cofiltered limit of affine etale stages, and uses `isIso_app_of_colimits` together with the invertible comparison unit from Step 1. This proves `raw_localClassical`, namely that the ordinary comparison counit is an isomorphism for the AsSmall image of H. Finally `model_counit_of_raw` composes the two AsSmall adjunctions with the comparison adjunction, uses `Adjunction.comp_counit_app`, and identifies the resulting left adjoint with `smallModelProEtaleComparison` by `Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq_hom_app_counit`; the AsSmall site comparison is the continuous inclusion described in [dag-lem-route-small-model-comparison-inclusion-is-continuous]. Therefore the displayed canonical counit for H is an isomorphism. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`)
