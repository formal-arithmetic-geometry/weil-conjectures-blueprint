The hypothesis that the group is classical is essential. The group
$\mathbf Z_\ell^\times$ is not classical. The rank-one $\mathbf Z_\ell$-local system on
$\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$ described above is a $\mathbf Z_\ell^\times$-torsor
with no étale trivialization. For locally constant sheaves with finitely
presented constant models over a discrete ring, the relevant group is the
automorphism group of the model, which is discrete, and the lemma applies.
