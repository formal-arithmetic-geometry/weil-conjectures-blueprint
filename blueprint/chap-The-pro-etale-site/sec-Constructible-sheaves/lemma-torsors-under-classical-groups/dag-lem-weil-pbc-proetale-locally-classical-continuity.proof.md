1. For a weakly etale structure map U → X, the restriction of a pro-etale sheaf is precomposition with the continuous base-change functor, as in the lower-star construction [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] and the direct-image/precomposition formula `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, whose sheaf property follows from continuity of etale base change `lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-continuous`. In Lean, `MorphismProperty.Over.mapPullbackAdj` gives the map/pullback adjunction and `Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq` identifies its sheaf pushforward with `ProEtale.proEtaleComparisonAdjunction`; composing with `ProEtale.comparisonCoefficientPullbackIso` gives `classicalRestrictionIso`. The weakly etale and affine pro-etale hypotheses used here are exactly those packaged by [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms] and the pro-etale-site conventions [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, `lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-continuous`)
2. Fix an affine basis object V over X. Refine the given pro-etale cover by the family indexed by triples (Z,i,g), with Z an affine ind-etale object over V and g : Z → U_i over X; this is the `A := Σ Z, Σ i, E.obj Z ⟶ U i`, `W := Sigma.fst` construction in `affineClassicalCover`. The affine inverse-limit objects and their weakly etale maps are provided by the ind-etale algebra description [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], its same-universe filtered-colimit flatness `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, and the affine pro-etale morphism package [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], inside the affine basis [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. Cocontinuity of `ProEt.isCocontinuous_overMap` and of the affine inclusion, together with the generated affine topology, shows that W covers V. For a triple, base-change identifies the two restriction functors; transporting the given etale presentation through this identification, the etale base-change continuity `lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-continuous`, the precomposition constructions [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] and `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, and `classicalRestrictionIso` give the required classical presentation on W. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, `lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-continuous`, `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`)
3. Now let X be affine and work in its affine ind-etale basis [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. `affineHasTerminal` constructs a terminal object with `IsTerminal.hasTerminal`, and `hasFiniteLimits_of_hasTerminal_and_pullbacks` supplies the finite limits used below; the existence and inverse-limit interpretation of these objects is the ind-etale/affine-pro-etale package [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], with flatness of the filtered presentations supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. For an IsCofiltered diagram D with cone c, form `B := productSections F.obj`, `P := D.op ⋙ B`, and `Q := B.mapCocone c.op`; thus P is the pointwise diagram V ↦ F(C_j × V), and Q is V ↦ F(C × V). The product functor is continuous because `Over.star ⋙ Over.forget` is continuous and is naturally isomorphic to `Limits.prod.functor.obj B`, so `productSections_isSheaf` proves that every P-stage and Q are sheaves. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
4. The pointwise colimit `colimit P` is again a sheaf. The affine topology is generated by `AffineProEt.minimalPrecoverage`, whose generating presieves are finite and have pairwise pullbacks. The private lemmas `finite_sections_lift` and `finite_sections_equal`, using `Types.FilteredColimit.jointly_surjective_of_isColimit₂` and `IsFiltered.wideSpan`, lift finitely many sections and finitely many equality witnesses to one stage. Applying `finitePresieveSheafFilteredColimit` and then `finiteGeneratedSheafFilteredColimit` proves separatedness and amalgamation for every finite presieve; the same argument handles the empty presieve. Hence the canonical map from the colimit sheaf to Q can be formed in `productContinuity_of_cover`. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis])
5. On each member U_i of the refined cover, let V → U_i be any affine object over it. The transported classical presentation gives an isomorphism from the restricted sheaf to an etale comparison pullback. The set-valued classical continuity theorem `proEtaleComparisonSets_continuous` says that this comparison pullback preserves the filtered colimit of the refined diagram [dag-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-classical-continuity-sets]. The isomorphism transports that colimit using `preservesColimitsOfShape_of_natIso`; `continuity_over_basis_object` then uses `refinedDiagram`, `refinedCone`, `refinedConeIsLimit`, `refinedDiagramIso`, and `refinedConeIso` to identify the refined limit cone with the original cone. Therefore the comparison map `a : colimit P → Q` is an isomorphism after evaluation on every object mapping to every U_i. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-classical-continuity-sets])
6. Since the U_i cover the affine basis object, `Sheaf.isIso_of_coversTop` makes the sheaf morphism `a' : ⟨colimit P,hP⟩ → ⟨Q.pt,hQ⟩` an isomorphism globally. The underlying presheaf map is therefore an isomorphism, and evaluation preserves the resulting colimit; at the terminal affine object, the product right unitor (`Limits.prod.rightUnitor`) identifies `F(C × ⊤)` with `F(C)`. This proves `affine_continuity`, i.e. preservation of the required filtered colimit of sections over an affine base, with the canonical restriction maps. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-classical-continuity-sets])
7. For a general scheme X, apply the affine result to every affine basis object V using the refined cover constructed above. To prove the relative statement for a cofiltered presentation D, choose `j₀` with `IsCofiltered.nonempty`, pass to `Over j₀`, and regard `D∘Over.forget j₀` as a diagram of affine ind-etale objects over the affine stage U. The cone and its limit are transported by `Functor.Initial.isLimitWhiskerEquiv`; `continuity_over_basis_object` and the affine continuity result give the colimit there. Finally `Functor.Final.isColimitWhiskerEquiv` for `(Over j₀).op` identifies that colimit with the original one. The ind-etale and affine pro-etale interpretation needed for this passage is [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis])
