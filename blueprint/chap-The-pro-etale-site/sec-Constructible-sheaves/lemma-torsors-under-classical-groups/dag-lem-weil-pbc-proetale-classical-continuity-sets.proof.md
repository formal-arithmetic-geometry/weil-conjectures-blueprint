1. Fix a u-small filtered diagram of affine ind-etale objects over X and its colimit cocone. Pass to the opposite cofiltered diagram and form the corresponding cone c' in the pro-etale site with coneLeftOpOfCocone; isLimitConeLeftOpOfCocone, isLimitOfPreserves, and the map of the limit cone to Over X show that c' is the required affine limit. The algebra presentations and universe/flatness bookkeeping used here are exactly the conventions of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], with the same-universe filtered-colimit flatness supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`; the resulting stages and affine transition maps lie in the basis [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. For every etale X-scheme Y, its structure map is locally of finite presentation, while the affine stages have affine transition maps, compact underlying spaces, and quasi-separated underlying spaces. Thus Scheme.preservesColimit_yoneda, followed by the Yoneda and coyoneda comparison isomorphisms, proves that the lifted representable uliftYoneda.obj ((toProEt X).obj Y) preserves the colimit c'. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
2. Apply lan_preserves_colimit_of_representables [dag-lem-weil-pbc-lan-continuity-from-representables] to the preceding preservation statement. Since the left Kan extension is generated from these lifted representables, the presheaf ((toProEt X).op.lan.obj G.obj) carries c' to a colimit; transporting across the opposite-category equivalence gives the desired filtered-colimit statement for the Kan extension on the affine ind-etale basis. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-lan-continuity-from-representables])
3. The restriction of this Kan extension to the affine etale subsite is identified with G by the Kan-extension unit and full faithfulness. The generators and sheaf criterion for the affine ind-etale topology [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] therefore show, via Presheaf.isSheaf_of_generates_of_isRelativelyPresentable, that the Kan extension is already a sheaf on that basis. Functor.IsDenseSubsite.isIso_whiskerLeft_toSheafify then makes the sheafification map toSheafify (ProEt.topology X) ((toProEt X).op.lan.obj G.obj) invertible at every affine ind-etale stage and, since the limit c'.pt is itself an affine ind-etale object, at the limit as well. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis])
4. Use isColimitOfPreserves to obtain the Lan colimit cocone hL. Apply ProEtale.isColimitMapCoconeOfNatTrans to the sheafification natural transformation: its components at all stages and at c'.pt are isomorphisms by the preceding step, and its source cocone is hL. The mapped cocone is consequently a colimit. By the definition of the sheafPullback construction and its comparison unit, this mapped cocone is exactly the restriction cocone for the inverse image of G, with the actual restriction legs. Hence the restriction of nu_X^*G preserves the arbitrary u-small filtered colimit, including affine ind-etale stages.
