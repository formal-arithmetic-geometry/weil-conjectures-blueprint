1. The canonical comparison from the colimit of evaluations to evaluation at c.pt is natural in the presheaf. Both sides preserve presheaf colimits, since evaluation and restriction are pointwise and colimits commute.
2. The Kan extension of a lifted representable is the lifted representable of its image. The assumed preservation therefore makes the comparison invertible on every representable.
3. Express G as its canonical colimit of lifted representables. Kan extension is a left adjoint. The natural comparison is invertible on this colimit, and its invertibility is precisely preservation of K.
