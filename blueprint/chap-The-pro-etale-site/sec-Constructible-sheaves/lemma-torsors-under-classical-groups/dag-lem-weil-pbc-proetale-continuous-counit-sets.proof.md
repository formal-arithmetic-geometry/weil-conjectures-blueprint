1. Put L = ν_X^*, G = ν_{X,*}F, P = LG, and let a:P→F be the counit. The invertible comparison unit [dag-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-comparison-unit-sets] makes L fully faithful, by Adjunction.full_L_of_isSplitEpi_unit_app and Adjunction.faithful_L_of_mono_unit_app. The triangle identity, in Adjunction.whiskerRight_counit_iso_of_L_fully_faithful, makes ν_{X,*}a invertible. Since this direct image is restriction to the étale site, a is invertible on each étale stage. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-comparison-unit-sets])
2. Test a on the dense affine ind-étale subsite [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], whose restriction functor detects isomorphisms by [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. For arbitrary X, the same detection follows by taking these bases over an affine open cover of X. The inclusion into the weakly étale site uses the ind-étale algebra convention [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]. Its flatness ingredient is `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, ModuleCat.flat_of_colimitPresentation: an ind-étale algebra's same-universe filtered presentation, after forgetting to modules, is a colimit of flat étale-stage modules and is therefore flat. This supplies the flatness part of the ind-étale-to-weakly-étale construction underlying the inclusion. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
3. Present an affine ind-étale object U by a small nonempty cofiltered diagram of affine étale X-schemes, using its defining pro-affine-étale presentation [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. More precisely, AffineProEt.exists_relativeLimitPresentation replaces the affine stages by their canonical spectra and gives a relative limit presentation p through AffineEtale.toAffineProEt X, indexed by J in Type u. Write K:J→X.Etale for its étale diagram and π_i:U→K_i for its projections. The compatibility isomorphism AffineEtale.toAffineProEtCompToProEtIso identifies the resulting cone in the pro-étale site with the image of p.cone.
4. The hypothesis on F sends this presentation to the colimit cocone with vertex F(U) and legs F(K_i)→F(U). Classical continuity [dag-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-classical-continuity-sets], applied to G = ν_{X,*}F, does so for P = ν_X^*G; Lean applies isColimitOfPreserves to p.isLimit.op. Thus P(U) and F(U) are colimits of the respective diagrams of stage sections, with their actual restriction maps. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-classical-continuity-sets])
5. A natural transformation invertible on the stages is invertible at these colimits. Indeed, the stage maps a(K_i) form an isomorphism of diagrams by NatIso.ofComponents. IsColimit.precomposeHomEquiv transports the F-colimit along this isomorphism, and IsColimit.coconePointUniqueUpToIso gives an isomorphism P(U)→F(U). Naturality of a along every π_i identifies its component a(U) with this isomorphism, by IsColimit.hom_ext. Hence every component on the affine basis is invertible. NatTrans.isIso_iff_isIso_app and reflection of isomorphisms under dense affine restriction [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], followed by the fully faithful inclusion of sheaves into presheaves (isIso_of_reflects_iso), show that the counit itself is invertible. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis])
