1. Write A = ModuleCat Λ, and use the pullback g^* of [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star] and constant sheaves of [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf]. The Grothendieck coefficient and sheafification infrastructure for these functors is supplied by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]. The pushforward is restriction along base change [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star]; the adjunction g^* ⊣ g_* is `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, and the pullbacks are exact by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. In particular, for a weakly etale map p, the Lean comparison AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.upperStarIsoLocalizationRestriction identifies p^* with restriction along composition with p: both are left adjoint to p_*. Applying Mathlib's Functor.pushforwardContinuousSheafificationCompatibility to the constant presheaf therefore gives p^*((M)_Y) ≅ (M)_Z. This is the constantPullbackIso used below. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`)
2. Choose the finite modules M_j and isomorphisms e_j : g_j^*F ≅ (M_j)_{W_j} from the given pro-etale covering. Descend the loci where the model has type M_i to opens O_i covering X by `lem-sf-proetale-model-type-opens`. More precisely, for every j and y ∈ W_j, g_j(y) ∈ O_i if and only if there exists an isomorphism M_j ≅ M_i. (uses `lem-sf-proetale-model-type-opens`)
3. Fix i and let j_i : O_i → X be the open immersion. Base change the original covering to O_i: put P_{ij} = W_j ×_X O_i, with projections p : P_{ij} → W_j and q : P_{ij} → O_i. These q form a pro-etale covering, by Mathlib's Precoverage.mem_coverings_of_isPullback applied to (IsPullback.of_hasPullback (g_j) (j_i)).flip. The original maps g_j are weakly etale, as follows from proetalePrecoverage_le_precoverage_weaklyEtale; the projection p is weakly etale as a base change of the open immersion. These are the site and base-change properties of [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site].
4. If P_{ij} is nonempty, choose z ∈ P_{ij}. The pullback identity shows that g_j(p(z)) lies in O_i, so the characterization of O_i supplies a : M_j ≅ M_i. Transport the given trivialization through upperStarComp `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`, the equality p ≫ g_j = q ≫ j_i (Mathlib's pullback.condition), and constant pullback from the first step. Explicitly, q^*(j_i^*F) ≅ (j_i ∘ q)^*F ≅ (g_j ∘ p)^*F ≅ p^*(g_j^*F) ≅ p^*((M_j)_{W_j}) ≅ (M_j)_{P_{ij}} ≅ (M_i)_{P_{ij}}, where the last isomorphism is constantSheaf.mapIso a. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`)
5. On an empty member P_{ij}, every object of its pro-etale site is empty. Its sheaf sections are terminal by AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.ProEt.isTerminalSectionsOfIsEmpty, so any two A-valued sheaves on it are isomorphic: take the unique isomorphisms between their terminal section objects and assemble them by Mathlib's NatIso.ofComponents and ObjectProperty.isoMk; naturality follows from uniqueness of maps into terminal objects. Thus on empty members too q^*(j_i^*F) ≅ (M_i)_{P_{ij}}. Apply uniform finite-model etale descent `lem-sf-proetale-uniform-finite-model-etale-cover` on O_i, with its finite model M_i, to obtain a cover 𝒰_i indexed in the universe of X and isomorphisms (𝒰_i.f a)^*(j_i^*F) ≅ (M_i)_{𝒰_i.X a}. (uses `lem-sf-proetale-uniform-finite-model-etale-cover`)
6. For each x ∈ X choose i(x) with x ∈ O_{i(x)}. Take the family indexed by pairs (x,a), with a ∈ 𝒰_{i(x)}.I₀, consisting of the maps 𝒰_{i(x)}.X a → O_{i(x)} → X. This index type lies in the universe of X. Each composite is etale, and the family is jointly surjective: view x as a point of O_{i(x)} and lift it through its etale cover, using Mathlib's Scheme.ofArrows_mem_precoverage_iff. Mathlib's Cover.mkOfCovers therefore produces an etale covering of X, in the sense of `def-weil-pbc-etale-site`. For a member (x,a), compose upperStarComp `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp` with the isomorphism on 𝒰_{i(x)}.X a from the preceding step. This identifies the pullback of F with the constant sheaf of M_{i(x)}. Retain the given finiteness of M_{i(x)}; these are the required finite constant module models, allowed to vary with the covering member. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`)
