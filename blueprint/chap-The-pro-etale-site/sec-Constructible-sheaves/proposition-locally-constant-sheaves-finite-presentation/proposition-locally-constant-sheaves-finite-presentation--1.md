Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring and let $F$ be a pro-étale
$\Lambda_X$-module sheaf. Suppose there is a pro-étale covering
$\{g_i:W_i\to X\}$ and finite $\Lambda$-modules $M_i$ such that
$g_i^*F\simeq\underline{M_i}$. Then there is an étale locally constant
$\Lambda$-module sheaf $G$ with finitely generated geometric stalks and
an isomorphism $\nu_X^*G\simeq F$. In particular, $F$ is classical.
