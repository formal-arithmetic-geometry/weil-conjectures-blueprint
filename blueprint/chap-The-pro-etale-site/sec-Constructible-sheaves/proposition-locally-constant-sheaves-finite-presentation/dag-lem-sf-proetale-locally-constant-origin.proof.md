1. For the forward implication, let G be finite locally constant. Unpack its locally constant datum in the sense of `def-constructible-locally-constant` and apply the Lean equivalence finite_locally_constant_module_values_iff to obtain an etale locally constant datum d whose constant module values are finite, as recorded by `lem-weil-pbc-finite-locally-constant-module-values`. (uses `def-constructible-locally-constant`, `lem-weil-pbc-finite-locally-constant-module-values`)
2. Apply comparison_finite_models_of_etale_finite_values to (G,d). Using the fixed-coefficient comparison inverse image, viewed through the upper-star construction [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], this gives a scheme pro-etale covering W_i → X and finite Λ-modules M_i whose pullbacks of the comparison object are the corresponding constant sheaves, exactly as in `lem-sf-comparison-finite-models-of-etale-finite-values`. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], `lem-sf-comparison-finite-models-of-etale-finite-values`)
3. Feed those scheme models to AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.finiteModels_over. It converts them to a covering in the small pro-etale site on which the restriction of the comparison object is constant with a finite module value; hence the fixed-coefficient local-constancy conjunct follows from `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-local-constancy`. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-local-constancy`)
4. For every algebraically closed field Ω and geometric point x : Spec Ω → X, apply AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.finite_geometricStalk_of_finite_models to the same finite scheme models. It gives finiteness of the fixed-module geometric stalk, by `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-stalks`. Together with the preceding local-constancy result this proves the forward lisse assertion. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-stalks`)
5. For the converse, let F be lisse and apply AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.finite_models_of_fixed_lisse to its local-constancy and finite-stalk hypotheses. This produces a scheme pro-etale covering with finite constant models, precisely the conclusion of `lem-sf-proetale-lisse-finite-models`. (uses `lem-sf-proetale-lisse-finite-models`)
6. Finally apply AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.etale_origin_of_finite_models to those models. The theorem constructs G (the comparison lower star of F), proves that G is finite locally constant using the locally constant notion `def-constructible-locally-constant`, and supplies Nonempty ((comparisonUpperStar G) ≅ F). Thus G and the required comparison isomorphism are obtained, as asserted by [dag-lem-sf-proetale-origin-from-finite-models]. (uses `def-constructible-locally-constant`, `lem-sf-proetale-origin-from-finite-models`)
