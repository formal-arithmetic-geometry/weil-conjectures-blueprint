1. Apply [dag-lem-sf-proetale-finite-models-etale-cover] to the given pro-etale finite-model cover (using the pro-etale site and constant-model conventions [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], and [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star]). This gives an étale covering 𝒰 of X and, for every i in its index type, a finite Λ-module M_i together with an isomorphism e_i from the pro-etale upper-star restriction of F along 𝒰.f i to the constant pro-etale sheaf with value M_i. (uses `lem-sf-proetale-finite-models-etale-cover`)
2. Define G to be the comparison lower star of F, G := (comparisonLowerStar (ModuleCat Λ) X).obj F. The lower-star construction and its upper/lower-star adjunction are [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] and `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. For a cover member put V_i := 𝒰.etalePrecoverageToEtale i. The localized étale site at V_i is identified with the small étale site of V_i.left by `lem-weil-pbc-etale-site-localization-open`, while étale inverse image is restriction by `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction`; the inverse-image convention is `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`, in the same base-change setup recorded in `def-sc-field-extension`. Using these identifications, the identity-on-sections lower-star restriction isomorphism identifies the localization of G with the comparison lower star of the pro-etale localization of F. Compose the inverse of the pro-etale upper-star/localization isomorphism with e_i, then apply this lower star, the inverse of comparisonConstantIso, and the inverse of the comparison adjunction unit on the constant sheaf. The latter is invertible by `lem-weil-pbc-comparison-grothendieck-unit`, and the upper-star transport uses its exactness [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. Finally transport across the localized-site equivalence (using the constant-sheaf pullback compatibility `lemma-constructible-pullback-locally-constant`). This is exactly the Lean chain eG and yields, for each i, an isomorphism G.over (V_i) ≅ constantSheaf.obj M_i. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`, `lem-weil-pbc-comparison-grothendieck-unit`, `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction`, `lem-weil-pbc-etale-site-localization-open`, `lemma-constructible-pullback-locally-constant`)
3. The covering 𝒰 together with these local constant isomorphisms is a LocallyConstantDatum in the sense of `def-constructible-locally-constant`, with finite values M_i. Applying finite_locally_constant_module_values_iff, namely `lem-weil-pbc-finite-locally-constant-module-values`, to this datum gives IsFiniteLocallyConstantSheaf G; this is the finite-locally-constant conclusion of `def-constructible-finite-locally-constant`. (uses `def-constructible-locally-constant`, `lem-weil-pbc-finite-locally-constant-module-values`)
4. For each i, form the isomorphism eF_i from the pro-etale localization of F to the comparison upper star of the constant étale sheaf with value M_i by composing the inverse upper-star/localization restriction isomorphism, e_i, and the inverse comparisonConstantIso. Since eF_i is an isomorphism, CategoryTheory.Adjunction.isIso_counit_app_of_iso shows that the counit of the comparison adjunction on V_i.left, evaluated at the localized F, is invertible. This uses the upper/lower-star adjunction framework `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` together with the inverse-image convention `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`)
5. Apply AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.isIso_of_isIso_localizationRestriction to the global comparison counit, using the étale cover 𝒰 and the inequality of its étale precoverage with the pro-etale precoverage. For each i, `lem-sf-proetale-comparison-counit-localization` transfers the invertibility of the local counit from the preceding step to the restriction of the global counit. Hence the global counit on F is an isomorphism. Its asIso is an isomorphism from (comparisonUpperStar (ModuleCat Λ) X).obj G to F, so G together with the finite-locally-constant property above proves the claim. (uses `lem-sf-proetale-comparison-counit-localization`)
