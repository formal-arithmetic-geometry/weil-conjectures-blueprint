Let X be a scheme and Λ a finite commutative ring. Suppose a pro-etale sheaf F of Λ-modules has a scheme pro-etale covering with finite constant module models M_i [def-weil-pbc-proetale-site, def-weil-proetale-inverse-image, def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf]. Then it has an etale covering indexed in the universe of X with finite constant module models, allowed to vary with the covering member.
