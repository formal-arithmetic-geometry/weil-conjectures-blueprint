In clause [proposition-locally-constant-sheaves-finite-presentation--1], the sheaf of trivializations is a torsor under the classical group $\operatorname{Aut}_\Lambda(M)$. Lemma [lemma-torsors-under-classical-groups--2] gives an étale covering on which it has sections. Such a section is exactly a trivialization of the module sheaf.
