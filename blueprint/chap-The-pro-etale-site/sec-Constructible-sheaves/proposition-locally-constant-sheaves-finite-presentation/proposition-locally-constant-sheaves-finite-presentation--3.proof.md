The comparison inverse image is fully faithful by Theorem [theorem-classical-sheaves-essential-image]. It sends finite-presentation étale local systems to pro-étale local systems by pulling back a trivializing covering. Conversely, clause [proposition-locally-constant-sheaves-finite-presentation--2] supplies an étale trivializing covering for every such pro-étale local system, and clause [proposition-locally-constant-sheaves-finite-presentation--1] descends it. This gives essential surjectivity on the stated subcategories.
