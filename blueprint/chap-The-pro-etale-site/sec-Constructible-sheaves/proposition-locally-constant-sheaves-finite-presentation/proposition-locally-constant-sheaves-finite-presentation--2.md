Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring and let $F$ be a pro-étale
$\Lambda_X$-module sheaf. Suppose a pro-étale covering
$\{g_i:W_i\to X\}$ trivializes $F$ with finite constant models:
$g_i^*F\simeq\underline{M_i}$. Then there is an étale covering
$\{h_j:Y_j\to X\}$ and finite $\Lambda$-modules $N_j$ such that
$h_j^*F\simeq\underline{N_j}$.
