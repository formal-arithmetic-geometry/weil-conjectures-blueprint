Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring. If $G$ is an étale locally
constant $\Lambda$-module sheaf with finitely generated geometric
stalks, then $\nu_X^*G$ is locally constant as a pro-étale
$\Lambda_X$-module sheaf and its geometric stalks are finite sets.
Conversely, every locally constant pro-étale $\Lambda_X$-module sheaf
$F$ whose geometric stalks are finite sets admits such a $G$ and an
isomorphism $\nu_X^*G\simeq F$.
