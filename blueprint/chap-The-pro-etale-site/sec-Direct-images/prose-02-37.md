Since every bounded-below classical complex is of the form $\nu_X^*K$
(Corollary [corollary-bounded-below-classical-complexes]), the theorem
shows that, for $g$ quasi-compact and quasi-separated, $Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}$
carries bounded-below classical complexes to classical complexes.

In later arguments we transport maps built from units, counits and composition
from the étale to the pro-étale site, and for this we record how the
comparison map behaves under these operations. Write
$\beta_g:g_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*\nu_Y^*\simeq\nu_X^*g_{\acute{e}t}^*$ for the isomorphism
above and $c_g$ for the map (2.9).
