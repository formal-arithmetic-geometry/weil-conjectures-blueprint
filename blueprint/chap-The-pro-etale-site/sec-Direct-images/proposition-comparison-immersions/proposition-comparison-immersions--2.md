For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, the isomorphism
(2.10), applied termwise to complexes, induces a
canonical isomorphism

$$
\nu_X^*j_{\acute{e}t,!}\simeq j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,!}\nu_U^*
\quad\text{of functors }D(U_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)
$$

on the unbounded derived categories. Here the four functors are exact on
sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules, and on complexes they are applied termwise.
