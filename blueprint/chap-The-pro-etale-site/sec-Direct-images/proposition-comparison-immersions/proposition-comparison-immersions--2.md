Let $A$ be a Grothendieck abelian category and $j:U\hookrightarrow X$
an open immersion. There is a canonical isomorphism

$$
\nu_X^*j_{\acute{e}t,!}\simeq j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,!}\nu_U^*
\quad\text{of functors }D(U_{\acute{e}t},A)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A).
$$

Here $D(T,A)$ is the unbounded derived category of $A$-valued sheaves
on $T$, and the functors displayed are induced by the corresponding
exact functors on sheaves.
