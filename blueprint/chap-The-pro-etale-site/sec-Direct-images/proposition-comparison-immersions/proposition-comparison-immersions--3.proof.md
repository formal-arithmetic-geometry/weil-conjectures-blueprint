For the closed immersion, the first identity is
Theorem [theorem-comparison-direct-images], and the second is the
comparison of inverse images. Since $\nu_X^*$ is exact, it carries the
localization triangle to a distinguished triangle. Its first map is
carried to the counit on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ because the isomorphism of (1)
identifies the adjunction maps, and its second map to the unit by
Lemma [lemma-comparison-direct-images-compatibilities](1), applied to
$g=i$ and $L=K$.
