Let $A$ be a Grothendieck abelian category. For an open immersion
$j:U\hookrightarrow X$, there is a canonical isomorphism

$$
\nu_X^*j_{\acute{e}t,!}\simeq j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,!}\nu_U^*
\quad\text{of functors }\Sh(U_{\acute{e}t},A)\to\Sh(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A).
$$

For $A=\operatorname{Mod}(\Lambda)$, this is the isomorphism

$$
\nu_X^*j_{\acute{e}t,!}\simeq j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,!}\nu_U^*\quad\text{of functors }\operatorname{Mod}(U_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to\operatorname{Mod}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda).
\tag{2.10}
$$
