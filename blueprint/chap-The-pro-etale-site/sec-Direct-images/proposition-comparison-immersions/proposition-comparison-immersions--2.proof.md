The comparison pullbacks and extension-by-zero functors are exact.
Apply the isomorphism of [proposition-comparison-immersions--1]
termwise to complexes and pass to the derived categories. Identify
the functor induced by each composite of exact functors with the
composite of their induced functors. These two composition
isomorphisms and the induced comparison give the displayed isomorphism.
