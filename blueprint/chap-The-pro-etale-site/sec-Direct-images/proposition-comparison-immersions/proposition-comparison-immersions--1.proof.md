Restriction along $j$ commutes with restriction to the étale subsite:

$$
j_{\acute{e}t}^*\nu_{X,*}\simeq\nu_{U,*}j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*.
$$

Both sides are the same restriction functor. Compose the adjunctions
$j_{\acute{e}t,!}\dashv j_{\acute{e}t}^*$ and
$\nu_X^*\dashv\nu_{X,*}$ on one side, and
$\nu_U^*\dashv\nu_{U,*}$ and
$j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,!}\dashv j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*$ on the other.
The conjugate of the isomorphism of right adjoints gives the required
isomorphism of left adjoints, with the displayed orientation.
