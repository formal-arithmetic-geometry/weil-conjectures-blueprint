The inverse-image comparisons satisfy $\beta_{h\circ g}=\beta_g\circ g_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*(\beta_h)$ with the stated composition identifications. Transpose the proposed composite successively through the two adjunctions. It becomes this same composite of the $\beta$ comparisons and counits, hence the defining transpose of $c_{h\circ g}$. Uniqueness in the adjunction bijection proves equality.
