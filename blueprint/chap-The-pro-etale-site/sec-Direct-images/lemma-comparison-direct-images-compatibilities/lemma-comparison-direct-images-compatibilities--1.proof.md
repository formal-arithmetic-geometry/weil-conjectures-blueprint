Transpose the displayed composite by the pro-étale adjunction. By the defining transpose of $c_g$ and naturality of $\beta_g$, it becomes $\beta_g$ followed by $\nu_X^*$ of $g_{\acute{e}t}^*L\to g_{\acute{e}t}^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}g_{\acute{e}t}^*L\to g_{\acute{e}t}^*L$. The latter composite is the identity by the triangle identity. The transpose is therefore $\beta_g$, which is also the transpose of the asserted unit. The adjunction bijection gives equality.
