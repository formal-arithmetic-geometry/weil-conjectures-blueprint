Let $g:X\to Y$ be a morphism of schemes.

1. For $L\in D^+(Y_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, the composite
   $\nu_Y^*L\to\nu_Y^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}g_{\acute{e}t}^*L\xrightarrow{c_g}
  Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}\nu_X^*g_{\acute{e}t}^*L$ of $\nu_Y^*$ of the unit with $c_g$
   equals the unit $\nu_Y^*L\to Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}g_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*\nu_Y^*L$
   followed by $Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}(\beta_g)$.
2. For $K\in D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, the composite
   $g_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*\nu_Y^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}K\xrightarrow{g^*c_g}
  g_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}\nu_X^*K\to\nu_X^*K$ with the counit equals
   $\nu_X^*$ of the counit $g_{\acute{e}t}^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}K\to K$ precomposed with
   $\beta_g$.
3. For a second morphism $h:Y\to Z$, under the identifications
   $R(h\circ g)_*\simeq Rh_*Rg_*$ on both sites, $c_{h\circ g}$ is the
   composite
   $$
   \nu_Z^*Rh_{\acute{e}t,*}Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}K\xrightarrow{c_h}
   Rh_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}\nu_Y^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}K\xrightarrow{Rh_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}(c_g)}
   Rh_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}\nu_X^*K .
   $$
