By its definition, $c_g$ is the adjoint transpose of the inverse-image comparison followed by the image of the étale counit. Taking its transpose is exactly applying $g_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}^*$ and then the pro-étale counit. This is the displayed composite, so the defining adjunction equality proves the assertion.
