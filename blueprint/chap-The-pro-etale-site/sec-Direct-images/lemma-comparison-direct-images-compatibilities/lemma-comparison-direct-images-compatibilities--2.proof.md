On sheaves, restriction to the étale site commutes with direct image
along $g$. The canonical direct-image comparison is its mate under the
comparison adjunctions, and the pullback comparison is obtained from
the same commutation. The iterated-mate identity and the counit identity
for mates give the underived counit equation. Taking adjoint transposes
identifies the underived direct-image comparison with the mate of the
pullback comparison.

Pass to bounded-below derived categories. The factorization equations
for the termwise pullback comparison show that the derived mate and the
right derived underived comparison have the same factorization on
bounded-below homotopy categories. Uniqueness of right derived
transformations identifies them. Apply the counit identity for this
derived mate and cancel $\beta_g^{-1}\beta_g$. This gives the stated
equation without requiring $c_g$ to be invertible.
