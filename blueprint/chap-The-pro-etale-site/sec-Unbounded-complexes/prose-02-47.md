Since by Theorem [theorem-classical-sheaves-essential-image] the
classical sheaves of $A$-modules are the essential image of $\nu_X^*$ on
sheaves, and $\nu_X^*$ commutes with direct sums of sheaves, classical
sheaves of $A$-modules are closed under arbitrary direct sums in
$\operatorname{Mod}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$.
