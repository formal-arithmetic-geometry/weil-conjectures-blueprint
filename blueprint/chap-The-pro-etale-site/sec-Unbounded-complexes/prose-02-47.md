Since by Theorem [theorem-classical-sheaves-essential-image] the
[classical](@definition-classical-pro-etale-sheaves) sheaves of $A$-modules are the essential image of [$\nu_X^*$](@proposition-comparison-morphism-inverse-image) on
sheaves, and [$\nu_X^*$](@proposition-comparison-morphism-inverse-image) commutes with direct sums of sheaves, [classical](@definition-classical-pro-etale-sheaves)
sheaves of $A$-modules are closed under arbitrary direct sums in
$\operatorname{Mod}(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},A)$.
