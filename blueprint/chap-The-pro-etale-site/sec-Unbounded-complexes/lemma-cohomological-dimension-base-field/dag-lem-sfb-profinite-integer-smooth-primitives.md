Let G be a compact Hausdorff totally disconnected topological group with an element tau whose integral powers are dense. Suppose for each positive integer n a continuous homomorphism G→Multiplicative(ZMod n) sends tau to 1 (in additive notation). Let ell>1 be an integer and A an abelian group. For every function f:G→A with open right-translation stabilizer and ell*f(x)=0 for every x, there is a function h:G→A with open right-translation stabilizer and h(x*tau)-h(x)=f(x) for every x. Smooth means having open right stabilizer as in the discrete-module coinduction construction `def-exc-continuous-cohomology`.
