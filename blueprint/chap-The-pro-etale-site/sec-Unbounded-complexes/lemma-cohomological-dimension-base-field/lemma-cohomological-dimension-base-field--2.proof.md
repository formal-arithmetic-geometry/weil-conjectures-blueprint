For finite $k'$, the absolute Galois group is $\widehat{\mathbf Z}$. Its $\ell$-cohomological dimension is one, so Galois cohomology of $\ell$-primary torsion modules vanishes above degree one. The identification with étale cohomology gives the bound; this is the Galois-cohomology computation used in the source proof.
