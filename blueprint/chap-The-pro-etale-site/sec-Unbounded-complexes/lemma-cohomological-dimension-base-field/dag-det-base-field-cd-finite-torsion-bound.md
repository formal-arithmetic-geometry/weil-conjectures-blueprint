Let $K$ be a finite field. Every torsion abelian sheaf on the small étale site of $\operatorname{Spec}K$ has vanishing ordinary derived global-section cohomology in every degree greater than one. Equivalently, the torsion étale cohomological dimension of $\operatorname{Spec}K$ is at most one.
