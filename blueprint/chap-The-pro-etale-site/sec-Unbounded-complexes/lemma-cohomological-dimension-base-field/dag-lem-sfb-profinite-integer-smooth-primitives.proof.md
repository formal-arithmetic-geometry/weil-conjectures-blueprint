1. For the open right stabilizer of f choose an open normal subgroup U contained in it, using ProfiniteGrp.exist_openNormalSubgroup_sub_open_nhds_of_one. Let Q=G/U and let π:G→Q be the quotient homomorphism, continuous by QuotientGroup.continuous_mk. This quotient is finite since G is compact and U is open. Put n=orderOf(π(tau)); then n>0 by orderOf_pos. Put N=n*ell. Since ell>1, we have N>1, so the assumed residue homomorphism q:G→Multiplicative(ZMod N) is available and sends tau to 1 in additive notation.
2. Every element of Multiplicative(ZMod N) is an integral power of ofAdd(1): its least nonnegative representative supplies such an exponent. The order of this generator is N by orderOf_ofAdd_eq_addOrderOf and ZMod.addOrderOf_one, and n divides N. Thus monoidHomOfForallMemZpowers constructs a homomorphism a:Multiplicative(ZMod N)→Q sending ofAdd(1) to π(tau), as asserted by monoidHomOfForallMemZpowers_apply_gen. Its domain is discrete, so a is continuous by continuous_of_discreteTopology. The continuous homomorphisms a∘q and π agree on every integral power of tau by map_zpow and the assumed value of q(tau). Hence DenseRange.equalizer, applied as hd.equalizer, gives a∘q=π, since these powers are dense and Q is Hausdorff. If q(x)=q(y), then π(x)=π(y), so x⁻¹*y lies in U by QuotientGroup.eq. As U fixes f under right translation, f(y)=f(x*(x⁻¹*y))=f(x). Thus f is constant on fibers of q.
3. Let idx(x) be the least nonnegative representative of q(x), and write F(i)=f(tau^i) for natural i. The equality q(tau^idx(x))=q(x) and the preceding fiber calculation give f(x)=F(idx(x)). Also π(tau^(n*m+i))=π(tau^i) for all natural m,i, by map_pow, pow_add, pow_mul and pow_orderOf_eq_one. Their quotient difference therefore lies in U by QuotientGroup.eq, so F(n*m+i)=F(i). Split the sum over N terms into ell blocks of n terms. More explicitly, induction on m using Finset.sum_range_add and succ_nsmul proves that the sum of F over n*m terms is m times its sum over n terms. Taking m=ell and using Finset.smul_sum, the sum over N terms is the sum over i<n of ell • F(i), which is zero by the hypothesis that ell kills every value of f (Finset.sum_eq_zero). This also works when n=1.
4. Set h(x)=sum_{i<idx(x)} F(i). The kernel V of q is open: it is the inverse image under the continuous q of the open singleton {1} in its discrete target, using isOpen_discrete. If k lies in V, then q(x*k)=q(x), hence idx(x*k)=idx(x) and h(x*k)=h(x). Thus V is contained in the right stabilizer of h, which is open by Subgroup.isOpen_mono. Therefore h is smooth.
5. The residue of x*tau increments idx(x) modulo N: ZMod.val_add and ZMod.val_one give idx(x*tau)=(idx(x)+1) mod N, while ZMod.val_lt gives idx(x)<N. If idx(x)+1<N, Nat.mod_eq_of_lt and Finset.sum_range_succ give h(x*tau)-h(x)=F(idx(x))=f(x). Otherwise idx(x)+1=N, so idx(x*tau)=0. The vanishing full sum, again expanded by Finset.sum_range_succ, is h(x)+F(idx(x))=0. Hence h(x*tau)-h(x)=-h(x)=F(idx(x))=f(x), with the last rearrangement given by eq_neg_of_add_eq_zero_left. This proves the required identity for every x.
