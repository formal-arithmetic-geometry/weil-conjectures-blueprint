By [Stacks, [03QQ](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QQ)], étale cohomology of $\operatorname{Spec}k'$ is Galois cohomology. For algebraically closed $k'$ the absolute Galois group is trivial, so the invariants functor is exact and all positive-degree cohomology vanishes.
