1. Fix a prime $\ell$ and a field $K$ as in the statement. We use the definition of $\operatorname{cd}_\ell$ in [dag-sk-def-cd] and the local torsion convention in `def-sc-etale-torsion-cd`. The étale site and direct-image conventions underlying the comparison are those of `def-weil-pbc-direct-image` and its derived version `def-weil-derived-direct-image`; the ambient morphism terminology is fixed by `def-nagata-compactifiable` and `def-nagata-compactification`. (uses [definition-compactification-refinement--1], `def-sc-etale-torsion-cd`, `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, `def-weil-derived-direct-image`, `def-nagata-compactifiable`)
2. The formal sheaf and derived categories used below have the required size and resolution infrastructure: local presentability is supplied by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, derived Hom sets by `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`, and K-injective resolutions by `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`. The finite-limit and sheafification control comes from [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits] and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], while the closed tensor structure and left exact direct-image formalism are `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed` and `lem-weil-pbc-direct-image-left-exact`. The finite derived-functor and module push-pull conventions are `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors` and `sk-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull`, and the continuity-map convention is [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map]. These are the formal hypotheses in which `det-cd-etale-comparison` is applied to the affine spectrum and its identity morphism. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`, `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`, `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`, `lem-weil-pbc-direct-image-left-exact`, `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`, `sk-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull`, `det-cd-etale-comparison`)
3. Assume $K$ is algebraically closed. Lean obtains `IsSepClosed K` by `inferInstance`, so `lem-sf-separably-closed-torsion-cd` gives `HasTorsionEtaleCohomologicalDimensionLE (Spec (.of K)) 0`. Since $\operatorname{Spec}K$ is affine (hence qcqs), instantiate `det-cd-etale-comparison` with $S=X=Y=\operatorname{Spec}K$, $g=\mathord{\mathrm{id}}$, and $c=0$. Its fourth clause yields `hcmp : cohomologicalDimension \ell h\ell (Spec (.of K)) \le 0`. The reverse inequality is `bot_le`; therefore `le_antisymm hcmp bot_le` proves $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(K)=0$. (uses `lem-sf-separably-closed-torsion-cd`, `det-cd-etale-comparison`)
4. Assume instead that $K$ is finite. The theorem `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.hasTorsionEtaleCohomologicalDimensionLE_one_of_finite_baseField`, recorded as [dag-det-base-field-cd-finite-torsion-bound], supplies `HasTorsionEtaleCohomologicalDimensionLE (Spec (.of K)) 1` for every torsion étale sheaf. Apply the fourth clause of `det-cd-etale-comparison` with the same affine scheme, the identity morphism `let g : Spec (.of K) ⟶ Spec (.of K) := 𝟙 _`, and $c=1$. The resulting conclusion is `cohomologicalDimension \ell h\ell (Spec (.of K)) \le 1`, which is the finite-field assertion. (uses [dag-det-base-field-cd-finite-torsion-bound], `det-cd-etale-comparison`)
