1. By `det-base-field-cd-galois-group`, obtain the Frobenius element τ and a topological group isomorphism e from G = Gal(Ks/K) to the profinite completion of Multiplicative ℤ, with e(τ) = etaFn(1). Since etaFn has dense range (ProfiniteGrp.ProfiniteCompletion.denseRange) and e is a homeomorphism, the integral powers of τ have dense range in G; the Lean proof establishes this by composing the dense range with e.symm and identifying e.symm(etaFn(z)) with τ^z.toAdd. (uses `det-base-field-cd-galois-group`)
2. For every n > 0, use ProfiniteGrp.ProfiniteCompletion.lift with the multiplicative form of Int.castAddHom (ZMod n) to define a continuous homomorphism residue n : G → Multiplicative (ZMod n). The lift_eta identity and e(τ) = etaFn(1) show residue n τ = Multiplicative.ofAdd 1. Thus the hypotheses of the smooth-primitive construction hold for all positive cyclic residues, by `det-base-field-cd-galois-group`. (uses `det-base-field-cd-galois-group`)
3. Let X = trivialZMod G ℓ. For z in the cokernel coker₁ X, represent z by a smooth function f. Its values are killed by ℓ: after unfolding ULift, this is the pointwise calculation ℓ • (f.1 x).down = 0, using nsmul_eq_mul and the ZMod relation. Since ℓ is prime, (Fact.out : ℓ.Prime).one_lt; applying exists_smooth_primitive_of_cyclic_residues with τ, the dense-power result, and the residue maps gives a smooth h with h(x * τ) - h x = f x. The quotient class y = QuotientAddGroup.mk h then satisfies ((coker₁ X).obj.ρ τ).hom y - y = z, by coker₁_ρ_mk and the pointwise primitive equation. Hence translation by τ minus identity is surjective on coker₁ X, using [dag-lem-sfb-profinite-integer-smooth-primitives]. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-profinite-integer-smooth-primitives])
4. For any discrete G-module Y on which τ - 1 is surjective, prove Subsingleton (H G Y 1). Rewrite this as Ext¹ from intTrivial G into the first term of shortExact₁ Y with subsingleton_ext_one_iff, using shortExact₁ Y, subsingleton_H_coind₁, and forall_exists_comp_eq_iff. The coinduced Ext vanishing subsingleton_H_coind₁ is obtained from the exact forget/coind₁ adjunction via CategoryTheory.Adjunction.extAddEquiv, supplied by `lem-sc-ext-exact-adjunction`. Given an invariant class in the cokernel, lift it to f with QuotientAddGroup.mk_surjective. The equality at τ says that τ*f - f is the image of some x in Y; choose y with τ*y - y = x and replace f by f - toCoind₁(y). The new lift is fixed by τ. Its stabilizer is closed because it is open in the discrete module, and it contains every integral power of τ; density therefore makes it fixed by every g in G via fixed_of_dense_generator. Its quotient class is unchanged because the subtracted term maps to zero. Thus every invariant class lifts to an invariant element and H¹ vanishes. (uses `lem-sc-ext-exact-adjunction`)
5. The preceding degree-one argument applied to Y = coker₁ X, together with the surjectivity established in the primitive step, gives Subsingleton (H G (coker₁ X) 1). The short exact sequence shortExact₁ X and the acyclicity Subsingleton (H G (coind₁ G X.obj.V) 1) then give Subsingleton (H G X 2) by subsingleton_ext_succ_of_shortExact; the coinduced acyclicity again uses CategoryTheory.Adjunction.extAddEquiv from `lem-sc-ext-exact-adjunction`. (uses `lem-sc-ext-exact-adjunction`)
6. Finally instantiate the dimension-shifting result with X = trivialZMod G ℓ and the constructed cokernel surjectivity. This is exactly Subsingleton (H Gal(Ks/K) (trivialZMod Gal(Ks/K) ℓ) 2), under the given finite-field, separable-closure, prime, and nonzero-coefficient hypotheses.
