For a qcqs scheme $S$ and a qcqs morphism $g:X\to Y$, the
*$\ell$-cohomological dimensions* are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{cd}_\ell(S)&=\sup\{q: H^q(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},F)\neq0
\text{ for some classical $\ell$-power torsion sheaf $F$}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
