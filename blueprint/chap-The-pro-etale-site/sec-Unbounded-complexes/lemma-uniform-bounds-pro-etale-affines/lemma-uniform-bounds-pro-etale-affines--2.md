There is an integer $c$, depending only on $X$, such that for every classical sheaf $F$ of $A$-modules on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ and every pro-étale affine $V$ over $Y$, one has $H^q(X\times_YV,F)=0$ for $q>c$.
