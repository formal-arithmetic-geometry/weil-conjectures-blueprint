Choose a finite affine cover of $X$ with $r$ members and write $V=\varprojlim_iV_i$, with $V_i$ affine étale over $Y$. The intersections of the chosen affine opens and their pullbacks to $V_i$ are affine, because $X$ and $Y$ are separated over $k$. Those pullbacks are étale over the intersections and have dimension at most $\dim X$. Their cohomology vanishes above $d$ by [Stacks, [0F0V](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0V)]. The alternating Čech complex has length $r-1$, so its spectral sequence gives the uniform bound $c=d+r-1$. Passage to the affine inverse limit, using [Stacks, [03Q6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03Q6)] and the comparison theorem, gives the claim for $V$.
