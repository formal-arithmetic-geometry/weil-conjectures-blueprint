Lemma [lemma-cohomological-dimension-base-field] gives $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(k)<\infty$. Since $g$ is of finite type over $k$, the relative cohomological-dimension theorem [Stacks, [0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10)], used in the source proof, applies and gives $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(g)<\infty$.
