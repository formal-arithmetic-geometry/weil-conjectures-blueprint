Write $U=\varprojlim_iU_i$, with $U_i$ affine étale over $X$, and $F=\nu_X^*F_{\acute{e}t}$. The bounded-below cohomology comparison and [Stacks, [03Q6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03Q6)] identify $H^q(U,F)$ with the filtered colimit of $H^q(U_i,F_{\acute{e}t})$. Each $U_i$ has dimension at most $\dim X$, and [Stacks, [0F0V](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0V)] bounds its $\ell$-cohomological dimension by $d$. Every term, hence the colimit, vanishes for $q>d$.
