1. Work in the category of sheaves of modules supplied by `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`, with the sectionwise tensor and the monoidal presheaf tensor equipped with the canonical symmetric-monoidal structure of `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`. Fix the coefficient sheaf R and the right factor M.
2. For each presheaf module N and each site object U, define the component of siteTensorRightIso R M.val at U to be Iso.refl on the module tensor N(U) ⊗ M(U). PresheafOfModules.isoMk packages these section maps; their compatibility with restrictions and their naturality in N are checked by ModuleCat.MonoidalCategory.tensor_ext, since both sides agree on every pure tensor x ⊗ y (the remaining equality is rfl). Thus the explicit sectionwise tensor functor is naturally isomorphic to tensorRight M.val.
3. Let a be the presheaf module sheafification functor and i the forgetful functor from sheaves to presheaves. The adjunction PresheafOfModules.sheafificationAdjunction (𝟙 _) has counit an isomorphism on the sheaf M; using asIso and NatIso.ofComponents gives e : i ⋙ a ≅ 𝟭 _. The composite of (curriedTensor _).flip.mapIso (e.app M).symm, isoWhiskerRight e.symm (tensorRight (a.obj M.val)), and isoWhiskerLeft i (Functor.Monoidal.commTensorRight a M.val) is the declared tensorRightPresentation R M. Hence tensorRight M is canonically identified with forget followed by tensorRight M.val and then sheafification.
4. Sheafify the natural comparison from the second step by whiskering siteTensorRightIso R M.val on the right with a and on the left with i. Compose this with (tensorRightPresentation R M).symm using isoWhiskerLeft and isoWhiskerRight. The resulting siteSheafTensorRightIso R M is an isomorphism from the sheafified sectionwise tensor functor siteSheafTensorRight (A := R) M to tensorRight M, with the stated canonical pure-tensor presentation and naturality.
