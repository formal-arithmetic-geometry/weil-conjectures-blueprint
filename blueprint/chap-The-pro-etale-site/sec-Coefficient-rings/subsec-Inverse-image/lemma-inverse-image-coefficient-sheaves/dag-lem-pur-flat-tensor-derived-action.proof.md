1. Write Q_R and Q_S for cochain localization, T_L and T_M for termwise tensor with L and M, and C_F for termwise application of F. The tensor functors preserve zero morphisms by `lem-route-site-sheaf-tensor-right-preserves-zero-morphisms`, so their functors on complexes and the termwise comparison gamma^c = NatIso.mapHomologicalComplex gamma (.up ℤ) are defined. Use the published derived tensor and representativeIso of `def-sf-ringed-kflat-tensor-representative-data`; D_L and D_M are its right fixed-factor functors at Q_R(L$[0]$) and Q_S(M$[0]$), as in [dag-def-pur-signed-line-derived-action-data] and `lem-weil-pbc-kflat-fixed-tensor-triangulated`. Obtain the two flat-factor resolving comparisons from [dag-lem-pur-flat-line-resolving-normalization], applied to the assumed flat sheaves `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`. This gives K-flatness of L$[0]$ and M$[0]$ `def-pc-kflat-complex`, flatness of all their terms, and isomorphisms cR:Q_R⋙D_L≅T_L⋙Q_R and cS:Q_S⋙D_M≅T_M⋙Q_S. Their normalizations say that, on each K-flat complex with flat terms, cR or cS followed by localization of the inverse collapse equals the inverse of the corresponding representativeIso. (uses [dag-lem-pur-flat-line-resolving-normalization], `lem-route-site-sheaf-tensor-right-preserves-zero-morphisms`, `def-pc-kflat-complex`)
2. Let F_D = F.mapDerivedCategory and phi = F.mapDerivedCategoryFactors:Q_R⋙F_D≅C_F⋙Q_S, the exact derived-functor factorization of `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization`. Compose the two resolving comparisons with phi and the termwise image of gamma to obtain an isomorphism psi:Q_R⋙D_L⋙F_D≅Q_R⋙F_D⋙D_M. Explicitly, its component at E is the composite, in order, F_D(cR_E), phi_(T_L E), Q_S(gamma^c_E), the inverse of cS_(C_F E), and the inverse of D_M(phi_E). These are natural isomorphisms; the Lean construction uses Functor.isoWhiskerRight and Functor.isoWhiskerLeft to form this composite. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization`)
3. Lift psi by Localization.liftNatIso along DerivedCategory.Q, with the quasi-isomorphisms HomologicalComplex.quasiIso, to obtain eta:D_L⋙F_D≅F_D⋙D_M. Localization.liftNatTrans_app, with Localization.Lifting.iso simplified for these precomposed functors, gives eta_(Q_R E)=psi_E for every complex E. Substitute the preceding composite and cancel the inverse pairs using Functor.map_comp_assoc and Iso.inv_hom_id_app. Thus eta_(Q_R E) ≫ D_M(phi_E) ≫ cS_(C_F E) = F_D(cR_E) ≫ phi_(T_L E) ≫ Q_S(gamma^c_E). This is exactly the required termwise comparison square.
4. For uniqueness, let eta' be another natural isomorphism satisfying this square with the same cR and cS. At every Q_R E the two squares have equal right-hand sides. The common postcomposite D_M(phi_E) ≫ cS_(C_F E) is an isomorphism, hence a monomorphism; cancel_mono gives eta'_(Q_R E)=eta_(Q_R E). Localization.natTrans_ext then identifies their hom natural transformations, and Iso.ext gives eta'=eta. Finally take b=(cR,cS,eta). The K-flatness and flat-term witnesses, the two resolving normalizations, the square, and this uniqueness establish DerivedAction(F,L,M,gamma,b) by [dag-def-pur-signed-line-derived-action-data], proving the stated existence.
