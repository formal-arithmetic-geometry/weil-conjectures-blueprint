1. Use the existing module-sheaf categories, with the chosen sheaf and localization universes, as in [dag-sk-def-pbc-conventions] and `def-weil-size-convention`. Define F = pull(a) to be ringedSitePullbackFunctor a (module_pushforward_isRightAdjoint_of_universes a). Here ringedSitePullbackFunctor is the abbreviation for the existing module pullback recorded in [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-finite-free-dual-pullback], and [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes] supplies the right-adjoint instance for module pushforward under the continuity and sheafification hypotheses of the attachment. Thus this definition uses that specified pullback, with its actual adjunction unit; it does not select a new pullback or invoke the finite-locally-free dual isomorphism theorem. (uses [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-finite-free-dual-pullback], [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes])
2. Use the existing sheaf tensor product [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product], tensor powers and maps scalarTensorPower and scalarTensorPowerMap `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, and internal Hom [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom]. The signedLinePower definition recorded in `lem-pur-signed-line-scalar-tensor-tower` is P^$[n]$ = scalarTensorPower A P n and P^[-(n+1)] = homObj (scalarTensorPower A P (n+1)) (unit A). For b:P ≅ Q define signedIsoHom b n = scalarTensorPowerMap A b.hom n for n ≥ 0, and signedIsoHom b (-(n+1)) = homObjMap (scalarTensorPowerMap A b.inv (n+1)) (identity on unit A). The latter is precisely precomposition with the inverse positive-power map. These are the stated explicit morphisms, rather than isomorphisms chosen from an existence assertion. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product], [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`)
3. Use the published ordinary, total and derived tensor interfaces KFlatFixedTensor.ordinaryTensor, totalTensor and derivedTensor of `lem-weil-pbc-kflat-fixed-tensor-triangulated`. Define derivedLine A L by fixing the second argument of derivedTensor at DerivedCategory.Q.obj ((CochainComplex.singleFunctor _ 0).obj L); this is D_L = (- tensor-derived L$[0]$). Define complexLine A L = (siteSheafTensorRight L).mapHomologicalComplex (.up ℤ), using zero preservation from `lem-route-site-sheaf-tensor-right-preserves-zero-morphisms`. Define collapse by CochainComplex.map₂HomologicalComplexFlipObjSingleIso (KFlatFixedTensor.ordinaryTensor A) L, followed by NatIso.mapHomologicalComplex ((siteSheafTensorRightIso A L).symm) (.up ℤ). The first map collapses total tensor with the degree-zero single complex to termwise ordinary tensor. The second uses the explicit site-to-monoidal tensor presentation of [dag-def-pur-site-sheaf-tensor-presentation-data], recorded also in `lem-weil-pbc-independent-sheaf-tensor-colimits`, for the monoidal tensor of `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`. Its inverse has the required orientation from monoidal tensor to site tensor. This gives exactly the total-complex single-factor comparison, without asserting any additional descent or coherence theorem. (uses [dag-def-pur-site-sheaf-tensor-presentation-data], `lem-weil-pbc-independent-sheaf-tensor-colimits`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`)
4. Define ResolvingNormalization using the actual representativeIso of `def-sf-ringed-kflat-tensor-representative-data`. Given K-flatness and flat terms for L$[0]$, and c: Q followed by D_L ≅ complexLine A L followed by Q, require for every K-flat E with flat terms the equation HEq(c.hom.app E followed by Q.map ((collapse A L).inv.app E), (KFlatFixedTensor.representativeIso A E L$[0]$ hE hL hf hLf).inv). Equivalently, c on each such representative is the inverse representative comparison followed by the localized collapse. The quantifiers and flatness witnesses are part of this predicate; the definition does not assert that such a c exists.
5. For an additive F preserving finite limits and finite colimits, use Functor.mapDerivedCategory and its comparison F.mapDerivedCategoryFactors. Define DerivedActionData F L M as a structure with fst the comparison Q D_L ≅ complexLine R L Q, and snd the pair consisting of Q D_M ≅ complexLine S M Q and η: D_L D(F) ≅ D(F) D_M. Define DerivedActionSquare by equality, expressed as HEq, of the following composites for every complex E: first η at QE, then D_M applied to F.mapDerivedCategoryFactors at E, then the target localization comparison; or first D(F) applied to the source localization comparison, then F.mapDerivedCategoryFactors at complexLine R L(E), then Q applied to (NatIso.mapHomologicalComplex γ (.up ℤ)).hom.app E. Define DerivedActionUnique to require that every η satisfying this same equation with the same two localization comparisons equals the given η. Finally define DerivedAction by existentially quantifying K-flatness and flat-term witnesses for L$[0]$ and M$[0]$, and conjoining the two ResolvingNormalization predicates, DerivedActionSquare and DerivedActionUnique. This packages exactly the specified data and conditions; no witness of these conditions is constructed here.
6. Use OrdinaryLineConstraints of [dag-def-pur-signed-line-ordinary-constraint-data]: on every local pure section its unit sends x tensor r to r x, its positive product sends (x tensor y) tensor q to x tensor (y tensor q), and its evaluation sends (x tensor φ) tensor y to φ(y)x. Define LocalizedLineConstraints as a one-constructor proposition carrying ordinary unit and product isomorphisms and an evaluation transformation satisfying this ordinary predicate, together with their localization equations. Writing r_i for the ordinary functors, d_i for the derived functors and c_i: Q d_i ≅ r_i^complex Q, these equations say on QE that u = c_0 followed by Q of the termwise ordinary unit; that p_n followed by c_(n+2) equals d_1(c_(n+1)), then c_1 at r_(n+1)^complex(E), then Q of the termwise ordinary product; and that e_n equals d_(n+1)(c_(-(n+1))), then c_(n+1) at r_(-(n+1))^complex(E), then Q of the termwise ordinary evaluation. As in the attachment these equalities are HEq, using NatIso.mapHomologicalComplex and NatTrans.mapHomologicalComplex for the termwise maps. Define DerivedLineConstraints by specializing Q to DerivedCategory.Q, r_i to siteSheafTensorRight(P^$[i]$), d_i to derivedLine A (P^$[i]$), and the ordinary predicate to OrdinaryLineConstraints A P. Thus the ordinary formulas and their specified derived lifts are exactly the witnesses and equations in this predicate. (uses [dag-def-pur-signed-line-ordinary-constraint-data])
7. Define LocalizedLineFamily as a one-constructor proposition carrying source and target unit, positive product and evaluation constraints and proofs of the two given constraint predicates. For a fixed family η_i: d_i F ≅ F e_i, require on every object X the three equations: η_0(X) followed by the target unit equals F of the source unit; F of the source product followed by η_(n+2)(X) equals η_1 at d_(n+1)(X), then e_1 applied to η_(n+1)(X), then the target product; and F of the source evaluation equals η_(n+1) at d_(-(n+1))(X), then e_(n+1) applied to η_(-(n+1))(X), then the target evaluation. Define DerivedFamilyCoherence by taking F here to be F.mapDerivedCategory, the two functor families to be derivedLine on the signed powers, η_i = (b i).snd.snd, and the two constraint predicates to be DerivedLineConstraints with the comparisons (b i).fst and (b i).snd.fst. Hence it is the same derived action family that must commute with all three constraints on all derived objects; the generic propositions merely package these witnesses and equations.
8. Finally use the normalization predicates of [dag-def-pur-signed-line-pullback-normalization-data]. Define IsoNaturality for a given family T by requiring, for every compatible pair b:P ≅ P′ and c:Q ≅ Q′ and every normalized family T′ extending t′, that F(signedIsoHom b d) followed by T′_d equals T_d followed by signedIsoHom c d for every integer d, whenever F(b.hom) followed by t′.hom equals t.hom followed by c.hom. Define ScalarNaturality with the explicit SignedLineScalarPullbackSquare predicate recorded in `lem-pur-signed-line-scalar-pullback-square`. Quantify over coefficient extensions α:R → R′ and β:S → S′ and a compatible structural map a′; retain the attachment's ring inverse-image adjunction and the hypothesis that the adjunct of a′ is an isomorphism. For a normalized pullback family T′ and scalar families B,E satisfying SignedLineComparisonNormalized, require that the degree-one SignedLineScalarPullbackSquare implies that same canonical square for every signed power, with comparisons B_d,E_d,T_d,T′_d. The square uses the actual scalar/pullback reassociation tested on local adjunction-unit images. Neither α nor β is required to be flat or invertible. These are commuting-square predicates with the displayed hypotheses, not assertions of their validity or choices of normalized comparison families. No mathematical existence is assumed by these predicates. (uses [dag-def-pur-signed-line-pullback-normalization-data], `lem-pur-signed-line-scalar-pullback-square`)
