1. Work in the sheaf-of-modules categories and universe convention of `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category` and [dag-sk-def-pbc-conventions]. The actual module pullback is the functor supplied by the universe-sized adjunction of [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes], with its section map `ringedSitePullbackSection`; the finite-free dual pullback result [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-finite-free-dual-pullback] supplies the intended pullback/evaluation interpretation for the internal-dual (negative-power) clauses. The signed powers and their scalar normalizations are those fixed by [dag-def-pur-signed-line-scalar-normalization-data] and the tensor/scalar comparison data `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, with the tower conventions of `lem-pur-signed-line-scalar-tensor-tower`.
2. Unfold `SignedLinePullbackNormalized`. It is exactly a conjunction of four conditions on the family `P`: for every site object `V` and coefficient section `r`, `(P 0).hom` evaluated on the actual pullback section of the degree-zero power equals `a.hom` applied to `r`; `(P 1).hom` is `p.hom`; for every `n : ℕ`, the degree-`n+2` map sends the pulled tensor generator `siteSheafTensorTmul x y` to the tensor of the degree-`n+1` image of `x` with the degree-one image of `y`; and for `φ` in degree `-(n+1)` and `x` in degree `n+1`, the pulled functional evaluated at the pulled positive section is `a.hom` applied to the original evaluation. Thus the positive recursion and the negative evaluation are stated on precisely the section-level generators used by the Lean definition, while the scalar/tensor maps are the normalized ones of `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`. (uses `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`)
3. Unfold `SignedLineScalarPullbackSquare`. For every source object `V`, every local section `x` of `L`, and every scalar section `s` of `S`, require equality of the two displayed composites: the left side applies the pullback along `b` to the scalar-extension section of `c` on `s` and `x`, then applies `q`; the right side first pulls `x` along `a`, applies `p`, and inserts it with `b.hom(s)` into the scalar-extension section for `c'`. The scalar-extension sections and the canonical reassociation are the actual ones specified by `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, and the pullback maps are the adjunction-unit maps from [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes]. (uses `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`)
4. These conjunctions and the universal quantification over `V`, `x`, `s`, `φ`, and `r` are predicates defining the two notions. No witness is constructed and no further assertion of flatness, invertibility of coefficient maps, or existence of a normalized family is made.
