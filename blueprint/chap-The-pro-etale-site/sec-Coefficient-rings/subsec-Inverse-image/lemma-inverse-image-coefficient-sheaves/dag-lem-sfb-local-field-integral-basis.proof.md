1. Put $A=\mathcal O_E$. The DVR theorem `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr`, applied to the coefficient spectral absolute value (which extends the base absolute value by spectralNorm_extends), gives that $E$ is its fraction field and that $A$ is a DVR, hence a PID `def-sc-discrete-valuation-ring`. By `lem-weil-pbc-pid-noetherian-normal`, $A$ is Noetherian and integrally closed. The field $E$ has characteristic zero since the map from $\mathbf Q_\ell$ `def-weil-l-adic-numbers` is injective; Lean uses charZero_of_injective_algebraMap. Thus the finite extension $F/E$ is separable. All scalar maps used below are the composite $A\to E\to F$. (uses `def-sc-discrete-valuation-ring`, `lem-weil-pbc-pid-noetherian-normal`, `def-weil-l-adic-numbers`)
2. Construct $C=\operatorname{integralClosure}_A(F)$ with its natural inclusion into $F$. By `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-normal-separable-integral-closure-finite`, $C$ is finite over $A$; this is IsIntegralClosure.finite A E F C. As a subalgebra of the field $F$, with nonzero elements of $A$ remaining nonzero in $F$, it is torsion-free over $A$. Therefore `lem-sc-dvr-fg-torsionfree-free` makes it free. Lean obtains this conclusion as IsIntegralClosure.module_free A E F C. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-normal-separable-integral-closure-finite`, `lem-sc-dvr-fg-torsionfree-free`)
3. Apply [dag-lem-sfb-integral-closure-rank], namely IsIntegralClosure.rank A E F C, to obtain $\operatorname{finrank}_A C=\operatorname{finrank}_E F=d$, where the last equality is the meaning of the dimension in `def-weil-vector-space-dimension`. Module.finBasisOfFinrankEq now gives an $A$-basis $b$ of $C$ indexed by $\operatorname{Fin}(d)$. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-integral-closure-rank], `def-weil-vector-space-dimension`)
4. Let $i:E\to F$ be the canonical $\mathbf Q_\ell$-algebra homomorphism, given in Lean by IsScalarTower.toAlgHom. By [dag-lem-sfb-padic-integers-integral-closure], applied to $i$, an element $x\in F$ lies in $\mathcal O_F$ if and only if it lies in $C$. Consequently the images $\bar b_j\in F$ of the basis elements $b_j\in C$ all lie in $\mathcal O_F$. (uses `lem-sfb-padic-integers-integral-closure`)
5. For $x\in\mathcal O_F$, view it as $y\in C$ and set $a_j$ to the image in $E$ of $(b.\mathrm{repr}\,y)_j\in A$. Thus every $a_j$ belongs to $\mathcal O_E$. Applying the inclusion $C\to F$ to the basis expansion b.sum_repr gives $x=\sum_j\operatorname{algebraMap}_{E,F}(a_j)\bar b_j$; Algebra.smul_def and IsScalarTower.algebraMap_apply identify the scalar actions with these products. Any other integral tuple $a'_j$ defines $c_j\in A$ with image $a'_j$ in $E$. Its asserted expansion, together with injectivity of $C\to F$, gives $\sum_j c_j b_j=y$ in $C$. Apply b.repr and evaluate at each index, simplifying the coordinates of a basis vector using Finsupp.single_apply, to obtain $c_j=(b.\mathrm{repr}\,y)_j$. Taking images in $E$ proves $a'_j=a_j$ for every $j$, hence uniqueness. Relabeling the $d$ images $\bar b_j$ as $b_1,\ldots,b_d$ proves the statement.
