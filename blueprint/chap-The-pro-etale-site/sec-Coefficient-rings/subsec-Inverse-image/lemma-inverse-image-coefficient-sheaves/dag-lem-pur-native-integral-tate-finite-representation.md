Let ell be prime, let Y be an arbitrary scheme on which ell is invertible, let E be a finite subextension of the fixed p-adic algebraic closure, and let n be a natural number. The underlying sheaf of sets of spIntegralTwistLevel Y ell E n from [dag-sk-def-sp-tate-twist] is the lifted Yoneda sheaf of a finite etale object V of the small pro-etale site of Y. This is a single positive level of exponent n+1. It makes no assertion about tower transitions, infinite limits, or inverse image.
