1. By `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, $\Lambda_X=p_X^*\Lambda$ with $p_X^*=\pi_X^*\circ p^*$ of `det-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`, part 4, and $p^*=\mathrm{sheafify}\circ\operatorname{Lan}_{u^{\mathrm{op}}}$ of part 3. This is literally the functor $p_X^*$ of `def-sfb-upper-star-from-point`, whose $p^*$ is the same composite (`def-sfb-point-upper-star`) and whose $\pi_X^*$ is the same pullback of [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star]. The ring structure of $\Lambda_X$ in `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients` is the transport $\mu_X=p_X^*(\mu)\circ\mu^{p_X^*}_{\Lambda,\Lambda}$, $\eta_X=p_X^*(\eta)\circ\varepsilon^{p_X^*}$ of clause 1 of `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport`, word for word the ring object $\Lambda_X$ of `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients` used in the statements of the cited child nodes. Likewise the ring structures on $f^*\Lambda_Y$ and $f_*\Lambda_X$ named in the statement are those of clause 1 of `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport` for $F=f^*$ with the strong structure of `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal` and $F=f_*$ with the lax structure of `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, specialized to $\mathcal A=\Ab$. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, `det-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`, `def-sfb-upper-star-from-point`, `def-sfb-point-upper-star`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport`, `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`)
2. The comparison $c_f\colon f^*\circ p_Y^*\cong p_X^*$ of [dag-det-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp] is, by its statement, $p^*$ composed with the inverse of the composition isomorphism $(\pi_Y\circ f)^*\cong f^*\circ\pi_Y^*$ of `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`, followed by the identification $(\pi_Y\circ f)^*=\pi_X^*$. By step 1 the functors $p^*$, $p_X^*$, $p_Y^*$ are those of [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso], whose statement defines its natural isomorphism by exactly this composite. So $c_f$ is the isomorphism of [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso], and `def-sfb-alpha` says that its component at $\Lambda$ is $\alpha_f\colon f^*\Lambda_Y\to\Lambda_X$. [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom] says that $\alpha_f$ is a morphism of ring objects for the ring structure on $f^*\Lambda_Y$ transported along $f^*$ fixed in step 1. (uses [dag-det-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp], [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso], [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom], `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`, `def-sfb-alpha`)
3. `def-sfb-alpha-bundled` gives an isomorphism of ring objects $f^*\Lambda_Y\cong\Lambda_X$, the composite of the identification of $f^*$ applied to the ring object $p_Y^*\Lambda$ with the ring object $(f^*\circ p_Y^*)\Lambda$, followed by the component $c_f(\Lambda)$ of step 2. Write $F=p_Y^*$ and $G=f^*$. By the definition of the composite lax monoidal structure, both transported multiplications are $GF(\mu)\circ G(\mu^F_{\Lambda,\Lambda})\circ\mu^G_{F\Lambda,F\Lambda}$ and both units are $GF(\eta)\circ G(\varepsilon^F)\circ\varepsilon^G$, so the first identification has underlying map $\mathrm{id}_{GF\Lambda}$. Hence the underlying morphism of the bundled isomorphism is $c_f(\Lambda)=\alpha_f$: $\alpha_f$ underlies an isomorphism of ring objects. (uses `def-sfb-alpha-bundled`)
4. Let $u\colon\mathrm{id}\to f_*f^*$ be the unit of the adjunction of `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` for $\mathcal A=\Ab$. `def-sfb-eta` defines $\eta_f=f_*(\alpha_f)\circ u_{\Lambda_Y}\colon\Lambda_Y\to f_*\Lambda_X$, the mate of $\alpha_f$, with $\alpha_f$ as in step 2; this is the map of the statement. `lem-sfb-eta-ring-hom` says that $\eta_f$ is a morphism of ring objects for the ring structure on $f_*\Lambda_X$ induced by the lax structure of $f_*$, which is the structure fixed in step 1. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-sfb-eta`, `lem-sfb-eta-ring-hom`)
