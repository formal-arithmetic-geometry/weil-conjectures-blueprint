1. For each A-module sheaf M, use the comparison `sheafPullbackForgetIso` and its unit identity `sheafPullbackForgetIso_unit` to form the additive sheaf pullback unit presentation whose unit is the underlying abelian adjunction unit. Thus the set-valued inverse image of the underlying module is presented in the form required for transporting the ring action. (uses [dag-lem-pur-signed-line-sheaf-pullback-forget])
2. Apply `inverseImageAction_of_presentations` to the given ring presentation [dag-lem-pur-signed-line-pullback-unit-presentation], its equation identifying the presented ring unit with the structural map, and the additive presentation from the preceding step. This gives an `InverseImageAction` [dag-def-pur-signed-line-module-inverse-image-action] for every M, and hence an actual B-module sheaf on the same underlying additive inverse image via `InverseImageAction.toModule`. (uses [dag-lem-pur-signed-line-module-action-presentations], [dag-def-pur-signed-line-module-inverse-image-action])
3. For the scalar-linearity test, let a and b be maps out of the tensor of the presented ring inverse image with the additive inverse image of M, and assume they agree on every pair of source sections. The tensor presentation `pr.tensor (pa M)` and `SheafPullbackUnitPresentation.hom_ext` reduce equality of a and b to those pairs; rewriting the ring component with the given unit equation `hr` leaves exactly the assumed equality. This supplies the generator hypothesis used to test whether an abelian adjunct is B-linear. (uses [dag-lem-pur-signed-line-pullback-unit-presentation])
4. Use the action's unit-scalar equation together with the generator test to prove `linear_of_adjunct_linear`: an additive inverse-image map is B-linear whenever its abelian adjunct is linear. Define the lifted maps and the unit from the abelian adjunction, and verify the functor laws after applying the faithful underlying-sheaf map (`Q.map_injective`). The equivalence of module homs is then the two directions in `hlin`; `Adjunction.homEquiv_unit`, `adj.unit_naturality`, and `Functor.map_comp` establish the required adjunct equations. `AlgebraicGeometry.adjunctionOfLinearLifts` consequently supplies the module adjunction for the constructed functor, with underlying natural isomorphism assembled by `NatIso.ofComponents` from the action comparisons. (uses [dag-def-pur-signed-line-module-inverse-image-action])
5. Identify the constructed module left adjoint with the specified module pullback using `(pullbackPushforwardAdjunction φ).leftAdjointUniq`, and let q be the resulting comparison. The final isomorphism is `Functor.isoWhiskerRight q Q ≪≫ e`. Applying `Adjunction.unit_leftAdjointUniq_hom_app`, then mapping with the underlying functor and simplifying with `Ra.map_id`, `Category.comp_id`, and `Functor.map_comp`, gives precisely that the module pullback adjunction unit followed by this comparison equals the abelian inverse-image unit. Hence the stated natural isomorphism and unit compatibility hold. (uses [dag-def-pur-signed-line-module-inverse-image-action], [dag-lem-pur-signed-line-sheaf-pullback-forget])
