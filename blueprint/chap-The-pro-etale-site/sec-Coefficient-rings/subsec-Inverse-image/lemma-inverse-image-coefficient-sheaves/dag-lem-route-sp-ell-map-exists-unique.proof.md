1. The inclusions into the fixed algebraic closure identify the restricted norms on $E$ and $F$, so $j:\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_F$, $x\mapsto x$, is a continuous unital ring homomorphism: membership is $|x|\leq1$, and both topologies are subspace topologies. By `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map` and `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, $p_X^*\underline j$ is a ring-object morphism. Its composite with $\mathcal O_{F,X}\to F_X$ equals the composite $\mathcal O_{E,X}\to E_X\to F_X$: before pullback both send $a:S\to\mathcal O_E$ to the same function $S\to F$, and functors preserve composition. The two field and integer inclusion maps here are those of `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map` and `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`. The uniqueness clause of [dag-lem-route-sp-integers-map-exists-unique] therefore identifies $p_X^*\underline j=i_{E,F,X}$. (uses [dag-lem-route-sp-integers-map-exists-unique], `def-sfb-padic-integers-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map`, `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`)
2. Define $\bar j:B_{E,n}\to B_{F,n}$ by $[x]\mapsto[j(x)]$. This is well-defined: if $x-y=\ell^{n+1}a$ in $\mathcal O_E$, then $j(x)-j(y)=\ell^{n+1}j(a)$ in $\mathcal O_F$. Addition, multiplication and one are preserved by applying $j$ to representatives. Thus $\bar j q_{E,n}=q_{F,n}j$. The discrete quotient topologies make $\bar j$ continuous. For $G=E,F$ the absolute value $|x|_G=|x|$ restricted from $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ (`def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring`) restricts to $|\cdot|_\ell$ on $\mathbf Q_\ell\subseteq G$, so by the uniqueness in clause 1 of `def-weil-l-adic-coefficient-extension` it is the extending absolute value there; hence $\mathcal O_G=\{|x|_G\le1\}$ of `def-sfb-padic-integers-condensed-ring` is the ring of clause 2, with the topology of `def-sfb-coefficient-extension-integers-topology`. So [dag-lem-sp-ell-quotient-finite-discrete] applies to $E$ and $F$ with exponent $n+1$: the reductions are continuous and the quotients finite. (uses [dag-lem-sp-ell-quotient-finite-discrete], `def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring`, `def-weil-l-adic-coefficient-extension`, `def-sfb-padic-integers-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-coefficient-extension-integers-topology`)
3. Apply `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map` to $\bar j,q_{E,n},q_{F,n},j$. On a profinite test $S$, the two composites in their square send $a:S\to\mathcal O_E$ to $s\mapsto[j(a(s))]$, so they agree. Apply the functor $p_X^*$ of `sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point` and use the coefficient-map definition `def-sfb-coefficient-map`. The resulting morphism $\phi=p_X^*\underline{\bar j}$ satisfies $\phi r_{E,n}=r_{F,n}i_{E,F,X}$ by step 1. It is in particular a morphism of the underlying abelian sheaves, giving existence. (uses `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map`, `sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`, `def-sfb-coefficient-map`)
4. Put $q=\underline{q_{E,n}}$. For any profinite $S$ and $b\in C(S,B_{E,n})$, choose for each of the finitely many $t\in B_{E,n}$ a representative $a_t\in\mathcal O_E$. Then $a(s)=a_{b(s)}$ is continuous: each fibre $b^{-1}(t)$ is open, and the inverse image of any open subset of $\mathcal O_E$ under $a$ is a union of these fibres. Moreover $q_{E,n}a=b$. Hence every component $q(S)$ is surjective. If additive sheaf maps $u,v$ out of $\underline{B_{E,n}}$ agree after $q$, evaluation on such lifts gives $u_S(b)=v_S(b)$ for every $S,b$; thus $u=v$. This proves that $q$ is epimorphic directly, using precisely the finiteness from [dag-lem-sp-ell-quotient-finite-discrete] (applicable by step 2) and the section description of `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring`. (uses [dag-lem-sp-ell-quotient-finite-discrete], `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring`)
5. Use the adjunction $p_X^*\dashv p_{X*}$ stated in `sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`. For any abelian sheaf $H$ on $X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}}$, transpose maps $u,v:p_X^*\underline{B_{E,n}}\to H$ to $u^\#,v^\#:\underline{B_{E,n}}\to p_{X*}H$. Naturality of this Hom bijection in its source sends $u\circ p_X^*q=v\circ p_X^*q$ to $u^\#q=v^\#q$. Step 4 gives $u^\#=v^\#$; the bijection then gives $u=v$. Thus $r_{E,n}=p_X^*q$ is epimorphic. Apply this cancellation to any two morphisms satisfying the asserted square: their composites with $r_{E,n}$ both equal $r_{F,n}i_{E,F,X}$, hence they coincide. This proves uniqueness in the full abelian-sheaf Hom set. (uses `sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`)
