1. Let A be the finite-subfield coefficient diagram and M the rational twist diagram. The finite-field poset is filtered by composita `det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-subextensions`, hence sifted. The rational identity and composition laws [lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-id, lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-comp] give M as the specified functor. Form its abelian-sheaf colimit [lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaves-filtered-colimits-exact, def-weil-pbc-category-colimit]. The actual coefficient cocone c_A has vertex Qbar_X and is colimiting: use the point coefficient colimit [dag-lem-sfb-qlbar-point-abelian-colimit] and preservation under the left adjoint p_X^* [sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point, lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits, def-sfb-qlbar-x-cocone]. (uses [dag-lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-id], [dag-lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-comp], [dag-lem-sfb-qlbar-point-abelian-colimit], `det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-subextensions`, `lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaves-filtered-colimits-exact`, `def-weil-pbc-category-colimit`, `sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`, `lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits`, `def-sfb-qlbar-x-cocone`)
2. The stage actions A_E tensor M_E to M_E form a natural transformation A tensor M to M by [dag-lem-sp-rational-twist-transition-semilinear]. Tensor preserves colimits in each variable `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`; since the index is sifted, the tensor of the two colimit cocones is a colimit of the diagonal tensor diagram. Apply its universal property to the stage actions followed by the M coprojections. This gives an action a on the two colimit vertices with (c_E tensor eta_E);a = action_E;eta_E. (uses [dag-lem-sp-rational-twist-transition-semilinear], `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`)
3. Check the unit law on the colimiting coprojections of 1 tensor M. Each c_E preserves the unit [def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar, det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients], and the stage module unit law gives the result. Check associativity on the colimiting triple diagonal tensor cocone (A tensor A) tensor M: multiplicativity of c_E, associator naturality and the stage module associativity law give the desired equation. Thus a is a module-object action `sk-def-sfb-module-objects`. (uses `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`, `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, `sk-def-sfb-module-objects`, `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`)
4. For E,F contained in H, the coefficient and twist cocone equations rewrite c_E tensor eta_F as (i_EH tensor j_FH) followed by c_H tensor eta_H [def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map, def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar]. The diagonal action formula therefore gives exactly the requested common-field formula. Any other action with that formula has the same diagonal composites, by taking E=F=H and using the identity transition maps [def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map, lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-id]. The tensor-colimit hom_ext property identifies its underlying action arrow with a; module-object extensionality gives uniqueness.
