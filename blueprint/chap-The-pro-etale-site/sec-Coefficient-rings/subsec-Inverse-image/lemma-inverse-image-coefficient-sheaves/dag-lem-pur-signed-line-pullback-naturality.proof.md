1. Use the module pullback supplied by the universe-level adjunction [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes]. Its adjunction-unit naturality gives the section formula saying that pulling back a module morphism and then evaluating a pulled source section is the same as first applying the morphism and then pulling back; the tensor-power maps are the normalized maps of `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`. The sectionwise second-factor calculation needed for these maps is the tensor naturality of `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`, with the associator and unitor conventions supplied by `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`. We work throughout with the site, universe, and no-flatness conventions of [dag-sk-def-pbc-conventions]. (uses [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes], `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, `sk-def-pbc-conventions`)
2. For a compatible pair of line isomorphisms, prove the nonnegative-degree square by Nat.twoStepInduction. In degree zero, the two normalized unit maps agree after evaluating on every pulled section, so the pullback adjunction supplied by [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes] and module Hom extensionality give equality. In degree one the assertion is exactly the assumed square for b, c, and t. For degree n+2, evaluate on a pulled pure tensor, use pullback-section naturality and the pure-tensor formula for the power map from `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`, then use the two tensor-recursion clauses of the normalized families, the induction hypothesis in degree n+1, and the degree-one square. Bilinearity and generation by pure tensors give the morphism equality; this uses the power data of `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`. (uses [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes], `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`, `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`)
3. For degree -(n+1), first cancel the target isomorphism to turn the given square into its inverse square, and apply the preceding positive-degree result to b⁻¹ and c⁻¹ in degree n+1. Regard the negative powers as the internal Hom of [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom]. Postcompose the two candidate negative composites with the dual map of the positive comparison. Their evaluations on a local functional and a local positive-power section agree: for the first composite this is the negative evaluation clause of the primed normalized family, and for the second it is the positive inverse square followed by the negative evaluation clause of the unprimed family. The pairing-transfer uniqueness theorem [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-pullback-pairing-transfer] identifies the two postcomposites. The explicit inverse obtained by applying the internal-Hom map to the inverse positive comparison gives the cancellation, using the pullback adjunction of [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes]. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-pullback-pairing-transfer], [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-module-pullback-exists-universes])
4. The preceding positive and negative cases establish IsoNaturality for every integer degree, with the negative map explicitly given by inverse precomposition on internal Hom.
5. For arbitrary coefficient maps α:R→R′ and β:S→S′ satisfying the commuting ring square, use the given local unit covers of P and Q to obtain IsInvertible instances. The scalar-extension local-isomorphism theorem `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-local-iso` gives IsInvertible for the two scalar extensions and identifies their dual comparisons. These are the canonical unit, binary, dual, and tower maps of `def-sp-canonical-scalar-extension-maps`, with their normalized tensor behavior recorded in `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, and the explicit dual evaluation formula of `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-evaluation`; no flatness of α or β is introduced. The ambient ringed-site hypotheses remain those of [dag-sk-def-pbc-conventions]. (uses `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-local-iso`, `def-sp-canonical-scalar-extension-maps`, `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-evaluation`, `sk-def-pbc-conventions`)
6. From each IsInvertible instance, choose its covering local generators and convert each generator isomorphism to a trivialization by the unit; this supplies the four local-triviality witnesses required for the scalar square. The local dual identifications use the sheaf-Hom construction [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom] and the finite-free dual comparison [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-finite-free-dual-pullback], while `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-local-iso` ensures the scalar-extended source and target have the same kind of local rank-one charts. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-finite-free-dual-pullback], `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-local-iso`)
7. Apply the unique normalized scalar/pullback-square theorem `lem-pur-signed-line-scalar-pullback-square` to α, β, the two ring maps, the compatible degree-one isomorphisms, and the normalized scalar comparison families. Its construction uses the canonical scalar maps `def-sp-canonical-scalar-extension-maps`, the tensor comparison and second-variable tensor naturality `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data` and `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`, binary coherence `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, dual evaluation `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-evaluation`, local dual invertibility `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-local-iso`, finite-free dual pullback [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-finite-free-dual-pullback], and pairing transfer [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-pullback-pairing-transfer]. It returns a pair of normalized signed families and the scalar square in every integer degree, without a flatness hypothesis. (uses [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-finite-free-dual-pullback], [dag-lem-pur-ringed-site-pullback-pairing-transfer], `def-sp-canonical-scalar-extension-maps`, `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-evaluation`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-local-iso`)
8. The returned source and target families are normalized in the same data as T and T′. Apply normalized signed-line uniqueness [dag-lem-pur-signed-line-pullback-unique] separately to identify them with T and T′; substituting these identifications into the returned scalar square gives ScalarNaturality for T. Together with the integer-degree isomorphism result, this is exactly the asserted conjunction. (uses [dag-lem-pur-signed-line-pullback-unique])
