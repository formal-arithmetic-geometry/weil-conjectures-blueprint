1. Work in the category of A-module sheaves `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`, written KFlatFixedTensor.Modules A in `def-sf-ringed-kflat-tensor-representative-data` and `lem-weil-pbc-kflat-fixed-tensor-triangulated`. Write M ⊗ L for (siteSheafTensorRight L).obj M and x ⊗ y for siteSheafTensorTmul. By the power definitions `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, put L_n = scalarTensorPower A P (n+1), so the next positive power is L_n ⊗ P. The definition signedLinePower recorded in [dag-def-pur-signed-line-scalar-normalization-data] and `lem-pur-signed-line-scalar-tensor-tower` identifies degree zero with A, degree one with P, and degree −(n+1) with homObj L_n A.
2. First establish that maps (M ⊗ L) ⊗ N → H agree if they agree on every (x ⊗ y) ⊗ z. Apply scalarTensor_hom_ext from `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence` to reduce to an arbitrary inner section ξ and a section z. For the sheafification unit η of the presheaf tensor M ⊗ L, Presheaf.imageSieve_mem gives a covering sieve on which ξ lifts to presheaf tensor sections. Separatedness of H reduces equality to this cover; PresheafOfModules.naturality_apply and scalarTensorTmul_naturality identify the restrictions. TensorProduct.induction_on on a lift then reduces to zero, sums, and pure tensors. Linearity handles zero and sums, and the assumed equality handles pure tensors. This is the helper tensor_three_ext used below. (uses `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`)
3. Construct the right unitor by scalar multiplication and the positive product by the sheaf tensor associator: use scalarTensorRightUnitor and scalarTensorAssociatorIso from `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`. Their pure-section formulas are x ⊗ r ↦ r • x and (x ⊗ y) ⊗ q ↦ x ⊗ (y ⊗ q), by scalarTensorRightUnitor_tmul and scalarTensorAssociatorIso_tmul. For the unitor, scalarTensor_hom_ext reduces naturality in M to these formulas, scalarTensor_first_tmul and map_smul. For the associator use tensor_three_ext, then scalarTensor_first_tmul twice and the associator formula. NatIso.ofComponents therefore assembles these component isomorphisms into ordinary_unit and ordinary_product L N. (uses `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`)
4. Construct evaluation as sheafification of the explicit internal-Hom pairing. A section φ of homObj L A is a module map on the slice [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom]; its value on y at the identity slice object is φ.app (op (Over.mk (𝟙 V.unop))) y. The helpers LineEvaluation.evalLinear and LineEvaluation.eval_restrict in `lem-pur-sheaf-line-evaluation-inverse-pair` give bilinearity and compatibility with restriction for any L. TensorProduct.lift defines the pairing on the presheaf tensor homObj L A ⊗ L, and TensorProduct.ext' reduces its naturality to eval_restrict. Descend this presheaf map through PresheafOfModules.sheafificationAdjunction to obtain eval_pair : (homObj L A) ⊗ L → A. Applying the adjunction's homEquiv.apply_symm_apply to φ ⊗ₜ y shows that eval_pair sends φ ⊗ y to precisely the displayed value φ(y). (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], `lem-pur-sheaf-line-evaluation-inverse-pair`)
5. Reassociate and contract the last two factors, then apply the right unitor: define ordinary_eval_map L M as (M ⊗ homObj L A) ⊗ L → M ⊗ ((homObj L A) ⊗ L) → M ⊗ A → M. The middle arrow is siteSheafTensorSecondMap M eval_pair, obtained by sheafifying id ⊗ eval_pair; its presheaf restriction compatibility is siteTensorSecondMap_naturality `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`. The associator, scalarTensor_second_tmul and right-unitor formulas of `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, followed by the evaluation formula just proved, give (x ⊗ φ) ⊗ y ↦ φ(y) • x. Tensor generation proves naturality in M: use tensor_three_ext, apply scalarTensor_first_tmul twice, and use this contraction formula and map_smul. Thus these maps form the natural transformation ordinary_evaluation L. (uses `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`)
6. Take ordinaryUnit = ordinary_unit A, ordinaryProduct n = ordinary_product A L_n P, and ordinaryEvaluation n = ordinary_evaluation A L_n. The power identifications in the first step give exactly the required sources and targets. Their pure-section formulas, proved above, are respectively x ⊗ r ↦ r • x, (x ⊗ y) ⊗ q ↦ x ⊗ (y ⊗ q), and (x ⊗ φ) ⊗ y ↦ (φ.app (op (Over.mk (𝟙 V.unop))) y) • x. These natural isomorphisms and natural transformations supply all the asserted ordinary constraints.
