1. Put $R=\mathcal O_{E,X}$, $B_+=B_{n+2,X}$, $B_-=B_{n+1,X}$, and let $q_\pm:R\to B_\pm$ and $r:B_+\to B_-$ be the specified reductions. Reduction modulo $\ell^{n+1}$ after reduction modulo $\ell^{n+2}$ equals direct reduction, since both send $a$ to its residue class. Thus $r q_+=q_-$. At the condensed point these maps are postcomposition of continuous functions with the quotient ring maps, so, the ring operations of the continuous-map condensed rings being pointwise, they preserve one and multiplication on every profinite set. The ring-object statement and the pulled-back-coefficients functor therefore give ring-object morphisms $q_\pm,r$ with the same composition identity on $X$. In particular $r\mu_+=\mu_-\circ(r\otimes r)$, where $\mu_\pm$ are the multiplications of $B_\pm$. (uses `sk-def-sfb-ring-object`, `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring`, `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`)
2. Put $U_+=\mu_{\ell^{n+2}}$ and $U_-=\mu_{\ell^{n+1}}$. The roots-of-unity definition gives the abelian-sheaf map $v:U_+\to U_-$, $u\mapsto u^\ell$: $(u^\ell)^{\ell^{n+1}}=u^{\ell^{n+2}}=1$, the power map is multiplicative on the commutative unit groups, and ring restrictions preserve powers. The sheaf tensor bifunctor therefore gives $h=r\otimes v:B_+\otimes U_+\to B_-\otimes U_-$. This is exactly the underlying morphism specified in the conclusion; its second factor has exponent quotient $\ell^{n+2}/\ell^{n+1}=\ell$. (uses [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf], `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`)
3. Write $a_\pm:R\otimes(B_\pm\otimes U_\pm)\to B_\pm\otimes U_\pm$ for the restricted free actions. Expanding multiplication on the first factor and restriction of scalars gives $a_\pm=(\mu_\pm\otimes\mathrm{id}_{U_\pm})\circ((q_\pm\otimes\mathrm{id}_{B_\pm})\otimes\mathrm{id}_{U_\pm})\circ\alpha^{-1}_{R,B_\pm,U_\pm}$. Bifunctoriality and naturality of the associator give
$$h\circ a_+=\bigl((r\circ\mu_+\circ(q_+\otimes\mathrm{id}_{B_+}))\otimes v\bigr)\circ\alpha^{-1}_{R,B_+,U_+}=\bigl((\mu_-\circ(q_-\otimes r))\otimes v\bigr)\circ\alpha^{-1}_{R,B_+,U_+}=a_-\circ(\mathrm{id}_R\otimes h).$$
The middle equality is precisely the multiplicativity and reduction identity of step 1. This is the defining equation for a morphism of $R$-module objects, so $h$, together with this equation, defines the required $f$. This calculation is in abelian sheaves and does not assume their tensor product is computed on sections. (uses `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`, `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`, `sk-def-sfb-module-objects`)
4. If $g:T_{n+2}\to T_{n+1}$ is another module morphism with the prescribed underlying abelian-sheaf map, then both underlying maps equal $h$, hence equal one another. Faithfulness of the forgetful functor from module objects gives $g=f$. The constructed $f$ has underlying map $h$ by construction, proving existence and uniqueness. (uses `det-def-sfb-module-objects-category`)
