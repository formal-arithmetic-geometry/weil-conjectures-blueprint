1. Work in the abelian sheaf categories on the small pro-etale sites. The pullback f^* has the strong monoidal structure of `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, and [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact] records that this same pullback is exact in the abelian setting. Together `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` and `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction` give the monoidal adjunction f^* ⊣ f_*; hence f_* has its lax monoidal structure. The transport principles `sk-def-sfb-transport` and `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction` apply to these structures. By `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport`, the induced ring object on the image of a ring object has multiplication F(μ) ∘ μ^F and unit F(η) ∘ ε^F. Thus f^*Λ_Y and f_*Λ_X carry exactly the transported ring structures used in the statement. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport`)
2. Let c_f : f^* ∘ p_Y^* ≅ p_X^* be the canonical comparison. It is the comparison defined by [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso] (the Lean declaration is `upperStarFromPointComp`), and [dag-sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp] identifies it with the pullback-from-the-point comparison in the statement. Its hom is monoidal by [dag-lem-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-monoidal]. By `def-sfb-alpha`, the component c_f(Λ) has source f^*p_Y^*Λ = f^*Λ_Y and target p_X^*Λ = Λ_X; this component is the Lean map `condensedPullbackHom`, so it is precisely α_f. (uses [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso], [dag-sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp], [dag-lem-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-monoidal], `def-sfb-alpha`)
3. The monoidal comparison c_f induces an isomorphism on ring objects by Mathlib's `Functor.mapMonNatIso`. The source first has to be identified with the ring object obtained from the composite functor: Mathlib's `Functor.mapMonCompIso` gives (f^* ∘ p_Y^*).mapMon ≅ p_Y^*.mapMon ∘ f^*.mapMon, and its inverse component at Λ is the first factor in the Lean definition. The second factor is the component of `Functor.mapMonNatIso (upperStarFromPointComp f)`. Consequently the composite is exactly the bundled isomorphism described in `def-sfb-alpha-bundled`, namely Lean's `coefficientPullbackIso`, with underlying morphism c_f(Λ) = α_f. The transported ring-object maps are justified by `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport`, and the ring-hom assertion for this underlying map is [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom]. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom], [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso], [dag-lem-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-monoidal], `def-sfb-alpha-bundled`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport`, `def-sfb-alpha`)
4. For the adjunction of `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, let u : id ⇒ f_*f^* be its unit. By `def-sfb-eta`, the required second coefficient morphism is u_{Λ_Y} followed by f_*(α_f). In Lean, `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction` identifies the monoid-object unit with `Adjunction.unitAlgHom`, whose underlying map is u, and the definition `condensedPushforwardHom` is exactly `unitAlgHom (Λ_Y) ≫ f_*.map (condensedPullbackHom Λ f)`. Thus η_f has source Λ_Y and target f_*Λ_X and is the adjunction mate of α_f. The compatibility of this construction with the transported module and ring structures is the monoidal-adjunction transport of `sk-def-sfb-transport`. (uses `def-sfb-eta`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`, `def-sfb-alpha`)
5. The unit component is a ring-object morphism by the monoidal adjunction transport in `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction` (Lean theorem `Adjunction.isMonHom_unitAlgHom`), using the monoidal adjunction supplied by `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`. The map f_*(α_f) is a ring-object morphism because the lax functor transports ring morphisms as in `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport`, and α_f is the ring morphism from [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom]. Therefore their composite η_f is a ring-object morphism. Together with the isomorphism in step 3, this proves both morphism assertions and the claimed ring-object isomorphism for α_f. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom], `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-transport`)
