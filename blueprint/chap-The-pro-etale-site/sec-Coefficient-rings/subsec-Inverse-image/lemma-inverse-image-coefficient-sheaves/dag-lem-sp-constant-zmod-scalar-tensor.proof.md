1. Form the presheaf P(V)=A_X(V) tensor_Z M(V) with A_X(V) acting on the first factor and the tensor-product restriction maps. Sheafify it as a module sheaf Q. Its underlying abelian sheaf is the tensor of the underlying coefficient sheaf with U(M), since module sheafification retains abelian sheafification and abelian sheafification is strong monoidal. Forgetting the constant commutative ring agrees with the constant underlying additive group. (uses `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`, `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure`)
2. The bilinear map b,m -> b tensor_R m induces a module map p:Q -> A_X tensor_R M after sheafification. Conversely insertion m -> image(1 tensor_Z m) is additive. It is R-linear: locally every constant cyclic scalar lifts to an integer and every additive map commutes with integer multiplication; separatedness descends this equality. Apply the scalar-extension universal property to get q:A_X tensor_R M -> Q, with q(b tensor_R m)=image(b tensor_Z m). The additive-to-linear lift is proved directly: restrict to constant scalars along the locally surjective sheafification unit, lift every ZMod scalar to an integer, use preservation of integer multiplication, and descend with Mathlib restrictHomEquivOfIsLocallySurjective. (uses `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`)
3. On every integer pure tensor, q p is the sheafification unit; tensor generation and the sheafification adjunction therefore give q p=1_Q. On every relative pure tensor, p q is the identity, so scalar-extension tensor generation gives p q=1. Forget their coefficient actions and compose the coefficient and sheaf-tensor identifications from the first step. For N=0 the cyclic ring is Z and the integer-lift argument is unchanged; for the zero ring and the empty scheme the same formulas and identities apply. (uses `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`)
