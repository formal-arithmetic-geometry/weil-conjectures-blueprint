1. Work in the category of module sheaves `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`, denoted ringedSiteModules in [dag-sk-def-pbc-conventions], with the coefficient and module universe chosen to contain the site universes and with the sheafification hypotheses used by the scalar-extension constructions. Use scalarTensorPower from `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`: its degree-zero value is R, its degree-one value is P, and its degree-(n+2) value is P^{\otimes(n+1)}\otimes_R P. Write E_\alpha M for scalar extension, and s\otimes x for scalarExtensionSection \alpha M U s x. (uses `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`)
2. Define signedLinePower by the two integer constructors. On ofNat n set P^{$[n]$}=scalarTensorPower R P n. On negSucc n, which represents -(n+1), set P^{[-(n+1)]}=\mathcal Hom_R(P^{\otimes(n+1)},R), using homObj and the coefficient unit. The slice sheaf Hom of [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom] makes this an R-module sheaf. These two cases cover all integers and give exactly the stated signed powers. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom])
3. Fix \alpha, Q, b and the family B of isomorphisms in the statement. The first two clauses of SignedLineComparisonNormalized are equalities of forward morphisms: (B_0).hom=(scalarExtensionUnitIso \alpha).hom and (B_1).hom=b.hom. The unit comparison is the canonical map of `def-sp-canonical-scalar-extension-maps`; its normalization sends s\otimes r to s\alpha(r), as established in `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`. Since the degree-zero and degree-one powers are R and P, these equalities have the required endpoints. (uses `def-sp-canonical-scalar-extension-maps`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`)
4. For the positive recursion impose, for every n\geq0, object U, s\in S(U), x\in P^{\otimes(n+1)}(U) and y\in P(U), the equality B_{n+2}(s\otimes(x\otimes y))=B_{n+1}(s\otimes x)\otimes b(1\otimes y). Here both inner tensor sections are the actual sheaf tensor sections of `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`. This is the positive clause written in Lean: the binary scalar comparison distributes s into the first factor and 1 into the second, in accordance with the pure-tensor formula of `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, and the two factors are then compared by B_{n+1} and b. (uses `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`)
5. For each n\geq0 impose the negative clause (B_{-(n+1)}).hom=d_{\alpha,P^{\otimes(n+1)}} followed by precomposition with B_{n+1}^{-1}. The first morphism is scalarExtensionDualMap from `def-sp-canonical-scalar-extension-maps`, whose target is \mathcal Hom_S(E_\alpha(P^{\otimes(n+1)}),S); it is defined for arbitrary module sheaves and has the slice evaluation (s\otimes\varphi)(t\otimes x)=st\alpha(\varphi(x)) by `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-evaluation`. Applying homObjMap to B_{n+1}^{-1} and the identity of S, using the contravariant first argument of the sheaf Hom [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], gives a morphism to \mathcal Hom_S(Q^{\otimes(n+1)},S)=Q^{[-(n+1)]}. Thus this is precisely the required equality of morphisms in negative degree. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], `def-sp-canonical-scalar-extension-maps`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-evaluation`)
6. Define SignedLineComparisonNormalized to be the conjunction of these degree-zero, degree-one, positive-recursion and negative-dual clauses. Each clause is a proposition about the supplied family B, so their conjunction is a predicate with exactly the stated normalization conditions. The definition makes no assertion that such a family exists.
