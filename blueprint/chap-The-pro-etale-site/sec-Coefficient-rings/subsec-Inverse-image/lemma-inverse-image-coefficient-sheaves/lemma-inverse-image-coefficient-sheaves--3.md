This integral finite-extension assertion is bypassed by the Lean route;
the rational assertion in [lemma-inverse-image-coefficient-sheaves--4] is
proved directly from a basis of the coefficient field extension.

For a finite extension $E\subset E'$, the sheaf $\mathcal O_{E',X}$ is finite free over $\mathcal O_{E,X}$.
