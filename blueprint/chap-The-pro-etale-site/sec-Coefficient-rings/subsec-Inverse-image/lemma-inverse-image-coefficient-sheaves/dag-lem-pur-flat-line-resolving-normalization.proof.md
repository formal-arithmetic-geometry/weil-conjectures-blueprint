1. Work in the sheaf-of-modules category `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`, where the tensor is the sheafified sectionwise tensor of [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] and has the symmetric monoidal structure `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`. The hypothesis hL is the exactness/flatness condition of `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`; the right-tensor functor has the required zero-morphism behavior by `lem-route-site-sheaf-tensor-right-preserves-zero-morphisms`. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product], `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`)
2. Apply the Lean theorem `FlatSingleDerivedNormalization.flat_single_derived_normalization A L hL`. It returns hK, saying that the single complex L$[0]$ is K-flat in the sense of `def-pc-kflat-complex`, and hF, saying that every term of L$[0]$ is flat in the sense of `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`. It also returns the finite-limit and finite-colimit preservation witnesses for `tensorRight L`, together with the fixed-factor derived comparison package supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-kflat-fixed-tensor-triangulated`. The package uses the existing total tensor and resolving representative comparison of `def-sf-ringed-kflat-tensor-representative-data`, built from the tensor construction above. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product], `lem-pur-flat-single-derived-normalization`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`)
3. Install the returned finite-limit and finite-colimit proofs as local instances and unpack the returned package. In particular, obtain `chi : DerivedCategory.Q ⋙ derivedLine A L ≅ (KFlatFixedTensor.totalTensor A).flip.obj L$[0]$ ⋙ DerivedCategory.Q` and the normalization equation `hn`: on every K-flat, flat-term representative E, `chi.app E` is the inverse of `KFlatFixedTensor.representativeIso A E L$[0]$`. The definitions of `derivedLine` and the required resolving normalization are the ones recorded in [dag-def-pur-signed-line-derived-action-data]. (uses `lem-pur-flat-single-derived-normalization`)
4. Define `c` exactly as in Lean by `chi ≪≫ Functor.isoWhiskerRight (collapse A L) DerivedCategory.Q`, where `collapse A L` is the canonical total-complex single-factor collapse followed by the comparison from total tensor to `(siteSheafTensorRight L).mapHomologicalComplex (.up ℤ)`. Thus c has the target required in the statement, using the sheaf tensor product [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] and the existing representative/total-tensor data `def-sf-ringed-kflat-tensor-representative-data`. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product], `lem-pur-flat-single-derived-normalization`)
5. To prove `ResolvingNormalization A L hK hF c`, fix E with hE and hf. Unfold c and the whiskering maps, reassociate with `Category.assoc`, and use `← Functor.map_comp`, `Iso.hom_inv_id_app`, `CategoryTheory.Functor.map_id`, and `Category.comp_id`. The composite `c.hom.app E ≫ DerivedCategory.Q.map ((collapse A L).inv.app E)` therefore reduces to `chi.hom.app E`. Finally apply `congrArg Iso.hom` to the equality `hn E hE hf` and compose with the identity equality; this gives exactly the required HEq with `(KFlatFixedTensor.representativeIso A E L$[0]$ hE hK hf hF).inv`. Hence the witnesses hK, hF, and c establish the theorem. (uses `lem-pur-flat-single-derived-normalization`)
