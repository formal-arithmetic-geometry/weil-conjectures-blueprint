1. Use spIntegralTwistLevel_comparison from the native twist definition to identify the underlying native level with change of rings of the recorded roots module along the constant quotient coefficient map. This is precisely the finite-level tensor comparison already stated in the library. (uses [dag-sk-def-sp-tate-twist])
2. Use the proved purTwistIntegralBasis theorem of lem-pur-twist-integral-basis. The equality spIntegers_eq identifies that integral ring with the native integer ring. Base change of its finite basis along Z_ell -> Z_ell/(ell^(n+1)) identifies the integer quotient with a finite free module over this scalar quotient. PadicInt.ker_toZModPow and quotientKerEquivOfSurjective identify the scalar quotient with ZMod (ell^(n+1)). The quotient tensor equivalence and the integer-ring equality transport this basis to the native quotient. Any additive equivalence of modules over ZMod is automatically linear, so the basis works for the unique map in the native comparison; lift universes. (uses [dag-lem-pur-twist-integral-basis], [dag-sk-def-sp-tate-twist])
3. Apply constantFiniteFree_sectionBasis to the two finite coefficient rings. Its natural sectionwise bases feed the proved sheafifiedTensorIso helper of lem-pur-constant-finite-free-power-iso. This identifies the underlying scalar extension with a finite product of the original root sheaf. The fully faithful inclusion of sheaves into presheaves and the limit-preserving forgetful functor to sets give the same finite product in set sheaves. (uses [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf], `lem-pur-constant-finite-free-section-basis`, `lem-pur-constant-finite-free-power-iso`)
4. For N=ell^(n+1), the residue-field unit hypothesis implies N is a unit in global sections by the local-ring residue criterion and the germ criterion for units. Set R=Gamma(Y,O_Y). Pull back affineRootsOver R N along Y -> Spec R. Monicity of X^N-1 makes its adjoin-root algebra finite. AdjoinRoot.etale_X_pow_sub_C makes it etale since N is invertible. These properties survive base change. The base-change clause of affineRoots_representability gives natural bijections between maps into this pullback and Nth roots in the units of the test scheme. Compose with the canonical section equivalence of the pro-etale root kernel; compatibility with its inclusion into units proves naturality and hence the lifted Yoneda isomorphism. (uses [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf], `lem-asd-affine-roots-representability`)
5. Build a limiting finite fan of copies of this pro-etale object by induction. The empty product is the identity of Y; the successor is a binary fibre product. The forgetful functor to schemes over Y preserves binary products, so the structure morphism of the product is an isomorphism followed by a base change and a finite etale morphism. It is finite etale. Lifted Yoneda preserves products. Compose its product representation with the inverse finite-basis comparison to obtain the asserted representative, also on the empty base. (uses [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf], `lem-asd-affine-roots-representability`)
