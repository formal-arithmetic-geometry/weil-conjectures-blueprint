For the sheaf module categories and signed powers [dag-def-pur-signed-line-pullback-normalization-data], define F to be the specified module pullback. Define signed isomorphism maps by positive tensor powers and precomposition with inverse powers for negative indices. Define D_L as the published unbounded derived tensor with L$[0]$, and collapse as the total-complex single-factor comparison. ResolvingNormalization requires the comparison Q D_L ≅ (- tensor L) Q to agree with representativeIso followed by collapse on every K-flat representative. DerivedActionData consists of these comparisons on both sites and an isomorphism D_L D(F) ≅ D(F) D_M. DerivedAction requires flatness and the specified resolving normalizations, the termwise tensor comparison square, and uniqueness. DerivedLineConstraints specifies ordinary unit, positive reassociation and dual evaluation on all pure sections and specifies their derived lifts after Q. DerivedFamilyCoherence requires the same derived action family to commute with those three constraints on all derived objects. IsoNaturality and ScalarNaturality are the commuting signed-power squares for compatible line isomorphisms and arbitrary compatible extensions of coefficient rings. All definitions are the explicit formulas of the Lean attachment; no existence witness is used as definition data. DerivedActionData is represented by a structure with fst the source localization comparison and snd the pair of target comparison and derived action; these are exactly the preceding three pieces of data. LocalizedLineConstraints and LocalizedLineFamily are generic one-constructor propositions containing exactly the original witnesses and equations; DerivedLineConstraints and DerivedFamilyCoherence specialize them to the same sheaf-module functors. DerivedActionSquare and DerivedActionUnique name exactly the termwise square and uniqueness clauses used in DerivedAction.
