Successive quotient reductions compose to the lower reduction by the quotient factor formula [def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map, sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients]. Each coefficient reduction is epimorphic: on profinite tests, choose representatives of each discrete quotient value; the resulting map from the discrete quotient to the valuation ring is continuous, and composition lifts every continuous quotient-valued section. The left adjoint p_X^* preserves this epimorphism `sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`. Cancel the source reduction to obtain commutation of j_n with successive coefficient reductions. If another map has those formulas, limit hom_ext identifies it with this lift. This is the categorical universal property of the specified inverse limit [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit], without passage through sectionwise module equivalences.
