For a structural ring map a:R→U_*S and an R-module sheaf M, InverseImageAction is an S-action on the actual abelian sheaf inverse image of M, with semilinear restrictions and with eta(rm)=a(r)eta(m). It gives an S-module sheaf by retaining that same additive sheaf and those operations. Its underlying abelian sheaf comparison with the constructed abelian inverse image is the identity.
