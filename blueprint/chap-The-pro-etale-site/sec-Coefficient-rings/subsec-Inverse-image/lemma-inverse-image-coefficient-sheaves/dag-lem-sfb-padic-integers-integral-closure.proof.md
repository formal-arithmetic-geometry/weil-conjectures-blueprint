1. Write O = coefficientExtensionIntegers l E and T = coefficientExtensionIntegers l F. Applying extension_integers_dvr to the spectral norms, using Mathlib spectralNorm_extends, gives the membership tests hmemE : a ∈ O ↔ spectralNorm ℚ_$[l]$ E a ≤ 1 and hmemF : b ∈ T ↔ spectralNorm ℚ_$[l]$ F b ≤ 1; the same result gives that T has F as its fraction field and is a principal ideal ring. The norm compatibility supplied by `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-absolute-value` justifies these identifications. For the algebra embedding i, unfolding spectralNorm and applying Mathlib minpoly.algHom_eq (with i.injective) gives spectralNorm ℚ_$[l]$ F (i a) = spectralNorm ℚ_$[l]$ E a for every a ∈ E. (uses `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-absolute-value`)
2. Suppose x ∈ T. Let m = minpoly ℚ_$[l]$ x; Algebra.IsIntegral.isIntegral and Mathlib minpoly.monic make m monic, while hmemF gives spectralNorm ℚ_$[l]$ F x ≤ 1. Mathlib spectralValue_le_one_iff, namely [dag-lem-sfb-monic-spectral-value-coefficients], therefore bounds every coefficient of m by one. Map m to p = m.map (algebraMap ℚ_$[l]$ E). By Mathlib spectralNorm_extends and hmemE, every coefficient of p lifts through O.subtype. Polynomial.lifts_iff_coeff_lifts and Polynomial.lifts_and_degree_eq_and_monic then produce a monic q over O with q.map O.subtype = p. Using Polynomial.eval₂_map, the identity i.toRingHom.comp (algebraMap ℚ_$[l]$ E) = algebraMap ℚ_$[l]$ F from i.commutes, and Mathlib minpoly.aeval, the polynomial q evaluates to zero at x. Hence IsIntegral O x, which is exactly membership in the integral closure by `def-weil-pbc-integral-closure`. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-monic-spectral-value-coefficients], `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-absolute-value`)
3. Conversely, suppose x is in the integral closure of O in F. The DVR description `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr` gives that T is a principal ideal ring with fraction field F; `lem-weil-pbc-pid-noetherian-normal` supplies its Noetherian normal, hence integrally closed, structure. Mathlib isIntegrallyClosed and isIntegrallyClosed_iff_isIntegrallyClosedIn F therefore show that T is integrally closed in F. To apply Mathlib Subring.integralClosure_le_iff, check that the image of O lies in T: for a ∈ O, hmemE gives spectralNorm ℚ_$[l]$ E a ≤ 1, the equality from step 1 transfers this to spectralNorm ℚ_$[l]$ F (i a) ≤ 1, and hmemF gives i a ∈ T. Thus the integral closure of O in F is contained in T, so x ∈ T. Together with the forward implication this proves the stated equivalence. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-pid-noetherian-normal`)
