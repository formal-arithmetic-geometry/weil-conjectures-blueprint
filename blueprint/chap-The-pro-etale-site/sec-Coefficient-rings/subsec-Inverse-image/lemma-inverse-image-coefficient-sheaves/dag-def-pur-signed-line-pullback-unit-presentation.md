For a continuous functor U:C→D, set inverse image L and its adjunction unit eta, a unit presentation of a sheaf T on D by a sheaf S on C is a morphism j:S→U_*T and an isomorphism i:T≅L(S) such that i(j(x))=eta(x) on every section. The canonical presentation of L(S) has unit eta and identity isomorphism.
