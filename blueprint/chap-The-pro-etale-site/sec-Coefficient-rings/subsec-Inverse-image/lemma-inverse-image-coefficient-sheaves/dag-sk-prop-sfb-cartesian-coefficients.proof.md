1. Fix the coefficient presentations used by the Lean predicates. Finite rings use the discrete continuous-function condensed rings, the adic rings use the condensed models of $\mathbf Z_\ell$, $\mathbf Q_\ell$, and $\mathcal O_E$, and a finite subfield $E$ uses its subfield condensed ring: `def-sfb-padic-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-int-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-integers-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-subfield-coefficient-ring`. The algebraic-closure coefficient is the finite-subextension-valued ring `sk-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed`, with its inclusions `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map` and maps into the algebraic closure `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`. All coefficient changes are pulled back from the point as in `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, `def-sfb-subfield-coefficient-map`, and the integral-to-field maps `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield`. These are exactly the coefficient objects quantified in `CartesianUntwistedAssertions` and the later twist records. (uses `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`, `def-sfb-padic-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-int-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-integers-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`, `def-sfb-subfield-coefficient-map`, `def-sfb-subfield-coefficient-ring`, `sk-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed`)
2. For any one of these condensed commutative rings $\Lambda$, the coefficient map $\alpha_f:f^{-1}\Lambda_Y\to\Lambda_X$ is the point-pullback comparison `def-sfb-alpha` and the canonical coefficient morphism [dag-sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms]. It is a ring-object morphism by [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom] and an isomorphism by [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-iso]. Thus the `inferInstance` in `cartesianCoefficientProperty_proof` supplies the first conjunct `IsIso (condensedPullbackHom \Lambda f)` for every coefficient in the list. (uses `def-sfb-alpha`, `lem-sfb-alpha-iso`, `lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom`, `sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms`)
3. The underlying pro-etale inverse image, its direct image and their adjunction are the constructions of `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`, [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], and `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`; their lax/strong monoidal structures are `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal` and `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-braided`. The presheaf tensor and internal-hom model is `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`, while the sheaf tensor used by modules is `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`. The ambient condensed $E$-module categories are fixed by `def-weil-pbc-condensed-e-module-category`, and their local-presentability/finite-limit infrastructure is supplied by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits]. These are the instances used by Lean for `CondensedRing.upperStar`. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-weil-pbc-condensed-e-module-category`, `def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations`, `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`, `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`, `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-braided`, `sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback`, `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`)
4. The exactness instances used by the Lean proof are `PreservesFiniteLimits (CondensedRing.upperStar \Lambda f)` and `PreservesFiniteColimits (CondensedRing.upperStar \Lambda f)`, supplied by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. Module actions are transported by `def-sfb-modobj-transport` and the module pullback is defined by `def-sfb-module-upper-star`. Extension and restriction of scalars, with their adjunction, are `def-sfb-modobj-extension`, `def-sfb-modobj-restriction`, `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction`, and `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`. 5. The displayed lift is literally `(upperStar AddCommGrpCat f).mapMod (condensedRingObj \Lambda Y) \mathbin{\triangleright} Mod.comap (inv (condensedPullbackHom \Lambda f))`. By `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`, and `def-sfb-modobj-restriction`, both `mapMod` and restriction leave the underlying objects and arrows unchanged; therefore the final equality in `CartesianCoefficientProperty` is proved by `rfl`. The natural comparison with the ordinary module upper star is the scalar-extension/restriction comparison from `def-sfb-modobj-extension-along-iso`, and the whole module pullback construction is the one specified in `def-sfb-module-upper-star` and `sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback`. This proves every untwisted coefficient clause, without any invertibility hypothesis on $\ell$. (uses `def-sfb-modobj-extension-along-iso`, `def-sfb-modobj-restriction`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `def-sfb-module-upper-star`, `sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`)
6. Now assume the twist-domain hypotheses that $\ell$ is invertible on both schemes. The roots sheaves and their inverse-image map are the constructions [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf] and `def-pur-roots-of-unity-inverse-image`. Lean's `spRoots_cartesian f \ell n` supplies `IsIso` for every $n$. For a finite ring $A$, `spFiniteTwists_cartesian f \ell (ULift A)` supplies the witness family of `SpSignedComparison`s required by `CartesianFiniteTwistAssertions`. The finite line and pure tensor are those of `def-pur-finite-tate-line-signed-comparison`; the signed zero, positive-tensor, negative-evaluation and derived fields are exactly the interface `def-pur-signed-comparison-interface`, with negative degree expressed using [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom]. The finite coefficient/tower data are the strict operations `def-cbc-strict-constant-tower-operations`, the scalar/tensor coherence `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data` and `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, and the completed/discrete twist constructions [dag-def-pur-twist-coefficient-rings] and [dag-def-pur-twist-sheaves]. The local-freeness and all-integer twist interpretation is `det-def-sp-tate-twist-locally-free`, while the packaged inverse-image statement is [dag-det-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients-twists]. Thus the Lean branch `exact fun A _ _ ↦ spFiniteTwists_cartesian ...` proves the full finite-twist conjunct. (uses [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf], [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], [dag-def-pur-twist-coefficient-rings], [dag-def-pur-twist-sheaves], `def-cbc-strict-constant-tower-operations`, `def-pur-finite-tate-line-signed-comparison`, `def-pur-roots-of-unity-inverse-image`, `def-pur-signed-comparison-interface`, `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, `det-def-sp-tate-twist-locally-free`, `det-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients-twists`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`)

For the adic part, Lean destructs `spTwists_cartesian f \ell` as `\langle \rho, \kappa, h\rho, h\kappa, h_E, h_Q, h_I, h_R, h_U \rangle` and inserts these fields directly into `CartesianAdicComparison`: $\rho$ supplies the rational maps, $\kappa$ the $\Qlb$ map, $h_E$ the integral/rational scalar equation, $h_Q$ the finite-subextension coprojection equation, $h_I$ the two internal-cartesian clauses, $h_R$ the algebraic-closure clause, and $h_U$ the inclusion and root-transition equations. The required normalizations are the unique finite reductions and integral maps [dag-lem-sp-ell-quotient-finite-discrete], [dag-lem-sp-ell-point-reduction-ring-map], [dag-lem-route-sp-ell-map-exists-unique], [dag-lem-route-sp-integers-map-exists-unique], [dag-lem-route-sp-integral-twist-map-exists-unique], and [dag-lem-sp-integral-level-transition-unique]. (uses [dag-lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-comp], [dag-lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-id], [dag-lem-sp-ell-point-reduction-ring-map], [dag-lem-sp-ell-quotient-finite-discrete], `lem-route-sp-ell-map-exists-unique`, `lem-route-sp-integers-map-exists-unique`, `lem-route-sp-integral-twist-map-exists-unique`, `lem-route-sp-qbar-twist-action-exists-unique`, `lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-exists-unique`, `lem-sp-integral-level-transition-unique`)
8. For finite $E\subseteq F$, apply `cartesianCoefficients_finiteFree \ell h`. Its first component is a nonempty choice of the integral basis, and its second component gives the two basis-isomorphism families. Choose `b`, obtain `e₀` and `e₁`, and specialize both maps to $X$ exactly as in the Lean term `\langle\langle _,\langle e₀ X\rangle\rangle,\langle _,\langle e₁ X\rangle\rangle\rangle`. The resulting finite sums of regular modules are the finite-free objects of `def-amb-finite-locally-free-module` and `def-sfb-finitely-presented-module`; the coefficient maps and the two sheaf modules are the ones in [dag-det-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients-finite-free]. This proves both the $\mathcal O_F/\mathcal O_E$ and $F/E$ clauses for every $X$. (uses `def-amb-finite-locally-free-module`, `def-sfb-finitely-presented-module`, `det-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients-finite-free`)
9. The first five steps give `CartesianUntwistedAssertions \ell f`, the roots, finite, and adic pieces of `CartesianTwistAssertions \ell f`, and the exact inverse-image lift; the seventh gives `CartesianFiniteFreeAssertions \ell X`. Lean closes the theorem by pairing these terms with `refine \langle ?_, ?_, cartesianFiniteFreeAssertions_proof \ell\rangle` and the two preceding conjunction constructors. Therefore the stated conjunction follows, and the module pullback is the exact functor whose underlying sheaf functor is the inverse image, with the canonical scalar action transported through $\alpha_f$.
