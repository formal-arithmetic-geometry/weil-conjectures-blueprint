Let $E\subset E'$ be finite extensions of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ in the fixed
algebraic closure, with the coefficient sheaves of [prose-02-51]. There
is an integer $d\geq 0$, independent of the scheme $X$, such that

$$
E'_X\simeq E_X^{\oplus d}
$$

as $E_X$-modules, where $E_X$ acts on $E'_X$ through the coefficient
inclusion. In particular, $E'_X$ is finite free over $E_X$.
