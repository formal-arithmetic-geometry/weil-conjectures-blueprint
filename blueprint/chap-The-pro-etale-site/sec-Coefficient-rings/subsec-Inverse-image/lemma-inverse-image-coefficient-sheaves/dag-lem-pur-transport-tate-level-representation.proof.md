1. Identify the old sheafification presentation of roots with the kernel presentation, using the sectionwise roots-of-unity additive equivalence and its naturality with the inclusion into units. Sheafification of that presheaf isomorphism and its counit give rootsComparison. The kernel roots sheaf is killed by N. (uses [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf])
2. For any abelian sheaf M killed by N, apply the composite of constant sheafification and tensoring on the right by M to the cokernel Z --N--> Z --> Z/N. Both functors preserve this cokernel and are additive. The unit comparison and left unitor identify the first map with N times the identity of M, which is zero. Its cokernel is M with the identity projection. Uniqueness of cokernels gives residueTensor: (Z/N)_Y tensor M is isomorphic to M. This includes N=1. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison], `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`)
3. Use the proved free integral constant residue comparison, at level n, and forget module structure to identify R tensor C with the continuous residue sheaf, where R is the pulled-back condensed Z_l ring and C is the constant Z/(l^n) abelian sheaf. The continuous coefficient chart comparison and the finite discrete constant ring comparison identify that residue sheaf with C. Associativity of tensor products and residueTensor now give R tensor M = R tensor (C tensor M) = (R tensor C) tensor M = C tensor M = M. Combine with rootsComparison to identify the underlying tateModuleLevel with the kernel roots sheaf. (uses [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison], `lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations`, `lem-weil-pbc-finite-discrete-condensed-ring-constant`, `lem-weil-pbc-finite-discrete-scheme-ring-constant`)
4. The residue-field invertibility hypothesis implies that l is a unit in every stalk and hence in global sections. Thus N=l^n is a unit. Over R0=Gamma(Y,O_Y), take Spec(R0$[t]$/(t^N-1)) and base change to Y. The polynomial is monic, giving finiteness. AdjoinRoot.etale_X_pow_sub_C proves etaleness because N and the constant term 1 are units. Both properties survive base change, so this gives a pro-etale object V.
5. Apply the all-test-schemes affine roots representability theorem after base change. Its natural equivalence sends a map into V to its pulled-back distinguished root. Use the canonical kernel section equivalence to obtain, naturally on pro-etale tests, the lifted Yoneda sheaf of V isomorphic to the set sheaf underlying the roots sheaf. Compose with the inverse of the underlying Tate-level comparison from step 3. The same construction works for n=0 (N=1) and for the empty base. (uses [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf], `lem-asd-affine-roots-representability`)
