Pro-étale affines form a basis, so sheaves on the full site are determined by this basis. Clause [proposition-pro-etale-site-separably-closed--1] identifies the basis and its topology with the profinite site defining condensed sets, using the same set-theoretic cutoff. Their categories of sheaves are therefore equivalent.
