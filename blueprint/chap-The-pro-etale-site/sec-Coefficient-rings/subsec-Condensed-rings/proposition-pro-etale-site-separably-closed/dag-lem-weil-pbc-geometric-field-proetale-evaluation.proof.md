1. Use the specified profinite test-object functor and its finite-limit, finite-cover behavior `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`. Its continuity is supplied by the continuous profinite-to-pro-etale construction `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous`, while the base-change pushforward is restriction along the finite lower-star functor [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star]. The corresponding inverse image is the sheafified left Kan extension [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], left adjoint to that restriction `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. The locally presentable coefficient categories `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, the transfinite-plus construction and its finite-limit control [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], and the orthogonality of the resulting sheafification [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality] provide the size and sheafification infrastructure used by these adjunctions; the finite-upper-star exactness statement [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact] records the resulting finite-limit preservation. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous`, `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`)
2. For an algebraically closed K, the specified relative test functor is the dense subsite [dag-lem-weil-pbc-geometric-field-profinite-dense-subsite]. The affine/profinite classification used there is based on the profinite-to-pro-etale construction `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale` and its weakly-etale finite-limit and transfinite sheafification interfaces [lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous, lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits, lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]. Applying the dense-subsite comparison theorem `lem-pc-fppf-dense-sheaf-equivalence` gives equivalences for restriction of sheaves of sets, abelian groups, and rings. Mathlib's `Functor.IsDenseSubsite` restriction equivalence is instantiated in Lean by `inferInstance`. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, `lem-pc-fppf-dense-sheaf-equivalence`, `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous`, `lem-weil-pbc-geometric-field-profinite-dense-subsite`)
3. The point and abelian-point adjunctions in the node use these restriction functors as their actual right adjoints. Since the restrictions are equivalences, Lean obtains isomorphism instances for both units and counits by `infer_instance`. The singleton profinite object is terminal, and preservation of finite limits by the test functor makes its image terminal; `isIso_of_isTerminal` therefore proves that `geometricFieldTestFinalMap` is an isomorphism. For a ring sheaf R, the restriction with the identity scalar map is an equivalence by the dense-subsite module transport theorem [dag-lem-weil-pbc-dense-subsite-module-restriction], applied after `Functor.IsDenseSubsite.isCoverDense`. Thus the first theorem supplies all three coefficient equivalences, both specified point adjunction equivalences, the final-object identification, and the module restriction equivalence. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-dense-subsite-module-restriction], `lem-weil-pbc-geometric-field-profinite-dense-subsite`, `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`)
4. To prove the covering assertion, write a covering sieve as one generated by a pro-etale precoverage. A member has a nonempty weakly-etale scheme over the unique point of `Spec K`; the algebraically closed weakly-etale section result `lem-fix-weakly-etale-algclosed-section`, used in Lean as `exists_comp_eq_id_of_weaklyEtale`, supplies a section of that member. Downward closure of the sieve then contains the identity. Unpacking a generated presieve gives a member U, a map f : U ⟶ Spec K, and a section s with `s ≫ f = 𝟙`; this is exactly the first conjunct of `geometric_field_proetale_evaluation_exact`. (uses `lem-fix-weakly-etale-algclosed-section`)
5. For abelian evaluation, let T be the final pro-etale object and G any presheaf. The equalizer sieve and image sieve of the sheafification unit at T are covering sieves; the preceding split-cover result forces the identity into each, so the unit is bijective on T-sections. Lean packages this with `isIso_iff_bijective`, `isIso_iff_of_reflects_iso`, and `NatIso.isIso_of_isIso_app`. Presheaf evaluation preserves colimits, and `preservesColimitsOfShape_of_natIso` together with `sheafificationAdjunction.preservesColimitsOfShape_of_comp` transfers that preservation through sheafification. Finite limits are obtained by changing evaluation to `sheafToPresheaf _ _ ⋙ evaluation` and using the pointwise instance. Hence abelian evaluation preserves finite limits and all small colimits, in particular cokernels. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], `lem-fix-weakly-etale-algclosed-section`)
6. For a ring sheaf R and a diagram shape J, forget the evaluated R-module to abelian groups. The underlying functor is the abelian evaluation just shown to preserve J-colimits, while the forgetful functor from modules over R(T) reflects colimits; Lean applies `preservesColimitsOfShape_of_reflects_of_preserves`. Taking J to be `WalkingParallelPair` gives cokernels, and taking `Discrete (Fin n)` gives the colimit of n copies of the unit module, including n = 0. This proves the stated module evaluation assertions for arbitrary R, with no extra coefficient hypotheses. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-dense-subsite-module-restriction], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
7. For an algebraically closed extension K → K', use the canonical test-object base-change isomorphism built from the profinite test-object functor `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`. The continuous pushforward-composition isomorphism makes scheme direct image followed by restriction an equivalence; `Functor.isEquivalence_of_iso` and then `Functor.isEquivalence_of_comp_right` make the direct image itself an equivalence. Its adjunction unit is therefore an isomorphism by `infer_instance`. Unfolding the canonical restriction and evaluation comparisons, whiskering this unit with the test-object or final-object comparison, and applying `infer_instance` proves their isomorphism components. The argument is applied separately to `Type`, `Ab`, and `RingCat`, so the ring comparison remains a ring homomorphism. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`)
8. For composition, the canonical inverse-image comparison is the conjugate of the direct-image composition isomorphism. The uniqueness equation for conjugate adjunctions `lem-weil-pbc-conjugate-iso-unit-equation`, instantiated in Lean as `unit_conjugateEquiv_symm`, gives the componentwise comparison identity; naturality of the comparison and of the two adjunction units supplies the displayed composition equation. At the singleton, `geometricField_final_pullback_hom_ext` identifies maps into the pulled-back final object by cancelling the pullback projection. On every profinite test object, the same identity follows by checking the two pullback projections in `geometricFieldTestBaseChange` and `MorphismProperty.Over.pullbackComp`; the test-object functor and its functoriality are those of `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`. This proves the evaluation and restriction coherence clauses. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-conjugate-iso-unit-equation`, `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`)
9. For the identity map, apply the same conjugate-unit equation `lem-weil-pbc-conjugate-iso-unit-equation` to the canonical identity direct-image comparison `Functor.sheafPushforwardContinuousId'`. The triangle identity yields the inverse-image identity comparison; `MorphismProperty.Over.pullbackId` identifies its test-object map. Evaluating the resulting component identity at the singleton proves the evaluation equation, and extending over every profinite object proves the restriction equation. The Lean proof uses `Functor.map_comp`, `r.inv_hom_id_app`, and `Functor.map_id` to reduce both composites to identities. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-conjugate-iso-unit-equation`)
10. Finally, for a finite family of sheaves of sets, base change preserves finite limits; Lean obtains representable flatness with `flat_of_preservesFiniteLimits`, the needed universe-level `AB5OfSize` instance with `AB5OfSize_shrink`, and finite-limit preservation of the inverse-image functor by `infer_instance`. Naturality of `geometricFieldEvaluationComparison` at the operation map a gives the operation equation, and naturality at each product projection `Pi.π F k` gives the product comparison equations. The same argument covers the empty finite family and, by taking the operation to be an action map, arbitrary ring-sheaf module actions; no commutativity, algebraicity, or nonzero-ring assumption is added.
