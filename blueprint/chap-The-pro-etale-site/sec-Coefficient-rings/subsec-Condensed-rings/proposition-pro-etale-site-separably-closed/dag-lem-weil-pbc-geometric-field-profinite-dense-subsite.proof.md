1. For a profinite S and a commutative ring A, prove the affine scalar-extension calculation by defining the projection from a ring map LC(S,A) → B through LocallyConstant.homeomorphPrimeSpectrum. At each prime p of B, the characteristic function of the clopen fibre of a locally constant g is idempotent and becomes 1 in the localization at p; hence localization_eq identifies the image of g with the scalar obtained by evaluating g at that projection. The maps fromIntegers and toIntegers are inverse by PrimeSpectrum.eq_of_forall_algebraMap_localization_eq, so IsPushout.of_isColimit gives the pushout of LC(S,Z) and A over ULift Z. The same-universe filtered-colimit flatness input `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe` and the weak-etale constant-function map `lem-sfb-lc-weakly-etale` provide the size and site instances used here. By `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, this pushout is the base-change ring for the specified relative test functor, and isPullback_SpecMap_of_isPushout gives the explicit pullback isomorphism lcIso with both projection equations. The prime-spectrum description [dag-lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-prime-spectrum] supplies the point calculation used throughout. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-prime-spectrum], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, `lem-sfb-lc-weakly-etale`, `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`)

3. Let B be a weakly etale K-algebra with K separably closed. From Algebra.WeaklyEtale.flat_lmul' obtain Ring.AbsolutelyFlat B. For a prime p, the localization Localization.AtPrime p is a field; localization preserves weak etaleness (the localization weak-etale fact `lem-weil-pbc-localization-ring-weakly-etale`), and transitivity gives weak etaleness over K. The field-extension result `lem-weil-pbc-weakly-etale-field-separable` makes this localization algebraic and separable over K, so IsSepClosed.algebraMap_bijective makes K → Localization.AtPrime p bijective. The comparison with the localization at p.comap (algebraMap K B), using IsLocalization.atUnits and the scalar-tower identity, proves Algebra.BijectiveOnStalks K B. This is the local-ring part of the classification and uses the weak-etale algebra notion of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], `lem-weil-pbc-localization-ring-weakly-etale`, `lem-weil-pbc-weakly-etale-field-separable`)
4. The clopen basis and total separation criterion give TotallySeparatedSpace (PrimeSpectrum B). The map φ := LocallyConstant.liftPrimeSpectrum (algebraMap K B) ⟨id, continuous_id⟩ is bijective on spectra by LocallyConstant.comap_liftPrimeSpectrum and LocallyConstant.toPrimeSpectrum_surjective. 5. For an affine pro-etale object U over Spec K, set R = Γ(U,top) and let φ : K → R be Spec.preimage of U.left.isoSpec.inv ≫ U.hom, with its induced Algebra K R. The resulting composite of U.isoSpec, Spec.map of this algebra equivalence, and lcIso is an isomorphism U ≅ test(S) over Spec K; the over-equation is checked by e.commutes and the pullback second-projection equation. For each test object, proAffineEtale is proved by proAffineEtale_Spec_iff: LC(S,K) is ind-etale over K, with the required filtered presentation and flatness supplied by the ind-etale algebra conventions [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and the same-universe colimit-flatness result `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. The affine classification therefore factors every member of the affine ind-etale basis [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] through a test object. Since the affine basis is cover-dense in the pro-etale site, this proves testCoverDense, hence cover density of the functor described in `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`. 7. To reflect coverings, pull a covering sieve on a test object back along its presentation in the affine ind-etale basis [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. The finite jointly-surjective refinement supplied by Scheme.AffineProEt.exists_finite_jointly_surjective_of_mem_topology has arrows factoring through images of members of the original sieve. 8. Conversely, start with a coherent covering sieve and choose its finite effective-epi family. The criterion Profinite.effectiveEpiFamily_tfae turns this into a jointly surjective family of profinite maps; after replacing the index by ULift, `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor` supplies the fpqc cover of the corresponding relative schemes. The weak-etale part of the cover follows from the test-object functor's structure maps, with the constant-function base case supplied by `lem-sfb-lc-weakly-etale`, and the weak-etale/ind-etale terminology is that of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. Generating the pro-etale topology from this precoverage proves preservation of covering sieves. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`, `lem-sfb-lc-weakly-etale`)
9. The final theorem assembles the Full and Faithful result, testCoverDense, and the two sieve implications as the field-specific IsDenseSubsite record. Thus the specified relative profinite test functor is cover dense and locally full and faithful, and its pushforward identifies coherent covering sieves with pro-etale covering sieves, including the empty-object and empty-family cases handled by the effective-epi criteria.
