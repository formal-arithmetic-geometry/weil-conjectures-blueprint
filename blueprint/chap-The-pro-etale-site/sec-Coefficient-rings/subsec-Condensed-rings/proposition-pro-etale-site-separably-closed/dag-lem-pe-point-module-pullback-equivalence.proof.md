1. Let Λ be the assumed commutative condensed ring, viewed as a commutative monoid object in the sheaf category by the ring-object conventions `sk-def-sfb-ring-object`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object`, and `det-def-sfb-ring-object-rings`; the sheafified tensor product has the required closed symmetric monoidal structure by `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`. The pullback p_X^* and its identification of the coefficient on X with Λ_X are the constructions of `def-sfb-upper-star-from-point`, `det-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`, and `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`. (The coefficient-ring instances, including the standard ℚ̄_ℓ coefficient, are covered by `pur713-def-coefficient-rings` and `def-sfb-qlbar-coefficient-ring`.) Thus the target functor is the module-object transport of p_X^* as in `sk-def-sfb-module-objects`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `det-def-sfb-transport`, and `sk-def-sfb-transport`.
2. For X = Spec Ω with Ω separably closed, the underlying pullback functor p^* = upperStarFromPoint (Spec Ω) is the left adjoint in the composite adjunction adj := pointUpperStarLowerStarAdjunction.comp (upperStarLowerStarAdjunction Ab (toSpecZ (Spec Ω))). The right adjoint is an equivalence by the established point comparison, so the Lean result Adjunction.isEquivalence_left_of_isEquivalence_right gives the underlying equivalence isEquivalence_upperStarFromPoint. Both constituent adjunctions are monoidal: the sheaf part uses isMonoidal_upperStarLowerStarAdjunction, while the point part has the required oplax monoidal structure; Adjunction.isMonoidal_comp therefore makes adj monoidal. Since its left functor is fully faithful and its right functor is fully faithful, Adjunction.unit_isIso_of_L_fully_faithful and Adjunction.counit_isIso_of_R_fully_faithful make the unit and counit of adj isomorphisms.
3. Apply the monoidal adjunction's module construction to Λ. By the module-transport construction `def-sfb-modobj-transport` and its abstract formulation `sk-def-sfb-transport`, adj.mapModAdjunction Λ is an adjunction between Mod_Λ and Mod_{p^*Λ}; its left functor is exactly upperStarFromPointMod Λ (Spec Ω), with p^*Λ = Λ_X as above. For every module M, Mod.forget_map together with Adjunction.mapModAdjunction_unit_app_hom identifies the underlying map of the module unit with adj.unit.app M.X, and Adjunction.mapModAdjunction_counit_app_hom does the same for the counit. The latter underlying maps are isomorphisms by NatIso.isIso_app_of_isIso applied to adj.unit and adj.counit, and the forgetful functor reflects isomorphisms via isIso_of_reflects_iso. Hence the module unit and counit are isomorphisms; adjMod.toEquivalence.isEquivalence_functor proves that upperStarFromPointMod Λ (Spec Ω) is an equivalence, which is the claimed pullback equivalence.
