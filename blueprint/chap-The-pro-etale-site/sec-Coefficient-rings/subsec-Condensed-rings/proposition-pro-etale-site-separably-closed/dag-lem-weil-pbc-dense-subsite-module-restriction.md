Let F:C→D be continuous for Grothendieck topologies J,K. Assume restriction on sheaves of sets and on sheaves of abelian groups is an equivalence and F is K-cover-dense. For every sheaf of rings R on (D,K), restriction with the identity scalar map induces an equivalence from R-module sheaves to module sheaves over the restricted R `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`. Coefficient universe max(u₁,v₁,u₂,v₂,w) accommodates both sites.
