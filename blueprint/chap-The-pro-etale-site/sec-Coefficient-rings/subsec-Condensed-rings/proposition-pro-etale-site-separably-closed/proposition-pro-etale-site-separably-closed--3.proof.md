The condensed ring $\underline R$ has as sections on a profinite $S$ the continuous maps $S\to R$. For discrete $A$, these are locally constant functions, exactly the sections of the classical constant sheaf. For a profinite or locally profinite ring the same definition gives the asserted continuous-function sheaf.
