A finitely presented étale $\Omega$-algebra is a finite product of copies of the separably closed field $\Omega$. Filtered colimits of these algebras correspond under $\operatorname{Spec}$ to inverse limits of finite sets. Faithfully flat covers correspond to jointly surjective families of profinite sets, and quasi-compactness refines these to finite families. This identifies both the categories and their covering families.
