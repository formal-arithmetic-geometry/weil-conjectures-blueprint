1. On the category of profinite sets use `CategoryTheory.coherentTopology`. The Lean facts `CategoryTheory.coherentTopology.mem_sieves_iff_hasEffectiveEpiFamily` and `Profinite.effectiveEpiFamily_tfae` identify its covering sieves with finite effective-epimorphic, hence jointly surjective, families, which is the pro-etale point site in the statement. The abbreviation `Profinite.CondensedRing := Mon (Sheaf (coherentTopology Profinite) Ab)` is therefore the asserted condensed-ring object. At presheaf level the tensor and its unit are objectwise by `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`, and sheafification supplies the symmetric tensor and constant-sheaf unit by `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure`, `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`, and `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`. The ring-object construction used by the Lean declaration is the presheaf construction and its sheafification in `det-def-sfb-ring-object-presheaf`, with the sectionwise ring-object laws of `det-def-sfb-ring-object-rings` and the general ring-object convention of `det-def-sfb-ring-object`. The coefficient-side sheafification infrastructure invokes the locally-presentable and transfinite finite-limit/orthogonality results `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]. (uses `det-def-sfb-ring-object-presheaf`, `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`)
2. For a topological ring `T`, `Profinite.continuousMapRingPresheaf` has value the lifted ring of continuous maps `C(S,T)`, restriction by precomposition, and pointwise ring operations. The theorem `Profinite.isSheaf_continuousMapRingPresheaf` proves the coherent sheaf condition by reducing with `Presheaf.isSheaf_iff_preservesFiniteProducts_and_equalizerCondition`, `Presieve.isSheaf_comp_uliftFunctor_iff`, and `Presheaf.isSheaf_coherent_of_hasPullbacks_of_comp`; compatible maps descend along the quotient maps supplied by `Profinite.effectiveEpi_tfae` and `equalizerCondition_yonedaPresheaf`. Thus `Profinite.continuousMapRingSheaf` has exactly the sections and restrictions in the statement, and `Profinite.continuousMapRingObj` is its underlying abelian sheaf via `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.sheafCompose`.
3. The declaration `Profinite.continuousMapRingObjOfUniverseMonObj` obtains the ring-object structure by `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.monObj_sheafCompose_obj`, transporting the presheaf structure through sheafification. If `T` is commutative, `Profinite.isCommMonObj_continuousMapRingObjOfUniverse` applies `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.isCommMonObj_sheafCompose_obj` and the pointwise equality `mul_comm`; hence the underlying condensed ring is commutative exactly when `T` is. This is the ring-object interpretation specified by `det-def-sfb-ring-object` with the tensor framework already fixed above.
4. For a continuous ring homomorphism `φ : T →+* T'` with proof `hφ : Continuous φ`, `Profinite.continuousMapRingSheafMapOfUniverse` is postcomposition. Its component formula is the theorem `Profinite.continuousMapRingSheafMapOfUniverse_app_apply`, namely `a ↦ φ ∘ a`; `Profinite.isMonHom_continuousMapRingObjMapOfUniverse` uses `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.isMonHom_sheafCompose_map` to make the underlying abelian-sheaf map a morphism of ring objects. The identities `Profinite.continuousMapRingSheafMapOfUniverse_id` and `Profinite.continuousMapRingSheafMapOfUniverse_comp` prove strict identity and composition, so this gives the claimed functorial map construction `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map`.
5. For the fixed prime `ℓ`, the Lean abbreviations `Profinite.padicIntCondensedRing` and `Profinite.padicCondensedRing` are the continuous-map sheaves for `ℤ_[ℓ]` and `ℚ_[ℓ]`. For a field `E` embedded in `PadicAlgCl ℓ`, `Profinite.padicSubfieldCondensedRing` uses the restricted topology on `E`, while `Profinite.padicIntegersCondensedRing` uses `PadicAlgCl.integers ℓ E` with its induced topology. Consequently their sections are respectively `C(S,ℤ_ℓ)`, `C(S,ℚ_ℓ)`, `C(S,E)`, and `C(S,𝒪_E)`, with the pointwise operations above. These coefficient rings are commutative by the commutative continuous-map ring-object theorem, and no finiteness hypothesis on `E` is introduced.
6. Finally, `Profinite.padicIntCondensedRingToPadicCondensedRing`, `Profinite.padicCondensedRingToPadicSubfieldCondensedRing`, `Profinite.padicIntegersCondensedRingToPadicSubfieldCondensedRing`, and `Profinite.padicIntCondensedRingToPadicIntegersCondensedRing` are the corresponding `Profinite.continuousMapRingSheafMap` instances. They are postcomposition with the inclusions `ℤ_ℓ → ℚ_ℓ`, `ℚ_ℓ → E`, `𝒪_E → E`, and `ℤ_ℓ → 𝒪_E`; the latter two involving `ℚ_ℓ` exist under exactly the compatible scalar-tower hypotheses in the statement. The component formula and the composition theorem above show that these are the displayed morphisms and that their composites are postcomposition with the composite inclusions, completing the construction `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map`.
