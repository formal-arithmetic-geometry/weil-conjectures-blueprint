Let $\Omega$ be a separably closed field, and fix a universe containing
$\Omega$. For a profinite set $S$ in this universe, put

$$
(\operatorname{Spec}\Omega)[S]
=\operatorname{Spec}LC(S,\mathbf Z)
\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z}\operatorname{Spec}\Omega,
$$

where $LC$ denotes locally constant functions. The functor
$S\mapsto(\operatorname{Spec}\Omega)[S]$ is an equivalence from the
category $\operatorname{ProFin}$ of profinite sets onto the category of
pro-étale affines over $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$, and it identifies
$\operatorname{ProFin}$ with a dense subsite of $(\operatorname{Spec}\Omega)_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$.
The topology on this subsite is the coherent topology on profinite sets,
generated by finite jointly surjective families, including the empty
cover of the empty set.
