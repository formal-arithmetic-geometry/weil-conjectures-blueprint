2. For the integral comparison, put R = O_E and Q_n = R/(pi_E)^(n+1), with q_n : R →+* Q_n the quotient and t_n : Q_(n+1) →+* Q_n the factor map. The continuous-integral coefficient construction supplies the topological ring and reduction maps (`def-weil-pbc-continuous-integral-coefficients`) and the presentation in `def-sfb-integral-comparison-coefficients`; hπ identifies the powers of (pi_E) with powers of the maximal ideal, so each Q_n is finite and discrete. The maps satisfy t_n(q_(n+1)(x)) = q_n(x), every q_n is surjective by `Ideal.Quotient.mk_surjective`, and the q_n are jointly injective by `IsHausdorff.eq_iff_smodEq` together with adic separatedness. Compactness of R comes from the closed unit ball in the finite p-adic extension. Hence the nested fibres are nonempty and compact, and `IsCompact.nonempty_iInter_of_sequence_nonempty_isCompact_isClosed` gives a compatible lift of every point of the inverse-limit set; joint injectivity gives uniqueness. The Lean construction `completedProfiniteTowerLimit` therefore makes the represented profinite tower a limit. Transporting it through the relative test-object functor, using `def-sfb-profinite-test-objects`, `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`, `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous`, and `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-finite-limits`, gives the corresponding limit of coefficient sheaves. The underlying limit is lifted to a limit of ring objects by `completedRingLimit`; its unit and multiplication are detected by the projections, using the presheaf ring construction `det-def-sfb-ring-object-presheaf`. Finally each finite quotient coefficient is identified with the constant ring sheaf by `completedFiniteIso`, whose naturality is the finite discrete comparison `lem-weil-pbc-finite-discrete-scheme-ring-constant` together with the constant-sheaf pullback comparison `def-sfb-constant-sheaf-scheme-pullback-comparison`. This is the first displayed isomorphism with exactly the reduction maps in the statement. (uses `def-sfb-constant-sheaf-scheme-pullback-comparison`, `def-sfb-integral-comparison-coefficients`, `def-sfb-profinite-test-objects`, `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, `def-weil-pbc-continuous-integral-coefficients`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-presheaf`, `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous`, `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-finite-limits`, `lem-weil-pbc-finite-discrete-scheme-ring-constant`, `lem-weil-pbc-profinite-test-object-functor`)
3. For the rational comparison, the integral inclusion is the coefficient map of `def-sfb-coefficient-map` associated to the continuous inclusion in `def-weil-pbc-continuous-integral-coefficients`. The Lean proof first transports the point localization cocone through the coefficient functor and its module transport: `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`, and `sk-def-sfb-coefficient-change` describe the induced module objects, while `def-sfb-modobj-coextension`, `def-sfb-modobj-restrict-coextend-adjunction`, and `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars` provide the restriction/coextension and change-of-scalars structures used by `Mod.comap`. The forgetful functor creates the relevant module limits and tensor-preserved colimits by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, and the closed tensor product is the one in `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`. Applying `completedSequenceUniversal` to the preserved localization colimit gives maps j_n from the integral regular module, with j_0 equal to the integral inclusion and l • j_(n+1) = j_n. Since multiplication by l on the rational coefficient sheaf is an isomorphism (`completedScalarIso`), these maps satisfy the unique module-colimit property: any compatible family b_n factors through one and only one map from the rational module. `completedSequenceNaturality` and `completedRingComparisonNaturality` show that the legs commute with every scheme inverse image. Thus the telescope is the localization ring E_X obtained by inverting l, with its inherited ring structure, proving the second displayed isomorphism. 4. For the algebraic-closure comparison, form the finite-subextension diagram and its cocone at the point, then apply p_X^*. The index of finite intermediate fields is filtered (hence sifted), and the point cocone is the canonical colimit `isColimitPadicAlgClRingObjCocone`. The left-adjoint colimit theorem `lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits` transports this additive colimit along `sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point`. The Lean proof upgrades it to a colimit of ring objects with `siftedRingCoconeIsColimit`: for a candidate ring map, the underlying additive map is forced by the colimit, the unit is checked at the base field, and multiplication is checked after tensoring in either variable; `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor` makes those tensor functors preserve colimits, and the braided ring-object transport is supplied by `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`, and the coefficient-ring definition `def-sfb-qlbar-coefficient-ring`. Therefore the cocone of the E_X and Qbar_X coefficient maps is a colimit in commutative ring objects, which is the third displayed isomorphism.
