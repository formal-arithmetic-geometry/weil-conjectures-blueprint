$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ of $\underline{\mathcal O_E}$, $\underline E$ and

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O_{E,X}\simeq\varprojlim_n(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_X,
\end{aligned}
