1. The local finite-dimensional span condition is equivalent to local containment in a finite subextension. Indeed choose a finite basis of the span on an open neighbourhood. By det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-subextensions its finitely many basis elements lie in one finite field E, and hence so do all their Q_ell-linear combinations. Conversely an E-valued function has span contained in the finite-dimensional space E. Composita of finitely many such fields remain finite; therefore these local conditions are preserved by addition, multiplication and precomposition. To check the specified B_T really is a sheaf, first glue the underlying continuous Omega-valued functions in the continuous-function sheaf of lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations (use its topological-ring sheaf, not its isomorphism clause). For an affine open W of a site object, the covering definition supplies finitely many affine pieces upstairs covering W. On each such quasi-compact piece a finite subcover of local finite-field neighbourhoods gives one finite compositum; the compositum for all pieces contains every value of the glued function on W. Thus the glued function is locally finite on W, and B_T is a subsheaf. Pointwise ring operations give its commutative ring structure. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], `det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-subextensions`, `lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations`)
2. For each finite E, give E the restricted norm topology. The nonarchimedean inequality in lem-weil-l-adic-extension-absolute-value makes balls clopen: if y is outside a ball about x, every point sufficiently close to y is still outside it. Distinct points can thus be separated by clopen balls, so E is totally disconnected. Apply lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations to obtain the ring isomorphism c_(T,E):p_T^*underline(E) ≅ C(|-|,E). Its continuous-ring-map clause says that for E contained in F the chart followed by E→F is the F-chart after the coefficient inclusion. Both rings fit its Type-0 coefficient convention; the site and sheaf universes are those of the statement. (uses `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-absolute-value`, `lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations`)
3. Postcompose c_(T,E) with E→Omega to obtain a ring map j_(T,E):p_T^*underline(E)→B_T. Its image satisfies the local condition by step 1. Step 2 makes these maps a compatible cocone. The last ring-sheaf colimit of sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x gives a unique ring map theta_T:p_T^*C→B_T with theta_T i_(T,E)=j_(T,E). This equation is exactly the required finite-field normalization, rather than merely an unspecified isomorphism. (uses [dag-sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x], `lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations`)
4. Construct the inverse on a section b in B_T(V). By step 1 choose an open cover V_a of V and fields E_a containing all values of b on V_a. Regard b|V_a as an E_a-valued continuous function (E_a has the subspace topology), apply c_(T,E_a)^(-1), and then the colimit inclusion i_(T,E_a). On V_a∩V_b, pass to the finite compositum E_a E_b; the chart naturality in step 2 identifies both sections with the same function b. Thus they agree and glue uniquely in the sheaf p_T^*C. Refining the two covers proves independence of choices. Restricting a cover along a site arrow proves naturality; the same refinement and pointwise operations prove that this inverse is a ring map. Open covers are pro-etale covers, and no overlap is assumed affine. (uses [dag-sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x], [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], `det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-subextensions`, `lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations`)
5. Call the glued map tau_T. On each V_a, theta_T tau_T sends b to b by the defining equation of theta_T. Sheaf separation gives theta_T tau_T=id. On each finite-field colimit leg, tau_T theta_T sends a section to itself, since the cover with one member and that field can be used in step 4. The ring-colimit universal property in sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x therefore gives tau_T theta_T=id. This proves the canonical isomorphism, for arbitrary T including the empty or non-quasi-compact case. (uses [dag-sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x])
6. For h:T′→T let beta_h:B_T→h_*B_T′ precompose functions with V×_T T′→V. The coefficient morphisms eta_h of sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms are natural in the condensed ring, since they are the specified unit followed by pushforward of the canonical comparison. To compare h_*(theta_T′) eta_h and beta_h theta_T, precompose with every i_(T,E). Coefficient naturality changes the first composite to the E-coefficient mate followed by h_*(j_(T′,E)). The chart-scheme naturality theorem identifies this with the E-chart on T followed by precomposition with V×_T T′→V and E→Omega. This is exactly beta_h j_(T,E), the second composite. The ring-colimit universal property gives equality. In particular this uses the actual adjunction mate, not an arbitrary coefficient comparison. (uses [dag-sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x], `sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms`, `lem-weil-pbc-continuous-chart-scheme-naturality`)
