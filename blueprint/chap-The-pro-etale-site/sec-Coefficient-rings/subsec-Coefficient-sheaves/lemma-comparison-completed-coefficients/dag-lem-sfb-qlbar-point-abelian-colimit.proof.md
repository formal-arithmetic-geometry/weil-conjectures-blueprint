1. The coefficient definitions `def-sfb-padic-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-int-condensed-ring`, and `def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring` identify the finite-stage objects with the continuous-map condensed rings used by the Lean construction, while `def-sfb-qlbar-coefficient-ring` identifies the point vertex with the underlying abelian sheaf of the finite-subextension-valued ring. By `sk-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed`, the finite intermediate fields are directed and nonempty, giving the `IsFiltered` instance via `isFiltered_of_directed_le_nonempty`. The transition maps are the strictly functorial maps of `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map`, and the cocone legs and their compatibility are those of `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`; hence the Lean objects `padicSubfieldRingObjFunctor` and `padicAlgClRingObjCocone` are exactly the stated diagram and cocone. (uses `def-sfb-padic-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-int-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-qlbar-coefficient-ring`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`, `sk-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed`)
2. The Lean proof first installs preservation of the relevant filtered colimits with `preservesSmallestFilteredColimits_of_preservesFilteredColimits (forget Ab)` and reflection of colimits along the forgetful functor using `reflectsColimitsOfShape_of_reflectsIsomorphisms`. It then applies `isColimitOfReflects (sheafToPresheaf _ _)`, so it is enough to prove that the underlying presheaf cocone is a colimit.
3. For each profinite set S, `evaluationJointlyReflectsColimits` reduces the presheaf claim to its section at S, and `isColimitOfReflects (forget Ab)` reduces that section to a colimit of types. The criterion `Types.FilteredColimit.isColimitOf'` asks for (i) a representative at some finite stage for every target element and (ii) eventual equality of two representatives.
4. For the representation condition, an element of the target section is, by `sk-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed` and the vertex identification in `def-sfb-qlbar-coefficient-ring`, a lifted continuous map f:S→Q̄_l together with a finite intermediate field E, a witness that f takes values in E, and the finite-valuedness condition. Define the E-valued map pointwise by t↦⟨f(t),h_f(t)⟩; its continuity is `Continuous.subtype_mk` (the Lean term is `f.continuous.subtype_mk _`). Lifting this map with `ULift.up` gives a section at E, and `rfl` verifies that the cocone leg `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar` sends it to the original target element. (uses `def-sfb-qlbar-coefficient-ring`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`, `sk-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed`)
5. For the equality condition, take two E-stage representatives whose images in the target section are equal. The Lean proof chooses the same stage E and the identity transition `𝟙 E`; after unpacking the `ULift`s, applying `congrArg` to the underlying Q̄_l-valued maps gives pointwise equality. Since the inclusion leg is the one from `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`, `ContinuousMap.ext` followed by `Subtype.val_injective` yields equality of the E-valued continuous maps themselves. Substitution then makes the identity transition equality `rfl`, establishing eventual equality (with the transition maps of `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map`). (uses `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`)
6. The two clauses above complete `Types.FilteredColimit.isColimitOf'` at every S. The successive reflection steps—`isColimitOfReflects (forget Ab)`, `evaluationJointlyReflectsColimits`, and `isColimitOfReflects (sheafToPresheaf _ _)`—therefore prove that `padicAlgClRingObjCocone` is a colimit in sheaves of abelian groups. By the explicit universal-property formulation `def-weil-pbc-category-colimit`, this is exactly the asserted colimit of the finite-subextension condensed abelian sheaves.
