All three coefficient sheaves are obtained from the point by inverse image. The identities $p_Y\circ f=p_X$ identify successive inverse images canonically. For the inverse-limit description, use the representing pro-étale scheme from the first clause; its base change represents the corresponding limit over $X$. For localization and the filtered colimit, inverse image commutes with colimits. These identifications are induced by the same coefficient maps, so they preserve the displayed comparisons.
