1. Fix a cocone s on the diagram D in ring objects, with legs s_i : D(i) → S, and forget its ring structure. Let d := hc.desc ((Mon.forget C).mapCocone s). By the underlying colimit universal property `def-weil-pbc-category-colimit`, d is the unique morphism in C satisfying d ∘ c_i = s_i for every i (in Lean's composition notation this is (c.ι.app i).hom ≫ d = (s.ι.app i).hom). We show that d preserves the unit and multiplication.
2. Because I is filtered, `IsFiltered.nonempty` supplies an index i. The unit equations `IsMonHom.one_hom` for the cocone leg c_i and for s_i, together with the factorization equation from step 1 and associativity, give d ∘ η_A = η_S (that is, η_A ≫ d = η_S in Lean notation). Thus d preserves the unit.
3. For each i, compare the two maps from c.pt.X ⊗ c.pt.X to s.pt.X, namely μ_A ≫ d and (d ⊗ d) ≫ μ_S, after precomposition by c_i ⊗ c_i. The multiplication equations `IsMonHom.mul_hom` for c_i and s_i, the factorization equation, and tensor bifunctoriality `tensorHom_comp_tensorHom_assoc` and `Category.assoc` show that these composites are equal. Call this diagonal equality hdiag i; the tensor identities used here are part of `def-sfb-monoidal-category`.
4. For arbitrary i and j, choose the common successor k := `IsFiltered.max i j`, with u := `IsFiltered.leftToMax i j` and v := `IsFiltered.rightToMax i j`. Cocone naturality gives (D.map u).hom ≫ c_k.hom = c_i.hom and (D.map v).hom ≫ c_k.hom = c_j.hom. By `tensorHom_comp_tensorHom`, c_i ⊗ c_j factors as ((D.map u).hom ⊗ (D.map v).hom) followed by c_k ⊗ c_k. Whiskering hdiag k by the first tensor morphism via `congrArg` and reassociating therefore gives the same equality for c_i ⊗ c_j; this is the mixed-leg equality.
5. Use the assumed preservation of the underlying colimit in each tensor variable. First apply `(isColimitOfPreserves (tensorRight c.pt.X) hc).hom_ext` to reduce equality of the two maps out of c.pt.X ⊗ c.pt.X to equality after each c_i ⊗ id. For a fixed i, apply `(isColimitOfPreserves (tensorLeft (D.obj i).X) hc).hom_ext` to reduce further to equality after id ⊗ c_j. Using `tensorHom_def'`, `Functor.const_obj_obj`, and `Category.assoc` for the tensor bifunctor from `def-sfb-monoidal-category`, these successive legs are exactly c_i ⊗ c_j, so the mixed-leg equality proves μ_A ≫ d = (d ⊗ d) ≫ μ_S.
6. The unit equation from step 2 and the multiplication equation from step 5 are precisely the two fields of `IsMonHom`; package d together with this proof as a ring-object morphism `desc s`. Its cocone factorization is proved by `Mon.Hom.ext` and the underlying equations `hc.fac`.
7. If m is any other ring-object morphism with the same factorization equations, `Mon.Hom.ext` reduces equality m = desc s to equality of their underlying morphisms, and `hc.hom_ext` reduces that to the legs. The hypotheses hm and `hc.fac`, combined with `congrArg Mon.Hom.hom` and symmetry, give the required equality on every leg. Hence the packaged cocone is colimiting in ring objects, yielding `Nonempty (IsColimit c)` and the canonical ring-object mediator for every s.
8. In the braided commutative case, map the commutative cocone along the fully faithful forgetful functor `CommMon.forget₂Mon` and apply `Mon.filtered_cocone_isColimit`. Its underlying ordinary ring-object cocone is colimiting by the preceding argument; `isColimitOfReflects (CommMon.forget₂Mon C)` reflects this colimit back to `CommMon C`. This gives the asserted colimit in the full subcategory of commutative ring objects.
