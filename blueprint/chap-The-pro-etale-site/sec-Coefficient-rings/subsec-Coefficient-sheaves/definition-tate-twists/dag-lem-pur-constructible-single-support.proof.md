1. Work in the category of sheaves of modules over the constant ring A_Y and its ordinary derived category, with the conventions of [dag-sk-def-pbc-conventions]. The sheafification infrastructure used below is available on the small site models and their localized sites: `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable` makes Grothendieck abelian coefficient categories locally presentable, so a separator is presentable for a sufficiently large regular cardinal. Choose that cardinal also larger than the set of site arrows. For the resulting sheaf-valued transfinite plus construction, [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality] supplies the sheafification adjunction by unique factorization into sheaves, and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits] supplies preservation of finite limits. Transporting along the small-site equivalences gives the sheafification instances used in the module constructions. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], `sk-def-pbc-conventions`, `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
2. First, pullback of a zero module sheaf is zero. Use the inverse-image and scalar-extension construction of `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image` and the canonical constant-ring comparison proEtaleConstantRingComparison_isIso in `def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations`. The module operations are additive: their construction uses `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-module-inverse-image-underlying` to identify the underlying module inverse image on the model site with abelian sheaf inverse image, giving finite-limit preservation; the module adjunction gives finite-colimit preservation and additivity. Thus Functor.map_isZero applies to the identified-coefficient pullback, and hence to proEtaleConstantModulePullback. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-module-inverse-image-underlying`)
3. Prove zero sheaves constructible using the one-stratum identity partition and the zero constant model, as in `def-weil-proetale-constructible-sheaf`. For a zero sheaf Z, its restriction along the identity immersion is zero by the preceding step. The singleton identity covering is a covering by Precoverage.mem_coverings_of_isIso. The constant presheaf of the zero module is zero, since its identity morphism is zero componentwise (IsZero.iff_id_eq_zero and PresheafOfModules.hom_ext); its module sheafification is zero by Functor.map_isZero. The over-site restriction functor preserves zero morphisms, so the restricted Z is isomorphic to this constant zero model. Its geometric stalks `def-weil-proetale-geometric-stalk` are zero: the defining composite forgets to module presheaves, restricts to pointed neighbourhoods, then takes the module colimit. The first two functors preserve zero morphisms and the last is left adjoint by PresheafOfModules.colimitAdjunction, so Functor.map_isZero applies. ModuleCat.subsingleton_of_isZero then makes each stalk finite. The whole scheme is a locally closed constructible stratum, including when it is empty. (uses `def-weil-proetale-geometric-stalk`)
4. Transport constructible strata along a sheaf isomorphism using module pullback and stalk functor isomorphisms. More precisely, if E is constructible and E is isomorphic to G, keep its stratification and pull the isomorphism back to each stratum by proEtaleConstantRingPullback. Keep the same trivializing cover and constant modules, composing each trivialization with the inverse restricted isomorphism via SheafOfModules.overFunctor. Applying geometricRingModuleStalkFunctor `def-weil-proetale-geometric-stalk` to the pulled-back isomorphism gives a linear equivalence of stalks; Finite.of_injective transports finiteness from the stalks of E to those of G. This verifies both clauses of constructibility for G. (uses `def-weil-proetale-geometric-stalk`)
5. The standard t-structure kills all homology of F$[0]$ outside degree zero. For i < 0 use DerivedCategory.isZero_of_isGE with bound 0, and for i > 0 use DerivedCategory.isZero_of_isLE with bound 0; the single object has the corresponding bounds. Apply the homology functor to e : K ≅ F$[0]$ and use IsZero.of_iso to obtain IsZero H^i(K) for every i ≠ 0. By the definition of cohomological support `def-weil-cohomological-support`, this proves that the support of K is contained in {0}.
6. The single-functor homology comparison DerivedCategory.singleFunctorCompHomologyFunctorIso, composed with the image of e under the zeroth homology functor, gives h0 : H^0(K) ≅ F. Transport the given constructibility of F along h0 inverse. For the other degrees, take Z = kernel(identity F), which is zero by isZero_kernel_of_mono. The zero-sheaf argument makes Z constructible, and its zero-object isomorphism to each vanishing H^i(K) transports constructibility to that homology sheaf. Thus every homology sheaf of K is constructible.
7. The support inclusion implies finiteness by (Set.finite_singleton 0).subset. If F is nonzero, then H^0(K) cannot be zero, since IsZero.of_iso along h0 inverse would make F zero. Hence 0 belongs to the support, and Set.Subset.antisymm with the already proved inclusion gives support equal to {0}. All constructions above allow the empty scheme and the zero coefficient ring.
