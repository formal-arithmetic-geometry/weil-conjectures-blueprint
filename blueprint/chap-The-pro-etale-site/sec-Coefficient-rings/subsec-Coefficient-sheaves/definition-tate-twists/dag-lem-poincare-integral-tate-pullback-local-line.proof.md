1. Apply the local triviality theorem [dag-lem-poincare-integral-tate-local-basis] to R = condensedRingObj (Profinite.continuousMapRingObj Z_l) on X and M = tateModule X l. It gives a pro-etale cover U of X and, for every i, a Nonempty witness eM : (upperStar (U.f i)).mapMod M ≅ Mod.regular (upperStar (U.f i)).obj R. The module-valued pullback and mapMod appearing here are exactly the transport constructions of `sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback`; the upperStar functors have the strong monoidal structure of `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, together with the exactness recorded in [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. (uses [dag-lem-poincare-integral-tate-local-basis], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`)
2. Set V = U.pullback_1 f. For i put g = pullback.snd f (U.f i). The pullback cover is a pro-etale cover of Y, and pullback.condition gives the equality of composites (V.f i) ≫ f = g ≫ (U.f i). Using the pullback composition isomorphisms `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp` (whose construction is the mate of the pushforward composition isomorphism `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp` for the pushforwards [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star]), form e : upperStar f ⋙ upperStar (V.f i) ≅ upperStar (U.f i) ⋙ upperStar g as (upperStarComp (V.f i) f).symm, followed by upperStarCongr (pullback.condition), followed by upperStarComp g (U.f i). (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`)
3. The comparison e is monoidal. For the inverse of each upperStarComp, the Lean proof uses the monoidal upperStar-lowerStar adjunction `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` and its monoidal compatibility `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, composes these adjunctions, and transfers monoidality across the conjugate equivalence. The lax monoidal structures on the lower stars are those of `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, and compatibility of the pushforward composition comparison is `lem-sfb-pushforward-comp-monoidal`. The equality comparison upperStarCongr is monoidal by congruence, so composition of these pieces makes e.hom an instance of NatTrans.IsMonoidal. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `lem-sfb-pushforward-comp-monoidal`)
4. Use the Lean lemma twoStep_regular with F = upperStar (U.f i), G = upperStar g, H = upperStar f, K = upperStar (V.f i), and the monoidal comparison e. Its auxiliary twoStepComparison_linear proves that the component e.app M.X is linear by tensor naturality (NatTrans.IsMonoidal.tensor) and the lax-monoidal composition formula Functor.LaxMonoidal.comp_μ; the component e.app R is an IsMonHom by Functor.monObjObj_comp. Hence Mod.comap along e.app R transports the module isomorphism, and Mod.isoMk gives the required module isomorphism. The input regularization is obtained from eM by (G.mapMod (F.obj R)).mapIso eM followed by G.mapModUnitIso.symm, where mapModUnitIso identifies the image of the regular module with the regular module. Therefore each member of V satisfies exactly the displayed regular-module conclusion for the two raw mapMod pullbacks in the statement `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal` `sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback`. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`)
