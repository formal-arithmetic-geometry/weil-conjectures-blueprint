1. Unpack the hypothesis `hM : IsConstructibleModuleObject R M`. It supplies a finite stratification and, on every stratum, pro-etale local finite-presentation data. In this data the restriction functors are the pullbacks `g^*` of [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], and their module structures are transported by `mapMod` as in `def-sfb-modobj-transport` and `sk-def-sfb-transport`. The symmetric-monoidal compatibility `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-braided` and exactness [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact] are the structural properties of these pullbacks used by the constructibility setup; the local condition is precisely `def-sfb-locally-finitely-presented`, with its finite presentations understood as in `def-sfb-finitely-presented-module`. Thus `hM` is exactly the constructibility witness needed for the one nonzero homology object below. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finitely-presented-module`, `def-sfb-locally-finitely-presented`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-braided`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`)
2. Put `K := (shiftFunctor _ n).obj ((DerivedCategory.singleFunctor _ 0).obj M)`. The shifted-single isomorphism `((DerivedCategory.singleFunctors _).shiftIso n (-n) 0 (by simp)).app M` gives an isomorphism `e : K ≅ (DerivedCategory.singleFunctor _ (-n)).obj M`.
3. Transport the canonical t-structure bounds across `e` with `DerivedCategory.TStructure.t.isGE_of_iso e.symm (-n)` and `DerivedCategory.TStructure.t.isLE_of_iso e.symm (-n)`. Applying `mem_boundedConstructible_iff R K`, choose the interval `[-n,-n]`. If `i < -n`, `DerivedCategory.isZero_of_isGE K (-n) i` makes the `i`-th homology zero; if `-n < i`, `DerivedCategory.isZero_of_isLE K (-n) i` does the same. Hence all homology outside this interval vanishes.
4. For `i = -n`, the composite `((DerivedCategory.homologyFunctor _ (-n)).mapIso e) ≪≫ ((DerivedCategory.singleFunctorCompHomologyFunctorIso _ (-n)).app M)` identifies `H⁻ⁿ(K)` with `M`. Its inverse transports `hM` by the private helper `constructible_of_iso R`, so the homology in the sole allowed degree is constructible.
5. For every `i ≠ -n`, split with `lt_or_gt_of_ne hi`; the corresponding `isZero_of_isGE` or `isZero_of_isLE` result above applies, and the private helper `constructible_of_isZero R` makes that homology constructible. The vanishing interval and these homology constructibility proofs are exactly the two clauses of `mem_boundedConstructible_iff`, proving `boundedConstructible R K`.
