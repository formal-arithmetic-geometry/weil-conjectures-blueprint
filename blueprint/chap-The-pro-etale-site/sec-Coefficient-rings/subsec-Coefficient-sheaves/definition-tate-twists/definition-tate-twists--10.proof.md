The finite twists are rank one locally by clause [definition-tate-twists--2] and scalar extension. A compatible system of roots trivializes the inverse-limit twist on a pro-étale cover, and therefore trivializes $\mathcal O_{E,X}(1)$. Inverting $\ell$ and extending scalars to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ preserve a rank-one free module. Thus every admissible coefficient twist is locally free of rank one.
