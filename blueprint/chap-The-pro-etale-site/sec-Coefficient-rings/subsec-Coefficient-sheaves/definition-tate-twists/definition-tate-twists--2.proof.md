The map $a\mapsto\zeta^a$ kills $N$, so it factors through the presheaf cokernel of multiplication by $N$ on the constant presheaf $\mathbf Z$. The induced map to the presheaf of roots of unity is locally injective and locally surjective. Sheafification therefore makes it an isomorphism. Since sheafification preserves cokernels, the resulting map $\mathbf Z_X\to\mu_{N,X}$ is a cokernel of multiplication by $N$.
