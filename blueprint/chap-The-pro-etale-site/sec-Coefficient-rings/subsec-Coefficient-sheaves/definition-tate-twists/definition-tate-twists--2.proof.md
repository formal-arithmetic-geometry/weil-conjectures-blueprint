Since $\ell$ is invertible on $X$, adjoining a primitive $\ell^n$th root of unity gives an étale covering on which $\mu_{\ell^n,X}$ is cyclic of order $\ell^n$. Sending $1$ to that root identifies it with the constant sheaf $(\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)_X$. Pulling the covering to $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ gives the stated local identification.
