1. Set R_X to the condensed integral coefficient object and use the canonical profinite test objects. The functor from profinite sets to the pro-etale site is the one of `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, and its base change to X and finite-quotient description are `def-sfb-profinite-test-objects`; its continuity is `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous`. The projection to a profinite test set is the map of `def-zl-profinite-test-projection`. The pro-etale pullback is sheafified left Kan extension [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], its restriction pushforward is [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], and their comparison is the adjunction `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`; finite-limit preservation and exactness are [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. On affine weakly contractible objects, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-point-components-maps` and `lem-weil-pbc-point-lan-affine-evaluation` identify these Kan-extension sections with continuous functions on the profinite component quotient, while `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-affine-wcontractible-covering-family` and `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-sheafification-wcontractible-sections` allow the sectionwise calculations to be reflected back to the sheaf. Thus the continuous-function realization and all its reduction maps can be checked on the indicated site objects. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-sfb-profinite-test-objects`, `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, `def-zl-profinite-test-projection`, `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous`, `lem-weil-pbc-point-lan-affine-evaluation`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-affine-wcontractible-covering-family`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-point-components-maps`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-sheafification-wcontractible-sections`)
2. For every site object U and n, the map of continuous functions induced by PadicInt.toZModPow n is surjective. If f:U→ZMod (l^n) is continuous, lift it by composing f with the continuous integer-representative map z↦(z.val:Z_l); the identity `map_natCast (PadicInt.toZModPow n)` together with `ZMod.natCast_zmod_val` gives the required lift. If a continuous integral function f has zero n-th residue, then `ker_toZModPow` puts each value in the span of l^n; `norm_le_pow_iff_mem_span_pow` and the p-adic norm facts from `def-weil-l-adic-numbers` show that f/l^n is again Z_l-valued. Division by the fixed nonzero scalar is continuous, and subtype extensionality gives f=l^n·g. Hence the residue map is sectionwise surjective with kernel the image of multiplication by l^n. (uses `def-weil-l-adic-numbers`)
3. The preceding sectionwise surjectivity and kernel computation prove that the continuous residue map is the cokernel of multiplication by l^n. In Lean this is `residue_nsmul_condition`, followed by `residueCokernel`: `sheafToPresheaf` is used with `map_injective`, evaluation on every U, `AddCommGrpCat.epi_iff_surjective`, and `ShortComplex.ab_exact_iff`; the resulting exact short complex has a colimit cokernel by `hS.gIsCokernel`. The canonical condensed-to-continuous coefficient identification is the ring-sheaf isomorphism supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-condensed-field-continuous-realization`; the locally constant prime-spectrum description [dag-lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-prime-spectrum] is what identifies the profinite test evaluations used in that comparison. The objectwise tensor and module calculations are legitimate in the presheaf monoidal structure of `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-prime-spectrum], `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`, `lem-weil-pbc-condensed-field-continuous-realization`)
4. Compose the continuous residue map with `chartComparison` to obtain the actual coefficient projection and regard it as an R_X-module map. The equation that l^n times this projection is zero is `residueModule_condition`; after applying `Mod.forget`, it reduces to the already proved residue equation. The forgetful functor is faithful by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful` and creates the relevant limits and colimits by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, so its reflected cokernel is computed on underlying sheaves. In `residueModuleCokernel`, compare the parallel pairs for R_X and the continuous integral sheaf using the isomorphism `asIso (chartComparison X Z_l)`, transport the continuous cokernel, and obtain the actual linear cokernel with projection `tateResidueModuleProjection`. (uses `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful`)
5. In Ab, the map `intResidue (l^n)` from the integral tensor unit to ULift(ZMod (l^n)) is the cokernel of multiplication by l^n: `ZMod.intCast_surjective` proves epimorphy, and `ZMod.intCast_zmod_eq_zero_iff_dvd` identifies its kernel, so `ShortComplex.ab_exact_iff` and `gIsCokernel` give `intResidueCokernel`. Apply the constant-sheaf functor and the free module functor to this cokernel. The free functor is a left adjoint, hence preserves this colimit; its unit comparison, together with `Mod.isoMk` and the monoidal unit comparison, is `freeConstantUnitIso`. The mapped cokernel and the actual module cokernel from the preceding step are therefore compared by `CokernelCofork.mapIsoOfIsColimit`, producing an isomorphism `freeConstantResidueIso` at every n. This is exactly the free-module/regular-module comparison and uses the presheaf tensor structure of `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`. (uses `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`)
6. For successive levels, `integralResidueAbTransition` is induced by `ZMod.castHom`, and `intResidue_transition` proves that it carries the integer projection at l^(n+1) to the one at l^n. The defining projection identity `freeConstantResidueIso_hom` identifies each universal cokernel map with `tateResidueModuleProjection`. Since the integer projection is epi (`Cofork.IsColimit.epi`), cancel it; functoriality of the free functor, the projection identity, and `tateResidueCoefficientMap_compat` then prove commutativity of the transition square, with `Mod.hom_ext` supplying equality of module morphisms. Thus the levelwise isomorphisms form a natural transformation by `NatTrans.ofOpSequence`.
7. Every component of this natural transformation is an isomorphism because it is `freeConstantResidueIso X l n`; `NatIso.isIso_of_isIso_app` upgrades the componentwise isomorphisms to an isomorphism of diagrams. The construction is uniform for all n∈N, including n=0 (where l^0=1 and ZMod 1 is the zero group), and no step assumes that X is nonempty, invertible, or finite. Hence the constant residue system followed by `Mod.freeFunctor R_X` is naturally isomorphic to the actual residue module system.
