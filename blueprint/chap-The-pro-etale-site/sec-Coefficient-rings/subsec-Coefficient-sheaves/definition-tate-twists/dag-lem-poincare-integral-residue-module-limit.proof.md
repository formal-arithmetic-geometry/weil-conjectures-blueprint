The identity toZModPow_ofCompatible gives toZModPow n (f x) = a n x. For continuity at x, choose n with exists_pow_neg_lt ℓ ε; the fiber of a n through a n x is open because the target is discrete, and continuity of a n makes its preimage a neighborhood.
