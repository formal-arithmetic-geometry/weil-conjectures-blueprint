1. Treat hL as a typeclass instance, as in the Lean proof (`let := hL`). The rank-one trivializing-cover input is the locally free condition of `def-amb-finite-locally-free-module`. The fixed-scalar module is formed by `def-pur-proetale-module-scalar-restriction`, with the pro-etale module operations and inverse images understood through `def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations` and `def-weil-proetale-inverse-image`. The chartwise pullback and pushforward used in the finite-model construction are the functors of [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star] and [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], related by `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. Their sheafification and exactness are supplied by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], and [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], `def-pur-proetale-module-scalar-restriction`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
2. Apply the proved Lean theorem `WeilConjectures.line_finite_models S A L`. The rank-one cover from `def-amb-finite-locally-free-module` is transported to a pro-etale cover whose restriction to constant scalars has a constant model with value the regular module over `ULift A`; because A is finite, this is a finite module. The passage from finite geometric local data to finite local models uses `lem-sf-proetale-lisse-finite-models`, while identifying chart restrictions with localized pro-etale sites uses `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`. Thus we obtain
`hf : ProEtale.IsFiniteLocallyConstant (ULift.{u + 1, u} A) ((proEtaleModuleRestriction S A).obj L)`. (uses `lem-sf-proetale-lisse-finite-models`, `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`)
3. Use the proved helper `finiteLocal_iff S A L` in the forward direction, exactly as `(finiteLocal_iff S A L).mp hf`. The constant-module encoding `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-constant-module-encoding` leaves the underlying additive stalk diagram unchanged, and the finite-model local-constancy and stalk comparisons are `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-local-constancy` and `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-stalks`. Together with the lisse finite-model result `lem-sf-proetale-lisse-finite-models` and the exact chart pullback [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], this gives `hl : IsLocallyConstantProEtaleModuleSheaf S A L` and `hs : ∀ (Ω : Type u) [Field Ω] [IsAlgClosed Ω] (s : Spec (.of Ω) ⟶ S), Finite (proEtaleGeometricModuleStalk s (proEtaleConstantRing S A) L)`. Hence the first and third components of the target conjunction are proved. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-constant-module-encoding`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-local-constancy`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-stalks`, `lem-sf-proetale-lisse-finite-models`)
4. For the middle component, apply `(proEtaleModuleRestriction_constructible_iff S A L).mpr`. This is the constructibility comparison `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-constant-module-constructibility`, in the finite-coefficient framework `def-weil-finite-torsion-constructibility`, and it transports the fixed-scalar predicate through the scalar restriction and pro-etale operations above. The finite-model and stalk ingredients in that comparison are precisely `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-local-constancy`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-stalks`, and `lem-sf-proetale-lisse-finite-models`; the empty-scheme and zero-ring conventions are included by [dag-sk-def-pbc-conventions]. (uses `def-weil-finite-torsion-constructibility`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-constant-module-constructibility`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-local-constancy`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-model-stalks`, `lem-sf-proetale-lisse-finite-models`)
5. To prove the fixed-scalar constructibility required by that equivalence, choose `Stratification.trivial S`. Its sole stratum is the identity of S, so the required condition is finite local constancy of the fixed-scalar sheaf itself. The Lean proof obtains this from `ProEtale.isFiniteLocallyConstant_of_iso` applied to `((ProEtale.upperStarId _ S).app _).symm` and the previously obtained `hf`; `ProEtale.upperStarId` identifies pullback along the identity with the original sheaf. Combining this constructibility with `hl` and `hs` yields exactly the stated conjunction.
