For $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ finite, write $\varpi=\pi_E$ and $e_E=v_{\varpi}(\ell)$. Put $\mathcal O_{E,X}(1)=\varprojlim_n(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^{e_En})_X(1)$. These are exactly the finite quotients in the original $\ell$-power tower, since the corresponding principal ideals agree.
