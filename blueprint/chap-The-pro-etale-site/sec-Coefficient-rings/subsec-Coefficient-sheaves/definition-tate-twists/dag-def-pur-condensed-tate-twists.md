Use the universe and small-site convention of `def-weil-size-convention`. Let $\ell$ be a prime, with $\mathbf Z_\ell\subset\mathbf Q_\ell$ as in `def-weil-l-adic-numbers`, and let $X$ be an arbitrary scheme, using the small pro-etale site of [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]. Use the geometric coefficient pullback $p_X^*$ and internal module objects of `def-weil-pbc-condensed-e-module-category`. Let $\underline{\mathbf Z}_\ell(S)=C(S,\mathbf Z_\ell)$ on profinite sets, and let $\underline\Omega$ be the condensed ring whose sections on $S$ are continuous maps with values in one finite intermediate extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ in $\Omega=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, as specified in `prop-weil-pbc-qbar-condensed-two-colimit`. Put $R_X=p_X^*\underline{\mathbf Z}_\ell$ and $B_X=p_X^*\underline\Omega$; thus $B_X$ is exactly condensedRingObj (Profinite.padicAlgClRingObj ell) X. The ring map $R_X\to B_X$ is the pullback of the inclusion $\underline{\mathbf Z}_\ell\to\underline\Omega$ through the bottom finite intermediate field $\mathbf Q_\ell$.

For every $n\in\mathbf N$, including $n=0$, let $F_n=R_X\otimes_{\mathbf Z}\mu_{\ell^n,X}$ be the free internal $R_X$-module on the abelian roots-of-unity sheaf of [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf]. Here free means tensoring the abelian sheaf with the ring object, not the free module on its underlying set: $R_X$ acts on $R_X\otimes_{\mathbf Z}\mu_{\ell^n,X}$ by multiplication on the first factor, $r\cdot(a\otimes z)=(ra)\otimes z$. Its transition $F_{n+1}\to F_n$ is $1_{R_X}\otimes(u\mapsto u^\ell)$ on these tensor products; for $m\geq n$ the system map $F_m\to F_n$ is the composite of the successive transitions, with identity at $m=n$. Define $T_X=\varprojlim_{n\in\mathbf N^{op}}F_n$ in internal $R_X$-modules, and $L_X=B_X\otimes_{R_X}T_X$ by extension of scalars. Use the inverse-limit and sheaf-tensor constructions of [def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit, def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] in these module categories. To read these as constructions on internal modules, use `def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence`: the associated section-ring sheaf has multiplication and unit induced by the ring object, and internal modules are the corresponding sectionwise module sheaves `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`. The tensor product is sheafified sectionwise tensor, with the sheafification comparison of `lem-pc-sites-module-sheafification-tensor`; extension of scalars carries the $B_X$-action on its first tensor factor. The inverse limit is indexed by all $n\geq0$ (equivalently apply the positive-index convention to the tower $G_r=F_{r-1}$ for $r\geq1$).

Define $B_X(0)=B_X$ (the tensor unit), $B_X(d+1)=B_X(d)\otimes_{B_X}L_X$ for $d\in\mathbf N$, and $B_X(-d-1)=\mathcal Hom_{B_X}(B_X(d+1),B_X)$ using the internal Hom of [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom]. Define tateTwistDerived X ell m for every $m\in\mathbf Z$ to be this internal module $B_X(m)$ placed in degree zero of $D(\operatorname{Mod}_{B_X})$: take the complex with $B_X(m)$ in degree zero, zero objects in all other degrees, and all differentials zero, and apply the localization of `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`. These are the actual constructions tateModuleLevel, tateModuleTransition, tateModuleSystem, tateModule, tateTwistOne, tateTwistNat, tateTwist and tateTwistDerived at the recorded upstream revision. No finite-field structure on $X$, invertibility hypothesis on $\ell$ on $X$, choice of a trivialization, local-freeness theorem, or identification with the common-stage coefficient category is part of this definition.
