1. Let $J$ be the topology of the small pro-etale site [dag-def-weil-pbc-small-proetale-site], and let $\mathcal O_S$ be the commutative-ring-valued sheaf in the statement. Define the units presheaf by postcomposing its underlying presheaf with $F=\texttt{forget₂ CommRingCat CommMonCat}\mathbin{\circledast}\texttt{CommMonCat.units}\mathbin{\circledast}\texttt{CommGrpCat.toAddCommGrp}\mathbin{\circledast}\texttt{AddCommGrpCat.uliftFunctor}$, where $\circledast$ denotes functor composition in the displayed order. The functor `CommMonCat.units` sends a commutative monoid to its group of units and a homomorphism to `Units.map` of that homomorphism. Thus the sections are $\operatorname{ULift}(\operatorname{Additive}(\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)^\times))$, and the restrictions are the induced maps on units. Addition here means multiplication of units; changing notation and lifting universes leave the stated groups and restriction maps canonically identified. Functoriality follows from functor composition.
2. The functor $F$ preserves the limits needed for the sheaf condition. Indeed, `forget₂ CommRingCat CommMonCat` is a right adjoint, and `CommMonCat.units` is a right adjoint by `CommGrpCat.forget₂CommMonAdj`; right adjoints preserve limits. The functor `CommGrpCat.toAddCommGrp` is the functor of `commGroupAddCommGroupEquivalence`, so it too preserves limits. Finally, `AddCommGrpCat.uliftFunctor_preservesLimitsOfSize` supplies limit preservation for the universe lift. Limit preservation is preserved under composition.
3. Apply `CategoryTheory.Presheaf.isSheaf_comp_of_isSheaf` to $J$, the underlying presheaf of $\mathcal O_S$, the functor $F$, and the sheaf condition already carried by $\mathcal O_S$ (in Lean, `(proEtaleStructureSheaf S).property`). This result transports the limit diagrams expressing the sheaf condition through a limit-preserving functor, and therefore proves the sheaf condition for the units presheaf. Pairing this presheaf with that proof gives `proEtaleUnitsSheaf S`. Under the sectionwise identifications above, it is precisely $\mathbf G_{m,S}=\mathcal O_S^\times$, with existence and uniqueness of gluing for every compatible family of units.
