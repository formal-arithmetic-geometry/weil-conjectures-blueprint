1. Retain the original actual root tower limit: define purTwistZlOne as the limit of the tower of root sheaves from [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf], indexed by the positive exponents n+1. By [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit], evaluation computes this limit as compatible tuples of sections, and its projections are jointly injective. Use the finite coefficient rings, their reductions, and the completed coefficient ring R from [dag-def-pur-twist-coefficient-rings]. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit])
2. For purTwistForgetPreservesSheafification, a sheafification equivalence remains locally injective and locally surjective after forgetting commutativity: the equalizer and image sieves on underlying sections are unchanged. Mathlib Presheaf.equalizerSieve_mem and Presheaf.imageSieve_mem give these covering sieves, and GrothendieckTopology.W_iff_isLocallyBijective gives the required equivalence. Mathlib constantCommuteCompose then supplies purTwistFiniteRingIso. Restricting the recorded finite roots module along this isomorphism defines purTwistRootsModule on the same underlying abelian sheaf.
3. The root transitions respect the finite scalar reductions. Indeed, pull the target's normalized torsion action back along the coefficient reduction. It is normalized over the source coefficient ring because ring maps preserve integer casts and Int.cast_smul_eq_zsmul identifies their action with integer multiplication; its restriction compatibility follows from naturality. Both root sheaves are killed by the source exponent, since the target exponent divides it. Apply torsionSheaf_hom_linear from [dag-lem-pur-torsion-sheaf-constant-module] to this pulled-back action and the source action, using torsionSheafModuleStructure_spec. Transporting through purTwistFiniteRingIso gives purTwistTransition_smul. (uses [dag-lem-pur-torsion-sheaf-constant-module])
4. For a section a of R and a root-limit section x, prescribe the nth coordinate of a*x to be q_n(a)*p_n(x), where q_n and p_n are the coefficient and root projections. The transition identity just proved and limit.w make these coordinates compatible. The sectionwise limit property [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit], implemented by isLimitOfPreserves and the limiting cone's lift and fac, produces the section a*x. Concrete.isLimit_ext gives joint injectivity of the projections, so all module laws follow from their finite-level versions. This is purTwistCoordinateModule; Module.ext' and joint injectivity also give uniqueness. Naturality of the projections and finite-level semilinear restrictions prove restriction compatibility coordinatewise. Thus purTwistZlAction_existsUnique holds. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit])
5. Define purTwistZlAction by choosing the action from this existence-uniqueness statement; its uniqueness specification fixes the choice. Its restriction clause is purTwistZlAction_restriction. PresheafOfModules.ofPresheaf packages this action and restriction identity into purTwistZlModule, retaining exactly the original root-limit sheaf and its sheaf property.
6. For the finite extension E, use its valuation ring O_E and uniformizer from `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr`. Write A=(Z_l)_S, B=(O_E)_S, and T for the completed integral coefficient ring, with maps a:A→R, b:A→B, c:R→T and d:B→T from [dag-def-pur-twist-coefficient-rings]. Retain the prescribed scalar tensor: purTwistIntegralOne is extension along b of purTwistZlModule restricted along a, using SheafOfModules.changeOfRingsOfUniverses. The sheafification unit applied to b0 tensor x defines purTwistIntegralTensor. By [dag-lem-pur-twist-coefficient-tensor], the specified sectionwise map B(U) tensor over A(U) R(U)→T(U) is bijective. RingEquiv.ofBijective turns it into a ring equivalence, whose value on b0 tensor r is d(b0)c(r) by definition. Apply CategoryTheory.tensorPushoutAction_existsUnique from [dag-lem-pur-tensor-pushout-action]. It gives the unique restriction-compatible T-action agreeing with the original B-action along d and satisfying c(r)*(b0 tensor x)=b0 tensor (r*x) after sheafification. This proves purTwistIntegralAction_existsUnique. (uses [dag-lem-pur-twist-coefficient-tensor], [dag-lem-pur-tensor-pushout-action])
7. Choose this uniquely specified action as purTwistIntegralAction and take its restriction clause as purTwistIntegralAction_restriction. PresheafOfModules.ofPresheaf then defines purTwistIntegralModule on the unchanged integral tensor sheaf. Finally, define purTwistFiniteOne at level n by SheafOfModules.changeOfRingsOfUniverses along purTwistIntegralQuotient from B to the constant coefficient sheaf O_E/(pi^(n+1)). This is precisely the ordinary positive-level finite coefficient tensor reduction of purTwistIntegralOne.
