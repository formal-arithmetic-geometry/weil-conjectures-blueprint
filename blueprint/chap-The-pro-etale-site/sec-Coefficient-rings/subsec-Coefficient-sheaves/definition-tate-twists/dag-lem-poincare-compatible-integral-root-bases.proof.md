1. Apply exists_compatible_roots_diagram_basis from [dag-lem-poincare-compatible-primitive-roots] to obtain one cover 𝒰 and, for every i, an isomorphism eRoots between the pulled-back root diagram and the pulled-back constant residue diagram. Obtain eQ from constantTateResidueSystem_free_iso in [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison], identifying the free module on the constant residue diagram with the actual residue-module diagram. Both natural isomorphisms are diagram isomorphisms and include level 0. (uses [dag-lem-poincare-compatible-primitive-roots], [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison])
2. For R the integral condensed coefficient sheaf, F the raw inverse-image functor for the chosen cover map, Φ = F.mapMod R, and P = Mod.freeFunctor R, the private tateSystemFreeIso is the natural transformation built by NatTrans.ofOpSequence with identity components. Its transition squares reduce by the definitions of the actual Tate systems [dag-def-pur-condensed-tate-twists] and Category.id_comp/Category.comp_id; inferInstanceAs (IsIso (𝟙 _)) followed by NatIso.isIso_of_isIso_app turns it into an isomorphism from the actual integral Tate system to the free root system.
3. Let Q = Mod.freeFunctor (F.obj R). The private freePullIso compares P ⋙ Φ with F ⋙ Q. The sheaf pullback/pushforward adjunction and its lower-star construction are supplied by [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] and `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, with the lax and strong monoidal structures of `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal` and `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`. Their monoidal compatibility is `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, the pullback exactness instance is [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], and the monoidal adjunction lifts to module objects by `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`. Hence adj.mapModAdjunction R exists. Mod.freeForgetAdjunction, Adjunction.comp, and Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq then identify P ⋙ Φ and F ⋙ Q as left adjoints to the same right adjoint, producing the required e. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`)
4. For each i, compose the following natural isomorphisms: whisker tateSystemFreeIso on the right by Φ; reassociate with Functor.associator; whisker e on the left by the root diagram and use the inverse associator; whisker eRoots on the right by Q; reassociate with the constant residue diagram; whisker e.symm on the left and use the inverse associator; finally whisker eQ on the right by Φ. In Lean this is exactly the displayed chain of Functor.isoWhiskerRight, Functor.isoWhiskerLeft, Functor.associator (and .symm), and ≪≫. It has source tateModuleSystem X ℓ ⋙ Φ and target tateResidueModuleSystem X ℓ ⋙ Φ, so it gives the required Nonempty diagram isomorphism on the cover from [dag-lem-poincare-compatible-primitive-roots] and [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison]. (uses [dag-lem-poincare-compatible-primitive-roots], [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison])
