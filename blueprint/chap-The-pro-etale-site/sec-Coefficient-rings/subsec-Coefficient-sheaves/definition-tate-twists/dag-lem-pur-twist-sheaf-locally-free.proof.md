1. Apply clauses (2) and (3) of `det-def-sp-tate-twist-locally-free` with $X=S$ and $A=A_{E,n}$. Its coefficient objects are exactly those specified here. This proves clause 1 on the common cover of its clause (1), simultaneously for every positive level; no second proof of local freeness or finite-exponent independence is needed. (uses `det-def-sp-tate-twist-locally-free`)
2. On that cover choose the compatible roots $\zeta_r$ of the cited theorem (1). The coefficient-limit comparison for $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$ and the sectionwise-limit formula identify $a=(a_r)\in R_V$ with $(\zeta_r^{a_r})_r\in T_S|_V$. This is $R_V$-linear, with inverse given by the unique exponents in each root basis. The projection $p_r$ is reduction in these coordinates. Tensor restriction identifies the clause-2 map with $(\mathbf Z/\ell^r\mathbf Z)_V\to\mu_{\ell^r,V}$, $a\mapsto\zeta_r^a$, an isomorphism. Local detection proves clause 2 globally. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit], `det-def-sp-tate-twist-locally-free`, `det-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x-integral-limit`, `lem-sfb-coefficient-sections-localization`, `lem-sfb-localized-site-tensor-restriction`, `lem-sfb-sheaf-cover-local-isomorphism`)
3. The clause-3 maps exist globally: balancing holds at every finite coordinate, and $p_{n+1}(t)^\ell=p_n(t)$ makes the first coordinates compatible. On $V$, the finite bases $\zeta_n\otimes1$ commute with residue reduction; sectionwise limits and the integral coefficient comparison identify $\mathcal O_E(1)|_V$ with $\mathcal O_{E,V}$ and $q_n$ with coefficient reduction. In the basis of step 2, both displayed maps are therefore multiplication: $1\otimes b\mapsto b$ and $b\otimes a\mapsto\bar b a$. Tensor restriction and local detection prove both are isomorphisms. Their transition compatibility holds coordinatewise by these defining formulas.
