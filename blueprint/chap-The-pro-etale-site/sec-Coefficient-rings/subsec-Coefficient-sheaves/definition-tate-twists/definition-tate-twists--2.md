Let $X$ be a scheme over a field $k$, let $N\geq1$, and let $\zeta\in k$ be a primitive $N$th root of unity. Then $a\mapsto\zeta^a$ exhibits $\mu_{N,X}$ as the cokernel of multiplication by $N$ on $\mathbf Z_X$. In particular, it gives an isomorphism
$$
(\mathbf Z/N\mathbf Z)_X\simeq\mu_{N,X}.
$$
