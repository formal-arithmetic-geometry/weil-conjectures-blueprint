1. The spectral norm of l is its Q_l norm, which is less than one. Thus l belongs to the maximal ideal of O_E. The chosen uniformizer generates that ideal. Taking the (n+1)st ideal power puts l^(n+1) in (pi_E)^(n+1), so its image in the quotient is zero. (uses `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr`)
