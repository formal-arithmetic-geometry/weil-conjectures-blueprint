Let C be a monoidal category, R,B,D monoid objects, and p:R -> B, q:R -> D, r:B -> D monoid morphisms with p;r=q. For any v:U -> V there is a unique R-module morphism from the restriction of the free B-module B tensor U to the restriction of the free D-module D tensor V whose underlying arrow is r tensor v.
