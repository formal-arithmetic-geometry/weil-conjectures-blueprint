1. The presheaf tensor product is the sectionwise module tensor from [dag-def-pur-presheaf-tensor-product]. The Lean construction `siteTensorPresheaf` uses exactly these sectionwise tensors and restriction maps, and `siteSheafTensorRight` is obtained by applying module sheafification, whose universal property is `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`. Thus the construction has the stated tensor-product meaning; the change-of-rings and positive tensor-power clauses are interpreted by the corresponding sectionwise extension and recursive tensor definitions.
2. Let W be the inverse-image class of local equivalences used by the localization. If f is in W, its fields `hf.isLocallyInjective` and `hf.isLocallySurjective` provide the hypotheses of `PresheafOfModules.sheafification_tensorHom_isIso`. Applying that theorem to `siteTensorHom f N` shows that sheafifying the sectionwise map f tensor id is an isomorphism. The identity-on-sections comparison `tensorPresheafIso` identifies the generic presheaf tensor with `siteTensorPresheaf`; `PresheafOfModules.inverseImage_W_toPresheaf_eq_inverseImage_isomorphisms` and `W.arrow_mk_iso_iff` then transport this isomorphism back to W. This is exactly the local tensor-descent result `lem-weil-pbc-tensor-local-bijection-universes`. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-tensor-local-bijection-universes`)
3. The preceding right-tensor closure gives the left-tensor closure by conjugating with the comparison isomorphisms `β_ P F` and `β_ Q F`; `Arrow.isoMk` and `W.arrow_mk_iso_iff` justify transport along those isomorphisms. Therefore the record `IsMonoidal` is assembled with `whiskerLeft` from this transport and `whiskerRight` from the direct right-tensor argument. The existing symmetric module-tensor construction supplies the associator, unit, braiding, and their coherence, as recorded in `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`; the Lean declarations instantiate its generic `monoidalCategory` and `symmetricCategory` for this localization. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`)
4. For a fixed sheaf M, let L be module sheafification and let `adj := PresheafOfModules.sheafificationAdjunction`. Sectionwise tensoring by M.val preserves coefficient-sized colimits; `preservesSmallestColimits_of_preservesColimits` gives preservation of the smallest universe. The monoidal structure on L and the counit isomorphism `e := (asIso adj.counit).app M` produce the natural isomorphism `i : L ⋙ tensorLeft M ≅ tensorLeft M.val ⋙ L` via `Functor.isoWhiskerLeft`, `curriedTensor.mapIso`, and `Functor.Monoidal.commTensorLeft`. Hence `preservesColimits_of_natIso i.symm` gives colimit preservation for `L ⋙ tensorLeft M`, and `Adjunction.preservesColimitsOfSize_iff` transfers it across the fully faithful right adjoint to `tensorLeft M`.
5. The resulting declaration `cochainTensorLeftPreservesColimits` records the preceding argument. For right tensoring, the symmetric braiding comparison `BraidedCategory.tensorLeftIsoTensorRight M` identifies `tensorRight M` with a left-tensor functor, so `preservesColimits_of_natIso` yields `cochainTensorRightPreservesColimits`.
6. Preservation of small colimits implies preservation of binary coproducts, and in this additive module-sheaf category binary coproducts are binary biproducts. `preservesBinaryBiproducts_of_preservesBinaryCoproducts` followed by `Functor.additive_of_preservesBinaryBiproducts` makes every `tensorLeft M` additive. The braiding isomorphism and `Functor.additive_of_iso` make every `tensorRight M` additive. The four fields of `cochainModulesPreadditive` are then the corresponding `map_zero` and `map_add` equations for left and right whiskering.
