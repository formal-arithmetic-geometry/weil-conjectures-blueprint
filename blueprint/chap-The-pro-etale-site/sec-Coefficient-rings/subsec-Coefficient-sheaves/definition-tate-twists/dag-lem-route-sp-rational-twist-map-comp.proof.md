1. The condensed inclusions compose by the strict functoriality in `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map`. Their pullbacks therefore give $c_{FH}\circ c_{EF}=c_{EH}$: coefficient change is application of the inverse-image functor [def-sfb-coefficient-map, sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients]. By [dag-lem-route-sp-integers-map-exists-unique] the maps $r_{KL}$ preserve the ring structure and satisfy $a_Lr_{KL}=c_{KL}a_K$. Consequently $a_Hr_{FH}r_{EF}=c_{FH}a_Fr_{EF}=c_{FH}c_{EF}a_E=c_{EH}a_E$. The composite of the two ring maps is a ring map, since it preserves unit and multiplication successively. Thus it satisfies both defining conditions for $r_{EH}$; the uniqueness clause of the same cited statement gives $r_{FH}r_{EF}=r_{EH}$. (uses `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-map`, `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, `lem-route-sp-integers-map-exists-unique`)
2. For each $n\geq0$, [dag-lem-route-sp-ell-map-exists-unique] gives $q_{KL,n}d_{K,n}=d_{L,n}r_{KL}$. Hence $q_{FH,n}q_{EF,n}d_{E,n}=q_{FH,n}d_{F,n}r_{EF}=d_{H,n}r_{FH}r_{EF}=d_{H,n}r_{EH}$. The uniqueness clause, now for the pair $E\subseteq H$, gives $q_{FH,n}q_{EF,n}=q_{EH,n}$. In particular this argument does not assume the quotient inclusion is injective or that reduction is surjective on sections of every pro-etale object. (uses `lem-route-sp-ell-map-exists-unique`)
3. Use the projection equations of [dag-lem-route-sp-integral-twist-map-exists-unique]. At every $n$ they give $p_{H,n}u_{FH}u_{EF}=(q_{FH,n}\otimes1)p_{F,n}u_{EF}=(q_{FH,n}\otimes1)(q_{EF,n}\otimes1)p_{E,n}=(q_{EH,n}\otimes1)p_{E,n}$. The middle tensor identity follows from [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]: on the presheaf tensors both composites send $b\otimes m$ to $q_{FH,n}(q_{EF,n}(b))\otimes m$, hence agree on finite sums and after sheafification. Thus $u_{FH}u_{EF}$ satisfies precisely the equations characterizing $u_{EH}$; uniqueness in the cited integral-map statement gives $u_{FH}u_{EF}=u_{EH}$. No exactness or surjectivity of inverse limits is used. (uses `lem-route-sp-integral-twist-map-exists-unique`, `def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product`)
4. Apply the normalization equations of [dag-lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-exists-unique] successively. They give $(j_{FH}j_{EF})e_E=j_{FH}e_F(c_{EF}\otimes u_{EF})=e_H(c_{FH}\otimes u_{FH})(c_{EF}\otimes u_{EF})=e_H(c_{EH}\otimes u_{EH})$. For the last equality use the two identities already proved; tensor bifunctoriality here is the direct calculation $a\otimes t\mapsto c_{FH}(c_{EF}(a))\otimes u_{FH}(u_{EF}(t))$ on presheaf pure tensors, extended to sums and sheafification [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. Therefore $j_{FH}j_{EF}$ satisfies the defining tensor equation for the pair $E\subseteq H$. The uniqueness clause of the rational-map statement implies $j_{FH}j_{EF}=j_{EH}$, which is the required equality. This compares morphisms via their tensor quotient, without assuming all sections of the sheafified tensor are pure tensors. (uses `lem-route-sp-rational-twist-map-exists-unique`, `def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product`)
