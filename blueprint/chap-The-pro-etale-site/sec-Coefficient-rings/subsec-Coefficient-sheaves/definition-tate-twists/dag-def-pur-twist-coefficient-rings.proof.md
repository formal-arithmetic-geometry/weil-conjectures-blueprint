1. Retain the original constant sheaves, quotient maps and tower limits. Limit universal properties give existence and uniqueness of the constant-to-completion maps. Integrality supplies the unique coefficient-ring inclusion. Each coefficient quotient kills a power of l; its factorization through Z/l^r yields a compatible cone, hence the completed scalar map. Cancellation of the epimorphic constant quotient maps and joint faithfulness of limit projections prove uniqueness and commutativity of the scalar square. Define its comparison by pushout.desc. These are exactly the already proved parent constructions, moved to avoid an import cycle with the coefficient-only tensor lemma.
