1. Work on the small pro-étale site $S_{\mathrm{proét}}$ [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]. The displayed $\mu_{\ell^r,S}$ is the kernel of $u\mapsto u^{\ell^r}$ on $\mathbf G_{m,S}$, hence a sheaf of abelian groups and a $(\mathbf Z/\ell^r)_S$-module [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf]; $u\mapsto u^\ell$ is a morphism $\mu_{\ell^{r+1},S}\to\mu_{\ell^r,S}$. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf])
2. $\Lambda_S=p_S^*\Lambda$ is a commutative ring sheaf, functorial in $\Lambda$ `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`; the reductions give ring maps $\mathcal O_{E,S}\to(A_{E,n+1})_S\to(A_{E,n})_S$. As $\ell\in\pi_E\mathcal O_E$, $\ell^nA_{E,n}=0$, so annihilating exponents exist. For such $r$, $\ell^r=0$ in $(A_{E,n})_S$, so the constant sheaf [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf] maps to it, $(\mathbf Z/\ell^r)_S\to(A_{E,n})_S$, and the balanced tensor is the sheaf tensor [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. For $r'\ge r$, $u\otimes a\mapsto u^{\ell^{r'-r}}\otimes a$ is balanced, as $(u^b)^{\ell^{r'-r}}=(u^{\ell^{r'-r}})^b$: these are the Kummer comparisons. The constructions do not use that they are bijective or that the lines are invertible; both are proved downstream (det-def-sp-tate-twist-locally-free (2),(3), which cites this node). With $r=n$ at level $n$, $u\otimes a\mapsto u^\ell\otimes\bar a$ is a balanced map $A_{E,n+1}(1)_S\to A_{E,n}(1)_S$, linear over $\mathcal O_{E,S}$ acting through the ring maps above; $\mathcal O_E(1)_S$ is the limit in $\mathcal O_{E,S}$-modules [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product], [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit], `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`)
3. $E(1)_S$ is the change of rings [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] along $\mathcal O_{E,S}\to\mathcal O_{E,S}[1/\ell]\cong E_S$ `det-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x-invert-ell`. For $\Qlb$: $\mathbf Z_{\ell,S}\otimes\mu_{\ell^r,S}$ is a $\mathbf Z_{\ell,S}$-module through the first factor, the maps $1\otimes(u\mapsto u^\ell)$ are linear, and $\mathbf Z_\ell(1)_S$ is their limit in $\mathbf Z_{\ell,S}$-modules [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit]; $\Qlb(1)_S$ is its change of rings along $\mathbf Z_{\ell,S}\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,S}$, the pullback of $\mathbf Z_\ell\to\Qlb$ `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product], [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit], `det-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x-invert-ell`, `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`)
4. For $m>0$, $\Lambda(m)$ is the sheaf tensor power [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]; for $m<0$ it is the sheaf Hom into $\Lambda_S$ [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom]; both are $\Lambda_S$-modules. $D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ is the derived category of $\Lambda_S$-modules on the site of [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], whose size convention makes it small for the module sheaves. `def-weil-derived-tensor` gives the K-flat formula only over constant rings $A_Y$; for the ring sheaf $\Lambda_S$ (not constant for $\mathcal O_E,E,\Qlb$) the same K-flat total tensor gives the bifunctor $\otimes^L_{\Lambda_S}$ of `lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent` with $R=\Lambda_S$. With $\Lambda(m)[0]$ in the second variable, $K(m)=K\otimes^L_{\Lambda_S}\Lambda(m)$ is a functor; identities and composites are preserved by bifunctoriality. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product], [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], `def-weil-derived-tensor`, `lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent`)
