1. Let R_X be the pulled-back condensed ring of continuous maps into Z_l and S_X the pulled-back condensed ring of the algebraic closure of Q_l. The constructions of the p-adic integral and p-adic condensed rings, together with the pointwise integral-to-subfield map and its passage through finite subextensions into Qbar_l, give commutative ring objects and a ring morphism a_X : R_X -> S_X; these are the maps described by `def-sfb-padic-int-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`, and `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`. By `def-sfb-coefficient-map` and `sk-def-sfb-coefficient-change`, a_X is the coefficient map used in the scalar extension defining tateTwistOne (equivalently, the construction in [dag-def-pur-condensed-tate-twists]). (uses `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`, `def-sfb-padic-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-int-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-padic-subfield-condensed-ring`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`, `sk-def-sfb-coefficient-change`)
2. For any scheme morphism g, the lower-star functor is the sheaf pushforward of [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] and is lax symmetric monoidal by `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`. Its adjunction with upper star is `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, while `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal` and [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact] provide the strong monoidal and exact pullback structure; `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction` says this is a monoidal adjunction. Consequently the transported module functors and their adjunctions are available by `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`. Apply this to F=f^* and, after choosing a member u_i of a cover of Y, to G=u_i^*. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`)
3. By [dag-lem-poincare-integral-tate-pullback-local-line], there is a pro-etale covering (u_i : V_i -> Y) and, for every i, an isomorphism e_i from G.mapMod (F.mapMod R_X) applied to the integral Tate module tateModule(X,l) to the regular R-module (the module tensor unit). (uses [dag-lem-poincare-integral-tate-pullback-local-line])
4. For a coefficient morphism a : R -> S, naturality of the units of the two upper-star/lower-star adjunctions gives the commutative square of algebra maps required by `def-sfb-comap-mapmod-square`. Using the extension/restriction adjunction of `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction` (with extension defined in `def-sfb-modobj-extension` and restriction as in `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`) and the transported module adjunction from `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`, the Lean proof applies `Adjunction.pullIsoOfComapIso` to this square and uses `Mod.comapMapModComapIso`. Thus there is a natural isomorphism `F.mapMod` followed by extension along `F.map a` to extension along `a` followed by `F.mapMod`; apply its inverse first for F and then for G. (uses `def-sfb-comap-mapmod-square`, `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction`, `def-sfb-modobj-extension`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`, `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`)
5. For any commutative ring morphism a : R -> S, extension of scalars is the relative tensor-product functor of `def-sfb-modobj-extension`. The free-module functors and the extension/restriction adjunction have the same right adjoint, so `CategoryTheory.Mod.freeForgetAdjunction` and `Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq` give the Lean isomorphism `extendFreeIso : free R ⋙ extendScalars a ≅ free S`. At the unit object, the free module is isomorphic to the regular module by the right-unitor calculation using `rightUnitor_naturality` (and the monoidal coherence proof); composing these unit isomorphisms with `extendScalars a` yields `extendRegularIso : extendScalars a (regular R) ≅ regular S`. (uses `def-sfb-modobj-extension`, `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction`, `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`)
6. Fix i and write E for extension of scalars along G.map(F.map a). Starting with mapMod(G) mapMod(F) of tateTwistOne, unfold tateTwistOne as extension of scalars along a, use the inverse comparison from the preceding pullback step for F, then the inverse comparison for G, then apply E.mapIso to e_i. The result is E(regular (G.map(F.obj R_X))), and `extendRegularIso` identifies this with regular (G.map(F.obj S_X)), which is the unit module in the conclusion. Hence the chosen cover and these composites give the required Nonempty isomorphism for every i. (uses [dag-lem-poincare-integral-tate-pullback-local-line], `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, `def-sfb-comap-mapmod-square`, `def-sfb-modobj-extension`)
