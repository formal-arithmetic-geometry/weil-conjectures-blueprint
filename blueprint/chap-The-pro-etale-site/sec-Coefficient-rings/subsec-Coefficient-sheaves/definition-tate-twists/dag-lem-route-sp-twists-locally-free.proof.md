1. Apply `lem-route-sp-twists-common-cover` with this $X$, prime $\ell$ and fixed algebraic closure $\Omega$. Its hypotheses are exactly the residue-field invertibility and size hypotheses here. It supplies a pro-etale covering family $U_i\to X$. Clause 3, specialized to the chosen $E$, gives $R_E|_{U_i}$-linear isomorphisms $L_E|_{U_i}\cong R_E|_{U_i}$ and $K_E|_{U_i}$-linear isomorphisms $Q_E|_{U_i}\cong K_E|_{U_i}$. Clause 4 gives $A|_{U_i}$-linear isomorphisms $Q_\Omega|_{U_i}\cong A|_{U_i}$. These are the actual inverse-limit, scalar-extension and sheafified-colimit objects of this statement; in particular the algebraic-closure clause is over the pulled-back condensed ring, not a common-stage rational coefficient category. The cited lemma supplies all three on one family without a quasi-compactness hypothesis. (uses `lem-route-sp-twists-common-cover`)
2. Under `def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence`, underlying sections and restrictions are unchanged, so the linear isomorphisms in step 1 are isomorphisms of the sectionwise module sheaves occurring in the recorded Lean conclusion. The signed-twist convention of [dag-sk-def-sp-tate-twist] at exponent $1$ is the one-fold tensor power of the line, canonically the line itself. With the recursive tensor-power convention starting at the unit, its unit constraint is explicitly $a\otimes m\mapsto am$, inverse $m\mapsto1\otimes m$. These formulas commute with restrictions and are linear, so the same local trivializations apply to the signed exponent-$1$ objects; exponent $0$ is exactly the respective coefficient unit. (uses `def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence`, `sk-def-sp-tate-twist`)
3. For each of the three module sheaves $M$ with coefficient ring $R$, write $e_i:M|_{U_i}\cong R|_{U_i}$ for step 1's isomorphism (composed with step 2's unit constraint when required). On the localized site of [dag-def-pur-localized-ringed-site], set $s_i=e_i^{-1}(1)$. Linearity gives $e_i^{-1}(a)=a s_i$ for every local scalar $a$, so the singleton family $\{s_i\}$ is a basis: its free-module map $a\mapsto a s_i$ has inverse $e_i$. Thus this covering and these singleton generating families give the local generators data with generator index $\operatorname{Fin}(1)$ in the definition of finite locally free of rank one. This proves each of the three clauses and hence their conjunction. On the empty scheme the covering criterion is vacuous, as is that same definition. (uses `def-pur-localized-ringed-site`)
