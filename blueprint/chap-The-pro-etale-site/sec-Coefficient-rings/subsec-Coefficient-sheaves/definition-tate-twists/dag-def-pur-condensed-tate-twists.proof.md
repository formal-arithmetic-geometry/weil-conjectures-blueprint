1. Fix the prime ell and scheme X. The integral coefficient ring is the condensed continuous-function ring on Z_ell, while the target coefficient ring is the finite-intermediate-extension-valued condensed algebraic closure. At the bottom finite intermediate field E = Q_ell, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point` supplies the continuous condensed-ring inclusion from Z_ell to E, and `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar` supplies the inclusion of that finite subextension into the condensed algebraic-closure ring. Pulling their composite back to X with the coefficient-change construction `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, equivalently the pullback operation of `sk-def-sfb-coefficient-change`, gives exactly the Lean morphism coefficientMap (Profinite.padicIntRingObjToPadicAlgCl ell) X : R_X -> B_X. (uses `def-sfb-coefficient-map`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`, `def-sfb-qlbar-subextension-to-qlbar`, `sk-def-sfb-coefficient-change`)
2. For each n : N, tateModuleLevel X ell n is the Lean object (Mod.free _).obj (rootsOfUnitySheaf X (ell ^ n)). Thus its underlying abelian sheaf is the tensor R_X tensor_Z mu_(ell^n), with the module action supplied by multiplication on the first factor. tateModuleTransition X ell n is the image under Mod.free.map of the recorded ell-power map on roots of unity (the eqToHom only identifies ell^(n+1) with ell^n times ell). Functor.ofOpSequence (tateModuleTransition X ell) then forms the opposite-index tower, including the identity at equal indices and composites for longer transitions. This is exactly the definitions tateModuleLevel, tateModuleTransition, and tateModuleSystem, including n = 0.
3. The object tateModule X ell is CategoryTheory.Limits.limit (tateModuleSystem X ell) in the category of internal R_X-modules. The underlying sheaf limit carries a unique compatible R_X-action and is limiting in the module category by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`. Hence this is the inverse limit over all n >= 0 of the free root modules, with precisely the transition maps just described. (uses `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`)
4. Because R_X is commutative, every internal module has the canonical symmetric bimodule structure obtained by braiding its action, as in `lem-sfb-modobj-symmetric-bimodules`. The relative tensor in the module category is the balanced coequalizer of the two actions by `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, and its specialization to sheaves is the coequalizer described in `pur713-def-relative-tensor`. These are the module tensors used below, so the balancing is over the coefficient ring and the induced action is the one on the indicated factor. (uses `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, `lem-sfb-modobj-symmetric-bimodules`, `pur713-def-relative-tensor`)
5. Applying Lean's Mod.extendScalars to the coefficient morphism from Step 1 gives tateTwistOne X ell. By the extension construction `def-sfb-modobj-extension` and the change-of-scalars specification `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`, its underlying object is the balanced relative tensor B_X tensor_{R_X} T_X, with B_X acting on the first tensor factor. Thus it is exactly the stated scalar extension L_X, rather than a tensor over Z. (uses `def-sfb-modobj-extension`, `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`)
6. The natural-number recursion tateTwistNat now gives B_X(0) = 1_(B_X) = B_X and tateTwistNat (d+1) = tateTwistNat d tensor tateTwistOne X ell; by the balanced tensor description in `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor` and `pur713-def-relative-tensor`, these are exactly the successive tensor powers of L_X over B_X. For a negative integer, the branch of tateTwist is ihom (tateTwistNat X ell (d+1)) evaluated at the unit. The internal-Hom construction `def-sfb-modobj-internal-hom` gives this equalizer module and its tensor/Hom adjunction, while `lem-weil-pbc-module-relative-tensor-adjoint` supplies the corresponding closed module-category structure. Therefore the negative branch is precisely Hom_{B_X}(B_X(d+1), B_X). (uses `def-sfb-modobj-internal-hom`, `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-relative-tensor-adjoint`, `pur713-def-relative-tensor`)
7. Finally, tateTwistDerived X ell m is defined as (DerivedCategory.singleFunctor _ 0).obj (tateTwist X ell m). Unfolding DerivedCategory.singleFunctor gives the complex with the constructed module in degree zero, zero objects elsewhere, and zero differentials, followed by the derived-category localization. This establishes the stated degree-zero object for every m : Z and completes the definition without adding any finiteness, invertibility, trivialization, or local-freeness hypothesis.
