For $s\geq r$, the power map $\mu_{\ell^s,X}\to\mu_{\ell^r,X}$ sends a primitive root to a primitive root. After the étale trivializations of clause [definition-tate-twists--2], it is the reduction map $\mathbf Z/\ell^s\to\mathbf Z/\ell^r$. Tensoring with $A$, which is killed by $\ell^r$, makes this an isomorphism. The power maps compose, so these identifications satisfy transitivity and identify all admissible choices of the exponent.
