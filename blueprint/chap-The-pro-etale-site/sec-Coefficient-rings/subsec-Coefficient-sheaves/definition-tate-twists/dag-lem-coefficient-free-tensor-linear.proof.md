1. Use r tensor v as the underlying arrow. Its module-linearity equation follows from the commuting coefficient square p;r=q and multiplicativity of r, after naturality of the tensor associator. The two sides both apply q to the R-factor, r to the B-factor and v to U, then multiply in D. Module morphisms with equal underlying arrows are equal, giving uniqueness.
