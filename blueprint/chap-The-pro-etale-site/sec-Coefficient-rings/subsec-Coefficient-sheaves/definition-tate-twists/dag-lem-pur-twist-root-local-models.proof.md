1. Put N = ell^(n+1). The residue-field invertibility assumption makes ell a unit in global sections, by IsLocalRing.residue_ne_zero_iff_isUnit and S.toRingedSpace.isUnit_of_isUnit_germ; hence N is a unit too. Use the section rings and their pullback maps of [dag-def-pur-proetale-structure-sheaf]. Pull the universal primitive-root scheme for N back along S -> Spec Gamma(S,O). The results etale_primitiveRootsCover and surjective_primitiveRootsCover, together with Scheme.Pullback.exists_preimage_pullback, make the resulting map V -> S etale and surjective. Its universal root z satisfies z^N = 1, and distinct powers with exponents less than N have unit differences, by PrimitiveRootsAlgebra.zeta_pow and PrimitiveRootsAlgebra.isUnit_zeta_pow_sub_zeta_pow. Thus this single map gives the required etale covering family. The proved constantRootsIso identifies the constant cyclic abelian sheaf with the source root sheaf on V. Identify the latter with the concrete kernel sheaf [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf] via proEtaleRootsOfUnitySections. Localization restriction and the dense slice equivalence of `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization` transport this isomorphism to the required slice; constantCommuteCompose identifies the additive sheaf underlying the finite coefficient unit with the constant cyclic sheaf. Every additive map between the resulting finite coefficient modules is linear: locally lift a scalar through the constant-presheaf sheafification map, represent it by an integer using ZMod.intCast_surjective, and use Int.cast_smul_eq_zsmul and preservation of integer scalar multiplication. PresheafOfModules.restrictHomEquivOfIsLocallySurjective descends this linearity. Apply this to both directions of the additive isomorphism to obtain the asserted finite module basis. (uses [dag-def-pur-proetale-structure-sheaf], `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`)
2. For a geometric point s: Spec Omega -> S, use the site fiber functor sending U to the lifts of s to U. It sends coverings to jointly surjective families: choose a covering member above the image of the point, form its nonempty weakly etale base change to Spec Omega, and split that map by `lem-fix-weakly-etale-algclosed-section`. Its category of elements is equivalent to the category of pointed geometric neighborhoods, so Functor.Final.colimitIso identifies its sheaf fiber with the specified geometric stalk. Lift the terminal fiber element through the finite cover of the preceding step, giving a point w over some U_i. A second use of Functor.Final.colimitIso, with FunctorToTypes.fromOverFunctorElementsEquivalence and the over-category forgetful functor, identifies the fiber of F|U_i at w with the original fiber of F. Apply this to the additive finite basis. Compatibility of constant sheafification with slice restriction makes its target the constant sheaf on ULift(ZMod N) on that slice. The point's sheafFiberConstantSheafIso identifies the fiber of this constant sheaf with ULift(ZMod N), which is finite. Finite.of_equiv therefore proves the required finiteness of the concrete root stalk. (uses `lem-fix-weakly-etale-algclosed-section`)
3. Apply exists_compatible_roots_diagram_basis of [dag-lem-poincare-compatible-primitive-roots], using the global unit already obtained. This gives one pro-etale cover and an isomorphism between the root diagram and the constant residue diagram after raw pullback. Here the raw pullback [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star] is the exact abelian pullback of [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], left adjoint by `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` to the base-change pushforward [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star]. For a weakly etale map, localization restriction is another left adjoint to this pushforward; Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq gives ProEtale.upperStarIsoLocalizationRestriction. Compare the concrete positive-index root tower with the source sheafified root tower shifted by one. The concrete transition followed by its kernel inclusion is the ell-th power, so the canonical sections equivalences form a diagram isomorphism; NatTrans.ofOpSequence checks compatibility on successive transitions, and sheafification transports it to sheaves. The coefficient tower of [dag-def-pur-twist-coefficient-rings], after forgetting to abelian sheaves, is similarly identified with the shifted constantTateResidueSystem of [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison]: constantCommuteCompose gives the component isomorphisms, and its naturality for the literal residue reductions gives transition compatibility. Finally use the dense slice equivalence of `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization` and its full faithfulness to transport the pulled-back diagram isomorphism to the slice over each member of the cover. Reindexing the covering family by ULift changes neither its arrows nor its covering property. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-poincare-compatible-primitive-roots`, `lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison`, `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`)
4. Take limits of these slice diagram isomorphisms, using the sectionwise inverse limits of [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit]. Slice restriction and forgetting commutative rings to abelian sheaves preserve these limits. Thus preservesLimitIso and HasLimit.isoOfNatIso give an additive isomorphism a from the restricted root limit to the underlying additive sheaf of the restricted completed coefficient ring. The identities preservesLimitIso_inv_π, HasLimit.isoOfNatIso_hom_π, and preservesLimitIso_hom_π_assoc show that a commutes with every projection: alpha_n(a(x)) = b_n(p_n(x)), where p_n is the root-limit projection, alpha_n is the coefficient projection, and b_n is the corresponding finite diagram basis. Each b_n is linear by the finite cyclic argument of step 1. The action specification purTwistZlAction_existsUnique from [dag-def-pur-twist-sheaves] says p_n(r x) = alpha_n(r) p_n(x). Applying all coefficient projections therefore shows a(r x) = r a(x); their joint injectivity is Concrete.isLimit_ext applied after evaluation on sections. Hence a defines the completed module basis e. The forgetful functor to abelian sheaves reflects isomorphisms, so isIso_of_reflects_iso proves that e is a module isomorphism. These bases on the single pro-etale cover give the claimed completed unit trivialization. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit])
5. For each n, the actual root-limit projection p_n is linear into the finite root module with scalars restricted along alpha_n, by the same coordinate specification of the completed action. Extend it by scalarExtensionLift of `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence` to a global map rho_n from changeOfRingsOfUniverses(alpha_n)(purTwistZlModule S ell) to purTwistRootsModule S ell n. With the actual scalar-extension sections of `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, scalarExtensionLift_tmul specifies rho_n(s tensor x) = s p_n(x). Thus this map is induced by the prescribed projection, rather than by an arbitrary choice of local bases. (uses `def-sp-scalar-tensor-comparison-data`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`)
6. On a covering slice U, let e be the completed basis, b_n its finite component, and v = e^(-1)(1). The coordinate functional e is a section of the sheaf Hom into the coefficient unit [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom]. Apply the construction scalarDualBaseSection and its formula scalarDualBaseSection_tmul from `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-evaluation` to this functional. It gives an extended coordinate map d on the scalar extension with d(s tensor x) = alpha_n(e(x)) s. The reverse map sends s to s tensor v. The identities e(v) = 1 and e(x) v = x, together with scalarExtensionSection_base_smul and scalarExtensionSection_smul from `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`, show that these maps are inverse. This is the extendedBasisIso construction. To check the composite on the scalar-extension source, equality on pure tensors suffices: the sheafification unit is locally surjective after restriction to the slice, TensorProduct.induction_on proves equality on the presheaf tensors, and sheaf separatedness descends it to all sections. This is precisely extension_over_hom_ext. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-hom], `lem-sp-scalar-extension-dual-evaluation`, `lem-sp-scalar-extension-binary-coherence`)
7. The projection identity from step 4 now gives alpha_n(e(x)) = b_n(p_n(x)). Consequently, on a pure tensor, b_n^(-1)(d(s tensor x)) = b_n^(-1)(s b_n(p_n(x))) = s p_n(x) = rho_n(s tensor x), using commutativity of the finite coefficient ring and linearity of b_n^(-1). By extension_over_hom_ext, rho_n restricted to U equals d followed by b_n^(-1), and is therefore an isomorphism. The covering family covers the terminal object, hence satisfies GrothendieckTopology.CoversTop, by GrothendieckTopology.coversTop_iff_of_isTerminal and Precoverage.generate_mem_toGrothendieck. Apply Sheaf.isIso_of_coversTop to the underlying additive map and then isIso_of_reflects_iso for the module forgetful functor. Thus every global rho_n is an isomorphism, proving that the ordinary finite scalar reductions are the prescribed finite root modules. All constructions allow empty covering members and the empty base; in the latter case there are no geometric points.
