1. The integral closure of Z_l in E is O_E: the spectral norm criterion for the coefficients of the monic minimal polynomial proves one implication, and integral closedness of the valuation ring proves the other. Finite integral closure over the complete DVR is finite as a module. Torsion freeness in E and the PID criterion therefore give a finite Z_l-basis of O_E. The valuation decomposition of l writes it as a unit times pi^e for e≥1. The private Lean helpers integral_basis, prime_uniformizer_power and coefficient_filtrations prove these parts completely. (uses `lem-sc-dvr-fg-torsionfree-free`)
2. Apply constantFiniteFree_sectionBasis to the finite integral basis. This gives finite free B(U) over A(U), with its specified scalar map, on every site object. A discrete coefficient ring has constant sheaf equal to its sheaf of continuous functions: the map from constants is locally bijective by the open-fibre cover, and the empty cover handles the empty object. A surjective coefficient homomorphism with principal kernel therefore remains surjective on constant-sheaf sections, with kernel generated by the corresponding constant section. Choose lifts and quotients pointwise; these choices are continuous on discrete rings. The private helpers discreteCoefficientComparison, continuous_principal_quotient, constantSection_principal_quotient and constantSection_power_kernel prove these assertions. (uses `lem-pur-constant-finite-free-section-basis`, `def-weil-pbc-lisse-lattice-coefficients`)
3. The positive quotient tower of any ring has its algebraic adic completion as a limit: use the quotient isomorphisms determined by surjectivity and the kernel, and insert the unique zero-th quotient coordinate. The helpers completion_positive_cone and completion_positive_iso_normalized construct the limiting cone and preserve its actual projections. Apply this to the Z/l^(n+1) constant-sheaf sections to obtain zl_section_completion. Restrict the uniformizer quotient tower to indices e(n+1)-1. This is an initial reindexing of the opposite sequence, and its ideals are l^(n+1)O_E by coefficient_filtrations. Evaluating the recorded limit and applying the same quotient argument proves integral_section_completion, including its projection and constant-section formulas. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit])
4. The helper finiteFreeCompletionTensor reduces the algebraic completion/tensor map to a finite power via a basis and uses Mathlib AdicCompletion.ofTensorProduct_bijective_of_pi_of_fintype. Identify the A-module completion of B(U) with the ring completion for the extended ideal using the identity on every quotient; mapCompletionEquiv and its evaluation formula construct this identification, following the proved private helpers of WeilConjectures.RingTheory.CompletionFiniteExtensionTensorBijective. Compose these bijections with the two section-completion isomorphisms. To verify that the resulting map is purTwistCoefficientTensorMap, test a pure tensor at any cofinal target projection. The specified completed scalar map factors through a finite Z/l^k level. Approximate its completed scalar simultaneously at that level and the target completion level by a single constant-sheaf section. The scalar-square identity then identifies the two projected products. The target projections are jointly injective, and tensor induction proves equality on all tensors. This is tensor_completion_comparison. Empty section rings and the zero-th quotient are covered by the same constructions; no Noetherian hypothesis on section rings and no invertibility hypothesis on l on S is used. (uses [dag-def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit])
