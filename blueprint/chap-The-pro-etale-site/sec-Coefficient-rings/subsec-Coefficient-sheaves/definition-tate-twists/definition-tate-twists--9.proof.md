On a pro-étale covering choose a compatible system of primitive $\ell^n$th roots of unity. It identifies $\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}(1)$ with $\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}$ and, compatibly, the integral and field twists with their coefficient sheaves. On that covering the asserted map is the associativity isomorphism for scalar extension. These maps are independent of the chosen generator, since a change of generator multiplies both sides by the same unit of $\mathbf Z_\ell$. They descend and give the required identification after the filtered colimit over finite $E$.
