Let $\ell$ be a prime, $E\subseteq\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ with ring of integers $\mathcal O_E$ `def-weil-l-adic-coefficient-extension`, topologized by its absolute value `def-sfb-coefficient-extension-integers-topology`, and $n\ge0$. Give $\mathcal O_E/\ell^{n+1}\mathcal O_E$ the discrete topology. The morphism of condensed abelian groups $\underline{\mathcal O_E}\to\underline{\mathcal O_E/\ell^{n+1}}$ between the continuous-function condensed rings `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring`, given on each profinite set by composing continuous maps with the reduction $\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_E/\ell^{n+1}$, is a morphism of ring objects `sk-def-sfb-ring-object`: it is compatible with the units and the multiplications.
