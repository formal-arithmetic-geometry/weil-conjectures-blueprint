1. Take the single pro-etale cover (u_i:U_i->X) and compatible finite-level diagram isomorphism of [dag-lem-poincare-compatible-integral-root-bases], namely exists_tateModule_residue_diagram_iso. Write R=R_X, T=tateModuleSystem X l and Q=tateResidueModuleSystem X l, both indexed by the opposite of the natural numbers, including zero. For each i, put F=u_i^* and Phi=F.mapMod R; the supplied isomorphism is e:T followed by Phi isomorphic to Q followed by Phi. (uses [dag-lem-poincare-compatible-integral-root-bases])
2. Use the canonical monoidal structures on these functors. The pushforward is the restriction functor of [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], with the lax monoidal structure of `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, and F is its left adjoint by `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. The finite-limit preservation of [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact] and the invertible tensor and unit comparisons of `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-tensor` and `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-unit` give the strong monoidal structure `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`; its compatibility with the adjunction is `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`. Together with the braidedness of `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-braided`, this supplies the module transport Phi and the unit comparison F.mapModUnitIso used below. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-tensor`, `lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-unit`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`)
3. Each covering morphism u_i is weakly etale. Its inverse-image functor is naturally isomorphic to site restriction by upperStarIsoLocalizationRestriction: restriction sends a sheaf G to the sheaf whose value on V->U_i is G(V->U_i->X). Since sheaf limits are computed objectwise, this restriction preserves limits of the countable inverse system. Transfer this preservation to F using the Mathlib result preservesLimitsOfShape_of_natIso. Lift it to Phi through the module forgetful functors, which create limits by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`. Indeed, forgetting after Phi is forgetting followed by F, so this composite preserves these limits; the target forgetful functor reflects them, as expressed by reflectsLimitsOfShape_of_reflectsIsomorphisms. Thus preservesLimitsOfShape_of_reflects_of_preserves shows that Phi preserves limits of shape Natᵒᵖ. (uses `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`)
4. The actual tateModule is the limit of its free root diagram T by [dag-def-pur-condensed-tate-twists]. Use preservesLimitIso for Phi and T, apply HasLimit.isoOfNatIso to e, and invert preservesLimitIso for Phi and Q. Their composite identifies Phi(tateModule X l) with Phi(limit Q). By [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-module-limit], tateResidueModuleCone_isLimit supplies a limiting cone on Q with vertex the regular R-module. The inverse of IsLimit.conePointUniqueUpToIso, comparing this cone with limit.isLimit Q, identifies limit Q with that regular module. Apply Phi.mapIso to this identification and then F.mapModUnitIso.symm to obtain the regular module over F(R). This gives the required basis for every member of the chosen cover. The argument imposes no further hypothesis on X; if the covering family is empty, the assertion for its members is vacuous. (uses [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-module-limit])
