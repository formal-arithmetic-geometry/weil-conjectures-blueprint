1. We use the abelian sheaves and canonical transitions of [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf]. The sheafification instance implicit in Lean's epimorphism criterion is obtained as follows. The Grothendieck abelian category AddCommGrpCat is locally presentable by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable` (CategoryTheory.IsGrothendieckAbelian.isLocallyPresentable), so its separator is presentable. On a small model of the pro-etale site choose an infinite regular cardinal large enough for this separator to be cardinal-presentable and larger than the cardinality of the arrows. The transfinite plus construction corestricts to sheaves under these bounds; [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality] (CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.W_toTransfinitePlus_app) gives its adjunction to the inclusion, and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits] (CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.preservesFiniteLimits_transfinitePlusSheaf) gives preservation of finite limits. Transporting this left exact sheafification to the essentially small pro-etale site gives the instance CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.hasSheafify_proEtTopology used in the proof. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
2. Invertibility in residue fields implies invertibility in stalks and global sections. Indeed, isUnit_of_invertible makes the image of l in each residue field nonzero; IsLocalRing.residue_ne_zero_iff_isUnit then makes l a unit in each stalk. Apply RingedSpace.isUnit_of_isUnit_germ to obtain IsUnit (l : Γ(S, ⊤)). For a test object U over S, pull this unit back along U.hom.appTop, using IsUnit.map and map_natCast. Thus l is a unit in Γ(U, ⊤). These rings and pullback maps are exactly those of [dag-def-pur-proetale-structure-sheaf]. (uses [dag-def-pur-proetale-structure-sheaf])
3. Fix a test object U and a section x of μ_(l^(n+1)). Its image under the canonical inclusion is a unit a of Γ(U, ⊤) with a^(l^(n+1)) = 1, by proEtaleRootsOfUnitySections, existsUnique_proEtaleRootsOfUnitySections and mem_rootsOfUnity in the sectionwise description of [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf]. Apply AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.exists_etale_surjective_pow_eq_appTop to l ≠ 0, the invertibility just proved and the unit a. It gives an etale surjective f : V → U and ω in Γ(V, ⊤) with ω^l = f^*a. The etale covering is a pro-etale covering: V → U → S is weakly etale, and etalePrecoverage_le_proetalePrecoverage, ProEt.mem_precoverage_iff and Precoverage.generate_mem_toGrothendieck show that the singleton arrow g : W → U, with W the object V → S, generates a covering sieve. Pullback of functions here is the restriction map of [dag-def-pur-proetale-structure-sheaf]. (uses [dag-def-pur-proetale-structure-sheaf])
4. Since ω^l = f^*a is a unit and l ≠ 0, isUnit_pow_iff shows that ω is a unit z. Using pow_succ', pow_mul and map_pow, compute z^(l^(n+2)) = (z^l)^(l^(n+1)) = f^*(a^(l^(n+1))) = 1. The canonical sectionwise kernel identification of [dag-def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf] therefore supplies a section y of μ_(l^(n+2)) over W whose image in units is z. Naturality of the inclusion into units and proEtaleRootsOfUnityTransition_prime show that the images of t(y) and x restricted to W are respectively z^l and f^*a, hence equal. The kernel inclusion is a monomorphism, and AddCommGrpCat.mono_iff_injective makes its component on W injective, so t(y) is the restriction of x. Thus the image sieve of every x contains the covering sieve generated by g; GrothendieckTopology.superset_covering gives local surjectivity. Apply the abelian-group-valued criterion Sheaf.isLocallySurjective_iff_epi', the form of the sheaf epi criterion `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective` used by Lean, with the sheafification instance above. The canonical transition t is an epimorphism for every n. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective`)
