1. Let A be X.affineOpenCover. For each affine chart, apply exists_isWContractibleRing, the source same-universe faithfully-flat ind-etale extension theorem [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-wcontractible-extension]. Its w-contractible hypotheses are the ring conditions described by [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible] and [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], and its ind-etale and weakly-etale terminology is that of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. The flatness of the filtered presentation is the same-universe result `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, and this is the concrete chartwise realization of the general w-contractible-cover principle [dag-sk-thm-pe-wc-cover]; the available ind-etale faithfully-flat refinement interface is [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-weakly-etale-indetale-refinement]. For p : Spec(D j) -> Spec(A.X j), RingHom.IndEtale.weaklyEtale gives WeaklyEtale p and flat_and_surjective_SpecMap_iff together with faithful flatness gives Surjective p. Weaken the affine open cover to the weakly-etale precoverage, take the singleton pro-etale cover p on every chart, and bind them to obtain the single Scheme.Cover B in the required universe. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-weakly-etale-indetale-refinement], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-wcontractible-extension], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], [dag-sk-thm-pe-wc-cover], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
2. Fix a chart of B and write R for its w-contractible coordinate ring. The primitiveRootsCover R l is etale and surjective, so exists_comp_eq_id_of_isWContractibleRing, using the retraction theorem [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-wcontractible-indetale-retraction], supplies a section and hence a first unit z_1 with z_1^l=1 and all differences of distinct powers units. For every unit a, the Kummer algebra AdjoinRoot (T^l-C(a)) is etale by AdjoinRoot.etale_X_pow_sub_C and faithfully flat by AdjoinRoot.faithfullyFlat_of_monic; its spectrum is therefore a surjective affine weakly-etale map, and the same retraction theorem supplies an l-th root. Iterating this choice gives z : N -> Rˣ with z 0 = 1, z (n+1)^l = z n, z n^(l^n)=1. In every field image, orderOf_eq_prime_pow shows that z n has order l^(n+1); the resulting power differences are nonzero in every field image and hence units by the proved field-image criterion. Thus the tower has exactly the hypotheses needed below. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-wcontractible-indetale-retraction])
3. For a chart scheme Y and each n, use the chosen unit z n to define rootZModHom with ZMod.lift, and use rootZModHom_natCast to identify natural-number representatives with powers of z n. The unit differences make the equalizer sieve of zmodToRootsPresheaf trivial: if two distinct residues have equal images, a unit difference maps to zero, forcing the section ring to be subsingleton and the test scheme empty. For surjectivity, take a root x, apply Polynomial.sum_inverse_mul_prod_erase_eq_one and Polynomial.X_pow_sub_one_eq_prod_of_isUnit_sub, and cover by the basic opens of the factors t-z^r; on each such open x equals the corresponding selected power. Hence ProEt.topology.W_of_isLocallyBijective and GrothendieckTopology.W_iff make the sheafification of this presheaf map an isomorphism. These are abelian sheaf maps only; the residue objects are the levelwise constant objects specified by [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison].
4. The maps at successive levels commute with l-th-power transitions: on representatives, integralResidueAbTransition is reduction modulo l^n, rawRootsTransition is computed by rawRootsTransition_val, and z (n+1)^l=z n gives the equality after raising to every representative power. NatTrans.ofOpSequence therefore assembles the level maps into rawRootsBasis; componentwise isomorphism detection via NatIso.isIso_of_isIso_app, followed by rootsSheafificationIso, yields rootsBasisIso from constantTateResidueSystem to tateRootsSystem, including n=0. The identification of the constant residue diagram is the abelian residue comparison recorded in [dag-lem-poincare-integral-residue-free-comparison].
5. For each cover morphism f : Y -> X, its weakly-etale inverse image is represented by upperStar from [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star]; the lower-star restriction description is [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], and the upper/lower adjunction is `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. The finite-limit and abelian exactness interface is [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. In Lean, upperStarIsoLocalizationRestriction identifies this inverse image with restriction of presheaves, while pushforwardContinuousSheafificationCompatibility commutes that restriction with presheafToSheaf. The raw roots and raw residues restrict by the explicit rawRootsRestrictionIso and rawResiduesRestrictionIso, so whiskering these comparisons with rootsSheafificationIso and composing rootsBasisIso.symm gives tateRootsSystem X l ⋙ upperStar f ≅ constantTateResidueSystem X l ⋙ upperStar f. Applying this to every i of B proves the required cover statement. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`)
