For every morphism of schemes $f:X\to Y$, there is a canonical monoidal
natural isomorphism
$$
f^{-1}\circ p_Y^*\xrightarrow{\sim}p_X^*
$$
on condensed abelian groups. Its naturality applies to multiplication
by $\pi_E^n$, to reduction modulo $\pi_E^n$, and to the quotient
transition maps. Thus it identifies the inverse images of the coefficient
exact sequences, two-term resolutions and their transition maps on $Y$
with the corresponding sequences, resolutions and maps on $X$.
