It suffices to work over the point, since $p_X^*$ is exact. On a profinite set $S$, multiplication by $\pi_E^n$ on continuous maps to $\mathcal O_E$ is injective and its image is the kernel of reduction. A continuous map to $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$ has finite image. Lift its finitely many values on their clopen inverse images to obtain a continuous lift to $\mathcal O_E$. This proves surjectivity.
