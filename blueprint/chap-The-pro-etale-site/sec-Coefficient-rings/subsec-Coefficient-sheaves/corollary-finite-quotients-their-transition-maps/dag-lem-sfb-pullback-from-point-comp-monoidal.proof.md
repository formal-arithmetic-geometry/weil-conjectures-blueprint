1. Work in the abelian sheaf categories with the tensor product of `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`. For a scheme morphism g, use the pullback and pushforward functors from [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star] and [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], together with the adjunction `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. The pushforwards have their lax monoidal structures from `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, and their composition isomorphism is the canonical restriction isomorphism `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`. The exactness and sheaf-level instances needed by the subsequent typeclass constructions are supplied by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]. The point construction and the identity $f\circ\pi_Y=\pi_X$ are those of `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
2. Put $a=\pi_Y$, $h=f\circ a=\pi_X$, and write $A=a^*$, $B=f^*$, $H=h^*$ and $U=a_*$, $V=f_*$, $K=h_*$. By `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal` these pullbacks carry the strong monoidal structures used in the statement, while the pushforwards carry the lax structures fixed above. The adjunctions $A\dashv U$, $B\dashv V$, and $H\dashv K$ are monoidal by `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`. Hence the composite adjunction obtained from $A\dashv U$ and $B\dashv V$ is monoidal by the Mathlib result `Adjunction.isMonoidal_comp`; its right adjoint is $UV$ and its left adjoint is $BA$ with the composite structures. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`)
3. For the canonical pushforward comparison $c=\operatorname{lowerStarComp}(Ab,a,f):UV\xrightarrow{\sim}K$, unfolding `lowerStarComp` and applying Lean's `infer_instance` gives `NatTrans.IsMonoidal c.hom`; this is the lax-monoidal compatibility of the iterated base-change restriction encoded by `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp` and `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal` in the ambient sheaf instances. Since $c$ is an isomorphism, the same elementary tensor and unit equations give monoidality of $c^{-1}$. (uses `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`)
4. By the definition of the pullback composition isomorphism `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`, its inverse is the conjugate mate of $c^{-1}$ under the composite adjunction and the adjunction $H\dashv K$. The project lemma `isMonoidal_conjugateEquiv_symm` (applied to the monoidal adjunctions from step 2) therefore yields that $(\operatorname{upperStarComp}(Ab,f,a))^{-1}:BA\to H$ is monoidal. This is exactly the monoidal comparison obtained from the lower-star comparison, with no independent mate theorem assumed. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`)
5. For the equality $h=\pi_X$ from `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, the natural isomorphism `eqToIso (congrArg (upperStar Ab) (h = \pi_X))` is monoidal: after substituting the equality its hom is the identity natural transformation, and `infer_instance` gives its unit and tensor equations. Thus the equality identification contributes no extra comparison beyond a monoidal identity. (uses `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`)
6. Finally unfold [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso] (equivalently the definition of `upperStarFromPointComp`) and reassociate the composites. Its hom is the associator followed by `pointUpperStar` whiskering of the monoidal inverse comparison from step 4 and the monoidal equality map from step 5. The functor `pointUpperStar=p^*` is strong monoidal and braided by `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided`, and `def-sfb-upper-star-from-point` identifies $p_Y^*$ and $p_X^*$ with the corresponding composites. Whiskering and composition preserve the unit and tensor equations by the definitions of the monoidal structures, so Lean's final `infer_instance` proves `NatTrans.IsMonoidal (upperStarFromPointComp f).hom`. By [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso] this is precisely the natural isomorphism $f^*\circ p_Y^*\cong p_X^*$, and hence it is compatible with both the unit and tensor comparison isomorphisms required. (uses `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided`)
