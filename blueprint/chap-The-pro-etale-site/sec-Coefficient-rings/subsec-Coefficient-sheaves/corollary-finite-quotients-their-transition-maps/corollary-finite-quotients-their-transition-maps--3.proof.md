For $f:X\to Y$, the equality $p_Yf=p_X$ identifies inverse image of the coefficient sheaves and of multiplication by $\pi_E$. Exact inverse image therefore carries the complexes and the maps $(\pi_E,\mathrm{id})$ of clause [corollary-finite-quotients-their-transition-maps--2] to their counterparts on $X$.
