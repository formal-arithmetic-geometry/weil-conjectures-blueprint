Put $R=\mathcal O_{E,X}$ and $B_n=(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_X$. For $n\geq1$, the
augmentation

$$
[R\xrightarrow{\pi_E^n}R]\longrightarrow B_n[0],
$$

with the two terms in degrees $-1,0$ and given in degree $0$ by the quotient
map of clause [corollary-finite-quotients-their-transition-maps--1], is a quasi-isomorphism. Reduction from level
$n+1$ to level $n$ is represented by the chain map $(\pi_E,\mathrm{id})$ from
$[R\xrightarrow{\pi_E^{n+1}}R]$ to $[R\xrightarrow{\pi_E^n}R]$: it is compatible
with the two augmentations and the reduction map $B_{n+1}\to B_n$, so under
these quasi-isomorphisms it is that map in $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$.
