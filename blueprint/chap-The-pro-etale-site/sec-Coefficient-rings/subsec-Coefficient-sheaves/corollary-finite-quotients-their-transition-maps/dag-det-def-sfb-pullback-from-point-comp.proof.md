1. First fix the sheafification used in the pullbacks. The coefficient category $\Ab$ is Grothendieck abelian, hence locally presentable by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`. On a small model of each site, choose an infinite regular cardinal large enough that a generator is presentable and the site has fewer arrows than that cardinal. The transfinite-plus sheaf functor of [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits] preserves finite limits. By [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], its canonical map induces a natural bijection of maps into every sheaf, so this functor is sheafification. Transport along the equivalence with the small model supplies the left exact sheafification implicit in the inverse-image constructions. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
2. By `det-def-sfb-pullback-from-point` and `def-sfb-upper-star-from-point`, the functors in the statement are $p_T^*=\pi_T^*\circ p^*$ for $T=X,Y$, and $\pi_Y\circ f=\pi_X$. The scheme pullbacks are the sheafified left Kan extensions of [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star]; they preserve finite limits and are exact by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. Their specified strong monoidal structures are those of `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, and $p^*$ is strong monoidal by `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided`. Thus the composites have the specified composite strong monoidal structures. Throughout, the tensor products are $a(\iota F\otimes_p\iota G)$ with unit $a(\mathbf1)$, as in `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`, specialized to $\mathcal A=\Ab$. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided`)
3. Put $g=\pi_Y\circ f$. The pushforwards of [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] are restriction along base change. Their composition isomorphism $r:\pi_{Y*}\circ f_*\xrightarrow{\sim}g_*$ is the one of `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`, induced by the canonical pullback-pasting isomorphism. Using the adjunctions of `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.conjugateIsoEquiv` takes $r^{-1}$ to an isomorphism $d:f^*\circ\pi_Y^*\xrightarrow{\sim}g^*$. In Lean, `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.upperStarComp` is defined as the inverse of this mate, so $d$ is precisely its inverse, rather than an unspecified comparison of these functors. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`)
4. Define the required natural isomorphism by first reassociating $f^*\circ(\pi_Y^*\circ p^*)$, then composing $d$ with $p^*$, and finally using $g=\pi_X$. These are exactly Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Functor.associator`, `CategoryTheory.Functor.isoWhiskerLeft`, and `eqToIso` applied to the equality of pullback functors induced by `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.comp_toSpecZ`. On a condensed abelian group $F$, its component is $c_{p^*F}^{-1}$ followed by the equality identification, where $c=d^{-1}$. Thus its direction, components, and action on morphisms agree with the construction required here.
5. It remains to prove monoidality of this very comparison. Equip every pushforward with the lax structure of `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`. By `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, $g^*\dashv g_*$ is monoidal, and so are $\pi_Y^*\dashv\pi_{Y*}$ and $f^*\dashv f_*$. Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.isMonoidal_comp` therefore makes their composite adjunction $f^*\circ\pi_Y^*\dashv\pi_{Y*}\circ f_*$ monoidal, for the composite structures already fixed above. (uses `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`)
6. Unfolding $r$ gives `CategoryTheory.Functor.sheafPushforwardContinuousComp'`; its hom is monoidal by `CategoryTheory.Functor.isMonoidal_sheafPushforwardContinuousComp'_hom`. Concretely, restriction along the pullback-pasting isomorphism commutes with the unit and tensor maps of the lax pushforwards. Hence $r^{-1}$ is monoidal as well. Apply `CategoryTheory.isMonoidal_conjugateEquiv_symm` to the two monoidal adjunctions above: its mate $d$, the inverse of `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.upperStarComp`, is monoidal. This proves the needed compatibility for the specific composition comparison built from `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`. (uses `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`)
7. The equality identification $g^*\cong\pi_X^*$ is monoidal: eliminate the equality $g=\pi_X$, after which its hom is the identity. Whiskering the monoidal comparison $d$ and this identification by the strong monoidal $p^*$ of `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided` preserves monoidality, as expressed by Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.NatTrans.IsMonoidal.whiskerLeft`. The functor associator is monoidal, and monoidal natural transformations are closed under composition. Therefore the natural isomorphism constructed above is compatible with both the tensor comparison and the unit comparison for the specified composite strong monoidal structures, as required. (uses `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided`)
