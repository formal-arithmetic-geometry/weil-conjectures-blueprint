Clause [corollary-finite-quotients-their-transition-maps--1] identifies the cokernel of multiplication by $\pi_E^n$ with the quotient and makes its kernel zero, so the two-term complex is its resolution. The equality $\pi_E^n\pi_E=\pi_E^{n+1}$ verifies the chain-map condition, and its degree-zero identity induces the quotient projection.
