1. Work with the abelian sheaf categories on the pro-étale sites and their sheaf tensor products. For every scheme morphism g, [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] defines g_* by restriction along base change, and `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` gives the adjunction g^* ⊣ g_*. The sheafification and size hypotheses needed for these constructions are supplied by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]; the resulting pullback is exact as recorded in [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
2. Give each g_* its lax monoidal structure from `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`. The pullback g^* has the specified strong monoidal structure of `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, and `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction` identifies the adjunction g^* ⊣ g_* as monoidal. For the point, the functor of profinite test objects is the one defined in `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, and its inverse image p^* is strong monoidal and braided by `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided`. These are the structures used when the comparison is whiskered by p^*. (uses `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, `lem-sfb-point-upper-star-braided`)
3. Put π_Y=toSpecZ Y. Unfolding `upperStarFromPointComp`, Lean defines the natural isomorphism by `Functor.associator`, followed by `Functor.isoWhiskerLeft pointUpperStar` applied to `(upperStarComp Ab f π_Y).symm` and then to the equality isomorphism `eqToIso (congrArg (upperStar Ab) (comp_toSpecZ f))`. Here `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp` supplies the pullback composition isomorphism as the mate of the pushforward composition isomorphism `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`, so its inverse has the direction f^* ∘ π_Y^* ≅ (π_Y ∘ f)^*. Since `comp_toSpecZ f` says π_Y ∘ f=π_X, this composite is exactly f^*∘p_Y^* ≅ p_X^*. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`)
4. For the monoidality proof, the Lean declaration constructs `h₁ : Adjunction.IsMonoidal` for the adjunction of the composite f∘π_Y and `h₂ : Adjunction.IsMonoidal` for the composite of the adjunctions of f and π_Y; the latter is `Adjunction.isMonoidal_comp`, using `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`. The hom of the pushforward compositor `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp` is monoidal for the composite lax structures supplied by `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`. Therefore `isMonoidal_conjugateEquiv_symm` makes `(upperStarComp Ab f π_Y).inv` monoidal, exactly the `hc` step in the Lean proof; `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp` identifies this inverse as the mate used above. (uses `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-comp`)
5. Finally, `Functor.isoWhiskerLeft` and the associator preserve `NatTrans.IsMonoidal`, while the equality isomorphism is monoidal by the Lean proof's `he` case: after substituting the equality of the two structure morphisms it is `eqToIso rfl`, whose component is the identity and is handled by typeclass inference. The final `infer_instance` therefore proves `NatTrans.IsMonoidal (upperStarFromPointComp f).hom`; together with the explicit composite in the preceding step, this gives the required monoidal natural isomorphism f^*∘p_Y^* ≅ p_X^*.
