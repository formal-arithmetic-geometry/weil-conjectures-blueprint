For every $n\geq1$ there is a canonical exact sequence on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{E,X}
\xrightarrow{\pi_E^n}\mathcal O_{E,X}
\longrightarrow(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_X\longrightarrow0.
$$

Here the coefficient sheaves are obtained by pulling back the continuous
condensed coefficients from the point, and the quotient carries its
restricted $\mathcal O_{E,X}$-action. The arrows are the pullbacks of
multiplication by the chosen uniformizer power and the quotient map.
There is no assumption that $\ell$ is invertible on $X$.
