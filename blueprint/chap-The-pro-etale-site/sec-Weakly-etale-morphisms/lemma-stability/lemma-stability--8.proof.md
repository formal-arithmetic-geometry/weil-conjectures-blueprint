The diagonal of $\operatorname{Spec}(B)\to\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ is the closed immersion $\operatorname{Spec}(B)\to\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_RB)$ given by the multiplication map. Hence the morphism and its diagonal are flat exactly when $R\to B$ and $B\otimes_RB\to B$ are flat, which is the definition of weak étaleness.
