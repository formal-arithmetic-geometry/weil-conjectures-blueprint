For $V\to U\to X$, the composite $V\to X$ is flat. Its diagonal factors as $\Delta_{V/U}$ followed by $V\times_UV\to V\times_XV$. The second map is a base change of $\Delta_{U/X}$. Both factors are flat, hence so is the diagonal of $V\to X$.
