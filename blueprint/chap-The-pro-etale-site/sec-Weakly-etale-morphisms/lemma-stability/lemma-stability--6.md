Let $R\to B$ be an ind-étale map of commutative rings and let
$R\to R'$ be any ring map. Then $R'\to R'\otimes_RB$ is ind-étale.
Equivalently, in any pushout square of commutative rings, the base
change of an ind-étale map is ind-étale.
