Write $B=\varinjlim_\alpha B_\alpha$ with $B_\alpha$ étale over $R$. For an $R$-algebra $R'$, tensor product gives $B\otimes_RR'=\varinjlim_\alpha(B_\alpha\otimes_RR')$. Each algebra in the latter system is étale over $R'$, so the base-changed map is ind-étale.

*Source:* the proof of Lemma [lemma-stability].
