Flatness of $U\to X$ is local on $U$. The diagonal can be checked near every point of its source $U$; after replacing $U$ by an open neighbourhood, its restriction is the diagonal for that neighbourhood. Thus the two required flatness conditions hold on $U$ if they hold on a Zariski cover.
