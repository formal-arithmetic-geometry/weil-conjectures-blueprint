By (2) the pro-étale topology is finer than the étale topology. By (1),
over an affine object a covering may always be replaced by a single
faithfully flat map. For example, the single map
$\operatorname{Spec}\overline{\mathbf F}_q\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$
is a pro-étale covering, although it is not an étale one. These
coverings define a topos with the expected functoriality in $X$.
