1. A weakly étale $V\to Y$ base changes to a weakly étale $V\times_YZ\to Z$, so base change
is a functor of sites. It sends covering families to covering families, since pro-étale
covers (fpqc covers by weakly étale maps) are stable under base change. It preserves
finite limits, which exist in the slice $Y_{\mathrm{proét}}$, so it is representably flat
and preserves compatible families. Hence it is continuous, and $g_*G$ is a sheaf for every
sheaf $G$.
