1. The small pro-étale sites [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site] are essentially small, and a Grothendieck abelian category is cocomplete, so the pointwise left Kan extension along $b^{\mathrm{op}}$ exists. More precisely, the indexing comma categories are essentially small in the coefficient universe; Mathlib's CategoryTheory.Limits.hasColimitsOfShape_of_essentiallySmall supplies their colimits, as used in CategoryTheory.Functor.hasPointwiseLeftKanExtension_of_hasColimitsOfSize.
2. For sheafification on $Z_{\mathrm{proét}}$, first pass to a small equivalent site $(C,J)$. By CategoryTheory.IsGrothendieckAbelian.isLocallyPresentable `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, the separator $G$ of $\mathcal A$ is presentable. Choose an infinite regular cardinal $\kappa$ such that $G$ is $\kappa$-presentable and $|\operatorname{Mor}(C)|<\kappa$. The separator is a detector since $\mathcal A$ is abelian. Under these hypotheses the transfinite plus construction corestricts to sheaves, and this corestriction preserves finite limits by CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.preservesFiniteLimits_transfinitePlusSheaf [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits]. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
3. For every presheaf $P$ and sheaf $H$ on $(C,J)$, precomposition with $P\to Q_\kappa(P)$ is a natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}(Q_\kappa(P),H)\simeq\operatorname{Hom}(P,H)$ by [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]. Thus the corestriction is left adjoint to the inclusion of sheaves; this is CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.transfinitePlusAdjunction, using CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.W_toTransfinitePlus_app. Together with finite-limit preservation this gives HasSheafify via CategoryTheory.HasSheafify.mk'. Transporting along the equivalence with the small model, using Mathlib's CategoryTheory.hasSheafifyEssentiallySmallSite, gives sheafification on $Z_{\mathrm{proét}}$. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality])
4. Define $g^*$ by composing the inclusion of sheaves on $Y_{\mathrm{proét}}$ into presheaves, the left Kan extension along $b^{\mathrm{op}}$, and sheafification on $Z_{\mathrm{proét}}$. This is precisely Mathlib's CategoryTheory.Functor.sheafPullbackConstruction.sheafPullback used in the Lean definition: its value on $F$ is $\operatorname{sheafify}(\operatorname{Lan}_{b^{\mathrm{op}}}F)$, as asserted.
