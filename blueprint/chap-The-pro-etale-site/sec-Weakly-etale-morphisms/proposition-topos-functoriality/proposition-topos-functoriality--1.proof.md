The precoverage is induced from the pro-étale precoverage on schemes.
Its closure under composition and base change follows from the
corresponding properties of fpqc coverings and the stability of weakly
étale morphisms in [lemma-stability]. Isomorphisms are coverings as well.
The sheafification instances for this topology supply the left exact
left adjoint to the inclusion of sheaves into presheaves, for sets and
for abelian groups in universe $u+1$.
