1. Let $\mathcal A$ be the Grothendieck abelian category of [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], and write $C=Y_{\mathrm{proét}}$, $D=Z_{\mathrm{proét}}$, $b(V)=V\times_Y Z$. Work in the small-site and coefficient universe fixed by [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]. In Lean these are the categories of weakly étale objects in the scheme slices, and $b$ is `MorphismProperty.Over.pullback`. The identity of $Y$ is terminal in $C$, by Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.mkIdTerminal`. Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.hasPullbacks` gives pullbacks in this slice subcategory: the weakly étale property is stable under composition and base change and satisfies post-cancellation, so a morphism between weakly étale objects is weakly étale and its pullback remains in the subcategory. Thus [lem-weil-pbc-finite-limits-from-terminal-pullbacks](1), implemented by `CategoryTheory.Limits.hasFiniteLimits_of_hasTerminal_and_pullbacks`, gives finite limits in $C$ (and likewise in $D$). Base change sends the terminal object $Y$ to $Z$ and preserves these pullbacks: $(V_1\times_{V_0}V_2)\times_Y Z$ and $(V_1\times_Y Z)\times_{V_0\times_Y Z}(V_2\times_Y Z)$ represent the same compatible pairs of maps over $Z$. Hence [lem-weil-pbc-finite-limits-from-terminal-pullbacks](2) gives finite-limit preservation by $b$, as in Mathlib's slice pullback instance. No weakly étale hypothesis on $g$ is used. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], `lem-weil-pbc-finite-limits-from-terminal-pullbacks`)
2. For $W\in D$, form $I_W$ with objects $(V,\phi:W\to b(V))$ and morphisms $a:V\to V'$ with $b(a)\phi=\phi'$. It is cofiltered. It contains $(Y,W\to Z)$, since $Z=b(Y)$ is terminal. Two objects $(V,\phi),(V',\phi')$ receive arrows from $(V\times_Y V',(\phi,\phi'))$, using the product isomorphism preserved by $b$. For parallel arrows $a,a':(V,\phi)\rightrightarrows(V',\phi')$, take their equalizer $e:E\to V$ in $C$. Because $b$ preserves it and $b(a)\phi=b(a')\phi$, the universal property gives a unique $\psi:W\to b(E)$ with $b(e)\psi=\phi$; then $e:(E,\psi)\to(V,\phi)$ equalizes the arrows. This is the representable flatness of $b$ supplied in Lean by Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.flat_of_preservesFiniteLimits`. The equivalence `CategoryTheory.structuredArrowOpEquivalence` identifies $I_W^{\mathrm{op}}$ with `CostructuredArrow b.op (op W)`. It is therefore a nonempty filtered category in the sense of `def-weil-pbc-category-colimit`, small in the chosen coefficient universe (or replaced by an equivalent small model). (uses `def-weil-pbc-category-colimit`)
3. By [dag-lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits], $\mathcal A$ has the small colimits required by [dag-lem-sfb-pointwise-kan-colimit]. Apply the latter to $b:C\to D$. At $W$ it identifies $\operatorname{Lan}_{b^{\mathrm{op}}}$, naturally in the presheaf, with $F\mapsto\operatorname{colim}_{I_W^{\mathrm{op}}}F(V)$. In particular an arrow opposite to $a:V\to V'$ acts as $F(a):F(V')\to F(V)$, so the colimit has the required variance. In the Lean proof this natural identification is `CategoryTheory.Functor.lanEvaluationIsoColim'`, the version of Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Functor.lanEvaluationIsoColim` using existence of pointwise Kan extensions and colimits of the comma-category shape. Smallness is checked in the same universe as cocompleteness, not in the scheme universe alone. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-pointwise-kan-colimit])
4. Let $K$ be a finite diagram of $\mathcal A$-valued presheaves on $C$. Limits of presheaves are objectwise by [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise](1), so restriction to $I_W^{\mathrm{op}}$ commutes with its limit. Step 2 makes this index filtered; [dag-lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits] therefore makes the canonical comparison $\operatorname{colim}_{I_W^{\mathrm{op}}}\lim_j K_j(V)\to\lim_j\operatorname{colim}_{I_W^{\mathrm{op}}}K_j(V)$ invertible. Under the natural isomorphism of [dag-lem-sfb-pointwise-kan-colimit], this is exactly the finite-limit comparison for evaluation at $W$ after $\operatorname{Lan}_{b^{\mathrm{op}}}$. As this holds for every $W$, the detection assertion of [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise](1), used in Lean as Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Limits.preservesLimitsOfShape_of_evaluation`, proves that the Kan-extension functor preserves finite limits. This is the argument of `CategoryTheory.preservesFiniteLimits_op_lan_of_flat`, specialized by `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.preservesFiniteLimits_op_lan_overPullback_of_isGrothendieckAbelian`. It includes the empty finite diagram, since filtered colimits preserve the terminal object as well. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-pointwise-kan-colimit], `lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise`)
5. We next obtain left exact sheafification by the transfinite plus construction used in Lean. By `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, implemented by Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.IsGrothendieckAbelian.isLocallyPresentable`, the Grothendieck abelian category $\mathcal A$ is locally presentable. Its generator $G$ is therefore presentable for some regular cardinal; enlarge that cardinal to an infinite regular $\kappa$ for which $G$ is $\kappa$-presentable and each of the chosen small sites has fewer than $\kappa$ arrows. The small limits and colimits and AB5 needed for the plus iteration are supplied by [dag-lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits]. For either site $(E,J)$, iterate $P^+(U)=\operatorname{colim}_{S\in J(U)^{\mathrm{op}}}M_S(P)$ through $\kappa$, taking colimits at limit stages, to obtain $Q_\kappa(P)$ with unit $\eta_P$. By [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits] its corestriction $Q_\kappa^{\operatorname{Sh}}$ to sheaves preserves finite limits; this is `CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.preservesFiniteLimits_transfinitePlusSheaf`. By [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], precomposition with $\eta_P$ gives a natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}(Q_\kappa(P),F)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(P,F)$ for every sheaf $F$. Since $Q_\kappa(P)$ is sheaf-valued here, this identifies the corestriction as left adjoint to the sheaf inclusion, as in `CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.transfinitePlusAdjunction`. Thus it is sheafification. Any chosen sheafification is naturally isomorphic to it by uniqueness of left adjoints (Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq`), so sheafification preserves finite limits on both sites. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
6. The inclusion of sheaves on $C$ into presheaves preserves finite limits by [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise](2). Combining this with steps 4 and 5 and the defining formula of [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], the canonical comparison for $g^*=a_D\operatorname{Lan}_{b^{\mathrm{op}}}\iota_C$ is the composite of three invertible comparison maps. Thus $g^*$ preserves finite limits. This is precisely the unfolding of `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.upperStar` and `CategoryTheory.Functor.sheafPullbackConstruction.sheafPullback` in `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.preservesFiniteLimits_upperStar`, followed by the composition instances `CategoryTheory.Limits.comp_preservesFiniteLimits`. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise`)
7. The adjunction of `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` and `lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits` show that $g^*$ preserves finite colimits as well; Lean uses Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.leftAdjoint_preservesColimits`. The sheaf categories are abelian by Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.sheafIsAbelian`, using the left exact sheafification established in step 5. The restriction functor $g_*$ of [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] is additive, since addition of natural transformations is objectwise. Hence the same adjunction makes $g^*$ additive by Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.left_adjoint_additive`, exactly as in `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.additive_upperStar`. Finite-limit preservation now supplies preservation of kernels, and finite-colimit preservation supplies preservation of cokernels; additivity supplies preservation of zero morphisms. These are the two fields of Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Functor.PreservesHomology`, filled in `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.preservesHomology_upperStar`. For a short exact sequence $0\to F'\xrightarrow{i}F\xrightarrow{p}F''\to0$, $i$ is a kernel of $p$ and $p$ is a cokernel of $i$. Their images have the same universal properties, so the image sequence is short exact. This proves the asserted exactness. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits`)
