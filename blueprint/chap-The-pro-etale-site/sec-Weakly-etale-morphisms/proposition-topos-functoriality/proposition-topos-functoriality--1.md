The coverings of [definition-small-pro-etale-site] contain isomorphisms
and are stable under composition and base change. For the resulting
topology on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$, sheafification exists for
presheaves of sets and for presheaves of abelian groups, and preserves
finite limits. If $X$ is a scheme in a universe $u$, the sets and abelian
groups are taken in universe $u+1$.
