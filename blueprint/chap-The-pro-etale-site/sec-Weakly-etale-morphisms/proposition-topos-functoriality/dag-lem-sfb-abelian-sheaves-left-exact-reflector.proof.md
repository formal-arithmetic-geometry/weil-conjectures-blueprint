1. Write P = Fun(C^op, B) and S = Sh(C, J; B). The stated condition on Hom_B(T, F(-)) is exactly Mathlib's CategoryTheory.Presheaf.IsSheaf, so S is its category CategoryTheory.Sheaf J B and i is CategoryTheory.sheafToPresheaf J B. Since B is abelian `def-sp-abelian`, P is abelian by CategoryTheory.Abelian.functorCategoryAbelian. The full subcategory S inherits its preadditive structure, and finite products of sheaves are computed in P, so S has finite products.
2. Let adj be the given adjunction L ⊣ i. The hypothesis that L preserves finite limits `def-galois-finite-limits-colimits` allows us to apply CategoryTheory.HasSheafify.mk' J B adj. This packages the adjunction and left exactness as HasSheafify J B: the chosen left adjoint i.leftAdjoint is naturally isomorphic to L by Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq, and preservesLimitsOfShape_of_natIso transfers preservation of each finite diagram along this isomorphism. Thus the canonical presheafToSheaf J B is left exact and has the adjunction CategoryTheory.sheafificationAdjunction J B with i.
3. Apply CategoryTheory.sheafIsAbelian. Its proof takes adj' = CategoryTheory.sheafificationAdjunction J B and applies CategoryTheory.abelianOfAdjunction with F = i, G = presheafToSheaf J B, and the natural isomorphism asIso adj'.counit : i ⋙ G ≅ 𝟭 S. The counit is invertible because i is fully faithful (Adjunction.counit_isIso_of_R_fully_faithful). The required preservation of zero morphisms by G follows from its preservation of the zero object: it preserves initial objects as a left adjoint, and the initial sheaf is also terminal since limits of sheaves are computed in P. The transfer theorem therefore applies to the abelian category P, the preadditive category S with finite products, and the left exact left adjoint G. It constructs kernels and cokernels in S and transfers the invertibility of coimage-to-image comparisons from P, yielding Abelian S, as required.
