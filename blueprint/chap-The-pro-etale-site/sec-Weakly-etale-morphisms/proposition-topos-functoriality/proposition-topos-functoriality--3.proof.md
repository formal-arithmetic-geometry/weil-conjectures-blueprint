The canonical isomorphism
$(V\times_ZY)\times_YX\simeq V\times_ZX$ induces, by restriction,
the natural isomorphism $g_*\circ f_*\simeq(g\circ f)_*$.
Compose the adjunctions for $f$ and $g$ in
[proposition-topos-functoriality--2], and take the corresponding
isomorphism between their left adjoints. This is the stated
comparison for inverse images. The unit identity for conjugate
natural transformations gives its compatibility with the
chosen adjunctions.
