The base change functors for $f$ and $g$ compose via the canonical identification $(V\times_ZY)\times_YX\simeq V\times_ZX$. The induced morphisms of topoi therefore compose. Equivalently, composing the inverse-image left adjoints gives the inverse image for $g\circ f$, and uniqueness of adjoints gives the stated canonical isomorphism.
