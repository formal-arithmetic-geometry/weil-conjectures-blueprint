Let $(\mathcal C,J)$ be a small site `def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology` and $\mathcal B$ an abelian category `def-sp-abelian`. Let $\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal C,J;\mathcal B)$ be the full subcategory of $\operatorname{Fun}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}},\mathcal B)$ consisting of $F$ such that $U\mapsto\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal B}(T,F(U))$ is a sheaf of sets for every $T\in\mathcal B$. Suppose its inclusion $i$ has a left adjoint $L$ which preserves finite limits `def-galois-finite-limits-colimits`. Then $\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal C,J;\mathcal B)$ is abelian.
