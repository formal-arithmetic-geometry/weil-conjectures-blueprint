A scheme morphism $u:U\to X$ is *weakly étale* if $u$ and its
diagonal $\Delta_u:U\to U\times_XU$ are flat. On affine charts this
means that both

$$
R\longrightarrow B,
\qquad B\otimes_RB\longrightarrow B
$$

are flat.
