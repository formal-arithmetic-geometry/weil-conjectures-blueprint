An étale morphism is weakly étale and is open, since it is flat and locally of finite presentation. On a quasi-compact affine test object, finitely many affine pieces of an étale covering therefore cover. This verifies the covering condition; Zariski coverings are a special case. See [Stacks, [098B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/098B)].
