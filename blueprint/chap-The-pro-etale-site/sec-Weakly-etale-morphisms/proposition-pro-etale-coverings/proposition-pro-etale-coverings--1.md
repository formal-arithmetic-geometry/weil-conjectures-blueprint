Let $U$ be a quasi-compact, quasi-separated object of
$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$, and let $R$ be a covering sieve on $U$.
There are finitely many objects $V_1,\ldots,V_n$ with affine underlying
schemes and morphisms $g_i:V_i\to U$ belonging to $R$ such that the
sieve generated by the $g_i$ is covering.
