This converse is bypassed by the mainline Lean proof of the Weil
conjectures, although it is proved in the Lean source.

Let $f:X\to Y$ be a weakly étale morphism of schemes which is
locally of finite presentation. Then $f$ is étale. In particular,
if $R\to B$ is weakly étale and of finite presentation, it is étale.
