For (2) we show that $R\to B$ is unramified, which together with
flatness and finite presentation means that it is étale. The
multiplication map $B\otimes_RB\to B$ is surjective, and it is flat by
assumption. It is of finite presentation because $B$ is of finite
presentation over $R$. Hence the diagonal of
$\operatorname{Spec}(B)\to\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ is a closed immersion
that is flat and of finite presentation. Its ideal $I$ is then finitely
generated with $I=I^2$, hence generated by an idempotent, so the diagonal
is also an open immersion. A morphism locally of finite type whose diagonal is
an open immersion is unramified, and this proves (2).
