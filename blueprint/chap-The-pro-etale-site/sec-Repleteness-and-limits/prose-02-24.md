More generally, the $R^1\varprojlim$ term vanishes if, on every affine
weakly contractible $W$, the tower of section modules satisfies the
Mittag–Leffler condition. To see this, note that evaluation on $W$
preserves cokernels
(Corollary [corollary-evaluation-weakly-contractible-objects]). So the
cokernel of $1-T$ evaluated on $W$ is the cokernel of $1-T$ on the tower
$(M_n(W))$, which vanishes by the Mittag–Leffler lemma [Stacks, [07KV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07KV)]. As
evaluation on these $W$ detects vanishing, the $R^1\varprojlim$ term
vanishes.
