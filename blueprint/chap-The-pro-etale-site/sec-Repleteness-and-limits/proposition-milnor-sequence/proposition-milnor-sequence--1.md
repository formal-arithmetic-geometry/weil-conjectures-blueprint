For a countable tower of $\mathcal R$-modules, $R^p\varprojlim=0$
for $p>1$, and

$$
R^1\varprojlim_n M_n
=\coker\left(\prod_n M_n\xrightarrow{\,1-T\,}\prod_n M_n\right).
$$
