For a tower $(K_n)$ of complexes there is a short exact sequence of
sheaves

$$
0\longrightarrow R^1\varprojlim_n H^{q-1}(K_n)
\longrightarrow H^q(R\varprojlim_nK_n)
\longrightarrow \varprojlim_n H^q(K_n)
\longrightarrow 0.
\tag{2.2}
$$
