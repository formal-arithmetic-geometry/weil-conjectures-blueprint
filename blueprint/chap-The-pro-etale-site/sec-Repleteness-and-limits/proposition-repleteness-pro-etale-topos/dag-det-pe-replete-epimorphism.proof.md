1. Fix a scheme X, an inverse sequence F of sheaves of sets on the site described in `sk-def-pe-site`, its epimorphic adjacent maps, and an index m. Let p_m = limit.π(F, op m), the coordinate projection from [dag-det-pe-replete-definition]. By Mathlib CategoryTheory.Sheaf.isLocallySurjective_iff_epi, in the form recorded in `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective`, it is enough to prove local surjectivity: for every site object U and y in F_m(U), the image sieve of y under p_m is covering. Fix U and y. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective`)
2. Use AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.ProEt.exists_isWContractibleRing_cover U to obtain objects W_i = Spec(D_i), maps f_i : W_i → U, w-contractible ring data, and a covering sieve generated by the f_i. This is the affine pro-etale covering supplied by [dag-sk-thm-pe-wc-cover]: its ring extension is the closed construction [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-wcontractible-extension], with ind-etale meaning [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and the corresponding pro-etale and weakly-etale morphism convention [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. The geometric w-local and w-contractible conditions used for the rings are those of [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal] and [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible]; the same-universe flatness ingredient in the source construction is `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. Thus the cover is available for this arbitrary U, with no quasi-compactness assumption. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, `lem-weil-pbc-source-wcontractible-extension`, `sk-thm-pe-wc-cover`)
3. For each i, let e_i : W_i.left ≅ Spec(D_i) be the displayed affine isomorphism. The source evaluation result [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-affine-wcontractible-evaluation], together with the weak-contractibility predicate [dag-sk-def-pe-weak-contractible], allows Lean to apply AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.ProEt.isWeaklyContractible_of_iso e_i; write the resulting assertion as hW_i that W_i is weakly contractible in the section universe of F. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-affine-wcontractible-evaluation], [dag-sk-def-pe-weak-contractible])
4. Apply `det-pe-replete-on-contractible` to the original tower F, its epimorphic transitions, and each (W_i, hW_i). Its Lean theorem proetale_surjective_sections_of_epi_tower gives surjectivity of every evaluated limit projection, so for the restriction of y to W_i there is a section x_i of (limit F)(W_i) with p_m(W_i)(x_i) = y restricted to W_i. If an arrow a : V → W_i is followed by f_i, naturality restricts x_i to a lift over V of y restricted to V. (uses `det-pe-replete-on-contractible`)
5. For every arrow g : V → U in the generating covering sieve, write g = f_i ∘ a for some i and a : V → W_i. The preceding lift says that g belongs to the image sieve of y under p_m; in Lean this is CategoryTheory.Presheaf.imageSieve followed by Sieve.downward_closed applied to the lift over W_i. Hence (ProEt.topology X).superset_covering _ hcov makes that image sieve covering. The local-surjectivity criterion `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective` now gives that p_m is an epimorphism. Since F and m were arbitrary, [dag-det-pe-replete-definition] yields that the sheaf topos on X_proet is replete. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective`)
