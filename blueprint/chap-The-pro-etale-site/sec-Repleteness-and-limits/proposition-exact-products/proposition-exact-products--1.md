Let $X$ be a scheme, $\mathcal R$ a sheaf of rings on
$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$, and $I$ a set. The product functor
$$
\prod_{i\in I}:
\operatorname{Mod}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal R)^I
\longrightarrow \operatorname{Mod}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal R)
$$
is exact. In particular, the product of any $I$-indexed family of short
exact sequences is short exact.
