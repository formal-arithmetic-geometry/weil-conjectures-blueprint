Let $\mathcal R$ be a sheaf of rings on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$. For every set $I$ and every
family of short exact sequences $0\to A_i\to B_i\to C_i\to0$ of $\mathcal R$-modules,
the sequence $0\to\prod_iA_i\to\prod_iB_i\to\prod_iC_i\to0$ is exact. Equivalently, the
functor $\prod_{i\in I}\colon\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal R)^I\to
\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal R)$ is exact.
