The functor $R\underline\Gamma_Z$ is computed by

$$
R\underline\Gamma_ZK\simeq i^*\fib(K\longrightarrow Rj_*j^*K),
$$

where $K\to Rj_*j^*K$ is the unit of $j^*\dashv Rj_*$.
