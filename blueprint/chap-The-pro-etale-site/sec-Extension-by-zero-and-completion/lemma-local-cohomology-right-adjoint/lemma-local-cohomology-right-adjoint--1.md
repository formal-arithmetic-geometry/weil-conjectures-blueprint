The functor $R\underline\Gamma_Z$ is right adjoint to the exact fully
faithful functor $i_*:D(Z_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},i^*\mathcal R)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal R)$.
