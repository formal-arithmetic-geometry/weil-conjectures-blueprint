Represent $K$ by a K-injective complex $J$ with injective terms, so that
$Rj_*j^*K$ is represented by $j_*j^*J$. The unit $J\to j_*j^*J$ is
surjective in each degree: for an injective $\mathcal R$-module $I$ and an
object $V$, a section of $j_*j^*I$ over $V$ is a map
$j_!j^*\mathcal R_V\to I$, and it extends along the monomorphism
$j_!j^*\mathcal R_V\to\mathcal R_V$. Hence the termwise kernel of
$J\to j_*j^*J$ represents the fibre of the unit. By definition,
$R\underline\Gamma_ZK$ is $i^*$ of this kernel, and $i^*$ is exact, so
$R\underline\Gamma_ZK\simeq i^*\fib(K\to Rj_*j^*K)$.
