On the point, multiplication by $\pi_E^n$ is injective on continuous
$\mathcal O_E$-valued functions. Continuous division identifies its
image with the kernel of reduction, and the finite discrete quotient
admits continuous lifts on clopen fibres. Exact inverse image transports
this coefficient sequence to $X$, giving the two-term resolution in
[corollary-finite-quotients-their-transition-maps].

Its two terms are free of rank one, so this bounded complex is K-flat.
The comparison between derived tensor with its cokernel and the cone of
its scalar action identifies tensoring with this resolution with the
mapping cone. On complexes the differential is
$(x,y)\mapsto(dx+\pi_E^n y,-dy)$. This comparison is natural in chain
maps and gives the displayed isomorphism after localization.
