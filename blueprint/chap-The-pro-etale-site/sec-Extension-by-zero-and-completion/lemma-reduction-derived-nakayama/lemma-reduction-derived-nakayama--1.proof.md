Corollary [corollary-finite-quotients-their-transition-maps] resolves $(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_X$ by the two-term complex $[\mathcal O_{E,X}\xrightarrow{\pi_E^n}\mathcal O_{E,X}]$. Derived tensoring this resolution with $K$ gives precisely the cone of $\pi_E^n:K\to K$, which is $K_n$ by definition.
