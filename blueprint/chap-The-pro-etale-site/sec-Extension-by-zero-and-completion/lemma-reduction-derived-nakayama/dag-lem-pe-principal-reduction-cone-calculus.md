Let $\ell$ be prime, $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ finite with valuation ring $\mathcal O_E$ and uniformizer $\pi$, and $X$ any scheme. Put $R=\mathcal O_{E,X}=p_X^*\underline{\mathcal O_E}$, with the coefficient sheaves of `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`. Use unbounded cochain complexes of $R$-module sheaves and the cone, shift and localization conventions of `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`. For $n\ge1$ define $D_n(M)=\operatorname{Cone}(\pi^n:M\to M)$, with diagonal maps on chain maps, transition $D_{n+1}(M)\to D_n(M)$ given by $(x,y)\mapsto(x,\pi y)$, and coaugmentation $j_n:M\to D_n(M)$ given by $m\mapsto(m,0)$. This is a functorial strict model of the derived reduction tower of [dag-sk-def-pe-derived-completion]: writing $K_n=K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_R(\mathcal O_E/\pi^n)_X$, regarded as an $R$-module object by restriction of scalars, there are isomorphisms $QD_n(M)\cong(QM)_n$ in $D(R)$, natural in $M$, carrying $QD_n$ of the transition to the canonical transition $K_{n+1}\to K_n$ and $Q(j_n)$ to the canonical reduction map $K\to K_n$. Each $D_n$ preserves quasi-isomorphisms and induces an additive exact endofunctor of $D(R)$. For a chain map $f:A\to B$ its cone comparison
$$D_n(\operatorname{Cone}(f))\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Cone}(D_n(f))$$
is $(b,a,b',a')\mapsto(b,b',a,-a')$ in the ordered coordinates $B^q,A^{q+1},B^{q+1},A^{q+2}$. The shift comparison $D_n(M[r])\xrightarrow{\sim}D_n(M)[r]$ is $(x,y)\mapsto(x,(-1)^r y)$ for every integer $r$. These comparisons are natural, commute with all tower transitions, preserve cone inclusions and the boundary after the shift comparison, and carry $j_{\operatorname{Cone}(f)}$ to the cone map of $(j_A,j_B)$ and $j_{M[r]}$ to $j_M[r]$.
