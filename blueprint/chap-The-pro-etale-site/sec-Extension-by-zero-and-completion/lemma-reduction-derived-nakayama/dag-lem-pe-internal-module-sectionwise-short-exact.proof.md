1. Write $\mathcal B=\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal C,\mathrm{Ab})$, let $\iota$ be the sheaf inclusion and $a$ abelian sheafification. The sheafification instances used in Lean are obtained on a small model of the essentially small site and transported back by `hasSheafifyEssentiallySmallSite`. On the small model, `GrothendieckTopology.hasSheafify_of_isGrothendieckAbelian` applies to $\mathrm{Ab}$. Its construction uses local presentability, `IsGrothendieckAbelian.isLocallyPresentable` from `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, to choose a regular cardinal large enough for a presentable generator and the arrows of the small site. The resulting sheaf-valued transfinite-plus construction preserves finite limits by [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], and its unit has the natural unique-factorization property into sheaves by [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]. Thus it provides a left exact reflector. The sheaf category is abelian by [dag-lem-sfb-abelian-sheaves-left-exact-reflector]; transport along the equivalence with the small model preserves these properties. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], [dag-lem-sfb-abelian-sheaves-left-exact-reflector], `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`)
2. Use the coefficient tensor $\otimes_{\mathbf Z}$ with unit $\mathbf Z$, as in `det-def-sfb-ring-object-rings` and `det-def-sfb-ring-object`. The symmetric monoidal structure on $\mathcal B$ is precisely `sfbSheafTensorStructure`, supplied by `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure`: its tensor is the sheafification of the objectwise tensor and its unit is $a(\underline{\mathbf Z})$. The supplied ring object $\mathcal R$ is a monoid object (`MonObj`) for this tensor, as in `sk-def-sfb-ring-object`. Let $U=\operatorname{Mod.forget}(\mathcal R)$, and package the prescribed arrows and their zero composite as the short complex $S=(M_0\xrightarrow F M_1\xrightarrow G M_2)$. Write $P_i=\iota U(M_i)$, $f=\iota U(F)$ and $g=\iota U(G)$. (uses `det-def-sfb-ring-object-rings`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object`, `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure`, `sk-def-sfb-ring-object`)
3. By `lem-weil-pbc-abelian-presheaves-evaluation-exact`, abelian presheaves form an abelian category and every evaluation functor preserves finite limits, finite colimits and zero morphisms. These evaluation functors jointly reflect isomorphisms: if a natural transformation is invertible at every object, its componentwise inverses form a natural inverse, as expressed by Mathlib's `NatIso.isIso_of_isIso_app`. Apply `JointlyReflectIsomorphisms.shortExact_iff`, namely `lem-weil-pbc-jointly-reflect-short-exact`, to the evaluation family and the short complex $(S\mathbin{.}\mathrm{map}\,U)\mathbin{.}\mathrm{map}\,\iota$. Each evaluated complex is short exact by the hypothesis (using $V^{\mathrm{unop}}$ for an index $V\in\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}$). Consequently $0\to P_0\xrightarrow fP_1\xrightarrow gP_2\to0$ is short exact in the sense of `def-weil-pbc-exact-sequence`. This is the first conclusion. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-abelian-presheaves-evaluation-exact`, `lem-weil-pbc-jointly-reflect-short-exact`)
4. By `lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact`, $a$ preserves finite limits and finite colimits, so Mathlib's `ShortComplex.ShortExact.map_of_exact` sends the presheaf short exact complex to a short exact complex of sheaves. By `lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise`, the counits $\epsilon_i:a\iota U(M_i)\xrightarrow{\sim}U(M_i)$ are isomorphisms. Their naturality says $\epsilon_1\circ a(f)=U(F)\circ\epsilon_0$ and $\epsilon_2\circ a(g)=U(G)\circ\epsilon_1$. Thus these counits identify the sheafified complex with $S\mathbin{.}\mathrm{map}\,U$, with precisely the prescribed arrows. In Lean this is the inverse of the short-complex isomorphism obtained from `sheafificationNatIso` by `ShortComplex.mapNatIso`; `ShortComplex.shortExact_of_iso` transfers short exactness across it. This proves the second conclusion. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact`, `lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise`)
5. The tensor functors in $\mathcal B$ preserve colimits by closedness in `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`, as recorded in `sfbSheafTensor_properties`. They are additive by `lem-sfb-preserving-colimits-additive`; this is the `sfbSheafTensor_monoidalPreadditive` construction, using `rightExactFunctor_le_additiveFunctor` for left tensor and symmetry for right tensor. In particular $\mathcal R\otimes-$ preserves finite colimits. Thus all hypotheses of `lem-sfb-modobj-abelian` hold. The instance `CategoryTheory.Mod.instAbelian`, also recorded in `lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck`, therefore gives an abelian category $\operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(\mathcal B)$. Here only this generic abelian-category instance is used; its hypotheses require a monoid object and do not require commutativity of $\mathcal R$. (uses `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`, `lem-sfb-preserving-colimits-additive`, `lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck`)
6. Under these hypotheses `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-left-exact` and `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-right-exact` say that $U$ preserves finite limits and finite colimits. Its additive forgetful structure preserves zero morphisms, and it is faithful by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful`. It also reflects isomorphisms, as used in Lean by `isIso_of_reflects_iso`: if a module map $h:M\to N$ has underlying inverse $k$, its action equation is $h\circ\gamma_M=\gamma_N\circ(\mathcal R\otimes h)$. Composing on the left with $k$ and on the right with $\mathcal R\otimes k$ gives $\gamma_M\circ(\mathcal R\otimes k)=k\circ\gamma_N$. Thus $k$ is a module map by `sk-def-sfb-module-objects`, and faithfulness gives its two inverse equations as module maps. (uses `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-left-exact`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-right-exact`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful`)
7. Apply `lem-weil-pbc-jointly-reflect-short-exact`, Mathlib's `JointlyReflectIsomorphisms.shortExact_iff`, to the family indexed by `Unit` consisting of $U$. Both categories are abelian; $U$ preserves zero morphisms, finite limits and finite colimits; and the singleton family reflects isomorphisms by the preceding step. Step 4 supplies short exactness of its image $S\mathbin{.}\mathrm{map}\,U$. The reverse implication therefore proves $S.\mathrm{ShortExact}$, that is, short exactness of $0\to M_0\xrightarrow F M_1\xrightarrow G M_2\to0$ with the prescribed arrows. Together with steps 3 and 4, this proves every clause. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-jointly-reflect-short-exact`)
