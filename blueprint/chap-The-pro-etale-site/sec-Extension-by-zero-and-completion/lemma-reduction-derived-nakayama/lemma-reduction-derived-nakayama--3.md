Let $E$, $X$ and the first reduction be as in
[lemma-reduction-derived-nakayama--2]. Let $u:K\to L$ be a morphism in
$D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$. Suppose that for every $Z$
with $Z_1=0$, every morphism $Z\to K$ and every morphism $Z\to L$ is
zero. If $u_1:K_1\to L_1$ is an isomorphism, then $u$ is an isomorphism.
