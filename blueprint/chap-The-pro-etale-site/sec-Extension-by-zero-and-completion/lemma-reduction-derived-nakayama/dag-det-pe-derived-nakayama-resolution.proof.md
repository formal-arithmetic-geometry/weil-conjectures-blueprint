1. Let A be the recorded integer subring of E, pi its recorded uniformizer, and B_n=A/(pi^n). The nonzero maximal ideal generated by pi implies pi is nonzero, by `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr`. Thus multiplication by pi^n is injective, including n=0. Transport the spectral-norm neighbourhood basis to the absolute-value topology using equality of their metrics. The coset-basis theorem `lem-sfb-integers-coset-basis` makes reduction modulo pi^(n+1) locally constant: use IsLocallyConstant.iff_eventually_eq and its isOpen_fiber property. Then isQuotientMap_quot_mk and discreteTopology_iff_isOpen_singleton show that the quotient topology is discrete. At n=0 the quotient is a singleton. We will use continuous_uniformizer_division from `lem-sfb-continuous-uniformizer-division` for continuous division on the image of pi^n. (uses `lem-sfb-integers-coset-basis`, `lem-sfb-continuous-uniformizer-division`)
2. Form the continuous-function sheaves R(S)=C(S,A) and B_n(S)=C(S,A/(pi^n)), with their pointwise ring structures, using the continuous-coefficient construction of `def-weil-pbc-condensed-e-module-category`. Their underlying abelian sheaves are commutative ring objects by `det-def-sfb-ring-object-presheaf`, with the ring-object and tensor conventions of `det-def-sfb-ring-object`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-rings`, and `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`. Continuous postcomposition with the actual quotient ring map defines reductionRingSheaf. The functoriality Sheaf.isMonHom_sheafCompose_map recorded in `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point` makes its underlying abelian-sheaf map reductionRing a ring-object morphism. Restrict the regular B_n-action along this map to obtain quotientModule. Construct the regular-module endomorphism d by pointwise multiplication by pi. Sheaf.tensor_hom_ext and the sheaf-to-presheaf tensor comparison reduce its linearity to pi(fg)=f(pi g) on pure tensors. The ring-morphism multiplication identity IsMonHom.mul_hom gives the linear reduction map r_n. Induction computes d^n sectionwise as multiplication by pi^n, and Ideal.Quotient.eq_zero_iff_mem together with Ideal.span_singleton_pow proves d^n followed by r_n is zero. (uses `def-weil-pbc-condensed-e-module-category`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-presheaf`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`)
3. For every profinite S, multiplication by pi^n on continuous sections is injective by mul_left_cancel₀ and pow_ne_zero. A section in the kernel of reduction has values in pi^n A, by Ideal.Quotient.eq_zero_iff_mem and Ideal.mem_span_singleton. It therefore has a continuous quotient by pi^n, by `lem-sfb-continuous-uniformizer-division`. A section of the discrete quotient lifts by composition with a set-theoretic section of A onto A/(pi^n), chosen using Function.surjInv and Ideal.Quotient.mk_surjective; every map from this discrete quotient is continuous. These arguments include the empty profinite set and n=0. ShortComplex.ab_exact_iff, AddCommGrpCat.mono_iff_injective, and AddCommGrpCat.epi_iff_surjective now give the short exact sequence on each S. (uses `lem-sfb-continuous-uniformizer-division`)
4. Sectionwise kernel forks form a kernel in the presheaf category by evaluationJointlyReflectsLimits. The sheaf inclusion reflects this kernel; NatTrans.epi_of_epi_app makes the reduction epic in presheaves, and Functor.epi_of_epi_map reflects that epimorphism to sheaves. Thus the underlying abelian-sheaf sequence is short exact. The internal-module forgetful functor is faithful by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful`, creates the relevant limits and colimits by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, and is left and right exact by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-left-exact` and `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-right-exact`. Functor.mono_of_mono_map and Functor.epi_of_epi_map reflect the two endpoint properties, and ShortComplex.exact_map_iff_of_faithful reflects exactness. This proves pointResolution for every n. (uses `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-left-exact`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-right-exact`)
5. Use upperStarFromPoint=p_X^* of `def-sfb-upper-star-from-point`. Its first factor is sheafified left Kan extension along the profinite test-object functor of `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, which is continuous by `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous` and preserves finite limits by `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-finite-limits`. The source theorem preservesFiniteLimits_op_lan_of_flat, AB5 for abelian groups, and left-exact sheafification give finite-limit preservation of this factor. The scheme factor is the sheafified Kan-extension pullback [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], exact by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. Its adjunction to restriction [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, together with the point adjunction, gives finite-colimit preservation of the composite. Transport the ring and module actions by mapMod as in `def-sfb-modobj-transport` and `sk-def-sfb-transport`. Since this functor commutes with the exact forgetful functors, it preserves finite limits and finite colimits. ShortComplex.ShortExact.map_of_exact therefore pulls pointResolution back to X. The pulled-back regular module is the regular module of R_X, through regularPullbackIso, so this is exactly the asserted coefficient sequence. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale`, `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-continuous`, `lem-sfb-profinite-to-proetale-finite-limits`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`)
6. For the derived comparison work in the actual category M=Mod(R_X) of internal modules. Abelian sheaves have their closed additive symmetric tensor by `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`; their Grothendieck structure and that of M are supplied by `lem-sfb-proetale-sheaves-grothendieck`, `lem-sfb-modobj-abelian`, and `lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck`. Their relative tensor is the balancing coequalizer of `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor` and `pur713-def-relative-tensor`, with unit the regular module, and its symmetry is `def-sfb-modobj-symmetric-tensor`. The internal-hom adjunction `def-sfb-modobj-internal-hom`, equivalently the relative-tensor adjoint of `lem-weil-pbc-module-relative-tensor-adjoint`, supplies closedness and hence preservation of colimits by tensoring on either side. These are the module tensor and unit used in reductionFunctor. 7. The derived-tensor construction has the required functorial flat resolutions: start with those of abelian groups `lem-sfb-abelian-functorial-flat-resolution`, pass to sheaves by `lem-sfb-sheaf-functorial-flat-resolution`, and then to internal modules by `lem-sfb-modobj-flat-resolutions` and `lem-weil-pbc-module-functorial-flat-resolution`. The last construction uses free modules on the ambient flat covers and the action map as augmentation. Flatness here means exactness of tensor, as in `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`. K-flatness means that tensoring with acyclic complexes remains acyclic `def-pc-kflat-complex`; the generic predicate MorphismProperty.kFlat of `def-pc-module-kflat-complex` expresses the equivalent two-sided preservation of quasi-isomorphisms used by Lean. (uses `lem-sfb-abelian-functorial-flat-resolution`, `lem-sfb-sheaf-functorial-flat-resolution`, `lem-sfb-modobj-flat-resolutions`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-functorial-flat-resolution`, `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`, `def-pc-kflat-complex`, `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`)
8. More explicitly, the K-flat machinery used here iterates flat covers on kernels to obtain the natural augmented objectwise cochain resolutions of `lem-sfb-left-resolution-objectwise-cochain`. Its totalization comparisons are HomologicalComplex₂.mapTotalIso from `lem-sfb-left-resolution-map-total-iso` and HomologicalComplex₂.singleRow₀ObjTotal from `lem-sfb-left-resolution-single-row-total`. The bounded-above columnwise quasi-isomorphism criterion `lem-pc-bounded-above-double-complex-total-comparison` proves the bounded-stage total augmentations are quasi-isomorphisms. Bounded-above complexes of flat objects are K-flat by `lem-sfb-bounded-above-flat-kflat`. Taking the sequential colimits in this construction gives the unbounded K-flat left derivability structure `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability`, since filtered colimits are exact and tensor preserves them. Thus no boundedness assumption on the input complex is needed. 11. Apply DerivedCategory.reductionIso to this short exact sequence. The cone is K-flat because the tensor unit is K-flat and K-flat objects form a triangulated subcategory: use HomotopyCategory.mappingCone_triangleh_distinguished and ObjectProperty.ext_of_isTriangulatedClosed₃. Consequently its tensor comparison is invertible for every complex C by `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`. The chain isomorphism CochainComplex.mapBifunctorMappingCone₂Iso `lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso`, followed by the right unitor, identifies C tensor cone(a) with cone(a on C). Degree by degree, the left-unit action is exactly scalarOnComplex, hence the latter is cone(pi^n:C to C). Finally DerivedCategory.reductionIso_hom_naturality and NatIso.ofComponents assemble these comparisons into the stated natural isomorphism Q followed by reductionFunctor isomorphic to coneFunctor. (uses `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`, `lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso`)
12. For all m,n use Mathlib's kernelCokernelCompSequence.snakeInput with f=d^m and g=d^n. Its last three cokernels form an exact sequence, its last arrow is epic, and its first arrow is monic because isZero_kernel_of_mono gives ker(g)=0. The point resolutions identify these cokernels canonically with B_m, B_(n+m), B_n by IsColimit.coconePointUniqueUpToIso, using d^m followed by d^n=d^(n+m). The cokernel equations prove r_m followed by i equals d^n followed by r_(n+m), and r_(n+m) followed by q equals r_n. Thus i and q are exactly the normalized maps $[x]$ to [pi^n x] and $[y]$ to $[y]$. This construction takes place in the abelian module category of `lem-sfb-modobj-abelian` and works also when an exponent is zero.
