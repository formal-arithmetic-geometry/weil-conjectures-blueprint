1. Fix a profinite set $S$ and write $\alpha_i:A\to T_i$ for the scalar maps. The scalar action is continuous by continuity of the scalar maps and multiplication. Use the continuous-section module objects of `def-sfb-continuous-sections-module`, whose sections have pointwise action $(a\cdot u)(s)=\alpha_i(a(s))u(s)$. Set $F_S(u)=f\circ u$ and $G_S(v)=g\circ v$, as in Lean's sectionsLinear. Continuity follows by composition. The pointwise equalities $f(u+v)=f(u)+f(v)$ and $f(\alpha_0(a)u)=\alpha_1(a)f(u)$, and the same equalities for $g$, make these maps $C(S,A)$-linear. The module axioms hold by evaluation, using the ring and algebra axioms. (uses `def-sfb-continuous-sections-module`)
2. If $F_S(u)=F_S(v)$, then $f(u(s))=f(v(s))$ for every $s$, so injectivity of $f$ gives $u=v$. Moreover $g(f(t))=0$ for every $t\in T_0$, since $\operatorname{im}f=\ker g$. Hence $G_SF_S=0$ and $\operatorname{im}F_S\subseteq\ker G_S$.
3. Let $v\in C(S,T_1)$ satisfy $G_S(v)=0$. Its values lie in $K=\ker g$. The map $\bar v:S\to K$ with the same values is continuous for the subspace topology: the inverse image of $K\cap U$ is $v^{-1}(U)$ for every open $U\subseteq T_1$. Lean packages this continuity with Continuous.subtype_mk. By hypothesis $e:T_0\xrightarrow{\sim}K$, $t\mapsto f(t)$, is a homeomorphism. Therefore $u=e^{-1}\circ\bar v$ is continuous and $F_S(u)=v$, using Homeomorph.apply_symm_apply and the prescribed formula for $e$. Combined with step 2, this proves $\operatorname{im}F_S=\ker G_S$, or Function.Exact F_S G_S.
4. Choose a right inverse $r:T_2\to T_1$ of $g$, using surjectivity of $g$: Lean uses Function.surjInv and Function.surjInv_eq. Since $T_2$ is discrete, $r$ is continuous by continuous_of_discreteTopology. Given $w\in C(S,T_2)$, the continuous map $r\circ w$ satisfies $G_S(r\circ w)=w$. This proves surjectivity and completes the short exact sequence on sections. The choices need not define a linear map; only the lift of each individual section is required. This argument uses discreteness and does not require the finiteness hypothesis.
5. For the internal assertion, take profinite sets in universe $u$ and abelian groups in $u+1$, using ULift on section groups. The coherent profinite site is essentially small relative to these groups. Write $\mathcal B=\operatorname{Sh}(*_{\mathrm{proét}},\Ab)$ and $a$ for sheafification [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. The sheafification instances used here come from local presentability of Grothendieck abelian categories `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable` and the transfinite-plus construction: its orthogonality gives the universal mapping property [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], and its corestriction preserves finite limits [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits]. Transporting from a small model gives sheafification on this site. Thus $a$ preserves finite limits as well as colimits, and is exact. In particular, $\mathcal B$ is Grothendieck abelian with the additive closed symmetric monoidal sheaf tensor of `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`. This tensor is the sheafification of the objectwise presheaf tensor `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`, and sheafification is strong monoidal `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification], `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`)
6. Use the continuous-function ring sheaves of `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring`, with sections $C(S,T)$ and precomposition restrictions, as in [dag-det-def-sfb-condensed-ring]. Their underlying abelian sheaves have the ring-object structures of `det-def-sfb-ring-object` and `sk-def-sfb-ring-object`: multiplication on pure section tensors is pointwise multiplication, as supplied by `det-def-sfb-ring-object-rings` and `det-def-sfb-ring-object-presheaf`. Each continuous scalar map $\alpha_i$ induces the postcomposition sheaf map of `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map`. Its underlying abelian-sheaf map is a ring-object morphism by Sheaf.isMonHom_sheafCompose_map, the general construction recorded in `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`. Restrict the regular $\underline{T_i}$-module along this map, using Mod.comap and the restriction construction of `det-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`. This is precisely continuousSectionsModule of `def-sfb-continuous-sections-module`; its underlying sheaf and pointwise action are those used in step 1. (uses `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-presheaf`, `def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`, `det-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`, `def-sfb-continuous-sections-module`)
7. The maps $F_S,G_S$ commute with precomposition, hence give abelian-sheaf maps. To make them internal module maps as in `sk-def-sfb-module-objects`, Lean uses Mod.homMk and checks action compatibility. By `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor` and the universal property [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification], equality of maps out of a sheaf tensor can be tested after the presheaf tensor comparison (Sheaf.tensor_hom_ext). Naturality of the lax monoidal comparison and Sheaf.μ_comp_map_mul reduce the action equation to the pointwise ring multiplication. AddCommGrpCat.hom_ext_toIntLinearMap and TensorProduct.ext' then reduce it to pure tensors. On $a\otimes u$ the equation is $f(\alpha_0(a(s))u(s))=\alpha_1(a(s))f(u(s))$, which is f.map_smul after Algebra.smul_def, and similarly for $g$. Thus Lean's sectionsMap gives internal arrows $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$ with precisely the required postcomposition formulas. Their composite is zero by step 2, checking equality on sections with Mod.hom_ext. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification], `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`)
8. Since tensoring with $\underline A$ preserves colimits by step 5, `lem-sfb-modobj-abelian` supplies the abelian structure Mod.instAbelian on $\operatorname{Mod}_{\underline A}(\mathcal B)$; this is also the abelian structure recorded in `lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck`. The forgetful functor $U=\operatorname{Mod.forget}$ to $\mathcal B$ preserves zero morphisms and is faithful by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful`. Let $M$ be the short complex formed by $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$ and their zero composite. By Mathlib's ShortExact.reflects_shortExact_of_faithful, it suffices to prove $M.map\,U$ short exact. (uses `lem-sfb-modobj-abelian`, `lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful`)
9. Forget the sheaf condition as well: put $P=\operatorname{sheafToPresheaf}$ and $N=(M.map\,U).map\,P$. Evaluating $N$ at $S$ gives the section maps of steps 1--4 on ULift groups. Injectivity, exactness and surjectivity transfer immediately through ULift.up and ULift.down. Mathlib's ShortComplex.ab_exact_iff_function_exact, AddCommGrpCat.mono_iff_injective and AddCommGrpCat.epi_iff_surjective therefore give a short exact evaluated complex via ShortComplex.ShortExact.mk'. The componentwise criteria NatTrans.mono_iff_mono_app and NatTrans.epi_iff_epi_app give a monomorphism $N.f$ and an epimorphism $N.g$; ShortComplex.exact_iff_evaluation gives exactness of $N$. Hence $N$ is short exact as an abelian-presheaf complex.
10. Apply exact sheafification $a=\operatorname{presheafToSheaf}$ from step 5 to $N$, using ShortComplex.ShortExact.map_of_exact. The counit isomorphism sheafificationNatIso of [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification] identifies the sheafification of the underlying presheaf of each sheaf with that sheaf, naturally in the arrows. Thus ShortComplex.mapNatIso and ShortComplex.shortExact_of_iso transport this short exactness to $M.map\,U$. Step 8 now reflects it to $M$. Its arrows are the postcomposition arrows constructed in step 7, proving the asserted short exact sequence of condensed $\underline A$-module objects. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification])
