Let $\ell$ be prime, let $E$ be a field with an embedding
$E\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, and let $X$ be a scheme.
Use the valuation induced by this embedding to form $\mathcal O_E$ and
its residue field $\mathbf F_E$, and pull their continuous condensed
coefficient rings back to $X$. For
$M\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$, write
$$
M_1=M\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}\mathbf F_{E,X},
$$
regarded as a complex of pro-étale sheaves of $\mathbf F_E$-modules.

Suppose $K$ satisfies the following form of derived completeness:
for every $Z$ with $Z_1=0$, every morphism $Z\to K$ is zero.
If $K_1=0$, then $K=0$.
