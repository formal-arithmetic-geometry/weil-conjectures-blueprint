1. Work in the derived monoidal category of the coefficient sheaves. The K-flat complexes and their module-object specialization are the models used for total tensoring `def-pc-kflat-complex` `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`; the sequential K-flat derivability and unary left-derived calculus justify passing this total tensor to the derived category `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability` `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`. Its value is independent of the chosen K-flat model by `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`. The chain-level total tensor is homotopy exact `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact` and tensoring a cone is identified with the cone of the tensor map by `lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso`. The coefficient module actions and the pullback/derived-tensor constructions are transported along the relevant lax-monoidal functors using `def-sfb-modobj-transport` and `sk-def-sfb-transport`. (uses `def-pc-kflat-complex`, `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`, `lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso`, `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact`, `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability`, `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`)
2. Apply the two-term coefficient resolution [dag-det-pe-derived-nakayama-resolution] to obtain, for every n, a morphism p_n := AdicCompletion.finiteUnit l E X n from the tensor unit to the finite coefficient object and a short exact sequence whose first map is multiplication by the scalar b_n := scalar l E X (d^(n+1)). The pointwise hypothesis hd identifies the resolution's endomorphism d₀ with d: `Mod.hom_ext`, `Sheaf.hom_ext`, `NatTrans.ext`, `AddCommGrpCat.hom_ext`, `ULift.ext`, `ContinuousMap.ext` reduce the equality to hd. After substituting d₀ = d, the resolution gives `(b_n • 𝟙) ≫ p_n = 0` and `ShortComplex.shortExact_of_iso` transfers its short exactness across `regularPullbackIso`. The quotient objects and quotientStep maps are those fixed by [dag-sk-def-pe-derived-completion].
3. Identify the scalar action on every cochain complex by the private `scalar_complex` calculation: `DerivedNakayama.scalarOnComplex l E X (d^(n+1)) M = b_n • 𝟙 M`, and similarly for d. The conjugated pullback scalar is multiplicative by `scalar_mul`; hence `hb : b_n * scalar l E X d = b_(n+1)` follows from `← scalar_mul` and `← pow_succ`. Functoriality of the pullback and `Functor.map_comp` give `hq : p_(n+1) ≫ (pullback l E X).map (quotientStep n) = p_n`. The hypotheses required by the generic cone normalization are therefore exactly the scalar family b_n, the fixed scalar s, the p_n, w_n, q_n, the scalar natural transformations τ and σ, the definitional shift identity `hshift`, and the supplied power equation hs.
4. Instantiate the private `normalize` theorem with these data. Define `τ n` and `σ` by `NatTrans.mapHomologicalComplex` applied to `scalarOnModules`; `hτ` and `hσ` are the componentwise `scalar_complex` equalities. For each n and complex M, take κ n to be the componentwise inverse of the canonical `DerivedCategory.reductionIso (w_n) (hw_n) M`; `NatIso.ofComponents` is natural because `DerivedCategory.reductionIso_hom_naturality` is natural in M. The cone side is the mapping cone of the scalar natural transformation, and the tensor side is `DerivedCategory.Q ⋙ tensorRight ((DerivedCategory.singleFunctor _ 0).obj (N n))`; the identification is exactly the tensor-cone comparison `lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso` transported through the derived K-flat model `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`. (uses `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`, `lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso`)
5. The first conjunct of `normalize` is obtained by `DerivedCategory.whiskerLeft_reductionIso_hom` with hb, w_n, hw_n, q_n and hq; cancellation of the two reductionIso components yields precisely the map induced by `AdicCompletion.quotientStep l E n`, which unfolds to `DerivedNakayama.reductionMap`. The second conjunct is `DerivedCategory.reduction_reductionIso_hom`; after cancellation it identifies `CochainComplex.mappingCone.inr` with the inverse right unitor followed by tensoring the derived-category lax-unit map and the degree-zero map of `p_n`, namely the stated `finiteUnit` formula. The unfolding uses only the transported module/tensor structures from `def-sfb-modobj-transport` and `sk-def-sfb-transport`. (uses `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`)
6. For an integer shift r, the componentwise shift equation is `rfl` because `scalarOnComplex` is termwise scalar multiplication. Apply `DerivedCategory.reductionIso_inv_signed_shift`, the signed comparison for the same canonical reductionIso components, to obtain the inverse `CochainComplex.mappingCone.shiftIso` square. The total-tensor shift is the standard signed shift supplied by `DerivedCategory.reductionIso_inv_signed_shift` and the derived tensor CommShift structure, and after unfolding it is exactly `tensorReductionShift`; this is the generic signed-shift result [dag-lem-pe-reduction-iso-signed-shift]. Thus the three conjuncts produced by `normalize` are the transition, unit, and shift equations in the statement. (uses `lem-pe-reduction-iso-signed-shift`)
