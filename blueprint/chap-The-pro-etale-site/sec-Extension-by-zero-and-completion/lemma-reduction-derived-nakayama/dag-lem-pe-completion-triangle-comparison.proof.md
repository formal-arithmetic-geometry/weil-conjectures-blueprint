Choose the canonical principal-reduction data from [dag-lem-pe-principal-reduction-cone-calculus] and assemble its reductions and transition maps degreewise into the strict tower functor `tower`; apply the Roos functor to obtain `G = complexCompletion`. The supplied transition-compatible cone coordinates make `tower` additive, while [dag-lem-pe-roos-cone-calculus] and `RoosConeCalculus.assemble_calculus` make Roos additive and preserve levelwise quasi-isomorphisms. The product and shifted-product coordinates use the signed convention of `def-pur-roos-coordinate-shift-formula`, with coherent product shifts from `lem-weil-product-comm-shift` and signed mapping-cocone shifts from [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor] and `lem-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-shift`. 3. The compatible principal units `j_n` satisfy `j_{n+1} T_n = j_n`; therefore the Roos augmentation `a : id ⟶ G` is the strict map with coordinates `(((m,0))_n,0)`. Its coordinate identity is exactly `roosAugment_χ`, and its naturality follows by evaluation at each tower index and the transition equations. The Roos triangle is the localized derived-limit triangle: [dag-lem-pe-roos-cone-calculus] gives the cone and its localized triangle, while `def-weil-derived-limit` and `lem-weil-derived-countable-products-core` identify it with the derived limit and justify the termwise product cone. (uses [dag-lem-pe-roos-cone-calculus], `def-weil-derived-limit`, `lem-weil-derived-countable-products-core`)
4. Let `W` be quasi-isomorphisms and `Q` the derived localization. Since `G` inverts `W`, Mathlib `Localization.lift` and `Localization.fac`, together with `Localization.liftNatTrans` and `Localization.liftNatTrans_app`, produce `F` and `c : id ⟶ F` and give `c.app (Q.obj M) ≫ κ.hom.app M = Q.map (a.app M)`. Additivity descends through the homotopy quotient using `Functor.additive_of_full_essSurj_comp` and through `Qh` using `Localization.functor_additive_iff`; the factorization and uniqueness are precisely `lem-weil-pbc-localization-universal-property`. The localized shift structure is supplied by Mathlib `Functor.commShiftOfLocalization`, and `NatTrans.commShift_iso_hom_of_localization` plus `Localization.natTrans_ext` descend the strict augmentation shift equation to `c.app (K⟦r⟧) ≫ commShiftIso(F,r).hom.app K = (c.app K)⟦r⟧`. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-localization-universal-property`)

The total tensor and its homotopy invariance are governed by `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact`, `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy`, and `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`. 6. Pull back the exact coefficient sequence for the principal quotient. The DVR and uniformizer facts in `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr`, `lem-sfb-continuous-uniformizer-division`, and `lem-sfb-integers-coset-basis` identify the scalar with the canonical power of the uniformizer and identify the quotient map. Exactness is preserved under the relevant tensor and module constructions using `lem-sfb-extend-scalars-kflat`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, and the exactness/colimit properties of `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. The canonical map from the scalar cone to the quotient is a quasi-isomorphism by `mappingCone.quasiIso_descShortComplex` and `lem-pc-cone-short-exact-quasi-iso`. For an arbitrary K-flat representative, replace it by a flat K-flat complex using `CochainComplex.exists_quasiIso_flat_kFlat`; `MorphismProperty.whiskerRight_of_kFlat`, cone triangles, and two-out-of-three transfer the quasi-isomorphism, while `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined` supplies model independence. The explicit coordinate calculations show compatibility with every tower transition and send each `j_n` to the prescribed `levelUnit`. (uses `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr`, `lem-sfb-continuous-uniformizer-division`, `lem-sfb-integers-coset-basis`, `lem-sfb-extend-scalars-kflat`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`, `lem-pc-cone-short-exact-quasi-iso`, `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`)
7. The transition-compatible cone-to-tensor maps form a map from the principal tower to the levelwise tensor tower. Roos preserves this map as a quasi-isomorphism by [dag-lem-pe-roos-cone-calculus]. In the target, the product isomorphism identifies the Roos product with the completion product, and the explicit signed biproduct map `(v,u) ↦ (v,-u)` is a chain isomorphism; its first coordinate is the product augmentation and its second coordinate vanishes, so it fixes `a`. The coordinate convention is the one in `def-pur-roos-coordinate-shift-formula`. After localization, `DerivedCategory.isIso_Q_map_iff_quasiIso` and the Roos coordinate comparison give an isomorphism `ρ : QG(P) ≅ Q(roos(P))` with `Q.map(a_P) ≫ ρ.hom = Q.map(augmentation_P)`. The resulting cone is the derived-limit cone of `def-weil-derived-limit`, with products justified by `lem-weil-derived-countable-products-core`. For any `e : QP ≅ K`, transport by `F.map(e.symm)`, `κ`, and `ρ`; naturality of `c` gives exactly `completionMap`. The definition of derived completeness and its criterion against a K-flat representative then imply `IsDerivedComplete K ↔ IsIso(c.app K)`. (uses [dag-lem-pe-roos-cone-calculus], `def-pur-roos-coordinate-shift-formula`, `def-weil-derived-limit`, `lem-weil-derived-countable-products-core`)
8. For a chain map `f`, evaluation at each tower index gives the cone isomorphism `D_n(Cone(f)) ≅ Cone(D_n(f))`; the supplied transition equations make these assemble into `towerConeIso`. Its inclusion and signed-boundary identities are exactly the hypotheses used by `TowerCalculus.towerTriangleIso`. The Roos cone-triangle comparison from [dag-lem-pe-roos-cone-calculus] is assembled with `roosTriangleIso`; the signed boundary is the one fixed by [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor] and `lem-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-shift`. Thus `G.mapTriangle` of every chain-map cone triangle is isomorphic to the cone triangle of `G.map f`, with the prescribed shift on the last arrow. (uses [dag-lem-pe-roos-cone-calculus], [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor], `lem-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-shift`)
9. By Mathlib `DerivedCategory.mem_distTriang_iff`, every distinguished triangle is isomorphic to a chain-map cone triangle. The cone comparison above, transported through `Functor.mapTriangleCompIso`, `Functor.mapTriangleIso`, and `DerivedCategory.Q.mapTriangle.mapIso`, shows that `F` preserves distinguished triangles; closure under isomorphism is `isomorphic_distinguished`. Finally define the triangle morphism with components `c.app` (Mathlib `Triangle.mk`/`Triangle.isoMk`). The first two squares are `c.naturality`. For the last square, naturality for `w : C ⟶ K` shifted by one gives `F(w)c_C = c_(K shifted by one) w`; composing with the shift comparison and the descended shift equation yields `commShiftIso(F,1).hom.app K ≫ F(w)c_C = c_K shifted by one ≫ w`, exactly the signed last square. Hence the image triangle and all three completion maps satisfy the asserted morphism-of-triangles statement.
