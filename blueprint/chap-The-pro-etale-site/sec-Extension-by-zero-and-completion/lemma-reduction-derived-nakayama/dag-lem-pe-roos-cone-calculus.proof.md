1. Put A = ringedSiteModules for the chosen pro-etale site and coefficient sheaf, as specified by [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site] and `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`. The pro-etale product theorem supplies the CountableAB4Star instance used by the Lean proof. For a strict tower, the product universal property `def-weil-category-product` defines the product maps and the transition endomorphism; `towerTransitionEndomorphism_eq_iff` gives its projection formula and the commutation with a strict tower map from `def-weil-inverse-system` gives the diagonal Roos map. The existing strict model `def-weil-strict-roos-model` is the shifted cone of this endomorphism, so its component maps are the maps `roosProj`, `roosU`, and `roosP` used below. (uses `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`)
2. The coordinate calculations establish the additive and differential parts of the assertion. `roos_additive` proves additivity by `Pi.hom_ext` for `roosPmap` and `roos_ext` for the two fibre coordinates. `differential_coordinates` obtains the product coordinate from `roosProj.comm` and the shifted coordinate from `mappingCone.d_snd_v`; `map_coordinates` follows from `roosF_map_χ`, `ucoord_χ`, and `biprod.map_fst`/`biprod.map_snd`. For a compatible cone family, `augmentation` is the existing strict augmentation of `def-weil-strict-roos-model`, and `augment_coordinates` follows from `roosAugment_χ` and the same two projections. Thus `CoordinateCalculus` holds, with the displayed signs coming from the cone and shift conventions in `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`.
3. Let f be levelwise a quasi-isomorphism. The exact countable-product instance makes the product map `roosPmap f` a quasi-isomorphism: at each degree, `quasiIsoAt_iff_isIso_homologyMap` reduces the claim to the homology product comparison `piComparison`, whose naturality with `Limits.Pi.map` and the levelwise isomorphisms gives `IsIso (H.map (roosPmap f))`. The map of the two mapping-cone triangles is then `mappingCone.triangleMap`; the distinguished cone triangles supplied by `def-weil-abstract-derived-category` and `def-weil-triangulated-category`, together with `isIso₃_of_isIso₁₂`, show that the localized cone map is an isomorphism. `DerivedCategory.isIso_Q_map_iff_quasiIso` and invariance under the shift by -1 give `QuasiIso (roosF.map f)`. (uses `def-weil-triangulated-category`)
4. For each tower N, the Lean proof takes the limit cone of the evaluated tower and uses `derivedCategory_termwiseProduct_isLimit` for its localized product. `Fan.isLimitMapConeEquiv` and `productIsProduct` give the unique product isomorphism e whose projection equations are recorded by `hfan.conePointUniqueUpToIso_hom_comp`. Define the derived transition endomorphism with `towerTransitionEndomorphism`; applying `towerTransitionEndomorphism_eq_iff` projectionwise proves `Q.map (roosU N) ≫ e = e ≫ sd`, exactly the transition formula of `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`. The localized mapping-cocone triangle is distinguished by `DerivedCategory.mappingCocone_triangle_distinguished`; transporting it through e with `Triangle.isoMk` and `distinguished_iff_of_iso` yields the triangle with first map the localized product projection and last map the localized cone boundary. By the definition `def-weil-derived-limit` (in the triangulated setting `def-weil-triangulated-category`), this is `DerivedRoos N`. (uses `def-weil-derived-limit`, `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`, `def-weil-triangulated-category`)
5. For shifts, form the level evaluation functors and their product functor. `ProductShift.productCommShift` supplies the product shift, and `ProductShift.productShift_hom_ext` together with the projection formula `ProductShift.productShiftIso_π` proves that the transition endomorphism has a compatible shift; the required equality is checked with `towerTransitionEndomorphism_eq_iff` at every projection. `SignedCoconeShift.coconeCommShift` gives the signed cone shift, while `cofiberMap_eq_coneMap` and the identity-component `signedRoosIso` identify that functor with the strict Roos fibre from `def-weil-strict-roos-model`. Transporting the signed shift produces `roosCommShift`. The equations `roosShift_v`, `roosShift_u`, and `productShift_π` prove `shift_formula`: the v-coordinate is preserved and the u-coordinate is multiplied by `r.negOnePow`, with coherence inherited from the signed cocone shift and the shift conventions of `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`.
6. For a tower map f, define `productTarget` and `productSource` by the product universal property `def-weil-category-product` from the target and source coordinates of the levelwise mapping cones. `productTarget_π` and `productSource_π` are their projection equations. Using `π_d`, `π_map`, and the transition formula from `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`, `product_ext` proves their differential and transition identities (`productTarget_d`, `productSource_d`, `productTarget_u`, `productSource_u`). The fibre pair and cone pair recover the two coordinates by `fibrePair_v`, `fibrePair_u`, `conePair_target`, and `conePair_source`. Define `coneHomComponent` and its inverse from these pairs. The equations `coneHom_t_v`, `coneHom_t_u`, `coneHom_s_v`, and `coneHom_s_u`, followed by `coneHom_comm` using `cone_d_target`, `cone_d_source`, `differential_v`, and `differential_u`, show that the component map is a chain map. `productCone_ext` and `roos_ext` prove the two inverse identities, so `HomologicalComplex.Hom.isoOfComponents` gives `coneIso : roosF.obj (mappingCone f) ≅ mappingCone (roosF.map f)`. Its four projection equations are exactly `coneIso_formula`, the categorical realization of the permutation and sign in the cone comparison, with cones interpreted by `def-weil-abstract-derived-category` and the strict maps of `def-weil-strict-roos-model`. (uses `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`)
7. Naturality of the cone comparison is proved by `coneMap_target`, `coneMap_source`, `productTarget_natural`, and `productSource_natural`, followed by `roos_ext_at`; this is `coneIso_natural`. The same coordinate tests show that `roosF.map (mappingCone.inr f)` is carried to the cone inclusion (`cone_inclusion`). For the third arrow, `cone_boundary_component` identifies the mapping-cone boundary with the negative source projection, and `roosShift_one_u` supplies the sign of the degree-one shift; `cone_boundary` then gives the boundary equation after `roosCommShift`. These are precisely the inclusion and boundary clauses of `ConeCompatibility`, using the cone-triangle conventions of `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`.
8. For compatible augmenting cone families, `augmentation_natural` uses `Pi.hom_ext` to compare the two product lifts and then `roos_ext` with `map_coordinates` and `augment_coordinates`; hence the zero-second-coordinate augmentations are natural for strict tower maps. The identity `augment_v_π` records their levelwise projections. Given the two levelwise hypotheses in `ConeAugmentation`, `cone_augmentation` applies `mappingCone.ext_to`, `product_ext`, `coneHom_t_v/u`, `coneHom_s_v/u`, and `augment_v_π`/`augment_u` to identify the induced cone map with the augmentation through `coneIso`. This proves the conditional cone-augmentation equation for the actual augmentations of `def-weil-strict-roos-model`.
9. Finally, `shift_augmentation` compares a shifted augmentation with the coherent shift by applying `roos_ext_at` to its v and u coordinates. `roosShift_v` and `productShift_π` reduce the v-coordinate to the assumed levelwise equation, while `roosShift_u` and `augment_u` make the u-coordinate zero; this proves `ShiftAugmentation`, including its signed convention from `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`. The Lean theorem `assemble_calculus` packages `roos_additive`, `CoordinateCalculus`, the quasi-isomorphism result, `DerivedRoos`, `shift_formula`, `coneIso_formula`, `cone_compatibility`, `cone_augmentation`, and `shift_augmentation`; the final specialization installs the pro-etale sheaf-module product instance for the X and R in the statement from [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site] and `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`.
