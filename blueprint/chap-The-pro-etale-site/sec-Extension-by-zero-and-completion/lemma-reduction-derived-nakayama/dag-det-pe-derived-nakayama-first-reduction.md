Let $K\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ be derived complete in the sense of
[dag-sk-def-pe-derived-completion]. If $K_1=0$, then $K_n=0$ for all $n\geq1$ and
$K=0$. Derived complete complexes are closed under cones (and shifts), and a
morphism $f\colon K\to L$ of derived complete complexes is an isomorphism as soon
as $f_1=f\otimes^{\mathbf L}(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E)_X\colon K_1\to L_1$ is an
isomorphism.
