1. Fix a strict tower C. By `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`, its adjacent transitions determine the product shift s_C and endomorphism b_C=1-s_C. By [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor], applying the signed mapping-cocone construction to b_C gives Cone(b_C)$[-1]$, with the prescribed shifted-cone differential and product projections. (uses [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor], `def-weil-category-product`, `def-weil-inverse-system`, `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`)
2. For a natural transformation α : C ⟶ C' of towers, let Pα = Limits.Pi.map (fun n ↦ α.app (op n)). The transition endomorphisms satisfy (1-s_C) ≫ Pα = Pα ≫ (1-s_C'). Indeed, after composing with the nth product projection, the two sides are the difference of the nth component and the next transition followed by that component; these agree by the naturality equation for α on (homOfLE (Nat.le_succ n)).op. Product extensionality from `def-weil-category-product` gives the equality, using the tower-morphism meaning of `def-weil-inverse-system`. This is exactly the naturality clause of `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`. (uses `def-weil-category-product`, `def-weil-inverse-system`, `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`)
3. Use this commuting square in the mapping-cocone construction of [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor]. The induced shifted-cone map has both coordinates equal to the product map Pα, hence is the canonical map between the two Roos complexes. (uses [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor])
4. For the identity transformation, product extensionality and the componentwise identity calculation show that the product of identity maps is the identity. Arrow extensionality identifies the induced mapping-cocone map with the identity arrow, and functoriality of the shift preserves this identity. Hence the Roos map of an identity tower morphism is the identity Roos map. (uses [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor], `def-weil-category-product`)
5. For composable tower maps α and β, functoriality of the shift and of the mapping-cocone construction reduce the claim to equality of the two arrow maps. Arrow extensionality followed by product extensionality checks this coordinatewise; the product of the composites is the composite of the product maps. Hence the Roos map of α ≫ β is the composite of the Roos maps. (uses [dag-def-weil-signed-mapping-cocone-functor], `def-weil-category-product`)
6. Taking the Roos construction as the object assignment and these Roos maps as the map assignment, the identity and composition equalities fill the map_id and map_comp fields. Thus the construction is a genuine functor on strict towers, retaining the chosen product projections and explicit shifted-cone coordinates.
