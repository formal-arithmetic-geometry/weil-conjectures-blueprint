1. Fix the coefficient ring R=O_{E,X} and a uniformizer pi. The DVR and uniformizer facts needed for the coefficient quotients come from `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr`. The coefficient presheaves and their pullbacks are commutative ring objects by `pur713-def-coefficient-rings`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`, `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, and `def-sfb-qlbar-coefficient-ring`. The relative tensor products, symmetric module structures, and extension of scalars used below are the constructions of `pur713-def-relative-tensor`, `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, `def-sfb-modobj-symmetric-tensor`, `lem-sfb-modobj-symmetric-bimodules`, and `def-sfb-modobj-extension`, with restriction and its adjunction supplied by `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`. Closedness of the module category and the existence of the limits used for towers follow from `def-sfb-modobj-internal-hom`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-relative-tensor-adjoint`, and `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`. (uses `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr`, `pur713-def-coefficient-rings`, `def-sfb-integers-to-subfield-point`, `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, `def-sfb-qlbar-coefficient-ring`, `pur713-def-relative-tensor`, `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, `def-sfb-modobj-symmetric-tensor`, `lem-sfb-modobj-symmetric-bimodules`, `def-sfb-modobj-extension`, `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars`, `def-sfb-modobj-internal-hom`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-relative-tensor-adjoint`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`)
2. The flat and K-flat resolutions needed to compute the derived scalar extensions exist by `lem-sfb-abelian-functorial-flat-resolution`, `lem-sfb-sheaf-functorial-flat-resolution`, `lem-sfb-modobj-flat-resolutions`, and `lem-weil-pbc-module-functorial-flat-resolution`, using the notions in `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`, `def-pc-kflat-complex`, and `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`. Thus the derived extension-of-scalars functor is computed on K-flat representatives and is adjoint to termwise restriction by `sk-def-sfb-derived-extension-of-scalars` and `def-sfb-derived-extend-levelwise-adjunction`; `lem-sfb-extend-scalars-kflat` ensures that quasi-isomorphisms remain quasi-isomorphisms after extension. The general derived push-pull and finite-derived-functor interfaces are the ones fixed in `sk-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull` and `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`. In particular, the reduction functors used below are left adjoints and preserve zero morphisms. (uses `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`, `def-pc-kflat-complex`, `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`, `lem-sfb-abelian-functorial-flat-resolution`, `lem-sfb-sheaf-functorial-flat-resolution`, `lem-sfb-modobj-flat-resolutions`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-functorial-flat-resolution`, `sk-def-sfb-derived-extension-of-scalars`, `def-sfb-derived-extend-levelwise-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-extend-scalars-kflat`, `sk-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull`, `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`)
3. For an unbounded representative M, put D_n(M)=Cone(pi^n:M to M), with transition (x,y) maps to (x,pi y) and coaugmentation m maps to (m,0). The strict-model result [dag-lem-pe-principal-reduction-cone-calculus], together with the cone and shift conventions of `def-weil-triangulated-category`, identifies QD_n(M) naturally with the actual reduction K tensor^L_R (O_E/pi^n)_X, carries the transitions and coaugmentations to the canonical ones, and gives the signed comparison D_n(Cone(f)) isomorphic to Cone(D_n(f)) and the shift comparison (x,y) maps to (x,(-1)^r y). The exact coefficient triangles used for induction are supplied by [dag-det-pe-derived-nakayama-resolution], namely K_1 to K_(n+1) to K_n to K_1$[1]$. (uses [dag-lem-pe-principal-reduction-cone-calculus], `def-weil-triangulated-category`)
4. For the strict reduction tower, form its product P and transition endomorphism 1-s using `def-weil-category-product` and `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`, and form the shifted Roos cone H=Cone(1-s)$[-1]$ as in `def-weil-strict-roos-model`. The resulting Roos triangle is a derived-limit triangle by `def-weil-derived-limit`. The completion comparison theorem [dag-lem-pe-completion-triangle-comparison] therefore supplies an additive endofunctor F on the derived category, a natural map c:Id to F representing the canonical derived-completion map, coherent shift comparisons, and distinguished image triangles with (c_K,c_L,c_C) as a triangle morphism. (uses [dag-lem-pe-completion-triangle-comparison], `def-weil-category-product`, `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`, `def-weil-strict-roos-model`, `def-weil-derived-limit`)
5. The resolution triangles and the natural level identifications from step 3 show that, for every K and n, the integral level K_n is isomorphic to the (n+1)-st derived reduction. If the first level is zero, induction on n uses the triangle K_1 to K_(n+1) to K_n to K_1$[1]$ and Mathlib's Triangle.isZero₂_of_isZero₁₃ to prove that every level is zero. The comparison of the zero triangle with each such triangle is justified by `lem-sf-triangle-morphism-iso`, while the cone-triangle axioms are those of `def-weil-triangulated-category`. (uses `lem-sf-triangle-morphism-iso`, `def-weil-triangulated-category`)
6. If all integral levels are zero, the discrete tower functor is zero by the zero-object criterion. Give its product the zero cone; `def-weil-category-product` and Mathlib's IsLimit.ofIsZero and IsLimit.isZero_pt show that this is a limiting product and that the product object is zero. The Roos triangle from `def-weil-derived-limit` then has both product vertices zero, so Triangle.isZero₁_of_isZero₂₃ makes the Roos object zero. The completion map c_K is an isomorphism by [dag-lem-pe-completion-triangle-comparison] and completeness, hence K is zero. Finally, each actual finite reduction is zero because the derived extension/reduction functor is a left adjoint by `def-sfb-derived-extend-levelwise-adjunction` and therefore its map_isZero sends the zero object to zero. (uses [dag-lem-pe-completion-triangle-comparison], `def-weil-category-product`, `def-weil-derived-limit`, `def-sfb-derived-extend-levelwise-adjunction`)
7. Let T be a distinguished triangle with first two objects complete. The triangle part of [dag-lem-pe-completion-triangle-comparison] gives a distinguished F(T) and a morphism (c_K,c_L,c_C). Since c_K and c_L are isomorphisms by the definition of derived completeness, `lem-sf-triangle-morphism-iso` makes c_C an isomorphism; the criterion in [dag-lem-pe-completion-triangle-comparison] then says that the cone object is complete. Its shift comparison satisfies sigma_{K,r} c_{K$[r]$}=c_K$[r]$, so the same argument and the isomorphism of shifts show that every integer shift of a complete object is complete. (uses [dag-lem-pe-completion-triangle-comparison], `lem-sf-triangle-morphism-iso`)
8. For a morphism f:K to L between complete objects, complete it to a distinguished cone triangle using `def-weil-triangulated-category`. Step 7 makes its cone C complete. By the cone comparison in [dag-lem-pe-principal-reduction-cone-calculus], the first reduction of this triangle is distinguished. If the first reduction of f is an isomorphism, Mathlib's Triangle.isZero₃_of_isIso₁ and `lem-kun-distinguished-cone-zero` give that the first reduction of C is zero. The vanishing argument of steps 5 and 6 gives C=0, and applying `lem-kun-distinguished-cone-zero` to the original triangle gives IsIso f. This proves detection of isomorphisms by the first reduction, while steps 5 and 6 give the asserted vanishing and all finite-level vanishings. (uses [dag-lem-pe-principal-reduction-cone-calculus], `def-weil-triangulated-category`, `lem-kun-distinguished-cone-zero`)
