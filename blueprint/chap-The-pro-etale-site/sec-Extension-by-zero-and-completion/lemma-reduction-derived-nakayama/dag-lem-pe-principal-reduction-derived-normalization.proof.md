1. Use the K-flat model of the derived tensor product: K-flat complexes of sheaves and of ordinary modules are the objects specified by `def-pc-kflat-complex` and `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`. The module derived tensor and its closed derived-tensor formalism are the structures of `def-sfb-derived-tensor-modules` and `sk-def-sfb-derived-tensor-hom`. The K-flat inclusion is left-derivable by `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability`, the derived tensor is consequently a well-defined left derived functor by `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus` and `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`, and its monoidal localization is supplied by `lem-sfb-derived-monoidal`. (uses `def-pc-kflat-complex`, `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`, `def-sfb-derived-tensor-modules`, `sk-def-sfb-derived-tensor-hom`, `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability`, `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`, `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`, `lem-sfb-derived-monoidal`)
2. The resolution calculation used for that derived tensor is valid for the unbounded complexes in question: the objectwise cochain resolution and augmentation are given by `lem-sfb-left-resolution-objectwise-cochain`, its one-row total is identified by `lem-sfb-left-resolution-single-row-total`, and additive functors commute with the required double-complex totals by `lem-sfb-left-resolution-map-total-iso`. The bounded-above flat terms are K-flat by `lem-sfb-bounded-above-flat-kflat`, while the total tensor and fixed-argument total bifunctor carry the homotopy and triangulated structures of `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy` and `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact`. The columnwise total comparison `lem-pc-bounded-above-double-complex-total-comparison` supplies the quasi-isomorphism comparison, and `lem-sc-derived-tensor-shift-independent-universe` together with `lem-sc-source-kflat-tensor-left-triangulated` gives the coherent integer shifts and their triangulated behavior independently of the localization universe. (uses `lem-sfb-left-resolution-objectwise-cochain`, `lem-sfb-left-resolution-single-row-total`, `lem-sfb-left-resolution-map-total-iso`, `lem-sfb-bounded-above-flat-kflat`, `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy`, `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact`, `lem-pc-bounded-above-double-complex-total-comparison`, `lem-sc-derived-tensor-shift-independent-universe`, `lem-sc-source-kflat-tensor-left-triangulated`)
3. Apply [dag-lem-pe-principal-cone-tensor-normalization] to d, hd, and hs. It supplies, for every n, a natural isomorphism c_n from the scalar mapping-cone functor to Q followed by the derived right-tensor functor with the pulled-back quotient in degree zero. Its first equation sends the cone map built from the power identity hs to tensoring the quotientStep, its second sends cone.inr to the right-unitor inverse followed by the lax-monoidal Q-unit and finiteUnit map, and its third is the signed mapping-cone shift equation for the standard tensor shift. These are statements in the pre-triangulated setting of `def-weil-triangulated-category`. (uses `lem-pe-principal-cone-tensor-normalization`, `def-weil-triangulated-category`)
4. Apply [dag-lem-pe-principal-tensor-scalar-extension]. It supplies isomorphisms e_n from the same derived tensor functor to the canonical derived extension followed by exact restriction, together with the equation identifying tensoring quotientStep with integralStep and the equation identifying the right-unitor/Q/finiteUnit expression with the actual derived scalar-extension adjunction unit. Thus this comparison uses the existing extension and restriction functors and their existing adjunction, without changing any coefficient object. (uses [dag-lem-pe-principal-tensor-scalar-extension])
5. Put e'_n := e_n and define κ_n := c_n ≪≫ Functor.isoWhiskerLeft DerivedCategory.Q (e'_n). Transport the tensor shift tensorReductionShift through e'_n with Functor.CommShift.ofIso, obtaining sF_n; its compatibility is Functor.CommShift.ofIso_compatibility. Since tensoring on the right is additive and triangulated with its signed shift, Functor.additive_of_iso and Functor.isTriangulated_of_iso transport these properties across e'_n. Hence each reduction functor has the required Additive and IsTriangulated structures. (uses [dag-lem-pe-principal-tensor-scalar-extension], `lem-pe-principal-cone-tensor-normalization`, `def-weil-triangulated-category`, `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact`, `lem-sc-source-kflat-tensor-left-triangulated`)
6. For the transition clause, substitute the first equation for c_n and then the transition equation for e_n; reassociation by Category.assoc gives exactly κ_n followed by the canonical integralStep equal to κ_(n+1) after the cone transition. For the inclusion clause, substitute the second equation for c_n and then the unit equation for e_n; Category.assoc gives the specified derived scalar-extension adjunction unit. No additional comparison map is introduced. (uses [dag-lem-pe-principal-tensor-scalar-extension], `lem-pe-principal-cone-tensor-normalization`)
7. For a shift r, start with the signed shift equation supplied by c_n. The transported shift on e'_n satisfies NatTrans.shift_app_comm by Functor.CommShift.ofIso_compatibility. Apply this equality to the shifted component, use Functor.map_comp and Category.assoc, and then use the naturality equation (e'_n).hom.naturality on the Q.commShiftIso component. The resulting equation is precisely the required square with the inverse CochainComplex.mappingCone.shiftIso, the Q shift, κ_n, the reduction functor map, and Functor.commShiftIso (self := sF_n). (uses [dag-lem-pe-principal-tensor-scalar-extension], `lem-pe-principal-cone-tensor-normalization`)
