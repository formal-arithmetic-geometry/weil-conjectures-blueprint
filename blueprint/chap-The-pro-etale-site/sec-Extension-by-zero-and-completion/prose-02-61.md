Once $i^!$ exists, it agrees with $R\underline\Gamma_Z$ on the
localizing category on which $i^!$ is defined, since both are right
adjoints of $i_*$ there.

We treat integral coefficients through their reductions modulo
powers of $\pi_E$. This is possible for complexes that are recovered
from their reductions, the derived complete ones. Derived completeness
is the analogue for complexes of the condition $M=\varprojlim_nM/\pi_E^nM$
on a module. For example, $\mathcal O_{E,X}$ itself is derived complete.
The sheaf $E_X$ is not, since all its reductions vanish.
