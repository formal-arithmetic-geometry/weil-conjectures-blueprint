For arguments with closed supports we need a right adjoint of $i_*$
before $i^!$ is constructed by Brown representability. We build it from
$i^*$, $j^*$ and $Rj_*$ alone. We keep the notation of
Proposition [proposition-extension-zero-restriction-closed-subscheme].
