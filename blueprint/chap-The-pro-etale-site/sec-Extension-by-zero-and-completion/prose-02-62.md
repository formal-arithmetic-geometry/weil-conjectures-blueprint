We write $K_n=K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^{n})_X$
for the reductions of $K$. The canonical transition maps
$K_{n+1}\to K_n$ induce isomorphisms
$K_{n+1}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^{n+1})_X}(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^{n})_X\simeq K_n$,
by associativity of the derived tensor product. We call $(K_n)$, with these isomorphisms, the *tower of reductions*
of $K$.
