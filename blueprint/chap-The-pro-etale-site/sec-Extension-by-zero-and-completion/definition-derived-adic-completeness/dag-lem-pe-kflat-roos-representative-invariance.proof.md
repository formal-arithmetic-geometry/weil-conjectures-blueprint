1. Work in the category A = Mod(R) `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category` and its derived localization Q `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`, using the module-sheaf and derived-category interfaces recorded in [dag-sk-def-pbc-conventions]. Regard each B_n as an R-module by restriction of scalars. Compatibility of the structure maps makes each algebra transition R-linear. For a fixed coefficient N, tensoring on the right preserves zero morphisms by `lem-route-site-sheaf-tensor-right-preserves-zero-morphisms`, so it acts termwise on complexes. The sectionwise maps id tensor g commute with restrictions by `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`; after sheafification they give the coefficient maps, natural in the first tensor variable by `lem-route-site-sheaf-tensor-second-map-naturality`. Thus the level complexes and transitions in the statement are defined, and a tensor id commutes with every transition. Its product A_a:V(P) to V(P') therefore satisfies A_a(1-s_P) = (1-s_P')A_a. Since 1-s_P is a chain map, the displayed Roos differential squares to zero: its second coordinate in d squared is (1-s_P)dx-d(1-s_P)x+d squared y = 0. The diagonal map with entries A_a is consequently the specified chain map L(a). (uses `sk-def-pbc-conventions`, `lem-route-site-sheaf-tensor-right-preserves-zero-morphisms`, `lem-route-site-tensor-second-map-naturality`, `lem-route-site-sheaf-tensor-second-map-naturality`)
2. The sectionwise maps p to p tensor 1 are R-linear, commute with restrictions, and, after sheafification, form the natural transformation tensorUnit. Naturality of the sheafification unit identifies the coefficient map on these tensors with p tensor 1 mapped to p tensor g(1). Algebra transitions preserve 1, so the product unit u_P:P to V(P) satisfies (1-s_P)u_P = 0. Hence eta_P = (u_P,0) is a chain map. Naturality of tensorUnit in each complex, followed by Pi.hom_ext and biprod.hom_ext, proves the strict augmentation equation L(a) eta_P = eta_P' a for every chain map a.
3. Convert independent-coefficient K-flatness to categorical K-flatness in the homotopy category using HomotopyCategory.kFlat_quotient_obj_iff and HomotopyCategory.kFlat_iff_preserves_acyclic: the assumed acyclicity of N tensor P for acyclic N gives acyclicity of P tensor N by the signed braiding, and HomotopyCategory.quotient_obj_mem_subcategoryAcyclic_iff_acyclic identifies these with the acyclic homotopy-category objects. Fix n and put N = B_n concentrated in degree zero. CochainComplex.exists_isKFlatResolutionIndependentCoefficients supplies a K-flat resolution q:K to N. For a quasi-isomorphism a:P to P', categorical K-flatness of K makes a tensor id_K a quasi-isomorphism, while K-flatness of P and P' makes id_P tensor q and id_P' tensor q quasi-isomorphisms. The commuting tensor square, expressed by MonoidalCategory.whisker_exchange, and two-out-of-three (W.comp_mem and W.precomp_iff for the quasi-isomorphism class W) show that a tensor id_N is a quasi-isomorphism. CochainComplex.mapBifunctorSingle₂Iso and siteSheafTensorRightIso identify this map with the level map a tensor_R B_n; their naturality and quasiIso_iff_of_arrow_mk_iso transfer the conclusion to that level map.
4. Exact countable products preserve the level quasi-isomorphisms, by HomologicalComplex.quasiIso_limMap. Thus A_a = productMap a is a quasi-isomorphism. Apply CochainComplex.quasiIso_of_roos_coordinates with the actual products, oneSubShift, roos and roosMap; their coordinate equations hold by definition. This comparison uses the degreewise split short exact sequences 0 to V(P)$[-1]$ to L(P) to V(P) to 0, with inclusion into the second coordinate and projection onto the first. The diagonal map induces A_a$[-1]$ and A_a on the outer terms, both quasi-isomorphisms; the long exact homology sequences give a quasi-isomorphism on the middle term (HomologicalComplex.HomologySequence.quasiIso_τ₂). This proves the strict Roos quasi-isomorphism, and the preceding naturality equation proves the first assertion.
5. Given a derived isomorphism e:Q(P) to Q(P'), use the calculus of fractions `lem-pc-quasi-isomorphisms-multiplicative-system` and right-fraction representation `lem-pc-right-fraction-representation`, in the chain-map form DerivedCategory.right_fac, to obtain t:Z to P and f:Z to P' with Q(t) invertible and e = Q(f) Q(t)^{-1}. DerivedCategory.isIso_Q_map_iff_quasiIso shows that t is a quasi-isomorphism. Since Q(f) = e Q(t) is invertible, it shows that f is also a quasi-isomorphism. Resolve Z by q:W to Z using CochainComplex.exists_isKFlatResolutionIndependentCoefficients. This gives independent-coefficient K-flatness and flat terms for W; transport the termwise tensor exactness through siteSheafTensorRightIso, using (exactFunctor _ _).prop_of_iso, to obtain IsFlatOnSite for each term. The composites u = t q and b = f q are quasi-isomorphisms between the specified K-flat flat-term complexes, so the proved strict result makes L(u) and L(b) quasi-isomorphisms. (uses `lem-pc-quasi-isomorphisms-multiplicative-system`, `lem-pc-right-fraction-representation`)
6. The inverse of Q(L(u)) followed by Q(L(b)) defines an isomorphism v:Q(L(P)) to Q(L(P')). Applying Q to strict naturality gives Q(L(u)) Q(eta_W) = Q(eta_P) Q(u) and Q(L(b)) Q(eta_W) = Q(eta_P') Q(b). The roof identity gives Q(b) = e Q(u). Substitute these identities into v Q(eta_P) Q(u): the result is Q(eta_P') e Q(u). Cancelling the invertible Q(u), as in cancel_epi, proves v Q(eta_P) = Q(eta_P') e. This is the required augmentation-compatible comparison for every supplied e.
7. For a supplied derived isomorphism e use the comparison v. Rewrite QuasiIso of both augmentations as IsIso of their Q-images using DerivedCategory.isIso_Q_map_iff_quasiIso. In the comparison equation, invertibility of Q(eta_P) implies invertibility of Q(eta_P') by IsIso.of_isIso_comp_left applied to e; conversely invertibility of Q(eta_P') implies invertibility of Q(eta_P) by IsIso.of_isIso_comp_right applied to v. This proves both directions of the criterion, so the augmentation being a quasi-isomorphism depends only on the derived isomorphism class of P.
