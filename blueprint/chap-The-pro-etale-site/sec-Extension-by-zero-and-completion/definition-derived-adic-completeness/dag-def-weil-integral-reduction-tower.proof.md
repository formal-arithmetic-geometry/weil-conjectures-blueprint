1. The quotient ring maps satisfy $\rho_n a_{n+1}=a_n$: both send $x\in\mathcal O_E$ to its class modulo $\pi_E^n$. The coefficient pullback is a functor on ring objects by `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, so it preserves this identity. Give $B_n$ the action $r\cdot b=a_n(r)b$. The ring-map laws give the unital and associative action equations of `det-def-sfb-module-objects`. The same identity shows that $\rho_n$ and $a_n$, with $R$ its regular module, commute with these actions. Thus they are morphisms in $\Mod_R$. In Lean, Ideal.Quotient.factor_comp_mk proves the quotient identity; Profinite.continuousMapRingSheafMap_comp and the two pullback functors preserve it. Mod.Hom.mk' constructs the transition using IsMonHom.mul_hom and whisker_exchange; Mod.hom_ext proves uniqueness. Classical.choose_spec gives the prescribed underlying map of the existing unique-choice transition. (uses `det-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, `det-def-sfb-module-objects`)
2. Use the non-finite coefficient row of `det-def-sfb-derived-categories` with $\Lambda=\underline{\mathcal O_E}$: its category is precisely $\mathcal D$. The specialization for a commutative condensed ring and any scheme in `def-sfb-derived-tensor-modules` supplies its derived tensor bifunctor, hence the functor $T_K$. Its relative tensor has regular module $R$ as unit by `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, and the derived unit constraints are induced from complexes by `lem-sfb-derived-monoidal`; thus the unit is $R[0]$ and the right unitor is $r_K$. Place the coefficient maps in degree zero and apply $T_K$ to obtain exactly $K_n,t_n,c_n$ of the statement. All three use the same $R$-module derived category. (uses `det-def-sfb-derived-categories`, `def-sfb-derived-tensor-modules`, `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, `lem-sfb-derived-monoidal`)
3. Functoriality now gives
$$t_n\circ c_{n+1}=T_K(\rho_n[0])\circ T_K(a_{n+1}[0])\circ r_K^{-1}=T_K((\rho_n a_{n+1})[0])\circ r_K^{-1}=T_K(a_n[0])\circ r_K^{-1}=c_n.$$
The tower definition in `def-weil-inverse-system` forms all longer maps by finite composition, with identities at equal indices. Associativity gives composition of these maps, and induction on $m-n$ gives their compatibility with $c_m,c_n$ from the displayed adjacent identity. This is the tower and its compatible family of maps from $K$. The Lean proof first checks the composite of Mod.regularToComapRegular with integralReductionModuleTransition by Mod.hom_ext and integralReductionModuleTransition_hom, then applies Functor.map_comp twice and associativity. (uses `def-weil-inverse-system`)
