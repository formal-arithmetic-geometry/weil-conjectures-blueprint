3. Form the product V equal to the product of the V_n and the endomorphism 1-s whose n-th component is the transition V_(n+1) to V_n, using `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`. Exact countable products in the native module category and the derived product calculation are supplied by `lem-weil-derived-countable-products-core`. The Roos complex is the signed mapping-cone model: in degree i it is V^i direct-sum V^(i-1) with differential (dx,(1-s)x-dy), and the augmentation is (P to V,0). The direct calculation gives d squared equal to zero. The signed coordinate isomorphism from CochainComplex.mappingCocone (the second coordinate is negated) is a chain isomorphism; its induced diagonal map is a quasi-isomorphism by [dag-lem-pe-roos-coordinates-quasi-iso]. The distinguished mapping-cocone triangle `def-weil-abstract-derived-category` therefore yields a derived-limit triangle for the tower, and its first map composed with the augmentation is the product of the canonical unit maps. (uses [dag-lem-pe-roos-coordinates-quasi-iso], `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`, `lem-weil-derived-countable-products-core`, `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`)
4. For consecutive finite levels, restriction along B_(n+1) to B_n composes canonically with restriction along R to B_(n+1); the quotient factorization is the displayed coefficient transition. The derived reduction comparison is consequently the unique left-adjoint comparison for the composite restriction. The Lean proof uses Adjunction.leftAdjointUniq through adjunctionAssociation, and derivedReduction_isNormalized applies Adjunction.homEquiv_unit to the defining unit followed by this comparison; isIso_mate_unit then gives that every consecutive comparison is invertible. The same left-adjoint uniqueness identity, expressed by Adjunction.unit_leftAdjointUniq_hom_app, proves reductionUnit_compatible, so the unit maps form the compatible tower used above.
5. The result is independent of the chosen K-flat representative. Given an isomorphism in the derived category, refine it to a common right-fraction roof using DerivedCategory.right_fac and DerivedCategory.isIso_Q_map_iff_quasiIso, then replace its vertex by the K-flat resolution of `lem-pc-source-module-kflat-resolution`. A quasi-isomorphism between K-flat complexes remains a quasi-isomorphism after tensoring with each B_n: the proof uses CochainComplex.acyclic_tensorRight_of_kFlat, since the K-flat cone is acyclic, with the total-tensor and resolution comparisons supplied by `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy`, `lem-sfb-derived-monoidal`, `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact`, `lem-sc-source-kflat-tensor-left-triangulated`, `lem-sc-derived-tensor-shift-independent-universe`, `lem-sfb-left-resolution-objectwise-cochain`, `lem-sfb-left-resolution-single-row-total`, and `lem-pc-bounded-above-double-complex-total-comparison`. Exact products give QuasiIso of productMap via HomologicalComplex.quasiIso_limMap, and the Roos comparison is a quasi-isomorphism via CochainComplex.quasiIso_of_roos_coordinates and [dag-lem-pe-roos-coordinates-quasi-iso]. Naturality of the two-coordinate (unit,0) maps lets IsIso.of_isIso_comp_left and IsIso.of_isIso_comp_right cancel the roof legs, proving isDerivedComplete_iff and hence that completionMap is invertible exactly for one, equivalently every, K-flat representative. 6. Thus the canonical map in the statement is represented on a K-flat P by the augmentation P to Roos(P) and, after localization, is exactly completionMap; roos_isDerivedLimit identifies its target with the derived inverse limit of the reductions. The final warning is an elementary calculation: for R equal to the l-adic integers and K equal to [R mapped by l to R] in degrees -1 and 0, reduction modulo l has H^-1 equal to the residue field while the ordinary cohomology transition from the next quotient is the zero map between nonzero copies of that field, so it is not invertible (an inverse would force 1=0). The complexes themselves still satisfy the derived reduction identities from the normalized tower, showing why normalization is a property of K_n rather than of their cohomology sheaves.
