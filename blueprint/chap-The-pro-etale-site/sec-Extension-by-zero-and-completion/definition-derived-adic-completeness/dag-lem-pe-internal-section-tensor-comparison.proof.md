1. Use the fixed sectionModuleEquivalence and its sectionModuleFunctor E from `def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence`. The size hypothesis gives essential smallness by essentiallySmall_of_small_of_locallySmall. The ambient tensor is the sheafified objectwise tensor `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`, `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure`, `sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor`. Its closedness makes left tensor preserve colimits; preservesColimits_of_natIso and BraidedCategory.tensorLeftIsoTensorRight give the same for right tensor. The ring-object conventions `sk-def-sfb-ring-object`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object`, `det-def-sfb-ring-object-rings` and transport along the lax monoidal sheaf inclusion `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `sk-def-sfb-transport` reconstruct R_sec and its actions: a section r acts on x by applying the internal action to the image of r tensor x under the inclusion tensorator. Thus E leaves the underlying abelian sheaves and maps unchanged. (uses `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`)
2. The module-object abelian instances `lem-sfb-modobj-abelian`, `lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck` apply to the closed additive tensor on abelian sheaves. As an equivalence, E preserves finite limits and finite colimits; exactFunctor_iff therefore gives its exactness. For additivity, transfer binary products with Adjunction.hasLimitsOfShape_of_equivalence and apply Functor.additive_of_preserves_binary_products. Module sheaves have the required small colimits: form the presheaf colimit and sheafify, using the adjunction `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`. Adjunction.has_colimits_of_equivalence transfers these colimits to internal modules. In particular the coproducts indexed by p+q=n exist, giving comparison_internalTotalExists, and E preserves them because it is an equivalence. (uses `lem-sfb-modobj-abelian`, `lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck`, `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`)
3. Reuse the fully proved universal-property construction exists_sectionTensorIso from `lem-c2-internal-section-module-flatness`. It gives an isomorphism from E(M tensor_R N) to the sheafification of the canonical sectionwise balanced tensor presheaf, with an explicit equation on pure tensors. Here the internal pairing is the lax sheaf-inclusion tensorator followed by Mod.tensorObjπ, the coequalizer projection `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, `pur713-def-relative-tensor`. Compose this isomorphism with the inverse of SheafOfModules.tensorObjIsoSheafification for the tensor of [dag-def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. The resulting e(M,N) sends this internal pure tensor to the image of x tensor_{R_sec(U)} y under the module-sheafification unit `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification` and then the inverse canonical comparison. This is precisely comparison_sectionTmul, so the displayed normalization follows from exists_sectionTensorIso.choose_spec. (uses `lem-c2-internal-section-module-flatness`, `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`)
4. Maps from E(M tensor_R N) to a module sheaf are determined by their values on these local pure tensors. Indeed, forget to abelian sheaves, cancel the epimorphism Mod.tensorObjπ, apply Sheaf.tensor_hom_ext for the sheaf tensor `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure`, and use TensorProduct.induction_on sectionwise. For triples cancel both relative tensor projections, the inner one tensored with the third factor. This remains epic since tensor preserves colimits. The sheaf-inclusion tensorator is a local equivalence: it is the adjoint of the strong sheafification comparison followed by the counits, as Adjunction.homEquiv_unit shows. Monoidality of local equivalences lets one tensor this map with the third presheaf; maps from it into a sheaf are determined uniquely. A second TensorProduct.induction_on therefore reduces maps from E((M tensor_R N) tensor_R P) to nested pure tensors. Naturality of e now follows on pure tensors from Mod.tensorObjπ_whiskerRight, Mod.tensorObjπ_whiskerLeft and Functor.LaxMonoidal.μ_natural. (uses `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure`)
5. For associativity and symmetry use the relative tensor structure `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor`, `def-sfb-modobj-symmetric-tensor` and the module-sheaf symmetric monoidal structure `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`. Internally, Mod.tensorObjπ_associator_hom and Mod.tensorObjπ_braidingHomX, together with the lax monoidal associativity and braided identities of abelian-sheaf inclusion, send nested pure tensors to reassociated tensors and send x tensor y to y tensor x. On the section side, the pairing is the tensorator of the right adjoint in PresheafOfModules.sheafificationAdjunction `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`. Functor.LaxMonoidal.associativity and Functor.LaxBraided.braided give the same formulas. The normalization of e and the two- and three-factor extensionality just proved give exactly the asserted associator and braiding equations. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`, `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`)
6. The internal unit is the regular module. Its transported sectionwise action `def-sfb-modobj-transport` is multiplication in R_sec, so PresheafOfModules.isoMk and ModuleCat.isoMk construct the unit comparison e₀ as the identity on every section. For the internal unitors, use the symmetric bimodules attached to modules `lem-sfb-modobj-symmetric-bimodules`: unfolding the bimodule unitors and applying coequalizer.π_desc shows that the left unitor composed with Mod.tensorObjπ is the action, and the right one is the braiding followed by the action. Thus they send r tensor x and x tensor r to r times x. For the section unitors `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`, the inclusion unit comparison is the identity by Adjunction.homEquiv_unit and Adjunction.right_triangle_components. Functor.LaxMonoidal.left_unitality and Functor.LaxMonoidal.right_unitality give the same action formulas. Pure-tensor extensionality and e₀(r)=r now prove both unitor equations. (uses `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-tensor-symmetric-monoidal`)
7. Apply E termwise to cochain complexes `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`. Form the isomorphism of commuting tensor bicomplexes by e(K^p,L^q) in each bidegree, using HomologicalComplex.Hom.isoOfComponents twice; naturality checks both differentials. Apply HomologicalComplex₂.total.mapIso and compose with the inverse of HomologicalComplex₂.mapTotalIso from `lem-sfb-left-resolution-map-total-iso`. Its hypotheses hold by additivity, existence of the diagonal coproducts and their preservation proved above. The coproduct-injection formulas for mapTotalIso and total.map give F(ι_{p,q}) followed by t^n equal to e(K^p,L^q) followed by the target injection. This is the asserted summand formula for the direct-sum total tensor `def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves`; the bicomplex totalization supplies the differential d₁+(-1)^p d₂. No boundedness assumption is used. (uses `lem-sfb-left-resolution-map-total-iso`)
8. For each degree use ShortComplex.exact_map_iff_of_faithful for the exact faithful equivalence E. Acyclicity is exactness of these short complexes, so it is preserved and reflected. For flat modules `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`, use flat_iff_sectionModule from `lem-c2-internal-section-module-flatness`. If tensorRight M is exact, BraidedCategory.tensorLeftIsoTensorRight M and (exactFunctor _ _).prop_of_iso make tensorLeft M exact too, giving the two-sided internal flatness condition required by that result. Conversely sectionwise flatness gives this two-sided condition, whose right-tensor component is the required internal exactness. (uses `lem-c2-internal-section-module-flatness`)
9. Finally use the acyclic-total-tensor test for K-flatness `def-pc-kflat-complex`. For a sectionwise acyclic test complex B take A to be its image under the inverse termwise equivalence, with counit isomorphism E(A) ≅ B. Invariance of exactness under isomorphism and the acyclicity comparison make A acyclic. Internal K-flatness makes Tot(A tensor K) acyclic; transport by E and the constructed total-complex isomorphism, then by the tensor functor applied to the counit, to obtain acyclicity of Tot(B tensor E(K)). Conversely apply sectionwise K-flatness to E(A) for every internal acyclic A, use the inverse total-complex isomorphism and reflect acyclicity. These are all the tests in CochainComplex.IsKFlatIndependentCoefficients, proving the final equivalence.
