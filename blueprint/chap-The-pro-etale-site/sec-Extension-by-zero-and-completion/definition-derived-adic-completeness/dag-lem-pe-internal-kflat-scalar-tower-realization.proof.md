1. Work in the unbounded derived-category localization Q of `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`; in the sheaf-module instances the exact right-derived-functor convention is the one recorded in `def-weil-derived-direct-image`. The native total tensor on complexes passes to homotopy by `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy` and its additive bifunctorial homotopy behavior is `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact`. The K-flat subcategory is a left derivability structure by `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability`. The resolution construction uses objectwise cochain resolutions `lem-sfb-left-resolution-objectwise-cochain`, the single-row total identification `lem-sfb-left-resolution-single-row-total`, and preservation of the relevant total coproducts `lem-sfb-left-resolution-map-total-iso`; whenever a bounded double-complex total is compared, the columnwise quasi-isomorphism criterion is `lem-pc-bounded-above-double-complex-total-comparison`. Bounded-above flat rows are K-flat by `lem-sfb-bounded-above-flat-kflat`, and the resulting derived tensor is independent of the chosen K-flat model by `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`. (uses `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`, `def-weil-derived-direct-image`, `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy`, `lem-sf-total-bifunctor-homotopy-exact`, `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability`, `lem-sfb-left-resolution-objectwise-cochain`, `lem-sfb-left-resolution-single-row-total`, `lem-sfb-left-resolution-map-total-iso`, `lem-pc-bounded-above-double-complex-total-comparison`, `lem-sfb-bounded-above-flat-kflat`, `lem-kun-derived-tensor-well-defined`)
2. For a ring map q_n, use the native extension-restriction adjunction of `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction`. Its balanced coequalizer comparison `Mod.extendForgetTensorObjIso` identifies restriction of ordinary extension with tensoring by the restricted regular module. The comparison is made R-linear by `Mod.hom_ext` after precomposition with the epimorphic `Mod.extendπ`; `Mod.whiskerLeft_extendπ_smul_assoc` and the balanced tensor projection prove the action equation. Compose with `extendTensorNatIso` and the braiding to obtain `extendTensorRightNatIso`, i.e. the right-tensor comparison used below. Termwise units and counits are morphisms of complexes by `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`. (uses `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction`, `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`)
3. For each q_n, form `computationCounit` from the universal left-derived counit. The universal property and its K-flat criterion are `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`, so `Mod.extendScalars q_n` has an invertible counit component on the given K-flat P (the Lean instance is `isIso_leftDerivedFunctorCounit_app_of_kFlat`). The cochain adjunction transpose is obtained from `adjunction_cochain_transpose` in `lem-pur-cochain-adjunction-compatibility`, and the exact restriction factorization is the inverse formula `Functor.exactDerivedRestrictionComposition_inv_factors` supplied by `lem-pur-exact-restriction-composition`. Consequently `computationCounit_unit` identifies the derived adjunction unit followed by the computed counit and the restriction factor with the Q-image of the ordinary termwise adjunction unit. (uses `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`, `lem-pur-cochain-adjunction-compatibility`, `lem-pur-exact-restriction-composition`)
4. The ordinary unit is calculated with `ordinaryUnit_tensorLeft` and then `ordinaryUnit_tensorRight`: the first is the unit of `Mod.extendRestrictAdjunction` from `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction` followed by `extendTensorIso`, and the second uses the braiding identity `leftUnitor_inv_braiding`. The fact that this unit is a termwise complex map is the adjunction-on-complexes result `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`. With `hι`, the coefficient map is exactly ι_n, so `computationRight_unit` says that the derived q_n-unit followed by the right-tensor computation is the Q-image of `tensorUnit R B ι P n`; no flatness of individual terms is used. (uses `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction`, `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`)
5. For consecutive levels, define the canonical coefficient map `canonicalτ := (eB (n+1)).inv ≫ τ_n ≫ (eB n).hom`. The hypothesis hτ gives `canonicalτ.hom = r_n`. The ordinary balanced scalar-change identity is `ordinaryScalarChange_tensor`, whose formula is s⊗p ↦ r_n(s)⊗p; taking its adjunction mate uses `Adjunction.homEquiv_unit`, `Adjunction.homEquiv_naturality_right`, and the uniqueness of left adjoints in `derivedAssociation`. The termwise mate calculation is justified by `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`. The equality q_{n+1}≫r_n=q_n is inserted through `restrictionComp`, and `lem-pur-exact-restriction-composition` supplies the required quotient-factor comparison. The Lean equalities `rawDerivedStep_transpose`, `rawStep_computation`, `restriction_hom_factor`, and `computationRight_step` then give the transition equation for the right-tensor computation; `transportTensorStep` converts canonicalτ back to τ_n through eB. (uses `lem-pur-exact-restriction-composition`, `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`)
6. Define c_n on complexes as `computationRight (q n) ≫ Q.map (transport n)`, where `transport n` is the inverse of right tensoring with eB_n. Naturality follows from the cochain transpose compatibility `lem-pur-cochain-adjunction-compatibility`, the exact restriction factorization `lem-pur-exact-restriction-composition`, and the naturality of `computationCounit`, `extendTensorRightNatIso`, and the tensor transport. The K-flat counit isomorphism supplied by `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus` makes each c_n(P) invertible, while the unit and transition calculations give exactly the two equations required by `natural_computation`. (uses `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`, `lem-pur-cochain-adjunction-compatibility`, `lem-pur-exact-restriction-composition`)
7. For the derived right-tensor interface, use the lax monoidal structure of Q from `lem-sfb-derived-monoidal`, with the complex-level total tensor supplied by `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy`. The Lean `tensorComparison` is `Functor.LaxMonoidal.μ` followed by `CochainComplex.mapBifunctorSingle₂Iso`; its naturality is `Functor.LaxMonoidal.μ_natural_left/right` together with `CochainComplex.mapBifunctorSingle₂Iso_hom_naturality`, and its unit identity uses `Functor.LaxMonoidal.right_unitality_inv_assoc` and `single_unit`. Its component is invertible on K-flat complexes by `DerivedMonoidal.isIso_counit_app_app_of_kFlat_left`. Combining it with c_n and descending through the fully faithful K-flat localization supplied by `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability` (`Localization.fullyFaithfulWhiskeringLeft` and `Localization.natTrans_ext`) gives the natural derived realization and preserves both transition and unit equations. The localization and tensor conventions used here are those of `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`. (uses `lem-sfb-derived-monoidal`, `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`, `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability`, `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy`)
8. Finally apply the Lean `realizeTransport` lemma with F_n = reduction_n ⋙ restriction_n, t_n = `step`, unit_n the derived q_n-unit, V_n = Q(tensorLevel_n P), d_n = Q(tensorStep_n), and c_n as above. The existence and invertibility of c_n on K-flat representatives come from the left-derived calculus `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`, while the meaning of Q and its functorial transport is `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`. Set `e_n = (c_n(P)).symm ≪≫ F_n.mapIso eP`. Naturality of `step` is proved by `towerStep_naturality`; the two hypotheses of `realizeTransport` are precisely the transition and unit equations already established for c_n. Thus each e_n has the required source and target, and it satisfies both displayed identities for K. The argument is formal for P=0 as well, and no assumption that q_n or any term of P is flat is introduced. (uses `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`, `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`)
