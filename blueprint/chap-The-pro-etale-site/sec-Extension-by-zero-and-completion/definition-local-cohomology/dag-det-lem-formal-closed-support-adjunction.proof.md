1. Work on the small site models in [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], in the common enlarged universe of `def-weil-size-convention`. Write $B=\Lambda_X=p_X^*\Lambda$ as in `sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients`, and work with left module objects over this ring object. For $f:Y\to X$, the canonical comparison $f^*p_X^*\cong p_Y^*$ of [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso] is monoidal by [dag-sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp]. Its component $\alpha_f$ is `def-sfb-alpha`; it is a ring morphism by [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom] and an isomorphism by [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-iso]. The coefficient morphisms of [dag-sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms] therefore identify $f^*B$ with $\Lambda_Y$. Thus the coefficient rings on $Z,V$ really are pulled-back rings. In Lean, supportCoefficientEquivalence is the equivalence given by restriction of scalars along $\alpha_f^{-1}$ and $\alpha_f$; Mod.comapId, Mod.comapComp and Mod.comapCongr provide its unit and counit. This uses no multiplicative commutativity. (uses [dag-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-iso], [dag-sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp], [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-ring-hom], [dag-lem-sfb-alpha-iso], [dag-sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms], `def-sfb-alpha`)
2. On abelian sheaves use the actual inverse/direct images and their adjunction `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. The strong monoidal inverse image `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal` and lax monoidal direct image `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal` form the monoidal adjunction `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`. Transport module actions by mapMod as in `def-sfb-modobj-transport` and `sk-def-sfb-transport`. Explicitly, supportPullback is $\operatorname{Res}_{\alpha_f^{-1}}\circ\operatorname{mapMod}(f^*_{\mathrm{Ab}})$, whereas $f_*$ transports along the abelian direct image and restricts along the mate $\Lambda_X\to f_*\Lambda_Y$. Apply the lifted adjunction `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`, namely Adjunction.mapModAdjunction, and compose with the scalar equivalence of Step 1. The mate identity identifies its right adjoint with the given module pushforward through comapPullbackHomCompIso; Adjunction.ofNatIsoRight gives supportPullbackAdjunction, $f^*\dashv f_*$. This is the transport-and-change-of-scalars construction of `sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback`, using inverse scalar restriction to extend it to arbitrary condensed rings. In the commutative case inverse restriction along the isomorphism $\alpha_f$ agrees with the usual extension of scalars. (uses `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-star-monoidal`, `def-sfb-finite-lower-star-lax-monoidal`, `lem-sfb-finite-monoidal-adjunction`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport`, `sk-def-sfb-transport`, `def-sfb-modobj-transport-adjunction`)
3. These module functors are additive. The faithful forgetful functor `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful` creates the relevant limits and colimits by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, and preserves finite limits and colimits by `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-left-exact` and `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-right-exact`. It reflects isomorphisms: the inverse of an invertible underlying linear map is linear. Since abelian inverse image is exact by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], its module transport is exact. Scalar restriction is exact by Mod.preservesFiniteLimits_comap and Mod.preservesFiniteColimits_comap, the restriction-of-scalars results exported in `sk-def-sfb-derived-extension-of-scalars`; these preservation statements hold for arbitrary monoid morphisms. Thus supportPullback preserves finite limits and finite colimits. The abelian sheaf categories are Grothendieck by `lem-sfb-proetale-sheaves-grothendieck`. Lean's Mod.isGrothendieckAbelian gives the Grothendieck structure on supportModules for an arbitrary monoid object. Consequently the stated unbounded derived categories and K-injective resolutions exist, as in `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-faithful`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-creates`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-left-exact`, `lem-sfb-modobj-forget-right-exact`, `sk-def-sfb-derived-extension-of-scalars`, `lem-sfb-proetale-sheaves-grothendieck`)
4. For the open immersion $j$, start with the abelian extension by zero `def-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero` and its adjunction to restriction `def-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero-adjunction`. It is exact by `lem-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero-exact` and fully faithful as recorded in `sk-def-sfb-finite-lower-shriek`. The open projection formula is the one supplied by hasProjectionFormula_openImmersion in `sk-def-sfb-module-lower-shriek`. In the terminology of `def-sfb-adjunction-has-projection-formula`, its canonical projection maps are invertible; `def-sfb-adjunction-projection-formula` assembles them into the projection formula. Apply `def-sfb-modobj-linearization`: the action on $j_!^{\mathrm{Ab}}M$ is $B\otimes j_!^{\mathrm{Ab}}M\cong j_!^{\mathrm{Ab}}(j^*B\otimes M)\to j_!^{\mathrm{Ab}}M$. By `def-sfb-modobj-linearization-transport-adjunction`, Adjunction.mapModLeftAdjunction makes this linearization left adjoint to mapMod of abelian restriction. Compose with the inverse scalar equivalence to obtain supportOpenExtensionAdjunction, $j_!\dashv j^*$. Its underlying sheaf is abelian extension by zero; the exactness and forgetful-functor arguments of Step 3 make it exact. These constructions require only a monoid object, so also apply to noncommutative $\Lambda$. Adjunction.preservesKInjectiveComplexes now shows that $j^*I$ is K-injective whenever $I$ is. (uses `def-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero`, `def-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero-adjunction`, `lem-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero-exact`, `sk-def-sfb-finite-lower-shriek`, `sk-def-sfb-module-lower-shriek`, `def-sfb-adjunction-has-projection-formula`, `def-sfb-adjunction-projection-formula`, `def-sfb-modobj-linearization`, `def-sfb-modobj-linearization-transport-adjunction`)
5. The localized open restriction/direct-image adjunction has invertible counit, the counit used in `def-sfb-finite-lower-shriek-to-lower-star`. Hence the ordinary abelian direct image is fully faithful. Transfer this to modules using Adjunction.mapModAdjunction_counit_app_hom, reflection of isomorphisms by the forgetful functor, and the scalar equivalence of Step 1. This proves supportOpenCounit. Similarly `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-abelian-closed-support` gives the invertible closed counit and the invertible closed unit on any abelian sheaf whose open restriction vanishes. Adjunction.mapModAdjunction_unit_app_hom and Adjunction.comp_unit_app lift these actual units to modules. Thus $i_*$ is fully faithful and a module $N$ with $j^*N=0$ has invertible unit $N\to i_*i^*N$. Also $j^*i_*A=0$: by the base-change formula [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] and the open restriction formula, every evaluation is on an empty base change, since the images of $i,j$ are disjoint. Scheme.ProEt.isTerminalSectionsOfIsEmpty makes these abelian sections zero, and faithfulness of the module forgetful functor gives the module vanishing. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `def-sfb-finite-lower-shriek-to-lower-star`, `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-abelian-closed-support`)
6. Put $N(M)=\ker(M\to j_*j^*M)$ and $H=\underline\Gamma_Z=N\,;\,i^*$, exactly supportKernel followed by supportPullback. Naturality of the open unit defines the maps of $N$ through kernel.map. Kernel-map addition is checked by equalizer.hom_ext, so $H$ is additive. The open counit and the triangle identity make the open pullback of the unit invertible. Since $j^*$ preserves kernels, isZero_kernel_of_mono and PreservesKernel.iso give $j^*N(M)=0$. Apply the supported-unit assertion of `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-abelian-closed-support` and its module lift from Step 5. The resulting natural isomorphism $N(M)\to i_*H(M)$ is precisely closedSupportKernelComparison. Its inverse followed by the kernel inclusion is the specified support counit $\varepsilon:i_*H\to\mathrm{id}$. Every map $i_*A\to M$ has zero composite with the open unit by the open adjunction and $j^*i_*A=0$. Factor it through the kernel and use full faithfulness of $i_*$ to obtain the natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_X(i_*A,M)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_Z(A,H(M))$. Adjunction.mkOfHomEquiv constructs the adjunction with counit $\varepsilon$, and Adjunction.ext_counit proves its uniqueness with this normalization. Its right adjoint preserves limits by Adjunction.rightAdjoint_preservesLimits, proving left exactness of $H$. Moreover Adjunction.leftAdjoint_preservesColimits makes $i_*$ preserve finite colimits; together with its left exactness this proves exactness of closed direct image under precisely the complementary quasi-compact-open hypotheses here. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-abelian-closed-support`)

9. For clause (3), first lift full faithfulness of abelian extension by zero through mapModLeft and scalar transport as in Step 4. The abelian adjunction triple identifies invertibility of the extension unit with invertibility of the open direct-image counit; after the module lift, Adjunction.unit_isIso_of_L_fully_faithful gives the invertible module extension unit. The triangle identity then makes restriction of its counit $c_M:j_!j^*M\to M$ invertible. Its kernel has zero open restriction and its underlying abelian kernel embeds in an extension by zero. ProEtale.isZero_of_mono_openImmersionLowerShriek from the finite extension-by-zero machinery `sk-def-sfb-finite-lower-shriek` makes that kernel zero; faithfulness and Preadditive.mono_of_isZero_kernel prove $c_M$ monic. Put $F=j_*j^*I$. Let $\varphi:j_!j^*F\to I$ be adjoint to the open direct-image counit $j^*F\to j^*I$. Injective.factorThru extends $\varphi$ along the monic $c_F$ to $\sigma:F\to I$, with $\sigma c_F=\varphi$ by Injective.comp_factorThru. Apply the extension adjunction and its triangle identity to get $j^*\sigma$ equal to the open direct-image counit. The open adjunction and its triangle identity then give $\eta_I\sigma=\mathrm{id}_F$. Hence the open unit is split epic, and therefore epic. (uses `sk-def-sfb-finite-lower-shriek`)

11. For the fibre formula, let $K\cong QI$ with $I$ K-injective, and let $g:I\to j_*j^*I$ be the actual termwise sheaf unit. By Step 4, $j^*I$ is K-injective, so the right-derived unit for $j_*$ is invertible there. Adjunction.derivedη_fac_app compares the derived open unit at the homotopy localization of $I$ with the homotopy class of $g$. Adjunction.mapHomotopyCategory_unit_app_quotient identifies that class, and naturality of DerivedCategory.quotientCompQhIso transports the comparison to $QI$. Thus there is an isomorphism $Rj_*j^*(QI)\cong Q(j_*j^*I)$ under which the derived open unit is exactly $Q(g)$. Take CochainComplex.mappingCocone.triangle of $g$; its first vertex is mappingCone(g)$[-1]$, and DerivedCategory.mappingCocone_triangle_distinguished makes its image under $Q$ distinguished. Rotate this triangle and the support triangle from Step 10. Their first two vertices and first arrow agree through the unit comparison. isoTriangleOfIso₁₂ identifies their third vertices, and the fully faithful shift functor cancels the common $[1]$, identifying $i_*RH(QI)$ with $Q(\operatorname{mappingCone}(g)[-1])$. The closed counit of `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-abelian-closed-support` lifts as in Step 5, so the derived closed pullback/direct-image counit is invertible by full faithfulness. Apply exact $i^*$, acting on derived categories by `lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated`, to the isomorphism and use this counit to cancel $i^*i_*$. Finally transport $RH$ along the given $K\cong QI$. This proves $R\underline\Gamma_ZK\cong i^*\operatorname{fib}(K\to Rj_*j^*K)$, with the fibre represented by the shifted mapping cone of the actual open unit. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-abelian-closed-support`, `lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated`)
