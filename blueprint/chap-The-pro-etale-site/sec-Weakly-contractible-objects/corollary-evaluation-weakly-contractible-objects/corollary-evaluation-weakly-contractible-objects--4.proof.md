In the common module universe, limits of sheaves of modules are
computed on sections, so evaluation preserves them. The category of
module sheaves is Grothendieck abelian and has a cogenerator. After
replacing it by an equivalent category whose morphisms lie in the
common universe, the special adjoint functor theorem makes evaluation
a right adjoint. It therefore preserves existing limits of every
diagram size. Transporting this property back along the equivalence
gives the stated result for the original evaluation functor.
