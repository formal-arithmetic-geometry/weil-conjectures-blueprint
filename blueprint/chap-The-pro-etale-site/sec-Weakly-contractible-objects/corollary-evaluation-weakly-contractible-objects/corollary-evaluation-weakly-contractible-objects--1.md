If $W$ is affine, evaluation $F\mapsto F(W)$ from abelian sheaves to
abelian groups is exact and preserves all small colimits in the chosen
universe. Here weak contractibility means that every epimorphism of
sheaves of sets induces a surjection on $W$-sections.
