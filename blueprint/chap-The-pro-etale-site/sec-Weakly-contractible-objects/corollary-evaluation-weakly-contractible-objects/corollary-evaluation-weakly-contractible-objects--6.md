Let $A\xrightarrow{f}B\xrightarrow{g}C$ be a sequence of
$\mathcal R$-module sheaves with $g\circ f=0$. It is exact at $B$ if and
only if

$$
A(W)\xrightarrow{f(W)}B(W)\xrightarrow{g(W)}C(W)
$$

is exact for every affine weakly contractible $W$.

Consequently a complex $K$ of $\mathcal R$-module sheaves is acyclic if
and only if $K(W)$ is acyclic for every such $W$. No boundedness
assumption is needed.
