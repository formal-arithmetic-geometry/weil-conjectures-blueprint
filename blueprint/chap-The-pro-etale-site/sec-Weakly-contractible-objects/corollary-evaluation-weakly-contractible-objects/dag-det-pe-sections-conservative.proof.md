1. First record precisely the local lifting criterion for module exactness. Put $Z=\ker(g)$ and let $u:A\to Z$ be induced by $f$; its existence follows from $gf=0$. The sequence is exact at $B$ precisely when $\operatorname{coker}(u)=Z/\operatorname{im}(f)=0$. By [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful](1), the underlying abelian sheaf of this cokernel is the abelian cokernel of $u$. Faithfulness reflects the zero object: if the identity of its underlying object is zero then the identity of the module is zero. Hence exactness is equivalent to the underlying abelian map $u$ being epic. By `lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaf-epi-locally-surjective` this is equivalent to local lifting of every section of $Z$. Finally $Z(V)=\ker(g(V))$ by `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`. Thus exactness at $B$ is equivalent to: every $b\in B(V)$ with $g(b)=0$ lifts through $f$ on some covering of $V$. This argument applies to any sheaf of rings, without commutativity. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful`, `lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaf-epi-locally-surjective`)
2. By `det-pe-wcb-cover`, every site object V admits a covering by objects W_i with an isomorphism Spec D_i ≅ W_i.left and D_i w-contractible. Affineness transfers across this isomorphism. Apply the closed source theorem isWeaklyContractible_of_iso, recorded by [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-affine-wcontractible-evaluation], to its inverse. This makes each W_i weakly contractible in the full module universe max u (t+1). No equivalence with representable projectivity is needed. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-affine-wcontractible-evaluation], `det-pe-wcb-cover`)
3. Assume the sequence exact. The kernel lift u is epic, and the exact forgetful functor of step 1 preserves epimorphisms. The general abelian-sheaf epi criterion `lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaf-epi-locally-surjective` therefore makes its underlying abelian sheaf map locally surjective. Mathlib Presheaf.isLocallySurjective_iff_whisker_forget identifies this with local surjectivity after forgetting to sheaves of sets. Apply the definition GrothendieckTopology.IsWeaklyContractible at W directly to get surjectivity on sections. Evaluation preserves kernels, so ShortComplex.exact_map_iff_epi_map_kernel_lift and ModuleCat.epi_iff_surjective give exactness after evaluation. There is no conversion to an epimorphism of set sheaves and no use of the full weak-contractibility equivalence theorem. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaf-epi-locally-surjective`)
4. Conversely, suppose the evaluated sequence is exact for every $W\in\mathcal W$. Given $b\in B(V)$ with $g(b)=0$, take the covering $\{W_i\to V\}$ of step 2. Exactness on $W_i$ gives $a_i\in A(W_i)$ with $f(a_i)=b|_{W_i}$. The sections $a_i$ need not agree on overlaps: step 1 requires only local lifts. It therefore proves exactness at $B$, completing clause 1.
5. For any cochain complex $K$, its cohomology object in degree $q$ is $\ker(d^q)/\operatorname{im}(d^{q-1})$ by `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`. It vanishes exactly when $K^{q-1}\to K^q\to K^{q+1}$ is exact. Apply clause 1 for each $q\in\mathbf Z$; exchanging the two universal quantifiers $q$ and $W$ proves clause 2. No boundedness hypothesis is used.
6. An isomorphism evaluates to an isomorphism, hence to a bijective module map. Conversely, apply the exactness equivalence from steps 1-4 to the short complexes A --0--> A --f--> B and A --f--> B --0--> B. Bijectivity of each evaluated f makes these evaluated complexes exact by ShortComplex.exact_iff_mono and ShortComplex.exact_iff_epi. The original complexes are therefore exact, making f both mono and epi. Since the module-sheaf category is abelian, isIso_of_mono_of_epi f finishes. This uses only the exactness equivalence already proved and standard abelian category lemmas; no separate inverse-gluing proof is necessary.
7. Let $v:K\to L$ be a morphism of complexes and use its cone $C(v)^q=L^q\oplus K^{q+1}$ with differential $(l,k)\mapsto(d_Ll+v(k),-d_Kk)$. By `lem-pur-quasi-iso-cone-acyclic`, $v$ is a quasi-isomorphism exactly when $C(v)$ is acyclic; the same lemma applies in the abelian category of $\mathcal R(W)$-modules. Finite biproducts are finite products, and `lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-limits-sectionwise` computes these sectionwise. Evaluation is additive by `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`, so the displayed differential evaluates to precisely the differential of $C(v(W))$. Thus $C(v)(W)\cong C(v(W))$ as complexes. Clause 2 now gives that $v$ is a quasi-isomorphism if and only if every $v(W)$ is, completing clause 3. (uses `lem-pur-quasi-iso-cone-acyclic`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-limits-sectionwise`)
