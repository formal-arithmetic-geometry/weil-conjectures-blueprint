*Shared context.* Let $W$ be weakly contractible in
$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$, for example $\operatorname{Spec}(D)$ with $D$
w-contractible. Let $\mathcal R$ be a sheaf of rings, not necessarily
commutative. Complexes are cochain complexes indexed by $\mathbf Z$,
with no boundedness assumption.

For a scheme $X$ in universe $u$, the abelian sheaves in the first detail
take values in universe $u+1$. If the coefficient rings are in universe
$r$, the module detection statements use the common universe
$\max\{u+1,r\}$. For limits and colimits this may be enlarged to
$\max\{u+1,r,v\}$, and the diagram universes are independent. Weak
contractibility is tested on sheaves of sets in the relevant universe;
limit and colimit preservation concerns all existing limits and colimits
of the specified sizes.
