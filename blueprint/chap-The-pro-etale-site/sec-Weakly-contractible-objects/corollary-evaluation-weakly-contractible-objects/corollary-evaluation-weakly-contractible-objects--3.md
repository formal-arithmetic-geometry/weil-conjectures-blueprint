This detail is bypassed by the Lean route, which uses exact evaluation
and detection of acyclicity and quasi-isomorphisms without a separate
unbounded derived-sections comparison at $W$.

For every complex $K$, $R\Gamma(W,K)\simeq K(W)$, with the right side
computed term by term.
