By clause [corollary-evaluation-weakly-contractible-objects--1], termwise evaluation takes acyclic complexes to acyclic complexes and quasi-isomorphisms to quasi-isomorphisms. Consequently it already computes its derived functor. For any complex $K$, an injective replacement evaluates to a complex quasi-isomorphic to $K(W)$, giving the asserted identification without a boundedness restriction.
