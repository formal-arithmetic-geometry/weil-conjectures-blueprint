Note that the splitting condition concerns a covering family as a whole. If $W$
is disconnected, a lift of $\mathrm{id}_{h_W}$ along a covering family
may use different members of the family on different clopen pieces
of $W$.

w-contractible ring is weakly contractible. By
Theorem [theorem-enough-affine-weakly-contractible-objects], such
