Cover $X$ by affine opens. These opens are pro-étale affines over $X$,
and their Zariski covering is a pro-étale covering by
[proposition-pro-etale-coverings--2]. Localizing at each such open
identifies the two sites with the corresponding sites over that open:
morphisms between pro-étale affines are again pro-étale affine, and
morphisms between weakly étale $X$-schemes are weakly étale by
[lemma-stability]. Thus it suffices to work over an affine base.

Now cover the source by affine opens and use transitivity of coverings.
We are reduced to a weakly étale ring map $R\to B$.
By [theorem-pro-etale-refinements-weakly-etale], there is a faithfully
flat ind-étale map $B\to B'$ such that $R\to B'$ is ind-étale.
Then $\operatorname{Spec}(B')$ is a pro-étale affine over
$\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, while
$\operatorname{Spec}(B')\to\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ is surjective,
quasi-compact and weakly étale, using [proposition-etale-implications].
It is therefore a singleton pro-étale covering. The sieve generated by
maps from pro-étale affines contains this covering on each chart, and
is consequently covering on every object.
