Cover $X$ by affine opens and their inverse images in $U$ by affine opens. These are pro-étale coverings, so it suffices to treat a chart corresponding to a weakly étale map $R\to B$. Theorem [theorem-pro-etale-refinements-weakly-etale] provides a faithfully flat cover $\operatorname{Spec}(B')\to\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ with $B'$ ind-étale over $R$. Hence $\operatorname{Spec}(B')$ is a pro-étale affine over $X$. Composing the coverings proves the assertion.
