By clause [proposition-pro-etale-affines-form-basis--1], the full subsite of pro-étale affines covers every object of $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$. The induced topology is the restriction of the original topology. The comparison lemma for sites therefore makes restriction an equivalence on sheaves; it applies both to sets and, by preserving the abelian group operations, to abelian sheaves. This is the comparison used in [bhatt-scholze15, Lemma 4.2.4].
