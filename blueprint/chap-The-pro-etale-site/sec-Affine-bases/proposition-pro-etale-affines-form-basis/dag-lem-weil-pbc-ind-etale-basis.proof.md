1. Write $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$. For an affine open $W=\operatorname{Spec}B$ of a pro-etale object $U$, the restriction $W\to X$ is flat because its stalk maps are unchanged, by `def-weil-open-immersion` and `def-weil-flat-morphism`. Its diagonal is the pullback of the flat diagonal of $U/X$ along the open immersion $W\times_X W\to U\times_X U$, so it too is flat. On affines this diagonal corresponds to the multiplication map $B\otimes_A B\to B$. The affine flatness criterion `lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-affine-criterion`, applied to the restriction and its diagonal, therefore shows that $A\to B$ is weakly etale in the ring sense of [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. This is the affine translation used by `WeaklyEtale.Spec_iff` in the Lean proof. (uses `def-weil-open-immersion`, `def-weil-flat-morphism`, `lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-affine-criterion`)
2. Apply [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-weakly-etale-indetale-refinement], namely `RingHom.WeaklyEtale.exists_indEtale_comp`, to obtain a ring $B'$ in the same universe with $B\to B'$ ind-etale and faithfully flat and $A\to B'$ ind-etale. By `proAffineEtale_Spec_iff`, the composite $\operatorname{Spec}B'\to X$ is an affine pro-etale object, hence a basis object by [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. The arrow $\operatorname{Spec}B'\to W$ is weakly etale by `RingHom.IndEtale.weaklyEtale` and `WeaklyEtale.Spec_iff`. Here the flatness part of the ind-etale-to-weakly-etale implication passes from the flat etale terms to their filtered colimit by `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`; `RingHom.IndEtale.weaklyEtale` also supplies flatness of the multiplication map. The faithfully-flat-surjective-spectrum lemma `lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-surjective-spectrum`, used as `RingHom.FaithfullyFlat.iff_flat_and_comap_surjective`, makes this arrow surjective. It is affine and therefore quasi-compact, so `Hom.singleton_mem_propQCPrecoverage` makes its singleton a covering in [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]. The affine opens of $U$ form a Zariski covering, which is a pro-etale covering by `zariskiPrecoverage_le_propQCPrecoverage`. Transitivity of covering sieves, as in `GrothendieckTopology.transitive_of_presieve`, now shows that the sieve of arrows from basis objects to $U$ is covering. This is the affine-base part of `AffineProEt.isCoverDense_toProEt`. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-weakly-etale-indetale-refinement], `lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-surjective-spectrum`)
3. For a covering of a basis object $V$, let $u$ denote the full inclusion and let $T$ consist of the basis arrows to $V$ factoring through members of the original covering. Cover-density from step 2, applied to the sources of that covering, and transitivity show that $u_*T$ is covering; hence $T$ is covering for the restricted topology. With the restricted topology of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], `Functor.mem_restrictedTopology_iff` says that the pushforward sieve $u_*T$ is covering in the full pro-etale site. The proof of `AffineProEt.exists_finite_jointly_surjective_of_mem_topology` uses `Precoverage.mem_toGrothendieck_iff_of_isStableUnderComposition` and `Precoverage.mem_iff_exists_zeroHypercover` to choose a pro-etale covering family $E_j\to V$ contained in $u_*T$. Since $V$ is affine, its whole space is compact. By `QuasiCompactCover.exists_isAffineOpen_of_isCompact`, finitely many affine opens in the $E_j$ have images covering $V$. For each selected index $j_k$, membership in $u_*T$ gives a factorization $E_{j_k}\to u(Z_k)\to V$ with $Z_k$ a basis object and $t_k:Z_k\to V$ in $T$. The images of the $t_k$ contain those of the selected affine opens, so this finite family is jointly surjective. These arrows are weakly etale and are morphisms between affine schemes, hence affine. Thus inverse images of affine opens of $V$ are affine and are jointly surjective; the family is a pro-etale covering by [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]. Since every $t_k$ belongs to $T$, it refines the original covering.
4. For fibre products, Lean first observes in `AffineProEt.proAffineEtale_hom` that a morphism between basis objects is itself pro-affine-etale, by the postcomposition-cancellation property of `proAffineEtale`. In affine ring terms this is `RingHom.IndEtale.of_comp`: if $A\to D$ and $A\to B$ are ind-etale, then a compatible map $D\to B$ is ind-etale. For basis objects $\operatorname{Spec}B$ and $\operatorname{Spec}C$ over $\operatorname{Spec}D$, base change gives $C\to B\otimes_D C$ ind-etale, and composition with $A\to C$ makes $A\to B\otimes_D C$ ind-etale. Thus the scheme fibre product is in the basis of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]. The Lean construction packages this as closure of `proAffineEtale.overObj` under cospan limits in `Over X`; `MorphismProperty.Comma.hasLimitsOfShape_of_closedUnderLimitsOfShape` gives the basis pullbacks, and `AffineProEt.isPullback_left` says that their underlying squares are scheme pullback squares. These are precisely the required fibre products.
5. Take the small skeleton and universes of [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], and its full subcategory of basis objects. The inclusion $u:\mathcal B_X\to X_{\mathrm{proet}}$ is fully faithful because every morphism between basis objects is retained. In particular it is locally full: any morphism between its images lifts already on the identity cover; it is locally faithful: equality of images is equality before restriction, again on the identity cover. Step 2 gives cover-density. The basis topology is the restricted topology `AffineProEt.topology`, so a sieve is covering exactly when its pushforward is covering in the full site. These are the hypotheses of the dense-subsite comparison theorem `lem-pc-fppf-dense-sheaf-equivalence`. Apply it with a coefficient category having the required limits; in Lean the hypothesis is `HasLimitsOfSize.{u, u + 1}`, and the dense-subsite instances give `AffineProEt.isEquivalence_sheafPushforwardContinuous_toProEt`. For sets the required structured-arrow limits are subsets of products consisting of compatible families; coordinatewise operations give them for abelian groups and modules over a fixed commutative ring, in a sufficiently large universe. The resulting equivalence is specifically restriction along $u$, the functor defined as `AffineProEt.sheafPushforward`. (uses `lem-pc-fppf-dense-sheaf-equivalence`)
6. An inverse to the restriction equivalence in step 5 extends a basis sheaf, uniquely up to the unique isomorphism compatible with its specified restriction. Full faithfulness identifies the sets of sheaf morphisms with the morphisms of their restrictions. If a sheaf morphism restricts to an isomorphism, lift its inverse by fullness; the two inverse identities lift by faithfulness. Thus restriction determines sheaves and their morphisms in the usual categorical sense and detects isomorphisms.
