Let $U$ be an object of $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ whose underlying scheme is
isomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec}(D)$, with $D$ w-contractible. For sheaves of
sets $F,G$ in any fixed universe, every locally surjective morphism
$\varphi:F\to G$ induces a surjection

$$
F(U)\longrightarrow G(U).
$$
