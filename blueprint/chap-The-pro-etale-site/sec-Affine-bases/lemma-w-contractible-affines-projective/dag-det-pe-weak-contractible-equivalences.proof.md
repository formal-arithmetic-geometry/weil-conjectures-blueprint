1. Assume condition 1. Given an epimorphism φ : F → G and a map y : h_W → G, form the categorical pullback of φ and y in sheaves. To show that its second projection is epi, use `Sheaf.isLocallySurjective_iff_epi` (the local-surjectivity criterion of `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective`). For a section t over V, take the covering sieve `Presheaf.imageSieve_mem J φ.hom (y.hom.app _ t)` and, over an arrow g : U → V in that sieve, choose a with φ(a) equal to the restriction of y(t). The mapped pullback square `IsPullback.of_hasPullback φ y` and `Limits.Types.exists_of_isPullback` then give a pullback section z whose second coordinate is t restricted along g. Hence the pullback projection is epi. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective`)
2. By condition 1, the epimorphic pullback projection has a section s. Composing s with the first pullback projection gives a map h_W → F; the pullback condition and `Limits.pullback.condition`, together with s ≫ `pullback.snd` = 1, show that its composite with φ is y. This is the required lift, so condition 1 implies Projective h_W. Conversely, if h_W is projective, `Projective.factors` applied to the identity map of h_W and any epimorphism F → h_W supplies a section, proving condition 2 implies condition 1.
3. Assume Projective h_W. For an epimorphism φ : F → G and a section y of G over W, use the Yoneda bijection `lem-galois-yoneda-hom-bijection` in the form `J.uliftYonedaEquiv.symm y` to regard y as a map h_W → G. `Projective.factors` gives s : h_W → F with s ≫ φ = `J.uliftYonedaEquiv.symm y`. Applying `J.uliftYonedaEquiv` and rewriting with `GrothendieckTopology.uliftYonedaEquiv_comp` and `Equiv.apply_symm_apply` shows that the section represented by s maps to y. Thus evaluation F(W) → G(W) is surjective. (uses `lem-galois-yoneda-hom-bijection`)
4. Conversely, assume every epimorphism of sheaves is surjective on sections over W. Given an epimorphism φ : F → G and a map y : h_W → G, apply the evaluation-surjectivity hypothesis to `J.uliftYonedaEquiv y` to obtain a section a of F(W) with φ(a) = `J.uliftYonedaEquiv y`. Convert a back with `J.uliftYonedaEquiv.symm`. The equality after composition follows by applying the injectivity of `J.uliftYonedaEquiv` and using `GrothendieckTopology.uliftYonedaEquiv_comp` and `Equiv.apply_symm_apply`; hence h_W is Projective. (uses `lem-galois-yoneda-hom-bijection`)
5. Finally, let W = Spec D with D w-contractible in the sense of [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible]. The theorem [dag-det-pe-wcb-projective] supplies a section for every epimorphism onto the represented sheaf of this affine object; in the Lean proof this is exactly `(proetale_wContractible_projective X D f).1`. Therefore condition 1 holds for W, and the two equivalences above give all three formulations. (uses `det-pe-wcb-projective`)
