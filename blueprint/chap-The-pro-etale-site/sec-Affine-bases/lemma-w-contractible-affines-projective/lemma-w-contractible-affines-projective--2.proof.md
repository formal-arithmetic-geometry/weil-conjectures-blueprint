By the Yoneda lemma, $h_W$ is projective exactly when every epimorphism
$F\to G$ induces a surjection $F(W)=\Hom(h_W,F)\to\Hom(h_W,G)=G(W)$. If
every epimorphism onto $h_W$ splits, pull an epimorphism $F\to G$ back
along a section $h_W\to G$; the resulting epimorphism onto $h_W$ splits,
so the section lifts to $F$. Conversely, if every epimorphism is
surjective on sections over $W$, then $\mathrm{id}_{h_W}$ lifts along any
epimorphism onto $h_W$, which is a section.
