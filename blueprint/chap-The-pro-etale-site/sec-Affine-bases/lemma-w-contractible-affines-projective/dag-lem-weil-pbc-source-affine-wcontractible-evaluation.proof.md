1. The w-local, strictly henselian, and extremally disconnected hypotheses on R are precisely the source class IsWContractibleRing, as recorded in [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible] and [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal]. Thus for every commutative R-algebra T which is ind-etale and faithfully flat, the retraction theorem [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-wcontractible-indetale-retraction] supplies a ring map r : T →+* R with r.comp (algebraMap R T) = RingHom.id R; here ind-etale has the meaning fixed in [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-wcontractible-indetale-retraction])
2. For W in the small pro-etale site [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site] and an isomorphism e : W.left ≅ Spec R `def-weil-affine-spectrum`, apply GrothendieckTopology.isWeaklyContractible_of_forall_mem. Given a covering sieve S, mem_topology_iff, mem_precoverage_iff, mem_propQCPrecoverage_iff_exists_quasiCompactCover, and Cover.QuasiCompact.exists_hom produce a quasi-compact weakly etale cover and a finite affine refinement. Reindex its members by a type κ in Type u, with maps v j : V j → W.left. The source-local coproduct lemma IsZariskiLocalAtSource.sigmaDesc proves that Sigma.desc v is weakly etale, while Surjective.sigmaDesc_of_union_range_eq_univ proves it is surjective; these are the morphism notions used in [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. (uses [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms])
3. The coproduct of the finite affine V j is affine, so apply exists_comp_eq_id_of_forall_exists_retraction to (Sigma.desc v) ≫ e.hom. In that lemma, flat_and_surjective_SpecMap_iff turns the affine weakly etale surjection into a faithfully flat algebra map, and WeaklyEtale.Spec_iff followed by RingHom.WeaklyEtale.exists_indEtale_comp gives an algebra T and map g whose map is ind-etale and faithfully flat and whose composite with the original algebra map is ind-etale. This is exactly the refinement recorded in [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-weakly-etale-indetale-refinement], with ind-etale understood via [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]. The retraction from the first step then yields a section s : W.left ⟶ ∐ V with s ≫ Sigma.desc v = 𝟙 _, and the construction records that every morphism factoring through some v j belongs to S. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-wcontractible-indetale-retraction], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-existing-weakly-etale-indetale-refinement])
4. Apply exists_disjoint_opens_of_comp_sigmaDesc_eq_id to this section. The opens O j given there are the inverse images of the component opens of ∐ j, V j; they cover W.left, are pairwise disjoint, and each inclusion (O j).toScheme → W.left factors through v j. The ProEtale packaging exists_disjoint_cover_of_comp_sigmaDesc_eq_id therefore gives a covering family of open-immersion arrows lying in S, each arrow Mono, and an empty covering sieve on every cross-overlap. This uses the affine/pro-etale morphism conventions of [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. (uses [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms])
5. The preceding family satisfies every hypothesis of GrothendieckTopology.isWeaklyContractible_of_forall_mem, so it yields (topology T).IsWeaklyContractible.{w} W, the section-surjectivity predicate described in [dag-sk-def-pe-weak-contractible]. The theorem isWeaklyContractible_of_iso is exactly this argument with hR instantiated by (fun T _ _ _ _ ↦ IsWContractibleRing.exists_retraction R T). In section universe u+1, `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-epi-locally-surjective` converts sheaf epimorphisms to the locally-surjective form, giving the stated epi consequence; no nonempty or nontrivial hypothesis is used.
