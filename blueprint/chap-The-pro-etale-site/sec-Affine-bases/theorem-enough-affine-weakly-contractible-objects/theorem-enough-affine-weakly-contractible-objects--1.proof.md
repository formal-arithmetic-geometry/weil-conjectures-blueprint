Choose an affine open covering $\{V_i=\operatorname{Spec}(B_i)\to V\}_{i\in I}$.
It is a covering in $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ by
Proposition [proposition-pro-etale-coverings]. For each $i$, choose a
faithfully flat ind-étale $B_i$-algebra $D_i$ that is w-contractible,
using Theorem [theorem-existence-contractible-covers].

Put $U_i=\operatorname{Spec}(D_i)$. The map $U_i\to V_i$ is weakly
étale by Proposition [proposition-etale-implications], and is surjective
by faithful flatness. Since it is a morphism between affine schemes,
it is quasi-compact, so it is a singleton pro-étale covering.
The composites $U_i\to V_i\to V\to X$ are weakly étale by
Lemma [lemma-stability], giving objects $U_i$ and morphisms $u_i:U_i\to V$
in $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$. Transitivity of coverings now shows that
the sieve generated by the $u_i$ is covering.
