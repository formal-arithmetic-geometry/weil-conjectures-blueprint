Cover the given object by pro-étale affines
$\operatorname{Spec}(B)$
(Proposition [proposition-pro-etale-affines-form-basis]), and for
each of them choose, by
Theorem [theorem-existence-contractible-covers], a faithfully flat
ind-étale $B$-algebra $D$ that is w-contractible. Then
$\operatorname{Spec}(D)\to\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ is weakly étale by
Proposition [proposition-etale-implications], so
$\operatorname{Spec}(D)$ is an object of $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ by
Lemma [lemma-stability], and as a faithfully flat map of affines it
is a covering. Composing these coverings gives the result.
