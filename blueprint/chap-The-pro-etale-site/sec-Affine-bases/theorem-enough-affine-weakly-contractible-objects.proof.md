Cover the given object by [pro-étale affines](@definition-ind-etale-pro-etale-weakly)
$\operatorname{Spec}(B)$
(Proposition [proposition-pro-etale-affines-form-basis]), and for
each of them choose, by
Theorem [theorem-existence-contractible-covers], a faithfully flat
[ind-étale](@definition-ind-etale-pro-etale-weakly) $B$-algebra $D$ that is [w-contractible](@definition-w-local-w-strictly-local). Then
$\operatorname{Spec}(D)\to\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ is [weakly étale](@definition-ind-etale-pro-etale-weakly) by
Proposition [proposition-etale-implications], so
$\operatorname{Spec}(D)$ is an object of [$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$](@definition-small-pro-etale-site) by
Lemma [lemma-stability], and as a faithfully flat map of affines it
is a covering. Composing these coverings gives the result.
