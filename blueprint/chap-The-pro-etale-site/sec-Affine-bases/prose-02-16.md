Over $X=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$, for example, the [weakly étale](@definition-ind-etale-pro-etale-weakly)
$X$-scheme $\operatorname{Spec}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$ is already a
[pro-étale affine](@definition-ind-etale-pro-etale-weakly), and $X$ itself is covered by it. Applying
Theorem [theorem-existence-contractible-covers] to the charts of such a
basis, we pass from [pro-étale affines](@definition-ind-etale-pro-etale-weakly) to [w-contractible](@definition-w-local-w-strictly-local) ones. The next two
results show that the spectra of [w-contractible](@definition-w-local-w-strictly-local) rings have the splitting
property for sheaves and that they cover every object.
