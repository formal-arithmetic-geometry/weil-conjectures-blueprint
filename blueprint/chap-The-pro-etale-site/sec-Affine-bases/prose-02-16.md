Over $X=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$, for example, the weakly étale
$X$-scheme $\operatorname{Spec}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$ is already a
pro-étale affine, and $X$ itself is covered by it. Applying
Theorem [theorem-existence-contractible-covers] to the charts of such a
basis, we pass from pro-étale affines to w-contractible ones. The next two
results show that the spectra of w-contractible rings have the splitting
property for sheaves and that they cover every object.
