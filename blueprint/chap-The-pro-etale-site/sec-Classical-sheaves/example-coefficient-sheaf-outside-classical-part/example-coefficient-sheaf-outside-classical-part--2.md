Hence the identity function does not come from any
finite stage of the profinite presentation of $S$, and
$\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}$ is not classical. Its reductions
$\mathbf Z/\ell^n$, on the other hand, are classical. So étale arguments
apply at each finite level, while the coefficient sheaf itself is defined
once on the pro-étale site.
