Every étale morphism is weakly étale by
[proposition-etale-implications], so the inclusion is defined by retaining
the underlying $X$-scheme and its morphisms. Finite limits are computed in
schemes over $X$, using stability under base change and composition
[lemma-stability], and the inclusion preserves them. It is also
representably flat, which gives preservation of compatible families.
Every étale covering is a pro-étale covering
[proposition-pro-etale-coverings]. Thus the inclusion is continuous, and
precomposition with its opposite takes sheaves to sheaves. This is
$\nu_{X,*}$.
