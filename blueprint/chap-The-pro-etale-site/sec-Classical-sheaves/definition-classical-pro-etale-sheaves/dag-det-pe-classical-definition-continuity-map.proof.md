1. Work in the universe fixed by `def-weil-size-convention`. For a presentation P, the Lean structure `AffinePresentation` already contains a small cofiltered index I, a diagram `P.diagram : I ⥤ X.ProEt` whose objects are affine and étale over X, a cone with affine vertex, and its limiting property. Thus the stages and the vertex are objects of the pro-étale site [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], and the projection and transition maps are site morphisms. Passing to the opposite diagram gives `sectionDiagram P F := P.diagram.op ⋙ F`; applying the presheaf F to `P.cone.op` gives the compatible restriction cocone `sectionCocone P F`.
2. The canonical continuity morphism is exactly `colimit.desc (sectionDiagram P F) (sectionCocone P F)`, with the colimit formed in the ambient category `def-weil-pbc-category-colimit`. Its restriction to the i-th summand is the projection restriction, by `colimit.ι_desc`; for a Type-valued presheaf this is the displayed representative formula `[i,s] ↦ F(u_i)(s)` under the usual set-colimit description. For a natural transformation α, `colimit.hom_ext` reduces the naturality square to α.naturality on each projection, proving `continuityMap_natural`.
3. Define set classicality by requiring the Type-valued continuity map of every `AffinePresentation` to be bijective. This is the Lean predicate `ProEtale.IsClassical`, so it applies to every presentation in the fixed universe and makes no additional choice of presentation or colimit model.
4. For module coefficients, restrict the section modules along the constant-section map using `PresheafOfModules.restrictScalarsObj`; the resulting functor is `moduleSectionPresheaf`, and its continuity morphism is the same `continuityMap` computed in `ModuleCat`. The existence of these module colimits is supplied by the Grothendieck-abelian/module-colimit fact `lem-weil-pbc-ring-modules-grothendieck-abelian`. If V is the forgetful functor from modules to sets, the filtered-colimit comparison `preservesColimitIso V (sectionDiagram P (moduleSectionPresheaf Λ F))` is the categorical form of the elementwise statements in `lem-weil-pbc-filtered-colimit-modules-elementwise`: every element comes from a stage and equality is witnessed at a common later stage. The equation `preservesColimitIso_inv_comp_desc` identifies the underlying set map of the module continuity morphism with the set continuity map. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-ring-modules-grothendieck-abelian`, `lem-weil-pbc-filtered-colimit-modules-elementwise`)
5. The forgetful functor reflects isomorphisms, so `isIso_iff_of_reflects_iso` together with `CategoryTheory.isIso_iff_bijective` shows that the module continuity morphism is an isomorphism exactly when its underlying continuity function is bijective. Quantifying over all presentations proves `isClassicalModule_iff`, hence the module definition agrees with classicality of the underlying sheaf of sets.
6. If e : F ≅ G is a sheaf isomorphism, whiskering e with the presentation diagram gives an isomorphism of the two section diagrams and hence an isomorphism of their colimits. `continuityMap_natural` identifies the two continuity maps after these source and target isomorphisms; `isIso_comp_left_iff` and `CategoryTheory.isIso_iff_bijective` therefore show `isClassical_iso_iff`. The same argument in `ModuleCat` gives the module-isomorphism version used later.
7. For a cochain complex, define classicality by requiring `IsClassicalModule Λ (K.homology q)` for every q. For an object of the recorded derived category `def-weil-abstract-derived-category` of the module sheaf category `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`, use the actual `DerivedCategory.homologyFunctor`. The natural isomorphism `DerivedCategory.homologyFunctorFactors` identifies the homology of `Q.obj K` with the homology of K, so `isClassicalModule_iff` and the sheaf-isomorphism invariance above prove `isClassicalComplex_Q_iff`. Applying `DerivedCategory.homologyFunctor.mapIso` degreewise and the same module-isomorphism argument proves `isClassicalComplex_iso_iff`; thus the predicate depends only on the derived isomorphism class.
8. Assume X is affine and a presentation P of an object is given. Forget the relative cone to schemes; its limit is preserved by `isLimitOfPreserves (Over.forget X)`. Write every affine stage and the affine vertex as spectra of their global sections using `def-weil-affine-spectrum`. The naturality equations for `Scheme.isoSpec_hom_naturality` identify the transported cone with the original one, and the fully faithful Spec functor reflects its limit. Taking opposites and applying `isColimitOfConeRightOpOfCocone` makes the global-sections cocone a ring colimit. By `lem-weil-pbc-etale-ring-map-iff-etale-affine`, each affine étale stage gives an étale ring map; the Over equations give the compatible maps from Γ(X), so `RingHom.IndEtale.iff_ind_etale` and `CommRingCat.ind_etale_small_index` show that the vertex structure map is ind-étale. `proAffineEtale_iff_appTop` and `MorphismProperty.cancel_left_of_respectsIso` transport this property across the given isomorphism from the cone vertex to U. (uses `def-weil-affine-spectrum`, `lem-weil-pbc-etale-ring-map-iff-etale-affine`)
9. Conversely, suppose `U.hom` is pro-affine-étale. Unfold its `MorphismProperty.pro` witness and enlarge the index from universe u to u+1 with `MorphismProperty.pro_of_univLE`; this yields a filtered diagram D of affine schemes over X, étale stage maps, a cone to U, and the limit equations. Lift D and its structure maps to `X.ProEt` with `MorphismProperty.Over.lift'`, and form the cone with vertex U using `MorphismProperty.Over.homMk`. The stage affine and étale properties are inherited from the witness, while the cone naturality follows from the witness equations. Since a cofiltered index is connected by `IsCofiltered.isConnected`, `isLimitOfReflects (Over.forget X)` reflects the underlying scheme limit to the required relative limit. This produces an `AffinePresentation` with vertex U (using `Iso.refl`), proving the converse and hence the affine equivalence.
