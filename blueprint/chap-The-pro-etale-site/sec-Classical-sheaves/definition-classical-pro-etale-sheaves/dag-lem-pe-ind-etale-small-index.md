Let $R$ and $B$ be commutative rings with underlying types in universe $u$, and let $f:R\to B$ be a ring homomorphism. Suppose that $f$ has a filtered presentation by finitely presented étale $R$-algebras indexed by a category whose objects and morphisms lie in universe $u+1$. Then $f$ has such a presentation indexed by a category whose objects and morphisms lie in universe $u$. A presentation means a categorical ring colimit equipped with compatible structure maps from $R$, as in [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra].
