1. Fix an arbitrary scheme X and a discrete commutative ring \Lambda.  The Lean object `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme` supplies X `def-pc-scheme`, and `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.ProEt` with `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.ProEt.topology` supplies its small pro-etale site `sk-def-pe-site`.  Form the coefficient ring sheaf R := `(CategoryTheory.constantSheaf (Scheme.ProEt.topology X) RingCat).obj (RingCat.of \Lambda)`.  By the constant-sheaf definition this is precisely the constant sheaf \Lambda_X [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf].
2. The Lean category `CategoryTheory.ringedSiteModules R` is the category of sheaves of R-modules, by the sheaf-of-modules definition `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`.  Its derived category is `DerivedCategory (ringedSiteModules R)`, using the derived-category construction `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`.  Restricting to objects whose cohomology vanishes in all sufficiently negative degrees gives exactly the bounded-below subcategory D+ `def-weil-pbc-bounded-derived-subcategories`.
3. For a derived object K in this module category, unfold the Lean declaration `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.IsClassicalModuleComplex`: it is the proposition `\forall q : \mathbb Z, IsClassicalModuleSheaf ((DerivedCategory.homologyFunctor (SheafOfModules R) q).obj K)`.  Thus every integer-degree cohomology sheaf of K is classical, which is exactly the classicality condition imposed on the bounded-below constant-coefficient objects in the statement.  No additional theorem or coefficient hypothesis is introduced by this definition.
4. The properness convention used alongside this construction is the existing Mathlib class `AlgebraicGeometry.IsProper`, whose fields are separated, universally closed, and locally of finite type; this is the properness clause recorded in `sk-def-conv`.  Hence the Lean construction records the stated arbitrary-scheme, constant-coefficient classical D+ convention, with no invertibility restriction on coefficients.
