Let X be a scheme in universe u, I a small cofiltered category in universe u+1, D : I → X.ProEt a diagram with affine étale stages, and c a cone whose image under the forgetful functor to schemes over X is limiting. Let V be an affine étale object of X.ProEt. These objects and the forgetful functor are as in [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map]. For each map a : c.pt → V in X.ProEt there are an index i in I and a map b : D(i) → V with c.π_i ≫ b = a.
