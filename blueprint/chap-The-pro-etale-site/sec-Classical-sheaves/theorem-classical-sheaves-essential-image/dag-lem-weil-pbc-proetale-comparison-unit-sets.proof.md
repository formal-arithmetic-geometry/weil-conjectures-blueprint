1. Fix an etale sheaf F of (u+1)-valued sets and write L=(toProEt X)^op.lan.obj F.obj. The affine pro-etale objects used in the argument are the affine ind-etale basis [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], whose coverage and dense restriction properties are recorded in [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] and whose inverse-limit presentations by affine etale stages are the pro-etale morphisms of [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]; the filtered-colimit presentations and same-universe flatness bookkeeping are supplied by [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. The Lean lemma setLanRelative proves that L preserves the required relative filtered colimits, and Presheaf.isSheaf_of_generates_of_isRelativelyPresentable (setLanSheaf) then proves that L is a sheaf on the affine ind-etale basis. Applying Functor.IsDenseSubsite.isIso_whiskerLeft_toSheafify gives that the sheafification map toSheafify (ProEt.topology X) L is an isomorphism after restriction to that basis; therefore its component is invertible at every affine pro-etale object, in particular at the image of every affine etale object. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
2. For an affine etale object W over X, setComparisonUnit rewrites the component of the comparison unit as the composite of the left-Kan-extension unit (toProEt X).op.lanUnit.app F.obj evaluated at W and the sheafification map evaluated at (toProEt X).obj W. The first factor is invertible because the affine-etale inclusion is fully faithful, using the Kan-extension unit calculation Adjunction.map_restrictFullyFaithful_unit_app; the second factor is invertible by the preceding basis argument. Hence setComparisonUnit_isIso proves that the comparison-unit component is an isomorphism at every affine etale W. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
3. Now apply NatTrans.isIso_iff_isIso_app to reduce the claim to each sheaf F. Let R be the sheaf pushforward along the affine-etale inclusion AffineEtale.Spec X. The composite R ⋅ sheafToPresheaf reflects isomorphisms, so by isIso_of_reflects_iso it suffices to prove that it sends the unit component to an isomorphism. After changing this map to the restriction along (AffineEtale.Spec X).op, apply NatTrans.isIso_iff_isIso_app again; its component at U is exactly the comparison-unit component for the affine etale object (AffineEtale.Spec X).obj U.unop, which is invertible by setComparisonUnit_isIso. Thus every unit component is invertible, and the comparison unit natural transformation is an isomorphism. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
