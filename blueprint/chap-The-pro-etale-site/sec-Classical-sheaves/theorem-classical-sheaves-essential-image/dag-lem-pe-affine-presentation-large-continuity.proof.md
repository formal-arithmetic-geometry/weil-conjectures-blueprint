1. Fix a stage i of P and restrict the diagram to the cofiltered over-category Over i. The restricted cone is still limiting by Functor.Initial.isLimitWhiskerEquiv. Since every stage and the vertex are affine, the private helper affineSectionsColimit identifies the global sections of this limit with the filtered colimit of the corresponding rings. Etale cancellation (Etale.of_comp) makes every transition algebra etale over the chosen stage, so RingHom.IndEtale.iff_ind_etale followed by CommRingCat.ind_etale_small_index gives a universe-u ind-etale presentation of the vertex map to that stage. This is exactly the affine ind-etale/pro-affine-etale setup of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra] and [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]; the same filtered-colimit presentation is compatible with the flat-colimit bookkeeping in `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. Composing with the chosen stage's etale map to X proves, via proAffineEtale.comp_mem, that the vertex map P.cone.pt.hom is pro-affine-etale. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-lem-pe-ind-etale-small-index], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
2. Use the resulting AffineProEt object U and U.exists_relativeLimitPresentation to choose a relative-limit presentation p indexed by a category J in universe u, with affine etale stages Q and the same vertex. Writing d for its image under Scheme.AffineProEt.toProEt, the transported cone is limiting; hence hF and isColimitOfPreserves (T.op ⋙ F.obj) p.isLimit.op give the colimit hdF of the section cocone on Q. The existence and interpretation of U and p are precisely the affine pro-etale presentation conventions in [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. (uses [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms])
3. Let E be the full subcategory of X.ProEt on affine etale objects, let L be its opposite inclusion, and let K and K' be the opposite lifts of the original diagram P and the small diagram Q. For each V in E, affineEtale_largeLimit_preservesColimit gives preservation of the original limit cocone, using the finite-presentation factorization and eventual-equality results `lem-weil-pbc-finite-presentation-map-limit-factorization` and `lem-weil-pbc-locally-finite-type-limit-map-equality`. For Q, the private helper affineEtale_smallLimit_preservesColimit applies Scheme.preservesColimit_yoneda and transports the resulting colimit through Scheme.ProEt.forgetOpCompYonedaIso and Coyoneda.objOpOp, using preservesColimit_of_preserves_colimit_cocone. Thus final_costructuredArrowLift_of_isFinitelyPresentable makes both lifts A and B final into CostructuredArrow L (op U); the original preservation input is [dag-lem-pe-affine-etale-large-limit-maps]. (uses [dag-lem-pe-affine-etale-large-limit-maps], `lem-weil-pbc-finite-presentation-map-limit-factorization`, `lem-weil-pbc-locally-finite-type-limit-map-equality`)
4. On the common costructured-arrow category take the cocone cc with vertex F.obj (op P.cone.pt) and component at an arrow V given by F.obj.map V.hom. Its whiskering along B is exactly hdF, so Functor.Final.isColimitWhiskerEquiv B cc hdF proves cc is a colimit. Applying the inverse equivalence Functor.Final.isColimitWhiskerEquiv A cc identifies the resulting colimit with the whiskering along K, which unfolds to sectionCocone P F.obj. Therefore the canonical section cocone for the original large presentation is a colimit.
