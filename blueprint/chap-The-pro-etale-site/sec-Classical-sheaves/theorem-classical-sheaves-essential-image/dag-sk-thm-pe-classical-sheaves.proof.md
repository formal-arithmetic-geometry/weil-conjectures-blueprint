1. Let L be the sheaf inverse-image functor in the comparison adjunction of `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism`. The second conjunct of `det-pe-classical-sheaves-unit` gives bijectivity on every Hom set for L. Applying Mathlib's `Functor.FullyFaithful.nonempty_iff_map_bijective` converts these bijections into `Nonempty L.FullyFaithful`, which is exactly the first conjunct of the theorem. (uses `def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism`, `det-pe-classical-sheaves-unit`)
2. For a pro-etale sheaf G, [dag-det-pe-classical-sheaves-image] identifies classicality (the continuity-map condition of [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map]) with the existence of an etale sheaf F and an isomorphism `G ≅ L.obj F`. The essential-image predicate `L.essImage G` uses the opposite orientation, `L.obj F ≅ G`; `Iff.trans` with this equivalence is therefore justified by taking the inverse of the displayed isomorphism in each existential witness. This proves `L.essImage G ↔ IsClassical G`. (uses [dag-det-pe-classical-sheaves-image])
3. Fix a discrete commutative ring Λ and use the module comparison adjunction supplied by `det-pe-classical-sheaves-modules`, in the module-sheaf sense of `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category` (with the pulled-back constant coefficients from [dag-def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf]). Its Hom-set bijectivity is converted by `Functor.FullyFaithful.nonempty_iff_map_bijective` into a fully faithful comparison functor L, and its classical-module essential-image clause gives the needed characterization. Concretely, the private equivalence used by the Lean proof compares `IsClassicalModuleFor X Λ G` with classicality of the underlying set sheaf: on each continuity map, `preservesColimitIso_inv_comp_desc` identifies the underlying map with the module map, and `isIso_iff_of_reflects_iso` transfers invertibility along the forgetful functor; this is the continuity-map notion of [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map]. Thus, writing P for `IsClassicalModuleFor X Λ`, we have `P G ↔ L.essImage G`. Apply `ObjectProperty.lift` to L and the membership proof `(P G)` obtained from this equivalence. The lift is fully faithful because L is. For every object G of the full subcategory P, choose an object A and an isomorphism `L.obj A ≅ G` from the essential-image characterization; `ObjectProperty.isoMk` lifts that isomorphism to the full subcategory, proving essential surjectivity. The resulting lift has an `IsEquivalence` instance, and its composite with the inclusion is definitionally L. Mathlib's equivalence instances (the `inferInstance` calls in the Lean proof) provide `PreservesFiniteLimits` and `PreservesFiniteColimits`, giving the final module clause. The arbitrary-coefficient route is supported by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], so no finite-coefficient hypothesis is introduced. (uses `det-pe-classical-sheaves-modules`)
