Let X be a scheme in universe u, and F a sheaf of sets valued in Type (u+1) on its pro-etale site. Suppose the restriction of F to the opposite affine ind-etale site preserves every universe-u filtered colimit. For every affine etale presentation P of det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map, whose cofiltered index has objects and morphisms in universe u+1 and whose affine vertex is a limit in schemes over X, the canonical restriction cocone from the section sets at the stages to the section set at the vertex is a colimit.
