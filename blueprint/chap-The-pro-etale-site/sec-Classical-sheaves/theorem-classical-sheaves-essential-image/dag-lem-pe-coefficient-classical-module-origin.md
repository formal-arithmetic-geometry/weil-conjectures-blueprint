Let X be a scheme in universe u, A a commutative ring in max(v,u+1), and F an intrinsic module over its constant ring on the coefficient-sized pro-etale site of [dag-lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-data]. Suppose that every affine-presentation continuity map of [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map] for the fixed-scalar realization of F is invertible. Then there is an intrinsic constant-A module G on the coefficient-sized etale site with L(G) isomorphic to F. No boundedness, derived category, Noetherianity, constructibility or qcqs hypothesis occurs.
