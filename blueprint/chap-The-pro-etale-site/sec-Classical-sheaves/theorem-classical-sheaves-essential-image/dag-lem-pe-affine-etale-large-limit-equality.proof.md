1. The target V is affine, so V → X is separated, and it is étale. Its relative diagonal is therefore both an open and a closed immersion. Pull it back along the pair (a,b). The resulting open-and-closed subscheme is their agreement locus: a test map equalises a,b exactly when its pointwise image lies in this locus. Let U be its complement. U is closed in the affine stage D(i), hence affine.
2. Suppose no refinement equalises a,b. For each object j → i of Over(i), pull U back to D(j). The agreement-locus criterion implies that this pullback is nonempty. These pullbacks are affine. Each transition D(j) → D(i) is étale, by cancellation from the two étale structure maps to X, so the pullbacks form a cofiltered diagram E of nonempty affine schemes étale over U.
3. For any such diagram over an affine base S, the images of its structure maps are nonempty compact opens in S. They are closed in the constructible topology, and S with that topology is compact. Cofilteredness makes the images downward directed. The compact intersection theorem gives a point x in every image (Mathlib.Topology.Spectral.ConstructibleTopology and IsCompact.nonempty_iInter_of_directed_nonempty_isCompact_isClosed).
4. Choose the geometric point s:Spec Ω → S with Ω the algebraic closure of the residue field at x. Each set of lifts of s to a stage is nonempty by Scheme.exists_fac_of_etale_of_isSepClosed. It is finite: base change gives an affine étale scheme Y over Ω, which has finitely many points by finite_preimage_singleton. Sections of Y → Spec Ω are determined by their image point. Indeed, a section is an open immersion because the structure map is unramified, and two sections with the same image factor through one another and agree over Spec Ω. Thus the finite lift sets form a functor on the original cofiltered index category. Apply nonempty_sections_of_finite_cofiltered_system, whose index and value universes are independent, to obtain compatible lifts, hence a cone with nonempty vertex Spec Ω. (uses `lem-trf-cofiltered-finite-sets-section`)
5. Apply this geometric-cone construction to E. Forget its maps to U and compose with the pullback projections, obtaining a cone over the original diagram restricted to Over(i). The forgetful functor Over(i) → I is initial for cofiltered I, so the given limit is still limiting on this restriction. Its universal property produces a map from Spec Ω to c. At i its image lies in U by the pullback equation, but hab and the agreement-locus criterion force it outside U. Nonemptiness of Spec Ω gives a contradiction. Therefore some refinement equalises the underlying scheme maps; extensionality reflects this equality in X.ProEt.
