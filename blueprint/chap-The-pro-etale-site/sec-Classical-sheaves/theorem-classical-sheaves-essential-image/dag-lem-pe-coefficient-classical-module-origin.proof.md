1. Restriction along the constant-section unit is fully faithful by local surjectivity of that unit. Extend a fixed-scalar sheaf by module sheafification; the underlying additive sheaf is unchanged, giving essential surjectivity. The constant-section equation for constructibleComparisonRingMap identifies intrinsic and fixed-scalar direct images. Uniqueness of left adjoints identifies their inverse images. These are proved private constructions; no arbitrary ring map or unproved existence witness is chosen. (uses `lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-data`)
2. For a fixed-scalar pro-etale module sheaf F, consider the canonical comparison counit. At every affine etale stage of an AffinePresentation, the comparison unit is the left Kan extension unit followed by the sheafification unit. The former is invertible since the inclusion is fully faithful, and the latter is invertible by lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-presentation-sheafification. The right triangle identity therefore makes the counit invertible at each stage. (uses `lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-presentation-sheafification`)
3. An affine ind-etale basis object has a pro-affine-etale presentation. Enlarge its cofiltered index from u to u+1, lift its affine etale stages to X.ProEt, and reflect its scheme limit into the over category to obtain the required AffinePresentation with that exact vertex. The source of the counit has a colimit restriction cocone by lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-continuity. The hypothesis that continuityMap is invertible makes the target restriction cocone a colimit as well. The stagewise invertible counit is a natural isomorphism of the diagrams. Uniqueness of their colimits makes its vertex component invertible. The scheme-general source-library affine-basis restriction declaration attached to lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis, together with the AsSmall equivalence, reflects isomorphisms, so the counit is invertible as a sheaf morphism. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-det-pe-classical-definition-continuity-map], `lem-pe-coefficient-comparison-continuity`)
4. Take the fixed-scalar direct image as antecedent, and use its invertible counit. Choose its preimage under the proved etale constant-scalar equivalence. Compose the inverse-image comparison with the equivalence counit and the fixed-scalar origin isomorphism, then lift this isomorphism through the fully faithful pro-etale constant-scalar functor. This gives the intrinsic antecedent. All constructions allow the zero ring, zero module, and empty scheme.
