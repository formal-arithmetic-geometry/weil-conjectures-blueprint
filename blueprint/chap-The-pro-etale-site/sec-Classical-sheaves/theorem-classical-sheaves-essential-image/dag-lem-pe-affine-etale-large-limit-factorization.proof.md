1. Let F be the fully faithful inclusion of the spectrum-based affine etale category of X into X.ProEt. For each stage, the canonical affine isomorphism with Spec of its global sections exhibits it in the essential image A of F. Mathlib gives EssentiallySmall in universe u for X.AffineEtale, hence for A. The category of structured arrows from c.pt to the inclusion of A is also essentially small in u, since the pro-etale category is locally small in u. Lift the given diagram into this category using its projections, and let E be the full subcategory on its actual object-image. E is essentially small in u by full faithfulness of its inclusion.
2. The category E is cofiltered. It is nonempty because I is. For two objects represented by i and j, use a common predecessor in I and the images of its two arrows. For parallel arrows f,g from the object represented by i to that represented by j, their structured-arrow equations say that the composites with the limit projection are equal. Apply lem-pe-affine-etale-large-limit-equality with target D(j), which is affine and etale by the given hypotheses. Its refinement k -> i gives an arrow in E equalizing f and g; faithfulness of forgetting the structured arrows reflects this equality. (uses [dag-lem-pe-affine-etale-large-limit-equality])
3. Let K:E -> X.ProEt forget the structured arrows. The maps belonging to those structured arrows define a cone d with vertex c.pt. Its image in schemes over X is limiting: restrict any competing cone along the object-surjective functor H:I -> E and use the supplied limit hc. The factorization equations for all E-objects follow because each is H(i), and uniqueness follows by restricting the equations to all H(i). This explicitly verifies the universal property, without asserting an initial functor or a small coinitial subcategory of I.
4. Replace E by its universe-u SmallModel using equivSmallModel, whisker d with the inverse equivalence, and transport its limit property. Cofilteredness transports along this equivalence. Forgetting schemes over X to schemes preserves this connected limit. Every stage remains affine, so the underlying schemes are quasi-compact and quasi-separated and their transition maps are affine. The target V -> X is locally of finite presentation because it is etale. Apply lem-weil-pbc-finite-presentation-map-limit-factorization to this small diagram and the underlying map a, with the structure maps supplying the compatibility equation. This gives a stage map b over X with the required projection composite. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-finite-presentation-map-limit-factorization`)
5. The selected small-model object maps to an actual image object H(i) of E. Transport b along this equality, regard it as a pro-etale morphism by fullness over X, and use naturality of the cone d to identify its composite with c.pi_i with a. The construction imposes no nonemptiness condition on a stage or on the limit, so also includes the empty scheme.
