For $X=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$ and $U=\operatorname{Spec}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$,
formula (2.6) says that $(\nu_X^*F)(U)$ is the
colimit of the $F(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_{q^m})$, that is, the
underlying set of the Galois module corresponding to $F$. Formula
(2.6) also shows at once that every sheaf
$\nu_X^*F$ is classical. The next theorem says that there are no other
classical sheaves.
