For $X=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$ and $U=\operatorname{Spec}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$,
formula (2.6) says that [$(\nu_X^*F)(U)$](@proposition-comparison-morphism-inverse-image) is the
colimit of the $F(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_{q^m})$, that is, the
underlying set of the Galois module corresponding to $F$. Formula
(2.6) also shows at once that every sheaf
[$\nu_X^*F$](@proposition-comparison-morphism-inverse-image) is [classical](@definition-classical-pro-etale-sheaves). The next theorem says that there are no other
[classical](@definition-classical-pro-etale-sheaves) sheaves.
