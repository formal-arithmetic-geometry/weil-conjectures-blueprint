In particular, $H^q(U,-)$ commutes with direct sums of sheaves of
modules. Quasi-compactness of $U$ is needed already for $H^0$. Let $X$ be a
geometric point and $U=\coprod_{n\in\mathbf N}X$, and consider the direct
sum $\bigoplus_{i\in\mathbf N}\mathbf Z$ of copies of the constant sheaf.
Its sections over $U$ are $\prod_n\bigoplus_i\mathbf Z$, which contains the
element whose $n$th component is the $n$th basis vector, whereas
$\bigoplus_i H^0(U,\mathbf Z)=\bigoplus_i\prod_n\mathbf Z$ consists of the
elements with only finitely many nonzero indices $i$.

For direct sums of unbounded complexes the common lower bound is replaced
by uniform bounds on cohomological dimension
(Proposition [proposition-unbounded-comparison-finite-coefficients]).
