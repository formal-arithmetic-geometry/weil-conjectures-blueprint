1. Let e = PrimeSpectrum.sigmaToPiHomeo D. For each summand, sigma_closed (proved using continuous_sigmaMk, sigma_mk_injective, and isClosed_sigma_iff) identifies closedness of a singleton in the sigma spectrum with closedness of the corresponding singleton in PrimeSpectrum (D i). Give the finite index type the discrete topology. Mapping a specialization along the continuous first projection and applying specializes_iff_eq forces both points to have the same index; the factor WLocalSpace instances then give uniqueness of the closed specialization, while sigma_closed gives closedness of the sigma-space closed-point set. Thus the sigma spectrum is w-local in the sense of [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local] and [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal]. Transporting these two WLocalSpace properties through e gives IsWLocalRing (forall i, D i), including the empty-index case. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-w-local])
2. For a maximal prime m of the product, PrimeSpectrum.sigmaToPi_bijective gives an index i and a prime q of D i whose evaluation comap is m. Since Pi.evalRingHom D i is surjective, Ideal.map_eq_top_or_isMaximal_of_surjective together with Ideal.map_comap_of_surjective shows that q is maximal (the top alternative is excluded by q.isPrime.ne_top). The factor's strict-local hypothesis supplies strict henselianity of Localization.AtPrime q.asIdeal; the bijection Localization.AtPrime.mapPiEvalRingHom_bijective yields a ring equivalence with the product localization at m, and IsStrictlyHenselianLocalRing.of_ringEquiv transports strict henselianity. Hence the product is w-strictly local, with the terminology of `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology` and [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal] (equivalently the hierarchy recorded in [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible]). (uses `def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology`)
3. For every i, IsWContractibleRing.extremallyDisconnected_connectedComponents gives extremal disconnectedness of the factor component space. The homeomorphism WLocalSpace.closedPointsHomeomorph, together with extremally_disconnected_of_homeo, transfers this to closedPoints (PrimeSpectrum (D i)). The explicitly defined sigmaClosedPointsEquiv identifies the sigma spectrum's closed points with the sigma type of these factor closed-point spaces; its continuity follows from continuous_sigma, Continuous.subtype_mk, continuous_sigmaMk, and continuous_subtype_val, and its bijectivity gives a homeomorphism by isHomeomorph_iff_continuous_bijective. Extremal disconnectedness therefore transfers to closedPoints (Sigma i, PrimeSpectrum (D i)). (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-w-local-closed-points-components-homeomorphism])
4. Applying WLocalSpace.closedPointsHomeomorph to the w-local sigma spectrum transfers extremal disconnectedness from its closed points to ConnectedComponents (Sigma i, PrimeSpectrum (D i)). Finally let h = PrimeSpectrum.sigmaToPiHomeo D. Every fiber h ⁻¹' {y} is the singleton {h.symm y}, hence is connected by isConnected_singleton. The quotient-map/coinducing result h.isQuotientMap.isCoinducing.connectedComponentsHomeomorph is therefore a homeomorphism from the sigma connected-component space to ConnectedComponents (PrimeSpectrum (forall i, D i)); extremal disconnectedness transfers across it by extremally_disconnected_of_homeo. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-w-local-closed-points-components-homeomorphism])
5. The preceding w-strict locality and extremal disconnectedness of the product component space are exactly the two fields of IsWContractibleRing in [dag-sk-def-pe-wlocal] and [dag-det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible], so constructing those fields proves that every finite dependent product of w-contractible rings is w-contractible. The argument also covers the empty finite index type.
