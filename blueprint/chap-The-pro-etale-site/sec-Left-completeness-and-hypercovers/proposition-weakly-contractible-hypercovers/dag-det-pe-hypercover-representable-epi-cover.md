Let X be any scheme and mu:A to B a morphism in its pro-etale site. If the underlying scheme of A is quasi-compact, that of B is quasi-separated, and the map of represented sheaves induced by mu is an epimorphism in Type-u-valued sheaves, then the singleton presieve of mu is a covering in the pro-etale precoverage.
