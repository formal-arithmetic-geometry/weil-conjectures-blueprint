For a finite type I in any universe and a family of schemes A_i in universe u, suppose each A_i is isomorphic to Spec D_i for a w-contractible commutative ring D_i in universe u. Then the scheme coproduct of the A_i is isomorphic to the spectrum of a w-contractible commutative ring in universe u.
