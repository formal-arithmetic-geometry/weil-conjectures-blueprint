1. Choose spectrum isomorphisms and transport each structure map to X and augmentation to U. Define every simplicial map by conjugation. Cancellation of adjacent inverse pairs proves identities and composition. Transport each proper-face diagram by the induced natural isomorphism, retaining the old cone point; the cone remains limiting. Compose the old matching arrow with the source isomorphism. Naturality gives every projection equation. Isomorphisms are singleton covers, and the precoverage is stable under composition, so augmentation and matching arrows remain covers. Empty spectra satisfy the same equations. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-covering])
