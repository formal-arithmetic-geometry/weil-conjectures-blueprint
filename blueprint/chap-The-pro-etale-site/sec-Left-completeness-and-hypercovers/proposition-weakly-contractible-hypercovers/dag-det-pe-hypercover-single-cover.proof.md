1. Because V is quasi-compact, apply Scheme.ProEt.exists_affine_weaklyContractible_cover (the affine-cover theorem `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-qc-affine-contractible-cover`) to obtain an affine pro-etale object A and g : A ⟶ V with Presieve.singleton g in the pro-etale precoverage. Write R = Γ(A.left, ⊤) for the coordinate ring of A. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-qc-affine-contractible-cover`)
2. Apply the already proved exists_isWContractibleRing theorem [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-wcontractible-extension], also recorded as the ring-extension result [dag-sk-thm-pe-wc-cover], to R. It supplies a same-universe commutative ring D, an R-algebra structure, Algebra.IndEtale R D, Module.FaithfullyFlat R D, and IsWContractibleRing D. The ind-etale algebra convention is [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra]; when its filtered presentation is used in the same universe, ModuleCat.flat_of_colimitPresentation `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe` gives the required flatness. Thus D is w-contractible and has the ind-etale faithfully flat extension needed below. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-source-wcontractible-extension], [dag-sk-thm-pe-wc-cover], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
3. Let φ : R → D be the algebra map. By WeaklyEtale.Spec_iff and RingHom.IndEtale.weaklyEtale, using RingHom.IndEtale.algebraMap_iff and the ind-etale structure above (the affine pro-etale/weakly-etale interpretation is [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]), Spec.map φ is weakly etale. By RingHom.faithfullyFlat_algebraMap_iff followed by flat_and_surjective_SpecMap_iff, its spectrum map is surjective. Since source and target are affine, Scheme.Hom.singleton_mem_propQCPrecoverage (P := @WeaklyEtale) shows that the singleton of Spec.map φ is a pro-etale covering in the sense of [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-covering]. (uses [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-covering])
4. Compose Spec.map φ with the inverse A.left.isoSpec.inv of the canonical affine-spectrum isomorphism, and call the resulting map p : Spec D → A.left. The isomorphism singleton is a covering by Precoverage.mem_coverings_of_isIso _, and the two singleton coverings compose by Precoverage.comp_mem_coverings (the private singleton_comp_cover helper, with the Presieve.ofArrows/PUnit identification). Hence p is weakly etale and its singleton covers A.left; composing once more with g gives a singleton cover of V. Set f = p ≫ A.hom and use infer_instance for WeaklyEtale f, then take the site object @Scheme.ProEt.mk X _ f hf and the arrow a ≫ g. Its source is Spec D, D is w-contractible, and the transported singleton precoverage membership is exactly Presieve.singleton (a ≫ g) ∈ (Scheme.ProEt.precoverage X) V, proving the required statement. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-covering])
