For a single sheaf this is the cochain complex
$M(W_0)\to M(W_1)\to M(W_2)\to\cdots$. It may be nonzero in every degree,
so the hypercover gives no bound on cohomological dimension.
For unbounded $K$, by left completeness
(Proposition [proposition-left-completeness]) and since
$R\Gamma(U,-)$ commutes with derived inverse limits, we get
$R\Gamma(U,K)\simeq
R\varprojlim_n R\Gamma(U,\tau_{\geq-n}K)$, and the hypercover complex
computes each term.
