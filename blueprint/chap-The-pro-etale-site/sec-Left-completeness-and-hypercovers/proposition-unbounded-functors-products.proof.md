K-injective resolutions of arbitrary complexes exist in the
Grothendieck category of $\mathcal R$-modules [Stacks, [079P](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/079P)], which
defines both functors. Both are right adjoints on derived categories:
$R\Gamma(U,-)$ is right adjoint to the functor sending a complex of
abelian groups $M$ to $\mathcal R[h_U]\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathbf Z}M$,
where $\mathcal R[h_U]$ is the free $\mathcal R$-module on $h_U$, and
$Rf_*$ is right adjoint to the derived pullback of modules. Right
adjoints preserve products. Since (2.1) is built
from products and a cone, an exact functor preserving products also
preserves derived inverse limits [Stacks, [0940](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0940)].
