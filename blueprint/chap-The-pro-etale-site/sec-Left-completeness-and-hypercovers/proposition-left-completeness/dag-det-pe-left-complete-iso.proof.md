1. Write $F_n=\tau_{\geq-n}K$, $\pi_n:K\to F_n$, and $P=\prod_nF_n$. The product exists by `det-pe-products-derived`. Let $\ell:K\to P$ have components $\pi_n$. By `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`, the $n$th component of $(1-T)\ell$ is $\pi_n-t_n\pi_{n+1}=0$, by compatibility of the truncation maps; product uniqueness gives $(1-T)\ell=0$. (uses `det-pe-products-derived`, `def-weil-category-product`, `def-weil-tower-transition-endomorphism`)
2. Complete $1-T$ to a triangle and inverse-rotate it, using TR1 and TR2 of `def-weil-triangulated-category`, obtaining $L\xrightarrow{\iota}P\xrightarrow{1-T}P\to L[1]$. By [lem-weil-pbc-hom-into-triangle-exact](ii), the zero composite in Step 1 gives $u:K\to L$ with $\iota u=\ell$. This triangle makes $L$ a homotopy limit by `def-weil-derived-limit`. Its leg to $F_n$ is $p_n=\operatorname{pr}_n\iota$, so $p_nu=\pi_n$ for every $n$. (uses `def-weil-triangulated-category`, `lem-weil-pbc-hom-into-triangle-exact`, `def-weil-derived-limit`)
3. For every $q$, `det-pe-milnor-sequence` gives $0\to R^1\varprojlim H^{q-1}(F_n)\to H^q(L)\xrightarrow{b_q}\varprojlim H^q(F_n)\to0$, with $\lambda_n b_q=H^q(p_n)$. Apply [det-pe-left-complete-truncation-tower](2) first in degree $q-1$, making the left term zero, then in degree $q$, identifying the limit by an isomorphism $a_q:H^q(K)\xrightarrow{\sim}\varprojlim H^q(F_n)$ whose components are $H^q(\pi_n)$. Exactness makes $b_q$ both monic and epic, hence invertible in the abelian category `def-sp-abelian`. The same hypotheses (K-injective resolutions and exact countable products) are those of the Milnor theorem, and both degrees are arbitrary integers. (uses `det-pe-milnor-sequence`, `det-pe-left-complete-truncation-tower`, `def-sp-abelian`)
4. For every $n$, $\lambda_n b_qH^q(u)=H^q(p_nu)=H^q(\pi_n)=\lambda_na_q$. Limit uniqueness gives $b_qH^q(u)=a_q$, hence $H^q(u)=b_q^{-1}a_q$ is invertible. The cohomology criterion `lem-weil-pbc-unbounded-derived-iso-cohomology` makes $u$ an isomorphism. Transport the distinguished triangle of Step 2 along $(u,1_P,1_P)$; closure under isomorphism of triangles `def-weil-triangulated-category` gives the required triangle starting at $K$, with first map $\iota u=\ell$ and components exactly $\pi_n$. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-unbounded-derived-iso-cohomology`, `def-weil-category-product`, `def-weil-triangulated-category`)
