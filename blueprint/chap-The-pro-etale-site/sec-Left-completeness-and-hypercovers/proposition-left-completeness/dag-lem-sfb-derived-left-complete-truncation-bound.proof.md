1. For K, let F := truncGETower K be the tower with terms (t.truncGE (-(n : ℤ))).obj K and let c := truncGECone K be its canonical cone.  Let P be the product fan of F, with hP := productIsProduct, and let τ := towerShift hP.  Complete 1 - τ to a distinguished triangle W → P.pt → P.pt → W【1】, and lift the canonical projections of c to ℓ : K → P.pt.  The relation ℓ ≫ (1 - τ) = 0 and triangle exactness give u : K → W with u ≫ (W → P.pt) = ℓ.  For every j, `isLimitFanMkObjOfIsLimit` identifies H^j(P.pt) with the product of the H^j(F_n).  The tower terms have zero H^j for n < (-j).toNat by `isZero_of_isGE`, and the canonical maps from K to F_n induce isomorphisms in H^j for n ≥ (-j).toNat by `isIso_homologyFunctor_map_truncGEπ_app`.  Hence `CategoryTheory.Limits.epi_one_sub_of_isZero_of_isIso` makes H^j(1 - τ) epic.  The long exact homology sequence `lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact` then makes H^j(W → P.pt) monic; the kernel-fan limit `isLimitOfIsZeroOfIsIso` and `isIso_of_mono_of_isLimit_kernelFork` show H^j(u) is an isomorphism.  By `DerivedCategory.isIso_iff`, the cohomology criterion `lem-weil-pbc-unbounded-derived-iso-cohomology`, u is an isomorphism.  Transporting the distinguished triangle along u gives `DerivedCategory.isHomotopyLimit_truncGECone`, so c is a homotopy-limit cone with the specified truncation projections. (uses `lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact`, `lem-weil-pbc-unbounded-derived-iso-cohomology`)
