An unbounded complex on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ is determined by its
bounded-below truncations, and its cohomology is computed from theirs
by a derived limit. On the étale site of a scheme of infinite
cohomological dimension this can fail. On $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ it holds because
products are exact, so that the Milnor sequence applies.

Left completeness (Proposition [proposition-left-completeness]) lets
us treat an unbounded complex as the derived limit of bounded below ones.
Injective resolutions and the comparison theorems apply to the latter. In
this way we prove the unbounded comparison of
Proposition [proposition-unbounded-comparison-finite-coefficients].

For bounded below complexes we also give an explicit model of cohomology.
On a quasi-compact quasi-separated object $U$,
Proposition [proposition-weakly-contractible-hypercovers] provides a
hypercover all of whose terms $W_r$ are affine weakly contractible. These
terms have no higher cohomology. The hypercover spectral sequence therefore
collapses, and $R\Gamma(U,K)$ is the total complex of the sections
$K^b(W_r)$. This model has a consequence we use later. In each total degree
only finitely many terms occur, and evaluation on each $W_r$ commutes with
filtered colimits. Hence cohomology commutes with filtered colimits of
uniformly bounded below complexes
(Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology]). We use this to show that
cohomology and direct images commute with inverting $\ell$ and with
extension of scalars to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, the colimit over the finite extensions
$E'$ of $E$.
