Let $U$ be an object of $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ and $f:X\to Y$ a morphism of
schemes. The functor $R\Gamma(U,-)$, and the functor $Rf_*$ from
$D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal R)$ to the derived category of sheaves of
$f_*\mathcal R$-modules on $Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$, are defined on the unbounded
derived categories.
