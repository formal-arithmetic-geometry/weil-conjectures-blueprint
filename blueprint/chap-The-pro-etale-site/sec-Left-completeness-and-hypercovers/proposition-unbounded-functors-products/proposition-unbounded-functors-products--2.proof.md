The functor $R\Gamma(U,-)$ is right adjoint to $M\mapsto\mathcal R[h_U]\otimes_{\mathbf Z}^{\mathbf L}M$, and $Rf_*$ is right adjoint to derived pullback. Hence both preserve products. They are exact functors of triangulated categories, and the cone formula (2.1) constructs the derived inverse limit from products and a cone. They therefore preserve that construction as well; see [Stacks, [0940](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0940)].
