The category of sheaves of $\mathcal R$-modules is Grothendieck. Arbitrary complexes therefore admit K-injective resolutions by [Stacks, [079P](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/079P)]. Applying sections on $U$, respectively direct image along $f$, to these resolutions defines the two indicated derived functors on the unbounded derived categories.
