1. Work in the universe of sheaf values fixed for the small pro-étale sites. The categories of abelian sheaves on these sites are Grothendieck abelian by `lem-sfb-proetale-sheaves-grothendieck`, hence have exact filtered colimits and enough injectives. The sheafification infrastructure is supplied by local presentability `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable` and the finite-limit and universal-property assertions [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits], [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality]; the essentially small site instance used by Lean is `CategoryTheory.GrothendieckTopology.isGrothendieckAbelian_sheaf_of_essentiallySmall`, recorded in the Grothendieck-abelian clause of `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`. For the derived-category comparisons below, `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective` supplies K-injective resolutions, and `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small` supplies `DerivedCategory.locallySmall`, allowing `HasDerivedCategory.ofIsGrothendieckAbelian` in the same Hom universe. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`, `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`, `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`)
2. Put v=f_*. By [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], v is restriction along base change: (vF)(W)=F(W×_Y X), and it is additive. Limits of sheaves are computed on underlying presheaves, so this restriction preserves finite limits. Lean uses `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.preservesFiniteLimits_lowerStar`, the general finite-limit instance recorded with `lem-exc-proetale-closed-exact`. Thus v is left exact. The inverse image g^* is the sheafified left Kan extension of [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], is left adjoint to g_* by `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, and is exact by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. In particular, inverse image preserves the colimits needed below. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `lem-exc-proetale-closed-exact`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`)
3. Fix the filtered indexing category J. As in Lean, replace an essentially small J by `SmallModel J`: `IsFiltered.of_equivalence (equivSmallModel J)` makes this model filtered, and `preservesColimitsOfShape_of_equiv` transports preservation back along the equivalence. It therefore suffices to work with a small filtered category in the universe of sheaf values. We shall prove preservation of its colimits by R^qv by induction on q.
4. We use the affine pro-étale objects to test sheaf morphisms. Over an affine base these are the affine ind-étale objects of [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis]: an ind-étale algebra is a filtered colimit of finitely presented étale algebras [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], and its spectrum has the inverse-limit presentation of [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms]. In passing from these presentations to objects of the weakly étale site, flatness of the filtered presentations is supplied in the same universe by `ModuleCat.flat_of_colimitPresentation` `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`. By [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], these objects form a cover-dense basis, with finite covering refinements and scheme-computed fibre products; restriction detects isomorphisms. Applying this on affine opens gives the affine pro-étale testing category over Y used by `AffineProEt.toProEt` and `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.isIso_of_isIso_app_affineProEt`. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], [dag-def-weil-pbc-ind-etale-algebra], [dag-sk-def-pe-morphisms], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis], `lem-weil-pbc-flat-colimit-presentation-same-universe`)
5. Here is the finite-cover argument used for sections. For a qcqs object T of a small pro-étale site, the covering condition [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site] gives a finite covering family of affine objects inside every covering sieve. Its iterated fibre products are qcqs: their maps to products of the affine members are obtained by base change from the quasi-compact diagonal of T. This is `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.ProEt.exists_finite_cechNerve_of_mem_topology`. If F=colim_j F_j, sheafification of the presheaf colimit shows that sections of F locally lift to stages, and sections that become zero locally vanish at later stages. Finite covering refinements and filteredness put all these local data at one stage. Applying the same argument on the finitely many overlaps makes the lifts compatible, and the sheaf condition glues them. Consequently every section of F(T) comes from some F_j(T), and a section that becomes zero vanishes at a later stage. These are `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.exists_app_eq_of_isColimit_of_finite` and `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.exists_map_eq_zero_of_isColimit_of_finite`.
6. We first show that v itself preserves filtered colimits, following `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.isColimit_lowerStar_mapCocone`. For a colimit cocone F_j→F, let φ:colim_j vF_j→vF be its canonical comparison. Test φ at an affine pro-étale W→Y, and put V=W×_Y X. The base changes of the quasi-compact and quasi-separated morphism f make V qcqs. By the formula in [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], sections of vF_j and vF over W are sections of F_j and F over V. The preceding finite-cover argument shows that φ(W) is surjective. For injectivity, first represent a section of (colim_j vF_j)(W) at one stage; if its image is zero in F(V), it becomes zero at a later stage over V, and hence represents zero in the source colimit. Thus φ(W) is bijective. `ConcreteCategory.isIso_iff_bijective` and affine pro-étale detection of isomorphisms [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis] show that φ is an isomorphism. This proves `preservesColimitsOfShape_lowerStar`. (uses [dag-lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis])
7. Next let I_j be a filtered diagram of injective abelian sheaves on X and I its colimit. Again fix affine pro-étale W→Y and V=W×_Y X, which is qcqs. The projection a:V→X is weakly étale. Its inverse image [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star] agrees with restriction to the localized site V_proét. This restriction preserves injectives: its left adjoint is left Kan extension along the localization functor followed by sheafification, and on presheaves the Kan extension is a direct sum over sections W'→V. Direct sums in a Grothendieck abelian category and sheafification are exact, so this left adjoint preserves monomorphisms. Mathlib's `Functor.preservesInjectiveObjects_of_adjunction_of_preservesMonomorphisms` therefore applies, giving `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.injective_upperStar_obj_of_weaklyEtale`. Since a^* is a left adjoint `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `isColimitOfPreserves` identifies a^*I with colim_j a^*I_j, a filtered colimit of injective sheaves on V. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`)
8. The finite-cover argument also proves that this colimit on V has zero positive cohomology. For a finite covering family with qcqs Čech nerve, each degree of its Čech complex is a finite product of section groups, so sections commuting with filtered colimits and exactness of filtered colimits of abelian groups pass exactness of the injectives' Čech complexes to their colimit (`CategoryTheory.Sheaf.exactAt_cechComplex_of_isColimit`). The Čech vanishing criterion then gives H^{r+1}(V,a^*I)=0, as formalized by `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.subsingleton_H_succ_of_isColimit_of_injective`. The criterion uses dimension shifting: embed a sheaf with vanishing positive Čech cohomology into an injective with cokernel Q; Čech H^1-vanishing gives surjectivity onto Q on the qcqs objects, the resulting short exact sequences of Čech complexes pass the vanishing to Q, and the cohomology exact sequence shifts the degree. Finally `new-sheaf-cohomology-derived-evaluation`, namely `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.cohomology_nonempty_addEquiv_rightDerived_evaluation_succ`, identifies this vanishing with R^{r+1}Γ(V,a^*I)=0 for every r≥0. (uses `new-sheaf-cohomology-derived-evaluation`)
9. The exact localization restriction a^* also preserves injectives, so an injective resolution remains an injective resolution after restriction. Thus derived sections over the object V of X_proét are R^rΓ(V,a^*(-)); Lean uses `Functor.rightDerivedCompIsoOfExact` in `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.isZero_rightDerived_sectionsFunctor_of_upperStar`. Its derived-category construction uses the termwise functor on bounded-below derived categories `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image` and the identification of its cohomology on a sheaf in degree zero with classical right derived functors `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification`. Hence the preceding vanishing gives zero positive derived sections of I over every W×_Y X. To conclude, apply v to an injective resolution of I. Evaluation of that complex at W is exactly the sections complex of the resolution over W×_Y X, which is exact in every positive degree. Restriction to the affine pro-étale basis is faithful, and sheafifying the objectwise exact complexes shows that the complex of direct images is exact in those degrees. This is `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.rightAcyclic_lowerStar_of_isZero_rightDerived`, proving `rightAcyclic_lowerStar_of_isColimit_of_injective`: I is v-acyclic, meaning R^{r+1}v(I)=0 for every r≥0 `def-sp-acyclic`, equivalently the predicate `Functor.rightAcyclic` recorded in `sk-def-groth-ss`. (uses `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`, `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification`, `def-sp-acyclic`, `sk-def-groth-ss`)
10. Complete the induction. In degree zero, left exactness gives Mathlib's natural isomorphism `v.rightDerivedZeroIsoSelf`; transport the preservation of colimits by v along its inverse using `preservesColimitsOfShape_of_natIso`. If R^qv preserves the given filtered colimits, apply `lem-weil-pbc-right-derived-filtered-colimit-successor`, `CategoryTheory.Functor.preservesColimitsOfShape_rightDerived_succ`. Its hypotheses hold because both sheaf categories have exact filtered colimits and, by the preceding argument, every filtered colimit of injectives has zero R^{q+1}v. Concretely the successor lemma embeds a diagram F into a diagram of injectives J with cokernel Q. The short exact sequence remains exact after taking colimits. The natural connecting maps give exact rows colim R^qv(J)→colim R^qv(Q)→colim R^{q+1}v(F)→0 and R^qv(colim J)→R^qv(colim Q)→R^{q+1}v(colim F)→0. The first two comparison maps are isomorphisms by the induction hypothesis, so the third is an isomorphism by the cokernel property (implemented using Mathlib's `Abelian.isIso_of_epi_of_isIso`). This proves preservation in degree q+1. Induction and transport back from the small model prove the asserted canonical comparison is an isomorphism for every q≥0. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-right-derived-filtered-colimit-successor`)
