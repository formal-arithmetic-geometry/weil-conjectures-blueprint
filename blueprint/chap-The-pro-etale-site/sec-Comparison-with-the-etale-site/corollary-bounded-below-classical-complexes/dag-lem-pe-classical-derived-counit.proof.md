1. Write A and B for the small etale and pro-etale module-sheaf categories, F for the exact comparison inverse-image functor on D+ and R for the right-derived direct image. The exact functor comparison gives F its triangulated/shift structure by the exact-derived-functor result `lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated`. Starting with the shift structure on F, `Adjunction.rightAdjointCommShift` and `Adjunction.commShift_of_leftAdjoint` (the compatible structure supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-shift-compatibility-unique`) give R and the adjunction compatible shift structures; `Adjunction.isTriangulated_rightAdjoint` then makes R triangulated by `lem-sf-triangulated-right-adjoint`. The hypothesis hunit makes the derived unit invertible, so `Adjunction.fullyFaithfulLOfIsIsoUnit` supplies fullness and faithfulness of F. (uses `lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated`, `lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-shift-compatibility-unique`, `lem-sf-triangulated-right-adjoint`)
2. Let P be the property `IsClassicalModuleFor X Λ`. The module clause of `proEtaleClassicalSheaves` in [dag-sk-thm-pe-classical-sheaves] writes every P-object G as the image, up to an isomorphism, of an etale module M. Exactness and the single-complex comparison from `lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated` produce an isomorphism from F(single(M,0)) to single(G,0). The adjunction triangle identity, together with full faithfulness from Step 1, is implemented by `Adjunction.isIso_counit_app_of_iso`, and proves that the counit is an isomorphism on every single P-object. Naturality of `continuityMap_natural` transports P across isomorphisms, so P is closed under isomorphisms as required by the devissage theorem. (uses [dag-sk-thm-pe-classical-sheaves], `lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated`)
3. Apply `DerivedCategory.Plus.isIso_app_of_forall_homology` to the counit, with P and the single-object result of Step 2. Its first lower-bound hypothesis concerns the composite R then F: for T with lower bound n and q<n, `Functor.isZero_homologyFunctor_rightDerivedFunctorPlus_obj_of_isGE` gives the required vanishing for R from the right-derived negative-vanishing statement `lem-weil-pbc-derived-functor-negative-vanishing`, whose t-exactness input is the estimate `lem-kun-tensor-t-est`; `F₀.map_isZero` and the exact-functor homology comparison `def-sfb-exact-derived-cohomology-comparison` transport that vanishing through F (equivalently, through `mapDerivedCategoryPlusHomologyFunctorIso`). The second lower-bound hypothesis is the identity functor, and is exactly `DerivedCategory.isZero_of_isGE`. The finite truncation induction in the devissage theorem uses distinguished truncation triangles and `NatTrans.isIso_app_obj₂_of_isIso_app_obj₁₃`, i.e. the two-out-of-three principle `lem-sf-triangle-two-out-of-three`. Thus the theorem reduces the desired counit isomorphism to the homology-classicality condition. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-derived-functor-negative-vanishing`, `lem-kun-tensor-t-est`, `def-sfb-exact-derived-cohomology-comparison`, `lem-sf-triangle-two-out-of-three`)
4. For each q, compare the homology functor used by `ClassicalPlus` with the standard-model homology functor using the explicitly constructed `classicalHomologyIso`. Its construction cancels the localization functor with `Localization.fullyFaithfulWhiskeringLeft` and the two factorizations `DerivedCategory.homologyFunctorFactors`; the remaining model-change isomorphism is the `ShrinkHoms.equivalence`. Transport P across the resulting isomorphism using the naturality argument from Step 2. The exact-functor bounded-below comparison `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-bounded-below` and its normalized cohomology comparison `def-sfb-exact-derived-cohomology-comparison` justify these homology identifications. Therefore the homology condition required in Step 3 is exactly the hypothesis `ClassicalPlus X Λ K`, and `DerivedCategory.Plus.isIso_app_of_forall_homology` yields `IsIso` of the derived counit at K. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-bounded-below`, `def-sfb-exact-derived-cohomology-comparison`)
