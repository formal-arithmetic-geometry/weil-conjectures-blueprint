There is an equivalence

$$
D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)
\simeq D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda),
$$

with inverse functors $R\nu_{X,*}$ and $\nu_X^*$.
