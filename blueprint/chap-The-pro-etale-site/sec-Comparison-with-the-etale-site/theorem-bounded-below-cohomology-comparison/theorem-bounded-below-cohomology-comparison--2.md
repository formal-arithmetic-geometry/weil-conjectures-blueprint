For every $K\in D(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ and every $M\in D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$,
the map

$$
\Hom_{D(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)}(K,M)\longrightarrow
\Hom_{D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)}(\nu_X^*K,\nu_X^*M),\qquad
f\longmapsto\nu_X^*f,
$$

is bijective. Here $\nu_X^*$ is applied termwise to complexes, and no
boundedness assumption on $K$ is needed. Consequently
$\nu_X^*:D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^+(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is fully faithful.
