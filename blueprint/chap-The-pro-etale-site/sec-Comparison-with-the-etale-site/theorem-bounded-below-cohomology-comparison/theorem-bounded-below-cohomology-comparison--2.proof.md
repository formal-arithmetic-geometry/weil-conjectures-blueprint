For an adjunction, the left adjoint is fully faithful precisely when its unit is an isomorphism. The unit is an isomorphism by clause [theorem-bounded-below-cohomology-comparison--1], so $\nu_X^*$ is fully faithful on the stated bounded-below derived categories.
