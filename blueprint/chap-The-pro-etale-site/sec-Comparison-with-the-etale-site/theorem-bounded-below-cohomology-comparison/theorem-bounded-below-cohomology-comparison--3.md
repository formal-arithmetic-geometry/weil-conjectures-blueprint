Let $X$ be a scheme and $\mathcal A$ a Grothendieck abelian category.
For every $L\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal A)$ there is a canonical
isomorphism, natural in $L$,

$$
R\Gamma(X_{\acute{e}t},R\nu_{X,*}L)
\simeq R\Gamma(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},L).
$$

No boundedness assumption on $L$ is needed. In particular, for a discrete
commutative ring $\Lambda$ and $K\in D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, there is
a canonical isomorphism

$$
R\Gamma(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\nu_X^*K)
\simeq R\Gamma(X_{\acute{e}t},K).
$$
