1. Fix the chosen small site skeletons and the category of left Lambda-modules. The locally-presentable size result `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable` and the local-smallness result for derived categories `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small` justify the universe choices. The sheaf-Grothendieck result `lem-sfb-proetale-sheaves-grothendieck`, together with the closed sheaf tensor result `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed` and its transfinite sheafification ingredients [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits] and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], supplies the Grothendieck abelian coefficient categories required by the derived constructions `def-sfb-finite-derived-global-sections` and `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`. Thus `lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives` gives enough injectives. For the bounded-below complex J, `lem-sc-bounded-below-injective-resolution-existence` gives a quasi-isomorphism J -> I with I bounded below and termwise injective, and `lem-weil-pbc-bounded-below-injectives-kinjective` makes I K-injective. This is the resolution model used by the global-sections definitions `def-kun-rgamma-unbounded` and `pur7710-def-derived-sections`. (uses `lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives`, `lem-sc-bounded-below-injective-resolution-existence`, `lem-weil-pbc-bounded-below-injectives-kinjective`)
2. For the comparison adjunction, the inverse-image functor nu^* is exact (the Lean instance is preservesFiniteLimits_comparisonUpperStar) and its right adjoint is nu_*. The exact-left-adjoint injective result `lem-sp-right-adjoint-preserves-injectives` shows that nu_* sends each injective term I^r to an injective object; it also preserves the lower bound, so `lem-weil-pbc-bounded-below-injectives-kinjective` makes nu_*I K-injective. More generally, the exact-adjunction homotopy result `lem-weil-pbc-right-adjoint-preserves-kinjective` gives that nu_* preserves every K-injective complex, in the sense defined by `def-sfb-preserves-kinjective-complexes`. This is the precise PreservesKInjectiveComplexes hypothesis used below by the Lean theorem Functor.rightDerivedFunctorCompIso'. (uses `lem-sp-right-adjoint-preserves-injectives`, `lem-weil-pbc-bounded-below-injectives-kinjective`, `lem-weil-pbc-right-adjoint-preserves-kinjective`, `def-sfb-preserves-kinjective-complexes`)
3. At the underived level, let c be the Lean isomorphism comparisonLowerStarCompGlobalSectionsIso. The comparison direct image is restriction along the inclusion of the etale site into the pro-etale site, and that inclusion sends the terminal object T/T to the same terminal object. Hence for every module sheaf F and every degree r, (nu_*F)(T)=F(T); these identifications are Lambda-linear, commute with restrictions and differentials, and are natural in F. By the terminal-object descriptions in `def-sfb-finite-derived-global-sections` and `def-kun-rgamma-unbounded`, this is exactly the canonical evaluation comparison between etale sections after nu_* and pro-etale sections. The direct-image and finite-derived-functor interfaces `def-weil-derived-direct-image`, `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`, and `sk-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull` identify these functors with the coefficient-module right-derived constructions in the statement.
4. Apply the Lean composition theorem Functor.rightDerivedFunctorCompIso' to F=nu_*, G=Etale.rightDerivedGlobalSections, and the underived isomorphism c. Step 1 supplies the K-injective derived categories and resolution hypotheses, while step 2 supplies F.PreservesKInjectiveComplexes. The theorem produces a canonical natural isomorphism R(nu_*) ⋙ Etale.rightDerivedGlobalSections ≅ rightDerivedGlobalSections. Evaluated at J, its left side is RGamma(T_Et, Rnu_{T,*}J) and its right side is RGamma(T_Proet,J), by the definitions of derived direct image and derived sections `def-weil-derived-direct-image`, `def-kun-rgamma-unbounded`, and `pur7710-def-derived-sections`. Restricting this natural isomorphism to bounded-below J gives exactly the claimed comparison.
5. The component supplied by Functor.rightDerivedFunctorCompIso' is natural in the derived object because it is built functorially from c and the right-derived units; its component on a representative is the derived image of the degreewise terminal-object identities from step 3. Therefore it is the canonical comparison induced by evaluation on the final object, rather than an arbitrary isomorphism. The bounded-below interpretation agrees with the ordinary right-derived sections conventions recorded in `def-sfb-finite-derived-global-sections`, `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`, and `sk-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull`, completing the stated natural isomorphism.
