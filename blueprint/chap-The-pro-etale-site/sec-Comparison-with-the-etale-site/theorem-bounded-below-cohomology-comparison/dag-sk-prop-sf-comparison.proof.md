5. Prove filteredHomologyPreserves directly from exact filtered colimits of left modules. Regard a diagram of short complexes as a short complex S of diagrams. Evaluation identifies S.homology with the diagram of homologies, naturally. Taking the three adjacent terms preserves colimits degreewise, so the same holds for complex homology in every degree. Since Q(p) is invertible, p is a quasi-isomorphism and H^n(p) is invertible.
