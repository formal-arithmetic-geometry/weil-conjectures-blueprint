For $K\in D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ the unit of the adjunction between
$\nu_X^*:D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^+(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ and
$R\nu_{X,*}$ is an isomorphism

$$
K\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }R\nu_{X,*}\nu_X^*K.
\tag{2.7}
$$
