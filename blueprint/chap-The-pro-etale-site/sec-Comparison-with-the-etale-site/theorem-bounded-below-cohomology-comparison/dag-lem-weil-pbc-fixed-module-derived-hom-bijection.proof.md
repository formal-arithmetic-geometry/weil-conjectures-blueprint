1. Work in the sheaf categories with the closed symmetric monoidal and additive structure needed for derived tensor and Hom. The presheaf tensor/internal-Hom construction `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf` and the sheaf-level flat-resolution transfer `lem-sfb-sheaf-functorial-flat-resolution` provide the coefficient-side flat resolutions. The total tensor descends to homotopy categories `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy`, and the sequential K-flat derivability theorem `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability` together with the unary left-derived calculus `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus` supplies the K-flat derived constructions. In the module presentations these are exactly the site and ordinary-module K-flat notions `def-pc-kflat-complex` and `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`, and the resulting extension-of-scalars and finite/module push-pull constructions are the derived functors of `sk-def-sfb-derived-extension-of-scalars`, `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`, and `sk-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull`. (uses `det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf`, `lem-sfb-sheaf-functorial-flat-resolution`, `lem-sfb-total-tensor-homotopy`, `lem-sfb-sequential-kflat-derivability`, `lem-sfb-unary-left-derived-calculus`, `def-pc-kflat-complex`, `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`, `sk-def-sfb-derived-extension-of-scalars`, `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`, `sk-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull`)
2. For the comparison adjunction, the inverse image is applied termwise because it is exact; `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image` constructs the induced triangulated functor D(ν*) and its bounded-below restriction, while `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-bounded-below` proves that exactness preserves the lower bound. The direct image is taken by the corresponding right-derived construction described in `def-weil-derived-direct-image` and `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`. The underived unit and counit extend termwise to complexes and homotopy categories by `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`, and the bounded-below inverse/direct-image adjunction is normalized by the derived-adjunction result `prop-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image-adjunction` and the canonical comparison normalization `lem-trf-bounded-below-derived-adjunction`. (uses `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`, `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-bounded-below`, `def-weil-derived-direct-image`, `sk-def-sfb-finite-derived-functors`, `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`, `prop-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image-adjunction`, `lem-trf-bounded-below-derived-adjunction`)
3. At the abelian level, the comparison unit η: id ⟶ ν_*ν* is invertible by `lem-weil-pbc-comparison-grothendieck-unit`. For every injective étale object I, its comparison pullback ν*I is ν_*-acyclic: the general abelian comparison acyclicity is [dag-lem-cbc-abelian-comparison-injective-acyclicity], with the arbitrary-ring module form [dag-lem-pe-comparison-injective-left-modules-acyclic] and the finite-module specialization [dag-lem-weil-pbc-comparison-grothendieck-injective-acyclicity]. Thus the exact comparison left adjoint has an invertible unit on injectives and sends injectives to right-derived-acyclic objects. (uses [dag-lem-cbc-abelian-comparison-injective-acyclicity], [dag-lem-pe-comparison-injective-left-modules-acyclic], [dag-lem-weil-pbc-comparison-grothendieck-injective-acyclicity], `lem-weil-pbc-comparison-grothendieck-unit`)
4. Apply the injective-acyclic derived-unit criterion (the result recorded as `lem-cbc-derived-unit-injective-acyclicity`, implemented in Lean as `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.isIso_derivedPlus_unit_app_of_rightAcyclic`) to the comparison adjunction. For M with `DerivedCategory.TStructure.t.plus M`, it gives that the unit of the bounded-below derived adjunction at ⟨M, hM⟩ is an isomorphism. Lean then rewrites this bounded-below adjunction using `Adjunction.derivedAdjunctionPlus_eq_derivedPlus`, so the isomorphism is the bounded-below restriction of the unbounded derived adjunction. (uses `lem-cbc-derived-unit-injective-acyclicity`)
5. Define the unbounded comparison adjunction exactly as in Lean: `derivedComparisonAdjunction := (comparisonAdjunction A X).derivedAdjunction.ofNatIsoLeft (ν^*(X)$[A]$).leftDerivedFunctorIsoMapDerivedCategory`. The bounded-below inclusion is fully faithful, and the left and right comparison functors carry their bounded-below restrictions through the compatibility isomorphisms `Functor.mapDerivedCategoryPlusCompatibility` and `Functor.rightDerivedFunctorPlusCompatibility`; the latter is the compatibility theorem `src-right-derived-functor-plus-compatibility`. Using `Adjunction.map_restrictFullyFaithful_unit_app`, Lean identifies the image of the bounded-below unit with the unbounded unit at M. Since the former is invertible by the preceding step, `IsIso.of_isIso_comp_right` proves `IsIso ((derivedComparisonAdjunction A X).unit.app M)`. (uses `src-right-derived-functor-plus-compatibility`, `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-bounded-below`, `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`, `def-weil-derived-direct-image`)
6. For any g : K ⟶ M, the adjunction hom-equivalence for `derivedComparisonAdjunction A X` sends `D(ν^*(X)$[A]$).map g` to g ≫ ((derivedComparisonAdjunction A X).unit.app M). Lean proves the function equality by extensionality and `simp `Adjunction.homEquiv_counit``. Therefore the desired map is the composite of the inverse adjunction equivalence and postcomposition with the unit. The inverse adjunction equivalence is bijective, and postcomposition with the isomorphism-valued unit is bijective via `(asIso ((derivedComparisonAdjunction A X).unit.app M)).homToEquiv.bijective`; `Function.Bijective.comp` gives the required bijectivity.
