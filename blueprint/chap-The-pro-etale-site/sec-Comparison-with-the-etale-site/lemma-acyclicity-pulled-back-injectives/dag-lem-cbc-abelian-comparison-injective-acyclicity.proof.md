1. Work with the Grothendieck abelian coefficient category of sheaves on the small pro-etale site [dag-def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]: it is Grothendieck by `lem-sfb-proetale-sheaves-grothendieck`, locally presentable by `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable`, and has enough injectives by `lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives`. Thus K-injective resolutions are available by `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`, and the derived categories have the required size by `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`. The pro-etale inverse and direct images are the Kan-extension and restriction constructions [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star] and [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], with adjunction `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` and exact inverse image [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]; their sheafified construction uses the transfinite-plus finite-limit and orthogonality results [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits] and [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality], together with [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. The corresponding etale inverse/direct-image and bounded-below derived inverse-image conventions are `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`, `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, and `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`. The argument is uniform for an arbitrary scheme `def-pc-scheme` (the field-extension notation of `def-sc-field-extension` imposes no extra hypothesis). Let M=nu_X^* I. By `def-sp-injective-resolutions` choose an injective resolution R of M. For each q>=0, R.isoRightDerivedObj together with `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification` identifies the positive degree-(q+1) derived object of nu_{X,*} on M with the degree-(q+1) homology of the complex obtained by applying nu_{X,*} to R. Hence, by the definition of acyclicity `def-sp-acyclic` (the same acyclicity clause that appears in the composable-pair framework `sk-def-groth-ss`), it remains to prove that this homology vanishes. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sc-field-extension`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`, `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`, `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`, `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`, `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification`, `lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives`)
2. Fix q and write S for the degree-(q+1) short complex of the comparison direct image of R, namely the (q+1)-st ShortComplex.sc of (comparisonLowerStar A X).mapHomologicalComplex applied to R.cocomplex. The Lean proof changes the target homology-vanishing statement to S.Exact, then restricts S along the continuous functor from the affine etale site into X.etale. The restriction is faithful, and its underlying presheaf is tested objectwise; after sheafification, ShortComplex.mapNatIso with sheafificationNatIso identifies the resulting sheaf complex with the restricted S. Therefore it is enough to establish exactness after evaluation at every affine etale object W. This is the exactness-reflection and sheafification step implemented using the sheafification universal construction [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification])
3. For W in X.AffineEtale, let T be its image in X.ProEt. Its structure map is etale and its source is affine. Set j to be the localizationRestriction along T.hom. The etale inverse-image restriction result `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction` identifies the étale pullback of I along T.hom with restriction, and Etale.injective_upperStar_obj_of_etale' makes that pullback injective. Apply `stage3-affine-comparison-injective-derived-vanishing` to obtain vanishing of all positive derived global sections of this injective pullback on T.left. The isomorphism comparisonUpperStarUpperStarIso identifies the corresponding pro-etale sheaf with the restriction of M. The localization comparison ProEtale.upperStarIsoLocalizationRestriction is an isomorphism; its exactness and preservation of finite colimits give the hypotheses for Functor.rightDerivedCompIsoOfExact. Evaluation at the identity object of T.left is naturally evaluation at T via MorphismProperty.Over.isoMk (Iso.refl _). Transporting these isomorphisms through rightDerived proves the private helper affine_etale_sections_vanishing: for every q, the (q+1)-st right-derived sectionsFunctor at T applied to M is zero. (uses `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction`, `stage3-affine-comparison-injective-derived-vanishing`)
4. For each W, transport the vanishing from the preceding step across R.isoRightDerivedObj. HomologicalComplex.exactAt_iff_isZero_homology turns it into exactness of the evaluated short complex S at W, and ShortComplex.exact_iff_evaluation gives exactness of the underlying presheaf complex on the affine etale site. The presheaf-to-sheaf functor preserves homology, so the sheafified short complex is exact; ShortComplex.exact_iff_of_iso and ShortComplex.mapNatIso with sheafificationNatIso then identify it with the affine restriction of S. Faithful restriction reflects exactness, so S is exact. Thus its degree-(q+1) homology, and hence the degree-(q+1) derived object of nu_{X,*} on nu_X^*I, is zero. Since q was arbitrary, nu_X^*I is nu_{X,*}-acyclic in the sense of `def-sp-acyclic`. No step identifies sections of a higher direct-image sheaf with derived sections; all vanishing is obtained from the explicit evaluated resolution complex. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification])
