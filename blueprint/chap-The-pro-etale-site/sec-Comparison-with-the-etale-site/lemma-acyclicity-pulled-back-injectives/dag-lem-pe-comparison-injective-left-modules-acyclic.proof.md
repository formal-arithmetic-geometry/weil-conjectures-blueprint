1. The Lean argument works for an arbitrary Grothendieck abelian coefficient category A; take A = ModuleCat Λ, the category of left modules over the associative unital ring Λ. Mathlib.Algebra.Category.ModuleCat.AB supplies its Grothendieck abelian structure without a commutativity assumption. The sheaf categories are Grothendieck abelian by `lem-sfb-proetale-sheaves-grothendieck`, whose recorded essentially-small-site construction also applies to the étale site. The required sheafification [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification] is available: `lem-coef-grothendieck-locally-presentable` supplies presentable generators, and the transfinite plus construction has the universal property [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-sheaf-orthogonality] and preserves finite limits [dag-lem-sfb-transfinite-plus-finite-limits]. As a left adjoint it preserves colimits, hence is exact. Enough injectives follow from `lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives`. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification], `lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives`)
2. Fix n ≥ 0 and choose an injective resolution R of ν_X^* I in A-valued pro-étale sheaves. R.isoRightDerivedObj identifies R^(n+1)ν_X,* (ν_X^* I) with the degree-(n+1) homology of ν_X,* R. By HomologicalComplex.exactAt_iff_isZero_homology, it suffices to prove exactness of its short complex S in that degree. Restrict S along AffineEtale.Spec X, writing Φ for this restriction to the affine étale site. It is faithful and exact, so S.exact_map_iff_of_faithful reduces the goal to exactness of S.map Φ. Sheafification on this essentially small site exists by GrothendieckTopology.hasSheafify_of_essentiallySmall, using the construction in step 1 [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification])
3. For W in X.AffineEtale, let T be its pro-étale image and g:T.left → X its structure morphism. This morphism is étale and T.left is affine. Use Etale.upperStar for g^* on étale sheaves, the inverse-image construction `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image` also recorded among the pullback declarations of `def-sc-field-extension`. By the restriction identification Etale.upperStarIsoLocalizationRestriction `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction` and Etale.injective_upperStar_obj_of_etale', g^*I is injective. Apply `stage3-affine-comparison-injective-derived-vanishing`, namely AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.isZero_rightDerived_globalSections_comparisonUpperStar_of_isAffine, to g^*I on the affine scheme T.left. Its comparison pullback has zero derived global sections in degree n+1. (uses `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`, `def-sc-field-extension`, `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction`, `stage3-affine-comparison-injective-derived-vanishing`)
4. The comparison pullback of g^*I is naturally isomorphic to the pro-étale pullback of ν_X^*I. Indeed, the pro-étale direct image [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] and the étale direct image `def-weil-pbc-direct-image` commute with comparison restriction: both composites evaluate a sheaf on the same base change. This is lowerStarComparisonLowerStarIso. Taking the conjugate of this identity under the adjunctions for the pullbacks [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, and `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image` gives comparisonUpperStarUpperStarIso, the identification used in the Lean proof. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`)
5. Let j = ProEtale.localizationRestriction A g and let Γ be evaluation at the identity object of T.left_proét. Evaluation of j at that identity is naturally evaluation at T: MorphismProperty.Over.isoMk (Iso.refl _) gives the object isomorphism, and NatIso.ofComponents gives sectionsFunctor A T ≅ j ⋙ Γ. ProEtale.upperStarIsoLocalizationRestriction identifies j with g^*; exactness of g^* [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact] gives exactness of j. ProEtale.preservesInjectiveObjects_localizationRestriction shows that j preserves injectives, as it is right adjoint to a left exact sheafified left Kan extension. Thus an injective resolution is sent to an injective resolution. Functor.rightDerivedCompIsoOfExact identifies R^(n+1)(j ⋙ Γ) with (R^(n+1)Γ) ∘ j. Its derived-category construction uses K-injective resolutions `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`, the locally small derived categories `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`, the termwise derived functor of an exact functor `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`, and the identification on objects in degree zero `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification`. Transport the sections isomorphism by NatTrans.rightDerived and apply the derived Γ functor to the isomorphism in step 4. The vanishing in step 3 becomes vanishing of R^(n+1)(sectionsFunctor A T)(ν_X^*I). This is the private helper affine_etale_sections_vanishing. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`, `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`, `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`, `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification`)
6. R.isoRightDerivedObj for evaluation turns step 5 into exactness of the evaluated resolution at every affine étale object. These are definitionally the evaluations of the underlying presheaf complex of S.map Φ. ShortComplex.exact_iff_evaluation gives presheaf exactness. Exact sheafification [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification] preserves it; ShortComplex.mapNatIso with sheafificationNatIso, followed by ShortComplex.exact_iff_of_iso, identifies the sheafified short complex with S.map Φ. Finally faithful exact restriction reflects exactness, so the original degree-(n+1) homology is zero. This proves every positive-degree vanishing, hence ν_X,*-acyclicity in the sense of `def-sp-acyclic` and the acyclicity terminology of `sk-def-groth-ss`. Taking A = ModuleCat Λ gives the assertion for arbitrary left-module sheaves on any X. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification])
