Let $A$ be a Grothendieck abelian category, $W$ an affine scheme, and $I$ an
injective sheaf on $W_{\acute{e}t}$ with values in $A$. Then

$$
H^q(W_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\nu_W^*I)=0
\qquad(q>0).
$$
