1. Fix the coefficient category A of left Lambda-modules and the arbitrary scheme X. The comparison inverse-image and direct-image functors are the constructions of `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`, `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, with the corresponding termwise derived inverse-image formalism in `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`. The pro-etale sheaf category is Grothendieck by `lem-sfb-proetale-sheaves-grothendieck`, while the finite-level lower and upper stars are [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star] and [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], related by `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction` and exact by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]. The required derived categories have the needed local-smallness supplied by `DerivedCategory.locallySmall` from `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`. The field-extension notation recorded in `def-sc-field-extension` is only part of the coefficient/base-change conventions and imposes no affineness or quasi-compactness condition on X. (uses [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], `def-sc-field-extension`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`, `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`, `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, `def-weil-pbc-inverse-image`, `lem-pc-derived-category-locally-small`)
2. Let G := nu^* I. Since the relevant sheaf categories are Grothendieck, `lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives` and the K-injective resolution result `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective` provide an injective resolution R of G; in Lean this is `injectiveResolution G`. For each q, the definition of acyclicity `def-sp-acyclic` reduces the claim to vanishing of the (q+1)-st right-derived object of the comparison direct image. The single-object identification `homologyRightDerivedFunctorPlusObjSingleFunctorIso` from `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification`, together with `R.isoRightDerivedObj`, identifies that object with the homology of the complex obtained by applying the comparison direct image to R. (uses `lem-sfb-grothendieck-k-injective`, `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification`, `lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives`, `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`, `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image`)
3. Fix q and let S be the degree-(q+1) short complex of the comparison direct image of R. By `HomologicalComplex.homologyFunctor_obj` and `HomologicalComplex.exactAt_iff_isZero_homology`, it is enough to prove S.Exact. Restrict S along the continuous affine-etale comparison functor Phi. The affine-etale inverse-image/restriction identification `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction` supplies the relevant restriction functor, and the faithful exactness argument is the Lean step `S.exact_map_iff_of_faithful Phi`: exactness of S follows from exactness of its restricted complex. The passage between presheaves and sheaves uses the sheafification adjunction [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification], `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction`, `def-weil-pbc-direct-image`)
4. After restriction, write T for the underlying presheaf short complex. `ShortComplex.exact_iff_evaluation` reduces T.Exact to exactness at every affine-etale object W. Applying `R.isoRightDerivedObj` at W and again `HomologicalComplex.homologyFunctor_obj` together with `HomologicalComplex.exactAt_iff_isZero_homology` shows that this evaluation is exact once the corresponding derived sections over T := (toProEt X).obj ((AffineEtale.Spec X).obj W) vanish. Sheafification preserves the required homology, and `ShortComplex.mapNatIso` with `sheafificationNatIso` identifies the sheafified complex with the restricted sheaf complex, using [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification], `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction`)
5. For such W, T.hom is etale and T.left is affine. Let j := `ProEtale.localizationRestriction A T.hom T.prop` and let e identify T with the image of the identity object of T.left via `MorphismProperty.Over.isoMk (Iso.refl _)`. The etale pullback of I is injective by `Etale.injective_upperStar_obj_of_etale'`, the injectivity/restriction comparison being the content of `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction`. The affine theorem `stage3-affine-comparison-injective-derived-vanishing` gives zero for every positive derived global section of the comparison pullback of this injective sheaf on the affine scheme T.left. The localization comparison `ProEtale.upperStarIsoLocalizationRestriction` and `comparisonUpperStarUpperStarIso` transport that affine vanishing to the derived sections over T; the exactness needed for this transport is the finite-limit/finite-colimit exactness furnished by [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact] and the adjunction `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`. In Lean this transport is assembled with `Functor.rightDerivedCompIsoOfExact` and `NatTrans.rightDerived`. (uses [dag-lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact], [dag-def-sfb-finite-upper-star], [dag-def-sfb-finite-lower-star], `stage3-affine-comparison-injective-derived-vanishing`, `lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction`, `def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction`)
6. Thus every affine-etale evaluation of the restricted degree-(q+1) complex is exact. The sheafification comparison in the preceding steps gives exactness of the restricted sheaf complex, and faithful exact restriction reflects this to S.Exact. Therefore its (q+1)-st homology is zero; transporting through `R.isoRightDerivedObj` proves the (q+1)-st right-derived comparison direct image of nu^* I is zero. Since q was arbitrary, `nu_*(X)$[A]$.rightAcyclic (nu^*(X)$[A]$.obj I)` holds, which is exactly the asserted vanishing for every n >= 0 by `def-sp-acyclic`. (uses [dag-def-weil-pbc-sheafification], `lem-sp-derived-functor-single-identification`)
