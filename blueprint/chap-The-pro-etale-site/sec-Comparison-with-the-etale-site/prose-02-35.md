To apply statements proved on the étale site, such as proper base
change, on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$, we also need the essential image of $\nu_X^*$
on $D^+$. It consists of the classical complexes.
Write $D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ for the full
subcategory of $D^+(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ of classical complexes.
