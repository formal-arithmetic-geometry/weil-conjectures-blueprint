To apply statements proved on the étale site, such as [proper base
change](@proposition-proper-base-change-etale-site), on [$X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$](@definition-small-pro-etale-site), we also need the essential image of [$\nu_X^*$](@proposition-comparison-morphism-inverse-image)
on $D^+$. It consists of the [classical](@definition-classical-pro-etale-sheaves) complexes.
Write $D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\Lambda)$ for the full
subcategory of $D^+(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\Lambda)$ of [classical](@definition-classical-pro-etale-sheaves) complexes.
