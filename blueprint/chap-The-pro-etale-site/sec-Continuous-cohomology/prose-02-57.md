When the $F_n$ come from the étale site, as the sheaves
$(\mathbf Z/\ell^n)_X$ do, each term on the right is étale cohomology
by the comparison theorem, and the proposition identifies pro-étale
cohomology of the limit with continuous étale cohomology
$R\varprojlim_nR\Gamma(X_{\acute{e}t},F_n)$.
