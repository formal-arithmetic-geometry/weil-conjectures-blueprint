By Theorem [theorem-bounded-below-cohomology-comparison], each
$R\Gamma(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},F_n)$ is $R\Gamma(X_{\acute{e}t},F_{n,\acute{e}t})$. The derived
functor of $(G_n)\mapsto\Gamma(X_{\acute{e}t},\varprojlim_nG_n)$ is the composite
of the derived functors of sections and of inverse limit, so its value at
$(F_{n,\acute{e}t})$ is $R\varprojlim_nR\Gamma(X_{\acute{e}t},F_{n,\acute{e}t})$
[bhatt-scholze15, Proposition 5.6.2].
