The comparison theorems above concern discrete coefficients, whereas the integral
and rational $\ell$-adic coefficient rings of Section [sec-Coefficient-rings]
are defined directly on the pro-étale site, because the discrete constant
sheaves with the same values have the wrong cohomology
(Example [example-first-cohomology-finite-field]). The main result of
this section,
Proposition [proposition-continuous-cohomology-pro-etale-cohomology],
says that pro-étale cohomology with coefficients $\mathbf Z_\ell$ is
the classical continuous cohomology, that is, the derived limit of
cohomology with coefficients $\mathbf Z/\ell^n$.

The sheaf $\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}$ is the inverse limit of the classical sheaves
$(\mathbf Z/\ell^n)_X$, with surjective transition maps
(Lemma [lemma-comparison-completed-coefficients]). For such a limit,
the cohomology is the derived limit of the cohomology of the terms. When
the terms are classical, this is continuous étale cohomology.
