In the setting of [prop-functor-package], for
$K\in D^b_c(X,\Lambda)$ and $L\in D^b_{ctf}(Y,\Lambda)$, the
projection transformation is an isomorphism:

$$
Rf_!K\otimes^L L\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf_!(K\otimes^L Lf^*L).
$$

## Proof

The assertion is item (5) of [prop-functor-package] of the terminal assembly, on exactly the domains stated there.
