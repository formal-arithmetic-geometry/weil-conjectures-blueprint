For $Q\in D(T_{\Et})$ and a geometric point $\bar t$ of $T$,

$$
\mathcal H^q(Q)_{\bar t}=\mathcal H^q(Q_{\bar t}),
$$

where $Q_{\bar t}$ denotes the pullback of $Q$ to the point.
