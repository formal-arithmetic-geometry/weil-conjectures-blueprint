For an open immersion $g:V\hookrightarrow T$, the derived functors satisfy

$$
Lg^*Rg_*\cong\operatorname{id},
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
Lg^*i_*=0
$$

for the closed complement $i:Z\hookrightarrow T$ of $g$.
