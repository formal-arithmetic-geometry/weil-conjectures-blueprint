Let $K$ be a field with $\operatorname{cd}_{\rm tor}(K)\leq c$,
and let $X$ be a finite-type
$K$-scheme of dimension $d$.
Then $\operatorname{cd}_{\rm tor}(X)\leq2d+c$, in the sense of
[def-sc-etale-torsion-cd].
