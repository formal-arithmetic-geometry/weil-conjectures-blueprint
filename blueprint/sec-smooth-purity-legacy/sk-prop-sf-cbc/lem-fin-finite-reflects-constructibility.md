Let $p:Z\to S$ be a
finite morphism of
Noetherian schemes, let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring, and
let $F$ be an etale sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules on $Z$.
If its direct image $p_*F$ is
constructible, then
$F$ is constructible.
