The basic square [def-cbc-open-immersion-square] and the functor
[def-cbc-extension-by-zero], whose stalk description makes it commute with an
arbitrary base change [lem-cbc-extension-zero-base-change] and hence with
base change on derived categories [cor-cbc-derived-extension-zero-base-change].
Extension by zero is compatible with composition of open immersions
[lem-cbc-extension-zero-composition] and sits in the open-closed recollement
triangle [lem-cbc-open-closed-recollement].
