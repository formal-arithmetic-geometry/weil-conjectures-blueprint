The projection formula for extension by zero
[lem-cbc-extension-zero-projection-formula] and the commutation of exact
functors with derived limits of towers [lem-cbc-exact-functor-rlim] give the
completed extension-by-zero base change
[lem-cbc-completed-extension-zero-comparison], hence the completed exchange
map [def-cbc-completed-exchange-map] and its isomorphy
[thm-cbc-complete-proetale]. Rationalizing gives the statement over a finite
extension of $\Ql$ [thm-cbc-finite-extension] and, after descent, over
$\Qlb$ [thm-cbc-qbar].
