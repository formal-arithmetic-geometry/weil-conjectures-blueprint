This chapter decomposes the base change theorem for compact-support direct
images: extension by zero along an open immersion commutes with arbitrary base
change, and combined with proper base change and Nagata compactification it
yields the exchange isomorphism $\Ex_!$ for an arbitrary base-change morphism
-- at finite coefficients on the etale site, then in the bounded-below and
bounded constructible categories, and finally at the finite-level pro-etale,
complete, rational, and algebraically closed coefficient stages.

The route is as follows. Extension by zero is the left adjoint of the inverse
image along an open immersion and is characterized by geometric stalks, so
$g^{-1}j_!=j'_!g_U^{-1}$ holds for an arbitrary base change (Milne, *Etale
Cohomology* II.3). The compact-support direct image is defined via
compactifications, so $\Ex_!$ is the composite of the proper-base-change
exchange along the compactification with the extension-by-zero
identification; both factors are isomorphisms for an arbitrary base change.
The coefficient transports use the completed pullback and rational/Karoubi
machinery, which is independent of properness assumptions.
