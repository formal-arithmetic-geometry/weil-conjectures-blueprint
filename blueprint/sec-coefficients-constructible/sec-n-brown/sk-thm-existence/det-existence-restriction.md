Let $\Lambda$ and $f\colon X\to Y$ be as in [det-existence-localizing]. The right adjoint
$f^!$ of $Rf_!$ on the localizing categories sends $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
Consequently the adjunction $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ restricts to an adjunction
$Rf_!\colon\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\rightleftarrows\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)\colon f^!$.
