This is the first part of the argument in the proof of
[lem-sfb-dc-proper-pushforward-left-adjoint-e]. On each level $\cO_E/\pi_E^n$, the étale $Rp^!$
exists (SGA 4, XVIII) and preserves bounded constructible complexes (SGA 4½,
*Th. finitude*). The levels are compatible with reduction, and their derived limit is right
adjoint to $Rp_*$ on $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(-,\underline{\cO}_E)$ (Bhatt–Scholze, §6.8).
