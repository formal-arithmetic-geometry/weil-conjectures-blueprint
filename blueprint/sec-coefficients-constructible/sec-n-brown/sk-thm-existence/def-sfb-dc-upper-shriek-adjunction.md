In the situation of [def-sfb-dc-upper-shriek-functor], the adjunction

$$
Rf_!\dashv Rf^!
$$

between the lower shriek $Rf_!=Rf_!^{c_f}$ of [det-def-sfb-dc-lower-shriek] and
$Rf^!=Rf^!_{c_f}$ is the adjunction $Rf_!^{c_f}\dashv Rf^!_{c_f}$ of
[def-sfb-dc-upper-shriek-of-compactification-adjunction]. In particular $Rf_!$ is a left adjoint
and $Rf^!$ is a right adjoint.
