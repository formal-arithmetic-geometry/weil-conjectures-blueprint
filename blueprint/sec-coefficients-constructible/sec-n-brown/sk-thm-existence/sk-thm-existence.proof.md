The functor $Rf_!:\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda))$
and its right adjoint $f^!$ are constructed for all $\Lambda$ at once in Theorem [sk-prop-sf-existence].
Its hypotheses on compactness and direct images are verified for finite $A$
in Propositions [sk-prop-ctf-finite-compact] and [sk-prop-ctf-finite-proper],
for fields in Theorem [sk-thm-constructible-compactness] and
Proposition [sk-prop-proper-image-localizing], and for
$\mathcal O_E$ in Proposition [sk-prop-int-compactness].

The fact that $f^!$ preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda)$ is
Theorem [sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation] for finite $A$ and
Theorem [sk-thm-restriction] for $\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ (recall that in the integral
and rational cases $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}=\Dbc$).
