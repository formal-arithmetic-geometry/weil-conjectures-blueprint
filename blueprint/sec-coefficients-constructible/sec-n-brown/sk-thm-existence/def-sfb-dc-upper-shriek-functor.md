Let $\Lambda$ and $f\colon X\to Y$ be as in [def-sfb-dc-upper-shriek-of-compactification], and let
$c_f=(X\xrightarrow{i_f}Z_f\xrightarrow{p_f}Y)$ be the chosen compactification of
[def-sfb-compactification-over-field]. Assume that $Rp_{f*}$ on $D_{\mathrm{cons}}$ has a right
adjoint. The *exceptional inverse image* is

$$
Rf^!:=Rf^!_{c_f}\colon D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Y,\Lambda)\to D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Lambda).
$$
