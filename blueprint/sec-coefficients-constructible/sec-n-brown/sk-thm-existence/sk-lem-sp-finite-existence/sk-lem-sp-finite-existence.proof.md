The adjunction is that of [sk-prop-sf-existence] and restricts to $\Dctf$ by
[sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation]. By (G10) of [sk-lem-geo-affine-local],
its restriction to $U$ is $e^!p_d^!$, which is $e^*p_d^*(-)(d)[2d]$ by the same
lemma, with the composite counit. [sk-prop-sp-et-proet] transfers the
adjunction to the pro-étale sites.
