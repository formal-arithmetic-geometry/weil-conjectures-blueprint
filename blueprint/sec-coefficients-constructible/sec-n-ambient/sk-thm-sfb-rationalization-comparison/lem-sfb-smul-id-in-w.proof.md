Let $X\xrightarrow{m}X\xrightarrow{g}Z\xrightarrow{h}X[1]$ be distinguished. Then
$g\circ m=0$, so $(m\cdot\mathrm{id}_Z)\circ g=m\cdot g=0$. The long exact sequence of
$\Hom(-,Z)$ gives $u\colon X[1]\to Z$ with $m\cdot\mathrm{id}_Z=u\circ h$. Also
$m[1]\circ h=0$, i.e. $m\cdot h=0$. Hence
$m^2\cdot\mathrm{id}_Z=m\cdot(u\circ h)=u\circ(m\cdot h)=0$. Since $m^2\in M$, the cone is
$M$-torsion. A distinguished triangle on $m\cdot\mathrm{id}_X$ exists by the axioms.
