1. For $n\ge1$ let $M_n\in D(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$ be perfect, with
   maps $M_{n+1}\to M_n$ inducing isomorphisms
   $M_{n+1}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^{n+1}}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n\simeq M_n$.
   Then there is a bounded complex $P^\bullet$ of finite free
   $\mathcal O_E$-modules with isomorphisms $P^\bullet/\pi_E^n\simeq M_n$
   in $D(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, compatible with the transition maps.
2. Let $Q^\bullet$ be a bounded complex of finite free
   $\mathcal O_E$-modules, and let $\pi_E^e$ kill the torsion of
   $H^{q+1}(Q^\bullet)$. Then for $m\ge n+e$

$$
\operatorname{im}\bigl(H^q(Q^\bullet/\pi_E^m)\to
H^q(Q^\bullet/\pi_E^n)\bigr)
=\operatorname{im}\bigl(H^q(Q^\bullet)\to
H^q(Q^\bullet/\pi_E^n)\bigr).
$$
