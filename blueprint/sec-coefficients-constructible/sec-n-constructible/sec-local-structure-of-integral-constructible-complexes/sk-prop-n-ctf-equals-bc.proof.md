For $\mathcal O_E$ this is part of the criterion above. For a field
$\Lambda(*)$, a constructible sheaf $M$ is, on each stratum and locally on
a cover $\{U_i\}$, of the form $\Lambda_{U_i}\otimes_{\Lambda(*)}P_i$
with $P_i$ a finite-dimensional vector space, hence a finite free
$\Lambda_{U_i}$-module. Since restriction to the strata is exact and the restrictions are jointly
conservative, as in the proof of the criterion, every constructible sheaf
has Tor amplitude in $[0,0]$. A bounded complex whose finitely many cohomology
sheaves have finite Tor amplitude has finite Tor amplitude, by the
triangles of its canonical filtration (see also Lemma [sk-lem-int-tensor]).
