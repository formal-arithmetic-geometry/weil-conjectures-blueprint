For an algebraic group $H$ over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, an
*algebraic character* is a regular group homomorphism
$\chi:H\to\mathbf G_m$. The character group is
$X^*(H)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{alg.grp}}(H,\mathbf G_m)$.
