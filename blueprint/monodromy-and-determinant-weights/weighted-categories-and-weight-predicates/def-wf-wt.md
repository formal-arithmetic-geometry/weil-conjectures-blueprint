For $u\in\mathbf Z$ and $K\in\mathrm{WeilD}(X_0,E)$, the predicate
$\mathrm{Wt}(K,u)$ holds if every weight of an eigenvalue of the
Frobenius of $\varphi_K$ is equal to $u$.
Each of the predicates $\mathrm{WtLe},\mathrm{WtLt},\mathrm{WtGe}, \mathrm{WtGt},\mathrm{Wt}$ of [def-wf-wtle], [def-wf-wtge]
and the present definition has an *integral* variant (weights
required to be integers) and an *all-conjugates* variant (the
bound required for all conjugates of the eigenvalues).
