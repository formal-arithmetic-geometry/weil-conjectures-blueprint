Each $E_{r+1}$ is the homology of the page $E_r$
([def-sp-spectral-sequence]); a morphism of complexes which is an
isomorphism induces an isomorphism on homology, and the page
identifications are compatible with the morphism. Hence the
$E_{r_0}$-isomorphism gives the $E_{r_0+1}$-isomorphism, and induction gives
all $E_r$, $r\geq r_0$. In the first-quadrant case the stable term
$E_\infty^{p,q}$ is the entry of the page $E_{p+q+2}$, on which $\varphi$ is
an isomorphism.
