The sheafification is needed: invertible sheaves satisfy effective
descent, but automorphisms can prevent their isomorphism classes from
forming a sheaf, and hence neither the displayed quotient nor
$T\mapsto\operatorname{Pic}(C_T)$ need be an fppf sheaf
[Stacks, Tag 0B9L](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B9L). If $f$ has a section, the next lemma
describes the sheafification directly. As usual, we also write
$\operatorname{Pic}_{C/S}$ for the Picard scheme when it exists; the
Stacks Project writes $\underline{\mathrm{Pic}}_{C/S}$.
