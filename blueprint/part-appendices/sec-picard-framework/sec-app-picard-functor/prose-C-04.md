For a smooth proper geometrically connected curve $C$ over a field
$k$, we have $H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=k$. For every field extension $k'/k$
the curve $C_{k'}$ is again smooth, proper and integral, and flat base change
for coherent cohomology gives $H^0(C_{k'},\mathcal O_{C_{k'}})=k'$ and
$\dim_{k'}H^1(C_{k'},\mathcal O_{C_{k'}})=\dim_kH^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$. Since every
$k$-scheme is flat over $k$, the same flat base change shows that $(\ast)$
holds for $C\to\operatorname{Spec}k$. If $C$ has a $k$-point $P$, the preceding lemma applies
with $\sigma=P$ (see [Stacks, Tag 0B9U](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B9U)).
