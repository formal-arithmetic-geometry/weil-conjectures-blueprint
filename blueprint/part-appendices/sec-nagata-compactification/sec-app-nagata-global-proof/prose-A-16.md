Both approximation results rest on an elementary fact about affine
schemes: a finitely presented algebra, its morphisms, and
finitely many gluing identities involve only finitely many coefficients
and equations, and hence they are defined at a finite stage.

A scheme of finite type over $S$ need not descend to a Noetherian
stage, since an algebra $R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/I$ is finitely presented
only when $I$ is finitely generated, and this can fail if $R$ is not
Noetherian. This is why we first embedded $X$ into $X_0$, which is of
finite presentation and therefore descends.
