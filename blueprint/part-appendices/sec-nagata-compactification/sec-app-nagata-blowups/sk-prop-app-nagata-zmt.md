A separated, quasi-compact, locally quasi-finite morphism
$Z\to T$ locally of finite type factors as an open immersion
$Z\hookrightarrow T_0$ followed by an affine morphism $T_0\to T$.
Consequently $Z\to T$ is quasi-affine.
