On an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, write
$I=(a_1,\ldots,a_r)$. The charts of the blow-up have rings

$$
R[I/a_i]=\bigl((\bigoplus_{m\geq0}I^mt^m)_{a_it}\bigr)_0.
$$

This is the degree-zero part of the localization at $a_it$ of the
Rees algebra, where $t$ is a variable of degree one. It is the quotient
of $R[x_1,\ldots,x_r]/(a_ix_j-a_j)_j$ by its $a_i$-power torsion, that
is, by the elements killed by some power of $a_i$, and this torsion can
be nonzero. In the
chart $R[I/a_i]$, the element $a_i$ is a nonzerodivisor and generates
the extended ideal $IR[I/a_i]$. Hence the center becomes an effective
Cartier divisor, and the blow-up is an isomorphism away from the
center. Since $I$ is finitely generated, the blow-up of an affine base
is a closed subscheme of a projective space over it, and hence $b$ is proper.
These constructions glue under restriction to opens.
