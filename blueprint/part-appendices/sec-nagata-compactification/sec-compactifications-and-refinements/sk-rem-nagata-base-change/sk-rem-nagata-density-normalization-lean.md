The Lean formalization proves [sk-lem-nagata-density-normalization], and with
it the independence of $Rf_!$ of the compactification
([sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), only for the complexes it uses: bounded below
complexes of étale origin with finite coefficients, bounded constructible
complexes with coefficients $E$ and $\Qlb$, and Weil complexes. The statements
for all of $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda_X)$, and on the unbounded categories
$\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$, are not formalized. For the comparison of a
compactification with the schematic closure, these classes suffice for
everything that is used later.
