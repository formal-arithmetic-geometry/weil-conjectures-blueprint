On an affine chart $\operatorname{Spec}(A)\subseteq S$, choose a finite
presentation
$B=A[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ for an affine chart
$\operatorname{Spec}(B)\subseteq X$. The corresponding chart of
$X\times_SX$ has ring $B\otimes_A B$, and the diagonal is represented by the
multiplication map

$$
\mu\colon B\otimes_A B\longrightarrow B,\qquad b\otimes b'\longmapsto bb'.
$$

This map is surjective, and its kernel is generated by the finite list
$x_i\otimes1-1\otimes x_i$. Hence $B$ is finitely presented as a
$(B\otimes_A B)$-algebra. The opens $U\times_SU$, for affine opens $U=\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ of $X$
mapping into affine opens of $S$, satisfy
$\Delta_{X/S}^{-1}(U\times_SU)=U$, and these $U$ cover $X$; being locally of
finite presentation is local on the source, so this chart calculation shows
that $\Delta_{X/S}$ is locally of finite presentation. It is quasi-compact
because $f$ is quasi-separated, and quasi-separated because it is a
monomorphism. Hence $\Delta_{X/S}$ is of finite presentation
([Stacks, Tag 0818]).
