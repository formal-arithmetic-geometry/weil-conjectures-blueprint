Let $G$ be a connected algebraic group and $r:G\to\GL(M)$ an
algebraic representation. A subspace of $M$ is $G$-stable if and only if it is
$\operatorname{Lie}(G)$-stable. Moreover

$$
\operatorname{Lie}\bigl(Z(G)^\circ\bigr)
=Z\bigl(\operatorname{Lie}(G)\bigr).
$$
