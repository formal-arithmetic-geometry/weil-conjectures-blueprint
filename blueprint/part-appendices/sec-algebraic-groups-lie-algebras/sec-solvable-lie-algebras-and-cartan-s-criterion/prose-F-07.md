See (Hum, §4.3). By
Lemma [sk-lem-walg-engel] it suffices to show that every element $X$ of
$[\mathfrak a,\mathfrak a]$ is nilpotent. Pairing $X$ with operators
that are polynomials in its semisimple part, the hypothesis gives
$\sum_i\lambda_i f(\lambda_i)=0$ for the eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ of the
semisimple part and every $\mathbf Q$-linear functional $f$ on their
span; for $f$ a rational coordinate this is a vanishing sum of squares,
so all $\lambda_i$ are zero. This step uses characteristic zero.
