The degree identifies $H^2(C,\mu_n)$ with $\mathbf Z/n$ without any choice.
Since $\mu_n=(\mathbf Z/n)(1)$, twisting by $-1$ gives

$$
H^2(C,\mathbf Z/n)=(\mathbf Z/n)(-1),
$$

and identifying this group with $\mathbf Z/n$ requires the choice of a
primitive $n$th root of unity. For $C=\mathbf P^1_k$ the Jacobian is zero,
so the cohomology with constant coefficients is concentrated in degrees
zero and two, and the Kummer boundary of $\mathcal O(1)$ corresponds to
$1\in\mathbf Z/n$. Section [sec-pbc-projective-line] uses this
normalization on the geometric fibre.
