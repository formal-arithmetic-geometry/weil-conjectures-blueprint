The calculations so far treat constant coefficients on smooth curves.
Theorem [sk-thm-curve-package] concerns arbitrary curves of dimension at
most one and arbitrary torsion sheaves, and the gap is bridged by
sheaves supported on finitely many points, which have no higher
cohomology (Lemma [sk-lem-curve-finite-support]). This allows us to pass
between a curve, its normalization and a dense open subset, changing
cohomology only in degrees zero and one, and only by finite groups that
do not change under field extension. For the nodal cubic
$C:y^2=x^3+x^2$, the normalization $\mathbf A^1\to C$ has two points over
the node, and for $M=\mathbf Z/n$ the normalization sequence of
Lemma [sk-lem-curve-normalization] has cokernel $M^2/\Delta M\simeq M$
at the node. If $n$ is invertible in $k$, then
$H^1(\mathbf A^1,\mathbf Z/n)=0$, and the long exact sequence gives
$H^1(C,\mathbf Z/n)\simeq\mathbf Z/n$; this is the analogue of the loop
created by the node. Removing points
from a curve (Lemma [sk-lem-curve-dense-open]) is the other reduction,
used to pass from a constructible sheaf to its extension by zero from
an open on which it is locally constant.
