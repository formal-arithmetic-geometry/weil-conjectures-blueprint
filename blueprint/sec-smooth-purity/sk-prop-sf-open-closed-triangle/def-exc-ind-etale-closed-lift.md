For an ideal $I\subset A$ and an ind-etale $A/I$-algebra $\overline C$, let
$\mathcal N_A(\overline C)$ have objects factorizations
$A\to B\to\overline C$, with $B/A$ etale of finite presentation, and
morphisms the compatible $A$-algebra maps. Define the $A$-algebra
$$
 H_A(\overline C)=\varinjlim_{\mathcal N_A(\overline C)}B
$$
and retain its map to $\overline C$. A map of the algebras $\overline C$
acts by postcomposition on the indexing categories, and hence defines the
maps of this construction.
