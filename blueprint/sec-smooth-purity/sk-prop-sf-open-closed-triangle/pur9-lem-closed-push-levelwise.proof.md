By Input [pur9-input-closed-immersion], $i_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is exact, so it preserves quasi-isomorphisms; for a K-injective resolution $M\to I$, the map $i_*M\to i_*I$ is a quasi-isomorphism, natural in $M$, identifying the derived value with the levelwise value. Both functors of the adjunction are thus levelwise functors of exact functors, and the termwise sheaf unit and counit satisfy the triangle identities termwise, hence after localization; by uniqueness of adjoint data for given functors and Hom bijection, the adjunction of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived inverse-image adjunction” (`pur713-lem-star-adjunction`), being the derived adjunction of the same sheaf adjunction (governing reference, §5), has these unit and counit.
