Under Input [pur9-input-closed-immersion], $Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived direct image” (`pur713-def-derived-push`) is naturally isomorphic to $\mathrm D(i_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, and under this identification and that of Lemma “The finite-coefficient derived inverse image is levelwise” (`pur713-lem-finite-pull-levelwise`) the unit and counit of the adjunction $Li^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived inverse-image adjunction” (`pur713-lem-star-adjunction`) are induced termwise by those of the sheaf adjunction of Input “Adjunction and exactness of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-adjunction`).
