By Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-smooth], $\widetilde g$ is smooth of pure relative dimension $d$, so Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-invertible] gives $\omega_{\widetilde g}\cong L\nu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal L_{\widetilde g})[2d]$; applying $L\widetilde s^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and Definition “Inverse image across the two sites” (`pur7710-def-site-pullback-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) gives the first claim with $\mathcal L_{\mathcal F}=\widetilde s^*_{\Et}\mathcal L_{\widetilde g}$, which is lisse of rank one. The second is the same argument for $g$ and $s$.
