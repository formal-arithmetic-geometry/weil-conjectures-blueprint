By Lemma [pur9-dnc-lem-line] and Definition “Inverse image across the two sites” (`pur7710-def-site-pullback-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), both sides are $L\nu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of shifted lisse rank-one étale sheaves, $\mathcal L_{\mathcal F}[2d]$ and $a^*_{\Et}\mathcal L_1[2d]$. By Lemma “Full faithfulness of the finite-coefficient site inverse image” (`pur7710-lem-site-fully-faithful`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), morphisms between them are morphisms $\mathcal L_{\mathcal F}\to a^*_{\Et}\mathcal L_1$ of sheaves on $(\Aaf^1_B)_{\Et}$, compatibly with restriction along $j$ and along étale maps. Uniqueness: two morphisms of lisse sheaves on $\Aaf^1_B$ agreeing on $\mathbf G_{m,B}$ agree, by the uniqueness statement for lisse sheaf maps of -prop-a1-invZZ. Existence: let $\{B_i\to B\}$ be an étale covering on which $(g,s)$ admits coordinates as in Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-smooth]; by Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-base-change] the deformation over $B_i$ is that of the restricted pair, and the coordinates identify it, étale-locally on $V$, with the standard pair, so Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-standard] gives isomorphisms $\kappa_i$ over $\Aaf^1_{B_i}$ which restrict to $\tau^\circ$ over $\mathbf G_{m,B_i}$. On overlaps $\kappa_i$ and $\kappa_j$ agree over $\mathbf G_{m,B_{ij}}$, hence everywhere by uniqueness, and the sheaf axiom for $\mathcal Hom(\mathcal L_{\mathcal F},a^*_{\Et}\mathcal L_1)$ on $(\Aaf^1_B)_{\Et}$ glues them to $\kappa_{g,s}$, an isomorphism since it is one locally.
