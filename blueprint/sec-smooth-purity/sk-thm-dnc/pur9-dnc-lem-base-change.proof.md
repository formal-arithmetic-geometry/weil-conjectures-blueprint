By Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-base-change] the deformation data base change. The pullback of $\kappa_{g,s}$ restricts over $\mathbf G_{m,B'}$ to the pullback of $\tau^\circ$, which is $\tau^\circ$ for the base-changed pair by its construction from base-change isomorphisms; uniqueness in Lemma [pur9-dnc-lem-extension] identifies it with $\kappa_{g',s'}$. Restriction along $i_0$ gives the claim.
