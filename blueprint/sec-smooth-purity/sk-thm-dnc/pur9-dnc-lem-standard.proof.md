By Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-standard], the isomorphism $\mathcal F\cong La^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal C(p_d,0)$ restricts to $\tau^\circ$ over $\mathbf G_{m,B}$, so it is $\kappa_{p_d,0}$ by the uniqueness in Lemma [pur9-dnc-lem-extension]. Its restriction along $i_0$ is the identity after the identifications of Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-fibres], so $\delta_{p_d,0}$ is the identity.
