For a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ with ideal sheaf $\mathcal I$, the normal cone is

$$
N_{Z/X}:=\operatorname{Spec}_Z\Bigl(\bigoplus_{r\geq0}\mathcal I^r/\mathcal I^{r+1}\Bigr),
$$

with projection to $Z$ and zero section given by the projection of the graded algebra onto its degree-zero part $\mathcal O_Z$.
