For finite admissible $A$, the category $\Dctf(T,A)\subset D(T_{\mathrm{proét}},A)$ is
equivalent to the category $\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ of constructible complexes of
finite Tor amplitude on the étale site. The equivalence respects pullback,
qcqs derived direct image and extension by zero, and hence $Rf_!$. On the
categories $\Loc(\Dctf(-,A))$ these comparisons are compatible with the
adjunction $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ of [sk-prop-sf-existence].

So an adjunction between $Rf_!$ and some functor $G$ on the étale categories
$\Dctf(-_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ transfers to the pro-étale categories
$\Dctf(-_{\mathrm{proét}},A)$: the Hom groups agree by full faithfulness, and
$Rf_!$ corresponds to $Rf_!$.
