For a proper $p:\overline X\to Y$ of $k$-schemes of finite type and an integer $N\geq0$, the condition $\mathrm{FullCD}(p,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n;N)$ means: for every morphism $v:Y'\to Y$ of $k$-schemes of finite type, with base change $p':\overline X\times_YY'\to Y'$, and every sheaf $M$ of $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-modules on $(\overline X\times_YY')_{\Proet}$ placed in degree zero,

$$
\mathcal H^r(Rp'_{*,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}M)=0\qquad(r>N),
$$

with $Rp'_{*,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived direct image” (`pur713-def-derived-push`). The bound is uniform in $v$ and $M$. This is a separate optional condition; it is neither a definition of the proper adjoint nor a replacement for its existence. Quantifying only over sheaves of étale origin gives a weaker condition, which must be stated separately; base change along pro-étale objects not of finite type requires an explicitly extended framework.
