Under Input “Finite-coefficient proper-adjoint input” (`pur713-input-proper-adjoint`, in [det-def-sf-operations]), the finite-coefficient ambient exceptional adjunction for $f$ is the adjunction

$$
Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}\dashv Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n},\qquad
Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}:D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)\longrightarrow D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n),
$$

with the functors of Definitions “Finite-coefficient compact-support direct image” (`pur713-def-lower-shriek`) and [pur713-def-upper-shriek] and the unit and counit of the composite adjunction of Lemma “Finite-coefficient compact-support adjunction” (`pur713-lem-shriek-adjunction`, in [det-def-sf-operations]), built from the proper right adjoint, unit and counit supplied by Input “Finite-coefficient proper-adjoint input” (`pur713-input-proper-adjoint`, in [det-def-sf-operations]). Mere existence of an unrelated adjoint does not specify this datum. No cohomological-dimension or preservation assertion is part of it.
