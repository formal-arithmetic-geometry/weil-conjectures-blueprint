A finite-coefficient admissibility datum for $f$ consists of the adjunction of Definition [pur456-def-adjoint-datum], integers $c_f^-\leq c_f^+$ for which it has uniform constructible cohomological amplitude in the sense of Definition [pur456-def-amplitude], and the property that it preserves the finite-Tor constructible categories in the sense of Definition [pur456-def-preservation]. Supplying such a datum is an additional hypothesis unless these properties have been proved for the chosen ambient adjunction.
