An object $K\in D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$ has Tor amplitude in $[u,v]$, for integers $u\leq v$, if $\mathcal H^r(K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}M)=0$ for $r\notin[u,v]$ and every sheaf $M$ of $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-modules on $W_{\Et}$ in degree zero, with the tensor product of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`) for $\tau=\Et$. This is the étale analogue of Definition [pur713-def-tor-amplitude].
