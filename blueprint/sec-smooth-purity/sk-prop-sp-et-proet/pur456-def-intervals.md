For a $k$-scheme $W$ of finite type and integers $a\leq b$, let

$$
D_c^{[a,b]}(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)
\subseteq D^b_c(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)
$$

be the full subcategory of objects of the category of Definition [pur713-def-constructible-category] whose cohomology sheaves vanish outside $[a,b]$.
