In the notation of [def-weil-exceptional-adjoint-admissibility]: in
the setting of the global finite-field datum, that is for $S=\Spec k$ with
$k=\Fbar$ and for finite-type $S$-schemes, clause (1) is false in general,
but the analogues of all three clauses for the constructible exceptional
inverse image $Rf^!$ of [def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image] are theorems on
the blueprint's coefficient class [def-sf-fullcd-used-class], at every
finite coefficient ring and with
constants independent of the stage: the class-restricted form of clause (1)
— the dimension datum on that class and the right adjoint relative to the
bounded-below objects of étale origin — by [thm-sf-fullcd-class] and
[thm-sf-ambient-adjoint-class], clauses (2) and (3) by
[thm-sf-preservation-class] and [lem-weil-verdier-preservation-strata] of the
six-functor chapter, the preservation being proved there by the chartwise
assembly of the smooth-purity chapter under the open-complement condition of
[lem-exc-open-complement-known].
