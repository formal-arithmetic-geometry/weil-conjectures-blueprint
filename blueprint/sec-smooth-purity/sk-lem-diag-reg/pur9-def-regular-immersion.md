title = "Regular closed immersions"

A closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ with ideal sheaf $\mathcal I$ is regular of pure codimension $c\geq0$ if every point of $X$ has an affine open neighbourhood $U$ such that $\mathcal I|_U$ is generated by a regular sequence $f_1,\dots,f_c\in\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)$. Such a sequence is a *frame* of $Z$ on $U$.
