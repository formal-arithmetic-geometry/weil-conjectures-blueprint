For a smooth morphism $f:X\to Y$, the relative tangent bundle is $T_{X/Y}:=\operatorname{Spec}_X\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathcal O_X}(\Omega_{X/Y})\to X$, whose zero section corresponds to the augmentation $\operatorname{Sym}(\Omega_{X/Y})\to\mathcal O_X$.
