The conormal sheaf $\mathcal I_\Delta/\mathcal I_\Delta^2$ is $\Omega_{X/Y}$, by the construction of $\Omega_{X/Y}$ from the ideal of the diagonal. For a regular immersion the natural map $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2)\to\bigoplus_r\mathcal I^r/\mathcal I^{r+1}$ is an isomorphism, so $N_\Delta=\operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{Sym}(\Omega_{X/Y})=T_{X/Y}$.
