$\Delta$ is a closed immersion because $f$ is separated. By the normal form of -prop-normal-formZZ, $f$ is étale-locally the projection $\Aaf^d_Y\to Y$ after an étale map; on $\Aaf^d_Y\times_Y\Aaf^d_Y$ the diagonal is cut out by the regular sequence $x_i-y_i$, and since the étale chart map is unramified its diagonal is open, so étale-locally $\Delta(X)$ is cut out by the same sequence. Regularity and codimension are étale-local.
