Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth, separated, of pure relative dimension $d$, put
$W=X\times_YX$ with projections $p_1,p_2$ and diagonal
$\Delta:X\hookrightarrow W$. Then $\Delta$ is a regular closed immersion of
pure codimension $d$.
