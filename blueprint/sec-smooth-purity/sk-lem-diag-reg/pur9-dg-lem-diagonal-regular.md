Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth, separated, of pure relative dimension $d$, and $\Delta:X\to W=X\times_YX$ the diagonal. Then $\Delta$ is a closed immersion, regular of pure codimension $d$ in the sense of Definition [pur9-def-normal-cone].
