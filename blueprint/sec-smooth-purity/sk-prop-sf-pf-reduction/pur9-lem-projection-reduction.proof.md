By Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection], $\operatorname{PF}$ is the composite of $P_{p,\mathrm D(j_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})K,L,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\sigma^{-1})$ and the composition comparison $\mu_{p,j}$. The first factor is handled by Lemma [pur9-lem-push-projection-reduction]; the second and third by the compatibility of $r^*$ with $\sigma$ and with the composition comparisons in Input [pur9-input-reduction-monoidal], together with naturality of $r_{*,p}$. Composing the three commutative squares gives the equation.
