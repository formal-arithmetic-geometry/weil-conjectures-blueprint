Let $Y$ have the small pro-etale site
[def-weil-pbc-proetale-site], with the coefficient categories and
pullbacks of [def-weil-derived-pullback-tensor-hom]. For finite
$\Lambda=A_{E,n}$, a complex $K$ is *perfect on the coefficient site* if a
covering $\{U_\alpha\to Y\}$ makes each $K|_{U_\alpha}$ isomorphic to a
bounded complex of finite projective $\Lambda_{U_\alpha}$-modules. Here a
finite projective module is a direct summand of a finite sum
$\Lambda_{U_\alpha}^{\oplus m}$. At the integral stage use the completed
coefficient sheaf [def-weil-completed-integral-coefficients]
$\widehat{\mathcal O}_{E,U_\alpha}$. At the rational stage
[def-weil-rationalization-karoubi] use bounded complexes of direct summands
of finite sums of the unit $E_{U_\alpha}$ in that localized category. An
object of the common-stage $\Qlb$-category
[def-weil-algebraically-closed-coefficients] is site-perfect if some
finite-field representative is site-perfect.
