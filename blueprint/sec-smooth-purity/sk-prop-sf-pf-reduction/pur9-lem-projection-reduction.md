Under Input [pur9-input-reduction-monoidal], for $K\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $L\in D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
&Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^m}(1\otimes r^*_{f,L})\circ Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^m}(r^\otimes)\circ r_{!,f,K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L}\circ\mathrm{red}_{n,m}(\operatorname{PF}_{f,K,L,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\\
&\quad=\operatorname{PF}_{f,\mathrm{red}_{n,m}K,\mathrm{red}_{n,m}L,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^m}\circ(r_{!,f,K}\otimes1)\circ r^\otimes_{Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K,L}
\end{aligned}
$$

in $D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^m)$,
with the finite-coefficient maps $\operatorname{PF}$ of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection] at levels $n$ and $m$.
