For $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E\}$ and every separated $k$-scheme of finite type $X$, an object $\mathcal R_{X,T}(1)$ of $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},T)$ is specified that is invertible for $\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}$, together with isomorphisms $Lf^*_T\mathcal R_{Y,T}(1)\cong\mathcal R_{X,T}(1)$ for all $f:X\to Y$, compatible with the composition comparisons of Lemma [pur713-lem-pull-composition-module]. For $d\geq0$, $\mathcal R_{X,T}(d)$ denotes its $d$-fold $\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}$-power, with $\mathcal R_{X,T}(0)=\mathcal R_{X,T}$. *Status:* no Tate twist of module objects over $\mathcal R_{X,T}$ is defined in the governing reference or in this manuscript.
