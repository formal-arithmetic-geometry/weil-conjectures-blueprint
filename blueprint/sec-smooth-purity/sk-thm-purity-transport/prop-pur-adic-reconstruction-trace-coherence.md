In the situation of [thm-pur-adic-reconstruction], the same
transport statement holds for the smooth trace of
[def-pur-smooth-trace] and for the coherence statements of
[thm-pur-composition-coherence] and
[thm-pur-smooth-base-change-coherence].

## Proof

The trace of [def-pur-smooth-trace] is the counit of the realized
adjunction and its transport is
included in the same reconstruction
([lem-weil-pbc-reconstruction-uniform-tower]), as in the trace-formula
blueprint; the coherence statements are the compatible systems of the
finite-level coherence statements of
[thm-pur-composition-coherence] and
[thm-pur-smooth-base-change-coherence].
