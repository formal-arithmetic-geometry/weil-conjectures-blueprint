For $T=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, the governing reference, §11.2, proves that, for $X$ topologically Noetherian, the extensions of scalars $T_{E,F}$ and $T_{E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$ along $\mathcal R_{X,E}\to\mathcal R_{X,F}\to\mathcal R_{X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$, for finite subextensions $E\subseteq F$, restrict to functors $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},E)\to D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ exhibiting $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ as the filtered colimit of the $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},E)$ in the sense of three conditions on ordinary categories: every object is isomorphic to some $T_{E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}(K_E)$; every morphism between such images comes from some finite level; and two morphisms at a finite level with the same image agree at some larger finite level. It also proves that these extensions of scalars commute with $Lf^*_E$, $Lf^*_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$ and with $Rf_{!,E}$, $Rf_{!,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$. It does not treat their compatibility with $Rf^!_E$, $Rf^!_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$, with $\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,E}}$, $\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}}$, or with twists (no twist over $\mathcal R_{X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$ is defined), and it does not identify the preimage $K_E$ of a finite-Tor object as finite-Tor. Transporting Lemma [pur9-tr-lem-e] to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ would need these compatibilities as additional inputs, together with the independence of the result from the chosen level $E$. They are not formulated here, and no $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-purity statement is asserted.
