By Theorem [pur9-tr-thm-oe], $\gamma_{f,K_0,\mathcal O_E}$ is an isomorphism. Apply the functor $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}-$ to it. Its source becomes $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}(Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E}K_0\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,\mathcal O_E}}\mathcal R_{X,\mathcal O_E}(d)[2d])$, which is isomorphic to $Lf^*_E(E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}K_0)\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,E}}\mathcal R_{X,E}(d)[2d]$ by the tensor and twist clauses of Input [pur9-tr-input-rational] (applied $d$ times to the tensor power, and commuting with the shift, $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}-$ being a triangulated functor) and by Input [pur9-tr-input-rational-pull]. Its target becomes $Rf^!_E(E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}K_0)$ by the exceptional clause of Input [pur9-tr-input-rational]. Finally $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}K_0\cong K$.
