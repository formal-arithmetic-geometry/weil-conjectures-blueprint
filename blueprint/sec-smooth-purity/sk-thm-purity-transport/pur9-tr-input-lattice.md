Every object of $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},E)$ is isomorphic to $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}K_0$ for some $K_0\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$. *Status:* not stated in the governing reference; the localization property of Input [pur713-input-rationalization] gives essential surjectivity of $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}-$ on the ambient categories, but not that a finite-Tor constructible $K$ admits a finite-Tor constructible lattice $K_0$. Not established.
