Assume Inputs [pur9-tr-input-twist], [pur9-tr-input-reduction] and [pur9-tr-input-map], and for each $n\geq1$ the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-sp-lem-purity-map]. Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth, separated, of pure relative dimension $d$, and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ such that the source and the target of $\gamma_{f,K,\mathcal O_E}$ lie in $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$. Then $\gamma_{f,K,\mathcal O_E}$ is an isomorphism.
