The purity isomorphisms of
[thm-pur-rational-and-qbar-purity] commute with the smooth trace of
[def-pur-smooth-trace] and with the composition and
base-change coherence.

## Proof

The smooth trace of [def-pur-smooth-trace] and the composition and
base-change coherence statements over $E$ and $\Qlb$ – those of
[thm-pur-composition-coherence] and
[thm-pur-smooth-base-change-coherence] – are the $E$-
and $\Qlb$-reductions of the corresponding $\mathcal O_E$-level
statements of [thm-pur-adic-reconstruction] and [prop-pur-adic-reconstruction-trace-coherence], transported along
the same exact functors and restricted to the direct summands (the
trace of a summand is the summand of the trace, by naturality).
