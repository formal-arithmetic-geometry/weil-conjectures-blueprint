For smooth separated $f:X\to Y$ of pure relative dimension $d$ and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$, a morphism

$$
\gamma_{f,K,\mathcal O_E}:Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E}K\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,\mathcal O_E}}\mathcal R_{X,\mathcal O_E}(d)[2d]\longrightarrow Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E}K
$$

in $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ is specified, whose image under $\mathrm{red}_{X,n}$ is, after the isomorphisms of Input [pur9-tr-input-reduction], the map $\gamma^{\mathrm{sm}}_{f,\mathrm{red}_{Y,n}K}$ of Definition [pur9-sp-def-purity-map], for every $n\geq1$. The source lies in $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ by Inputs [pur713-input-cons-preservation-module] and [pur9-tr-input-twist]. *Status:* not constructed here; the earlier sketch obtained it from a tower of finite maps, which presupposes a reconstruction theorem not available in these categories.
