For $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, a module-object admissibility datum for $f$ consists of the adjunction of Definition [pur456-def-adjoint-datum-module], bounds as in Definition [pur456-def-amplitude-module], and the preservation property of Definition [pur456-def-preservation-module]. Supplying it is an additional hypothesis.
