In the situation of [thm-pur-adic-reconstruction], the realization
of the normalized system via the uniform-tower reconstruction of
[lem-weil-pbc-reconstruction-uniform-tower] gives the corresponding
isomorphism for the
derived-complete integral
coefficients.

## Proof

The reconstruction of [lem-weil-pbc-reconstruction-uniform-tower] is
applied to the normalized def:weil-pbc-adic-coefficient-datumadic
datum of [thm-pur-adic-reconstruction], which is a normalized
datum by
the levelwise compatibility established there.
