For every $X$: the functor $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}-$ carries $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ into $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},E)$; for every $f:X\to Y$ there is a natural isomorphism $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E}\cong Rf^!_E(E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}-)$ of functors on $D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$; for $A,B\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ there are natural isomorphisms $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}(A\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,\mathcal O_E}}B)\cong(E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}A)\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,E}}(E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}B)$; and $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}\mathcal R_{X,\mathcal O_E}(1)\cong\mathcal R_{X,E}(1)$ for the twists of Input [pur9-tr-input-twist]. *Status:* the governing reference, §11.1, does not treat compatibility of extension of scalars with $\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}$, $R\mathcal Hom_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}$ or $Rf^!_T$; not established.
