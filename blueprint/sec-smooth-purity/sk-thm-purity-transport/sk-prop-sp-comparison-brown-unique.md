In the situation of [det-sp-comparison-brown-explicit], let $f^!$ be the right
adjoint of $Rf_!$ on the localizing hulls $\Loc(\Dctf(-,\Lambda))$ constructed by
Brown representability, restricted to $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. Then
this restriction is a right adjoint of
$Rf_{!,\Lambda}:\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ with counit the restricted
counit, the functor $Rf_{!,\Lambda}$ being the same in both adjunctions, and
there is a unique isomorphism $f^*_\Lambda(-)(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!$ of
functors on $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ compatible with the counits.
