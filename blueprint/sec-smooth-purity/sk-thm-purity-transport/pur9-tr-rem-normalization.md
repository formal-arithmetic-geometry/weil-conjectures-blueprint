The earlier claims that the counit of $Ra_{X,!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Ra^!_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, for the structure morphism $a_X:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, is a normalized integration map, and that the purity maps are compatible with the reductions $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n\to\mathcal O_E/\varpi^m$, are not proved here: the first needs a trace map for the abstractly chosen proper adjoint of Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint], and the second needs a compatibility of the chosen proper adjoints at the two levels.
