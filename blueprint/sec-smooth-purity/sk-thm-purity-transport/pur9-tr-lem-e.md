Under the hypotheses of Theorem [pur9-tr-thm-oe] and Inputs [pur9-tr-input-rational-pull], [pur9-tr-input-rational] and [pur9-tr-input-lattice], let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth, separated, of pure relative dimension $d$, and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},E)$. Choose $K_0$ as in Input [pur9-tr-input-lattice] and assume that the source and target of $\gamma_{f,K_0,\mathcal O_E}$ lie in $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$. Then there is an isomorphism

$$
Lf^*_EK\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,E}}\mathcal R_{X,E}(d)[2d]\cong Rf^!_EK
$$

in $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},E)$.
