Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $d$, with a
compactification $f=\bar f\circ j$, and $\Lambda$ admissible: a finite ring $A$
killed by a power of $\ell$, $\mathcal O_E$, $E$ or $\Qlb$ ($E/\Ql$ finite). Then
the functor $K\mapsto f^*_\Lambda K(d)[2d]$, $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$,
is right adjoint to $Rf_{!,\Lambda}=R\bar f_*\circ j_!:\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$,
with an explicit unit and counit normalized by integration on the affine line.
