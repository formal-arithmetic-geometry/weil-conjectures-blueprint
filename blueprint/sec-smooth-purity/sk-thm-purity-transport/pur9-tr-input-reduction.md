For every $n\geq1$ and every separated $k$-scheme of finite type $X$, a functor $\mathrm{red}_{X,n}:D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)\to D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ is specified, together with: the property that $\mathrm{red}_{X,n}$ carries $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ into $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$; for every $f:X\to Y$, natural isomorphisms $\mathrm{red}_{X,n}Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E}\cong Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathrm{red}_{Y,n}$ and $\mathrm{red}_{X,n}Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E}\cong Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathrm{red}_{Y,n}$ of functors on $D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$; natural isomorphisms $\mathrm{red}_{X,n}(A\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,\mathcal O_E}}B)\cong\mathrm{red}_{X,n}A\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathrm{red}_{X,n}B$ for $A,B\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$; and an isomorphism $\mathrm{red}_{X,n}\mathcal R_{X,\mathcal O_E}(1)\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(1)$ with the twist of Definition [pur9-def-tate-twist]. In addition: a morphism of $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ is an isomorphism if its image under $\mathrm{red}_{X,n}$ is an isomorphism for every $n\geq1$. *Status:* the governing reference lists the comparison of the module-object coefficients with finite coefficients by reduction modulo $\varpi^n$ among its open directions (§12); not established.
