Under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-cp-lem-coprojection] and Input [pur9-cs-input-site-monoidal], for every integer $m$ and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ there is an isomorphism $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(m)[2m])\cong Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z(m)[2m]$.
