The first and fourth bijections are the standing adjunction for $i$. The second uses that $-\otimes L'$ is an equivalence with quasi-inverse $-\otimes L$, by $e$ and the associativity, symmetry and unit isomorphisms of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`). The third is precomposition with $\operatorname{PF}_{i,A,L'}$ of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection], invertible by Lemma [pur9-lem-proet-projection-formula] since $A\in D^b_c(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $L'\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. The fifth uses that $Li^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ is invertible with inverse $Li^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L'$, by Lemma [pur713-lem-monoidal-pullback] applied to $e$. All bijections are natural in $A$ (Lemma [pur713-lem-projection-naturality] for the third). Both $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(K\otimes L)$ and $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K\otimes Li^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ lie in the full subcategory $D^b_c(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, by Inputs [pur9-input-preservation] and [pur9-cs-input-constructible-closure]; the Yoneda lemma in this full subcategory gives the unique isomorphism $\chi_{i,K,L}$ representing the composite bijection.
