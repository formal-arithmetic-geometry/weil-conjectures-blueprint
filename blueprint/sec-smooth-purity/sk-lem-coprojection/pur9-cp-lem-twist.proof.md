By Definition [pur9-def-tate-twist], $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(m)$ is $L\nu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of a sheaf on $X_{\Et}$ that is lisse of rank one, with inverse the twist by $-m$; such a sheaf is flat with constructible cohomology, so the twist and its inverse lie in $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and are inverse to one another by Input [pur9-cs-input-site-monoidal]. The shift is compatible with $\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$. Apply Lemma [pur9-cp-lem-coprojection], and identify $Li^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the twist with the twist on $Z$ by Definition [pur9-def-tate-twist].
