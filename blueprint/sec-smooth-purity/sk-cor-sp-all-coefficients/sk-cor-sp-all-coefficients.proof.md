Apply smooth purity with coefficients $\Lambda$ ([sk-thm-purity-transport]) to
$K=\Lambda_{\operatorname{Spec}k}$: since $a^*_\Lambda\Lambda_{\operatorname{Spec}k}=\Lambda_X$,
$\gamma_{a,\Lambda}(\Lambda_{\operatorname{Spec}k})$ is the stated isomorphism.

*Remark.* Over algebraically closed $k$, taking global sections turns the counit into a
map $R\Gamma_c(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda_X(d))[2d]\to\Lambda$ in $D^b(\Lambda)$,
normalized by finite-level integration, and in degree zero into
$H_c^{2d}(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda_X(d))\to\Lambda$; for $X=\mathbf P^1_k$ it sends
$c_1(\mathcal O(1))$ to $1$.
