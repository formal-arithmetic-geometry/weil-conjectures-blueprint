Apply [sk-thm-purity-transport] to $f=a_X$ and $K=\Lambda_{\Spec k}$. Since
$a_X^*\Lambda_{\Spec k}=\Lambda_X$, the purity isomorphism
$\gamma_{a_X,\Lambda}(\Lambda_{\Spec k})$ identifies $\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$ with
$\omega_{X,\Lambda}$. By definition of the smooth trace ([sk-def-sp-trace]), the
counit $\varepsilon_{a_X}(\Lambda)$ composed with $Ra_{X!}$ of this identification
is $\operatorname{Tr}_{a_X}(\Lambda_{\Spec k})$. Its description through the
finite traces is the final assertion of [det-sp-purity-coefficient-change],
whose items (1)–(3) treat reduction, inversion of $\ell$ and extension of
scalars, applied to the finite traces of
[cor-pur-finite-stage-smooth-dualizing-complex].
