Let $a:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be smooth of pure dimension $d$, and $\Lambda$ any
admissible coefficient ring. There is a natural isomorphism
$a_\Lambda^!\Lambda_{\operatorname{Spec}k}\simeq\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$ in $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
