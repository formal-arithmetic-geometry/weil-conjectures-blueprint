Applying Proposition [sk-prop-sp-unique-adjoint] once more and inverting, we get
$\gamma_{f,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}:f_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}^*(-)(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$, whose counit is
obtained by extension of scalars from a finite extension of $\Ql$. This proves
Theorem [sk-thm-purity-transport] up to the compatibilities with change of coefficients, base
change and composition, and the trace normalization, which we prove next.
