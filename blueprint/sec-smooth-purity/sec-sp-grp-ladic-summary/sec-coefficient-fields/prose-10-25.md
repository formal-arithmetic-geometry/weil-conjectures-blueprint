With coefficients in $E$ there is no tower to pass to the limit over, but none is
needed. An object of $\Dbc(T,E)$ comes from an integral complex by inverting $\ell$, and its Hom
groups are the integral Hom groups tensored with $E$ (10.20). An
adjunction over $\mathcal O_E$ is a natural bijection of Hom groups, and tensoring it
with $E$ gives an adjunction over $E$ (Proposition [sk-prop-sp-rational]). For $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$
the Hom groups are colimits over finite extensions of $\Ql$, and the adjunctions over
these fields pass to the colimit (Theorem [sk-thm-sp-qbar]). For each coefficient
ring we thus construct an explicit right adjoint of $Rf_!$, and we identify it with $f^!$
by the uniqueness of adjoints, which we record first.
