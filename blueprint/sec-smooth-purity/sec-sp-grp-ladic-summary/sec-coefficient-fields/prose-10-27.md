Proposition [sk-prop-sp-unique-adjoint] now gives an isomorphism
$f^!_E\xrightarrow{\sim}f_E^*(-)(d)[2d]$ compatible with the counits, and we let
$\gamma_{f,E}:f_E^*(-)(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_E$ be its inverse. For
a finite extension $E\subset F$, extension of scalars along the finite free map
$\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_F$ commutes with $R\varprojlim$ and is, at level $n$, extension
along $\mathcal O_E/\ell^n\to\mathcal O_F/\ell^n$. We compare the counits in
$D(\operatorname{Tow})$. Extending scalars in the pair of towers $(f_\bullet^!K_\bullet,\varepsilon)$
for $\mathcal O_E$ gives a pair over $\mathcal O_F$ whose levels represent
$\operatorname{Hom}(Rf_!(-),K_n)$ by Lemma [sk-lem-sp-reduction], so by the five lemma,
levelwise, it represents $\operatorname{Hom}(Rf_{!,\bullet}(-),K_\bullet)$. By uniqueness it is
isomorphic to the corresponding pair for $\mathcal O_F$ by a unique counit-preserving isomorphism,
and applying $R\varprojlim$ and inverting $\ell$, the adjunctions over $E$ and over $F$
correspond under extension of scalars, transitively in $E\subset F\subset F'$.
