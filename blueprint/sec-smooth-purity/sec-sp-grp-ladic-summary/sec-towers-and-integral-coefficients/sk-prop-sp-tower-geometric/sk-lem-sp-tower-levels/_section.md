For $g:T\to S$, define
$g_\bullet^*:\operatorname{Tow}(S,\mathcal O_E)\to\operatorname{Tow}(T,\mathcal O_E)$ and
$g_{*,\bullet}:\operatorname{Tow}(T,\mathcal O_E)\to\operatorname{Tow}(S,\mathcal O_E)$
levelwise ([sk-def-sp-coefficient-towers]); the first is exact, and
$Rg_{*,\bullet}:D^+(\operatorname{Tow}(T,\mathcal O_E))\to D^+(\operatorname{Tow}(S,\mathcal O_E))$
is the derived functor of the second. Then for
$C_\bullet\in D^+(\operatorname{Tow}(T,\mathcal O_E))$,

$$
\bigl(Rg_{*,\bullet}C_\bullet\bigr)_n\simeq Rg_{*,\mathcal O_E/\ell^n}C_n
\quad\text{in }D^+(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^n).
$$
