For a listed map $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ and $K,M\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$, the natural maps
$(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}M)_{\Lambda'}\to K_{\Lambda'}\otimes^{\mathbf L}M_{\Lambda'}$ and
$v_{K,M}\colon R\mathcal Hom_\Lambda(K,M)_{\Lambda'}\to R\mathcal Hom_{\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'},M_{\Lambda'})$ (defined by tensoring
evaluation with $\Lambda'$ and transposing) are isomorphisms. (For finite rings this is [thm-coef-finite-hom].)
