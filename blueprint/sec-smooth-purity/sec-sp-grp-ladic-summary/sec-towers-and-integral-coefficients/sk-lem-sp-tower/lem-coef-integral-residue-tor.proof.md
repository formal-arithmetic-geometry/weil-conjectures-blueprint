Associativity of coefficient tensor product gives
$K_n\otimes^L_{A_n}\kappa=K_1$.
Each $K_n$ is bounded constructible by
[det-prop-integral-criterion-reductions]. The residue Tor test
[lem-exc-residue-tor-test], applied to the local Artinian
ring $A_n$, therefore gives the same Tor interval $[a,b]$
at every $n$. This is the integral finite-Tor condition of
[def-weil-finite-tor-amplitude], uniform Tor amplitude of all reductions;
the reverse inclusion is part of that definition. For the Tor amplitude over
$\mathcal O_{E,X}$ itself ([sk-def-sfb-constructible]), the equality
$D^b_c(X,R)=D^b_{ctf}(X,R)$ is recorded in [sk-thm-existence].
