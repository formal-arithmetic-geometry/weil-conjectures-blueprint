For the support-localization triangle,
*canonicality* means independence of the chosen injective resolution
under the derived comparison maps. *Functoriality* means that a
morphism of input complexes induces the specified morphism of triangles,
compatible with composition in the derived category. *Etale
base-change compatibility* means that, for an
etale map $v:X'\to X$,
pullback identifies this triangle with the triangle for
$Z'=Z\times_XX'$, $U'=U\times_XX'$ and $v^*K$.
