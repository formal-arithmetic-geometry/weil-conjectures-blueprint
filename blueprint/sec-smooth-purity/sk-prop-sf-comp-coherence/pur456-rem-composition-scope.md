A composition law for $Rf_{!,T}$ in $D(X_{\Proet},T)$, $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, would require an open–proper comparison for the module-object functors of Definitions [pur713-def-derived-push-module] and [pur713-def-open-shriek-module], its identity and pasting equations, and the comparisons of Input [pur713-input-comparison-module]. None of these follows from the finite-coefficient law of Lemma “The composition law for $Rf_!$ at finite coefficients” (`pur456-lem-proet-composition`) by replacing the coefficient subscript, and none is established here.
