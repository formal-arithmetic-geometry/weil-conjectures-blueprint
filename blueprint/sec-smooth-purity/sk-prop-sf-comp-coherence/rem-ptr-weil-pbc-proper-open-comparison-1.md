In the notation of [def-weil-pbc-proper-open-comparison], the following holds. The map $p$,
the section $s$, and the position of $X$ inside
$V=u^{-1}(j_2(X))$ are described in
[lem-weil-pbc-proper-open-section], and the specified isomorphism

$$
\theta_{u,K}:j_2^{-1}Ru_*j_{1!}K\xrightarrow{\sim}K,
$$

built from the restriction of
extension by zero along
$s$ and the derived direct
image along $p$, is the one of
[lem-weil-pbc-proper-open-identification].
