The comparisons $\theta^{\Proet}_{d,c_f,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “Pro-étale compactification comparison” (`pur456-def-proet-independent`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) are defined only on the étale-origin subcategory $\mathscr E^+(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, so they do not satisfy the hypothesis of Lemma [pur456-lem-adjoint-comparison-finite], which needs a comparison on all of $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$; that hypothesis is Input [pur713-input-comparison]. At the stages $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ no comparison is proved at all. A reduction tower, a rationalization or a colimit over coefficient fields gives no comparison in the categories $D(X_{\Proet},T)$ of Definition [pur713-def-module-category] without a theorem comparing those categories and their functors; the governing reference lists the comparison of module-object coefficients with finite coefficients via reduction modulo $\ell^n$ among its open directions. Input [pur713-input-comparison-module] therefore remains explicit wherever it is used.
