Let $d$ be a compactification of $f:X\to Y$, and assume Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint], so that $R^!_d$, $Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition [pur713-def-upper-shriek] and the adjunctions of Lemma [pur713-lem-shriek-adjunction] are given, with units $\eta_d,\eta_f$ and counits $\varepsilon_d,\varepsilon_f$. Suppose given a natural isomorphism $\theta_{d,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:R_d\to Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ on the whole category $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. Then there is a unique natural transformation $\theta^!_{d,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\to R^!_d$ on $D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ satisfying the equation of Lemma [pur456-lem-adjoint-comparison] with $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$ in place of $T$; it is an isomorphism, with component $R^!_d(\varepsilon_{f,L})\circ R^!_d(\theta_{d,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L})\circ\eta_{d,Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L}$ at $L$.
