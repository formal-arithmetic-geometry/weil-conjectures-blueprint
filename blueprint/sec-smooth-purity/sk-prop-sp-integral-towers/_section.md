With integral coefficients, finite Tor amplitude is automatic, and membership in
$\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$ can be tested modulo $\ell$.

1. **(1)** Every $K\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$ has finite Tor amplitude, and its reductions
   $K/\ell^n$ lie in $\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^n)$, with bounds independent of
   $n$.
2. **(2)** Conversely, if $K$ is derived complete and $K/\ell$ lies in
   $\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell)$, then $K\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$.
3. **(3)** Inverse image preserves $\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E)$ and needs no completion.
