Part “Duality and the trace formula” proves Weil's axioms (a)–(c) for $\ell$-adic
cohomology and deduces rationality and the functional equation. Duality
needs an explicit description of $f^!$ for smooth $f$, and smooth purity
$f^!\simeq f^*(d)[2d]$ provides it; the twist, the shift and the trace
of Poincaré duality all come from this isomorphism. Duality in turn gives
the Künneth formula for Verdier duals, from which the trace class of a
correspondence is built, and the trace formula then reduces to a
calculation at a fixed point of Frobenius on a curve. Frobenius and Weil
complexes, with the compatibility of smooth purity and duality with Frobenius, are
introduced at the start of Section “Frobenius, trace classes and the Lefschetz–Verdier formula”, where they
are first needed.
