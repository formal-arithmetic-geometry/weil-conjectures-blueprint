Assume, in addition to the hypotheses of this subsection, Input [pur9-cs-input-bc-composition]. Let $v:Y'\to Y$ be a morphism of separated $k$-schemes of finite type, with base change $f':X'\to Y'$ of $f$ and projection $u:X'\to X$, and let $\beta_v:Lu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_f\xrightarrow{\sim}\omega_{f'}$ be the isomorphism of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-bc-dualizing]. Then

$$
\Pi_{f'}\circ\beta_v=\tau_u\circ Lu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\Pi_f)
$$

as isomorphisms $Lu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_f\to(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{X'}(d)[2d]$ in $D(X'_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, where $\Pi$ is the composite of Theorem [pur9-thm-smooth-purity] and $\tau_u:Lu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(d)[2d]\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{X'}(d)[2d]$ is the inverse-image isomorphism of twists of Definition [pur9-def-tate-twist].
