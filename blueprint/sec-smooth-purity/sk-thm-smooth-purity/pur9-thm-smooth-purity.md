There is an isomorphism $\omega_f\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(d)[2d]$ in $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, namely the composite $\Pi_f$ of

$$
\omega_f\cong L\Delta^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lp^*_{2,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_f\cong L\Delta^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_{p_1}=\mathcal C(p_1,\Delta)\xrightarrow{\delta_{p_1,\Delta}}\mathcal C(\pi_T,0_T)=L(0_T)^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_{\pi_T}\cong L(0_T)^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{T}(d)[2d]\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(d)[2d].
$$
