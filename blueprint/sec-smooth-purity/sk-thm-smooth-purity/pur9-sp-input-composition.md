For smooth separated $f:X\to Y$, $g:Y\to S$ of pure relative dimensions $d,e$, the isomorphism $\Pi_{gf}$ of Theorem [pur9-thm-smooth-purity] equals the composite of the chain-rule isomorphism of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-chain-rule], $Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\Pi_g\otimes\Pi_f$, and the twist-additivity isomorphism $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(e)[2e]\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(d)[2d]\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(d+e)[2(d+e)]$ of Definition [pur9-def-tate-twist]. *Status:* the earlier sketch asserted this by naming its constituents; no proof is given in this manuscript.
