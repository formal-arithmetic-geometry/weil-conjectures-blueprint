The identity adjunction and the unit comparisons of the monoidal structure of Lemma [pur713-lem-monoidal-pullback] identify the adjoint defining $P$ with the identity. The map $\sigma$ of Definition [pur713-def-open-projection] is likewise the identity for the identity open immersion, by the triangle identity of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-lem-open-adjunction`), and $\mu_{\mathrm{id},\mathrm{id}}$ is the identity comparison by Lemma “Identity equations for the finite-coefficient inverse-image comparison” (`pur713-lem-pull-composition-unit`). Their composite defining $\operatorname{PF}$ is therefore the identity. All the functors are additive and send the zero object to zero, so the calculation includes the zero and empty cases.
