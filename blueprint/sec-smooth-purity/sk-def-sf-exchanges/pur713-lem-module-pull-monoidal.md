Under Input [pur713-input-coefficient-structure], for $f:X\to Y$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ the functor $f^*_T$ of Definition [pur713-def-module-pull] is strong symmetric monoidal for the relative tensor products of Definition [pur713-def-relative-tensor]: there are natural isomorphisms $f^*_T(M\otimes_{\mathcal R_{Y,T}}N)\cong f^*_TM\otimes_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}f^*_TN$ and $f^*_T\mathcal R_{Y,T}\cong\mathcal R_{X,T}$, satisfying the associativity, symmetry and unit coherence equations of a strong symmetric monoidal functor.
