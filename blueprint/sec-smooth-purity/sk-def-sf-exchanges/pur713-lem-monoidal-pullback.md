Under Input “Fixed-coefficient inverse image preserves K-flat complexes” (`pur713-input-kflat-fixed`), for $f:X\to Y$ the functor $Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\to D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ carries a strong symmetric monoidal structure for the derived tensor products of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`): there are natural isomorphisms

$$
Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(M\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}N)
\xrightarrow{\sim}Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}N,
\qquad
Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X,
$$

satisfying the associativity, symmetry and unit coherence equations.
