Under the inputs of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection], for $f=\mathrm{id}_X$, whose chosen compactification is $(\mathrm{id}_X,\mathrm{id}_X)$, the transformations $P_{\mathrm{id}_X,K,L,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and $\operatorname{PF}_{\mathrm{id}_X,K,L,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ are the identity of $K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ after the identity comparisons $\iota_X$ and $R\mathrm{id}_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm{id}$, for all $K,L\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, including zero objects and the empty scheme.
