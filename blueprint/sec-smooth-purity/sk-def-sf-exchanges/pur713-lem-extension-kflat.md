For a morphism $\phi:\mathcal R\to\mathcal S$ of commutative ring objects of $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$, the functor $\operatorname{Ext}_\phi$ of Definition [pur713-def-extension], applied termwise, sends K-flat complexes of $\mathcal R$-modules to K-flat complexes of $\mathcal S$-modules in the sense of Definition [pur713-def-kflat-module].
