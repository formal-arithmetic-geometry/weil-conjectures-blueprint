When $g=\mathrm{id}_Y$, so that $X'=X$, $g'=\mathrm{id}_X$, $\bar g=\mathrm{id}_{\overline X}$ and $c'=c_f$, the exchanges $\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition [pur713-def-star-exchange] and $\operatorname{Ex}_{!,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-exchange] are identity transformations after the identity comparisons $\iota$ of Definition “Composition comparison for finite-coefficient derived inverse image” (`pur713-def-pull-composition`).
