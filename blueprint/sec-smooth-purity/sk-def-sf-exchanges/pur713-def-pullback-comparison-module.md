Under the inputs of Lemma [pur713-lem-pull-composition-module], for the Cartesian square $\square$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, define

$$
c_{\square,T}:=\mu_{f,g',T}^{-1}\circ\mu_{g,f',T}:Lf'^*_TLg^*_T\xrightarrow{\sim}Lg'^*_TLf^*_T,
$$

a natural isomorphism of functors $D(Y_{\Proet},T)\to D(X'_{\Proet},T)$, with $\mu$ of Lemma [pur713-lem-pull-composition-module] and $L(gf')^*_T=L(fg')^*_T$.
