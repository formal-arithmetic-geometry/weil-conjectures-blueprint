The transport $(f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z})_{\mathcal R_{Y,T}}$ is strong symmetric monoidal for relative tensor products, because $f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}$ is strong symmetric monoidal (Input “Monoidality of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-monoidal`)) and preserves coequalizers, being a left adjoint (Input “Adjunction and exactness of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-adjunction`)). Extension of scalars along a morphism of commutative ring objects is strong symmetric monoidal by the coequalizer universal properties: $\mathcal S\otimes_{\mathcal R}(M\otimes_{\mathcal R}N)\cong(\mathcal S\otimes_{\mathcal R}M)\otimes_{\mathcal S}(\mathcal S\otimes_{\mathcal R}N)$ and $\mathcal S\otimes_{\mathcal R}\mathcal R\cong\mathcal S$. Composing gives the assertion.
