Under Inputs [pur713-input-coefficient-structure], [pur713-input-coefficient-composition] and [pur713-input-kflat-transport], for $v:W\to Z$, $u:Z\to X$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, there is a unique natural isomorphism

$$
\mu_{u,v,T}:Lv^*_TLu^*_T\xrightarrow{\sim}L(uv)^*_T
$$

of functors $D(X_{\Proet},T)\to D(W_{\Proet},T)$ whose value at a K-flat complex $P$ of $\mathcal R_{X,T}$-modules is the termwise map $\sigma_{u,v,T,P}$ of Definition [pur713-def-pull-composition-module-underived], the derived functors being computed on $P$ and on the K-flat complex $u^*_TP$.
