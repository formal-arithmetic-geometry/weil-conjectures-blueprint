Under the inputs of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection-module] and Lemma [pur713-lem-pull-composition-module-unit], for $f=\mathrm{id}_X$, whose chosen compactification is $(\mathrm{id}_X,\mathrm{id}_X)$, the transformations $P_{\mathrm{id}_X,K,L,T}$ and $\operatorname{PF}_{\mathrm{id}_X,K,L,T}$ are the identity of $K\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}L$ after the identity comparisons, for all $K,L\in D(X_{\Proet},T)$.
