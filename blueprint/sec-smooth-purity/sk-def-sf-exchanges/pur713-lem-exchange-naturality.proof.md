The comparison $c_{\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition [pur713-def-pullback-comparison] and the counit $\varepsilon_f$ are natural transformations, so the composite whose adjoint defines the component is natural in $K$. The adjunction bijection of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived inverse-image adjunction” (`pur713-lem-star-adjunction`) for $f'$ is natural in both variables, so taking adjoints preserves the displayed equation.
