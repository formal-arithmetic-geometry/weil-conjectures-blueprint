Under the inputs of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection-module], for arbitrary $f$, and $K\in D(X_{\Proet},T)$, the transformations $P_{f,K,\mathcal R_{Y,T},T}$ and $\operatorname{PF}_{f,K,\mathcal R_{Y,T},T}$ are the identities of $Rf_{*,T}K$, respectively $Rf_{!,T}K$, after the tensor-unit isomorphisms of Definition [pur713-def-tensor-module] and the unit comparison $Lf^*_T\mathcal R_{Y,T}\cong\mathcal R_{X,T}$ of Lemma [pur713-lem-monoidal-pullback-module].
