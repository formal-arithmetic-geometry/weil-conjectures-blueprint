In the notation of [def-exchange-projection], the following holds. For any factorization
$X\xrightarrow{j}\overline X\xrightarrow{\bar f}Y$ with
$\overline X$ a
finite-type $S$-scheme,
$j$ an open immersion, and
$\bar f$
proper, use the
extension-by-zero functor $j_!$
and $R\bar f_*$; refinements are
proper maps over $Y$, and
their comparison maps are the natural isomorphisms of
[prop-compactification-coherence-detailed], which are independent of the
chosen refinement and satisfy the identity and cocycle conditions.
