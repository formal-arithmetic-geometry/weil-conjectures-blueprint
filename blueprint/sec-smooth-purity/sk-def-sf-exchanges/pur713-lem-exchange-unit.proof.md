By Lemma “Identity equations for the finite-coefficient inverse-image comparison” (`pur713-lem-pull-composition-unit`), after the identity comparisons $c_{\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is the identity, and the map whose adjoint defines direct-image exchange is the counit $\varepsilon_{f}$. Its adjoint is the identity by the triangle identity. The open-extension map $b'$ of Definition [pur713-def-open-exchange] is, for $\bar g=\mathrm{id}$, the adjoint of the unit, which is the identity by the triangle identity; so the compact-support composite is the identity.
