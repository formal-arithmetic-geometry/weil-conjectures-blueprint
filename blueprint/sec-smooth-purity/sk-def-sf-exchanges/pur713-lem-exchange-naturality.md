For every morphism $u:K\to K'$ in $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the components of Definition [pur713-def-star-exchange] satisfy
$\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K'}\circ Lg^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(u)=Rf'_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lg'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(u)\circ\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K}$, all functors at the coefficient $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$.
