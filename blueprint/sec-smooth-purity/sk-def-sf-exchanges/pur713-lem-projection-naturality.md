Under Input “Fixed-coefficient inverse image preserves K-flat complexes” (`pur713-input-kflat-fixed`), for all morphisms $u:K\to K'$ in $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $v:L\to L'$ in $D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the maps of Definition [pur713-def-star-projection] satisfy
$P_{f,K',L',\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\circ(Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(u)\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}v)=Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(u\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(v))\circ P_{f,K,L,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, all at the coefficient $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$.
