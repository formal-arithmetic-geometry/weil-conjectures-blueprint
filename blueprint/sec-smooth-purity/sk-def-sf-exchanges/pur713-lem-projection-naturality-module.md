Under the inputs of Definition [pur713-def-star-projection-module], for all morphisms $u:K\to K'$ in $D(X_{\Proet},T)$ and $v:L\to L'$ in $D(Y_{\Proet},T)$,
$P_{f,K',L',T}\circ(Rf_{*,T}(u)\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{Y,T}}v)=Rf_{*,T}(u\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}Lf^*_T(v))\circ P_{f,K,L,T}$.
