The map $b'_{j,\bar g,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition [pur713-def-open-exchange] is a natural isomorphism. Write $b_{j,\bar g,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:L\bar g^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathrm D(j_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm D(j'_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})Lg'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ for its inverse.
