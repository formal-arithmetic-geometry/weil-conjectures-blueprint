First the underived map is an isomorphism. On underlying abelian sheaves, $\bar g^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}j_{!,\mathbf Z}\cong j'_{!,\mathbf Z}g'^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}$ by the argument of Lemma [pur713-lem-open-exchange] with $\mathsf{Ab}$ in place of $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$. Then
$\bar g^*_Tj_{!,T}M=\mathcal R_{\overline X',T}\otimes_{\bar g^{-1}\mathcal R_{\overline X,T}}j'_{!,\mathbf Z}g'^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}M$, and Lemma [pur713-lem-open-projection-relative] for $j'$, the invertibility of $\alpha_{j',T}$ (Input [pur713-input-coefficient-open]) and the composition equation of Input [pur713-input-coefficient-composition] for $jg'=\bar gj'$ identify it with $j'_{!,\mathbf Z}(\mathcal R_{X',T}\otimes_{g'^{-1}\mathcal R_{X,T}}g'^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}M)=j'_{!,T}g'^*_TM$; the identification is the adjoint of the same composite as in Definition [pur713-def-open-exchange-module]. To derive it, let $P\to K$ be a K-flat resolution. Then $j_{!,T}P$ is K-flat: for an acyclic complex $N$ of $\mathcal R_{\overline X,T}$-modules, Lemma [pur713-lem-open-projection-relative] gives $N\otimes j_{!,T}P\cong j_{!,T}(j^*_TN\otimes P)$, acyclic because $j^*_T$ and $j_{!,T}$ are exact (Lemmas [pur713-lem-open-exact-module] and [pur713-lem-open-pull-exact-module]). Hence both $L\bar g^*_T\mathrm D(j_{!,T})K$ and $\mathrm D(j'_{!,T})Lg'^*_TK$ are computed by the underived functors on $P$, using the proof of Lemma [pur713-lem-pull-composition-module] for K-flatness of $g'^*_TP$, and $b'$ is the underived isomorphism on $P$.
