Under the unit comparisons, the map whose adjoint defines $P$ becomes the adjunction counit, whose adjoint is the identity. The open-extension projection map with the tensor unit is the unit isomorphism, by its defining adjoint and the same triangle identity. Thus each factor in Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection] becomes the identity.
