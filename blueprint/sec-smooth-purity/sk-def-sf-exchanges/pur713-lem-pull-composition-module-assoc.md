Under the inputs of Lemma [pur713-lem-pull-composition-module], for $w:V\to W$, $v:W\to Z$ and $u:Z\to X$,
$\mu_{uv,w,T}\circ(Lw^*_T\mu_{u,v,T})=\mu_{u,vw,T}\circ(\mu_{v,w,T}Lu^*_T)$ as natural transformations $Lw^*_TLv^*_TLu^*_T\to L(uvw)^*_T$.
