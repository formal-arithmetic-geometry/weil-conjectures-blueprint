Under the inputs of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection], for $u,v$ as in Lemma [pur713-lem-projection-naturality], the maps of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection] satisfy
$\operatorname{PF}_{f,K',L',\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\circ(Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(u)\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}v)=Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(u\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(v))\circ\operatorname{PF}_{f,K,L,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, all at the coefficient $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$.
