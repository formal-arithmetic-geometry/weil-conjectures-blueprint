Under the inputs of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection], for arbitrary $f$, and $K\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the transformations $P_{f,K,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and $\operatorname{PF}_{f,K,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ are the identities of $Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K$, respectively $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K$, after the tensor-unit isomorphisms of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`) and the unit comparison $Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X$ of Lemma [pur713-lem-monoidal-pullback].
