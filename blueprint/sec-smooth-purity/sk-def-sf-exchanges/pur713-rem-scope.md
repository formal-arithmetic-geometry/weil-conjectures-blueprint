The maps above exist on the stated ambient categories. Invertibility of proper or compact-support exchange and of the proper or compact-support projection map is a separate theorem with a specified range of coefficients and objects. The earlier proper-base-change theorem [sk-thm-properbasechange] concerns torsion étale sheaves; applying it here requires the site comparisons of -prop-sf-comp-coherenceZZ. It does not establish an unrestricted module-object assertion. Likewise, a theorem in a derived-complete or rationalized framework does not supply a comparison in the categories of Definition [pur713-def-module-category] without an explicit comparison functor and compatibility theorem. Proper-adjoint existence, constructible preservation, coefficient comparisons, and Inputs [pur713-input-comparison] and [pur713-input-comparison-module] are explicit boundaries of these constructions. Fixing $c_f$ removes the compactification from the notation; it does not remove the need for Input [pur713-input-comparison] when a base-changed compactification $c'\neq c_{f'}$ appears.
