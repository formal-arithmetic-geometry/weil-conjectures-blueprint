Let $P\to K$ and $Q\to N$ be K-flat resolutions. As in Lemma [pur713-lem-open-projection], the source and target of $\sigma$ are $j_{!,T}(P\otimes_{\mathcal R_{U,T}}j^*_TQ)$ and $j_{!,T}P\otimes_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}Q$, using Lemmas [pur713-lem-open-exact-module] and [pur713-lem-open-pull-levelwise], and $\sigma$ is computed termwise by the map of Lemma [pur713-lem-open-projection-relative], up to symmetry. That map is an isomorphism in each degree.
