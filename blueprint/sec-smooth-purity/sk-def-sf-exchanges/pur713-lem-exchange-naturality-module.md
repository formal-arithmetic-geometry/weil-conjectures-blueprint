Under the inputs of Definition [pur713-def-star-exchange-module], for every morphism $u:K\to K'$ in $D(X_{\Proet},T)$,
$\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,T,K'}\circ Lg^*_TRf_{*,T}(u)=Rf'_{*,T}Lg'^*_T(u)\circ\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,T,K}$.
