For every morphism $u:K\to K'$ in $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the components of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-exchange] satisfy
$\operatorname{Ex}_{!,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K'}\circ Lg^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(u)=R_{c'}Lg'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(u)\circ\operatorname{Ex}_{!,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K}$, all functors at the coefficient $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$.
