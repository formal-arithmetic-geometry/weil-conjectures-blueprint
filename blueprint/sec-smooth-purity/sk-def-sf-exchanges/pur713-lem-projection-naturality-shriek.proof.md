The first factor of the defining composite is natural by Lemma [pur713-lem-projection-naturality], applied to $\mathrm D(j_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})(u)$ and $v$. The inverse $\sigma^{-1}$ is natural because $\sigma$ is (its defining adjoint is built from natural maps), and $\mu_{p,j,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is natural. A composite of natural transformations is natural.
