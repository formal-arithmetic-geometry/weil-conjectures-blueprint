For a complex $Q$ of $\mathcal R$-modules and a complex $N$ of $\mathcal S$-modules, associativity of relative tensor products gives $(\mathcal S\otimes_{\mathcal R}Q)\otimes_{\mathcal S}N\cong Q\otimes_{\mathcal R}\operatorname{Res}_\phi N$. If $N$ is acyclic, so is $\operatorname{Res}_\phi N$ by Lemma [pur713-lem-restriction-exact], and the right side is acyclic when $Q$ is K-flat.
