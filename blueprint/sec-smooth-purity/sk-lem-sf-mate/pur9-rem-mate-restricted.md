The source node also stated a composition isomorphism for the transported functors $f^{\mathrm{tr}}$ of Definition [pur9-def-transported-adjoint], obtained from étale mate calculus and full faithfulness of $L\nu^*_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ on bounded-below objects. Those functors are not the exceptional functors of this manuscript; the composition isomorphism used below is Lemma [pur9-lem-upper-shriek-composition], conditional on Input [pur9-input-ambient-composition]. On objects where Lemma [pur9-lem-transported-adjoint-comparison] applies, a comparison of the two composition isomorphisms would require an additional compatibility of $\phi$ with composition, which is not established here.
