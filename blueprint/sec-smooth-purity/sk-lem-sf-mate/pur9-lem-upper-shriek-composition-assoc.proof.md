Both sides are maps into $Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rg^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rh^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$; compare their adjoints under the composite adjunction $Rh_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rg_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rg^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rh^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$. The adjoint of $c_{hg,f,L}$ under $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is a map $\phi:Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}R(hgf)^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L\to R(hg)^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ whose adjoint under the standing adjunction for $hg$ is $\psi=\varepsilon_{hgf}\circ\alpha_{hg,f}$ (Definition [pur9-def-upper-shriek-composition]). The adjoint of $c_{h,g}\circ\phi$ under $Rh_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rg_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rg^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rh^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is $(\varepsilon_{hg}\circ\alpha_{h,g})\circ Rh_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rg_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\phi=\varepsilon_{hg}\circ R(hg)_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\phi\circ\alpha_{h,g}=\psi\circ\alpha_{h,g}$, using naturality of $\alpha_{h,g}$. Hence the left side is adjoint to $\varepsilon_{hgf}\circ\alpha_{hg,f}\circ(\alpha_{h,g}Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, evaluated at $R(hgf)^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$. The same computation, with the roles of the factorizations exchanged, shows that the right side is adjoint to $\varepsilon_{hgf}\circ\alpha_{h,gf}\circ(Rh_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\alpha_{g,f})$. These agree by the associativity equation of Input [pur9-input-ambient-composition], and the adjunction bijection gives the equality.
