By Lemma [pur9-lem-open-immersion-upper-shriek], under these identifications and that of Lemma [pur9-lem-open-immersion-lower-shriek], the standing adjunction for an identity becomes the identity adjunction, so the composite adjunction for $(\mathrm{id}_Y,f)$ becomes $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$. By Input [pur9-input-ambient-composition], $\alpha_{\mathrm{id}_Y,f}$ becomes the identity, so $c_{\mathrm{id}_Y,f,L}$ corresponds to the counit $\varepsilon_{f,L}$, whose adjoint is $\mathrm{id}_{Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L}$ by a triangle identity. The case $c_{f,\mathrm{id}_X}$ is the same with $\alpha_{f,\mathrm{id}_X}$.
