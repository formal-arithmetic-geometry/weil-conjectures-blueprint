For composable $f:X\to Y$, $g:Y\to S$, $h:S\to Z$ as in Definition [pur9-def-standing-choices],

$$
(Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}c_{h,g})\circ c_{hg,f}=(c_{g,f}Rh^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\circ c_{h,gf}
$$

as natural transformations $R(hgf)^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\to Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rg^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rh^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of functors $D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\to D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
