For the Cartesian square $\square$, define

$$
c_{\square,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}:=\mu_{f,g',\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}^{-1}\circ\mu_{g,f',\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}:
Lf'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}Lg^*_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}\xrightarrow{\sim}Lg'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n},
$$

a natural isomorphism of functors $D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)\to D(X'_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, using $L(gf')^*_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}=L(fg')^*_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}$ since $gf'=fg'$, and $\mu$ of Definition “Composition comparison for finite-coefficient derived inverse image” (`pur713-def-pull-composition`), invertible by Lemma “The finite-coefficient inverse-image composition comparison is invertible” (`pur713-lem-pull-composition`).
