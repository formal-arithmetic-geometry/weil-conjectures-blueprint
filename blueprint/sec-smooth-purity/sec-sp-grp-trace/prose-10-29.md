The isomorphism $\gamma_f$ now exists for every admissible coefficient ring, but an
isomorphism $f^*K(d)[2d]\simeq f^!K$ for each $f$ separately is not yet enough. The
compatibility with Frobenius (Proposition [sk-prop-prop-sp-frobenius-purity]) compares
$\gamma_f$ with its pullback along the Frobenius square, which is a base change, and
the transitivity of the trace map (Proposition [sk-prop-sp-trace-composition])
compares $\gamma$ for $f$, $g$ and $g\circ f$. The topological analogue is that
orientations of a submersion restrict to fibres and multiply under composition. Here
we prove the corresponding statements (Propositions [sk-prop-sp-purity-bc]
and [sk-prop-sp-composition]) and then define the trace map
$\operatorname{Tr}_f$ (Definition [sk-def-sp-trace]), the analogue of integration
over the fibres, which is what Poincaré duality uses. With finite coefficients the
compatibilities follow from the construction, since every ingredient of the
orientation commutes with base change; the work is in passing them to $\Zl$, where
the base change map itself has to be built as a limit.
