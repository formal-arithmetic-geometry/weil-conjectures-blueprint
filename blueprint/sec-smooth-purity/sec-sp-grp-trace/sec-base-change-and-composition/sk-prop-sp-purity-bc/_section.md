Let $\Lambda$ be any admissible coefficient ring and let
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$. Identify the sources by
$g'^{*}f^{*}\simeq(f')^{*}g^{*}$
and the targets by exceptional base change. Then

$$
g'^{*}\gamma_f(K)
=\gamma_{f'}(g^{*}K)
\quad\text{in }D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X',\Lambda).
$$
