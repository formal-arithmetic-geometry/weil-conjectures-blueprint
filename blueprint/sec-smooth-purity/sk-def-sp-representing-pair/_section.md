Let $f$ be smooth, and work in $D(Y,\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)$ and
$D(X,\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)$ on either the étale or the pro-étale sites. For $K$
on $Y$, a *representing pair* for $K$ is an object $H$ on $X$ with a morphism
$\varepsilon:Rf_!H\to K$ such that $b\mapsto\varepsilon\circ Rf_!(b)$ is a
bijection $\operatorname{Hom}(M,H)\to\operatorname{Hom}(Rf_!M,K)$ for all
$M\in\Dctf(X,\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)$.
