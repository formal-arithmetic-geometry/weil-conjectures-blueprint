title = "Representing pairs are unique and given by the Brown adjoint"

Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth (separated, of finite type), $\Lambda_n=\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z$,
and $K\in\Dctf(Y,\Lambda_n)$, on the étale or the pro-étale sites.

1. If $(H,\varepsilon)$ and $(H',\varepsilon')$ are representing pairs for $K$
with $H,H'\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda_n)$, there is a unique morphism $\phi:H\to H'$ with
$\varepsilon'\circ Rf_!(\phi)=\varepsilon$, and $\phi$ is an isomorphism.
2. The pair $(f^!K,\varepsilon_K)$, with $f^!$ the Brown adjoint and
$\varepsilon_K$ its counit, is a representing pair with $f^!K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda_n)$.
Hence every representing pair $(H,\varepsilon)$ with $H\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda_n)$ is
canonically isomorphic to $(f^!K,\varepsilon_K)$, by the unique isomorphism
compatible with the counits.
