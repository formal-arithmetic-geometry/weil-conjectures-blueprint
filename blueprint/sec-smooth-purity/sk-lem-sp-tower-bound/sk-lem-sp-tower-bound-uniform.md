title = "Uniform Tor bound for compactly supported direct images"
order = 2999

Let $f:X\to Y$ be separated of finite type with all fibres of dimension
$\leq d$, and let $A$ be a finite ring killed by a power of $\ell$. If
$M\in\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{proét}},A)$ has Tor amplitude in $[a,b]$, then
$Rf_{!}M\in\Dctf(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},A)$ has Tor amplitude in $[a,b+2d]$. In
particular, if the levels $C_n$ of a tower have a common Tor interval $[a,b]$,
the levels of $Rf_{!,\bullet}C_\bullet$ have the common Tor interval $[a,b+2d]$,
and similarly common cohomological bounds.
