Let $f=\bar f\circ j:X\to Y$ be a compactification. Put
$Rf_{!,\bullet}=R\bar f_{*,\bullet}\circ j_{!,\bullet}$ on
$D^+(\operatorname{Tow}(X,\mathcal O_E))$. Then:

1. $(Rf_{!,\bullet}C_\bullet)_n\simeq Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\ell^n}C_n$ in
   $D^+(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^n)$, naturally in $C_\bullet$;
2. the projection formula, proper base change and the localization triangles
   hold for towers (with tensor products over $\mathcal O_{E,T}/\ell^\bullet$ and
   levelwise twists), the tower maps evaluating at each level to the finite
   ones;
3. a morphism of towers is an isomorphism in $D(\operatorname{Tow})$ if and only if it
   is an isomorphism at every level.
