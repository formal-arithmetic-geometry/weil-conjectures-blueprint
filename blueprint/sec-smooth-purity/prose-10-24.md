The trace map is the counit of the adjunction $Rf_!\dashv f^!$, rewritten by
means of $\gamma_f$ so that its source is the twisted and shifted
inverse image. For $X$ smooth of dimension $d$ over an algebraically closed
field $k$, it is the trace map
$H_c^{2d}(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}(d))\to\Zl$ used in the Weil
conjectures.
