Let $E\subset F$ be finite extensions of $\Ql$, $f:X\to Y$ smooth of pure
relative dimension $d$, and write $(-)_{\mathcal O_F}=-\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,T}}\mathcal O_{F,T}$.

1. $(-)_{\mathcal O_F}$ preserves $\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E)\to\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_F)$, commutes
   with $R\varprojlim$, and its reductions are the extensions along
   $\mathcal O_E/\ell^n\to\mathcal O_F/\ell^n$.
2. The finite comparisons $b$ and $c$ of [det-sp-reduction-direct-image] and
   [det-sp-reduction-exceptional] for $\mathcal O_E/\ell^n\to\mathcal O_F/\ell^n$
   form maps of towers, and their derived limits are isomorphisms
   $(Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E}M)_{\mathcal O_F}\simeq Rf_{!,\mathcal O_F}(M_{\mathcal O_F})$ and
   $(f^!_{\mathcal O_E}K)_{\mathcal O_F}\simeq f^!_{\mathcal O_F}(K_{\mathcal O_F})$, compatible
   with units, counits, the purity maps $\gamma_{f,\mathcal O_E},\gamma_{f,\mathcal O_F}$
   and composition of extensions.
3. Inverting $\ell$ gives the comparisons of the sketch lemma over $E\subset F$,
   with the same compatibilities.
