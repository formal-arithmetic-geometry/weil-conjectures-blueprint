For the standard pair $(p_d:\Aaf^d_B\to B,0)$, the deformation space is $\Aaf^d_B\times_B\Aaf^1_B$ with coordinates $x_k=tu_k$, and under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-fibres] and Input [pur9-cs-input-bc-composition] the family object is isomorphic to $La^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal C(p_d,0)$, with $\mathcal C(p_d,0)\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_B(d)[2d]$ by Lemma [pur9-lem-vector-bundle-purity]; this isomorphism restricts over $\mathbf G_{m,B}$ to $\tau^\circ$ of Definition [pur9-dg-def-punctured] and over $t=1$ to the identity.
