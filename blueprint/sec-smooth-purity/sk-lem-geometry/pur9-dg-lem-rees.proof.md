On the blow-up of $V\times_B\Aaf^1_B$ along $s(B)\times\{0\}$, the complement of the strict transform of $V\times_B\{0\}$ is the chart on which the ideal of the centre is generated by $t$; its coordinate ring is the extended Rees algebra. Inverting $t$ gives $\mathcal O_V[t,t^{-1}]$, whence the restriction over $\mathbf G_{m,B}$ and the fibre over $t=1$. Setting $t=0$ gives $\bigoplus_{r\ge0}\mathcal I_s^{\,r}/\mathcal I_s^{\,r+1}$, the normal cone of Definition [pur9-def-normal-cone]. The section $\widetilde s$ is the closure of $s\times\mathrm{id}$, which meets the zero fibre in the zero section.
