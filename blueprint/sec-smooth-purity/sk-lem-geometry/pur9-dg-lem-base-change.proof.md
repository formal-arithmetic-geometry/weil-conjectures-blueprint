For base change, $\mathcal I_s$ is generated étale-locally by a regular sequence that remains regular after base change (Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-smooth]), so the extended Rees algebra and the graded algebra of the normal cone commute with base change. Restriction along étale $V'\to V$ is flat, and formation of the Rees algebra commutes with flat base change.
