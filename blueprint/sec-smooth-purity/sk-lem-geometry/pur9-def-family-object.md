For $g,s$ as in Definition [pur9-def-section-invariant], the family object is

$$
\mathcal F_{g,s}:=L\widetilde s^*_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}\omega_{\widetilde g}\in D\bigl((\Aaf^1_B)_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n\bigr),
$$

with $\widetilde g,\widetilde s$ of Definition [pur9-def-deformation-space]; that is, $\mathcal F_{g,s}=\mathcal C(\widetilde g,\widetilde s)$ when $\widetilde g$ is smooth (Definition [pur9-def-section-invariant]).
