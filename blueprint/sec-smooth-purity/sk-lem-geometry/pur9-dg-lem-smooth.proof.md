By the normal form of -prop-normal-formZZ applied at points of $s(B)$, $g$ is étale-locally a composite $V\to\Aaf^d_B\to B$ of an étale map and the projection; translating the coordinates by $s$ arranges $s$ to be $x_i=0$. In these coordinates $\mathcal I_s=(x_1,\dots,x_d)$ is generated by a regular sequence, and the extended Rees algebra is $\mathcal O[u_1,\dots,u_d,t]/(tu_i-x_i)$ over the étale chart, in which the $x_i$ are eliminated; so the chart is étale over $\Aaf^d\times\Aaf^1$ over $B$, with $\widetilde g$ the last projection. Setting $t=0$ shows that $N_s$ is étale-locally $\Aaf^d_B$.
