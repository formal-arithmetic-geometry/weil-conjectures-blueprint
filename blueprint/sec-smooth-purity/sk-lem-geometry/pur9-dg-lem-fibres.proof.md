By Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-smooth], $\widetilde g$ is smooth. Let $e\in\{0,1\}$ and let $i'_e$ be the inclusion of the fibre of $\widetilde g$ over $t=e$, identified with $V$, resp. $N_s$, by Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-rees]; then $\widetilde s i_e=i'_e s$, resp. $i'_0 0_N$. The composition comparisons $\mu$ of Lemma “The finite-coefficient inverse-image composition comparison is invertible” (`pur713-lem-pull-composition`) give $Li^*_{e,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L\widetilde s^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_{\widetilde g}\cong Ls^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Li'^*_e\omega_{\widetilde g}$ (resp. with $0_N$), and Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-bc-dualizing] for the Cartesian square of $\widetilde g$ along $i_e$ gives $Li'^*_e\omega_{\widetilde g}\cong\omega_g$, resp. $\omega_{\pi_N}$. Definition [pur9-def-section-invariant] concludes.
