$\mathcal D_sV$ is the relative spectrum over $V$ of the extended Rees algebra $\bigoplus_{r\in\mathbf Z}\mathcal I_s^{\,r}t^{-r}$, with $\mathcal I_s^{\,r}=\mathcal O_V$ for $r\le0$ and $\widetilde g$ induced by $t$. Its fibre over $t=1$ is $(g,s)$, its restriction over $\mathbf G_{m,B}$ is $V\times_B\mathbf G_{m,B}$ with $\widetilde s=s\times\mathrm{id}$, and its fibre over $t=0$ is $(\pi_N,0_N)$.
