Étale-locally on $V$ there are relative coordinates $x_1,\dots,x_d$ over $B$ in which $s$ is $x_1=\cdots=x_d=0$; on the corresponding chart of $\mathcal D_sV$, $\widetilde g$ is the composite of an étale map to $\Aaf^d_{\Aaf^1_B}$ with the projection. Consequently $\widetilde g$ is smooth of pure relative dimension $d$, and $N_s\to B$ is a vector bundle of rank $d$.
