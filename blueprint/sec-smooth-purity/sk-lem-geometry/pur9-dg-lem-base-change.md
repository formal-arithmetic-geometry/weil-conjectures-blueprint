For a morphism $B'\to B$ and an étale morphism $V'\to V$ through which $s$ factors, the deformation space, its section, the normal cone and the identifications of Lemma [pur9-dg-lem-rees] are compatible with base change to $B'$ and with restriction to $V'$.
