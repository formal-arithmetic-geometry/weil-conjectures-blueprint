For smooth $f:X\to Y$ and any admissible $\Lambda$, the *relative dualizing
complex* is

$$
\omega_{f,\Lambda}=f_\Lambda^!\Lambda_Y\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda).
$$

For finite coefficients $\omega_{f,\Lambda}$ can be computed on the pro-étale
site or, equivalently, on the étale site ([sk-prop-sp-et-proet]).
