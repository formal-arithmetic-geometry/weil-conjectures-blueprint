**Change of coefficients.**
The maps $\gamma_f$ and the traces are compatible with change of coefficients. For
finite rings this is the compatibility $c_{A,B}(K)\circ(\gamma_{f,A}(K))_B=\gamma_{f,B}(K_B)$ for the maps $\gamma_f$,
and for the traces it follows from this compatibility together
with the counit identity in [sk-lem-sp-reduction]. For $\mathcal O_E$ we
pass to towers, where the finite identities hold level by level, and
apply $R\varprojlim$. For $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we invert $\ell$ on integral
models and extend scalars from a finite extension of $\Ql$.

For the structure morphism of a smooth scheme, Theorem [sk-thm-purity-transport] computes the
dualizing complex used in Poincaré duality
(Section “Verdier and Poincaré duality”).
