Under Input [pur9-input-closed-immersion], $Lj^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}A=0$ for every $A\in D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. Consequently $\operatorname{Hom}(Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}A,Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}B[n])=0$ for all $A\in D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, $B\in D(U_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $n\in\mathbf Z$.
