Assume Inputs [pur713-input-proper-adjoint], [pur9-input-closed-immersion] and [pur9-input-open-closed]. For every $L\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ there is a morphism $\partial_L:Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lj^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L\to Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L[1]$ such that

$$
Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L\xrightarrow{\ \varepsilon^!_L\ }L\xrightarrow{\ \eta^j_L\ }Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lj^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L\xrightarrow{\ \partial_L\ }Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L[1]
$$

is a distinguished triangle in $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
