For $W\in U_{\Proet}$, $W\times_XU=W$, so the sheaf counit $j^*j_*G\to G$ is the identity (Definition “Fixed-coefficient direct image” (`pur713-def-sheaf-push`) and Input “Inverse image along a weakly étale morphism” (`pur713-input-fixed-weakly-etale`)). Since $j^*$ is exact and left adjoint to $j_*$, $j_*$ carries K-injective complexes to K-injective complexes: $\operatorname{Hom}_{K}(N,j_*I)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_K(j^*N,I)$ vanishes for acyclic $N$ because $j^*N$ is acyclic. For a K-injective resolution $B\to I$, $Lj^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}B$ is therefore represented by $j^*j_*I=I$, and the derived counit is the identity of $I$ under these identifications.
