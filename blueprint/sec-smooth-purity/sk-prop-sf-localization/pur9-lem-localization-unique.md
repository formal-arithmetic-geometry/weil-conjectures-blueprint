Under the inputs of Lemma [pur9-lem-localization-triangle], the morphism $\partial_L$ is the only morphism completing $\varepsilon^!_L,\eta^j_L$ to a distinguished triangle. For every morphism $f:L\to L'$, the triple $(Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}f,f,Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lj^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}f)$ is a morphism of these triangles, and it is the only morphism of triangles with middle component $f$.
