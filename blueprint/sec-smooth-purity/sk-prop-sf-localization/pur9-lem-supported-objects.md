Under Inputs [pur9-input-closed-immersion] and [pur9-input-open-closed], if $N\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ satisfies $Lj^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}N=0$, then $\eta^i_N:N\to Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Li^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}N$ is an isomorphism.
