Let $\Lambda$ be admissible, $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ closed, $u\colon S\hookrightarrow Z$ open,
$a=i\circ u$, and $L$ a finite locally projective $\Lambda_S$-module with dual
$L^\vee=\mathcal Hom_{\Lambda_S}(L,\Lambda_S)$. Then, naturally in $K\in D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$,
$$
R\operatorname{Hom}_X(a_!L,K)\cong R\operatorname{Hom}_Z(u_!L,R\underline\Gamma_ZK)
\cong R\operatorname{Hom}_S(L,u^*R\underline\Gamma_ZK)\cong R\Gamma\bigl(S,L^\vee\otimes u^*R\underline\Gamma_ZK\bigr).
$$
In particular $\Hom_{D(X)}(a_!L,K[q])=H^q(S,L^\vee\otimes u^*R\underline\Gamma_ZK)$.
