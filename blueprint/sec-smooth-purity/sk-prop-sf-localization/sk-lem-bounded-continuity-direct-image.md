title = "Higher direct images commute with bounded-below filtered colimits"

Let $g\colon Y\to Z$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism of schemes, $A$ a sheaf of
rings on $Y_{\mathrm{proét}}$, and $(K_i^\bullet)$ a filtered diagram of complexes of
$A$-modules vanishing in degrees below a fixed $a$. Then for every $q$ the natural map
$\varinjlim_iR^qg_*K_i^\bullet\to R^qg_*(\varinjlim_iK_i^\bullet)$ of sheaves on $Z_{\mathrm{proét}}$
is an isomorphism.
