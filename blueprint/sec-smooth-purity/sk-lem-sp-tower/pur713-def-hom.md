For $K\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, the functor $R\mathcal Hom_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}(K,-)$ on $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$ is the right adjoint of $K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}-$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`). Its existence, and its functoriality in $K$ making it a bifunctor contravariant in the first variable, are part of the closed derived monoidal structure of the governing reference, §5.
