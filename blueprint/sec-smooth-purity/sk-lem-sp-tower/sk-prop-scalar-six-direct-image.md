Let $f\colon X\to Y$ be a morphism of the standing range and $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ one of
$\mathcal O_E\to A$ ($A$ finite), $\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_F$, $E\to F$, $\mathcal O_E\to E$, $E\to\Qlb$. For $K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$
the comparison $(Rf_*K)_{\Lambda'}\to Rf_*(K_{\Lambda'})$ is an isomorphism. (For maps $A\to B$ of finite rings this
is [lem-coef-finite-direct-image].)
