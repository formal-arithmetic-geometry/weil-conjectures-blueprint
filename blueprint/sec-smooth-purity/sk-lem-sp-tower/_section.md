Let $C_\bullet\in\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^\bullet)$ be normalized
([sk-def-sp-normalized-towers]). Then $R\varprojlim_n C_n$ lies in
$\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$, and for every $m$ the natural map

$$
\bigl(R\varprojlim_n C_n\bigr)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,T}}
\mathcal O_{E,T}/\ell^m\longrightarrow C_m
$$

is an isomorphism in $D(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^m)$.
