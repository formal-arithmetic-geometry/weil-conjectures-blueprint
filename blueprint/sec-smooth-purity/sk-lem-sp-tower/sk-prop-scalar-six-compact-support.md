Let $f\colon X\to Y$ be separated of finite type with compactification $f=p\circ j$, and $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ a listed
map. For $K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ the composite

$$
(Rf_!K)_{\Lambda'}=(Rp_*j_!K)_{\Lambda'}\to Rp_*((j_!K)_{\Lambda'})\simeq Rp_*j_!(K_{\Lambda'})=Rf_!(K_{\Lambda'})
$$

is an isomorphism, independent of the compactification, and compatible with composition.
