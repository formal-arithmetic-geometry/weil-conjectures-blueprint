Let $K',M'\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Et},A)$ have Tor amplitude $[a,b]$
and $[c,d]$, and assume $K'$ is site-perfect. Then
$R\mathcal Hom_A(\nu_X^*K',\nu_X^*M')$ is bounded constructible
with Tor amplitude in $[c-b,d-a]$.
