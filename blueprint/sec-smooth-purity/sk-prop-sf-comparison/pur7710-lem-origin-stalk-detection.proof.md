By Lemma “Detection by cohomology stalks” (`pur456-pbc-stalk-detection`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), $b$ is an isomorphism in $D^+(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. Hence $\psi a\phi^{-1}=L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}b$ is an isomorphism, and so is $a$. This proves the assertion for the stated presentations only; no geometric-stalk detection is asserted for arbitrary pro-étale objects.
