If $a$ is an isomorphism, so is each $Lu^*_{i,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}a$. Conversely, let $C$ be a cone of $a$. Since $Lu^*_{i,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is levelwise and exact (Lemma “The finite-coefficient derived inverse image is levelwise” (`pur713-lem-finite-pull-levelwise`)), $\mathcal H^r(Lu^*_{i,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}C)=u^*_{i,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal H^r(C)$, and for the weakly étale $u_i$ this inverse image is restriction along the localization functor (Input “Inverse image along a weakly étale morphism” (`pur713-input-fixed-weakly-etale`)). Thus every section of $\mathcal H^r(C)$ over an object $V$ of $W_{\Proet}$ restricts to zero on the covering $\{V\times_WW_i\to V\}$, and by the sheaf axiom it is zero. Hence $\mathcal H^r(C)=0$ for every $r$, with no boundedness hypothesis, and $C=0$.
