Under Inputs “The bounded-below site-comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-site-unit`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) and [pur7710-input-constructible-sheaves], for a $k$-scheme $W$ of finite type the functor $L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “The derived site inverse image” (`pur456-def-site-inverse-derived`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) restricts to an equivalence

$$
D^b_c(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)
\xrightarrow{\sim}D^b_c(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)
$$

between the full subcategories of Definitions [pur713-def-constructible-category-et] and [pur713-def-constructible-category].
