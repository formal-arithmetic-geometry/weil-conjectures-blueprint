Under Input [pur7710-input-ind-etale-basis], let $W$ be a scheme and $a:K\to M$ a morphism in $D^+(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. Assume that

$$
R\Gamma_{B,\Proet,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(Lu^*_{B,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}a)
$$

is an isomorphism in $D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ for every test object $u_B:B\to W$ of Definition [pur7710-def-ind-etale-tests], with derived sections of Definition [pur7710-def-derived-sections]. Then $a$ is an isomorphism.
