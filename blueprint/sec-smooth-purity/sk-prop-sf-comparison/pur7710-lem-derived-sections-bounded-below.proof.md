The sheaf category is Grothendieck abelian (Definition “Sites and sheaf categories” (`pur713-def-sites`), Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived categories” (`pur456-pbc-finite-category`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])) and has enough injectives. If $\mathcal H^r(K)=0$ for $r<m$, the truncation $\tau^{\geq m}K$ is isomorphic to $K$ and admits a quasi-isomorphism to a complex $I$ of injectives with $I^r=0$ for $r<m$. A bounded-below complex of injectives is K-injective, so the derived functor is computed by $\Gamma(W_\tau,I)$, which vanishes in degrees below $m$.
