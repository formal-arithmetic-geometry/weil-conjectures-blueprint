Let $W$ be a scheme, $a:K\to M$ a morphism in $D(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, and $\{u_i:W_i\to W\}$ a covering family in $W_{\Proet}$ (Definition “Sites and sheaf categories” (`pur713-def-sites`)); the $W_i$ are weakly étale over $W$ and need not be of finite type over $k$. Then $a$ is an isomorphism if and only if $Lu^*_{i,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}a$ is an isomorphism in $D(W_{i,\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ for every $i$.
