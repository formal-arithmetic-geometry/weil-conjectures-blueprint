For $W$, $\tau$ as in Definition [pur7710-def-derived-sections] and $K\in D^+(W_\tau,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, $R\Gamma_{W,\tau,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K$ is computed by applying $\Gamma(W_\tau,-)$ termwise to any bounded-below complex of injective sheaves quasi-isomorphic to $K$, and $H^rR\Gamma_{W,\tau,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K=0$ for $r$ smaller than the least degree of a nonzero cohomology sheaf of $K$.
