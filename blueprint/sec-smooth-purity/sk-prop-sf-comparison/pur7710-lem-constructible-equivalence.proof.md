The functor $L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is levelwise and exact (Definition “The derived site inverse image” (`pur456-def-site-inverse-derived`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence])), so it commutes with cohomology sheaves; by Input [pur7710-input-constructible-sheaves] it therefore preserves boundedness and constructibility, and the restriction is defined. Full faithfulness is Lemma “Full faithfulness of the finite-coefficient site inverse image” (`pur7710-lem-site-fully-faithful`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), since bounded objects are bounded below. For essential surjectivity, induct on the number of nonzero cohomology sheaves of a bounded constructible pro-étale complex. The zero complex is the image of zero. A complex with one nonzero cohomology sheaf is a shift of that sheaf, which lifts by Input [pur7710-input-constructible-sheaves]. For the induction step, the truncation triangle at the top nonzero degree expresses the complex, up to a shift, as the cone of a morphism between objects with fewer nonzero cohomology sheaves, which lift by induction. Full faithfulness lifts that morphism, and the exact functor carries the cone of the lifted morphism to a cone of the original one; two cones of the same morphism are isomorphic.
