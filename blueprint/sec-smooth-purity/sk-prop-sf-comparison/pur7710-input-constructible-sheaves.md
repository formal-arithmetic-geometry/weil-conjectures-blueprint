For a $k$-scheme $W$ of finite type, the exact functor $\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “Site-comparison inverse image of sheaves” (`pur456-def-site-inverse`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) induces an equivalence between the full subcategories of constructible sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $W_{\Et}$ and on $W_{\Proet}$, with constructibility as in Definition [pur713-def-finite-constructible]. The equivalence includes essential surjectivity, not only preservation of constructibility. *Status:* this is the finite constructible-sheaf comparison underlying Bhatt–Scholze, *The pro-étale topology for schemes*, Lemma 6.3.2, and the blueprint node `lem-weil-pbc-pullback-preserves-bounded-constructible`; it is imported and outside the governing reference.
