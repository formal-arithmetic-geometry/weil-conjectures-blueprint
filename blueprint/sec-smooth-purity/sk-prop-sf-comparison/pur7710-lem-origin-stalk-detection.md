Under Inputs “The bounded-below site-comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-site-unit`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) and “Geometric stalks on the small étale site” (`pur456-pbc-input-stalks`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), let $W$ be a $k$-scheme of finite type and $a:K\to M$ a morphism in $D^+(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. Let $\phi:K\cong L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ and $\psi:M\cong L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}N$ be isomorphisms with $L,N\in D^+(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ (so $K,M\in\mathscr E^+(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, Definition “The bounded-below étale-origin subcategory” (`pur456-def-etale-origin`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence])), and let $b:L\to N$ be the unique morphism with $L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}b=\psi a\phi^{-1}$, given by Lemma “Full faithfulness of the finite-coefficient site inverse image” (`pur7710-lem-site-fully-faithful`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]). If $b$ induces isomorphisms on all cohomology stalks at all geometric points of $W$ (Definition “Geometric points and stalks” (`pur456-pbc-stalk`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])), then $a$ is an isomorphism.
