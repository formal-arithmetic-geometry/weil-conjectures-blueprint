For finite $\Lambda$ and $K\in\Dplus(T_{\Et},\Lambda)$, the derived unit
induces a natural isomorphism

$$
 R\Gamma(T_{\Et},K)\xrightarrow{\sim}
 R\Gamma(T_{\Proet},\nu_T^*K).
$$

Both functors are ordinary derived sections.
