For a scheme $W$, every object $V$ of $W_{\Proet}$ admits a covering family $\{V_i\to V\}$ in $W_{\Proet}$ such that each composite $V_i\to V\to W$ is a test object of Definition [pur7710-def-ind-etale-tests]. *Status:* this follows from the faithfully flat ind-étale refinement theorem for weakly étale affine objects, Stacks Project, Tag `097Z`, and the stability of ind-étale objects under the necessary restrictions and fibre products (blueprint nodes `thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-refinement` and `lem-weil-pbc-ind-etale-basis`); it is imported and outside the governing reference.
