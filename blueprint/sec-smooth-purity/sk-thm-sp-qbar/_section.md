Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $d$. With coefficients
$\Qlb$ the functors are $f_{\Qlb}^*=f^{-1}:\Dbc(Y,\Qlb)\to\Dbc(X,\Qlb)$ and
$Rf_{!,\Qlb}=R\bar f_{*,\Qlb}\circ j_{!,\Qlb}:\Dbc(X,\Qlb)\to\Dbc(Y,\Qlb)$ for a
compactification $f=\bar f\circ j$. Then, as functors between $\Dbc(X,\Qlb)$
and $\Dbc(Y,\Qlb)$,

$$
Rf_{!,\Qlb}\dashv\bigl(K\longmapsto f_{\Qlb}^*K(d)[2d]\bigr).
$$
