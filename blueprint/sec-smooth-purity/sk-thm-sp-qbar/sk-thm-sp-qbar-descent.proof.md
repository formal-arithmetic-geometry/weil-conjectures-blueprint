(1) In a filtered $2$-colimit of categories, Hom sets between images of objects
from one stage are the filtered colimits of the Hom sets at later stages; apply
this to $K$ and $L[q]$.

(2) Each object of the $2$-colimit comes from some stage, and by (1) each
morphism between objects coming from a stage $F$ comes from a later stage. With
finitely many objects and morphisms, a common finite $F$ suffices (the system
of finite extensions inside $\Qlb$ is filtered). Each relation is an equality
of two morphisms after extension to $\Qlb$, hence by (1) holds already after
extension to some finite $F'$; finitely many relations hold over a common
$F''$.

(3) Extension of scalars from $F$ to $\Qlb$ commutes with pullback (pullback
of module sheaves commutes with tensor products with the constant coefficient
sheaf) and with $j_!$ (exact), and with $R\bar f_*$ for proper $\bar f$ on bounded
constructible objects (the coefficient comparisons of the six operations,
proved on integral models by the finite-level projection formula and passage to
the limit, then inverting $\ell$ and passing to the colimit). Hence $Rf_{!,\Qlb}$
and $f^*_{\Qlb}$ commute with extension of scalars.
