Let $W$ be a noetherian scheme, $F$ finite locally constant on $W_{\Et}$ and $\sigma\in\Gamma(W,F)$. The set of points $w\in W$ at which the stalk of $\sigma$ at a geometric point over $w$ vanishes is open and closed in $W$.
