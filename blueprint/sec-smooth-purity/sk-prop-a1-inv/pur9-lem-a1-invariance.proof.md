On $\Aaf^1_{Y_{\alpha\beta}}$, $\kappa_\alpha$ and $\kappa_\beta$ agree after $i^*_{1,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$; hence after $j^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ by Lemma [pur9-lem-flc-uniqueness-gm] (applied to their restrictions to $\mathbf G_{m,Y_{\alpha\beta}}$) and then on $\Aaf^1_{Y_{\alpha\beta}}$ by Lemma [pur9-lem-flc-uniqueness-line]. The sheaf axiom for $\mathcal Hom(F,p^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}C)$ on the étale covering $\{\Aaf^1_{Y_\alpha}\to\Aaf^1_Y\}$ glues them to a unique $\kappa$. It is an isomorphism because it is one locally, and $i^*_{1,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\kappa=\varphi$ holds locally, hence globally.
