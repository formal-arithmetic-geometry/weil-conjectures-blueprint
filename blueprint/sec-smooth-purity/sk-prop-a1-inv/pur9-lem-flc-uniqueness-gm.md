Let $L,M$ be finite locally constant on $(\mathbf G_{m,Y})_{\Et}$. Two morphisms $a,b:L\to M$ with $i^*_{1,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}a=i^*_{1,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}b$ are equal.
