$\mathcal Hom(L,M)$ is finite locally constant: on a common trivialization it is the constant sheaf of homomorphisms between the finite (finitely presented) modules. The section $a-b$ vanishes on $\mathbf G_{m,Y}$; by Lemma [pur9-lem-flc-vanishing-locus] its vanishing locus is open and closed and contains the dense open $\mathbf G_{m,Y}$ (every irreducible component of $\Aaf^1_Y$ is $\Aaf^1$ over an irreducible component of $Y$ and meets $\mathbf G_{m,Y}$), so it is all of $\Aaf^1_Y$. Stalks are conservative, so $a=b$.
