In Lemma [pur9-lem-a1-invariance], $i^*_{0,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\kappa:i^*_{0,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F\xrightarrow{\sim}i^*_{0,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}p^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}C=C$ is an isomorphism, so $i^*_{0,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F\cong C\cong i^*_{1,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F$ by $i^*_{0,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\kappa$ and $\varphi$.
