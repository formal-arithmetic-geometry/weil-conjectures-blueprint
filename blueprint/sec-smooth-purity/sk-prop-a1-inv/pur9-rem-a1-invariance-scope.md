For complexes $F\in D^b((\Aaf^1_Y)_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ whose only nonzero cohomology sheaf, in a fixed degree $m_0$, is finite locally constant of rank one, Lemmas [pur9-lem-a1-invariance] and [pur9-lem-a1-invariance-ends] apply to $\mathcal H^{m_0}$ and give the derived statements through the truncation isomorphisms $F\cong\mathcal H^{m_0}(F)[-m_0]$. Twisting by Definition [pur9-def-tate-twist] preserves these hypotheses. For pro-étale objects of $D^b_c$, the statements are transported along the equivalence of Lemma [pur7710-lem-constructible-equivalence]; no analogue for arbitrary pro-étale objects is asserted.
