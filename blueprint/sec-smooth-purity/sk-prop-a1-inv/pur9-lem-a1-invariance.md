Let $F$ be finite locally constant of rank one on $(\Aaf^1_Y)_{\Et}$, $C$ finite locally constant of rank one on $Y_{\Et}$, and $\varphi:i^*_{1,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F\xrightarrow{\sim}C$. Suppose there are an étale covering $\{Y_\alpha\to Y\}$ and isomorphisms $\kappa_\alpha:F|_{\Aaf^1_{Y_\alpha}}\to p^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}C|_{\Aaf^1_{Y_\alpha}}$ with $i^*_{1,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\kappa_\alpha=\varphi|_{Y_\alpha}$. Then there is a unique morphism $\kappa:F\to p^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}C$ with $\kappa|_{\Aaf^1_{Y_\alpha}}=\kappa_\alpha$ for all $\alpha$; it is an isomorphism, and $i^*_{1,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\kappa=\varphi$.
