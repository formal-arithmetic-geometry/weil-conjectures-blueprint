On a trivializing $W_\alpha$, $\sigma$ is a locally constant function to a finite module, so its vanishing locus and its complement are unions of connected components, hence open in $W_\alpha$. Étale morphisms are open, and the vanishing condition at $w$ depends only on $w$; so both the vanishing set and its complement are unions of images of open sets.
