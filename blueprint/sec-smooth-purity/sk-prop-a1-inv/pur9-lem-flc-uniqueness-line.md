Let $Y$ be a separated $k$-scheme of finite type and $L,M$ finite locally constant on $(\Aaf^1_Y)_{\Et}$. Two morphisms $a,b:L\to M$ with $j^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}a=j^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}b$ are equal.
