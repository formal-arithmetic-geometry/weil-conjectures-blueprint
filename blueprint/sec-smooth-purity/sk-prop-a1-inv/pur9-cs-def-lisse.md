A sheaf $\mathcal L$ of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $X_{\Et}$ is lisse of rank one if there is an étale covering $\{u_\alpha:X_\alpha\to X\}$ such that each $u^*_{\alpha,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal L$ (Definition “Finite-coefficient étale inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback`, in [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])) is isomorphic to the constant sheaf $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{X_\alpha}$.
