As in Lemma [pur9-lem-flc-uniqueness-line], the vanishing locus $Z$ of $a-b$ is open and closed. Its complement meets every fibre $\mathbf G_{m,y}$, $y\in Y$, in an open and closed subset of the connected fibre not containing $i_1(y)$, hence in the empty set. So $Z=\mathbf G_{m,Y}$.
