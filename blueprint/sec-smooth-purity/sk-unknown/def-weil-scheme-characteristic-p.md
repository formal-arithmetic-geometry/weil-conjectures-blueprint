Let $p$ be a prime number. A scheme $X$ is *of characteristic $p$* (also: *has characteristic $p$*, *is in characteristic $p$*) if $p$ is zero in $\mathcal O_X$, i.e. $p\cdot1=0$ in the ring of global sections $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$; equivalently $p\cdot1=0$ in $\mathcal O_X(U)$ for every open $U\subseteq X$ (restriction from $X$ to $U$ is a ring homomorphism), equivalently in every stalk $\mathcal O_{X,x}$, equivalently $X\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$ factors (uniquely) through $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_p$. The empty scheme is of characteristic $p$ for every $p$.
