When we compare coefficient rings, we write the ring as a subscript of the
functor, as in $Rf_{!,A}$ or $f^!_{\mathcal O_E}$; otherwise we omit the
subscript.

With coefficients $A$, we have $Rf_!=R\bar f_*\circ j_!$ for
a compactification $f=\bar f\circ j$. On $\Dctf$, these functors and
their right adjoints are the pro-étale functors constructed in
Theorem [sk-prop-sf-existence] and restricted in
Theorem [sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation]. Proper base change, the
projection formula and the localization triangle are the geometric
identities of Section “Torsion coefficients: the strategy”, and in their proofs we may
compute on the étale sites and use the comparison of
Proposition [sk-prop-ctf-finite-comparison]. Finally, for an
étale map, extension by zero is exact on sheaves of $A$-modules on the
pro-étale sites, and on the derived categories it is
left adjoint to inverse image, with counit given on
geometric stalks by the sum over the fibre.
