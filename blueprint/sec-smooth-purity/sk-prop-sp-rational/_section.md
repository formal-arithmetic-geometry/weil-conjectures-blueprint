Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $d$, and $E/\Ql$ finite.
Then, as functors between $\Dbc(X,E)$ and $\Dbc(Y,E)$,

$$
Rf_{!,E}\dashv\bigl(K\longmapsto f_E^*K(d)[2d]\bigr).
$$
