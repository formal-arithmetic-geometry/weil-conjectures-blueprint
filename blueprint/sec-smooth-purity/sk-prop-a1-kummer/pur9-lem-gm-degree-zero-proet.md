Under the inputs of Lemma [pur9-lem-a1-acyclic-proet], the pro-étale analogues of the maps of Lemmas [pur9-lem-gm-degree-zero] and [pur9-lem-gm-restriction-degree-zero], formed with $R\pi_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, $Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and the units of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived inverse-image adjunction” (`pur713-lem-star-adjunction`), are isomorphisms of sheaves on $Y_{\Proet}$.
