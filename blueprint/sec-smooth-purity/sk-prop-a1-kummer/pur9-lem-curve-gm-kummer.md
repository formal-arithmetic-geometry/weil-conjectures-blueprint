Let $M$ be a finite abelian group with $\ell^NM=0$, and let $t$ be the coordinate of $\mathbf G_{m,k}$. The map $M\to H^1(\mathbf G_{m,k,\Et},\mu_{\ell^N}\otimes_{\mathbf Z/\ell^N}M)$ sending $m$ to the image of $\kappa_N(t)$ (Definition [pur9-def-kummer-class]) under $\zeta\mapsto\zeta\otimes m$ is an isomorphism.
