Let $S'$ be a strictly henselian local $k$-scheme, $C\in\{\Aaf^1_k,\mathbf G_{m,k}\}$, $h:C\times_kS'\to C$ the projection, and $M$ a finite abelian group of $\ell$-power order. Then $h^*:H^q(C_{\Et},M)\to H^q((C\times_kS')_{\Et},M)$ is an isomorphism for every $q$.
