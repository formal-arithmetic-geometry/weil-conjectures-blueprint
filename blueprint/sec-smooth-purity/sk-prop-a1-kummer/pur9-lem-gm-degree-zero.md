For $Y$ and $m$ as in Lemma [pur9-lem-a1-acyclic], the unit of $L\pi^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ (Lemma “The étale derived inverse-image adjunction” (`pur456-pbc-adjunction`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])) induces an isomorphism $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y(m)\to\mathcal H^0(R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y}}(m))$ of sheaves on $Y_{\Et}$.
