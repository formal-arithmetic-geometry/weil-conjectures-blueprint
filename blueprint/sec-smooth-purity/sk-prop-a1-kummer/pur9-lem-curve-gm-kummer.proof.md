Both sides are additive functors of $M$, and the map is natural in $M$. For an exact sequence $0\to M'\to M\to M''\to0$ of such groups, $\mu_{\ell^N}\otimes-$ is exact ($\mu_{\ell^N}$ is free of rank one over $\mathbf Z/\ell^N$ by Lemma [pur9-lem-roots-constant]), and Lemma [pur9-lem-curve-gm-vanishing] makes $H^0$ right exact on these sequences and kills $H^2$; so $0\to H^1(M')\to H^1(M)\to H^1(M'')\to0$ is exact, and by the five lemma it suffices to treat $M=\mathbf Z/\ell$. Then $\mu_{\ell^N}\otimes\mathbf Z/\ell\cong\mu_\ell$ by $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{\ell^{N-1}}$, carrying $\kappa_N(t)$ to $\kappa_1(t)$ (proof of Lemma [pur9-lem-kummer-independence]). The Kummer and Hilbert 90 sequence of the proof of Lemma [pur9-lem-curve-a1] reads $\Gamma(\mathbb G_m)\xrightarrow{\ell}\Gamma(\mathbb G_m)\to H^1(\mathbf G_{m,k},\mu_\ell)\to\Pic(\mathbf G_{m,k})$. Here $\Gamma(\mathbf G_{m,k},\mathbb G_m)=k[T,T^{-1}]^\times=k^\times\times T^{\mathbf Z}$ and $\Pic(\mathbf G_{m,k})=0$, $k[T,T^{-1}]$ being a principal ideal domain. Divisibility of $k^\times$ gives $H^1\cong T^{\mathbf Z}/T^{\ell\mathbf Z}\cong\mathbf Z/\ell$, generated by the boundary of $T$, which is $\kappa_1(t)$.
