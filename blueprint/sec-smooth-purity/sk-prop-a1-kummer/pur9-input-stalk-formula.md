Let $h:W\to Y$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism, $F$ a torsion abelian sheaf on $W_{\Et}$, $\bar y$ a geometric point of $Y$, and $S'=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{Y,\bar y}$. Then the natural map $(R^qh_*F)_{\bar y}\to H^q(W\times_YS',F|_{W\times_YS'})$ is an isomorphism for every $q$, compatible with morphisms of sheaves and with cup products by global classes on $W$. *Status:* the strict-henselization stalk formula of étale cohomology; imported, not proved here.
