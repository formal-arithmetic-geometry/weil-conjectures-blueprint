Let $X$ be of finite type over a field, let
$j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be an
open immersion,
and let $F$ be a
constructible
module sheaf over a finite commutative ring on $U_{\Et}$.
Then $j_*F$ is
constructible on $X_{\Et}$.
