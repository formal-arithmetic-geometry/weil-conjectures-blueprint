$Lg^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is exact and commutes with cohomology sheaves (Lemma “Exactness of étale inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback-exact`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])). The exchange map is built from the unit and counit, so on global classes it is the inverse-image map along the projection $\mathbf G_{m,Y'}\to\mathbf G_{m,Y}$; it carries $\kappa(t)$ to $\kappa(t)$ for the coordinate $t$ pulled back, by Lemma [pur9-lem-kummer-base-change].
