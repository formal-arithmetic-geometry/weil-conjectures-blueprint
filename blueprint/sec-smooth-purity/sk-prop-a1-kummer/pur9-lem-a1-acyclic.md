For a separated $k$-scheme $Y$ of finite type and $m\in\mathbf Z$, the unit $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y(m)\to Rp_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}p^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y(m)=Rp_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^1_Y}(m)$ of Lemma “The étale derived inverse-image adjunction” (`pur456-pbc-adjunction`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) is an isomorphism in $D^+(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
