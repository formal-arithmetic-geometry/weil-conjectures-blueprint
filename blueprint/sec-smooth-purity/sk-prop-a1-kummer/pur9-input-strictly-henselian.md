For a strictly henselian local scheme $S'$ and a finite abelian group $M$, $H^0(S'_{\Et},M_{S'})=M$ and $H^q(S'_{\Et},M_{S'})=0$ for $q>0$. *Status:* imported (the global-sections functor of a strictly henselian local scheme is the stalk at the closed point); not proved here.
