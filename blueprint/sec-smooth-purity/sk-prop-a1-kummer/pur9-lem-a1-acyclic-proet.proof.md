By Lemma [pur9-lem-site-inverse-constant] the pro-étale constant sheaves are $L\nu^*$ of the étale ones. The map $\lambda_{p,L}$ of Definition “The canonical direct-image comparison” (`pur7710-def-direct-image-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), for $L$ the étale constant sheaf, is an isomorphism by Input “The qcqs direct-image comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-qcqs-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) ($p$ is quasi-compact quasi-separated). Being the adjoint of $L\nu^*(\varepsilon)\circ\beta_p$, it carries $L\nu^*$ of the étale unit to the pro-étale unit, by the triangle identities. Lemma [pur9-lem-a1-acyclic] and exactness of $L\nu^*$ conclude.
