By Lemma [pur9-lem-site-inverse-constant] and the isomorphism $\lambda_\pi$ (Input “The qcqs direct-image comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-qcqs-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence])), the object is $L\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of $R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y}}(m)$; $\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is exact (Input “The site comparison is a morphism of sites” (`pur456-input-site-morphism`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence])), so it commutes with cohomology sheaves, and Lemma [pur9-lem-gm-vanishing] gives the vanishing on the étale site.
