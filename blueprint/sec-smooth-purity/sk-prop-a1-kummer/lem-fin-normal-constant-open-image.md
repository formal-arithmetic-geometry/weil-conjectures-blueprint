Let $Y$ be a Noetherian
normal integral
scheme, let $v:V\hookrightarrow Y$ be a nonempty
open immersion,
and let $M$ be a finite module. The canonical map
$\underline M_Y\to v_*\underline M_V$, with
direct image on the
etale sites, is an isomorphism.
