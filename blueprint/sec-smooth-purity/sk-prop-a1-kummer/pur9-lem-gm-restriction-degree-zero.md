For $Y$ and $m$ as in Lemma [pur9-lem-a1-acyclic], the map

$$
\mathcal H^0\bigl(Rp_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^1_Y}(m)\bigr)\longrightarrow\mathcal H^0\bigl(R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y}}(m)\bigr)
$$

induced by $Rp_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the unit of $Lj^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rj_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and the composition isomorphism $Rp_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rj_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ (Lemma “Composition of étale derived direct images” (`pur456-pbc-push-composition`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])) is an isomorphism.
