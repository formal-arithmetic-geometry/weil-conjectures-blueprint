For a morphism $g:Y'\to Y$ of separated $k$-schemes of finite type, with base change $\pi':\mathbf G_{m,Y'}\to Y'$ of $\pi$, the map $\mathcal H^1$ of the direct-image exchange $Lg^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y}}(1)\to R\pi'_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y'}}(1)$ (the adjunction-mate construction of -def-exchangeZZ) carries $g^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\kappa_Y$ to $\kappa_{Y'}$ of Definition [pur9-def-kummer-generator].
