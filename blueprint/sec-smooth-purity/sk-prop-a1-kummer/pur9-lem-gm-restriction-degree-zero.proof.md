The map is compatible with the units for $p$ and $\pi$, because $\pi=pj$ and the units compose. These units are isomorphisms in degree zero by Lemmas [pur9-lem-a1-acyclic] and [pur9-lem-gm-degree-zero], so the map is one as well.
