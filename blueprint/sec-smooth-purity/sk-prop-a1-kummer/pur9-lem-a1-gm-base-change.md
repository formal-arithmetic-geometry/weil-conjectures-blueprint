For $g$, $\pi'$ as in Lemma [pur9-lem-kummer-generator-base-change] and the base change $p':\Aaf^1_{Y'}\to Y'$ of $p$, the direct-image exchange maps $Lg^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rp_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^1_Y}(m)\to Rp'_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^1_{Y'}}(m)$ and $Lg^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y}}(m)\to R\pi'_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y'}}(m)$ are isomorphisms in $D^+(Y'_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
