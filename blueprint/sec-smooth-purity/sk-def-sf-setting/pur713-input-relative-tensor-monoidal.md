For $\mathcal R$ as in Definition [pur713-def-relative-tensor], the relative tensor product makes $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$ a symmetric monoidal category with unit $\mathcal R$, with associativity, symmetry and unit isomorphisms induced from those of $\otimes_{\mathbf Z}$. Source: governing reference, §4.1 (established there).
