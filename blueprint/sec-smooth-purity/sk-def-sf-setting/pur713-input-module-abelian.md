For a commutative ring object $\mathcal R$ of $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$, the category $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$ of Definition [pur713-def-modules] is a Grothendieck abelian category; its kernels, cokernels and finite direct sums are computed on underlying abelian sheaves, and the forgetful functor to $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$ is exact and faithful. Source: governing reference, §4.1 (established there).
