For a commutative ring object $\mathcal R$ of $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$, an $\mathcal R$-module $M$ is finitely presented if there exist integers $r,s\geq0$ and an exact sequence $\mathcal R^r\to\mathcal R^s\to M\to0$ in $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$. Here $\mathcal R^r$ is the direct sum of $r$ copies of $\mathcal R$, and $\mathcal R^0$ is the zero module.
