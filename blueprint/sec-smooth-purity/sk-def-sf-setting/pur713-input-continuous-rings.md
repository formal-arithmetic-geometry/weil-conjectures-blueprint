For $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E\}$, the presheaf $\underline T$ of Definition [pur713-def-continuous-rings] is a sheaf on $*_{\Proet}$, hence a condensed ring in the sense of Definition [pur713-def-point-site]. Source: governing reference, §3.1 (established there: a surjection of profinite sets is a quotient map, so continuous maps descend along it).
