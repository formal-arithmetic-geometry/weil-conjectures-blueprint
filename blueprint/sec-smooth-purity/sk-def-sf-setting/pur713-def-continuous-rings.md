For $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E\}$, with the topology induced from $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, let $\underline T$ be the presheaf of commutative rings on $*_{\Proet}$ with

$$
\underline T(S)=C(S,T),\qquad S\in *_{\Proet},
$$

the ring of continuous maps $S\to T$ with pointwise operations, restriction being composition. No completeness or limit presentation of $T$ enters this definition: $\underline E$ is not defined as $\underline{\mathcal O}_E[1/\varpi]$, nor $\underline{\mathcal O}_E$ as $\lim_n\underline{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$.
