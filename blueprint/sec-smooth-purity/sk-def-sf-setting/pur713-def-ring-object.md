Let $(\mathscr C,\otimes,\mathbb 1,\beta)$ be a symmetric monoidal category. A commutative ring object of $\mathscr C$ is an object $\mathcal R$ with a multiplication $\mu:\mathcal R\otimes\mathcal R\to\mathcal R$ and a unit $e:\mathbb 1\to\mathcal R$, satisfying associativity, the two unit identities and commutativity $\mu\circ\beta_{\mathcal R,\mathcal R}=\mu$; these equations use the associativity and unit isomorphisms of $\mathscr C$. A morphism of ring objects is a morphism of $\mathscr C$ compatible with multiplications and units. In this section $\mathscr C$ is $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$ with the tensor product $\otimes_{\mathbf Z}$ of Definition “Tensor product of abelian sheaves” (`pur713-def-abelian-tensor`) and unit $\mathbf Z_X$, unless another symmetric monoidal category is named.
