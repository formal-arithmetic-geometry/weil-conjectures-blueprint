The presheaf $\underline{\overline{\mathbf Q}}_\ell$ of Definition [pur713-def-qbar-ring] is a sheaf on $*_{\Proet}$, without sheafification, hence a condensed ring. Source: governing reference, §3.1 (established there: coverings of $*_{\Proet}$ are refined by finite families, and finitely many finite subextensions have a finite compositum).
