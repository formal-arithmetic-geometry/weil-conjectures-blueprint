Let $\underline{\overline{\mathbf Q}}_\ell$ be the presheaf of commutative rings on $*_{\Proet}$ with

$$
\underline{\overline{\mathbf Q}}_\ell(S)=\{a\in C(S,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)\mid a(S)\subseteq F\text{ for some finite subextension }F/\mathbf Q_\ell\text{ of }\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\},
$$

$C(S,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ being formed with the valuation topology and the subextension being allowed to depend on $a$; restriction is composition. This is not the presheaf of all continuous maps to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
