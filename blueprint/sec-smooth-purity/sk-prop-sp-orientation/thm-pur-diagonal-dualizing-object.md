In the purity conventions of [def-pur-conventions],
let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth,
separated, of pure
relative dimension $d$. Then there is a canonical isomorphism

$$
\omega_f=Rf^!\Lambda_Y\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\Lambda_X(d)[2d].
$$
