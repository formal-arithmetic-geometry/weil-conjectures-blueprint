Let $T$ be a scheme. For $M\in D(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ the reductions
$M/\ell^n=M\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,T}}\mathcal O_{E,T}/\ell^n$ form an
object $\operatorname{red}_TM=(M/\ell^n)_n$ of
$D(\operatorname{Tow}(T,\mathcal O_E))$, functorial in $M$, whose reduction maps
are isomorphisms; this construction and normalization hold for arbitrary $T$.
If moreover $T$ is separated of finite type over $k$, the setting of the
integral local-structure lemma [sk-lem-int-integral-heart], and
$M\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$, then
$\operatorname{red}_TM\in\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^\bullet)_{\mathrm{norm}}$
([sk-def-sp-normalized-towers]), with the Tor amplitude bounds of $M$.
