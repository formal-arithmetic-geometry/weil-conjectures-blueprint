Let $\Lambda\in\{A,\Zl,\mathcal O_E,\Ql,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ be any admissible
coefficient ring, with $A$ finite, and let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $d$.
There is a canonical isomorphism, natural in $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$,

$$
\gamma_f(K):
f^{*}K(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^{!}K.
\tag{10.1}
$$

The isomorphism is compatible with
change of coefficients, base change and composition, and it is
normalized by the trace map of the affine line.
