For finite coefficients, $\omega_f$ can be computed on the
pro-étale site or, equivalently, on the étale site
(Proposition [sk-prop-ctf-finite-comparison]).

**Plan of the proof.**
We first prove the theorem for finite coefficients
(Section [sec-sp-grp-finite]). There the projection formula reduces it to an
isomorphism $\omega_f\simeq\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$, which is given locally on
$X$ by integration along affine lines and which we must show to be
independent of the chart. For $\mathcal O_E$ we take the
limit over $n$ of the isomorphisms with coefficients $\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$.
This is not automatic, because a compatible family of morphisms at the
finite levels need not come from a morphism over $\mathcal O_E$, and we
therefore repeat the construction for inverse systems. Coefficients in $E$
and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ then follow by inverting $\ell$ and passing to finite
extensions.
