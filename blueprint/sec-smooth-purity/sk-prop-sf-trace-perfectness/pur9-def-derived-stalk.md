For a scheme $X$, a geometric point $\bar x:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to X$ with $\Omega$ separably closed, and $K\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, put

$$
K_{\bar x}:=R\Gamma_{\operatorname{Spec}\Omega,\Proet,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\bigl(L\bar x^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K\bigr)\in D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}),
$$

with Definitions “Finite-coefficient derived inverse image” (`pur713-def-derived-pull`) and [pur7710-def-derived-sections]; here $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$ need not be of finite type over $k$, as permitted by Definition “Geometric data” (`pur713-def-data`).
