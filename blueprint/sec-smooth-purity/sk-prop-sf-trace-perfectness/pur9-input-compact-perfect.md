For $X$ as in Definition [pur9-def-compact-cohomology] and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the complex $R\Gamma_c(X,K)$ is perfect (Definition [pur9-def-perfect-complex]). *Status:* this is the detailed node `prop-trf-compact-support-perfectness`; its proof (boundedness and constructibility of the compact-support direct image, finiteness of the cohomology modules, and the fact that over the Artinian local ring $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$ a bounded complex with finitely generated cohomology is perfect exactly when it has finite Tor amplitude) is not given here. Through Definition [pur9-def-compact-cohomology] it presupposes Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint] for notation only; transferring the étale computations to the pro-étale functor requires the site comparison of Lemma “Invertibility of the candidate comparison” (`pur456-lem-candidate-site-iso`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]).
