For a scheme $X$ of finite type over $k$, $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and every geometric point $\bar x$ of $X$, the complex $K_{\bar x}$ of Definition [pur9-def-derived-stalk] is perfect. *Status:* part of the detailed node `prop-trf-compact-support-perfectness`; not proved here.
