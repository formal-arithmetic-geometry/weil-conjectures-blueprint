An object of $D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n})$, the ordinary derived category of $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-modules, is perfect if it is isomorphic to a bounded complex of finitely generated projective $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-modules.
