Let $X$ be a separated $k$-scheme of finite type with structure morphism $a_X:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$. Under Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint] (needed only for the notation of Definition [pur9-def-shriek-notation]), for $K\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ put

$$
R\Gamma_c(X,K):=R\Gamma_{\operatorname{Spec}k,\Proet,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\bigl(Ra_{X,!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K\bigr)\in D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}),
$$

with derived sections of Definition [pur7710-def-derived-sections].
