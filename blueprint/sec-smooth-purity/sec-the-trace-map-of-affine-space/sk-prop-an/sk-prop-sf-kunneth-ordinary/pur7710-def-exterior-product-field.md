In the situation of Definition [pur7710-def-exterior-product], for $r\geq1$, $K\in D(X_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})$ and $L\in D(Y_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})$, define

$$
K\boxtimes^L_{\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}L
:=Lp^*_{X,\Et,\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}K\otimes^L_{\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}Lp^*_{Y,\Et,\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}L
\in D(Z_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r}),
$$

with the functors of Definition [pur7710-def-finite-field-functors].
