Let $K$ be a separably closed
field and $X$ a scheme of finite type over $K$. Let $F$ be an
abelian sheaf on $X_{\Et}$ whose support
is contained in the set of
closed points of $X$. Then
$H^q(X_{\Et},F)=0$ for $q>0$.
