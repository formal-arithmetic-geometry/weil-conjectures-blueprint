The group-scheme axioms of [def-sc-group-scheme] hold for $\mathbf G_m$
because the identities $(t_1t_2)t_3=t_1(t_2t_3)$, $1\cdot t=t=t\cdot1$ and
$t^{-1}t=1$ hold in the Laurent polynomial ring; for $\mathbf G_a$ they are the
corresponding identities for addition.
