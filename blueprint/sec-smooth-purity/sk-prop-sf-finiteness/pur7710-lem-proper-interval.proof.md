If $f$ is an open immersion, it is an open and closed immersion, and Lemma “Extension by zero along an open and closed immersion” (`pur456-pbc-open-closed-extension`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) identifies $Rf_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ with $\mathrm D(f_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})=Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$. Otherwise $c_f=(\mathrm{id}_X,f)$ (Definition “Chosen compactifications” (`pur456-pbc-chosen-compactification`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])), so $Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}=Rf_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, and the invertible unit of Lemma “The étale open-extension adjunction” (`pur456-pbc-open-adjunction`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) for the identity open immersion identifies $\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ with the identity. Apply Lemma [pur7710-lem-compact-interval].
