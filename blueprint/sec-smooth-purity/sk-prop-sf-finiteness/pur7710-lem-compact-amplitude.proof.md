Let $\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$ be a geometric point, with fibres $X_{\bar y}\subseteq\overline X_{\bar y}$ and open immersion $j_{\bar y}$. Lemma “The derived proper-base-change comparison” (`pur456-pbc-derived`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) for the proper $p$ and the base change $\bar y$, together with Lemma “Open base change on the étale site” (`pur7710-lem-etale-open-base-change`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) for $j$, identifies the stalk $\mathcal H^q(Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F)_{\bar y}$ with $H^q((\overline X_{\bar y})_{\Et},j_{\bar y,!}(F|_{X_{\bar y}}))$, with its original coefficients. Let $C$ be the reduced closure of $X_{\bar y}$ in $\overline X_{\bar y}$. Every irreducible component of $C$ has a dense open subset in $X_{\bar y}$, so $\dim C\leq e$. For the closed immersion $i:C\hookrightarrow\overline X_{\bar y}$, the unit $j_{\bar y,!}(F|_{X_{\bar y}})\to i_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}i^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}j_{\bar y,!}(F|_{X_{\bar y}})$ is an isomorphism, since by Lemma “Exactness of étale extension by zero” (`pur456-pbc-open-exact`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) both sides have the same geometric stalks (zero outside $C$) and Input “Geometric stalks on the small étale site” (`pur456-pbc-input-stalks`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) applies. Closed direct image is exact on the étale site, so the cohomology on $\overline X_{\bar y}$ is the cohomology on $C$ of $i^*j_{\bar y,!}(F|_{X_{\bar y}})$. By Input “Forgetting finite coefficient actions in direct images” (`pur7710-input-cohomology-coefficients`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), this is the cohomology of the underlying torsion abelian sheaf, and Lemmas [pur7710-lem-separably-closed-cd] and [pur7710-lem-finite-type-cd], with $c=0$, give vanishing above $2\dim C\leq2e$. By Input “Geometric stalks on the small étale site” (`pur456-pbc-input-stalks`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), $\mathcal H^q(Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F)=0$ for $q>2e$. The argument bounds the support of the sheaf in the compactified fibre; it does not bound the dimension of the whole compactified fibre by $e$.
