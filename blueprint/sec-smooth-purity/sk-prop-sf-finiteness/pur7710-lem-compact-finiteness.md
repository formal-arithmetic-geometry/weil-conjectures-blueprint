Under Input [pur7710-input-proper-finiteness], for a separated morphism of finite type $f:X\to Y$ between $k$-schemes of finite type,

$$
Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\bigl(D^b_c(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\bigr)\subseteq
D^b_c(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n).
$$
