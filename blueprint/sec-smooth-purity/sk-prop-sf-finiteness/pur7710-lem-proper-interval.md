Under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur7710-lem-compact-amplitude], assume moreover that $f$ is proper. If $L\in D^+(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ has nonzero cohomology sheaves only in degrees $[a,b]$, then $Rf_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ has nonzero cohomology sheaves only in degrees $[a,b+2e]$.
