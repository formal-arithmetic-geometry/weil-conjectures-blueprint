For a proper morphism $p:Z\to Y$ between $k$-schemes of finite type, the functor $Rp_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived direct image” (`pur456-pbc-pushforward`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) sends $D^b_c(Z_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ into $D^b_c(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ (Definition [pur713-def-constructible-category-et]). *Status:* the proper finite-coefficient finiteness theorem, in the derived form of `lem-fin-proper-bounded-constructible`; imported. It uses $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$ as in Definition “Geometric data” (`pur713-def-data`) and is not inferred from proper base change alone.
