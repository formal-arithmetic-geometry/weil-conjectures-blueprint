Under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur7710-lem-compact-amplitude], if $a\leq b$ are integers and $L\in D^+(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ has nonzero cohomology sheaves only in degrees $[a,b]$, then $Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ has nonzero cohomology sheaves only in degrees $[a,b+2e]$.
