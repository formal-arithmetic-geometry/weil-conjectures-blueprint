For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow Z$ of $k$-schemes of finite type, the functor $Rj_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived direct image” (`pur456-pbc-pushforward`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) sends $D^b_c(U_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ into $D^b_c(Z_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. *Status:* Deligne's open-direct-image finiteness theorem in the finite allowable-coefficient form `cor-fin-deligne-open-finite-coefficients`; imported. No claim that it follows from a generic-constructibility sketch is made here.
