For an open immersion $j:X\hookrightarrow Z$ of $k$-schemes of finite type, $\mathrm D(j_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ of Lemma “Exactness of étale extension by zero” (`pur456-pbc-open-exact`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) sends $D^b_c(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ into $D^b_c(Z_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
