Present the input as $L\nu^*_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ with $L\in D^b_c(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ by Lemma [pur7710-lem-constructible-equivalence]. The map $\kappa_{f,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,L}$ of Definition “Comparison of compactification candidates across sites” (`pur456-def-candidate-site-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), an isomorphism by Lemma “Invertibility of the candidate comparison” (`pur456-lem-candidate-site-iso`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), identifies the compact-support value with $L\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$, which lies in $D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ by Lemmas [pur7710-lem-compact-finiteness] and [pur7710-lem-constructible-equivalence]. This result and Lemma [pur7710-lem-proet-finiteness] are at finite coefficients; neither asserts preservation for continuous-coefficient module objects.
