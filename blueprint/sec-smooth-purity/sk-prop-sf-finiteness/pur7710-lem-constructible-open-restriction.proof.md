Let $i_s:W_s\to W$ be a finite partition of $W$ by locally closed constructible subschemes on which $M$ is locally constant with finite values. The subschemes $U_s=W_s\times_WU$ form a finite partition of $U$ by locally closed constructible subschemes, with inclusions $i'_s:U_s\to U$ and open immersions $u_s:U_s\to W_s$, $ui'_s=i_su_s$. The composition isomorphisms of Lemma “Composition of étale inverse images” (`pur456-pbc-pullback-composition`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) identify $i'^*_su^*M$ with $u^*_si^*_sM$. The restriction to $U_s$ of a sheaf that is locally, on $(W_s)_{\Et}$, isomorphic to a constant sheaf with finite value is locally, on $(U_s)_{\Et}$, isomorphic to the same constant sheaf, since inverse image sends constant sheaves to constant sheaves.
