Under Input [pur7710-input-affine-cd], let $c,d\geq0$ be integers, $K$ a field with $\mathrm{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(K)\leq c$, and $W$ a $K$-scheme of finite type of dimension at most $d$. Then $\mathrm{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(W)\leq2d+c$. No separatedness assumption is imposed on $W$.
