Every étale $K$-scheme is a disjoint union of copies of $\operatorname{Spec}K$, since finite separable extensions of $K$ are trivial. Every covering family of such a scheme therefore has a section over each copy of $\operatorname{Spec}K$, so evaluation at $\operatorname{Spec}K$ identifies abelian sheaves on $(\operatorname{Spec}K)_{\Et}$ with abelian groups, and global sections with the identity functor. Global sections are therefore exact, and $H^q$ vanishes for $q>0$ on every abelian sheaf, in particular on every torsion sheaf.
