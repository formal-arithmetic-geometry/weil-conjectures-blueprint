If $X$ is empty the direct image is zero. Constructibility and boundedness on $Y$ may be tested on a finite affine open cover $\{Y_\beta\}$ of $Y$, since finite partitions of the $Y_\beta$ refine to a finite partition of $Y$. For an open $Y_\beta\subseteq Y$, restriction to $Y_\beta$ of $Rf_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K$ is the direct image along $f^{-1}(Y_\beta)\to Y_\beta$ of the restriction of $K$: open restriction is restriction along the localization functor, exact and preserving injectives (its left adjoint, extension by zero, is exact by Lemma “Exactness of étale extension by zero” (`pur456-pbc-open-exact`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])), and on sheaves both composites send $G$ to $V\mapsto G(V\times_YX)$ for $V$ étale over $Y_\beta$. So suppose $Y$ affine. Take a finite affine open cover $X_1,\ldots,X_r$ of $X$. Each nonempty finite intersection $X_J$ is a quasi-compact open subscheme of an affine $X_i$, hence separated of finite type over the affine $Y$; it need not be affine. By the Nagata compactification theorem [sk-thm-nagata-compactification], write $f_J=p_Jj_J$ with $j_J$ an open immersion and $p_J$ proper. The composition isomorphism of Lemma “Composition of étale derived direct images” (`pur456-pbc-push-composition`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) gives $Rf_{J,*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong Rp_{J,*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rj_{J,*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, and Inputs [pur7710-input-proper-finiteness] and [pur7710-input-open-finiteness] make its values on bounded constructible complexes bounded constructible. The restrictions of the given complex to the $X_J$ are bounded constructible by Lemma [pur7710-lem-constructible-open-restriction].

For a bounded-below injective resolution of the given complex, the augmented alternating Čech resolution for the finite open cover is exact degreewise. Restriction to each $X_J$ preserves injectives, since its left adjoint, extension by zero, is exact (Lemmas “Exactness of étale extension by zero” (`pur456-pbc-open-exact`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) and “The étale open-extension adjunction” (`pur456-pbc-open-adjunction`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])). Applying direct image and totalizing therefore computes $Rf_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ by the finite Čech diagram of the $Rf_{J,*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the restricted complexes. There are only finitely many $J$, in Čech degrees $0,\ldots,r-1$, so their cohomological ranges have a finite union. Input [pur7710-input-constructible-serre] preserves constructibility through the resulting finite spectral sequence and its finite abutment filtrations.
