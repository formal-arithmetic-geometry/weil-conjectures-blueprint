Let $c,d\geq0$ be integers and $K$ a field with $\mathrm{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(K)\leq c$ (Definition [pur7710-def-torsion-cd]). For every affine $K$-scheme $W$ of finite type and of dimension at most $d$, every torsion abelian sheaf $F$ on $W_{\Et}$ and every $q>d+c$, $H^q(W_{\Et},F)=0$. The quantifier includes nonconstructible torsion sheaves. *Status:* the input `thm-sc-affine-torsion-cd` of the detailed blueprint; the affine theorem and its field-descent prerequisites are imported, and their formalization bindings have not been checked.
