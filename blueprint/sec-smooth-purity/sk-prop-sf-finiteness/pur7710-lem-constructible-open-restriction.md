Let $u:U\hookrightarrow W$ be an open immersion of $k$-schemes of finite type and $M$ a constructible sheaf of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $W_{\Et}$ (Definition [pur713-def-finite-constructible]). Then $u^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M$ is constructible on $U_{\Et}$.
