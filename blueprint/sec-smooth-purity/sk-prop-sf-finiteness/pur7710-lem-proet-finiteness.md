Assume Inputs “The bounded-below site-comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-site-unit`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), [pur7710-input-constructible-sheaves], “The qcqs direct-image comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-qcqs-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), and the three inputs of Lemma [pur7710-lem-finite-type-finiteness]. For a morphism of finite type $f:X\to Y$ between $k$-schemes of finite type, $Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived direct image” (`pur713-def-derived-push`) sends $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ into $D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
