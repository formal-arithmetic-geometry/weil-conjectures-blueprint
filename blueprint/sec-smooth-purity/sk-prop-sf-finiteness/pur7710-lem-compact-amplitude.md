Assume Inputs “Forgetting finite coefficient actions in direct images” (`pur7710-input-cohomology-coefficients`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), [pur7710-input-affine-cd] and “Geometric stalks on the small étale site” (`pur456-pbc-input-stalks`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]). Let $f:X\to Y$ be a separated morphism of finite type between $k$-schemes of finite type, and $e\geq0$ an integer such that every geometric fibre $X_{\bar y}$ has dimension at most $e$. For every sheaf $F$ of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $X_{\Et}$, placed in degree zero, with $c_f=(j,p)$ the chosen compactification,

$$
\mathcal H^q(Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F)=0\qquad(q>2e).
$$

There is no constructibility hypothesis on $F$.
