The functor is exact (Lemma “Exactness of étale extension by zero” (`pur456-pbc-open-exact`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])), so it commutes with cohomology sheaves and preserves the cohomological range; it suffices to treat a constructible sheaf $M$ on $X_{\Et}$. A finite constructible partition of $X$ adapted to $M$, together with the closed complement $Z\setminus X$ with its reduced structure, is a finite constructible partition of $Z$. By the stalk description of Lemma “Exactness of étale extension by zero” (`pur456-pbc-open-exact`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), the restriction of $j_!M$ to a stratum contained in $X$ is the restriction of $M$, which is locally constant with finite values, and its restriction to $Z\setminus X$ has zero stalks, hence is zero by Input “Geometric stalks on the small étale site” (`pur456-pbc-input-stalks`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]).
