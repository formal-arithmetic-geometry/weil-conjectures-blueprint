Under Inputs [pur7710-input-constructible-serre], [pur7710-input-proper-finiteness] and [pur7710-input-open-finiteness], every morphism of finite type $f:X\to Y$ between $k$-schemes of finite type satisfies

$$
Rf_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\bigl(D^b_c(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\bigr)\subseteq
D^b_c(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n).
$$

The morphism $f$ need not be separated.
