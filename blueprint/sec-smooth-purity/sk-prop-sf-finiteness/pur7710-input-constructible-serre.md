On a $k$-scheme $W$ of finite type, kernels, cokernels and extensions of constructible sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $W_{\Et}$, in the sense of Definition [pur713-def-finite-constructible], are constructible. *Status:* the finite-coefficient Serre-subcategory theorem `thm-constructible-serre`; imported.
