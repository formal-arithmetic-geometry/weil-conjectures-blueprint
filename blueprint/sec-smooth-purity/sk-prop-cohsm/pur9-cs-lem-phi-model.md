Under the hypotheses of Definition [pur9-cs-def-phi], let $p_d:\Aaf^d_Y\to Y$ be the projection, with the purity comparison $\gamma_{p_d,K}$ of Definition [pur9-def-purity-comparison]. For $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$,

$$
\varphi_{p_d,K}\circ\bigl(1\otimes\gamma_{p_d,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y}\bigr)=\gamma_{p_d,K}
$$

as maps $Lp^*_{d,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^d_Y}(d)[2d]\to Rp^!_{d,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K$ in $D(\Aaf^d_{Y,\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, after the identification of the source with $Lp^*_{d,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K(d)[2d]$ by the twist conventions of Definition [pur9-def-tate-twist].
