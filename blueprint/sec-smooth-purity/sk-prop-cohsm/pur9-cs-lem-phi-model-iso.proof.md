Both $\gamma_{p_d,K}$ and $\gamma_{p_d,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y}$ are isomorphisms by Lemma [pur9-lem-vector-bundle-purity] applied to the trivial bundle of rank $d$, so $1\otimes\gamma_{p_d,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y}$ is invertible and Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-phi-model] gives $\varphi_{p_d,K}=\gamma_{p_d,K}\circ(1\otimes\gamma_{p_d,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y})^{-1}$, a composite of isomorphisms.
