Under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-phi-model], for $p_d:\Aaf^d_Y\to Y$ and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the map $\varphi_{p_d,K}$ of Definition [pur9-cs-def-phi] is an isomorphism in $D(\Aaf^d_{Y,\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
