Source and target lie in $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$: the source by Inputs [pur9-input-preservation] and [pur9-cs-input-constructible-closure], the target by Input [pur9-input-preservation]. By Lemma [pur7710-lem-constructible-equivalence] both are presented by bounded constructible étale complexes, so they belong to $\mathscr E^+(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ (Definition “The bounded-below étale-origin subcategory” (`pur456-def-etale-origin`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence])), and by Lemma [pur7710-lem-origin-stalk-detection] it suffices to show that $\varphi_{f,K}$ induces isomorphisms on all étale geometric stalks.

Charts. By the normal form of -prop-normal-formZZ, every $x\in X$ has an affine open neighbourhood $u_x:X_x\hookrightarrow X$, an affine open $w_x:Y_x\hookrightarrow Y$ containing $f(x)$ with $f(X_x)\subseteq Y_x$, and an étale $v_x:X_x\to\Aaf^d_{Y_x}$ with $p_d v_x=f|_{X_x}$, $p_d:\Aaf^d_{Y_x}\to Y_x$. Apply Input [pur9-cs-input-phi-local] twice: to the square $fu_x=w_x(p_dv_x)$, which identifies $Lu^*_{x,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\varphi_{f,K}$ with $\varphi_{p_dv_x,Lw^*_{x,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K}$; and to the square $p_dv_x=\mathrm{id}\circ(p_dv_x)$, which identifies $Lv^*_{x,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\varphi_{p_d,Lw^*_{x,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K}$ with $\varphi_{p_dv_x,Lw^*_{x,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K}$. The latter is an isomorphism by Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-phi-model-iso], hence so is $Lu^*_{x,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\varphi_{f,K}$.

Stalks. Let $\bar x$ be a geometric point of $X$ over $x$; it lifts to $X_x$. By Definition “Inverse image across the two sites” (`pur7710-def-site-pullback-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), $Lu^*_{x,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of an object presented by an étale complex $G$ is presented by $Lu^*_{x,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G$, and by Lemma “Stalks of étale inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback-stalk`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) the stalk of $u^*_{x,\Et}\mathcal H^r(G)$ at the lift is $\mathcal H^r(G)_{\bar x}$. Hence the stalk of $\mathcal H^r(\varphi_{f,K})$ at $\bar x$ is the stalk of $\mathcal H^r(Lu^*_{x,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\varphi_{f,K})$ at the lift, an isomorphism. This holds for every $r$ and $\bar x$.
