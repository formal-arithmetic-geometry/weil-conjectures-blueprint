Assume the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-tensor-formula] and Input [pur9-cs-input-site-monoidal]. For smooth separated $f:X\to Y$ of pure relative dimension $d$, there is a lisse sheaf of rank one $\mathcal L_f$ on $X_{\Et}$ (Definition [pur9-cs-def-lisse]) and an isomorphism $\omega_f\cong L\nu^*_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal L_f)[2d]$ in $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
