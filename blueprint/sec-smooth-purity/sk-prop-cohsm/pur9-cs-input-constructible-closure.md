For every morphism $f:X\to Y$ of separated $k$-schemes of finite type, $Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ carries $D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ into $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ into $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$; and for $K\in D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, $L\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the object $K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ lies in $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, and in $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ if $K$ does. The categories are those of Definitions [pur713-def-constructible-category] and [pur713-def-ctf]. *Status:* the corresponding étale statements are classical; their pro-étale form is not proved in this manuscript.
