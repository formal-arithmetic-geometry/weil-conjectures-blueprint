Assume the hypotheses of Definition [pur9-cs-def-phi] and Inputs “The bounded-below site-comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-site-unit`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), [pur7710-input-constructible-sheaves], [pur9-input-etale-normalization] and [pur9-cs-input-phi-local]. For every smooth separated $f:X\to Y$ of pure relative dimension $d$ and every $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the map $\varphi_{f,K}$ of Definition [pur9-cs-def-phi] is an isomorphism.
