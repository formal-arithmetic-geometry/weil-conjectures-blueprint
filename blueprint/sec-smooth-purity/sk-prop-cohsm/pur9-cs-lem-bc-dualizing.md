Under the hypotheses of Definition [pur9-cs-def-bc], Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-tensor-formula] and Inputs [pur9-cs-input-bc-phi] and [pur9-cs-input-bc-model], for smooth separated $f$ of pure relative dimension $d$ the map $b_{v,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y}$, followed by $Rf'^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the unit comparison $Lv^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{Y'}$, is an isomorphism $Lu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_f\xrightarrow{\sim}\omega_{f'}$ in $D(X'_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
