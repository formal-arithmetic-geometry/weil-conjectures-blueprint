For a separated $k$-scheme of finite type $W$, the functor $L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “The derived site inverse image” (`pur456-def-site-inverse-derived`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) carries a strong symmetric monoidal structure $L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(A\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}B)\cong L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}A\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}B$, $L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W$, for the derived tensor products of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`) on the two sites. *Status:* the sheaf functor $\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is an inverse image with constant coefficients; its monoidality and preservation of K-flat complexes are not established here.
