Under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-invertible], for smooth separated $f:X\to Y$ of pure relative dimension $d$, the object $\omega_f$ is invertible in $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ in the sense of Definition [pur9-cs-def-invertible], with inverse $L\nu^*_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal L_f^{-1})[-2d]$, where $\mathcal L_f$ is the sheaf of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-invertible] and $\mathcal L_f^{-1}:=\mathcal Hom(\mathcal L_f,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X)$ is the internal Hom of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $X_{\Et}$.
