The square with vertices $W,X,X,Y$, arrows $p_2:W\to X$, $p_1:W\to X$, $f$ and $f$, is Cartesian, and $f$ is smooth separated of pure relative dimension $d$; apply Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-bc-dualizing] with $v=f$. Only the isomorphism for $p_1$ is used below; the one for $p_2$ follows by exchanging the factors.
