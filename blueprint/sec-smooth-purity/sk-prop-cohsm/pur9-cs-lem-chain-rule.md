Under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-tensor-formula], for smooth separated $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to S$ there is an isomorphism $\omega_{gf}\cong Lf^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_g\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_f$.
