Under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-bc-dualizing], let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth separated, $W=X\times_YX$ with projections $p_1,p_2$. Then the map of Lemma [pur9-cs-lem-bc-dualizing] for the Cartesian square with $v=f$, $f'=p_1$ and $u=p_2$ is an isomorphism $Lp^*_{2,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_f\xrightarrow{\sim}\omega_{p_1}$ in $D(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
