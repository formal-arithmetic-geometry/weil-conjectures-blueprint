For a scheme $W$, the finite reduction

$$
\mathrm{red}_{n,m}:D(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\longrightarrow D(W_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^m)
$$

is the total left derived functor, computed on K-flat resolutions (Definition “K-flat complexes of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules” (`pur713-def-kflat`)), of the sheaf functor $M\mapsto(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^m)_W\otimes_{(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W}M$, with the $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^m$-action of the left factor. For $m=n$ it is identified with the identity functor by the unit isomorphism of the tensor product.
