$\mu_{\ell^N,W}(U)$ is the set of $W$-morphisms $U\to W\times_k\operatorname{Spec}k[x]/(x^{\ell^N}-1)$. As $x^{\ell^N}-1$ is separable with all roots in $k$ (proof of Lemma [pur9-lem-twist-free]), $\operatorname{Spec}k[x]/(x^{\ell^N}-1)$ is the disjoint union of $\ell^N$ copies of $\operatorname{Spec}k$ indexed by $\mu_{\ell^N}(k)$. Morphisms from $U$ into a disjoint union of copies of $W$ are locally constant functions $U\to\mu_{\ell^N}(k)$, which are the sections of the constant sheaf.
