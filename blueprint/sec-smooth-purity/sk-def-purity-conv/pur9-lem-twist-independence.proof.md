The map $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{\ell^{N'-N}}$ sends a generator of the cyclic group $\mu_{\ell^{N'}}(k)$ to an element of order $\ell^N$, i.e. to a generator of $\mu_{\ell^N}(k)$. After $-\otimes\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$, both sides are free of rank one by Lemma [pur9-lem-twist-free] with these generators as bases ($\ell^N$ kills $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$), and $\rho_{N',N}$ sends basis to basis. Transitivity holds because $(\zeta^{\ell^{N''-N'}})^{\ell^{N'-N}}=\zeta^{\ell^{N''-N}}$. Tensor powers and duals of isomorphisms are isomorphisms, and the equations are preserved.
