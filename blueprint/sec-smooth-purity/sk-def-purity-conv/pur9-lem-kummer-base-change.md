For a morphism $u:W'\to W$ of $k$-schemes and $t\in\Gamma(W,\mathcal O_W^\times)$, the inverse-image map $H^1(W_{\Et},(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W(1))\to H^1(W'_{\Et},(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{W'}(1))$, induced by the unit of $Lu^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Ru_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ (Lemma “The étale derived inverse-image adjunction” (`pur456-pbc-adjunction`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])) and $u^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W(1)\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{W'}(1)$, sends $\kappa(t)$ to $\kappa(u^*t)$.
