Classically $\mathcal D_sV$ is described as $\operatorname{Bl}_{s(B)\times\{0\}}(V\times_B\Aaf^1_B)$ minus the strict transform of $V\times_B\{0\}$. That description is not part of Definition [pur9-def-deformation-space] and is not used; the properties of the fibres of $\widetilde g$ are proved in -lem-geometryZZ.
