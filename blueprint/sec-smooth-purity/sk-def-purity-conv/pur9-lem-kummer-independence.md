For admissible $N\leq N'$, the classes $\kappa(t)$ of Definition [pur9-def-kummer-class] formed with $N'$ and with $N$ correspond under the isomorphism induced by $\rho_{N',N}$ of Lemma [pur9-lem-twist-independence].
