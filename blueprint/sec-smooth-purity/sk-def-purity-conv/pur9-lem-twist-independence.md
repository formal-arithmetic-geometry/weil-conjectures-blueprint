title = "Independence of the admissible exponent"

For admissible $N\leq N'$, the homomorphism $\mu_{\ell^{N'}}(k)\to\mu_{\ell^N}(k)$, $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{\ell^{N'-N}}$, induces an isomorphism $\rho_{N',N}:P_{N'}(1)\to P_N(1)$. For admissible $N\leq N'\leq N''$ one has $\rho_{N',N}\rho_{N'',N'}=\rho_{N'',N}$ and $\rho_{N,N}=\mathrm{id}$. The induced maps on tensor powers and duals have the same properties.
