For a separated $k$-scheme $Y$ of finite type, $\Aaf^1_Y=\Aaf^1_k\times_kY$ has coordinate $t$, projection $p:\Aaf^1_Y\to Y$, sections $i_0=s:Y\to\Aaf^1_Y$ ($t=0$) and $i_1:Y\to\Aaf^1_Y$ ($t=1$), open complement $j:\mathbf G_{m,Y}\hookrightarrow\Aaf^1_Y$ of $s(Y)$, and $\pi=pj:\mathbf G_{m,Y}\to Y$. The section $i_1$ factors through $\mathbf G_{m,Y}$; its factorization is again written $i_1$. The coordinate $t$ restricts to a unit on $\mathbf G_{m,Y}$.
