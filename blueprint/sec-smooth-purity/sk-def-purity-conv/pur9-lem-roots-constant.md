title = "Roots of unity form a constant sheaf"
order = 2232
tags = ["latex:bplemma"]

For a $k$-scheme $W$ and $N\geq1$, let $\mu_{\ell^N,W}$ be the sheaf on $W_{\Et}$ sending $U$ to the group of $\ell^N$-th roots of unity in $\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)$. The inclusion $\mu_{\ell^N}(k)\subseteq k^\times$ induces an isomorphism from the constant sheaf with value $\mu_{\ell^N}(k)$ onto $\mu_{\ell^N,W}$.
