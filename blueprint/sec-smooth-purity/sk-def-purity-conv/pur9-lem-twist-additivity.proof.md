All modules are free of rank one (Lemma [pur9-lem-twist-free]); the evaluation pairing of a free rank-one module with its dual is an isomorphism, and concatenation is associative, so the maps are isomorphisms satisfying associativity. For complexes, use the associativity isomorphism of the derived tensor product (Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`)) and the isomorphism of constant sheaves induced by the module isomorphism; the constant sheaf on $P(a)\otimes P(b)$ is the tensor product of the constant sheaves, both being sheafifications of the corresponding constant presheaves (Definition “Tensor product of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules” (`pur713-def-abelian-tensor-finite`)), and a constant sheaf of free modules is flat, so the underived tensor product computes the derived one.
