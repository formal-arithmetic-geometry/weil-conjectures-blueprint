Inverse image is exact (Lemma “Exactness of étale inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback-exact`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])) and carries the Kummer sequence of $W$ to a sequence mapping to the Kummer sequence of $W'$ by the natural maps on $\mu$ and $\mathbb G_m$.
