For $a,b\in\mathbf Z$ there are isomorphisms $P(a)\otimes_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}P(b)\to P(a+b)$, given for $a,b\geq0$ by concatenation of tensor factors and otherwise by concatenation followed by the evaluation pairing $P(1)\otimes P(-1)\to\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$ on adjacent factors of opposite sign; they are associative. Consequently there are natural isomorphisms $(K(a))(b)\cong K(a+b)$ on $D(W_\tau,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, associative in $a,b,c$.
