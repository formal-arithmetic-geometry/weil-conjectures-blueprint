The maps $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{\ell^{N'-N}}$, $\omega\mapsto\omega^{\ell^{N'-N}}$ and $\mathrm{id}$ form a morphism from the Kummer sequence for $\ell^{N'}$ to the one for $\ell^N$: the right square commutes because $(\omega^{\ell^{N'-N}})^{\ell^N}=\omega^{\ell^{N'}}$. Connecting homomorphisms are natural for morphisms of short exact sequences, so $\kappa_{N'}(t)\mapsto\kappa_N(t)$. The maps $\mu\to P(1)$ of Definition [pur9-def-kummer-class] are compatible with $\rho_{N',N}$ by its definition.
