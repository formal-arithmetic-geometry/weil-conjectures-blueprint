Since $\ell\ne p$, the polynomial $x^{\ell^N}-1$ is separable over $k$, and $k$ is algebraically closed, so $\mu_{\ell^N}(k)$ has $\ell^N$ elements. As a finite subgroup of $k^\times$ it is cyclic, hence free of rank one over $\mathbf Z/\ell^N$. Base change to $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$ gives a free module of rank one; tensor powers and duals of free modules of rank one are free of rank one.
