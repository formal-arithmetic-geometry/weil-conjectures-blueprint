Additivity and the unit are clear. For multiplicativity, a product $(f\,t^{-r})(f'\,t^{-r'})$ with $r>0$ or $r'>0$ and $r+r'\leq0$ has $f\in\mathcal I^r$ or $f'\in\mathcal I^{r'}$ with positive exponent, so $s^*(ff')=0$, matching the product $0$ of the images; the remaining cases are the multiplicativity of $s^*$. The composite $\mathcal O_B[t]\to\mathcal R_s\to\mathcal O_B[t]$ is $t\mapsto t$ on $t$ and $gs=\mathrm{id}$ on $\mathcal O_B$, whence $\widetilde g\widetilde s=\mathrm{id}$.
