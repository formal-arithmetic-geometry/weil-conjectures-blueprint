For $r\geq1$, $\mathcal I^rt^{-r}=(\mathcal I\,t^{-1})^r$, so $\mathcal R_s$ is generated as an $\mathcal O_V[t]$-algebra by $\mathcal I\,t^{-1}$. Since $V$ is noetherian, $\mathcal I$ is coherent, hence locally finitely generated; so $\mathcal R_s$ is a quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_V[t]$-algebra of finite type, and $\mathcal D_sV=\operatorname{Spec}_V(\mathcal R_s)$ is affine of finite type over $\operatorname{Spec}_V\mathcal O_V[t]=V\times_k\Aaf^1_k$. The morphism $\widetilde g$ is this affine morphism followed by $g\times\mathrm{id}:V\times_k\Aaf^1_k\to B\times_k\Aaf^1_k=\Aaf^1_B$, which is separated of finite type because $g$ is; affine morphisms are separated, and composites of separated morphisms of finite type are separated of finite type.
