Let $W$ be a $k$-scheme of finite type and $M$ an $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-module. Under Input “The site comparison is a morphism of sites” (`pur456-input-site-morphism`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), $L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “The derived site inverse image” (`pur456-def-site-inverse-derived`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) carries the constant sheaf $M_W$ on $W_{\Et}$, in degree zero, to the constant sheaf $M_W$ on $W_{\Proet}$, by a natural isomorphism. In particular $L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W(m))\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W(m)$.
