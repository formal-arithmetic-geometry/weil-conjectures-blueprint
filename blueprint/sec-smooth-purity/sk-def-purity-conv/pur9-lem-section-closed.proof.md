The morphism $s$ is the base change of the diagonal $\Delta_{V/B}:V\to V\times_BV$, a closed immersion since $g$ is separated, along $(sg,\mathrm{id}_V):V\to V\times_BV$: the fibre product is the locus where $sg(v)=v$, and $s:B\to$ this locus is an isomorphism with inverse $g$. Closed immersions are stable under base change.
