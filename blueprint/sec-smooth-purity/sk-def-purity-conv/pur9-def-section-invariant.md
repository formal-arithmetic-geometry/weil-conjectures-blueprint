Let $g:V\to B$ be a smooth (in the sense of -def-smoothZZ) separated morphism of separated $k$-schemes of finite type, and $s:B\to V$ a section. Under Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint], the section invariant is

$$
\mathcal C(g,s):=Ls^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_g\in D(B_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n),
$$

with $\omega_g$ of Definition [pur9-def-dualizing] and $Ls^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived inverse image” (`pur713-def-derived-pull`). By Lemma [pur9-lem-section-closed], $s$ is a closed immersion.
