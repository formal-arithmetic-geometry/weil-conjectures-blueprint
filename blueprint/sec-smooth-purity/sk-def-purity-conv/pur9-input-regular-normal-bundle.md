If $i$ is regular of pure codimension $c$ (Definition [pur9-def-regular-immersion]), then $\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2$ is a locally free $\mathcal O_Z$-module of rank $c$ with basis the images of any frame, and the natural map $\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathcal O_Z}(\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2)\to\bigoplus_{r\geq0}\mathcal I^r/\mathcal I^{r+1}$ is an isomorphism; hence $N_{Z/X}\to Z$ is the vector bundle of rank $c$ attached to $\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2$ (Definition [pur9-def-vector-bundle]), with the same zero section. *Status:* commutative algebra of regular sequences; imported, not proved here.
