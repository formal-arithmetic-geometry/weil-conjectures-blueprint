Under Input [pur9-input-linear-invariance-trace] and the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-lem-bundle-compact-concentration], there is a unique morphism $\int_\pi:R\pi_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_V(c))[2c]\to(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z$ whose restriction to every trivializing open $U$, under the identification of Lemma [pur9-lem-bundle-compact-concentration] and the trivialization, is $\int_{p_c}$ over $U$. It is an isomorphism.
