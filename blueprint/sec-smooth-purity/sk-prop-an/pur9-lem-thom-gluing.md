Under Input [pur9-input-linear-invariance-thom] and in the situation of Lemma [pur9-lem-thom-concentration], there is a unique morphism $\mathrm{th}_V:(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z\to Rz^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_V(c))[2c]$ whose restriction to every open $U\subseteq Z$ with a trivialization, transported by $r_{z,u}$ of Lemma [pur9-lem-open-restriction-upper-shriek] and the trivialization, is $\mathrm{th}_c$ over $U$.
