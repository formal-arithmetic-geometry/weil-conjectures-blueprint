For $Y$ separated of finite type over $k$, let $q:\mathbf P^1_Y\to Y$ be the projective line, $\bar\jmath:\Aaf^1_Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^1_Y$ the standard open immersion with complement the section $\infty:Y\to\mathbf P^1_Y$. Then $d_p:=(\bar\jmath,q)$ is a compactification of $p:\Aaf^1_Y\to Y$. Let $h\in H^2(\mathbf P^1_{Y,\Et},(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)(1))$ be the image of the class of $\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1_Y}(1)$ under $\Pic(\mathbf P^1_Y)=H^1(\mathbf P^1_{Y,\Et},\mathbb G_m)$ (§[sk-prop-hilbert90]), the Kummer connecting map to $H^2(\mu_{\ell^n})$, and the map to the twist of Definition [pur9-def-kummer-class].
