For $Y$ as in Definition [pur9-def-projective-line] and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the morphism $Lq^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K(1)[2]\to Rq^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K$ corresponding under the standing proper adjunction to $(t_q\otimes1)\circ\operatorname{P}^{-1}$, where $\operatorname{P}$ is the direct-image projection transformation of Definition [pur713-def-star-projection] for $q$ and the twist sheaf, is an isomorphism. *Status:* the older proof exhibits an explicit adjunction $Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv q^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(-)(1)[2]$ with unit induced by the class of the diagonal of $\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y$ and checks the triangle identities on fibres; imported (detailed blueprint `thm:amb-p1-adjunction`, `cor:amb-affine-space-adjunction`), not proved here. The invertibility of $\operatorname{P}$ used in the statement is part of this input.
