Lemma [pur9-lem-open-closed-triangle] for $(\bar\jmath,\infty)$ and $M=(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf P^1_Y}(1)[2]$, followed by $Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and $Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}R\infty_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong R(q\infty)_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm{id}$ (Definition “Composition comparison for finite-coefficient derived direct image” (`pur713-def-push-composition`); $\infty$ is a closed immersion, so $\infty^*$ and $R\infty_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ are those of the triangle), gives a triangle $Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathrm D(\bar\jmath_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\bar\jmath^*M\to Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M\to\infty^*M\to$. Under the pro-étale form of Input [pur9-input-p1-cohomology] (via $\lambda_q$ and Lemma [pur9-lem-site-inverse-constant]), $Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y(1)[2]\oplus(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y$, and the second map is $(1,0)$: on the first summand it is the unit followed by restriction along the section $\infty$, the identity since $q\infty=\mathrm{id}$; on the second it is $\infty^*h=0$ by Lemma [pur9-lem-p1-infinity]. In a triangulated category, a distinguished triangle $A\to X\oplus X'\xrightarrow{(1,0)}X\to$ forces the composite $A\to X\oplus X'\to X'$ to be an isomorphism. This composite is $t_q\circ Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\text{counit})$; precomposing with the isomorphism $\theta_{d_p}^{-1}$ gives $\int_p$.
