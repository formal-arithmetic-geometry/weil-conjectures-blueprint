Let $\pi:V\to Z$ be a vector bundle of rank $c$ with zero section $z$ (Definition [pur9-def-vector-bundle]), $Z$ separated of finite type over $k$. Then $\mathcal H^m(Rz^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_V(c))[2c])=0$ for $m\neq0$.
