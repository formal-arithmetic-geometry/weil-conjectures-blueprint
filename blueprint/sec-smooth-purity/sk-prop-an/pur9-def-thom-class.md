Under the hypotheses of Lemma “Gluing the Thom class” (`pur9-lem-thom-gluing`), the Thom class of $\pi:V\to Z$ is the morphism $\mathrm{th}_V$ of that lemma. For $V=\Aaf^c_B$ with its standard trivialization, $\mathrm{th}_V=\mathrm{th}_c$.
