Under Input “Cohomology of the projective line” (`pur9-input-p1-cohomology`), $t^{\Et}_q:Rq_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)(1))[2]\to(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y$ is the inverse of the isomorphism of that input followed by the projection to the second summand. The pro-étale trace $t_q:Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf P^1_Y}(1))[2]\to(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y$ is $L\nu^*_Y(t^{\Et}_q)$ precomposed with $\lambda_q^{-1}$ (Definition “The canonical direct-image comparison” (`pur7710-def-direct-image-comparison`, in [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), invertible by Input “The qcqs direct-image comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-qcqs-comparison`, in [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence])) and Lemma “Site inverse image of constant sheaves” (`pur9-lem-site-inverse-constant`, in [sk-def-purity-conv]).
