The constant sheaf is of étale origin (Lemma [pur9-lem-site-inverse-constant]). For the open immersion $u:U\hookrightarrow Z$, Lemma [pur7710-lem-origin-cbc] (under Input [pur7710-input-site-exchange]) and Definition [pur713-def-shriek-exchange-chosen], with the comparison $\theta_{c'}$ of Input [pur713-input-comparison] contained in Input [pur9-input-ambient-comparison], make the exchange $\operatorname{Ex}^\circ_{!}$ for $\pi$ and $u$ an isomorphism. Restriction to $U$ is $\mathrm D(u^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\cong Lu^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$. By Lemma [pur9-lem-affine-space-trace-iso] the restricted object is isomorphic to $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_U$ in degree zero, and vanishing of cohomology sheaves is checked on an open covering by the sheaf axiom.
