An integer $N\geq1$ is *admissible* if $\ell^N\cdot(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)=0$; $N=n$ is admissible, because $\ell\in\pi_E\mathcal O_E$. Let $\mu_{\ell^N}(k)\subseteq k^\times$ be the group of $\ell^N$-th roots of unity, a $\mathbf Z/\ell^N$-module. For admissible $N$ put

$$
P_N(1):=\mu_{\ell^N}(k)\otimes_{\mathbf Z/\ell^N}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n,
$$

an $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-module. For $m\geq0$ let $P_N(m)$ be the $m$-fold tensor power of $P_N(1)$ over $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$, with $P_N(0)=\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$, and for $m<0$ put $P_N(m):=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}(P_N(-m),\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$.
