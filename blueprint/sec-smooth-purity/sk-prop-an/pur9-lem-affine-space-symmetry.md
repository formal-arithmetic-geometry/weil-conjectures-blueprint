Under Input [pur9-input-linear-invariance-trace], for $g\in\mathrm{GL}_c(\Gamma(B,\mathcal O_B))$, in particular a permutation of the coordinates, $\gamma_{p_c,K}$ is carried to itself by the automorphisms of $Lp^*_{c,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K(c)[2c]$ and $Rp^!_{c,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K$ induced by $g$ (via $Lg^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lp^*_{c,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong Lp^*_{c,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and $Rg^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rp^!_{c,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong Rp^!_{c,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, $Rg^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}=\mathrm D(g^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$). No sign appears.
