Under the inputs of Lemma “Acyclicity of the affine line on the pro-étale site” (`pur9-lem-a1-acyclic-proet`, in [sk-prop-a1-kummer]), let $\kappa^{\Proet}_Y:(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_Y\to\mathcal H^1(R\pi_{*,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y}}(1))$ be $\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}(\kappa_Y)$ of Definition [pur9-def-kummer-generator], followed by the isomorphism $\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}\mathcal H^1(R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}(-))\cong\mathcal H^1(L\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}(-))\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^1(\lambda_\pi)}\mathcal H^1(R\pi_{*,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L\nu^*_{\mathbf G_{m,Y},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}(-))$ and Lemma “Site inverse image of constant sheaves” (`pur9-lem-site-inverse-constant`, in [sk-def-purity-conv]).
