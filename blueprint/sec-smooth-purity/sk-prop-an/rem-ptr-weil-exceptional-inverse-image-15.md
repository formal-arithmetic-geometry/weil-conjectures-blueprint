In the notation of [def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image], what the two displayed typings use
in addition, namely that the values of the right adjoint lie again in
$D^b_c$ and in $D^b_{ctf}$, is no longer an assumption on those two
categories either: for every finite coefficient ring, and with constants
independent of the stage, it is the theorem
[thm-sf-preservation-class], whose two clauses for a general separated
finite-type $f$ are those of [lem-weil-verdier-preservation-strata].
