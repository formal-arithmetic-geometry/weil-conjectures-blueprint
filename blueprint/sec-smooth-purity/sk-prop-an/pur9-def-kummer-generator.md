For $Y$ as in Lemma “Acyclicity of the affine line” (`pur9-lem-a1-acyclic`, in [det-prop-a1-kummer]), let $\kappa_Y:(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_Y\to\mathcal H^1(R\pi_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_{\mathbf G_{m,Y}}(1))$ be the morphism of sheaves sending $1$ to the global section defined by $\kappa(t)\in H^1(\mathbf G_{m,Y,\Et},(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)(1))$ (Definition [pur9-def-kummer-class], $t$ the coordinate), via the edge map $H^1R\Gamma(Y,R\pi_*(-))\to\Gamma(Y,\mathcal H^1R\pi_*(-))$ and $R\Gamma(Y,R\pi_*(-))\cong R\Gamma(\mathbf G_{m,Y},-)$ (Lemma “Composition of étale derived direct images” (`pur456-pbc-push-composition`, in [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])).
