Let $u':V_U\hookrightarrow V$ be the inclusion over a trivializing open $u:U\hookrightarrow Z$. By Lemmas [pur9-lem-open-immersion-upper-shriek] and [pur9-lem-upper-shriek-composition], $\mathrm D(u'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})R\pi^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong R\pi^!_{U,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathrm D(u^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$. Under this identification, $\mathrm D(u'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})(\gamma_{\pi,K})$ corresponds to $\gamma_{\pi_U,u^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K}$: their adjoints are built from $\operatorname{PF}$ and $\int$, which restrict to the corresponding maps over $U$ by the projection equation in Input [pur9-input-ambient-comparison] (for the open base change $u$) and by Lemma [pur9-lem-bundle-trace-gluing]. Through the trivialization $\gamma_{\pi_U}$ is $\gamma_{p_c}$, an isomorphism by Lemma [pur9-lem-affine-space-purity]. Lemma [pur7710-lem-cover-detection] for the open covering concludes. For $K=(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z$, $L\pi^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the constant sheaf is the constant sheaf (Lemma “The finite-coefficient derived inverse image is levelwise” (`pur713-lem-finite-pull-levelwise`)), giving the last assertion by Definition [pur9-def-dualizing].
