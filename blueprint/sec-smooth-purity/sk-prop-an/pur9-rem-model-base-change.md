The older statement also asserted compatibility of $\gamma_{\pi,K}$ with arbitrary base change in $Z$. With the present definitions this requires, besides Lemma [pur7710-lem-origin-cbc] and the exchange equation of Input [pur9-input-ambient-comparison], the compatibility of $\operatorname{PF}$ with compact-support exchange and the base-change part of Input [pur9-input-p1-cohomology] for $t_q$; the former is not established here, so base-change compatibility of $\gamma$ is not asserted. Compatibility with restriction to open subschemes is part of the proof of Lemma [pur9-lem-vector-bundle-purity].
