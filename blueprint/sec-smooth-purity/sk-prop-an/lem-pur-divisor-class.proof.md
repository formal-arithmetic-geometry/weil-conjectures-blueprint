Use the frame class of [def-pur-frame-class], independent of the local equation by [lem-pur-frame-independence], and the support boundary of [def-pur-support-cohomology] applied to the Kummer datum of [def-pur-affine-line-datum].
(a) is (def. frame class) $\operatorname{cl}(f)=\partial(\kappa(f))$.

For (c), put $W=U\setminus(D_1\cup D_2)$. The Kummer class is the image of a
unit under the connecting map
$H^0(-,\mathbf G_m)\to H^1(-,\mu_{\ell^N})$ of the Kummer sequence of
[def-pur-affine-line-datum], followed by the coefficient extension; both
are group homomorphisms, so $\kappa(f_1f_2)=\kappa(f_1)+\kappa(f_2)$ in
$H^1(W,\Lambda(1))$. The support boundary
$\partial:H^1(W,\Lambda(1))\to H^2_{D_1\cup D_2}(U,\Lambda(1))$ is a
homomorphism, and it is natural for the map of pairs
$(U,D_i)\to(U,D_1\cup D_2)$: the boundary of the restriction of
$\kappa(f_i)$ from $U\setminus D_i$ to $W$ is the extension of supports of
$\partial\kappa(f_i)$, because sections supported on $D_i$ are sections
supported on $D_1\cup D_2$ and the two support sequences of
[def-pur-support-cohomology] form a commutative ladder. Applying $\partial$
to the additivity of $\kappa$ gives (c). If $D_1$, $D_2$ and $D_1+D_2$ are
smooth over $B$ of pure codimension one, then on an open $U$ where
$D_1,D_2$ have local equations $f_1,f_2$, the product $f_1f_2$ is a local
equation of $D_1+D_2$, so by (a) and (c) the global classes
$\operatorname{cl}(D_1+D_2)$ and $\operatorname{cl}(D_1)+\operatorname{cl}(D_2)$
agree on every such $U$; they agree globally because they are glued from
these local classes (the definition of $\operatorname{cl}$ via
[lem-pur-frame-independence]).

For (b) we show $c_D=-\operatorname{cl}(D)$, where
$c_D\in H^2_D(T,\Lambda(1))$ is the class of [lem-amb-cartier-kummer-map];
since $c_D$ forgets support to $c_1(\mathcal O_T(D))$ and
$c_1(\mathcal O_T(-D))=-c_1(\mathcal O_T(D))$ by
[lem-pur-kummer-additivity], this gives (b). Both classes are global
classes supported on $D$ that are determined by their restrictions to the
members of a cover by opens $U$ on which $D$ has a local equation $f$ (this
is how $\operatorname{cl}(D)$ is defined globally), so it suffices to compare
them on such a $U$. Put $U^\circ=U\setminus D$ and work first with
$\mu_{\ell^N}$-coefficients.

*The relative class.* Let
$\partial_{\mathbf G}:H^0(U^\circ,\mathbf G_m)\to H^1_{D\cap U}(U,\mathbf G_m)$
be the support boundary for $\mathbf G_m$. It sends a unit $g$ on $U^\circ$
to the trivial line bundle together with the trivialization $g$ on $U^\circ$.
Multiplication by $f$ is an isomorphism
$\mathcal O_U(D)=f^{-1}\mathcal O_U\to\mathcal O_U$ carrying the canonical
section $1$ to $f$, so $\partial_{\mathbf G}(f)$ is the relative class of
$\mathcal O_T(D)$ with its canonical trivialization used in
[lem-amb-cartier-kummer-map], and $c_D|_U=\delta(\partial_{\mathbf G}(f))$,
where $\delta$ is the Kummer boundary on cohomology supported on $D\cap U$.
The same relative class is the boundary of $1$ in the divisor extension
$0\to\mathbf G_m\to\mathcal M_D\to i_*\mathbf Z_D\to0$, where
$\mathcal M_D\subset v_*\mathbf G_m$ ($v:T\setminus D\hookrightarrow T$) is
generated by $\mathbf G_m$ and $f^{-1}$ and $f^{-1}\mapsto1$, so that the
lifts of $1$ are the local generators of $\mathcal O_T(D)$; and the sign
is checked directly: if $\mathbf G_m\to I^\bullet$ is an injective resolution and
$\psi:\mathcal M_D\to I^0$ extends its augmentation, the connecting
morphism $i_*\mathbf Z_D\to\mathbf G_m[1]$ sends $1$ to the class of
$-d\psi(f^{-1})\in\Gamma_{D\cap U}(U,I^1)$ (on a horseshoe resolution
$I^\bullet\oplus J^\bullet$ of $\mathcal M_D$ with differential
$(a,b)\mapsto(da+hb,db)$, the connecting morphism is $h$ composed with the
augmentation of $J^\bullet$, and the augmentation $(\psi,\epsilon_J\pi)$ of
$\mathcal M_D$ is a chain map only if $h\epsilon_J\pi=-d\psi$), and
$\psi(f^{-1})$ extends $f^{-1}$ from $U^\circ$, so this class is
$-\partial_{\mathbf G}(f^{-1})=\partial_{\mathbf G}(f)$.

*The two boundaries anticommute.* By (a),
$\operatorname{cl}(D)|_U=\partial(\delta(f))$, with $\delta$ now the Kummer
boundary on $U^\circ$ and $\partial$ the support boundary for $\mu_{\ell^N}$.
Choose a short exact sequence of injective resolutions of the Kummer sequence
and apply sections supported on $D\cap U$, sections on $U$ and sections on
$U^\circ$. This gives a $3\times3$ diagram of complexes with exact rows (the
Kummer direction) and exact columns (the support direction, exact because
injective sheaves are flasque), and $\delta$, $\partial$ are the connecting
maps of its rows and columns. For a $3\times3$ diagram of complexes the two
composite connecting maps from the corner $(3,3)$ to the corner $(1,1)$ are
opposite: lift a cocycle $z$ of the corner $(3,3)$ to $x$ in the centre;
then $dx=b+g$ with $b$ in the corner $(2,1)$ and $g$ in the corner $(1,2)$,
the two composites are the classes of $db$ and $dg$, and
$db+dg=d^2x=0$. Applied to $z=f$,

$$
\operatorname{cl}(D)|_U=\partial\delta(f)=-\delta\partial_{\mathbf G}(f)=-c_D|_U.
$$

Hence $c_D=-\operatorname{cl}(D)$ with $\mu_{\ell^N}$-coefficients, and
with $\Lambda(1)$-coefficients after the coefficient extension of
[def-pur-affine-line-datum], which commutes with both boundaries.
