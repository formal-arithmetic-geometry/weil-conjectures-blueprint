An $\mathcal O_S$-module $\mathcal E$ on a scheme $S$ is
*locally free of constant rank $d$* if $S$ has a Zariski
open cover $(V_a)$ with
$\mathcal E|_{V_a}\cong\mathcal O_{V_a}^{\oplus d}$.

In particular it is a quasi-coherent module.
