Under Inputs [pur9-input-linear-invariance-thom], [pur9-input-linear-invariance-trace], [pur9-input-p1-duality], [pur9-input-projection-composition] and [pur9-input-zero-section-class], for a vector bundle $\pi:V\to Z$ of rank $c$ the composite

$$
(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z\xrightarrow{\mathrm{th}_V}Rz^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\bigl((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_V(c)\bigr)[2c]
\xrightarrow{Rz^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\gamma_{\pi,(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z})}Rz^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}R\pi^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z
\xrightarrow{c_{\pi,z}^{-1}}R(\pi z)^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z=(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z
$$

is the identity, with $\mathrm{th}_V$ of Definition [pur9-def-thom-class], $\gamma$ of Definition [pur9-def-purity-comparison], $c$ of Lemma [pur9-lem-upper-shriek-composition], and $R\mathrm{id}^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}=\mathrm{id}$ by Definition [pur9-def-standing-choices].
