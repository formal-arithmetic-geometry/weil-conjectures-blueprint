For $Y$ as in Definition [pur9-def-projective-line], the morphism $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y\to Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\mathbf P^1_Y}(1))[2]$ obtained from $\mathrm{pur}_s$ (Definition [pur9-def-orientation]) by the counit of $Rs_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, the extension $\mathrm D(\bar\jmath_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\to\mathrm{id}$ and $Rq_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}R(\bar\jmath s)_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm{id}$, corresponds to the hyperplane class $h$ of Definition [pur9-def-projective-line] under $\lambda_q$ and Lemma [pur9-lem-site-inverse-constant]. *Status:* this is the statement that the Kummer boundary with supports of a local equation of the divisor $\{t=0\}$ forgets to the Kummer first Chern class of $\mathcal O(\{t=0\})\cong\mathcal O(1)$; supported Kummer boundaries are not defined in the present framework, so this is imported, not proved here.
