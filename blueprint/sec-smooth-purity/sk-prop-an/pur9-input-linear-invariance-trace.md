For $g\in\mathrm{GL}_c(\Gamma(B,\mathcal O_B))$ as in Input [pur9-input-linear-invariance-thom], $\int_{p_c}\circ Rp_{c,!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(g_\sharp)=\int_{p_c}$, where $g_\sharp$ is the automorphism of $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^c_B}(c)[2c]$-valued compact-support cohomology induced by $g$, i.e. $Rp_{c,!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong R(p_cg)_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong Rp_{c,!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rg_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ (Input [pur9-input-ambient-composition]) and $Rg_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm D(g_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, $g_!$ of the constant sheaf being the constant sheaf. *Status:* imported with Input [pur9-input-linear-invariance-thom] (detailed blueprint `lem:amb-linear-trace-invariance`); not proved here.
