Under Inputs [pur9-input-linear-invariance-trace], [pur9-input-p1-duality] and [pur9-input-projection-composition], for a vector bundle $\pi:V\to Z$ of rank $c$ and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, $\gamma_{\pi,K}$ of Definition [pur9-def-purity-comparison] is an isomorphism. In particular $\omega_\pi\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_V(c)[2c]$.
