For $c\geq1$, $\Aaf^c_B$ has coordinates $t_1,\dots,t_c$, projection $p_c:\Aaf^c_B\to B$ and zero section $z_c:B\to\Aaf^c_B$; $p_0=z_0=\mathrm{id}_B$. For $c\geq1$ write $\Aaf^c_B=\Aaf^1_{B'}$ with $B'=\Aaf^{c-1}_B$, the last coordinate being $t=t_c$; let $p':\Aaf^c_B\to B'$ and $s':B'\to\Aaf^c_B$ be the projection and zero section of Definition [pur9-def-affine-line] over $B'$. Then $p_c=p_{c-1}p'$ and $z_c=s'z_{c-1}$.
