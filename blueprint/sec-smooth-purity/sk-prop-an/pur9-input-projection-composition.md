For composable $f,g$ as in Definition [pur9-def-standing-choices], $K\in D^b_c$ and $L\in D^b_{ctf}$ on the appropriate pro-étale sites, the compact-support projection transformations of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-projection] for $f$, $g$ and $gf$ (standing compactifications) satisfy the pasting equation $\operatorname{PF}_{gf}\circ(\alpha_{g,f}\otimes1)=R(gf)_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\text{assoc})\circ\alpha_{g,f}\circ Rg_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\operatorname{PF}_f)\circ\operatorname{PF}_g$, after the composition comparison $\mu$ of Lemma “The finite-coefficient inverse-image composition comparison is invertible” (`pur713-lem-pull-composition`). *Status:* not established here; the governing reference does not state compatibility of projection maps with composition.
