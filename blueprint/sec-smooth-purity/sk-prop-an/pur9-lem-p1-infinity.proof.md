The Kummer connecting map and the identification $\Pic=H^1(\mathbb G_m)$ are natural for inverse image, so $\infty^*h$ is the image of the class of $\infty^*\mathcal O(1)$. With $\infty=\{T_1=0\}$ in homogeneous coordinates $[T_0:T_1]$, the section $T_0$ of $\mathcal O(1)$ does not vanish along $\infty$, so $\infty^*\mathcal O(1)\cong\mathcal O_Y$ has class zero.
