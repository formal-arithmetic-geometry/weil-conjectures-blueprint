Choose a finite covering of $Z$ by opens $U$ over which $V$ is trivial. A sheaf on $Z_{\Proet}$ is zero if its restrictions to the members of a covering are zero (sheaf axiom). Restriction to $U$ is exact and, by Lemma [pur9-lem-open-restriction-upper-shriek], carries $Rz^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\ldots)$ to $Rz^!_{U,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the restricted twist sheaf; through the trivialization this is $Rz^!_{c,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^c_U}(c))$, concentrated as stated by Lemma [pur9-lem-thom-iso-affine].
