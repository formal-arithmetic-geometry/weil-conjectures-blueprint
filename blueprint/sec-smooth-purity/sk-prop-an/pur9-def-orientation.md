By Lemma “Concentration of the zero-section exceptional image” (`pur9-lem-orientation-concentration`, in [sk-prop-a1]), $M:=Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L_1[2]$ has cohomology only in degree zero, so the truncation maps $\mathcal H^0(M)[0]\leftarrow\tau_{\leq0}M\to M$ are isomorphisms. The orientation

$$
\mathrm{pur}_s:(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_Y\longrightarrow Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}\bigl((\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_{\Aaf^1_Y}(1)\bigr)[2]
$$

is the composite of $o_Y$ of Definition [pur9-def-orientation-sheaf-map] (with $\mathcal H^2(Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L_1)=\mathcal H^0(M)$), placed in degree zero, with these truncation isomorphisms.
