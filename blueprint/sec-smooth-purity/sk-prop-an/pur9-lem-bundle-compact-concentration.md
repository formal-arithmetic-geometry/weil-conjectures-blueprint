title = "Concentration of compact-support cohomology of a vector bundle"
order = 2323
tags = ["latex:bplemma"]
+++
For a vector bundle $\pi:V\to Z$ of rank $c$, $R\pi_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_V(c))[2c]$ has cohomology only in degree zero, and over every trivializing open $U$ its restriction is identified with $Rp_{c,!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^c_U}(c))[2c]$ by the compact-support exchange.
