Under Inputs [pur9-input-p1-duality] and [pur9-input-projection-composition], for $c\geq2$ and $K\in D^b_{ctf}(B_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$,

$$
\gamma_{p_c,K}=c_{p_{c-1},p'}^{-1}\circ\gamma_{p',Rp^!_{c-1,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K}\circ Lp'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\gamma_{p_{c-1},K})(1)[2],
$$

up to the twist and shift isomorphisms of Lemmas [pur9-lem-twist-additivity] and [pur9-lem-twist-passage] and $\mu_{p_{c-1},p'}$; here $Rp^!_{c-1,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K\in D^b_{ctf}$ by Input [pur9-input-preservation].
