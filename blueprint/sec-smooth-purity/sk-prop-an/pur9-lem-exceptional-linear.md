For $f$ as in Definition [pur9-def-standing-choices], the functors $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and $Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ are $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-linear on morphisms, and there are natural isomorphisms $Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(L[1])\cong(Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L)[1]$.
