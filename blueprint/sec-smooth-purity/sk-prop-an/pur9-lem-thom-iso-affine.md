title = "The Thom class of affine space is an isomorphism"

For every $c\geq0$, $\mathrm{th}_c$ is an isomorphism; in particular $Rz^!_{c,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^c_B}(c))[2c]$ has cohomology only in degree zero.
