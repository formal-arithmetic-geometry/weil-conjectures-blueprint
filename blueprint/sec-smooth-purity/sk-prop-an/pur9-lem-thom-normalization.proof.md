An endomorphism of $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z$ is a global section (Lemma [pur9-lem-hom-from-constant]), so it suffices to check the equality over trivializing opens, where all constructions restrict to those for $\Aaf^c_U$ (Lemmas [pur9-lem-thom-gluing], [pur9-lem-bundle-trace-gluing] and the proof of Lemma [pur9-lem-vector-bundle-purity]). For $c=1$: by Definition [pur9-def-upper-shriek-composition], the composite corresponds under $Rs_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and $Rp_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rp^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ to $\int_p$ applied to the image of $\mathrm{pur}_s$ in $\operatorname{Hom}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y,Rp_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)(1))[2])$; by Definition [pur9-def-affine-line-trace] and Input [pur9-input-zero-section-class] this is $t_q(h)$, which is $1$ by Definition [pur9-def-p1-trace]. For $c\geq2$, Definition [pur9-def-thom-class-affine] and Lemma [pur9-lem-affine-space-composition] express both $\mathrm{th}_c$ and $\gamma_{p_c}$ through the corresponding objects for $p'$ and $p_{c-1}$; the associativity of $c$ (Lemma [pur9-lem-upper-shriek-composition-assoc]) for $\pi z=p_{c-1}p's'z_{c-1}$, naturality of $\tau$, and the cases $c-1$ and $1$ give the identity.
