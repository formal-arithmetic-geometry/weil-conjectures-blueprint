For $K\in D((\Aaf^1_Y)_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$ and $q\in\mathbf Z$, put $H^q_{s(Y)}(\Aaf^1_{Y,\Proet},K):=\operatorname{Hom}_{D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)}\bigl((\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_Y,Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}K[q]\bigr)$.
