$A=A_{E,n}$, let $d\geq0$, and let $K\in D^b_{ctf}(Y,\Lambda)$. The
comparison

$$
\gamma_{p_d,K}:Lp_d^*K(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Rp_d^!K
$$

of [def-pur-affine-space-comparison] is an isomorphism.
