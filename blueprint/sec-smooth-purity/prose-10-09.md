To compare this orientation with the one given by coordinates, we have to
transport the comparison of [sk-thm-dnc] along base change on
$S$ and along étale maps that preserve the section. In both cases we use the
uniqueness in Lemma [sk-thm-dnc], which says that any construction that is
the identity at $t=1$ produces the comparison of that lemma.

In part (1) the morphism $h$ is arbitrary. Blowups in general commute
only with flat base change, but by its
description in local coordinates, the deformation space of a section of a
smooth morphism commutes with arbitrary base change.
