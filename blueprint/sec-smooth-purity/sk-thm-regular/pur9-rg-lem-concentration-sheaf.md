Under the hypotheses of Lemma [pur9-rg-lem-concentration], $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(c)[2c]$ is isomorphic to $L\nu^*_{Z,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the constant sheaf $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z$ on $Z_{\Et}$, placed in degree $0$, which is free of rank one.
