Under Input “Supported cohomology and the exceptional inverse image” (`pur9-rg-input-supported`), the fundamental class $[Z\subset X]\in H^{2c}_Z(X_{\Et},(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)(c))$ is the unique section of the rank-one sheaf of Lemma “Concentration of the exceptional inverse image of a regular immersion” (`pur9-rg-lem-concentration-sheaf`) restricting on every frame to its class; it exists by Lemma “Frame classes are independent of the frame” (`pur9-rg-lem-frame-independent`) and the sheaf axiom on $Z_{\Et}$, frames existing locally because $i$ is regular. Let $\operatorname{cl}_i:(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z\to Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(c)[2c]$ be the morphism corresponding to $[Z\subset X]$ under Input “Supported cohomology and the exceptional inverse image” (`pur9-rg-input-supported`).
