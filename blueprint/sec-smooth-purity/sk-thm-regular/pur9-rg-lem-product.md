For two pairs $(X_1,Z_1)$, $(X_2,Z_2)$ as above of codimensions $c_1,c_2$, the pair $(X_1\times_kX_2,Z_1\times_kZ_2)$ is of the same kind, of codimension $c_1+c_2$, and $[Z_1\times Z_2\subset X_1\times X_2]=\mathrm{pr}_1^*[Z_1\subset X_1]\cup\mathrm{pr}_2^*[Z_2\subset X_2]$.
