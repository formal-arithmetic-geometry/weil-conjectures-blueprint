Lemma [pur9-cp-lem-twist] with $K=(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(c)[2c]$ and $m=-c$ gives $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X\cong Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(c)[2c])\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z(-c)[-2c]$, using the twist-additivity isomorphism of Definition [pur9-def-tate-twist] for $(c)+(-c)=(0)$. By Theorem [pur9-thm-regular-purity] the first factor is isomorphic to $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z$, and the unit isomorphism of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`) concludes.
