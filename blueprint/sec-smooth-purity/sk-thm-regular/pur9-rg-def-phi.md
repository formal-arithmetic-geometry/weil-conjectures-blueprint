On a connected component of $X$ of dimension $N$, with $m=N-c$ (Lemma [pur9-rg-lem-dimension]), let $\Phi_i:(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z\to Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(c)[2c]$ be the composite in $D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ of: $\Pi_{a_Z}^{-1}$ of Theorem [pur9-thm-smooth-purity], twisted by $(-m)[-2m]$; the comparison $c_{a_X,i}:\omega_{a_Z}\cong Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_{a_X}$ of Lemma [pur9-lem-upper-shriek-composition] and Lemma [pur9-rg-lem-smooth], twisted by $(-m)[-2m]$; $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\Pi_{a_X}$, twisted by $(-m)[-2m]$; and the isomorphisms of Lemma [pur9-cp-lem-twist] moving the twists $(N)[2N]$ and $(-m)[-2m]$ through $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, with $N-m=c$; assembled over the components of $X$, the categories over a disjoint union being products.
