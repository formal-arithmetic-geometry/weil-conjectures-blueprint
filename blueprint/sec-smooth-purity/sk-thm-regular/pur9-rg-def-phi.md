On a connected component of $X$ of dimension $N$, with $m=N-c$ (Lemma “Dimension of a regular immersion” (`pur9-rg-lem-dimension`, in [sk-lem-regular-smooth])), let $\Phi_i:(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Z\to Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_X(c)[2c]$ be the composite in $D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ of: $\Pi_{a_Z}^{-1}$ of Theorem “Finite-coefficient smooth purity” (`pur9-thm-smooth-purity`, in [sk-thm-smooth-purity]), twisted by $(-m)[-2m]$; the comparison $c_{a_X,i}:\omega_{a_Z}\cong Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\omega_{a_X}$ of Lemma “The exceptional composition comparison is invertible” (`pur9-lem-upper-shriek-composition`) and Lemma “Smoothness of the two structure morphisms” (`pur9-rg-lem-smooth`, in [sk-lem-regular-smooth]), twisted by $(-m)[-2m]$; $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\Pi_{a_X}$, twisted by $(-m)[-2m]$; and the isomorphisms of Lemma “Twists pass through the exceptional inverse image of a closed immersion” (`pur9-cp-lem-twist`, in [sk-lem-coprojection]) moving the twists $(N)[2N]$ and $(-m)[-2m]$ through $Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, with $N-m=c$; assembled over the components of $X$, the categories over a disjoint union being products.
