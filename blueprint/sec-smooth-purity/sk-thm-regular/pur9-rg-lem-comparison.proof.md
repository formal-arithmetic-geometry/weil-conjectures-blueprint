Both correspond to sections of the rank-one sheaf of Lemma [pur9-rg-lem-concentration-sheaf], so the equality is étale-local on $X$. Near $z\in Z$ choose a frame $f$ and functions $g_1,\dots,g_m$ whose restrictions to $Z$ have differentials forming a basis of $\Omega_{Z/k}\otimes k(z)$; by the conormal sequence $w=(f,g):U\to\Aaf^c_k\times\Aaf^m_k$ is étale near $z$, with $w^{-1}(0\times\Aaf^m_k)=Z_U$. Let $w':Z_U\to\Aaf^m_k$ be the restriction and $i_0$ the zero section of $p:\Aaf^c_{\Aaf^m_k}\to\Aaf^m_k$. By Lemma [pur9-lem-upper-shriek-composition] and Input [pur9-input-etale-normalization], $Ri^!_{U,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lw^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong Lw'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Ri^!_{0,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, and by Input [pur9-sp-input-composition] applied to $a_U=a_{\Aaf^N}w$, $a_{Z_U}=a_{\Aaf^m}w'$ and to $a_{\Aaf^N}=a_{\Aaf^m}p$, where $\Aaf^N=\Aaf^c_k\times\Aaf^m_k$, $\Phi_{i_U}=Lw'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\Phi_{i_0}$ and $\Phi_{i_0}$ is $Ri^!_{0,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of $\Pi_p$ composed with $Ri^!_{0,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rp^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm{id}$. By Lemma [pur9-sp-lem-model], $\Pi_p=\gamma_p^{-1}$, and by Lemma [pur9-lem-thom-normalization] the resulting map is the inverse of the Thom class of Definition [pur9-def-thom-class], whose class is $\partial\kappa(t_1)\cup\cdots\cup\partial\kappa(t_c)$, the class of the coordinate frame. Kummer classes, boundaries and cup products commute with pullback, and $w^*t_r=f_r$, so $\Phi_i$ and $\operatorname{cl}_i$ agree on $Z_U$. Such $U$ cover $Z$.
