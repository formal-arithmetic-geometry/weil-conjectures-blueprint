For a closed subscheme $C\subseteq U$ with open complement $h:U\setminus C\hookrightarrow U$ and a sheaf $F$ of $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-modules on $U_{\Et}$, choose an injective resolution $F\to I$. Then $\Gamma(U_{\Et},I)\to\Gamma((U\setminus C)_{\Et},h^*I)$ is surjective in each degree; its kernel complex is $R\Gamma_C(U_{\Et},F)$, with cohomology $H^q_C(U_{\Et},F)$, and the resulting short exact sequence of complexes gives the boundary $\partial:H^{q}((U\setminus C)_{\Et},F)\to H^{q+1}_C(U_{\Et},F)$. These are independent of $I$ up to unique isomorphism, by the uniqueness of injective resolutions up to homotopy. The cup product with supports $H^a_{C_1}(U_{\Et},F_1)\otimes H^b_{C_2}(U_{\Et},F_2)\to H^{a+b}_{C_1\cap C_2}(U_{\Et},F_1\otimes F_2)$ is the product induced on these kernel complexes by a map of injective resolutions $\operatorname{Tot}(I_1\otimes I_2)\to I_{12}$ of $F_1\otimes F_2$.
