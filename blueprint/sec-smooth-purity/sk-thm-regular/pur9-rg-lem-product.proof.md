$Z_1\times_kZ_2$ is smooth over $k$, and frames $f$ on $U_1$, $g$ on $U_2$ give the frame $(\mathrm{pr}_1^*f,\mathrm{pr}_2^*g)$ on $U_1\times_kU_2$, whose class is $\mathrm{pr}_1^*\operatorname{cl}(f)\cup\mathrm{pr}_2^*\operatorname{cl}(g)$ by naturality and associativity of supported cup products, without signs since all degrees are even. Such product frames cover, so Definition [pur9-rg-def-fundamental] gives the equality.
