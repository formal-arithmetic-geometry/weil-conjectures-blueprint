For every étale $u:U\to X$ and integer $q$, a natural isomorphism

$$
\operatorname{Hom}_{D(Z_{U,\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)}\bigl((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{Z_U},Ri^!_{U,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_U(c)[q]\bigr)\cong H^q_{Z_U}(U_{\Et},(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)(c))
$$

is specified, compatible with restriction along étale maps (through Input [pur9-input-etale-normalization]), with the Gysin boundary of the localization triangle of Lemma [pur9-lem-localization-triangle], and with cup products. *Status:* the left side uses the abstractly chosen proper adjoint; its identification with étale supported cohomology is not established here.
