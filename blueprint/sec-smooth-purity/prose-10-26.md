If $k$ is algebraically closed, taking global sections turns the trace
$\operatorname{Tr}_a(\Lambda_{\operatorname{Spec}k})$ into a map
$R\Gamma_c(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda_X(d))[2d]\to\Lambda$, and taking $H^0$ of
it gives the trace map
$H_c^{2d}(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda_X(d))\to\Lambda$.
For $X=\mathbf P^1_k$ it sends $c_1(\mathcal O(1))$ to $1$.

In each coefficient case, $\gamma_f$ was obtained from an explicit right
adjoint $K\mapsto f^*K(d)[2d]$ of $Rf_!:\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$
as the unique counit-preserving isomorphism with the functor $f^!$ of
Theorem [sk-prop-sf-existence], restricted to $\Dctf$ by
Theorem [sk-thm-existence](4) (Propositions [sk-thm-smooth-purity],
[sk-prop-sp-integral-adjunction], [sk-prop-sp-rational]
and [sk-prop-sp-unique-adjoint], Theorem [sk-thm-sp-qbar]). Together with
Propositions [sk-prop-sp-purity-bc] and [sk-prop-sp-composition] and the
change of coefficients above, this completes the proof of
Theorem [sk-thm-purity-transport].
