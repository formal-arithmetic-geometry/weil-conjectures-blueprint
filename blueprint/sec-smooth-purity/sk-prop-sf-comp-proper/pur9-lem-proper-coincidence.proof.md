For $K\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, Definition [pur713-def-shriek-exchange] with $j=\mathrm{id}_X$, $p=f$, $\bar g=g'$, $\square_p=\square$ gives $\operatorname{Ex}_{!,\square,K}=Rf'_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(b_{\mathrm{id},g',K})\circ\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})K}$. The map $b'_{\mathrm{id},g'}$ of Definition [pur713-def-open-exchange] is the adjoint, under the identity-open-immersion adjunction of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-lem-open-adjunction`), of the unit followed by $\mu_{\mathrm{id},g'}$ and $\mu^{-1}_{g',\mathrm{id}}$; the latter are identities after the identity comparisons of Lemma “The finite-coefficient inverse-image composition comparison is invertible” (`pur713-lem-pull-composition`), so after identifying $\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ and $\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ with the identity as in Lemma [pur9-lem-proper-lower-shriek], $b'_{\mathrm{id},g'}$, hence $b_{\mathrm{id},g'}$, is the identity by a triangle identity. Naturality of $\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square}$ (Lemma [pur713-lem-exchange-naturality]) along the unit isomorphism $K\cong\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})K$ identifies $\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})K}$ with $\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,K}$ under the same identifications.
