Let $f:X\to Y$ be a proper morphism of separated $k$-schemes of finite type that is not an open immersion, so that $c_f=(\mathrm{id}_X,f)$ (Definition “Chosen compactifications” (`pur456-pbc-chosen-compactification`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])). Let $g:Y'\to Y$ be a morphism of separated $k$-schemes of finite type, with Cartesian square $\square$, base change $f':X'\to Y'$, projection $g':X'\to X$, and base-changed compactification $c'=(\mathrm{id}_{X'},f')$. Then, under the isomorphisms $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Lemma [pur9-lem-proper-lower-shriek] and $R_{c'}\cong Rf'_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ (proved in the same way), the compact-support exchange $\operatorname{Ex}_{!,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-exchange] equals the direct-image exchange $\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition [pur713-def-star-exchange].
