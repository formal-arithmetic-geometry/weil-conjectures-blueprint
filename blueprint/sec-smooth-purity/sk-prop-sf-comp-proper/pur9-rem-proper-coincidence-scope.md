If $f'$ is an open immersion, $c'\neq c_{f'}$, and identifying the target with $Rf'_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ uses $\theta_{c'}$ of Input [pur713-input-comparison]; otherwise $c'=c_{f'}$ and no comparison is needed. Lemma [pur9-lem-proper-coincidence] combined with Lemma [pur7710-lem-origin-cbc] shows that $\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is invertible on $\mathscr E^+(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ under the inputs of that lemma. The excluded case of a proper open immersion (an open and closed immersion) would require comparing $\mathrm D(f_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ with $Rf_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ on the pro-étale site, which is not done here.
