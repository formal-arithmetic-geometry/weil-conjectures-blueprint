(2)$\Rightarrow$(1): $P$ is bounded, hence K-flat by Input [pur9-input-flat-resolutions](a), so $K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M$ is represented by $P\otimes M$, whose terms vanish outside $[u,v]$.

(1)$\Rightarrow$(2): Taking $M=(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W$, the unit of the tensor product, shows $\mathcal H^q(K)=0$ for $q\notin[u,v]$. By Input [pur9-input-flat-resolutions](b) choose a quasi-isomorphism $P\to K$ with $P$ a complex of flat sheaves and $P^q=0$ for $q>v$; it is K-flat by part (a). Let $Q=\operatorname{coker}(P^{u-1}\to P^u)$ and $P'=[Q\to P^{u+1}\to\cdots\to P^v]$, with $Q$ in degree $u$. Since $\mathcal H^q(P)=0$ for $q<u$, the projection $P\to P'$ is a quasi-isomorphism. It remains to show $Q$ flat. The terms of $P'$ in degrees $>u$ form a bounded complex $P''$ of flat sheaves, and there is a short exact sequence of complexes $0\to P''\to P'\to Q[-u]\to0$. For a sheaf $M$, $P''\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M=P''\otimes M$ has cohomology only in degrees $\geq u+1$, and $P'\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M\cong K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M$ has none in degree $u-1$; the long exact sequence of the triangle $P''\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M\to P'\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M\to Q[-u]\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M$ gives $\mathcal H^{-1}(Q\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M)=0$. For a short exact sequence $0\to M'\to M\to M''\to0$ of sheaves, the long exact sequence of $Q\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}-$ and $\mathcal H^0(Q\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}N)=Q\otimes N$ (right exactness of $Q\otimes-$, computing with the K-flat $P'$) show that $Q\otimes M'\to Q\otimes M$ is injective. Hence $Q$ is flat, and $P'$ is the required model.
