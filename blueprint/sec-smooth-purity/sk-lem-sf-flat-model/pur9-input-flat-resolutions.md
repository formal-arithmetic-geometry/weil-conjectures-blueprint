Let $\tau\in\{\Et,\Proet\}$ and $W$ a scheme. (a) A bounded-above complex of flat sheaves (Definition [pur9-def-flat-sheaf]) on $W_\tau$ is K-flat in the sense of Definition “K-flat complexes of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules” (`pur713-def-kflat`). (b) If $K$ is a complex of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $W_\tau$ with $\mathcal H^q(K)=0$ for $q>b$, there is a quasi-isomorphism $P\to K$ from a complex of flat sheaves with $P^q=0$ for $q>b$. *Status:* standard properties of sheaves of modules on a site (every sheaf is a quotient of a direct sum of extensions by zero of the constant sheaf, which are flat); outside the governing reference and imported.
