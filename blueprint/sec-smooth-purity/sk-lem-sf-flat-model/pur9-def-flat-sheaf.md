Let $\tau\in\{\Et,\Proet\}$ and $W$ a scheme. A sheaf $Q$ of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $W_\tau$ is flat if the functor $Q\otimes_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}-$ of Definition “Tensor product of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules” (`pur713-def-abelian-tensor-finite`) on $\mathsf{Sh}(W_\tau,\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ is exact.
