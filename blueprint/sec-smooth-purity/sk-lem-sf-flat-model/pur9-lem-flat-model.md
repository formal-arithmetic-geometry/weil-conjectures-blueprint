Assume Input [pur9-input-flat-resolutions]. Let $\tau\in\{\Et,\Proet\}$, $W$ a scheme, $u\leq v$ integers, and $K\in D(W_\tau,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ with only finitely many nonzero cohomology sheaves. The following are equivalent:

1. $K$ has Tor amplitude in $[u,v]$ (Definition [pur713-def-tor-amplitude] for $\tau=\Proet$, Definition [pur9-def-tor-amplitude-etale] for $\tau=\Et$);
1. $K$ is isomorphic to a complex $P$ of flat sheaves with $P^q=0$ for $q\notin[u,v]$.
