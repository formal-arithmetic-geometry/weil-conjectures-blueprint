*Remark (sketch).*

The five moves are the whole proof. Conveniently located: the
rank-one computation (Kummer $c_1$, excision, Gysin identification of
$Rs^{!}(1)[2]$ with $H^2_s$) — inside [sk-prop-a1-kummer], proved from
the Kummer sequence and the cohomological-dimension bound of §[sec-curve-estimates], with the
orientation itself derived in [sk-prop-a1]; the Künneth and
frame-independence arguments (the Thom class is the $\Aaf^1$-class written in
coordinates) — inside [sk-prop-an]; the geometry of the deformation of a
*section* — [sk-lem-geometry], used by [sk-thm-dnc] and through it
by [sk-thm-smooth-purity]. Purity for regular closed immersions
([sk-thm-regular]) is *not* one of the moves: it is a consequence of
[sk-thm-smooth-purity] for the two structure morphisms together with the
co-projection [sk-lem-coprojection], and the deformation to the normal cone
is never applied to a general regular immersion. The formal six-functor
bookkeeping (base change, transpose composition, cup products, coefficient
transport, Frobenius equivariance, the trace normalization for the Lefschetz
formula) is all downstream of [sk-thm-smooth-purity] and absent here.
