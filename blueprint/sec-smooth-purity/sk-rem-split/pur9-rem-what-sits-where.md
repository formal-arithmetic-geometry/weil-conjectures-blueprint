The rank-one computation (Kummer classes, the affine line and $\mathbf G_m$, the orientation of the zero section) is in §-prop-a1-kummerZZ–[sk-prop-a1]; the Thom class and the affine-space and vector-bundle purity comparisons are in -prop-anZZ; the tensor formula, invertibility and base change of $\omega_f$ are in -prop-cohsmZZ; the deformation of a section and its invariance are in §-lem-geometryZZ–[sk-thm-dnc]; Theorem [pur9-thm-smooth-purity] combines them through the diagonal of -lem-diag-regZZ. Theorem [pur9-thm-regular-purity] is a consequence of Theorem [pur9-thm-smooth-purity] for the two structure morphisms and of Lemma [pur9-cp-lem-twist]; the deformation to the normal cone is applied only to sections of smooth morphisms. All these results are at the finite coefficient $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$ on the pro-étale sites and are conditional on the inputs of §[pur9-sec-standing] and of this section; the non-finite stages are conditional on the further inputs of -thm-purity-transportZZ.
