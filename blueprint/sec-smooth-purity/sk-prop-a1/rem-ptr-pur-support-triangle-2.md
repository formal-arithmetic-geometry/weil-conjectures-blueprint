In the notation of [def-pur-support-triangle], the following holds. The map
$\operatorname{Cone}(u)\to C(K)$, $(b,a)\mapsto[b]$, is a
quasi-isomorphism by [lem-pur-support-exact].
The *support-localization triangle* is the image of the cone
triangle in the derived category, with its third term identified with
$C(K)$.
