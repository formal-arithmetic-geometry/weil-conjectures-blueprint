title = "Morphisms from the constant sheaf to a sheaf in degree zero"
order = 2299
tags = ["latex:bplemma"]
+++
For a scheme $W$, $\tau\in\{\Et,\Proet\}$ and $M\in D(W_\tau,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ with $\mathcal H^m(M)=0$ for $m\neq0$, there is a natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W,M)\cong\Gamma(W_\tau,\mathcal H^0(M))$, compatible with restriction to objects of $W_\tau$.
