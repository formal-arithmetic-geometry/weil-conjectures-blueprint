By Lemma [pur9-lem-hom-from-constant] both sides are global sections of $\mathcal H^2(Rs'^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1)$, so it suffices to compare the sheaf maps $o$. Restriction to an open is the inverse image along a localization functor (Input “Inverse image along a weakly étale morphism” (`pur713-input-fixed-weakly-etale`)); it commutes with $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, $R\pi_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, $Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ by Definition “Fixed-coefficient direct image” (`pur713-def-sheaf-push`) and because it preserves K-injective complexes (its left adjoint, extension by zero, is exact, Input “Fixed-coefficient open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-input-open-fixed`)). Hence it carries the triangle of Lemma [pur9-lem-orientation-triangle] for $Y$ to that for $Y'$, with $r_{s,u}$ on the first term; this uses that the counit of $Rs_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ restricts to the counit for $s'$, which is the mate description of $c$ in Definition [pur9-def-upper-shriek-composition] together with the associativity of Lemma [pur9-lem-upper-shriek-composition-assoc]. The Kummer class restricts to the Kummer class (Lemma [pur9-lem-kummer-base-change]) and $\lambda$ is compatible with restriction to opens (Lemma “Composition law for the inverse-image site comparison” (`pur7710-lem-site-pullback-coherence`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence])). Hence $o_Y$ restricts to $o_{Y'}$.
