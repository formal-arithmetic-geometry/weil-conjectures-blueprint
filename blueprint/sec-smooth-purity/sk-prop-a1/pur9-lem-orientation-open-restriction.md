Under the inputs of this subsection and Input [pur9-input-ambient-composition], for an open $u:Y'\hookrightarrow Y$, with $u':\Aaf^1_{Y'}\hookrightarrow\Aaf^1_Y$ and $s'$ the zero section over $Y'$, one has $r_{s,u}\circ\mathrm D(u^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})(\mathrm{pur}_s)=\mathrm{pur}_{s'}$, after $u^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y=(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{Y'}$ and $u'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1=L_1|_{\Aaf^1_{Y'}}$.
