$L_1$ is a constant sheaf of free rank-one modules (Lemma [pur9-lem-twist-free]), hence in $D^b_{ctf}((\Aaf^1_Y)_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ (Definition [pur713-def-ctf]). Lemma [pur9-lem-localization-triangle] for the closed immersion $s$ and its open complement $j$ gives the triangle $Rs_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1\to L_1\to Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}j^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1\to$. Apply the triangulated functor $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and the composition comparisons of Definition “Composition comparison for finite-coefficient derived direct image” (`pur713-def-push-composition`) for $ps=\mathrm{id}_Y$ and $pj=\pi$.
