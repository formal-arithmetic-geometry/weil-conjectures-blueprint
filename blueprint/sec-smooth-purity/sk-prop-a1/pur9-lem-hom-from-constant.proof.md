The truncation maps identify $M$ with $\mathcal H^0(M)[0]$. The functor placing an object of an abelian category in degree zero is fully faithful into its derived category, so morphisms $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W\to\mathcal H^0(M)[0]$ are morphisms of sheaves; these are global sections, by the adjunction between constant sheaves and global sections.
