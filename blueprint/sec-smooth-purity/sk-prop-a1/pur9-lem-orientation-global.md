The group $H^2_{s(Y)}(\Aaf^1_{Y,\Proet},(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_{\Aaf^1_Y}(1))$ of Definition [pur9-def-supported-cohomology] is identified with $\Gamma(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal H^2(Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1))$ by Lemma [pur9-lem-hom-from-constant], and $\mathrm{pur}_s$ corresponds to the global section $o_Y(1)$.
