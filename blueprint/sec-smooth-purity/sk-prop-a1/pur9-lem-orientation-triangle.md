There is a distinguished triangle in $D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$

$$
Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1\longrightarrow Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1\longrightarrow R\pi_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}j^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1\xrightarrow{\ \delta\ }Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1[1],
$$

whose first map is $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the counit $Rs_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1\to L_1$ after $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rs_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong R(ps)_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}=R\mathrm{id}_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm{id}$, and whose second map is $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of the unit $L_1\to Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}j^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1$ after $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong R\pi_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$.
