The identification of Lemma [pur9-lem-orientation-global] is one of modules, not of sheaves: for $Y=\operatorname{Spec}k\sqcup\operatorname{Spec}k$ the sheaf $\mathcal H^2(Rs^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L_1)$ is free of rank one on each component while its module of global sections is $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)^2$. The older normalization of $\mathrm{pur}_s$ by its values at geometric points is not available on the pro-étale site for arbitrary objects; it is replaced by Definition [pur9-def-orientation]. Compatibility of $\mathrm{pur}_s$ with restriction to open subschemes of $Y$ is Lemma [pur9-lem-orientation-open-restriction]; compatibility with arbitrary base change in $Y$ is not established here.
