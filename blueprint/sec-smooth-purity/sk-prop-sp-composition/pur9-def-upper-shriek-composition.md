Assume Input “Composition of standing compact-support direct images” (`pur9-input-ambient-composition`). For composable $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to S$ as in Definition “Standing choices” (`pur9-def-standing-choices`) and $L\in D(S_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, let

$$
c_{g,f,L}:R(gf)^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L\longrightarrow Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}Rg^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L
$$

be the morphism corresponding, under the composite adjunction $Rg_{!,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}\dashv Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}Rg^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}$ of Definition “Standing compact-support and exceptional functors” (`pur9-def-shriek-notation`), to the composite

$$
Rg_{!,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}R(gf)^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L
\xrightarrow{\alpha_{g,f}}R(gf)_{!,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}R(gf)^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L
\xrightarrow{\varepsilon_{gf,L}}L,
$$

where $\varepsilon_{gf}$ is the counit of the standing adjunction for $gf$.
