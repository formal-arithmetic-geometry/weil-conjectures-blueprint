Exact extension by zero sends $L$ to a bounded-below complex on $\overline X_{\Et}$ (Lemma “Étale extension by zero preserves bounded-below objects” (`pur456-pbc-open-plus`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition])). The first arrow of Definition [pur7710-def-etale-shriek-exchange] is therefore an isomorphism by Lemma “The derived proper-base-change comparison” (`pur456-pbc-derived`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]); the second is an isomorphism by Lemma “Open base change on the étale site” (`pur7710-lem-etale-open-base-change`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]). Their composite is the specified exchange.
