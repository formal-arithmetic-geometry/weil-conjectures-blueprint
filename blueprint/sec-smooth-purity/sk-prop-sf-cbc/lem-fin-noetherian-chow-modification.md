Let $S$ be affine Noetherian and $X\to S$
proper, with $X$ reduced.
There are a proper map $\pi:Y\to X$ and a dense open $U\subset X$
such that $\pi^{-1}(U)\to U$ is an isomorphism and $Y\to S$ is
projective.
