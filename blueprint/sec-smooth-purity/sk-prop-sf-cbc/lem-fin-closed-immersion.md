Let $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion of schemes, and let $G$ be a constructible $\Lambda$-sheaf on $Z_{{\mathrm{et}}}$. Then $i_*G$ is constructible on $X_{{\mathrm{et}}}$ and $R^qi_*G=0$ for $q>0$.
