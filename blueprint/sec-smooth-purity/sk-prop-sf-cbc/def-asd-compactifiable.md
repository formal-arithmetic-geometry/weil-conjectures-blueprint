A separated finite-type
morphism $f:X\to S$ is *compactifiable* if it admits a
factorization $f=rj$ with $j:X\hookrightarrow\overline X$ an open
immersion and $r:\overline X\to S$ proper; such a factorization is a
compactification of
$f$.
