For a Noetherian scheme $S$ and $n\geq1$, the
symmetric-product map
$\sigma_n:(\mathbf P^1_S)^n\to\mathbf P^n_S$ is finite and surjective,
in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions].
