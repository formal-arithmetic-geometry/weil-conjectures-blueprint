Let $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, and use the module-object category $D(X_{\Proet},T)$ of Definition [pur713-def-module-category], under the inputs of Lemma [pur713-lem-open-exchange-module]. For the Cartesian square, the chosen compactification $c_f=(j,p)$ and a specified $K\in D(X_{\Proet},T)$, suppose that the component of the proper exchange $\operatorname{Ex}_{*,\square_p,T}$ of Definition [pur713-def-star-exchange-module] at $\mathrm D(j_{!,T})K$ is an isomorphism. Then

$$
\operatorname{Ex}_{!,\square,T,K}:Lg^*_T Rf_{!,T}K
\longrightarrow R_{c',T}Lg'^*_T K
$$

of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-exchange-module] is an isomorphism in $D(Y'_{\Proet},T)$.
