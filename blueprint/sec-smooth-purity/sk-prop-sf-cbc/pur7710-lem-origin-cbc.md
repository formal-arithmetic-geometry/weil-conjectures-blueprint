Assume Inputs “The qcqs direct-image comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-qcqs-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) and [pur7710-input-site-exchange]. In the situation of Definition [pur7710-def-etale-shriek-exchange], for every $K$ in the étale-origin subcategory $\mathscr E^+(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ of Definition “The bounded-below étale-origin subcategory” (`pur456-def-etale-origin`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), the component

$$
\operatorname{Ex}_{!,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K}:Lg^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K
\longrightarrow R_{c'}Lg'^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K
$$

of the exchange of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-exchange] is an isomorphism in $D(Y'_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. Under Input [pur713-input-comparison], so is $\operatorname{Ex}^\circ_{!,\square,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K}$ of Definition [pur713-def-shriek-exchange-chosen].
