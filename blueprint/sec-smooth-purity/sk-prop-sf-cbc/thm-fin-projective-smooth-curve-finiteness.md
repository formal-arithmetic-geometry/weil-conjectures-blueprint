Let $S$ be Noetherian
and let $f:X\to S$ be
projective and
smooth, with geometric fibres
of pure dimension one. If $\ell$ is a prime invertible on $S$,
every higher direct image
$R^rf_*\mathbf F_\ell$ is
constructible.
