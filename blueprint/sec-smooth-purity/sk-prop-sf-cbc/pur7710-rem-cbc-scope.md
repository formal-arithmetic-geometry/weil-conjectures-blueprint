The proper-exchange hypothesis of Lemma [pur7710-lem-module-cbc-criterion] is a remaining theorem in the continuous-coefficient module-object categories; an adic-completion, rationalization, or finite-extension-colimit theorem does not imply it without a comparison functor preserving the specified exchange. No unrestricted assertion for those coefficients is made here. Even at finite coefficients, the base-changed compactification $c'$ need not be the chosen compactification $c_{f'}$; identifying the target of the exchange with $Rf'_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ on all objects requires Input [pur713-input-comparison].
