Let $\Lambda$ be any admissible coefficient ring, let $f_\Lambda^!$ be the right
adjoint of $Rf_{!,\Lambda}:\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ with its counit
$\varepsilon_{f,\Lambda}$, and let $J:\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ be any
other right adjoint of $Rf_{!,\Lambda}$, with counit $\varepsilon^J$. There is a
unique natural isomorphism $\gamma:f_\Lambda^!\xrightarrow{\sim}J$ of functors
$\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ compatible with the counits, that is, with
$\varepsilon^J(K)\circ Rf_{!,\Lambda}(\gamma(K))=\varepsilon_{f,\Lambda}(K)$ in
$\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$.
