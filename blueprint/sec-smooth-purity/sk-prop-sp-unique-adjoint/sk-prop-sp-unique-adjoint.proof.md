Let $\gamma(K)$ be the $J$-transpose of $\varepsilon_{f,\Lambda}(K)$ and
$\delta(K)$ the $f_\Lambda^!$-transpose of $\varepsilon^J(K)$; these are natural,
and the displayed identity holds by definition. Then
$\delta(K)\circ\gamma(K)$ and the identity both have $f^!_\Lambda$-transpose
$\varepsilon_{f,\Lambda}(K)$, so they are equal, and symmetrically
$\gamma(K)\circ\delta(K)=\mathrm{id}$. A map compatible with the counits has
$J$-transpose $\varepsilon_{f,\Lambda}(K)$, hence equals $\gamma(K)$.
