Let $\mathcal R$ be a commutative ring object of a symmetric monoidal category $\mathscr C$. An $\mathcal R$-module is an object $M$ of $\mathscr C$ with a unital associative action $\mathcal R\otimes M\to M$, the associativity and unit isomorphisms of $\mathscr C$ being included in these identities. Morphisms are action-preserving morphisms of $\mathscr C$. Write $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(\mathscr C)$ for this category. For $\mathscr C=\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$ write $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$, and for $\mathcal R=\mathcal R_{X,T}$ also $\mathsf{Mod}(X_{\Proet},T)$.
