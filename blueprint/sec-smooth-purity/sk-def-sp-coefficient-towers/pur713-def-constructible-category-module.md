For $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$,

$$
D^b_c(X_{\Proet},T)=D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X_{\Proet},T)
\subseteq D(X_{\Proet},T)
$$

is the full subcategory of objects with only finitely many nonzero cohomology module objects, all constructible in the sense of Definition [pur713-def-module-constructible]. Morphisms are those of $D(X_{\Proet},T)$.
