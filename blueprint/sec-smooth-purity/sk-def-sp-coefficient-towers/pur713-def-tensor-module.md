For $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, the bifunctor

$$
-\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}-:
D(X_{\Proet},T)\times D(X_{\Proet},T)\longrightarrow D(X_{\Proet},T)
$$

is the two-variable total left derived functor of the relative tensor product $\otimes_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}$ of Definition [pur713-def-relative-tensor], computed using K-flat resolutions in $\mathsf{Mod}(X_{\Proet},T)$ (Definition [pur713-def-kflat-module]). It is not a derived tensor product over $\mathbf Z$ followed by a quotient. Its unit is $\mathcal R_{X,T}$ in degree zero.
