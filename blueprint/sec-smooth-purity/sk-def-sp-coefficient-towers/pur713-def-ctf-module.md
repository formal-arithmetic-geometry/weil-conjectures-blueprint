For $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, the full subcategory $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},T)\subseteq D^b_c(X_{\Proet},T)$ consists of the objects of the category of Definition [pur713-def-constructible-category-module] that have finite Tor amplitude as in Definition [pur713-def-tor-amplitude-module]. It is not defined by a tower of reductions or by a rationalized coefficient category.
