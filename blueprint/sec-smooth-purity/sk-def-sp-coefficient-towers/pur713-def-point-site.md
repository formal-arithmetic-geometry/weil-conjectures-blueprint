The site $*_{\Proet}$ has as objects the profinite sets and as morphisms the continuous maps; a finite family $\{S_i\to S\}$ is a covering if $\coprod_iS_i\to S$ is surjective, and the topology is the one generated by these finite coverings (the coherent topology). A condensed ring is a sheaf of commutative rings on $*_{\Proet}$; equivalently, by Definition [pur713-def-ring-object], a commutative ring object of $\mathsf{Sh}(*_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$ with the sheafified tensor product. Source: governing reference, §3.1.
