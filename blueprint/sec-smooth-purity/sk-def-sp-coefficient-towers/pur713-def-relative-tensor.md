Let $\mathcal R$ be a commutative ring object of $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$. For $\mathcal R$-modules $M,N$, the relative tensor product $M\otimes_{\mathcal R}N$ is the coequalizer, in $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$, of the two maps

$$
M\otimes_{\mathbf Z}\mathcal R\otimes_{\mathbf Z}N\rightrightarrows M\otimes_{\mathbf Z}N,
$$

given by the action on $M$ (after the symmetry $M\otimes_{\mathbf Z}\mathcal R\cong\mathcal R\otimes_{\mathbf Z}M$) and by the action on $N$. Its $\mathcal R$-action is induced by the action on $N$; because $\otimes_{\mathbf Z}$ preserves coequalizers in each variable (each $M\otimes_{\mathbf Z}-$ is a left adjoint, governing reference, §2), this induced map is defined, and by commutativity of $\mathcal R$ it agrees with the action induced by $M$.
