The adjunction of Definition [pur456-def-adjoint-datum] preserves the finite-Tor constructible categories if

$$
Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\bigl(D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\bigr)\subseteq D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)
\quad\text{and}\quad
Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\bigl(D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\bigr)\subseteq D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n),
$$

with the full subcategories of Definition [pur713-def-ctf]. These are conditions on the ambient functors, not new definitions of functors on smaller categories; no uniform bound on Tor amplitude is imposed.
