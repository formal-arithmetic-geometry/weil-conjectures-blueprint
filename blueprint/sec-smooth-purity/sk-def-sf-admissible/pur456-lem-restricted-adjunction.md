If the adjunction of Definition [pur456-def-adjoint-datum] preserves the finite-Tor constructible categories (Definition [pur456-def-preservation]), then it restricts to an adjunction

$$
Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\big|_{D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)}
\dashv
Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\big|_{D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)}
$$

whose unit and counit are the ambient ones. This does not identify the restriction with a separately transported exceptional functor.
