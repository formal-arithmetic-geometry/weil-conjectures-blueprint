Under Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint-module] and the inputs of Lemma [pur713-lem-shriek-adjunction-module], for $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, the module-object constructible exceptional adjunction for $f$ is the adjunction

$$
Rf_{!,T}\dashv Rf^!_T,\qquad Rf^!_T:D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},T)\longrightarrow D^b_c(X_{\Proet},T),
$$

between the bounded constructible categories of Definition [pur713-def-constructible-category-module],
with the functors of Definitions [pur713-def-lower-shriek-module] and [pur713-def-upper-shriek-module] and the unit and counit of the composite adjunction of Lemma [pur713-lem-shriek-adjunction-module], built from the data supplied by Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint-module].
