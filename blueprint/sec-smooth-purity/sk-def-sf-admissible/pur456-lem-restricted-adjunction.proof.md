The preservation clauses ensure that the restrictions are functors with the displayed sources and targets. Since the subcategories are full, their Hom sets are the ambient Hom sets. Restrict the adjunction bijection of Lemma [pur713-lem-shriek-adjunction] to these objects. Its unit and counit have endpoints in the full subcategories, and their ambient triangle identities remain equalities there.
