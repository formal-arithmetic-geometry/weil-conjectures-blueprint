At finite coefficients all preservation assertions in this subsection concern the specified ambient functors; at the non-finite coefficients $T$ they concern the specified functors on the bounded constructible categories. An adjunction on a constructible or étale category alone does not prove that it is the restriction of this ambient adjunction. Similarly, a cohomological-dimension bound on the étale-origin class does not establish $\mathrm{FullCD}$ on every pro-étale module sheaf. Assertions elsewhere in the manuscript about ambient-adjoint nonexistence and about unconditionally transported constructible adjoints must be reconciled with the governing reference; neither is used here to establish or refute the proper-adjoint input.
