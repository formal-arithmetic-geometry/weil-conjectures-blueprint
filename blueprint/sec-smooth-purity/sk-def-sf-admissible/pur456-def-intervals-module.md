For a $k$-scheme $W$ of finite type, $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ and integers $a\leq b$, let $D_c^{[a,b]}(W_{\Proet},T)\subseteq D^b_c(W_{\Proet},T)$ be the full subcategory of objects of the category of Definition [pur713-def-constructible-category-module] whose cohomology module objects vanish outside $[a,b]$. Constructibility is that of Definition [pur713-def-module-constructible], including its restriction convention.
