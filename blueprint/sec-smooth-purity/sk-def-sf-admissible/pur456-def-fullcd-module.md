For $p$, $N$ as in Definition [pur456-def-fullcd] and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, the condition $\mathrm{FullCD}(p,T;N)$ means: for every $v:Y'\to Y$ as there and every $\mathcal R_{\overline X\times_YY',T}$-module $M$ placed in degree zero, $\mathcal H^r(Rp'_{*,T}M)=0$ for $r>N$, with $Rp'_{*,T}$ of Definition [pur713-def-derived-push-module].
