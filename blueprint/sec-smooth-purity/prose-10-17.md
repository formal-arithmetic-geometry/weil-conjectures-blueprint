Applying $R\varprojlim$ to the isomorphism $\gamma_{f,\bullet}$ at $\operatorname{red}_YK$, we obtain
$\gamma_{f,\mathcal O_E}:f^*_{\mathcal O_E}K(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_{\mathcal O_E}K$,
whose reductions are the isomorphisms $\gamma_{f,\mathcal O_E/\ell^n}$. This is the case $\mathcal O_E$ of
Theorem [sk-thm-purity-transport], and for $E=\Ql$ the case $\Zl$.
