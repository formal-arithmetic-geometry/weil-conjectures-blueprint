For a $k$-scheme $W$ of finite type, there are natural isomorphisms $m_{W,A,B}:L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(A\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}B)\cong L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}A\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}B$ for $A,B\in D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, induced on K-flat resolutions by the strong monoidal structure of the sheaf functor $\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, together with $\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W$. *Status:* requires that $\nu^*_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ be strong monoidal and preserve K-flat complexes; outside the governing reference and imported.
