Assume Inputs “The bounded-below site-comparison theorem” (`pur7710-input-site-unit`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) and [pur9-input-site-monoidal]. If $H\in D(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $L\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H$ has Tor amplitude in $[u,v]$ (Definition [pur713-def-tor-amplitude]), then $H$ has Tor amplitude in $[u,v]$ (Definition [pur9-def-tor-amplitude-etale]).
