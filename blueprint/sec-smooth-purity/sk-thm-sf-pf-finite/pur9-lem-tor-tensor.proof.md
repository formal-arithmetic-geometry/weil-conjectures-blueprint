Induct on $b-a\geq0$. If $a=b$, $G\cong\mathcal H^a(G)[-a]$ and the claim is Definition [pur9-def-tor-amplitude-etale]. Otherwise tensor the truncation triangle $\tau^{\leq b-1}G\to G\to\mathcal H^b(G)[-b]\to$ with $H$ and use the long exact sequence of cohomology sheaves.
