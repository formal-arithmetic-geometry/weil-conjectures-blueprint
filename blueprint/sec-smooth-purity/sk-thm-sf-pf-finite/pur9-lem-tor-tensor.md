Let $W$ be a scheme, $H\in D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ with Tor amplitude in $[u,v]$, and $G\in D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ with cohomology only in degrees $[a,b]$. Then $G\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H$ has cohomology only in degrees $[a+u,b+v]$.
