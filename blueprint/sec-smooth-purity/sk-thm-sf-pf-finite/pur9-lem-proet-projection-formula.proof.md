By Lemma [pur7710-lem-constructible-equivalence], $K\cong L\nu^*_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G$ and $L\cong L\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H$ with $G\in D^b_c(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $H\in D^b_c(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$; by naturality of $\operatorname{PF}$ (Lemma [pur713-lem-projection-naturality-shriek]) it suffices to treat $(L\nu^*_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G,L\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H)$. By Lemmas [pur9-lem-tor-descends] and [pur9-lem-tor-pullback], $H$ and $Lf^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H$ have finite Tor amplitude; by Lemma [pur7710-lem-compact-finiteness], $Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G$ is bounded; so Lemma [pur9-lem-tor-tensor] puts $G\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Lf^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H$ and $Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H$ in $D^+$, and Input [pur9-input-site-projection] applies. In its equality, $\kappa$ is invertible by Lemma “Invertibility of the candidate comparison” (`pur456-lem-candidate-site-iso`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), $\operatorname{PF}^{\Et}$ by Lemma [pur9-lem-etale-projection-formula], $\beta$ by Definition “Inverse image across the two sites” (`pur7710-def-site-pullback-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), and $m$ by Input [pur9-input-site-monoidal]. Hence $\operatorname{PF}_{f,L\nu^*_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G,L\nu^*_{Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is an isomorphism.
