Assume Input [pur9-input-flat-resolutions]. If $H\in D(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ has only finitely many nonzero cohomology sheaves and Tor amplitude in $[u,v]$, then $Lf^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}H$ has Tor amplitude in $[u,v]$.
