Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $d$ and let $U\subset X$
be open with an étale map $e:U\to\mathbf A^d_Y$ over $Y$. Then the
restriction to $U$ of the diagonal orientation
$\omega_f\xrightarrow{\sim}(\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)_X(d)[2d]$ of
[thm-pur-diagonal-dualizing-object] equals the affine-space orientation of
$U$ given by its coordinate adjunction, i.e. the identification
$\omega_{f|_U}\simeq e^*\omega_{p_d}\simeq(\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)_U(d)[2d]$
obtained from $e^!=e^*$ and integration on $\mathbf A^d_Y$.
