Let $\pi:V\to S$ be a vector bundle of rank $d$, with coefficients a
finite coefficient ring $\Lambda=A$ of [def-pur-conventions] (for instance
$\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z$). The diagonal orientation
$\omega_\pi\xrightarrow{\sim}\Lambda_V(d)[2d]$ of
[thm-pur-diagonal-dualizing-object] (constructed from the diagonal of $\pi$,
the deformation to its normal bundle and vector-bundle purity) equals the
vector-bundle orientation, i.e. the inverse of the vector-bundle purity
isomorphism given by integration along the fibres of $\pi$.
