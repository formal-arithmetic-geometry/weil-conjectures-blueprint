To compare this orientation with the one given by coordinates, we have to
transport the comparison (10.12) along base change on
$S$ and along étale maps that preserve the section. In both cases we use the
uniqueness in Lemma [sk-thm-dnc], which says that any construction that is
the identity at $t=1$ produces the comparison of that lemma.
