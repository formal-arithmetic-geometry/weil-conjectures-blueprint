For every $f$ as in Definition [pur9-def-standing-choices], the standing exceptional adjunction $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ preserves the finite-Tor constructible categories in the sense of Definition [pur456-def-preservation]. *Status:* this is the preservation clause of an admissibility datum (Definition [pur456-def-admissibility]); it is not proved here for the abstractly chosen proper adjoints.
