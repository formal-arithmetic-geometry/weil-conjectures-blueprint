Under Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint], the functors $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ and $Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ are those of Definitions “Finite-coefficient compact-support direct image” (`pur713-def-lower-shriek`) and [pur713-def-upper-shriek], with the adjunction $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Lemma [pur713-lem-shriek-adjunction]. This adjunction is called the standing adjunction. Whenever another compactification is used, the comparison is Input [pur713-input-comparison].
