For a proper morphism $p:Z\to Y$ of separated $k$-schemes of finite type that is not an open immersion, there is a natural isomorphism $Rp_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of functors $D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\to D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, induced by the unit isomorphism $\mathrm{id}\to\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-lem-open-adjunction`) for the identity open immersion and the canonical isomorphism $\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\cong\mathrm{id}$ of Lemma “The finite-coefficient inverse-image composition comparison is invertible” (`pur713-lem-pull-composition`).
