For a separated morphism of finite type $f:X\to Y$ of separated $k$-schemes of finite type, the relative dualizing complex is

$$
\omega_f:=Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\bigl((\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y\bigr)\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n),
$$

with the functor of Definition [pur713-def-upper-shriek] and the constant sheaf $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_Y$ in degree zero.
