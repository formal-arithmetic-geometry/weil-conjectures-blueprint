For a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ of $k$-schemes of finite type, the sheaf direct image $i_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:\mathsf{Sh}(Z_{\Proet},\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\to\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ of Definition “Fixed-coefficient direct image” (`pur713-def-sheaf-push`) is exact, and the counit $i^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}i_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\to\mathrm{id}$ of Input “Adjunction and exactness of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-adjunction`) is an isomorphism. *Status:* imported from the pro-étale theory of Bhatt–Scholze; outside the governing reference and not proved here.
