For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ of separated $k$-schemes of finite type, the canonical isomorphism $R\mathrm{id}_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm{id}$ induces a natural isomorphism $Rj_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm D(j_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ of functors $D(U_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\to D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
