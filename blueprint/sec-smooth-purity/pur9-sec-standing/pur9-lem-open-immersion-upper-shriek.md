For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ of separated $k$-schemes of finite type, $Rj^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}=\mathrm D(j^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ as functors $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\to D(U_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, and the adjunction $Rj_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rj^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is the adjunction of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-lem-open-adjunction`) after the identification of Lemma [pur9-lem-open-immersion-lower-shriek].
