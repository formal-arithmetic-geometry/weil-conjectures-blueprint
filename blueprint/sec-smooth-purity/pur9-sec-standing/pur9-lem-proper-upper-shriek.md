For $p$ as in Lemma [pur9-lem-proper-lower-shriek], $Rp^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}=\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\circ Rp^{!,\mathrm{prop}}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, where $Rp^{!,\mathrm{prop}}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\to D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ denotes the right adjoint of $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ fixed in Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint] (written $Rp^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ there; the superscript $\mathrm{prop}$ is used only in this lemma and its proof to separate it from the exceptional functor of Definition [pur713-def-upper-shriek]). Under the isomorphism of Lemma [pur9-lem-proper-lower-shriek] and the identity comparison $\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\cong\mathrm{id}$, the standing adjunction $Rp_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rp^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ corresponds to the fixed proper adjunction $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rp^{!,\mathrm{prop}}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$.
