The displayed equality is Definition [pur713-def-upper-shriek] for $c_p=(\mathrm{id}_Z,p)$. The adjunction of Lemma [pur713-lem-shriek-adjunction] is the composite of the fixed proper adjunction with the adjunction $\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})\dashv\mathrm D(\mathrm{id}^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-lem-open-adjunction`). The latter has invertible unit and counit, and both its functors are identified with the identity by the comparisons named in Lemma [pur9-lem-proper-lower-shriek]; transporting along these identifications leaves the fixed proper adjunction.
