Let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ and $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ be complementary open and closed immersions of $k$-schemes of finite type. For every $F\in\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, the sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow j_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}j^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F\longrightarrow F\longrightarrow i_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}i^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F\longrightarrow0,
$$

whose first map is the counit of Input “Fixed-coefficient open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-input-open-fixed`) and whose second map is the unit of Input “Adjunction and exactness of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-adjunction`), is exact. *Status:* imported; outside the governing reference. On the étale site the corresponding statement is proved by geometric stalks, which are not available for arbitrary pro-étale sheaves.
