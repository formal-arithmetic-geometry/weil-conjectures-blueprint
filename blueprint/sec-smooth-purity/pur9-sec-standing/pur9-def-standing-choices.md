The compactifications $c_f$ are those fixed once and for all in Definition “Chosen compactifications” (`pur456-pbc-chosen-compactification`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]); in particular $c_f=(f,\mathrm{id}_Y)$ for an open immersion, $c_f=(\mathrm{id}_X,f)$ for a proper morphism that is not an open immersion, and $c_{\mathrm{id}_X}=(\mathrm{id}_X,\mathrm{id}_X)$. The proper adjoints $Rp^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, units and counits are those fixed in Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint]; for an identity morphism this is the identity functor with the identity adjunction, after the canonical isomorphism $R\mathrm{id}_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\cong\mathrm{id}$ given by the levelwise identity functor. In the rest of this section, schemes are separated $k$-schemes of finite type and morphisms between them are separated of finite type.
