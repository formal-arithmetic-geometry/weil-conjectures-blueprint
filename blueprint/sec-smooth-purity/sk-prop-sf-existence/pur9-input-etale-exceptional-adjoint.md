For $f$ as in Definition [pur9-def-etale-unbounded-candidate], a right adjoint

$$
f^{\times}:D(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\longrightarrow D(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)
$$

of $Rf^{\mathrm{un}}_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ is supplied, with unit and counit satisfying both triangle identities. *Status:* the source node obtains it by Brown representability (Stacks Project, Tag `0F5Y`) applied to the coproduct-preserving functor $Rf^{\mathrm{un}}_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ on the ordinary derived categories of the small étale sites (`thm-amb-etale-exceptional-adjoint`); it is imported here, lies outside the governing reference, and is not the exceptional functor of Definition [pur9-def-shriek-notation].
