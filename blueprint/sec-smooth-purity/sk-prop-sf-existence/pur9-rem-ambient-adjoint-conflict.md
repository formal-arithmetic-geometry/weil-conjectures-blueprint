The source node asserted that $\mathrm{FullCD}(p,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n;N)$ of Definition [pur456-def-fullcd] fails for a general proper $p$, already for an elliptic curve $E_0$ over $k$ and that, for the structure morphism $p:E_0\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ and a finite field $\mathbf F$ of characteristic different from $p$, the pro-étale functor $Rp_{*,\mathbf F}:D(E_{0,\Proet},\mathbf F)\to D((\operatorname{Spec}k)_{\Proet},\mathbf F)$ does not preserve coproducts and has no right adjoint, nor does its restriction to the bounded-below subcategories. If correct, this contradicts Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint], whose source is the choice in the governing reference of an abstract right adjoint of $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ on the finite-coefficient ambient categories (§7.1 there, attributed to Brown representability). For module objects the governing reference does not define an exceptional inverse image on the ambient category at all (§8.2 there); it constructs one only on the bounded constructible categories, from a chosen right adjoint of the restricted $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ (§9 there). Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint-module] is therefore a stronger hypothesis than the governing reference makes. Neither the assertion nor the existence of the adjoint is proved here; the conflict is unresolved. No result of this manuscript uses the assertion, and every result citing Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint], directly or through Definition [pur9-def-shriek-notation], is conditional on that input. The source also asserted that the adjunction of Lemma [pur9-lem-transported-adjoint-comparison] is compatible with refinements of compactifications; that compatibility is not asserted here.
