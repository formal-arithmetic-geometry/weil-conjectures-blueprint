The bijections are, in order: the standing adjunction of Definition [pur9-def-shriek-notation]; precomposition with the isomorphism $\kappa_{f,G}$ of Definition “Comparison of compactification candidates across sites” (`pur456-def-candidate-site-comparison`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), invertible by Lemma “Invertibility of the candidate comparison” (`pur456-lem-candidate-site-iso`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]); the adjunction of Lemma “The derived site-comparison adjunction” (`pur456-lem-site-adjunction`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), which applies because $Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G\in D^+(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $L\in D^+(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$; the adjunction of Input [pur9-input-etale-exceptional-adjoint], using $Rf_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G=Rf^{\mathrm{un}}_{!,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}G$ (Definition [pur9-def-etale-unbounded-candidate]); and Lemma “Full faithfulness of the finite-coefficient site inverse image” (`pur7710-lem-site-fully-faithful`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), which applies by hypothesis (2). Each is natural in $G\in D^+(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, because $\kappa$ and the adjunction bijections are natural.

By hypothesis (1) and by hypothesis (2) with Definition “The bounded-below étale-origin subcategory” (`pur456-def-etale-origin`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]), both $Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L$ and $f^{\mathrm{tr}}L$ lie in the full subcategory $\mathscr E^+(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. Every object of this subcategory is isomorphic to some $L\nu^*_XG$, and by Lemma “Full faithfulness of the finite-coefficient site inverse image” (`pur7710-lem-site-fully-faithful`, see [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]) every morphism $L\nu^*_XG\to L\nu^*_XG'$ is $L\nu^*_{X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of a unique morphism $G\to G'$. Hence the bijections above, transported along chosen presentations, define a natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}(-,Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(-,f^{\mathrm{tr}}L)$ of functors on $\mathscr E^+(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)^{\mathrm{op}}$, independent of presentations by this uniqueness. Both representing objects lie in that subcategory, so the Yoneda lemma in $\mathscr E^+(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ gives a unique isomorphism $\phi_L$ inducing it. Morphisms in the full subcategory are those of $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
