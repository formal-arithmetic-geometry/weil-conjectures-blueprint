Assume Inputs [pur713-input-proper-adjoint] and [pur9-input-closed-immersion]. Let $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion of separated $k$-schemes of finite type. For every $Q\in D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the unit $Q\to Ri^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Ri_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Q$ of the standing adjunction of Definition [pur9-def-shriek-notation] is an isomorphism.
