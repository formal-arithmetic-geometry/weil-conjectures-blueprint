Under Input [pur9-input-closed-immersion], for a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ of $k$-schemes of finite type, $Ri_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\to D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ is fully faithful.
