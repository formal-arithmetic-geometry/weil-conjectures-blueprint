Under Input [pur713-input-cons-preservation-module], for each proper $p:Z\to Y$ of separated $k$-schemes of finite type and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, supply a right adjoint

$$
Rp^!_T:D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},T)\longrightarrow D^b_c(Z_{\Proet},T)
$$

of the restriction $Rp_{*,T}:D^b_c(Z_{\Proet},T)\to D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},T)$ of Definition [pur713-def-derived-push-module], together with a unit and a counit satisfying both triangle identities, chosen once and for all (Axiom of Choice), the identity adjunction for the identity morphism. Source: governing reference, §9 (the abstract choice of a right adjoint on the bounded constructible categories, used only through the adjunction; that the restricted $Rp_{*,T}$ is a left adjoint is an instance argument supplied there only for $\mathcal O_E$, $E$, $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$). No right adjoint on the unbounded $D(Z_{\Proet},T)$ is used: the governing reference does not define a module-object exceptional inverse image on the ambient category (§8.2).
