Under Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint-module] and the inputs of Lemma [pur713-lem-open-adjunction-module], for every compactification $d=(j,p)$ of $f:X\to Y$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, the restriction of $R_{d,T}$ to $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},T)$, which lands in $D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},T)$ by Input [pur713-input-cons-preservation-module], is left adjoint to $R^!_{d,T}:D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},T)\to D^b_c(X_{\Proet},T)$; the adjunction is the composite of the restriction of the adjunction of Lemma [pur713-lem-open-adjunction-module] and the supplied proper adjunction. For $d=c_f$ it is $Rf_{!,T}\dashv Rf^!_T$ on the bounded constructible categories.
