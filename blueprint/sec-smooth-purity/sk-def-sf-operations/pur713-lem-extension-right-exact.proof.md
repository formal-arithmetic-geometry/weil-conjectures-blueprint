By Input [pur713-input-change-scalars], $\operatorname{Ext}_\phi$ is a left adjoint between abelian categories (Input [pur713-input-module-abelian]); a left adjoint preserves all colimits, in particular cokernels and finite direct sums, hence it is right exact.
