Let $\mathcal R$ be a commutative ring object of $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$. A complex $P$ in $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$ is K-flat if for every acyclic complex $N$ in $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$ the total tensor complex $P\otimes_{\mathcal R}N$, formed with the relative tensor product of Definition [pur713-def-relative-tensor] and with direct sums, is acyclic. A K-flat resolution of a complex $M$ is a quasi-isomorphism $P\to M$ with $P$ K-flat. The governing reference, §§4.1 and 5, supplies functorial K-flat resolutions in $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$.
