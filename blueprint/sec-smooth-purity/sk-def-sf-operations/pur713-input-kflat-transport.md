For $f:X\to Y$ and a commutative ring object $\mathcal R$ of $\mathsf{Sh}(Y_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$, the exact functor $(f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z})_{\mathcal R}$ sends K-flat complexes of $\mathcal R$-modules to K-flat complexes of $f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\mathcal R$-modules (Definition [pur713-def-kflat-module]). Source: governing reference, §8.2 (``$\mathrm{mapMod}(f^*)$ is exact and preserves K-flat complexes'', established there).
