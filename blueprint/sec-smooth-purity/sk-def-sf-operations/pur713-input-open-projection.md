For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, an abelian sheaf $L$ on $X_{\Proet}$ and an abelian sheaf $M$ on $U_{\Proet}$, let

$$
\pi_{j,L,M}:j_{!,\mathbf Z}(j^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}L\otimes_{\mathbf Z}M)\longrightarrow L\otimes_{\mathbf Z}j_{!,\mathbf Z}M
$$

be the adjoint, under Input “Fixed-coefficient open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-input-open-fixed`), of
$j^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}L\otimes_{\mathbf Z}M\xrightarrow{1\otimes\mathrm{unit}}j^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}L\otimes_{\mathbf Z}j^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}j_{!,\mathbf Z}M\cong j^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}(L\otimes_{\mathbf Z}j_{!,\mathbf Z}M)$,
the last map being the monoidal comparison of Input “Monoidality of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-monoidal`). Then $\pi_{j,L,M}$ is a natural isomorphism, compatible with the associativity and unit isomorphisms of $\otimes_{\mathbf Z}$. Source: governing reference, §8.1 (the projection formula for the $\mathsf{Ab}$-valued $j_!$, established there).
