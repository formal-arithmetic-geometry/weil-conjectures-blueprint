For $K\in D^b_c(X_{\Proet},T)$ and $L\in D^b_c(Y_{\Proet},T)$, the object $\mathrm D(j_{!,T})K$ is bounded constructible by Input [pur713-input-cons-preservation-module]. The supplied proper adjunction and Lemma [pur713-lem-open-adjunction-module] give natural bijections
$\operatorname{Hom}(Rp_{*,T}\mathrm D(j_{!,T})K,L)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathrm D(j_{!,T})K,Rp^!_TL)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(K,\mathrm D(j^*_T)Rp^!_TL)$,
the Hom sets being those of the full subcategories, i.e. of $D(Y_{\Proet},T)$, $D(\overline X_{\Proet},T)$ and $D(X_{\Proet},T)$; the second bijection is the ambient one restricted to full subcategories. They are natural in both variables and define the composite adjunction. No coefficient-comparison theorem is used.
