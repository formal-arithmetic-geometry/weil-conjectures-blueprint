In Definition [pur713-def-mapmod], if $F\dashv G$ is a monoidal adjunction with $F$ strong and $G$ lax monoidal, with unit $\iota$, then $\iota_{\mathcal R}:\mathcal R\to GF\mathcal R$ is a morphism of ring objects and $F_{\mathcal R}\dashv\operatorname{Res}_{\iota_{\mathcal R}}\circ G_{F\mathcal R}$. Source: governing reference, §4.3 (established there).
