For a morphism $\phi:\mathcal R\to\mathcal S$ of commutative ring objects of $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$, define
$\operatorname{Res}_{\phi}:\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal S}(X_{\Proet})\to\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$
by precomposing the module action with $\mathrm{id}\otimes\phi$. The underlying abelian sheaf and the underlying morphisms are unchanged.
