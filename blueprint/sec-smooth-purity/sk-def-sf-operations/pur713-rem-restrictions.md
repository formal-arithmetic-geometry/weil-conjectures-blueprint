At finite coefficients, a restriction of an ambient functor to the subcategory of Definition [pur713-def-ctf] requires the corresponding preservation theorem; a bounded-below finite-coefficient exceptional inverse image, when preservation of bounded-below objects has been supplied for the chosen adjoint, is its restriction, not a separately chosen adjoint (governing reference, §7.1). At the non-finite coefficients $T$, the functors $Lf^*_T$, $Rp_{*,T}$ for proper $p$, $\mathrm D(j_{!,T})$, $\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}$ on $D^b_c$ are restrictions of the ambient ones (Input [pur713-input-cons-preservation-module]; governing reference, §9), whereas $Rf^!_T$ exists only on $D^b_c$ (Definition [pur713-def-upper-shriek-module]); restriction to the finite-Tor subcategory of Definition [pur713-def-ctf-module] is a further condition. Comparison with finite reduction or with derived-complete coefficients remains a separate input; the rationalization $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}-$ is a localization (governing reference, §5.1), but its compatibility with the exceptional inverse images $Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E}$, $Rf^!_E$ is not treated there (§11.1).
