The full subcategory $D^b_{ctf}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\subseteq D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ consists of the objects of the category of Definition [pur713-def-constructible-category] that have finite Tor amplitude as in Definition [pur713-def-tor-amplitude]. This is an additional condition; it is not defined by a tower of reductions.
