The functors are exact by Lemmas [pur713-lem-open-exact-module] and [pur713-lem-open-pull-exact-module], and Lemma [pur713-lem-open-adjunction-underived] gives the underived adjunction with invertible unit. Exact functors act levelwise on complexes and preserve quasi-isomorphisms; localizing the adjunction and its two triangle identities gives the asserted levelwise adjunction, with levelwise invertible unit.
