For $K\in D(X_{\Proet},T)$, the functor $R\mathcal Hom_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}(K,-)$ on $D(X_{\Proet},T)$ is the right adjoint of $K\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}-$ of Definition [pur713-def-tensor-module]. Its existence and bifunctoriality are part of the closed derived monoidal structure of the governing reference, §§5 and 8.2.
