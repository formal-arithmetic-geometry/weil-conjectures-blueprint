Precomposing an action with $\mathrm{id}\otimes\psi$ and then with $\mathrm{id}\otimes\phi$ is precomposing it with $\mathrm{id}\otimes\psi\phi$, by functoriality of $\otimes_{\mathbf Z}$; precomposing with the identity changes nothing. Underlying sheaves and morphisms are unchanged by all these functors.
