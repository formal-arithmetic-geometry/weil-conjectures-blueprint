Apply the ordinary derived-adjunction theorem of the governing reference, §8.2, to the underived adjunction of Lemma [pur713-lem-module-adjunction]. The left derivability of $f^*_T$ on K-flat complexes is the property recorded in Definition [pur713-def-derived-pull-module], which uses Input [pur713-input-kflat-transport]; the right derivability of $f_{*,T}$ uses K-injective resolutions in the Grothendieck abelian category of Input [pur713-input-module-abelian]. The derived functors are those of Definitions [pur713-def-derived-pull-module] and [pur713-def-derived-push-module]. This argument is on the unbounded categories; no preservation of bounded-below objects by $Lf^*_T$ is inferred.
