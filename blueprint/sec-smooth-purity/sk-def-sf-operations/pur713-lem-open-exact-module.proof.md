By Input [pur713-input-module-abelian], exactness of a sequence of module objects is exactness of the underlying abelian sheaves. The underlying sheaf of $j_{!,T}M$ is $j_{!,\mathbf Z}M$, and $j_{!,\mathbf Z}$ is exact by Input “Exactness of fixed-coefficient extension by zero” (`pur713-input-open-fixed-exact`).
