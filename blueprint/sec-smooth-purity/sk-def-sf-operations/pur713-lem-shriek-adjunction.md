Under Input [pur713-input-proper-adjoint], for every compactification $d=(j,p)$ of $f:X\to Y$ there is an adjunction
$R_d\dashv R^!_d$
between $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the composite of the adjunction of Lemma “Finite-coefficient derived open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-lem-open-adjunction`) and the supplied proper adjunction. For $d=c_f$ this is the adjunction $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$.
