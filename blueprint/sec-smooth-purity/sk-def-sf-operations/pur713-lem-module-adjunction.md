Under Input [pur713-input-coefficient-structure], for $f:X\to Y$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ there is an adjunction $f^*_T\dashv f_{*,T}$ between $\mathsf{Mod}(Y_{\Proet},T)$ and $\mathsf{Mod}(X_{\Proet},T)$.
