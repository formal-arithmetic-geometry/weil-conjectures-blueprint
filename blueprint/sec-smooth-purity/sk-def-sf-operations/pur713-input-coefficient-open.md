For every morphism $f:X\to Y$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, the map $\alpha_{f,T}$ of Definition [pur713-def-coefficient-map] is an isomorphism; in particular this holds for open immersions $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, which is the case cited below. Source: governing reference, §3.3, Proposition ``Cartesian coefficient system'' (established there). It is immediate from Definition [pur713-def-coefficient-map], $\alpha_{f,T}$ being a component of a natural isomorphism.
