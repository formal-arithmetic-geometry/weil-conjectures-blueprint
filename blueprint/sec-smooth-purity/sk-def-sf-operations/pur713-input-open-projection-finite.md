For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, $L\in\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ and $M\in\mathsf{Sh}(U_{\Proet},\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, the map

$$
\pi_{j,L,M}:j_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(j^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L\otimes_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M)\longrightarrow L\otimes_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}j_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M,
$$

defined by the construction of Input [pur713-input-open-projection] with $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, the tensor product of Definition “Tensor product of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules” (`pur713-def-abelian-tensor-finite`) and the adjunction $j_{!,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv j^*_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Input “Fixed-coefficient open-extension adjunction” (`pur713-input-open-fixed`), is a natural isomorphism, compatible with the associativity and unit isomorphisms. This lies outside the governing reference and is a framework input here.
