By Input “Monoidality of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-monoidal`), $f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\dashv f_{*,\mathbf Z}$ is a monoidal adjunction, so Input [pur713-input-mapmod-adjunction] gives $(f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z})_{\mathcal R_{Y,T}}\dashv\operatorname{Res}_{\iota}\circ(f_{*,\mathbf Z})_{f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\mathcal R_{Y,T}}$, with $\iota:\mathcal R_{Y,T}\to f_{*,\mathbf Z}f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\mathcal R_{Y,T}$ the unit. Input [pur713-input-change-scalars] gives $\operatorname{Ext}_{\alpha_{f,T}}\dashv\operatorname{Res}_{\alpha_{f,T}}$. Composing, $f^*_T$ is left adjoint to $\operatorname{Res}_\iota\circ(f_{*,\mathbf Z})_{f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\mathcal R_{Y,T}}\circ\operatorname{Res}_{\alpha_{f,T}}$. Transport commutes with restriction of scalars, $(f_{*,\mathbf Z})_{f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\mathcal R_{Y,T}}\circ\operatorname{Res}_{\alpha_{f,T}}=\operatorname{Res}_{f_{*,\mathbf Z}\alpha_{f,T}}\circ(f_{*,\mathbf Z})_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}$, since both send $M$ to $f_{*,\mathbf Z}M$ with the action $f_{*,\mathbf Z}$ of the action of $M$ precomposed with $f_{*,\mathbf Z}(\alpha_{f,T}\otimes\mathrm{id})\circ m$, and since $\alpha_{f,T}$ is the adjoint of $\eta_{f,T}$, one has $\eta_{f,T}=f_{*,\mathbf Z}(\alpha_{f,T})\circ\iota$. By Lemma [pur713-lem-restriction-composition] the right adjoint is therefore $\operatorname{Res}_{\eta_{f,T}}\circ(f_{*,\mathbf Z})_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}=f_{*,T}$.
