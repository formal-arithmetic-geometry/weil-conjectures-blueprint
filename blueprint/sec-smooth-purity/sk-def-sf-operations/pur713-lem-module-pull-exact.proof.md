The transport $(f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z})_{\mathcal R_{Y,T}}$ is exact by Inputs “Adjunction and exactness of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-adjunction`) and [pur713-input-mapmod-exact]. Since $\alpha_{f,T}$ is an isomorphism, $\operatorname{Ext}_{\alpha_{f,T}}$ is exact, being quasi-inverse to $\operatorname{Res}_{\alpha_{f,T}}$ (Input [pur713-input-change-scalars-iso]). This is the argument of the governing reference, §8 (``The module-object pullback is exact'').
