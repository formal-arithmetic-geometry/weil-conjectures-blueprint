For $f:X\to Y$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, the maps $\eta_{f,T}$ and $\alpha_{f,T}$ of Definition [pur713-def-coefficient-map] are morphisms of commutative ring objects, where $f_{*,\mathbf Z}\mathcal R_{X,T}$ and $f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\mathcal R_{Y,T}$ carry the ring structures transported by Definition [pur713-def-mapmod]. Source: governing reference, §3.3 (established there; the isomorphism $f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}p_Y^*\cong p_X^*$ is monoidal).
