For $\phi$ as in Definition [pur713-def-restriction], $\operatorname{Ext}_\phi$ of Definition [pur713-def-extension] is left adjoint to $\operatorname{Res}_\phi$, with unit $M\to\operatorname{Res}_\phi(\mathcal S\otimes_{\mathcal R}M)$ induced by the unit of $\mathcal S$, and these adjunctions are compatible with identities and composition of ring-object morphisms. Source: governing reference, §4.2 (established there).
