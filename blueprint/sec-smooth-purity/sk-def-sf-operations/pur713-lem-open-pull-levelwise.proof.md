By Lemma [pur713-lem-open-pull-exact-module], $j^*_T$ is exact and therefore preserves quasi-isomorphisms. For a K-flat resolution $P\to M$, $j^*_TP\to j^*_TM$ is a quasi-isomorphism, natural in $M$, identifying the derived value with the levelwise value.
