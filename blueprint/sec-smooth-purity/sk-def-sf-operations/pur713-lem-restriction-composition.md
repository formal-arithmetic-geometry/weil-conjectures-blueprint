tags = ["latex:bplemma"]

For composable morphisms of commutative ring objects $\phi:\mathcal R\to\mathcal S$ and $\psi:\mathcal S\to\mathcal U$ of $\mathsf{Sh}(X_{\Proet},\mathsf{Ab})$, $\operatorname{Res}_{\psi\phi}=\operatorname{Res}_\phi\operatorname{Res}_\psi$ as functors $\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal U}(X_{\Proet})\to\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})$, and $\operatorname{Res}_{\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal R}}=\mathrm{id}$.
