For $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, $\alpha_{\mathrm{id}_X,T}$ is the identity after the identity comparison $\mathrm{id}^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\cong\mathrm{id}$ of Input “Composition of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-composition`), and for $u:Z\to X$ and $f:X\to Y$,

$$
\alpha_{fu,T}=\alpha_{u,T}\circ u^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}(\alpha_{f,T})
$$

after the composition isomorphism $u^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}f^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}\cong(fu)^{-1}_{\mathbf Z}$ of Input “Composition of fixed-coefficient images” (`pur713-input-fixed-composition`). Source: governing reference, §3.3 (compatibility of $\eta$ with identities and compositions, established there; the equations for $\alpha$ are their mates).
