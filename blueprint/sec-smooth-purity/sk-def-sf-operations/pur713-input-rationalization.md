For a finite subextension $E\subseteq\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ and every scheme $X$, the functor $E\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E}-$ of Definition [pur713-def-rationalization] is a localization of $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E)$ at the class of morphisms whose cone is annihilated by a power of $\ell$. Source: governing reference, §5.1 (established there). The governing reference does not treat the compatibility of this functor with $R\mathcal Hom$ or with exceptional inverse images (§11.1).
