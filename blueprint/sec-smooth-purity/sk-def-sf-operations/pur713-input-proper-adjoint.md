For each proper $p:Z\to Y$ of $k$-schemes of finite type, supply a right adjoint

$$
Rp^!_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:D(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\longrightarrow D(Z_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)
$$

of $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ (Definition “Finite-coefficient derived direct image” (`pur713-def-derived-push`)), together with a unit and a counit satisfying both triangle identities, chosen once and for all (Axiom of Choice). For the identity morphism choose the identity adjunction. This is the abstractly chosen right adjoint used in the exceptional-pullback construction of the governing reference, §7.1, where its existence is attributed to Brown representability; no proof of that existence on these ambient categories is supplied here. It is not supplied by an adjoint on a different site or on a constructible subcategory.
