Under Inputs [pur713-input-coefficient-structure], [pur713-input-coefficient-open] and [pur713-input-kflat-transport], for every $f:X\to Y$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ there is a natural isomorphism $Lf^*_T\cong\mathrm D(f^*_T)$ of functors $D(Y_{\Proet},T)\to D(X_{\Proet},T)$, whose value at a K-flat complex is the identity. In particular $Lf^*_T$ carries $D^+(Y_{\Proet},T)$ into $D^+(X_{\Proet},T)$.
