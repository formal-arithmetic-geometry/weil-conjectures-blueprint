Let $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$, and let all schemes be separated of finite type over $k$. For every morphism $f:X\to Y$, every proper morphism $p:Z\to Y$ and every open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, the functors $Lf^*_T$ (Definition [pur713-def-derived-pull-module]), $Rp_{*,T}$ (Definition [pur713-def-derived-push-module]), $\mathrm D(j_{!,T})$ and $\mathrm D(j^*_T)$ (Lemma [pur713-lem-open-adjunction-module]), and $-\otimes^L_{\mathcal R_{X,T}}-$ (Definition [pur713-def-tensor-module]) carry the bounded constructible subcategories of Definition [pur713-def-constructible-category-module] into the bounded constructible subcategories. Source: governing reference, §9; these preservation statements are the finiteness theorems for the $\ell$-adic coefficient rings, and are inputs of the statements there.
