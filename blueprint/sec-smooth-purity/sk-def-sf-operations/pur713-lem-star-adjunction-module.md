Under Inputs [pur713-input-coefficient-structure] and [pur713-input-kflat-transport], for $f:X\to Y$ and $T\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ there is an adjunction

$$
Lf^*_T\dashv Rf_{*,T}
$$

between $D(Y_{\Proet},T)$ and $D(X_{\Proet},T)$, obtained by deriving the adjunction of Lemma [pur713-lem-module-adjunction].
