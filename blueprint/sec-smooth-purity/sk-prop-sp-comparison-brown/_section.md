Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $d$ and let $\Lambda$ be any
admissible coefficient ring. There is a unique isomorphism of functors
$\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$, with components for $K\in\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$

$$
f_\Lambda^*K(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }f^!K
$$

compatible with the counits, where $f^!$ is the functor of
[sk-prop-sf-existence] restricted to $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$.
