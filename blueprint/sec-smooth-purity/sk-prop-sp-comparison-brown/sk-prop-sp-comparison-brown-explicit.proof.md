*Finite $A$.* The finite adjunction $Rf_{!,A}\dashv f^!_A$ is constructed on
coordinate charts by the projective-line adjunction and glued, and
$\gamma_{f,A}:f^*_A(-)(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_A$ is an isomorphism
([thm-pur-finite-coefficient-smooth-purity] for $A=A_{E,n}$,
[det-sp-finite-general-ring] for general $A$). Transporting the adjunction along
$\gamma_{f,A}$ makes $f^*_A(-)(d)[2d]$ a right adjoint.

*$\mathcal O_E$.* By [det-sp-integral-adjunction-bijection], $H_{f,\mathcal O_E}$ is
right adjoint to $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E}$ on $\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E)$ and
$f^*_{\mathcal O_E}(-)(d)[2d]\simeq H_{f,\mathcal O_E}$; transport the adjunction.

*$E$ and $\Qlb$.* These are [det-sp-rational-adjunction] (1) and
[det-sp-qbar-adjunction].

In each case the counit restricts on charts to iterated affine-line integration
(at finite level, and by transport in the other cases), which is the stated
normalization. For $\mathcal O_E$, $E$, $\Qlb$ the categories $\Dctf$ and $\Dbc$
coincide.
