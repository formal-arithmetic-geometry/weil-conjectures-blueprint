Let $A\to B$ be a map of finite commutative rings, each killed by a power of
$\ell$, and let $T$ be a scheme. For $K\in\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},A)$ we write

$$
K_B=K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{A_T}B_T\in\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},B),
$$

and for a morphism $u:K\to L$ we write
$u_B=u\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{A_T}\mathrm{id}_{B_T}$; this is a functor
$\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},A)\to\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},B)$. For a further map
$B\to C$, associativity of the tensor product gives $(K_B)_C\simeq K_C$,
compatibly with morphisms.
