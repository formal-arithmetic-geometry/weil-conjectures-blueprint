For $\phi$ as in Definition [pur713-def-restriction], regard $\mathcal S$ as an $\mathcal R$-module through $\phi$ and as an $\mathcal S$-module through multiplication on the left. Define
$\operatorname{Ext}_{\phi}:\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal R}(X_{\Proet})\to\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal S}(X_{\Proet})$
by $M\mapsto\mathcal S\otimes_{\mathcal R}M$, the relative tensor product of Definition [pur713-def-relative-tensor], with the $\mathcal S$-action induced by the left factor.
