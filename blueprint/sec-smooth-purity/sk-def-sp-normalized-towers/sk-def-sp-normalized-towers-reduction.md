For $M\in D(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ the reductions
$M/\ell^n=M\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,T}}\mathcal O_{E,T}/\ell^n$ form an
object $\operatorname{red}_TM=(M/\ell^n)_n$ of
$D(\operatorname{Tow}(T,\mathcal O_E))$, functorial in $M$, whose reduction maps
are isomorphisms. If $M\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$, then
$\operatorname{red}_TM\in\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^\bullet)_{\mathrm{norm}}$,
with the Tor amplitude bounds of $M$.
