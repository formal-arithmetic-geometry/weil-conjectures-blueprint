Let $\tau\in\{\Et,\Proet\}$. A sheaf $M$ of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $X_\tau$ is constructible if $X$ has a finite partition by locally closed constructible subschemes $i_s:X_s\to X$ such that each inverse-image restriction of $M$ to $(X_s)_\tau$ is locally constant with values finite $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules. Here the restriction is $i^*_{s,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback`, in [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) for $\tau=\Et$ and $i^*_{s,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M$ of Definition “Inverse image of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules” (`pur713-def-abelian-pull-finite`) for $\tau=\Proet$, and locally constant means locally isomorphic, on $(X_s)_\tau$, to the constant sheaf associated to such a finite module. This is the finite-coefficient definition of the detailed blueprint node `def-weil-finite-torsion-constructibility`.
