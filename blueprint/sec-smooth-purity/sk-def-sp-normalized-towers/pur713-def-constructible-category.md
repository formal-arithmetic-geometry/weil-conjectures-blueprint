The full subcategory $D^b_c(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\subseteq D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ consists of the objects with only finitely many nonzero cohomology sheaves, all constructible in the sense of Definition [pur713-def-finite-constructible] for $\tau=\Proet$. Morphisms are those of $D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. This definition imposes no preservation property on a geometric functor.
