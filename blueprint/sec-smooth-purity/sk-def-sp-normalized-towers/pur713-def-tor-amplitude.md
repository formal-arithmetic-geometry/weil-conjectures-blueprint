An object $K\in D(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ has Tor amplitude in $[u,v]$, with integers $u\leq v$, if

$$
\mathcal H^r(K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M)=0\quad(r\notin[u,v])
$$

for every sheaf $M$ of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $X_{\Proet}$, placed in degree zero, with the tensor product of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`). The interval is uniform in $M$. Finite Tor amplitude means the existence of such an interval.
