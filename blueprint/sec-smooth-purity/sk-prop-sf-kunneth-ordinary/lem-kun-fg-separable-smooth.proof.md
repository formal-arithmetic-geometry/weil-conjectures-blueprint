This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, the finitely generated case of Stacks Project, Algebra, Lemma 10.158.11 ([Tag 07BV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07BV)): a finitely generated separable field extension is a filtered colimit of finitely generated smooth subalgebras. The separable and algebraic cases are reduced to it in [lem-kun-separable-smooth-colimit] and [lem-kun-algebraic-separable-smooth]; no separate proof of the finitely generated case is given.
