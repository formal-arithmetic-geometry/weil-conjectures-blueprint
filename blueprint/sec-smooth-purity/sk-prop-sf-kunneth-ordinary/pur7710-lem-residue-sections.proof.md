Lemma [pur7710-lem-finite-type-module-cd] supplies the dimension hypothesis of Input [pur7710-input-constant-projection]. Apply that input with $M=\mathcal O_E/\varpi$ in degree zero: $P_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K,\mathcal O_E/\varpi}$ is an isomorphism. By Lemma [pur7710-lem-residue-extension-restriction], its source and target are the restrictions of scalars of the two displayed objects; the target is identified with derived sections at residue coefficients by Input [pur7710-input-cohomology-coefficients-unbounded]. The comparison commutes with the $\mathcal O_E/\varpi$-actions, which come from the factor $\mathcal O_E/\varpi$ on both sides, so it is an isomorphism in $D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi})$.
