Under Input [pur7710-input-prime-field-kunneth], for every $r\geq1$ and all $k$-schemes $X,Y$ of finite type, the map $\operatorname{Kun}_{\mathbf F_{\ell^r},K,L}$ of Definition [pur7710-def-kunneth-map-field] is an isomorphism for all $K\in D(X_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})$ and $L\in D(Y_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})$, unbounded.
