Assume Inputs “Forgetting finite coefficient actions in direct images” (`pur7710-input-cohomology-coefficients`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), [pur7710-input-affine-cd], “Geometric stalks on the small étale site” (`pur456-pbc-input-stalks`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), [pur7710-input-constant-projection] and [pur7710-input-unbounded-pbc]. For a proper morphism $f:X\to Y$ between $k$-schemes of finite type, the map $P_{f,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K,M}$ of Definition [pur7710-def-etale-projection] is an isomorphism for all $K\in D(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $M\in D(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, unbounded.
