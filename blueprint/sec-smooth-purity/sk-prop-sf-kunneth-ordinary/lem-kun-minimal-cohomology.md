Let $X$ be a scheme and $E\in\Dplus(X_{\Et})$. If $i$ is minimal
with $\mathcal H^i(E)\ne0$, then
$H^i(X_{\Et},E)=H^0(X_{\Et},\mathcal H^i(E))$.
