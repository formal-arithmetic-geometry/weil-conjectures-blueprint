For $f:X\to Y$ and $K\in D^+(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the functor of Definition [pur7710-def-unbounded-etale-push] and that of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived direct image” (`pur456-pbc-pushforward`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) have canonically isomorphic values at $K$, naturally in $K$.
