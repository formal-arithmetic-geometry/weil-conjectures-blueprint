For a morphism of schemes $f:X\to Y$ and a $\Lambda$-module (or
complex of $\Lambda$-modules) $M$, the canonical morphism
$f^{-1}\underline{M}\to\underline{M}$ is an isomorphism on
$X_{\Et}$.
