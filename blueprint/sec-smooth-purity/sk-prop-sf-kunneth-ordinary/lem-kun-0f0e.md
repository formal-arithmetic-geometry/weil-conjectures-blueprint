Let $X$ be a
quasi-compact and
quasi-separated scheme. For $E\in\Dplus(X_{\Et})$ and
$K\in\Dplus(\mathbf Z)$, the canonical map

$$
\RGamma(X_{\Et},E)\kunotimes{\mathbf Z}K\longrightarrow
\RGamma\bigl(X_{\Et},E\kunotimes{\mathbf Z}\underline{K}\bigr)
$$

is an isomorphism.
