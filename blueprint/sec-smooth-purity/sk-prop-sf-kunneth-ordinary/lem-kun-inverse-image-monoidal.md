Let $u:U\to V$ be a morphism of schemes and $\Lambda$ a commutative
ring. For $K,L\in D(V_{\Et},\Lambda)$ the canonical comparison
morphism

$$
u^{-1}K\kunotimes{\Lambda}u^{-1}L\longrightarrow
u^{-1}\bigl(K\kunotimes{\Lambda}L\bigr)
$$

of [def-kun-derived-tensor] associated with the
inverse image $u^{-1}$ is an
isomorphism, bifunctorial in $K$ and $L$.
(*Source:* Stacks Project, Lemma 21.18.4,
[Tag `07A4`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07A4),
for the morphism of ringed topoi induced by $u$.)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Lemma 21.18.4 ([Tag 07A4](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07A4)), for the morphism of ringed topoi induced by the morphism of schemes: the inverse image is monoidal for the derived tensor product. No separate proof is given.
