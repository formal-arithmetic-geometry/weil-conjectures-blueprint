Restrict coefficients along $\mathbf F_\ell\subseteq\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$. The resulting prime-field Künneth map respects the two $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$-actions, hence the action of $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}\otimes_{\mathbf F_\ell}\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$. Since $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}/\mathbf F_\ell$ is separable, this algebra is a product of fields, and the multiplication map onto $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$ is the projection onto one factor, corresponding to a central idempotent. Applying this idempotent summand to the tensor product over $\mathbf F_\ell$ gives the tensor product over $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$; this holds for complexes of vector spaces and for module sheaves, since the summand functor is exact and K-flat resolutions over $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$ are K-flat over $\mathbf F_\ell$. Derived sections commute with this summand. They also commute with restriction of these field coefficients: restriction is exact and preserves K-injective complexes because its left adjoint, field scalar extension, is exact. Taking the summand of the prime-field isomorphism therefore gives the map of Definition [pur7710-def-kunneth-map-field]; its construction uses the same tensor of counits, so this identifies the specified map.
