Let $W$ be a scheme. Under Input “Geometric stalks on the small étale site” (`pur456-pbc-input-stalks`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), the constant-sheaf functor $M\mapsto M_W$ from $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules to $\mathsf{Sh}(W_{\Et},\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ is exact. The same holds, with the same proof, for $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$-modules, $r\geq1$.
