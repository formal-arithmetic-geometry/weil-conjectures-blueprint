Let $W$ be a scheme and $d\geq0$ an integer such that $H^q(W_{\Et},F)=0$ for every sheaf $F$ of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $W_{\Et}$ and every $q>d$, where $H^q$ is the cohomology of $R\Gamma_{W,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}F$. Then the map $P_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n,K,M}$ of Definition [pur7710-def-constant-projection] is an isomorphism for every $K\in D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $M\in D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, without boundedness conditions. *Status:* Stacks Project, Tag `0F12`, in the form `lem-kun-0f12`; imported. Its proof uses preservation of coproducts by derived sections under the dimension hypothesis and generation of $D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ by the free module of rank one; it does not assert generation of all module sheaves on $W_{\Et}$ by global constant sheaves.
