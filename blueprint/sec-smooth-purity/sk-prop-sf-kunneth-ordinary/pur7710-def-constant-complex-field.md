For a scheme $W$ and $r\geq1$, define $c_{W,\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}:D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathbf F_{\ell^r}})\to D(W_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})$ as the levelwise functor of the constant-sheaf functor $M\mapsto M_W$ on $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$-modules, exact by Lemma “Exactness of constant sheaves” (`pur7710-lem-constant-exact`).
