Let $\pi:X'\to X$ and $h:Z\to X'$ be morphisms of schemes with
$\pi$ finite. For every
abelian sheaf $G$ on $Z$ and every $q\ge0$,

$$
R^q(\pi\circ h)_*G=\pi_*R^qh_*G.
$$
