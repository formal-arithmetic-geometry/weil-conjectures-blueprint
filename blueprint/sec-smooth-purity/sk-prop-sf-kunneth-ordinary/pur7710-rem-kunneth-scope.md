The results in this subsection concern ordinary cohomology on the étale site and finite coefficients. They are not a compact-support Künneth theorem, an exceptional-functor Künneth theorem, or an ambient pro-étale module-object projection theorem. Their extension to the continuous-coefficient categories requires the appropriate coefficient and functor comparisons. A statement with one proper factor and arbitrary torsion complexes over other coefficient rings is a separate theorem and is not part of Lemma [pur7710-lem-finite-ring-kunneth]. None of these finite-level results by itself proves existence of an ambient exceptional adjoint.
