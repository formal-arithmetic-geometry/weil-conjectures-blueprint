Under Input “Unbounded étale derived inverse-image adjunction” (`pur713-input-etale-unbounded-adjunction`), for $f:X\to Y$ the levelwise inverse image $Lf^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback-derived`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) is left adjoint to the functor $Rf_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ of Definition [pur7710-def-unbounded-etale-push] on the unbounded categories $D(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ and $D(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$.
