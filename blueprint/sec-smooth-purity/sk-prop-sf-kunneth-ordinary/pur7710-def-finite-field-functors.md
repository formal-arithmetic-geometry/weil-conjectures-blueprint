For $r\geq1$ and a morphism $g:W\to V$, the functors

$$
Lg^*_{\Et,\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}:D(V_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})\to D(W_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r}),\qquad
-\otimes^L_{\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}-:D(W_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})\times D(W_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})\to D(W_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})
$$

are defined by Definitions “Finite-coefficient étale derived inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback-derived`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) and “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`) with $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$ in place of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$: the levelwise inverse image of sheaves of $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$-modules, and the two-variable total left derived functor of the sheaf tensor product over $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$, computed on K-flat resolutions (which exist by Input “K-flat and K-injective resolutions on the small étale sites” (`pur713-input-etale-kflat`)).
