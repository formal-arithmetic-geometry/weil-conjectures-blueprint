For a Cartesian square of schemes with proper vertical map $r:W\to V$, arbitrary horizontal map $s:V'\to V$, base change $r':W'\to V'$ and projection $s':W'\to W$, the adjunction exchange $Ls^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Rr_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\to Rr'_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}Ls'^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$, formed with the unbounded functors and adjunctions of Definition [pur7710-def-unbounded-etale-push] and Lemma [pur7710-lem-unbounded-etale-adjunction] as in -def-exchangeZZ, is an isomorphism of functors on $D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$. In particular, for $s$ a geometric point it identifies the derived stalk of a proper direct image of an unbounded complex with the derived sections of the geometric fibre, by the specified map. *Status:* the unbounded torsion-ring theorem, Stacks Project, Tag `095V`; imported. The bounded-below Lemma “The derived proper-base-change comparison” (`pur456-pbc-derived`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) alone does not establish it.
