For a scheme $W$, there is an adjunction $c_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\dashv R\Gamma_{W,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}$ between $D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$ and $D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, and likewise $c_{W,\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}\dashv R\Gamma_{W,\Et,\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}$ for $r\geq1$, with the functors of Definitions [pur7710-def-constant-complex], [pur7710-def-constant-complex-field], [pur7710-def-derived-sections] and [pur7710-def-derived-sections-field], induced by the sheaf adjunction $(-)_W\dashv\Gamma(W_{\Et},-)$.
