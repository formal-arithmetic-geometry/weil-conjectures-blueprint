For a morphism $g:W\to V$ and $K,L\in D(V_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, there are natural isomorphisms
$(K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}L)\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal O_E/\varpi\cong(K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal O_E/\varpi)\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi}(L\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal O_E/\varpi)$ and $Lg^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi}(K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal O_E/\varpi)\cong(Lg^*_{\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K)\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal O_E/\varpi$, compatible with the constraints of the tensor products, and the analogous isomorphism for complexes of modules.
