Let $L/K$ be a finitely generated separable extension of fields. Then $L$
is a filtered colimit of finitely generated smooth $K$-algebras, and the
colimit may be taken over smooth $K$-subalgebras of $L$ with the inclusions
as transition maps.
(*Source:* the finitely generated case of Stacks Project, Algebra, Lemma
10.158.11, [Tag `07BV`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07BV);
the general separable case is deduced from it in
[lem-kun-separable-smooth-colimit], and the algebraic case is proved there
in [lem-kun-algebraic-separable-smooth].)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, the finitely generated case of Stacks Project, Algebra, Lemma 10.158.11 ([Tag 07BV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07BV)): a finitely generated separable field extension is a filtered colimit of finitely generated smooth subalgebras. The separable and algebraic cases are reduced to it in [lem-kun-separable-smooth-colimit] and [lem-kun-algebraic-separable-smooth]; no separate proof of the finitely generated case is given.
