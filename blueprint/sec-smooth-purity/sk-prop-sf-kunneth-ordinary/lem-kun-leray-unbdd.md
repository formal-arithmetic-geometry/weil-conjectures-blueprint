Let $f:X\to Y$ be a qcqs morphism of schemes with
$\kuncd(f)<\infty$ and let $n\ge1$. Then

$$
\RGamma(X_{\Et},E)\cong\RGamma\bigl(Y_{\Et},Rf_*E\bigr)
$$

canonically for every $E\in D(X_{\Et},\kunZn)$.
