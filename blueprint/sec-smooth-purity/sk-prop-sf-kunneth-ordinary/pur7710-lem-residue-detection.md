For any $M\in D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, if

$$
M\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi)=0
\quad\text{in }D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi}),
$$

with the functor of Definition [pur7710-def-residue-extension-module], then $M=0$. There is no boundedness or finite-generation hypothesis on $M$.
