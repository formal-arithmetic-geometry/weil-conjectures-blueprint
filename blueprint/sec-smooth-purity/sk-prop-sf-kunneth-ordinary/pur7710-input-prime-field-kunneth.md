For $k$-schemes $X,Y$ of finite type, the map $\operatorname{Kun}_{\mathbf F_\ell,K,L}$ of Definition [pur7710-def-kunneth-map-field] with $r=1$ is an isomorphism for all $K\in D(X_{\Et},\mathbf F_\ell)$ and $L\in D(Y_{\Et},\mathbf F_\ell)$. Neither separatedness nor properness of the factors is required. *Status:* Stacks Project, Tag `0F1P`, specialized to the integer $\ell$, and the node `thm-kun-0f1p`; imported. This theorem with $\mathbf Z/\ell$-coefficients is not itself a theorem with arbitrary finite-ring coefficients.
