For $K\in D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, restriction of scalars along $\rho$ identifies $K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal O_E/\varpi$ of Definition [pur7710-def-residue-extension] with $K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}c_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi)$, the derived tensor product of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`) on $W_{\Et}$, naturally in $K$. The same holds for Definition [pur7710-def-residue-extension-module] with $\mathcal O_E/\varpi$ viewed as an $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-module.
