Let $j:U\to X$ be an
open immersion. Then
$j^{-1}j_*=\mathrm{id}$ on $\Ab(U_{\Et})$, and for every
$F\in D(U_{\Et})$ the canonical morphism
$\RGamma(X_{\Et},Rj_*F)\to\RGamma(U_{\Et},F)$ of
[lem-kun-leray-bdd] is an isomorphism (Leray for $j$).
