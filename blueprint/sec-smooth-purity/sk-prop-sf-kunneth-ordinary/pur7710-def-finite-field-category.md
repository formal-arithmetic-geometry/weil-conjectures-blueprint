For a finite field $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$, with $r\geq1$, and a scheme $W$, define

$$
D(W_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})
:=D\bigl(\mathsf{Sh}(W_{\Et},\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathbf F_{\ell^r}})\bigr),
$$

the ordinary derived category of the abelian category of sheaves of $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$-modules on the small étale site, in the sense of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived categories” (`pur456-pbc-finite-category`, in [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]). Its bounded variants use the cohomological convention of that definition. The residue field $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$ is the case $n=1$ of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived categories” (`pur456-pbc-finite-category`, in [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]).
