For a morphism $f:X\to Y$, let $Rf_{*,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}:D(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\to D(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$ be the total right derived functor, computed on K-injective resolutions, of the sheaf direct image of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]).
