For a scheme $W$ and $\tau\in\{\Et,\Proet\}$, the derived sections functor

$$
R\Gamma_{W,\tau,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}:D(W_\tau,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)
\longrightarrow D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n})
$$

is the total right derived functor, computed on K-injective resolutions, of the global-sections functor $\Gamma(W_\tau,-)$ on sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-modules; here $D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n})$ is the ordinary derived category of $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-modules, in the sense of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived categories” (`pur456-pbc-finite-category`, in [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]). The finite-level sheaf categories of Definitions “Finite-coefficient étale derived categories” (`pur456-pbc-finite-category`, in [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) and “Finite-coefficient pro-étale derived category” (`pur713-def-finite-category`) are used here also for the test schemes of Definition [pur7710-def-ind-etale-tests], which need not be of finite type. The value retains the $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-action on the section complex.
