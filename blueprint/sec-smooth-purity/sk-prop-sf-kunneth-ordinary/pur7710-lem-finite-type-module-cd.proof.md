By Input “Forgetting finite coefficient actions in direct images” (`pur7710-input-cohomology-coefficients`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) and the structure morphism $W\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, the derived sections of $F$ agree with those of its underlying abelian sheaf, which is torsion because it is killed by $\varpi^n$, hence by a power of $\ell$. Lemma [pur7710-lem-separably-closed-cd] gives $\mathrm{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(k)\leq0$, and Lemma [pur7710-lem-finite-type-cd] with $c=0$ gives the bound $2d$.
