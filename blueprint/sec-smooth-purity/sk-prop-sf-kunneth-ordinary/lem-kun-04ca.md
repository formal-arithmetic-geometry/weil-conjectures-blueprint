Let $i:Z\to X$ be a
closed immersion of
schemes. The functor $i_*:\Ab(Z_{\Et})\to\Ab(X_{\Et})$ of
[def-weil-pbc-direct-image] is fully faithful, $i^{-1}$ is a
left inverse, and the essential image consists of those abelian sheaves
on $X_{\Et}$ whose
support is contained in $Z$.
