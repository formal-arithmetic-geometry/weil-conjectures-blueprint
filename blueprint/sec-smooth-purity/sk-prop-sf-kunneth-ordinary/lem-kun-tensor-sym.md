For a commutative ring $\Lambda$ and $A,B\in D(T_{\Et},\Lambda)$
there is a canonical isomorphism

$$
A\kunotimes{\Lambda}B\cong B\kunotimes{\Lambda}A
$$

in $D(T_{\Et},\Lambda)$, natural in $A$ and $B$.
