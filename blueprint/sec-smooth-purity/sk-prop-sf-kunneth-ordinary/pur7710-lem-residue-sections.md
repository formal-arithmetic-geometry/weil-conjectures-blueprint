Under Inputs “Forgetting finite coefficient actions in direct images” (`pur7710-input-cohomology-coefficients`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]), [pur7710-input-affine-cd], [pur7710-input-constant-projection] and [pur7710-input-cohomology-coefficients-unbounded], for every $k$-scheme $W$ of finite type and every $K\in D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)$, the constant-tensor comparison induces a natural isomorphism in $D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi})$:

$$
R\Gamma_{W,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi)
\xrightarrow{\sim}
R\Gamma_{W,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\varpi}
\bigl(K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal O_E/\varpi\bigr),
$$

with the functors of Definitions [pur7710-def-residue-extension] and [pur7710-def-residue-extension-module].
