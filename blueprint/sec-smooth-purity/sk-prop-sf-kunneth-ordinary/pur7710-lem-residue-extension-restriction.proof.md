For a K-flat resolution $P\to K$, both sides are computed on $P$: the left side by definition, and the right side because one K-flat factor computes a derived tensor product. After restriction of scalars the underived functors agree termwise, $(\mathcal O_E/\varpi)_W\otimes_{(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W}P=P\otimes_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi)_W$. The module case is the same computation.
