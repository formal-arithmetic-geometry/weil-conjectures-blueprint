Let $X$ and $Y$ be schemes of finite type over a field $K$ and
$z\in X\times_KY$ with images $x\in X$ and $y\in Y$. Then $z$
is a closed point of $X\times_KY$ if and only if $x$ and $y$ are
closed points.
