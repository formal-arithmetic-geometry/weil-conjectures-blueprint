Let $K$ be a field and let $f:X\to Y$ be a morphism between
schemes of finite type over $K$.

1. **(1)** $\kuncd(f)<\infty$.
1. **(2)** If $K$ is separably
closed, then $\kuncd(X)<\infty$ for every scheme $X$ of finite type
over $K$.
