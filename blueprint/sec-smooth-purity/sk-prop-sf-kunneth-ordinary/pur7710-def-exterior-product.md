Let $X,Y$ be $k$-schemes, $Z=X\times_kY$, with projections $p_X:Z\to X$, $p_Y:Z\to Y$. For $K\in D(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$ and $L\in D(Y_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, define

$$
K\boxtimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L
:=Lp^*_{X,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}K\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}Lp^*_{Y,\Et,\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}L
\in D(Z_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n),
$$

with the inverse images of Definition “Finite-coefficient étale derived inverse image” (`pur456-pbc-pullback-derived`, in [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) and the étale derived tensor product of Definition “Finite-coefficient derived tensor product” (`pur713-def-tensor`).
