Assume Inputs “Forgetting finite coefficient actions in direct images” (`pur7710-input-cohomology-coefficients`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) and [pur7710-input-affine-cd]. For a $k$-scheme $W$ of finite type and of dimension at most $d$, $H^q(W_{\Et},F)=0$ for every sheaf $F$ of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules on $W_{\Et}$ and every $q>2d$. Thus $W$ satisfies the hypothesis of Input [pur7710-input-constant-projection].
