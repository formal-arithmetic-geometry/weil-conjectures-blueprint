For a scheme $W$ and $M,N\in D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n})$, there are natural isomorphisms $c_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(M\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}N)\cong c_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}M\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}c_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}N$ and $c_{W,\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\cong(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W$, compatible with the associativity, symmetry and unit isomorphisms; the same holds with $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$ in place of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$.
