For a commutative ring $\Lambda$ and
$E\in D(T_{\Et},\Lambda)$, both canonical unit constraints
$E\kunotimes{\Lambda}\underline{\Lambda}\to E$ and
$\underline{\Lambda}\kunotimes{\Lambda}E\to E$ are isomorphisms.
