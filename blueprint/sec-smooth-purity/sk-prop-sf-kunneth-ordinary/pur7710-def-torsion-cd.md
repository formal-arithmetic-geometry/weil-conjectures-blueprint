An abelian sheaf $F$ on the small étale site $W_{\Et}$ of a scheme $W$ is torsion if every local section of $F$ is killed by some nonzero integer. For an integer $q\geq0$, $H^q(W_{\Et},F)$ denotes the $q$-th right derived functor of the global-sections functor on $\mathsf{Sh}(W_{\Et},\mathsf{Ab})$. For an integer $d\geq0$, the assertion $\mathrm{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(W)\leq d$ means that $H^q(W_{\Et},F)=0$ for every torsion abelian sheaf $F$ on $W_{\Et}$ and every $q>d$. For a field $K$, $\mathrm{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(K)$ means $\mathrm{cd}_{\mathrm{tor}}(\operatorname{Spec}K)$. The bound concerns all torsion sheaves, not only constructible sheaves or a fixed constant sheaf.
