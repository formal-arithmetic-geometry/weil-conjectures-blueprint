Sheafification does not change geometric stalks, and the geometric stalk of the constant presheaf with value $M$ is the filtered colimit of copies of $M$ along identity maps, that is $M$. Hence the stalk functor composed with $M\mapsto M_W$ is the identity, which is exact, and Input “Geometric stalks on the small étale site” (`pur456-pbc-input-stalks`, see [sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]) shows that a sequence of sheaves is exact if its geometric stalks are.
