Let $\rho:\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n\to\mathcal O_E/\varpi$ be the reduction homomorphism and $W$ a scheme. Residue scalar extension

$$
-\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n}\mathcal O_E/\varpi:
D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)\longrightarrow D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\varpi)
$$

is the total left derived functor, computed on K-flat resolutions (Definition “K-flat complexes of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$-modules” (`pur713-def-kflat`)), of the sheaf functor $M\mapsto(\mathcal O_E/\varpi)_W\otimes_{(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n)_W}M$, whose target carries the $\mathcal O_E/\varpi$-action of the left factor.
