Let $\rho:\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n\to\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$ be the reduction homomorphism and $W$ a scheme. Residue scalar extension

$$
-\otimes^L_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E:
D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)\longrightarrow D(W_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E)
$$

is the total left derived functor, computed on K-flat resolutions (Definition “K-flat complexes of sheaves of $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$-modules” (`pur713-def-kflat`)), of the sheaf functor $M\mapsto(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E)_W\otimes_{(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_W}M$, whose target carries the $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$-action of the left factor.
