For a scheme $W$ and $r\geq1$, let
$R\Gamma_{W,\Et,\mathbf F_{\ell^r}}:D(W_{\Et},\mathbf F_{\ell^r})\to D(\mathsf{Mod}_{\mathbf F_{\ell^r}})$
be the total right derived functor, computed on K-injective resolutions, of global sections of sheaves of $\mathbf F_{\ell^r}$-modules on $W_{\Et}$, as in Definition [pur7710-def-derived-sections].
