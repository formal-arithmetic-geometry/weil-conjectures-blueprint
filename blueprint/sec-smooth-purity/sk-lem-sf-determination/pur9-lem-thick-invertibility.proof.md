The full subcategory of objects $X$ with $\alpha_X$ invertible contains $0$ and is closed under isomorphisms by naturality, under shifts by Lemma [pur9-lem-shift-invertibility], under cones by Lemma [pur9-lem-two-of-three], and under summands by Lemma [pur9-lem-summand-invertibility]. It is therefore a thick subcategory containing $\mathscr S$.
