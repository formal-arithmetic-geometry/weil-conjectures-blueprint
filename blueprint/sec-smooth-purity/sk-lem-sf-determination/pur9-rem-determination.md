Lemmas [pur9-lem-two-of-three]–[pur9-lem-thick-invertibility] propagate invertibility only. Two morphisms of exact functors agreeing on a generating class, or on all geometric stalks, need not be equal. The source node records an example, not verified here and not used: over $R=\mathbf F_2[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ with the derivation $\partial\epsilon=1$, a bimodule extension yields a morphism of exact functors $\mathrm{Id}\to[1]$ on perfect $R$-complexes vanishing on $R$ but not on $[R\xrightarrow{\epsilon}R]$. Accordingly, equalities between comparison maps in this manuscript are proved from their constructions.
