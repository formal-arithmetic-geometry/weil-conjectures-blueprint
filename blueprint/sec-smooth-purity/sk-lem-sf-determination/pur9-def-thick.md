A full subcategory of a triangulated category is thick if it contains $0$, is closed under isomorphisms, shifts in both directions, and cones (if two vertices of a distinguished triangle lie in it, so does the third), and under direct summands. The thick subcategory generated by a class $\mathscr S$ of objects is the intersection of all thick subcategories containing $\mathscr S$.
