In the setting of [def-sf-datum], a natural transformation $\alpha$ between
two exact functors that is an isomorphism on a class of objects is an
isomorphism on the thick subcategory generated by that class; in particular,
if the class generates, $\alpha$ is an isomorphism.

This propagates invertibility only. A natural transformation is *not*
determined by its values on a generating class: for
$R=\mathbf F_2[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ the derivation $\partial\epsilon=1$
gives $\eta:\mathrm{Id}\to[1]$ on $\operatorname{Perf}(R)$ vanishing on $R$
but nonzero on $P=[R\xrightarrow{\epsilon}R]$, so $\mathrm{id}$ and
$\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\ \eta&1\end{pmatrix}$ are natural automorphisms of
$\mathrm{Id}\oplus[1]$ agreeing on $R$ and differing on $P$. Equalities of
comparison maps must be proved from their constructions.

## Proof

The class of objects on which $\alpha$ is invertible is closed under
cones by [lem-sf-triangle-two-out-of-three], under shifts because
$\alpha_{X[1]}=\alpha_X[1]$ by exactness, and under direct summands because
$\alpha_{X\oplus Y}=\alpha_X\oplus\alpha_Y$ and the inverse of an invertible
diagonal map is diagonal. It is therefore a thick subcategory containing the
given class.
