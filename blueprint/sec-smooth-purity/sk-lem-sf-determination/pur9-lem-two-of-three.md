Let $\alpha:\Phi\to\Psi$ be a morphism of exact functors (Definition [pur9-def-exact-transformation]) and $X\to Y\to W\to X[1]$ a distinguished triangle in $\mathscr T$. If two of $\alpha_X,\alpha_Y,\alpha_W$ are isomorphisms, so is the third.
