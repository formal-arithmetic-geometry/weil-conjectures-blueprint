Let $\iota_X,\pi_X$ be the inclusion and projection of the summand $X$. Exact functors are additive, and naturality gives $\alpha_{X\oplus Y}\Phi(\iota_X)=\Psi(\iota_X)\alpha_X$ and $\alpha_X\Phi(\pi_X)=\Psi(\pi_X)\alpha_{X\oplus Y}$. Then $\beta=\Phi(\pi_X)\alpha_{X\oplus Y}^{-1}\Psi(\iota_X)$ satisfies $\alpha_X\beta=\Psi(\pi_X)\Psi(\iota_X)=1$ and $\beta\alpha_X=\Phi(\pi_X)\alpha_{X\oplus Y}^{-1}\alpha_{X\oplus Y}\Phi(\iota_X)=1$. The same holds for $Y$.
