Let $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion of $k$-schemes of finite type with $X$ smooth over $k$ and $Z$ regular. Then $a_X:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ and $a_Z:Z\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ are smooth and $a_Z=a_Xi$.
