If moreover $i$ is regular of pure codimension $c$ (Definition [pur9-def-normal-cone]) and $X_0$ is a connected component of $X$ of dimension $N$, then $Z\cap X_0$, if nonempty, has pure dimension $N-c$. (The letter $N$ is used for dimensions to keep $n$ for the coefficient ring $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$.)
