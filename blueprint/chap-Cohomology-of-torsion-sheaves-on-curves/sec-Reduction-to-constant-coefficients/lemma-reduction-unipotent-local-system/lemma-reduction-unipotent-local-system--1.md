Let $\ell$ be a prime, let $U$ be a connected scheme, and let $G$ be a
finite locally constant sheaf of $\mathbf F_\ell$-vector spaces on
$U_{\acute{e}t}$. There are a connected scheme $V$, a finite étale
morphism $v:V\to U$, and a natural number $d$ prime to $\ell$ such that
every geometric fibre of $v$ has $d$ points and $v^*G$ has a finite
filtration

$$
0=W_0\hookrightarrow W_1\hookrightarrow
\cdots\hookrightarrow W_n\simeq v^*G
$$

whose successive cokernels are isomorphic to the constant sheaf
$\mathbf F_\ell$ on $V_{\acute{e}t}$. The length $n$ may be zero.
