Let $\ell$ be a prime, let $U$ be a connected scheme, and let $G$ be a finite classical
local system of
$\mathbf F_\ell$-vector spaces.  There is a finite étale cover
$v:V\to U$ of degree $d$ prime to $\ell$ such that $v^*G$ has a
finite filtration with successive quotients the constant
sheaf $\mathbf F_\ell$.
