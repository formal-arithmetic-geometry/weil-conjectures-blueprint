Let $\ell$ be a prime, let $U$ and $V$ be schemes with $V$ connected,
and let $G$ be a finite locally constant sheaf of $\mathbf F_\ell$-vector
spaces on $U_{\acute{e}t}$. Let $v:V\to U$ be finite étale, and suppose
that every geometric fibre of $v$ has the same cardinality $d$, with
$\ell\nmid d$. The unit $\eta_G:G\to v_*v^*G$ has a retraction
$r:v_*v^*G\to G$:

$$
r\circ\eta_G=\id_G.
$$

In particular, $G$ is a direct summand of $v_*v^*G$.
