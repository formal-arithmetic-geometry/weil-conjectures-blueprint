Quasi-coherent cohomology on an affine scheme vanishes in positive
degrees, and, as in the proof of the proposition, étale and Zariski
cohomology of $\mathbf G_a$ agree. In the long exact sequence
associated to the Artin–Schreier sequence on $U_{\acute{e}t}$, the terms
$H^q(U,\mathbf G_a)$ for $q\geq1$ are therefore zero. What remains is
the exact sequence
$0\to H^0(U,\mathbf F_p)\to\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)\xrightarrow{\varphi-1}\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)\to H^1(U,\mathbf F_p)\to0$,
together with $H^q(U,\mathbf F_p)=0$ for $q>1$.  By
Theorem [theorem-bounded-below-cohomology-comparison] these statements carry over to $U_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$.
