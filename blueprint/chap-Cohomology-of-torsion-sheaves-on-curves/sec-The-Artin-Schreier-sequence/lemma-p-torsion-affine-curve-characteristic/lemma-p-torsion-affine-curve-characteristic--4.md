For any affine scheme $X$, the sheaf $\mathbf G_a$ on $X_{\acute{e}t}$
has no positive-degree cohomology:

$$
H^{q+1}(X_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_a)=0\qquad(q\geq0).
$$

Here $\mathbf G_a$ assigns to an étale $X$-scheme $V$ the additive
group of $\Gamma(V,\mathcal O_V)$.
