The quotient formula follows in natural language from the beginning of
the long exact sequence for
[lemma-p-torsion-affine-curve-characteristic--3]. Since
$H^1(U_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_a)=0$ by
[lemma-p-torsion-affine-curve-characteristic--4], its connecting map
identifies $H^1(U_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf F_p)$ with the cokernel of
$\varphi-1$ on $\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)$. Theorem
[theorem-bounded-below-cohomology-comparison] gives the classical
pro-étale formulation. This deduction is not packaged as a quotient
isomorphism in the mainline declarations used here.
