Let $U$ be an affine scheme and $p$ a prime such that
$p=0$ in $\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)$. For the constant étale sheaf
$\mathbf F_p$, we have

$$
H^q(U_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf F_p)=0\qquad(q\geq2).
$$
