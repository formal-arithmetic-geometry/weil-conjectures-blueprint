Every section $t$ on an étale $X$-scheme $V$ acquires a root of
$y^p-y-t$ on the base change to $V$ of
$\operatorname{Spec}(\Gamma(V,\mathcal O_V)[y]/(y^p-y-t))$.
This cover is étale because the derivative is $-1$, and surjective
because the algebra is faithfully flat, being free of positive rank
$p$. Hence $\varphi-1$ is locally surjective and therefore an
epimorphism of sheaves. Together with its kernel inclusion, this
gives the short exact sequence.

The constants in $\mathbf F_p$ lie in $K$. Distinct constants cannot
agree on a nonempty étale scheme, since their difference is a unit.
Conversely, if $x^p-x=0$, put
$y_r=\prod_{s\in\mathbf F_p,\,s\ne r}(x-s)$. On $D(y_r)$ we have
$x=r$, and these basic opens cover: Lagrange interpolation expresses
$1$ as a linear combination of the $y_r$. Thus the map from the
constant presheaf is locally injective and locally surjective onto
$K$. After sheafification it is an isomorphism.
