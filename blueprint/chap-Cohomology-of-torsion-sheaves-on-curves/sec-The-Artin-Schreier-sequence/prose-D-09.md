For the constant sheaf $\mathbf F_p$ on smooth curves, we obtain
$(C_1)$, $(C_3)$, $(C_5)$ and $(C_7)$ from the proposition on proper
curves, and $(C_1)$, $(C_2)$ and $(C_3)$ from the lemma on affine
curves, while $(C_8)$ holds because $H^2$ vanishes in both cases.
