The degree-zero cohomology of a sheaf is its group of sections over
the terminal object of the small étale site. For the constant sheaf,
these sections are the locally constant functions $X\to\mathbf Z/n$.
Since $X$ is connected, every such function is constant. This gives
the required additive isomorphism; the universe lift in the constant
sheaf is removed by the canonical additive equivalence.
