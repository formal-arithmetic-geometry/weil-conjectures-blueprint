Let $C$ be a smooth separated connected curve of finite type and
of dimension one over an algebraically closed field of characteristic
$p>0$. Then

$$
H^q(C_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf F_p)=0\qquad(q>1).
$$

Properness is not required. In Lean this is stated as the vanishing
of the degree-$q$ cohomology object of derived global sections of the
constant abelian sheaf placed in degree zero.
