When $j$ is dense, Lemma [lemma-removing-finitely-many-points], together with
the calculation for constant $\mathbf F_\ell$ on $C$ via
Lemma [lemma-normalization-constant-sheaf], shows that $j_!\underline{\mathbf F_\ell}$
satisfies the assertions of Theorem [theorem-cohomology-curves].
An arbitrary open is dense in the reduced union of the components it
meets, a closed subscheme of $C$, so we can apply the dense case
there and pass back to $C$: direct image along a finite
morphism is exact [Stacks, [0A3Q](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A3Q)] and, by
Lemma [lemma-finite-morphisms-cohomology], does not change cohomology.
