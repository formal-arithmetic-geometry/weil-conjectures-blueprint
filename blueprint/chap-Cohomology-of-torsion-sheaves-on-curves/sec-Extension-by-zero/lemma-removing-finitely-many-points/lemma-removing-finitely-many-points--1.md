Let $C$ be a reduced separated curve of finite type over an
algebraically closed field $k$, let $j:U\hookrightarrow C$ be a dense
open with finite complement $i:Z\hookrightarrow C$, let $\ell$ be a prime, and let $F$ be a
classical constructible sheaf with $\ell F=0$. For each of the
assertions $(C_1)$–$(C_8)$ of Theorem [theorem-cohomology-curves],
under its hypotheses on $C$ and on the prime $\ell$, the assertion
holds for $F$ if and only if it holds for $j_!j^*F$.
