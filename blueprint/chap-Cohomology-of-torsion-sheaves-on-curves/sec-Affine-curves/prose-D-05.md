For the constant sheaf $\mathbf F_\ell$, with $\ell$ a prime different
from $\operatorname{char}k$,
the two preceding propositions prove $(C_1)$–$(C_8)$ on smooth curves.
Indeed, a primitive $\ell$th root of unity in $k$ identifies
$\mathbf F_\ell$ with $\mu_\ell$, compatibly with the pullback maps to
$C_{k'}$, since the root of unity stays primitive in $k'$.
For $(C_8)$, note that an open in a smooth connected proper curve is
either the whole curve or an affine curve, which has $H^2=0$, and that
an open in a smooth affine curve is affine.
