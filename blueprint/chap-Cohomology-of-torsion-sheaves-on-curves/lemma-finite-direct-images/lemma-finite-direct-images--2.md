Let $u:V\to W$ be finite, and consider abelian sheaves, or sheaves of
modules over a constant coefficient ring. The functor $u_*$ from such
sheaves on $V_{\acute{e}t}$ to such sheaves on $W_{\acute{e}t}$ is exact.
