title = "Finite direct images"

Let $u:V\to W$ be finite. For an abelian sheaf $F$ on $V_{\acute{e}t}$ and a
geometric point $\bar w:\operatorname{Spec}(\Omega)\to W$, with $\Omega$
algebraically closed, one has

$$
(u_*F)_{\bar w}
\simeq \prod_{\substack{\bar v:\operatorname{Spec}(\Omega)\to V\\
u\bar v=\bar w}}F_{\bar v}.
$$

The product is finite. The functor $u_*$ from abelian sheaves on
$V_{\acute{e}t}$ to abelian sheaves on $W_{\acute{e}t}$ is exact. The same holds for
sheaves of modules over a constant coefficient ring.
