This compatibility statement is bypassed by the Lean route, which
transfers field-extension invariance using the pulled-back short exact
sequence and the cohomology of a sheaf with finite support.

Let $C$ be a reduced curve of finite type over an algebraically closed
field, let $\pi:C^{\mathrm{nor}}\to C$ be its normalization, and let $M$
be a finite abelian group. The cokernel of
$\underline M_C\to\pi_*\underline M_{C^{\mathrm{nor}}}$ and the
resulting short exact sequence commute with algebraically closed field
extension. This assertion about the normalization sequence is not a
conclusion of the Lean comparison lemma.
