Let $C$ be a reduced separated scheme of finite type and dimension at
most one over an algebraically closed field $k$. Let $\nu:C'\to C$ be
finite and surjective, and let $V\subset C$ be a dense open over which
$\nu$ is an isomorphism. Let $j:U\hookrightarrow C$ be an open immersion,
and write $U'=C'\times_C U$, with projections $j':U'\hookrightarrow C'$
and $\nu':U'\to U$. Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative ring and $\ell$ a
prime such that $R=\Lambda/\ell\Lambda$ is finite.

There is a monomorphism of étale sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules

$$
\alpha:j_!\underline R_U\longrightarrow\nu_*j'_!\underline R_{U'}
$$

whose cokernel $Q$ is constructible and satisfies $Q|_V=0$.
