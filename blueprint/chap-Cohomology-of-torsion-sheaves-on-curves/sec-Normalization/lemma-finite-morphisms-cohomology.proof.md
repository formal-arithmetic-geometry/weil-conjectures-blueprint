On the étale sites, Lemma [lemma-finite-morphisms] shows that $h_*$
is exact, that its stalks are finite products of stalks of $G$, and that its
formation commutes with arbitrary base change.  Since $h_*$ is exact, the Leray spectral
sequence gives $H^q(C,h_*G)=H^q(Y,G)$.  The comparison of classical
direct images and
Theorem [theorem-bounded-below-cohomology-comparison] transport these statements to
the pro-étale site; see [Stacks, [03QP](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QP), [0959](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0959)].
