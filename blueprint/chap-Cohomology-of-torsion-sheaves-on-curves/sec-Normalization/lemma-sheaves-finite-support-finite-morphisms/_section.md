Let $F$ be a classical abelian sheaf supported on finitely many closed
points of a scheme $C$ of finite type over an algebraically closed field
$k$. Then $H^q(C,F)=0$ for $q>0$, and $H^0(C,F)$ is the direct sum of
the stalks of $F$ at these points; the pullback map on $H^0$ is an
isomorphism under algebraically closed extension of $k$.
