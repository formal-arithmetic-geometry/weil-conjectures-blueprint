Let $k\subseteq k'$ be algebraically closed fields, and let $C$ be a
separated scheme of finite type over $k$ with $\dim C\leq0$.
Put $C'=C\times_k k'$ and write $g:C'\to C$ for the projection.
For every abelian sheaf $F$ on $C_{\acute{e}t}$, the canonical pullback
map on global sections is an isomorphism

$$
H^0(C,F)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H^0(C',g^*F).
$$
