The sheaf $F$ is $h_*$ of a sheaf on a finite disjoint union of
copies of $\operatorname{Spec}k$, where $h$ is the inclusion of the
points. On these points taking sections is exact
because $k$ is algebraically closed, and the sections are the
product, here a finite direct sum, of the stalks, which do not
change under extension of $k$. Finally, Lemma [lemma-finite-morphisms-cohomology] carries these assertions over to $C$.
