Let $h:Y\to C$ be a finite morphism of schemes. The direct image $h_*$
from abelian sheaves on $Y_{\acute{e}t}$ to abelian sheaves on
$C_{\acute{e}t}$ preserves finite colimits. Since it also preserves
finite limits, it is exact.

More generally, the same assertion holds for sheaves with values in a
Grothendieck abelian category $\mathcal A$ admitting small-site
sheafification and a faithful forgetful functor to sets which reflects
isomorphisms, preserves filtered colimits, and takes $\mathcal A$-valued
sheaves on $C_{\acute{e}t}$ to sheaves of sets. In particular, it holds
for sheaves of modules over any ring.
