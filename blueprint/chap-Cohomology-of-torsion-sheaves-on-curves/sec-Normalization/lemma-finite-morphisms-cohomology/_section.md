Let $h:Y\to C$ be a finite morphism of schemes. The direct image $h_*$
of [classical](@definition-classical-pro-etale-sheaves) sheaves is exact, and $H^q(C,h_*G)=H^q(Y,G)$ for every [classical](@definition-classical-pro-etale-sheaves) abelian
sheaf $G$ on [$Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$](@definition-small-pro-etale-site) and every $q$.
