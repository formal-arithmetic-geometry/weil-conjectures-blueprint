For a smooth separated scheme $C$ of finite type and dimension at most
one over an algebraically closed field,

$$
H^2(C_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_m)=0.
$$

Neither connectedness nor properness is required. By
[lemma-cohomology-multiplicative-group--5], this says that $\operatorname{Br}(C)=0$.
