For a smooth separated scheme $C$ of finite type and dimension at most
one over an algebraically closed field,
$$
\operatorname{Br}'(C)=H^2(C_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_m)=0,
$$
where $\operatorname{Br}'$ is the cohomological Brauer group of
[lemma-cohomology-multiplicative-group--5]. Neither connectedness nor
properness is required.
