Let $X$ be an integral regular noetherian scheme, and let
$i:\operatorname{Spec}(k(X))\to X$ be its generic point. The pullback map
$$
H^2(X_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_m)\longrightarrow
H^2(\operatorname{Spec}(k(X))_{\acute{e}t},i^*\mathbf G_m)
$$
is injective.
