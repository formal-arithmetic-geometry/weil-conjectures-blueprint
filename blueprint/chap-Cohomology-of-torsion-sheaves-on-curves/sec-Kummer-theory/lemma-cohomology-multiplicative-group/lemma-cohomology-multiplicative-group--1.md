For any scheme $X$, there is a canonical isomorphism of abelian groups
$$
\operatorname{Pic}(X)\simeq H^1(X_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_m),
\qquad
[\mathcal L]\longmapsto[\operatorname{Isom}(\mathcal O_X,\mathcal L)].
$$
Here tensor product on the Picard group corresponds to addition in cohomology.
