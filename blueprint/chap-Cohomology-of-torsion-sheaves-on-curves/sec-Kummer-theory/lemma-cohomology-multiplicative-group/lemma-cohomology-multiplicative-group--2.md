This all-degree assertion is bypassed by the Lean route, which uses the
étale degree-two vanishing in [lemma-cohomology-multiplicative-group--6].
The statement retained here is that, for every $q\geq2$,
$$
H^q(C_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\nu_C^*\mathbf G_m)=0.
$$
The mainline has no declaration proving the vanishing for all these degrees.
