*Shared context.* Let $C$ be a smooth separated curve of finite type over
an algebraically closed field, with dimension at most one. The Picard
identification and its naturality below are stated more generally for
schemes $X$ and $Y$, on the small étale site. The comparison of
Theorem [theorem-bounded-below-cohomology-comparison] relates étale
cohomology to the cohomology of $\nu_C^*\mathbf G_m$ on the pro-étale site;
the mainline's Kummer calculation uses the étale statements.
