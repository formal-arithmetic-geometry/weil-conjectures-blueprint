*Shared context.* Let $C$ be a smooth separated curve of finite type over
an algebraically closed field, with dimension at most one. Some
statements below are made more generally for schemes $X$ and $Y$, on the
small étale site. The comparison of
Theorem [theorem-bounded-below-cohomology-comparison] relates étale
cohomology to the cohomology of $\nu_C^*\mathbf G_m$ on the pro-étale site.
