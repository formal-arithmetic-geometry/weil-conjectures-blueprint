For any scheme $X$, the cohomological Brauer group is, by definition,
$$
\operatorname{Br}'(X)=H^2(X_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_m).
$$
This is `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.CohomologicalBrauerGroup`, the group used
in the Lean route. No comparison with the group of Azumaya algebras is
asserted here.
