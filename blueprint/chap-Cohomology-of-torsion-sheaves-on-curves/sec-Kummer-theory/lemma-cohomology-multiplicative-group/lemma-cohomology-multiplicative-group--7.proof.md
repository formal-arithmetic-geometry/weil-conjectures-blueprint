Choose a nonempty affine open $U\subseteq C$. Its ring of functions is a
finitely generated $k$-domain of dimension at most one, and its fraction
field is $k(C)$. Noether normalization identifies the transcendence
degree with the dimension, giving $\operatorname{trdeg}_k k(C)\leq1$.
In transcendence degree zero the field is $k$, which is algebraically
closed. Otherwise it is algebraic over a rational function field
$k(t)$. The latter is $C_1$ by a count of the coefficients of
polynomial unknowns, and the $C_1$ property passes to algebraic
extensions.

It remains to see that a $C_1$ field $K$ has
$H^2(\operatorname{Spec}(K)_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_m)=0$. Compute
degree-two cohomology on finite Galois covers by Čech cohomology.
Hilbert 90 supplies the required exactness on sections, and the
crossed-product argument for a $C_1$ field kills the second Galois
Čech cohomology.
