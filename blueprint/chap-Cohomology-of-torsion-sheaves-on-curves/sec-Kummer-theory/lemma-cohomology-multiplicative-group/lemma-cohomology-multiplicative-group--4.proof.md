For an étale $Y$-scheme $V$, pull a trivialization of $\mathcal L$ back
along $V\times_YX\to V$. The resulting maps of torsors are natural in
$V$ and equivariant for pullback of units. Naturality of the $H^1$-class
under these maps gives
`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.classH1_etaleIsoTorsor_pullback`.
Represent a Picard class by an invertible sheaf and apply this equality.
