Restrict the torsor of trivializations of $\mathcal L$ along $f$.
Pullback of morphisms of modules identifies it with the torsor of
trivializations of $f^*\mathcal L$, compatibly with the action of
$\mathbf G_m$; this is
`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.etaleIsoTorsor_restrict_equiv`.
The compatibility of torsor classes with restriction,
`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.classH1_etaleRestriction`, gives the formula.
