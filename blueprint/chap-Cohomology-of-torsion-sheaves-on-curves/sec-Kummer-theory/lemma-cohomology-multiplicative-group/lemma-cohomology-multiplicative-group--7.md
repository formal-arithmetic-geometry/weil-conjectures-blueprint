Let $C$ be an integral separated scheme of finite type and dimension at
most one over an algebraically closed field $k$. Its function field
$k(C)$ is $C_1$: every homogeneous form of positive degree $d$ in more
than $d$ variables has a nonzero zero. Moreover,

$$
H^2(\operatorname{Spec}(k(C))_{\acute{e}t},\mathbf G_m)=0.
$$
