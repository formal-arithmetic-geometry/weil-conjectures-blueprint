On a proper curve, the lemma and the Kummer sequence reduce the
cohomology of $\mu_n$ to the groups $\operatorname{Pic}(C)[n]$ and
$\operatorname{Pic}(C)/n$, both computed in Appendix [chap-The-Picard-scheme-of-a-curve].
