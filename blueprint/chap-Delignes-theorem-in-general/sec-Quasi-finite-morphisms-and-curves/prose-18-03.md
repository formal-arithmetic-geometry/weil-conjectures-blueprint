A lisse sheaf stays geometrically irreducible on a curve $C$ in $X$ as
soon as the image of $\pi_1(C)$ is large enough. The next lemma shows
that it suffices for this image to surject onto one finite quotient of
$\pi_1(X)$, and in Lemma [lemma-curve-preserving-finite-cover] we
construct curves with this property.
