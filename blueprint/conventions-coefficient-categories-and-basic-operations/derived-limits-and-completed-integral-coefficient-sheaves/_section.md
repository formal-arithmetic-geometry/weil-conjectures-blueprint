The integral stage. Derived limits are taken inside one triangulated
category, by the Roos triangle [def-weil-derived-limit], and exist there
[lem-weil-derived-limit-existence]. The coefficient object is the completed
sheaf $\widehat{\mathcal O}_{E,Y}=\varprojlim_n(A_{E,n})_Y$
[def-weil-completed-integral-coefficients] — modules over it, not over the
discrete constant sheaf $(\mathcal O_E)_Y$. Derived completeness
[def-weil-derived-complete] together with uniform bounded constructibility of
the reductions cuts out $D^b_c(Y,\mathcal O_E)$
[def-weil-derived-complete-integral-detailed].
