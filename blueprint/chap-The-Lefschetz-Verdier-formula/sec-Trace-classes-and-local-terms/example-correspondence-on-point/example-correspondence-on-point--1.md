Let $X=s$, let $C=\coprod_{i=1}^ns$ be $n$ points, with $c_1=c_2$ the
structure map, and let $u:c_2^*A=A_C\to c_1^!A=A_C$ be the identity, using
$c_1^!=c_1^*$ for the finite étale map $c_1$. Pullback along $c_2$ is the
diagonal $A\to A^n$, and the counit $Rc_{1!}c_1^!A=A^n\to A$ is the sum.
