Let $X=s$, let $C=\coprod_{i=1}^ns$ be $n$ points, with $c_1=c_2$ the
structure map, and let $u:c_2^*A=A_C\to \href{@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image}{c_1^!A}=A_C$ be the identity, using
$\href{@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image}{c_1^!}=c_1^*$ for the finite étale map $c_1$. Then pullback along $c_2$ is the diagonal $A\to A^n$, and the counit $\href{@proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek}{Rc_{1!}}\href{@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image}{c_1^!A}=A^n\to A$ is the sum.
Hence [$T_c(u)$](@definition-action-correspondence) is multiplication by $n$ on $R\Gamma(s,A)=A$, with trace $n$. Since
$X\times X=s$, the [fixed locus](@definition-correspondence) is all of $C$. On each of these $n$ points $u$ restricts to the identity [correspondence](@definition-correspondence) of $s$ on $A$, whose [local term](@definition-trace-class-local-terms) is
$\href{@definition-categorical-trace}{\operatorname{tr}_A(1\mid A)}=1$ by Lemma [lemma-local-terms-point].
The right side of the theorem is therefore $n$ as well. Note that the [local terms](@definition-trace-class-local-terms) are indexed by the points of $C$, not of $X$: here $X$ has one point and the [fixed locus](@definition-correspondence) has
$n$.
