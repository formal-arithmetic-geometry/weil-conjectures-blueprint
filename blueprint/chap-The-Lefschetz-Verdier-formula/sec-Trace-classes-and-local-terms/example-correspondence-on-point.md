Let $X=s$, let $C=\coprod_{i=1}^ns$ be $n$ points, with $c_1=c_2$ the
structure map, and let $u:c_2^*A=A_C\to c_1^!A=A_C$ be the identity, using
$c_1^!=c_1^*$ for the finite étale map $c_1$. Pullback along $c_2$ is the
diagonal $A\to A^n$, and the counit $Rc_{1!}c_1^!A=A^n\to A$ is the sum.
Hence $T_c(u)$ is multiplication by $n$ on $R\Gamma(s,A)=A$, with trace $n$. Since
$X\times X=s$, the fixed locus is all of $C$. On each of its $n$ points $u$
restricts to the identity correspondence of $s$ on $A$, whose local term is
$\operatorname{tr}_A(1\mid A)=1$ by Lemma [lemma-local-terms-point].
The right side of the theorem is therefore $n$ as well. The local terms are indexed by
the points of $C$, not of $X$: here $X$ has one point and the fixed locus has
$n$.
