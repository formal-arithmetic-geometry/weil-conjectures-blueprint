An isomorphism $u:P\to Q$ transports the duality maps by $u$ on the object and $(u^{-1})^\vee$ on its dual. Insert these transports into the composite defining the trace of $uvu^{-1}$. The adjacent $u^{-1}u$ and their duals cancel, leaving the composite defining the trace of $v$.
