Let $B^i\subset Z^i\subset P^i$ be the boundaries and cycles. They are stable under $v$, with quotients $Z^i/B^i=H^i(P)$ and $P^i/Z^i\simeq B^{i+1}$ equivariantly. Thus $\operatorname{Tr}(v^i)=\operatorname{Tr}(v|B^i)+\operatorname{Tr}(H^i(v))+\operatorname{Tr}(v|B^{i+1})$. In the alternating sum of clause [proposition-properties-trace--4], the two boundary sums cancel after shifting the index, leaving the cohomological traces.
