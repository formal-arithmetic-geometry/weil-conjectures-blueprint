In the evaluation–coevaluation definition the endomorphism $v$ occurs exactly once. Tensoring and composition are $A$-linear in this entry. Replacing $v$ by $av+bw$ therefore replaces its trace by $a\operatorname{tr}(v)+b\operatorname{tr}(w)$.
