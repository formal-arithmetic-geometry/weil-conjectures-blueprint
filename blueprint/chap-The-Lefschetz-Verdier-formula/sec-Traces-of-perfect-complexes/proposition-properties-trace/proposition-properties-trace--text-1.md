*Shared context.* The trace of [definition-categorical-trace] is defined by the
same evaluation, braiding and coevaluation composite in a braided monoidal
category. In the details below it takes values in the endomorphisms of the
unit, denoted $\mathbf 1$. For perfect complexes over a commutative ring $A$,
the endomorphism ring of the unit is $A$. Where a statement does not name its
category, $v$ is an endomorphism of a perfect complex $P$ over a commutative
ring $A$ (over a field $L$ in the last statement), and the trace is
$\operatorname{tr}_A(v\mid P)\in A$.
