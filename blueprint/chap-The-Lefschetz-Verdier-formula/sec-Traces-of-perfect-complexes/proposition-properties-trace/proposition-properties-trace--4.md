For a bounded complex of finite projective modules and a chain
endomorphism $v^\bullet$ representing $v$ one has

$$
\operatorname{tr}_A(v\mid P)
=\sum_i(-1)^i\operatorname{Tr}_A(v^i\mid P^i).
$$
