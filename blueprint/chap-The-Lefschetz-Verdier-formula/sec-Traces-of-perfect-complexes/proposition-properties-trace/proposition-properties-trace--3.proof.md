Represent $P$ by a bounded finite-projective complex. Tensoring its finite dual bases with $B$ transports evaluation and coevaluation to those of $P\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}B$, and preserves the symmetry with its degree signs. The new trace composite is consequently the scalar extension of the old one.
