In the additivity statement of the proposition we ask for an endomorphism of
the filtered object, and not merely of the three vertices of a triangle.
The reason is that the middle component is not determined by the outer
ones, and even for a morphism of triangles the trace of the middle
component need not be the sum of the outer traces.
