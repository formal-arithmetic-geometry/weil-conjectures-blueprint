Both sides of the trace formula are traces of endomorphisms of perfect
$A$-complexes, namely of $R\Gamma_c(X,K)$ on the global side and of the
stalks of $K$ on the local side.

Over a field the trace of an endomorphism of a complex is the
alternating sum of its traces on cohomology. Over $A=\mathbf Z/\ell^2$
the cohomology modules need not be free, so they have no trace, and
Example [example-trace-not-determined-cohomology] below shows that
the maps on cohomology do not determine the trace. We therefore define
the trace on the complex. On a bounded complex of finite projective
modules it is the alternating sum of the traces of the components.
The definition below expresses this sum through evaluation and
coevaluation, so that it depends only on the morphism in $D(A)$. In
this form it is compatible with every change of rings
(Proposition [proposition-properties-trace]). This is how we pass from the trace
formula over $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$ to the trace formula over $\mathcal O_E$ and $E$
(Section [sec-ell-adic-coefficients-5]).
