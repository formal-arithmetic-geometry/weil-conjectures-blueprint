For $P=A$ in degree zero and $v$ multiplication by $a$, coevaluation is
$1\mapsto1\otimes1$ and evaluation is multiplication, so the trace is
$a$. The next proposition lists the properties of the trace that we use.
Compatibility with change of rings lets us move the trace formula between coefficient rings, and the chain-level formula is how we compute traces in examples.
