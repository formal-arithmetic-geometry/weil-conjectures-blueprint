Here $c_2$ is the leg along which $K$ is pulled back and $c_1$ is the
leg along which we take $c_1^!$; in [sga5, III, §3] the two names are
interchanged. Since $X$ is separated, $\Delta_X$ and $i_c$ are closed
immersions.

The example to keep in mind is the graph of an endomorphism
$\varphi:X\to X$, that is, $C=X$ with $c_1=\operatorname{id}_X$ and
$c_2=\varphi$. Then $c_1^!K=K$, and a cohomological correspondence is a
morphism $u:\varphi^*K\to K$. In this case the fixed locus $\Fix(c)$ is the fixed point scheme of $\varphi$, which we also denote $\Fix(\varphi)$. For the Frobenius $\Phi_X$ and a Weil complex this is the correspondence whose trace formula we want.
