The *fixed locus* of $c$ is the closed subscheme

$$
\Fix(c)=C\times_{X\times X,\Delta_X}X,
\qquad i_c:\Fix(c)\longrightarrow C,\quad d_c:\Fix(c)\longrightarrow X,
$$

where $i_c$ and $d_c$ are the two projections.
