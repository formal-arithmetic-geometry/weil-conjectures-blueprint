The operations are derived using filtered flat and filtered injective
resolutions. For finite filtrations these can be built
successively on the graded pieces by mapping cylinders, and on them
the three formulas hold termwise. The filtered evaluation is
the tensor–Hom adjoint of $\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal K}$, whose graded
map in weight zero is $\operatorname{id}_{K_a}$ on each summand. Hence on
$K_a\otimes^{\mathbf L}\mathbb D_XK_a$ it is the ordinary evaluation.
The Koszul signs are those of the underlying complexes, and the
filtration weights contribute no signs.
