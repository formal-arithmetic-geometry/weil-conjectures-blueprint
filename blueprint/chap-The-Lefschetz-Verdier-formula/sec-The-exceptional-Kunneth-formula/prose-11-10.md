In linear algebra the trace is evaluation $W\otimes W^\vee\to A$ on
the diagonal. Here evaluation takes values in [$\omega_X$](@definition-verdier-dual-evaluation) on the diagonal, and
by applying [$c^!$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image) we move it to the [fixed locus](@definition-correspondence).
