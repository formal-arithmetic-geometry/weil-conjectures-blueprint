Let $\ell$ be a prime, let $k$ be a field of positive characteristic
different from $\ell$, and let $X,C$ be separated $k$-schemes of finite
type. Let $K\in\Dbc(X,\Qbarl)$ and let
$u:c_2^*K\to c_1^!K$ be a cohomological correspondence with support
$c=(c_1,c_2):C\to X\times_kX$. Put
$P_X=K\boxtimes\mathbb D_XK$. There is a canonical isomorphism

$$
\mathcal H_c(K):=\cHom_{\Qbarl}(c_2^*K,c_1^!K)
\xrightarrow{\sim}c^!P_X.
$$

This isomorphism depends only on $K$ and the support map $c$.
