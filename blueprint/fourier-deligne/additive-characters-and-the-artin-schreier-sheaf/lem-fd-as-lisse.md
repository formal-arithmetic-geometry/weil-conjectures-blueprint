The sheaf $\mathcal L_{\psi,f}$ is lisse of rank one on $X_0$, and

$$
\mathcal L_{\psi,f}\otimes\mathcal L_{\psi,g}\cong\mathcal L_{\psi,f+g},\qquad
(\mathcal L_{\psi,f})^{-1}\cong\mathcal L_{-\psi,f}.
$$

This is the second assertion of [lem-fd-as-characters].

## Proof

This is immediate from the decomposition of [lem-fd-as-characters] (the tensor and inverse of the rank-one isotypic components correspond to the sum and the negative of the characters).
