Let $\pi:V\to S$ be a vector bundle of rank $d$.
For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)$, the trace map induces an
isomorphism

$$
\pi^{*}K(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}\pi^{!}K.
$$
