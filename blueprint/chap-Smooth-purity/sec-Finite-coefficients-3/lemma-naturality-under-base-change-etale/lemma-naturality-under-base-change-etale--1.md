Let $h:S'\to S$ be any morphism for which the schemes below
satisfy the standing hypotheses. Put $V'=V\times_SS'$, and denote
the base changes of the maps by $g',s',\pi',z'$. There are canonical
identifications

$$
N_{s'}\simeq N_s\times_SS'.
$$
