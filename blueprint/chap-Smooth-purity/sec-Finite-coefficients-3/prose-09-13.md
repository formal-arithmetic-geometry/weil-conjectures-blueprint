The orientation of $\omega_f$ will be the comparison (9.13)
for the diagonal. Two things have to be checked about it. On a coordinate
chart it must be the identification given by coordinates. And it must
commute with base change, as property (2) of
Theorem [theorem-smooth-purity-all-admissible-coefficients] requires. A
chart is an étale map that preserves the section. So both checks need
to transport (9.13) along base change on $S$ and along
étale maps that preserve the section. In both cases we use the uniqueness in
Lemma [lemma-section-normal-bundle]: any construction that is the
identity at $t=1$ produces the comparison of that lemma.
