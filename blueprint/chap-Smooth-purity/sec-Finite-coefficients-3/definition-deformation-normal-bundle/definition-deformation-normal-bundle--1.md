The *deformation to the normal bundle* of $s$ is the open subscheme
$\mathcal D_{s(S)}V$ of the blowup of $V\times\mathbf A^1$ along
$s(S)\times\{0\}$ obtained by removing the strict transform of
$V\times\{0\}$.
