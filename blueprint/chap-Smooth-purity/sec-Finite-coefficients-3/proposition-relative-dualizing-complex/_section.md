Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $d$. There is a
canonical isomorphism

$$
\omega_{f}\xrightarrow{\sim}(\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)_{X}(d)[2d].
\tag{9.14}
$$
