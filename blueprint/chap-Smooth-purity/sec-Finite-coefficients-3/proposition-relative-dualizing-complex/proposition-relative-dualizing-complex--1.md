Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring equipped with a normalized
Kummer class, and let $k$ be a field. Let $g:V\to S$ be smooth of pure
relative dimension $d$ and admit a compactification, with $S$ separated
and locally of finite type over $k$. Suppose that the exponent of
$\Lambda$ is invertible on $V$, and let $s:S\to V$ be a closed section
of $g$. There is a canonical isomorphism in $D(S_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$

$$
Ls^{*}\omega_g\xrightarrow{\sim}\Lambda_S(d)[2d].
$$
