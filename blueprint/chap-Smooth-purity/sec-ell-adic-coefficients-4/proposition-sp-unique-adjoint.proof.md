Let $\rho(K)$ be the $J$-transpose of $\varepsilon_f(K)$ and $\rho'(K)$ the
$f^!$-transpose of $\varepsilon^J(K)$. Since $\rho'\rho$ and the identity have the
same transpose, they are equal, and by symmetry $\rho\rho'=\mathrm{id}$. A map
compatible with the counits has $J$-transpose $\varepsilon_f(K)$, so it is
$\rho(K)$.
