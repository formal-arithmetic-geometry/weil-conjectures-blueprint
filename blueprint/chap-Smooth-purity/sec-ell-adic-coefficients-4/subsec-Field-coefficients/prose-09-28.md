Applying Proposition [proposition-sp-unique-adjoint] once more and inverting the resulting isomorphism, we get
$\gamma_{f,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}:f_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}^*(-)(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$, whose counit is
obtained by extension of scalars from a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$. What is left of
Theorem [theorem-smooth-purity-all-admissible-coefficients] are the compatibilities with change of coefficients, base
change and composition, and the trace normalization.
