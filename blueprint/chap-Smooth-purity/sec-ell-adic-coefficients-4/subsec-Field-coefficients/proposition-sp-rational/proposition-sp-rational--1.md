Let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be finite. Then $Rf_{!,E}\dashv\bigl(K\mapsto f_E^*K(d)[2d]\bigr)$ as functors
between $\Dbc(X,E)$ and $\Dbc(Y,E)$.
