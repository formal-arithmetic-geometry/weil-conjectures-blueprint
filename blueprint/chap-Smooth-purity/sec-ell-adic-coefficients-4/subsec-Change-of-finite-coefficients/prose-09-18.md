The levels $\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$ of a tower are related by extension of
scalars. The first thing to know is therefore how $Rf_!$ and $f^!$ behave
under a map of finite rings $A\to B$. The comparison for $f^!$ is the
transpose of a counit. The counits commute with $-\otimes^{\mathbf L}B$,
because on a chart they are built from the trace of the projective line and
sums over fibres.

Let $A\to B$ be a map of finite commutative rings killed by a power of $\ell$.
