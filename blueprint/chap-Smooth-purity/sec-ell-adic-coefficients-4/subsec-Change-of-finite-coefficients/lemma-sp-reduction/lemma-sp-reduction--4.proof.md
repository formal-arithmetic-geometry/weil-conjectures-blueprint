For $A\to B\to C$, both compact-support comparisons are obtained by extension of scalars through a compactification. Extension by zero commutes with the associativity comparison for tensor, and the proper-direct-image comparison is induced termwise on free resolutions. Associativity of tensor identifies the direct comparison with the successive comparisons, proving transitivity.
