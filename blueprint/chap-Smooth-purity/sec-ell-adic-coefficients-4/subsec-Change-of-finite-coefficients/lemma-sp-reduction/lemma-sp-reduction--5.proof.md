Transpose the direct and successive exceptional comparisons by the final adjunction. By clause [lemma-sp-reduction--3], each transpose is its counit composite. Applying the $B\to C$ counit identity after the $A\to B$ identity, and using clause [lemma-sp-reduction--4], gives exactly the $A\to C$ identity. The transposes agree, so the original maps agree.
