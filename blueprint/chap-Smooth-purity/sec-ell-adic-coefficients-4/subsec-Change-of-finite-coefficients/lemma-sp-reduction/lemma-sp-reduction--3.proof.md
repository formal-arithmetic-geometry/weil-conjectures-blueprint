The comparison $c_{A,B}(K)$ is defined as the adjoint transpose of the right side of (9.16). Under the adjunction, transposition means composing its direct image with the counit, which is exactly that equation. The adjunction is a bijection on these Hom sets, so the counit equation also uniquely characterizes the comparison.
