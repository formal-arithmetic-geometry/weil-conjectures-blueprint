Associativity of derived tensor gives

$$
(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{A_T}B_T)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{B_T}C_T
\simeq K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{A_T}(B_T\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{B_T}C_T)
\simeq K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{A_T}C_T.
$$

The first and last terms are $(K_B)_C$ and $K_C$ by definition. The associativity maps are natural in $K$, so this is the canonical comparison.
