Applying $R\varprojlim$ to (9.19) at $\operatorname{red}_YK$, we obtain
$\gamma_{f,\mathcal O_E}:f^*_{\mathcal O_E}K(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_{\mathcal O_E}K$,
whose reductions are the isomorphisms $\gamma_{f,\mathcal O_E/\ell^n}$. This gives the
isomorphism (9.2) for $\mathcal O_E$, and for $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$ the one for $\mathbf Z_\ell$.
