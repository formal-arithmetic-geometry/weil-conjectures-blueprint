For (1), by Lemma [lemma-structure-integral-constructible-complexes], $K$ is a finite extension of
shifted sheaves $a_!C$ with $C$ of Tor amplitude $[-1,0]$ on a stratum.
For a single such piece the reductions $(a_!C)/\ell^n=a_!(C/\ell^n)$ are
constructible with Tor amplitude in $[-1,0]$ for every $n$, since reduction
commutes with extension by zero. Moreover, Tor amplitude and constructibility of the
reductions are preserved by extensions and shifts, and since the number of steps and
the shifts do not depend on $n$, the bounds do not either.
For (2), we reduce further from $\ell$ to the uniformizer. The reduction
$K/\pi_E=(K/\ell)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E/\ell}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$
is bounded constructible, because $K/\ell$ has finite Tor amplitude over
$\mathcal O_E/\ell$. Since $K$ is derived complete,
$K\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$ by the
criterion (2)$\Rightarrow$(1) of Proposition [proposition-integral-constructibility-criterion].
