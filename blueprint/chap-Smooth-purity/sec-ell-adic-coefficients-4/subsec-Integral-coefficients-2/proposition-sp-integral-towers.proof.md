For (1), by Lemma [lemma-structure-integral-constructible-complexes], $K$ is a finite extension of
shifted sheaves $a_!C$ with $C$ of [Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves) $[-1,0]$ on a stratum.
For a single such piece the reductions $(a_!C)/\ell^n=a_!(C/\ell^n)$ are
[constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) with [Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves) in $[-1,0]$ for every $n$, since reduction
commutes with extension by zero. Moreover, [Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves) and constructibility of the
reductions are preserved by extensions and shifts, and since the number of steps and
the shifts do not depend on $n$, the bounds do not either.
For (2), we reduce further from $\ell$ to the uniformizer. The reduction
$K/\pi_E=(K/\ell)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E/\ell}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$
is bounded [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves), because $K/\ell$ has finite [Tor amplitude](@definition-constructible-sheaves) over
$\mathcal O_E/\ell$. Since $K$ is [derived complete](@definition-derived-adic-completeness),
$K\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$ by the
criterion (2)$\Rightarrow$(1) of Proposition [proposition-integral-constructibility-criterion].
