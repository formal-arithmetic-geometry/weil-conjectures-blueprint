The functor $\operatorname{red}_TM=(M/\ell^n)_n$ is left adjoint to
$R\varprojlim:D(\operatorname{Tow}(T))\to D(\href{@definition-small-pro-etale-site}{T_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}},\mathcal O_E)$, with unit the
[derived completion map](@definition-derived-adic-completeness) $M\to R\varprojlim_nM/\ell^n$. This adjunction restricts to an
equivalence between [$\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) and the normalized constructible towers.
