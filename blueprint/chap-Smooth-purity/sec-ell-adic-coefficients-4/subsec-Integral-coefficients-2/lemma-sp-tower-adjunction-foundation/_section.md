The functor $\operatorname{red}_TM=(M/\ell^n)_n$ is left adjoint to
$R\varprojlim:D(\operatorname{Tow}(T))\to D(T_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$, with unit the
derived completion map $M\to R\varprojlim_nM/\ell^n$. This adjunction restricts to an
equivalence between $\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$ and the normalized constructible towers.
