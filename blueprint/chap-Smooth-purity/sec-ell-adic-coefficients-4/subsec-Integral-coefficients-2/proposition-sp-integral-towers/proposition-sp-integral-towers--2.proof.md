For this implication, we reduce further from $\ell$ to the uniformizer. The reduction
$K/\pi_E=(K/\ell)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E/\ell}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$
is bounded constructible, because $K/\ell$ has finite Tor amplitude over
$\mathcal O_E/\ell$. Since $K$ is derived complete,
$K\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$ by the
criterion (2)$\Rightarrow$(1) of Proposition [proposition-integral-constructibility-criterion].
