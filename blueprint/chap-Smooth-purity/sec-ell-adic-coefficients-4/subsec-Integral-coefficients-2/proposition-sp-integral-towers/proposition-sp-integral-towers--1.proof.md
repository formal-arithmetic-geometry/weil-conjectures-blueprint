For (1), by Lemma [lemma-structure-integral-constructible-complexes], $K$ is a finite extension of
shifted sheaves $a_!C$ with $C$ of Tor amplitude $[-1,0]$ on a stratum.
For a single such piece the reductions $(a_!C)/\ell^n=a_!(C/\ell^n)$ are
constructible with Tor amplitude in $[-1,0]$ for every $n$, since reduction
commutes with extension by zero. Tor amplitude and constructibility of the
reductions are preserved by extensions and shifts. The number of steps and
the shifts do not depend on $n$, so the bounds do not either.
