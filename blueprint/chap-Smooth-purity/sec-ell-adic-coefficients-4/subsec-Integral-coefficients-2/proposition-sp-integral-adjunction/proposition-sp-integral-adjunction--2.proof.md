Clause [proposition-sp-integral-adjunction--1] and the restriction of the adjunction in Theorem [theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image] give two right adjoints to the same $Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E}$ on the full constructible categories. By Yoneda there is a unique isomorphism identifying their Hom bijections; under those bijections the counits correspond to identities, so this isomorphism preserves the counit. Conversely, preserving the counit determines the same transpose, proving uniqueness.
