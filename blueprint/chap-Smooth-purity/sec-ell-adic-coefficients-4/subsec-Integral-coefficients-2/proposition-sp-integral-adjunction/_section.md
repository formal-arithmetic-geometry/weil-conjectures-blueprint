With the unit and counit obtained from towers, $H_{f,\mathcal O_E}$ is right adjoint to
[$Rf_{!,\mathcal O_E}$](@proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek) on [$\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves). Hence there is a unique counit-preserving
isomorphism $H_{f,\mathcal O_E}\simeq f^!_{\mathcal O_E}$ with the right adjoint of
Theorem [theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image].
