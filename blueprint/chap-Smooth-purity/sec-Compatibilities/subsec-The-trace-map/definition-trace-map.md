For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$, the *trace map* is the composite

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{f}(K):\quad
Rf_!\bigl(f^{*}K(d)[2d]\bigr)
\xrightarrow{\ Rf_!\gamma_{f}(K)\ }
Rf_!f^!K
\xrightarrow{\ \varepsilon_{f}(K)\ }K.
\tag{9.25}
$$
