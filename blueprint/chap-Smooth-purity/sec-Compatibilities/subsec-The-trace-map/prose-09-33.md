By the first part of the remark, for affine space the trace map is the explicit integration along lines
built from $\mathbf P^1$. The trace map of a general smooth morphism is
glued from such charts, and the next statement says that the gluing
respects composition, as integration over the fibres of a composite of
submersions is iterated integration.
