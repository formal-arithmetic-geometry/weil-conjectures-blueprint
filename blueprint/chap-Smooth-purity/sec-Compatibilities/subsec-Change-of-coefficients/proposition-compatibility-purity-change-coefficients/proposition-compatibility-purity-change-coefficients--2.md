Assume $\Lambda=A$, $\Lambda'=B$ for a homomorphism of finite admissible rings. The coefficient comparison for $Rf_!$ carries $(\operatorname{Tr}_{f,\Lambda}(K))_{\Lambda'}$ to $\operatorname{Tr}_{f,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$.
