For a homomorphism $A\to B$ of finite admissible rings, formula (9.17) identifies the scalar extension of the specified orientation-and-tensor purity map with $\gamma_{f,B}$. Its target identification is characterized by the counit identity (9.16), so it is the same exceptional coefficient comparison used in this statement. This proves the required equality.
