Assume $\Lambda=E$ and $\Lambda'=F$ for a finite extension $F/E$. The coefficient comparison for $Rf_!$ carries $(\operatorname{Tr}_{f,\Lambda}(K))_{\Lambda'}$ to $\operatorname{Tr}_{f,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$.
