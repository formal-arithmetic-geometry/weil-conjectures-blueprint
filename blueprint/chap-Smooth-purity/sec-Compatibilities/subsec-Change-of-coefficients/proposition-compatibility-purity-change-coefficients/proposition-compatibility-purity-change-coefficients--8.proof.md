By Definition [definition-trace-map], the trace is $\varepsilon_f\circ Rf_!(\gamma_f)$. Scalar extension carries $\gamma_f$ to the required purity map by clause [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--7]. Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients] identifies the $Rf_!$ comparison and carries the counit to the counit over the new coefficient ring. Applying these identifications to the composite gives precisely the new trace, proving the statement.
