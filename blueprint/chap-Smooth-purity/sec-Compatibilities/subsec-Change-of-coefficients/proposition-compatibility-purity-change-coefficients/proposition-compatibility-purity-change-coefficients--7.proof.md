Apply inversion of $\ell$ to the integral finite-extension compatibility in clause [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--5]. The adjunctions and purity maps over $E$ and $F$ are defined by that inversion. Since their scalar comparisons preserve the counit, the induced exceptional comparison is the map specified here. The integral equality therefore becomes the asserted field-coefficient equality.
