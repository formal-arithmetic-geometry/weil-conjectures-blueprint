The construction in Section [subsec-Field-coefficients] extends the adjunction and its purity map from finite coefficient fields to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. The finite-extension compatibilities make these choices agree after further extension, so they define the designated $\gamma_{f,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$. The counit-preserving comparison is the exceptional coefficient comparison. Thus the extension of $\gamma_{f,E}$ is carried to $\gamma_{f,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}$.
