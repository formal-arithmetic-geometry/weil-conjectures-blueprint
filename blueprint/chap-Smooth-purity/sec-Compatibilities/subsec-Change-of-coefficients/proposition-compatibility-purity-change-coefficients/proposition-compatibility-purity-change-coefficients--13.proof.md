The comparisons for $f^*$ and $f^!$ are transitive for composite coefficient maps by Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients]. Apply the corresponding purity compatibility from clauses [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--1], [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--3], [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--5], [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--7], [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--9] and [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--11] at each step of a factorization. Composition identifies their chain of equalities with the required equality for the composite map.
