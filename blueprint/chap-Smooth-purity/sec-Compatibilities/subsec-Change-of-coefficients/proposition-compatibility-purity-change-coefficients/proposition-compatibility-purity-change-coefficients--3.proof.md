A continuous map $\mathcal O_E\to A$ factors through $\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$ for some $n$. The tower defining integral purity, equivalently its cofinal $\ell$-power subsequence, reduces to the specified purity map at each finite level. The integral counit reduces to the tower counit. Hence the induced identification of targets is the exceptional coefficient comparison, since both are transposes of that counit after the $Rf_!$ comparison. This proves the assertion for a finite reduction. Compose with the finite map to $A$ and use clause [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients--1].
