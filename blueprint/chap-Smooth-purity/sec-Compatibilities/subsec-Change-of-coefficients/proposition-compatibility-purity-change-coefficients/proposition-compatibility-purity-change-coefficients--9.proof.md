By the construction in Section [subsec-Field-coefficients], the adjunction and purity map over $E$ are obtained by inverting $\ell$ in their integral versions. The identification with the specified right adjoint preserves the counit, and thus equals the exceptional coefficient comparison by its defining characterization. Under it, the scalar extension of $\gamma_{f,\mathcal O_E}$ is exactly $\gamma_{f,E}$.
