Assume $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$ and $\Lambda'=A$ for a continuous map to a finite admissible ring (in particular $A=\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$, where $\varpi=\pi_E$). The comparison $(f^!_\Lambda K)_{\Lambda'}\to f^!_{\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$ carries $(\gamma_{f,\Lambda}(K))_{\Lambda'}$ to $\gamma_{f,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$, after identifying the sources by the coefficient comparison for $f^*$.
