Assume $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$ and $\Lambda'=A$ for a continuous map to a finite admissible ring (in particular $A=\mathcal O_E/\varpi^n$, where $\varpi=\pi_E$). The coefficient comparison for $Rf_!$ carries $(\operatorname{Tr}_{f,\Lambda}(K))_{\Lambda'}$ to $\operatorname{Tr}_{f,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$.
