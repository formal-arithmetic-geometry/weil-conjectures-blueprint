The comparison of integral adjunctions before Theorem [theorem-sp-qbar] carries the pair of normalized towers for $\mathcal O_E$ to the pair for $\mathcal O_F$ by a counit-preserving isomorphism. It carries their specified orientations and tensor maps to one another. Apply $R\varprojlim$ to this comparison. The resulting target identification is the exceptional coefficient comparison by its counit characterization, so it carries the integral purity map to the one over $\mathcal O_F$.
