Assume $\Lambda=E$ and $\Lambda'=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. The comparison $(f^!_\Lambda K)_{\Lambda'}\to f^!_{\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$ carries $(\gamma_{f,\Lambda}(K))_{\Lambda'}$ to $\gamma_{f,\Lambda'}(K_{\Lambda'})$, after identifying the sources by the coefficient comparison for $f^*$.
