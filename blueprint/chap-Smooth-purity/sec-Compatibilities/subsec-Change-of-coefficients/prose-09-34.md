The last compatibility compares the maps $\gamma_f$ for different coefficient
rings. For a map $A\to B$ of finite rings the comparison
$c_{A,B}$ of Lemma [lemma-sp-reduction] is the change-of-coefficients map
for $f^!$ of Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients],
since both are defined as the transpose of the extended counit composed with
the inverse of the comparison for $Rf_!$.
