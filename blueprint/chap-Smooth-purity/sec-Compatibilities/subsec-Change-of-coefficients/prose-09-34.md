The last compatibility compares the maps [$\gamma_f$](@theorem-smooth-purity-all-admissible-coefficients) for different coefficient
rings. For a map $A\to B$ of finite rings the comparison
$c_{A,B}$ of Lemma [lemma-sp-reduction] is the change-of-coefficients map
for [$f^!$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image) of Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients],
since both are defined as the transpose of the extended counit composed with
the inverse of the comparison for [$Rf_!$](@proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek).
