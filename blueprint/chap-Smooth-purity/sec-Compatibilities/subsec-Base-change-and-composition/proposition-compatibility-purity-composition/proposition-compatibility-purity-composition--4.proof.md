The specified purity maps over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ are obtained from the preceding coefficient stage by extension of scalars from a finite coefficient field. These operations preserve the counits, the orientation and tensor comparison defining purity. Apply this scalar extension to the equality in clause [proposition-compatibility-purity-composition--3]. The scalar comparisons identify its two sides with the two maps asserted here, so they are equal.
