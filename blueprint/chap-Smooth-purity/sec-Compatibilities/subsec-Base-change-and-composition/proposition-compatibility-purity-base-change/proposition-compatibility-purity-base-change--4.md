Let
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$. Identify the sources by
$g'^{*}f^{*}\simeq(f')^{*}g^{*}$
and the targets by the exceptional base change map of
Definition [definition-exceptional-base-change-map]. Then

$$
g'^{*}\gamma_f(K)
=\gamma_{f'}(g^{*}K)
\quad\text{in }D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell).
$$
