The isomorphism $\gamma_f$ now exists for every admissible coefficient ring,
and it remains to prove the properties (2)–(4) of
Theorem [theorem-smooth-purity-all-admissible-coefficients]. In topology, orientations of a submersion
restrict to fibres and multiply under composition, and
Propositions [proposition-compatibility-purity-base-change]
and [proposition-compatibility-purity-composition] are the corresponding
statements. These propositions also let us define the trace map
$\operatorname{Tr}_f$ (Definition [definition-trace-map]), the analogue
of integration over the fibres, on which Poincaré duality rests. With
finite coefficients the compatibilities follow from the construction, since
every ingredient of the orientation commutes with base change. Passing them
to $\mathbf Z_\ell$, however, is harder, because there the base change map itself has to be
built as a limit.
