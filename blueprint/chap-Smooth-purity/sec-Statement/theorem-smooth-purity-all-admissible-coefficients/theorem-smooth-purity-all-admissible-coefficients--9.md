For $g:Y'\to Y$ as in
Definition [definition-exceptional-base-change-map], with exceptional
base change map $\beta:g'^*f^!K\to f'^!g^*K$, we have
$\beta\circ g'^*\gamma_f(K)=\gamma_{f'}(g^*K)$, the sources being
identified by $g'^*f^*\simeq f'^*g^*$.
