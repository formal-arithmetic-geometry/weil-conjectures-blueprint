For $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$, the specified natural isomorphism is

$$
\gamma_{f,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}(K):f^*_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}K(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f^!_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}K.
$$
