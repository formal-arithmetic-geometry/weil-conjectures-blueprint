The purity map and the affine-line trace over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ are obtained from the preceding coefficient stage by scalar extension. Proposition [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients] identifies these maps, and Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients] identifies the counit and $Rp_!$ comparison. Apply scalar extension to the normalization equality in clause [theorem-smooth-purity-all-admissible-coefficients--7]. After those identifications, it is exactly the normalization equality of this clause.
