The identity `AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.CondensedRing.smoothTrace_eq` says that the smooth trace is the composite of $Rf_!\gamma_f$ with the counit; this is its definition. It does not identify that composite with the independently defined affine-line trace (9.1). Extending the equality of [theorem-smooth-purity-all-admissible-coefficients--7] would require that equality to have been established.
