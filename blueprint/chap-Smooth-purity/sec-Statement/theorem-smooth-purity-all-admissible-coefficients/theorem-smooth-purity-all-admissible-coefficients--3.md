This clause is bypassed by the Lean route: the construction extends an integral orientation through $E$ to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ without establishing a separate natural purity family with coefficients in $E$.

The retained blueprint assertion is that, for $K\in\Dctf(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$, there is a specified natural isomorphism

$$
\gamma_{f,E}(K):f_E^*K(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}f_E^!K.
$$
