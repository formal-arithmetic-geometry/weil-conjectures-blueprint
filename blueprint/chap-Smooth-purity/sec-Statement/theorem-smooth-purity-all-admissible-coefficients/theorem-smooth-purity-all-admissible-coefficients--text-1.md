*Shared context.* Let $f:X\to Y$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $d$. The clauses below assert the existence and properties of one specified family $\gamma_{f,\Lambda}$, with the naturality and simultaneous compatibilities of the reference; the clauses do not choose unrelated isomorphisms. The original items (1)–(4) are retained. The first four clauses specify the coefficient stages of its construction, including $\mathbf Z_\ell,\mathbf Q_\ell$ via $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$.
