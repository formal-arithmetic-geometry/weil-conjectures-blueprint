*Shared context.* For the integral and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ statements below, let $\ell$ be prime and let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$, with $p\ne\ell$. Let $f:X\to Y$ be a morphism of separated schemes of finite type over $k$, smooth of pure relative dimension $d$. The categories $\Dbc(-,\Lambda)$ are the bounded constructible derived categories on the pro-étale site, with the indicated condensed coefficient ring. The finite-coefficient statement specifies its own étale setting.

The Lean construction first obtains an integral isomorphism at the constant object, then extends scalars to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. The orientation is chosen to lift the finite orientations when such an invertible lift exists; otherwise an isomorphism is chosen without that compatibility. It is then transported through the open image of $f$. This construction does not establish the simultaneous normalizations of the human statement. The clauses bypassed by this route are retained below and marked explicitly.
