As for finite coefficients (clause [definition-exceptional-base-change-finite--2]): $g$ and $g'$ are open
immersions, so $g^*\simeq g^!$ and $g'^*\simeq g'^!$, and under these
identifications the map is the composition isomorphism
$g'^!f^!\simeq f'^!g^!$ on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ (Theorem [theorem-f-shriek-ell-adic-coefficients]).
