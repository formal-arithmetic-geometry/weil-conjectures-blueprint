The second map is integration along the affine line. Let
$p:\mathbf A^1_Y\to Y$ be the projection, and factor it as $p=q\circ j$,
where $j:\mathbf A^1_Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^1_Y$ is the complement of
the section at infinity and $q:\mathbf P^1_Y\to Y$ is the projection.
