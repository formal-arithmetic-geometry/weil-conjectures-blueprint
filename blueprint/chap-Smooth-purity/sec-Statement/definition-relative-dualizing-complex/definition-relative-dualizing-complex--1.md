For smooth $f:X\to Y$ and any admissible $\Lambda$, the
*relative dualizing complex* is

$$
\omega_f=f^{!}\Lambda_Y
\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda).
\tag{9.3}
$$
