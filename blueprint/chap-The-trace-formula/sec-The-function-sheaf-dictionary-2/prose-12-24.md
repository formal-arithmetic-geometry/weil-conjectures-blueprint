Under the trace formula, $Rf_!$ becomes summation over fibres and $\otimes$
becomes multiplication of functions on rational points. This is Grothendieck's
function–sheaf dictionary [sga4-5, Sommes trig.]
(Section [sec-The-function-sheaf-dictionary]), and in Part III we compute with
sheaves through their functions in this way.
