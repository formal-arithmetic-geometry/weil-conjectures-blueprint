The other operations on functions also have counterparts on sheaves.
Together with the corollary, they are the entries of the dictionary that we
use. Below, morphisms come from morphisms of separated $\mathbf F_q$-schemes
of finite type, and the operations carry the Weil structures described
after Definition [definition-weil-structure].
