Let $\ell$ be a prime with $\ell\nmid q$ and let $X_0$ be locally of finite type over $\Fq$, with
geometric base change $X$. Let $G$ be a lisse Weil $\Qbarl$-sheaf and $K$ a
bounded constructible Weil $\Qbarl$-complex on $X$. Then, for $m\geq1$,
$$
t_{G\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Qbarl}K,m}=t_{G,m}\,t_{K,m}.
$$
Here tensoring with $G$ is realized as $R\mathcal{H}om(G^\vee,-)$.
