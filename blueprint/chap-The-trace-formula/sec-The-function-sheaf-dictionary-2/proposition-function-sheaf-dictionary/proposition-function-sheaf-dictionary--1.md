Let $\ell$ be a prime with $\ell\nmid q$, let $g_0:W_0\to X_0$ be a morphism of $\Fq$-schemes,
and let $g:W\to X$ be its geometric base change. For a bounded constructible
Weil $\Qbarl$-complex $K$ on $X$, $m\geq1$, and
$w\in W_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$, one has
$$
t_{g^*K,m}(w)=t_{K,m}(g_0(w)).
$$
No finite-type or separatedness hypothesis on $W_0$ or $X_0$ is needed here.
