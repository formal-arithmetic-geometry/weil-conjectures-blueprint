For $U_0\subset X_0$ open with closed complement $Z_0$, and
$j:U\to X$, $i:Z\to X$ the inclusions,
$t_{K,m}=t_{j_!j^*K,m}+t_{i_*i^*K,m}$, where $t_{j_!j^*K,m}$ is
$t_{K,m}$ on $U_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$ and $0$ on $Z_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$.
