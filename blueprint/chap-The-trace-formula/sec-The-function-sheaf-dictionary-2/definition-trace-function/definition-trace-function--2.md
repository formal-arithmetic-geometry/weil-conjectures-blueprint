Let $g_0:W_0\to X_0$ be a morphism of separated $\mathbf F_q$-schemes of
finite type, with geometric base change $g:W\to X$, and give $g^*K$ the
Weil structure induced by $\phi$ (after
Definition [definition-weil-structure]). Then for every $m\geq1$ and
$w\in W_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$,

$$
t_{g^*K,m}(w)=t_{K,m}(g_0(w)).
$$
