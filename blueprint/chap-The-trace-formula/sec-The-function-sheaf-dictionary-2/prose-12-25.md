For $\Lambda$ a field, $t_{K,m}(x)$ is the alternating sum of the traces of
Frobenius on the stalks $\mathcal H^i(K)_{\bar x}$
(Proposition [proposition-properties-trace]). For example, the constant sheaf $\Lambda_X$ with its constant Weil structure has trace function $1$. In terms of trace functions, the theorem says that $\operatorname{tr}_\Lambda(\operatorname{Frob}_{X,K}^m
\mid R\Gamma_c(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},K))$ is the sum of $t_{K,m}$ over
$X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$. Applied to the fibres of a morphism, it shows
that direct image with compact support sums trace functions over the fibres.
