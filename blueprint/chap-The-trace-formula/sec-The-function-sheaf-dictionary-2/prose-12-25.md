For $\Lambda$ a field, [$t_{K,m}(x)$](@definition-trace-function) is the alternating sum of the traces of
Frobenius on the stalks $\mathcal H^i(K)_{\bar x}$
(Proposition [proposition-properties-trace]). For example, the constant sheaf $\Lambda_X$ with its constant [Weil structure](@definition-weil-structure) has [trace function](@definition-trace-function) $1$. In terms of [trace functions](@definition-trace-function), the theorem says that [$\operatorname{tr}_\Lambda(\operatorname{Frob}_{X,K}^m
\mid R\Gamma_c(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},K))$](@definition-categorical-trace) is the sum of [$t_{K,m}$](@definition-trace-function) over
$X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$. Applied to the fibres of a morphism, it shows
that [direct image with compact support](@proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek) sums [trace functions](@definition-trace-function) over the fibres.
