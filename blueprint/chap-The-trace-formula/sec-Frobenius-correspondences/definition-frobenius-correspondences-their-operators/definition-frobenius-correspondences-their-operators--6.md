For $x\in X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$ let $\bar x:s\to X$ be the
associated fixed point. The stalk of $\psi_m$ at $\bar x$ is an endomorphism
$\psi_{m,\bar x}:K_{\bar x}\to K_{\bar x}$.
