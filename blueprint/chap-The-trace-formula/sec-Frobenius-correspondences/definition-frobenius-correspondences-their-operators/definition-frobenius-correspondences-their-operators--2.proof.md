Choose the $q$-power Frobenius $F_0:X_0\to X_0$ in the model
Frobenius data. The cartesian square defining $X$, together with
$p\Phi_X=F_0p$, identifies $\Phi_X$ as a base change of $F_0$.
The scheme Frobenius is a universal homeomorphism, and it is finite
when $X_0$ is locally of finite type over the finite field $k_0$.
Both properties are preserved by base change, so they hold for
$\Phi_X$. The identity has both properties, and induction using
$\Phi_X^{m+1}=\Phi_X^m\circ\Phi_X$ proves the assertions for every
$m\geq0$, since both properties are preserved by composition.
