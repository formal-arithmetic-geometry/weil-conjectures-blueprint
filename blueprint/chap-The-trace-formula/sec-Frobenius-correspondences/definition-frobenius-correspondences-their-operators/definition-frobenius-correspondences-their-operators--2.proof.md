On an affine chart of $X_0$, a finitely generated $\mathbf F_q$-algebra is finite over its subring of $q$th powers: monomials in a finite set of generators with exponents less than $q$ generate it as a module over that subring. Thus the $q$-power Frobenius is finite. It is integral, surjective and radicial, hence a universal homeomorphism. Both properties survive base change to $k$. By its construction as that base change, $\Phi_X$ is a morphism over $k$.
