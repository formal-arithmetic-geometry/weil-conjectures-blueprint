Let $k_0$ be a finite field, let $k/k_0$ be a field extension, and let
$X_0$ be a $k_0$-scheme. Put $X=X_0\times_{k_0}k$, and let
$\Phi_X:X\to X$ be the base change of the $q$-power scheme Frobenius
of $X_0$, where $q=\#k_0$, as in
[definition-frobenius-correspondences-their-operators--1].

If $X_0$ is locally of finite type over $k_0$, then $\Phi_X^m$ is a
finite $k$-morphism for every $m\geq0$. For arbitrary $X_0$, every
$\Phi_X^m$ is a universal homeomorphism.
