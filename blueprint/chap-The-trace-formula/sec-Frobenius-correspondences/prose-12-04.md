The operator $T_{c_m}(\psi_m)$ is defined with the $k$-linear
$\Phi_X$, while the Frobenius operator of Section [sec-Weil-complexes], whose
trace the theorem is about, is defined with $\operatorname{Fr}_X$. The
next lemma says that the two agree, so that the trace formula proved for
$\Phi_X$ is a statement about $\operatorname{Frob}_{X,K}$.
