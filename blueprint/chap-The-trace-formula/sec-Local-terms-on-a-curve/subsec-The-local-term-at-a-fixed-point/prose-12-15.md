The point $y$ is stable under $\Phi_X^m$,
so additivity of trace classes applies to the localization triangle
for $X\setminus\{y\}$ and $\{y\}$. The open piece has zero stalk at
$y$, so its local term vanishes by Lemma [lemma-vanishing-local-term-zero-stalk].
The closed piece is pushed forward from the point, where the local
term is a trace by Lemma [lemma-local-terms-point]. The argument
never uses that $K$ is lisse at $y$. This is why, in the proof of
Theorem [theorem-trace-formula-frobenius-correspondence], we can apply the result to
$j_!K$ at the point at infinity of $\mathbf P^1$.
