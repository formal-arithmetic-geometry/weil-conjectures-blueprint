For $K=A_X$ on $X=\mathbf A^1_k$ the proposition gives
$\operatorname{LT}_y(\psi_m)=1$ at each of the $q^m$ fixed points of
$\Phi^m_X$. This matches the intersection count at the start of the section.
For general $K$ the proposition is not a formal consequence of the
definitions, as Remark [remark-locality-warning] explains.
