Let $X_0$ be a smooth curve over $k_0$, let
$X=X_0\times_{k_0}k$, let $K\in \href{@definition-constructible-sheaves}{D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)}$ and let
$\psi:(\href{@definition-frobenius-correspondences-their-operators}{\Phi_X^m})^*K\to K$ be any morphism. For
$y\in X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$ one has

$$
\href{@definition-trace-class-local-terms}{\operatorname{LT}_y(\psi)}
=\href{@definition-categorical-trace}{\operatorname{tr}_A(\psi_{\bar y}\mid K_{\bar y})}.
$$
