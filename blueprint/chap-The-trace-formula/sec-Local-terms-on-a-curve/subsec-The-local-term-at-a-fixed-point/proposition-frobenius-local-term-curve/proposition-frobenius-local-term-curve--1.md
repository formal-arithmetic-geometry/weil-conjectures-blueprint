Let $X_0$ be a smooth curve over $k_0$, let
$X=X_0\times_{k_0}k$, let $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ and let
$\psi:(\Phi_X^m)^*K\to K$ be any morphism. For
$y\in X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$ one has

$$
\operatorname{LT}_y(\psi)
=\operatorname{tr}_A(\psi_{\bar y}\mid K_{\bar y}).
$$
