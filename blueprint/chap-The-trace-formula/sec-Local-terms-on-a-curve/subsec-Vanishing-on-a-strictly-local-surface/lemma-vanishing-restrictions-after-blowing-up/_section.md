Let $T$ be the strict localization of a smooth surface at a closed
point $t$. Let $H$ be a regular curve through $t$, and let $D$ and $C$ be reduced
closed curves through $t$ such that the tangent cone of $D$ at $t$ meets
the tangent cones of $C$ and of $H$ only in $0$.
Let $j':T-(D\cup H)\hookrightarrow T-D$ be the open immersion and let $N$ be a bounded
complex on $T-(D\cup H)$ with finite locally constant cohomology and
finite $\ell$-group monodromy. Then the restriction map

$$
R\Gamma(T-D,j'_!N)\longrightarrow
R\Gamma(C-t,(j'_!N)|_{C-t})
$$

is zero in $D(A)$.
