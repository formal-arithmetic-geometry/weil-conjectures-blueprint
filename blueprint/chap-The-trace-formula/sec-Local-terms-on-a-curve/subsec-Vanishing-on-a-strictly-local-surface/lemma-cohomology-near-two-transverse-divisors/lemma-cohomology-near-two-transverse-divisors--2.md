With $X,D_1,D_2,z,A,N$ and $M$ as in
[lemma-cohomology-near-two-transverse-divisors--1], assume that $D_2$ is
connected. For every geometric point
$\bar w:\Spec\Omega\to D_2$ with $\Omega$ separably closed and image
different from $z$, the restriction map

$$
R\Gamma(D_2,M|_{D_2})\longrightarrow M_{\bar w}
$$

is zero in $D(A)$.
