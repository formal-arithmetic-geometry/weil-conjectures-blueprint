In the situation of Lemma [lemma-cohomology-near-two-transverse-divisors] with $D_2$
connected, let $C\subset X$ be a closed curve with $C\cap D_2=\{w\}$,
and let $C_{(w)}$ be its strict localization at $w$. Then the
restriction map

$$
R\Gamma(X-D_2,j'_!N)\longrightarrow
R\Gamma(C_{(w)}-w,(j'_!N)|_{C_{(w)}-w})
$$

is zero in $D(A)$.
