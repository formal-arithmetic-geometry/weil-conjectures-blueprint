Let $S$ be the strict localization of a smooth surface over an
algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $p$ at a closed point
$z$, let $\ell\neq p$, and let $D_1,D_2\subset S$ be regular divisors meeting
transversely at $z$. Then the restriction map

$$
R\Gamma(S-D_2,\mathbf F_\ell)\longrightarrow R\Gamma(D_1-z,\mathbf F_\ell)
$$

is an isomorphism.
