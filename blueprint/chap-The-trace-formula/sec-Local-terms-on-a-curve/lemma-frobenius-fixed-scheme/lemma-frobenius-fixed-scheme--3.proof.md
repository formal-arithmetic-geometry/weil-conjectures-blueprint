By uniqueness of equalizers and [lemma-frobenius-fixed-scheme--1], there
is an isomorphism $T\simeq\coprod_{x\in I_m}s$ over $s$. The inverse
image of $z$ lies in one component. Composing that component's open
immersion with the isomorphism gives a $k$-rational open immersion
$s\to T$ whose image is $\{z\}$. These open copies of $s$ cover $T$,
so $T$ is reduced.
