For $i=1,2$ let $X_i'\to X_i$ be a finite flat morphism of degree $d$ as in
Lemma [lemma-covers-degree-prime-to-ell], with $y_i'$ the point over
$y$. Let $T'$ be the strict localization of $X_1'\times X_2'$ at
$y'=(y_1',y_2')$, and write $\Gamma',V',\Delta',H'$ for the inverse images
of $\Gamma,V,\Delta,H$ in $T'$. Then $T'$ is the strict localization of a
smooth surface over $k$ at a closed point, the morphism $T'\to T$ is finite
flat of degree $d^2$ and étale over $T-(H\cup V)$, the reduction
$H'_{\mathrm{red}}$ is regular,
