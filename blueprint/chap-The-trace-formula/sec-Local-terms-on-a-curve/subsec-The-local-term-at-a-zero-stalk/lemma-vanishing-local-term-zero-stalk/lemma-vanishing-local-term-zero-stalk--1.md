In the setting of Lemma [lemma-local-term-restriction-map], let $y$ be a
fixed point of $\Phi_X^m$, and suppose $K_{\bar y}=0$. Then

$$
\rho:R\Gamma_\Gamma(T,P)\longrightarrow R\Gamma_y(\Delta,\delta^*P)
$$

is zero in $D(A)$.
