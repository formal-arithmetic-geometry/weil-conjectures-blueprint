Let $f:W'\to W$ be a finite étale morphism of schemes of constant degree
$n$, with $n$ invertible in $A$, and let $L\in D(W_{\acute{e}t},A)$. Then the
pullback map $R\Gamma(W,L)\to R\Gamma(W',f^{-1}L)$ has a left inverse in
$D(A)$.
