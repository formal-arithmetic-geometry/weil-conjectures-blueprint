Since $f$ is finite étale, $f_*=f_!$ is exact, and the trace map
$f_*f^{-1}\mathcal F\to\mathcal F$ composed with the adjunction unit
$\mathcal F\to f_*f^{-1}\mathcal F$ is multiplication by $n$ for every
sheaf of $A$-modules $\mathcal F$ [Stacks, [03SH](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03SH)]. Since both maps are natural and
the functors are exact, applying them termwise to a complex
representing $L$ gives maps $L\to f_*f^{-1}L\to L$ in $D(W_{\acute{e}t},A)$ whose
composite is $n$. Applying $R\Gamma(W,-)$ and using
$R\Gamma(W,f_*f^{-1}L)=R\Gamma(W',f^{-1}L)$, we see that $n^{-1}$ times the
map induced by the trace is a left inverse of the pullback.
