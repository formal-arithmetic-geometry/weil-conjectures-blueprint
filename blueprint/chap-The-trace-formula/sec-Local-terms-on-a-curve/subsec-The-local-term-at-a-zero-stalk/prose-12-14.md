The map $\rho$ of Lemma [lemma-local-term-restriction-map] depends on $K$ and on the geometry
of $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$ near $(y,y)$, but not on $\psi$. We therefore state
the vanishing of local terms at a zero stalk as a statement about $\rho$ alone.
