In the setting of Lemma [lemma-local-term-restriction-map] we compute on the
classical étale models. This is legitimate because the classical comparison
identifies tensor products, duality, exceptional adjunctions and the
pairing between graph and diagonal with their versions there. Let $X_1$
and $X_2$ be two copies of the strict localization of $X$ at $y$. The
strict localization of $X_1\times X_2$ at $(y,y)$ is the strictly local
surface $T$ of Lemma [lemma-local-term-restriction-map]. Write

$$
H=X_1\times\{y\},\qquad V=\{y\}\times X_2,
$$

and keep $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$ for the transposed Frobenius graph and the
diagonal in $T$. Let $u$ and $v$ be the pullbacks to the two factors $X_1$
and $X_2$ of the same uniformizer of $X$ at $y$, so that $\Delta$, $V$ and
$H$ have equations $u-v=0$, $u=0$ and $v=0$. Write
$\varphi(v)=(\Phi_X^m)^\#u$, a power series in $v$; the graph $\Gamma$ has
equation $u-\varphi(v)=0$. Since $d\Phi_X=0$, the graph $\Gamma$ is
tangent to $V$, while $\Delta$ and $H$ have other tangent directions.

The next three lemmas prepare the proof of
Lemma [lemma-vanishing-local-term-zero-stalk].
