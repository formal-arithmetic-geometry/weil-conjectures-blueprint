The vanishing of the local term at a rational point $y$ with $K_{\bar y}=0$, proved below, is a
geometric fact about the restriction from cohomology with supports in the
graph of $\Phi_X^m$ to cohomology with supports in $y$, and it does not follow from a locality property
of internal Hom. For example, if $j:\mathbf G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$
and $K=j_!A$, then the stalk of $K$ at the origin is zero, but
$\cHom_A(j_!A,j_!A)\simeq Rj_*A$ has stalk $A$ in degree zero there.
