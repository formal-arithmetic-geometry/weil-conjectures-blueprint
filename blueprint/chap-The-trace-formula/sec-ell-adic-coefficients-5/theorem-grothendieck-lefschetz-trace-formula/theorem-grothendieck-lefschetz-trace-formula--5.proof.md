Apply the compactly supported cohomology assertion of Lemma [lemma-perfect-complexes-geometric-point] to $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$. Its hypotheses are exactly the finite-type hypothesis on $X$ and the constructibility and finite Tor-amplitude of $K$. It identifies $R\Gamma_c(X,K)$ with a perfect $\Lambda$-complex.
