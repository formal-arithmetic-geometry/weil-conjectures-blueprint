The pullbacks, $T_{c_m}$, and iterated Frobenius correspondences commute with the coefficient comparisons of Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients]. The scalar-extension identity for categorical traces in Proposition [proposition-properties-trace--3] therefore transports every term of the equality to the corresponding term over the new ring. For $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, independence of the chosen finite stage follows from the conjugacy and common-extension argument in clause [theorem-grothendieck-lefschetz-trace-formula--4].
