Apply the stalk assertion of Lemma [lemma-perfect-complexes-geometric-point] to each geometric point $\bar y$. Restriction of a constructible finite-Tor-amplitude complex to that point is a bounded complex with finite coefficients and finite Tor-amplitude, hence a perfect $\Lambda$-complex.
