This compatibility assertion is bypassed by the Lean route: its trace formula has fixed coefficients $\Qlb$, and the comparison in [theorem-grothendieck-lefschetz-trace-formula--9] compares two Weil formalisms over that coefficient field. The following compatibility for all admissible coefficient maps is retained from the human statement and has no matching declaration here.

The trace formula is compatible with the coefficient comparisons of Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients].
