Compose the integral Weil structure with $\kappa_1$ to obtain $\Phi_X^*K\to K$. The integral comparison in Lemma [lemma-scheme-frobenius-geometric-frobenius] identifies its global and stalk operators with those of the Weil structure. Proposition [proposition-integral-trace-formula], obtained by applying the finite formula to every reduction and passing to the complete coefficient ring, therefore gives this identity.
