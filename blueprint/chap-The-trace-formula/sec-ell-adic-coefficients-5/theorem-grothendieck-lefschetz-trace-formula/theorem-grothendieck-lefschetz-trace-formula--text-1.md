*Shared context.* Let $X_0$ be separated of finite type over $\mathbf F_q$, let $X=X_0\times_{\mathbf F_q}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$, and assume $\ell\nmid q$. The ring $A$ may be any finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring, and $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ is finite. Take $K$ with the Weil structure of Definition [definition-weil-structure]; the global and stalk Frobenius operators are exactly those in the reference. For integral and rational coefficients $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}=\Dbc$; $\mathbf Z_\ell,\mathbf Q_\ell$ are included by $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$.
