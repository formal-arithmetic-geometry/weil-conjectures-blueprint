*Shared context.* Let $X_0$ be separated of finite type over $\Fq$, put $X=X_0\times_{\Fq}\overline{\Fq}$, and assume $\ell\nmid q$. The first three details retain the finite, integral and rational cases of the human proof, which the mainline bypasses. For the mainline formula, $K$ is a bounded constructible $\Qlb$-Weil complex on $X$. The bounded constructible Weil objects used in [theorem-grothendieck-lefschetz-trace-formula--4] instead live on $X_0$ with a structure for a finite-order automorphism. Their global and local traces are compared with those of $K$ in [theorem-grothendieck-lefschetz-trace-formula--9].
