Put $\psi=\phi\kappa_1$. Lemma [lemma-scheme-frobenius-geometric-frobenius] identifies $T_{c_m}(\psi_m)$ with $\operatorname{Frob}_{X,K}^m$ and its fixed-point endomorphisms with $\operatorname{Frob}_{y,m}$. Apply Theorem [theorem-trace-formula-frobenius-correspondence] with these finite coefficients. Substituting the identified endomorphisms gives exactly the asserted equality.
