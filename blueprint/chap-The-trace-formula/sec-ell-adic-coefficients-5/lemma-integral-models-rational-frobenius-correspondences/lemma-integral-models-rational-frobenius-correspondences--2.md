satisfy

$$
T_{c_m}((\psi_E)_m)=\pi_E^{-Nm}\bigl(T_{c_m}(\theta_m)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E}E\bigr).
\tag{12.2}
$$
