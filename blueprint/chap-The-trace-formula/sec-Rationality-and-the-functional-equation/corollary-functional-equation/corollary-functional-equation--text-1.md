*Shared context.* Let $X_0/\mathbf F_q$ be smooth and proper of pure dimension $d$, with $\ell\nmid q$. Put $H^i=H^i(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathbf Q_\ell)$ and let $F_i$ be its Frobenius operator, as in the reference.

The numerical definitions and the details marked as bypassed retain this
context. The linear algebra statements [corollary-functional-equation--7] and
[corollary-functional-equation--10] specify their own hypotheses. In the Lean
application, $X=X_0\times\overline{\mathbf F}_q$, $X_0$ is also geometrically
connected, and the coefficient field is $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
