By clause [corollary-functional-equation--4], the terms of $\chi$ outside the middle degree pair up modulo $2$, giving $\chi\equiv b_d\pmod2$. If $d$ is even then $d\chi$ is even. If $d$ is odd, Corollary [corollary-cup-product-pairing] gives a perfect alternating pairing on middle cohomology on each connected component, so $b_d$ is even. This again makes $d\chi$ even.
