The integer $d\chi$ is even by clause [corollary-functional-equation--5], so the power occurring in the definition of $\varepsilon$ is defined over $\mathbf Q$. Squaring that definition and applying $D^2=q^{d\chi}$ from clause [corollary-functional-equation--6] gives $\varepsilon^2=1$. In the characteristic-zero coefficient field this implies $\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$.
