The determinant identity of Theorem [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--9] gives $\det F_i\det F_{2d-i}=q^{db_i}$. Multiply these identities with exponents $(-1)^i$. Since $(-1)^{2d-i}=(-1)^i$, the left side is $D^2$ and the right side is $q^{d\sum_i(-1)^i b_i}=q^{d\chi}$.
