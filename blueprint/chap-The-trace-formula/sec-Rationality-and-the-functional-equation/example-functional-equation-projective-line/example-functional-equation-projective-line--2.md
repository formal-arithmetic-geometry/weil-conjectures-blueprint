$$
\frac{qT^2}{(qT-1)(T-1)}=\frac{qT^2}{(1-T)(1-qT)}.
$$

Here the factor $T^{\chi}$ is not trivial, unlike for the elliptic curve, and
the constant $q^{d\chi/2}=q$ comes from the twist $(-1)$ on $H^2$.
