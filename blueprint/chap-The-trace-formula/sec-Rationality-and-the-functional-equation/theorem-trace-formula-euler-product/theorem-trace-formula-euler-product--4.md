L(X_0,K,T)
=\prod_i\det\bigl(1-T\operatorname{Frob}_{X,K}\mid H_c^i(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},K)\bigr)^{(-1)^{i+1}}.
\tag{12.7}
$$
