Let $X_0$ be proper over $\mathbf F_q$, let $\ell\nmid q$, and let
$K\in\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ carry a Weil structure, where
$X=X_0\times_{\mathbf F_q}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$. For every $i\in\mathbf Z$
there is an isomorphism of Frobenius modules

$$
H_c^i(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},K)
\xrightarrow{\sim}H^i(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},K).
$$

Consequently their Frobenius characteristic polynomials agree, and in
[theorem-trace-formula-euler-product--4] we may replace $H_c^i$ by $H^i$.
