Put $n=\dim_KV$. Then $\det(1-TF\mid V)=T^n\det(T^{-1}-F\mid V)$ is the
reversed characteristic polynomial of $F$. It has degree at most $n$,
and its coefficient of $T^n$ is $(-1)^n\det F$, which is nonzero because
$F$ is invertible.
