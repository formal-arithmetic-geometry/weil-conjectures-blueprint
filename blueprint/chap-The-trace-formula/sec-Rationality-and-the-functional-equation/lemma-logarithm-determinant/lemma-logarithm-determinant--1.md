Let $L$ be a field of characteristic zero. For an endomorphism $v$ of
a finite-dimensional $L$-vector space $V$ we have

$$
\det(1-Tv\mid V)^{-1}
=\exp\left(\sum_{m\geq1}
\operatorname{Tr}(v^m\mid V)\frac{T^m}{m}\right)
\quad\text{in }1+TL[[T]].
\tag{12.5}
$$
