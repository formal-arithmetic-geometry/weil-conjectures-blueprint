The functional equation is an
identity of rational functions, not of power series at $T=0$, since
$1/(q^dT)$ is not a power series in $T$.

What duality does not give is the absolute value of the eigenvalues
$\alpha$. In Sections [chap-Weights]–[chap-Proof-of-the-Weil-conjectures] we prove
that the eigenvalues of $F_i$ have absolute value $q^{i/2}$ under every
embedding of $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ into $\mathbf C$. It follows that the factors
$\det(1-TF_i)$ for different $i$ have no common zeros, and hence we can
read them off from $Z(X_0,T)$ alone. In Section [chap-Proof-of-the-Weil-conjectures] we use
this to show that they are polynomials with integer coefficients that do
not depend on $\ell$.
