Apply Theorem [theorem-grothendieck-lefschetz-trace-formula] to the constant Weil sheaf $\Lambda_X$. Each rational-point stalk is $\Lambda$ in degree zero, with identity Frobenius and categorical trace $1_\Lambda$. Summing these identical stalk contributions gives the number of $\mathbf F_{q^m}$-points times $1_\Lambda$.
