Let $X_0$ be any scheme of finite type over $\mathbf F_q$. There are
polynomials $P,Q\in\mathbf Q[T]$ with $Q(0)=1$ such that

$$
Z(X_0,T)Q(T)=P(T)\quad\text{in }\mathbf Q[[T]].
$$

In particular, $Z(X_0,T)$ belongs to $\mathbf Q(T)$.
