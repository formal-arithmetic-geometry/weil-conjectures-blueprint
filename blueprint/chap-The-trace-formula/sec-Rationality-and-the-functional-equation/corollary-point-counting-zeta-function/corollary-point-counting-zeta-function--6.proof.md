Each factor $(1-T^{\deg(x)})^{-1}$ of clause [corollary-point-counting-zeta-function--4] has integral coefficients and constant term $1$. There are only finitely many closed points of bounded degree on a scheme of finite type over a finite field, so each coefficient of the product is a finite sum of integers. Thus $Z$ belongs to $1+T\mathbf Z[[T]]$.
