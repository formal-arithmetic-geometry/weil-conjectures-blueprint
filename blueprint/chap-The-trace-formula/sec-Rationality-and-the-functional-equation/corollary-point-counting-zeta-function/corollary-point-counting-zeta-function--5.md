For $\Lambda=\mathbf Q_\ell,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, in $\Lambda[[T]]$,

$$
Z(X_0,T)=\prod_i\det(1-T\operatorname{Frob}_{X,\Lambda_X}\mid H_c^i(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda_X))^{(-1)^{i+1}}.
$$
