For a finite-dimensional endomorphism $F$, the formal identity $-\log\det(1-TF)=\sum_{m\ge1}\operatorname{Tr}(F^m)T^m/m$ follows after scalar extension by multiplying the linear eigenvalue factors. Insert clause [corollary-point-counting-zeta-function--2] into the exponential defining $Z$ and apply this identity in every cohomological degree. The alternating signs give precisely the determinant product.
