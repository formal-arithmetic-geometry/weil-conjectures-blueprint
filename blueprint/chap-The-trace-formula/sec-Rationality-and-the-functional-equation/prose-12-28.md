Over a field of characteristic zero, the traces of all powers of an
endomorphism determine its characteristic polynomial. Taking the trace
formula for all $m\geq1$, we therefore obtain an identity of power
series: a product over closed points equals an alternating product of
characteristic polynomials of Frobenius on cohomology. This
step divides by $m$, so in the determinant formulas below $\Lambda$ is one of the
fields $\mathbf Q_\ell$, $E$, $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. Characteristic zero is
needed: on $\mathbf F_p^{\oplus p}$ every power of the identity has trace
$p=0$, as for the zero endomorphism, but
$\det(1-T\cdot\operatorname{id})=(1-T)^p=1-T^p$ is not $1$.

For $K=\Lambda_X$ the product over cohomology is finite, so $Z(X_0,T)$ is
rational, and for smooth proper $X_0$ duality pairs the factors in degrees $i$
and $2d-i$ (Corollary [corollary-functional-equation]).
