The compactification comparison and what is defined through it. [the-isomorphism-j-2-k-to-ru-j-1-k-and-its-base-change]
(chapter 10 of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex`) proves, on geometric fibres,
that $Rf_!$ does not depend on the compactification
([prop-compactification-coherence-detailed]). The source defines $Rf_!$, the
exceptional inverse image and the exchange-projection map in its first chapter
with a forward reference to that coherence, and several Weil-structure
definitions on compactly supported cohomology rest on them; all of these are
here, after the coherence they need, instead of in [foundations]. The block
stands after [chap-proper-base-change] and before the base change blocks,
which cite it.
