This project's own extension of the Stacks material: the theorem is
transported up the coefficient tower. The site comparison carries the
bounded étale statement to the pro-étale site
[sbc-thm-finite-proetale-smooth-base-change]; derived limits give the adic
form [sbc-thm-adic-smooth-base-change]. Admissible lattices are fixed in
[sbc-def-admissible-lattices], with their Karoubi presentation
[sbc-lem-lattice-karoubi-presentation]; inverting $\varpi$ gives the
$E$-coefficient form [sbc-thm-e-coefficient-smooth-base-change], and a
limit over finite stages the $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ form
[sbc-thm-overline-mathbf-q-ell-coefficient-smooth-base-ch].
