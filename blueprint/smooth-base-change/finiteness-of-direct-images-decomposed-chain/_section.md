Throughout this section $k$ is a field of characteristic prime to $N$,
$\Lambda$ is the finite commutative ring annihilated by $N$ of
[sbc-def-coefficient-conventions], and schemes are of finite type over $k$;
such schemes are Noetherian, quasi-compact, and quasi-separated. For a
finite abelian group $M$ the symbol $\underline M$ denotes the constant
$\Lambda$-sheaf, and for a locally closed immersion $j$ the functor $j_!$
denotes extension by zero along it, i.e. the composite of extension by zero
of the open immersion into the closure with the direct image of the closed
immersion, with the stalk description $(j_!G)_{\bar x}=G_{\bar x}$ for
$\bar x$ in the open part and $0$ otherwise.

The chain: dévissage of a constructible sheaf into extensions by zero of
lisse sheaves [lem-fin-constructible-devissage], with extension by zero
preserving constructibility [lem-fin-extension-by-zero]; étale splitting of
a finite morphism [lem-fin-finite-splitting] and constructibility of finite
pushforwards [lem-fin-finite-constant-pushforward],
[lem-fin-finite-morphism], which also reflect constructibility
[lem-fin-finite-reflects-constructibility] and its bounded derived form
[lem-fin-finite-reflects-derived], with the closed-immersion case
[lem-fin-closed-immersion]; and finite étale trivialization of a lisse sheaf
[fin2-lem-lisse-trivialized-finite-etale].
