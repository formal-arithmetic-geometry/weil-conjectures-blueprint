Outline module 7. The curve-test argument uses the preceding curve
calculations; its output is consumed by direct-image finiteness and by the
six operations.

*Route.* The statements follow the Stacks Project's account of smooth base
change — Étale Cohomology, Sections 59.51, 59.80, 59.83, 59.86–59.89 and
59.93 — i.e. the **second** proof of Theorem 59.89.2 ([Tag
0EYU](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EYU)): the theorem is proved
directly, from the local structure of smooth morphisms together with a
universal curve test and the cohomology of curves, and local acyclicity of
smooth morphisms is deduced *afterwards* (Section 59.93). The former
SGA 4½-style route (local acyclicity first, then the open-immersion theorem)
is not used. The derived-category, pro-étale and adic transport sections at
the end are this project's own extension, kept after the Stacks part.

The chapter also carries the long finiteness-and-constructibility chain for
direct images (§§10–15), which the Stacks account needs and which later
chapters consume.
