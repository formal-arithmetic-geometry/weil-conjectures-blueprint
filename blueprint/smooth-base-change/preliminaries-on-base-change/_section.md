Four formal facts about the base-change map: it can be checked
étale-locally [sbc-lem-etale-locality]; it composes along a tower of
Cartesian squares [sbc-lem-base-change-composition], with the identity and
composition laws spelled out [sbc-lem-identity-and-base-change-composition];
and it is an isomorphism exactly when a comparison of cohomology of strict
localizations holds [sbc-lem-stalk-form].
