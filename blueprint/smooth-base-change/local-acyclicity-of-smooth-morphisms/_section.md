Local acyclicity is *deduced* from smooth base change, following Stacks
Section 59.93 — the reverse of the SGA 4½ order. The vocabulary:
strict localizations [sbc-def-strict-localization], Milnor tubes
[sbc-def-milnor-tube], the specialization map
[sbc-def-specialization-map], and local and universal local acyclicity
[sbc-def-local-acyclicity], [sbc-def-universal-local-acyclicity]. The
constant sheaf on a strictly henselian local scheme is acyclic
[sbc-lem-strict-henselian-constant-cohomology], which reduces local
acyclicity to a condition on the tubes
[sbc-lem-local-acyclicity-constant]; smooth base change then gives the
theorem [sbc-thm-smooth-morphisms-are-universally-locally-acyclic], with
the usual stability properties [sbc-lem-stability-and-locality].

Two statements recorded for later use, not needed for the proof of smooth
base change itself: base change for open immersions under local acyclicity
[sbc-thm-truncated-open-immersion-base-change] and its derived form
[sbc-cor-derived-open-immersion-base-change].
