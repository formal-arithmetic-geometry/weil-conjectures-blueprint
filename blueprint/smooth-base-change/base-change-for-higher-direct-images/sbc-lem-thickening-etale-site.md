If $T''\to T'$ is a thickening of schemes (a closed immersion inducing a
homeomorphism of the underlying spaces), then the small étale site of $T''$
is the same as that of $T'$: pullback along $T''\to T'$ induces an
isomorphism of sites, hence an equivalence of the categories of étale
sheaves identifying the cohomology of the two schemes.

(*Stacks Project, Étale Cohomology, Proposition 59.45.4, [Tag
03SI](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03SI).*)
