The dévissage that turns a hypothetical failure of $BC(f,n,q)$ into a
concrete curve-test datum. Granted $BC(f,n,q-1)$, the degree-$q$
base-change map is injective and its cokernel embeds into an injective
[sbc-lem-bc-injectivity]. Direct image along an integral morphism is exact
[sbc-lem-integral-etale-direct-image-exact] and a thickening does not change
the étale site [sbc-lem-thickening-etale-site]; hence a failure survives
integral base change on either side
[sbc-lem-bc-integral-surjective], [sbc-lem-bc-integral-base]. A Zorn
argument locates a minimal counterexample over a point
[sbc-lem-bc-minimal-counterexample], so a failure is detected on the generic
fibre [sbc-lem-bc-failure-field], and normalizing produces the universal
test datum [sbc-lem-bc-universal-test].
