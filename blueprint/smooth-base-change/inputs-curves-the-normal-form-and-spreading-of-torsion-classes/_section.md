The external inputs the curve test consumes: a smooth morphism is
étale-locally a projection from affine space
[sbc-thm-etale-local-normal-form] and can be sliced into affine families with
geometrically connected fibres and a section [sbc-lem-slicing]; over an
integral normal scheme with separably closed function field, separated étale
morphisms split [sbc-lem-etale-over-normal]. From the curve chapters: the
cohomological dimension of an affine curve
[sbc-thm-cohomological-dimension-of-an-affine-curve] and invariance under
extension of algebraically closed fields
[sbc-thm-algebraically-closed-invariance]. Finally the two spreading
ingredients: a Galois cover of the generic fibre extends over the family
[sbc-thm-spreading-finite-etale-galois], and torsors compute $H^1$
[sbc-prop-torsors-h1].
