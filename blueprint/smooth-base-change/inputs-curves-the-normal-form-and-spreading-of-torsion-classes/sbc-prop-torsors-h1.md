For a finite abelian group $M$ and any scheme $Y$, the pointed set of
isomorphism classes of $M$-torsors is canonically
$\operatorname{Tor}^1(Y,M)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H^1(Y_{\Et},\underline M)$.

(*The finite-étale chapter's torsor–$H^1$ proposition.*)

## Proof

This is the assertion of the torsor–$H^1$ proposition of the finite-étale
chapter, restated for use below.
