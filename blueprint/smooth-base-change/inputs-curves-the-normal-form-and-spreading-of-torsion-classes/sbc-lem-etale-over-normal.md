Let $X$ be an
integral normal scheme with
separably closed function field.

1. A separated etale morphism $U\to X$ is a disjoint union of open
immersions.
1. All local rings of $X$ are strictly henselian.

(*Stacks Project, Lemma 59.80.4, [Tag `09Z9`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09Z9).*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Lemma 59.80.4 ([Tag 09Z9](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09Z9)): over an integral normal scheme with separably closed function field, separated etale morphisms are disjoint unions of open immersions, and all local rings are strictly henselian. No separate proof is given.
