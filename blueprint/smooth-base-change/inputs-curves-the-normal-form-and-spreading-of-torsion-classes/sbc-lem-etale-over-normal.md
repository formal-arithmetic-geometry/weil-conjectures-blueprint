Let $X$ be an integral normal [def-sbc-normal-dominant-galois] scheme with
separably closed function field.

1. A separated étale morphism $U\to X$ is a disjoint union of open
   immersions.
2. All local rings of $X$ are strictly henselian.

(*Stacks Project, Lemma 59.80.4, [Tag
09Z9](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09Z9).*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project,
Lemma 59.80.4 ([Tag 09Z9](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09Z9)): over
an integral normal scheme with separably closed function field, separated
étale morphisms are disjoint unions of open immersions, and all local rings
are strictly henselian. No separate proof is given.
