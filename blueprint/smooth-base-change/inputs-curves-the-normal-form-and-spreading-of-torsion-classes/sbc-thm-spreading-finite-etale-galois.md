Let $S$ be a
quasi-compact and
quasi-separated integral
normal scheme with generic point $\eta$. Let
$C\to S$ be a quasi-compact and quasi-separated
smooth morphism with
geometrically connected
fibres, and fix a section $\sigma:S\to C$. Let $Z\to C_\eta$
be a finite etale
Galois cover with group
$G$, assume $|G|$ is
invertible on $S$, and assume $Z$ has a $\kappa(\eta)$-rational point
mapping to $\sigma(\eta)$. Then there is a finite etale
Galois cover
$\widetilde Z\to C$ with group $G$ whose restriction to $C_\eta$ is the
given cover $Z\to C_\eta$.
(*Stacks Project, Fundamental Groups of Schemes, [Tag `0EZJ`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EZJ) = Lemma 58.31.9.*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Fundamental Groups of Schemes, Lemma 58.31.9 ([Tag 0EZJ](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EZJ)): a finite etale Galois cover of the generic fibre whose group has order invertible on the base spreads out to a finite etale Galois cover of the whole smooth family with geometrically connected fibres. The blueprint uses it in this form; no separate proof is given.
