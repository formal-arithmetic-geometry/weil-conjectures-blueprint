*An independent degree-zero open-image argument* (the source's subsection
heading, flattened here). An integral finite-type scheme has a dense normal
open [lem-fin-dense-normal-open], normality ascends along étale maps
[lem-fin-etale-normal-components], and a constant sheaf extends across a
dense normal open [lem-fin-normal-constant-open-image]. Normalization
extends a finite cover [lem-fin-normalization-extends-cover], so a lisse
sheaf on a normal open has constructible direct image
[lem-fin-normal-open-lisse-image]; Noetherian induction then gives the
degree-zero theorem [thm-fin-open-degree-zero] and the constructibility of
the boundary sheaf [lem-fin-boundary-sheaf].

The remaining lemmas prepare the higher-degree statements: a lisse sheaf is
lisse on a dense open [fin2-lem-lisse-dense-open], recollement for a proper
morphism [fin2-lem-recollement-proper], reduction to the reduced case
[fin2-lem-constructible-reduction], finite étale descent of
constructibility [fin2-lem-constructibility-finite-etale-descent], and the
Betti bound for a curve family [fin2-lem-curve-family-betti].

*Note.* `def:fin-normalization-extension` and
`lem:fin-normalization-extension-finite` are not repeated here: the source
moved them to the Jacobian chapter, and they are referenced as unresolved
dependencies.
