Fix the finite coefficient ring $\Lambda$ of
[sbc-def-coefficient-conventions]. For a morphism $f:X\to S$ between
finite-type schemes, let $\mathcal C(f)$ denote the assertion that
$R^qf_*F$ is constructible for every $q\geq0$ and every constructible
$\Lambda$-sheaf $F$.

This predicate includes no uniform vanishing assertion.
