The longest section of the chapter. A sheaf with finite stalks is
constructible exactly when the stalk-cardinality level sets are
constructible [fin4-lem-stalk-constructibility-criterion] — with a remark on
what that does and does not give [fin4-rem-stalk-criterion-scope]. Coherent
base change makes fibre dimensions constructible
[lem-fin-projective-flat-coherent-ranks], hence the Betti cardinalities in a
smooth projective curve family [lem-fin-projective-curve-betti-cardinalities],
which settles the constant-coefficient case
[thm-fin-projective-smooth-curve-finiteness].

The rest assembles the geometric input: finite covers of projective schemes
are projective [lem-fin-finite-over-projective]; a constant sheaf on a
normal scheme is its generic pushforward [lem-fin-normal-generic-constant],
so every constructible sheaf embeds in a finite sum of finite pushforwards
of constant sheaves [lem-fin-finite-type-pushforward-embedding]; and the
curve-model constructions [lem-fin-projective-finite-fibres] through
[lem-fin-generic-curve-model]. Finally the property $C$ of
[def-finrepair-property-c] is shown to be stable under the operations that
the induction needs.
