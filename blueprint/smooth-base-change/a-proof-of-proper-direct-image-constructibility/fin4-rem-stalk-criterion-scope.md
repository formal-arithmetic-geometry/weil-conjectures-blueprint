The conclusion of [fin4-lem-stalk-constructibility-criterion] is a finite
stratification, not local constancy on all of $X$.

For a closed point $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ of an irreducible curve with open
complement $j$, the sheaf $j_!\mathbf F_\ell\oplus i_*\mathbf F_\ell$ has
cardinality $\ell$ at every stalk, but its generization map from $Z$ to its
complement is zero. In the proof above, injectivity comes from lifting the
generic stalk; it is not assumed for arbitrary generization maps.

derived from its subject.
