The theorem [sbc-thm-smooth-base-change]: for $f$ smooth and $g$
quasi-compact quasi-separated, $f^{-1}R^qg_*\mathcal F=R^qh_*e^{-1}\mathcal F$
for every torsion $\mathcal F$ of orders invertible on the base. The proof
runs the induction: degree zero is the flat case, filtered torsion and étale
locality reduce to the affine relative-dimension-one situation, and the
curve test supplies the step. Two restatements follow — the classical form
with the smooth morphism on the base [sbc-cor-classical-form], and the
affine relative-dimension-one form
[sbc-thm-relative-dimension-one-affine-smooth-base-change] used later in the
chapter.
