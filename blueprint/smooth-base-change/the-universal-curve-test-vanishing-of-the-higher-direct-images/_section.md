The heart of the argument. On the universal test datum the higher direct
images of a constant torsion sheaf vanish
[sbc-thm-universal-test-vanishing] — above degree one by the cohomological
dimension of an affine curve, and in degree one by spreading the
corresponding torsor out from the generic fibre. Combined with the dévissage
of the previous sections this gives the induction step in relative dimension
one [sbc-lem-rel-dim-one-induction].
