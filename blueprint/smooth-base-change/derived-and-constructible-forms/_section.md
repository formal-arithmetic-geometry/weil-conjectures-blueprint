The sheaf-level theorem is promoted to the derived category. Extension by
zero commutes with base change [sbc-prop-extension-by-zero-and-arbitrary-base-change];
the derived categories with allowable torsion are fixed in
[sbc-def-derived-categories-with-allowable-torsion]. A finite-range
comparison of the two hypercohomology spectral sequences
[sbc-lem-finite-range-comparison] gives the bounded constructible form
[sbc-thm-bounded-derived-smooth-base-change] and the bounded
allowable-torsion form [sbc-thm-bounded-allowable-torsion]; a truncation
criterion [sbc-lem-bounded-below-truncation-criterion] then removes
boundedness above [sbc-thm-bounded-below-derived-smooth-base-change].
