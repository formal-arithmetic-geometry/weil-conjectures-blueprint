Let $\mathcal P$ be a set of primes. Write
$$
D^b_{\mathcal P\text{-tor}}(T_{\Et}),\qquad D^+_{\mathcal P\text{-tor}}(T_{\Et})
$$
for the full triangulated subcategories of the derived category of abelian
étale sheaves on $T$ consisting respectively of bounded and bounded-below
complexes $K$ such that every geometric stalk of every $H^q(K)$ is a torsion
group whose prime divisors belong to $\mathcal P$.
