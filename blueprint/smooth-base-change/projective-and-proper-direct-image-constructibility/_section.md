The pieces of the previous section are assembled. Constant coefficients on
projective families of relative dimension $\le1$
[thm-fin-projective-curve-constant], hence finite constant groups
[cor-fin-projective-curve-finite-group], hence constructible coefficients on
the projective line [thm-fin-projective-line-constructible]; the
symmetric-product cancellation then gives the projective case
[thm-fin-projective-constructibility-complete]. Chow's lemma in the form of
a projective modification [lem-fin-noetherian-chow-modification] extends it
to the proper case [thm-fin-proper-constructibility-complete], with sharp
vanishing bounds for smooth proper families
[fin2-thm-smooth-proper-lisse-constructible] and in general
[thm-fin-proper-case].

A closing remark [fin4-rem-stalk-criterion-scope-repeated] repeats, verbatim
in the source, the earlier caution about the stalk-cardinality criterion.
