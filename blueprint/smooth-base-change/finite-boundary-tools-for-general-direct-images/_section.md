Four tools for the finite-boundary induction used by the later
general-constructibility chapter. A proper direct image preserves $\Dbc$
[lem-fin-proper-bounded-constructible]; constructibility can be recovered
from the open part plus a proper direct image when the boundary is finite
[lem-fin-finite-boundary-reconstruction]; a proper direct image detects an
isomorphism off a finite boundary [lem-fin-finite-boundary-comparison]; and
coordinate complements produce a finite exceptional locus
[lem-fin-coordinate-finite-boundary].

*Note.* The source records here that the two finite-type direct-image
statements formerly placed in this section — the open-immersion finiteness
theorem and the finite-type finiteness theorem — are proved in the chapter
"General direct-image constructibility" by Deligne's finite-boundary
induction; the proper case remains [obl-fin-comparison], and the
compact-support amplitude bound uses the original geometric fibres of the
morphism. The earlier pending note (that the open-immersion argument was
circular and that a compactification need not retain the fibre-dimension
bound) is thereby resolved: the new proof never obtains the ordinary
direct-image statement from a compactification, and uses no
constructibility of $R^qj_*$ for $q>0$ as a hypothesis.
