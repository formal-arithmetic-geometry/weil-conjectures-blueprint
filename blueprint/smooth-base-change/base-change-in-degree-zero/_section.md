In degree zero the base-change map is an isomorphism for any flat morphism
of finite presentation with geometrically reduced fibres — in particular for
a smooth morphism [sbc-lem-degree-zero-flat-base-change]. This is the case
$q_0=0$ of the theorem, and the base of the induction that follows.
