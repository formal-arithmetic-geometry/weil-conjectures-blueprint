The older reduction chain, retained as a record of the route. Finite
morphisms preserve constructibility
[fin4-thm-finite-morphisms-constructible]; the domination trick
[fin4-lem-domination-trick] and additivity of the vanishing bound under
composition [fin4-lem-composition-constructibility] reduce the proper case
to the projective case [fin4-thm-reduction-to-projective], then to the
projective line [fin4-thm-reduction-to-projective-line], then to constant
coefficients [fin4-prop-p1-constant-reduction] and finally to smooth
projective curve families [fin4-thm-smooth-projective-curve-reduction]. The
fibrewise Betti computation [fin4-prop-curve-family-betti] closes the chain,
and [obl-fin-comparison] records the comparison-and-finitude statement in
the form later chapters cite.

*Note.* Four of these reduction nodes are stated as implications whose
conclusion the chapter has already established independently
([thm-fin-proper-case]); their proofs in the source say exactly that, and
note that no circularity arises because the independent route goes through
the projective line and finite covers and only afterwards extracts the
bound from geometric fibres.
