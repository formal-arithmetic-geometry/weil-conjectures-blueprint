In the notation of [def-trf-weil-extension-zero], the fact that the pair
$(\bar K,\bar\varphi)$ is again a strict Weil complex is
*The Weil extension by zero is a strict Weil complex*; that the construction is
functorial in $(K,\varphi)$ is
*Functoriality of the Weil extension by zero*.
