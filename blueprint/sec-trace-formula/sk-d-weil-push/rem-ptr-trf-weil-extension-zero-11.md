In the notation of [def-trf-weil-extension-zero], the following holds. That the pair
$(\bar K,\bar\varphi)$ is again a strict Weil complex is
[lem-trf-weil-extension-strict]; that the construction is
functorial in $(K,\varphi)$ is
[lem-trf-weil-extension-functorial].
