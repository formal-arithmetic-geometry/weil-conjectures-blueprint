The map $f^\cdot:\mathcal O_{X_T}[t^{-1}]\to f_*\mathcal O_{C_T}[t^{-1}]$ sends a generator
$a/t$, $a\in I$, to $f^\cdot a/t$ with $f^\cdot a\in J$, which lies in
$\mathcal O_{C_T}[J/t]$. Hence $f^\cdot$ maps $\mathcal O_{X_T}[I/t]$ into
$f_*\mathcal O_{C_T}[J/t]$, giving the lift; it is unique because both deformation algebras
are subalgebras of the localizations at $t$, that is, $t$-torsion free. Modulo $t$ the
degree-$r$ part of $\mathcal O_{X_T}[I/t]$ is spanned by $a/t^r$ with $a\in I^r$, and
reduction modulo $t$ identifies the special fibre algebra with
$\bigoplus_rI^r/I^{r+1}$, the element $a/t^r$ mapping to the class of $a$. The lift sends
$a/t^r$ to $f^\cdot a/t^r$, which gives the asserted graded map.
