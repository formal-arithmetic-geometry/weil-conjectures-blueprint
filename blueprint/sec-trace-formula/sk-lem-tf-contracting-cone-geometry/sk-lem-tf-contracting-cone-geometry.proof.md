The normal-cone algebra is
$\operatorname{gr}_I\mathcal O_X=\bigoplus_{r\geq0}I^r/I^{r+1}$,
and the deformation algebra is the subalgebra of
$\mathcal O_{X_T}[t^{-1}]$ generated by
$\mathcal O_{X_T}$ and the fractions $a/t$, $a\in I$. Since
$c_i^\cdot I\subset J$ for both legs (for $i=2$ this is the
hypothesis $L\subset J$), the maps on generic fibres preserve these
subalgebras, which gives the lifts.

The zero section of $N_ZX$ is defined by the positive-degree ideal of
$\operatorname{gr}_I\mathcal O_X$. Since $J$ is the ideal generated
by $c_1^\cdot I$, the images of degree-one elements under the first
leg generate $J/J^2$ as an $\mathcal O_E$-module, and hence the
positive-degree ideal of $\operatorname{gr}_J\mathcal O_C$. The
quotient by the pulled-back zero-section ideal is therefore
$\mathcal O_C/J=\mathcal O_E$, which is (1).

Under the second leg, a product of $n$ images of degree-one
elements is the class of an element of $L^n$ in $J^n/J^{n+1}$,
and $L^n\subset J^{n+1}$ makes it zero. The pulled-back zero-section
ideal is therefore nilpotent, so its vanishing set is all of $N_EC$,
which is (2). In general $\gamma_2$ factors through $Z$ only after
passing to reductions, and for constructible sheaves this suffices by
nilpotent invariance.
