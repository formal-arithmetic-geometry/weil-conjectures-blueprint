Let $T=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]_{(t)}$, let $f:C\to X$ be a morphism of schemes, let $I$ be a
quasi-coherent ideal of $\mathcal O_X$ and $J$ a quasi-coherent ideal of $\mathcal O_C$ with
$f^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_C\subset J$. Let $\mathcal D_ZX=\operatorname{Spec}_{X_T}\mathcal O_{X_T}[I/t]$,
where $\mathcal O_{X_T}[I/t]\subset\mathcal O_{X_T}[t^{-1}]$ is the subalgebra generated by
$\mathcal O_{X_T}$ and the fractions $a/t$, $a\in I$, and define $\mathcal D_EC$ in the same
way from $J$. Then $f_T$ lifts uniquely to a $T$-morphism
$\widetilde f:\mathcal D_EC\to\mathcal D_ZX$ that agrees with $f$ on the generic fibres. Its
special fibre is the map of normal cones ([pur9-def-normal-cone])
$\gamma:N_EC=\operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{gr}_J\mathcal O_C\to N_ZX=\operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{gr}_I\mathcal O_X$
induced by the graded algebra map sending the class of $a\in I^r$ to the class of
$f^\cdot a\in J^r$.
