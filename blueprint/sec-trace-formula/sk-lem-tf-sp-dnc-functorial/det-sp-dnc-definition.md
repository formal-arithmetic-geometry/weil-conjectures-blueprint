Let $k$ be algebraically closed and $S$ the strict henselization of
$\operatorname{Spec}k[t]_{(t)}$, with generic point $\eta$ and closed point $s$. Let $X$ be a
separated finite-type $k$-scheme and $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ a closed subscheme with ideal
$I$. Let $\mathcal O_X[t,I/t]\subset\mathcal O_X[t,t^{-1}]$ be the subalgebra generated by
$\mathcal O_X[t]$ and the fractions $a/t$, $a\in I$. The *deformation to the normal cone* of $Z$
over $S$ is

$$
\mathcal D_ZX=\operatorname{Spec}_{X\times S}\bigl(\mathcal O_{X\times S}[I/t]\bigr),
\qquad
\mathcal O_{X\times S}[I/t]=\mathcal O_X[t,I/t]\otimes_{k[t]}\mathcal O_S.
$$

Its generic fibre is $X_\eta$ and its special fibre is the normal cone
$N_ZX=\operatorname{Spec}_Z\bigl(\bigoplus_{r\geq0}I^r/I^{r+1}\bigr)$ of [pur9-def-normal-cone].
The quotient of $\mathcal O_{X\times S}[I/t]$ killing $I$ and every $a/t$ defines a closed
immersion $\widetilde e\colon Z\times S\hookrightarrow\mathcal D_ZX$; its special fibre
$e\colon Z\hookrightarrow N_ZX$ is the zero section.
