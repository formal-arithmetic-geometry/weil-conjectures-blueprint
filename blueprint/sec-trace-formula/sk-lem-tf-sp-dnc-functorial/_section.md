Let $k$ be algebraically closed and $S$ the strict henselization of
$\operatorname{Spec}k[t]_{(t)}$. For a separated finite-type $k$-scheme $X$ and a closed subscheme
$Z\subset X$ with ideal $I$, the deformation to the normal cone of $Z$ over $S$ is
$\mathcal D_ZX=\operatorname{Spec}_{X\times S}\bigl(\mathcal O_{X\times S}[I/t]\bigr)$, where the
algebra is the subalgebra of $\mathcal O_X[t,t^{-1}]$ generated by $\mathcal O_X[t]$ and the
fractions $a/t$, $a\in I$, followed by base change to $S$; its generic fibre is $X_\eta$ and its
special fibre is the normal cone
$N_ZX=\operatorname{Spec}_Z\bigl(\bigoplus_{r\geq0}I^r/I^{r+1}\bigr)$.

Let $f:X'\to X$ be a morphism of separated finite-type $k$-schemes, let $Z'\subset X'$ be a closed
subscheme with ideal $I'$, and suppose that $f^{\cdot}I\subset I'$. Then $f$ induces
$\widetilde f:\mathcal D_{Z'}X'\to\mathcal D_ZX$. If $Z'=f^{-1}Z$ schematically, then

$$
\mathcal D_{Z'}X'\longrightarrow X'\times_X\mathcal D_ZX
$$

is a closed immersion and is an isomorphism if $f$ is flat; moreover $\widetilde f_s^{-1}(Z)=Z'$
schematically. In this case $\widetilde f$ is proper when $f$ is proper and smooth when $f$ is
smooth.
