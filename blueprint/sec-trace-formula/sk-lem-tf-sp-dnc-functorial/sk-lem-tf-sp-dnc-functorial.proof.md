The map $a/t\mapsto f^{\cdot}(a)/t$ defines the first morphism. If $I'=I\mathcal O_{X'}$, the
natural map from the base-changed Rees algebra onto $\mathcal O_{X'}[t,I'/t]$ is surjective, and is
injective after flat base change. On the associated graded algebra the positive-degree generators
of $\operatorname{gr}_I$ generate the positive-degree ideal of $\operatorname{gr}_{I'}$. Killing
them gives $\mathcal O_{Z'}$, which proves the assertion about the zero section. The proper
assertion is the closed immersion followed by the proper base change of $f$; the smooth assertion
uses the flat isomorphism.
