The Euler product proves rationality of the zeta function and turns
statements about Frobenius on cohomology into statements about point
counts. Poincaré duality supplies the pairing used in the functional
equation for smooth proper schemes. The bounds on the absolute values
of Frobenius eigenvalues require, in addition, the theory of
weights, which lies beyond these notes.
