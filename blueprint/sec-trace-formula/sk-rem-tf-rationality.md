[sk-cor-tf-point-counting] and [sk-cor-tf-functional-equation] prove
rationality and the functional equation. The latter is an identity of
rational functions, not of power series at $T=0$, and its sign is determined
by $D$. In fact the integer $d\chi$ is even, so that
$D=\varepsilon q^{d\chi/2}$ with $\varepsilon=\pm1$. Indeed, for odd $d$ the
middle pairing on $H^d$ is perfect and alternating, so $b_d$ is even, and
$\chi\equiv b_d\pmod 2$ because $b_{2d-i}=b_i$. The bounds on the absolute
values of the eigenvalues $\alpha$ are proved in
§[sec-purity-real-sheaves]–§[sec-zeta-weil]. Their purity separates the
cohomological factors and permits recovery of integral polynomials
independent of the coefficient prime.
