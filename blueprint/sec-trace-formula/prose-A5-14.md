The morphism $u_Z$ is the base change of $u$ along the first leg, and
its construction is the first step of the proof. For $Z$ a point, Corollary [sk-cor-tf-contracting-input] specializes
this: under its hypotheses the local term at $F_0$ is the
categorical trace of $u_Z$, and for $c=(\mathrm{id}_X,h)$ it is
$\operatorname{tr}(u_{\bar y}\mid K_{\bar y})$.

If $Z$ is a point, the restricted correspondence is a correspondence of
the point, and the theorem computes the local term as a trace on a
stalk.
