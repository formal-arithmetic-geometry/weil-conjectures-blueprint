In the setting of [det-tf-fixed-ideal-nilpotent], assume moreover $L\subset J$, let $E=V(J)$,
let $\widetilde c=(\widetilde c_1,\widetilde c_2):\mathcal D_EC\to\mathcal D_ZX\times_T\mathcal D_ZX$
be the lifted legs of [det-tf-cone-leg-lifts] over $T=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]_{(t)}$, and let
$\widetilde F=F_{\widetilde c}$ be its fixed scheme. Then the closed immersion
$F\hookrightarrow C$ induces a closed immersion
$\mathcal D_{a^{-1}(Z)}F\hookrightarrow\mathcal D_EC$ that factors through $\widetilde F$, and
the reduction of $\mathcal D_{a^{-1}(Z)}F$ is $(F_T)_{\mathrm{red}}$. This gives a closed
immersion $(F_T)_{\mathrm{red}}\hookrightarrow\widetilde F_{\mathrm{red}}$.
