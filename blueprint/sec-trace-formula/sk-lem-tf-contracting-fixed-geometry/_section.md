As in Proposition [sk-prop-tf-trace-specialization], $F=F_c=C\times_{X\times X}X$ is the fixed
scheme of the correspondence $c$, and $a=c_1|_F=c_2|_F:F\to X$ is the common map. As in
Lemma [sk-lem-tf-contracting-cone-geometry], $T=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]_{(t)}$, $F_T=F\times T$,
and $\widetilde c_i:\mathcal D_EC\to\mathcal D_ZX$ are the lifted legs.

In the situation of Lemma [sk-lem-tf-contracting-cone-geometry],
suppose $F$ is connected and $a(F)$ meets $Z$. Then
$a^{-1}(Z)_{\mathrm{red}}=F_{\mathrm{red}}$. Moreover, if
$\widetilde F=F_{\widetilde c}$ is the fixed scheme of
$\widetilde c=(\widetilde c_1,\widetilde c_2)$, there is a closed
immersion, constructed in the proof,

$$
 (F_T)_{\mathrm{red}}\hookrightarrow\widetilde F_{\mathrm{red}},
$$

and it is an isomorphism.

Thus, up to nilpotents, the fixed scheme of the deformed
correspondence is the constant family $F_T$.
