In the notation of [det-nearby-exchange-maps], let $X$ be separated of finite type over $S$, with
projections $p_1,p_2\colon X\times_SX\to X$, and let
$\xi\colon p_2^*a_X^!A\to p_1^!a_X^*A$ be the external-duality map, so that the normalized
external-duality map is $\kappa_M=t_{p_1}(\xi\otimes1)\colon p_2^*\omega_X\dt p_1^*M\to p_1^!M$.
Then

$$
\beta_{p_1}\,S(\xi)\,\alpha_{p_2}=p_{1s}^!\sigma_X\,\xi_s\,p_{2s}^*\theta_X,
$$

and for every $M$

$$
\beta_{p_1}\,S(\kappa_M)\,\mu\,(\alpha_{p_2}\otimes\alpha_{p_1})
=\kappa_{S_XM}(p_{2s}^*\theta_X\otimes1).
$$
