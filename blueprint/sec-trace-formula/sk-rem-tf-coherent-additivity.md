The coherence condition is essential over a general ring: three endomorphisms
of a distinguished triangle whose squares commute only in the triangulated
category need not satisfy trace additivity
([Stacks, Tag 087J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/087J)). Every
localization sequence used below comes functorially from the Frobenius
correspondence and the open–closed filtration, so it lifts to the filtered
derived category. Traces are taken of perfect complexes, since the cohomology
modules of a perfect $A$-complex are in general not perfect.
