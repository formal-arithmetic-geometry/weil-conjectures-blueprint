For a finite universal homeomorphism $r:X\to Y$,
pullback is an equivalence
$r^*:D^b_{ctf}(Y,A)\simeq D^b_{ctf}(X,A)$, with inverse $Rr_*=Rr_!$ and
invertible unit and counit. This includes reduction.
