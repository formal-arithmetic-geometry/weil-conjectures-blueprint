Let $S$ be an excellent strictly henselian trait with special point $s$, generic point
$\eta$ and geometric generic point $\bar\eta$, let $A$ be a finite commutative
$\ell$-primary ring with $\ell$ invertible on $S$, write $S_X=R\Psi_{X/S}$ for nearby
cycles, and let $\delta_{X,M}:S_X\mathbb D_{X_\eta}M\to\mathbb D_{X_s}(S_XM)$ be the
tensor–Hom adjoint of the pairing
$\theta_X\circ S_X(\operatorname{ev})\circ\mu_X:S_XM\otimes S_X\mathbb D_{X_\eta}M\to\omega_{X_s}$,
where $\mu_X$ is the lax tensor map and $\theta_X=\beta_{a_X}(A)$.

1. **(1)** For a proper morphism $f:X\to Y$ of separated finite-type $S$-schemes and
   $M\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_\eta,A)$, proper exchange
   $\chi_f:S_YRf_{\eta*}\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf_{s*}S_X$ and proper Verdier duality
   $Rf_*\mathbb D\simeq\mathbb DRf_*$ on both fibres identify $Rf_{s*}\delta_{X,M}$ with
   $\delta_{Y,Rf_{\eta*}M}$.
2. **(2)** For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, the smooth exchange
   isomorphism $\alpha_j$ and $j^!=j^*$ identify $j_s^*\delta_{X,M}$ with
   $\delta_{U,j_\eta^*M}$.
3. **(3)** If $X$ is proper over $S$, proper exchange for the structure map $a$ and
   global Verdier duality identify $R\Gamma(X_s,\delta_{X,M})$ with the identity of
   $R\operatorname{Hom}_A(R\Gamma(X_{\bar\eta},M),A)$.
