First let the action factor through a finite $p$-group quotient $G$. Since $|G|$ is
a power of $p$, it is invertible in $A$, and invariants are the image of the averaging
idempotent $e=|G|^{-1}\sum_{h\in G}h$; in particular $E^G$ is a direct summand of $E$.
Verdier duality sends $e$ to the averaging idempotent of the contragredient action,
since inversion $h\mapsto h^{-1}$ permutes the summands. The image of
$\mathbb D(e)$ is therefore the dual of $\operatorname{im}e$, which gives
$(\mathbb DE)^G\simeq\mathbb D(E^G)$, and the pairing between the two summands is the
restriction of evaluation.

For the pro-$p$ group $P$, invariants $R\Gamma(P,-)$ are exact on the discrete
prime-to-$p$ torsion coefficients, and by continuity of the action they are computed
from the invariants under the finite quotients of $P$. Exactness and continuity
therefore carry the finite case over to $P$, with the same description of the pairing.
