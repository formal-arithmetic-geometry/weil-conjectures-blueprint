title = "Verdier duality commutes with invariants under a pro-p group"
order = 3597
+++
Let $P$ be a pro-$p$ group, where $p$ is invertible in the finite commutative
$\ell$-primary ring $A$, and let $Y$ be a separated finite-type scheme over a
separably closed field of characteristic exponent $p$. Write
$(-)^P=R\Gamma(P,-)$, which is exact on discrete $A$-modules. For a bounded
constructible complex $E$ of finite Tor dimension on $Y$ with continuous $P$-action,
let $P$ act on $\mathbb DE$ by the contragredient action. Then the natural comparison

$$
 (\mathbb DE)^P\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbb D(E^P)
$$

is an isomorphism, and the corresponding pairing $E^P\otimes(\mathbb DE)^P\to\omega_Y$
is the restriction of evaluation to the two invariant direct summands.
