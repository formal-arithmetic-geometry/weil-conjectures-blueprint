order = 3562
uses = ["det-sp-defect-definition"]

For $X=\mathbf A^1_k$, $Z=V(x^m)$ with $m\geq1$, and every finite $A$-module $M$, the zero-section
map $\epsilon_{X,Z}(M_X)\colon e^*\operatorname{Sp}_{X,Z}(M_X)\to M_Z$ of
[det-sp-defect-definition] is an isomorphism; equivalently $C_{X,Z}(M_X)=0$.
