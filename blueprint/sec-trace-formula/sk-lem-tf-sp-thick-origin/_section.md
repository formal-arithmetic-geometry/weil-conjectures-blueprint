For a separated finite-type $k$-scheme $X$ and a closed subscheme $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$,
let $C_{X,Z}(L)$ be the defect of [sk-lem-tf-sp-defect-exchanges], the cofibre of the zero-section
map $\epsilon_{X,Z}(L)\colon e^*\operatorname{Sp}_{X,Z}(L)\to i^*L$; for a finite $A$-module $M$,
$M_X$ denotes the constant sheaf on $X$.

For $X=\mathbf A^1_k$, $Z=V(x^m)$ with $m\geq1$, and every finite $A$-module $M$, one has
$C_{X,Z}(M_X)=0$.
