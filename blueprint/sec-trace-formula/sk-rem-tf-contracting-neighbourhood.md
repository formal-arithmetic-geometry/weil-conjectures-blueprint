Here $c:C\to X\times X$ is a correspondence with fixed scheme $F_c$, $K$ is its coefficient
complex, and the ideal inclusions are $c_2^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_C\subset c_1^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_C$
and $(c_2^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_C)^n\subset(c_1^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_C)^{n+1}$ for the ideal $I$ of a
closed subscheme $Z\subset X$.

Let $\beta\subset F_c$ be a connected component; it is open and
closed. Replacing $C$ by an open neighbourhood of $\beta$ on which
the ideal inclusions hold, and then removing $F_c\setminus\beta$,
gives an open subscheme of $C$ whose fixed scheme is $\beta$.
Restriction of trace classes along this open immersion identifies
the trace class of $u$ on $\beta$ with the full trace class of the
restricted correspondence. Hence the hypothesis that $F$ is
connected in Lemma [sk-lem-tf-contracting-fixed-geometry] is no loss of generality. Both
modifications change only $C$, so $X$ and $K$ stay the same.
