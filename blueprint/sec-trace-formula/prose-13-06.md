In the induction on affine coordinates in the next subsection, we push a
Frobenius correspondence forward along a morphism $f$, and for the
additivity below, we restrict one to an open and to a closed
piece. The square formed by $f$ and the two Frobenii is usually not
cartesian, and the relative Frobenius measures the difference. The
next lemma describes the induced Frobenius correspondence on $Rf_!K$
and its operators.
