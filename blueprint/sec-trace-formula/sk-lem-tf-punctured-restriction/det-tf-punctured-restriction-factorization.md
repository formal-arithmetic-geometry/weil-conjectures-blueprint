id = "det-tf-punctured-restriction-factorization"
kind = "lemma"
title = "Restriction to a punctured curve through the boundary stalk"
+++
Let $Z$ be a scheme, $Y\subset Z$ a closed subscheme with open
complement $j:Z-Y\hookrightarrow Z$, let $A$ be a ring, let $F$ be a
complex of $A$-module sheaves on the étale site of $Z-Y$, and put
$M=Rj_*F$. Let $C\subset Z$ be a closed curve with $C\cap Y=\{s\}$ for
a closed point $s$, let $C_{(s)}$ and $Z_{(s)}$ be the strict
localizations at a geometric point over $s$, and let
$\iota:C_{(s)}-s\to Z-Y$ be the induced map. Then the restriction map

$$
 R\Gamma(Z-Y,F)\longrightarrow R\Gamma(C_{(s)}-s,\iota^*F)
$$

is the composite

$$
 R\Gamma(Z-Y,F)=R\Gamma(Z,M)\longrightarrow R\Gamma(Y,M|_Y)
 \longrightarrow M_s=R\Gamma(Z_{(s)}-Y,F)
 \longrightarrow R\Gamma(C_{(s)}-s,\iota^*F)
$$

of restriction to $Y$, restriction to the stalk at $s$, and restriction
along $C_{(s)}-s\to Z_{(s)}-Y$. In particular it is zero as soon as
$R\Gamma(Y,M|_Y)\to M_s$ is zero, or $M_s=0$.
