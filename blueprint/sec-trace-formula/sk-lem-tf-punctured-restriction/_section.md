In the situation of Lemma [sk-lem-tf-transverse-boundary] with $Y$
connected, let $C\subset Z$ be a closed curve with $C\cap Y=\{s\}$,
and let $C_{(s)}$ be its strict localization at $s$. Then the
restriction map

$$
 R\Gamma(Z-Y,i_!E)\longrightarrow
 R\Gamma(C_{(s)}-s,(i_!E)|_{C_{(s)}-s})
$$

is zero in $D(A)$.
