In the setting of [def-sf-datum] and [def-sf-orientation], let
$X$ be smooth and geometrically connected of pure dimension $d$, and
either proper or of the form $X=X_0\times_{k_0}k$ for a smooth, separated
finite-type, geometrically connected $k_0$-scheme $X_0$ (the two cases of
[thm-sf-global-duality]; no properness is needed in the second), and let $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X,\Lambda)$ be perfect over $\Lambda_X$. Then
$\mathbb D_XK\cong K^\vee(d)[2d]$, and $R\Gamma_c(X,K)^\vee\cong R\Gamma(X,K^\vee(d))[2d]$.

## Proof

This is the orientation of [def-sf-orientation] substituted into
[thm-sf-global-duality].
