In [sk-lem-tf-rational-model], $K_0$ is an arbitrary object of
$\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, possibly without a lisse lattice, and $\theta$
is an arbitrary morphism, and one cannot ask for more. Already on a point the automorphism $\ell^{-1}$ of $E$ preserves no
integral lattice. More generally, the two eigenvalues of
$\operatorname{diag}(1,\ell)$ cannot both become units after
multiplying the operator by one scalar. [sk-lem-tf-rational-model]
only makes $\pi_E^N\psi_E$ integral, and its reductions may fail to be
invertible; this is why [sk-thm-tf-coefficient-morphism-formula]
is stated for arbitrary Frobenius correspondences.
