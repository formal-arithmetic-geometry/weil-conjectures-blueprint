title = "Zeta function of the constant sheaf"
order = 3864
+++
For the constant sheaf every local factor is $(1-T^{\deg(x)})^{-1}$, and the
Euler product becomes the zeta function of $X_0$. This proves
rationality of $Z(X_0,T)$ over $\Ql$, and a short argument with linear
recurrences descends it to $\mathbf Q$.
