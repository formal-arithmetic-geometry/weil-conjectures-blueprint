Here $k$ is algebraically closed, $A$ is a finite commutative ring annihilated by a power of
a prime $\ell$ invertible in $k$, $c=(c_1,c_2):C\to X\times X$ is a correspondence of
separated finite-type $k$-schemes with fixed scheme $F_c$, and $\operatorname{Tr}_c(u):A_{F_c}\to\omega_{F_c}$
is the trace class of [sk-d-trace-class] of a morphism $u:c_2^*K\to c_1^!K$; for an ideal
$I$ and a morphism $f$, $f^\cdot I\,\mathcal O$ is the ideal generated by the inverse image of
$I$.

Let $c=(c_1,c_2):C\to X\times X$ be a correspondence, and let
$Z\hookrightarrow X$ have ideal $I$. Let $\beta$ be a connected open-and-closed component of $F_c$
whose image meets $Z$. Choose an open neighbourhood $W\subset C$
of $\beta$ on which

$$
 L:=c_2^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_W\subset
 J:=c_1^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_W,
 \qquad L^n\subset J^{n+1}\quad\text{for some }n\geq1.
$$

As in Proposition [sk-i-additivity], let
$C_Z=(W\cap c_1^{-1}Z)_{\mathrm{red}}$, and let
$c_Z=(c_{Z,1},c_{Z,2}):C_Z\to Z_{\mathrm{red}}\times Z_{\mathrm{red}}$
be the correspondence induced by the two legs restricted to $W$.
For every $K\in\Dctf(X,A)$ and every morphism
$u:c_2^*K\to c_1^!K$, there is a morphism $u_Z$ from
$c_{Z,2}^*$ to $c_{Z,1}^!$ of the restriction of $K$ to
$Z_{\mathrm{red}}$, constructed in the proof, such that

$$
 \operatorname{Tr}_c(u)|_\beta
   =(h_\beta)_*\bigl(\operatorname{Tr}_{c_Z}(u_Z)|_{\beta_Z}\bigr),
 \qquad \beta_Z=F_{c_Z}\times_{F_c}\beta.
$$

Here the projection $h_\beta:\beta_Z\to\beta$ is an isomorphism
after reduction, and its pushforward identifies the dualizing
sections. If $\beta$ is proper, the local terms of $u$ on $\beta$ and
of $u_Z$ on $\beta_Z$ are equal.

Both sides depend only on a neighbourhood of $\beta$, so $W$ may be
shrunk around $\beta$. The theorem holds for every morphism $u$,
including non-invertible ones.
