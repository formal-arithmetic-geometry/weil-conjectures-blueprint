Let $c=(c_1,c_2):C\to X\times X$ be a correspondence of separated finite-type schemes over an
algebraically closed field $k$, let $Z\subset X$ have ideal $I$, and assume that
$L=c_2^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_C$ and $J=c_1^\cdot I\,\mathcal O_C$ satisfy $L\subset J$ and
$L^n\subset J^{n+1}$ for some $n\geq1$. Assume that the fixed scheme $F_c$ is connected and that
its image meets $Z$. Let $i:Z_{\mathrm{red}}\hookrightarrow X$, let $K\in\Dctf(X,A)$ with
$i^*K=0$, and let $u:c_2^*K\to c_1^!K$. Then $\operatorname{Tr}_c(u)=0$.
