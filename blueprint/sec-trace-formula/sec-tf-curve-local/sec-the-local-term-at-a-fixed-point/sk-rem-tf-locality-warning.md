The vanishing for $K_{\bar y}=0$ is a geometric fact about the
restriction from cohomology with supports in $\Gamma$ to cohomology with
supports in $y$, and it does not follow from a locality property
of internal Hom. For example, if $j:\mathbf G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$
and $K=j_!A$, then the stalk of $K$ at the origin is zero, but
$\cHom_A(j_!A,j_!A)\simeq Rj_*A$ has stalk $A$ in degree zero there. Hence a local term cannot be computed by commuting
a geometric stalk functor past this internal Hom.
