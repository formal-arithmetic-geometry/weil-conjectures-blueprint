In the situation of [det-tf-restriction-zero-localization], there are finite covers
$X'\to X$ and $Y'\to Y$ by strictly local regular traits, both of the same degree $d$ prime
to $\ell$ and generically étale, with the following properties. Let $T'$ be the
localization of $X'\times Y'$ at its unique closed point $y'$, and let $G',V',H',B'$ be the
reduced inverse images of $G,V,H,B$.

1. **(i)** The pullbacks $N',Q'$ of $N,Q$ admit bounded finite projective lisse models whose
   termwise monodromy factors through finite $\ell$-groups.
2. **(ii)** $T'$ is a strictly local smooth surface with completed local ring
   $k[[\xi,\eta]]$, finite flat of rank $d^2$ over $T$ and étale away from $H\cup V$; in
   particular $B'-y'\to B-y$ is finite étale of degree $d^2$.
3. **(iii)** The support of the tangent cone of $G'\cup V'$ meets neither that of $B'$ nor
   that of the horizontal axis $H'$ outside the origin.
