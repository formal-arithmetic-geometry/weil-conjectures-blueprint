Let $X_0$ be a smooth curve over $k_0$, let $X=X_0\times_{k_0}k$, let
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$, let $y$ be a fixed point of $\Phi_X^m$, and let $T$,
$\Gamma$, $\Delta$, $P$ and $\rho$ be as in
Lemma [sk-lem-tf-curve-supported-pairing]. If $K_{\bar y}=0$, then

$$
\rho:R\Gamma_\Gamma(T,P)\longrightarrow R\Gamma_y(\Delta,\delta^*P)
$$

is zero in $D(A)$. Consequently $\operatorname{LT}_y(\psi)=0$ for every
morphism $\psi:(\Phi_X^m)^*K\to K$.
