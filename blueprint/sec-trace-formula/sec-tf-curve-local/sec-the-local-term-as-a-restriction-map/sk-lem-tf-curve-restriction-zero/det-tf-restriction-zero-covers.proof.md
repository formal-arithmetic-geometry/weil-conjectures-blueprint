*(i).* $N$ and $Q$ have bounded finite projective constructible models; a punctured
strictly local trait is its generic point, so these terms are lisse. Choose finite Galois
covers trivializing all their terms and pass to the quotients by $\ell$-Sylow subgroups.
Normalizing gives $X'\to X$ and $Y'\to Y$ of degrees $d_1,d_2$ prime to $\ell$ over which
the termwise monodromy factors through finite $\ell$-groups. To equalize the degrees, take
further generically étale totally ramified extensions and normalize, reaching
$d=\operatorname{lcm}(d_1,d_2)$, which is prime to $\ell$. An extension of degree $e>1$ of a
trait with uniformizer $t$ is given by the Eisenstein equation $z^e+tz-t=0$: its derivative
is nonzero, and it defines a regular trait with uniformizer $z$, generically separable
even when $p\mid e$.

*(ii).* The two covers are finite flat of rank $d$, so their product, localized at the
unique closed point, is finite flat of rank $d^2$ over $T$. The data descend to curve
neighbourhoods, and normalization over the perfect field $k$ gives smooth curves; hence
the completed local ring of $T'$ is $k[[\xi,\eta]]$ with $\xi,\eta$ uniformizers of the two
factors. The covers are étale over the punctured traits, so $T'\to T$ is étale away from
$H\cup V$, and $B-y\subset T-(H\cup V)$.

*(iii).* For parameters $u,v$ on the original factors write
$u=\alpha\xi^d+O(\xi^{d+1})$ and $v=\beta\eta^d+O(\eta^{d+1})$ with $\alpha\beta\ne0$. Write
$\varphi(v)=(\Phi_X^m)^\#u$; the graph has equation $u=\varphi(v)$ with
$\operatorname{ord}_v\varphi=q^m$, and the diagonal has equation $u=v$. Their pulled-back
initial forms are, up to nonzero constants, $\xi^d$ and $\alpha\xi^d-\beta\eta^d$. The
vertical axis has initial form $\xi^d$ and the horizontal axis $\eta^d$. The zero loci of
$\xi^d$ and of $\alpha\xi^d-\beta\eta^d$, and of $\xi^d$ and $\eta^d$, meet only at the
origin. Replacing the inverse-image curves by their reductions does not change these
supports, and only supports enter, so this holds also when $p\mid d$.
