On finite projective modules evaluation and coevaluation are given by a finite
dual basis. On a complex, the symmetry interchanging a term of degree $i$ and
its dual of degree $-i$ contributes $(-1)^i$. This proves the chain formula,
including its invariance under chain homotopy: the supertrace of $dh+hd$ is
zero by cyclicity and a shift of index. Tensoring a dual basis with $B$
preserves evaluation and coevaluation, which proves scalar compatibility for
every ring map $A\to B$. Linearity and conjugacy follow from the same
description. A coherent two-step filtration may be represented by a filtered
projective complex with degreewise split filtration; its endomorphism is block
triangular, so the supertrace is the sum of the supertraces on the two graded
pieces. Over a field the kernels and images of the differential give invariant
subspaces, and their contributions cancel, leaving the cohomological formula.
