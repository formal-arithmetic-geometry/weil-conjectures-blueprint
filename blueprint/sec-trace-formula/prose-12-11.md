To compute local terms, we cut $K$ into pieces along decompositions of
$X$ into an open and a closed part which are invariant under the
correspondence, and for this we need the trace class to be additive. By Remark [sk-rem-tf-coherent-additivity],
additivity can only be expected for filtrations lifted to the filtered
derived category.
