On a finite quotient $G$ of $P$, invariants of an $A[G]$-module are the image of the averaging
idempotent $e_G=|G|^{-1}\sum_{g\in G}g$, since the $p$-power $|G|$ is invertible in $A$. Taking the
image of an idempotent is exact, so $G$-invariants are exact, and $e_G$ commutes with tensoring by
a module with trivial action, so $(V\otimes_AB)^G=V^G\otimes_AB$. A discrete module with continuous
$P$-action is the union of its invariants under the open normal subgroups, so $P$-invariants are
the filtered colimit of the invariants of the finite quotients acting on these pieces; continuity
therefore passes the exactness and the projection identity from the finite quotients to $P$. In
particular $R\Gamma(P,-)$ is computed by underived invariants. Resolving $B$ by flat $A$-modules
with trivial action gives the derived identity.
