Let $S$ be an excellent strictly henselian trait, with separably closed residue field $k$, generic
point $\eta$ and a chosen geometric generic point $\bar\eta$, and let $A$ be a finite commutative
$\ell$-primary ring with $\ell$ invertible on $S$. For $X$ separated of finite type over $S$, let
$R\Psi_{X/S}L=\bar i^*R\bar j_{X*}L_{\bar\eta}$ be the geometric nearby cycles of $L$ on $X_\eta$,
where $\bar i$ and $\bar j_X$ are the inclusions of the special fibre and of the geometric generic
fibre. For $X,Y$ separated of finite type over $S$, $L$ on $X_\eta$ and $M$ on $Y_\eta$, the
comparison map $\Pi_{L,M}$ is the restriction to the special fibre of the map

$$
R\bar j_{X*}L_{\bar\eta}\boxtimes R\bar j_{Y*}M_{\bar\eta}
\longrightarrow
R\bar j_{X\times Y,*}(L_{\bar\eta}\boxtimes M_{\bar\eta})
$$

defined by the pullback exchanges and the lax tensor map of direct image.

Let $X,Y$ be separated of finite type over $S$, and let $L\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_\eta,A)$ and
$M\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_\eta,A)$. The comparison map

$$
\Pi_{L,M}:R\Psi_{X/S}L\boxtimes R\Psi_{Y/S}M
\longrightarrow
R\Psi_{(X\times_SY)/S}(L\boxtimes M)
\tag{1}
$$

is an isomorphism in $D^b_c((X_s\times_kY_s)_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$.
