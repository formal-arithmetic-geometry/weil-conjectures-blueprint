title = "Invariants under a pro-p group commute with tensor products"
order = 3590
+++
Let $P$ be a pro-$p$ group, where $p$ is a prime or $p=1$, and let $A$ be a finite commutative ring
in which $p$ is invertible. For a bounded complex $E$ of discrete torsion $A$-modules with
continuous $P$-action and a bounded complex $B$ of $A$-modules with trivial $P$-action, write
$(-)^P=R\Gamma(P,-)$. Then $(-)^P$ is exact on discrete torsion $A$-modules, and the projection map
is an isomorphism

$$
(E\mathbin{\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}}B)^P\xrightarrow{\sim}E^P\mathbin{\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}}B.
$$
