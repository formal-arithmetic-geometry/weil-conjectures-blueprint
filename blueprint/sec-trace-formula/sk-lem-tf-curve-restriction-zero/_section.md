In [sk-lem-tf-curve-supported-pairing], suppose $K_{\bar y}=0$.
On the classical strictly local models, the supported
restriction

$$
 \rho_{a,b}:R\Gamma_a(T,(\mathbb D_XK\boxtimes K)|_T)
 \longrightarrow R\Gamma_y(b,b^*(\mathbb D_XK\boxtimes K))
$$

is zero in $D(A)$.
