In the situation of [det-tf-restriction-zero-covers], suppose that the upstairs punctured
restriction

$$
 R\Gamma\bigl(T'-(V'\cup G'),g'_!(N'\boxtimes Q')\bigr)\longrightarrow
 R\Gamma\bigl(B'-y',(N'\boxtimes Q')|_{B'-y'}\bigr)
$$

is zero. Then the downstairs restriction of
[det-tf-restriction-zero-localization] (iii) is zero in $D(A)$, and hence so is the
supported restriction $\rho_{a,b}$.
