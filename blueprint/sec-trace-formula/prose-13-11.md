The trace formula can be read as a statement about functions on
rational points. For a Weil complex $(K,\phi)$ on $X$ as in
Theorem [sk-thm-tf-uniform-formula] and $m\geq1$, its *trace
function* is

$$
t_{K,m}:X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})\longrightarrow\Lambda,\qquad
 t_{K,m}(x)=\operatorname{tr}_\Lambda\bigl(\operatorname{Frob}_{x,m}
   \mid K_{\bar x}\bigr).
$$

The theorem then says that $\operatorname{tr}_\Lambda(\operatorname{Frob}_{X,K}^m
\mid R\Gamma_c(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},K))$ is the sum of $t_{K,m}$ over
$X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$. Applied to the fibres of a morphism, it shows
that direct image with compact support sums trace functions over the fibres.
