Let $k$ be algebraically closed, let $\ell$ be invertible in $k$, and let $A$ be a finite
commutative ring annihilated by a power of $\ell$. Let $S$ be the strict henselization of
$\operatorname{Spec}k[t]_{(t)}$ with a chosen geometric generic point $\bar\eta$. For
$\mathcal Y\to S$ separated of finite type, let $\bar i$ and $\bar j$ be the inclusions of the
special fibre and of the geometric generic fibre of the pullback of $\mathcal Y$ to the integral
closure of $S$ in the chosen separable closure of its fraction field; for $L$ on $\mathcal Y_\eta$
with pullback $L_{\bar\eta}$ to the geometric generic fibre, the geometric nearby cycles are
$R\Psi_{\mathcal Y}(L)=\bar i^{*}R\bar j_*L_{\bar\eta}$ (the inertia action is forgotten). For a
morphism $f\colon\mathcal Y'\to\mathcal Y$ of separated finite-type $S$-schemes there are the
pullback exchange $f_s^*R\Psi_{\mathcal Y}\to R\Psi_{\mathcal Y'}f_\eta^*$, invertible for smooth
$f$, and for proper $f$ the proper-direct-image exchange
$Rf_{s*}R\Psi_{\mathcal Y'}\xrightarrow{\sim}R\Psi_{\mathcal Y}Rf_{\eta*}$. For a separated
finite-type $k$-scheme $Y$, the constant family $Y\times S$ has special fibre $Y$; for $L$ on $Y$
with pullback $L_\eta$ to the generic fibre, the *specialization unit* $\sigma_Y(L)$ is the
restriction to the special fibre of the adjunction unit of $\bar j$ on the pullback of $L$.

For a separated finite-type $k$-scheme $Y$ and $L\in\Dbc(Y,A)$, the specialization unit is an
isomorphism in $\Dbc(Y,A)$

$$
\sigma_Y(L):L\xrightarrow{\sim}R\Psi_{Y\times S}(L_\eta).
$$

It is natural in $L$ and compatible with the proper and smooth exchanges above.
