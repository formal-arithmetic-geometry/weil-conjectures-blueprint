title = "Constant coefficients near a transverse corner"
uses = ["thm-pur-finite-coefficient-smooth-purity", "lem-sc-affine-cohomological-dimension", "def-sc-kummer-sequence"]

Let $S$ be the strict localization at a closed point $z$ of a smooth
surface over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p$ (or of
an étale limit with affine transition maps of such surfaces), and let
$X_S,Y_S\subset S$ be regular divisors meeting transversely, so that
$X_S\cap Y_S=\{z\}$ and $X_S-z=X_S\cap(S-Y_S)$. Let $\ell\ne p$. Then
both $R\Gamma(S-Y_S,\mathbf F_\ell)$ and
$R\Gamma(X_S-z,\mathbf F_\ell)$ have cohomology $\mathbf F_\ell$ in
degree $0$, $\mathbf F_\ell(-1)$ in degree $1$ and zero in all other
degrees, and the restriction map

$$
 R\Gamma(S-Y_S,\mathbf F_\ell)\longrightarrow R\Gamma(X_S-z,\mathbf F_\ell)
$$

is an isomorphism.
