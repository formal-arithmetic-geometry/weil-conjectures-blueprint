Let $Z$ be a regular smooth surface over an algebraically closed field
of characteristic $p$, or an étale limit with affine transition
maps of such surfaces. Let $X,Y$ be regular divisors meeting
transversely at exactly one point $z$, and let

$$
 j:Z-Y\hookrightarrow Z,\qquad
 i:Z-(X\cup Y)\hookrightarrow Z-Y.
$$

Let $\ell\ne p$, let $A$ be a finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring,
and let $E$ be a bounded complex of $A$-modules on $Z-(X\cup Y)$
with finite locally constant cohomology whose monodromy factors
through a finite $\ell$-group. Put $M=Rj_*i_!E$. Then $M_z=0$, and
$M|_{Y-z}$ has finite locally constant cohomology. If $Y$ is
connected, then for every closed geometric point $s\in Y-z$ the
restriction map

$$
 R\Gamma(Y,M|_Y)\longrightarrow M_s
$$

is zero in $D(A)$.
