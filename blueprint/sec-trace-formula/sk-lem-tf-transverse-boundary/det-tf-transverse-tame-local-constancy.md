title = "Local constancy along the boundary divisor"
order = 3521

Let $Z$ be a regular smooth surface over an algebraically closed field
of characteristic $p$, or an étale limit with affine transition maps of
such surfaces, let $X,Y\subset Z$ be regular divisors meeting
transversely at exactly one point $z$, and let
$j:Z-Y\hookrightarrow Z$ and $i:Z-(X\cup Y)\hookrightarrow Z-Y$. Let
$\ell\ne p$, let $A$ be a finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring, and
let $E$ be a bounded complex of $A$-modules on $Z-(X\cup Y)$ with finite
locally constant cohomology whose monodromy factors through a finite
$\ell$-group. Then $M=Rj_*i_!E$ restricted to $Y-z$ has finite locally
constant cohomology sheaves: its stalks at geometric points of $Y-z$ are
the continuous cohomology of the tame inertia representation of $E$
along $Y$, and the specialization maps between them are isomorphisms.
