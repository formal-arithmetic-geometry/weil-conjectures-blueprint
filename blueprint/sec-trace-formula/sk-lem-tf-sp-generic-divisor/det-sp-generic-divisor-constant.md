title = "Generic vanishing for constant coefficients off a divisor"

Let $X$ be a normal separated finite-type $k$-scheme, $Z\subset X$ an effective principal Cartier
divisor with open complement $j\colon U=X\setminus Z\hookrightarrow X$, $\zeta$ a generic point of
$Z$, and $M$ a finite $A$-module. Then the defect $C_{X,Z}(j_!M_U)$ of [det-sp-defect-definition]
vanishes on a neighbourhood of $\zeta$ in $Z$.
