For a separated finite-type $k$-scheme $X$ and a closed subscheme $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$,
let $C_{X,Z}(L)$ be the defect of [sk-lem-tf-sp-defect-exchanges], the cofibre of the zero-section
map $\epsilon_{X,Z}(L)\colon e^*\operatorname{Sp}_{X,Z}(L)\to i^*L$, for
$L\in D^b_c(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$.

Suppose $X$ is normal, $Z\subset X$ is an effective principal Cartier divisor, and $i^*L=0$. Then
$C_{X,Z}(L)$ vanishes at every generic point of $Z$.
