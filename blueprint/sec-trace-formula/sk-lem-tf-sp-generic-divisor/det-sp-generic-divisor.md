Let $X$ be a normal separated finite-type $k$-scheme, $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ an effective
principal Cartier divisor, and $L\in D^b_c(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$ with $i^*L=0$. Then the
defect $C_{X,Z}(L)$ of [det-sp-defect-definition] vanishes at every generic point of $Z$.
