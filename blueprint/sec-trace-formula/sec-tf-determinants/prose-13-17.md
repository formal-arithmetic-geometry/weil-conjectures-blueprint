Over a field of characteristic zero, the traces of all powers of an
endomorphism determine its characteristic polynomial. Taking the trace
formula for all $m\geq1$, we therefore obtain an identity of power
series: a product over closed points equals an alternating product of
characteristic polynomials of Frobenius on cohomology. Since this
step involves dividing by $m$, in the determinant formulas below $\Lambda$
is one of the fields $\Ql$, $E$, $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.

This is how the trace formula gives Weil's rationality conjecture. The
zeta function is the case $K=\Lambda_X$
(Corollary [sk-cor-tf-point-counting]), and the product over cohomology
is finite, so $Z(X_0,T)$ is a rational function. Characteristic zero is
needed: on $\mathbf F_p^{\oplus p}$, every power of the identity has trace
$p=0$, as for the zero endomorphism, but
$\det(1-T\cdot\operatorname{id})=(1-T)^p=1-T^p$ is not $1$. For the
functional equation we add Poincaré duality with Frobenius
(Theorem [sk-thm-frobenius]), which matches the factor in degree $i$
with the factor in degree $2d-i$ through
$\alpha\leftrightarrow q^d/\alpha$
(Corollary [sk-cor-tf-functional-equation]). The Euler product for general
$K$ (Theorem [sk-thm-trace-transport]) is the form in which the trace
formula enters the theory of weights.
