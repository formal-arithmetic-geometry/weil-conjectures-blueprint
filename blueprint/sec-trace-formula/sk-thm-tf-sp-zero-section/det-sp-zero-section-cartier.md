Let $X$ be a separated finite-type $k$-scheme, $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ a closed subscheme,
$b\colon X'=\operatorname{Bl}_ZX\to X$ the blow-up, $Z'=b^{-1}Z$ schematically and
$b_Z\colon Z'\to Z$ the restriction of $b$. Then $Z'$ is an effective Cartier divisor on $X'$, and
for every $L\in D^b_c(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$ with $i^*L=0$ the defect of
[det-sp-defect-definition] satisfies

$$
C_{X,Z}(L)\simeq Rb_{Z*}C_{X',Z'}(b^*L).
$$
