Let $X=X_0\times_{k_0}k$ and the graph $c_m=(\operatorname{id}_X,\Phi_X^m)$ of the $k$-linear
Frobenius be as in [sk-d-weil]; the fixed locus $F_{c_m}$ of $c_m$ is the fixed-point
scheme $\operatorname{Fix}(\Phi_X^m)$.
For $m\geq1$, the fixed locus of the graph $c_m$ satisfies

$$
 \operatorname{Fix}(\Phi_X^m)
 \simeq\coprod_{x\in X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})}s.
$$

In particular the fixed scheme is finite, reduced and proper over $k$.
