The fixed scheme is the equalizer of $\Phi_X^m$ and $\operatorname{id}_X$, the closed
subscheme $V(\mathfrak b)$ with
$\mathfrak b=\bigl(\Phi_X^{m\#}(a)-a: a\in B\bigr)$. The map $\Phi_X^{m\#}-\operatorname{id}$ is
additive and sends $a\otimes b$ to $(a^{q^m}-a)\otimes b$, so $\mathfrak b$ is generated by the
elements $a^{q^m}-a$ with $a$ in the image of $k_0[t]$. For $a=\sum_Jc_Jt^J$ with $c_J\in k_0=\mathbf F_q$ one has
$c_J^{q^m}=c_J$, so

$$
 a^{q^m}-a=\sum_Jc_J\bigl(t^{q^mJ}-t^J\bigr).
$$

Each monomial difference lies in $(t_i^{q^m}-t_i)_i$, by the telescoping identity

$$
 \prod_iu_i-\prod_iv_i=\sum_i\Bigl(\prod_{h<i}u_h\Bigr)(u_i-v_i)\Bigl(\prod_{h>i}v_h\Bigr),
 \qquad u_i=t_i^{q^mJ_i},\ v_i=t_i^{J_i},
$$

since $t_i^{q^m}-t_i$ divides $(t_i^{q^m})^{J_i}-t_i^{J_i}$. Conversely each
$t_i^{q^m}-t_i$ lies in $\mathfrak b$. Hence $\mathfrak b=(t_i^{q^m}-t_i)_iB$. The
equalizer is formed Zariski locally, so the descriptions on affine opens of $X_0$ agree on
overlaps.
