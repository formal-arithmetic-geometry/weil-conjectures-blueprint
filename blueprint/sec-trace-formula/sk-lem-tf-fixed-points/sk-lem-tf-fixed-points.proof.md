On an affine presentation over $\mathbf F_q$, the fixed-point
equations add $t_i^{q^m}-t_i=0$ to the defining equations. Since each
polynomial $t^{q^m}-t$ is separable, the algebra
$k[t_1,\ldots,t_r]/(t_i^{q^m}-t_i)_i$ is a product of copies of $k$
indexed by $\mathbf F_{q^m}^r$. A quotient by the defining equations
retains exactly the factors at the rational solutions of those
equations. These affine descriptions glue, and finitely many affine
opens cover all fixed points, giving the asserted finite disjoint
union. The argument applies equally to nonreduced $X_0$.
