The vanishing for $K_{\bar y}=0$ is a geometric fact about the
supported restriction; a locality property of internal Hom would not
give it. For example, if $j:\mathbf G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$
and $K=j_!A$, then $K_0=0$ but
$\cHom_A(j_!A,j_!A)\simeq Rj_*A$ has a nonzero degree-zero
stalk at $0$. A local term therefore cannot be computed by commuting
a geometric stalk functor past this internal Hom.
