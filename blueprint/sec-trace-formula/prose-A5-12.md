As in Proposition [sk-prop-tf-trace-specialization], write $F=\Fix(c)$ for
the fixed scheme, and let $d=c_1|_F=c_2|_F:F\to X$ be the common map.
Since the two legs agree on $F$, the hypothesis
$I_2^n\subset I_1^{n+1}$ forces a connected component of $F$ that meets
$Z$ to lie in $Z$ set-theoretically, and the
deformed fixed scheme is constant up to nilpotents.
