In the notation of [def-trf-integration], the following holds. The composite is a morphism
$A\to A$ of $D(A)$, i.e. an element of
$\operatorname{End}_{D(A)}(A)\cong A$; its $A$-linearity is
[lem-trf-integration-linear].
