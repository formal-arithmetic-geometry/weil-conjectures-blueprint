Let $X_0$ be a separated scheme of finite type over $\mathbf F_q$, let
$X=X_0\times_{\mathbf F_q}\overline{\mathbf F}_q$, and let $\ell\nmid q$.
The points of $X_0$ over $\mathbf F_{q^m}$ are the fixed points of the
$m$-th power of the Frobenius $\Phi_X$ on $X$ (Lemma [sk-lem-tf-fixed-points]).
For a self-map $\varphi$ of a compact manifold with transverse fixed
points, the Lefschetz formula counts each fixed point $x$ with the sign
of $\det(1-d\varphi_x)$. The differential of Frobenius is zero, so every
sign should be $+1$. On a curve, let $u$ be a uniformizer at a fixed
point $y$ that comes from $X_0\otimes\mathbf F_{q^m}$, and $v$ the same
function on the second factor. Near $(y,y)$ the graph of $\Phi_X^m$ is
$v=u^{q^m}$, and it meets the diagonal $v=u$ with multiplicity one,
since $u^{q^m}-u$ has a simple zero at $u=0$.
