Theorem [sk-thm-tf-sp-zero-section] asserts that $\tau_{X,Z}$ is an
isomorphism. To prove this, we consider the cofibre

$$
C_{X,Z}(L)=\operatorname{cofib}\bigl(
       \tau_{X,Z}(L)\bigr),
$$

computed in an enhancement, so that $C_{X,Z}$ is an exact functor.
The proof that $C_{X,Z}=0$ has three steps.  Using the base change maps of
the next lemma, we reduce to the case $i^*L=0$ and, after a blow-up, to
the case of an effective Cartier divisor.  Along a principal divisor in
a normal scheme, an explicit contraction of the affine line shows that
$C_{X,Z}(L)$ vanishes at the generic points of $Z$.  Finally, we remove
the remaining support using an embedding into affine space and an
induction on its dimension, by projection along a line.
