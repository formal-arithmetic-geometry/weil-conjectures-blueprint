The case of the affine line in the proof of
[sk-thm-tf-coefficient-morphism-formula] applies
[sk-p-frob] to $j_!K$ on $\mathbf P^1$,
which is not lisse at infinity, and to an arbitrary finite
$\ell$-primary ring $A$, regular or not. This is possible because the supported
restriction of [sk-lem-tf-curve-restriction-zero] is zero as a
morphism in $D(A)$, before any trace is taken. In
[sk-lem-tf-transverse-boundary], forgetting the $A$-action serves
only to detect a zero object; the vanishing of morphisms comes from
the generization square there and from the prime-to-$\ell$ trace
splitting.
