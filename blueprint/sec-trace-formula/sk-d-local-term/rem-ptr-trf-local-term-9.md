In the notation of [def-trf-local-term], the identification of the
local term with the element of $A$ corresponding to
$\operatorname{Tr}_c(u)|_\beta$ in the point case $\beta\simeq\Spec k$ is [lem-trf-local-term-point]. The restricted pairing
$j_B^*\langle u,v\rangle_{\mathrm{LV}}$ and the local term
$\operatorname{LT}_B(u,v)$ of an opposite pair are
[def-trf-local-term-pair].
