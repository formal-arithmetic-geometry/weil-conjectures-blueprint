title = "Finite Tor amplitude gives perfectness at the point"
order = 3472

Let $A$ be a finite commutative ring and let $C$ be a bounded complex of
$A$-modules with finitely generated cohomology modules and finite Tor
amplitude. Then $C$ is a perfect $A$-complex. Consequently, in the setting of
[det-compact-trace-point-objects], $R\Gamma_c(X,K)$ and every geometric stalk
$K_{\bar x}$ are perfect $A$-complexes.
