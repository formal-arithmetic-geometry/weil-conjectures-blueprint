Let $X$ be a separated scheme of finite type over $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_q$
and let $A$ be a finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring, with
$\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$.
If $K\in\Dctf(X,A)$, then $R\Gamma_c(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},K)$ and every
geometric stalk $K_{\bar x}$ are perfect $A$-complexes, so the traces of their
endomorphisms are defined.
