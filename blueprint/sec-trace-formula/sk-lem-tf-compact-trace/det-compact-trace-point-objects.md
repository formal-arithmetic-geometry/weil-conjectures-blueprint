Let $k$ be algebraically closed, $s=\operatorname{Spec}k$, let $X$ be a
separated scheme of finite type over $k$ with structure morphism
$a_X:X\to s$, let $A$ be a finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring with
$\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$, and let $K\in\Dctf(X,A)$. Then
$R\Gamma_c(X,K)=Ra_{X!}K$ lies in $\Dctf(s,A)$. For every geometric point
$\bar x$ of $X$, the stalk $K_{\bar x}$ is a bounded complex of $A$-modules
with finitely generated cohomology modules and finite Tor amplitude.
