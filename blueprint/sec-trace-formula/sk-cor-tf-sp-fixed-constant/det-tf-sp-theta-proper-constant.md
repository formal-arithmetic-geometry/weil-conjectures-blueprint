Let $S$ be the strict henselization of $\operatorname{Spec}k[t]_{(t)}$, $k$ algebraically
closed, $A$ a finite commutative ring annihilated by a power of a prime $\ell$ invertible
in $k$, and let $B$ be a proper $k$-scheme with structure map $a$. For the constant family
$B\times S$ write $S_B=R\Psi_{B\times S/S}$,
$\sigma_B(L):L\to S_B(L_\eta)$ for the specialization unit and
$\theta_B:S_B\omega_{B_\eta}\to\omega_B$ for the dualizing map, the exceptional adjoint of
$Ra_!S_B\omega_{B_\eta}\xrightarrow{\nu_a}S_{\operatorname{Spec}k}Ra_{\eta!}\omega_{B_\eta}\xrightarrow{\varepsilon}A$.
Then $\theta_B\circ\sigma_B(\omega_B)=\operatorname{id}_{\omega_B}$, and for every dualizing
section $z:A_{B_\eta}\to\omega_{B_\eta}$, identified with a section of $\omega_B$,

$$
 \theta_BS_B(z)\sigma_B(A)=z.
$$
