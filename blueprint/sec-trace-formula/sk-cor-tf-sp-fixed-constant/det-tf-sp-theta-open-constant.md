title = "The dualizing map of a constant family"
uses = ["det-tf-sp-theta-proper-constant"]

In the setting of [det-tf-sp-theta-proper-constant], let $B$ be separated and of finite type
over $k$, not necessarily proper. Then $\theta_B\sigma_B(\omega_B)=\operatorname{id}_{\omega_B}$
and $\theta_BS_B(z)\sigma_B(A)=z$ for every dualizing section $z$. The same holds for a
family $F$ over $S$ with $F_{\mathrm{red}}=B\times S$, under the nilpotent-invariance
identifications.
