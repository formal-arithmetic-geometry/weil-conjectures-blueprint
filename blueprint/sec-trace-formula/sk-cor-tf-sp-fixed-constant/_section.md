Here $c:C\to X\times_SX$ is a correspondence over the trait $S$ with fixed scheme $F$, and
$\operatorname{sp}_F(z)=\theta_FS_F(z)\sigma_F$ is the specialization of dualizing
sections of [sk-prop-tf-trace-specialization].

If $F_{\mathrm{red}}=B\times S$, with $B$ separated
and of finite type over $k$, then the specialization $\operatorname{sp}_F$ of
Proposition [sk-prop-tf-trace-specialization] is the identity under
the constant-family and nilpotent-invariance
identifications. In particular it is injective.
