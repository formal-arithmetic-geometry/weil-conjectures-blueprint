Surfaces are over the algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic
$p$; $\ell\ne p$ and $A$ is a finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring.
Let $T$ be the strict localization of a smooth surface at a closed
point $t$. Let $H$ be a regular curve through $t$, and $D,B$ reduced
closed curves through $t$, whose tangent cones satisfy

$$
 C_tD\cap C_tB=\{0\},\qquad C_tD\cap T_tH=\{0\}.
$$

Let $i:T-(D\cup H)\hookrightarrow T-D$ and let $E$ be a bounded
complex on $T-(D\cup H)$ with finite locally constant cohomology and
finite $\ell$-group monodromy. Then the restriction map

$$
 R\Gamma(T-D,i_!E)\longrightarrow
 R\Gamma(B-t,(i_!E)|_{B-t})
$$

is zero in $D(A)$.
