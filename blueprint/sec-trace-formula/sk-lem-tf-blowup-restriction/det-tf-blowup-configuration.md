title = "The blown-up configuration is a transverse boundary"
order = 3525
+++
Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field, let $T$ be the strict
localization of a smooth $k$-surface at a closed point $t$, let $H$ be a
regular curve through $t$ and $D$ a reduced closed curve through $t$
with $C_tD\cap T_tH=\{0\}$. Let $f:\widetilde T\to T$ be the blowup of
$t$, with exceptional divisor $P\simeq\mathbf P^1_k$, and let $D',H'$ be
the strict transforms. Put

$$
 Z=\widetilde T-D',\qquad Y=P-D',\qquad X=H'\cap Z.
$$

Then:

1. **(1)** $Z$ is an étale limit with affine transition maps of regular
smooth $k$-surfaces, and $X,Y$ are regular divisors on $Z$ meeting
transversely at exactly one point $z$, the point of $P$ given by the
tangent line $T_tH$; moreover $Y$ is connected.
1. **(2)** $f$ restricts to isomorphisms $Z-Y\simeq T-D$ and
$Z-(X\cup Y)\simeq T-(D\cup H)$, compatible with the open immersions
$i:T-(D\cup H)\hookrightarrow T-D$ and
$i:Z-(X\cup Y)\hookrightarrow Z-Y$.
1. **(3)** For a complex $E$ on $T-(D\cup H)$ and its pullback $E'$ to
$Z-(X\cup Y)$, one has $R\Gamma(T-D,i_!E)\simeq R\Gamma(Z-Y,i_!E')$, and
$E'$ has finite locally constant cohomology with finite $\ell$-group
monodromy whenever $E$ has.
