These notes prove the Weil conjectures, which concern the number of
solutions of polynomial equations over finite fields. Weil saw that two of them would follow from a cohomology theory for
varieties over finite fields with a Lefschetz fixed point formula and
Poincaré duality. The deepest one, an analogue of the Riemann
hypothesis, needs in addition a bound on the eigenvalues of Frobenius. We
construct such a theory, $\ell$-adic cohomology, and prove the first two
properties. Then we prove the bound, which is Deligne's theorem.
