These notes prove the [Weil conjectures](@theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes), which concern the number of
solutions of polynomial equations over finite fields. Weil saw that
[rationality](@corollary-point-counting-zeta-function) and the [functional equation](@corollary-functional-equation) would follow from a cohomology
theory for varieties over finite fields with a Lefschetz fixed point
formula and Poincaré duality, while the deepest conjecture, an analogue of the
Riemann hypothesis, needs in addition a bound on the eigenvalues of
Frobenius. Following this plan, we construct such a theory, $\ell$-adic cohomology, and prove the
[trace formula](@theorem-grothendieck-lefschetz-trace-formula) and [Poincaré duality](@theorem-poincare-duality) for it. Then we prove the bound, which
is the [Deligne–Laumon theorem](@theorem-weights-rf-shriek).
