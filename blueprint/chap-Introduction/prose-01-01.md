These notes prove the Weil conjectures, which concern the number of
solutions of polynomial equations over finite fields. Weil saw that
rationality and the functional equation would follow from a cohomology
theory for varieties over finite fields with a Lefschetz fixed point
formula and Poincaré duality, while the deepest conjecture, an analogue of the
Riemann hypothesis, needs in addition a bound on the eigenvalues of
Frobenius. Following this plan, we construct such a theory, $\ell$-adic cohomology, and prove the
trace formula and Poincaré duality for it. Then we prove the bound, which
is the Deligne–Laumon theorem.
