All operations on towers are defined levelwise, and $Rg_*$ is computed levelwise as well,
since an injective tower is levelwise a summand of finite products of injective sheaves.
Hence proper base change and the projection formula hold for towers, and $Rf_{!,\bullet}$
preserves constructible towers. Indeed, each level lies in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ by
Proposition [sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability], and the bounds are independent of $n$: for a
compactification $f=pj$, $Rf_!$ has amplitude $[0,\operatorname{cd}_\ell(p)]$ for every
torsion coefficient ring [Stacks, Tag 0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10). For Tor amplitude we use the projection formula
$(Rf_!C_n)\otimes^{\mathbf L}N\simeq Rf_!(C_n\otimes^{\mathbf L}N)$ for every
$\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$-module $N$, which follows from the comparison $b$; so if all $C_n$
have Tor amplitude in $[a,b]$, all $Rf_!C_n$ have Tor amplitude in
$[a,b+\operatorname{cd}_\ell(p)]$.
