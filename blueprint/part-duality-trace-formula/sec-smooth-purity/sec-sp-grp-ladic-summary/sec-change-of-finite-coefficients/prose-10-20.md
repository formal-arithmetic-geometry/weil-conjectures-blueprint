It follows that the isomorphisms $\gamma_{f,A}$ are compatible with change of coefficients: for
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$,

$$
c_{A,B}(K)\circ(\gamma_{f,A}(K))_B=\gamma_{f,B}(K_B),
\tag{10.16}
$$

the sources being identified by $(f_A^*K(d)[2d])_B\simeq f_B^*(K_B)(d)[2d]$. Indeed,
$\gamma_{f,A}$ is the map (10.9) composed with the inverse of the
orientation. The map (10.9) is built from the projection formula and the counit, so it commutes with change of
coefficients by (10.15). The orientation is a map between invertible
sheaves placed in degree $-2d$, so its compatibility is local; for vector bundles it
follows from the construction via the projective line (Proposition [sk-prop-an]),
and on the deformation family from the uniqueness in Lemma [sk-thm-dnc] over the base
$B$ of the family. To apply it, note first that $c$ commutes with smooth exceptional base
change and with composition with étale maps: both sides are transposes of counits, and
they agree on charts by the proof of Lemma [sk-lem-sp-reduction]. Hence the extended
comparison is again a comparison of the kind in Lemma [sk-thm-dnc], and it is the
identity at $t=1$.
