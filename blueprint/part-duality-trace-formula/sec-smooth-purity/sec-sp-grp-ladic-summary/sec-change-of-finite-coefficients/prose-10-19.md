The levels $\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$ of a tower are related by extension of scalars, so the
first thing to know is how $Rf_!$ and $f^!$ behave under a map of finite rings $A\to B$.
For $Rf_!$ there is a natural comparison $b$, which is the change of coefficients of
Proposition [sk-prop-scalar-six]. For $f^!$ the comparison $c_{A,B}$ is defined as the
transpose of a counit, and it is an isomorphism because on a chart both functors are
computed by the trace of the projective line, which commutes with extension of
scalars. Lemma [sk-lem-sp-reduction] records this, and (10.16)
deduces that the maps $\gamma_{f,A}$ are compatible with $c_{A,B}$.

Let $A\to B$ be a map of finite commutative rings killed by a power of $\ell$.
