The fibres of $\widetilde s^{*}\omega_{\widetilde g}$ at $t=1$ and $t=0$
are the two sides of (10.12). In the proof we use coordinates to
show that this shifted sheaf of rank one is pulled back from $S$, étale
locally on $S$. Since the geometric fibres of $\mathbf A^1_S\to S$ are
connected, a comparison fixed at $t=1$ is unique, and therefore the local
comparisons glue. We need coordinates for the first step, because in
positive characteristic a locally constant sheaf on $\mathbf A^1_S$ need
not be pulled back from $S$. The sheaf $\widetilde s^{*}\omega_{\widetilde g}[-2d]$
is pulled back from $S$ because near the section the
deformation is étale over
$\mathbf A^d_{\mathbf A^1_S}=\mathbf A^d_S\times\mathbf A^1$, étale
locally on $S$, and the latter family is pulled back from $S$.
