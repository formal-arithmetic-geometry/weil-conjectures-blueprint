Let $\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^\bullet)$ be the full subcategory
of $D(\operatorname{Tow}(T,\mathcal O_E))$ (see [sk-def-sp-coefficient-towers])
of objects $C_\bullet$ such that each $C_n$ lies in
$\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^n)$, with cohomological and Tor
amplitude bounds independent of $n$. Such a tower is *normalized* if all its
reduction maps
$C_n\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,T}/\ell^n}\mathcal O_{E,T}/\ell^m\to C_m$
($m\leq n$) are isomorphisms. The full subcategory of normalized towers is
denoted $\Dctf(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\ell^\bullet)_{\mathrm{norm}}$.
