1. For $K\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$ there are integers $a\le b$ such that
   every reduction $K/\ell^n$ lies in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(T_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E/\ell^n)$,
   with cohomology and Tor amplitude in $[a,b]$.
2. If $K\in D(T_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$ is derived complete and $K/\ell$
   lies in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(T_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E/\ell)$, then $K\in\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)$.
