The local factors of the Euler product are indexed by closed points.
A closed point $x\in|X_0|$ of degree $d$ over $\mathbf F_q$
corresponds to a Frobenius orbit of $d$ geometric points, and its
Frobenius on the stalk is the Frobenius going once around this orbit.
