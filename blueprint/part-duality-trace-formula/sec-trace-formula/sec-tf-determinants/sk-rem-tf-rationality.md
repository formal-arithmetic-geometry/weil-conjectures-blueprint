The functional equation is an
identity of rational functions, not of power series at $T=0$.

What duality does not give is the absolute value of the eigenvalues
$\alpha$. In Sections [sec-weights-deligne-theorem]–[sec-zeta-weil] we prove
that the eigenvalues of $F_i$ have absolute value $q^{i/2}$ under every
embedding of $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ into $\mathbf C$. Then the factors
$\det(1-TF_i)$ for different $i$ have no common zeros, so they can be
read off from $Z(X_0,T)$ alone. In Section [sec-zeta-weil] we use
this to show that they are polynomials with integer coefficients that do
not depend on $\ell$.
