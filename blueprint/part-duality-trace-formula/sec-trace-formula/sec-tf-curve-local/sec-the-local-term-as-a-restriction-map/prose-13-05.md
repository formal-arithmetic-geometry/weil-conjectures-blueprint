By additivity, the local term of $\psi$ at $y$ is the trace on the
stalk plus the local term of the induced correspondence on $j_!j^*K$,
where $j$ is the inclusion of $X\setminus\{y\}$. It remains to show
that a local term vanishes when $K_{\bar y}=0$, and the definition
through the trace morphism of Lemma [sk-thm-sf-kunneth-verdier] gives no
direct hold on this. The next lemma replaces it by a description
familiar from topology. There the intersection number of two
submanifolds $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$ is computed by restricting the Thom
class of $\Gamma$, which is supported on $\Gamma$, to $\Delta$, where it
becomes a class supported on $\Gamma\cap\Delta$. Here $\psi$ defines a
class supported on the graph, the restriction $\rho$ to the diagonal
lands in cohomology supported at $y$, and the local term is the
evaluation of the result. Since $\rho$ does not depend on $\psi$, it
suffices to show that $\rho$ itself is zero when $K_{\bar y}=0$
(Lemma [sk-lem-tf-curve-restriction-zero]). This is why the vanishing holds
for every $\psi$, invertible or not.
