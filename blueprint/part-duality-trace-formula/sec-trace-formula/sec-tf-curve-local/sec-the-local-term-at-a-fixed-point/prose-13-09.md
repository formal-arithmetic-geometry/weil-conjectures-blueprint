We now assemble the pieces. The point $y$ is stable under $\Phi_X^m$,
so additivity of trace classes applies to the localization triangle
for $X\setminus\{y\}$ and $\{y\}$. The open piece has zero stalk at
$y$, so its local term vanishes by Lemma [sk-lem-tf-curve-restriction-zero],
and the closed piece is pushed forward from the point, where the local
term is a trace by Lemma [sk-p-pairing]. Nothing requires
$K$ to be lisse at $y$, and this is what allows the proof of
Theorem [sk-thm-tf-coefficient-morphism-formula] to apply the result to
$j_!K$ at the point at infinity of $\mathbf P^1$.
