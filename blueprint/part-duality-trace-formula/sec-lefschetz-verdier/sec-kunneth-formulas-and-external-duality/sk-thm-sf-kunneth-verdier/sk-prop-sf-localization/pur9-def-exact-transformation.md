Let $\mathscr T,\mathscr T'$ be triangulated categories and $(\Phi,s^\Phi),(\Psi,s^\Psi):\mathscr T\to\mathscr T'$ exact functors, with $s^\Phi:\Phi(-[1])\cong\Phi(-)[1]$ and $s^\Psi$ their commutation isomorphisms with the shift. A morphism of exact functors is a natural transformation $\alpha:\Phi\to\Psi$ with $s^\Psi_X\circ\alpha_{X[1]}=\alpha_X[1]\circ s^\Phi_X$ for all $X$.
