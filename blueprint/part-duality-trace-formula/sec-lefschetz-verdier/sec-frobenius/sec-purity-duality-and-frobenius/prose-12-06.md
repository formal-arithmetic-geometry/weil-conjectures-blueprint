For $X_0$ proper and geometrically connected and $K=\Lambda_X$,
Theorem [sk-cor-trace] shows that $F$ is the identity on
$H^0(X,\Lambda)=\Lambda$ and acts by $q^d$ on
$H^{2d}(X,\Lambda)\simeq\Lambda(-d)$.

In the trace formula of Section [sec-trace-formula], Frobenius is
again the geometric Frobenius, which acts on $\Lambda(-1)$ by $q$.
