The coherence condition is needed over a general ring: three
endomorphisms of a distinguished triangle whose squares commute only in
the triangulated category need not satisfy trace additivity
[Stacks, Tag 087J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/087J). Every localization sequence used below comes
functorially from the Frobenius correspondence and the filtration by an open
subscheme and its closed complement, so it lifts to the filtered derived category.
