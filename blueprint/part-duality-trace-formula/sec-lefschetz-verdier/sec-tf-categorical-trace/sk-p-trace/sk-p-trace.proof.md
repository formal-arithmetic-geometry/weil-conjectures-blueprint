On finite projective modules, evaluation and coevaluation are given by
a finite dual basis. On a complex, the symmetry interchanging a term
of degree $i$ and its dual of degree $-i$ contributes $(-1)^i$.
This proves the chain formula, including its invariance under chain
homotopy, since the supertrace of $dh+hd$ is zero by cyclicity and a shift of
index. Tensoring a dual basis with $B$ preserves evaluation and
coevaluation, which proves compatibility with extension of scalars for every ring map
$A\to B$. Linearity and invariance under conjugacy follow from the same
description. A coherent two-step filtration may be represented by a
filtered complex of projective modules whose filtration is split in each degree. The
endomorphism is then block triangular, so its supertrace is the sum of the
supertraces on the two graded pieces. Over a field the kernels and
images of the differential give invariant subspaces, and their
contributions cancel, leaving the cohomological formula.
