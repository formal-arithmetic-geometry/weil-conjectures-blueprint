We take $f=a_X$ in the first two Verdier exchange formulas of
Corollary [sk-thm-sf-verdier-exchange]. On $s$ the dualizing complex is
$\Lambda_s$, so $\mathbb D_s=\cHom_s(-,\Lambda_s)$. When $k$ is
algebraically closed, Lemma [sk-lem-finite] identifies
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(s,\Lambda)$ with $\operatorname{Perf}(\Lambda)$, and
$\mathbb D_s$ becomes $R\!\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(-,\Lambda)$. The two exchange
formulas then read (11.4) and
(11.5). The exchange formula (11.2) was constructed
in Lemma [sk-thm-sf-tensor-hom] from evaluation and the counit of
$Rf_!\dashv f^!$, so for $f=a_X$ it is the adjoint of the displayed
pairing. Over a finite base field we read the same exchange formulas in
$D(s_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, which gives the last assertion.
