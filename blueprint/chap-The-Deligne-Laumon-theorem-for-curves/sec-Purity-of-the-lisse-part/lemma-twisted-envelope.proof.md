As in the proof of Proposition [proposition-twisting-weight-zero-adding-dual], the local
eigenvalues of $\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal L$ have absolute value one
after applying $\iota$, and those of the second summand are their
inverses, hence their complex conjugates. The extension by zero is
real as well, because its stalks outside the open are zero, and so its
local characteristic polynomials there are equal to $1$.
