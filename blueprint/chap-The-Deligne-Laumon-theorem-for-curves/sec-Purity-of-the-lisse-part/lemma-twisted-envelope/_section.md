Let $\mathcal A$ be lisse and pure of weight zero, and let $\mathcal L$
be lisse of rank one with local eigenvalues of finite order. Then

$$
(\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal L)
\oplus(\mathcal A^\vee\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})
$$

is real and pure of weight zero, and its extension by zero along an
open immersion is real as well.
