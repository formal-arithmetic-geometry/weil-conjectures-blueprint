At $0'$ the Fourier kernel is trivial, by
Lemma [lemma-artin-schreier-character-sheaf]. The stalk formula of
Proposition [proposition-base-change-fourier-inversion], together with
the shift in $(j_*M)[1]$, identifies the degree-zero cohomology of the
stalk of its transform with $H_c^2(\mathbf A^1,j_*M)$.
The assumed isomorphism identifies this transform with $(j'_*M')[1]$,
whose degree-zero cohomology sheaf is zero. Its stalk at $0'$ is
therefore zero, and the stalk formula gives the required vanishing.
