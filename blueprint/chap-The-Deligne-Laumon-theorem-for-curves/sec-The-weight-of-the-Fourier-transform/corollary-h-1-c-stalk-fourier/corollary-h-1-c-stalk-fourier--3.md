Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell$ be a prime different from
$\operatorname{char}(k_0)$, and fix
$\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$ and a
nontrivial additive character
$\psi:k_0\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$.
Let $M$ be a nonzero irreducible lisse Weil
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf on a dense open
$j:U_0\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1_{k_0}$, punctually $\iota$-pure of
weight $w\in\mathbf R$. Suppose that no geometric irreducible
constituent of $M$ is the restriction of $\mathcal L_{\psi(ax)}$ for
any $a\in\bar k_0$, including $a=0$.
Then $H_c^1(\mathbf A^1,j_*M)$ has $\iota$-weights at most $w+1$,
relative to $q=\#k_0$.
