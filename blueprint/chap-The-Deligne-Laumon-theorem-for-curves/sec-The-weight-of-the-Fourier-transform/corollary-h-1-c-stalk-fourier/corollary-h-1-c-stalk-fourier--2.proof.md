The trivialization of the Fourier kernel at $0'$ and
Proposition [proposition-base-change-fourier-inversion] give
$$
\bigl(\operatorname{FT}_\psi((j_*M)[1])\bigr)_{0'}
\simeq R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,j_*M)[2].
$$
Thus its degree $-1$ cohomology is $H_c^1(\mathbf A^1,j_*M)$.
Apply the degree $-1$ cohomology-sheaf functor to the assumed
Fourier-partner isomorphism and then take the stalk at $0'$.
Since $\mathcal H^{-1}((j'_*M')[1])=j'_*M'$, composing these
identifications gives the asserted isomorphism. Each identification
commutes with Frobenius.
