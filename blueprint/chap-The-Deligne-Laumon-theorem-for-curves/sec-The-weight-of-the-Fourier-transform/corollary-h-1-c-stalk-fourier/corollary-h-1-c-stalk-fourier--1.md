Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell$ be a prime different from
$\operatorname{char}(k_0)$, and let
$\psi:k_0\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$ be an additive character.
Let $M$ and $M'$ be lisse Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaves on
dense opens $j:U_0\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1_{k_0}$ and
$j':U'_0\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^{1\vee}_{k_0}$, and suppose that
$$
\operatorname{FT}_\psi((j_*M)[1])\simeq(j'_*M')[1].
$$
Then $H_c^2(\mathbf A^1,j_*M)=0$.
