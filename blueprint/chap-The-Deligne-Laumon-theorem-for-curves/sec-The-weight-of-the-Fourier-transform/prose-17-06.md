In the example of the section opening, $M$ is the Kummer sheaf of the
quadratic character over $\mathbf F_3$, of weight $0$, and the trace of
$M'$ at $y\in\mathbf F_3^\times$ is $-\chi(y)g$ with $g^2=-3$. Its
absolute value is $\sqrt3=3^{1/2}$, which is weight $1=w+1$, as the
theorem predicts.

The weight of $M'$ bounds $H^1_c$, because the stalk of the transform
at the origin of the dual line is the compactly supported cohomology of
$j_*M$.
