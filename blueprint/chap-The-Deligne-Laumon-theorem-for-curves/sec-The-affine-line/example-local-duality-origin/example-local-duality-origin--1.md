Let $j:\mathbf G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$ over $k_0$, let $F=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$,
which is pure of weight $0$, and let $s=0$. Inertia at the origin acts
trivially on the constant sheaf, so
$(j_*\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)_{\bar 0}=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ by Lemma [lemma-boundary-stalk], with
Frobenius acting by $1$, of weight $0$. The Kummer covers
$t\mapsto t^{\ell^n}$ of a punctured neighbourhood of the origin give
$(R^1j_*\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)_{\bar 0}=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(-1)$
(Section [chap-Constructibility-of-f]), on which geometric
Frobenius acts by $q$, of weight $2$. The pairing of
Lemma [lemma-local-duality-point-c-u] is the multiplication
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\otimes\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(-1)\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(-1)$. So in this case the two stalks have
weights $0$ and $2$. The gap of two comes entirely from the Tate
twist. With coefficients $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(1)$, $H^1$ of a punctured disc is spanned
by the Kummer class of the coordinate, which Frobenius fixes. With
coefficients $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(1)\otimes\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(-1)$, Frobenius therefore acts
by $q$.
