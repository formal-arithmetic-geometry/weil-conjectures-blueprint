For $F$ [pure](@definition-weights) [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) of weight $w$, the pairing shows that
$(R^1j_*F)_{\bar s}$ has weights at least $w+2$. Indeed, it puts this
stalk in duality with a stalk of $j_*F^\vee$, which has [weights at most](@definition-weights)
$-w$, and the pairing takes values in [$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(-1)$](@definition-tate-twists), which has weight $2$.
The next proposition uses this gap between the source and the target
of the connecting map.
