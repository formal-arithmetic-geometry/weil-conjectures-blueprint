If $F$ is a pure lisse sheaf of weight $w$ on $C_0$, then the sheaf
$K=\pi_*j_!F$, where $j:C_0\hookrightarrow C'_0$, is lisse and
pure of weight $w$ outside the branch locus and the image of
$C'_0\setminus C_0$.
