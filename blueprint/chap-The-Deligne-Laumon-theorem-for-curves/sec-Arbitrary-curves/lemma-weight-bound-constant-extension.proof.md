An extension of degree $m$ of the constant field does not change the
geometric scheme $X$, the sheaf on it, or its cohomology, and it
replaces the Frobenius of $k_0$ by its $m$th power. So the eigenvalues
$\beta$ are the $\alpha^m$, and $|\iota\alpha^m|\le q^{mu/2}$
holds if and only if $|\iota\alpha|\le q^{u/2}$.
