Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow C_0$ be a dense open in a smooth curve over $k_0$,
and let $F$ be a pure lisse Weil sheaf of weight $w$ on $U_0$. Then for every $r\in\mathbf Z$ the group $H_c^r(C,j_*F)$ has weights
at most $w+r$.
