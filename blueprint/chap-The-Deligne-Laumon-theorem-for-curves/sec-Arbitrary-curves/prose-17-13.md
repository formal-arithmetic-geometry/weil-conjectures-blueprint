By the lemma, for any open of the projective line and any [pure](@definition-weights) [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves)
sheaf on it, we may bound $H^1_c$ with
Theorem [theorem-weight-bound-affine-line]. To reach an arbitrary
affine curve we map it to the line, and we have to choose the map so
that the direct image is again [pure](@definition-weights) and [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) on a dense open.
