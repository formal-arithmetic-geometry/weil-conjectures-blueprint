Let $W_0$ be a dense open of $\mathbf P^1_{k_0}$, and let $F$ be a
[lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) [Weil sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) on $W_0$, [pure](@definition-weights) of weight $w$. There are a finite
extension $k_m$ of $k_0$, a dense open $V_0$ of $\mathbf A^1_{k_m}$, a
[lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) [Weil sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) $G$ on $V_0$, [pure](@definition-weights) of weight $w$ and unramified at
infinity, and a surjection

$$
H_c^1(V,G)\twoheadrightarrow H_c^1(W,F)
$$

that commutes with the [geometric Frobenius](@definition-frobenius-operator) of $k_m$.
