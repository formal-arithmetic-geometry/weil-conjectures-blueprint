title = "Moving a lisse point to infinity"

Let $W_0$ be a dense open of $\mathbf P^1_{k_0}$, and let $F$ be a
lisse Weil sheaf on $W_0$, pure of weight $w$. There are a finite
extension $k_m$ of $k_0$, a dense open $V_0$ of $\mathbf A^1_{k_m}$, a
lisse Weil sheaf $G$ on $V_0$, pure of weight $w$ and unramified at
infinity, and a surjection

$$
H_c^1(V,G)\twoheadrightarrow H_c^1(W,F)
$$

that commutes with the geometric Frobenius of $k_m$.
