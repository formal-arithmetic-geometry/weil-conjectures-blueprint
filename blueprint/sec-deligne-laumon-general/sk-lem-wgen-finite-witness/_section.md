Let $\Pi$ be profinite and let $\rho:\Pi\to\operatorname{GL}_E(V)$
be continuous and absolutely irreducible, with $\dim_E V=n$.
There is a stable lattice $L$ and an integer $N$ such that every
subgroup with the same image as $\Pi$ on $L/\pi_E^NL$ acts
absolutely irreducibly on $V$.
