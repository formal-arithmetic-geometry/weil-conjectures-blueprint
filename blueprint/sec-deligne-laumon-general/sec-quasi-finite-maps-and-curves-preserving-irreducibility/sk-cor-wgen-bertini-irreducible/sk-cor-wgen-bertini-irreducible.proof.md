Choose a finite extension $E$ of $\Ql$ over which all the sheaves are
defined, and apply Lemma [sk-lem-wgen-finite-witness] to their geometric
representations. Let $T\to X$ be the connected Galois cover
corresponding to the intersection of the kernels of the resulting
finite quotients of $\pi_1(X)$, and let $Q$ be its group. In dimension
one, we take $C=X$. Otherwise $\dim X\ge2$, and we apply
Lemma [sk-lem-wgen-bertini-cover] to $T$ and the given points, adding one
point if there are none, since the lemma needs $S$ nonempty. Since
$T\times_XC$ is connected, $\pi_1(C)$ acts transitively on the fibre
of the $Q$-torsor $T\times_XC\to C$, and therefore its image in $Q$ is
all of $Q$. Now
Lemma [sk-lem-wgen-finite-witness] shows that the restrictions remain
geometrically irreducible.
