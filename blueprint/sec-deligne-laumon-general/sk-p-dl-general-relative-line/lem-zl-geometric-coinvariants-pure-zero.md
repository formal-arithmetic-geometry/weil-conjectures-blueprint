Let $C_0$ be a nonempty smooth geometrically connected curve over
$\mathbf F_q$, and let $\mathcal E$ be a lisse Weil sheaf which is
punctually $\iota$-pure of weight zero.
For its geometric fundamental
representation $V$, the coinvariant quotient
$W=V_{\pi_1(C)}$, with its induced Frobenius, is
$\iota$-pure of weight zero.

## Proof

This is the case $w=0$ of [lem-a1-geometric-coinvariants-pure], applied
to the nonempty smooth geometrically connected curve $C_0$ and the lisse
sheaf $\mathcal E$: the coinvariant quotient $W=V_{\pi_1(C)}$ with its
induced Frobenius is $\iota$-pure of weight zero in the sense of
[def-weil-frobenius-module-weights], that is, all its Frobenius
eigenvalues have $\iota$-modulus one.
