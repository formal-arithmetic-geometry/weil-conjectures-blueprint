Let $A(T),B(T)\in\mathbf C[T]$ be nonzero, and let $R<S$ be real
numbers. If every root $z$ of $A$ satisfies $|z|^{-1}\le R$ and every
root $z$ of $B$ satisfies $|z|^{-1}=S$ (for a root $z=0$, read
$|z|^{-1}$ as $0$), then $A$ and $B$ are coprime.
