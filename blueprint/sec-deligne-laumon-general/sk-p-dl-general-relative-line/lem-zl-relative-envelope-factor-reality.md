Let $h:U_0\to Y_0$ be as in [lem-zl-relative-curve-punctual], and
let $\mathcal E$ be lisse, $\iota$-real, and punctually $\iota$-pure
of weight zero. Then every $R^ih_!\mathcal E$ is $\iota$-real.
