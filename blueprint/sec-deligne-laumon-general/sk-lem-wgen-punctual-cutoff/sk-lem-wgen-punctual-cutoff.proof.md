Take a finite filtration with pure quotients. Each nonzero quotient
has a nonzero stalk at some closed point $y$ of its constructible
support, and since taking stalks is exact, its eigenvalues there are
eigenvalues of $G$. If its weight is $u$, then
$q_y^{u/2}\leq q_y^{v/2}$, so $u\leq v$. Hence the same filtration
shows that $G$ is mixed of weights at most $v$.
