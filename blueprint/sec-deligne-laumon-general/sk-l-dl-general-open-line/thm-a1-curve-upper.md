Let $C_0$ be a smooth geometrically connected curve and let
$\mathcal F$ be a lisse Weil sheaf that is punctually $\iota$-pure of
weight $w$. Then every $H_c^r(C,\mathcal F)$ has weights at most
$w+r$.
