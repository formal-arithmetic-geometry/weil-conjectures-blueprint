Take a composition series for the Weil representation. By
[sk-i-restriction], applied to the normal geometric
subgroup of the Weil group, the geometric restriction of each
irreducible factor is semisimple. Frobenius permutes the finitely
many geometric isotypic components of the factor, and we choose a
power $F^m$ fixing all of them. On a component $S\otimes M$, geometric
monodromy acts irreducibly on $S$ and trivially on $M$. Since $F^m$
fixes the isomorphism type of $S$, there is an intertwiner $A$ on
$S$, and by Schur's lemma the action of $F^m$ on the component has the
form $A\otimes B$ with $B$ invertible on $M$. Triangularizing $B$, we
obtain a stable flag whose geometric factors are $S$.
Take a common $m$ for the finitely many factors. The subgroup generated
by geometric monodromy and $F^m$ is the Weil group after the
constant extension of degree $m$. Choose a finite extension of the
coefficient field containing bases for these flag subspaces. Their
stability descends from $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, since the
extension of scalars is faithful, and so the flag defines lisse Weil
subsheaves and quotients.
