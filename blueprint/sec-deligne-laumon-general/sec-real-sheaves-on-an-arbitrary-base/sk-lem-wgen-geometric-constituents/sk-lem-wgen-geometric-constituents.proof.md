An irreducible Weil representation is geometrically semisimple, and a
power of Frobenius fixes each isotypic component, on which it can be
triangularized. In detail, take a composition series for the Weil representation. By
Lemma [sk-i-restriction], applied to the geometric
fundamental group, which is normal in the Weil group, the geometric
restriction of each irreducible factor is semisimple. Frobenius
permutes the finitely many geometric isotypic components of the
factor, and we choose a lift $s$ of geometric Frobenius to the
Weil group and a power $s^m$ fixing all of them. On a component
$S\otimes M$, geometric monodromy acts irreducibly on $S$ and
trivially on $M$. Since $s^m$ fixes the isomorphism type of $S$, there
is an isomorphism $u$ from $S$ to its conjugate by $s^m$, and by
Schur's lemma the action of $s^m$ on the component has the form
$u\otimes v$ with $v$ invertible on $M$.
Triangularizing $v$, we obtain a stable flag whose geometric factors
are $S$.

We take a common $m$ for the finitely many factors. The subgroup
generated by geometric monodromy and $s^m$ is the Weil group after the
constant extension of degree $m$. Choose a finite extension $E'$ of
the coefficient field over which these flags are defined. A subspace
defined over $E'$ is stable under this group if and only if its
extension to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ is, so the flags define lisse Weil subsheaves,
and refining the composition series by them gives the required
filtration.
