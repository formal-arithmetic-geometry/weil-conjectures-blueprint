Choose a finite extension $E$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ over which all the
sheaves are defined, and apply [sk-lem-wgen-finite-witness] to their
geometric representations. Let $T\to X$ be the connected Galois cover
corresponding to the intersection of the kernels of the resulting
finite quotients of $\pi_1(X)$, and let $Q$ be its group. In dimension
one, take $C=X$. Otherwise, add one closed geometric point if
necessary and apply [sk-lem-wgen-bertini-cover]. Since
$T\times_XC$ is connected, the image of $\pi_1(C)$ acts transitively
on this geometric $Q$-torsor, and therefore surjects onto $Q$. By
[sk-lem-wgen-finite-witness], the restrictions remain
geometrically irreducible.
