We now prove the weight bound for a family of affine curves. For the
direct image sheaves, Theorem [sk-thm-wft-curve-middle-upper] bounds the
eigenvalues at each stalk, and Theorem [sk-i-dl-general-real] will give
a filtration with pure quotients. The following lemma shows that these
two facts together give a weight bound.

Neither fact suffices alone; together they work because each pure
quotient has a nonzero stalk somewhere, and its eigenvalues there are
among those of the whole sheaf. The real
input of Theorem [sk-p-dl-general-relative-line] is therefore that the
sheaves $R^ih_!G$ are real, for the real sheaf $G$ built from $F$ as in
Proposition [sk-prop-wrp-real-envelope]. Over a single point $y$ of the
base this is a statement about the fibre $U_y$: its $L$-function is
real, and since $H^1_c$ and $H^2_c$ of an affine curve have no common
eigenvalues by the curve case, each of the two characteristic
polynomials is real on its own. This is the use of
Theorem [sk-thm-wft-curve-middle-upper], mentioned in the section opening, that
separates $H^1_c$ from $H^2_c$.
