Let $f_0:X_0\to Y_0$ be a separated finite-type morphism of
$k_0$-schemes, with $Y_0$ quasi-separated of finite type over $k_0$. Let
$j_0:U_0\hookrightarrow X_0$ be a quasi-compact open immersion and
$i_0:Z_0\hookrightarrow X_0$ a closed immersion whose image is the
complement of the image of $j_0$. If the direct-image assertion holds for
$f_0j_0$ and $f_0i_0$, it holds for $f_0$.
