For composable separated finite-type morphisms
$X_0\xrightarrow fY_0\xrightarrow gZ_0$ of separated finite-type
$k_0$-schemes, the exceptional direct images on
$\Dbc(-,\Qlb)\subset D(-_{\mathrm{proét}},\Qlb)$ ([sk-def-sfb-bounded-constructible])
satisfy a functorial isomorphism $R(gf)_!\simeq Rg_!Rf_!$ compatible with
Frobenius, so that it carries Weil structures ([sk-def-sf-weil-complexes])
on the source to the corresponding ones on the target.
