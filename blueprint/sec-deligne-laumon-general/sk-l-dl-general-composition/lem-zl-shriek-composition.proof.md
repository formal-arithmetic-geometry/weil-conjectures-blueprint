This is the composition constraint in the six-operation formalism,
item (3) of [prop-functor-package] of the framework chapter, i.e. the
composition isomorphism for $Rf_!$ on $\Dbc(-,\Qlb)$ of
[sk-thm-sf-comp-composition]. The schemes and morphisms are defined over
$k_0$, so the composition isomorphism for $X\to Y\to Z$ commutes with the
base change isomorphisms along the Frobenius automorphisms; this is the
compatibility with Frobenius.
