title = "Curves keeping lisse sheaves irreducible"

A curve $C$ in $X$ keeps a lisse sheaf geometrically irreducible as soon
as the image of $\pi_1(C)$ is large enough. The next lemma shows that
it suffices for this image to surject onto one finite quotient of
$\pi_1(X)$, and Lemma [sk-lem-wgen-bertini-cover] produces curves with
this property.
