Let $h_0:X_0\to Z_0$ and $g_0:Z_0\to Y_0$ be separated finite-type
morphisms of $k_0$-schemes, where $Z_0$ and $Y_0$ are quasi-separated of
finite type over $k_0$. If the direct-image weight assertion (every
constructible Weil sheaf mixed of weights at most $w$ has its $R^r(-)_!$
mixed of weights at most $w+r$, for all $w$ and $r$) holds for $h_0$ and
for $g_0$, it holds for $g_0h_0$.
