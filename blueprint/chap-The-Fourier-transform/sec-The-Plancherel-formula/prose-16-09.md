For the constant sheaf $E$ on the line and $n=1$, formula
(16.3) gives the trace $-\sum_x\psi(xy)$, which is $-q$ at
$y=0$ and $0$ elsewhere, in agreement with
$\operatorname{FT}_\psi(E)=\delta_0(-1)[-1]$. Both sides of Plancherel
are then $q^2$, and over $\mathbf F_{q^n}$ they are $q^{2n}$. This gives
$\nu(E)=0$ and $\nu(\operatorname{FT}_\psi E)=1$, as the corollary
predicts.
