*Relative form used in Lean.* For the first two details, let $k_0=\Fq$,
let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k_0$ be prime, and take coefficients in
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. Let $X$ be a quasi-separated scheme of finite
type over $k_0$ and let $\pi:Y\to X$, together with
$s:Y\to\mathbf A^1_{k_0}$, identify $Y$ with
$X\times_{k_0}\mathbf A^1$. Write $s$ also for the fibre coordinate.
The complexes below are Weil complexes in the sense of
[definition-weil-structure], and $R\pi_!$ denotes their compactly
supported direct image. These relative statements are the inputs used
by Lean in the calculation of the Fourier kernel.
