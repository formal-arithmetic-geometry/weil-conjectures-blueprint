$$
\sum_{x\in\mathbf F_3}\omega^{x^2}=1+2\omega.
$$

and $(1+2\omega)^2=-3$.
The sheaf $h^*\mathcal L_\psi$ is lisse of rank one. It is geometrically
nontrivial, since the cover $u^3-u=x^2$ is geometrically irreducible.
