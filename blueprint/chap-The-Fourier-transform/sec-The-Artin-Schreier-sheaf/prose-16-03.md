Over $\mathbf F_p$ with $a=1$, the fibre of the cover over $x=1$ is
$u^p-u=1$, which has no root in $\mathbf F_p$. Frobenius acts on the
$p$ roots by $u\mapsto u^p=u+1$, a translation of order $p$. The
trace of $\mathcal L_\psi$ at $x=1$ is therefore $\psi(1)=\psi_0(1)\ne1$.
Over $x=0$ the roots are the elements of $\mathbf F_p$. They are all
fixed, and the trace is $1$.
