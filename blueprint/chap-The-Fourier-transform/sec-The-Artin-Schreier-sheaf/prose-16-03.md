We check the lemma by hand at two points. Over $\mathbf F_p$ with
$a=1$, the fibre of the cover over $x=1$ is $u^p-u=1$, which has no root in
$\mathbf F_p$. Indeed, Frobenius acts on the
$p$ roots by $u\mapsto u^p=u+1$, a translation of order $p$, and the
trace of $\mathcal L_\psi$ at $x=1$ is therefore $\psi(1)=\psi_0(1)\ne1$.
Over $x=0$, by contrast, the roots are the elements of $\mathbf F_p$, so
they are all fixed and the trace is $1$.
