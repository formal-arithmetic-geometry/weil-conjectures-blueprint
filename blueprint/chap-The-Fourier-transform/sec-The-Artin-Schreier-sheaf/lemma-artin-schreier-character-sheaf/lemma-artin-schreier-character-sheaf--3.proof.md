On a fibre of the cover in clause [lemma-artin-schreier-character-sheaf--1], use the translation-invariant pairing $(f,g)\mapsto p^{-1}\sum_u f(u)g(u)$; $p$ is invertible in $E$. Distinct character spaces are orthogonal unless their characters multiply to $1$, and the pairing between the $\psi_0$ and $\psi_0^{-1}$ lines is perfect. The inverse-character line is exactly $\mathcal L_{\psi^{-1}}$, so this pairing induces the stated duality isomorphism. The factor $p^{-1}$ fixes its normalization at zero.
