On the simultaneous Artin–Schreier cover of the pair of functions
$(x,x)$, the sheaves $\mathcal L_{\psi(ax)}$ and
$\mathcal L_{\psi^{-1}(ax)}$ are isotypic components for inverse
characters of $k_0^2$. Their product is the trivial character.
Apply `AlgebraicGeometry.nonempty_iso_ihom_adicConstant_artinSchreierPlane`
to identify the inverse-character component with
$\mathcal H om(\mathcal L_{\psi(ax)},\Qbarl)$, then use the definition of
the dual of a lisse Weil sheaf.
