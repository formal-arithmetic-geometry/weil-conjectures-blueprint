For every additive character $\psi:k_0\to\Qbarl^\times$, every
$a\in k_0$, every $n\geq1$, and every $x\in\Fqn$, the trace of local
geometric Frobenius is

$$
t_{\mathcal L_{\psi(ax)},n}(x)
=\psi\bigl(\operatorname{Tr}_{\Fqn/k_0}(ax)\bigr).
$$

In particular, $a=1$ gives the asserted trace function of $\mathcal L_\psi$.
