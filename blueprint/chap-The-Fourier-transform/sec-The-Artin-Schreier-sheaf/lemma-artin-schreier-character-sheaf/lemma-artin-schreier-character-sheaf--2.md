For the addition map $m$, the specified sheaf has an isomorphism

$$
m^*\mathcal L_\psi\simeq\operatorname{pr}_1^*\mathcal L_\psi\otimes_E\operatorname{pr}_2^*\mathcal L_\psi.
$$
