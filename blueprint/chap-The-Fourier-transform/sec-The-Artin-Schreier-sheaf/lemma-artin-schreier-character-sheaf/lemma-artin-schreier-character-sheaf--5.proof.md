In the fibre over zero, the character eigenfunction taking value $1$ at the root $0$ takes values $\psi_0(c)$ at the other roots, and the inverse-character eigenfunction takes $\psi_0(c)^{-1}$. Their pointwise products are all $1$. The normalized pairing in clause [lemma-artin-schreier-character-sheaf--3] therefore evaluates to $p^{-1}\sum_{c\in\mathbf F_p}1=1$. This identifies the chosen inverse-character basis with the dual chosen basis, as required.
