Adding two roots of $u^p-u=ax$ and $v^p-v=ay$ gives a root of $(u+v)^p-(u+v)=a(x+y)$. This identifies the contracted product of the two $\mathbf F_p$-torsors with the torsor for $x+y$. Passing to their $\psi_0$-eigenspaces sends the tensor of the two character lines to the character line of the sum, giving the asserted multiplication isomorphism.
