Lean bypasses this normalization clause: the duality theorem asserts
the existence of an isomorphism and does not assert its compatibility
with a chosen trivialization at zero.

The additional clause in the human statement is that the dual-character
isomorphism respects the specified trivializations at zero. The
isomorphism of [lemma-artin-schreier-character-sheaf--3] suffices for the
Lean arguments with dual Artin–Schreier sheaves.
