The Frobenius scalars computed in clause [lemma-artin-schreier-character-sheaf--6] are values of the finite additive character, hence roots of unity. Every embedding sends a root of unity to a complex number of absolute value one. Since the sheaf has rank one, this is exactly punctual purity of weight zero.
