*Shared context.* Let $\psi:k_0\to E^\times$ be a nontrivial additive character. The existence statement and all following properties concern one and the same sheaf with its specified trivialization at zero, as in the original lemma. The properties are required simultaneously; no independent witnesses are introduced.
