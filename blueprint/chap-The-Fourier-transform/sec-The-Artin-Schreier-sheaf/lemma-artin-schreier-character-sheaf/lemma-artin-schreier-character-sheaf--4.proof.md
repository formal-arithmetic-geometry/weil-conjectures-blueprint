The chosen trivializations in clause [lemma-artin-schreier-character-sheaf--1] use the root $0$ in the fibre over zero. Under the torsor addition in clause [lemma-artin-schreier-character-sheaf--2], the pair $(0,0)$ maps to $0$. Thus the tensor of the two chosen basis vectors maps to the chosen basis vector, which is the required normalization.
