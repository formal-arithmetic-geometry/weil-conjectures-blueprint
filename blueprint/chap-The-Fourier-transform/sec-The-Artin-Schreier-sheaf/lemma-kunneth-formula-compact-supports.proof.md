We integrate first along the fibres of $p_1$, which are copies of the
fibres of $g$. Since $h=f\circ p_1$, we have $Rh_!=Rf_!Rp_{1!}$. The
square formed by $p_1$, $p_2$, $f$ and $g$ is cartesian, so proper base
change gives $Rp_{1!}p_2^*L\simeq f^*Rg_!L$. The two projection formulas
are isomorphisms, and hence so is the composite.
