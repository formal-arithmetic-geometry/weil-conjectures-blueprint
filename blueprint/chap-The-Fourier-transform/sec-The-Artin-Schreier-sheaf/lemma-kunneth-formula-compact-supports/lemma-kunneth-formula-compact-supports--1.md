In this situation, let $K,L$ be bounded constructible complexes with
coefficients in a field. The map

$$
Rh_!(p_1^*K\otimes p_2^*L)\simeq Rf_!(K\otimes Rp_{1!}p_2^*L)
$$

given by the projection formula for $p_1$ is an isomorphism.
