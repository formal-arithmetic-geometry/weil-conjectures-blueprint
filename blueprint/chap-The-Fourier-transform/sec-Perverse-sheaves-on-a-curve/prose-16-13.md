Unwinding the definition, a [perverse sheaf](@definition-perverse-sheaves-curve) on $C$ is a complex in
ordinary degrees $-1,0$ whose $\mathcal H^0$ is [punctual](@definition-punctual-sheaf) and whose
$\mathcal H^{-1}$ has no nonzero [punctual](@definition-punctual-sheaf) subsheaf.
