Frobenius pullback is an exact equivalence for the ordinary
t-structure, and it preserves [punctual](@definition-punctual-sheaf) support and the absence of
[punctual](@definition-punctual-sheaf) subsheaves. It is therefore t-exact for the [perverse
t-structure](@definition-perverse-sheaves-curve), which means that it carries the kernel and cokernel of a
compatible morphism of [perverse sheaves](@definition-perverse-sheaves-curve) to the kernel and cokernel of the
pulled-back morphism. The Weil isomorphisms and their inverses
therefore induce inverse isomorphisms on the kernel and cokernel, and by
naturality the coimage–image map is compatible with them. So
[perverse Weil sheaves](@definition-perverse-sheaves-curve) form an abelian category, with kernels and
cokernels computed in [perverse sheaves](@definition-perverse-sheaves-curve) on $C$, and this is the assertion
about the forgetful functor.
