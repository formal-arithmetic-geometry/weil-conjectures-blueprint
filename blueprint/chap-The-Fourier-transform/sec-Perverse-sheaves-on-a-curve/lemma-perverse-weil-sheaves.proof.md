Frobenius pullback is an exact equivalence for the ordinary
t-structure, and it preserves punctual support and the absence of
punctual subsheaves. It is therefore t-exact for the perverse
t-structure, which means that it carries the kernel and cokernel of a
compatible morphism of perverse sheaves to the kernel and cokernel of the
pulled-back morphism. The Weil isomorphisms and their inverses
therefore induce inverse isomorphisms on the kernel and cokernel, and by
naturality the coimage–image map is compatible with them. So
perverse Weil sheaves form an abelian category, with kernels and
cokernels computed in perverse sheaves on $C$, and this is the assertion
about the forgetful functor.
