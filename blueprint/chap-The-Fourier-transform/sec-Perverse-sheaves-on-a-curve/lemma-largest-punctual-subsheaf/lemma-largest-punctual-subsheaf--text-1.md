*Shared context.* In the human lemma, $C$ is a smooth curve over a
perfect field, $F$ is constructible, and $j:U\hookrightarrow C$ is a
dense open on which $F$ is lisse. The Lean statements below concern
Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaves on $\Aone_{k_0}$, with
$k_0=\Fq$ and $\ell\nmid q$; their hypotheses are stated explicitly.
