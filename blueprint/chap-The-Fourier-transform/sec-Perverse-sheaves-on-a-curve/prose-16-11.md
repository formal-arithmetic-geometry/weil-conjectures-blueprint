The truncation triangle of the perverse t-structure splits a sheaf into
a punctual part and a part without punctual subsheaves, so we first
construct the largest punctual subsheaf. In the following lemma
“sheaf” means a constructible sheaf with coefficients $E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
