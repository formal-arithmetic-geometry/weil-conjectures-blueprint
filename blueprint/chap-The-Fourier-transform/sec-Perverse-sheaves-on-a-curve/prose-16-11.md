The truncation triangle of the perverse t-structure splits a sheaf into
a [punctual](@definition-punctual-sheaf) part and a part without [punctual](@definition-punctual-sheaf) subsheaves, so we first
construct the largest [punctual](@definition-punctual-sheaf) subsheaf. In the following lemma
“sheaf” means a [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) sheaf with coefficients $E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
