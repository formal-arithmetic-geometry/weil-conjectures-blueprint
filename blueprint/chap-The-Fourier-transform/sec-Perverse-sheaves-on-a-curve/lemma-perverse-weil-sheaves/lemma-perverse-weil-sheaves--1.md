This assertion is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem.
The perverse Weil sheaves on $C_0$ form an abelian category.

Lean works with the full subcategory of curve-perverse Weil complexes.
The needed facts about monomorphisms are proved using distinguished
triangles and their long exact sequences of ordinary cohomology sheaves;
no abelian structure on this full subcategory is constructed.
