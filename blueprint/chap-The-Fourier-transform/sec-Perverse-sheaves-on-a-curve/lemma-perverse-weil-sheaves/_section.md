The [perverse Weil sheaves](@definition-perverse-sheaves-curve) on $C_0$ form an abelian category, and the
functor forgetting the [Weil structure](@definition-weil-structure), to [perverse sheaves](@definition-perverse-sheaves-curve) on $C$, is
exact and reflects exactness.
