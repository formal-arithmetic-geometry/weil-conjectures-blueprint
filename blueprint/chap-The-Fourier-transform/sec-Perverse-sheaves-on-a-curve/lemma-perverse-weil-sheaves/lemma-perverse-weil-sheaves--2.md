This assertion is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem.
The functor forgetting the Weil structure, to perverse sheaves on $C$, is
exact and reflects exactness.

Lean proves preservation and reflection of curve perversity directly for
the Fourier transform on Weil complexes. Fourier inversion then restricts
to an equivalence of the corresponding full subcategories, which preserves
simple objects without using exactness of a forgetful functor between
perverse hearts.
