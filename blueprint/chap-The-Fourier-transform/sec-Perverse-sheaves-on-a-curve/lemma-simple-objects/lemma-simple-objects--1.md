In the category of perverse sheaves on $C$, and in that of perverse
Weil sheaves on $C_0$,
the simple objects are the irreducible punctual objects and the objects
$(j_*M)[1]$ with $M$ nonzero irreducible lisse on a dense open, where
irreducibility is taken in the geometric or in the Weil category,
respectively.
