For (1), the formation of $V\times_SV^\vee$, of the pairing and of
$\mathcal K_\psi$ commutes with base change along $g$, and proper base
change for $Rp_{2!}$ gives the isomorphism. For (2), we have
$p_1^*\pi^*N=p_2^*\pi^{\vee*}N$, and the projection formula for $p_2$
gives the isomorphism. For (3), the fibre of $p_2$ over $\bar y$ is
$V_{\bar y}$, on which $\mathcal K_\psi$ restricts to
$\mathcal L_{\psi\langle x,y\rangle}$, and proper base change for
$Rp_{2!}$ along $\bar y\to V^\vee$ gives the formula.
