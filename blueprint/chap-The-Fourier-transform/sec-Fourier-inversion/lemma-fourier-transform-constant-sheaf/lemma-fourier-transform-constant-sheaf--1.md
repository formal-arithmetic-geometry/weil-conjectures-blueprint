$\delta_0$ be the constant skyscraper sheaf $E$ at the origin. There are
isomorphisms of Weil complexes

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{FT}_\psi(E)\simeq\delta_0(-1)[-1],
\end{aligned}
$$
