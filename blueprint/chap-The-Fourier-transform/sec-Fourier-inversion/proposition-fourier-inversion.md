Under $V^{\vee\vee}=V$ there are natural isomorphisms of functors
$\Dbc(V,E)\to\Dbc(V,E)$

$$
\operatorname{FT}_{\psi^{-1}}\operatorname{FT}_\psi(K)
\simeq K(-r),
\qquad
\operatorname{FT}_{\psi}\operatorname{FT}_\psi(K)
\simeq [-1]_V^*K(-r).
$$
