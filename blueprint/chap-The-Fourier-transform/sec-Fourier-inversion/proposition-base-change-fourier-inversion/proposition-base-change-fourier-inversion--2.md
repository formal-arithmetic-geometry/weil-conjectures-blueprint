isomorphism $\operatorname{FT}_\psi(K\otimes\pi^*N)\simeq
  \operatorname{FT}_\psi(K)\otimes\pi^{\vee*}N$, where $\pi:V\to S$ and
$\pi^\vee:V^\vee\to S$ are the projections.
