For a geometric point $\bar y$ of $V^\vee$, the proper base change
map gives

$$
(\operatorname{FT}_\psi K)_{\bar y}
\simeq
R\Gamma_c\bigl(V_{\bar y},
K|_{V_{\bar y}}\otimes
\mathcal L_{\psi\langle x,y\rangle}\bigr)[r].
\tag{16.2}
$$
