Work in the derived category of pro-étale Weil sheaves. The constant
twist commutes with $p_1^*$, with the derived internal Hom out of the
dual of the lisse kernel, with $Rp_{2!}$, and with the shift $[1]$.
These are the four operations defining $\operatorname{FT}_\psi$;
their Weil structures are described in [prose-10-20]. Composing these
isomorphisms gives
$$
\operatorname{FT}_\psi(T_bK)\simeq T_b\operatorname{FT}_\psi(K).
$$
Apply the derived Fourier functor to the given isomorphism
$K'\simeq T_bK$, and use the comparison of the Fourier functor on
Weil complexes with its functor on the ambient derived category.
