We show that the transform of a middle extension is again a middle
extension, unless the sheaf is built from linear characters.

The hypothesis of the next proposition excludes these sheaves. For them
the analogy with Gauss sums indeed fails: for $a\ne0$ the sheaf
$\mathcal L_{\psi(ax)}$ has no cohomology with compact support on the
line by Lemma [lemma-integral-linear-character], and its transform is
a skyscraper rather than a lisse sheaf of weight one. Excluding them
costs nothing in the application. A sheaf unramified at infinity has no
such constituent other than the constant one, and constant constituents
contribute nothing, because $H^1_c(\mathbf A^1,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)=0$. For the
remaining sheaves we obtain a lisse sheaf $M'$ on a dense open of the
dual line, which we study in Section [chap-Delignes-theorem-for-curves].

Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1_{k_0}$ be a dense open, and let
$M$ be an irreducible lisse Weil sheaf on $U_0$.
