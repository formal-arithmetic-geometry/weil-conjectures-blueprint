Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k_0$ be prime, and let
$\psi:k_0\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$ be a nontrivial additive
character. Work with bounded constructible Weil complexes on
$\mathbf A^1_{k_0}$. For every $n\in\mathbf Z$, both
$\operatorname{FT}_\psi$ and its quasi-inverse
$\operatorname{FT}_{\psi^{-1}}(-)(1)$ preserve ${}^pD^{\le n}$.
Here the upper class is the cohomological condition of
Definition [definition-perverse-sheaves-curve]: $\mathcal H^aK=0$ for
$a>n$, and $\mathcal H^nK$ has finite support.
