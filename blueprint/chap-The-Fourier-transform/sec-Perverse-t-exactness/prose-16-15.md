The transform $\operatorname{FT}_\psi$ is
t-exact for the [perverse t-structure](@definition-perverse-sheaves-curve) on the line
(Proposition [proposition-fourier-transform-perverse-heart]). By the [stalk formula](@proposition-base-change-fourier-inversion)
(16.2), this comes down to the [compactly
supported cohomology](@proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek) of sheaves on an affine curve in the extreme
degrees zero and two.
