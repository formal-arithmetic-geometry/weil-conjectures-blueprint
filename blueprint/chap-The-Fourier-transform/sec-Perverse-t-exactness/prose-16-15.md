The transform $\operatorname{FT}_\psi$ is
t-exact for the perverse t-structure on the line
(Proposition [proposition-fourier-transform-perverse-heart]). By the stalk formula
(16.2), this comes down to the compactly
supported cohomology of sheaves on an affine curve in the extreme
degrees zero and two.
