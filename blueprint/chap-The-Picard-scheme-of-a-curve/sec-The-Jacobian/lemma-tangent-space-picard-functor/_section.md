Let $X$ be a scheme over a field $k$ and $X_\varepsilon=X\times_k\operatorname{Spec}k[\varepsilon]$.
The kernel of the restriction map
$\operatorname{Pic}(X_\varepsilon)\to\operatorname{Pic}(X)$ is
$H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)$.
