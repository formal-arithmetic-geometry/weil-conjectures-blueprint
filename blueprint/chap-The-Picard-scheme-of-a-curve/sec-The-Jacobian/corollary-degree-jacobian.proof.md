The degree is additive under tensor product, so it is a homomorphism.
By Theorem [theorem-picard-scheme-jacobian-over-separably], its kernel
is $\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)=J(C)(k)$. Since $\deg\mathcal O_C(dP)=d$,
the map $d\mapsto\mathcal O_C(dP)$ is a section of the degree, which is
therefore surjective.
