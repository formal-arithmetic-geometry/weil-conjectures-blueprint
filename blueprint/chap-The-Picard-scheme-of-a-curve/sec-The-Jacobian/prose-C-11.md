We glue the translates of the chart of
Proposition [proposition-divisors-degree-g-open-chart] to the Picard
scheme, whose component of degree zero is the Jacobian. Gluing uses twice
that $k$ is separably closed: to find a rational point for the
rigidification, and to obtain the twists of
Lemma [lemma-twisting-h-0-1-h] over $k$. For an elliptic curve, the Abel map $C=\operatorname{Hilb}^{1}_{C/k}\to\operatorname{Pic}^1_{C/k}$ is
an isomorphism, and translation by $\mathcal O_C(-P)$ identifies $C$ with
its own Jacobian. In general, the tangent space of the Jacobian at the
origin is computed by deforming the trivial sheaf over the dual numbers.
