For an elliptic curve the Abel map $C=\operatorname{Hilb}^{1}_{C/k}\to\operatorname{Pic}^1_{C/k}$ is
an isomorphism. Then translation by $\mathcal O_C(-P)$ identifies $C$ with
its own Jacobian. The tangent space of the Jacobian at the origin is
computed by deforming the trivial sheaf over the dual numbers.

Gluing the charts uses twice that $k$ is separably closed: to find a
rational point for the rigidification, and to obtain the twists of
Lemma [lemma-twisting-h-0-1-h] over $k$.
