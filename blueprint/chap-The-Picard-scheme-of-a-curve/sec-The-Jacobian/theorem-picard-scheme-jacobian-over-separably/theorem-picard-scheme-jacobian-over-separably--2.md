Let $k$ and $C$ be as in
[theorem-picard-scheme-jacobian-over-separably--1]. For every
$d\in\mathbf Z$, any $k$-scheme representing
$\operatorname{Pic}^d_{C/k}$ is smooth of dimension $g$, proper and
geometrically integral over $k$. In particular, each degree component
is a smooth proper geometrically connected variety of dimension $g$.
