For the same representing variety $J_0$ and representation $r$,
$J_0\times_k\bar k$ represents $\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C/\bar k}$. The
representation is compatible with $r$: for a $\bar k$-scheme $T$ and a
class $y\in F(T)$, the point attached to its image in
$\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C/\bar k}(T)$ is the morphism
$T\to J_0\times_k\bar k$ corresponding to $r^{-1}(y):T\to J_0$.
