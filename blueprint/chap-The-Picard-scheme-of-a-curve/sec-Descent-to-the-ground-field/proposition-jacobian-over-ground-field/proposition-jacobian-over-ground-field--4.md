The representation $r$ induces a group isomorphism

$$
e:\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }J_0(\bar k).
$$

It sends an invertible sheaf of degree zero on $C$ to the point which
$r$ attaches to its class in $F(\operatorname{Spec}\bar k)$, using
$C\simeq C_0\times_k\bar k$.
