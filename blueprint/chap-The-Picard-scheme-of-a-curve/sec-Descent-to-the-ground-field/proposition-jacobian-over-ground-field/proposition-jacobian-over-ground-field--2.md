Let $X/k$ be proper and locally of finite presentation, let $k'/k$ be
a field extension, and put $X'=X\times_kk'$. For every integer $d$,
the restriction of the degree-$d$ Picard fppf sheaf
$\operatorname{Pic}^d_{X/k}$ to $k'$-schemes is canonically isomorphic
to $\operatorname{Pic}^d_{X'/k'}$, through the identifications
$X'\times_{k'}T\simeq X\times_kT$. Here the fibrewise degree is the
cohomological degree $\chi(\mathcal L_t)-\chi(\mathcal O_{X_t})$.

In particular, if $J_0$ represents $\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_0/k}$,
then $J_0\times_kk'$ represents
$\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_0\times_kk'/k'}$.
