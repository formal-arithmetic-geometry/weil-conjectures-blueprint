There is a commutative group scheme $J_0/k$, proper, smooth of relative
dimension $g$ and geometrically integral, representing
$F=\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_0/k}$. Its group law corresponds under the
representation $r$ to tensor product of invertible sheaves. Thus $J_0$
is an abelian variety of dimension $g$; no $k$-point of $C_0$ is assumed.
