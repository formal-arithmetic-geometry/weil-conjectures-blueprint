The functor $\operatorname{Hilb}^{d}_{C/k}$ is represented by a smooth proper geometrically
integral $k$-scheme of dimension $d$, with $\operatorname{Hilb}^{0}_{C/k}=\operatorname{Spec}k$.
Its $T$-points are precisely the relative effective Cartier divisors
that are finite locally free of degree $d$. It has a universal
divisor $D_d\subset C\times_k\operatorname{Hilb}^{d}_{C/k}$ and an associated invertible sheaf
$\mathcal O(D_d)$.
