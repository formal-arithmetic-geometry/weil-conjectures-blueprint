The ideal sheaf $\mathcal O_C(-x)$ is invertible because $C$ is smooth
of dimension one. Its pullback is the ideal sheaf of $x_{k'}$, since
$C_{k'}\to C$ is flat. Tensoring the ideal sequence with $\mathcal L$
gives

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal L(-x_{k'})
\longrightarrow\mathcal L
\longrightarrow\mathcal L|_{x_{k'}}\longrightarrow0,
$$

where the last sheaf is viewed as a sheaf on $C_{k'}$. Its space of
global sections is one-dimensional over $k'$, because $x_{k'}$ is a
$k'$-rational point. The image of $s$ in this space is nonzero, so
evaluation on global sections is surjective. Left exactness of
$H^0$, finiteness of coherent cohomology on a proper curve, and the
dimension formula for a linear map give the asserted equality.
