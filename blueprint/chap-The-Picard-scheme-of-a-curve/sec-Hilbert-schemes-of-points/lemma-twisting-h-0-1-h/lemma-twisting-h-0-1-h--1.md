Let $k$ be a field, let $C$ be a smooth proper curve over $k$, not
necessarily connected, and let $k'/k$ be a field extension. Let
$\mathcal L$ be an invertible sheaf on $C_{k'}$, let
$s\in H^0(C_{k'},\mathcal L)$, and let $x\in C(k)$ satisfy
$s(x_{k'})\ne0$. Then

$$
h^0(C_{k'},\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal O_C(-x)_{k'})+1
=h^0(C_{k'},\mathcal L).
$$
