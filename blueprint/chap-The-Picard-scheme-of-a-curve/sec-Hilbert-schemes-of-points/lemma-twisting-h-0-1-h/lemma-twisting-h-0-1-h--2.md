Let $k$ be separably closed, let $C$ be a smooth proper curve over $k$,
not necessarily connected, and let $k'/k$ be a field extension. Let
$\mathcal L$ be an invertible sheaf on $C_{k'}$ and let $t\geq0$ be an
integer. Suppose that an invertible sheaf $\mathcal M$ on $C$ satisfies

$$
h^0(C_{k'},\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M_{k'})=t+1,
\qquad h^1(C_{k'},\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M_{k'})=0.
$$

Then there is an invertible sheaf $\mathcal M_0$ on $C$ such that

$$
h^0(C_{k'},\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M_{0,k'})=1,
\qquad h^1(C_{k'},\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M_{0,k'})=0.
$$
