Assume now that $C(k)$ contains a point $P$, and let us describe
$\operatorname{Pic}_{C/k}$ by rigidified invertible sheaves as in
Lemma [lemma-rigidification-along-section]. We define a subfunctor
$F\subset\operatorname{Pic}_{C/k}$ as follows. A rigidified class
$\mathcal L$ on $C_T$ lies in $F(T)$ if, in the derived category of
$\mathcal O_T$-modules,

$$
Rf_{T,*}\mathcal L\simeq\mathcal N[0]
\quad\text{for some invertible }\mathcal N\text{ on }T.
$$

By Lemma [lemma-coherent-base-change-invertible-sheaves], this condition defines
an open locus of $T$ compatible with base change, so that
the inclusion $F\subset\operatorname{Pic}_{C/k}$ is representable by
open immersions.
