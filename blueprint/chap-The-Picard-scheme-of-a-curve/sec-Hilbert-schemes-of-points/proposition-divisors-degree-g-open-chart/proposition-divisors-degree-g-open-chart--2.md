For the smooth proper geometrically integral curve $C$ over the separably
closed field $k$, with $P\in C(k)$ and $g$ its genus, there are a
$k$-scheme $H$ smooth of relative dimension $g$, an open immersion
$j:W\to H$, and a representation of $F$ by $W$, with structure morphism
$W\xrightarrow{j}H\to\operatorname{Spec}k$.
Consequently, $F$ is represented by a scheme smooth of relative dimension
$g$ over $k$.
