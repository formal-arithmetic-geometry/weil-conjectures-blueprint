Equivalently, one may sheafify
$T\mapsto\operatorname{Pic}(C_T)$, since an invertible sheaf on $T$
becomes trivial on a Zariski cover. The sheafification is needed: invertible sheaves satisfy effective descent, but their automorphisms can
prevent their isomorphism classes from forming a sheaf. For this reason
neither the displayed quotient nor $T\mapsto\operatorname{Pic}(C_T)$ need
be an fppf sheaf [Stacks, [0B9L](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B9L)]. If $f$ has a section, the next lemma
describes the sheafification directly. We also write
$\operatorname{Pic}_{C/S}$ for the Picard scheme when it exists.
