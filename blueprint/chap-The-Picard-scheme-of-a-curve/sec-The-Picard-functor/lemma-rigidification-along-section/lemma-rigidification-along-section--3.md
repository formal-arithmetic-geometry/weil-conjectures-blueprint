There is a split
exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(T)
\xrightarrow{f_T^*}\operatorname{Pic}(C_T)
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}_{C/S}(T)
\longrightarrow0,
$$

whose retraction is $\sigma_T^*$.
