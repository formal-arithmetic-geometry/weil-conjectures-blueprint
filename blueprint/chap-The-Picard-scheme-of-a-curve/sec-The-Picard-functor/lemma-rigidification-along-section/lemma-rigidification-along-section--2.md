Let $p:Z\to B$ be a morphism of schemes with a section $s:B\to Z$, and
suppose that

$$
\Gamma(B,\mathcal O_B)\longrightarrow\Gamma(Z,\mathcal O_Z)
$$

is surjective. Let $\mathcal A$ be an invertible $\mathcal O_Z$-module,
let $\mathcal M$ be an $\mathcal O_Z$-module, and let
$\varphi,\psi:\mathcal A\to\mathcal M$ be morphisms with $\psi$ an
isomorphism. If $s^*\varphi=s^*\psi$, then $\varphi=\psi$.

In particular, under condition $(\ast)$, every automorphism of an
invertible sheaf on $C_T$ which preserves a trivialization along
$\sigma_T$ is the identity.
