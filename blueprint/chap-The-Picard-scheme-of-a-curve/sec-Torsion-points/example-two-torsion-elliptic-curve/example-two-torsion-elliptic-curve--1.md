Let $k$ be algebraically closed of characteristic different from two
and let $E\colon y^2=(x-e_1)(x-e_2)(x-e_3)$ with distinct $e_i\in k$,
with origin $O$ at infinity and $P_i=(e_i,0)$. The function $x-e_i$
has a double zero at $P_i$, because $y$ is a local parameter there. It
has a double pole at $O$.
