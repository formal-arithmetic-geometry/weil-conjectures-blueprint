Let $A/k$ be an abelian variety of dimension $d$. There is a
noncanonical isomorphism of abelian groups

$$
A(k)[n]\simeq(\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z)^{2d}.
$$

In particular, taking $A=J(C)$ and $d=g$ gives
$J(C)[n](k)\simeq(\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z)^{2g}$.
