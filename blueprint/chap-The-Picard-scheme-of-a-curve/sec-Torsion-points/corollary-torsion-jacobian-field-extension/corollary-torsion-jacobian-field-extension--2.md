Let $A/k$ be an abelian variety. For every algebraically closed
extension $k'/k$, precomposition with
$\operatorname{Spec}k'\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ gives a bijection

$$
A[n](k)\longrightarrow A[n](k').
$$

Here $A[n](k')$ denotes the $k'$-valued points of the same group
scheme over $k$, equivalently the $k'$-points of its base change.
