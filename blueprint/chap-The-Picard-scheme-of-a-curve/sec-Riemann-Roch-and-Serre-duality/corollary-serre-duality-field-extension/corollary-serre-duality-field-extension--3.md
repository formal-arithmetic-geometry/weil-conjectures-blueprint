Let $X$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated $k$-scheme and let $k'/k$ be a
field extension. Then
$$
\operatorname{finrank}_{k'}H^1(X_{k'},\mathcal O_{X_{k'}})
=\operatorname{finrank}_kH^1(X,\mathcal O_X).
$$
In particular, the genus $g=\dim_kH^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$ of a smooth proper
curve is unchanged by field extension.
