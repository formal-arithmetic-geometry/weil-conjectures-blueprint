Apply Serre duality [theorem-riemann-roch-coherent-serre-duality--2] to
$\mathcal O_C$, with cohomological degrees $1$ and $0$. The identification
$\mathcal O_C^\vee\otimes\omega_{C/k}\simeq\omega_{C/k}$ gives
$$
H^1(C,\mathcal O_C)^\vee\simeq H^0(C,\omega_{C/k}).
$$
Taking dimensions and using equality of the dimensions of a
finite-dimensional vector space and its dual gives the assertion.
