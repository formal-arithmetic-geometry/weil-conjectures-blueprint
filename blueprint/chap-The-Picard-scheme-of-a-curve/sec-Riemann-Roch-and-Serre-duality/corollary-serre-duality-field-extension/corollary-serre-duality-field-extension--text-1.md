*Shared context.* Let $k'/k$ be a field extension. For the curve statements,
let $C$ be smooth and proper of relative dimension $1$ over $k$, put
$\omega_{C/k}=\Omega^1_{C/k}$, and write
$g=\dim_kH^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$. The field $k$ need not be separably closed,
and $C$ need not be connected. In the statements for a general
quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme, $\operatorname{finrank}$ denotes
the vector-space dimension when it is finite, and $0$ otherwise.
