The structure sheaf is quasi-coherent, and its pullback to $X_{k'}$ is
$\mathcal O_{X_{k'}}$. Apply
[corollary-serre-duality-field-extension--1] with
$\mathcal F=\mathcal O_X$ and $q=1$. Taking finite ranks gives the
assertion, which is the equality of the two arithmetic genera by
definition. For a smooth proper curve these cohomology groups are
finite-dimensional, so the equality is the usual invariance of genus.
