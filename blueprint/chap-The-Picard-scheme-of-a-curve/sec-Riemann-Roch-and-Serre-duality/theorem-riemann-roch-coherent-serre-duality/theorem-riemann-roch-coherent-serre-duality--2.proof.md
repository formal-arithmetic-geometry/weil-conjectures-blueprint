The Serre-duality part of [Stacks, [0B5B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B5B)], quoted in the reference, uses the residue trace on the canonical sheaf $\omega_{C/k}$. It identifies the dual of $H^1(C,\mathcal L)$ with $H^0(C,\mathcal L^{-1}\otimes\omega_{C/k})$. Symmetry of tensor product gives the displayed order of factors.
