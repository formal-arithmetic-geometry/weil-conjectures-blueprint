If $\deg\mathcal L>2g-2$, clause [theorem-riemann-roch-coherent-serre-duality--3] implies that $\omega_{C/k}\otimes\mathcal L^{-1}$ has negative degree. A nonzero section of a line bundle on an integral curve has an effective zero divisor, so a negative-degree line bundle has no nonzero section. Clause [theorem-riemann-roch-coherent-serre-duality--2] therefore gives $H^1(C,\mathcal L)=0$.
