Let $C$ be a smooth proper curve over a field $k$ such that
$k\to H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)$ is an isomorphism, and put
$g=h^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$. If $\mathcal L$ is invertible and
$2g-1\leq\deg\mathcal L$, then

$$
h^1(C,\mathcal L)=0.
$$

Since $H^1(C,\mathcal L)$ is finite-dimensional, this is equivalent to
$H^1(C,\mathcal L)=0$. The degree is an integer, so the hypothesis is
equivalent to $\deg\mathcal L>2g-2$.
