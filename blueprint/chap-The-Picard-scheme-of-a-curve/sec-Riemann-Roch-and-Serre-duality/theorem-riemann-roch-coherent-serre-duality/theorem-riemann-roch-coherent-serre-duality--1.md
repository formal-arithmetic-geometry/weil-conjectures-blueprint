Let $C$ be a smooth proper curve over a field $k$ such that
$k\to H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)$ is an isomorphism, and put
$g=h^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$. For an invertible sheaf $\mathcal L$ on $C$,
the cohomology of $\mathcal L$ is finite-dimensional and vanishes above
degree one, and the cohomological degree
$\chi(C,\mathcal L)-\chi(C,\mathcal O_C)$ agrees with the divisor degree
$\deg\mathcal L$. Since $\chi(C,\mathcal O_C)=1-g$, this gives

$$
\chi(C,\mathcal L)=\deg\mathcal L+1-g.
$$
