Serre duality gives $h^0(C,\omega_{C/k})=g$ and $h^1(C,\omega_{C/k})=h^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=1$, under the standing hypotheses on $C$. Thus $\chi(C,\omega_{C/k})=g-1$. Apply clause [theorem-riemann-roch-coherent-serre-duality--1] to $\omega_{C/k}$ to obtain $g-1=\deg\omega_{C/k}+1-g$, which is equivalent to the claim.
