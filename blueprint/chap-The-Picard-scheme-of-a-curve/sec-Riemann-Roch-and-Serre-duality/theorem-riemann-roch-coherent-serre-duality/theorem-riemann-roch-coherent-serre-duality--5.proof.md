Unfold the cohomological definition of degree and use
$\chi(C,\mathcal O_C)=1-g$ and
$\chi(C,\mathcal L)=h^0(C,\mathcal L)-h^1(C,\mathcal L)$.
By [theorem-riemann-roch-coherent-serre-duality--4], the last
$h^1$ is zero. Rearranging the resulting equality of integers gives

$$
h^0(C,\mathcal L)=\deg\mathcal L+1-g.
$$
