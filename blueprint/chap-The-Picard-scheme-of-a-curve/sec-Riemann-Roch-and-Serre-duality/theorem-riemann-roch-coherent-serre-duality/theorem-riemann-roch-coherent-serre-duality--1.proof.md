Apply Riemann–Roch for the smooth proper curve $C$ and the invertible sheaf $\mathcal L$, in the form [Stacks, [0B5B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B5B)] quoted in the reference. With $g=\dim_kH^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$ and the degree convention fixed in this appendix, it gives exactly $\chi(C,\mathcal L)=\deg\mathcal L+1-g$.
