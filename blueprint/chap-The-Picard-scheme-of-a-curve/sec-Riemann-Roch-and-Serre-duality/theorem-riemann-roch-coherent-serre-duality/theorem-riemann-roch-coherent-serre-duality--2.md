Let $C$ be a smooth proper curve over a field $k$, and put
$\omega_{C/k}=\Omega^1_{C/k}$. For an invertible sheaf $\mathcal L$ on $C$
there is a $k$-linear isomorphism

$$
H^1(C,\mathcal L)^\vee
\simeq H^0(C,\omega_{C/k}\otimes\mathcal L^{-1}).
$$

No assumption on $H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)$ is needed.
