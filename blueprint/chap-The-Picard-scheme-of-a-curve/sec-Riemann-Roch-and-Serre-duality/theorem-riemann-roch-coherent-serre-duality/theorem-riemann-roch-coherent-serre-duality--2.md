Let $C$ be a smooth proper curve over a field $k$, and put
$\omega_{C/k}=\Omega^1_{C/k}$. For $p,q\geq0$ with $p+q=1$, there is a
$k$-linear isomorphism

$$
H^q(C,\mathcal F)^\vee
\simeq H^p(C,\mathcal F^\vee\otimes\omega_{C/k}),
$$

natural in finite locally free $\mathcal O_C$-modules $\mathcal F$.
No assumption on $H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)$ is needed. In particular, for an
invertible sheaf $\mathcal L$ and $(p,q)=(0,1)$ this gives
$H^1(C,\mathcal L)^\vee\simeq
H^0(C,\omega_{C/k}\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})$.
