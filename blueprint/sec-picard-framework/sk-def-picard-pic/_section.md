*Definition (sketch).*

For $f:X\to S$,
$\Pic_{X/S}(T)=\Pic(X_T)/\operatorname{pr}_T^*\Pic(T)$ (the fppf
sheafification of $T\mapsto\Pic(X_T)$), and for a section
$\sigma$:

$$
\Pic_{X/S,\sigma}(T)=\{\cL\in\Pic(X_T):\sigma_T^*\cL\cong\cO_T\}.
$$
