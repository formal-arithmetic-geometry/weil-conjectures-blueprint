Let $T$ be a scheme and $\{U_i\to T\}_{i\in I}$ a Zariski covering
(each $U_i\to T$ an open immersion, the $U_i$ covering $T$). Then
$\{U_i\to T\}$ is an
fppf covering of $T$.
