For a curve $C$ over a field, the *divisors* ($D=\sum_Pn_P[P]$,
$\operatorname{Div}(C)$, principal divisors, the divisor class group
$\operatorname{Cl}(C)$ and the associated module $\mathcal{O}_C(D)$ of a
divisor, defined via $k(C)$) are those of [def-sc-divisor].
In this blueprint they are used for the curve $X$ and its base changes
$X_K$; for an effective Cartier divisor, the module $\mathcal{O}_C(D)$ has
the same class in $\operatorname{Pic}(C)$ as the invertible module of
[def-pc-divisor-line-bundle] ([lem-pc-divisor-line-bundle-agrees-sc]).
