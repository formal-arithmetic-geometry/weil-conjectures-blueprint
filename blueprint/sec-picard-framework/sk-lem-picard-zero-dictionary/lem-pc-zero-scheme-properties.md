Let $X$ be a scheme, $\mathcal{M},\mathcal{N}$ invertible
$\mathcal{O}_X$-modules and $\varphi\colon\mathcal{M}\to\mathcal{N}$ a morphism.
On an open $U$ on which $\mathcal M$ and $\mathcal N$ are free on generators
$e_{\mathcal M}$ and $e_{\mathcal N}$, write
$\varphi(e_{\mathcal M})=b\cdot e_{\mathcal N}$ with $b\in\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_X)$.

1. There is a unique closed subscheme $Z(\varphi)\subseteq X$, the *zero
scheme* of $\varphi$, such that for every affine open
$U=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ as above $Z(\varphi)\cap U=\operatorname{Spec}(A/(b))$.
In particular $Z(\varphi)$ is a locally principal closed subscheme.
1. For every morphism $g\colon Y\to X$, the zero scheme of the pullback
$g^*\varphi\colon g^*\mathcal M\to g^*\mathcal N$ is
$Z(g^*\varphi)=Z(\varphi)\times_XY$.
1. $Z(\varphi)=X$ if and only if $\varphi=0$.

For a global section $s\in H^0(X,\mathcal{M})$, viewed as the morphism
$s\colon\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal M$, this is the zero scheme
$Z(s)=Z(\varphi)$, whose ideal sheaf is
$\operatorname{im}(s^\vee\colon\mathcal M^\vee\to\mathcal O_X)$.

## Proof

(1) Changing the generators $e_{\mathcal M},e_{\mathcal N}$ on $U$
multiplies them by units of $\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_X)$, hence multiplies $b$
by a unit, so the ideal $(b)\subseteq\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_X)$ depends only on
$U$. If $V\subseteq U$ is a smaller such affine open, restricting the
generators to $V$ shows that the ideal attached to $V$ is generated by the
restriction of $b$. Hence the ideals $(b)$ on the trivializing affine
opens, which cover $X$, are compatible with localization and define a
quasi-coherent ideal sheaf $\mathcal I_{Z(\varphi)}$ which is locally
generated by one element; let $Z(\varphi)$ be the closed subscheme it
defines. Uniqueness is clear since a closed subscheme is determined by
its intersections with an open cover. For $\varphi=s\colon\mathcal
O_X\to\mathcal M$ and $e_{\mathcal O}=1$, the image of
$s^\vee\colon\mathcal M^\vee\to\mathcal O_X$ on $U$ is generated by
$s^\vee(e_{\mathcal M}^\vee)=b$, so $\mathcal I_{Z(s)}=\operatorname{im}(s^\vee)$.

(2) Let $y\in Y$, and choose an affine open $U\ni g(y)$ on which
$\mathcal M,\mathcal N$ are free on $e_{\mathcal M},e_{\mathcal N}$, with
$\varphi(e_{\mathcal M})=b\,e_{\mathcal N}$, and an affine open
$V\subseteq g^{-1}(U)$ containing $y$. Then $g^*e_{\mathcal M}$ and
$g^*e_{\mathcal N}$ are generators of $g^*\mathcal M$ and $g^*\mathcal N$
on $V$, and $(g^*\varphi)(g^*e_{\mathcal M})=g^\sharp(b)\,g^*e_{\mathcal N}$.
By (1), the ideal of $Z(g^*\varphi)$ on $V$ is generated by
$g^\sharp(b)$, which is also the ideal of the scheme-theoretic inverse image
$Z(\varphi)\times_XY$ on $V$, namely the ideal generated by the image of
$(b)$. As such $V$ cover $Y$, the two closed subschemes agree.

(3) If $\varphi=0$, every local coefficient $b$ is $0$, so
$\mathcal I_{Z(\varphi)}=0$ and $Z(\varphi)=X$. Conversely, if
$Z(\varphi)=X$, then $\mathcal I_{Z(\varphi)}=0$, so on every trivializing
affine open $U$ the coefficient $b$ is $0$, i.e. $\varphi|_U=0$. These $U$
cover $X$, hence $\varphi=0$.
