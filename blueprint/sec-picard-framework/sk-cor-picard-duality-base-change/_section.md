Let $K/k$ be a field extension. Under the flat base change identifications
$H^i(C_K,\cL_K)=H^i(C,\cL)\otimes_kK$ and
$\omega_{C_K/K}\simeq(\omega_{C/k})_K$, the duality isomorphism
$H^1(C_K,\cL_K)^\vee\simeq
H^0(C_K,\omega_{C_K/K}\otimes\cL_K^{-1})$ is the $K$-linear
extension of the one for $\cL$.
