Let $k$ be a field, $C$ a scheme which is smooth of relative dimension
$1$ and proper over $k$ with $H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=k$, and $\mathcal M$ a
line bundle on $C$ with $\deg\mathcal M<0$ ([def-sc-pic0]). Then
$H^0(C,\mathcal M)=0$.
