The local rings of $C$ at closed points are discrete valuation rings
([lem-sc-smooth-regular], [lem-sc-regular-dim1-dvr]), and the ideal
sheaves $\mathcal I_P$ of closed points are invertible
([lem-sc-point-cartier]), hence so are the $\mathcal I_E$.

Fix a generator $s$ of $\mathcal L$ over a nonempty open $U_0$; this is
the rational section. Every point $x$ has an open neighbourhood $V_x$ on
which $\mathcal L$ is generated by some $e_x$; since $C$ is integral,
$s=g_x\,e_x$ on $U_0\cap V_x$ for a unique $g_x\in k(C)^*$, and $g_x/g_y$
is a unit on $V_x\cap V_y$. So $n_P:=\operatorname{ord}_P(g_x)$, for a
closed point $P\in V_x$, does not depend on $x$; it is nonzero for only
finitely many $P$, since finitely many $V_x$ cover $C$ and each $g_x$ has
finitely many zeros and poles. Put
$E_1=\sum_P\max(n_P,0)[P]$ and $E_2=\sum_P\max(-n_P,0)[P]$.

Define $\psi:\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal I_{E_1}\to k(C)$ by
$t\otimes a\mapsto a\cdot(t/s)$, where $t/s\in k(C)$ is the rational
coordinate of $t$ with respect to $s$ (so $e_x/s=g_x^{-1}$). Near a closed
point $P\in V_x$ the source is generated by $e_x\otimes\pi_P^{\max(n_P,0)}$,
$\pi_P$ a uniformizer, which $\psi$ sends to
$\pi_P^{\max(n_P,0)}g_x^{-1}$, an element of order exactly
$\max(-n_P,0)$ at $P$ and of order $0$ at the other closed points near
$P$. Hence $\psi$ takes values in $\mathcal O_C$ ($C$ is normal), its image
is $\mathcal I_{E_2}$, and it is injective ($C$ is integral): so
$\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal I_{E_1}\cong\mathcal I_{E_2}$. Tensoring with
$\mathcal I_{E_1}^\vee$ and using $\mathcal I_{E_1}\otimes\mathcal I_{E_1}^\vee\cong\mathcal O_C$
gives $\mathcal L\cong\mathcal I_{E_1}^\vee\otimes\mathcal I_{E_2}$. By
construction $E_1-E_2=\sum_P n_P[P]$ is the divisor of zeros and poles
of $s$.
