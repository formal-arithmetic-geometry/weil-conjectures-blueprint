Let $k$ be a field and let $\mathcal F$ be a
coherent module on $\mathbf P^1_k$.
Then $H^0(\mathbf P^1_k,\mathcal F)$ and $H^1(\mathbf P^1_k,\mathcal F)$
are finite-dimensional over $k$.
