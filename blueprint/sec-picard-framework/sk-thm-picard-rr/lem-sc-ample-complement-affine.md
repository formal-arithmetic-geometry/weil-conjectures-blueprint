Let $X$ be a proper scheme over a field $k$, $D\subseteq X$ an
effective Cartier divisor ([def-nagata-invertible-ideal])
and $\mathcal L:=\mathcal O_X(D)$
ample. Then $X\setminus D$ is affine.
