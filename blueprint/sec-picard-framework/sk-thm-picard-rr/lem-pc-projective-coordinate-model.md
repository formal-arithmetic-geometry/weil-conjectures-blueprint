Let $X$ be an integral scheme over a field $k$, equipped with a
closed immersion
$i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_k$ over $k$. There are an integral
standard graded $k$-algebra $S$ and an isomorphism
$X\cong\operatorname{Proj}(S)$ of schemes over $k$.
