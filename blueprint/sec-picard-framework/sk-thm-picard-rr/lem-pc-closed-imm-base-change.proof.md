The base change $Z'=Z\times_XX'\to X'$ is a closed immersion (Schemes,
Lemma 26.18.2, Tag [01JY](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01JY)).
For the ideal sheaf, $g^*$ is right exact, so the exact sequence
$0\to\mathcal{I}_Z\to\mathcal{O}_X\to i_*\mathcal{O}_Z\to0$ gives an exact
sequence $g^*\mathcal{I}_Z\to\mathcal{O}_{X'}\to g^*i_*\mathcal{O}_Z\to0$, in
which $g^*i_*\mathcal{O}_Z=i'_*\mathcal{O}_{Z'}$ and the last map is the
canonical $\mathcal{O}_{X'}\to i'_*\mathcal{O}_{Z'}$: on affine opens
$\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ and $\operatorname{Spec}A'\subseteq X'$ with
$g(\operatorname{Spec}A')\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $Z$ cut out by
$I\subseteq A$, the scheme $Z'$ is
$\operatorname{Spec}(A'\otimes_AA/I)=\operatorname{Spec}(A'/IA')$. Hence the
ideal sheaf of $Z'$, the kernel of $\mathcal{O}_{X'}\to i'_*\mathcal{O}_{Z'}$,
is the image of $g^*\mathcal{I}_Z\to\mathcal{O}_{X'}$, locally the ideal $IA'$
generated by the image of $I$; this is Tag
[01JU](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01JU). It is only the image:
$g^*\mathcal{I}_Z$, locally $I\otimes_AA'$, need not inject into $A'$. In the
example $A=k[t]$, $I=(t)$ and $A'=k=A/I$, so $I\otimes_Ak=I/tI\cong k$ is
generated by the class of $t$, which maps to $t\cdot1=0$ in $k$: the image is
$0$, the ideal of $Z'=Z$ in $X'=Z$.

If $g$ is flat, $g^*$ is exact, so $g^*\mathcal{I}_Z\to\mathcal{O}_{X'}$ is
injective and $\mathcal{I}_{Z'}=g^*\mathcal{I}_Z$. For the last assertion,
$\operatorname{Spec}K\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is flat ([lem-pc-flat-field])
and flatness is stable under base change, so $X_K\to X$ is flat; the flat
case applied to the closed immersion $x\to X$ gives
$\mathcal{I}_{x_K}=\mathcal{I}_x\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X}\mathcal{O}_{X_K}$.
