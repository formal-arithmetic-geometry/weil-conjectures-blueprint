Let $C$ be a scheme smooth of relative dimension one over a field
$k$ (for instance a smooth connected curve over $k$) and
$P\in C$ a closed point. Then $P$, with its reduced structure,
is an effective Cartier divisor on $C$: locally at $P$, the ideal
of $P$ is generated by one non-zero-divisor. Consequently every
effective divisor of [def-sc-divisor] is an effective Cartier
divisor.
