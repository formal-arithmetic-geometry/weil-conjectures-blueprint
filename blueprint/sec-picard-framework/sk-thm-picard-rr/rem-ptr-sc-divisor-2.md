In the notation of [def-sc-divisor], the following holds. When all
$n_P\geq0$, $D$ is *effective*; that an effective divisor
on the smooth curve $C$ is then an effective Cartier divisor in
the sense of [def-nagata-invertible-ideal], and that
conversely every effective Cartier divisor on $C$ arises from an
effective divisor, is [lem-sc-point-cartier] together with
[lem-sc-cartier-divisor-points], and it is not part of the
definition.
