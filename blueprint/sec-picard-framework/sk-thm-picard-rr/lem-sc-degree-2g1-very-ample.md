Let $C$ be a proper smooth connected curve of
genus $g$ ([def-sc-genus]) over an algebraically closed
field $k$, and $\mathcal M$ a line bundle on $C$ with
$\deg\mathcal M\geq2g+1$ ([def-sc-pic0]). Then
$\mathcal M$ is very ample.
