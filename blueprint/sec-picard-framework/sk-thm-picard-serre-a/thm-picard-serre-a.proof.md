*Step 1: reduction to $P$.* By
[lem-pc-closed-immersion-coherent-cohomology] the module
$\mathcal G:=i_*\mathcal F$ is coherent on $P$, and for every $m$ the
natural map $\mathcal G\otimes\mathcal O_P(m)\to i_*(\mathcal F(m))$ is an
isomorphism. Moreover $H^0(P,i_*(\mathcal F(m)))=H^0(X,\mathcal F(m))$ by
definition of $i_*$, and for $x\in X$ the stalk of $i_*(\mathcal F(m))$ at
$i(x)$ is $\mathcal F(m)_x$ (the preimages of the open neighbourhoods of
$i(x)$ are cofinal among the open neighbourhoods of $x$, $i$ being a
homeomorphism onto a closed subset), with $\mathcal O_{P,i(x)}$ acting
through the surjection $\mathcal O_{P,i(x)}\to\mathcal O_{X,x}$. Hence if
the germs of global sections generate $\mathcal G(m)_{i(x)}$ over
$\mathcal O_{P,i(x)}$, the same germs generate $\mathcal F(m)_x$ over
$\mathcal O_{X,x}$. It therefore suffices to show that $\mathcal G(m)$ is
generated by its global sections on $P$ for $m\gg0$.

*Step 2: twisting modules of non-negative degree.* Let $d\ge0$ and
$0\le j\le n$. On the standard chart $U_l=D_+(x_l)$ put
$\sigma_{j,l}:=(x_j/x_l)^d e_l^{(d)}\in\Gamma(U_l,\mathcal O_P(d))$. By the
transition rule of [def-pc-projective-space-twist], on $U_l\cap U_{l'}$
we have
$(x_j/x_l)^de_l^{(d)}=(x_j/x_l)^d(x_l/x_{l'})^de_{l'}^{(d)}=(x_j/x_{l'})^de_{l'}^{(d)}$,
so the $\sigma_{j,l}$ glue to a global section $x_j^d$ of
$\mathcal O_P(d)$, whose restriction to $U_j$ is the basis $e_j^{(d)}$.
As the $U_j$ cover $P$, every stalk of $\mathcal O_P(d)$ is generated by
the germ of one of the global sections $x_0^d,\ldots,x_n^d$.

*Step 3: a quotient of such modules.* By
[lem-pc-projective-space-twist-quotient] (with $A=k$) there are
$m_1,\ldots,m_r\ge0$ and a surjection
$\varphi:\bigoplus_{a=1}^r\mathcal O_P(-m_a)\to\mathcal G$. Put
$m_0:=\max(0,m_1,\ldots,m_r)$ and let $m\ge m_0$. Tensoring with the
invertible module $\mathcal O_P(m)$ ([def-sc-invertible-module]) preserves
surjectivity, and [lem-pc-twist-tensor] identifies
$\mathcal O_P(-m_a)\otimes\mathcal O_P(m)\cong\mathcal O_P(m-m_a)$, so we get
a surjection
$\varphi(m):\bigoplus_a\mathcal O_P(m-m_a)\to\mathcal G(m)$ with all
$d_a:=m-m_a\ge0$. The images $\varphi(m)(x_j^{d_a}\text{ in the $a$-th
summand})$ are global sections of $\mathcal G(m)$. For $p\in P$ the stalk
map $\varphi(m)_p$ is surjective, and by Step 2 its source is generated
by the germs of the $x_j^{d_a}$ (in the $a$-th summand, for $j$ with
$p\in U_j$); hence $\mathcal G(m)_p$ is generated by the germs of their
images. (If $r=0$ then $\mathcal G=0$ and there is nothing to prove.)
By Step 1 this proves the theorem.

(The sketch follows the same route: the finite presentation by twisting
modules, which also drives the proof of Serre vanishing, gives the
conclusion directly.)
