*Theorem (sketch).*

Let $X\subseteq\PP^n_k$ be a closed subscheme and $\cF$ coherent on
$X$. Then $\cF\otimes\cO_X(m)$ is generated by its global
sections for $m\gg0$.

## Proof

The same induction on $\dim X$ as in [sk-thm-picard-serre-b], run on
the quotients of the Koszul surjections: the twisted sequence
$0\to\cF(-1)\otimes\cO(m)\to\cF\otimes\cO(m)\to\cF|_Z\otimes \cO(m)\to0$ reduces generation on $X$ to generation on the
hypersurface section $Z$, the obstruction to lifting sections being
the $H^1$ that [sk-thm-picard-serre-b] kills for $m\gg0$.
