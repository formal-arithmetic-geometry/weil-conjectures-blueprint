*Definition (sketch).*

The *Jacobian* of $C$ is the open and closed subgroup scheme

$$
J(C):=\underline{\Pic}^0_{C/k}\subseteq\underline{\Pic}_{C/k}
$$

of [sk-prop-picard-degree-decomposition], representing the subfunctor
$\Pic^0_{C/k}$ of classes of fibrewise degree $0$. That it is smooth,
proper and connected of dimension $g$ is [sk-prop-jacobian], and is
not part of this definition.

Uses: [sk-prop-picard-degree-decomposition]
