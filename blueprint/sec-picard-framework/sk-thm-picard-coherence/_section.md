*Theorem (sketch).*

With $f$ and $\cF$ as above, $R^qf_*\cF$ is a coherent
$\cO_T$-module for all $q$, and it vanishes for $q\gg0$.

## Proof

This is the coherence of higher direct images for proper morphisms,
proved with the Čech-to-derived machinery
([sk-thm-picard-cech], [sk-lem-picard-cech-computes]) and the
projective-space case [sk-thm-picard-pn].
