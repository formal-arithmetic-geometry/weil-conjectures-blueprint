Let $C$ be a smooth connected curve over $k$ of
dimension one. Then $\omega_C=\Omega^1_{C/k}$
([def-sc-kahler-differentials]) is an invertible
$\mathcal O_C$-module ([def-sc-invertible-module]), i.e. a
line bundle.
