Let $k$ be an infinite field and $S$ an integral
standard graded $k$-algebra
such that $X=\operatorname{Proj}(S)$ is a
curve. There exist
$l_0,l_1\in S_1$ such that $D_+(l_0)\cup D_+(l_1)=X$.
