Let $k$ be a field and let $X$ be a
smooth projective curve over
$k$ which is geometrically
irreducible. The *genus* of $X$ is

$$
g(X):=\dim_kH^1(X,\mathcal{O}_X)\in\Z_{\geq0}
$$
