Let $k$ be a field and let $X$ be a
curve over $k$ which is smooth and
projective. For every
quasi-coherent $\mathcal{O}_X$-module $\mathcal{F}$ one has

$$
H^i(X,\mathcal{F})=0\qquad\text{for all }i\ge2 .
$$
