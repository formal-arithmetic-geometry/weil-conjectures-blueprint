Let $f:X\to S$ be a morphism of schemes and $s\in S$. If $f$ is
locally of finite type and $f^{-1}(\{s\})$ is a finite set, then $X_s$
is a finite discrete space and $f$ is quasi-finite at each point of
$X$ lying over $s$.
