title = "Hilbert schemes of points and an open chart"

We glue the Picard scheme from charts given by effective divisors of
degree $g$, and these are parametrized by a Hilbert scheme of points.
On a smooth curve a finite subscheme of length $d$ is the same as an
effective divisor of degree $d$, and $H_d$ is the symmetric power
$C^{(d)}$: iterated addition of points gives a finite surjection
$C^d\to H_d$, from which $H_d$ inherits properness, irreducibility and
dimension $d$ (Theorem [sk-thm-picard-hilb-curves]). For
$C=\mathbf P^1$, an effective divisor of degree $d$ is the zero set of a
binary form of degree $d$ up to scalars, and $H_d=\mathbf P^d$. A divisor
$D$ gives the invertible sheaf $\mathcal O(D)$ with its canonical
section, and conversely an invertible sheaf with a section up to scalars
gives a divisor. When $h^0=1$ the section is unique up to scalars, and
the divisor is determined by the sheaf. This identifies the open part
of $H_g$ where $h^0(\mathcal O(D))=1$ and $h^1=0$ with an open subfunctor
of the Picard functor (Proposition [sk-thm-picard-f-representable]).
For $d\geq0$, let $H_d=\operatorname{Hilb}^d_{C/k}$ be the
functor on $k$-schemes sending $T$ to the set of closed subschemes
$D\subset C_T$ that are finite locally free of rank $d$ over $T$.
