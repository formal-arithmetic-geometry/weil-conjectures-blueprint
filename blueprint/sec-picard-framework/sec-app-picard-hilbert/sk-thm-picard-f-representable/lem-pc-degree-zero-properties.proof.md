The cohomology $H^i$ of complexes is invariant under quasi-isomorphisms, so
it factors through the localization $D(\mathcal{A})$ and is a functor on
$D(\mathcal{A})$; in particular the isomorphism $M\cong E[-n]$ in
$D(\mathcal{A})$ gives $H^i(M)\cong H^i(E[-n])$ for every $i$. The complex
$E[-n]$ has a single non-zero term, $E$ in degree $n$, with zero
differentials on both sides; so $H^i(E[-n])=0$ for $i\neq n$ and
$H^n(E[-n])=E$. The second assertion is the case $\mathcal{A}$ the category of
$\mathcal{O}_X$-modules, $n=0$ and $E=\mathcal{N}$.
