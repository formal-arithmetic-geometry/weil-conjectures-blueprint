Both facts are cited from the Stacks Project as above (Tags 0569 and 033M).
For the consequence, let $X$ be regular, connected and Noetherian. Then $X$ is
normal, so it is a finite disjoint union of integral schemes. These are open
and closed in $X$, so connectedness leaves exactly one of them, and $X$ is
integral, in particular irreducible.
