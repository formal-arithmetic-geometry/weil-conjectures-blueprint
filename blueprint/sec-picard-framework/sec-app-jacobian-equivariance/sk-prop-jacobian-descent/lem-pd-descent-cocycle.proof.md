With $\theta_f$ the bijection between the $T$-points of $V$ over $f$ and
$F(T\to Y)$ given by the representation, $\tau_{f_1,f_2}=\theta_{f_2}^{-1}\circ\theta_{f_1}$.
Hence $\tau_{f_2,f_3}\circ\tau_{f_1,f_2}=\theta_{f_3}^{-1}\theta_{f_2}\theta_{f_2}^{-1}\theta_{f_1}=\theta_{f_3}^{-1}\theta_{f_1}=\tau_{f_1,f_3}$.
