Let $X$ be a scheme and $E\in D(\mathcal{O}_X)$. $E$ is *perfect* if it is
locally (on an affine open cover of $X$) isomorphic to a bounded complex
of finite projective modules; the subcategory
is $D_{\mathrm{perf}}(\mathcal{O}_X)$.
