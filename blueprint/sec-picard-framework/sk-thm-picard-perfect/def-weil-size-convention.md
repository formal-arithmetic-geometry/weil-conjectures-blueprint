Fix Grothendieck universes $\mathcal U\in\mathcal V$. Schemes are
$\mathcal U$-small; coefficient rings, and the modules over them in which
sheaves take values, are $\mathcal V$-small. Every site considered (such
as the small pro-etale site of a scheme) is essentially $\mathcal V$-small,
i.e. equivalent to a $\mathcal V$-small skeleton, so that its categories of
sheaves are $\mathcal V$-categories. Derived categories and filtered
colimits are formed in $\mathcal V$.
