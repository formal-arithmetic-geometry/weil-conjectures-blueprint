In the notation of [def-pc-finite-locally-free], the following holds. $f$ is *finite locally
free of degree $d$* (for $d\geq0$) if $f$ is
finite, flat and locally of
finite presentation, and for every $s\in S$ the fibre $X_s$ is a finite
set of points with

$$
\dim_{\kappa(s)}\Gamma(X_s,\mathcal{O}_{X_s})=d .
$$

(The resulting structure of $f_*\mathcal{O}_X$ is [lem-pc-flf-properties].)
