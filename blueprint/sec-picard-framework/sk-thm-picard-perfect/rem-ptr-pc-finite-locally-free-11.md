In the notation of [def-pc-finite-locally-free], if
$f$ is finite locally free of degree $d$, then $f_*\mathcal{O}_X$ is a
locally free $\mathcal{O}_S$-module of rank $d$ whose fibres are the rings
$\Gamma(X_s,\mathcal{O}_{X_s})$; this is [lem-pc-flf-properties].
