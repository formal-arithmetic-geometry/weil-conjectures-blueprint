Let $k$ be a field, let $i:X\hookrightarrow P=\mathbf P^n_k$ be a closed
immersion, put $\mathcal O_X(m):=i^*\mathcal O_P(m)$
([def-pc-projective-space-twist]) and let $\mathcal F$ be a
coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module ([pqf-def-coherent]). Then there is
$m_0\ge0$ such that for every $m\ge m_0$ the module
$\mathcal F(m):=\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal O_X(m)$ is
*generated by its global sections*: for every $x\in X$ the stalk
$\mathcal F(m)_x$ is generated as an $\mathcal O_{X,x}$-module by the
germs of elements of $H^0(X,\mathcal F(m))$ (equivalently, the evaluation
map $\bigoplus_{s\in H^0(X,\mathcal F(m))}\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal F(m)$ is
surjective).
