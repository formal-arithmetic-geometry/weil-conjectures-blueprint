The statement is local on $T$, so we may replace $T$ by an affine open
neighbourhood $S=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ of $t$. Then $f$ is proper of finite
presentation over an affine base, $X$ has a finite affine cover, and clause (1)
of [lem-pc-rf-perfect-base-change] gives the neighbourhood $U$, the integer
$d$ and the complex $P$ with the required isomorphisms.
