title = "The chart condition is open"
order = 733
+++
The chart of the Picard scheme consists of the invertible sheaves
$\mathcal L$ for which $Rf_{T,*}\mathcal L$ is an invertible sheaf in
degree $0$. Coherent base change shows that this condition is open on
$T$ and stable under base change.
