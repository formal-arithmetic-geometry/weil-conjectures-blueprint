Let $C$ be a smooth proper connected curve of genus $g$ over an
algebraically closed field $k$. We construct the Picard scheme of $C$
and show that its component of degree zero, the Jacobian $J(C)$, is an
abelian variety of dimension $g$ with $J(C)(k)=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)$
(Theorem [sk-thm-picard-main]). This is used in two places. First, by
the Kummer sequence, $H^i(C,\mu_n)$ is computed from the kernel and
cokernel of multiplication by $n$ on $\operatorname{Pic}(C)$
(Appendix “Cohomology of curves”). Second, over a finite field the Tate
module of the Jacobian controls the action of Frobenius on $H^1$
(Proposition [sk-prop-wrp-h1-no-fixed]).
The construction starts from the observation that an invertible sheaf
of degree $g$ with a one-dimensional space of sections is determined by
its divisor of zeros. Such sheaves are therefore parametrized by an open subscheme of
the Hilbert scheme of effective divisors of degree $g$, and translates
of this chart cover the Picard functor. The construction uses only
coherent cohomology and descent, and the fppf topology is used only to
define the Picard functor.
