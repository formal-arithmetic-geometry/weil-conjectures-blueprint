The open chart of Proposition [sk-thm-picard-f-representable] is an open
subscheme of a Hilbert scheme of divisors. Its translates by invertible
sheaves defined over $k$ cover the Picard functor, by
Lemma [sk-thm-picard-twist], and glue to the Picard scheme, whose
component of degree zero is the Jacobian (Theorem [sk-thm-picard-main]).
Local properties, representability and smoothness of dimension $g$, are
read off from the charts. Properness is global and cannot be seen on an
open cover. It comes from the Abel map $H_g\to\operatorname{Pic}^g_{C/k}$,
$D\mapsto\mathcal O_C(D)$, which is surjective because Riemann–Roch
gives every invertible sheaf of degree $g$ a nonzero section, so that
$\operatorname{Pic}^g_{C/k}$ is the image of a proper irreducible scheme.
For an elliptic curve the Abel map $C=H_1\to\operatorname{Pic}^1_{C/k}$ is
an isomorphism, and translation by $\mathcal O_C(-P)$ identifies $C$ with
its own Jacobian. The tangent space of the Jacobian at the origin is
computed by deforming the trivial sheaf over the dual numbers.
