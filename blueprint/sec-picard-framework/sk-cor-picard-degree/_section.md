For $C/k$ as in [sk-thm-picard-main] ($k$ algebraically closed, $C$ a
smooth proper connected curve of genus $g$), there is an exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow J(C)(k)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(C)
\xrightarrow{\ \deg\ }\Z\longrightarrow0.
$$

A choice $P\in C(k)$ splits it by $d\mapsto\cO_C(dP)$. The kernel and the
degree map are intrinsic; the splitting depends on $P$.
