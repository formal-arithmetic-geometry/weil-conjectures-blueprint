Let $A\to A'$ be a faithfully flat ring map and $M$ an $A$-module.
Then the augmented Amitsur (Cech) complex

$$
0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow M\otimes_AA'
\longrightarrow M\otimes_AA'\otimes_AA'\longrightarrow\cdots
$$

is exact.
