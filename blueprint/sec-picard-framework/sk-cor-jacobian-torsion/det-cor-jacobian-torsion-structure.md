title = "The group structure of $A(k)[n]$"
uses = ["det-cor-jacobian-torsion-kernel", "sk-cor-av-commutative", "sk-prop-av-divisible-structure"]

Let $k$ be algebraically closed, let $A/k$ be an abelian variety of
dimension $g$, and let $n>0$ be invertible in $k$. Then there is an
isomorphism of groups

$$
A[n](k)=A(k)[n]\simeq(\Z/n\Z)^{2g}.
$$

The set of such isomorphisms is a torsor under
$\mathrm{GL}_{2g}(\Z/n\Z)$; the argument below produces no distinguished
element of it, which is the sense in which the isomorphism is not
canonical. In particular $J(C)[n](k)\simeq(\Z/n\Z)^{2g}$ for $C/k$ a smooth
proper connected curve of genus $g$.
