In the notation of [def-sc-picard-functor], the following holds. The statements that the
$\operatorname{Pic}^d_{C/k}$ decompose
$\operatorname{Pic}_{C/k}$ as a disjoint union of open and closed
subfunctors, and that $\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C/k}$ is represented by an
open and closed subgroup scheme, are
[prop-pd-picard-degree-decomposition]. The statement that
$\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C/k}(k)=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)$ for
$k$ algebraically closed (more generally separably closed) is part of
[thm-sc-theorem-of-the-base] in the Jacobian part of the
smooth-curve material (the Jacobian chapter). None of these statements is
part of the definition.
