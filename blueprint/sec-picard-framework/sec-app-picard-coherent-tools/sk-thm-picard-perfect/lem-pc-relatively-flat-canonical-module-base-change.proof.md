Whether a map in the derived category of
$\mathcal O_T$-modules is an isomorphism can be checked on its cohomology
sheaves, hence on the members of an open cover of $T$. Both $Lg^*Rf_*$ and
$Rf_{T,*}$ commute with restriction to open subschemes of $S$ and $T$. So it
suffices to treat affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}(B)\subseteq T$ mapping
into affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}(A)\subseteq S$. There $f$ is
quasi-compact and separated over an affine base, so $X$ has a finite cover
by affine opens, and the base-change map is identified with the Čech
comparison of [lem-pc-rf-base-change-flat-module]. The flatness of $\mathcal G$ over $S$ also gives
$Lg'^*\mathcal G=g'^*\mathcal G$ for the projection $g':X_T\to X$, since
locally $\mathcal G\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal O_{X_T}=
\mathcal G\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal O_S}\mathcal O_T$.
