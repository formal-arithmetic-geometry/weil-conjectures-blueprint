The assertion is Zariski-local on $X$ and on $S$, so we may assume
$S=\operatorname{Spec}R$ and $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $A$ a smooth
$R$-algebra of relative dimension one; then $\Omega^1_{X/S}$ is the
sheaf associated with $\Omega^1_{A/R}$. By the local structure of
smooth algebras (commutative algebra: locally, a smooth $R$-algebra of
relative dimension $n$ has a presentation
$A=R[x_1,\dots,x_{n+c}]/(f_1,\dots,f_c)$ with Jacobian minor
$\det(\partial f_i/\partial x_{n+j})_{i,j\le c}$ invertible in $A$, and
then

$$
\Omega^1_{A/R}=A^{\oplus(n+c)}\big/(df_1,\dots,df_c)
$$

is free of rank $n$, generated by $dx_1,\dots,dx_n$ – the Jacobian
criterion), $\Omega^1_{A/R}$ is free of rank one on a neighbourhood of
each point, i.e. $\Omega^1_{X/S}$ is invertible. For a smooth connected
curve $C$ of dimension one over $k$, $C$ is irreducible (smooth, hence
normal, and connected), so it is smooth of relative dimension one over
$\operatorname{Spec}k$, and the first part applies.
