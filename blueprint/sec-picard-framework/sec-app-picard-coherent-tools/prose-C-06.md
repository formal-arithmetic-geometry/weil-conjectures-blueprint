This is coherent duality and the Riemann–Roch theorem for smooth curves
[Stacks, Tag 0B5B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B5B), where the duality is given by the residue trace on
differentials. For the displayed vanishing, recall that a nonzero
section of an invertible sheaf on an integral curve has an effective
divisor of zeros, so an invertible sheaf of negative degree has no
nonzero section. If $\deg\mathcal L>2g-2$, then
$\omega_{C/k}\otimes\mathcal L^{-1}$ has negative degree, so
$H^1(C,\mathcal L)=0$ by duality, and the dimension formula follows from
Riemann–Roch.
