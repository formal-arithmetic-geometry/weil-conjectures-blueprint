title = "Why a Jacobian over the ground field"

To compare the Galois action on the cohomology of a curve over $\bar k$
with a Tate module, we need the Jacobian over the ground field $k$.
Since the curve need not have a $k$-point, we construct it from the
fppf Picard sheaf rather than by rigidification.
