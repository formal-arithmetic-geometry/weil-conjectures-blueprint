Let $f:X\to S$ be an affine morphism and $\mathcal{F}$ a quasi-coherent
$\mathcal{O}_X$-module. Then $R^if_*\mathcal{F}=0$ for all $i>0$, and $f_*\mathcal{F}$ is
quasi-coherent.
