Let $Y$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme] and fix the universes $\mathcal U\in\mathcal V$ of the size convention [def-weil-size-convention]. The *small pro-etale site* $Y_{\mathrm{proet}}$ is the following site. Its objects are the weakly etale morphisms [def-weil-weakly-etale-morphism] $U\to Y$ with $U$ a $\mathcal U$-small scheme; its morphisms are morphisms of schemes over $Y$ (each of them is itself weakly etale: if $g$ and $g\circ h$ are weakly etale, so is $h$). A family $\{U_i\to U\}_{i\in I}$ of morphisms of $Y_{\mathrm{proet}}$ is a covering family if the underlying family of morphisms of schemes is a pro-etale covering [def-weil-pbc-proetale-covering], equivalently an fpqc covering [def-weil-pbc-fpqc-covering]. The Grothendieck topology of $Y_{\mathrm{proet}}$ is the one generated by these covering families (a sieve on $U$ covers if it contains a covering family).
