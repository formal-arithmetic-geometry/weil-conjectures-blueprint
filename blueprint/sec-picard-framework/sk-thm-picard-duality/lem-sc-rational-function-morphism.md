Let $C$ be a proper smooth connected curve over
$k$ and $f\in k(C)\setminus k$ ([def-sc-function-field]).
Then $f$ defines a morphism

$$
\pi_f:C\to\mathbf P^1_k
$$

with $\pi_f^*t=f$, where $t$ is the standard coordinate on
$\mathbf P^1$.
