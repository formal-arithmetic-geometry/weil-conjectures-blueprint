*Definition (sketch).*

For $X\to S$ and $d\ge0$,

$$
\Hilb^d_{X/S}(T)\;=\;\Bigl\{Z\subseteq X_T:\ Z\to T\ \text{finite,
flat, locally of finite presentation of degree }d\Bigr\}.
$$
