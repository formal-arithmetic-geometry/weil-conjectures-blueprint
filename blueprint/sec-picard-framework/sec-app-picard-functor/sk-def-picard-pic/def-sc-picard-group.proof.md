By [lem-pc-tensor-dual-invertible], tensor products and duals of
invertible modules are invertible, so the law is well defined on
isomorphism classes. Associativity, commutativity and the unit property
of $\otimes$ are the standard properties of the tensor product of
quasi-coherent modules. By [lem-pc-tensor-dual-invertible] the evaluation
$\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal L^{\vee}\to\mathcal O_C$ is an isomorphism, so
$\mathcal L^{\vee}$ is an inverse of $\mathcal L$.
