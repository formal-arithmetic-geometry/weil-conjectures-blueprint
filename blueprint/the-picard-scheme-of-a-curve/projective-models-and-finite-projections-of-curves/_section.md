Every projective curve over an infinite field admits a finite morphism to
$\mathbf P^1$ [lem-pc-projective-curve-finite-projection], which is what
transports coherent finiteness from the line to the curve. The route:
standard graded algebras [def-pc-standard-graded-algebra] and
$\operatorname{Proj}$ [def-pc-proj] give a coordinate model of an integral
projective scheme [lem-pc-projective-coordinate-model]; avoiding finitely
many proper subspaces [lem-pc-finite-linear-avoidance] produces two linear
forms without a common zero [lem-pc-projective-curve-linear-pair]; those
define a morphism to $\mathbf P^1$ [def-pc-linear-pair-morphism] which
glues [lem-pc-linear-pair-morphism-gluing],
[rem-ptr-pc-linear-pair-morphism-16] and is finite by a degree bound
[lem-pc-linear-pair-graded-finite],
[lem-pc-linear-pair-morphism-finite].
