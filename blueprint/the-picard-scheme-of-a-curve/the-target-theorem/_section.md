The conclusion of the chapter: over a separably closed field the Picard
functor of a smooth projective curve is representable by a group scheme
[thm-pc-picard-representable]. Two remarks close the chapter — one on the
meaning of "curve" and on why the chain is stated over separably closed
fields [rem-pc-conventions], one on where the group structure comes from
[rem-pc-group-structure].
