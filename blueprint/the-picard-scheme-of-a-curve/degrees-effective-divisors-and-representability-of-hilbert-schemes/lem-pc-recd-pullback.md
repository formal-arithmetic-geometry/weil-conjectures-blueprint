Let $f:X\to S$ be a flat morphism of finite presentation, let $D\subset X$
be a relative effective Cartier divisor
[def-pc-relative-effective-cartier] on $X/S$, and let $g:T\to S$ be a
morphism. Then $D_T:=D\times_ST$ is a relative effective Cartier divisor on
$X_T/T$ and, with $g_X:X_T\to X$,
$$
\mathcal{O}_{X_T}(D_T)\cong g_X^*\mathcal{O}_X(D).
$$

## Proof

$D\to S$ is flat (part of being a relative effective Cartier divisor) and
of finite presentation (as a closed subscheme of the finitely presented $X$
with the Cartier property: the ideal sheaf is locally generated by one
element, which gives locally $D=\operatorname{Spec}(A/(f))$ of finite
presentation over the affine base); hence the base change $D_T\to T$ is
flat and of finite presentation. It is the closed subscheme
$D\times_ST\subseteq X\times_ST=X_T$ (closed immersions are stable under
base change and the base change of a closed subscheme followed by the
projection is the closed subscheme of the product), with ideal sheaf the
pullback $g_X^*\mathcal{I}_D$ ([lem-pc-closed-imm-base-change]): as
$\mathcal{I}_D$ is an invertible ideal sheaf, $g_X^*\mathcal{I}_D$ is
invertible, so $D_T$ is an effective Cartier divisor on $X_T$ (the pullback
of a locally principal ideal is locally principal, and locally free of rank
$1$ base changes to locally free of rank $1$). As $D_T\to T$ is flat, $D_T$
is a relative effective Cartier divisor.

For the module: by [lem-pc-divisor-line-bundle-properties],
$$
\mathcal{O}_{X_T}(D_T)=(g_X^*\mathcal{I}_D)^\vee=g_X^*(\mathcal{I}_D^\vee)=g_X^*\mathcal{O}_X(D)
$$
(the pullback of a dual is the dual of the pullback, i.e. $g_X^*$ commutes
with $\mathcal{H}om$ of the pair $(\mathcal{I}_D,\mathcal{O}_X)$ — valid
for $\mathcal{I}_D$ invertible by the local trivialization check; see also
Divisors, Lemma 31.19.1, [Tag
056Q](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/056Q)).
