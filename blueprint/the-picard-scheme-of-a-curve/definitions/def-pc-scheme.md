A *scheme* is a locally ringed space in the sense of Stacks Project,
Schemes, Definition 26.9.1 ([Tag
01IJ](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01IJ)); $\mathit{Sch}$ denotes
the category of schemes, and a *morphism of schemes* is a morphism in
$\mathit{Sch}$. A scheme is *affine* if it is isomorphic to
$\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ for a commutative ring $A$; an *open subscheme* of
$X$ is a subscheme whose underlying topological space is open in $X$.
