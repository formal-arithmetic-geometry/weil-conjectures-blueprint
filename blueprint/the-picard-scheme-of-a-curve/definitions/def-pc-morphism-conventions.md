All morphism-theoretic notions used below are the ones already introduced
in the ambient-foundations chapter: *quasi-compact*, *quasi-separated*,
*separated*, *locally of finite type* and *of finite type*, *locally of
finite presentation* and *of finite presentation*, *surjective*, *open
immersion*, *closed immersion*, *immersion*, *universally closed*,
*proper*, *finite*, *integral*, *flat*, *unramified*, *etale*, *geometric
fibre* and *geometric point*. One further notion is fixed here:
*projective* [def-pc-projective-morphism].
