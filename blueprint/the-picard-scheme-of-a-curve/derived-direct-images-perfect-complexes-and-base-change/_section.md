The homological input for the representability argument. The projection
formula [lem-pc-projection-formula] and the Čech computation of $Rf_*$
[lem-pc-cech-resolution] are the two basic tools; together they give
derived base change for a relatively flat module
[lem-pc-rf-base-change-flat-module].

Perfectness is then established in two steps: a purely algebraic criterion
over a Noetherian ring [lem-pc-noetherian-flat-complex-perfect], and its
geometric consequence for a projective family
[lem-pc-projective-noetherian-rf-perfect], with arbitrary base change as a
corollary [cor-pc-projective-noetherian-rf-perfect-base-change]. The
general proper case [lem-pc-rf-perfect-base-change] is a recorded classical
input (Stacks, Tag 0B91), not decomposed. Finally, the locus where a
perfect complex is a locally free module in one degree is open
[lem-pc-perfect-locus], hence so is the locus where $Rf_{T,*}\mathcal F_T$
is invertible [lem-pc-flat-rf-open] — the openness fed into the
representability criterion.
