In the notation of [def-pc-divisor-line-bundle], the *associated invertible
module* is
$$
\mathcal{O}_S(D):=\mathcal{H}om_{\mathcal{O}_S}(\mathcal{I}_D,\mathcal{O}_S)=\mathcal{I}_D^\vee .
$$
The properties of this construction (invertibility, behaviour under flat
base change, coincidence with the divisor module of the earlier chapter for
finite unions of closed points) are
[lem-pc-divisor-line-bundle-properties] and
[lem-pc-divisor-line-bundle-agrees-sc].
