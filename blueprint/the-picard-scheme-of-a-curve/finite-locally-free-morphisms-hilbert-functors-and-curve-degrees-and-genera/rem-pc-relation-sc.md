The curve-calculation chapter uses a slightly coarser notion: for a field
$k$, a *curve over $k$* there is a nonempty separated scheme of finite type
over $k$ of dimension at most $1$. A smooth projective curve
[def-pc-smooth-projective-curve] in our sense is in particular a smooth
curve in that sense [lem-pc-curves-are-sc]; conversely, for smooth proper
one-dimensional curves the two notions coincide.
