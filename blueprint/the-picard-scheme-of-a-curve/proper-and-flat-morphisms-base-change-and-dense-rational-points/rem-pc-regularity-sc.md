The local regularity facts used in this blueprint are already decomposed in
the curve-calculation chapter: a smooth curve over a field has regular,
hence domain, local rings; a regular local ring of dimension $1$ is a
discrete valuation ring; and a closed point of a smooth curve is an
effective Cartier divisor with ideal generated by one non-zero-divisor.
They apply in our situation because a smooth projective curve
[def-pc-smooth-projective-curve] over a field is in particular a *smooth
curve* in that chapter's sense (nonempty, separated, of finite type,
dimension $\le1$; see [rem-pc-relation-sc]), and every base change $X_K$ is
again such a curve (the base-change lemma there, and
[prop-pc-XK-integral]).
