*Regularity on curves.* Three remarks record what is imported rather than
reproved [rem-pc-regularity-sc], [rem-pc-regular-normal],
[rem-pc-closed-point-cartier-sc]. Closed points with finite separable
residue field are dense on a smooth scheme
[lem-pc-dense-separable-points], so over a separably closed field the
rational points are dense [lem-pc-rational-points-dense].

*Morphisms and base change.* Base change along a purely inseparable
extension is a homeomorphism [lem-pc-homeo-pure-insep]; projective implies
proper [lem-pc-projective-proper]; a projective embedding gives an ample
bundle [lem-pc-very-ample-ample]; closed immersions and their ideal sheaves
base-change [lem-pc-closed-imm-base-change]; proper and smooth are stable
under base change [lem-pc-proper-base-change]; smooth implies flat
[lem-pc-smooth-flat]; flatness is transitive [lem-pc-flat-compose] and
automatic over a field [lem-pc-flat-field]; and dimension is unchanged
under extension of the ground field [lem-pc-dim-base-change].

*Import note.* A `proof` environment stands in the source immediately
before [lem-pc-proper-base-change] with no statement of its own and with
text identical to that lemma's proof — a duplication artifact of the
conversion. It is not imported as an object.
