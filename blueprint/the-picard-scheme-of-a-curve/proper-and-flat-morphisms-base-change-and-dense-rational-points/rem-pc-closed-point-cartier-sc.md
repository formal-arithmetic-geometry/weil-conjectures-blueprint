For the proofs below we use the following consequence of the local theory
(for smooth curves this is the closed-point lemma of the divisors chapter;
in general: Stacks Project, Varieties, Lemma 33.43.8, [Tag
0B8Y](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B8Y)): a closed point $P$ of a
smooth curve [def-pc-curve] over a field has invertible ideal sheaf, i.e.
is an effective Cartier divisor, and $\mathcal{O}_{C,P}$ is a discrete
valuation ring.
