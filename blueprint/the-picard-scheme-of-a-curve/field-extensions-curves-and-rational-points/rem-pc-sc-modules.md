The notions of *invertible module* (and its dual), *tensor product of
modules* and *Picard group* used below are those already introduced in the
curve-calculation chapter, together with the duals lemma and the group law.
The basic properties that we will use are:

- an invertible module is closed under duals and tensor products
  [lem-pc-tensor-dual-invertible];
- $\operatorname{Pic}(S)$ is trivial for $S=\operatorname{Spec}(k)$, $k$ a
  field [lem-pc-pic-of-field].
