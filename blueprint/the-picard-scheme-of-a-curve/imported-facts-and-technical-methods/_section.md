Čech machinery for quasi-coherent modules. The Amitsur complex of a
faithfully flat ring map is exact [lem-pc-cech-exact], as is the Čech
complex of a standard affine covering [lem-pc-affine-cech-complex]; hence
quasi-coherent modules have no higher cohomology on an affine
[lem-pc-affine-vanishing], the Čech complex of an affine covering computes
cohomology [lem-pc-cech-computes], affine morphisms have no higher direct
images [lem-pc-affine-morphism-hi], and for a quasi-compact quasi-separated
morphism the higher direct images vanish above a bound that survives base
change [lem-pc-finite-support-hi].
