*Fields.* Over a separably closed field every finite separable extension
is trivial [lem-pc-sepclosed-finite-sep-trivial],
[rem-ptr-pc-separably-closed-1]; a finite extension of an algebraically
closed field is trivial [lem-pc-finite-extension-alg-closed]; and the
algebraic closure of a separably closed field is purely inseparable over
it [lem-pc-kbar-pure-insep].

Global sections of a proper variety form a finite field extension, equal to
$k$ in the geometrically integral case
[lem-pc-h0-proper-variety]; for a proper geometrically reduced scheme
$H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=k$ is equivalent to geometric connectedness
[lem-pc-h0-geom-connected].
