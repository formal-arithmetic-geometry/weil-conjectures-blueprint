Let $k$ be a separably closed field [def-pc-separably-closed], let $X$ be a
smooth projective curve [def-pc-smooth-projective-curve] of genus $g$
[def-pc-genus] over $k$ with a $k$-rational point $\sigma$
[def-pc-rational-point]. Let $T$ be a scheme over $k$ and let
$\mathcal{L}\in F(T)\subseteq\operatorname{Pic}_{X/k,\sigma}(T)$ (with $F$ as
in [lem-pc-f-open-subfunctor]). Let $\mathcal{N}$ be an invertible
$\mathcal{O}_T$-module with $Rf_{T,*}\mathcal{L}\cong\mathcal{N}[0]$, and let
$s$ be the section of
$\mathcal{L}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X_T}}f_T^*\mathcal{N}^{-1}$ corresponding
to the adjunction map $f_T^*\mathcal{N}\to\mathcal{L}$ (via
$\operatorname{Hom}(f_T^*\mathcal{N},\mathcal{L})\cong H^0(X_T,\mathcal{L}\otimes f_T^*\mathcal{N}^{-1})$,
the tensor–Hom adjunction, $\mathcal{N}$ invertible). Then the zero scheme
[def-pc-zero-scheme] $D:=Z(s)$ is a relative effective Cartier divisor
[def-pc-relative-effective-cartier] on $X_T/T$ which is finite locally free
of degree $g$ over $T$.

## Proof

$\mathcal{M}:=\mathcal{L}\otimes f_T^*\mathcal{N}^{-1}$ is an invertible
module on $X_T$ and $s\in H^0(X_T,\mathcal{M})$. By [def-pc-zero-scheme] the
ideal sheaf $\mathcal{I}_{Z(s)}=\operatorname{im}(s^\vee)$ is locally
generated by one element, i.e. $D=Z(s)$ is a locally principal closed
subscheme of $X_T$. Also $X_T\to T$ is smooth (base change,
[lem-pc-proper-base-change]), hence flat ([lem-pc-smooth-flat]), of finite
presentation, and of relative dimension $1$ (fibres are base changes of $X$
by fields, of dimension $1$ by [lem-pc-dim-base-change]).

We verify that every fibre $D_t\subset X_t$ ($t\in T$,
$X_t=X_{\kappa(t)}$) is an effective Cartier divisor on $X_t$; then $D$ is
a relative effective Cartier divisor by [lem-pc-recd-locally-principal].

Let $\kappa=\kappa(t)$ and pass to the fibre: the base change of
$Rf_{T,*}\mathcal{L}\cong\mathcal{N}[0]$ along
$\operatorname{Spec}\kappa\to T$ gives
([lem-pc-rf-base-change-flat-module])
$Rf_{\kappa,*}\mathcal{L}_t\cong\mathcal{N}_t[0]$ with $\mathcal{N}_t$ an
invertible $\mathcal{O}_\kappa$-module, i.e. a $1$-dimensional
$\kappa$-vector space. (The hypotheses apply: $X_t\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa$
is proper of finite presentation and $\mathcal{L}_t$ is of finite
presentation and flat over $\kappa$.) Hence
$H^0(X_t,\mathcal{L}_t)\cong\mathcal{N}_t$ and $H^1(X_t,\mathcal{L}_t)=0$
(and $H^i(X_t,\mathcal{L}_t)=0$ for $i\ge2$ by
[lem-pc-vanishing-above-dim], $\dim X_t=1$). In particular
$\dim_\kappa H^0(X_t,\mathcal{L}_t)=1$.

The same analysis for $\mathcal{O}$: $X_t$ is a smooth projective curve
over $\kappa$ with
$H^0(X_t,\mathcal{O})=H^0(X,\mathcal{O})\otimes_k\kappa=\kappa$
([lem-pc-flat-base-change-cohom], [lem-pc-rational-hyp] and
[prop-pc-XK-integral]) and
$\dim_\kappa H^1(X_t,\mathcal{O})=\dim_\kappa(H^1(X,\mathcal{O})\otimes_k\kappa)=g$
([lem-pc-flat-base-change-cohom], the genus being $\dim_kH^1(X,\mathcal{O})$,
[def-pc-genus]); by [lem-pc-vanishing-above-dim] the higher cohomology of
both vanishes. Therefore
$$
\deg(\mathcal{L}_t)=\chi(X_t,\mathcal{L}_t)-\chi(X_t,\mathcal{O}_t)=1-(1-g)=g
$$
([def-pc-degree-line-bundle]; $\chi$ is defined by
[def-pc-euler-characteristic]; the finite-dimensionality and the finiteness
of the sum are [lem-pc-coherent-hi]).

The section $s_t\in H^0(X_t,\mathcal{M}_t)$ with
$\mathcal{M}_t=\mathcal{L}_t\otimes f_t^*\mathcal{N}_t^{-1}$ is non-zero: by
the tensor–Hom adjunction applied to the fibre data we have
$H^0(X_t,\mathcal{M}_t)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{N}_t,H^0(X_t,\mathcal{L}_t))\cong\kappa$
and $s_t$ corresponds to the identity endomorphism of $\mathcal{N}_t$. Since
$X_t$ is integral ([prop-pc-XK-integral] — the base change of a smooth
projective curve over the separably closed field $k$ is integral), a
non-zero global section $s_t$ of an invertible module has non-zero stalk
$s_{t,x}\neq0$ in $\mathcal{M}_{t,x}$ for every $x\in X_t$ (the restriction
map $\mathcal{M}_{t,x}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{M}_{t,\eta}$ to the generic
stalk is injective by integrality, and $s_{t,\eta}\neq0$). Hence the ideal
$\mathcal{I}_{Z(s_t),x}$ is generated by a non-zero element of the local
ring $\mathcal{O}_{X_t,x}$, which is regular of dimension $1$, hence a DVR;
in a DVR a non-zero element generates an invertible ideal. Therefore
$\mathcal{I}_{Z(s_t)}$ is invertible: $D_t$ is an effective Cartier
divisor.

Thus $D$ is a relative effective Cartier divisor on $X_T/T$ by
[lem-pc-recd-locally-principal]. Since $X_T\to T$ is smooth of relative
dimension $1$ and proper (base change of proper,
[lem-pc-proper-base-change]), [lem-pc-cartier-criterion] gives
$(2)\Rightarrow(1)$: $D\to T$ is finite locally free. Its degree: the
degree of the fibre divisor $D_t$ equals the degree of its associated
module,
$$
\deg(D_t)=\deg(\mathcal{O}_{X_t}(D_t))=\deg(\mathcal{M}_t)
=\deg(\mathcal{L}_t\otimes f_t^*\mathcal{N}_t^{-1})=\deg(\mathcal{L}_t)=g,
$$
by [lem-pc-degree-base-change] (the module $f_t^*\mathcal{N}_t^{-1}$ is a
pullback from a point, hence of degree $0$) and the computation above.
Since $D\to T$ is finite locally free and the fibre degree at $t$ equals
$\dim_{\kappa(t)}\Gamma(D_t,\mathcal{O}_{D_t})=\deg(D_t)=g$, the morphism
$D\to T$ is finite locally free of degree $g$
([def-pc-finite-locally-free]), as claimed.
