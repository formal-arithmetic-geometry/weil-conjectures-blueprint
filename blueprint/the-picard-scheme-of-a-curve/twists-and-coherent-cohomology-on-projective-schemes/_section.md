*Cohomology of schemes.* Flat base change for higher direct images
[lem-pc-flat-base-change-cohom]. The twisting modules on projective space
[def-pc-projective-space-twist] — well defined by the cocycle rule
[lem-pc-twist-cocycle], multiplicative [lem-pc-twist-tensor], trivial in
degree zero [lem-pc-twist-zero], [rem-ptr-pc-projective-space-twist-17] —
have the explicit monomial cohomology
[lem-pc-projective-space-twist-cohomology]. Clearing denominators
[lem-pc-section-clearing-denominators] presents every coherent module as a
quotient of a finite sum of twists
[lem-pc-projective-space-twist-quotient]; with the vanishing above $n$
[lem-pc-projective-space-cohomology-bound] this gives coherent finiteness
[lem-pc-projective-space-coherent-finiteness] and Serre vanishing
[lem-pc-projective-space-serre-vanishing] on $\mathbf P^n$, hence on any
projective scheme by a closed immersion
[lem-pc-closed-immersion-coherent-cohomology],
[lem-pc-projective-coherent-finiteness],
[lem-pc-projective-coherent-higher-images], and on a curve
[lem-pc-curve-coherent-finiteness]. The section closes with the general
statements the chapter quotes: coherent higher direct images
[lem-pc-coherent-hi], vanishing above the dimension
[lem-pc-vanishing-above-dim] and Serre vanishing
[lem-pc-serre-vanishing].
