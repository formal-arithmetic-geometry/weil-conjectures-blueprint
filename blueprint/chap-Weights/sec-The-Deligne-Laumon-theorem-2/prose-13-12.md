For a separated $k_0$-scheme of finite type $X_0$, we write
$D_{\leq w}(X_0)$ for the category of bounded constructible Weil
complexes $K$ on $X_0$ of weights at most $w$ in the sense of
Definition [definition-weights], that is, such that $\mathcal H^i(K)$ is
mixed of weights at most $w+i$ for every $i$. The Deligne–Laumon theorem says that
for a separated morphism of finite type $f:X_0\to Y_0$, the functor $Rf_!$
maps $D_{\leq w}(X_0)$ into $D_{\leq w}(Y_0)$. The shift by $i$ makes
this one statement for all degrees: for the structure morphism of $X_0$,
one condition on $R\Gamma_c(X,K)$ bounds all the groups $H^n_c(X,K)$ at
once. Moreover, by Proposition [proposition-weights-under-usual-operations],
replacing $K$ by $K[r]$ only moves the bound by $r$. The proof occupies
Chapter [chap-The-Deligne-Laumon-theorem-in-general] and, for a single curve, the sections
before it, and Corollary [corollary-purity-cohomology-smooth-proper-variety]
deduces from the theorem the purity of the cohomology of a smooth proper
variety.
