Let $X_0$ and $Y_0$ be separated schemes of finite type over $k_0=\Fq$,
and let $f:X_0\to Y_0$ be a separated morphism of finite type over $k_0$.
Fix $w\in\R$, a prime $\ell\nmid q$, and an embedding
$\iota:\Qbarl\hookrightarrow\C$. For bounded constructible Weil
complexes with coefficients in $\Qbarl$,

$$
Rf_!D_{\leq w}(X_0)\subset D_{\leq w}(Y_0).
$$
