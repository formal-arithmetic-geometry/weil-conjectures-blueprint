In terms of weights of complexes, Deligne's theorem says that $Rf_!$
preserves a class of complexes. The shift by $i$
in Definition [definition-weights] makes this statement uniform in the
degree. For the structure morphism of $X_0$, one condition on
$R\Gamma_c(X,K)$ bounds all the groups $H^n_c(X,K)$ at once. By
Proposition [proposition-weights-under-usual-operations], replacing $K$ by $K[r]$ only
moves the bound by $r$. The proof occupies Section [chap-Delignes-theorem-in-general] and,
for a single curve, the sections before it.
Corollary [corollary-purity-cohomology-smooth-proper-variety] deduces from the theorem
the purity of the cohomology of a smooth proper variety.

For a separated $k_0$-scheme of finite type $X_0$, we write
$D_{\leq w}(X_0)$ for the category of bounded constructible Weil
complexes $K$ on $X_0$ of weights at most $w$ in the sense of
Definition [definition-weights], that is, such that $\mathcal H^i(K)$ is
mixed of weights at most $w+i$ for every $i$.
