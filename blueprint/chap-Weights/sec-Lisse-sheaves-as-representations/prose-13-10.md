We apply the lemma to the [scheme of lattices](@lemma-lattices-lisse-e-sheaves) in a [lisse sheaf](@definition-lisse-sheaves). There the
valuative hypothesis says that a [lattice](@definition-lattice) defined at the generic point of
a valuation ring extends over the whole ring. This holds because,
locally, [lattices](@definition-lattice) form a constant sheaf on a discrete set.
