Let $X$ be a connected normal scheme of finite type over a field and $L$ a
[lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) $E_X$-module. Lemma [lemma-lattices-after-finite-stratification]
gives [lattices](@definition-lattice) in $L$ only on the strata of a finite stratification, and the
nodal curve of Section [sec-Lattices-after-stratification] shows that without normality a
global [lattice](@definition-lattice) need not exist. On a normal $X$, however, we find a [lattice](@definition-lattice) on
all of $X$, and as a consequence [lisse sheaves](@definition-lisse-sheaves) are the same as continuous
representations of the fundamental group. Here normality is used to show that
the étale $X$-scheme representing [lattices](@definition-lattice) in $L$ has a component that is
finite étale and surjective. Once we have such a component, we argue as
in Lemma [lemma-lattices-after-finite-stratification].
