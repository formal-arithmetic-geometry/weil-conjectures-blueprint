Let $X$ be a connected normal scheme of finite type over a field and $L$ a
lisse $E_X$-module. Lemma [lemma-lattices-after-finite-stratification]
gives lattices in $L$ only on the strata of a finite stratification, and the
nodal curve of Section [sec-Lattices-after-stratification] shows that without normality a
global lattice need not exist. On a normal $X$, however, we find a lattice on
all of $X$, and as a consequence lisse sheaves are the same as continuous
representations of the fundamental group. Here normality is used to show that
the étale $X$-scheme representing lattices in $L$ has a component that is
finite étale and surjective. Once we have such a component, we argue as
in Lemma [lemma-lattices-after-finite-stratification].
