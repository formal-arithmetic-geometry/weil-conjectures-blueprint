On a connected normal scheme a lattice exists globally. As a consequence,
lisse sheaves are the same as continuous representations of the fundamental
group. The stratified lattices of
Lemma [lemma-lattices-after-finite-stratification] do not give this, and the
nodal curve of Section [sec-Lattices-after-stratification] shows that normality is needed. The
proof has two steps. We show that the étale $X$-scheme representing lattices
in $L$ has a component that is finite étale and surjective. Then we argue as
in Lemma [lemma-lattices-after-finite-stratification].
