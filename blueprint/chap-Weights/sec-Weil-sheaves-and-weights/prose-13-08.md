At a closed point, each operation in the next lemma and proposition acts on
stalks by one of the constructions of the lemma: a subobject, a
quotient, a tensor product or a dual, or, for an inverse image, a
power of Frobenius. So the behaviour of weights is a matter of
eigenvalues. Mixedness needs in addition some bookkeeping to produce the
filtrations.
