This coefficient-extension statement is bypassed by the Lean route,
which works throughout with $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-coefficients;
no finite-extension mixedness statement is formalized here.

A constructible Weil $E$-sheaf on $\operatorname{Spec}k_0$
becomes mixed after extending scalars to a finite extension of $E$
containing the eigenvalues of Frobenius.
