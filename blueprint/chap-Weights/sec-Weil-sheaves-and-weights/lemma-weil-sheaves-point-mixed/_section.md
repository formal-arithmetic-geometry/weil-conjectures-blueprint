Every [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) [Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) on $\operatorname{Spec}k_0$ is
[mixed](@definition-weights). A [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) [Weil $E$-sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) on $\operatorname{Spec}k_0$
becomes [mixed](@definition-weights) after extending scalars to a finite extension of $E$
containing the eigenvalues of Frobenius.
