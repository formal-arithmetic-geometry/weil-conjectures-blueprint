In a basis adapted to the filtration, the matrix of $u$ is block upper
triangular, so its characteristic polynomial is the product of those
of the diagonal blocks. For the other constructions we may choose bases over
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ in which $u$ and $v$ are upper triangular. Then, in the induced tensor
basis and in the ordered wedge basis, the resulting matrices are upper
triangular with the stated diagonal entries. Finally, the matrix on the dual
is the inverse transpose.
