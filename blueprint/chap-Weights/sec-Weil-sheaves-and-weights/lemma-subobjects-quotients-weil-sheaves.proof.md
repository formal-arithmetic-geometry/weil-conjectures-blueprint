By Lemma [lemma-filtrations-spectra], the eigenvalues on a subobject or a
quotient occur among those of the whole. Hence subobjects and quotients of
[pure](@definition-weights) sheaves are [pure](@definition-weights). For [mixed](@definition-weights) sheaves, we intersect a [mixed](@definition-weights) filtration
with a subobject, or push it into a quotient. The graded pieces are then
subquotients of the original [pure](@definition-weights) pieces, hence [pure](@definition-weights) of the same
weights, and so the upper bound persists.
