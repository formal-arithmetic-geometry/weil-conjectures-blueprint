Deligne calls a complex $K$ pure of weight $w$ if $K$ has weights at most
$w$ and its Verdier dual $\mathbb D K$ has weights at most $-w$. Over a point
the two notions agree. In general neither implies the other, and we use ours
only for sheaves and over a point.

The cohomology groups of Theorem [theorem-weights-rf-shriek] are
Weil sheaves on $\operatorname{Spec}k_0$. For them, having weights at most $w$ is just a
condition on the eigenvalues of Frobenius. Indeed, by
Lemma [lemma-weil-sheaves-point-mixed] every Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf over a point is
mixed, and Lemma [lemma-bounding-weights-mixed-sheaf] reads the bound of a mixed
sheaf off its eigenvalues.
