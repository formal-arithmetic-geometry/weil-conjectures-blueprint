Deligne calls a complex $K$ pure of weight $w$ if $K$ has [weights at most](@definition-weights)
$w$ and its [Verdier dual](@definition-verdier-dual-evaluation) [$\mathbb D K$](@definition-verdier-dual-evaluation) has [weights at most](@definition-weights) $-w$. Over a point
the two notions agree. In general neither implies the other, and we use ours
only for sheaves and over a point.

The cohomology groups of Theorem [theorem-weights-rf-shriek] are
[Weil sheaves](@definition-weil-structure) on $\operatorname{Spec}k_0$. For them, having [weights at most](@definition-weights) $w$ is just a
condition on the eigenvalues of Frobenius. Indeed, by
Lemma [lemma-weil-sheaves-point-mixed] every [Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) over a point is
[mixed](@definition-weights), and Lemma [lemma-bounding-weights-mixed-sheaf] reads the bound of a [mixed](@definition-weights)
sheaf off its eigenvalues.
