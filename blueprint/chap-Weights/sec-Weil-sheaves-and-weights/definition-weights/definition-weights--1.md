Let $K$ be a constructible Weil sheaf on $X$, let $x$ be a closed point of
$X_0$, and let $\alpha$ be an eigenvalue of $\operatorname{Frob}_x$ on
$K_{\bar x}$. We say $\alpha$ has *$\iota$-weight $w$* if
$|\iota(\alpha)|=q_x^{w/2}$.
