A constructible Weil sheaf on $\operatorname{Spec}k_0$ is a
finite-dimensional vector space $V$ with an automorphism $F$, the
geometric Frobenius. In both cases we may assume that $V$ is defined over
a finite extension $E'$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ containing the eigenvalues of $F$. For
$w\in\mathbf R$ let $V_w\subset V$ be the sum of the generalized
eigenspaces of $F$ for the eigenvalues $\alpha$ with
$|\iota(\alpha)|=q^{w/2}$. These subspaces are $F$-stable, only finitely many are
nonzero, and $V=\bigoplus_wV_w$. Let $w_1<\dots<w_m$ be the weights that
occur. Then the subspaces $V_{w_1}\oplus\dots\oplus V_{w_j}$ form a finite
filtration by Weil subsheaves with quotients $V_{w_j}$, and $V_{w_j}$ is
pure of weight $w_j$.
