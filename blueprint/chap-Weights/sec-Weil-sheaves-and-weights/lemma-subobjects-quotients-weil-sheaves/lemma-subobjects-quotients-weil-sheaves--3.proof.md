Intersect a mixed filtration with a subobject and push the resulting filtration into a quotient. Each graded piece is a subquotient of an original pure graded piece. Clauses [lemma-subobjects-quotients-weil-sheaves--1] and [lemma-subobjects-quotients-weil-sheaves--2] show that each nonzero such piece is pure, of the weight of the piece it comes from, giving the required mixed filtration. Its weights are among the original ones, so an upper bound $w$ persists.

*Source:* the proof of Lemma [lemma-subobjects-quotients-weil-sheaves].
