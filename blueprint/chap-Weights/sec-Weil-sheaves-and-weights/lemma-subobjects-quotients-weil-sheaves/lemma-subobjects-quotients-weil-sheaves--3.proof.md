Intersect a mixed filtration with a subobject and push the resulting filtration into a quotient. Each graded piece is a subquotient of an original pure graded piece. Clauses [lemma-subobjects-quotients-weil-sheaves--1] and [lemma-subobjects-quotients-weil-sheaves--2] show that each nonzero such piece is pure, giving the required mixed filtration. The weights of these graded pieces are among the original ones, so an upper bound $w$ persists.

*Source:* the proof of Lemma [lemma-subobjects-quotients-weil-sheaves].
