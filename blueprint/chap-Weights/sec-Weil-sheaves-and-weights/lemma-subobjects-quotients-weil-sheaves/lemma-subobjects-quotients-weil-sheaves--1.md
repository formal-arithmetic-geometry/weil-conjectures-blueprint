Let $i:\mathcal G\hookrightarrow\mathcal F$ be a monomorphism of Weil
sheaves on a scheme locally of finite type over $k_0$.
If $\mathcal F$ is punctually $\iota$-pure of weight $w$, then so is
$\mathcal G$.
