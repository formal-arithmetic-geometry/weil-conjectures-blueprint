Subobjects and quotients of a [pure](@definition-weights) [Weil sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) of weight $w$ are [pure](@definition-weights) of
weight $w$. Subquotients of a [mixed](@definition-weights) sheaf are [mixed](@definition-weights), and a subquotient of
a sheaf that is [mixed](@definition-weights) of weights at most $w$ is [mixed](@definition-weights) of weights at most
$w$.
