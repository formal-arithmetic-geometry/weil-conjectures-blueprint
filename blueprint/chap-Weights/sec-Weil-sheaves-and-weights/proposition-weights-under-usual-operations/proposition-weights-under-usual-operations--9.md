The assertion about dual filtrations of mixed lisse sheaves is bypassed
by the Lean route. The informal assertion is that the dual of a mixed
lisse sheaf is mixed, with graded pieces the duals of the given ones,
of negated weights. No declaration in the inspected mainline states
this mixed-sheaf conclusion. The duality calculations used there are
instead at individual Frobenius stalks, as in
[proposition-weights-under-usual-operations--7].
