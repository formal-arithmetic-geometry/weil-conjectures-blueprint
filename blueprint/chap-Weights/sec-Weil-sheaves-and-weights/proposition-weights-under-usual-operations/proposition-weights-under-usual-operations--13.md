The scalar by which geometric Frobenius acts on the cyclotomic Tate
module is $q^{-1}$ in $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$.
Thus $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(1)$ has degree-one geometric Frobenius
$q^{-1}$; at a closed point $x$ its local Frobenius is $q_x^{-1}$.
