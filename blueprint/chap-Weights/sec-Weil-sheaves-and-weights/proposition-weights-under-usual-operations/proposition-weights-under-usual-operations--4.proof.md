The eigenvalues on a dual stalk are the reciprocals of the original nonzero eigenvalues by Lemma [lemma-filtrations-spectra]. Their absolute values therefore give the negative weight $-a$. The lisse hypothesis ensures that the sheaf dual has these dual stalks.
