Let $i:\mathcal G\hookrightarrow\mathcal F$ be a monomorphism of Weil
sheaves. If $\mathcal G$ and $\operatorname{coker}(i)$ are mixed of
weights at most $w$, then $\mathcal F$ is mixed of weights at most $w$.
