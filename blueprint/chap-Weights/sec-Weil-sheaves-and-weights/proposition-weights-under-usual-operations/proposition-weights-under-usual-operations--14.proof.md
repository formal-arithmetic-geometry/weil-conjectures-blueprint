By clause [proposition-weights-under-usual-operations--13], the twist $(r)$ has degree-one Frobenius $q^{-r}$. Apply clause [proposition-weights-under-usual-operations--12]: its weight contribution is $2\log_q(q^{-r})=-2r$.
