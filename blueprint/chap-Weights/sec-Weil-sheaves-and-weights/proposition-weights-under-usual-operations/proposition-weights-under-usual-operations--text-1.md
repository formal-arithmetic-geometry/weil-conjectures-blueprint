*Shared context.* Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell\nmid q$ be prime, and fix
$\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
Weights are real and have the normalization of [definition-weights].
Weil sheaves have coefficients in $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
The subobject, quotient and mixed-filtration assertions below are stated
on schemes locally of finite type over $k_0$; they do not require
separatedness or quasi-compactness. The pure tensor, pure inverse-image,
dual-stalk and pure constant-twist assertions hold on arbitrary
$k_0$-schemes. Mixed complexes are bounded constructible Weil complexes
on schemes locally of finite type over $k_0$.
