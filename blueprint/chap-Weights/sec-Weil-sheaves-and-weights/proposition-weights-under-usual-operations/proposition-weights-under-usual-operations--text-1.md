*Shared context.* Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell\nmid q$ be prime, and fix
$\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
Weights are real and have the normalization of [definition-weights].
Weil sheaves have coefficients in $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
The assertions below are stated on schemes locally of finite type over
$k_0$; they do not require separatedness or quasi-compactness. Mixed
complexes are bounded constructible Weil complexes on such schemes.
