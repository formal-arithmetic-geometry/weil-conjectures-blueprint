For a lisse Weil sheaf $\mathcal F$ and a closed point $x$, there is
a Frobenius-equivariant isomorphism
$$
(\mathcal F^\vee)_{\bar x}\simeq(\mathcal F_{\bar x})^\vee,
$$
where the Frobenius on the right is the inverse transpose. In
particular, if $\mathcal F$ is punctually pure of weight $a$, its dual
is punctually pure of weight $-a$.
