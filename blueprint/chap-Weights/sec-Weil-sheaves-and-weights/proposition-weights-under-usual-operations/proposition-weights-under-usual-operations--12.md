Let $b\in\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$ and put
$c=\lambda_\iota(b)=2\log_q|\iota(b)|$. If $\mathcal F$ is
punctually pure of weight $w$, then $\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal B_b$
is punctually pure of weight $w+c$. If $\mathcal F$ is mixed of
weights at most $w$, then $\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal B_b$ is mixed
of weights at most $w+c$.
