Let $\mathcal F$ be a lisse Weil sheaf and $\mathcal G$ a Weil sheaf.
If they are punctually $\iota$-pure of weights $a$ and $b$, respectively,
then $\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal G$ is punctually $\iota$-pure of
weight $a+b$.
