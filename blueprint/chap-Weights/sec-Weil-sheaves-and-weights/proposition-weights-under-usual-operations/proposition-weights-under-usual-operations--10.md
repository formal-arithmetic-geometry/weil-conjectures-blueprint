Let $f:X_0\to Y_0$ be a morphism of $k_0$-schemes. If $\mathcal F$
is punctually $\iota$-pure of weight $w$ on $Y_0$, then $f^*\mathcal F$
is punctually $\iota$-pure of weight $w$ on $X_0$.
