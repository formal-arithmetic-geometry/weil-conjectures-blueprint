By definition, $\mathcal H^j(K)$ has weights at most $w+j$. Since $\mathcal H^i(K[r])=\mathcal H^{i+r}(K)$, its bound is $w+i+r=(w+r)+i$, which is the bound defining weight at most $w+r$ for $K[r]$.
