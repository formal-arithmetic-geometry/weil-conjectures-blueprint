For an extension $0\to\mathcal F\to\mathcal G\to\mathcal H\to0$, start with a mixed filtration of $\mathcal F$ and append the inverse images in $\mathcal G$ of a mixed filtration of $\mathcal H$. The successive quotients are exactly those of these two filtrations, hence are pure.
