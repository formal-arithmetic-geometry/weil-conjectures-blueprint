Write mixedness with bound $w$ as the closure under finite filtrations
of the property of being pure of some weight $v\le w$, using
`AlgebraicGeometry.WeilSheaf.isMixedOfWeightsAtMost_iff_filtrationClosure`.
This property is stable under subobjects and quotients by
[proposition-weights-under-usual-operations--1] and
[proposition-weights-under-usual-operations--2]. The general closure
lemmas for finite filtrations then give the assertions: intersect the
filtration with a subobject, or take its images in a quotient.
For mixedness without a specified bound, choose a bound and keep it.
