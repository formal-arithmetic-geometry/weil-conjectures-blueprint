The lemma does not say that a sheaf [punctually of weights](@definition-weights) $\le w$ is
[mixed](@definition-weights); the filtration has to be found. Nor need the filtration split:
weights see only the characteristic polynomials of Frobenius, so a
Jordan block and its semisimplification have the same weights. The
following example is a [mixed](@definition-weights) sheaf that is not [pure](@definition-weights) and whose filtration
does not split.
