The lemma does not say that a sheaf punctually of weights $\le w$ is
mixed; the filtration has to be found. Nor need the filtration split:
weights see only the characteristic polynomials of Frobenius, so a
Jordan block and its semisimplification have the same weights. The
following example is a mixed sheaf that is not pure and whose filtration
does not split.
