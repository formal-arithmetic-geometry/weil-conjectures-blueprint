Let $K$ be algebraically closed and let $V$, $u$, and the filtration
be as in [lemma-filtrations-spectra--1]. Write $E(u)$ for the multiset
of roots of $\det(T-u\mid V)$, and similarly for the endomorphisms
$u_i$ of the graded pieces. Then

$$
E(u)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}E(u_i).
$$

Thus the eigenvalues, with algebraic multiplicity, are the union of
the eigenvalues on the graded pieces.
