Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $V$ a finite-dimensional
$K$-vector space, and $u$ a linear endomorphism of $V$ with eigenvalues
$\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_d$, counted with algebraic multiplicity.
For every integer $r\geq0$, the eigenvalues of $\bigwedge^r u$ on
$\bigwedge^rV$ are

$$
\alpha_{i_1}\cdots\alpha_{i_r}
\qquad(1\leq i_1<\cdots<i_r\leq d),
$$

one for each choice of indices, with repetitions retained. For $r=0$
this is the single eigenvalue $1$; for $r>d$ the multiset is empty.
