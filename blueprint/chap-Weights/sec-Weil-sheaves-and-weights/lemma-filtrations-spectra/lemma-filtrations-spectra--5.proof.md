Choose bases in which $u$ and $v$ have upper triangular matrices
$A$ and $B$. In the tensor basis the matrix of $u\otimes v$ is the
Kronecker product of $A$ and $B$. With pairs of basis indices ordered
lexicographically, it is upper triangular: below the diagonal,
one of the two factors in the corresponding matrix entry vanishes.
The diagonal entry at $(i,j)$ is $A_{ii}B_{jj}$. The roots of each
characteristic polynomial are its diagonal entries with
multiplicity, giving all pairwise products as asserted.
