Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $V$ and $V'$ finite-dimensional
$K$-vector spaces, and $u:V\to V$ and $v:V'\to V'$ linear
endomorphisms. If their eigenvalues, counted with algebraic
multiplicity, are $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_d$ and
$\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_e$, then the eigenvalues of $u\otimes v$ on
$V\otimes_K V'$ are $\alpha_i\beta_j$ for all $1\leq i\leq d$ and
$1\leq j\leq e$, with repetitions retained.
