In the situation of [prop-weight-purity], for every
$0\leq i\leq2d$ there is a Frobenius-equivariant isomorphism

$$
H_c^i(X,\Qlb)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^i(X,\Qlb)
$$

of Frobenius modules,
between the
compactly supported and
ordinary cohomology groups.

## Proof

Clause (3) of [thm-weil-deligne-purity] supplies the canonical
comparison $H_c^i(X,\Qlb)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^i(X,\Qlb)$ in the situation
of [prop-weight-purity]; the two groups are the
[def-weil-ordinary-compact-cohomology] groups fixed there, and the
comparison is equivariant for the operator of
[def-weil-frobenius-module-weights].
