Let $V,T,Y,S$ be schemes, let $g:Y\to S$ be separated, and let
$v:V\to T$ be scheme-theoretically dominant. If $a,b:T\to Y$ satisfy
$g\circ a=g\circ b$ and $a\circ v=b\circ v$, then $a=b$.
