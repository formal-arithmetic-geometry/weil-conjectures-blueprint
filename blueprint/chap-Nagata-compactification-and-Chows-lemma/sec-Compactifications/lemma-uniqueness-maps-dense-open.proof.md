The equalizer of the two maps is a closed
subscheme of $T$, since it is the inverse image of the diagonal of $Y/S$.
Its ideal restricts to zero on $U$, because the two maps agree there.
As a section of $\mathcal O_T$ that vanishes on a scheme-theoretically
dense open is zero, the ideal is zero and the equalizer is all of $T$.
