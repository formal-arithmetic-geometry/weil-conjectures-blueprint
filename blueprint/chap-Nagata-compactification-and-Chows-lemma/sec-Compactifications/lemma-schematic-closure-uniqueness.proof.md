The scheme-theoretic image is the smallest closed subscheme through
which $i$ factors. Its ideal is
$\ker(\mathcal O_T\to i_*\mathcal O_U)$, which is quasi-coherent
because $i$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated. Its formation
commutes with flat base change $T'\to T$: pushforward along the
quasi-compact quasi-separated $i$ commutes with flat base change, and
pullback along a flat morphism is exact, so the pullback of the ideal is
the ideal of the base change [Stacks, [081I](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/081I)]. Restriction to an open of
$T$ is a special case. On an open
where the immersion is closed the image is therefore $U$ itself, and
this shows that $U$ is open in its image. By construction, a section of
$\mathcal O_{\overline U}$ vanishing on $U$ is zero, which is the
definition of schematic density. For these statements see
[Stacks, [01R8](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01R8), [01RG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01RG)].
