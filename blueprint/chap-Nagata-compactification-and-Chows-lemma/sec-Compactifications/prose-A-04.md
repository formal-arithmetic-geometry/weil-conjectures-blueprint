This is [Stacks, [0F41](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F41)], and we give the proof in
Section [sec-Proof-of-Nagatas-theorem]. Proper base change over an
arbitrary qcqs base uses the theorem in this generality. For schemes of
finite type over $k$ the Noetherian case of the proof suffices.

Density is a condition on structure sheaves. For instance, a nilpotent
section supported on the boundary is invisible on $X$, but it prevents
$X$ from being schematically dense. The condition does two things for
us. Morphisms between dense compactifications are unique and common
refinements exist
(Proposition [proposition-dense-compactifications-form-cofiltered-category]).
And passing to a dense compactification does not change the direct
image with proper support (Lemma [lemma-passing-schematic-closure]).
