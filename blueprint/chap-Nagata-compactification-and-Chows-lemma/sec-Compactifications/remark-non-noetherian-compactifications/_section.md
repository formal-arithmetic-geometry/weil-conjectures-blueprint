The same proof works for qcqs $\overline X$ whenever
$\overline X\setminus Z$ is quasi-compact. For a non-Noetherian
[compactification](@definition-compactification-refinement) this can fail, and then
Proposition [proposition-extension-zero-restriction-closed-subscheme] does not apply to pushforward along
the closed immersion on the [pro-étale site](@definition-small-pro-etale-site). The schematic closure and [dense
refinements](@definition-compactification-refinement) exist in the generality of Theorem [theorem-nagata-s-compactification-theorem].
