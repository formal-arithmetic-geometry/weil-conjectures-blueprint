For example, $\mathbf A^1_k\subset\mathbf P^1_k$ is a dense
compactification of $\mathbf A^1_k\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, since a
regular function on an open of $\mathbf P^1_k$ that vanishes on
$\mathbf A^1_k$ is zero. The theorem says that such a factorization
always exists.
