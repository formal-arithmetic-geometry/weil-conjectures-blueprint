Let $X$ be qcqs over $S$, let $U\subseteq X$ be a dense
quasi-compact open and let $Y\to S$ be proper. Every $S$-map
$f:U\to Y$ extends to an $S$-map $X'\to Y$ for some
$U$-admissible blow-up $X'\to X$.
