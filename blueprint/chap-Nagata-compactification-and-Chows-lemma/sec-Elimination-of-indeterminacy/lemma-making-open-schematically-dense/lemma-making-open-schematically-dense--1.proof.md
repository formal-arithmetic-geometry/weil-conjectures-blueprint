Write $U=T\setminus V(\mathcal I)$ and let $j:U\to T$ be the inclusion.
Choose an affine cover of $T$ on which $\mathcal I$ is generated by a
nonzerodivisor. On such an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, with
generator $a$, the intersection with $U$ is $D(a)$, and the restriction
map on sections is the localization map $R\to R[1/a]$. This map is
injective, since a section killed by a power of $a$ is zero. Thus the
kernel ideal of $j$ vanishes on the affine cover and hence on $T$,
which is the required schematic density.
