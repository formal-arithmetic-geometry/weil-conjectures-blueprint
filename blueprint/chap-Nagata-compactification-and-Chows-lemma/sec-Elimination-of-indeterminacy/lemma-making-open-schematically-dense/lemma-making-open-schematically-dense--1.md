Let $T$ be a scheme and let $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_T$ be an
invertible quasi-coherent ideal, so that locally on affine opens it is
generated by a nonzerodivisor. Then $T\setminus V(\mathcal I)$ is
schematically dense in $T$.
