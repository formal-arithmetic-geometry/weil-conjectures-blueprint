Let $X$ and $Y$ be $S$-schemes, with $Y\to S$ separated. A
*quasi-domination of $X$ over $Y$* consists of a dense open
$U\subseteq X$ and an $S$-map $f:U\to Y$ whose graph is closed in
$X\times_SY$.
