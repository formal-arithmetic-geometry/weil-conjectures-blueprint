The scheme $T_0$ is the normalization of $T$ in $Z$, that is, the
relative spectrum of the integral closure of $\mathcal O_T$ in the
direct image of $\mathcal O_Z$; it is affine over $T$ by
construction. By Zariski's Main Theorem in the form [Stacks, [02LR](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02LR)], locally on $T$
the canonical map from $Z$ to the normalization $T_0$ of $T$ in $Z$ is
an open immersion, and $T_0\to T$ is affine. These factorizations agree
on overlaps and glue to the stated factorization. Over an affine open of $T$ the scheme $T_0$ is
affine, so $Z$ is a quasi-compact open of an affine scheme there,
which means that $Z\to T$ is quasi-affine.
