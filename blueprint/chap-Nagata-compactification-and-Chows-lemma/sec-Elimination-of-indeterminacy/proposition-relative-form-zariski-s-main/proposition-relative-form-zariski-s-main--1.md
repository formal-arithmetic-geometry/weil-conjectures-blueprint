The Lean proof bypasses the separate existential factorization lemma
and uses the normalization directly to prove the following statement.

Let $f:Z\to T$ be separated, quasi-compact, locally quasi-finite
and locally of finite type. If $T$ is quasi-affine, then $Z$ is
quasi-affine.
