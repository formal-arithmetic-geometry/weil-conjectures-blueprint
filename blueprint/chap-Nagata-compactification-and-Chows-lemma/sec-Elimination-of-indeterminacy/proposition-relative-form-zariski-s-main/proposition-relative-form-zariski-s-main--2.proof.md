Let $U\subseteq T$ be an affine open. The restricted morphism
$f^{-1}(U)\to U$ is separated, quasi-compact, locally quasi-finite
and locally of finite type. Since $U$ is affine, it is quasi-affine,
and [proposition-relative-form-zariski-s-main--1] shows that
$f^{-1}(U)$ is quasi-affine. This is the defining condition for
$f$ to be quasi-affine.
