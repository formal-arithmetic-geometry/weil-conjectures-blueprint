When the target is not proper, the extension is defined only on an
open of the blow-up, and we can still say what it looks like. Suppose
the target is a separated map $W\to T$ of finite type that is an
isomorphism over $U$, and the section is its inverse over $U$. Then the
extension identifies a blow-up of $W$ with an open of a blow-up of $T$.
