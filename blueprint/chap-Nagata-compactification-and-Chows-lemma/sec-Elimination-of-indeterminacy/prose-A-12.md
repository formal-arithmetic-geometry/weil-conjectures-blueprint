Over a chart of $T'$ where $E$ has equation $a$ and $W\times_TT'$ is
affine with ring $B$, the ring of the strict transform is

$$
B/\{b\in B:\ a^nb=0\text{ for some }n\geq0\}.
$$

If $U$ is the complement of the center, this is exactly the schematic
closure of $W_U$ in $W\times_TT'$, the object that occurs in
the elimination of indeterminacy.
By [Stacks, [080E](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/080E)], the strict transform is the blow-up of $W$ in the
pulled-back ideal, so it is proper over $W$.

In the proofs below we construct blow-ups locally and one after
another. By the next lemma, finitely many such constructions combine
into a single $U$-admissible blow-up of $T$.
