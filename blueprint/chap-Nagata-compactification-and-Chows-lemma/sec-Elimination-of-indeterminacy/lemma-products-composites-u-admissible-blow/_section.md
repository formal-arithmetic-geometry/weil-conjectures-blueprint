Finitely many $U$-[admissible blow-ups](@definition-admissible-blow-up) of $T$ admit a common
$U$-[admissible blow-up](@definition-admissible-blow-up). A composite of two $U$-[admissible blow-ups](@definition-admissible-blow-up) is
again such a blow-up. A $U$-[admissible blow-up](@definition-admissible-blow-up) on a quasi-compact
open $V\subseteq T$ containing $U$ extends to a $U$-[admissible
blow-up](@definition-admissible-blow-up) of $T$.
