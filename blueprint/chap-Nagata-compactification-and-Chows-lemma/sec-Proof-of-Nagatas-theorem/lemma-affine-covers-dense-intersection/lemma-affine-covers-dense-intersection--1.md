Let $X$ be a scheme of finite type over a Noetherian scheme $S$, or
more generally any quasi-compact scheme with finitely many irreducible
components. There is a finite affine open cover
$X=\bigcup_{r=1}^m U_r$ such that every generic point of an irreducible
component of $X$ belongs to $\bigcap_{r=1}^m U_r$. In particular,
this intersection is dense in $X$.
