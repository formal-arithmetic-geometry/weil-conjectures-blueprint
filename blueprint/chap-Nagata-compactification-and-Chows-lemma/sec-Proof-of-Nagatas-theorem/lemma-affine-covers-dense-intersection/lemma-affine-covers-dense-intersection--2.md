Let $f:Y\to S$ be a quasi-compact morphism locally of finite type,
where $Y$ is a quasi-affine scheme. Then $f$ has a
compactification

$$
Y\xrightarrow{j}\overline Y\xrightarrow{p}S
$$

with $j$ a quasi-compact open immersion and $p$ proper.
