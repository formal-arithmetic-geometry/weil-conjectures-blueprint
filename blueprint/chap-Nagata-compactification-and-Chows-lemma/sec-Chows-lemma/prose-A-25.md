Let $f:X\to S$ be proper. In Section [sec-Proper-morphisms] proper base change is first proved for projective morphisms. By
Lemma [lemma-composition-cancellation] it then descends along a
proper surjection $\pi:\widetilde X\to X$ as soon as it holds for $\pi$
and for the composite $f\pi$. Chow's lemma says precisely that such a $\pi$ exists with both $\pi$ and $f\pi$ projective. Some modification of $X$ is unavoidable here, since there are proper varieties that are not
projective.
