Let $f:X\to S$ be proper with $S$ affine. There are a scheme
$\widetilde X$, a proper surjection $\pi:\widetilde X\to X$, a finite
set $N$ of homogeneous coordinates and a closed immersion

$$
i_S:\widetilde X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P(N;S)
$$

such that $f\pi=q_Si_S$, where $q_S:\mathbf P(N;S)\to S$ is the
projection. Here $\mathbf P(N;S)$ denotes projective space with
homogeneous coordinates indexed by $N$.
