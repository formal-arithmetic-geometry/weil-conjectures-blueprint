Let $f:X\to S$ be separated, let $N$ be a set of homogeneous
coordinates, and suppose that $\pi:T\to X$ and a closed immersion
$i_S:T\hookrightarrow\mathbf P(N;S)$ satisfy $f\pi=q_Si_S$.
Then the induced map

$$
i_X=(\pi,i_S):T\longrightarrow
X\times_S\mathbf P(N;S)=\mathbf P(N;X)
$$

is a closed immersion.
