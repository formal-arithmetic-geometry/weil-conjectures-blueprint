Let $f:X\to S$ be separated and of finite type with $S$ qcqs.
There are an integer $n\geq 0$, a scheme $\widetilde X$, a proper
surjection $\pi:\widetilde X\to X$ and an immersion
$i_S:\widetilde X\to\mathbf P^n_S$ such that $f\pi=q_Si_S$, where
$q_S:\mathbf P^n_S\to S$ is the projection.
