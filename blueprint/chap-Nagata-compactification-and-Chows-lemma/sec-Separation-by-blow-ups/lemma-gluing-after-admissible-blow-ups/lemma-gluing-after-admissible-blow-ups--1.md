Let $S$ be qcqs, and let $U\subseteq T_i$ ($i=1,2$) be open immersions
of separated $S$-schemes of finite type, with $U$ quasi-compact.
There are $U$-admissible
blow-ups $T_i'\to T_i$ and open immersions $T_i'\to T$ agreeing
on $U$, with $T$ separated and of finite type over $S$.
If $U$ is dense in both $T_i$, then it is dense in $T$.
