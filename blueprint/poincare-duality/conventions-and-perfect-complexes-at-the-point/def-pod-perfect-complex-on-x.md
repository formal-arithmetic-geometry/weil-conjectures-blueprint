In the Poincare duality conventions of this chapter (the fixed setting),
$K\in D^b_{ctf}(X,\Lambda)$. Then $K$ is *perfect over
$\Lambda_X$* if it is quasi-isomorphic, locally on the
small pro-etale site of $X$,
to a bounded complex of finite projective $\Lambda_X$-modules.
