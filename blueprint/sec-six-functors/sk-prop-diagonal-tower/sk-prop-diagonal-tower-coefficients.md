1. Extension of scalars along $\Zl\to\mathcal O_E$ carries $\delta_{\Zl}$,
   $\operatorname{Tr}_q$ and the integration identities to $\mathcal O_E$-linear
   morphisms $\delta_{\mathcal O_E}$, $\operatorname{Tr}_q$ satisfying the same
   identities, whose reductions modulo $\pi_E^n$ (equivalently modulo
   $\ell^n$) are the torsion classes and traces.
2. Inverting $\ell$ and passing to the filtered colimit over finite
   extensions of $E$ inside $\Qlb$ gives $\delta_E$, $\delta_{\Qlb}$ and their
   traces, with the same identities.
3. For a separated morphism of finite type $g:Y'\to Y$ of the standing
   setting, the pullbacks of $\delta_\Lambda$ and $\operatorname{Tr}_q$ along
   the base changes of $g$ are the corresponding morphisms for $Y'$.
