title = "Local duals of integral constructible sheaves"
uses = ["det-lem-int-integral-heart-local-model"]
+++
Let $C\in\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E)$ and let $S$ be a stratum on which $C$ is
pro-étale locally the completion of a finitely generated $\mathcal O_E$-module
([det-lem-int-integral-heart-local-model](2)). Then $C|_S$ is dualizable in
$D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ with dual
$C^\vee=R\mathcal{H}om(C|_S,\mathcal O_{E,S})$; $C|_S$ has Tor amplitude in
$[-1,0]$ and $C^\vee$ has Tor amplitude in $[0,1]$. For instance, for
$C=\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$ one has $C^\vee=\mathcal O_E/\pi_E[-1]$.
