In the setting of [det-pd-generic-direct-image], let $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$
be a closed immersion and let $M\in D^b_c(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$. Then:

1. **(1)** the natural comparison $g_Z^*Ri^!M\to Ri_{\bar\eta}^!g_X^*M$ is an
   isomorphism in $D(Z_{\bar\eta,\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$;
2. **(2)** for $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$ the natural comparison
   $$
   g_X^*\cHom_X(K,M)\longrightarrow\cHom_{X_{\bar\eta}}(g_X^*K,g_X^*M)
   $$
   is an isomorphism in $D(X_{\bar\eta,\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$.

Both comparisons are formed from localization, pullback and adjunction, and
they are compatible with evaluation.
