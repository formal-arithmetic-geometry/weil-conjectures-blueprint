*Remark (sketch).*

*Unconditional*: clauses (2), (3), (4), (7), (8) of
[sk-thm-sf-package] except for the residues named below; the orientation
clause (5); the perfect case of clause (6); every statement of
§§ *What the earlier chapters prove* except where a *Conditional on* line says otherwise.
*Conditional*: (a) the pro-étale existence half of clause (1) and, with
it, the general clause (v) and (vi) of [sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability] and the
general biduality of [sk-prop-sf-biduality-general] rest on
$\operatorname{FullCD}$ for the compactification, whose exact residue is the
uniform vanishing on the pro-étale module sheaves that are *not* of
étale origin; on the étale-origin class — which contains every object of
$\Dctf$ — $\operatorname{FullCD}$ is the theorem
[sk-prop-sf-fullcd-etale-origin], so the residue affects only the ambient
half of [sk-prop-sf-existence] (b) ([sk-rem-sf-fullcd-scope]).
(b) Clause (vii) of [sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability] in the case of a reduction
tower with no uniform trivializing cover
(no uniform trivializing cover). (c) [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence],
[sk-thm-sf-comp-composition] and [sk-prop-sf-comp-proper] are proved on the
étale-origin complexes only ([sk-rem-sf-comp-scope]); their extension to
arbitrary pro-étale objects is not asserted. The two-open gluing step of
Nagata's theorem, a proviso of the summary's source version, is no longer one:
it is [sk-thm-nagata-sketch-two-open].
(d) The two henselian-pair inputs
of degree zero — Stein factorization over a henselian pair, and lifting of
idempotents of the structure sheaf; both are used only in degree zero and are
recorded as open problems of the expanded blueprint. (e) The standing
hypothesis
$\ell\neq p$ throughout, and the four coefficient-level trace formulas of the
trace layer, which are not used by the package.
*Open, with no assertion made*: nothing else; in particular no property of
the six operations is introduced as a definition anywhere in this brief
version, and every node of §§ *What the earlier chapters prove* and §§ *What this section proves* is a
theorem of the blueprint with a named proof (earlier chapter) or an idea-proof
(this section).
