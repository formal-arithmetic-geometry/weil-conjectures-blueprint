(1) The proofs of [sk-prop-ind-heart] and [sk-cor-ind-heart-ii] use only that
the heart is an essentially small abelian subcategory closed under
extensions, that Hom and $\Ext^1$ out of its objects commute with filtered
colimits, and the uniform Ext bound. For $\mathcal O_E$ these are
[sk-lem-int-integral-heart](1) and [sk-lem-int-ext](1),(2). So those proofs
give the realization and the vanishing $\Ext^q(C,B)=0$ for $q>d_C$, first for
$B$ constructible and then for filtered colimits $B$ by continuity.

(2) Direct sums and truncations of complexes with cohomology in
$\IndCons$ have cohomology in $\IndCons$ (cohomology commutes with direct
sums, and $\IndCons$ is closed under direct sums). $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ lies
in $\Dic$ by [sk-lem-int-integral-heart](1); since $\IndCons$ is closed under
extensions, kernels and cokernels, $\Dic$ is triangulated, so it contains the
localizing category generated by $\Dbc$. For the window: products of sheaves
on the pro-étale site are exact and $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ is left
complete ([sk-prop-pe-products], [sk-prop-pe-left-complete]), so
$K=R\varprojlim_n\tau_{\ge-n}K$ and there is a Milnor sequence
$0\to\varprojlim^1_n\Hom(C,\tau_{\ge-n}K[m-1])\to\Hom(C,K[m])\to\varprojlim_n\Hom(C,\tau_{\ge-n}K[m])\to0$.
The uniform vanishing $\Ext^q(C,B)=0$ for $q>d_C$, applied to the cohomology
objects of $K$ via the triangles $H^{-n-1}[n+1]\to\tau_{\ge-n-1}K\to\tau_{\ge-n}K$,
shows that the towers of Hom groups are eventually constant, so
$\varprojlim^1$ vanishes and $\Hom(C,K[m])=\Hom(C,\tau_{\ge m-d_C}K[m])$. On
the other side, $\Hom(C,\tau_{>m}K[m])=0$ since $C$ is in degree $0$. This is
the argument of [sk-lem-finite-window].

(3) For $C\in\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E)$ and a family $(K_\alpha)$ in
$\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))$, by (2) both $\Hom(C,\bigoplus K_\alpha[m])$ and
$\bigoplus\Hom(C,K_\alpha[m])$ only involve the truncations to the window
$[m-d_C,m]$, which commute with direct sums. On complexes with cohomology in a
finite range, $\Hom(C,-)$ commutes with direct sums by induction on the length
of the range, using the five lemma and continuity of Ext for sheaves
([sk-lem-int-ext](1)) in each degree. So $\Hom(C,-)$ commutes with direct
sums. For general $C\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, $C$ is a finite extension of
shifts of constructible sheaves ([sk-lem-int-integral-heart](1)), and
compactness is stable under shifts and extensions by the five lemma.
