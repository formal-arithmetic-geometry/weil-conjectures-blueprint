Let $\mathbb D_X$ be the Verdier dual of [sk-def-ops].

For every admissible $\Lambda$, $\mathbb D_X$ restricts to a functor
$\Dctf(X,\Lambda)^{\mathrm{op}}\to\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
