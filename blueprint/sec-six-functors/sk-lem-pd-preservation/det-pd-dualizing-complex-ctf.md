kind = "lemma"
title = "The dualizing complex lies in $\\Dctf$"
uses = ["det-existence-restriction", "lem-sf-mate"]
+++
Let $\Lambda$ be an admissible coefficient ring and let $X$ be separated of
finite type over $k$, with structure morphism
$a_X\colon X\to s=\operatorname{Spec}k$. Then the dualizing complex
$\omega_{X,\Lambda}=a_X^!\Lambda_s$ lies in $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. For a morphism
$f\colon X\to Y$ of such schemes there is a canonical isomorphism
$f^!\omega_{Y,\Lambda}\simeq\omega_{X,\Lambda}$.
