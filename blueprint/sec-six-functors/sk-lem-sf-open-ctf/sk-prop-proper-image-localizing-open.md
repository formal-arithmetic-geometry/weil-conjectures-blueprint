Let $\Lambda$ be admissible and $j\colon U\to X$ an open immersion. Then $j_!$ maps $\Dctf(U,\Lambda)$ into
$\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$, preserves direct sums, and restricts to an exact, sum-preserving functor
$\Loc(\Dctf(U,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$.
