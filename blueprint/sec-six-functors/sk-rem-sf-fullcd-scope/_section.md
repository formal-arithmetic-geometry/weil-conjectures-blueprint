*Remark (sketch).*

[sk-prop-sf-fullcd-etale-origin] is the form of
$\operatorname{FullCD}$ that every consumer downstream actually uses: all of
[sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation], [sk-prop-sf-biduality-general],
[sk-thm-sf-tensor-hom], [sk-def-sf-kv-maps] and the duality and trace
chapters work inside $\Dctf$, where 09AK puts every object in the
étale-origin class. It does *not* discharge
[sk-prop-sf-existence] (b) as stated: Brown representability needs
$Rf_!$ to preserve arbitrary coproducts on the whole of
$D(\overline X_{\Proet},A)$, and for that one needs the vanishing on
pro-étale $A$-module sheaves that are *not* of étale origin. That
step is the recorded input i:pe-direct-image-coproducts — the
pro-étale analogue of the *proved* étale lemma
lem:exc-direct-image-coproducts — and the compact generation of
$D(\overline X_{\Proet},A)$ by shifts of affine quasi-compact weakly
contractible objects is i:pe-compact-generation. Both are quoted rather
than proved, and are now recorded as external inputs of the expanded
blueprint.
