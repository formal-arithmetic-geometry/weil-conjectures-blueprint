In the notation of [def-trf-admissibility], the following holds. The two *preservation* clauses
(2) and (3) of the same definition — the typing
$Rf^!:D^b_{ctf}(Y,A)\to D^b_{ctf}(X,A)$ together with the interval
bounds — are theorems there as well, for *every* separated finite-type
morphism and at every finite coefficient stage, with constants independent of
the stage: they are [thm-sf-preservation-class] of the six-functor
chapter and its corollary [lem-weil-verdier-preservation-strata], proved there by
the chartwise assembly of the smooth-purity chapter, whose only hypothesis, the
open-complement condition, is the theorem
[lem-exc-open-complement-known] of the chapter on exceptional right
adjoints.
