Let $A=A_{E,n}$ be a
finite coefficient ring,
and let $e:V\to X$ be
finite etale.
On the small pro-etale sites,
direct image is exact and

$$
Re_!=Re_*=e_*\dashv e^*
\quad\hbox{on }D(V_{\mathrm{proet}},A),D(X_{\mathrm{proet}},A).
$$

Here $e^*$ is the
inverse image.
