[sk-prop-sf-fullcd-etale-origin] is the form of
$\operatorname{FullCD}$ that every consumer downstream actually uses: all of
[sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation], [sk-prop-sf-biduality-general],
[sk-thm-sf-tensor-hom], [sk-def-sf-kv-maps] and the duality and trace
chapters work inside $\Dctf$, where [09AK](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09AK) puts every object in the
étale-origin class, and there the exceptional adjoint is the unconditional
$Rf^!$ of [det-def-sf-operations] (vi), [thm-sf-ambient-adjoint-class]. It
does *not* discharge the ambient datum [def-weil-ambient-exceptional-adjoint], and nothing can:
Brown representability needs $Rf_!$ to preserve arbitrary coproducts on the
whole of $D(\overline X_{\Proet},A)$, which requires the vanishing on
pro-étale $A$-module sheaves that are *not* of étale origin. That vanishing is
*false in general*, and with it that datum: for the structure morphism of an
elliptic curve over $\overline{\mathbf F}_p$ and $A=\mathbf F_r$ the direct
image does not preserve coproducts (below). The compact generation of
$D(\overline X_{\Proet},A)$ by shifts of affine quasi-compact weakly
contractible objects is correct for ordinary ring coefficients, but it does
not help, the failing premise being the coproduct preservation.

*The counterexample and the repair boundary.*
Let $E_0/\mathbf F_q$ be an elliptic curve, $E=E_0\times\overline{\mathbf
F}_p$, $r\ne p$ a prime, $\Lambda=\mathbf F_r$ and $f=a_E:E\to\operatorname{Spec}
\overline{\mathbf F}_p$ (smooth, projective, geometrically connected, of
dimension one). Let $T=\operatorname{Spec}k(E)^{\mathrm{sep}}$, an object of
$E_{\Proet}$ representing the pro-étale geometric generic stalk
([0991](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0991)), and $G=\operatorname{Gal}(k(E)^{\mathrm{sep}}/k(E))$. The
connected finite étale multiplication covers $[r^m]:E\to E$ give a
surjection $\chi:G\twoheadrightarrow T_rE\cong\mathbf Z_r^2\twoheadrightarrow
\mathbf Z_r$ ([0BFH](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BFH), [0BF9](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BF9)). Pick $g$ with $\chi(g)=1$ and
countably many $\mathbf Q$-linearly independent elements of the pro-$r$ part
of the closed procyclic subgroup generated by $g$; they span an abstract
subgroup $H\cong\bigoplus_{\mathbf N}\mathbf Z$ on which $\chi$ is injective.
Evaluation at $T$ is an exact cocontinuous functor $U_H$ from pro-étale
$\Lambda$-module sheaves on $E$ to $\Lambda[H]$-modules; $H$ acts freely on
$\operatorname{Hom}_E(T,W)$ for every affine weakly contractible $W$ (an
$h$ fixing a map $T\to W$ fixes lifts to every multiplication cover, forcing
$\chi(h)=0$), so $U_H(\Lambda[h_W])$ is $\Lambda[H]$-free and the right
adjoint $V_H$ of $U_H$ is exact (its sections over such $W$ are products of
copies of the module, one per $H$-orbit, and these $W$ detect exactness,
[0990](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0990)). Put $\mathcal F=V_H(\Lambda)$. Then
$$
H^n(E_{\Proet},\mathcal F)\cong H^n(H,\Lambda)\ne0\qquad(n\ge1),
$$
because $\mathbf Z^n$ is a retract of $H$. Consequences: (i)
$\operatorname{FullCD}(f,\Lambda;c)$ fails for every $c$, so clause (1) of
[sk-def-sf-admissible] fails for this smooth proper $f$. (ii) With
$K_n=\mathcal F[n]$, exactness of products and coproducts of pro-étale
sheaves makes $\bigoplus_nK_n\to\prod_nK_n$ an isomorphism, while on $H^0$ the
coproduct comparison for $R\Gamma=Rf_*=Rf_!$ is the non-surjective map
$\bigoplus_{n\ge1}H^n(E,\mathcal F)\to\prod_{n\ge1}H^n(E,\mathcal F)$; so
$Rf_!$ does not preserve coproducts and has no right adjoint on
$D(E_{\Proet},\Lambda)$. (iii) Nor on $D^+$: with $I$ the image of
$\Lambda$ under the right adjoint of the exact evaluation at the base point
($I$ is injective), $\operatorname{Hom}(Rf_*\mathcal F[n],I)\cong
\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(H^n(E,\mathcal F),\Lambda)\ne0$ for all $n$,
whereas a right adjoint $G$ on $D^+$ would give
$\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal F[n],G(I))=0$ for $n$ large by $t$-structure
orthogonality. The construction works for every nonzero coefficient ring,
including $\mathbf Q_\ell$, so neither torsion coefficients nor
rationalization restore the ambient adjoint. *Repair boundary.* The
sheaf $\mathcal F$ is not of étale origin, and no statement of this
blueprint applies an adjunction $Rf_!\dashv Rf^!$ outside the étale-origin
class: the constructible exceptional adjoint $Rf^!$ of
[det-def-sf-operations] (vi) — for finite coefficients the étale adjoint
$f^{\times}$ of [def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image] transported to the
pro-étale site, and for every admissible condensed $\Lambda$ the right
adjoint of $Rf_!$ on $\Dctf$ of [sk-thm-existence] — is unconditional, preserves $\Dctf$
([sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation]), and is the only exceptional functor
the duality, trace and weight chapters use. Their terminal statements
([sk-thm-poincare-duality], [sk-input-weights],
[sk-cor-tf-point-counting], [sk-input-weights]) therefore hold for
every smooth proper geometrically connected $X_0$ with no admissibility
hypothesis.

*Where the class adjoint stops.* The comparison construction of
[thm-sf-ambient-adjoint-class] provides the exceptional right adjoint relative
to bounded-below inputs of étale origin. It suffices for the bounded
constructible class; its finite-coefficient preservation is
[thm-sf-preservation-class]. An adjunction for arbitrary unbounded pro-étale
complexes is not proved by that comparison and is not used as an automatic
consequence (see [rem-sf-class-adjoint-residue]); by the counterexample above
it is false in general. General finite-coefficient Verdier biduality is
independently proved in [thm-fix-general-biduality], and its boundary exchange
is [cor-weil-boundary-duality-discharge]; neither needs the unrestricted
ambient adjunction.
