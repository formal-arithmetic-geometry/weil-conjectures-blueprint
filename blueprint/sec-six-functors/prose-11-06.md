Over a field, this is equivalent to the perfectness of the pairings on
cohomology. Over $\mathcal O_E$ or a finite ring, the induced pairings

For smooth $X$, Corollary [sk-cor-sp-all-coefficients] identifies $\omega_X$ with
$\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$, and global duality becomes the Poincaré pairing
