Compactifications define a functor
$Rf_!:\Loc(\Dctf(X,\mathcal O_E))\to\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\mathcal O_E))$, independent of
the compactification, compatible with composition and base change, preserving
direct sums and sending $\Dctf(X,\mathcal O_E)$ into $\Dctf(Y,\mathcal O_E)$.
Its exact right adjoint $f^!$ is the right adjoint of [sk-prop-sf-existence]
for $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$.
