In the setting of [lem-sf-point], let $u:P\to Q$ be a morphism of the
point category. Then $u$ is an isomorphism if and only if
$\operatorname{Hom}_s(\Lambda[-r],u)$ is an isomorphism for every
$r\in\mathbf Z$.
