Let $X$ be a separated finite-type scheme over $k$ and $A$ a
finite coefficient ring as in [def-trf-coefficient-conventions].
The triangulated category $D^b_{ctf}(X,A)$ is the thick subcategory of
$D(X,A)$ generated by the objects

$$
Rg_*j_!L ,
$$

where $U$ is a locally closed stratum of $X$,
$j:U\hookrightarrow\overline U$ is the open immersion into its closure,
$g:\overline U\hookrightarrow X$ is the closed immersion, and $L$ is a lisse
sheaf of $A$-modules on $U$ with flat stalks. Constant coefficients
$L=A_U$ do not suffice in general.

## Proof

Step 0 (the generators are in $D^b_{ctf}$). A lisse sheaf with flat
stalks has Tor amplitude $[0,0]$; extension by zero along $j$ and the
exact pushforward along the closed immersion $g$ preserve
constructibility and Tor amplitude.

Step 1 (flat model). The truncations
$\tau_{\leq q}K$ of an object $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X,A)$ need not have finite
Tor amplitude when $A$ is not a field (e.g. $A=\mathbf Z/4$,
$K=[A\xrightarrow{2}A]$, whose cohomology sheaves $\mathbf Z/2$ have
infinite Tor dimension), so the cohomology sheaves cannot be used. Instead,
apply the constructible variant of [lem-sf-flat-model] on $X_{\mathrm{et}}$:
its input is that every constructible sheaf of $A$-modules is a quotient of a
finite direct sum of the flat constructible sheaves $j_{V!}A_V$, $V\to X$
affine étale (Stacks 095M; SGA $4\frac12$, Rapport, 4.6), that the
cohomology sheaves of $K$ are constructible
([def-weil-pbc-constructible-complexes]), and that kernels and cokernels of
maps of constructible sheaves are constructible; its conclusion is that $K$
is isomorphic to a bounded complex $P$ of flat constructible sheaves, the
flat truncation $Q$ in that proof being constructible as well. The
stupid-truncation triangles
$\sigma_{\geq q+1}P\to\sigma_{\geq q}P\to P^q[-q]\xrightarrow{+1}$
reduce the claim to a single flat constructible sheaf $F$: a thick
subcategory containing two terms of a distinguished triangle contains the
third, the two-out-of-three step for triangles of
[lem-sf-triangle-two-out-of-three].

Step 2 (extension across the strata). As in
[lem-fin-constructible-devissage], choose a dense open
$j_U:U\hookrightarrow X$ on which $F$ is lisse, with closed complement
$i:Z\hookrightarrow X$. The exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow j_{U!}(F|_U)\longrightarrow F
\longrightarrow i_*i^*F\longrightarrow0
$$

has $F|_U$ lisse with flat stalks and $i^*F$ flat constructible on $Z$,
of smaller dimension. By induction on dimension, pushing the resulting
filtration forward along the exact functor $i_*$, $F$ is a finite iterated
extension of objects $Rg_*j_!L$ as in the statement, with $L$ the
restriction of $F$ to a locally closed stratum; each extension is a
distinguished triangle, so $F$ lies in the thick closure.

Step 3 (why lisse coefficients). The lisse coefficients are not reduced
further. The abelian category of constructible sheaves is not of finite
length, and its simple objects are punctual
([lem-trf-simple-constructible]), so a lisse sheaf on a positive-dimensional
stratum is not a finite extension of simple constructible sheaves. Nor is
$L$ in general a direct summand of $\pi_*\pi^*L$ for a finite étale Galois
cover $\pi$ trivializing it, when $\ell$ divides the order of the
monodromy. And constant coefficients do not generate: on
$X=\mathbf G_m$ over $k$ with $p\neq2$ and $A=\mathbf F_3$, the complexes
whose generic-stalk cohomology has only trivial $\pi_1$-composition
factors form a thick subcategory containing every $Rg_*j_!A_U$ but not
the order-$2$ Kummer sheaf. A statement checked on the generators may,
when it is étale-local on $U$, still be reduced to constant coefficients
by passing to a trivializing finite étale cover.
