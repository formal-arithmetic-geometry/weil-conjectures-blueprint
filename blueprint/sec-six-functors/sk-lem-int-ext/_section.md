1. For $C\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, the functors
   $\Hom_{D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)}(C,-[q])$ commute with filtered
   colimits of complexes with a common lower bound.
2. For each $C\in\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E)$ there is an integer $d_C$ such that
   $$
   \Ext^q_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}(C,N)=0
   \qquad\text{for all }q>d_C\text{ and all }N\in\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E).
   $$
