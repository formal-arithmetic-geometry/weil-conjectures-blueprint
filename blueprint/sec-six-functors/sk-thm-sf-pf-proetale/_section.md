Let $K,L$ be the pro-étale realizations of bounded constructible finite-Tor
complexes, with étale-origin witnesses, at any coefficient stage. Then
$\operatorname{PF}_{f,K,L}$ is an isomorphism in
$D^+(Y_{\Proet},\Lambda)$.
