All geometric- and scheme-Frobenius equivariance squares of
[thm-pur-weil-equivariance-of-purity-and-trace] and of
[prop-pur-weil-equivariance-scheme-frobenius] commute with finite
reduction, scalar extension, composition, and base change.

## Proof

The compatibilities are those of the constituent purity, counit, and
trace statements established in
[thm-pur-weil-equivariance-of-purity-and-trace] (each being
compatible with the coefficient transport of
[thm-pur-adic-reconstruction]), transported by the scheme-Frobenius
identification of [prop-pur-weil-equivariance-scheme-frobenius].
