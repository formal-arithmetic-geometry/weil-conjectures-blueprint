In the Poincare duality conventions of this chapter (the fixed setting), let $\Lambda\in\{A,\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell}\}$ be an admissible coefficient ring, regarded as a condensed ring, and let $K,L\in D^b_{ctf}(X,\Lambda)\subseteq D(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda)$. The *mixed-support cup product* is the canonical map

$$
\smile _c:\  R\Gamma _c(X,K)\otimes _\Lambda^LR\Gamma (X,L) \longrightarrow R\Gamma _c\bigl(X,K\otimes ^L_{\Lambda_X}L\bigr)
$$

obtained as the composite of the projection transformation for $Rf_!$, applied to the pair $K,\; L'=Ra_{X*}L\in D^b_{ctf}(s,\Lambda)$,

$$
Ra_{X!}K\otimes _\Lambda^LRa_{X*}L \longrightarrow Ra_{X!}\bigl(K\otimes ^L_{\Lambda_X}La_X^*Ra_{X*}L\bigr),
$$

and the map induced by the counit $La_X^*Ra_{X*}L\to L$.

The cup product is associative and unital, graded-commutative, and
compatible with reduction modulo $\pi_E^n$ and extension of scalars along maps of admissible coefficient rings ([sk-prop-scalar-six]).
