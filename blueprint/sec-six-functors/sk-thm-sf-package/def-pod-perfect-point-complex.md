In the Poincare duality conventions of this chapter (the fixed setting), an
object $C$ of the
category at the point is
*perfect* if it is quasi-isomorphic to a bounded complex of finite
projective $A$-modules. The *derived dual* is
$C^\vee=R\operatorname{Hom}_A(C,A)$, and the canonical evaluation and
coevaluation are the maps of the tensor–internal-Hom adjunction.
