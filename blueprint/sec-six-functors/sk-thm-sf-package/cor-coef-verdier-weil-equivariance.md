Let $X_0$ be a scheme over $\mathbf F_q$ with geometric fiber $X$, and let
$\Lambda\in\{E,\Qlb\}$, regarded as a condensed ring. Duality
$\mathbb D_X=R\mathcal Hom(-,\omega_X)$ on $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ carries Weil
complexes $(K,\theta_K)$ in the sense of [sk-def-sf-weil-complexes] to Weil
complexes. Evaluation, biduality, the exceptional adjunction data, and the
comparisons under finite extension of the coefficient field are Weil
equivariant. This does not require a Frobenius-stable integral lattice for a
rational Weil complex.
