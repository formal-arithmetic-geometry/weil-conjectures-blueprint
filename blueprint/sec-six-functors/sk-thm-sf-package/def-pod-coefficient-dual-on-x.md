In the Poincare duality conventions of this chapter (the fixed setting),
$K\in D^b_{ctf}(X,\Lambda)$. The *coefficient dual* is

$$
K^\vee=R\mathcal Hom_X\bigl(K,\Lambda_X\bigr),
$$

with the canonical evaluation

$$
\operatorname{ev}_K:K\otimes^L_{\Lambda_X}K^\vee\longrightarrow\Lambda_X
$$

of the tensor–internal-Hom adjunction.
