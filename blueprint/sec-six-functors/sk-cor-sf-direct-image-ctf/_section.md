For $f$ separated of finite type with $\dim X\le d$, $Rf_*$ carries
$\Dctf(X_{\Proet},A)$ into $\Dctf(Y_{\Proet},A)$, with finite Tor amplitude
$[a,b]\mapsto[a,b+2d]$; no restriction to objects of étale origin is needed.
