The argument tests geometric stalks and therefore proves the coherence on the
étale-origin complexes $\nu_X^*D^+(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$ — the range in which
[def-fix-six-functor-datum] (iv) asserts compactification-independence.
Extending it to arbitrary objects of $D(X_{\Proet},\Lambda)$ is not proved
here; ordinary geometric stalks are
not conservative on all pro-étale sheaves. What the stalk computation
consumes is the *bounded-below* form of proper base change; this document
states [thm-weil-pbc-bounded-below] for torsion sheaves, and the passage to
$D^+$ is the standard dévissage along the canonical truncations. The
same remark applies to [thm-sf-comp-composition] and
[cor-cbc-proper-coincidence].
