The biduality clause of [def-sf-datum] is supplied at all the
specified coefficient stages by [thm-sf-biduality-class]. The
finite-stage boundary hypothesis is discharged by
[cor-sf-biduality-class-discharge]; the integral and field
assertions use the coefficient-comparison proofs cited in the theorem.
