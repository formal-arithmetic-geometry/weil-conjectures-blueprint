title = "The cone of the double-dual morphism has finite support"

Let $A$ be a finite coefficient ring and $d\geq1$. Assume that for every
algebraically closed field $k'$ of characteristic different from $\ell$, every
separated finite-type $k'$-scheme $Y$ of dimension at most $d-1$ and every
$L\in D^b_{ctf}(Y_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$ the double-dual morphism $\delta_L$
of [lem-weil-duality-pairing] is an isomorphism. Let $k$ be algebraically closed
of characteristic different from $\ell$, let $X$ be a proper $k$-scheme of
dimension $d$, and let $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$. Then
$\Delta=\operatorname{Cone}(\delta_K)$ is bounded constructible, and all its
cohomology sheaves are supported on one finite set of closed points of $X$.
