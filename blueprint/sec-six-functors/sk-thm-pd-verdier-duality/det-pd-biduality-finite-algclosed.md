Let $A$ be a finite coefficient ring, $k$ an algebraically closed field of
characteristic different from $\ell$, and $X$ a separated finite-type
$k$-scheme. For every $K\in D^b_{ctf}(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$ the double-dual
morphism $\delta_K\colon K\to D_XD_XK$ of [lem-weil-duality-pairing] is an
isomorphism.
