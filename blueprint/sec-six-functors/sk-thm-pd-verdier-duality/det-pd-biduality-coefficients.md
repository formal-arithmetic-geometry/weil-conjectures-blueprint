Assume the finite-coefficient biduality of [det-pd-biduality-finite-algclosed].
Then the double-dual morphism $\delta_K\colon K\to\mathbb D_X\mathbb D_XK$ is an
isomorphism for every $K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ in each of the following cases:

1. **(i)** $\Lambda=A$ finite, over an algebraically closed or a finite base field;
2. **(ii)** $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, in particular $\Lambda=\Zl$;
3. **(iii)** $\Lambda=E$ a finite extension of $\Ql$;
4. **(iv)** $\Lambda=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
