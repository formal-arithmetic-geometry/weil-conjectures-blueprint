The results used in the proof of [sk-thm-restriction] are proved before
$f^!$ is known on constructible complexes. Compactness
([sk-thm-constructible-compactness]) uses $R\underline\Gamma_Z$ only through
open–closed localization. The projective-line adjunction
([sk-prop-p1-adjunction-rational]) is built from the Cartier extension, Kummer
theory, the cohomology of $\mathbf P^1$, and properties of $Rf_!$ and $Rf_*$,
and the torsion theorem for open immersions behind
[sk-prop-open-coefficients] is built from that adjunction with torsion
coefficients.
