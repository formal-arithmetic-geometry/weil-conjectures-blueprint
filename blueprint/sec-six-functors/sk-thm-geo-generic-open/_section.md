Let $S$ be integral of finite type over the standing field $k$, let
$\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k)$, and let $j:X\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open
immersion of $S$-schemes of finite type. For a constructible $\Fell$-sheaf $F$
on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$, there is a nonempty open $S_0\subset S$ such that

$$
Rj_{S_0,*}(F|_{X_{S_0}})\in\Dbc(Y_{S_0},\Fell),
\qquad\text{(G21)}
$$

and such that for every finite-type $k$-scheme $T\to S_0$, with base change
$j_T:X_T\hookrightarrow Y_T$, the canonical base-change map

$$
(Rj_{S_0,*}F)|_{Y_T}\longrightarrow Rj_{T,*}(F|_{X_T})
\quad\text{in }D^+((Y_T)_{\mathrm{proét}},\Fell)
\qquad\text{(G22)}
$$

is an isomorphism.
