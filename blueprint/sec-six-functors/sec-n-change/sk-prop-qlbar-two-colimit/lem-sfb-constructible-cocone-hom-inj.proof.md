The proof of [lem-sfb-constructible-cocone-hom-surj] shows that the canonical map
$\operatorname{colim}_{H'\supseteq H}\Hom(T_{H,H'}K,T_{H,H'}L)\to
\Hom(T_{H,\Qlb}K,T_{H,\Qlb}L)$ is bijective. If $g$ and $g'$ have the same image, their classes in
the filtered colimit agree by injectivity, i.e. $T_{H,H'}(g)=T_{H,H'}(g')$ for some
$H'\supseteq H$.
