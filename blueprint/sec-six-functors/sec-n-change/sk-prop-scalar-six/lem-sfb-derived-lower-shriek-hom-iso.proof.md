By [def-sfb-derived-lower-shriek-hom], (Shriek) is a composite of associativity isomorphisms,
the whiskering of (Push) for $g$ by $D(i_!)$, and the whiskering by $R(g_*)$ of the isomorphism
of [def-sfb-derived-open-immersion-shriek-iso]. Whiskerings of isomorphisms are isomorphisms, so
if (Push) for $g$ is an isomorphism, so is the composite.
