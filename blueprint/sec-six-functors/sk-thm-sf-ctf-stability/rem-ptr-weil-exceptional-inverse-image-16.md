The exceptional inverse image of
[def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image] has finite coefficient
comparison on constructible finite-Tor inputs by
[lem-coef-finite-exceptional]. Its integral construction and
adjunction are [prop-coef-integral-exceptional-adjoint], and
the field-stage identifications are
[lem-coef-rational-operations], [thm-coef-qbar-verdier].
The unrestricted ambient adjunction outside the bounded comparison
and constructible categories remains a separate issue, described in
[rem-sf-class-adjoint-residue].
