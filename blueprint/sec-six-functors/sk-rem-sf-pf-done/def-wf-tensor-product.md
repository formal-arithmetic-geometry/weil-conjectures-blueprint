For $\Lambda\in\{E,\Qlb\}$, regarded as a condensed ring, the derived
tensor product $-\otimes^L_{\Lambda_X}-$ on $D(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda)$
is computed from a $K$-flat replacement of its first argument
([det-def-sfb-derived-tensor-hom]). For Weil complexes
$(K,\varphi_K)$, $(L,\varphi_L)$ in the sense of
[def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex] the tensor Frobenius is the composite of the
monoidal isomorphism $g_X^*(K\otimes^LL)\cong g_X^*K\otimes^Lg_X^*L$ with
$\varphi_K\otimes\varphi_L$.
