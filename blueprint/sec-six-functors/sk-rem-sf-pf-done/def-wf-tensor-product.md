On $D(X,E)$ the derived tensor product $-\otimes^L_E-$ is computed
from a $K$-flat replacement of its first argument. For Weil complexes
$(K,\varphi_K)$, $(L,\varphi_L)$ the tensor Frobenius is
$\varphi_K\otimes\varphi_L$.
