Let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be a quasi-compact open immersion.

1. $j_!$ is exact, preserves all direct sums, preserves all limits, and
   commutes with reduction modulo $\pi_E^n$.
2. $j_!$ maps $\Dbc(U,\mathcal O_E)$ into $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$.
3. $j_!$ maps $\Loc(\Dbc(U,\mathcal O_E))$ into $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))$.
