The functor $j_!$ is exact and preserves direct sums, being a left adjoint,
and it preserves $\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E)$ by [sk-def-sfb-dc-restricted-functors].
The localizing argument at the end of the proof of [sk-prop-int-compactness]
gives the second assertion.
