Under the hypothesis of Proposition [proposition-fourier-transform-middle-extension], the
Fourier transform of $(j_*M)[1]$ is again a middle extension. But
Plancherel applies only to complexes on the whole line, boundary points
included. So we need the theorem for $j_*$ and $j_!$ on the larger curve. The
boundary points do not change $\nu$, because their weights are small.
