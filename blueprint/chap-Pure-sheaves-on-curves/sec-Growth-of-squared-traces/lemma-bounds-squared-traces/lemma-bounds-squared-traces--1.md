Let $X_0$ be a smooth affine curve over $k_0$, and let $F$ be a
lisse Weil sheaf on $X_0$, punctually of weights $\le a$. There is a
constant $C\in\mathbf R$ such that

$$
S_n(F)\le Cq^{n(a+1)}\qquad(n\ge1).
$$
