At each point of $X_0(k_n)$, at most $r$ eigenvalues contribute and each has absolute value at most $q^{na/2}$. The trace has absolute value at most $rq^{na/2}$, so its squared absolute value is at most $r^2q^{na}$. Lemma [lemma-point-count-bound] bounds the number of points by $Dq^n$. Summing yields $S_n(F)\le Dr^2q^{n(a+1)}$.
