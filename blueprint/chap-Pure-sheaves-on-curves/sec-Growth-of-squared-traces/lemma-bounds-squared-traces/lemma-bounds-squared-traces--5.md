$b\in\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$, we have
$\nu(K\otimes\mathcal B_b)=\nu(K)+\lambda_\iota(b)$, with
$\mathcal B_b$ as in Proposition [proposition-weights-under-usual-operations].
