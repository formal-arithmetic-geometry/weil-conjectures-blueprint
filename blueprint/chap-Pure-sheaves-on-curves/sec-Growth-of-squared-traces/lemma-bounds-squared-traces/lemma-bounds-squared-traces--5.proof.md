At a $k_n$-point the scalar of $\mathcal B_b$ is $b^n$, so tensoring multiplies $S_n$ by $|\iota(b)|^{2n}=q^{n\lambda_\iota(b)}$. It therefore translates every exponent in the defining growth condition by $\lambda_\iota(b)$. Applying the same calculation to $b^{-1}$ proves equality of the two infimal growth exponents, including the zero-trace convention.
