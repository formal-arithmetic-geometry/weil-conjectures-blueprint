By Lemma [lemma-point-count-bound], the number of rational points in the finite support is bounded independently of $n$. Let $c$ be the finite sum of the dimensions of all nonzero cohomology stalks. The eigenvalue bound implies that the absolute value of each alternating trace is at most $cq^{na/2}$. Squaring and multiplying by the uniform point bound gives $S_n(K)\le Cq^{na}$ for a constant $C$ independent of $n$.
