Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow X_0$ be a dense open of a smooth geometrically
connected affine curve, and let $\mathcal G$ be a nonzero pure lisse
Weil sheaf of weight $w$ on $U_0$. Then

$$
\nu(j_!\mathcal G)=\nu(j_*\mathcal G).
$$
