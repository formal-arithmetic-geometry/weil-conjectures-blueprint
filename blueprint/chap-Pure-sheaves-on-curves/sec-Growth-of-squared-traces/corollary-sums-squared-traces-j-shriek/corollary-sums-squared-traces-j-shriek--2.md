Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1_{\mathbf F_q}$ be a dense open
with $U_0$ a smooth geometrically connected affine curve of finite
type over $\mathbf F_q$. Fix an embedding
$\iota:\Qbarl\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$, with
$\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\mathbf F_q$. If $\mathcal G$ is a nonzero
lisse Weil $\Qbarl$-sheaf on $U_0$, punctually $\iota$-pure of real
weight $w$, then

$$
\nu(j_*\mathcal G)=\nu(\mathcal G)=w.
$$
