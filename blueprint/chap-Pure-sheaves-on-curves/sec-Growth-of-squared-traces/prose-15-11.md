A cohomological shift changes the weight of a complex in the sense of
Definition [definition-weights]. But it only multiplies the [trace
function](@definition-trace-function) by a sign, and so it leaves [$\nu$](@definition-growth-squared-traces) unchanged.

In the theorem below we find [$\nu$](@definition-growth-squared-traces) from the radius of convergence of a
generating series, which is a logarithmic derivative of an [$L$-function](@theorem-trace-formula-euler-product).
