A cohomological shift changes the weight of a complex in the sense of
Definition [definition-weights]. But it only multiplies the trace
function by a sign, and so it leaves $\nu$ unchanged.

In the theorem below we find $\nu$ from the radius of convergence of a
generating series, which is a logarithmic derivative of an $L$-function.
