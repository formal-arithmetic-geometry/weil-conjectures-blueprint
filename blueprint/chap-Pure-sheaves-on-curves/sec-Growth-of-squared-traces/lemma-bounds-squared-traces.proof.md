For (1), the trace at
each point of $X_0(k_n)$ has absolute value at most
$rq^{na/2}$, and Lemma [lemma-point-count-bound] gives
$S_n\le Dr^2q^{n(a+1)}$. For (2),
Lemma [lemma-point-count-bound] bounds the number of points of
the support with values in $k_n$ independently of $n$, so it suffices
to bound the trace at each of them. Since the support consists of
finitely many points with finite-dimensional stalks, the sum $c$ of the
ranks of the stalks of the $\mathcal H^i(K)$ is finite, and at each such
point the trace has absolute value at most $cq^{na/2}$. For (3),
the trace of $\mathcal B_b$ at a $k_n$-point is $b^n$, so tensoring by
$\mathcal B_b$ multiplies $S_n$ by $|\iota(b)|^{2n}=q^{n\lambda_\iota(b)}$.
