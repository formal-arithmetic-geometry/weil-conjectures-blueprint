The number $\nu$ below is the growth exponent of the sums of squared
traces, shifted by $1$ so that it equals the weight. We take a $\limsup$
because the sums can vanish for infinitely many $n$, as the example of
$\mathcal B_b\oplus\mathcal B_{-b}$ at the start of this chapter shows.
