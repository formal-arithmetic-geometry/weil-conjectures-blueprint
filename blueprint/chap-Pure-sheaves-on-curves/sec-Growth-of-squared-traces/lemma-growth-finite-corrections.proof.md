The inequality $|z+u|^2\le2|z|^2+2|u|^2$, applied to
[$t_{L,n}$](@definition-growth-squared-traces) and the difference $\href{@definition-growth-squared-traces}{t_{K,n}}-\href{@definition-growth-squared-traces}{t_{L,n}}$, gives
$\href{@definition-growth-squared-traces}{S_n(K)}\le2\href{@definition-growth-squared-traces}{S_n(L)}+2Cq^{na}$, so that
$\href{@definition-growth-squared-traces}{\nu(K)}\le\max(\href{@definition-growth-squared-traces}{\nu(L)},a-1)$, and by symmetry
$\href{@definition-growth-squared-traces}{\nu(L)}\le\max(\href{@definition-growth-squared-traces}{\nu(K)},a-1)$.  If one of [$\nu(K)$](@definition-growth-squared-traces), [$\nu(L)$](@definition-growth-squared-traces) exceeds
$a-1$, these two inequalities force equality.
