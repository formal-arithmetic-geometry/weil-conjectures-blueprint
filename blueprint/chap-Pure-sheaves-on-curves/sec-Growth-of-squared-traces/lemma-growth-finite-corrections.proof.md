The inequality $|z+u|^2\le2|z|^2+2|u|^2$, applied to
$t_{L,n}$ and the difference $t_{K,n}-t_{L,n}$, gives
$S_n(K)\le2S_n(L)+2Cq^{na}$, so that
$\nu(K)\le\max(\nu(L),a-1)$, and by symmetry
$\nu(L)\le\max(\nu(K),a-1)$.  If one of $\nu(K)$, $\nu(L)$ exceeds
$a-1$, these two inequalities force equality.
