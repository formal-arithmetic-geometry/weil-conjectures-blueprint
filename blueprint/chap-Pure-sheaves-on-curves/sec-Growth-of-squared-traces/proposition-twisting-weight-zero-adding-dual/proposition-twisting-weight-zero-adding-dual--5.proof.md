By clause [proposition-twisting-weight-zero-adding-dual--4], the eigenvalues $\beta_j$ of $\mathcal F^0$ have absolute value one after applying $\iota$. The dual has reciprocal eigenvalues, whose images are $\overline{\iota(\beta_j)}$. The union of these two multisets, the eigenvalue multiset of $\mathcal E$, is stable under conjugation. This is the asserted reality condition.
