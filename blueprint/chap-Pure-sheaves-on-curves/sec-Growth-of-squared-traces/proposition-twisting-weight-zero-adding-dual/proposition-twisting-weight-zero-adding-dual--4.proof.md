By [proposition-weights-under-usual-operations], the constant twist adds
$2\log_q|\iota(b)|$ to the weight. The chosen norm gives
$2\log_q|\iota(b)|=-w$, so the new weight is $w-w=0$.
This is `AlgebraicGeometry.WeilSheaf.IsPunctuallyPureOfWeight.constantTwist_zero`,
obtained from `AlgebraicGeometry.WeilSheaf.IsPunctuallyPureOfWeight.constantTwist`.
