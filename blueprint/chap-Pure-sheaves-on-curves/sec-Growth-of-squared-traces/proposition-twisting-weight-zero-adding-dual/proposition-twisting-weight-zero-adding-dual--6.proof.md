At each closed point, the stalk of $\mathcal F^0$ is pure of weight zero
by [proposition-twisting-weight-zero-adding-dual--4]. Its dual is pure of
weight $-0=0$, and the direct sum of these two Frobenius modules is pure
of weight zero. Transport this conclusion along the stalk equivalences
for the dual sheaf and the direct sum. These are the steps of
`AlgebraicGeometry.WeilSheaf.IsPunctuallyPureOfWeight.realEnvelope`.
