Clause [proposition-twisting-weight-zero-adding-dual--4] gives purity of $\mathcal F^0$ of weight zero. Its dual is pure of weight zero by Proposition [proposition-weights-under-usual-operations--4]. Their direct sum has the union of those eigenvalue multisets and is therefore pure of weight zero.
