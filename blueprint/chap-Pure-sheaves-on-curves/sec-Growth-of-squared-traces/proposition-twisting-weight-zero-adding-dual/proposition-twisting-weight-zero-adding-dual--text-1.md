*Shared notation.* Let $k_0$ be a finite field of cardinality $q$, let
$\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k_0)$, and fix
$\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
The sheaves are constructible Weil sheaves on a $k_0$-scheme $X_0$.
For a unit $b\in\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$, write
$\mathcal F^0=\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal B_b$; when $\mathcal F$ is lisse,
write $\mathcal E=\mathcal F^0\oplus(\mathcal F^0)^\vee$.
The hypotheses of each clause are stated below. In the situation of the
proposition, one choice of $b$ gives all the asserted properties simultaneously.
