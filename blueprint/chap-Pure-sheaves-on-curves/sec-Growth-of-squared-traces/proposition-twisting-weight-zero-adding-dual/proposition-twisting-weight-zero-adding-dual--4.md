$b\in\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$ satisfy $|\iota(b)|=q^{-w/2}$.
Then the sheaf $\mathcal F^0$ of
[proposition-twisting-weight-zero-adding-dual--2] is pure of weight zero.
