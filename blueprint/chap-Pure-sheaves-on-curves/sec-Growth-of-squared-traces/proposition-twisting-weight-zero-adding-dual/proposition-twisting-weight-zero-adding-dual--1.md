Let $\mathcal F\ne0$ be a Weil sheaf pure of weight $w$ on a scheme
locally of finite type over $k_0$. There exists
$b\in\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$ with $|\iota(b)|=q^{-w/2}$.
