By its definition in clause [proposition-twisting-weight-zero-adding-dual--3], $\mathcal E=\mathcal F^0\oplus(\mathcal F^0)^\vee$. The inclusion into the first summand and projection onto it are morphisms of Weil sheaves and compose to the identity of $\mathcal F^0$, supplying the required direct summand.
