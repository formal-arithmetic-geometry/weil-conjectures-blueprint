Let $X_0/\Fq$ be smooth of relative dimension $1$, and let
$j:U_0\hookrightarrow X_0$ be a quasi-compact open immersion over
$\Fq$ with dense image. Let $\ell\nmid q$ be a prime and let
$\mathcal G$ be a lisse Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf on $X_0$.
The unit of the adjunction $j^*\dashv j_*$ is an isomorphism of Weil
sheaves:

$$
\mathcal G\xrightarrow{\sim}j_*j^*\mathcal G.
$$

Here $j_*$ is the ordinary direct image of sheaves.
