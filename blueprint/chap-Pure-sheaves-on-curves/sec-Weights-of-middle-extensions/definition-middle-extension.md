Let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be a dense open in a smooth curve and let
$\mathcal F$ be a lisse sheaf on $U$. The *middle extension* of
$\mathcal F$ is the ordinary direct image $j_*\mathcal F$ of sheaves.
