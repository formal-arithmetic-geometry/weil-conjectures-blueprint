Let $C$ be a smooth curve over an algebraically closed field and let
$F$ be a constructible sheaf on $C$ with coefficients in $E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
Then $H^i_c(C,F)=0$ for $i\notin\{0,1,2\}$.
