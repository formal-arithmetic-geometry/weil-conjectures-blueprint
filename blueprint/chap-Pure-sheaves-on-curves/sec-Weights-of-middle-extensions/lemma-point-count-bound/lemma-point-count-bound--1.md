Let $U_0$ be an affine scheme of finite type over the finite field
$k_0$, with $\dim U_0\le1$, and put $q=\#k_0$. There is a natural
number $D$ such that

$$
\#U_0(k_n)\le Dq^n\qquad(n\ge1).
$$

There is also a real constant $A$ such that, for every finite set $T$
of closed points of $U_0$ and every integer $e\ge0$,

$$
\#\{x\in T:\deg(x)=e\}\le Aq^e,
\qquad \deg(x)=[\kappa(x):k_0].
$$
