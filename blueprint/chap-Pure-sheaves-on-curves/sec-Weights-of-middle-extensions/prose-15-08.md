A [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) [Weil sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) on a curve $C_0$ over $k_0$ gives a
[constructible sheaf](@definition-constructible-sheaves) on $C=C_0\times_{k_0}k$, so the lemma applies to it
as well.
