We prove the formula for finite coefficients, where it is Galois
theory of the punctured henselian neighbourhood, and pass to the limit.

The stalk of $j_*\mathcal F$ at $\bar z$ is the colimit of the sections
of $\mathcal F$ over the inverse images of $U$ in étale neighbourhoods
of $\bar z$, that is, the group of sections over the
punctured spectrum of the strict henselization at $\bar z$. This
punctured spectrum is the spectrum of a field, whose absolute Galois
group is the inertia group $I_z$, so finite
locally constant sheaves there correspond to finite continuous
representations of the inertia group, and their sections are the
invariant vectors. Applying this to the finite reductions of a stable
lattice and passing to the inverse limit, we obtain the formula with
integral coefficients.

Two compatibilities take us to rational coefficients and to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
Rationalization commutes with taking invariants, since a power of the
uniformizer carries an invariant rational vector into the lattice.  A finite
extension of scalars also commutes with invariants. Indeed, it is exact, hence
commutes with finite intersections of kernels, and in a finite-dimensional space
the intersection of the kernels of all $g-1$ is the intersection of
finitely many of them.
This gives the formula with rational coefficients, after finite
extension of scalars, and hence also over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. Since all these maps
commute with the action of the decomposition group, the identification is
Frobenius equivariant. Finally, if $\mathcal F$ extends to a lisse sheaf on
$X_0$, the local inertia acts trivially, and so the adjunction map is
an isomorphism on every geometric stalk.
