Let $X_0$ be a smooth geometrically connected curve over $\Fq$, and let
$j:U_0\hookrightarrow X_0$ be a quasi-compact dense open immersion over $\Fq$.
Fix a prime $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$, an embedding
$\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$, and $a\in\mathbf R$.
If $\mathcal G$ is a lisse Weil sheaf on $X_0$ and $j^*\mathcal G$ is
punctually $\iota$-pure of weight $a$, then $\mathcal G$ is punctually
$\iota$-pure of weight $a$ on all of $X_0$.
