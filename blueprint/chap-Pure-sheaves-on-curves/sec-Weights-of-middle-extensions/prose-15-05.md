The proposition gives only an upper bound at the boundary. In
general nothing better is true. For a family of elliptic curves with
multiplicative reduction at $z$, the first cohomology of the fibres is
pure of weight $1$, while its inertia invariants at $z$ form a line
of weight $0$. When the inertia acts trivially, we get the lower
bound by duality.
