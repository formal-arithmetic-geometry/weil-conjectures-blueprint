The vanishing of $H^0_c$ was shown in the proof of
Proposition [proposition-weights-j-boundary-points]. For the higher degrees, let $i$ be the inclusion of $C_0-U_0$. Since sheaves with finite support
have compact-support cohomology only in degree zero, the
localization sequence for $0\to j_!F\to j_*F\to i_*i^*j_*F\to0$
identifies $H^r_c(C,j_*F)$ with $H_c^r(U,F)$ for $r\ge2$. Lemma [lemma-degree-zero-top-cohomology]
gives the value and the weight of this group for $r=2$, and its
vanishing for $r>2$.
