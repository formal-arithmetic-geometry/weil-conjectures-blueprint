Let $k_0=\Fq$, let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k_0)$ be a prime, and fix
$\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow X_0$ be a dense quasi-compact open immersion
over $k_0$, where $X_0$ is a smooth geometrically connected affine curve.
If a lisse Weil $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $U_0$
is punctually of weights $\le a$, for $a\in\mathbf R$, then
$j_*\mathcal F$ is punctually of weights $\le a$ on $X_0$.
