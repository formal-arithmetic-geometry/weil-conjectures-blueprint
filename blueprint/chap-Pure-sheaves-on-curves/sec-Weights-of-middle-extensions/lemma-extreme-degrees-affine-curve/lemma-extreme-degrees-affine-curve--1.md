Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow C_0$ be a quasi-compact open immersion into
a smooth affine curve over $\Fq$, and let $F$ be a lisse
$\Qbarl$-Weil sheaf on $U_0$, with $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$.
Write $U$ and $C$ for the base changes to $\Fbar$. Then
$H_c^r(C,j_*F)=0$ for every integer $r<0$ or $r>2$.

If moreover $C_0$ is geometrically connected and $j$ is dense, then
$H_c^0(C,j_*F)=0$. Thus in this case
$H_c^r(C,j_*F)=0$ for $r\ne1,2$.
