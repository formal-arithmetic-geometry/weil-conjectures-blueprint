In the situation of [def-sfb-dc-open-base-change-morphism], assume that the square is
cartesian, i.e. $X'=X\times_ZZ'=g^{-1}(X)$. Then the exchange morphism
$\mathrm{Ex}^{j}_{sq}\colon j'_!\circ Lg'^*\to Lg^*\circ j_!$ is an isomorphism, for every
commutative condensed ring $\Lambda$.
