Let $\Lambda=\underline E$ for a finite extension $E$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$, or $\Lambda=\underline{\Qlb}$.
In the situation of [det-def-sfb-shriek-base-change-morphism-definition], the base change morphism
$\mathrm{BC}\colon Lg^*Rp_*\Rightarrow Rp'_*L\bar g^*$ of the cartesian square formed by the proper
morphism $p\colon Z\to Y$, its base change $p'\colon Z'\to Y'$ and the projections $\bar g$, $g$ is a
natural isomorphism of functors $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Z,\Lambda)\to D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Y',\Lambda)$. Hence so
is its whiskering $\mathrm{BC}_{i_!}\colon Lg^*Rp_*i_!\Rightarrow Rp'_*L\bar g^*i_!$.
