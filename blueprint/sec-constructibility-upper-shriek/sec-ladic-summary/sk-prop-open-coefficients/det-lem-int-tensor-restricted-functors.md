Let $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, $E$ or $\Qlb$. Inverse image, extension by zero along an open
immersion and tensor product, and (by Deligne's finiteness theorems) direct image and
internal Hom ([lem-sfb-dc-hom-preserves]), restrict to the full subcategories
$D_{\mathrm{cons}}(-,\Lambda)$ of bounded constructible complexes: [def-sfb-dc-upper-star],
[def-sfb-dc-open-lower-shriek], [def-sfb-dc-tensor], [def-sfb-dc-lower-star],
[def-sfb-dc-hom]. The ambient adjunctions $Lf^*\dashv Rf_*$, $j_!\dashv Lj^*$ and
$K\otimes^{\mathbf L}-\dashv\RHom(K,-)$ and the composition isomorphisms
$L(g\circ f)^*\cong Lf^*\circ Lg^*$ restrict along the fully faithful inclusions:
[def-sfb-dc-upper-lower-adjunction], [def-sfb-dc-open-lower-shriek-adjunction],
[def-sfb-dc-tensor-hom-adjunction], [def-sfb-dc-upper-star-comp].
