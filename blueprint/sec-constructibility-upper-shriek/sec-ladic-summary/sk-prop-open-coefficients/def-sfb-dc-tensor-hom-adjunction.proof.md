On $D(\Mod_{\Lambda_X})$, the functor $\RHom_{\Lambda_X}(K,-)$ is right adjoint to
$K\otimes^{\mathrm L}_{\Lambda_X}-$ ([det-def-sfb-derived-tensor-hom]). Both functors preserve
bounded constructible complexes, and their restrictions commute with the inclusion
([def-sfb-dc-tensor], [def-sfb-dc-hom]). So the Hom-bijections restrict to $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Lambda)$.
