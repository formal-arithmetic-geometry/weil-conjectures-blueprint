By [det-def-sfb-shriek-base-change-morphism-definition],
$\mathrm{Ex}_!^c=Rp'_*(\mathrm{Ex}^j)^{-1}\circ\mathrm{BC}_{i_!}$. The first factor $\mathrm{BC}_{i_!}$ is a
natural isomorphism by [det-thm-sfb-shriek-base-change-proper-step]; this is the only place where
properness enters, through the proper morphism $p$ of the compactification, not through $f$. The
second factor is $Rp'_*$ applied to the inverse of the extension-by-zero exchange isomorphism of the
cartesian square $(g',i,i',\bar g)$, which is invertible for every commutative condensed ring
([thm-sfb-dc-open-base-change-iso]); a functor carries natural isomorphisms to natural isomorphisms.
A composite of natural isomorphisms is a natural isomorphism.
