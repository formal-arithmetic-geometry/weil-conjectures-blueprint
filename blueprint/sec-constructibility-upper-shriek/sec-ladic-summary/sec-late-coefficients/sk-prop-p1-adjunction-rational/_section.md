For $\Lambda=E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, the functor
$q^*(1)[2]:\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda))\to\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(\mathbf P^1_Y,\Lambda))$
is right adjoint to $Rq_*$. Hence $q^!\simeq q^*(1)[2]$ for the right
adjoint $q^!$ of Theorem [sk-prop-sf-existence].
