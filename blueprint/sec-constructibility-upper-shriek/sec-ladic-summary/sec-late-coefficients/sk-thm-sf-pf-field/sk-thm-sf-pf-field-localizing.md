Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and let $p:X\to Y$ be proper. For
$A\in\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$ and $B\in\Loc(\Dbc(Y,\Lambda))$ the projection
formula map

$$
(Rp_*A)\otimes^{\mathbf L}B\longrightarrow Rp_*(A\otimes^{\mathbf L}p^*B)
$$

is an isomorphism in $\Loc(\Dbc(Y,\Lambda))$.
