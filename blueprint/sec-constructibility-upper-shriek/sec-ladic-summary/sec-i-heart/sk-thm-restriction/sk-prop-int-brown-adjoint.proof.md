The category $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))$ is a triangulated category with
arbitrary direct sums (the ordinary ones in $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$),
generated as a localizing subcategory by $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, whose objects
are compact there by [sk-prop-int-compactness]; the isomorphism classes of
$\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ form a set, since $\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E)$ is essentially
small ([sk-lem-int-integral-heart]). So it is compactly generated, and
Brown representability applies: an exact functor out of it preserving direct
sums has a right adjoint. $Rf_!$ is such a functor by
[det-prop-int-brown-construction]. Its right adjoint $f^!$ is exact (a right
adjoint of an exact functor between triangulated categories is exact), and
it is unique up to unique isomorphism compatible with the counits. This is
exactly the construction of [sk-prop-sf-existence] specialised to
$\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$.
