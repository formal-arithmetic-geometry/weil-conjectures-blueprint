The conditions of Proposition [sk-prop-integral-criterion] are stable under
cones. Where the object is pro-étale locally of the form
$\mathcal O_{E,S}\otimes P^\bullet$ as in
Proposition [sk-prop-integral-local-structure], flatness gives
$H^i=\mathcal O_{E,S}\otimes H^i(P^\bullet)$, which yields truncations, (2)
and (1); essential smallness holds because a constructible sheaf is
determined up to isomorphism by a stratification, local presentations,
descent data and finitely many extension classes. A length one free
resolution of $P$ gives (3).
