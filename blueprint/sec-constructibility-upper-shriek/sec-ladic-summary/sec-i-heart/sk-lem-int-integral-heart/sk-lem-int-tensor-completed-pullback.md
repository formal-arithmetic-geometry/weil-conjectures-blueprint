Let $g\colon X\to Y$ be a morphism. For $K\in\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E)$ the inverse image $g^*K$ lies in
$\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, and the natural comparison map from $g^*K$ (after restriction of scalars to the constant
sheaf $\underline{\mathcal O_E}$) to the completed inverse image of the Stacks Project,
$R\varprojlim_ng^*(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, is an isomorphism.
