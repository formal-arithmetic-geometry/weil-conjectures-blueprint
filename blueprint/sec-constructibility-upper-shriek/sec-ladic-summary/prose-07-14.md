For $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we have $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}=\Dbc$, and we
show that $f^!$ preserves $\Dbc$, and for $\mathcal O_E$ we also check the
compactness hypotheses of Theorem [sk-prop-sf-existence]. The argument of
Section [sec-ctf-finite-geometric-restriction] needs two geometric facts, the
finiteness of $Rj_*$ for an open immersion and the adjunction for the
projective line, and we deduce both from torsion coefficients. An integral
complex lies in $\Dbc$ if it is derived complete with constructible
reduction modulo $\pi_E$ (Proposition [sk-prop-integral-criterion]), and
rational complexes are built from integral ones
(Lemma [sk-lem-integral-models](4)).

For $Rj_*$ this reduction is immediate, since $Rj_*$ commutes with
$R\varprojlim$ and with cones (Proposition [sk-prop-open-coefficients]).
For the projective line it is not, because the unit and counit are
morphisms, and a compatible family of morphisms modulo $\ell^n$ need not
come from a morphism of the limits. The class of the diagonal and the trace
are therefore constructed once, at the level of inverse systems
(Section [sec-late-coefficients]). The integral case needs, in addition,
the compactness of integral constructible complexes, without which Brown
representability does not produce $f^!$ in the first place (Section [sec-i-heart]).
