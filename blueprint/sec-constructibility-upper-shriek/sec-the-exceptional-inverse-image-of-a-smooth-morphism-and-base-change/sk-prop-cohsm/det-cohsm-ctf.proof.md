For $N\in\Dctf(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)$, (G11) of
[sk-prop-cohsm] gives $f^!N\simeq f^*N\otimes^{\mathbf L}\omega_f$. The complex
$f^*N$ is constructible of finite Tor amplitude, and tensoring with the
invertible object $\omega_f$ (locally $\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$, by part (1))
preserves this, so $f^!N\in\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)$.
Since $\Dctf$ is a full subcategory on both sides, (G11) and (G12) restrict to
isomorphisms of functors on these categories.
