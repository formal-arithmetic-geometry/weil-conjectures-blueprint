Let $f:X\to S$ be as in [sk-prop-cohsm] and $\Lambda=\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z$.
Then $f^!$ carries $\Dctf(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$
([sk-def-sfb-constructible]) into $\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, and (G11)
and (G12) of [sk-prop-cohsm] are isomorphisms of functors on these
categories.
