This is classical, Stacks Project, Lemma 29.35.12 ([Tag 02G1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02G1)): for a smooth morphism the module of differentials is finite locally free and its rank at a point is the dimension of the fibre there, whence the local constancy. No separate proof is given.
