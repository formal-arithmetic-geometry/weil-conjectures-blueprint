Over $S=\operatorname{Spec}\bar k$, (7.2) reads
$R\Gamma(\mathbf P^1_{\bar k},\Lambda)\simeq\Lambda\oplus\Lambda(-1)[-2]$.
The shift $[-2]$ places the second summand in degree $2$, so that it is
$H^2$. The twist $(-1)$ records how Galois acts on $H^2$: the
Kummer map identifies $H^2$ with
$\operatorname{Pic}(\mathbf P^1)\otimes\mu_{\ell^r}^{\otimes-1}$, that is, with
$\Lambda(-1)$. Twisting by $(1)$ and shifting
by $[2]$ moves this summand to degree $0$ with trivial twist, and
$\operatorname{Tr}_p$ is the projection onto it, which sends the class
of a point to $1$. This map is the counit of
$Rp_*\dashv p^*(1)[2]$ in Proposition [sk-prop-geo-p1-adjunction].
The same map is the trace map $\operatorname{Tr}_p$ of $\mathbf P^1$ in Section [sec-sp-grp-trace],
after its lift to $\mathcal O_E$ in Section [sec-late-coefficients].
As a check of the twist, over $\mathbf F_q$ the geometric Frobenius acts
by $1$ on $H^0$ and by $q$ on $H^2=\Lambda(-1)$, and the
alternating sum of the traces is $1+q=\#\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_q)$, as in
Corollary [sk-cor-tf-point-counting].
