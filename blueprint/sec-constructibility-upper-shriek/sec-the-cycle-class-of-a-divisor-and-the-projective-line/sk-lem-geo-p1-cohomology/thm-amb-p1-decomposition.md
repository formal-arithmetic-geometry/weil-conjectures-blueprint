Let $S$ be a Noetherian scheme on which $\ell$ is invertible, let $A$ be a
finite $\ell$-primary ring, killed by an integer $n$ that is a power of
$\ell$, and let $q:P=\mathbf P^1_S\to S$.
Let $h$ be the Kummer first Chern class of $\mathcal O_P(1)$.
Then

$$
(1,h):A_S\oplus A_S(-1)[-2]\xrightarrow{\sim}Rq_*A_P
$$

is an isomorphism, compatible with arbitrary change of $S$.
