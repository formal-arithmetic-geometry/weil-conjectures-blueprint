Multiplication by $n$ on a commutative group scheme is a homomorphism
[lem-sc-mult-n-homomorphism]. The general and special linear group schemes
[def-sc-general-linear-group], the units of polynomial and Laurent
polynomial rings [lem-sc-laurent-units], and the two consequences that
drive the character computation: a homomorphism from a split torus to
$\mathbf G_m$ is a monomial [lem-sc-torus-homomorphism-monomial], and
there is no nontrivial homomorphism $\mathbf G_a\to\mathbf G_m$
[lem-sc-ga-gm-homomorphism]. With Gaussian elimination
[lem-sc-elementary-generation] and the detection of morphisms on rational
points [lem-sc-points-detect-morphisms], every character of
$\mathrm{GL}_c$ is a power of the determinant [lem-sc-gl-characters].
