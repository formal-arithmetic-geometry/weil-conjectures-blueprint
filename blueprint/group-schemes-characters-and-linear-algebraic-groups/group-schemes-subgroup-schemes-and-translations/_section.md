Group schemes over a field and their kernels [def-sc-group-scheme],
subgroup schemes [def-sc-group-scheme-subgroup], and the four standard
examples $\mathbf G_m$, $\mathbf G_a$, $\mu_n$, $\alpha_p$
[def-sc-gm-ga]. A kernel is a closed subgroup scheme
[lem-sc-kernel-subgroup]; translations and conjugation
[def-sc-group-scheme-translate] are automorphisms
[lem-sc-translation-automorphism]; and the scheme-theoretic image of a
homomorphism from a proper group scheme is a closed subgroup scheme
[lem-sc-image-subgroup].
