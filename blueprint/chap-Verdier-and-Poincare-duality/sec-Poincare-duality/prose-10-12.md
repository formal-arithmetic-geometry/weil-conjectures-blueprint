Over a finite base field $k$ the same argument gives us isomorphisms
$Ra_{X*}\mathbb D_XK\simeq\mathbb D_sRa_{X!}K$ and
$Ra_{X!}\mathbb D_XK\simeq\mathbb D_sRa_{X*}K$ in $D(s_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$,
with $\mathbb D_s=\cHom_s(-,\Lambda_s)$. These are the first two formulas
of Corollary [corollary-verdier-exchange-formulas] for $f=a_X$. After
base change to an algebraic closure they become the isomorphisms of the
theorem.

The first isomorphism of the theorem is the adjoint of a pairing. We give a
name to pairings whose adjoint is an isomorphism.
