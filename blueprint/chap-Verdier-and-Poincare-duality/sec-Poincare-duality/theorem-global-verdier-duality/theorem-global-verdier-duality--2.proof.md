Apply the ordinary-direct-image exchange formula of Corollary [corollary-verdier-exchange-formulas--2] to $a_X$. The same point-category identification as in clause [theorem-global-verdier-duality--1] makes the dual on $s$ the linear derived dual. This gives the second global isomorphism.
