Suppose $k$ is algebraically closed. For every
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ there are natural isomorphisms

$$
\begin{aligned}
R\Gamma(X,\mathbb D_XK)&\simeq
R\!\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(R\Gamma_c(X,K),\Lambda),
\end{aligned}
\tag{10.5}
$$
