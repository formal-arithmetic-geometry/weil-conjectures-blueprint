The internal-adjunction comparison in Lemma [lemma-internal-adjunction-two-exchange-formulas] is defined by transposing evaluation after the projection formula and the counit. For $f=a_X$ this composite is cup product with mixed supports, evaluation into $\omega_X$, and $\varepsilon_{a_X}$. Thus its adjoint is exactly the first global isomorphism of clause [theorem-global-verdier-duality--1].
