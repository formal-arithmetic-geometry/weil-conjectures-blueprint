Apply the compact-direct-image exchange formula of Corollary [corollary-verdier-exchange-formulas--1] to $a_X:X\to s$. The dualizing object on $s$ is $\Lambda_s$. By Lemma [lemma-perfect-complexes-geometric-point], global sections identifies constructible complexes on this geometric point with perfect $\Lambda$-complexes and their internal dual with $R\!\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(-,\Lambda)$. The exchange formula is therefore the asserted first global isomorphism.
