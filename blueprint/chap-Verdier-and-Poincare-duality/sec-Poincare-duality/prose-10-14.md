For $X=\mathbf A^1$ and $K=\Lambda$ the theorem pairs
$R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,\Lambda)=\Lambda(-1)[-2]$ with
$R\Gamma(\mathbf A^1,\Lambda(1))[2]=\Lambda(1)[2]$, and the pairing is
the trace $H^2_c(\mathbf A^1,\Lambda(1))\simeq\Lambda$. Over a field we
can pass from this statement about complexes to statements about the
individual cohomology groups.
