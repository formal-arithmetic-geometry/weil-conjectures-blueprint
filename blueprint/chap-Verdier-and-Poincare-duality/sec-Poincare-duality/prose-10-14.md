For $X=\mathbf A^1$ and $K=\Lambda$ the theorem pairs
$R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,\Lambda)=\href{@definition-tate-twists}{\Lambda(-1)}[-2]$ with
$R\Gamma(\mathbf A^1,\href{@definition-tate-twists}{\Lambda(1)})[2]=\href{@definition-tate-twists}{\Lambda(1)}[2]$, and the pairing is
the trace $H^2_c(\mathbf A^1,\href{@definition-tate-twists}{\Lambda(1)})\simeq\Lambda$. Over a field we
can pass from this statement about complexes to statements about the
individual cohomology groups.
