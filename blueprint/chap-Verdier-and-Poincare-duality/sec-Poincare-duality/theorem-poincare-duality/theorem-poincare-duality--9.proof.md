The pairing is the composite of cup product, evaluation, the purity map $\gamma_{a_X}$ and the trace. Tensor, internal Hom and both direct images commute with the coefficient comparisons by Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients]. Purity and trace do so by Proposition [proposition-compatibility-purity-change-coefficients]. Applying these compatibilities to each factor transports the composite to $\beta_{K_{\Lambda'}}$, including its adjoint.
