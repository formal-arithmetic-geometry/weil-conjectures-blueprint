For the finite-projective local model of clause [theorem-poincare-duality--4], each ordinary Hom functor into the coefficient ring computes the corresponding derived Hom. The termwise dual complex therefore represents $\cHom_X(K,\Lambda_X)$ with its usual evaluation. These identifications are natural and glue, so it is the ordinary perfect-complex dual.
