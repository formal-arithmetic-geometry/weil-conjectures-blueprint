By clause [theorem-poincare-duality--6], $\beta_K$ is the global Verdier pairing transported by the purity identification $\omega_X\simeq\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$. The adjoint of that pairing is the isomorphism in Theorem [theorem-global-verdier-duality--1]. Transporting the adjunction gives exactly the displayed canonical isomorphism.
