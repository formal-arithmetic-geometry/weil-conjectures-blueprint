$\href{@definition-constructible-sheaves}{D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)^{\mathrm{op}}}\to \href{@definition-constructible-sheaves}{D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)}$.
