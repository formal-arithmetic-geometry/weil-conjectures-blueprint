We prove biduality by induction on dimension, restricting the
double-dual morphism to the geometric generic fibre of a map to
$\mathbf A^1_k$. This needs comparison maps between the operations on
a scheme and on its geometric generic fibre. Let $B/k$ be a
smooth integral curve and $\bar\eta=\operatorname{Spec}\overline{k(B)}$, and for a $B$-scheme $T$ of finite type let
$g_T:T_{\bar\eta}=T\times_B\bar\eta\to T$ be the projection, with
pullback $g_T^*:D(T_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)\to D(T_{\bar\eta,\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$, where $A$
is a finite coefficient ring. First, for a morphism $h:T\to X$ of $B$-schemes
the base change map $g_X^*Rh_*L\to Rh_{\bar\eta,*}g_T^*L$ is
adjoint to $g_T^*$ of the counit $h^*Rh_*L\to L$. Second, for a closed immersion $i:Z\to X$ of $B$-schemes, the pullback
$i_{\bar\eta,*}g_Z^*i^!M=g_X^*i_*i^!M\to g_X^*M$ of the counit
corresponds under $i_{\bar\eta,*}\dashv i_{\bar\eta}^!$ to a map $g_Z^*i^!M\to i_{\bar\eta}^!g_X^*M$. Finally, for internal Hom,
$g_X^*\cHom_X(K,M)\to\cHom_{X_{\bar\eta}}(g_X^*K,g_X^*M)$ is the
tensor–Hom adjoint of $g_X^*$ of evaluation
$\cHom_X(K,M)\otimes^{\mathbf L}K\to M$. The next lemma says that these
maps are isomorphisms.
