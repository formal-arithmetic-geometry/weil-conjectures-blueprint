In the setting above, let $X$ be a $B$-scheme of finite type, and let
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ and $M\in\Dbc(X,A)$. The map

$$
g_X^*\cHom_X(K,M)\longrightarrow
\cHom_{X_{\bar\eta}}(g_X^*K,g_X^*M)
$$

defined above, the tensor–Hom adjoint of $g_X^*$ of evaluation, is an
isomorphism.
