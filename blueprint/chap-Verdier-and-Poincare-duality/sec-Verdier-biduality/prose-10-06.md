The proof of Theorem [theorem-verdier-biduality] needs two preliminary facts. First, Lemma [lemma-verdier-duality-preserves-constructibility] shows that
$\mathbb D_X$ preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$. Second, Lemmas [lemma-restriction-geometric-generic-fibre] and [lemma-double-dual-geometric-generic-fibre] show that $\mathbb D_X$ and
$\delta$ commute with restriction to a geometric generic fibre up to the
twist $(1)[2]$, and this twist cancels in the double dual.
