The proof of Theorem [theorem-verdier-biduality] needs two preliminary facts. First, Lemma [lemma-verdier-duality-preserves-constructibility] shows that
[$\mathbb D_X$](@definition-verdier-dual-evaluation) preserves [$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves). Second, Lemmas [lemma-restriction-geometric-generic-fibre] and [lemma-double-dual-geometric-generic-fibre] show that [$\mathbb D_X$](@definition-verdier-dual-evaluation) and
[$\delta$](@definition-verdier-dual-evaluation) commute with restriction to a geometric generic fibre up to the
twist $(1)[2]$, and this twist cancels in the double dual.
