The preceding coefficient clauses give a natural isomorphism $\mathrm{id}\xrightarrow{\delta}\mathbb D_X\mathbb D_X$ on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$. Regard one occurrence of $\mathbb D_X$ as a functor to the opposite category and the other as a functor from it. The same natural biduality isomorphisms identify both composites with their identity functors. Thus these two functors are quasi-inverses, and $\mathbb D_X$ is an anti-equivalence.
