Let $k_0=\Fq$ have characteristic different from the prime $\ell$, and let $a_X:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k_0$ be separated and of finite type. Work in the bounded constructible derived category of $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-Weil sheaves, denoted $\mathcal W(X)$, with the exceptional inverse image $a_X^!$ right adjoint to $Ra_{X,!}$. Put $\omega_X=a_X^!\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ and $\mathbb D_X=\RHom(-,\omega_X)$.

The functor $\mathbb D_X:\mathcal W(X)^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal W(X)$ admits a compatibility with shifts for which it is triangulated. Thus Verdier duality is exact on Weil complexes.
