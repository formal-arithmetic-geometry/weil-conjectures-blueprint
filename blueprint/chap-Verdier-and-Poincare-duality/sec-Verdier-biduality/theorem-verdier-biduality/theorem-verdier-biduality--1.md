Let $k$ be a field, let $A$ be a finite commutative ring whose exponent is invertible in $k$, and let $a_X:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be separated and of finite type. Put $\omega_X=a_X^!A$ and $\mathbb D_XK=\RHom(K,\omega_X)$ on the étale site. For every $K\in\Dctf(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$, the double-dual morphism

$$
\delta_K:K\longrightarrow\mathbb D_X\mathbb D_XK
$$

is an isomorphism.
