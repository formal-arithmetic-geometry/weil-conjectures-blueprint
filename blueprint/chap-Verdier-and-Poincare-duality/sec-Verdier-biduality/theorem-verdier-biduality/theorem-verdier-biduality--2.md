Let $\ell$ be prime, let $k$ be a field of characteristic different from $\ell$ that is of positive characteristic or algebraically closed, and let $a_X:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be separated and of finite type. Let $E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ be a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$, with ring of integers $\mathcal O_E$. For every $K\in\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$, with $\omega_X=a_X^!\mathcal O_E$ and $\mathbb D_XK=R\!\operatorname{Hom}(K,\omega_X)$, the morphism

$$
\delta_K:K\longrightarrow\mathbb D_X\mathbb D_XK
$$

is an isomorphism.
