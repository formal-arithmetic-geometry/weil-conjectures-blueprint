Let $\ell$ be prime, let $k$ be a field of positive characteristic different from $\ell$, and let $a_X:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be separated and of finite type. For every $K\in\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$, with $\omega_X=a_X^!\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ and $\mathbb D_XK=\RHom(K,\omega_X)$, the morphism

$$
\delta_K:K\longrightarrow\mathbb D_X\mathbb D_XK
$$

is an isomorphism.
