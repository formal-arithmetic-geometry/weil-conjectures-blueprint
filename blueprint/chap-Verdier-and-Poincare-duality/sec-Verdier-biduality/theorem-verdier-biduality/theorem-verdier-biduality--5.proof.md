Derived internal Hom into a fixed object is an exact contravariant functor, as in clause [definition-verdier-dual-evaluation--3]. Lemma [lemma-verdier-duality-preserves-constructibility] puts its values on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ back in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$. Restricting this exact functor to the full triangulated subcategory therefore gives the stated exactness.

*Source:* the proof of Theorem [theorem-verdier-biduality].
