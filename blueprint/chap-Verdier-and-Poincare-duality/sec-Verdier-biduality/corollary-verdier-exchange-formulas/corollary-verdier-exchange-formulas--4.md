This formula is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem; it is
retained as the informal biduality companion of the inverse-image
exchange.

In the setting of the parent corollary, on
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ the formula is

$$
\mathbb D_Xf^!_\Lambda\simeq f^*_\Lambda\mathbb D_Y.
$$
