This formula is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem; it is
retained as the informal biduality companion of the compact-support
exchange.

In the setting of the parent corollary, on
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ the formula is

$$
\mathbb D_YRf_{*,\Lambda}\simeq Rf_{!,\Lambda}\mathbb D_X.
$$
