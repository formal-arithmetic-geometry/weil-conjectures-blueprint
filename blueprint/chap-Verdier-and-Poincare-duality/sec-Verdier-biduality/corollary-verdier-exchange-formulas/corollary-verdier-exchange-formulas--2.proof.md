Apply clause [corollary-verdier-exchange-formulas--1] to $\mathbb D_XK$ and then apply $\mathbb D_Y$. Biduality on $X$ and $Y$ (Theorem [theorem-verdier-biduality--1] and its coefficient extensions) cancels both double duals and gives the asserted comparison. The preservation assertions of Proposition [proposition-six-operations-dctf--1]–[proposition-six-operations-dctf--15] ensure that biduality applies to every object involved.
