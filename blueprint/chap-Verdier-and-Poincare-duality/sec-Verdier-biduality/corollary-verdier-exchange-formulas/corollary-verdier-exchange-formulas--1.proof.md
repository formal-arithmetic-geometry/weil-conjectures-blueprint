By Lemma [lemma-internal-adjunction-two-exchange-formulas], internal adjunction identifies $R\!\operatorname{Hom}_{\Lambda_Y}(Rf_{!,\Lambda}K,a_Y^!\Lambda)$ with $Rf_{*,\Lambda}R\!\operatorname{Hom}_{\Lambda_X}(K,f_\Lambda^!a_Y^!\Lambda)$.
