The general inverse-image exchange is bypassed by the Lean proof of the
main theorem; the mainline instead uses specific comparisons for open
restriction and for geometric generic fibres.

In the setting of the parent corollary, on
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ the retained
informal formula is

$$
\mathbb D_Xf^*_\Lambda\simeq f^!_\Lambda\mathbb D_Y.
$$
