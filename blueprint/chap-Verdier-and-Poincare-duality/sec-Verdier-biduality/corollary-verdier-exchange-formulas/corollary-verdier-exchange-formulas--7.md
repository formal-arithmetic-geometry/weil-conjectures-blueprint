This formula is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem; it is
retained as the informal closed-immersion case of the exceptional
inverse-image exchange.

In the setting of the parent corollary, for a closed immersion
$i:Z\hookrightarrow X$, the formula on
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is

$$
i^*_\Lambda\mathbb D_X\simeq\mathbb D_Zi^!_\Lambda.
$$
