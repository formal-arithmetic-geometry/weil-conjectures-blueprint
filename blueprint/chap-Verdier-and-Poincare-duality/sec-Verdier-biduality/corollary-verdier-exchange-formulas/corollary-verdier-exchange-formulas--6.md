This formula is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem; it is
retained as the informal open-immersion case of the ordinary direct-image
exchange.

In the setting of the parent corollary, for an open immersion
$j:U\hookrightarrow X$, the formula on
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(U_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is

$$
\mathbb D_XRj_{*,\Lambda}\simeq j_{!,\Lambda}\mathbb D_U.
$$
