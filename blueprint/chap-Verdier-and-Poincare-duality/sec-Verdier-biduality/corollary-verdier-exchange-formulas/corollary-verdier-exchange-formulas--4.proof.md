Apply clause [corollary-verdier-exchange-formulas--3] to $\mathbb D_YL$ and dualize on $X$. Biduality on $X$ and $Y$ cancels both double duals and gives $\mathbb D_Xf_\Lambda^!L\simeq f_\Lambda^*\mathbb D_YL$. The preservation results of Proposition [proposition-six-operations-dctf--1]–[proposition-six-operations-dctf--15] ensure that these biduality maps are isomorphisms.
