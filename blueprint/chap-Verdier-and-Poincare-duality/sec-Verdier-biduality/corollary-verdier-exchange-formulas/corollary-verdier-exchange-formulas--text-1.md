*Formalized context.* The compact-support exchange and the extension-by-zero
comparison below use finite commutative coefficients on the small étale
sites. They are available on the whole derived category, with explicit
compactification hypotheses. The other five formulas are retained in the
setting of the parent corollary and marked as bypassed by the mainline;
their presence here does not assert a formalization uniform in all
admissible coefficients.
