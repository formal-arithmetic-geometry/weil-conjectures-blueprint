For an open $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ and a closed $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$, the indicated specialization is

$$
\mathbb D_Xj_{!,\Lambda}\simeq Rj_{*,\Lambda}\mathbb D_U.
$$
