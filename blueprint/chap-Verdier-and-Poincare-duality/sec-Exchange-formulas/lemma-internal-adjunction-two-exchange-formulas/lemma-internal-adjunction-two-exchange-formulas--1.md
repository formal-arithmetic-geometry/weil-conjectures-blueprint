Let $f:X\to Y$ be a morphism, $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ and
$L\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$. The map
(10.1) and the two maps derived from it
and defined above are isomorphisms

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cHom_Y(Rf_!K,L)&\simeq
Rf_*\cHom_X(K,f^!L),
\end{aligned}
\tag{10.2}
$$
