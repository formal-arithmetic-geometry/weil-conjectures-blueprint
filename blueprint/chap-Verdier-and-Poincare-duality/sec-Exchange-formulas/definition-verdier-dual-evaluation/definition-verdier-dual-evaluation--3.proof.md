Choose a K-injective complex representing $\omega_X$. Derived internal Hom into this complex carries a mapping-cone triangle in the first variable to the corresponding contravariant mapping-cone triangle, with the usual shift and sign. Every distinguished triangle is isomorphic to a cone triangle. Hence $\cHom_{\Lambda_X}(-,\omega_X)$ sends distinguished triangles in the opposite category to distinguished triangles and has the required shift identification. It is therefore exact.
