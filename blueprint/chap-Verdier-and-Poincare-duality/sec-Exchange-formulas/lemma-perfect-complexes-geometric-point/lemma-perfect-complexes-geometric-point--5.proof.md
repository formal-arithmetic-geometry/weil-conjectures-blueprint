Choose a compactification $X\xrightarrow{j}\overline X\xrightarrow{\bar a}s$.
The open immersion $j$ is quasi-compact. Constructibility of direct image
and the Tor amplitude bound show that $Rj_*K$ belongs to
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(\overline X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$: a Tor interval
$[a,b]$ for $K$ becomes $[a,b+2d]$, where $d$ bounds the geometric fibre
dimensions of $a_X$. Since $\bar a$ is proper, the identifications

$$
R\Gamma_c(\overline X,Rj_*K)\simeq
R\Gamma(\overline X,Rj_*K)\simeq R\Gamma(X,K)
$$

reduce the assertion to [lemma-perfect-complexes-geometric-point--4].
