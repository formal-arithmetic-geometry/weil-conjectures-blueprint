Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring whose exponent is invertible in
the separably closed field $k$. Let $a_X:X\to s$ be quasi-compact, locally
of finite type, and admit a compactification. For
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, the complex
$R\Gamma(X,K)$ is perfect over $\Lambda$.
