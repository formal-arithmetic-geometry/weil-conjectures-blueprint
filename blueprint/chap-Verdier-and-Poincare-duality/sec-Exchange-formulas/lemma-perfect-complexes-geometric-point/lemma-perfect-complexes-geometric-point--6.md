The general assertion that every geometric stalk is a perfect complex is
bypassed by the Lean route; its finite-coefficient point input is the
following statement.

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring and let $S$ be a Noetherian
scheme with at most one point. Every
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(S_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is dualizable for the
derived tensor product.
