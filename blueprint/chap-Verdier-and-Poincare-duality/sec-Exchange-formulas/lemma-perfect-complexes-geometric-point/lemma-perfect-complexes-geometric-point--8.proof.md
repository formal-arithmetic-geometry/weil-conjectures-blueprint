Use a bounded finite-projective representative of $P$. For each finite projective module, evaluation into the double dual is an isomorphism. With the standard dual-complex signs these evaluation maps form the double-dual chain map. It is termwise an isomorphism and thus an isomorphism in the derived category, equal to the specified evaluation map.
