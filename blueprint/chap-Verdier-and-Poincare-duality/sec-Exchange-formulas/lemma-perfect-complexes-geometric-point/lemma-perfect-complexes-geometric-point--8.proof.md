Let $P^r$ be a right dual of $P$. Swap the duality pairing using the inverse
braiding, and identify $P^\vee$ with $P^r$ by the resulting internal-Hom
isomorphism. After this identification, the canonical double-dual map is
the transpose of the original evaluation $P\otimes P^r\to\mathbf 1$.
This transpose is an isomorphism by the duality pairing. The comparison
map is also an isomorphism, so the original double-dual map is one as well.
