A geometric stalk is the inverse image at a geometric point. The local perfect models on the constructible strata pull back to perfect complexes of the coefficient ring. Equivalently, apply inverse-image preservation and the description of the constructible category of a geometric point in clauses [lemma-perfect-complexes-geometric-point--1]–[lemma-perfect-complexes-geometric-point--3]. Thus $K_{\bar x}$ is perfect.
