Represent $K$ by a bounded complex of flat constructible sheaves. On a
Noetherian scheme with at most one point, each constructible sheaf is
locally constant: in the nonempty case a dense open on which it is locally
constant is the whole scheme, and the empty case is immediate. A flat
locally constant sheaf with finite stalks is dualizable. Hence the bounded
complex of these sheaves is dualizable, and so is $K$.
