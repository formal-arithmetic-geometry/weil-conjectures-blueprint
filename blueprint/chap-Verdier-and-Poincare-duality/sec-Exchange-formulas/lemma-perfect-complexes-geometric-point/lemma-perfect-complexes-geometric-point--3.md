Let $\ell$ be prime and let $k$ be separably closed of prime characteristic
$p\ne\ell$. There is an additive functor commuting with shifts

$$
\operatorname{Fib}_s:\Dbc(s,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)
\longrightarrow D(\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)
$$

which is fully faithful and whose essential image consists precisely of
the bounded complexes with finite-dimensional cohomology. It sends the
unit $\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,s}$ to
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell[0]$.
