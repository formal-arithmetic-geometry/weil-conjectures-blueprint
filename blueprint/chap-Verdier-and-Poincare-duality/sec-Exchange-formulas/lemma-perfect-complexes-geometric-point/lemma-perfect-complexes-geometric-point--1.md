Let $A$ be a commutative Noetherian ring and $C\in D(A)$. Suppose that $C$
is bounded, that every $H^i(C)$ is a finitely generated $A$-module, and that
$C$ has finite Tor amplitude. Then $C$ is perfect. This applies in
particular when $A$ is a finite coefficient ring.
