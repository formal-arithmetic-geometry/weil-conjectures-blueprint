*Shared context.* Put $s=\operatorname{Spec}k$. We state the hypotheses on
$k$ and the coefficients in each case. Here $\operatorname{Perf}(\Lambda)$
means bounded complexes of finite projective $\Lambda$-modules up to
quasi-isomorphism. Finite coefficients are treated on the étale site;
integral and field coefficients are treated as condensed coefficient rings
on the pro-étale site. The details below distinguish perfect complexes of
modules from dualizable objects in the categories of sheaves.
