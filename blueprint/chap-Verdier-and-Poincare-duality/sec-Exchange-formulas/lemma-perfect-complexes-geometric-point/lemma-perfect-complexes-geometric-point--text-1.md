*Shared context.* Suppose $k$ is algebraically closed and put $s=\operatorname{Spec}k$. All schemes and complexes satisfy the standing hypotheses; $\operatorname{Perf}(\Lambda)$ means bounded complexes of finite projective $\Lambda$-modules up to quasi-isomorphism.
