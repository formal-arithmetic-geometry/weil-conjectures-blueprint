Choose a right dual $P^r$ of $P$. Uniqueness of right adjoints identifies
$\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(P,\mathbf 1)$ with $P^r$. The braiding
interchanges the two objects in the duality pairing, giving $P$ as a right
dual of $P^r$. Transport this pairing along the identification to obtain a
right dual of $P^\vee$.
