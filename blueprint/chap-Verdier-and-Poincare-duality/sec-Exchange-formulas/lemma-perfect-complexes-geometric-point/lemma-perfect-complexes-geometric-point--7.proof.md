Represent $P$ by a bounded complex of finite projective $\Lambda$-modules. Its derived dual is computed by the ordinary termwise dual, whose terms are again finite projective and which is bounded. Hence $R\!\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(P,\Lambda)$ is perfect.
