Choose a Tor interval $[c,b]$ and argue by induction on its length. A bounded
flat model exists in these degrees. If $b\leq c$, its only possible nonzero
term is its cohomology in degree $b$, which is finitely presented and flat,
hence projective. Otherwise choose a finite free module surjecting onto
$H^b(C)$ and lift it to a map $F[-b]\to C$. The cone has finitely generated
cohomology and Tor amplitude in $[c,b-1]$. It is perfect by induction, and
$C$ is perfect because perfect complexes are closed under extensions.
