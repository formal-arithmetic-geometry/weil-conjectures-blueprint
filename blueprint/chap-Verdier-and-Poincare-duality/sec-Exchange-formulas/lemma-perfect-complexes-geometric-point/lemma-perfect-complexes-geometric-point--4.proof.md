Choose a compactification of $a_X$. Extension by zero followed by proper
direct image gives bounded constructible cohomology for $Ra_{X!}K$.
Over the separably closed point, sections are exact and the sections of a
constructible sheaf form a finite $\Lambda$-module. Thus
$R\Gamma_c(X,K)$ is bounded with finitely generated cohomology.

If $K$ has Tor amplitude in $[a,b]$ and the geometric fibres have dimension
at most $d$, the projection formula and the cohomological dimension bound
show that $Ra_{X!}K$ has Tor amplitude in $[a,b+2d]$. Sections over $s$
preserve this bound. Since $\Lambda$ is Noetherian, apply
[lemma-perfect-complexes-geometric-point--1].
