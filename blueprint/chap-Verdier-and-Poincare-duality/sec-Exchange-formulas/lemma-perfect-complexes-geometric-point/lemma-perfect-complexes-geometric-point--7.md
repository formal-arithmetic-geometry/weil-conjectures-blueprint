The duality argument uses the following formulation in terms of
dualizable objects. Let $\mathcal C$ be a braided monoidal closed category
with unit $\mathbf 1$. If $P$ is dualizable, then its internal dual
$P^\vee=\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(P,\mathbf 1)$ is dualizable.
