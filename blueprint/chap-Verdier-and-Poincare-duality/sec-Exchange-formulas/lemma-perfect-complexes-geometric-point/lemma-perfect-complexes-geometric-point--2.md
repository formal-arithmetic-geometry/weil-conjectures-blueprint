Let $\ell$ be prime, let $k$ be separably closed, and let
$E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ be a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$.
For $N\in\Dbc(s,\mathcal O_E)$, the complex
$\operatorname{Fib}_s(N)\in D(\mathcal O_E)$ obtained by applying sections
over $s$ is perfect. Here the point fibre functor is induced by the exact
functor $\Gamma(s,-)=\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal O_{E,s},-)$ on
$\mathcal O_{E,s}$-modules.
