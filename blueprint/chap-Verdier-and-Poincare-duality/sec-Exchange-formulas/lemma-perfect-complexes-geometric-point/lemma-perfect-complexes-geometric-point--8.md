Let $\mathcal C$ be a braided monoidal closed category with unit
$\mathbf 1$, and let $P$ have a right dual. Put
$P^\vee=\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(P,\mathbf 1)$. Then the canonical
map

$$
P\longrightarrow
\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(P^\vee,\mathbf 1)
$$

is an isomorphism. This map is the transpose of the evaluation
$P^\vee\otimes P\to\mathbf 1$, obtained using the braiding.
