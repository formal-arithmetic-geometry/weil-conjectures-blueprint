Sections over the separably closed point are exact, so they preserve the
cohomological bounds of $N$ and commute with its cohomology. Each
cohomology sheaf is finitely presented. Its sections are a quotient of a
finite direct sum of $\mathcal O_E$, since the endomorphisms of
$\mathcal O_{E,s}$ are the scalars $\mathcal O_E$. Thus
$\operatorname{Fib}_s(N)$ has bounded finitely generated cohomology.

Over the principal ideal domain $\mathcal O_E$, a finitely generated
module has a finite free resolution of length at most one. Induction on
the truncation triangles therefore shows that a bounded complex with
finitely generated cohomology is perfect.
