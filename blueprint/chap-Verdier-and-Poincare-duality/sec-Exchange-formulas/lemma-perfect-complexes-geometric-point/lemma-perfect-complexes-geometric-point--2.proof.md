On the geometric point, the comparison of Section [sec-Continuous-cohomology] identifies integral constructible complexes with complexes of $\mathcal O_E$-modules having bounded finitely generated cohomology. A DVR has global dimension one, so each such cohomology module has a finite free resolution of length at most one. The truncation triangles build the complex from finitely many of these resolutions, hence make it perfect. Conversely, a perfect complex has bounded finitely generated cohomology and finite Tor amplitude.
