Apply the exact sections functor on modules over the condensed coefficient
ring. Over $s$, constructible cohomology sheaves are finite direct sums of
the unit, and these sheaves are projective. The truncation triangles split,
so each bounded constructible complex is a finite direct sum of shifts of
such sheaves.

Sections induce bijections on morphisms between finite sums of the unit
and all their shifts; the decomposition extends this to all bounded
constructible complexes. Their images have bounded finite-dimensional
cohomology. Conversely, every bounded complex of
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-vector spaces with finite-dimensional cohomology
is a finite sum of shifts of $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell[0]$, so it lies in
the essential image. Sections identify the unit with
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell[0]$.
