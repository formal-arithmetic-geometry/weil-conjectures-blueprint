Over either field $E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, a complex with bounded finite-dimensional cohomology is quasi-isomorphic to the finite direct sum of those cohomology spaces placed in their degrees. Each space is finite free, so the complex is perfect. Conversely, a bounded complex of finite-dimensional vector spaces has exactly those finiteness properties. The comparison on the geometric point identifies this with the stated category.
