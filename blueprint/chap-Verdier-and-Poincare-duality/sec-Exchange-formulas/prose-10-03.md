On the point, then, duality is the linear duality of perfect complexes,
and the double dual is the identity. On a general $X$, however, we dualize into
[$a_X^!\Lambda_s$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image) instead of $\Lambda$. By the adjunction
$Ra_{X!}\dashv a_X^!$, maps into [$a_X^!\Lambda_s$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image) are the same as
linear forms on [compactly supported cohomology](@proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek).
