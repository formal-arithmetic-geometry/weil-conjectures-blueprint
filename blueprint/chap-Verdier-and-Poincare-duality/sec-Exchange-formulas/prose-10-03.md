On the point, then, duality is the linear duality of perfect complexes,
and the double dual is the identity. On a general $X$, however, we dualize into
$a_X^!\Lambda_s$ instead of $\Lambda$. By the adjunction
$Ra_{X!}\dashv a_X^!$, maps into $a_X^!\Lambda_s$ are the same as
linear forms on compactly supported cohomology.
