For a field $\Lambda$ and the constant sheaf, the trace isomorphism in top degree
turns Poincaré duality into the classical cup product pairing. On
$\mathbf P^1$, for instance, the only nontrivial instance is
$H^0\times H^2\to\Lambda(-1)$. After twisting by $(1)$ it pairs the
generators $1$ and $[x]$ to $1$ by Lemma [lemma-class-of-point], so it
is perfect.
