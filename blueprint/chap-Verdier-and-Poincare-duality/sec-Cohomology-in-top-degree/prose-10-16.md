For constant coefficients on a smooth, proper and connected $X$, we expect
$H^{2d}(X,\Lambda_X(d))$ to be free of rank one, generated by the class of a
point, as for a manifold. We use this normalization
in the trace formula and in point counting.

For a compact connected oriented manifold this is the statement that
integration $H^n(M)\to\mathbf R$ is an isomorphism sending a point to
$1$. Here the statement also has an arithmetic meaning, because geometric Frobenius
acts on $\Lambda(-d)$ by $q^d$. Once we know that the trace map
commutes with Frobenius (Theorem [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius]), the theorem below
says that Frobenius acts on $H^{2d}$ by $q^d$ and on $H^0$ trivially.
These give the factors $1-T$ and $1-q^dT$ of the zeta function.

Combined with
Corollary [corollary-degreewise-duality-over-fields], the theorem gives the cup product pairing
(Corollary [corollary-cup-product-pairing]).
