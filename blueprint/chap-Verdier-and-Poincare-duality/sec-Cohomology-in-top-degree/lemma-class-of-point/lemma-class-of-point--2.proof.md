By clause [lemma-class-of-point--1], the trace sends $[x]$ to $1$. The trace is an isomorphism by Theorem [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree]. Its inverse therefore sends the generator $1$ of $\Lambda$ to $[x]$, making $[x]$ a generator of the top twisted cohomology.
