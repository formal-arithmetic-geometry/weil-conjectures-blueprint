By definition, $[x]$ is the image
of $1$ under the map induced by the counit $x_*x^!\omega_X\to\omega_X$,
and $\operatorname{tr}_X(d)$ is induced by the counit
$Ra_{X*}\omega_X\to\Lambda$. Because exceptional adjunctions compose, the
composite of these two counits is the counit for $a_Xx=\mathrm{id}_s$,
which is the identity of $\Lambda$. Thus $\operatorname{tr}_X(d)([x])=1$.
