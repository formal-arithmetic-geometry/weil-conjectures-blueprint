In the setting of Theorem [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree], we
have $\operatorname{tr}_X(d)([x])=1$ for every closed point $x$ of $X$.
In particular $[x]$ generates $H^{2d}(X,\Lambda_X(d))$.
