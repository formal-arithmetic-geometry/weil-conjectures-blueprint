For $K=\Lambda_X$ we have $K^\vee=\Lambda_X$, and since $X$ is proper,
Corollary [corollary-degreewise-duality-over-fields] says that the pairing
$H^i\times H^{2d-i}\to\Lambda(-d)$ induced by $\beta_{\Lambda_X}$ is
perfect. This pairing is $\langle-,-\rangle$, because it is cup product
followed by the trace map in top
degree, that is, by the isomorphism $\operatorname{tr}_X$ of
Theorem [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree]. By graded commutativity of the
cup product we have $\langle b,a\rangle=(-1)^{i(2d-i)}\langle a,b\rangle
=(-1)^i\langle a,b\rangle$.
