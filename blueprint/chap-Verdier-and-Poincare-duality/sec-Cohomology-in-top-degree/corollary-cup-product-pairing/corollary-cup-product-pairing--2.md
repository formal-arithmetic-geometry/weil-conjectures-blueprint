Let $\Lambda$ be a field and let $H^i$, $i\in\Z$, be finite-dimensional
$\Lambda$-vector spaces with automorphisms $F_i$. Let $T$ be a
trace–cup structure of degree $n=2m$, where $m\in\Z$, and multiplier
$c\in\Lambda$: a Frobenius-compatible graded-commutative bilinear
cup product on $H^\bullet$, together with a linear trace
$\operatorname{tr}:H^n\to\Lambda$ satisfying
$\operatorname{tr}(F_nz)=c\operatorname{tr}(z)$.
For $i+j=n$, put $\langle a,b\rangle=\operatorname{tr}(a\cup b)$.
Then, for every $a\in H^i$ and $b\in H^j$,

$$
\langle b,a\rangle=(-1)^i\langle a,b\rangle.
$$
