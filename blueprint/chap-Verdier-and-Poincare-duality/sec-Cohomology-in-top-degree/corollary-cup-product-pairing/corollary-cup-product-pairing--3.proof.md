In middle degree $i=d$ with $d$ odd, clause [corollary-cup-product-pairing--2] gives $\langle a,a\rangle=-\langle a,a\rangle$. Characteristic zero makes $2$ invertible, so $\langle a,a\rangle=0$ for every $a$. The pairing is perfect by clause [corollary-cup-product-pairing--1], and is therefore the asserted perfect alternating pairing.
