This consequence is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem;
there is no separate declaration asserting that the middle pairing is
alternating.

In the setting of [corollary-cup-product-pairing--1], if $d$ is odd,
the pairing on $H^d(X,\Qbarl)$ is alternating, as for a compact
complex manifold of odd dimension.
