By the theorem, $H^{2d}(X,\Lambda_X(d))$ is free of rank one. It is
generated by the class of a point, which we define by the counit of the
exceptional adjunction for the inclusion of the point. Let
$x:\operatorname{Spec}k\to X$ be a closed point. Using
$x^!\omega_X=(a_Xx)^!\Lambda_s=\Lambda$ and
$\omega_X\simeq\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$, the counit $x_*x^!\omega_X\to\omega_X$
induces a map
$\Lambda\to R\Gamma(X,\omega_X)=R\Gamma(X,\Lambda_X(d))[2d]$, and we let
$[x]\in H^{2d}(X,\Lambda_X(d))$ be the image of $1$.
