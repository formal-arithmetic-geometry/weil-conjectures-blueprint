This finite-coefficient trace assertion is bypassed by the Lean route to the main theorem.

For the smooth, proper, connected $X/k$ of the theorem and finite admissible $A$, the specified trace $\operatorname{tr}_X:H^{2d}(X,A_X)\to A(-d)$ is an isomorphism.
