This integral-coefficient trace assertion is bypassed by the Lean route to the main theorem.

For the smooth, proper, connected $X/k$ of the theorem, the specified trace $\operatorname{tr}_X:H^{2d}(X,\mathcal O_{E,X})\to\mathcal O_E(-d)$ is an isomorphism.
