Let $S$ be a scheme of finite type and of dimension at most $d$ over a
separably closed field $k'$ in which $\ell$ is invertible. Then

$$
H^i(S,\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,S})=0\qquad(i\notin[0,2d]).
$$

In particular $H^i(X,\QbarX)=0$ for $i\notin[0,2d]$; properness and
connectedness of $X$ are not needed for this.
