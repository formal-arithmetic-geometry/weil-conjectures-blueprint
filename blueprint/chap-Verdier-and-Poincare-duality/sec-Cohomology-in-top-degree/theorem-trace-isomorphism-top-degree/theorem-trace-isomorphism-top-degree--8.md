Let $S$ be of finite type and of dimension at most $d$ over a separably closed field. For the constant $\ell$-adic coefficient sheaf,
$$
H^i(S,\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,S})=0\qquad(i>2d).
$$
In particular, let $X_0/\Fq$ be of finite type, quasi-separated and smooth of pure dimension $d$, and put $X=X_0\times_{\Fq}\Fbar$. For $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$, the cohomology of the constant Weil sheaf satisfies
$$
H^i(X,\QbarX)=0\qquad(i\notin[0,2d]).
$$
Properness and geometric connectedness are not required for this vanishing.
