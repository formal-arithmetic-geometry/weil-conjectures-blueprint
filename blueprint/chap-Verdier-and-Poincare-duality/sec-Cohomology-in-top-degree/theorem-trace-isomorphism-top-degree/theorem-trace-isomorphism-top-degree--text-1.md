*Shared context.* Let $X/k$ be smooth, proper and connected of pure dimension $d$, with $k$ algebraically closed. The trace in every clause is the twist by $(-d)$ of the map on $H^{2d}$ induced by Definition [definition-trace-map]; these are the original trace maps.
