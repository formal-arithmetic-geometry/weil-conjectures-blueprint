By Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients], the cohomology complex and its trace over $E$ are obtained from those over $\mathcal O_E$ by flat extension of scalars. Flatness commutes with cohomology and preserves vanishing. Clause [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree--4] therefore gives the assertion.
