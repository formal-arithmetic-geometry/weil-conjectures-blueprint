The informal argument takes the derived inverse limit of the finite-coefficient trace isomorphisms over $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$. Finiteness gives the Mittag–Leffler condition, and compatibility of the traces gives the integral trace isomorphism. This trace comparison is not a step of the reachable Lean proof; the profinite limit in [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree--4] is used only for vanishing.
