Apply the Milnor sequence in clause [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree--4] to the twist $(d)$ in degree $2d$. Clause [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree--3] identifies every finite-level trace with an isomorphism onto $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$. Compatibility of traces with reduction identifies their inverse limit with the integral trace. The inverse limit of these rings is $\mathcal O_E$, so this trace is an isomorphism. Undoing the twist gives the stated target.
