Let $X_0/\Fq$ be separated, of finite type and geometrically connected, and put $X=X_0\times_{\Fq}\Fbar$. For $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$, there is a linear isomorphism
$$
e:H^0(X,\QbarX)\xrightarrow{\sim}\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell,
\qquad e(Fz)=e(z).
$$
Smoothness and properness are not required.
