By Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients], the cohomology complex and its trace over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ are obtained from those over $E$ by flat extension of scalars through the filtered system of finite extensions of $E$. Flatness commutes with cohomology and preserves vanishing. Clause [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree--6] therefore gives the assertion.
