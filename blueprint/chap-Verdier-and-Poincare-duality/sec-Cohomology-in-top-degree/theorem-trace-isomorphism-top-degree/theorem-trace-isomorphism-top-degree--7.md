This finite-extension coefficient trace assertion is bypassed by the Lean route to the main theorem.

For the smooth, proper, connected $X/k$ of the theorem and a finite extension $E/\Ql$, the specified trace $\operatorname{tr}_X:H^{2d}(X,E_X)\to E(-d)$ is an isomorphism.
