The derived inverse limit description of $R\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_{E,X})$ gives the Milnor sequence $0\to\lim^1_n H^{i-1}(X,(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_X)\to H^i(X,\mathcal O_{E,X})\to\lim_n H^i(X,(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_X)\to0$. The finite groups form Mittag–Leffler systems, so the left term vanishes. Clause [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree--2] makes the right term zero outside $[0,2d]$.
