Let $S$ and $k'$ be as in clause [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree--2]. Let $A$ be a compact
topological abelian group with a decreasing sequence of open subgroups
$U_0\supset U_1\supset\cdots$ such that $\bigcap_nU_n=0$ and every quotient
$A/U_n$ has order invertible in $k'$. For the sheaf
$\mathcal C_A:V\mapsto C(V,A)$ on $S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$,

$$
H^i(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal C_A)=0\qquad(i\notin[0,2d]).
$$

This applies to $A=\mathcal O_E$ and $U_n=\pi_E^n\mathcal O_E$ when
$\ell$ is invertible in $k'$, and then $\mathcal C_A=\mathcal O_{E,S}$. In
particular $H^i(X,\mathcal O_{E,X})=0$ for $i\notin[0,2d]$.
