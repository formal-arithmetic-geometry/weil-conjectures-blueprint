Let $S$ be of finite type and of dimension at most $d$ over a separably closed field. Let $A$ be a compact topological abelian group with a decreasing sequence of open subgroups $U_n$ such that $\bigcap_nU_n=0$. For the sheaf $\mathcal C_A:V\mapsto C(V,A)$ on $S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$,
$$
H^i(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal C_A)=0\qquad(i>2d).
$$
This applies to $A=\mathcal O_E$ and $U_n=\ell^n\mathcal O_E$. Negative-degree vanishing is again formal for a sheaf in degree zero.
