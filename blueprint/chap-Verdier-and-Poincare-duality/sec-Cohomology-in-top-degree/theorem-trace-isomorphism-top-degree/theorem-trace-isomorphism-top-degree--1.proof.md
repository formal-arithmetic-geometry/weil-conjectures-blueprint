For finite coefficients, a global section of the constant sheaf is a locally constant function on the connected scheme $X$, hence a constant. For $\mathcal O_E$, take the inverse limit of these identifications over $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$; the transition maps are surjective. Inverting $\ell$ gives the assertion for $E$, and extension of scalars through finite extensions gives it for $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. These are the coefficient comparisons of Proposition [proposition-six-operations-change-coefficients].
