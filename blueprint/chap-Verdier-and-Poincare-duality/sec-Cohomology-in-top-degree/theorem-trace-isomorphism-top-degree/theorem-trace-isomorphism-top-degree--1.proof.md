Connectedness of $X$ identifies the global sections of the constant sheaf with $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, so $H^0$ has dimension one. The constant Weil structure makes Frobenius the identity on these sections. Choose a linear isomorphism with the coefficient field; it then commutes with Frobenius. In Lean these inputs are `AlgebraicGeometry.WeilSheaf.finrank_cohomology_zero_adicConstant` and `AlgebraicGeometry.WeilSheaf.frobenius_cohomology_zero_adicConstant`.
