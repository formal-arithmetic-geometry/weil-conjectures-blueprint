Let $S$ be a scheme of finite type and of dimension at most $d$ over a
separably closed field $k'$, and let $A$ be a finite abelian group whose order
is invertible in $k'$. Write $\mathcal C_A$ for the sheaf
$V\mapsto C(V,A)$ on $S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$. Then

$$
H^i(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal C_A)=0\qquad(i\notin[0,2d]).
$$

In particular $H^i(X,A_X)=0$ for $i\notin[0,2d]$; properness, smoothness and
connectedness of $X$ are not needed for this.
