Let $S$ be of finite type and of dimension at most $d$ over a separably closed field, and let $A$ be a finite discrete abelian group. Write $\mathcal C_A$ for the sheaf $U\mapsto C(U,A)$ on $S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$. Then
$$
H^i(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal C_A)=0\qquad(i>2d).
$$
For finite admissible coefficient rings this gives the upper bound in the theorem. Negative-degree vanishing follows from placing a sheaf in degree zero.
