Corollary [corollary-degreewise-duality-over-fields] makes the untwisted pairing perfect. In dual bases let $F_i$ and $F_{2d-i}$ be the two Frobenius matrices. Clause [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--4] gives $F_i^tF_{2d-i}=q^d I$. By clause [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--5], $F_i$ is invertible. Solving gives $F_{2d-i}=q^d(F_i^{-1})^t$.
