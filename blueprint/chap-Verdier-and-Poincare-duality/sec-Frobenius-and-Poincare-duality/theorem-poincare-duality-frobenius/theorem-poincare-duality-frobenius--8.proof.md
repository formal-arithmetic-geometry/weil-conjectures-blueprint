After extending scalars to an algebraic closure, transposition preserves the characteristic polynomial, while inversion replaces each nonzero eigenvalue by its reciprocal with algebraic multiplicity. Apply these facts to clause [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--6]; multiplication by $q^d$ gives the listed eigenvalues.
