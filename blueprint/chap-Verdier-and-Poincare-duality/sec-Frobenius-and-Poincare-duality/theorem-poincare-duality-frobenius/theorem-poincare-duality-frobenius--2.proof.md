Cup product with mixed supports and evaluation commute with Frobenius pullback. Purity and trace commute with Frobenius by Proposition [proposition-purity-trace-commute-frobenius]. By the contragredient structure of clause [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--1], evaluation intertwines the two Weil structures with the constant Weil structure. Their composite $\beta_K$ therefore intertwines Frobenius.
