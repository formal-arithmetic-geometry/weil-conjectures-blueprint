Take determinants in clause [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--6]. The common dimension is $b$ by clause [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--7], so $\det F_{2d-i}=q^{db}(\det F_i)^{-1}$. Multiplying by the nonzero determinant $\det F_i$ proves the identity.
