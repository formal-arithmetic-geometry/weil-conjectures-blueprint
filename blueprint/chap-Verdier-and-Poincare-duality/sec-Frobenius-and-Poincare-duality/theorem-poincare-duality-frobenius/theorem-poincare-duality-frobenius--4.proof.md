Move the twist $(d)$ in clause [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--3] from the second factor to the values. Geometric Frobenius acts on $\Lambda(d)$ by $q^{-d}$ and hence on $\Lambda(-d)$ by $q^d$. The equivariance equality becomes the asserted formula.
