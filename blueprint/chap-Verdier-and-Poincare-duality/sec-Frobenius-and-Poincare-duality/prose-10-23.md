Weil structures are transported along $f^!$,
$f^*$ and $Rf_!$. On $f^*K(d)[2d]$ the Weil
structure is the pullback of $\phi_K$ combined with the Kummer
identification. By the proposition above and naturality of $\gamma_f$ and
$\operatorname{Tr}_f$ applied to $\phi_K$, the maps $\gamma_f(K)$ and
$\operatorname{Tr}_f(K)$ are morphisms of Weil complexes.

The constant sheaf $\Lambda_Y$ has the Weil structure given by the
identification $\operatorname{Fr}_Y^*\Lambda_Y\simeq\Lambda_Y$ of pullbacks of
constant sheaves. For $K=\Lambda_Y$ with this structure, the previous
paragraph says that the isomorphism
$\omega_f\simeq\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$ and the trace
$\operatorname{Tr}_f(\Lambda_Y)$ are morphisms of Weil complexes.
