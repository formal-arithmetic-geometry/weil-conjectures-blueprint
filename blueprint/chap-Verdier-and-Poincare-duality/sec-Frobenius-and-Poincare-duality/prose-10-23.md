[Weil structures](@definition-weil-structure) are transported along [$f^!$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image),
$f^*$ and [$Rf_!$](@proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek). On $f^*K(d)[2d]$ the [Weil
structure](@definition-weil-structure) is the pullback of $\phi_K$ combined with the Kummer
identification. By the proposition above and naturality of [$\gamma_f$](@theorem-smooth-purity-all-admissible-coefficients) and
[$\operatorname{Tr}_f$](@definition-trace-map) applied to $\phi_K$, the maps [$\gamma_f(K)$](@theorem-smooth-purity-all-admissible-coefficients) and
[$\operatorname{Tr}_f(K)$](@definition-trace-map) are morphisms of [Weil complexes](@definition-weil-structure).

The constant sheaf $\Lambda_Y$ has the [Weil structure](@definition-weil-structure) given by the
identification $\operatorname{Fr}_Y^*\Lambda_Y\simeq\Lambda_Y$ of pullbacks of
constant sheaves. For $K=\Lambda_Y$ with this structure, the previous
paragraph says that the isomorphism
$\omega_f\simeq\Lambda_X(d)[2d]$ and the trace
[$\operatorname{Tr}_f(\Lambda_Y)$](@definition-trace-map) are morphisms of [Weil complexes](@definition-weil-structure).
