Let $X_0=\mathbf G_{m,\mathbf F_q}$, with coordinate $t$, let $X$ be its base
change to $k$, and let $\Lambda=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, with the constant Weil sheaf. Since
$\operatorname{Pic}(X)=0$ and the units of $k[t,t^{-1}]$ are the $ct^m$, the
Kummer sequence gives $H^0(X,\Lambda)=\Lambda$ and
$H^1(X,\Lambda(1))=\Lambda$, generated by the Kummer class of $t$
(Proposition [proposition-cohomology-smooth-affine-curve], with $g=0$ and
two punctures).
