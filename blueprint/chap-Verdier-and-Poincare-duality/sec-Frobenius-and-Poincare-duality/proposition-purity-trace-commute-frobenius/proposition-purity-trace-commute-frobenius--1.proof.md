The structural maps are defined over $\mathbf F_q$, so their geometric Frobenius automorphisms commute with $f$. A commutative square whose two horizontal arrows are these compatible isomorphisms is cartesian: its inverse comparison is induced by the inverse Frobenius on $X$.
