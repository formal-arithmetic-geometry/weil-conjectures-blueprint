There is a $W(U_0)$-stable filtration of $\bigwedge^rV$ with graded pieces

$$
\bigotimes_{a=1}^t\bigwedge^{n_a}A_a,
\qquad 0\le n_a\le\dim A_a,\qquad\sum_an_a=r.
$$
