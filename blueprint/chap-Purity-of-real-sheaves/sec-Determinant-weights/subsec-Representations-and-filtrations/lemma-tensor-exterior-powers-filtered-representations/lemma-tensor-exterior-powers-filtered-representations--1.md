Suppose $V$ and $V'$ have composition factors $A_1,\ldots,A_t$ and
$B_1,\ldots,B_u$. There is a $W(U_0)$-stable filtration of $V\otimes V'$
with graded pieces $A_a\otimes B_b$, one for each pair $(a,b)$.
