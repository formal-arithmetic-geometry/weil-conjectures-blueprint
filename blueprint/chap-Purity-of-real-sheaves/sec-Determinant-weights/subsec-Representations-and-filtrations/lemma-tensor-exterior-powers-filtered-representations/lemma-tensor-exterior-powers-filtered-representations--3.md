The comparison with multiplicities and the nonvanishing assertion below
are bypassed by Lean; neither is supplied by the tensor-subquotient lemma.

The simple factors of $V\otimes V'$ and $\bigwedge^rV$, counted with
multiplicity, are those of the respective graded pieces. Every graded
piece of $\bigwedge^rV$ displayed above is nonzero.

The mainline tensor result only locates each simple subquotient in a
tensor product of two simple subquotients. The exterior argument uses
characteristic polynomials and the weights of exterior powers of a
semisimple representation; it does not use the displayed graded pieces
or assert that every admissible tensor product of exterior powers occurs.
