It suffices to consider a simple $\Gamma$-module $V$. A nonzero
$N$-submodule $A$ of least dimension is simple, and since $N$ is
normal, each translate $gA$ is again a simple $N$-submodule. The sum
of all these translates is a nonzero $\Gamma$-submodule, hence equals
$V$. It remains to see that a finite sum of simple submodules is semisimple. Indeed, the
intersection of a simple submodule with the sum of
those already chosen is either zero or the whole submodule, so we add it
in the first case and omit it in the second, and after finitely many steps we
obtain a direct-sum decomposition.
