We treat lisse sheaves as representations of the Weil group. From the
geometry of the curve we use only one fact, proved in
Section [sec-Sheaves-of-rank-one]: the geometric fundamental group acts
through a finite group on every determinant. Everything else is linear
algebra and the structure of algebraic groups.

Throughout, $U_0$ is a smooth geometrically connected curve over $k_0$ and
$s\in W(U_0)$ is a lift of degree one, as in Convention [situation-weil], so that
$W(U_0)=\pi_1(U)\rtimes\langle s\rangle$.
