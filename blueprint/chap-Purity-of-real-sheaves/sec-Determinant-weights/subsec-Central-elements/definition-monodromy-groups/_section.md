Let $\rho:W(U_0)\to\GL(V)$ be a Weil representation. Its
*geometric* and *arithmetic monodromy groups* are the Zariski
closures $H$ of $\rho(\pi_1(U))$ and $G$ of $\rho(W(U_0))$ in $\GL(V)$.
