Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and let
$H\subseteq G\subseteq\GL(k^n)$ be closed subgroup schemes. Put

$$
R=k[\varepsilon,\eta]/(\varepsilon^2,\eta^2).
$$

Suppose that every commutator of two elements of $G(R)$ lies in $H(R)$,
and that the defining representation of $G^\circ(k)$ on $k^n$ is
semisimple. Then every $g\in G^\circ(k)$ can be written as

$$
g=th,\qquad t\in Z(G^\circ)^\circ(k),\quad h\in H^\circ(k).
$$

In particular, if $V$ is a semisimple $W(U_0)$-representation with
monodromy groups $H\subseteq G$ as in [definition-monodromy-groups], then
$G^\circ=Z(G^\circ)^\circ H^\circ$.
