Rankin's argument needs the [determinant weights](@definition-determinant-weight) of the simple factors
of $\mathcal G^{\otimes2m}$, of the exterior powers of $\mathcal G$ and
of the dual, so we give a name to the representations built in this way;
the [monodromy group](@definition-monodromy-groups) acts on all of them.
