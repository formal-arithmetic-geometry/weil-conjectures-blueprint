Each assertion holds for the images of $\pi_1(U)$ and $W(U_0)$, and we
pass to Zariski closures by continuity.
First, multiplication and inversion are continuous for the Zariski topology,
so the closure of a subgroup is a subgroup. Next, conjugation by $\rho(W(U_0))$
preserves $H$ because $\pi_1(U)$ is normal in $W(U_0)$, and since the normalizer
of a closed subgroup is closed, conjugation by $G$ preserves $H$ as well.
Finally, since $W(U_0)/\pi_1(U)\simeq\mathbf Z$ is abelian, commutators of elements of
$\rho(W(U_0))$ lie in $\rho(\pi_1(U))$. As the commutator map
$G\times G\to\GL(V)$ is continuous, the same holds on the closures.
