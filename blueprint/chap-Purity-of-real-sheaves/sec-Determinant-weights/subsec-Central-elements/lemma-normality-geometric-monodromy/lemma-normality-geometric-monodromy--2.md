In the setting of [lemma-normality-geometric-monodromy--1], for every
commutative $k$-algebra $R$ we have
$$
[G(R),G(R)]\subseteq H(R),
$$
where $[G(R),G(R)]$ denotes the subgroup generated by the commutators
$ghg^{-1}h^{-1}$ with $g,h\in G(R)$.
