The degree of a commutator in $W$ is zero because $\mathbf Z$ is
abelian. Thus $[\rho(W),\rho(W)]\subseteq\rho(P)$.

To pass to the closures, put $A=k[\GL(k^n)]/I_G$ and consider the
universal pair of points $x,y$ of $G$ over $A\otimes_k A$.
For $f\in I_H$, the element $f(xyx^{-1}y^{-1})$ evaluates to zero
at every pair of points of $\rho(W)$. The tensor-product density
argument in [lemma-normality-geometric-monodromy--1] therefore makes
it zero in $A\otimes_k A$. Specializing the universal pair to any
$g,h\in G(R)$ gives $f(ghg^{-1}h^{-1})=0$. Since $H(R)$ is a
subgroup, it contains the subgroup generated by these commutators.
Lean carries out this passage in
`Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.commutator_zeroLocus_vanishingIdeal_le`,
using `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.evalAlgHom_map_liftQuotient_eq_zero`.

Together with $H(R)\subseteq G(R)$, this also gives normality:
for $g\in G(R)$ and $h\in H(R)$,
$ghg^{-1}=(ghg^{-1}h^{-1})h\in H(R)$.
The downstream Lean arguments use inclusion and commutator containment
directly, without a separate normality declaration.
