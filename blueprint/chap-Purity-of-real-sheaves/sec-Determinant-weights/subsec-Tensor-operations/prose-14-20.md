The central element acts on each simple factor of a [tensor construction](@definition-tensor-construction)
by a scalar, and from these scalars we read off [determinant weights](@definition-determinant-weight). Since [Weil representations](@definition-weil-representation) are the [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) Weil
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaves, Definition [definition-determinant-weight] applies
to them.
