The central element acts on each simple factor of a tensor construction
by a scalar, and from these scalars we read off determinant weights. Since Weil representations are the lisse Weil
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaves, Definition [definition-determinant-weight] applies
to them.
