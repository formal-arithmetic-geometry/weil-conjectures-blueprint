The lemma reduces determinant weights of tensor constructions to a
computation with the scalars on the composition factors of $V$. By
Proposition [proposition-central-element-arithmetic-monodromy-group], the element $z$ acts
by a scalar on every simple subquotient of a tensor construction on $V$, so the formula
of the lemma applies to each of them. Since the scalars by which a single
element acts multiply under tensor products and exterior powers, this gives
