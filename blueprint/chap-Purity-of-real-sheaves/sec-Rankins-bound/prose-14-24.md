For the constant sheaf on $\mathbf G_m$ the lemma gives
$H^2_c=\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(-1)$, of weight $2$, as in the computation at the start
of the section. Now take a Kummer sheaf of a nontrivial character on
$\mathbf G_m$. Then $H^0_c=0$ because $\mathbf G_m$ is affine, and
$H^2_c=0$ because the coinvariants vanish.

The positivity we want is $a^{2m}\ge0$ for the traces $a$ of all powers
$\operatorname{Frob}_x^n$ on $\mathcal G$, and for this all these traces must be
real. It is not enough to ask that the trace of
$\operatorname{Frob}_x$ itself be real. For example, if the eigenvalues at $x$ are $i$
and $1-i$, the trace is $1$, but the trace of the square is
$-1+(1-i)^2=-1-2i$. By the Newton identities, on the other hand, all the power sums are real
exactly when the characteristic polynomial is real, and this is the condition
below. Its basic examples are the constant sheaf, its Tate twists and Kummer
sheaves of quadratic characters. More generally, in Chapter [chap-Pure-sheaves-on-curves],
Proposition [proposition-twisting-weight-zero-adding-dual]
makes every pure sheaf a summand of a real one after a twist. The
condition enters the proof of Theorem [theorem-purity-real-sheaves-curves]
through Lemma [lemma-positivity-euler-factors].
