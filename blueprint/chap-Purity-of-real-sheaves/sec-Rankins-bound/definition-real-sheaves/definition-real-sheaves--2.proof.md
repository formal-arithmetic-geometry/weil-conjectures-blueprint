At a closed point let $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_r$ be the eigenvalues after applying $\iota$, counted with multiplicity. Their multiset is stable under complex conjugation. For every positive integer $m$, conjugation therefore fixes $\sum_j\alpha_j^m$. This sum is the trace of the $m$th power of local Frobenius, so that trace is real.
