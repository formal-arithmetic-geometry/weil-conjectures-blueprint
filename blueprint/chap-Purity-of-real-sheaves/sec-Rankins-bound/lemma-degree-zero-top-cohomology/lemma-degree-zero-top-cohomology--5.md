Let $X_0/\Fq$ be of finite type and let $\mathcal G$ be a
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-Weil sheaf, where
$\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$. If $\beta$ is an eigenvalue of the
geometric Frobenius on $\Gamma(X,\mathcal G)$, there is a closed point
$x\in X_0$ such that $\beta^{\deg(x)}$ is an eigenvalue of
$\operatorname{Frob}_x$ on $\mathcal G_{\bar x}$.
