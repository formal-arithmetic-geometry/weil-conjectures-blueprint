Let $X_0/\Fq$ be of finite type, let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$,
and fix $\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
If $\mathcal F$ is a lisse $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-Weil sheaf
punctually of $\iota$-weights $\le a$, then

$$
Q(\mathcal F)=\Gamma(X,\mathcal F^\vee)^\vee
$$

has $\iota$-weights at most $a$ for its dual Frobenius.
