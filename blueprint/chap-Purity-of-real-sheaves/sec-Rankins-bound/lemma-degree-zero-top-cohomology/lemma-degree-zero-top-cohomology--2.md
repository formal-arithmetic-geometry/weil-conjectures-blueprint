Let $U_0/\Fq$ be a smooth geometrically connected affine curve, let
$\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$, and let $\mathcal F$ be a lisse
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-Weil sheaf. Put
$Q(\mathcal F)=\Gamma(U,\mathcal F^\vee)^\vee$, with its dual Frobenius.
There is a Frobenius-equivariant isomorphism

$$
H_c^2(U,\mathcal F)\simeq Q(\mathcal F)(-1).
$$

The twist $(-1)$ multiplies the Frobenius by $q$.
