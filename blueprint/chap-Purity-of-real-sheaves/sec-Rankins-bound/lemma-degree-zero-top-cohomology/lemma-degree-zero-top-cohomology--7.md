Let $X_0/\Fq$ be of finite type, let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$,
and fix $\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
If $\mathcal F$ is a lisse $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-Weil sheaf
punctually $\iota$-pure of weight $a$, then
$Q(\mathcal F)=\Gamma(X,\mathcal F^\vee)^\vee$ is $\iota$-pure of weight
$a$. If $X_0$ is moreover a smooth geometrically connected affine
curve, then $H_c^2(X,\mathcal F)$ is $\iota$-pure of weight $a+2$.
The purity assertions also allow the zero space.
