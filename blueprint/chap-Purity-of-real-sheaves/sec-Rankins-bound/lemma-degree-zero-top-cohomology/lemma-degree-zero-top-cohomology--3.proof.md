For a compactification $f=p\circ j$, compactly supported cohomology is
computed by $Rp_*j_!\mathcal F$. Extension by zero is exact, and the
right derived functor $Rp_*$ has no negative cohomology on a sheaf in
degree zero. This gives the negative-degree vanishing.

For $r>2$, Corollary [corollary-degreewise-duality-over-fields] gives a
perfect pairing of $H_c^r(X,\mathcal F)$ with
$H^{2-r}(X,\mathcal F^\vee(1))$. The latter vanishes because $2-r<0$.
Perfectness then forces $H_c^r(X,\mathcal F)=0$.
