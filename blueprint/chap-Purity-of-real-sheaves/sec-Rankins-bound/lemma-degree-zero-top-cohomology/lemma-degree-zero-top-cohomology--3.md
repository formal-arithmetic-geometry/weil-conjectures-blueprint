Let $X_0/\Fq$ be smooth, separated and of finite type, of pure dimension
one, and let $\mathcal F$ be a lisse $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-Weil
sheaf, where $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\Fq$. Then

$$
H_c^r(X,\mathcal F)=0\qquad(r<0\text{ or }r>2).
$$

The vanishing for $r<0$ holds for every Weil sheaf on a separated
scheme of finite type over $\Fq$, without smoothness or lissity.
