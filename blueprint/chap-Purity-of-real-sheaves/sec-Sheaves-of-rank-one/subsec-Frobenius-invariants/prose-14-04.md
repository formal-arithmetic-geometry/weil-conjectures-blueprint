Once we have a smooth projective model $C_0$, the Jacobian $J_0$ of
$C_0$ exists (Appendix [chap-The-Picard-scheme-of-a-curve]), and we can compare the cohomology of $C$ with its Tate module.
We first make the comparison with finite coefficients, where Kummer
theory applies directly. The proposition ends with a matrix description
of Frobenius, which is the form used in
Proposition [proposition-no-fixed-class-degree-one].
