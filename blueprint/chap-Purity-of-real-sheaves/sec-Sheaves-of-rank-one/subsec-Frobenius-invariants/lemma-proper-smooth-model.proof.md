We compactify by Nagata's theorem and then normalize. On a curve,
normality is the same as regularity, and over a perfect field
regularity is smoothness, so the normalization is the model we want.

By Theorem [theorem-nagata-s-compactification-theorem], $U_0$ is an open subscheme of a
proper $k_0$-scheme. We take the reduced closure of $U_0$ in it and
normalize this closure in $k_0(U_0)$.  Since a scheme of finite type
over a field is Nagata, the normalization is finite
[Stacks, [035B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/035B), [035S](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/035S)], and it is an isomorphism over the normal
open $U_0$.  The resulting proper normal curve is regular, because a
one-dimensional noetherian normal local domain is a discrete valuation
ring. Hence it is smooth over the perfect field $k_0$
[Stacks, [0B8Y](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B8Y)].  Its boundary is zero-dimensional and proper over $k_0$,
hence finite. Since $k_0$ is perfect, the reduced boundary is étale.  After base change to $k$, the curve $U$ is dense and
connected, and therefore its closure $C$ is connected.  Finally, a
proper scheme of dimension one over a field is projective
[Stacks, [0A26](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A26)].
