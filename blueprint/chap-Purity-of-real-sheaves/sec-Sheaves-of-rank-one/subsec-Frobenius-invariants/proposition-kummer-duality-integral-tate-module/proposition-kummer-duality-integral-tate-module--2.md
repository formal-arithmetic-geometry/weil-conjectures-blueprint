$$
\begin{aligned}
\varprojlim_n H^1(C,A_n)
\simeq\Hom_{\mathbf Z_\ell}(T_\ell J,\mathbf Z_\ell).
\end{aligned}
$$

Here the transition maps are multiplication by $\ell$ on
$J[\ell^n](k)$ and reduction of coefficients on $H^1(C,A_n)$.
