Let $k_0$ be a perfect field and let $U_0$ be a separated scheme of
finite type over $k_0$, smooth of relative dimension one and
geometrically connected. There is a compactification

$$
U_0\xrightarrow{j_0}C_0\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}k_0
$$

in the sense of [definition-compactification-refinement], with $C_0$
integral, smooth of relative dimension one and geometrically connected
over $k_0$. The open immersion $j_0$ has dense image, and
$C_0\setminus j_0(U_0)$ is a finite set of closed points.
