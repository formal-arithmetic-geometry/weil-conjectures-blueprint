For the elliptic curve $y^2=x^3-x$ over $\mathbf F_3$ of the introduction,
arithmetic Frobenius $B$ has eigenvalues $\pm i\sqrt3$ on $T_\ell J$. By the
proposition, geometric Frobenius acts on $\varprojlim_nH^1(C,A_n)$ with the
same eigenvalues. Since $\det(B^t-1)=4\ne0$, it has no fixed vector. The
next proposition proves the same for every smooth curve, including open ones.
