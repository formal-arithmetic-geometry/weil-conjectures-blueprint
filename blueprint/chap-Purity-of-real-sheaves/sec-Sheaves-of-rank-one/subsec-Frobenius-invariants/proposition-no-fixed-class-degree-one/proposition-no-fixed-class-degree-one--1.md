For a smooth geometrically connected curve $U_0/k_0$, geometric
Frobenius has no fixed vector on $H^1(U,E)$ for every finite
$E/\mathbf Q_\ell$.  More precisely,

$$
\left(\varprojlim_nH^1(U,\mathbf Z/\ell^n)\right)^{F=1}=0.
$$
