Over $\mathbf C$ the link between $H^1$ of a curve and its Jacobian is
classical. There the Jacobian is a torus $V/\Lambda$ with $\Lambda=H_1(C,\mathbf Z)$, and
$H^1(C,\mathbf Z)=\Hom(\Lambda,\mathbf Z)$.
Proposition [proposition-kummer-duality-integral-tate-module] is the $\ell$-adic version, with
$T_\ell J$ in place of $\Lambda\otimes\mathbf Z_\ell$. Here the link with the Jacobian is
Kummer theory, which is a statement about $\mu_{\ell^n}$ and line bundles.
For this reason we work with the coefficients $\mathbf Z/\ell^n$ and pass to
$E$ only at the end. Apart from this, the only arithmetic input is the finiteness of
$J_0(k_0)$, which holds because $J_0$ is of finite type over a finite
field.
