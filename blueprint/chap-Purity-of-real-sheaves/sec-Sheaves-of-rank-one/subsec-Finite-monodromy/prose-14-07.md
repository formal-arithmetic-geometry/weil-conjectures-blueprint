The invariance hypothesis of the lemma is automatic for characters of
the Weil group. If $\chi:W(U_0)\to E^\times$ is a character, then
$\chi(s^{-1}gs)=\chi(s)^{-1}\chi(g)\chi(s)=\chi(g)$, because $E^\times$
is commutative. So the lemma applies as soon as $\chi$ has been made
additive on $\pi_1(U)$.
