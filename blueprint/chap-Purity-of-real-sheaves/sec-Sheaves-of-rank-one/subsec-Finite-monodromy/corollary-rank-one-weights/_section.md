Every rank-one [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) [Weil sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) on a smooth geometrically connected
curve is [punctually $\iota$-pure](@definition-weights).  For $c=\chi(s)$ its weight is
$\lambda_\iota(c)=2\log_q|\iota(c)|$.
