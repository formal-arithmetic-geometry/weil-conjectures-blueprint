The corollary applies to every irreducible constituent of $\mathcal V$
and of every tensor or exterior power of $\mathcal V$. Indeed, a
finite-dimensional representation has a composition series, and each
factor is again lisse and defined over a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$,
because after enlarging $E$ the invariant subspaces and quotients have
bases defined over $E$.
