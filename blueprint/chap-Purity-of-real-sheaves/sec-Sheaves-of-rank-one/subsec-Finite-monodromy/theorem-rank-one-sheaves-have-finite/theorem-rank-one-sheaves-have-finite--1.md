Let $U_0/k_0$ be a smooth geometrically connected curve over a finite
field, and let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k_0)$ be prime. Let $W(U_0)$ be
its Weil group, with geometric subgroup $\ker(\deg)=\pi_1(U)$ as in
[sec-Weil-sheaves-and-weights].
If $\chi:W(U_0)\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$ is a character whose
restriction to $\pi_1(U)$ is continuous, then there is an integer $N>0$
such that

$$
\chi(g)^N=1\qquad(g\in\pi_1(U)).
$$

In particular, $\chi(\pi_1(U))$ is finite.
