On a smooth geometrically connected curve over $\mathbf F_q$, every
irreducible constituent of an $\iota$-[real](@definition-real-sheaves) [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) [Weil sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) is
[punctually $\iota$-pure](@definition-weights) of weight equal to its [determinant weight](@definition-determinant-weight).
In particular, every $\iota$-[real](@definition-real-sheaves) [lisse](@definition-lisse-sheaves) [Weil sheaf](@definition-weil-structure) on the curve is [mixed](@definition-weights).
