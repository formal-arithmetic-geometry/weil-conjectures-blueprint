By clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--2], $Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E}K$ is integrally constructible.

Proper base change holds by
Lemma [lemma-completed-ordinary-base-change]. The lemma applies
because $g^*Rp_*K$ and $g'^*K$ are derived complete, as inverse image
preserves integral constructibility (Lemma [lemma-integral-models](3)).
