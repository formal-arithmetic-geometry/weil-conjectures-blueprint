Let $\ell$ be prime, let $k$ be a field of positive characteristic different from
$\ell$, and let $E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ be finite over $\Ql$.
Let $p:X\to Y$ be a proper $k$-morphism of separated schemes of finite type over
$k$. For $K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ and $M\in\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E)$, the
projection formula map (4.14)
$$
Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E}K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E}M\longrightarrow
Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E}(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_E}p^*M)
$$
is an isomorphism, with the condensed integral coefficient sheaves.
