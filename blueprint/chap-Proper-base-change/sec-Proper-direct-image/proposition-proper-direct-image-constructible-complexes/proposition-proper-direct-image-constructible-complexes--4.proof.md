The results
`AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.CondensedRing.finiteTypeDirectImage_single_boundedAbove`
and
`AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.CondensedRing.finiteTypeDirectImage_single_constructible`
give an upper bound and constructible cohomology for the direct image of each
constructible $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf in degree zero. They descend the
sheaf to a finite extension of $\Ql$ and use exact extension of scalars and its
compatibility with direct image [lemma-change-coefficients-direct-image].
Left t-exactness gives a lower bound. Finite truncation dévissage, using that
$\Dbc(Y,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ is triangulated
[corollary-coefficient-heart-b], gives the assertion for bounded constructible
complexes. The theorem used here allows characteristic zero as well.
