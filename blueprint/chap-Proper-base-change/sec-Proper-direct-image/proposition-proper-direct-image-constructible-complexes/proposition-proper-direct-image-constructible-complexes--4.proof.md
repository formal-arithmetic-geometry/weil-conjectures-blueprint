Use the generators of Lemma [lemma-integral-models](4). Finite coefficient-field extension is a finite direct sum after choosing a basis, by Lemma [lemma-inverse-image-coefficient-sheaves](2), so it commutes with $Rp_*$. Extension from a finite field $E$ to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ is the filtered colimit over its finite extensions. These systems have a common lower bound, and Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology] again permits passage through $Rp_*$. Clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--3] therefore proves the assertion on generators. Exactness and closure under finite cones and shifts prove it on all of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$.
