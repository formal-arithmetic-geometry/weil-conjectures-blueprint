Let $A$ be a finite field and let $p:X\to Y$ be a proper morphism of schemes.
For $K\in\Dbc(X,A)$ and $M\in\Dbc(Y,A)$, both of étale origin, the
projection formula map
$$
Rp_{*,A}K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_A M\longrightarrow
Rp_{*,A}(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_A p^*M)
$$
is an isomorphism. This is the finite-field statement used at the residue field
$A=\mathbf F_E$ in the integral argument.
