Let $\ell$ be prime and let $k$ be a field of positive characteristic different
from $\ell$. Let $p:X\to Y$ be a proper $k$-morphism of separated schemes of
finite type over $k$. For $K\in\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ and
$M\in\Dbc(Y,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$, the projection morphism of the
restricted adjunction $p^*\dashv Rp_*$,
$$
Rp_*K\otimes^{\mathbf L}M\longrightarrow Rp_*(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}p^*M),
$$
is an isomorphism in $\Dbc(Y,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$. Its image in the
ambient derived category is the projection formula map (4.14).
