Let $\ell$ be prime and let $k$ be a field of positive characteristic different
from $\ell$. For a cartesian square of separated schemes of finite type over
$k$, with $p:X\to Y$ proper, $g:Y'\to Y$, $g':X'\to X$ and $p':X'\to Y'$,
the base change map (4.5)
$$
g^*Rp_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}\longrightarrow
Rp'_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}g'^*
$$
is an isomorphism of functors
$\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)\to\Dbc(Y',\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$,
with the condensed coefficient ring $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
