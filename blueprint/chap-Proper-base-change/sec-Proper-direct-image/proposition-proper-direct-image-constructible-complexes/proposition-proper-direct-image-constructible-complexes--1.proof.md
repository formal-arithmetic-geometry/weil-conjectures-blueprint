Apply Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-finite-coefficients] to the proper morphism $p$ and $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$. That proposition gives bounded constructible cohomology and the finite Tor-amplitude bound for $Rp_{*,A}K$. These are precisely the conditions for membership in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$.
