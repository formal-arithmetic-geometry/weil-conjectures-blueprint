The classical torsion projection formula [Stacks, [0F0G](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0G)] is an isomorphism in $D(Y_{\acute{e}t},A)$. The cohomology comparison transports it to the pro-étale site. Both maps are the adjoint transpose of the counit for $p_A^*\dashv Rp_{*,A}$, so the transported map is precisely (4.14).
