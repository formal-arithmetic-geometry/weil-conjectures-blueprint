For a cartesian square with $g:Y'\to Y$, $g':X'\to X$ and
$p':X'\to Y'$, the base change map (4.5)
$g^*Rp_{*,A}\to Rp'_{*,A}g'^*$ is an isomorphism of functors
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)\to D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$.
