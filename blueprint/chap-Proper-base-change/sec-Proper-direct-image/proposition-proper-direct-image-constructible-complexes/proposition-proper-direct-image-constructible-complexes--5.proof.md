Theorem [theorem-proper-base-change-classical-pro], with $\Lambda=A$, says that the specified base change map is an isomorphism. The source and target lie in the indicated constructible categories by clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--1] and preservation by inverse image. Restricting the ambient comparison therefore gives the claimed isomorphism of functors.
