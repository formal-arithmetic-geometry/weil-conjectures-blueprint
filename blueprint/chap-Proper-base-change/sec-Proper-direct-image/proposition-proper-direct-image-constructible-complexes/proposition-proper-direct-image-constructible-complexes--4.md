Let $\ell$ be prime and let $k$ be a field of characteristic different from
$\ell$. For any $k$-morphism $p:X\to Y$ of separated schemes of finite type over $k$,
$$
Rp_{*,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}:\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)
\longrightarrow\Dbc(Y,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell).
$$
In particular this applies to the proper morphism of the proposition. The
coefficient sheaf comes from the condensed ring $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$,
whose sections are continuous maps with values in a finite extension of $\Ql$.
