Let $\ell$ be prime, let $k$ be a field of positive characteristic different from
$\ell$, and let $E\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ be finite over $\Ql$.
For a cartesian square of separated schemes of finite type over $k$, with
$p:X\to Y$ proper, $g:Y'\to Y$, $g':X'\to X$ and $p':X'\to Y'$, the
base change map (4.5)
$$
g^*Rp_{*,\mathcal O_E}\longrightarrow Rp'_{*,\mathcal O_E}g'^*
$$
is an isomorphism of functors $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)\to\Dbc(Y',\mathcal O_E)$,
with the condensed integral coefficient sheaves.
