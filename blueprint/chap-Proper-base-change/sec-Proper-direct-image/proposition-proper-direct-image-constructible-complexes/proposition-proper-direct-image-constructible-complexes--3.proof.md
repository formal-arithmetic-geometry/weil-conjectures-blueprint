By Lemma [lemma-integral-models](4), the category $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$ is generated under finite cones and shifts by scalar extensions of integral objects. For such an object $K_0$, write $K_0[1/\ell]$ as the colimit of multiplication by $\ell$ on $K_0$. The system has a common lower bound, so Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology] gives $Rp_*(K_0[1/\ell])\simeq(Rp_*K_0)[1/\ell]$. The right side is constructible by clause [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes--2]. Exactness of $Rp_{*,E}$ and closure of the target category under cones and shifts extend the conclusion to every $K$.
