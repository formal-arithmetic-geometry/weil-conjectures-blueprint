The function–sheaf dictionary
(Section [sec-The-function-sheaf-dictionary],
Proposition [proposition-function-sheaf-dictionary]) turns the identity
$\#X_0(\mathbf F_q)=\sum_y\#f^{-1}(y)(\mathbf F_q)$ into a statement about
sheaves, and for this the trace function of $Rf_!K$ at a point $y$ must be
the sum of $t_K$ over the fibre $f^{-1}(y)$. That requires the stalks of
$Rf_!K$ to be the cohomology of the fibres. Nothing in the definition of
the derived direct image says so.
