For the cartesian square (4.1) with $f$ proper, let $F$ be an étale
sheaf of abelian groups on $X$, and let
$\bar y':\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y'$ be a geometric point with
$\Omega$ algebraically closed. The map on stalks induced by the
underived base change map [definition-underived-base-change-map],

$$
(g^*f_*F)_{\bar y'}\longrightarrow(f'_*g'^*F)_{\bar y'},
$$

is bijective.
