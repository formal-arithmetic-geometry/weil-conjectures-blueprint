This is the proper henselian-pair theorem [Stacks, [0A0C](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A0C)], quoted in the reference. Its hypotheses are exactly that $(R,J)$ is henselian and $Z\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is proper, and its comparison is restriction to $Z_0$. It applies to arbitrary sheaves of sets on $Z_{\acute{e}t}$ and gives the asserted bijection. Restriction commutes with morphisms of sheaves, which gives naturality in $F$.
