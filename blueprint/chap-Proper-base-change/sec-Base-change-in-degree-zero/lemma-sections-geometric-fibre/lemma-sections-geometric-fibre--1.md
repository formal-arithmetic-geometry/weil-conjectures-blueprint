Let $f:X\to Y$ be proper, let $F$ be an étale sheaf of sets on $X$, and
let $\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$ be a geometric point. Then
restriction is a bijection

$$
(f_*F)_{\bar y}\xrightarrow{\sim}
\Gamma(X_{\bar y},F|_{X_{\bar y}}).
\tag{4.6}
$$
