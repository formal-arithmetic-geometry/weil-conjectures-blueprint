Compute the composite direct image on a common injective resolution: direct image preserves injectives, so $R(gf)_*\simeq Rg_*Rf_*$ there. Expand the two maps as units, square comparisons and counits. Naturality and the triangle identity cancel the inner unit–counit pair; composition of the square comparisons gives exactly the map for the vertical rectangle.
