In Definition [definition-pro-etale-base-change-map], the square
isomorphism is a natural isomorphism of inverse image functors. Taking
its mate under the two derived adjunctions gives a natural
transformation. Equivalently, the unit, square isomorphism and counit in
the displayed construction are natural, and so is their composite.
