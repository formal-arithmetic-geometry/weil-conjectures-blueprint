The site comparisons of Theorem [theorem-comparison-direct-images] identify inverse-image composition maps and, by Lemma [lemma-comparison-direct-images-compatibilities], their units and counits. The exchange map is constructed from exactly these data by adjunction. Thus the comparison carries the étale exchange map to the pro-étale one.
