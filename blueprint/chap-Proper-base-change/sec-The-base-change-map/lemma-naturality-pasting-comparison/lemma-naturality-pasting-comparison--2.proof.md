The exchange map is the adjoint transpose of a counit preceded by the composition isomorphism for inverse images. A composite adjunction has the composite of the two counits as counit. Successive transposition therefore identifies each pasted exchange map with the displayed composite. For an identity square, the triangle identity cancels the unit and counit, as in Lemma [lemma-identity-pasting].
