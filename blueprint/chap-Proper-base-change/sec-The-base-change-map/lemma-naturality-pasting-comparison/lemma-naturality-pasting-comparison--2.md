This general pro-étale identity and pasting assertion is bypassed by the
Lean route: the comparison in [lemma-naturality-pasting-comparison--3]
is proved directly for a single cartesian square.

The base change map is the identity for an identity square, and
for a horizontal or vertical pasting of two commutative squares the base
change map of the pasted square is the composite of the two base change
maps, in the form displayed in Lemma [lemma-identity-pasting].
