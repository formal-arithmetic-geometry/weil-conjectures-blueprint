The maps (4.5) form a natural transformation
$g^*Rf_*\to Rf'_*g'^*$. It is the identity for an identity square, and
for a horizontal or vertical pasting of two commutative squares the base
change map of the pasted square is the composite of the two base change
maps, in the form displayed in Lemma [lemma-identity-pasting]. For a
discrete ring $\Lambda$ and $K\in D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$,
with $f$ and $f'$ qcqs, the comparison isomorphisms of
Theorem [theorem-comparison-direct-images] identify the base change map of
$\nu_X^*K$ with the inverse image on $Y'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ of the étale
base change map of $K$.
