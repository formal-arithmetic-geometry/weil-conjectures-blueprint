An injective sheaf of modules is acyclic for the section functor on every
object of the site, although it need not be injective as an abelian sheaf
[Stacks, [03FD](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03FD)]. Hence an injective resolution of modules computes the
cohomology of the underlying abelian sheaf, on every object. Applying this
to the objects over the target gives the statement for $R^qu_*$, and
bounded below resolutions give it for $Ru_*$.
