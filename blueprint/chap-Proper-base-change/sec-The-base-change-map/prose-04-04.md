Consider a cartesian square

$$
\begin{CD}
X' @>{g'}>> X\\
@V{f'}VV @VV{f}V\\
Y' @>{g}>> Y.
\end{CD}
\tag{4.1}
$$

Since $gf'=fg'$, the composition isomorphisms of inverse images yield
an isomorphism $f'^*g^*\simeq g'^*f^*$ on sheaves. Inverse images are
exact, so the same isomorphism exists on bounded below derived
categories.
