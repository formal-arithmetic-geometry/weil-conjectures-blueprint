On the pro-étale site we use the same construction, but here $\Lambda$ may
be any coefficient ring pulled back from the point, not only a torsion
ring. For such $\Lambda$ inverse images are exact
(Lemma [lemma-inverse-image-coefficient-sheaves]), and the adjunction
$f^*\dashv Rf_*$ exists on the unbounded derived categories.
