Let $A$ be a finite admissible coefficient ring and $p:X\to Y$ a proper
morphism of separated schemes of finite type over the standing field $k$.
For $C\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$, proper base change identifies the geometric stalks of
$Rp_*C$ with the cohomology of the fibres. From this identification and the
classical finiteness theorem we obtain that $Rp_*C$ is constructible, with
a bound on its Tor amplitude. Both facts are needed for the passage to
integral and rational coefficients below, whereas direct sums in the unbounded
localizing categories are treated in
Section [sec-Compactness-for-finite-coefficients].
