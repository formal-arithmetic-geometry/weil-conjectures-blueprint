For separated schemes of finite type over the standing field $k$ and a
finite admissible coefficient ring $A$, proper base change identifies
the geometric stalks of $Rp_*C$ with the cohomology of its fibres.
From this identification and the classical finiteness theorem we
obtain constructibility and a Tor bound. Both are needed for the passage
to integral and rational coefficients below. Direct sums in the unbounded
localizing categories will be treated in
Section [sec-Compactness-for-finite-coefficients].
