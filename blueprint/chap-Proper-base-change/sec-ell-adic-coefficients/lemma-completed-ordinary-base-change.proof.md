Both maps are built from the counit of $p^*\dashv Rp_*$, as in
Definition [definition-pro-etale-base-change-map], and this counit is
natural with respect to the quotient maps and the chain maps
$(\pi_E,\mathrm{id})$ relating the reductions. Hence the square commutes.

Now suppose that $g^*Rp_*K$ and $g'^*K$ are derived complete. Then the
first completion map is an isomorphism, and the second is $Rp'_*$ applied
to the completion map of $g'^*K$, so it is an isomorphism as well. By the commutative square, the
base change map is then a composite of isomorphisms.
