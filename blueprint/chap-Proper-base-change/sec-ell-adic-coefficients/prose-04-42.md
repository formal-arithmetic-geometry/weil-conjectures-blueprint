We will show that $Rp_*K$ is again [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) when $K$ is [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves).
Once this is known, the [completed inverse images](@definition-completed-inverse-image) in
Proposition [proposition-proper-base-change-complete-integral] agree with $g^*$ and $g'^*$, and the
proposition becomes the ordinary base change
isomorphism

$$
g^*Rp_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rp'_*g'^*K
\qquad(K\in\href{@definition-constructible-sheaves}{\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)}).
$$
