We will show that $Rp_*K$ is again constructible when $K$ is constructible.
Once this is known, the completed inverse images in
Proposition [proposition-proper-base-change-complete-integral] agree with $g^*$ and $g'^*$, and the
proposition becomes the ordinary base change
isomorphism

$$
g^*Rp_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rp'_*g'^*K
\qquad(K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)).
$$
