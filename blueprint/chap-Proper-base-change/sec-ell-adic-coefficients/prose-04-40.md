The natural map $g^*M\to\href{@definition-completed-inverse-image}{\hat g^*M}$ is the completion map of $g^*M$, since
$g^*$ commutes with the reductions. For [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) $M$ the completion
does nothing. Indeed, let $Y$ and $Y'$ be of finite type over
$k$ and $M\in \href{@definition-constructible-sheaves}{D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\mathcal O_E)}$. Then $g^*M$ lies in
[$\Dbc(Y',\mathcal O_E)$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) by Lemma [lemma-integral-models](3), hence it is
[derived complete](@definition-derived-adic-completeness) by
Proposition [proposition-integral-constructibility-criterion]
((1)$\Rightarrow$(2)), and $\href{@definition-completed-inverse-image}{\hat g^*M}=g^*M$. However, until we know that proper direct
image preserves [constructibility](@definition-constructible-sheaves), we can only state base change with the
[completed inverse image](@definition-completed-inverse-image).
