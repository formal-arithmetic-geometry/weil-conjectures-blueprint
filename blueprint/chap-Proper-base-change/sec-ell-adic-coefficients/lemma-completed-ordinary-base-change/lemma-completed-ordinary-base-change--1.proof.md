Both maps are built from the counit of $p^*\dashv Rp_*$, as in
Definition [definition-pro-etale-base-change-map], and this counit is
natural with respect to the quotient maps and the chain maps
$(\pi_E,\mathrm{id})$ relating the reductions. Hence the square commutes.
