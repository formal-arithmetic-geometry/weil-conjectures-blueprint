In the setting of
Proposition [proposition-proper-base-change-complete-integral], the square
formed by the base change map $g^*Rp_*K\to Rp'_*g'^*K$ of
(4.5), the completed base change isomorphism
$\hat g^*Rp_*K\to Rp'_*\hat g'^*K$, and the completion maps
$g^*Rp_*K\to\hat g^*Rp_*K$ and $Rp'_*g'^*K\to Rp'_*\hat g'^*K$ commutes. In
particular, if $g^*Rp_*K$ and $g'^*K$ are derived complete, then the base
change map $g^*Rp_*K\to Rp'_*g'^*K$ is an isomorphism.
