For a finite ring $A$, bounded constructible
complexes on quasi-compact quasi-separated
schemes are classical
(Theorem [theorem-comparison-constructible-sheaves]). Hence
Theorem [theorem-proper-base-change-classical-pro],
with $\Lambda=A$, applies to every
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ for schemes of finite type over $k$. For $\mathcal O_E$, the
theorem gives proper base change at
each finite level $\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n$, and we must pass
to the limit. Later, with the reduction principle of Section [sec-Reduction-to-finite-coefficients],
we make this passage once for all statements of this kind.

The passage from finite coefficients to $\mathcal O_E$ is a question
of completeness. The inverse image
$g^*:D(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)\to D(Y'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal O_E)$,
which is exact on sheaves of modules, need not preserve derived
completeness. We therefore complete after pulling back.
