By clause [proposition-proper-base-change-complete-integral--1], $Rp_*K$ is derived complete and its level-$n$ reduction is $Rp_*K_n$, compatibly in $n$. Insert these reductions into the definition of completed inverse image. It gives $\hat g^*Rp_*K\simeq R\varprojlim_n g^*Rp_*K_n$ with the asserted comparison.
