With the notation and hypotheses of the proposition, and under the
identifications of [proposition-proper-base-change-complete-integral--2]
and [proposition-proper-base-change-complete-integral--3], the derived limit
of the base change maps
$g^*Rp_*K_n\to Rp'_*g'^*K_n$ is an isomorphism
$\hat g^*Rp_*K\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Rp'_*\hat g'^*K$.
