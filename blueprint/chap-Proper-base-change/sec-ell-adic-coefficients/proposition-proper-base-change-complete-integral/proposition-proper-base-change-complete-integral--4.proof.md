Each finite-level map is an isomorphism by Theorem [theorem-proper-base-change-classical-pro], since each $K_n$ has classical torsion cohomology. Naturality in $K_n$ makes these maps a morphism of towers. Its cone is acyclic at every level, so its derived inverse limit is an isomorphism. Under clauses [proposition-proper-base-change-complete-integral--2] and [proposition-proper-base-change-complete-integral--3], this is the stated completed base-change map.
