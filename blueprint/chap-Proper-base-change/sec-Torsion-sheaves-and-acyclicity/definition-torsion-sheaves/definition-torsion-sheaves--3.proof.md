Geometric stalks commute with filtered colimits and finite limits. Thus the stalk of $\varinjlim_nF[n]\to F$ is the inclusion of the union of the subgroups killed by positive integers into the stalk of $F$. For an abelian group this inclusion is an equality precisely when every element is torsion. Geometric stalks detect isomorphisms in the setting of this definition, so the sheaf equality is equivalent to the stated torsion condition.
