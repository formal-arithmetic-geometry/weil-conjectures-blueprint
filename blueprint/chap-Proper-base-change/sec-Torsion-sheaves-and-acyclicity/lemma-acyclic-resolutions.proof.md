This is Leray's acyclicity lemma [Stacks, [015E](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/015E)], and the first assertion
is the case of a resolution of $F$. We recall the argument, which compares
$M^\bullet$ with an injective replacement through the cone.

In a bounded below exact complex of $v$-acyclic
objects the cycles are again $v$-acyclic, by induction along the short
exact sequences of cycles, so the complex stays exact after applying
$v$. We apply this to the cone of a quasi-isomorphism
$M^\bullet\to J^\bullet$ into a bounded below complex of injectives.
This cone is bounded below and exact, and its terms are $v$-acyclic,
being finite direct sums of terms of $M^\bullet$ and of $J^\bullet$.
It follows that $vM^\bullet\to vJ^\bullet$ is a quasi-isomorphism, and
the target computes $Rv(M^\bullet)$.
