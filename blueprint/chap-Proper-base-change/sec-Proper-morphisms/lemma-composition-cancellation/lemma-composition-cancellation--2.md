Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative ring that is torsion as an abelian group,
and let $X\xrightarrow{u}Y\xrightarrow{v}Z$ be arbitrary morphisms of schemes.
If $u$ is surjective and $\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda(u)$ and
$\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda(vu)$ hold, then $\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda(v)$ holds.
