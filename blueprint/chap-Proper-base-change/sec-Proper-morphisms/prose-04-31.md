Stability under composition and descent along proper surjections are
best proved on the level of [injective acyclicity](@definition-injective-acyclicity), since by
Theorem [theorem-pbc-injective-acyclicity] it is equivalent to [proper
base change](@definition-torsion-pbc-property) and it lends itself to a spectral sequence argument.
