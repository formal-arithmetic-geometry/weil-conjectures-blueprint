If $\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda(\mathbf P^1_S\to S)$ holds for every scheme $S$,
then $\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda(\mathbf P^n_S\to S)$ holds for every scheme $S$
and every $n\geq0$.
