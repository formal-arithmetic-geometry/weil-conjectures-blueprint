For $n=1$ the map $\sigma_1$ is the identity of $\mathbf P^1_S$. For $n=2$
it sends $([x_1:y_1],[x_2:y_2])$ to
$[x_1x_2:x_1y_2+y_1x_2:y_1y_2]$, and over a field it is the quotient by
swapping the two factors, of degree $2$.
