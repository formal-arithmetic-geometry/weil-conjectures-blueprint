Finiteness is preserved by base change, so [lemma-finite-morphisms--2]
makes both $u_*$ and $u'_*$ exact and gives the underived base change
isomorphism. Exactness makes the units comparing the termwise direct
images with their right derived functors isomorphisms on bounded below
complexes. The defining factorization of the derived base change map
[definition-derived-base-change-map] therefore expresses it, on such a
complex, as a composite of the termwise base change isomorphism and these
comparison isomorphisms. Every object of $D^+$ is represented by a bounded
below complex, so the map is an isomorphism on all of $D^+$.
