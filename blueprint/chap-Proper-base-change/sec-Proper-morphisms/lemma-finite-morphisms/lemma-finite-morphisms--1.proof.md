By Lemma [lemma-finite-direct-images], every geometric stalk of $u_*$ is a finite product of stalks at the points of the geometric fibre. Under base change the geometric fibres are unchanged, and the base-change map is the identity on the factors of that stalk formula. It is therefore an isomorphism on every geometric stalk.

*Source:* the proof of Lemma [lemma-finite-morphisms].
