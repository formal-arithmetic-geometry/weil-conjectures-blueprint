Both direct-image functors are exact by Lemma [lemma-finite-direct-images], so their derived functors are computed term by term. The underived base-change isomorphism of clause [lemma-finite-morphisms--1] applies in each degree and descends to the bounded-below derived category. Its derived map is the exchange map defined by the same adjunction.
