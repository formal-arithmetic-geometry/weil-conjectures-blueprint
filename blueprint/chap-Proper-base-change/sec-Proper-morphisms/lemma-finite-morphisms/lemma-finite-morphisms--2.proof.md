By clause [lemma-finite-morphisms--1], every geometric stalk of $u_*$ is a finite product of stalk functors, hence exact. Geometric stalks detect exactness, so $u_*$ is exact. Under base change the geometric fibres are unchanged, and the base-change map is the identity on the factors of that same stalk formula. It is therefore an isomorphism on every geometric stalk.
