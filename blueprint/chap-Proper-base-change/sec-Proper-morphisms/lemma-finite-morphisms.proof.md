By Lemma [lemma-finite-direct-images], $u_*$ is exact, and its stalks are
given by the stalk formula there. In a cartesian square over $W'\to W$, a
geometric point of $W'$ and its image in $W$ have the same geometric fibre.
Therefore the underived base change map is the identity on the corresponding
factors of the stalk formula, hence an isomorphism. Since both
direct images in the square are exact, their derived functors and
the base change map are computed term by term. Finally, the same argument applies to
sheaves of modules.
