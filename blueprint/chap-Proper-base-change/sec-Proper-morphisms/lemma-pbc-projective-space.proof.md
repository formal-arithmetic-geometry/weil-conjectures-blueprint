The case $n=0$ is the identity. For $n\geq1$, the projection
$(\mathbf P^1_S)^n\to S$ is a composite of $n$ projections of projective
lines over successive bases. Hence it satisfies $\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda$ by the
hypothesis and Lemma [lemma-composition-cancellation](i). The finite
surjection $\sigma_n$ of Lemma [lemma-finite-cover-projective-space]
is a morphism over $S$, and it satisfies $\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda$ by
Lemma [lemma-finite-morphisms]. Cancelling $\sigma_n$ by
Lemma [lemma-composition-cancellation](ii), we obtain
$\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda$ for $\mathbf P^n_S\to S$.
