Let $k'$ be a field, let $\Lambda$ be a ring that is torsion as an abelian
group, and let $g:X\to Y$ be a morphism of $k'$-schemes of finite type. Then
there is an integer $d$ such that $R^qg_*F=0$ for all $q>d$ and all
sheaves $F$ of $\Lambda$-modules on $X_{\acute{e}t}$ [Stacks, [0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10)].
