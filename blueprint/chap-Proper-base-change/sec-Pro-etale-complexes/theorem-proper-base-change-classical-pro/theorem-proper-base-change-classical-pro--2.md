Suppose moreover that $\Lambda$ is torsion as an abelian group. Then on the
étale sites the base change map

$$
g^*Rf_*K\longrightarrow Rf'_*g'^*K
$$

is an isomorphism for every $K\in D(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, without any
boundedness hypothesis [Stacks, [0F0C](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0C)].
