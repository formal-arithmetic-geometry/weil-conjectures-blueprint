Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring, let $f:X\to Y$ be a proper
morphism of schemes, and let $\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}(\Omega)\to Y$
be a geometric point, with $\Omega$ separably closed. Set
$X_{\bar y}=X\times_Y\operatorname{Spec}(\Omega)$, with projection
$i:X_{\bar y}\to X$. For every étale sheaf $F$ of $\Lambda$-modules on
$X$ and every $q\geq0$, there exists an isomorphism of abelian groups

$$
(R^qf_*F)_{\bar y}\simeq
H^q_{\acute{e}t}(X_{\bar y},i^*F).
$$

Here the right side is the cohomology of the underlying abelian sheaf
of $i^*F$.
