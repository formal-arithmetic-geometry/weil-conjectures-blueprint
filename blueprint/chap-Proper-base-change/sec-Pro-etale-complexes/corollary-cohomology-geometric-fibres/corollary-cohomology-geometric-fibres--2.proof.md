Take $K=F$ in clause [corollary-cohomology-geometric-fibres--1] and apply $H^q$. Geometric stalks are exact, so the left side is $(R^qf_*F)_{\bar y}$; the right side is, by definition, the degree-$q$ pro-étale cohomology of the fibre.
