Let $\pi:\mathbf P^1_S\to S$ be the projection. A geometric stalk of
$R^q\pi_*F$ is the cohomology of $\mathbf P^1$ over a strictly henselian
local ring. Proper base change for $\pi$ says that it is the cohomology
of the geometric fibre, and that this group does not change under base
change.
