For example, let $R$ be strictly henselian and $n$ invertible in $R$. The
residue field $\kappa$ is separably closed, and passing to its algebraic
closure does not change the étale topos [Stacks, [03SI](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03SI)]. Hence
$H^2(\mathbf P^1_\kappa,\mu_n)\simeq\mathbf Z/n$, generated by the Kummer
class of $\mathcal O(1)$
(Proposition [proposition-smooth-proper-curves-torsion-prime], in
Appendix [chap-Cohomology-of-torsion-sheaves-on-curves]). The proposition
says that $H^2(\mathbf P^1_R,\mu_n)$ is also $\mathbf Z/n$, generated by the
class of $\mathcal O(1)$.
