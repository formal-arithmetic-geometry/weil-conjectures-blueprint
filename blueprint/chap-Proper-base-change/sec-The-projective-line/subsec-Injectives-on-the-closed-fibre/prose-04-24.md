The sheaves on the right of (4.9)
are direct images along finite morphisms. By
Remark [remark-finiteness-small-etale-topoi](3), such direct
images have no higher cohomology and commute with restriction to the
closed fibre. Both assertions follow from the decomposition of a finite
scheme over a strict localization into strictly henselian local schemes
[Stacks, [03QP](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QP), [0959](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0959)]. They are part of
Lemma [lemma-finite-morphisms] below.
