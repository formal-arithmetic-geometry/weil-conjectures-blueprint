For the same $h,G$, let $h_0:Y_0=Y\times_{\mathbf P^1_R}\mathbf P^1_\kappa\to\mathbf P^1_\kappa$ and $i_Y:Y_0\to Y$ be the base changes of $h,i$. The underived map (4.2) is an isomorphism $i^*h_*G\xrightarrow{\sim}(h_0)_*i_Y^*G$.
