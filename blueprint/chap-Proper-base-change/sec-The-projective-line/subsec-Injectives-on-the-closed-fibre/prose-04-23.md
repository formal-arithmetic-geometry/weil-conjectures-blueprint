Let $R$ be a henselian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ and
residue field $\kappa$, and let $i:\mathbf P^1_\kappa\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^1_R$ be the
inclusion of the closed fibre. For a sheaf $G$ on $\mathbf P^1_{R,\acute{e}t}$ write
$G_0=i^*G$. To compare the cohomology of $\mathbf P^1_R$ with that of its
closed fibre, we apply the comparison in degree zero of
Theorem [theorem-comparison-over-henselian-pair-degree] to an
injective resolution. This works once we know that the restriction $I_0$
of an injective sheaf $I$ is acyclic on $\mathbf P^1_\kappa$
(Lemma [lemma-acyclicity-closed-fibre]). An injective sheaf is too
large to work with directly, so we approximate it by constructible sheaves
and embed these into direct images along finite morphisms, using three
standard finiteness facts about small étale topoi of quasi-compact
quasi-separated schemes.
