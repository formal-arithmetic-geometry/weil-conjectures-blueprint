Let $R$ be a henselian local ring with residue field $\kappa$, let $\ell$
be a prime, and let $i:\mathbf P^1_\kappa\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^1_R$
be the closed fibre. For every sheaf $F$ of $\mathbf F_\ell$-modules on
$\mathbf P^1_{R,\acute{e}t}$, restriction induces isomorphisms, with
$F_0=i^*F$,

$$
H^q(\mathbf P^1_R,F)\xrightarrow{\sim}
H^q(\mathbf P^1_\kappa,F_0)\qquad(q\geq0).
$$

Here cohomology is taken on the underlying abelian sheaves.
