Let $R$ be a henselian local ring with residue field $\kappa$, let
$i:\mathbf P^1_\kappa\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^1_R$ be the closed fibre, and
let $\ell$ be a prime. If $I$ is an injective sheaf of $\mathbf F_\ell$-modules
on $\mathbf P^1_{R,\acute{e}t}$ and $I_0=i^*I$, then

$$
H^q(\mathbf P^1_\kappa,I_0)=0\qquad(q>0).
$$
