For $\mathrm{IA}_\Lambda(\pi)$, let $I$ be a torsion injective sheaf of
$\Lambda$-modules. Then $R^q\pi_*I=0$ for $q>0$, and the case of sheaves gives
$R^q\pi'_*g'^*I\simeq g^*R^q\pi_*I=0$.
