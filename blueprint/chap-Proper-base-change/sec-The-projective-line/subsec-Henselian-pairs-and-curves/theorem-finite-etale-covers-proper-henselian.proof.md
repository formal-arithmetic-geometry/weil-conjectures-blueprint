The equivalence is [Stacks, [0GS2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0GS2)], proved by algebraization, approximation,
and descent of finite presentation. For the consequence, note that the groups in
(4.8) classify $M$-torsors, which are finite
étale schemes with an action of $M$ satisfying the equations of a simply
transitive action. The equivalence transports these schemes, their actions
and the equations, so it induces a bijection on isomorphism classes of
$M$-torsors.
