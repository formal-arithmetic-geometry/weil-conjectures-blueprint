Let $k$ be algebraically closed, let $C$ be a separated scheme of finite
type over $k$ with $\dim C\leq1$, and let $F$ be a torsion abelian sheaf
on $C_{\acute{e}t}$. Then
$$
H^q(C,F)=0\qquad(q>2).
$$
