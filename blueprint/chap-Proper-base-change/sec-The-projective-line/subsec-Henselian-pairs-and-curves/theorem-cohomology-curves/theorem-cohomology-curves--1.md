Let $k$ be algebraically closed, let $C$ be a separated scheme of finite
type over $k$ with $\dim C\leq1$, and let $F$ be a torsion abelian sheaf
on $C_{\acute{e}t}$. Then

$$
H^q(C,F)=0\qquad(q>2).
$$

By Theorem [theorem-bounded-below-cohomology-comparison], the same vanishing holds for the classical sheaf
$\nu_C^*F$ with cohomology taken on $C_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$; every classical
torsion abelian sheaf on $C_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ is of this form.
