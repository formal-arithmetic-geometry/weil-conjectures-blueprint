The model case is a complete discrete valuation ring $R$. Think of
$\operatorname{Spec}R$ as a small disc around its closed point, and of
$\mathbf P^1_R$ as a family of projective lines over the disc. In
topology such a family retracts onto the central fibre, and we expect
that, for torsion sheaves, the cohomology of $\mathbf P^1_R$ is that of
its closed fibre. Besides the comparison in degree zero of
Theorem [theorem-comparison-over-henselian-pair-degree], the proof
uses two theorems about henselian pairs from the Stacks Project. The first
is the affine analogue of what we want to prove.
