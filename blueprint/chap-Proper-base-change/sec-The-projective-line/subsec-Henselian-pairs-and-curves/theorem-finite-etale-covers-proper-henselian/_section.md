Let $(B,J)$ be a henselian pair, let $Y$ be proper over $B$, and put
$Y_0=Y\times_{\operatorname{Spec}B}\operatorname{Spec}(B/J)$. Then
restriction is an equivalence between the categories of finite étale
schemes over $Y$ and over $Y_0$,

$$
\textit{F\'Et}_Y\xrightarrow{\sim}\textit{F\'Et}_{Y_0}.
$$

Consequently, for a finite abelian group $M$,

$$
H^1(Y,\underline M)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^1(Y_0,\underline M).
\tag{4.8}
$$
