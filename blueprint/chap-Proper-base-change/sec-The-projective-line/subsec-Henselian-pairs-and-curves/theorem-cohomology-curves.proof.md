The proof is in Appendix [chap-Cohomology-of-torsion-sheaves-on-curves]. For a smooth proper connected curve and $n$
invertible in $k$, one computes $H^q(C,\mu_n)$ with the Kummer
sequence and the Jacobian (Appendix [chap-The-Picard-scheme-of-a-curve]), while for $p$-primary
coefficients one uses the Artin–Schreier sequence instead. The case of
general torsion sheaves is then reduced to these cases by normalization, removal of
finitely many points and a finite étale cover of degree prime to the
torsion order.
