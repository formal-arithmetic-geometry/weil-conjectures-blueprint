Fix an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$. Let $n\geq1$, and let the square in
Definition [def-direct-image-exchange] be an arbitrary Cartesian
square of finite-type schemes on which $\ell$ is invertible. If $f$ is
proper, then for every
$K\in D^b_c(X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n)$,

$$
\operatorname{Ex}_*(K):Lg^*Rf_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf'_*Lg'^*K.
$$

The exchange isomorphism is compatible with horizontal and vertical
composition of Cartesian squares and with pullback to every geometric
point.
