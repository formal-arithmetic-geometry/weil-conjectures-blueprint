Fix an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$ and an open immersion
$j:U\hookrightarrow\overline X$. The finite functors $j_{!,n}$
commute with reduction of coefficients and carry strict reduction towers
to strict reduction towers. For every $n\geq1$ and every
derived-complete integral $K\in D^b_c(U,\mathcal O_E)$, the resulting
comparison is an isomorphism

$$
\operatorname{red}_{\overline X,n}(j_!K)
\xrightarrow{\sim}j_{!,n}\operatorname{red}_{U,n}(K)
$$

.
These comparisons are transitive in
$n$, compatible with finite scalar extension, and induce the rational and
common-stage $\Qlb$ constructions. At every coefficient level,
extension by zero restricts from the ambient category to bounded
constructible complexes.
