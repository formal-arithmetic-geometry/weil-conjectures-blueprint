Fix an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$. Let $n\geq1$ and let
$f:X\to Y$ be proper. Then

$$
Rf_{*,n}:D^b_c(X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n)
\longrightarrow D^b_c(Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n).
$$

For every $K\in D^b_c(X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n)$ and
$j,r\in\mathbf Z$, each $R^jf_*\mathcal H^r(K)$ is constructible. If
$e\geq0$, $u\leq v$ are integers, the geometric fibers of $f$ have
dimension at most $e$, and
$K\in D_c^{[u,v]}(X,\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n)$, then
$Rf_{*,n}K$ has cohomology only in $[u,v+2e]$.
