In the coefficient situation and Cartesian square of
Definition [def-open-extension-base-change-mate], define

$$
\operatorname{Ex}_{j,g}(K):=\beta_{j,g}(K)^{-1}:
Lg^*j_!K\xrightarrow{\sim}j'_!Lg'^*K.
$$

Thus the exchange is the inverse of the preceding adjunction mate, not an
independent stalkwise notation.
