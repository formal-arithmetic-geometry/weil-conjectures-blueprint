Fix an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$, and let
$\Lambda\in\{\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n,\mathcal O_E,E,\Qlb\}$, with
$n\geq1$ in the finite case. If $f:X\to Y$ is smooth of pure relative
dimension $r$ and is
exceptional-adjoint admissible, then for
$L\in D^b_{ctf}(Y,\Lambda)$ there is a natural isomorphism

$$
\operatorname{pur}_f(L):Rf^!L\xrightarrow{\sim}Lf^*L(r)[2r].
$$

Finite reduction carries $\operatorname{pur}_f(L)$ to the finite-level
purity component; scalar extension and common-stage descent preserve the
same orientation and Tate-twist normalization.
