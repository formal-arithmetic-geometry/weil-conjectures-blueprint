Fix an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$ and a separated finite-type
morphism $f:X\to Y$. For
$\Lambda\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\Qlb\}$, the preceding construction is
an exact triangulated functor

$$
Rf_!:D^b_c(X,\Lambda)\longrightarrow D^b_c(Y,\Lambda).
$$

For every $K\in D^b_c(X,\mathcal O_E)$, every $n\geq1$, and
$K_n:=\operatorname{red}_{X,n}(K)$, there are natural isomorphisms

$$
\operatorname{red}_{Y,n}(Rf_!K)
\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf_{!,n}K_n.
$$

These isomorphisms, the compactification comparisons, and composition are
compatible with reduction, rationalization, finite scalar extension, and
the $\Qlb$-valued common-stage construction.
