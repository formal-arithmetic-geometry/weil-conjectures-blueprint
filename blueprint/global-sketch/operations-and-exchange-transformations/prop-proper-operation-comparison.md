Fix an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$. If $f:X\to Y$ is proper, the compactification
$X\xrightarrow{\operatorname{id}}X\xrightarrow{f}Y$ gives a natural
isomorphism

$$
Rf_!\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf_*
$$

at every coefficient level. Its reduction and scalar-extension squares
commute.
