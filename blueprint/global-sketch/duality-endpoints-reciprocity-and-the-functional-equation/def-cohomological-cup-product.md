For a smooth-proper datum $\mathscr X=(X_0,d,X)$ and a prime
$\ell\neq p$ and every pair of integers $i,j$, the
derived tensor product and multiplication
$(\Ql)_X\otimes^L(\Ql)_X\to(\Ql)_X$ define cup products

$$
\smile:H^i(X,\Ql)\otimes_{\Ql}H^j(X,\Ql)
\longrightarrow H^{i+j}(X,\Ql).
$$
