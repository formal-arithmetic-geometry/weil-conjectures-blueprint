Let $\mathscr X=(X_0,d,X)$ be a smooth-proper datum and let
$\ell\neq p$ be prime. Then there are Frobenius-equivariant isomorphisms

$$
H^0(X,\Ql)\cong\Ql,
\qquad
H^{2d}(X,\Ql)\cong\Ql(-d).
$$

Geometric Frobenius acts by $1$ on the first space and by $q^d$ on the
second. Consequently

$$
P_0(T)=1-T,
\qquad
P_{2d}(T)=1-q^dT,
\qquad
b_0=b_{2d}=1.
$$
