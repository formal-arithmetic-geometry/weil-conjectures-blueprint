Let $\mathscr X=(X_0,d,X)$ be a smooth-proper datum. For every prime
$\ell\neq p$, every algebraic closure $\Qlb/\Ql$, every abstract field
isomorphism $\iota:\Qlb\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbf C$, and every
$0\leq i\leq2d$, each eigenvalue $\alpha\in\Qlb$, counted with
algebraic multiplicity, of

$$
F_{i,\ell}\otimes_{\Ql}1
\quad\text{on}\quad V_{i,\ell}\otimes_{\Ql}\Qlb
$$

satisfies

$$
|\iota(\alpha)|=q^{i/2}.
$$

Equivalently, the same assertion holds for every reciprocal root of
$P_{i,\ell}$, not for its ordinary roots.
