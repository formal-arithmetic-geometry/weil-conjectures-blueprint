Fix the prime power $q=p^a$ of Definition
[def-weil-base-field] and let $\overline{\mathbf Q}$ be an algebraic
closure of $\mathbf Q$. For $w\in\mathbf Z$, a nonzero
$\alpha\in\overline{\mathbf Q}$ is a $q$-Weil number of weight
$w$ if

$$
|\tau(\alpha)|=q^{w/2}
$$

for every field embedding
$\tau:\mathbf Q(\alpha)\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
