For a smooth-proper datum $\mathscr X=(X_0,d,X)$, every prime
$\ell\neq p$, and every $0\leq i\leq2d$, use the cohomological family
of Definition [def-smooth-proper-cohomological-family] and define

$$
P_{i,\ell}(T)=
\det(1-TF_{i,\ell}\mid V_{i,\ell})\in\Ql[T].
$$

Every displayed symbol is a function of $\mathscr X$, even when that
argument is suppressed.
