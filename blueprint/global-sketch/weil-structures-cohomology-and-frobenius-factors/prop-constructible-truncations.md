Fix a scheme $Y$ and an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$. For every inclusion
$E'\subset L$ in $\mathcal E_\ell$, scalar extension
$-\otimes_{E'}L$ is $t$-exact. Hence the candidates of
Definition [def-constructible-truncation-candidates] are independent of
the common-stage representative, commute with scalar extension and
idempotent summands, and determine functorial candidates on
$D^b_c(Y,\Qlb)$.
