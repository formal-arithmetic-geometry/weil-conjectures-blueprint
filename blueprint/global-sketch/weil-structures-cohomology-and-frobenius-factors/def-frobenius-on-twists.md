Fix an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$ of
Definition [def-weil-finite-coefficient-datum].
The coefficient twists of Definition [def-coefficient-twists] on
$\Spec k_0$, pulled back to a geometric fiber, are derived Weil objects in
degree zero. For every $m\in\mathbf Z$, their specified isomorphisms make
geometric Frobenius act on $E(m)$ by $q^{-m}$. This formula commutes
with finite reduction and extension to $\Qlb$.
