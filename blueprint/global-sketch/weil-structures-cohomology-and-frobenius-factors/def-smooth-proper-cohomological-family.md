For a smooth-proper datum $\mathscr X=(X_0,d,X)$, every prime
$\ell\neq p$, and
$0\leq i\leq2d$, define

$$
V_{i,\ell}=H^i(X,\Ql),\qquad
F_{i,\ell}=F\vert_{V_{i,\ell}}.
$$

Proposition [prop-cohomological-finiteness] supplies the
finite-dimensionality, invertibility, and vanishing outside
$0\leq i\leq2d$; those conclusions are not part of this definition.
