For a scheme $Y$, fix an auxiliary finite coefficient datum
$(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)$ of
Definition [def-weil-finite-coefficient-datum].
For integers $a\leq b$ and $n\geq1$, let

$$
D_c^{[a,b]}\bigl(Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n\bigr)=
\left\{K\in D^b_c\bigl(Y,\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n\bigr):
\mathcal H^r(K)=0\text{ for }r\notin[a,b]\right\}.
$$
