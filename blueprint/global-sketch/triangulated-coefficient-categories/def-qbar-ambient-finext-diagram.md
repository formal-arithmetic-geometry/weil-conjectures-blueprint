For a scheme $Y$, fix a prime $\ell\neq p$, an algebraic closure
$\Qlb/\Ql$, and the index category $\mathcal E_\ell$ of
Definition [def-qbar-finext-diagram]. For every
$E\in\mathcal E_\ell$, put

$$
\mathcal A_E(Y)=D(Y_{\mathrm{proet}},E_Y),
$$

and for $E\subset L$ let
$T^{\mathrm{amb}}_{L/E}=-\otimes^L_{E_Y}L_Y$. Derived-tensor
associativity supplies the unit, tower, and shift isomorphisms and their
coherence equations.
