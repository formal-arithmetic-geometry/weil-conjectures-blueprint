For a scheme $Y$, fix a prime $\ell\neq p$, an algebraic closure
$\Qlb/\Ql$, and the
common-stage data of Definitions [def-qbar-finext-diagram]–
[def-qbar-pair-triangulation].
Define the algebraically closed coefficient category directly by the
filtered common-stage construction

$$
D^b_c(Y,\Qlb)=
\underset{E\in\mathcal E_\ell}{\operatorname{2-colim}^{\mathrm{pair}}}
D^b_c(Y,E).
$$

Its shifts, distinguished triangles, and $\Qlb$-scalar multiplication are
those of Definition [def-qbar-pair-triangulation].
