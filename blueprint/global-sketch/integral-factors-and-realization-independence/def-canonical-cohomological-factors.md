For a smooth-proper datum $\mathscr X=(X_0,d,X)$ and
$0\leq i\leq2d$, define $P_i=P_i(\mathscr X)$ to be the $i$-th
member of the unique tuple supplied by
Proposition [prop-integral-factor-existence]; thus

$$
P_i(\mathscr X)\in\mathbf Z[T],\qquad P_i(\mathscr X)(0)=1.
$$
