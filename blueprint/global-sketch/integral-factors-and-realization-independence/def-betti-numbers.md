For a smooth-proper datum $\mathscr X=(X_0,d,X)$ and
$0\leq i\leq2d$, define

$$
b_i(\mathscr X)=\deg P_i(\mathscr X).
$$

Inside a statement that has fixed $\mathscr X$, write this integer simply
as $b_i$.
