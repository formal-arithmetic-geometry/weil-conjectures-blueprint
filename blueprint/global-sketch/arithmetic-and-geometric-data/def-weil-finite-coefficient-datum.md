An auxiliary finite coefficient datum is a tuple

$$
(\ell,\Qlb;E,\mathcal O_E,\varpi_E)
$$

in which $\ell\neq p$ is prime, $\Qlb$ is an algebraic closure of
$\Ql$, $E/\Ql$ is a finite extension inside $\Qlb$,
$\mathcal O_E$ is its valuation ring, and $\varpi_E$ is a uniformizer.
For every $n\geq1$,
the finite coefficient ring is written in full as
$\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n$, and the transition homomorphism is the quotient
map

$$
\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^{n+1}\twoheadrightarrow
\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n.
$$
