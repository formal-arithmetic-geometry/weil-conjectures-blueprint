By Corollary [corollary-point-counting-zeta-function],
$Z(X_0,T)\in\mathbf Q(T)\cap(1+T\mathbf Z[[T]])$ for every
separated $X_0$ of finite type over $\mathbf F_q$. So
Lemma [lemma-reduced-fractions-integer-coefficients] applies. We
write $Z(X_0,T)=A/B$ for the resulting fraction, with $A$ and $B$
coprime in $\mathbf Z[T]$ and $A(0)=B(0)=1$.
