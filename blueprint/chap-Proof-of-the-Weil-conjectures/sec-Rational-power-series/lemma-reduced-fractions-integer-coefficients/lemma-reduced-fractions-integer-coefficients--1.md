If $C(T)\in1+T\mathbf Z[[T]]$ is rational over $\mathbf Q$, then
there are unique coprime $A,B\in\mathbf Z[T]$ such that

$$
C(T)=A(T)/B(T),\qquad A(0)=B(0)=1.
$$
