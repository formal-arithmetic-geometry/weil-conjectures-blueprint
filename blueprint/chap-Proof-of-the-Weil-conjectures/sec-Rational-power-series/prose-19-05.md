Applied with $K=\mathbf Q$ and $K'=\mathbf Q_\ell$, the lemma shows that the zeta
function, which the trace formula writes as a quotient of polynomials
over $\mathbf Q_\ell$, is a quotient of polynomials over $\mathbf Q$. The next
lemma shows that the integrality of its coefficients passes to the
reduced fraction.
