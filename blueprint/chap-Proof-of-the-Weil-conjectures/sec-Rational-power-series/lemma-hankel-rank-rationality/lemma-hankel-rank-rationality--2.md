Let $K\subset K'$ be fields and $C(T)\in K[[T]]$. Write $C_{K'}$
for the same series viewed in $K'[[T]]$. Then the following conditions
are equivalent:

1. there are $P',Q'\in K'[T]$ with $Q'(0)=1$ and $C_{K'}Q'=P'$;
2. there are $P,Q\in K[T]$ with $Q(0)=1$ and $CQ=P$.

Thus rationality, with regularity at zero, is preserved and reflected
by extension of the coefficient field.
