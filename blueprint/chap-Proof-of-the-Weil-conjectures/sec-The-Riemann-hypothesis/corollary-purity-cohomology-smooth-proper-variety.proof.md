The constant sheaf has weight zero, since its local Frobenius is the
identity. So Theorem [theorem-purity-higher-direct-images-under]
applies to the structure map $f_0:X_0\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$
with $w=0$. The base has a single closed point, with $q_y=q$. The
stalk of $R^if_{0*}\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ there is $H^i(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ with its geometric
Frobenius, and its eigenvalues therefore satisfy
$|\iota(\alpha)|=q^{i/2}$.
