For the Weil conjectures we need only the case where the base is a
point and the sheaf is constant. Then the fibre is $X$ itself, and the
stalk of $R^if_*\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ is $H^i(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$.
