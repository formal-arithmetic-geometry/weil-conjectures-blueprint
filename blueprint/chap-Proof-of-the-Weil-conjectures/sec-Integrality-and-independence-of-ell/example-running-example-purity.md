For the curve $C_0\colon y^2=x^3-x$ over $\mathbf F_3$ of the
introduction, the reciprocal roots $\pm i\sqrt3$ of $P_1=1+3T^2$
(Example [example-running-example-trace-formula]) have absolute value $\sqrt3$, as (2)
predicts. What purity adds to the earlier examples is a bound. For every
genus-one curve over $\mathbf F_3$ we get $|4-N_1|\le2\sqrt3$, that is,
$1\le N_1\le7$. In the proof, the upper bound $|\alpha|\le\sqrt3$ comes
from the weight bound for $Rf_!$ (Theorem [theorem-weights-rf-shriek])
applied to the constant sheaf, and $\alpha\beta=3$
(Example [example-running-example-duality]) turns it into the lower bound.
