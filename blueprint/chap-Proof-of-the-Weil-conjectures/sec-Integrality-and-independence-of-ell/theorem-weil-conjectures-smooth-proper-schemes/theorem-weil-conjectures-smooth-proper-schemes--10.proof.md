The space $\overline H^0_\ell$ is one-dimensional by [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--12], and Frobenius acts as the identity (`AlgebraicGeometry.WeilSheaf.frobenius_cohomology_zero_adicConstant`). The reverse characteristic polynomial is therefore $1-T$. For an integral Weil factorization the second equality is its `apply_zero` field.
