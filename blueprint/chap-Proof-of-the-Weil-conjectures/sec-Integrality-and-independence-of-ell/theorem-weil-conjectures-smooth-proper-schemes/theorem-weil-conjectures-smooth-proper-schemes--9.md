This degree statement is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem, which uses perfect pairings and root multisets directly.

For the integral family of [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--1], one has $b_i=b_{2d-i}$. It follows from the equality of root multisets under $z\mapsto(q^dz)^{-1}$; no separate degree-symmetry theorem is invoked.
