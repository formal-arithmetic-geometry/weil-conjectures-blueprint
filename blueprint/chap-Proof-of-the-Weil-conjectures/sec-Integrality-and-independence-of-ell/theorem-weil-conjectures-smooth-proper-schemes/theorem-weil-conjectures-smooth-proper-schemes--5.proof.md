The constant coefficient of a reverse characteristic polynomial is one. Since Frobenius is invertible, the roots of its reverse characteristic polynomial are the inverses of its eigenvalues, with multiplicity; extending coefficients along $\iota$ preserves these multisets. Apply [corollary-purity-cohomology-smooth-proper-variety] to each eigenvalue and take the inverse of its absolute value.
