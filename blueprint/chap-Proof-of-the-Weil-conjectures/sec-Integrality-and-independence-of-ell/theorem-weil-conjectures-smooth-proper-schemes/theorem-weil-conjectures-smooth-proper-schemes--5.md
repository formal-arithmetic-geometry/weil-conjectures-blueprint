For every prime $\ell\nmid q$, every embedding $\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$ and every $i\geq0$, the polynomial $\iota(\overline P_{i,\ell})$ has constant coefficient one, and every complex root $z$ satisfies
$$
\lvert z\rvert=q^{-i/2}.
$$
Equivalently, the reciprocal roots have absolute value $q^{i/2}$.
