Since $p(0)=1$, the root $z$ is nonzero. The reverse polynomial $U^{\deg p}p(U^{-1})$ is monic with integer coefficients and vanishes at $z^{-1}$. This is the witness used by `AlgebraicGeometry.isIntegral_inv_of_mem_roots_map_intCast`.
