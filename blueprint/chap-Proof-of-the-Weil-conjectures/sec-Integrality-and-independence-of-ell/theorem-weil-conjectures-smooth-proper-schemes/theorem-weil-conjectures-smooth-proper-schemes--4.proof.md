The construction in clause [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--1] takes each multiset $R_i$ from the reciprocal roots of $A$ or $B$. Those roots are algebraic integers by Lemma [lemma-reciprocal-roots-zeta--4]; the same is therefore true of every reciprocal root of $P_i$.
