For an integral Weil factorization $P$ of $Z\in\mathbf Q[[T]]$, one has
$$
Z(T)\prod_{\substack{0\leq i\leq2d\\i\ \mathrm{even}}}P_i(T)
=\prod_{\substack{0\leq i\leq2d\\i\ \mathrm{odd}}}P_i(T)
\quad\hbox{in }\mathbf Q[[T]].
$$
In particular this applies to $Z(X_0,T)$ and the family of [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--1]. Since all factors have constant coefficient one, it gives the stated alternating product for the rational function.
