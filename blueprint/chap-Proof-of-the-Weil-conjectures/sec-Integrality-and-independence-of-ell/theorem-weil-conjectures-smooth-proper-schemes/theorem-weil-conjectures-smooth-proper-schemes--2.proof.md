Any two proposed integral polynomials become the same $P_{i,\ell}$ after inclusion into $\mathbf Q_\ell[T]$ for one prime $\ell\nmid q$. The map $\mathbf Z[T]\to\mathbf Q_\ell[T]$ is injective, so the original polynomials are equal.
