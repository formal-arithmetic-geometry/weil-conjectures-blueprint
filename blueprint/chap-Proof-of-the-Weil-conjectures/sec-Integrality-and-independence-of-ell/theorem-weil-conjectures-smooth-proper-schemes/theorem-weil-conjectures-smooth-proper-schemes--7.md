Let $K$ be a field and $(V,F)$ a finite-dimensional Frobenius module over $K$, so that $F$ is an automorphism. Then
$$
\deg\det(1-TF\mid V)=\dim_K V.
$$
In particular, $\deg\overline P_{i,\ell}=\dim_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}\overline H^i_\ell$. For an integral realization $P_i$, injectivity of coefficient extension gives $b_i=\dim_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}\overline H^i_\ell$.
