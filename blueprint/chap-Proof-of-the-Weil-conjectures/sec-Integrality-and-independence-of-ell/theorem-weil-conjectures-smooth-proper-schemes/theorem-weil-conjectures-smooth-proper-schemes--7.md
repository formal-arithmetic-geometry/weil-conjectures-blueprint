Let $K$ be a field and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $K$-vector space
with an automorphism $F$. Then

$$
\deg\det(1-TF\mid V)=\dim_KV.
$$

In particular, since Frobenius acts on $H^i_\ell$ by an automorphism,
$b_i=\deg P_i=\deg P_{i,\ell}=\dim_{\mathbf Q_\ell}H^i_\ell$ for every
prime $\ell\nmid q$.
