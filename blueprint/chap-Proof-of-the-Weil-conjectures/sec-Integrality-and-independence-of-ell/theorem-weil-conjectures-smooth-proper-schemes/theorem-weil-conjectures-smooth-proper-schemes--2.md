Let $q>1$, $d\geq0$, and $Z\in\mathbf Q[[T]]$. Suppose that $P$ and $P'$ are integral Weil factorizations of $Z$ and that every complex root of $P_i$ or $P'_i$ has absolute value $q^{-i/2}$. Then $P_i=P'_i$ for every $0\leq i\leq2d$.
