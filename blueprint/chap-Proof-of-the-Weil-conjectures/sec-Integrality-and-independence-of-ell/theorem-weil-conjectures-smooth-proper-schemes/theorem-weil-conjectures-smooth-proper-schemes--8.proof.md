For each prime, extend $P_i$ to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ and use the degree formula [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--7]. An injective map of coefficient fields preserves the degree of a nonzero polynomial. This is a consequence of the polynomial comparison, rather than an intermediate step of the Lean assembly.
