Clause [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--7] identifies the degree-based Euler characteristic with the cohomological one. The parity assertion of Corollary [corollary-functional-equation--5] therefore gives that $d\chi$ is even, with exactly the Euler characteristic in this statement.
