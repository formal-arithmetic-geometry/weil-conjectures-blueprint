Identify degree-zero derived direct image with ordinary direct image, and its global sections with those of the constant sheaf on the geometric fibre. The latter fibre is connected, so these global sections form a one-dimensional space. This is the sequence of identifications in `AlgebraicGeometry.WeilSheaf.finrank_cohomology_zero_adicConstant`. For an integral realization, the degree formula [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--7] gives the assertion about $b_0$.
