Poincaré duality gives a bijective trace map $\overline H^{2d}_\ell\longrightarrow\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell(-d)$. Thus the source has dimension one, as in [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--13]. Frobenius compatibility of the trace, together with its injectivity, identifies Frobenius with scalar multiplication by $q^d$. Its reverse characteristic polynomial is $1-q^dT$. For an integral Weil factorization the second equality is its `apply_last` field.
