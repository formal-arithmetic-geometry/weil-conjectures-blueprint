The top-degree trace of Theorem [theorem-trace-isomorphism-top-degree--7] identifies $H^{2d}(X,\mathbf Q_\ell)$ with $\mathbf Q_\ell(-d)$. Frobenius compatibility of trace gives scalar $q^d$ on this one-dimensional space. Thus its determinant polynomial is $1-q^dT$, which equals $P_{2d}$ by clause [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--1].
