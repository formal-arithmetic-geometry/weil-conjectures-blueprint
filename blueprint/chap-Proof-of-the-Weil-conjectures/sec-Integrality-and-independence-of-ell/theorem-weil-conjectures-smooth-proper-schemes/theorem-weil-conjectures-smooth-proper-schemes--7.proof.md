Clause [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--1] identifies $P_i$ with $\det(1-TF_i)$ over $\mathbf Q_\ell$. Frobenius is invertible by Theorem [theorem-poincare-duality-frobenius--5], so by clause [lemma-logarithm-determinant--4] this determinant has degree equal to the dimension of $H^i_\ell$. The definition $b_i=\deg P_i$ gives the assertion.
