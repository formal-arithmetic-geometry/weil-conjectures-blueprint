For $X_0$ smooth, proper and geometrically connected of pure dimension $d$,
$$
\overline P_{2d,\ell}=1-q^dT.
$$
For any integral Weil factorization $P$, its top-degree factor is likewise $P_{2d}=1-q^dT$.
