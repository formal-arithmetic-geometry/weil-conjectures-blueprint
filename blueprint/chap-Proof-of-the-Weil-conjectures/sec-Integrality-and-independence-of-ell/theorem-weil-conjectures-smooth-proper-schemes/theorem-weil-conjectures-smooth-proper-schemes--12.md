For a geometrically connected, separated scheme $X_0$ of finite type over $\mathbf F_q$ and a prime $\ell\nmid q$,
$$
\dim_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell}\overline H^0_\ell=1.
$$
For any integral realization in degree zero this yields $b_0=1$.
