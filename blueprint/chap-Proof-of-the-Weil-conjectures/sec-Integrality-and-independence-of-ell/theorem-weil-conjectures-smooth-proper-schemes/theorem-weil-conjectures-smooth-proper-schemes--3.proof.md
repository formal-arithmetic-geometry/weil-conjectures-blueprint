By clause [theorem-weil-conjectures-smooth-proper-schemes--1], each integral polynomial becomes the corresponding cohomological determinant polynomial over $\mathbf Q_\ell$. Substitute these equalities into (19.1). Both sides belong to $\mathbf Q(T)$, whose inclusion into $\mathbf Q_\ell(T)$ is injective, so the product identity holds over $\mathbf Q$.
