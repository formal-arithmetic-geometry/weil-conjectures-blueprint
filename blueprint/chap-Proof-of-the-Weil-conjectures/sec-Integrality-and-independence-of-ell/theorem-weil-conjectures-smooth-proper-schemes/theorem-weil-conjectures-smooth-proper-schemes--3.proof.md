This is the factorization field of `AlgebraicGeometry.IsWeilFactorization`. For the family constructed from cohomology, `TraceDatum.exists_isWeilFactorization` obtains this field by transporting the trace-determinant identity to $\mathbf C[[T]]$, replacing the determinant factors by the integral factors, and descending along the injective map $\mathbf Q\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$.
