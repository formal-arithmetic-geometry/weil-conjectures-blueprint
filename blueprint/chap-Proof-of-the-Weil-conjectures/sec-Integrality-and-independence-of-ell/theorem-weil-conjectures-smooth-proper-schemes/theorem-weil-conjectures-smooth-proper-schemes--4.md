This assertion is bypassed by the Lean proof of the main theorem; the following polynomial lemma is proved in the later LeanEval adapter.

Let $p\in\mathbf Z[T]$ with $p(0)=1$. If $z\in\mathbf C$ is a root of $p$, then $z^{-1}$ is an algebraic integer. In particular every reciprocal root of $P_i$ is an algebraic integer.
