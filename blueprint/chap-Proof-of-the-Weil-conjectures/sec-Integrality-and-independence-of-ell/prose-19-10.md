The argument of Hasse's theorem works in every dimension. The
[point-counting formula](@corollary-point-counting-zeta-function) expresses $\#X_0(\mathbf F_{q^m})$ through the
traces of $F^m$, and [purity](@corollary-purity-cohomology-smooth-proper-variety) bounds each trace by the dimension of the
group times the common absolute value of its eigenvalues.
