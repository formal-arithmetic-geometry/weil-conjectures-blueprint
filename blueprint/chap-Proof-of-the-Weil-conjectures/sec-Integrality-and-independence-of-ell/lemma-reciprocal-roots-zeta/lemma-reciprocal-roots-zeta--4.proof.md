Since $A(0)=B(0)=1$, both $U^{\deg A}A(U^{-1})$ and $U^{\deg B}B(U^{-1})$ are monic polynomials with integer coefficients. Their roots, precisely the reciprocal roots in question, are therefore algebraic integers.
