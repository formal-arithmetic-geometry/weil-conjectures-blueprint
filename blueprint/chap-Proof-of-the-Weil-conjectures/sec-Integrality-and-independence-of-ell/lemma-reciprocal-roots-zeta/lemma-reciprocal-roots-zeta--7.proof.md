The product identities of clauses [lemma-reciprocal-roots-zeta--1] and [lemma-reciprocal-roots-zeta--2] show that the image of a reciprocal root under any coefficient embedding is an eigenvalue in some degree of the appropriate parity. Purity assigns it absolute value $q^{j/2}$ in that degree. Clause [lemma-reciprocal-roots-zeta--6] assigns $q^{i/2}$ for the degree already chosen; as $q>1$, these force $j=i$. Thus it occurs in the asserted degree for every such embedding.
