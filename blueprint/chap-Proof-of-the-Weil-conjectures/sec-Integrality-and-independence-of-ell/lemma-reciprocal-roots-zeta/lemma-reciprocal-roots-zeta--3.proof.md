If $A$ has degree $m$, the constant coefficient of its reciprocal polynomial $U^mA(U^{-1})$ is the leading coefficient of $A$, which is nonzero. Thus zero is not a reciprocal root. The same argument applies to $B$; a constant polynomial has no reciprocal roots, so the assertion is vacuous in that case.
