In the reduced-fraction comparison of clause [lemma-reciprocal-roots-zeta--1], uniqueness identifies denominators as well as numerators because both have constant term $1$. The denominator of the cohomological product is precisely the product over even degrees. It therefore equals $B(T)$ as asserted.
