A nonconstant rational function gives a morphism to $\mathbf P^1$
[lem-sc-rational-function-morphism], finite flat of degree $[k(C):k(f)]$
[lem-sc-morphism-from-function]; pulling divisors back multiplies degrees
by that degree [lem-sc-pullback-degree], which makes principal divisors
have degree zero [lem-sc-deg-principal-zero] and so makes $\deg$ on
$\operatorname{Pic}$ well defined, splitting it as
$\mathbf Z\oplus\operatorname{Pic}^0$
[lem-sc-pic-proper-decomposition]. Positive degree implies ample
[lem-sc-positive-degree-ample]; with the Segre embedding
[lem-sc-segre-embedding] this gives quasi-projectivity of every smooth
separated curve [lem-sc-curves-quasi-projective], and, through
normalization [lem-sc-normalization-properties], [lem-sc-smooth-normal], a
compactification [lem-sc-compactification]. Complements of ample divisors
are affine [lem-sc-ample-complement-affine]; a proper affine curve is
finite [lem-sc-proper-affine-finite], so $H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=k$
[lem-sc-global-functions] and every proper open subscheme of a proper
smooth curve is affine [lem-sc-affine-open-proper]. Two technical tools
close the section: constant fibre length forces flatness
[lem-sc-finite-constant-length-flat] and coherent cohomology commutes with
flat base change [lem-sc-flat-base-change-coherent].
