Serre duality, proved by transport along a finite flat map to
$\mathbf P^1$. A four-lemma for exact hexagons
[lem-sc-four-lemma] drives the dévissage that upgrades the explicit
duality on the twists to all coherent modules on $\mathbf P^1$
[thm-sc-p1-duality]; the trace/codifferent isomorphism along a finite flat
morphism of smooth curves [lem-sc-finite-duality] then gives Serre duality
on any proper smooth curve [thm-sc-serre-duality-curve]. With
$\deg K_C=2g-2$ [lem-sc-canonical-degree] this yields the very-ampleness
threshold $\deg\mathcal M\geq2g+1$ [lem-sc-degree-2g1-very-ample] that the
earlier sections used. The section closes with the three normalization
inputs [lem-sc-normalization-exists],
[lem-sc-normalization-over-normal-locus], [lem-sc-normal-dim1-dvr],
[lem-sc-normalized-curve-smooth].
