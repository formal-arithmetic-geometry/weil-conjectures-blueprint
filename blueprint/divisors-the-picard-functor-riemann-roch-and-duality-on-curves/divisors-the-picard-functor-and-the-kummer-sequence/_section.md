The divisor vocabulary. Effective Cartier divisors
[def-sc-effective-cartier-divisor], divisors, principal divisors and the
class group [def-sc-divisor], the degree homomorphism and
$\operatorname{Pic}^0$ [def-sc-pic0], and the Picard functor as an fppf
sheafification [def-sc-picard-functor], [rem-ptr-sc-picard-functor-3].
Smoothness gives regular local rings [lem-sc-smooth-regular] which in
dimension one are discrete valuation rings
[lem-sc-regular-dim1-dvr]; hence closed points are Cartier divisors
[lem-sc-point-cartier] and every effective Cartier divisor on a smooth
curve is a finite sum of points with positive multiplicities
[lem-sc-cartier-divisor-points], [rem-ptr-sc-effective-cartier-divisor-1],
[rem-ptr-sc-divisor-2] — though not necessarily reduced
[rem-sc-higher-multiplicities]. Rational sections
[lem-sc-line-bundle-rational-section] then identify the class group with
the Picard group [lem-sc-divisor-class-curve].
