In the notation of [def-sc-picard-functor], the statements that the
$\operatorname{Pic}^d_{C/k}$ decompose $\operatorname{Pic}_{C/k}$ as a
disjoint union of open and closed subfunctors and that
$\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C/k}(k)=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)$ for $k$
algebraically closed (more generally separably closed) are the theorem of
the base in the Jacobian chapter, and they are not part of the definition.
