In the notation of [def-sc-effective-cartier-divisor], an *effective
Cartier divisor* on $C$ is a closed subscheme $D\subseteq C$ which is
locally defined by a single non-zero-divisor: every point of $D$ has an
open neighbourhood $U$ and a non-zero-divisor $f\in\mathcal O_C(U)$ with
$D\cap U=V(f)$.

The description of the effective Cartier divisors on a smooth curve as the
finite sums $\sum_Pn_P[P]$ of closed points with positive integer
multiplicities — each closed point, with its reduced structure, being the
multiplicity-one case — is [lem-sc-point-cartier] together with
[lem-sc-cartier-divisor-points], and it is not part of that definition.
