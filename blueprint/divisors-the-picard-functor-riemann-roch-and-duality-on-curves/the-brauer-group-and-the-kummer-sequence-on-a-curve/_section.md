The divisor exact sequence [lem-sc-divisor-exact-sequence] makes a Brauer
class on a smooth proper scheme determined by its generic fibre
[lem-sc-brauer-injectivity]; with Tsen this gives
$H^2(C_{\Et},\mathbf G_m)=0$ for every smooth separated curve
[lem-sc-brauer-zero], and feeding that into the long exact sequence of the
Kummer sequence computes $H^0$, $H^1$ and $H^2$ with $\mu_n$-coefficients
[lem-sc-kummer-les].

appears here only as a placeholder comment recording that it moved to the
Jacobian chapter; it is not imported.
