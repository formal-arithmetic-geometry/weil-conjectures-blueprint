In the notation of [def-sc-p1-standard], that such a module exists and is
unique up to unique isomorphism is [lem-sc-p1-twist-exists]; the resulting
description of its sections is [lem-sc-p1-twist-sections], and its
identification with the tensor powers of $\mathcal O(1)$ for $d\geq0$ is
[lem-sc-p1-twist-tensor]; none of the three is part of the definition.
