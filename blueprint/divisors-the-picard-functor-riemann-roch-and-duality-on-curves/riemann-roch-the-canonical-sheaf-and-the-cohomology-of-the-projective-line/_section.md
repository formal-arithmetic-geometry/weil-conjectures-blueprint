The local-ring inputs the divisor section used, now proved: regular local
rings are domains [lem-sc-regular-local-domain], and a noetherian local
domain with principal maximal ideal is a DVR
[lem-sc-principal-ideal-dvr]. Riemann--Roch is a recorded input
[lem-sc-riemann-roch]; negative degree kills sections
[lem-sc-negative-degree-no-sections]. The canonical sheaf is the sheaf of
Kähler differentials [def-sc-kahler-differentials], a line bundle
[lem-sc-canonical-sheaf], and $\operatorname{Ext}$ against a finite locally
free module is sheaf cohomology of the dual
[def-sc-ext-sheaf], [lem-sc-ext-locally-free], [rem-ptr-sc-ext-sheaf-4].
The rest computes $\mathbf P^1$ explicitly: the standard cover and the
twists [def-sc-p1-standard], their existence
[lem-sc-p1-twist-exists], sections [lem-sc-p1-twist-sections], tensor
powers [lem-sc-p1-twist-tensor], [rem-ptr-sc-p1-standard-5], the full
cohomology table with $\omega_{\mathbf P^1}\cong\mathcal O(-2)$
[lem-sc-p1-monomial], vanishing above degree one
[lem-sc-p1-hi-vanishing], resolutions by twists
[lem-sc-p1-graded-resolution], and finiteness of coherent cohomology
[lem-sc-p1-coherent-finite].
