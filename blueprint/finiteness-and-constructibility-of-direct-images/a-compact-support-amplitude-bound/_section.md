$R^qf_!F=0$ for $q>2d$ when the *geometric fibres of $f$ itself* have
dimension at most $d$ [lem-dg-compact-support-amplitude]. The point of the
proof is that no bound is placed on the dimension of the compactified
fibre: the extension by zero is supported on the closure of the original
fibre, whose dimension is the original one.
