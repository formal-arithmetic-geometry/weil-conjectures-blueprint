The conclusions the rest of the blueprint cites: open-immersion direct
images are constructible and vanish in large degrees
[thm-fin-open-immersion]; every finite-type direct image is bounded
constructible, with the sharp $2d$ compact-support bound from the original
fibres [sbc-thm-finiteness-finite-type]; and smooth base change holds in the
bounded constructible categories [sbc-cor-bounded-constructible-categories],
whose proof carries the transport between the étale and pro-étale sites
explicitly.
