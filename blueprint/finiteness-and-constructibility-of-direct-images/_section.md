Part 3 of the layered split. This chapter must be read after the smooth
base-change chapter, the ambient smooth-duality chapter, and the ambient
foundations, whose results it consumes.

It opens with a compact-support amplitude bound that uses the *original*
fibres of the morphism [a-compact-support-amplitude-bound], then runs
Deligne's generic direct-image induction
[generic-finiteness-by-a-finite-boundary-induction] (SGA 4½, Finitude,
2.1–2.8), supplying the geometric and derived steps explicitly. The
finite-type direct-image statements follow
[the-finite-type-direct-image-statements], and the chapter closes with
compact-support constructibility and cohomological finiteness
[compact-support-constructibility-and-cohomological-finiteness].

*Note on ids.* Two propositions here — `prop:finite-compact-support-constructibility`
and `prop:cohomological-finiteness` — collide with identifiers already on
`main` from the earlier sketch import. They carry the `-detailed` suffix, as
in the other detailed-chapter imports.
