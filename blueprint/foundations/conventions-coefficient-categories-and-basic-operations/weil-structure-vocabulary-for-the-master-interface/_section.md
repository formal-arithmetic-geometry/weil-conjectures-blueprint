The vocabulary in which the master statements are phrased. The constructible
heart [def-weil-constructible-heart] is the abelian category of $\Lambda$-adic
sheaves; a Weil sheaf is one of those together with an isomorphism
$Lg_Y^*\mathcal F\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal F$
[def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex], and the resulting common-stage category is
again idempotent complete [lem-weil-weil-complex-qbar-idempotent-complete].
Geometric Frobenius acts on $E(m)$ by $q^{-m}$
[def-weil-frobenius-coefficient-twists]. Cohomology and compactly supported
cohomology are read off at the geometric point
[def-weil-finite-coefficient-point],
[def-weil-ordinary-compact-cohomology], and the operator $F$ they carry is
[def-weil-global-geometric-frobenius].
