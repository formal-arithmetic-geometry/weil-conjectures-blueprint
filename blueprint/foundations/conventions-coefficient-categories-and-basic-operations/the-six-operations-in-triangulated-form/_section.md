The operations themselves, each given at finite torsion level and then
transported up the coefficient tower by derived inverse limit,
rationalization, and the common-stage construction. Pullback, tensor and
internal Hom [def-weil-derived-pullback-tensor-hom]; derived direct image
[def-weil-derived-direct-image]; extension by zero
[def-weil-extension-by-zero], which is exact
[lem-weil-extension-by-zero-exact] and left adjoint to restriction
[lem-weil-extension-by-zero-adjunction]; the compact-support direct image,
built from a compactification [def-weil-compact-support-candidate] and shown
independent of it on the etale-origin subcategory
[def-compact-support-direct-image-detailed]; the exceptional inverse image,
which is available only under an explicit admissibility datum
[def-weil-exceptional-adjoint-admissibility],
[def-weil-ambient-exceptional-adjoint],
[def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image]; and the exchange and projection
transformations [def-exchange-projection].

The separation of existence of the exceptional adjoint from constructible and
finite-Tor preservation is deliberate; [rem-weil-admissibility-split] records
which half is proved where.
