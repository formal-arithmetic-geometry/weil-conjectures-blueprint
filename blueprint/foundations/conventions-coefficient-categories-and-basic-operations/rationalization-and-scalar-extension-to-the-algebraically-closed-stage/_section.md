The two field stages. Rationalization inverts the uniformizer on Hom-groups
[def-weil-rationalization-karoubi] and the algebraically closed stage is the
common-stage category over the directed set of finite subextensions of
$\Qlb$ [def-weil-algebraically-closed-coefficients]. Neither construction
takes a Karoubi completion; that both are nevertheless idempotent complete is
[lem-weil-rationalization-idempotent-complete] and
[lem-weil-qbar-idempotent-complete]. Scalar extension between finite stages
is well defined [lem-weil-scalar-extension-well-defined], exact
[lem-weil-scalar-extension-exact-triangulated], and compatible with
composition [lem-weil-scalar-extension-composition].
