The étale-site counterpart of the two preceding sections: the vanishing
statements, the stalks of pullbacks and of closed pushforwards, and the full
faithfulness of $i_*$, for sheaves on small étale sites. These are the
statements the Lean development proves on the étale site, and the
constructible-sheaf chapter and the proper base change chapter use them. The
pro-étale open–closed sequence above does not depend on them.

Throughout the section we fix the following data.

- A coefficient category $\mathcal A$, abelian and Grothendieck abelian,
on which $\mathcal A$-valued presheaves on every site of size $u+1$ admit
a left exact sheafification (in Lean the class
`CategoryTheory.HasSmallSiteSheafify`; the small étale sites and
their localizations are of that size). Mathematically this holds for
every Grothendieck abelian $\mathcal A$ of size $u+1$; in Lean it is so
far available only for those concrete over $\mathrm{Type}\,(u+1)$ whose
forgetful functor preserves limits and filtered colimits of that size and
reflects isomorphisms, such as $\mathrm{Mod}_\Lambda$, see
[rmk-openclosed-universes].
For the stalk statements [lemma-openclosed-point-ulift] and
[prop-openclosed-lowerstar-fully-faithful] we ask in addition that
$\mathcal A$ be concrete in the sense recalled in
[rmk-openclosed-universes]; the case of interest,
$\mathcal A=\mathrm{Mod}_\Lambda$ for the coefficient ring $\Lambda$ of
the $\ell$-adic formalism, satisfies this.
- A scheme $X$. For a scheme $Y$ we write $\et Y$ for its small étale
site: the objects are the schemes étale over $Y$, the morphisms are
the $Y$-morphisms, and the topology is generated by the jointly
surjective families. We write $\shv{\et Y}$ for the category of
$\mathcal A$-valued sheaves on it.
- A morphism of schemes $g\colon Y'\to Y$ induces an adjunction
$g^*\dashv g_*$ between $\shv{\et Y}$ and $\shv{\et{Y'}}$, with
$$
(g_*\mathcal G)(V)=\mathcal G(V\times_YY')
\qquad(V\in\et Y).
$$
Here $g_*$ is left exact and $g^*$ is exact. If $g$ is an open
immersion then $g^*$ has in addition a left adjoint $g_!$, the
extension by zero, which is exact, and the unit
$\mathrm{id}\to g^*g_!$ is an isomorphism.
- An open immersion $j\colon U\to X$ and a closed immersion
$i\colon Z\to X$ with disjoint ranges.
