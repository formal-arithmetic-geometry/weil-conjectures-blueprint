Finite coefficient fields. The rational category is the raw scalar
localization $\Dbc(T,\mathcal O_E)[1/\varpi]$, with no Karoubi envelope
taken [def-weil-pbc-rational-category]; every exact $\mathcal O_E$-linear
functor and every natural transformation between such functors extends
uniquely to it [prop-weil-pbc-rational-transport]. Applying this to the
integral base-change isomorphism gives proper base change with
$E$-coefficients [thm-weil-pbc-finite-extension], independent of the chosen
lattice and coherent [prop-weil-pbc-finite-extension-coherence], with the
geometric-fibre form [cor-weil-pbc-geometric-fiber-finite-extension]. The
coherent scalar-extension package along a finite $E\subset E'$ is recorded
as an admitted classical input [prop-weil-pbc-scalar-extension].
