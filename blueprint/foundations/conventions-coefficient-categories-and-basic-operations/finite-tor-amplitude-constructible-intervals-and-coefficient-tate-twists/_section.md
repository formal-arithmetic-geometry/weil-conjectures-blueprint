Three pieces of bookkeeping used by the operations. Finite Tor amplitude
[def-weil-finite-tor-amplitude] is a genuine extra condition at finite
torsion coefficients but automatic at field coefficients
[lem-weil-field-tor-amplitude]; the constructible interval subcategories
[def-weil-constructible-intervals] record where cohomology is allowed to sit;
and the Tate twist [def-coefficient-twists-detailed] is built from the
$\mu_{\ell^r}$ tower at every stage of the coefficient tower at once.
