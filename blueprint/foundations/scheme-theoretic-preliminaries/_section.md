General facts about schemes that the formalized foundations already use, most of
them Mathlib statements: closed immersions and quasi-affine schemes, relative
affine space, compactifications, and the Proj interface. The source records them
where [nagata-compactification], [chap-proper-base-change] and the
compact-support chapter first need them; the Lean proofs of the constructible
sheaves, the exchange morphism and the Segre embedding use them earlier, so they
stand here.
