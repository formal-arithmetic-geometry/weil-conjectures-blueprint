An $A$-algebra is *ind-etale* if it is a filtered colimit of etale algebras
of finite presentation [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions]. It is *ind-Zariski*
if such a presentation can be chosen with terms finite products of
localizations $A_f$. The corresponding affine scheme is an inverse limit of
affine etale schemes, with affine transition maps. A ring map is *weakly
etale* when its map on spectra is weakly etale in
[def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions]; equivalently, $B$ is flat over $A$ and
over $B\otimes_A B$ by multiplication.
