The construction that lets a weakly etale map be refined by an ind-etale one
[thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-refinement], which is what makes the etale and
pro-etale sites comparable. The route is Olivier's theorem — a weakly etale
local map over a strictly henselian ring is an isomorphism
[thm-weil-pbc-weak-etale-strict-local] — reached through the Tor formula for
a flat diagonal [lem-weil-pbc-weak-etale-tor], the field case
[lem-weil-pbc-weak-etale-field] and integral closure
[lem-weil-pbc-weak-etale-integral-closure]; and then the w-local machinery
[def-weil-pbc-w-local], [def-weil-pbc-w-localization],
[def-weil-pbc-retained-localization], [def-weil-pbc-etale-contraction],
[def-weil-pbc-strict-w-cover], which produces a cover whose local rings are
strictly henselian [lem-weil-pbc-strict-w-cover].
