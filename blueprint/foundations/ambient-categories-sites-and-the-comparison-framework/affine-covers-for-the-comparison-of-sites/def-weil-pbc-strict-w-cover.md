For a ring $A$, put $W=A^w$, let $I$ be the radical ideal defining its closed
point subset, whose closedness is proved in
[lem-weil-pbc-w-localization-points], and set

$$
 E=W^{\mathrm e},\qquad C_A=E_{[V(IE)]}.
$$

The operations are those of [def-weil-pbc-w-localization],
[def-weil-pbc-etale-contraction] and [def-weil-pbc-retained-localization].
