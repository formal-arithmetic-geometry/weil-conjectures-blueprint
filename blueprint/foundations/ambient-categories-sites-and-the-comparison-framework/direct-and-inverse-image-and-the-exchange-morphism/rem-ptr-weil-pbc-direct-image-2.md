quasi-compact and quasi-separated [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions] $u$, the
description of $R^qu_*F$ and of $Ru_*$ on $\Dplus$ by the cohomology of the
etale objects $W\times_VU$ is [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-higher-direct-image].
