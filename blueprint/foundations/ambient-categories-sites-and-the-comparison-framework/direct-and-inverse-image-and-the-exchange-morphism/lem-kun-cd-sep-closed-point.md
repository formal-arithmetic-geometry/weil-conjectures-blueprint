If $K$ is a separably closed field then $\kuncd(\Spec(K))=0$.

## Proof

By [def-kun-cd], $\kuncd(\Spec(K))=0$ means $H^q=0$ for every
torsion etale sheaf and every $q>0$, which is
[lem-kun-03qu-sep-closed].
