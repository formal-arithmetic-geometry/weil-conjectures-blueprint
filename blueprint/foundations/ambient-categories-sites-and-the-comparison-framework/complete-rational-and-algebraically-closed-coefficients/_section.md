Two blocks, kept apart here because the source keeps them apart only by
subject and not by heading.

The first is the subsection
[the-karoubi-envelope-and-bounded-t-structures]: idempotent completion is
shown to be redundant for a triangulated category with a bounded t-structure
[thm-weil-karoubian-bounded-t], which is what lets the rationalized and
common-stage coefficient categories be defined without formally adjoining
idempotents.

[def-weil-pbc-proetale-modules], the derived operations on a ringed
pro-etale topos [def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations], and the two

[prop-weil-pbc-proetale-constant-ring-map],
