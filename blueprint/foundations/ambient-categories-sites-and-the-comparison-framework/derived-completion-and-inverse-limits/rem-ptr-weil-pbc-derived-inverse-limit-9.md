In the notation of [def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-limit], the following holds.
No construction of the derived inverse limit beyond the derived limit of the
conventions chapter is made here: that it is computed by a functorial
K-injective resolution [def-weil-pbc-kinjective-resolutions] $M_\bullet\to
I_\bullet$ in the complex category of $\operatorname{Tow}_{A}(T)$ as
$\varprojlim_nI_n$, and that fixing such a resolution gives a functorial
model, is [prop-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-limit-computation] and
[prop-weil-pbc-rlim-functoriality].
