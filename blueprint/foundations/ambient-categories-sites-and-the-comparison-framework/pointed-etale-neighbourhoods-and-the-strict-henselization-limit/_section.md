The strict henselization presented as a limit rather than as a black box:
pointed etale neighbourhoods [def-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods],
among which the affine ones are cofinal
[lemma-weil-pbc-affine-neighbourhood-cofinal], so that $\mathcal
O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{S,\bar s}$ is their filtered colimit and $S_{(\bar s)}$
their inverse limit [def-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-limit]. Fibre
products commute with that limit [lemma-weil-pbc-fibre-product-limit], which is
what lets stalk computations be made after base change.

Extension by zero along an etale map and the preservation of injectives under
etale restriction, which the source files here, belong with the functors they
are about and stand in
[direct-and-inverse-image-and-the-exchange-morphism] instead.
