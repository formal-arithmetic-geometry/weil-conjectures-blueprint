For a finite morphism [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions] $h:V\to Y$ and
$a\in\Gamma(Y,F)$, define $h^\sharp a\in\Gamma(V,h^{-1}F)$ by the unit of the
inverse-image adjunction [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image].
