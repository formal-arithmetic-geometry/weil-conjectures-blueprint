Etale cohomology is continuous along an affine cofiltered limit
[thm-weil-pbc-etale-cohomology-continuity], also for bounded-below complexes
[lem-weil-pbc-derived-cohomology-continuity] and for filtered systems of
coefficients [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-filtered-colimit]. That gives the
strict-henselization stalk formula
[prop-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-stalk-formula-detailed], which combined
with the degree-zero comparison makes the *underived* proper exchange an
isomorphism [lem-weil-pbc-underived-proper-exchange]. The section closes with
the equivalence the whole proper-base-change chapter argues through:
$\mathrm{PBC}(f)$ holds if and only if $\mathrm{IA}_r(f)$ does for every
prime $r$ [prop-weil-pbc-property-iff-acyclicity], via devissage
[lem-weil-pbc-prime-factor-devissage], the five-lemma transfer
[lem-weil-pbc-five-lemma-transfer] and the filtered torsion union
[lem-weil-pbc-torsion-filtered-union].
