Let $p:Y\to S=\operatorname{Spec}A$ be a proper
[def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions] morphism with $A$ a commutative ring. Put
$B=H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y)$, a ring under the $A$-algebra structure induced by
$p$, and let $q:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}B$ be the canonical morphism and
$s:\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ the morphism of the algebra
structure, so that $p=s\circ q$. The factorization

$$
 Y\xrightarrow{\ q\ }\operatorname{Spec}B\xrightarrow{\ s\ }\operatorname{Spec}A
$$

is the *Stein factorization* of $p$ (*source: Stacks Project, More on
Morphisms, Section 37.53, Tag
[03GX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03GX)*).
