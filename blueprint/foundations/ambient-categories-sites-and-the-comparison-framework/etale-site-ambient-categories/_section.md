The scheme-theoretic vocabulary [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions], the small
etale site [def-weil-pbc-etale-site] and its sheaves
[def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves]. Geometric stalks
[def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks] are exact
[prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact] and jointly conservative
[lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative], on sheaves and on the derived
category [lem-weil-pbc-derived-stalk-conservative]; that pair of facts is
what every stalkwise argument later in the blueprint rests on. Cohomology is
computed by K-injective resolutions, which exist
[prop-weil-pbc-kinjective-resolutions-exist] and do not affect the answer
[prop-weil-pbc-cohomology-resolution-independent].
