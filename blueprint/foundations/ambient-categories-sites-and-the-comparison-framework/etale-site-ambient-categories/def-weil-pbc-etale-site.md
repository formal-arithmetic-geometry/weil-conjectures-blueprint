For a scheme $T$, the *small etale site* $T_{\Et}$ has as objects the etale
morphisms [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions] $U\to T$, as morphisms the
$T$-morphisms between them, and as covering families the finite or arbitrary
jointly surjective families $\{U_i\to U\}$ of etale morphisms. Fibre products
exist in $T_{\Et}$ and are computed as scheme-theoretic fibre products, so
$T_{\Et}$ is a site with finite limits.
