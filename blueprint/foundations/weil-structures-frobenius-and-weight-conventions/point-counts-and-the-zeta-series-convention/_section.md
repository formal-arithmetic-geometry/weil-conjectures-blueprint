The normalization of the Hasse--Weil zeta series used throughout
[def-weil-point-counts-zeta] — the exponential of $\sum N_nT^n/n$, a formal
power series in $1+T\mathbf Q[[T]]$ — together with the closed-point degree
counts $c_r$ that the Euler-product form uses. The base case
$Y_0=\Spec\Fq$ fixes the sign and direction of the convention
[ex-weil-finite-field-zeta].
