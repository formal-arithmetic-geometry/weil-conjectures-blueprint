The three weight notions, all relative to a fixed isomorphism
$\iota:\Qlb\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbf C$. Punctual purity of weight $w$
constrains the eigenvalues of every local Frobenius by
$|\iota(\alpha)|=q_y^{w/2}$ [def-weil-punctual-weights]; a Weil complex is
$\iota$-mixed of weights $\leq w$ when its cohomology sheaves are, with the
degree shift, and the lower bound is the upper bound for the Verdier dual
[def-weil-complex-weights]; and the same definitions for a bare
finite-dimensional Frobenius module [def-weil-frobenius-module-weights].
