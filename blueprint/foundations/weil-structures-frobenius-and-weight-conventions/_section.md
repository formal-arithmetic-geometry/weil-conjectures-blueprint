The arithmetic vocabulary in which the Weil conjectures are stated: the local
Frobenius at a closed point and its alternating trace
[def-weil-local-frobenius-trace], the smooth-proper cohomological family
$(V_{i,\ell},F_{i,\ell})$ whose characteristic polynomials the conjectures
constrain [def-weil-smooth-proper-cohomological-family], the Hasse--Weil zeta
series [def-weil-point-counts-zeta], the Poincare trace and cup-product
pairing [def-weil-poincare-trace-cup], and the three notions of weight —
punctual [def-weil-punctual-weights], for a Weil complex
[def-weil-complex-weights], and for a finite-dimensional Frobenius module
[def-weil-frobenius-module-weights].

This chapter is chapter 2 of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex`. Its
identifiers do not collide with anything already in the blueprint, so none
carries a `-detailed` suffix; its `uses` list is long because almost
everything it says is phrased in the vocabulary of chapter 1.
