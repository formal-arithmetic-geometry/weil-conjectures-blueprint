Let $f:X\to S$ be a morphism of schemes. The set of points of $X$
where $f$ is quasi-finite is an open $U\subset X$, and the induced
morphism $U\to S$ is locally quasi-finite.
(*Stacks Project, Morphisms of Schemes, Lemma 29.57.2, Tag
[01TI](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01TI).*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Morphisms of Schemes, Lemma 29.57.2 ([Tag 01TI](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01TI)): the locus where a morphism is quasi-finite is open, and the restriction of the morphism to it is locally quasi-finite. No separate proof is given.
