A morphism of schemes $f:X\to S$ is *affine* if for every affine
open $V\subset S$ the preimage $f^{-1}(V)$ is affine, in the sense of
the ambient-foundations chapter; any morphism
between affine schemes is affine, and a closed immersion is affine.
(*Stacks Project, Morphisms of Schemes, Section 29.11, Tag
[01S8](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01S8).*)
