Let $X$ be a scheme and $\mathcal F$ a quasi-coherent
$\mathcal O_X$-module. For any affine open $U\subset X$ we have
$\mathrm H^p(U,\mathcal F)=0$ for all $p>0$.
(*Stacks Project, Cohomology of Schemes, Lemma 30.2.2, Tag
[01XB](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XB).*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Cohomology of Schemes, Lemma 30.2.2 ([Tag 01XB](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XB)): a quasi-coherent module on an affine scheme has no higher cohomology. No separate proof is given.
