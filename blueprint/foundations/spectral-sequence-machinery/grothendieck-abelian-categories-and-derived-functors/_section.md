The homological-algebra vocabulary the rest of the chapter uses: abelian
[def-sp-abelian] and Grothendieck abelian [def-sp-grothendieck-abelian]
categories, which have enough injectives
[lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives]; injective resolutions
[def-sp-injective-resolutions] and the right derived functors they compute
[def-sp-left-exact-additive], [lem-sp-derived-well-defined]; acyclic objects
[def-sp-acyclic]; the fact that a right adjoint of an exact functor preserves
injectives [lem-sp-right-adjoint-preserves-injectives], which is what makes
the Grothendieck spectral sequence applicable to a composite of direct
images; and homological functors with their long exact sequences
[def-sp-homological-functor], [lem-sp-long-exact-of-triangle].
