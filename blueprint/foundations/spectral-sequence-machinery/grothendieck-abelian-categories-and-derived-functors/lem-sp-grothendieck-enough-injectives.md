Every Grothendieck abelian category [def-sp-grothendieck-abelian] has enough
injectives.
(*Source: the classical input; the sites used in this blueprint are
Grothendieck topoi, cf. Stacks Project, Cohomology on Sites, Section 12.24.*)

## Proof

The assertion is the input recorded in the source note; the blueprint uses it
throughout for injective resolutions in the sheaf categories of the etale and
pro-etale sites; it is not discharged here.
