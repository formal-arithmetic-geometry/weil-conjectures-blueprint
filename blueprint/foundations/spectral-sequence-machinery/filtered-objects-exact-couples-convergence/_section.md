The exact-couple formalism in the bidegree convention the hypercohomology
spectral sequence needs [def-sp-exact-couple], the recursive $Z_r/B_r$
description of convergence [def-sp-convergence], and the criterion actually
used downstream: in a half plane the pages stabilize and the abutment
filtration is finite, exhaustive and separated, so convergence needs no
derived inverse-limit condition [thm-sp-half-plane-convergence]. The four
lemmas that follow are the tools for comparing two spectral sequences
[lem-sp-page-induction], [lem-sp-finite-filtration-induction],
[lem-sp-couple-morphism], and for reading off an edge isomorphism when a
sequence collapses to one column [lem-sp-edge-collapse].
