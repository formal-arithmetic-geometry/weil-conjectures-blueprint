The spectral-sequence devices used by the proper-base-change chapter, built
from scratch so that nothing there rests on an unstated convention. The
chapter fixes the homological-algebra vocabulary
[grothendieck-abelian-categories-and-derived-functors], the notion of
spectral sequence [the-spectral-sequence-framework], the exact-couple
formalism together with a convergence criterion usable without a derived
inverse-limit condition [filtered-objects-exact-couples-convergence], the two
hypercohomology spectral sequences [hypercohomology-spectral-sequences], and
the Grothendieck spectral sequence of a composite with its base-change
naturality [the-grothendieck-spectral-sequence].

The items the proper-base-change chapter consumes are
[thm-sp-truncation-e2], [thm-sp-half-plane-convergence],
[lem-sp-couple-morphism], [lem-sp-shift-identification],
[lem-sp-finite-filtration-induction], [lem-sp-leray-acyclicity],
[thm-sp-grothendieck] and [thm-sp-grothendieck-base-change], read through the
dictionary [def-sp-etale-dictionary].
