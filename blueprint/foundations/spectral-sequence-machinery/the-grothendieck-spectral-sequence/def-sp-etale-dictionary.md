The spectral sequence statements used in the proper-base-change chapter are
obtained from the statements above by the substitutions: the abelian category
[def-sp-abelian] $\mathcal A$ is $\operatorname{Ab}(X_{\mathrm{et}})$
(Grothendieck abelian [def-sp-grothendieck-abelian]), the left exact additive
[def-sp-left-exact-additive] functor $v$ is the etale direct image $f_*$ with
right derived functors $R^nf_*$, $K\in\operatorname{Ch}^+(\mathcal A)$ is
$K\in\Dplus(X_{\mathrm{et}})$ (with the K-injective resolution
[def-sp-kinjective-bridge] presentation of the proper-base-change chapter),
the homological functors $\Phi$ are $Rf_*$, $Rf'_*g'^{-1}$, $g^{-1}Rf_*$, the
natural transformation $\eta$ of couples is the derived exchange morphism
$\Ex_{\square,K}$ (introduced in the conventions of the proper-base-change
chapter), and the composite pair $(F,G)$ is $(f_*,h_*)$ for $X\to Y\to Z$.
