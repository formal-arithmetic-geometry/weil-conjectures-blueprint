The first two chapters of Part 3 of `weil_conjectures_detailed.tex`, the
language of curves every other block of that part is built on:

- [chap-smooth-curve-calculations]: curves and their constants, invertible
modules and the Kummer sequence, the genus, ample line bundles, fields of type
$C_r$ and Tsen's theorem.
- [chap-picard-duality-curves]: divisors and the Picard functor, degrees,
Riemann--Roch, duality on curves, and the Brauer group of a curve.

One remark of the second chapter, [rem-ptr-sc-picard-functor-3], cites the
theorem of the base and is filed with it in [picard-schemes].
