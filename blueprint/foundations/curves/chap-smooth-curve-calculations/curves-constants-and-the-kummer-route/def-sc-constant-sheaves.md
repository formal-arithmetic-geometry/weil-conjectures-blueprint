Let $C$ be a curve and let $n\geq1$.
The *constant etale sheaf* $\mathbf F_n$ on $C_{\Et}$ is the
sheaf $\underline{\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z}$ associated to the constant
presheaf $U\mapsto\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$ (see
[def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves]); its geometric stalks are
$\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$.
