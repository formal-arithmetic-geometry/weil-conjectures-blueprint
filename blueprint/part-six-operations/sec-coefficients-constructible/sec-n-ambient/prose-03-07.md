The kernel contains more than the complexes killed by a power of $\ell$,
for example $\bigoplus_{n\ge1}\mathcal O_{E,X}/\ell^n$. It does so even
among constructible sheaves: on $\bigsqcup_{n\ge1}\operatorname{Spec}k$,
the sheaf equal to $\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$ on the $n$-th point is killed by
inverting $\ell$ but by no power of $\ell$. When the underlying space of
$X$ is Noetherian, this cannot happen in $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, and
inverting $\ell$ amounts to tensoring the Hom groups with $E$.
