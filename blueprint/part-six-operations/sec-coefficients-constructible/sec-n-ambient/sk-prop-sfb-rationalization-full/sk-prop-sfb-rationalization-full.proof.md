Inverting $\ell$ is exact, so $E_X$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{E,X}$ and
$H^q(E_X\otimes K)=H^q(K)[1/\ell]$. Restriction of scalars is right
adjoint to extension of scalars, and the counit is an isomorphism because
$\ell$ already acts invertibly on an $E_X$-module. Hence restriction is fully
faithful, and extension of scalars is the Verdier localization at its
kernel. By the formula for $H^q(E_X\otimes K)$, this kernel consists of
the complexes $K$ with $H^q(K)[1/\ell]=0$ for all $q$.
