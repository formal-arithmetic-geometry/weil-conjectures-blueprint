1. For Noetherian $X$, every locally closed subset is constructible,
   so in Definition [sk-def-sfb-constructible](2) any finite stratification
   by locally closed subschemes may be used.
2. For finite $A$ and qcqs $X$, $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ is the pullback of the
   usual étale category $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$ by
   Corollary [sk-cor-pe-finite-tor-comparison].
3. If $\Lambda=E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, the ring $\Lambda(*)$ is a field,
   so each $P_i$ is a finite-dimensional vector space and a module
   satisfying the condition of (1) is locally free of finite rank. In other
   words, a constructible $E$- or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaf is one that is
   *lisse*, that is, locally free of finite rank, on the strata of a
   finite stratification (BS, Definition 6.8.6).
4. If $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, locally on a stratum a constructible
   $\mathcal O_{E,X}$-module is $P\otimes_{\mathcal O_E}\mathcal O_{E,X}$
   for a finitely generated $\mathcal O_E$-module $P$. For example,
   $\mathcal O_{E,X}/\pi_E$ is constructible.
5. For the admissible coefficients, the constructible sheaves form an
   abelian category. For finite $A$ this is the classical theory of
   sheaves with finite stalks, and the other cases are
   Lemma [sk-lem-coefficient-heart],
   Corollary [sk-cor-coefficient-heart-ii] and
   Lemma [sk-lem-int-integral-heart].
