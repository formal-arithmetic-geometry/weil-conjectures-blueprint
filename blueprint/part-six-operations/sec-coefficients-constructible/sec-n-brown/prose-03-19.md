By Proposition [sk-prop-n-ctf-equals-bc], the theorem holds with $\Dbc$ in
place of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ for $\Lambda=\Zl,\mathcal O_E,\Ql,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.

For proper $p$ we may use the trivial compactification, so that $Rp_!=Rp_*$,
and by part (4) the functor $Rp_*:D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)\to D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$ has the
right adjoint $p^!$.
