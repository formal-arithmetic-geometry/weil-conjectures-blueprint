**Exercises.** (1) Compute the stalks of $a_!L$ for a locally
closed immersion $a$ and a lisse $L$. (2) For $K=\Lambda_X[1]$, in
which degree is the nonzero cohomology sheaf? (3) Show that
$j_!\Lambda_{\mathbf G_m}$ has no nonzero global sections on
$\mathbf A^1_k$.
