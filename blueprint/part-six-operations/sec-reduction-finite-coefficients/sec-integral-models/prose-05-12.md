To deduce statements over $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ from statements over
$\mathcal O_E$, we need integral objects that generate $\Dbc(X,E)$ and
$\Dbc(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$, and we want to know that our integral constructible
complexes are those of the Stacks Project, so that results proved there
apply. The next lemma provides both.
Recall from
Proposition [sk-prop-integral-criterion] in Section [sec-n-constructible] that an object lies in
$\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)=D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathcal O_E)$ precisely when it is derived complete and its
reduction modulo $\pi_E$ is a bounded constructible complex pulled back
from $X_{\acute{e}t}$.
The Stacks Project works instead with the category $\Dc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ of
[Stacks, Tag 09C1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09C1), built from modules over the constant sheaf $\mathcal O_E$
rather than over $\mathcal O_{E,X}$.
