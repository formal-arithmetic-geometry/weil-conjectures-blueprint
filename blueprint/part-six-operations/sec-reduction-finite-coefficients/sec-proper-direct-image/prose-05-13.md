The construction of $Rf_!=Rp_*\circ j_!$ in
Section [sec-brown-construction] is made for every admissible coefficient
ring and starts from proper direct image on $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$. We therefore need to
know, for every such ring, that $Rp_*$ preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ and satisfies proper
base change and the projection formula there. For $Y=\operatorname{Spec}k$ with
$k$ algebraically closed, this says that the cohomology groups
$H^i(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ of a proper $X$ are finite-dimensional and vanish outside
a bounded range of degrees.

With finite coefficients these statements are classical, except for the
Tor bound, which we check in Proposition [sk-prop-ctf-finite-proper]. The
integral case reduces to them modulo $\pi_E$, and the cases $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$
follow from the integral one by means of the generators of
Lemma [sk-lem-integral-models](4).
