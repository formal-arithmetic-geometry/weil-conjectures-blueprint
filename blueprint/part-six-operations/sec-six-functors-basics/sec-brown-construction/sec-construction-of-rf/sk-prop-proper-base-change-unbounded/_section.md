Let $\Lambda$ be admissible. Consider a cartesian square

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
X'&\xrightarrow{g'}&X\\
\downarrow p'&&\downarrow p\\
Y'&\xrightarrow{g}&Y
\end{array}
$$

with $p$ proper. The base change map $g^*Rp_*K\to Rp'_*g'^*K$ is an isomorphism for every
$K\in\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$.
