title = "Proper direct image of unbounded complexes"

To apply Brown representability, we need $Rf_!$ to preserve arbitrary
direct sums. Since $j_!$ is a left adjoint, it does, and so the question
is whether $Rp_*$ preserves direct sums for proper $p$.
For complexes with a common lower and upper bound, this would follow from
finiteness of $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(p)$, but the summands of a direct
sum in $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$ need not have any common bound. As in the
proof of compactness, we use a uniform bound instead. Let $d$ bound
the fibre dimensions of $p$. We show that $R^qp_*$ vanishes on
$\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$ for $q>2d+1$, so that $\mathcal H^q(Rp_*K)$ depends
only on the cohomology of $K$ in degrees $[q-2d-1,q]$. We prove the bound on sections over
affine weakly contractible objects and then pass to colimits, using
the following continuity property of pro-étale cohomology.
