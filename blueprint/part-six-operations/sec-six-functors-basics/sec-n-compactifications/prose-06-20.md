Brown representability gives $f^!$ one morphism at a time. To compute it
in Section [sec-constructibility-upper-shriek] by factoring $f$ locally, we pass to
right adjoints in the isomorphisms of compositions of left adjoints, such
as $Rg_!\circ Rf_!\simeq R(gf)_!$ or $Rf_!=Rp_*\circ j_!$; by uniqueness
of adjoints these give isomorphisms of the right adjoints in the opposite
order, listed below. Since $j^!=j^*$ for an open immersion $j$, this
makes $f^!$ local on $X$ and on $Y$. For example, a smooth $f$ is
locally the composite of an étale map and a projection
$\mathbf A^n_Y\to Y$, and by composition and locality $f^!$ is then
determined by its values on these two kinds of maps.

Base change for $f^!$ does not follow in this way. Passing to right
adjoints in the base change isomorphism $v^*Rf_!\simeq Rf'_!u^*$ relates
$f^!Rv_*$ and $Ru_*f'^!$, which involve direct images rather than the
inverse images $u^*f^!$ and $f'^!v^*$ that one wants to compare. We prove
base change for smooth morphisms in
Proposition [sk-prop-cohsm]. That
$f^!$ preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ is proved in
Theorems [sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation] and [sk-thm-restriction].
