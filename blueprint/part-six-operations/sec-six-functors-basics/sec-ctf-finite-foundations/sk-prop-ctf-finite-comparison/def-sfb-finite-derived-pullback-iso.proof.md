$g^*$ is exact ([lem-sfb-finite-upper-star-exact]), so it preserves quasi-isomorphisms and
induces $D(g^*)$ with $D(g^*)\circ Q\cong Q\circ g^*$ on complexes. This exhibits $D(g^*)$
as a left derived functor of $g^*$. Uniqueness of left derived functors then gives
$L(g^*)\cong D(g^*)$; concretely, the comparison map
$L(g^*)(QK)\to Q(g^*K)$ is an isomorphism for every complex $K$, not only for K-flat ones.
