In the long exact cohomology sequence of a triangle, each cohomology
sheaf of the third term is an extension of a kernel by a cokernel of maps
between cohomology sheaves of the other two, so
Proposition [sk-prop-ind-heart] implies closure under cones. As direct
sums of sheaves are exact, $H^n$ commutes with direct sums, and truncation only
removes cohomology sheaves. Hence $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ is a localizing
subcategory of $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ containing $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$, and
it therefore contains the smallest one, $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$.
