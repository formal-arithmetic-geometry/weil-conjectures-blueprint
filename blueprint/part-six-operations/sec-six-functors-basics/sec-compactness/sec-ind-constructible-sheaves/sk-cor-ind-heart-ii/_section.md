Let $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$, with $d_C$ as in
Lemma [sk-lem-uniform-constructible-ext]. Then $\Ext^q(C,B)=0$ for all $q>d_C$
and all $B\in\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$, and each functor $\Ext^q(C,-)$
commutes with direct sums of objects of $\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$.
