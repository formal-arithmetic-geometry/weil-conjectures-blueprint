For the maps of coefficient rings listed above, extension of scalars sends
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ into $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda')$.
For $f:X\to Y$, the canonical change-of-coefficients maps for
$f^*$, $Rf_*$, $Rf_!$, $f^!$, tensor product and internal Hom, such as
$(Rf_*K)_{\Lambda'}\to Rf_*(K_{\Lambda'})$ for
$K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$, are isomorphisms. They respect composition and the units,
counits and evaluation maps defining the comparisons.
