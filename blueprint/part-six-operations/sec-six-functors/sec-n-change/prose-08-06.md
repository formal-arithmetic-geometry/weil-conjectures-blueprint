Since Lemmas [sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit]
and [sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity] are proved before
the Ext bound and Brown representability, Proposition [sk-prop-qlbar-two-colimit]
may be used in the proof of Theorem [sk-thm-existence]. From now on we may
therefore pass freely between $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ and $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,E)$ for $E$
large enough.
