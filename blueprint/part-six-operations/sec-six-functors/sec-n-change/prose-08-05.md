Without finite Tor amplitude, the categories would not be preserved
under change of coefficients. For instance, if $X$ is a geometric point, the sheaf $\mathbf F_\ell$
belongs to $\Dbc(X,\mathbf Z/\ell^2)$ but not to
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathbf Z/\ell^2)$, and its extension of scalars
$\mathbf F_\ell\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathbf Z/\ell^2}\mathbf F_\ell$ is
unbounded. Thus $\Dbc$ is not preserved by arbitrary changes of finite
coefficient rings.

For coefficients in $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$,
Lemma [sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit] expresses $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$
in terms of the categories $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,E)$ for finite $E$. As a statement about
