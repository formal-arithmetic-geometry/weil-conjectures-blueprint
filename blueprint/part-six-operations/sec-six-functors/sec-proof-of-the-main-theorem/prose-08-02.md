Theorem [sk-thm-existence] was stated with the plan of its proof, and its
four parts have been proved in different places, and in slightly
different ways for each kind of coefficient ring. Parts (1) and (2)
were proved in Section [sec-six-functors-basics] for finite rings and fields
and in Section [sec-i-heart] for $\mathcal O_E$.
Part (3) is then Brown representability itself. Part (4) is the geometric
statement of the previous section; it used the adjunction for the
projective line and Deligne's theorem on $Rj_*$, and for finite rings also
the bound on Tor amplitude. The proof below collects the references case
by case.
