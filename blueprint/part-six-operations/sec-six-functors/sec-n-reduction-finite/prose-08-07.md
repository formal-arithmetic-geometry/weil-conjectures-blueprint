Many later theorems say that a canonical map is an isomorphism. They are
proved with finite coefficients and extended to all admissible rings by
the same three steps: reduction modulo $\pi_E$ and derived Nakayama for
$\mathcal O_E$, inverting $\ell$ on integral models for $E$, and descent to
a finite extension for $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. We state the argument once.

The integral step is where the topology of the coefficients enters, and
it needs derived completeness: $E$ itself is a nonzero
$\mathcal O_E$-module with $E/\pi_E E=0$. The hypotheses of the proposition only
ask that $F$, $G$ and $\theta$ commute with change of coefficients, which
Proposition [sk-prop-scalar-six] provides for everything built from the six
operations.
