The proposition applies also to some contravariant constructions after a
rewriting: for instance the double-dual map $K\to\mathbb D\mathbb DK$ is a
natural transformation of covariant exact functors. What does not reduce in this way is the construction of an adjoint or of
a map: the cycle class of the diagonal and the adjunction
$Rf_!\dashv f^*(d)[2d]$ for smooth $f$ must be built as single morphisms with $\mathcal O_E$-coefficients
(Sections [sec-ladic-summary] and [sec-sp-grp-ladic-summary]), and a
trace with values in $\Lambda$ is not a map of complexes.
