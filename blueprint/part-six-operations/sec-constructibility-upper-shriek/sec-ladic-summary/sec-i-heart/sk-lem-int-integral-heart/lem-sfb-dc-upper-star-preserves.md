Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring and let $f\colon X\to Y$ be a morphism of the
setting of [det-def-sfb-dc-setting]. If $K\in D(\Mod_{\Lambda_Y})$ is bounded constructible
([det-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]), then so is $Lf^*K\in D(\Mod_{\Lambda_X})$
([det-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull]).
