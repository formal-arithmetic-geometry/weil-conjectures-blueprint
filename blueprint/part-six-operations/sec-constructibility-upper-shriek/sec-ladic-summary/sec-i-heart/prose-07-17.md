For $\mathcal O_E$ we have not yet constructed $f^!$ at all: Brown
representability (Theorem [sk-prop-sf-existence]) needs integral
constructible complexes to be compact and $Rp_*$ to preserve direct sums,
and Section [sec-six-functors-basics] proved this only for finite rings and
fields. For $\mathcal O_E$ the rational compactness argument needs one
change.
Constructible sheaves are no longer locally free on strata
($\mathcal O_{E,X}/\pi_E$ is constructible), but since $\mathcal O_E$ is a
discrete valuation ring they have free resolutions of length one, and the
resulting Tor amplitude $[-1,0]$ is all the estimates use.
