Choose finite affine covers of $X$ and $Y$ and closed immersions
$U\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^d_V$; then $u^*f^!\simeq i^!p^!v^*$
(Proposition [sk-lem-sf-mate]), and being in $\Dbc$ is local
on $X$. For $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, the argument before
Example [sk-rem-closed-support] shows that $p^!$ and $i^!$ preserve $\Dbc$.
For $\mathcal O_E$ the same argument applies, with
Proposition [sk-prop-diagonal-tower] for the projective line,
Lemmas [sk-thm-sf-pf-adic] and [sk-lem-int-tensor] for the projection formula and
inverse image, and Propositions [sk-prop-open-coefficients]
and [sk-prop-proper-base-change-unbounded]; derived completeness is
local on $X$. The adjunction restricts because $Rf_!$ preserves $\Dbc$
(Proposition [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]).
