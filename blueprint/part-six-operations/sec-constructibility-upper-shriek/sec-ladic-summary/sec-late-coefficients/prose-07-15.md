With torsion coefficients, $Rq_*\dashv q^*(1)[2]$ is built from the cycle
class $\delta:\Delta_*\Lambda\to\Lambda(1)[2]$ of the diagonal of
$\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y$, the trace
$\operatorname{Tr}_q:Rq_*\Lambda(1)[2]\to\Lambda$ and two identities between
them (Proposition [sk-prop-geo-p1-adjunction]). The classes modulo
$\ell^n$ are morphisms in different derived categories, compatible under
reduction. Morphisms in a derived category are not computed levelwise, so
such a family need not give a morphism after $R\varprojlim$. We therefore
construct the class once, in the derived category of inverse systems of
sheaves, and then pass to the limit.
