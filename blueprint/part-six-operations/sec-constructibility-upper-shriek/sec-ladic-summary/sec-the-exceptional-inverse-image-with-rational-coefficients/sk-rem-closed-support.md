Let $X=\mathbf A^1_k$, $k$ algebraically closed, $Z$ the origin and
$U=\mathbf G_{m,k}$. Then $Rj_*\Lambda_U$ has $\mathcal H^0=\Lambda_X$ and
$\mathcal H^1=i_*\Lambda(-1)$, from the Kummer covers $t\mapsto t^{\ell^n}$.
The triangle gives $i^!\Lambda_X\simeq\Lambda(-1)[-2]$, as Theorem [sk-thm-purity-transport], applied to the structure maps of
$Z$ and $X$, predicts.
