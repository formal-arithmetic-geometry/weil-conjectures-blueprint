Let $A$ be a finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring. For a separated
morphism $f$ of finite type, Theorem [sk-prop-sf-existence] provides a right
adjoint $f^!$ of $Rf_!$ on $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,A))$, and we now show that $f^!$
preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,A)$ (Theorem [sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation]). By the
reduction at the start of this section and by the previous subsection, it
remains to show that direct image along an open immersion $j$ preserves
finite Tor amplitude. We shall see that the upper end of the Tor amplitude grows by the
$\ell$-cohomological dimension $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(j)$ of
Definition [sk-def-cd], which is finite for morphisms of finite type
over $k$.

Finite Tor amplitude cannot be checked on cohomology sheaves, since these
may have infinite Tor dimension over $A$ (for $A=\mathbf Z/\ell^2$, the
constant sheaf $\mathbf F_\ell$ already does). We therefore obtain the Tor
bounds below from flat models. By [Stacks, Tag 0F4M](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F4M), every
object of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ is represented by a bounded complex of
constructible flat $A$-sheaves, and on the strata of a finite
stratification such a model is locally a bounded complex of finite
projective $A$-modules.
