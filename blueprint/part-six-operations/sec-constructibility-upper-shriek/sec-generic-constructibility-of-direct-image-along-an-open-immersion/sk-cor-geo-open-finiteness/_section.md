Let $j:X\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion. Then

$$
Rj_*:\Dbc(X,\Lambda)\longrightarrow\Dbc(Y,\Lambda),
\tag{7.22}
$$

and for every $k$-scheme $T\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ of finite type and every
$K\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$, the base change map
$(Rj_*K)|_{Y_T}\to Rj_{T,*}(K|_{X_T})$
is an isomorphism.
