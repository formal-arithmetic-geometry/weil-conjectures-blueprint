Let $S$ be of finite type over the base field $k$.

1. For the projection $p_d:\mathbf A^d_S\to S$, the right adjoint
   $p_d^!$ of $Rp_{d!}:D_{\mathrm{class}}((\mathbf A^d_S)_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda) \to D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is $p_d^*(d)[2d]$, with unit and counit compatible
   with base change.
2. Let $f:X\to S$ be separated of finite type, let
   $N\in D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, and let $u:U\to X$ be separated, étale
   and of finite type. There is a canonical identification

$$
u^*f^!N=(fu)^!N.
\tag{7.10}
$$

It is compatible with units, counits, composition, and further
étale localization.
