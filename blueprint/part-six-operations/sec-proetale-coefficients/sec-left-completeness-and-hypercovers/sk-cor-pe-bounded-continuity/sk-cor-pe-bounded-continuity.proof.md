We use the same hypercover $W_\bullet$ for all $i$. By
Corollary [sk-cor-pe-sections], sections on each $W_r$ commute with
filtered colimits, and therefore so does the finite sum in each total degree
of (2.3). Since filtered colimits of modules are
exact, the first assertion follows. For direct images, we evaluate the
cohomology presheaves on affine objects $V$ of $Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$. Each
$X\times_YV$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated, so that the first
assertion applies, and sheafification commutes with colimits.
