The standard $t$-structure on $D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\mathcal R)$ is left complete:
for every complex $K$ the natural map

$$
K\longrightarrow R\varprojlim_{n\geq0}\tau_{\geq-n}K
$$

is an isomorphism.
