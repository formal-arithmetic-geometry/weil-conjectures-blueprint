For a single sheaf this is the cochain complex
$M(W_0)\to M(W_1)\to M(W_2)\to\cdots$, which may be nonzero in every
degree, so the hypercover gives no bound on cohomological dimension.
For unbounded $K$, left completeness
(Proposition [sk-prop-pe-left-complete]) and the fact that
$R\Gamma(U,-)$ commutes with derived inverse limits give
$R\Gamma(U,K)\simeq R\varprojlim_n R\Gamma(U,\tau_{\geq-n}K)$, and the hypercover complex
computes each term.
