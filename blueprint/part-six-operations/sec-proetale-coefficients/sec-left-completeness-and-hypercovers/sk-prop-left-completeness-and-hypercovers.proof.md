K-injective resolutions of arbitrary complexes exist in the
Grothendieck category of $\mathcal R$-modules [Stacks, Tag 079P](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/079P), which
defines both functors. As derived right adjoints they preserve products.
Since (2.1) is built from products, they also preserve
derived inverse limits [Stacks, Tag 0940](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0940).
