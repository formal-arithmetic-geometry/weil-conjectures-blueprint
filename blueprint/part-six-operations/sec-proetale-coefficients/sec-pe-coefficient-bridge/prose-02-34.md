The comparison theorems above concern discrete coefficients. Integral
and rational $\ell$-adic coefficients are defined directly on the
pro-étale site, and the discrete constant sheaves with the same
values have the wrong cohomology. What we need is that pro-étale
cohomology with coefficients $\mathbf Z_\ell$ is the classical
continuous cohomology, the derived limit of cohomology with coefficients
$\mathbf Z/\ell^n$. This is
Proposition [sk-prop-pe-continuous-cohomology]. Over a separably closed field the
pro-étale site is the site of profinite sets
(Proposition [sk-prop-pe-condensed-point]), so the completed coefficients
have all continuous functions as sections. Over a finite field the discrete and the completed coefficients
differ in first cohomology (Example [sk-rem-pe-finite-field-adic]).
