Let $\Omega$ be a separably closed field. The affine pro-étale
basis of $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$ is equivalent to the category
of profinite sets, with finite jointly surjective families as covers.
Consequently its topos is the topos of condensed sets. A discrete
ring $A$ defines the classical constant sheaf, while a profinite
or locally profinite coefficient ring defines its sheaf of continuous
functions on profinite sets.
