The first implication in (1) is immediate from the definition. For
the second, a filtered colimit of étale algebras is flat and has flat
multiplication map [Stacks, Tag 092A](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/092A). For (2), the multiplication map
$B\otimes_RB\to B$ is surjective, flat and of finite presentation, so
the diagonal is a flat closed immersion of finite presentation, hence
an open immersion. Thus $R\to B$ is flat, of finite presentation and
unramified, that is, étale.
