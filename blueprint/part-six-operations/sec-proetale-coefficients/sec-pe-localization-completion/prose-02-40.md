For derived complete $K$, derived global sections commute with the derived
limit, being a right adjoint, and therefore

$$
R\Gamma(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},K)
\simeq R\varprojlim_nR\Gamma(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},K_n).
$$

When the $K_n$ are classical bounded-below complexes, each term on the
right can be computed on the étale site by
Theorem [sk-prop-sf-comparison]. In this way
the cohomology of $K$ is computed by the normalized tower $(K_n)$.

In Section [sec-n-constructible] we define constructible complexes over
$\mathcal O_E$, and we show that for Noetherian $X$ they are exactly the
derived complete complexes $K$ whose reduction $K_1$ is constructible
(Proposition [sk-prop-integral-criterion]).

Derived complete complexes can equally be studied over the constant sheaf
with value the discrete ring $\mathcal O_E$
(Lemma [sk-lem-integral-models](1)).

Inverting $\ell$ and passing to the colimit over $E$, we get the rational
coefficient sheaves $E_X\simeq\mathcal O_{E,X}[1/\ell]$ and
$\QbarX\simeq\varinjlim_E E_X$ (Lemma [sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x]).
Section [sec-reduction-finite-coefficients] compares constructible complexes over
$E_X$ and $\QbarX$ with the classical adic categories, by lattices and by descent to a
finite extension.
