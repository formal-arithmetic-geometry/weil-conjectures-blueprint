A compact Hausdorff space $S$ is extremally disconnected if and only if
it is projective with respect to continuous surjections of compact
Hausdorff spaces. Every compact Hausdorff space $T$, in particular every
profinite space, admits a continuous surjection $S\to T$ with $S$ compact
Hausdorff and extremally disconnected; such $S$ is also profinite.
