For (1), both functors are left adjoint to restriction followed by
$\nu_{U,*}$, and this also identifies the adjunction maps
[Stacks, Tag 09AD](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09AD). As the functors are exact, the identity passes to derived
categories. In (2), the first identity is the comparison of inverse
images, and the second is Theorem [sk-thm-pe-direct-image-comparison].
Part (3) follows from (1) and Theorem [sk-thm-pe-direct-image-comparison]
for the proper map $\bar f$.
