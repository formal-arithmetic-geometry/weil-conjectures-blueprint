1. For an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, extension by zero
   commutes with comparison:

$$
\nu_X^*j_{\acute{e}t,!}\simeq j_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,!}\nu_U^*\quad\text{as functors }\operatorname{Mod}(U_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to \operatorname{Mod}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda).
\tag{2.10}
$$

The same identity holds as functors
$D(U_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$.
2. For a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$, we have
   $i^*\nu_X^*\simeq\nu_Z^*i^*$, and the corresponding identity holds for
   $Ri_*:D^+(Z_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ and its
   pro-étale counterpart. Hence the localization triangles for an open
   and a closed subscheme agree on the two sites.
3. Let $f:X\to Y$ be a separated morphism of finite type with a
   compactification $f=\bar f\circ j$, where $j$ is an open immersion and
   $\bar f$ is proper. Then the comparisons identify the functors
   $Rf_!:D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^+(Y_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$
   and $Rf_!:D^+(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^+(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$,
   both computed as $R\bar f_*j_!$.
