Étale cohomology of $\operatorname{Spec}k'$ is Galois cohomology
[Stacks, Tag 03QQ](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QQ). For algebraically closed $k'$ the Galois group is
trivial. For finite $k'$ it is $\widehat{\mathbf Z}$, which has
cohomological dimension one (see Serre, *Galois cohomology*,
II.§3).
