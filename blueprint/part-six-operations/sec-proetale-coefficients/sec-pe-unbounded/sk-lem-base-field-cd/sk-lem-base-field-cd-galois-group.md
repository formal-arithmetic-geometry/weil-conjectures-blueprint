Let $K$ be a finite field with $q$ elements and $K^{\rm sep}$ a separable
closure. For every $n\geq1$ there is exactly one subextension
$K\subseteq K_n\subseteq K^{\rm sep}$ of degree $n$, namely the set of roots of
$T^{q^n}-T$; it is Galois over $K$ with cyclic group of order $n$ generated by the
Frobenius $\phi(x)=x^q$. Consequently
$\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\rm sep}/K)\cong\varprojlim_n\mathbf Z/n=\widehat{\mathbf Z}$
as profinite groups, with $\phi$ corresponding to $1$.
