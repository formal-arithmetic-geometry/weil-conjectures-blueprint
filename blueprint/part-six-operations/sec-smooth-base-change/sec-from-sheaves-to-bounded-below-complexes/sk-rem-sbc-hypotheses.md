The hypothesis that one integer $n$ kills $\Lambda$ can be weakened.
The argument for sheaves allows torsion sections of varying orders,
provided their prime divisors are invertible on $Y$, because continuity of
cohomology for filtered colimits reduces us to sections killed by a single such
integer. The argument with spectral sequences then extends to bounded below
classical abelian complexes with this property. For a finite
admissible ring $A$ the hypothesis holds with $n=\ell^r$,
where $\ell^rA=0$.
