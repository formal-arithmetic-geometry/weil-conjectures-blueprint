In (9.1), assume that all schemes are of finite type over $k$
and that $g$ is smooth. For every admissible coefficient ring

$$
\Lambda\in\{A,\Zl,\mathcal O_E,\Ql,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}
$$

and every $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$, the
canonical map is an isomorphism

$$
g^*Rf_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf'_*g'^*K.
\tag{9.3}
$$

These isomorphisms respect pasting and all change-of-coefficients maps of
Proposition [sk-prop-scalar-six].
