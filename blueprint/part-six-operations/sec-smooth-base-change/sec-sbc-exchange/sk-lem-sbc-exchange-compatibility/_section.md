The maps (9.2) form a natural transformation
$g^*Rf_*\to Rf'_*g'^*$. It respects
composition of cartesian squares and is the identity for an identity
square. For a discrete ring $\Lambda$ and $K\in D^+(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$,
with $f$ and $f'$ qcqs, the comparison isomorphisms of
Theorem [sk-thm-pe-direct-image-comparison] identify the base change map of
$\nu_X^*K$ with the inverse image on $Y'_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$ of the étale
base change map of $K$. For a coefficient map $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ of
Proposition [sk-prop-scalar-six] and $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$, the
change-of-coefficients maps of that proposition identify the extension of scalars of
(9.2) with the base change map for $K_{\Lambda'}$.
