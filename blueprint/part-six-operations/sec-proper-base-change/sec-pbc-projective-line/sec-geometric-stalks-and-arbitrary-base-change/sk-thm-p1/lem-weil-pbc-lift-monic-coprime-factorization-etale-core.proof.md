Let $r=\deg g_0$ and $s=\deg h_0$, so $r+s=\deg f$. Let $P=A[c_0,\dots,c_{r-1},d_0,\dots,d_{s-1}]$, $g_u=X^r+\sum_ic_iX^i$, $h_u=X^s+\sum_jd_jX^j$, and let $B=P/\mathfrak J$, where $\mathfrak J$ is generated by the coefficients of $f-g_uh_u$. The coefficients of $g_0$ and $h_0$ define an $A$-algebra map $\tau:B\to A/I$. The Jacobian matrix of the coefficient map $(c,d)\mapsto$ (coefficients of $g_uh_u$) is the Sylvester matrix of $(g_u,h_u)$, so $A\to B$ is etale after inverting the resultant $\rho=\operatorname{Res}(g_u,h_u)$. Since $g_0,h_0$ are coprime, $\tau(\rho)=\operatorname{Res}(g_0,h_0)$ is a unit, so $\tau$ factors through the etale $A$-algebra $B_\rho$. The induced surjection $B_\rho/IB_\rho\to A/I$ is a map of etale $A/I$-algebras with a section, so its kernel is generated by an idempotent $1-\bar e$; for a lift $e\in B_\rho$ of $\bar e$, the localization $A'=(B_\rho)_e$ is etale over $A$ and $A/I\to A'/IA'$ is bijective. The images $g',h'$ of $g_u,h_u$ in $A'[X]$ are monic with $f=g'h'$, and $g'$ reduces to $g_0$ because $\tau$ sends the $c_i$ to the coefficients of $g_0$.
