Give S its R-module structure via φ and put J = IS. Then J^k = I^k S for every k, so S/J^k = S/(I^k S), and the J-adic completion B̂ of the ring S is the I-adic completion of the R-module S. The coordinate condition on ψ says that, under this identification, the Â-module structure on B̂ via ψ is the canonical action of the completed ring Â on the I-adic completion of the R-module S. Since K = IÂ is generated by the images of generators of I, K B̂ = I B̂ inside this completion.

Since I is finitely generated, for every R-module M with I-adic completion M̂ the projection M̂ → M/IM is surjective with kernel I M̂ (Stacks 05GG). Applied to M = S, and using K B̂ = I B̂, this gives an isomorphism B̂/K B̂ ≅ S/IS.

Applied to M = R, the first projection identifies Â/K with R/I. Under these two isomorphisms, the first-coordinate condition on ψ identifies the Â/K-action on B̂/K B̂ with the R/I-action on S/IS via the induced quotient homomorphism. Since S/IS is a finitely generated R/I-module, transporting a finite generating family across these isomorphisms shows that B̂/K B̂ is a finitely generated Â/K-module.
