*Step 1 (restriction to opens).* Throughout, a surjection of $\mathcal O_W$-modules means an epimorphism of the abelian category $\Mod(\mathcal O_W)$, and "of finite type" and "coherent" are as in [pqf-def-coherent]. For opens $V\subseteq W\subseteq X$ of the scheme $X$ [def-pc-scheme]: (a) $\mathcal O_X|_W=\mathcal O_W$ and $(\mathcal G|_W)|_V=\mathcal G|_V$ for every $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal G$, with $(\alpha|_W)|_V=\alpha|_V$ for morphisms: by the formula for restriction to an open subscheme (for inclusions of open subschemes $f(U)=U$ and $f^\sharp$ is the identity), both sides have sections $\mathcal G(V')$ over $V'\subseteq V$ open, the same restriction maps and the same $\mathcal O_X(V')$-action. (b) Restriction $\mathcal G\mapsto\mathcal G|_V$, $\Mod(\mathcal O_W)\to\Mod(\mathcal O_V)$, is a left adjoint, so it preserves coproducts, whence $(\mathcal O_W^{\oplus n})|_V\cong\mathcal O_V^{\oplus n}$ by (a); and it preserves epimorphisms: if $e$ is an epimorphism and $g\circ e|_V=h\circ e|_V$, the adjoint transposes satisfy $g^\flat\circ e=h^\flat\circ e$, so $g^\flat=h^\flat$ and $g=h$. (c) For a morphism $\varphi:\mathcal G\to\mathcal H$ of $\mathcal O_W$-modules, $\Ker(\varphi)|_V=\Ker(\varphi|_V)$: kernels in $\Mod(\mathcal O_W)$ and $\Mod(\mathcal O_V)$ are computed sectionwise, so both have sections $\ker(\varphi_{V'}:\mathcal G(V')\to\mathcal H(V'))$ over $V'\subseteq V$, with the same restriction maps and module structures. (d) Being of finite type is invariant under isomorphism (compose the surjection $\mathcal O_U^{\oplus n}\to\mathcal G|_U$ with the restricted isomorphism).

*Step 2 (finite type restricts).* Let $\mathcal G$ be an $\mathcal O_X$-module of finite type and $W\subseteq X$ open. For $x\in W$ choose by [pqf-def-coherent] an open $U\ni x$ of $X$ and a surjection $\mathcal O_U^{\oplus n}\to\mathcal G|_U$. Restricting it to the open $U\cap W$ gives by Step 1(a),(b) a surjection $\mathcal O_{U\cap W}^{\oplus n}\cong(\mathcal O_U^{\oplus n})|_{U\cap W}\to\mathcal G|_{U\cap W}=(\mathcal G|_W)|_{U\cap W}$, and $U\cap W$ is an open neighbourhood of $x$ in $W$. Hence $\mathcal G|_W$ is of finite type.

*Step 3 (part (1)).* Let $\mathcal F$ be coherent and $W\subseteq X$ open. $\mathcal F|_W$ is of finite type by Step 2. Let $W'\subseteq W$ be open and $\varphi:\mathcal O_{W'}^{\oplus n}\to(\mathcal F|_W)|_{W'}$ a morphism. By Step 1(a) $(\mathcal F|_W)|_{W'}=\mathcal F|_{W'}$, and $W'$ is open in $X$, so the kernel of $\varphi$ is of finite type by the kernel condition of [pqf-def-coherent] for $\mathcal F$. Hence $\mathcal F|_W$ is a coherent $\mathcal O_W$-module.

*Step 4 (finite type is local).* Let $W\subseteq X$ be open, $W=\bigcup_iW_i$ an open covering and $\mathcal G$ an $\mathcal O_W$-module with $\mathcal G|_{W_i}$ of finite type for every $i$. For $x\in W$ pick $i$ with $x\in W_i$; by [pqf-def-coherent] for $\mathcal G|_{W_i}$ there are an open $V\ni x$ of $W_i$ and a surjection $\mathcal O_V^{\oplus n}\to(\mathcal G|_{W_i})|_V=\mathcal G|_V$ (Step 1(a)). $V$ is open in $W$, so $\mathcal G$ is of finite type.

*Step 5 (part (2)).* Let $\mathcal F|_{U_i}$ be coherent for all $i$. $\mathcal F$ is of finite type by Step 4 (with $W=X$, $W_i=U_i$). Let $W\subseteq X$ be open and $\varphi:\mathcal O_W^{\oplus n}\to\mathcal F|_W$ a morphism with kernel $\mathcal K$. Put $W_i=W\cap U_i$, an open of $U_i$; these cover $W$. By Step 1, $\mathcal K|_{W_i}=\Ker(\varphi|_{W_i})$ and $\varphi|_{W_i}$ is, up to the isomorphism $\mathcal O_{W_i}^{\oplus n}\cong(\mathcal O_W^{\oplus n})|_{W_i}$, a morphism $\mathcal O_{W_i}^{\oplus n}\to\mathcal F|_{W_i}=(\mathcal F|_{U_i})|_{W_i}$; composing with an isomorphism of the source replaces the kernel by an isomorphic one. By the kernel condition of [pqf-def-coherent] for the coherent $\mathcal O_{U_i}$-module $\mathcal F|_{U_i}$ and its open $W_i$, this kernel is of finite type, hence so is $\mathcal K|_{W_i}$ (Step 1(d)). By Step 4, $\mathcal K$ is of finite type. So $\mathcal F$ satisfies both conditions of [pqf-def-coherent] and is coherent.
