*Step 1.* Put $I=\mathfrak m_RS$, a proper ideal since the map is local; the quotient $S\to S/I$ maps $\mathfrak m_S$ onto the maximal ideal of the closed fibre.

*Step 2.* Since $R\to S$ is flat, it satisfies going down, and $\mathfrak m_S$ lies over $\mathfrak m_R$. The dimension formula for flat local homomorphisms gives $\dim S=\dim R+\dim S/I$.

*Step 3.* Choose $x_1,\dots,x_a$ generating $\mathfrak m_R$ with $a=\dim R$ ($R$ regular) and $\bar y_1,\dots,\bar y_b$ generating $\mathfrak m_{S/I}$ with $b=\dim S/I$ (the closed fibre is regular). Lift the $\bar y_j$ to $y_j\in\mathfrak m_S$. The ideal generated by the images of the $x_i$ and the $y_j$ contains $I$ and maps onto $\mathfrak m_{S/I}$, hence equals $\mathfrak m_S$. So $\mathfrak m_S$ is generated by $a+b=\dim S$ elements.

*Step 4.* By Krull's height theorem $\mathfrak m_S$ needs at least $\dim S$ generators; so it is generated by exactly $\dim S$ elements and $S$ is regular. Empty generating sets are allowed.
