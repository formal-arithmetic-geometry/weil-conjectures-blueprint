title = "Restriction of injectives to the closed fibre"

Let $R$ be a henselian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ and
residue field $\kappa$, and let $i:\mathbf P^1_\kappa\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^1_R$ be the
inclusion of the closed fibre. For a sheaf $G$ on $\mathbf P^1_{R,\acute{e}t}$ write
$G_0=i^*G$. We will compare the cohomology of $\mathbf P^1_R$ with that of its closed fibre by
applying the comparison in degree zero of Theorem [sk-thm-pbc-henselian-h0]
to an injective resolution, once we know that the restrictions $I_0$
of injective sheaves are acyclic on $\mathbf P^1_\kappa$. This acyclicity is
Lemma [sk-lem-pbc-p1-injective-fibre]. In degrees at least two it follows
from Gabber's theorem on the henselizations of the two charts. In degree one we must show that
a class in $H^1(\mathbf P^1_\kappa,I_0)$ vanishes, and an injective
sheaf is too large to work with directly. We therefore approximate $I$
by constructible sheaves, embed these into direct images of constant
sheaves along finite morphisms, and use that over such direct images
degree-one classes are finite torsors on schemes proper over $R$, which
lift by fact (ii).

Three standard finiteness facts about small étale topoi of
quasi-compact quasi-separated schemes allow us to reduce questions about injective
sheaves to finite morphisms.
