*Spectrality.* $p$ is quasi-compact and separated, and $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is
quasi-compact, so $Y$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated; the underlying
space of a quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme is spectral
[def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions].

*Closedness.* $V(I)\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}A$ is closed and $p$ is
continuous, so $Y_0=p^{-1}(V(I))$ is closed.

*Connectedness.* This is input (ii) of [thm-weil-pbc-stein-inputs]: every
irreducible closed subset of $Y$ meets $Y_0$ in a nonempty connected subset.
