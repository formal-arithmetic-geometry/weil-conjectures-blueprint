We will show that $Rp_*K$ is again constructible when $K$ is constructible.
Once this is known, the completed inverse images in
Proposition [sk-prop-pbc-complete] agree with $g^*$ and $g'^*$, and the
proposition becomes the ordinary base change
isomorphism

$$
g^*Rp_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rp'_*g'^*K
\qquad(K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)).
$$

Proposition [sk-thm-sfb-proper-base-change] proves this finiteness,
using Theorem [sk-thm-properbasechange] to identify the
base change maps at finite levels, and then deduces the cases $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
For $E$ we reduce to the integral case by lattices on strata, since
inverting $\ell$ commutes with bounded below proper
direct image, and for $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we descend to a finite extension of $\Ql$.

The base change isomorphism $g^*Rp_*\simeq Rp'_*g'^*$ of functors
$\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y',\Lambda))$ is proved later, for every
admissible $\Lambda$, in Proposition [sk-prop-proper-base-change-unbounded],
by extending the isomorphism from the constructible generators once proper
direct image is known to preserve direct sums.
