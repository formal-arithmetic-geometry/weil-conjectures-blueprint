In the situation of Proposition [sk-prop-pbc-complete], suppose that
$g^*Rp_*K$ and $g'^*K$ are derived complete. Then the completed base
change isomorphism is the ordinary base change map
$g^*Rp_*K\to Rp'_*g'^*K$, which is therefore an isomorphism; see the
last paragraph of the proof.
