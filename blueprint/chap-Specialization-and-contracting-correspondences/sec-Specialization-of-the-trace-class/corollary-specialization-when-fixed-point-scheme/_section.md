If $F_{\mathrm{red}}=Y\times S$, with $Y$ separated
and of finite type over $k$, then the specialization $\operatorname{sp}_F$ of
Proposition [proposition-specialization-trace-class] is the identity under
the identifications for constant families and invariance under nilpotent
thickenings. In particular, it is injective.
