After taking tensor–Hom adjoints, we factor the ordinary
comparison map through $c_2=p_2c$ and the exceptional comparison map
through $c_1=p_1c$, and we apply (E.8) for
$f=c$.  What remains is the first equality of
(E.18) pulled back by $c_s^!$.
The formula for $k_{u_s}$ follows from
(E.19), the definition of $u_s$ and
$\mu(\sigma\otimes1)=1$.
