For a section $z:A_{F_\eta}\to\omega_{F_\eta}$ of the dualizing complex, its
specialization is

$$
\operatorname{sp}_F(z)=\theta_F \Psi_F(z)\sigma_F:
A_{F_s}\longrightarrow\omega_{F_s}.
$$
