Let $i_T:T\hookrightarrow Y$ be the finite closed reduced subscheme
with underlying set the support of $Q$, so that $Q\simeq i_{T*}i_T^*Q$.
Since the residue fields of the points of $T$ are finite and purely
inseparable over the separably closed base field, $T$ is étale
locally a finite disjoint union of geometric points, and
$R\Gamma(Y,Q)=\bigoplus_{t\in T}Q_{\bar t}$. Every stalk of $Q$
therefore vanishes, and $Q=0$.
