Write the two product maps in terms of the
tensor maps of $R\bar j_*$, commute proper direct image past these maps
on the generic fibres, and take the adjoints of the ordinary Künneth
arrows.  Both adjoints are
$f_\eta^*Rf_{\eta*}L\boxtimes
g_\eta^*Rg_{\eta*}M\to L\boxtimes M$, the tensor product of the two
counits. By the triangle identities of the adjunctions and
their compatibility with composition, the maps themselves agree.
