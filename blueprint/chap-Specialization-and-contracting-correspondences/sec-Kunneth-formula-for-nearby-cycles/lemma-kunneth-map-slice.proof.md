In the middle line $P$ acts trivially, because the action on this
complex of nearby cycles is the restriction of an action of $I$ and
$P$ is the kernel of $I'\to I$. By
(E.3), therefore, $(-)^P$ of the source of
$\Pi_{L',M'}$ is $(R\Psi_{X'/S'}L')^P\boxtimes R\Psi_{Y'/S'}M'$, the
restriction of the source of $\Pi_{L,M}$. By the compatibility of
(E.2) with the product maps obtained from
$\bar i^*R\bar j_*$, the map $(\Pi_{L',M'})^P$ is the restriction of
$\Pi_{L,M}$.
