In the slicing step we compare $\Pi$ with the Künneth map over the
trait $S'$ of (E.2). Let $X,Y$ be separated of
finite type over $S$, let $b:X\to\mathbf A^1_S$ be a morphism, and
put

$$
X'=X\times_{\mathbf A^1_S}S',\qquad Y'=Y\times_S S',\qquad
L'=L|_{X'_{\eta'}},\quad M'=M|_{Y'_{\eta'}}.
$$
