Let $X$ be flat, separated and of finite type over $S$, and let $x$ be
a closed point of $X_s$. Put $q=\dim_xX_s$. Then $q\leq\dim X_{\bar\eta}$,
and there are an open neighbourhood $V$ of $x$ in $X$ and a
quasi-finite morphism $b:V\to\mathbf A^q_S$. In particular
$\dim V_{\bar\eta}\leq q$.
