For every separated $S$-scheme $X$ of finite type and
$M\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_\eta,A)$, the map

$$
\delta_{X,M}:\Psi_X\mathbb D_{X_\eta}M
\longrightarrow\mathbb D_{X_s}(\Psi_XM)
$$

defined by (E.12) is an isomorphism.
