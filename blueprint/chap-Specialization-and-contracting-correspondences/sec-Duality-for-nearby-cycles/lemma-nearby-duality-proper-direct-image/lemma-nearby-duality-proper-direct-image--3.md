If
$X$ is proper over $S$, then under global Verdier duality on both fibres

$$
R\Gamma(X_s,\delta_{X,M})
\quad\text{is identified with the identity of}\quad
R\operatorname{Hom}_A(R\Gamma(X_{\bar\eta},M),A).
\tag{E.14}
$$
