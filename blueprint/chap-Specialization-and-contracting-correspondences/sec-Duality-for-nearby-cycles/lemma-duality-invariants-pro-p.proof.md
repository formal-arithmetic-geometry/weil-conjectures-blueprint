Let $G$ be a finite quotient of $P$. Since $G$ is a $p$-group, the
order $|G|$ is invertible in $A$, and the invariants are the image of
the idempotent $e=|G|^{-1}\sum_{g\in G}g$.  Verdier duality sends this
idempotent to the same averaging idempotent for the contragredient
action, since inversion permutes the summands. Hence the image of
$\mathbb D(e)$ is the dual of $\operatorname{im}e$, and the evaluation
pairing restricts as stated.  As invariants are exact, continuity gives
the assertion for the pro-$p$ group $P$.
