The argument is the one used for
Proposition [proposition-external-kunneth-formula-nearby-cycles].
As there, on a projective $X$ global duality shows that the cone of
$\delta$ has zero cohomology on $X_s$. Restriction to the generic point
of a coordinate line (E.2) and induction on
dimension show that the cone has finite support. For this we need two
compatibilities of $\delta$: with proper direct image, and with
restriction to the generic point of a coordinate line. In the second we
keep track of the twist $A(1)[2]$ on both fibres.
