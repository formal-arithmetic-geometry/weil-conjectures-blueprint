Let $f:X'\to X$ be a morphism of separated $k$-schemes of finite type,
let $Z'=f^{-1}Z$ schematically, and let $f_Z:Z'\to Z$ be the
restriction of $f$.  There are isomorphisms

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{X,Z}(Rf_*L')&\simeq Rf_{Z*}C_{X',Z'}(L')
&&(f\text{ proper}),
\end{aligned}
$$
