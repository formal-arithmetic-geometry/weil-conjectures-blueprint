The contracting argument needs a complex on the normal cone $N_ZX$ that
still remembers $K$ near $Z$. Pulling $K|_Z$ back along the projection
$N_ZX\to Z$ is not enough, since it forgets the directions leaving $Z$.
For example, take $X=\mathbf A^1$, $Z$ the origin, and $K=j_!A$ for
$j:\mathbf A^1\setminus\{0\}\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$. Then $K|_Z=0$,
so the pullback is zero. By contrast, the deformation to the normal cone is
$\operatorname{Spec}k[x,t,x/t]$ over the trait, that is, the constant
family $\mathbf A^1\times S$ in the coordinate $x'=x/t$, and on its
generic fibre $K$ is $j_!A$ for $x'\neq0$. Hence, by
Lemma [lemma-nearby-cycles-constant-family], the nearby cycles are $j_!A$ on
$N_ZX=\mathbf A^1$, which is not zero. This construction is the
specialization functor $\operatorname{Sp}_{X,Z}$ defined below, and by
Verdier's theorem
(Theorem [theorem-verdier-s-theorem-specialization-zero]) it still
recovers $K|_Z$ on the zero section.
Theorem [theorem-contracting-correspondences-local-terms] uses both
properties.

Let $X$ be a separated $k$-scheme of finite type, and let
$i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed subscheme with ideal $I$.
The deformation to its normal cone, over the chosen trait, is

$$
\mathcal D_ZX=
\operatorname{Spec}_{X\times S}
\bigl(\mathcal O_{X\times S}[I/t]\bigr).
$$

Here $\mathcal O_{X\times S}[I/t]$ is the base change to $S$ of the
subalgebra of $\mathcal O_X[t,t^{-1}]$ generated by $\mathcal O_X[t]$
and the fractions $a/t$ with $a\in I$.  Its generic fibre is $X_\eta$ and its special fibre is

$$
N_ZX=\operatorname{Spec}_Z
\left(\bigoplus_{r\geq0}I^r/I^{r+1}\right).
$$

The quotient by $I$ and all $a/t$ defines a closed immersion
$\widetilde z:Z\times S\hookrightarrow\mathcal D_ZX$, whose special
fibre $z:Z\hookrightarrow N_ZX$ is the zero section.  For $L$ on $X$,
let $L_\eta$ be its pullback to the generic fibre $X_\eta$ of
$\mathcal D_ZX$, and define the specialization functor
$\operatorname{Sp}_{X,Z}:D^b_c(X_{\acute et},A)\to
D^b_c((N_ZX)_{\acute et},A)$ by

$$
\operatorname{Sp}_{X,Z}(L)=R\Psi_{\mathcal D_ZX}(L_\eta).
$$

The base change map for pullback along $\widetilde z$, followed by the
inverse of the isomorphism of Lemma [lemma-nearby-cycles-constant-family], defines
a natural transformation

$$
\tau_{X,Z}(L):z^*\operatorname{Sp}_{X,Z}(L)
\longrightarrow R\Psi_{Z\times S}((i^*L)_\eta)
\xrightarrow{\sim}i^*L.
\tag{E.1}
$$

We use this map, in this direction, to specialize trace
classes.
