Near a generic point of a principal divisor in a normal scheme, the
pair $(X,Z)$ is a smooth pullback of $(\mathbf A^1,V(x^m))$, so by
Lemma [lemma-case-z-v-x-m] $C_{X,Z}$ vanishes there on constant
coefficients. The next lemma extends this to all sheaves that vanish on $Z$.
