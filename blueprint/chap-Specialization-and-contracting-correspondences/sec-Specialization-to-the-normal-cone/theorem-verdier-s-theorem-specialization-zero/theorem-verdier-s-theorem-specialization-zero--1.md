For every separated $k$-scheme $X$ of finite type, every closed subscheme
$Z\subset X$, and $K\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$, the
map (E.1)

$$
\tau_{X,Z}(K):z^*\operatorname{Sp}_{X,Z}(K)\longrightarrow i^*K
$$

is an isomorphism.
