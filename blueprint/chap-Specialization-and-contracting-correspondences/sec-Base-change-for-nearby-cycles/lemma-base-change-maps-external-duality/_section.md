Let $X$ be a separated $S$-scheme of finite type with projections
$p_1,p_2$ of $X\times_SX$, and let $M\in \href{@definition-constructible-sheaves}{D^b_c(X_\eta,A)}$. Then the
two maps
$p_{2s}^*\Psi_X\href{@definition-verdier-dual-evaluation}{\omega_{X_\eta}}\otimes^{\mathbf L} p_{1s}^*\Psi_XM\to \href{@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image}{p_{1s}^!}\Psi_XM$

$$
\beta_{p_1}\Psi(\kappa_M)\mu
(\alpha_{p_2}\otimes\alpha_{p_1})
=\kappa_{\Psi_XM}(p_{2s}^*\theta_X\otimes1)
\tag{E.10}
$$

are equal.
