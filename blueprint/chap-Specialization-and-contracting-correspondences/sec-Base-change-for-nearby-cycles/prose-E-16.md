Let $p_1,p_2$ be the projections of $X\times_SX$. On either
fibre, let $\xi:p_2^*\omega_X\to p_1^!A_X$ be the isomorphism of
Lemma [lemma-external-duality-trace-morphism] for $K=A_X$, and for a complex $M$ put
$\kappa_M=t_{p_1}(\xi\otimes1):p_2^*\omega_X\otimes^{\mathbf L} p_1^*M\to p_1^!M$, where
$t_{p_1}:p_1^!A_X\otimes^{\mathbf L} p_1^*M\to p_1^!M$ is the projection-formula mate of
the exceptional counit.

The next identity says that, under the base change maps, the map
$\kappa_M$ of external duality on the generic fibre corresponds to
$\kappa_{\Psi_XM}$ on the special fibre, up to $\theta_X$.
