For the last identity, let $c:C\to X\times_SX$ be a correspondence of
separated $S$-schemes of finite type, with fixed scheme $F$, closed
immersion $i:F\hookrightarrow C$, map $d:F\to X$, and closed diagonal
$\Delta:X\hookrightarrow X\times_SX$, so that $F=C\times_{X\times X}X$.
Let $\zeta:c^!R\Delta_*\xrightarrow\sim Ri_*d^!$ be the base change
isomorphism for exceptional inverse image and proper direct image in
this cartesian square, on either fibre.
