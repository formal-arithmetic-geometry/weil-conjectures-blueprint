Let $f:V\to W$ be a morphism of separated $S$-schemes of finite type,
and let $M,N\in D^b_c(W_\eta,A)$. Then the two maps
$\Psi_Vf_\eta^!M\otimes^{\mathbf L} f_s^*\Psi_WN\to f_s^!\Psi_W(M\otimes^{\mathbf L} N)$

$$
\beta_f \Psi_V(t_{f_\eta})\mu_V(1\otimes\alpha_f)
=f_s^!\mu_W\ t_{f_s}(\beta_f\otimes1)
\tag{E.8}
$$

are equal.
