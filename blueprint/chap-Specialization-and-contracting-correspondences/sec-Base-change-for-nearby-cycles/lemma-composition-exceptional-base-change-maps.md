Let $f:V\to W$ and $g:W\to Y$ be morphisms of separated $S$-schemes of
finite type. Then

$$
\beta_{gf}=f_s^!\beta_g\ \beta_f(g_\eta^!),
\qquad\theta_V=f_s^!\theta_W\ \beta_f(\omega_{W_\eta}).
\tag{E.9}
$$
