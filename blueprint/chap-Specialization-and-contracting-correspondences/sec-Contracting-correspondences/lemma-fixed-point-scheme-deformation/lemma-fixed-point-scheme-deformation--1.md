In the situation of Lemma [lemma-normal-cones-contracting-ideals],
suppose that $F$ is connected and that $d(F)$ meets $Z$. Then
$d^{-1}(Z)_{\mathrm{red}}=F_{\mathrm{red}}$, the reduction of
$\mathcal D_{d^{-1}(Z)}F$ is $(F_T)_{\mathrm{red}}$, and the map
$(\iota_F)_{\mathrm{red}}:(F_T)_{\mathrm{red}}\to\widetilde F_{\mathrm{red}}$
induced by (E.20) is an
isomorphism.
