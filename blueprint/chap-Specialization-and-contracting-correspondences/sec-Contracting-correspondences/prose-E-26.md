Let $c=(c_1,c_2):C\to X\times X$ be a correspondence, and let
$Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed subscheme. Suppose that the leg $c_2$,
along which we take ordinary pullback, contracts towards $Z$ faster
than $c_1$, as measured by powers of ideals. Then the trace class near
a connected component of the fixed point scheme meeting $Z$ can be
computed by restricting the correspondence to $Z$. With our convention
$u:c_2^*K\to c_1^!K$ for the legs, the leg that contracts is $c_2$; in
[varshavsky05] it is the first leg.

The contraction becomes geometric on the deformation to the normal cone
(Lemma [lemma-normal-cones-contracting-ideals] below). Let $I$ be the ideal
of $Z$ and $I_i=c_i^{-1}(I)\mathcal O_C$. Under the hypothesis
$I_2^n\subset I_1^{n+1}$, the special fibre of $c_2$ maps into the zero
section, while the inverse image of the zero section under $c_1$ is the
zero section. On the special fibre the trace class therefore only sees $Z$.
