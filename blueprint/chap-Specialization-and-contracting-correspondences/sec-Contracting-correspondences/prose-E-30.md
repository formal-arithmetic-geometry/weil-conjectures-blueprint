For $Z$ a point, the theorem specializes to
Corollary [corollary-contracting-local-terms-point]. Under its
hypotheses the [local term](@definition-trace-class-local-terms) at $F_0$ is the [categorical trace](@definition-categorical-trace) of $u_Z$.
For $c=(\mathrm{id}_X,h)$ this is
[$\operatorname{tr}(u_{\bar y}\mid K_{\bar y})$](@definition-categorical-trace).
