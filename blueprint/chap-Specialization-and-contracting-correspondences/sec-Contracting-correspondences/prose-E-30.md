For $Z$ a point, the theorem specializes to
Corollary [corollary-contracting-local-terms-point]. Under its
hypotheses the local term at $F_0$ is the categorical trace of $u_Z$.
For $c=(\mathrm{id}_X,h)$ this is
$\operatorname{tr}(u_{\bar y}\mid K_{\bar y})$.
