*Shared context.* Let $c=(c_1,c_2):C\to X\times X$ be a correspondence and let $Z\hookrightarrow X$ have ideal $I$. Put $I_1=c_1^{-1}(I)\mathcal O_C$, $I_2=c_2^{-1}(I)\mathcal O_C$, and $Z'=V(I_1)$. Assume $I_2\subset I_1$ and $I_2^n\subset I_1^{n+1}$ for some $n\geq1$. Over $T=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]_{(t)}$, whose strict henselization is the trait $S$ used for nearby cycles, put $\mathcal D_ZX=\operatorname{Spec}_{X_T}\mathcal O_{X_T}[I/t]$, and define $\mathcal D_{Z'}C$ from $I_1$ in the same way. All clauses concern the same lifts, with special fibres $\gamma_i:N_{Z'}C\to N_ZX$.
