We read everything off from the graded algebras. The first leg sends
$I$ onto generators of $I_1$, so it identifies the ideals of the zero
sections. The second leg, on the other hand, sends $I$ into $I_2$, and by the hypothesis
$I_2^n\subset I_1^{n+1}$ the images of degree-one classes are
nilpotent in $\operatorname{gr}_{I_1}\mathcal O_C$.

The algebra of the normal cone is
$\operatorname{gr}_I\mathcal O_X=\bigoplus_{r\geq0}I^r/I^{r+1}$,
and the algebra of the deformation is the subalgebra of
$\mathcal O_{X_T}[t^{-1}]$ generated by
$\mathcal O_{X_T}$ and the fractions $a/t$, $a\in I$. Since
$c_i^{-1}(I)\mathcal O_C\subset I_1$ for both legs (for $i=2$ this is the
hypothesis $I_2\subset I_1$), the maps on generic fibres preserve these
subalgebras. This gives the lifts.

The zero section of $N_ZX$ is defined by the ideal of elements of
positive degree of $\operatorname{gr}_I\mathcal O_X$. Since $I_1$ is the
ideal generated by $c_1^{-1}(I)$, the images of elements of degree one
under the first leg generate $I_1/I_1^2$ as an $\mathcal O_{Z'}$-module, and
hence they generate the ideal of elements of positive degree of
$\operatorname{gr}_{I_1}\mathcal O_C$. Therefore the quotient by the
pullback of the ideal of the zero section is
$\mathcal O_C/I_1=\mathcal O_{Z'}$, which proves (1).

Under the second leg, a product of $n$ images of elements of degree
one is the class of an element of $I_2^n$ in $I_1^n/I_1^{n+1}$, and this
class is zero since $I_2^n\subset I_1^{n+1}$. The pullback of the
ideal of the zero section is therefore nilpotent. Its vanishing set is
all of $N_{Z'}C$, which means that $(N_{Z'}C)_{\mathrm{red}}\to N_ZX$
factors through the zero section. This proves (2).
