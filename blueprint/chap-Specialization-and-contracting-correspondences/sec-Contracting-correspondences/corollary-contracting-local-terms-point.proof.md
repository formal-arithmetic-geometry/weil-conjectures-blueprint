We apply Theorem [theorem-contracting-correspondences-local-terms] with $Z=\{y\}$
and $W$ the neighbourhood of the statement;
$F_0$ meets $Z$ because $d(z)=c_1(z)=y$.
Here $C_Z$ is the reduced point $z$ and $F_{0,Z}=F_0$, so that $c_Z$
is the identity correspondence of the point, with coefficient
$K_{\bar y}$, and by Definition [definition-trace-class-local-terms]
its local term is the categorical trace. If the first leg is the
identity, the base change map in the construction of $u_Z$ is the
identity, and hence the restricted morphism is the stalk map $u_{\bar y}$.
