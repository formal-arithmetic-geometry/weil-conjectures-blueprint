The hypothesis in the preceding lemma that $F$ is connected is no loss
of generality. Indeed, let $F_0\subset \href{@definition-correspondence}{\Fix(c)}$ be a connected component
that is open and closed. If we replace $C$ by an open neighbourhood of
$F_0$ on which the ideal inclusions hold, and then remove
$\href{@definition-correspondence}{\Fix(c)}\setminus F_0$, we obtain an open subscheme of $C$, with the same
$X$ and $K$, whose [fixed scheme](@definition-correspondence) is $F_0$. Restriction of [trace classes](@definition-trace-class-local-terms)
along this open immersion identifies the [trace class](@definition-trace-class-local-terms) of $u$ on $F_0$
with the full [trace class](@definition-trace-class-local-terms) of the restricted [correspondence](@definition-correspondence).
