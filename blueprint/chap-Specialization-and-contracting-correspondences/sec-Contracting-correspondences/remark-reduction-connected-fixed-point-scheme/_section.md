The hypothesis in the preceding lemma that $F$ is connected is no loss
of generality. Indeed, let $F_0\subset \Fix(c)$ be a connected component
that is open and closed. If we replace $C$ by an open neighbourhood of
$F_0$ on which the ideal inclusions hold, and then remove
$\Fix(c)\setminus F_0$, we obtain an open subscheme of $C$, with the same
$X$ and $K$, whose fixed scheme is $F_0$. Restriction of trace classes
along this open immersion identifies the trace class of $u$ on $F_0$
with the full trace class of the restricted correspondence.
