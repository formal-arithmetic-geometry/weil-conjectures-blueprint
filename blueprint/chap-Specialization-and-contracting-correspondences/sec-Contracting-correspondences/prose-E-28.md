Up to nilpotents, then, the fixed scheme of the deformed
correspondence is the constant family $F_T$. This is why the
specialization of trace classes can be inverted in the proof of
Theorem [theorem-contracting-correspondences-local-terms].
The hypothesis that $F$ is connected and meets $Z$ is needed. In
Example [example-contracting-squaring], the component $F=\{0\}$
gives the constant family $x'=0$. The fixed point $x=1$ does not meet
$Z$, and there the deformation gives only the generic-fibre point
$x'=1/t$, not the constant family $\{1\}_T$.
