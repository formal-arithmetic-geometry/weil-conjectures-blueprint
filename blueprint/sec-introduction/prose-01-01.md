These notes prove the Weil conjectures from the six operations on
$\ell$-adic sheaves. Weil expected the conjectures to follow from a
cohomology theory with a trace formula, duality and purity; the trace
formula and duality are consequences of the six operations, and of
these only $f^!$ needs a new idea.
