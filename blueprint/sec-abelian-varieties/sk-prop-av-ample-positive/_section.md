*Proposition (sketch).*

Let $k$ be algebraically closed, $C$ a proper curve over $k$ and
$\cL$ an ample invertible $\cO_C$-module. Then $\deg\cL>0$.

## Proof

On a proper curve an ample invertible module has positive degree, the
degree being the coefficient of the leading term of the Hilbert
polynomial ([sk-def-av-ample-degree]).
