Let $X$ and $Y$ be proper and geometrically integral over a field $k$, let $x\in X(k)$
and let $Z$ be a $k$-scheme. If two invertible modules $\mathcal M,\mathcal N$ on
$X\times_kY\times_kZ$ are isomorphic on $X\times_kY\times_kU$ for the members $U$ of an
open cover of $Z$, and are isomorphic on the slice $x\times Y\times Z$, then
$\mathcal M\simeq\mathcal N$.
