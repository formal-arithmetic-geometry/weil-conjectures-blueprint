Let $Z_0\subseteq Z$ be the set of $t$ such that $\mathcal M\simeq\mathcal N$ on
$X\times Y\times U$ for some open $U\ni t$. It is open by definition and contains $z$ by
[lem-av-cube-open]. It is also the set of $t$ over whose fibre $\mathcal M$ and
$\mathcal N$ become isomorphic: an isomorphism over $U\ni t$ restricts to the fibre, and
conversely [lem-av-cube-open] spreads an isomorphism on the fibre over $\kappa(t)$ to a
neighbourhood. Hence $Z_0$ is closed by [lem-av-seesaw-closed]. As $Z$ is connected,
$Z_0=Z$, and [lem-av-cube-globalise], together with the isomorphism on
$x\times Y\times Z$, gives $\mathcal M\simeq\mathcal N$. The tex statement is the case
$\mathcal N=\mathcal O$ with $Z$ of finite type.
