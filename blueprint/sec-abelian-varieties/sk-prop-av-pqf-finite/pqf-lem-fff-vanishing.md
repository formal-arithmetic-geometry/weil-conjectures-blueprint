Let $f:X\to Y$ be a proper morphism of schemes and $y\in Y$. Assume
that $Y$ is locally Noetherian and that $f^{-1}(\{y\})$ is finite.
Then for any coherent sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $X$ we have
$(R^pf_*\mathcal F)_y=0$ for all $p>0$.
