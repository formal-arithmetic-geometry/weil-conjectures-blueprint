Let $f:X\to S$ be a morphism of schemes, where $S$ is
locally Noetherian. The following are
equivalent:

1. **(1)** $f$ is
finite;
1. **(2)** $f$ is
proper with finite
fibres, in the sense of
[pqf-def-quasifinite].
