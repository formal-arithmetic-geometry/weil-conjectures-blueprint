The derived couple is well defined. The map $f'$ lands in $D'$: for
$x\in\ker d$ one has $g(f(x))=0$, so
$f(x)\in\ker g=\operatorname{im}\alpha=D'$. It vanishes on
$\operatorname{im}d$, since $f\circ g=0$. The map $g'$ lands in
$\ker d$, because $d(g(y'))=g\circ f\circ g\,(y')=0$; and it does not
depend on the choice of $y'$, because changing $y'$ by
$\ker\alpha=\operatorname{im}f$ changes $g(y')$ by an element of
$\operatorname{im}(g\circ f)=\operatorname{im}d$.
