Let $f:X\to Y$ be a
proper morphism of
schemes. For every $d\geq0$ the set of points $y\in Y$ such that the
fibre $X_y$ has dimension at least $d$ is closed in $Y$.
(*Stacks Project, More on Morphisms, Lemma 37.30.5, Tag
[0D4I](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0D4I).*)

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, More on Morphisms, Lemma 37.30.5, Tag [0D4I](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0D4I) (Section 37.30, Dimension of fibres): for a proper morphism the function $y\mapsto\dim X_y$ is upper semi-continuous, so the locus of points whose fibre has dimension at least a given bound is closed. No separate proof is given.
