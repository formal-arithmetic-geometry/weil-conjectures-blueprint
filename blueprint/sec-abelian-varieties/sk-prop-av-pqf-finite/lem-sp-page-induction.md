Let $\varphi:E\to E'$ be a morphism of spectral sequences
[def-sp-spectral-sequence]. If $\varphi$ is an isomorphism on the
$E_2$-page in every bidegree, then it is an isomorphism on all pages $E_r$,
$2\leq r\leq\infty$.
