*Definition (sketch).*

An *abelian variety* over $k$ is a group scheme $A$ over $k$
which is proper, smooth and connected over $k$; its *dimension*
is $g:=\dim A$. A group scheme with these three properties is also
called a *complete group variety*; that this class is exactly the
classical one is a quoted input, [sk-prop-av-char], and is not part
of this definition.
