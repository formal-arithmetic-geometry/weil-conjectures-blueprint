An abelian variety over $k$ is a smooth proper geometrically connected
$k$-group scheme. An isogeny of abelian varieties is a surjective
homomorphism with finite kernel. If $A$ is an abelian variety, $[n]$
denotes multiplication by the integer $n$, and $A[n]=\ker[n]$ for $n>0$.
