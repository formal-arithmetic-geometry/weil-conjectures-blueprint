*Definition (sketch).*

Let $A,B$ be abelian varieties over $k$. An *isogeny*
$f:A\to B$ is a surjective homomorphism of $k$-group schemes with
finite kernel; its *degree* is the order of the kernel,
$\deg f:=\lvert\ker f\rvert$ ([sk-def-av-group-scheme]). That $f$ is
then finite and flat and that the degree of the finite flat morphism
agrees with this number is a theorem, [sk-prop-av-isogeny-degree], and
is not part of this definition.
