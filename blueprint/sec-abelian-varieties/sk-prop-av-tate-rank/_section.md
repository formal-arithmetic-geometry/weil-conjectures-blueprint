Let $A$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$ over an algebraically
closed field $k$ and let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$ be a prime. Then

$$
T_\ell A\cong\Z_\ell^{2g},\qquad V_\ell A\cong\Q_\ell^{2g}.
$$
