Let $b\in A[\ell^n](\bar k)$. The morphism $[\ell]$ is finite and surjective
([lem-sc-isogeny-facts], [lem-sc-mult-n-isogeny]), so $b$, viewed as a $\bar k$-point
of $A$, lifts to a $\bar k$-point $a$ of $A$ with $\ell a=b$
([lem-sc-finite-flat-surj-points]). Then $\ell^{n+1}a=\ell^nb=0$, so
$a\in A[\ell^{n+1}](\bar k)$.
