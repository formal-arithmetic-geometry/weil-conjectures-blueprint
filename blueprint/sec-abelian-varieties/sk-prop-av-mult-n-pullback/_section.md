Let $A$ be an abelian variety over $k$, let $\cN$ be an invertible
$\cO_A$-module with $[-1]^*\cN\cong\cN$, and let $n\ge0$. Then, with
$[n]$ as in [sk-def-av-mult-n],

$$
[n]^*\cN\cong\cN^{\otimes n^2}.
$$
