Let $A$ be an abelian variety over a field $k$ with algebraic closure $\bar k$, and let
$\ell>0$ and $n\ge0$ be integers. Then multiplication by $\ell$ maps
$A[\ell^{n+1}](\bar k)$ onto $A[\ell^n](\bar k)$.
