Let $k$ be a field and let $X$ be a
smooth projective curve over
$k$. Then $X$ is a smooth proper *connected curve over $k$* in the sense of
[def-sc-curves]: $X$ is nonempty, connected, separated, of finite type over
$k$ and of dimension one, and smooth and proper over $k$.
