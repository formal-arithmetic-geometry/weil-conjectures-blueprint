Let $k$ be a field. A *variety over $k$* is a scheme $X$ over $k$
which is integral and whose structure morphism $X\to\operatorname{Spec}(k)$ is
separated and of finite type. A
*curve over $k$* is a scheme over $k$ which is a variety of
dimension $1$ ([def-pc-dimension]). This differs from the convention
of [def-sc-curves] (a separated scheme of finite type of dimension at
most one over an *algebraically closed* field).
