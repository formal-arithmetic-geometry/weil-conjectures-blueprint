The notions of *invertible module* (and its dual),
*tensor product of modules* and *Picard group* used below are
those already introduced in file 06:
Definition [def-sc-invertible-module]
([def-sc-invertible-module], with [lem-sc-invertible-dual])
and
Definition [def-sc-picard-group]
([def-sc-picard-group], with the group law of
[lem-sc-pic-group]). The basic properties that we will use are

- an
invertible module is closed under
duals and tensor products ([lem-pc-tensor-dual-invertible]);
- $\operatorname{Pic}(S)$ is trivial for $S=\operatorname{Spec}(k)$, $k$ a field
([lem-pc-pic-of-field]).
