Let $k$ be a field. A *smooth projective curve over $k$* is a
curve $X$ over $k$ such that $X\to\operatorname{Spec}(k)$ is
smooth and
projective. In Stacks Project,
Chapter 44 (Picard Schemes of Curves) the phrase *smooth projective
curve over $k$* is used in exactly this sense;
