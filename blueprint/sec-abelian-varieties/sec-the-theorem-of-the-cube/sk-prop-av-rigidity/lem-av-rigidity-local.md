Let $k$ be a field, let $X$ be proper, geometrically connected and geometrically reduced
over $k$, let $Y$ and $Z$ be $k$-schemes, and let $f:X\times_kY\to Z$ be a morphism of
$k$-schemes. If $f$ maps the fibre $\operatorname{pr}_2^{-1}(y)$ over a point $y\in Y$
to a single point, then there are an open neighbourhood $U$ of $y$ and a $k$-morphism
$g:U\to Z$ with $f=g\circ\operatorname{pr}_2$ on $X\times_kU$.
