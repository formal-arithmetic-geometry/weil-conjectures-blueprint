By [lem-av-seesaw-criterion] (2), the set in question is the set of $t$ with
$\dim H^0\bigl(P_t,(\mathcal M\otimes\mathcal N^\vee)_t\bigr)\ge1$ and
$\dim H^0\bigl(P_t,(\mathcal N\otimes\mathcal M^\vee)_t\bigr)\ge1$. For an
invertible, hence finitely presented and flat over $T$, module on $P$, the function
$t\mapsto\dim H^0(P_t,-)$ is upper semicontinuous
(Stacks [0BDN](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BDN)), so both conditions are
closed. For the second statement, the projection $X\times_kY\times_kZ\to Z$ is the base
change of $X\times_kY\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, which is proper, flat, of finite
presentation and geometrically integral (Stacks [05P3](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05P3)).
