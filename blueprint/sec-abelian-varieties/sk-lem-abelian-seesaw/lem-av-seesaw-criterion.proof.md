The fibre $P_t$ is proper and geometrically integral over $\kappa(t)$, so
$H^0(P_t,\mathcal O_{P_t})=\kappa(t)$ (Stacks [0BUG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BUG)), and the spaces
$H^0(P_t,-)$ of the invertible modules in question are finite-dimensional.
For (1), if $s\in H^0(P_t,\mathcal A_t)$ and $s'\in H^0(P_t,\mathcal B_t)$ are nonzero,
then $ss'$ is a nonzero section of $(\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal B)_t\simeq\mathcal O_{P_t}$,
since $P_t$ is integral; it is a nonzero constant, hence a unit, so $s$ vanishes
nowhere and trivializes $\mathcal A_t$. Conversely, if $\mathcal A_t$ is trivial so is
$\mathcal B_t\simeq\mathcal A_t^{-1}$, and both have the nonzero section $1$. Part (2) is
(1) applied to $\mathcal A=\mathcal M\otimes\mathcal N^\vee$ and
$\mathcal B=\mathcal N\otimes\mathcal M^\vee$, whose tensor product is trivial; and
$\mathcal M_t\simeq\mathcal N_t$ if and only if $\mathcal A_t$ is trivial.
