The automorphism $F^m-1$ of [sk-p-tate] remains invertible after extension of scalars
to a finite extension $E/\Q_\ell$ or to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, and the integral
construction extends in the same way to $\mathcal O_E$. These extensions of scalars agree
with the extension of the condensed coefficient sheaves on the
pro-étale site. The comparison with the classical computation goes
through the finite étale group schemes $A[\ell^n]$.
