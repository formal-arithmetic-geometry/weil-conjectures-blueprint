title = "Compatible torsion points form the Tate module"

Fix a prime $\ell$ invertible in $k$. Compatible systems of
$\ell$-power torsion points of $A$ form the Tate module $T_\ell A$, a
$\Zl$-module with an action of $\operatorname{Gal}(\bar k/k)$.
