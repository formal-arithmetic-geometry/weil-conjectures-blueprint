Let $D$ be a divisible abelian group, $\ell$ a prime and $r\ge0$ with
$\lvert D[\ell]\rvert=\ell^r$. Then $D[\ell^n]\cong(\Z/\ell^n\Z)^r$
for every $n\ge0$.
