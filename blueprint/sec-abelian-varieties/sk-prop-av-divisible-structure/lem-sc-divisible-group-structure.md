Let $D$ be a divisible abelian group, $\ell$ a prime and
$r\geq0$ an integer such that $D[\ell]$ has order $\ell^r$.
Then $D[\ell^n]\cong(\mathbf Z/\ell^n\mathbf Z)^r$ for every
$n\geq0$.
