Let $y\in Y(k)$. The fibre $X_y=X\times_Yy$ is nonempty
(surjectivity), finite and flat over $k$, hence a nonempty finite
$k$-scheme, which has a $k$-point by
[lem-sc-finite-nonempty-point]; such a point is an
$x\in X(k)$ with $f(x)=y$.
