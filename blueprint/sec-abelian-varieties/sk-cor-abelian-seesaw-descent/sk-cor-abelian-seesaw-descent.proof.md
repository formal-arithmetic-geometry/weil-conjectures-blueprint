The morphism $f$ is proper and flat with geometrically integral fibres, so
$f_*\mathcal O_{P\times T}=\mathcal O_T$ (Stacks [0E0L](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0E0L)).
On $P\times U_\alpha$ we have $\mathcal L\simeq\mathcal O$, so $f_*\mathcal L$ is invertible
on $U_\alpha$ and the adjunction map $f^*f_*\mathcal L\to\mathcal L$ is an
isomorphism there. Both properties are local on $T$.
