In the situation of [sk-lem-abelian-seesaw], if $\mathcal L$ is trivial on
$P\times U_\alpha$ for an open cover $(U_\alpha)$ of $T$, then
$\mathcal L\simeq f^*\mathcal M$ with $\mathcal M=f_*\mathcal L$ invertible.
