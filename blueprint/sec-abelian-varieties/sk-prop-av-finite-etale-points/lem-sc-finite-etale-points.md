Let $G$ be a finite etale group
scheme of order $m$ over an algebraically closed field $k$, and
$k'\supseteq k$ an extension of algebraically closed fields. Then

$$
|G(k)|=m,\qquad G(k)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }G(k').
$$
