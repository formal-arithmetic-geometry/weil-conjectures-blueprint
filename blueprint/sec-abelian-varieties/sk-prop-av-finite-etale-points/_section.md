Let $G$ be a finite étale group scheme of order $m$ over an
algebraically closed field $k$, and $k\subseteq k'$ an extension of
algebraically closed fields. Then

$$
\lvert G(k)\rvert=m,\qquad G(k)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }G(k').
$$
