For a finite abelian group $M$, an $M$-torsor on $Y_{\Et}$ is a finite locally
constant sheaf $P$ with an $M$-action such that $M\times P\to P\times_YP$ is
an isomorphism. Write

$$
 \operatorname{Tor}^1(Y,M)
$$

for the pointed set of isomorphism classes of $M$-torsors on $Y_{\Et}$.
