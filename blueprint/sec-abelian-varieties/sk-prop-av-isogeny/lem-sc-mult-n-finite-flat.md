Let $A$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$ over a field $k$
and let $d\geq1$. Then $[d]:A\to A$ of [def-sc-mult-n] is
finite, flat and locally free of rank $d^{2g}$, with finite kernel
$A[d]$ of order $d^{2g}$.
