In the geometric heart of [sk-lem-wft-curve-heart], and in the perverse
Weil heart over $k_0$, the simple objects are the irreducible punctual
objects and the objects $(j_*M)[1]$ with $M$ nonzero irreducible lisse
on a dense open; here irreducibility is taken in the geometric or in
the Weil category, respectively. These middle extensions have no
nonzero punctual subobject or quotient in the perverse heart.
