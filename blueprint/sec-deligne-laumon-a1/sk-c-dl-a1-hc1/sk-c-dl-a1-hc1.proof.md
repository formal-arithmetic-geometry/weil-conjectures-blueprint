At the rational origin $0'$ of the dual line the kernel is canonically
trivial, and so the Frobenius-equivariant stalk formula
([sk-thm-inversion]) gives

$$
 \bigl(\operatorname{FT}_\psi((j_*M)[1])\bigr)_{0'}
      =R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,j_*M)[2].
$$

Comparing with $(j'_*M')[1]$ ([sk-p-dl-a1-middle]), we identify its
part in degree $-1$:

$$
 H_c^1(\mathbf A^1,j_*M)
       \simeq(j'_*M')_{0'}
       =(M'_{\bar\eta})^{I_{0'}}.
$$

The last equality is the usual formula for the stalk of a middle
extension ([sk-i-real-boundary]); the inertia group is trivial when the
origin lies in $U'$. By boundary semicontinuity ([sk-p-real-semi]), the
weights of this stalk are at most the weight $w+1$ of $M'$
([sk-p-dl-a1-fourier-weight]). The same comparison shows that
$H_c^2(\mathbf A^1,j_*M)=0$.
