For a pure lisse Weil sheaf $F$ of weight $w$ on a smooth
geometrically connected affine curve $C_0$,

$$
 H_c^0(C,F)=0,\qquad
 \operatorname{wt}H_c^1(C,F)\le w+1,\qquad
 H_c^2(C,F)\text{ is pure of weight }w+2.
$$

There is no cohomology outside degrees $0,1,2$.
