It remains to remove the two hypotheses of
Theorem [sk-thm-dl-a1-upper], that the curve is an open of the affine
line and that the sheaf is unramified at infinity. Moving a point where
the sheaf is lisse to infinity removes the second, and the direct image
along a finite map to the line removes the first.
