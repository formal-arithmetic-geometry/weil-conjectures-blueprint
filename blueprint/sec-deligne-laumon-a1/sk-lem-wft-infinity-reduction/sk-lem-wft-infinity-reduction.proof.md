An extension of degree $m$ leaves the geometric scheme and its
cohomology unchanged and replaces Frobenius by $F_q^m$, and the
absolute-value inequality for $\alpha^m$ relative to $q^m$
is equivalent to that for $\alpha$ relative to $q$.
For the second assertion, choose a closed point of $W_0$ and a finite
extension of the constant field over which it has a rational point
$s$. Some element of $\mathrm{PGL}_2$ sends $s$ to infinity, and we
transport $F|_{W\setminus\{s\}}$ by this automorphism. Since $F$ is
lisse at $s$, its local inertia there is trivial, and so the
transported sheaf extends to a lisse sheaf across the new infinity.
The surjection follows from localization
([sk-prop-sf-open-closed-triangle]) and $H_c^1(\{s\},F_{\bar s})=0$.
Every construction is defined over the chosen finite field, so the map
commutes with its Frobenius.
