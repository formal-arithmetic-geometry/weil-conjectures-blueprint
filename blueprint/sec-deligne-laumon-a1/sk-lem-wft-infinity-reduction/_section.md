A bound of the form "$H^r_c$ has weights at most $w+r$" that holds
after a finite extension of the constant field already holds over
$k_0$. Let $F$ be a
pure lisse sheaf on a dense open $W_0$ of a projective line. After
such an extension, we may delete one point where $F$ is lisse and
make it the new point at infinity. This gives a dense open $V_0$ of an
affine line, a pure lisse sheaf $G$ on $V_0$ unramified at the new
infinity, and a Frobenius-equivariant surjection

$$
 H_c^1(V,G)\twoheadrightarrow H_c^1(W,F).
$$
