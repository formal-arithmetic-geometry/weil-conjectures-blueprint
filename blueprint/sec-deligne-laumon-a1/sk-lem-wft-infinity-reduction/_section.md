A finite extension of the constant field reflects all normalized
weight bounds on compact-support cohomology: a bound that holds after
the extension holds before it. Let $F$ be a
pure lisse sheaf on a dense open $W_0$ of a projective line. After
such an extension, we may delete one point where $F$ is lisse and
make it the new point at infinity. This gives a dense open $V_0$ of an
affine line, a pure lisse sheaf $E$ on $V_0$ unramified at the new
infinity, and a Frobenius-equivariant surjection

$$
 H_c^1(V,E)\twoheadrightarrow H_c^1(W,F).
$$
