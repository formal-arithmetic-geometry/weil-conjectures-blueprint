With the heart in hand, we show that $\operatorname{FT}_\psi$ is
t-exact for the perverse t-structure on the line
(Proposition [sk-p-dl-a1-perverse]). By the stalk formula in
[sk-thm-inversion], this comes down to the compactly
supported cohomology of sheaves on an affine curve in the extreme
degrees zero and two. In the last subsection we combine the exactness
with the list of simple objects to determine the transform of a middle
extension.
