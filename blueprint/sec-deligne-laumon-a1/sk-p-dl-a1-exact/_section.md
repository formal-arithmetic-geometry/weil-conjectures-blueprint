If $0\to F_1\to F\to F_2\to0$ is a short exact sequence of
lisse Weil sheaves on $U_0$, with $F_1,F_2$ pure of the same
weight $w$, then

$$
 0\longrightarrow j_*F_1\longrightarrow j_*F
       \longrightarrow j_*F_2\longrightarrow0
$$

is exact on a smooth curve $C_0$ containing $U_0$ as a dense open.
