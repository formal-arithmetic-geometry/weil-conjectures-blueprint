Left exactness of $j_*$ reduces the claim to the vanishing of the
Frobenius-equivariant connecting map $\delta:j_*F_2\to R^1j_*F_1$,
which is zero on $U$. At a missing point, the stalk of the source has
weights at most $w$ by boundary semicontinuity ([sk-p-real-semi]).
Applying the same result to the dual $F_1^\vee$, which is pure of
weight $-w$, and using [sk-i-dl-a1-local], which identifies the stalk
of the target with the dual of the stalk of $j_*F_1^\vee$ twisted by
$(-1)$, we see that the target has weights at least $w+2$. The image
of $\delta$ is a quotient of the source and a submodule of the target,
so its eigenvalues would have weights both at most $w$ and at least
$w+2$. Hence $\delta$ vanishes at each missing point, and $\delta=0$.
