The normalized sheaf $\mathcal F^0$ of
[def-wf-normalized-real-envelope] is a direct summand of its real
envelope; in particular it is $\iota$-realizable in the sense of
[def-wf-iota-real].

## Proof

The first projection and the first inclusion split the direct sum in
[def-wf-normalized-real-envelope]. The ambient sum is
$\iota$-real by [lem-wf-envelope-real], so the definition of
$\iota$-realizability in [def-wf-iota-real] applies.
