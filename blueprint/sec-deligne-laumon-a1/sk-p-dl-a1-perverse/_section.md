On the affine line over $k_0$, $\operatorname{FT}_\psi$ is t-exact for
the perverse t-structure on Weil complexes, whose classes are those of
[sk-lem-wft-curve-heart] imposed on the Weil cohomology sheaves and
their supports, and it induces an exact equivalence of the categories
of perverse Weil sheaves, with inverse $\operatorname{FT}_{\psi^{-1}}(-)(1)$.
