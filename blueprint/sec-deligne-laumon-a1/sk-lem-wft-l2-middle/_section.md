For a nonzero pure lisse $M$ of weight $w$ on a dense open of a
line, the $L^2$ growth parameter $\nu$ of [sk-def-wrp-l2-definition]
satisfies

$$
 \nu(j_*M)=\nu(M)=w.
$$
