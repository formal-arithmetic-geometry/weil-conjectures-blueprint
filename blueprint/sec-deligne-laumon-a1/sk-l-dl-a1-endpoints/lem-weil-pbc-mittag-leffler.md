title = "Mittag--Leffler vanishing"
order = 3986

+++
A tower of finite abelian groups satisfies the Mittag--Leffler condition and
has $\varprojlim{}^1=0$. Consequently, for a tower $(K_n)$ of complexes of
abelian groups whose cohomology groups $H^q(K_n)$ are all finite, the
Milnor sequence of [sk-prop-pe-milnor] reduces to isomorphisms
$H^q(R\varprojlim_nK_n)\xrightarrow{\sim}\varprojlim_nH^q(K_n)$.
