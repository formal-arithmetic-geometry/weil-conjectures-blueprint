Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow C_0$ be a dense open in a smooth
geometrically connected affine curve, let $F$ be lisse on $U_0$, and
put $V=F_{\bar\eta}$. Then there are canonical identifications

$$
 H_c^0(C,j_*F)=0,\qquad
 H_c^2(C,j_*F)=V_{\pi_1(U)}(-1).
$$

If $F$ is pure of weight $w$, the group in degree two is pure of
weight $w+2$ (it may be zero). For every constructible sheaf on an
affine curve, compact-support cohomology is concentrated in degrees
$0,1,2$.
