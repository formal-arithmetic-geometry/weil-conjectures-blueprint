Let $j:U_0\hookrightarrow C_0$ be a dense open immersion of
smooth geometrically connected curves, and let $F$ be a pure
lisse sheaf of weight $w$ on $U_0$. Then for every integer $r$,

$$
 \operatorname{wt}H_c^r(C,j_*F)\le w+r.
$$
