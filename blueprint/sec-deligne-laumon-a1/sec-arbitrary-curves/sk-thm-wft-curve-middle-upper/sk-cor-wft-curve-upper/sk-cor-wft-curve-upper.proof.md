Apply [sk-thm-wft-curve-middle-upper] to the identity open
immersion. If a smooth curve has several geometric components, they
are defined over a finite extension of the constant field; we apply
the result to each of them and conclude by taking the finite direct
sum and using [sk-lem-wft-infinity-reduction].
