The second equality is [sk-thm-wrp-l2-dictionary].
The sum of squared traces for $j_*M$ is the sum for $M$ plus a
nonnegative term $E_n$ coming from the finitely many boundary points.
By boundary semicontinuity ([sk-p-real-semi]),
$E_n\le D(\operatorname{rank}M)^2q^{nw}$, where $D$ is the sum of the
residue degrees of the missing points.
For a nonzero pure lisse sheaf the critical growth exponent is
$w+1$, whereas the added term has exponent at most $w$.
Since $E_n\ge0$, we get $\nu(j_*M)\ge w$, and dividing the two terms
by $q^{n(\beta+1)}$ for every $\beta>w$ gives the reverse bound.
