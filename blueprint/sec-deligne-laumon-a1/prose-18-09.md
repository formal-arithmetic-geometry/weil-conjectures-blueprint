Since the transform is an exact equivalence of the categories of
perverse Weil sheaves, it sends simple objects to simple objects, and
Lemma [sk-lem-wft-curve-simples] lists these. The only question is when
the transform of a middle extension is punctual, and the hypothesis
of the next proposition excludes this.
