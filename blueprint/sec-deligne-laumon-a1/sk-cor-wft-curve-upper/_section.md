For every smooth geometrically connected curve $C_0$ over $k_0$ and
every pure lisse Weil sheaf $F$ of weight $w$ on $C_0$,

$$
 \operatorname{wt}H_c^r(C,F)\le w+r
 \qquad(r\in\mathbf Z).
$$
