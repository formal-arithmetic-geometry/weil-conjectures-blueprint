If $\mathcal F$ is a Weil sheaf, punctually $\iota$-pure of weight
$w$, and $2\log_q|\iota(b)|=c$, then
$\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal B_b$ is punctually $\iota$-pure of weight
$w+c$.
