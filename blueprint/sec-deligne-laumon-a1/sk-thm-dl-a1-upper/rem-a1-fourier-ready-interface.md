title = "Scope of the curve reduction"
order = 3059
+++
The degree-one estimate for an irreducible factor identifies the Fourier
transform of the ordinary middle extension $(j_*\mathcal M)[1]$ with a
middle extension $(j'_*\mathcal M')[1]$ on the dual line, determines the
weight of $\mathcal M'$ through the extension by zero and Plancherel, and
reads off $H^1_c(\mathbf A^1,j_*\mathcal M)$ as the origin stalk of
$j'_*\mathcal M'$. The curve reduction then only needs finite-map
transport, geometric constituents after a constant extension, and the
surjection supplied by deleting a regular point; it performs no further
Fourier-theoretic identification.
