For a finite map $g:C_0\to\mathbf A^1_{k_0}$ of separated finite-type
$k_0$-schemes and a lisse Weil sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $C_0$,

$$
H_c^i(C,\mathcal F)\simeq H_c^i(\mathbf A^1,g_*\mathcal F)
$$

for every $i$.
