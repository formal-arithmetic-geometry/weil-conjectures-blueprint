For an extension of degree $m$, Frobenius becomes $F^m$
and the field cardinality becomes $q^m$. If $\alpha$ is an eigenvalue
of $F$ in the convention of [def-weil-frobenius-module-weights], then
$\alpha^m$ is an eigenvalue of $F^m$, so by hypothesis
$|\iota(\alpha)|^{2m}=|\iota(\alpha^m)|^2\leq(q^m)^b=q^{mb}$.
Taking $m$-th roots gives $|\iota(\alpha)|^2\leq q^b$.
