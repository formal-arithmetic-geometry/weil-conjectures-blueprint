Let $v$ be the weight of $M'$ given by [sk-p-dl-a1-some-weight]. Since
shifts do not change the squared absolute values of traces
([sk-def-wrp-l2-definition]), Plancherel ([sk-cor-plancherel]) and
[sk-lem-wft-l2-middle], applied to $M'$ and to $M$, give

$$
 v=\nu(j'_*M')
  =\nu(\operatorname{FT}_\psi((j_*M)[1]))
  =\nu((j_*M)[1])+1=w+1.
$$

Here the outer equalities $\nu(j'_*M')=\nu(M')=v$ and
$\nu(j_*M)=\nu(M)=w$ combine [sk-lem-wft-l2-middle] with the dictionary
[sk-thm-wrp-l2-dictionary] between the growth parameter and pure
weights.
