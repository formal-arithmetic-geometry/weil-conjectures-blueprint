A stalk is inverse image to a geometric point [lem-kun-stalk-as-pullback];
Leray holds for bounded-below complexes [lem-kun-leray-bdd], unboundedly
when the cohomological dimension is finite [lem-kun-leray-unbdd], and
through a finite morphism [lem-kun-leray-finite]; open immersions restrict
back [lem-kun-open-immersion-restriction]. Then the bookkeeping lemmas:
first cohomology of a bounded-below complex [lem-kun-minimal-cohomology],
vanishing detection [lem-kun-d-zero-cohomology], stalks of cohomology
sheaves [lem-kun-stalks-of-cohomology] and stalk faithfulness for derived
objects [lem-kun-stalk-faithful-derived]; closed subsets of closed points
are finite [lem-kun-finite-closed-subscheme]; étale morphisms are locally of
finite presentation [lem-kun-etale-lfp]; and global generation passes to
filtered colimits [lem-kun-colim-gbl-gen].
