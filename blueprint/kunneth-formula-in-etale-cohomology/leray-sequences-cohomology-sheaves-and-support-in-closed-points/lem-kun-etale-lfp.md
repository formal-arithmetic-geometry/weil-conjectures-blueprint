An étale morphism of schemes is locally of finite presentation; hence an
étale morphism $U\to X$ which is quasi-compact, with $X$ of finite type over
a field $K$, has $U$ of finite type over $K$.

## Proof

By the ambient scheme conventions, étale means locally of finite
presentation, flat and unramified; with $X$ finite type over $K$ and
$U\to X$ quasi-compact, $U\to K$ is quasi-compact and locally of finite
type, hence of finite type.
