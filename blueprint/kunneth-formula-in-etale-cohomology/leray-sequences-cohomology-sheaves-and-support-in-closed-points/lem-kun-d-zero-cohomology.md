For $E\in D(T_{\Et})$ one has $E=0$ if and only if $\mathcal H^q(E)=0$ for
all $q$.

## Proof

A complex in the localization defining the derived category is zero
precisely when it is acyclic, i.e. all its cohomology sheaves vanish.
