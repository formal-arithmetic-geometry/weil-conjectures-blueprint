This section decomposes Lemma 59.97.2 of the Stacks project
([Tag `0F14`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F14)), split into its two
parts according to the separation of conclusions: integer coefficients with
one bounded-below torsion factor [lem-kun-0F14a], and
$\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$-coefficients with unbounded complexes
[lem-kun-0F14b].
