*Finite morphisms and the étale site of a point* (the source's subsection
heading, flattened here).

Pushforward along a closed immersion is fully faithful with image the
sheaves supported on the closed subscheme [lem-kun-04CA]; closed points of a
finite-type scheme have finite residue fields, separably closed when the
base is [lem-kun-closed-point-field]. On a separably closed point the
global-sections functor *is* a stalk functor, so higher cohomology vanishes
[lem-kun-03QU-sep-closed], every sheaf is globally generated
[lem-kun-03QU-global-generation], and the cohomological dimension is zero
[lem-kun-cd-sep-closed-point].
