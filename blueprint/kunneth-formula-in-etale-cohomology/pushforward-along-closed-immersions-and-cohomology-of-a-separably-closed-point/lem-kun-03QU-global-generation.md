If $K$ is separably closed, every abelian sheaf $F$ on $\Spec(K)_{\Et}$ is
generated by its global sections in the sense of
[def-kun-global-generated].

## Proof

By the argument of [lem-kun-03QU-sep-closed] (every étale $K$-scheme is a
disjoint union of copies of $\Spec K$, and the identity point is terminal
among the pointed étale neighbourhoods of itself), the stalk of $F$ at every
geometric point equals $\Gamma(\Spec K_{\Et},F)$.

The evaluation morphism
$\bigoplus_{s\in\Gamma(\Spec K_{\Et},F)}\underline{\mathbf Z}\to F$ of
[def-kun-global-generated] therefore has, at every geometric point, the
stalk
$\bigoplus_{s\in\Gamma(\Spec K_{\Et},F)}\mathbf Z\to\Gamma(\Spec K_{\Et},F)$,
the canonical surjection; it is thus surjective on every geometric stalk,
hence an epimorphism of sheaves because the stalk functors are exact and
conservative ([lem-kun-global-generated-stalkwise]). Hence $F$ is globally
generated.
