In the notation of [def-kun-derived-tensor], every complex admits a
quasi-isomorphism from a K-flat complex: this is
[lem-kun-flat-resolution-existence] in general, while
[lem-kun-bounded-flat-replacement] gives an explicit functorial construction
in the bounded-above case.
