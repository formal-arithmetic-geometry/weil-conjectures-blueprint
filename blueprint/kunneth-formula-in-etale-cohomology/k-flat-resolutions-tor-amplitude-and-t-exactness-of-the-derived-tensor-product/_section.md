K-flatness criteria [lem-kun-kflat-criteria] and a flatness criterion for a
cokernel [lem-kun-tor-cokernel-flat] identify tor amplitude with the
existence of a flat K-flat model [lem-kun-0F1M] (pointer:
[rem-ptr-kun-tor-amplitude-6]). Cohomology of unbounded complexes
[lem-kun-0BKZ]; the derived tensor product preserves direct sums
[lem-kun-derived-tensor-direct-sums]; every object is recovered from its
truncations [lem-kun-hocolim-truncations], sequential colimits represent
homotopy colimits [lem-kun-complex-colimit-hocolim], and exact
coproduct-preserving functors preserve them
[lem-kun-functor-preserves-hocolim]. The functorial bounded-above flat
replacement [lem-kun-bounded-flat-replacement] then yields flat K-flat
resolutions in general [lem-kun-flat-resolution-existence] (pointer:
[rem-ptr-kun-derived-tensor-4]).

Finally the t-exactness statements — left t-exactness of derived direct
image [lem-kun-tensor-t-est] and connectivity of the derived tensor product
[lem-kun-derived-tensor-t-est] — together with two geometric facts used
later: dimension under extension and restriction
[lem-kun-dimension-extension] and geometric reducedness over a perfect field
[lem-kun-geometric-reduced-perfect].
