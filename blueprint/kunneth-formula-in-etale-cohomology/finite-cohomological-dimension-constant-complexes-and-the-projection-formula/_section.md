*Cohomological dimension and projection formulas* (the source's subsection
heading, flattened here).

Finite-type morphisms over a field have finite cohomological dimension
[lem-kun-0F10], so their direct images commute with direct sums
[lem-kun-0F11] and with tensoring by a constant complex [lem-kun-0F12]. The
derived inverse image is monoidal [lem-kun-inverse-image-monoidal], which
defines the projection-formula morphism
[def-kun-projection-formula-map]; it is an isomorphism for proper morphisms
[lem-kun-0F0G], and with integer coefficients [lem-kun-0F0E],
[lem-kun-0F0F]. Finally proper base change for unbounded
$\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$-complexes [lem-kun-0F0C].
