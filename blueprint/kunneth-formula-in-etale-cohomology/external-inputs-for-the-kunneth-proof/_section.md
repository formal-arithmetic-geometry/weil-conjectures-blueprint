The results of this section are inputs for the decomposed proofs of the
remaining sections. Their statements are decomposed; in accordance with the
opt-in rule for proof decomposition, their proofs are intentionally omitted
whenever the result is cited from the Stacks Project, and then the Stacks
tag is displayed. Results already proved in the ambient chapters are
referenced directly.

direct images along limits of cartesian squares [lem-kun-0EZT]; a sheaf on
an affine inverse limit as a colimit [lem-kun-descent-limit]; descent of a
quasi-compact open [lem-kun-descent-limit-open]; and the commutation of
fibre products with inverse limits [lem-kun-fibre-product-limits].
