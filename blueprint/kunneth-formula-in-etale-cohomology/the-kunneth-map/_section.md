The relative cup product [def-kun-rel-cup] and the Kunneth map
[def-kun-kunneth-map] are defined by adjunction from the monoidality of the
inverse image. The Kunneth map is natural [lem-kun-kunneth-map-natural] and
compatible with the identifications attached to a cartesian square
[lem-kun-kunneth-map-compatible] (both recorded in
[rem-ptr-kun-kunneth-map-9]); it factors through the relative cup product
[lem-kun-kunneth-cup], and over a separably closed field with a proper factor
it is inverse to the composite of Leray, proper base change and the projection
formula [lem-kun-kunneth-map-agrees].
