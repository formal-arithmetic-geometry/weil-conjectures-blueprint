Projective morphisms [def-kun-projective] are proper
[lem-kun-projective-implies-proper]; properness survives base change and
composition [lem-kun-proper-stability], so a proper factor makes the second
projection of a product proper. The remaining monoidal constraints of the
derived tensor product: symmetry [lem-kun-tensor-sym] and unit
[lem-kun-tensor-unit], with a pointer collecting them
[rem-ptr-kun-derived-tensor-5]; and the inverse image of a constant sheaf is
constant [lem-kun-pullback-constant].
