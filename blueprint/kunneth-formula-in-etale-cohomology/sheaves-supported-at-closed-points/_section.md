This section decomposes Lemmas 59.97.3 and 59.97.4 of the Stacks project
([Tag `0F1F`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F1F),
[Tag `0F1G`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F1G)). A sheaf supported at
closed points is approximated by finitely generated subsheaves
[lem-kun-cp-approximation]; it has no higher cohomology
[lem-kun-0F1F-vanish] and is globally generated [lem-kun-0F1F-gen], whence a
complex with stalks only at closed points vanishes as soon as its cohomology
does [lem-kun-0F1G].
