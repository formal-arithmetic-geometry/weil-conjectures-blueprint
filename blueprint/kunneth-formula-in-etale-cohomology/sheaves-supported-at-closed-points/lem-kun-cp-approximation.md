Let $K$ be a separably closed field ([def-kun-separably-closed]), $X$ a
scheme of finite type over $K$, and $F$ an abelian sheaf on $X_{\Et}$ whose
support ([def-kun-support]) is contained in the set of closed points of $X$.
Then $F$ is the filtered colimit of its finitely generated subsheaves. Every
finitely generated subsheaf $G\subseteq F$ has support a finite set $Z$ of
closed points of $X$, and for $Z$ with its reduced closed subscheme structure
and $i:Z\to X$ the resulting closed immersion one has $G\cong i_*(G|_Z)$ in
the sense of [lem-kun-04CA].

(*Source:* this is the case of Stacks Project, Tag
[0F0N](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0N) used in this chapter, namely
the case in which the specialization-closed set $E$ is the set of closed
points of a scheme of finite type over a separably closed field; the
approximating sheaves are then supported on finite sets of closed points.)

## Proof

*Step 1 (filtered colimit of finitely generated subsheaves).* Let $I$ be
the set of subsheaves $G\subseteq F$ generated by finitely many sections,
ordered by inclusion. The system is filtered, the sum of two such subsheaves
being generated by the union of the two finite families of generators. Every
section $s\in F(U)$ lies in the subsheaf it generates, which belongs to $I$,
so the canonical map $\varinjlim_{G\in I}G\to F$ is an epimorphism, and it is
a monomorphism because the transition maps are the inclusions; hence it is an
isomorphism of sheaves.

*Step 2 (the support of a cyclic subsheaf is finite).* Let $s\in F(U)$ for an
etale $U\to X$ and let $\langle s\rangle\subseteq F$ be the subsheaf generated
by $s$. Its support ([def-kun-support]) is closed in $X$: by
[lem-kun-support-geometric-point-independent] membership in it may be tested
on any one chosen geometric point over $x$, and a point $x$ does not belong to
it exactly when the pullback of $s$ vanishes on some etale neighbourhood of
$x$, and those neighbourhoods form an open subset. The support of a subsheaf
is contained in the support of the ambient sheaf, because the stalk inclusion
$G_{\bar x}\hookrightarrow F_{\bar x}$ forces $G_{\bar x}=0$ whenever
$F_{\bar x}=0$. Hence the support of $\langle s\rangle$ is a closed subset of
$X$ all of whose points are closed, so it is a finite set of closed points by
[lem-kun-finite-closed-subscheme].

*Step 3 (finitely generated subsheaves).* A subsheaf generated by finitely
many sections $s_1,\dots,s_m$ is the sum of the cyclic subsheaves
$\langle s_j\rangle$, so its support is the union of their supports, which is
a finite set $Z$ of closed points by Step 2; in particular $G$ is supported on
$Z$, and therefore $G\cong i_*(G|_Z)$ for the closed immersion $i:Z\to X$ by
[lem-kun-04CA].
