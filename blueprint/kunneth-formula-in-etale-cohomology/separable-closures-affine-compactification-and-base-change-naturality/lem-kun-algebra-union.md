Every $K$-algebra $B$ is the filtered union of its finitely generated
$K$-subalgebras.

## Proof

For each finite subset $S\subseteq B$ let $K[S]\subseteq B$ be the
subalgebra generated by $S$; $K[S]$ is finitely generated, the family is
directed by inclusion, and $B=\bigcup_SK[S]$ since each element lies in the
subalgebra it generates.
