Pullback to a separable closure is conservative [lem-kun-sep-closure-conservative],
and closed points of a product are exactly the products of closed points
[lem-kun-closed-points-of-product]. An affine finite-type scheme embeds as a
dense open in a projective one [lem-kun-compactification-affine], and every
algebra is a filtered union of finite-type subalgebras
[lem-kun-algebra-union], so an algebraically closed fraction field is a
filtered colimit of localizations [lem-kun-colim-of-localizations]. The
section closes with the bookkeeping needed later: change of rings for the
derived tensor product [lem-kun-change-of-rings-tensor], base change for
open-closed immersions [lem-kun-base-change-open-closed], and the
naturality of the exchange morphism [lem-kun-bc-base-change],
[lem-kun-bc-map-on-cohomology] and of the projection formula
[lem-kun-proj-formula-natural].
