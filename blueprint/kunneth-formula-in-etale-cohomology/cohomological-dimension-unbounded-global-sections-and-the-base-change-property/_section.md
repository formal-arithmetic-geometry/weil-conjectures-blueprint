Vanishing of a higher direct image can be checked at a geometric point
[lem-kun-cd-stalk-criterion], which is the criterion behind cohomological
dimension [def-kun-cd]. Unbounded derived global sections
[def-kun-rgamma-unbounded] are independent of the K-injective resolution
[lem-kun-rgamma-unbounded-well-defined] and extend the bounded functor
[lem-kun-rgamma-unbounded-bounded]. The unbounded derived exchange morphism
[def-kun-unbounded-exchange] has two agreeing constructions
[lem-kun-unbounded-exchange-constructions-agree], is resolution-independent
[lem-kun-unbounded-exchange-resolution-independent] and natural
[lem-kun-unbounded-exchange-natural] (pointer:
[rem-ptr-kun-unbounded-exchange-7]), and extends the bounded one
[lem-kun-unbounded-exchange-agrees] (pointer:
[rem-ptr-kun-unbounded-exchange-8]). Finally the base-change predicate
[def-kun-BC-property].

*Note.* The source records here that the discrete-Galois-module formalism of
the Stacks Project (Tags 04JQ, 03QU) is deliberately **not** introduced: the
only facts about the étale site of the spectrum of a field that this chapter
uses are proved directly, from exactness and conservativity of geometric
stalks, without a Galois dictionary. This removes two imported inputs and
one imported definition from the chapter.
