Let $X$ be a scheme and $\Lambda$ a finite ring. The category of
constructible $\Lambda$-module sheaves on $X_{\Et}$ is closed under kernels,
cokernels, and extensions inside $\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$.

## Proof

This is the weak-Serre statement of the proper-base-change chapter,
restated in the notation of this chapter.
