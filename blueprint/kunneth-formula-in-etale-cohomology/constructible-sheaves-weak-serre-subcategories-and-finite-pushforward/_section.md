*Constructible sheaves* (the source's subsection heading, flattened here).

The constructible sheaves form a weak Serre subcategory
[lem-kun-03RZ] and generate every sheaf of modules under filtered colimits
[lem-kun-03SA]. Finite morphisms preserve constructibility and have no
higher direct images [lem-kun-095R], and constant sheaves with finite value
are constructible [lem-kun-constant-constructible].
