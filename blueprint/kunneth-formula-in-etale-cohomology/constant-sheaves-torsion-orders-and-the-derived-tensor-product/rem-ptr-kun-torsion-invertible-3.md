In the notation of [def-kun-torsion-invertible], a sheaf of
$\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$-modules is an abelian sheaf with a
$\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$-action; that for $n\in I_K$ every such sheaf has
orders invertible in $K$ is [lem-kun-Zn-sheaf-invertible-orders].
