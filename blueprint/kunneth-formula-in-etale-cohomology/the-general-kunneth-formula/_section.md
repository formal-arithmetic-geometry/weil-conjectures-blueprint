This section decomposes Lemma 59.97.9 of the Stacks project
([Tag `0F1P`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F1P)), the final goal of
this chapter. The Kunneth map is identified with the projection-formula
variant [lem-kun-kunneth-via-0F1N], which gives the Kunneth theorem
[thm-kun-0F1P] with no properness hypothesis, and its compactly supported
form [cor-kun-compact-kunneth]. The chapter closes with an inventory of the
imported inputs [rem-kun-input-inventory].
