*Standing conventions.* All schemes have a small étale site, written
$T_{\Et}$; sheaves are étale sheaves of abelian groups, or of
$\Lambda_T$-modules for a commutative coefficient ring $\Lambda$. Derived
categories are $D(T_{\Et},\Lambda)$, with $\Dplus$ and $D^b$ their
bounded-below and bounded subcategories; cohomology sheaves are
$\mathcal H^q$. For a scheme $T$, write
$D(T_{\Et})=D(T_{\Et},\mathbf Z)$. The (derived) direct and inverse images,
cartesian squares with their canonical $\theta_\square$, and the underived
and derived exchange morphisms $\Ex^0_\square$, $\Ex_\square$ are the
ambient ones; $\eta_u$, $\varepsilon_u$ are the unit and counit of
$u^{-1}\dashv Ru_*$. Stalks are geometric stalks; they are exact and jointly
conservative. Hypercohomology spectral sequences are the ambient
hypercohomology filtrations. Morphism-theoretic predicates are the ambient
ones. The fibre product $X\times_kY$ below is over $\Spec(k)$.

Separably closed fields [def-kun-separably-closed] and the two facts about
their finite extensions [lem-kun-sep-closed-finite-extension-purely-inseparable],
[lem-kun-sep-closed-finite-extension-sep-closed], with a pointer remark
[rem-ptr-kun-separably-closed-1]. Support [def-kun-support] is independent
of the chosen geometric point [lem-kun-support-geometric-point-independent];
dimension [def-kun-dimension] obeys the transcendence-degree formula
[lem-kun-dimension-formula] and is finite on a finite-type scheme
[lem-kun-dimension-finite-type].
