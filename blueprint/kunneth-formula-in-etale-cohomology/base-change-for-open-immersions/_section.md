This section decomposes Lemma 59.97.5 of the Stacks project
([Tag `0F1H`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F1H)), the geometric heart
of the general Kunneth formula: the ground field may be assumed separably
closed [lem-kun-0F1H-sep-reduction], the exchange morphism is the identity
through the Kunneth identification [lem-kun-bc-kunneth-compat], and the two
combine with a dimension induction to give the theorem [lem-kun-0F1H].
