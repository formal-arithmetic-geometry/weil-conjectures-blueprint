*Smooth and proper base change inputs* (the source's subsection heading,
flattened here).

Smooth base change passes to limits of smooth schemes [lem-kun-0F09]. Since
separable extensions are filtered colimits of smooth algebras
([lem-kun-fg-separable-smooth], [lem-kun-algebraic-separable-smooth],
[lem-kun-separable-smooth-colimit]), direct images commute with base change
along an arbitrary field extension [lem-kun-0F1C]. The remaining inputs are
the witness diagram for a failure of base change [lem-kun-0F1D], base change
for flat finitely presented morphisms with geometrically reduced fibres
[lem-kun-0EYS], the vanishing of positive-degree cohomology over a separably
closed field [lem-kun-0EZP], and the presentation of a strict henselization as
a limit of smaller-dimensional models [lem-kun-0F0U].
