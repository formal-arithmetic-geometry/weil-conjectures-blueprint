Two restriction lemmas -- for the derived direct image along an open
[lem-weil-pbc-derived-open-restriction] and for an open-and-closed section
[lem-weil-pbc-clopen-section-direct-image] -- set up the comparison map
attached to a proper morphism fixing an open
[def-weil-pbc-proper-open-comparison]. Its geometry is described by
[lem-weil-pbc-proper-open-p-proper],
[lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-from-composite],
[lem-weil-pbc-open-section-clopen] and [lem-weil-pbc-proper-open-section],
and the specified isomorphism $\theta_{u,K}$ is
[lem-weil-pbc-proper-open-identification] (both recorded in
[rem-ptr-weil-pbc-proper-open-comparison-1]).

The comparison map is then an isomorphism
[lem-weil-pbc-proper-open-comparison-iso], composes
[lem-weil-pbc-proper-open-comparison-composition] and transports to the
pro-etale site for complexes of etale origin
[lem-weil-pbc-proper-open-comparison-proetale]. Together these give
compactification coherence [prop-compactification-coherence-detailed], the
statement quoted throughout the blueprint as
[rem-ptr-exchange-projection-17].
