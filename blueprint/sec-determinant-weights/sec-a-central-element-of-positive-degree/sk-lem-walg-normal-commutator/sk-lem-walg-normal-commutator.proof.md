Multiplication and inversion are continuous for the Zariski topology,
so the closure of a subgroup is a subgroup. Since $\pi_1(U)$ is normal in $W(U_0)$,
conjugation by $\rho(W(U_0))$ preserves $H$.
The normalizer of a closed subgroup is closed, and so conjugation by
$G$ preserves $H$ as well. Since $W(U_0)/\pi_1(U)\simeq\mathbf Z$ is
abelian, commutators of elements of $\rho(W(U_0))$ lie in $\rho(\pi_1(U))$, and the
commutator map $G\times G\to\GL(V)$ is continuous, so the same
holds on the closures.
