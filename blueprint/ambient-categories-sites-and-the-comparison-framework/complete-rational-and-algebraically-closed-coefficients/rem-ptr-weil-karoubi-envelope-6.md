In the notation of [def-weil-karoubi-envelope], the following holds. That
this functor is fully faithful is
[lem-weil-karoubi-envelope-fully-faithful], and that the envelope is
idempotent complete is [lem-weil-karoubi-envelope-idempotent-complete].

(*Source note:* no Stacks Project entry states this form: the nearest entries
are Homological Algebra, Section 12.4 (Tag
[09SF](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SF), Karoubian categories),
Definition 12.4.1 (Tag [09SG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SG)) — a
preadditive category is *Karoubian* if every idempotent endomorphism of an
object of it has a kernel — and Lemma 12.4.2 (Tag
[09SH](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SH)), which shows this to be
equivalent to each of: every idempotent endomorphism has a cokernel, and
given an idempotent endomorphism $p:z\to z$ there exists a direct sum
decomposition $z=x\oplus y$ such that $p$ corresponds to the projection onto
$y$.)
