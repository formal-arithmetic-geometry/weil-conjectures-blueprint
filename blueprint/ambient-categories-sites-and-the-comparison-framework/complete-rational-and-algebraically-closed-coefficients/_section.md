Under the subsection heading *The Karoubi envelope and bounded t-structures*
in the source. The point of the first half is
[thm-weil-karoubian-bounded-t]: a triangulated category with a bounded
t-structure is already idempotent complete, so the Karoubi envelope
[def-weil-karoubi-envelope] — constructed here
[lem-weil-karoubi-envelope-fully-faithful],
[lem-weil-karoubi-envelope-idempotent-complete] and used as an auxiliary
model [lem-weil-envelope-tstructure] — is redundant for the coefficient
categories. That is what lets the rationalized and common-stage categories be
defined without formally adjoining idempotents. The second half fixes the
pro-etale site [def-weil-pbc-proetale-site] with its finite coefficients
[def-weil-pbc-proetale-modules] and derived operations
[def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations].
