nothing there rests on an unstated convention: the small etale site and its
sheaf and derived categories [etale-site-ambient-categories], the closure
properties of morphisms used throughout
[standard-closure-properties-of-morphisms], direct and inverse image with the
underived and derived exchange morphisms
[direct-and-inverse-image-and-the-exchange-morphism], the strict
henselization as a limit of pointed etale neighbourhoods
[pointed-etale-neighbourhoods-and-the-strict-henselization-limit], hypercover
descent at an inverse limit
[finite-hypercovers-and-descent-at-an-inverse-limit], the two predicates
$\mathrm{PBC}$ and $\mathrm{IA}$
[comparison-predicates-and-injective-acyclicity], the affine covers used to
compare the etale and pro-etale sites
[affine-covers-for-the-comparison-of-sites], the coefficient conventions with
Karoubi envelopes and bounded t-structures
[complete-rational-and-algebraically-closed-coefficients], the degree-zero
henselian comparisons [henselian-comparisons-in-degree-zero], the continuity
and acyclicity statements [continuity-exchange-and-the-acyclicity-criterion],
and derived completion [derived-completion-and-inverse-limits].

The sections are ordered as the proof of this chapter assembles its
statements: a section stands after the material it uses.

The two results the proper-base-change chapter uses most are the equivalence
of $\mathrm{PBC}$ with injective acyclicity
[prop-weil-pbc-property-iff-acyclicity] and the strict-henselization stalk
formula [prop-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-stalk-formula-detailed].

Nine of its identifiers already name objects imported earlier from the sketch
sources; those nine carry the suffix `-detailed` here so that the two
readings coexist.
