Restriction to the closed fibre is a bijection on global sections, for a
proper scheme over a henselian pair [lem-weil-pbc-etale-degree-zero] and in
the strictly henselian form used by the projective-line argument
[lem-weil-pbc-degree-zero-strict-henselian]. Both rest on the Stein
connectedness assembly [thm-weil-pbc-stein-connectedness], which is explicitly
conditional on the two classical inputs isolated in
[thm-weil-pbc-stein-inputs]; and on invariance of the etale site under a
universal homeomorphism [thm-weil-pbc-universal-homeomorphism-invariance],
which is also what identifies the geometric and closed fibres.
