The Picard functor is an fppf sheafification, and sheafification is
compatible with the passage to a slice of the site; this is the remark
following Definition 44.4.1 of the Stacks Project ([Tag
0B9K](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B9K)). We record it as a
statement [lem-pd-picard-base-change], because the whole chapter rests on
it, together with its consequence for the degree-zero part
[cor-pd-pic0-base-change].
