The fibrewise degree of an invertible module on a relative curve
[def-pd-fibrewise-degree] is locally constant, insensitive to twisting by a
pullback from the base, and compatible with base change
[lem-pd-degree-locally-constant]. It therefore glues to a morphism of fppf
sheaves $\deg:\operatorname{Pic}_{C/k}\to\underline{\mathbf Z}$, whose
kernel is the degree-zero part [def-pd-degree-morphism].
