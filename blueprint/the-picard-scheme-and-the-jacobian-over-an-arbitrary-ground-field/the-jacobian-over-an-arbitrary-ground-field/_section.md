Smoothness, properness and connectedness are fpqc-local on the target
[thm-pd-fpqc-local-properties], so the descended scheme is again an abelian
variety. Assembling the chapter gives the target theorem
[thm-pd-jacobian-general-field]: a Jacobian over any ground field, with no
$k$-rational point of $C$ assumed. A closing remark compares this with the
separably closed statement [rem-pd-scope].
