Because the Picard functor is defined over $k$ and its formation commutes
with base change, the two pullbacks of $J_s$ to $\Spec(k_s\otimes_kk_s)$
represent the *same* functor. Uniqueness of representing objects then hands
over a descent datum [lem-pd-canonical-descent-datum] — canonically, with no
Galois action chosen — and the same uniqueness makes the cocycle condition
automatic [lem-pd-descent-cocycle]. The vocabulary is fixed in
[def-pd-descent-datum].
