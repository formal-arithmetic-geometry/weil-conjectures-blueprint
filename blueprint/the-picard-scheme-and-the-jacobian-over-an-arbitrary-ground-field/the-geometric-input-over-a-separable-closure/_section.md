Fix a separable closure [def-pd-separable-closure]. The base-changed curve is
again a geometrically integral smooth projective curve of the same genus
[lem-pd-geometric-curve], so the Jacobian chapter applies and
$\operatorname{Pic}^0$ is represented over $k_s$ by an abelian variety
[thm-pd-geometric-pic0]. This is the only geometric input of the chapter;
everything after it is descent.
