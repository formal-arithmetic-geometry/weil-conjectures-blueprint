The descent datum of [lem-pd-canonical-descent-datum] lives over the infinite
cover $\Spec(k_s)\to\Spec(k)$. Effective descent is available for *finite
locally free* covers, so the datum has to be moved to a finite level; the
tool is the standard spreading-out lemma for finitely presented data at a
filtered limit of schemes [thm-pd-spreading-out], which puts the scheme, its
projective embedding, the descent isomorphism and the cocycle equation over a
finite separable subextension [cor-pd-finite-level].
