Along a surjective finite locally free cover, a descent datum on a projective
scheme is effective [thm-pd-effective-descent] — which is precisely why the
scheme was made projective at the finite level. Morphisms compatible with the
data descend [lem-pd-morphisms-descend], hence so does the group law
[lem-pd-group-scheme-descent].
