By P1 the kernel is lisse of rank one, hence constructible and of finite Tor
amplitude, so $p_1^*K\otimes^L\mathcal K_\psi\in\Dbc(V\times_SV^\vee,\Lambda)$.
With finite coefficients, the six-operations package prop:functor-package
shows that the derived pullback, the derived tensor product and the
compact-support direct image along the separated finite-type $p_2$ are exact
triangulated functors on their constructible and finite-Tor domains that
preserve bounded constructible objects. For $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E,E,\Qlb$,
where $\Dctf=\Dbc$, the same preservation is the stability of $\Dctf$ under
the condensed six operations ([sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability]). The shift $[r]$
preserves both conditions.
