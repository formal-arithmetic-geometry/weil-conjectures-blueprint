Let $p_2$ be of finite type and admit a compactification. If $\Lambda$ is a
finite commutative ring in which $p$ is invertible and whose exponent is
invertible on $S$, and $V'$ is locally of finite type over a field, then for
every $K\in\Dbc(V,\Lambda)$ one has $\FT_\psi(K)\in\Dbc(V',\Lambda)$. If
$\Lambda\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\Qlb\}$ and the schemes are of finite type over a
finite or algebraically closed field, then $\FT_\psi$ restricts to an exact
functor $\Dbc(V,\Lambda)\to\Dbc(V',\Lambda)$ of the bounded constructible
subcategories of the pro-étale derived categories.
