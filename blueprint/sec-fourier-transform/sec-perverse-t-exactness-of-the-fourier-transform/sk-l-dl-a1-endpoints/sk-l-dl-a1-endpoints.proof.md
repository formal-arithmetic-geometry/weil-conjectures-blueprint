The vanishing of $H^0_c$ was shown in the proof of
Proposition [sk-p-real-semi]. Sheaves with finite support
have compact-support cohomology only in degree zero, so localization
identifies the group in degree two with $H_c^2(U,F)$, and
Lemma [sk-lem-wrp-hc2] gives its value and its weight. For the range of
degrees, localization reduces a constructible sheaf to a lisse sheaf
on an open, where Lemma [sk-lem-wrp-hc2] applies, and a sheaf on the
finite boundary.
