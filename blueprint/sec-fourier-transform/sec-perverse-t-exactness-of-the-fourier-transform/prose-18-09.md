With the heart in hand, we show that $\operatorname{FT}_\psi$ is
t-exact for the perverse t-structure on the line
(Proposition [sk-p-dl-a1-perverse]). By the stalk formula
(18.2), this comes down to the compactly
supported cohomology of sheaves on an affine curve in the extreme
degrees zero and two. In the last subsection we combine the exactness
with the list of simple objects to determine the transform of a middle
extension.

Since the transform is an equivalence with a known inverse, it is
enough to show that it and its inverse carry ${}^pD^{\le0}$ into itself;
the other half then follows by orthogonality. For a skyscraper this
follows from the stalk formula: the transform of a skyscraper at $a$
is the lisse sheaf $\mathcal L_{\psi(ay)}$ shifted by one, which for
$a=0$ is the computation $\operatorname{FT}_\psi(\delta_0)=E[1]$ of
Proposition [sk-thm-inversion]. For $G[1]$ with $G$ a constructible sheaf, the transform lies in
ordinary degrees $-2,-1,0$, so the only thing to check is that its
degree $0$ part is punctual. Its stalk at $y$ is $H^2_c$, which up to
finitely many boundary points is the space of coinvariants of
geometric monodromy on $G\otimes\mathcal L_{\psi(yx)}$, and it is
nonzero only if $\mathcal L_{\psi(-yx)}$ is a rank-one quotient of the
generic representation of $G$. These characters are pairwise distinct,
so at most $\operatorname{rank}G$ points $y$ contribute. The first lemma
below records the facts about $H^0_c$ and $H^2_c$ on an affine curve
that this argument and the next section use.
