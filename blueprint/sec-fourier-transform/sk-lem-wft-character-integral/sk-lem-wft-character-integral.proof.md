From the cohomology of $\mathbf P^1$ and localization at the point at
infinity, we get $R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,E)=E(-1)[-2]$, identified by
the trace map (Appendix, [sk-prop-an]). Every translation acts
trivially on it, since it preserves the trace and the cohomology is
concentrated in degree two. Now consider the finite étale cover
$u\mapsto u^p-u$ of $\mathbf A^1$, whose total space is again an
affine line. The deck translations act trivially on its compactly
supported cohomology, so the summands for nontrivial characters
vanish. Thus $R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,\mathcal L_\Psi)=0$, and the same
holds after pulling back by any nonzero scalar (by the comparison of the two
models of $\mathcal L_\psi$ in [sk-lem-as-properties], this covers every
nontrivial $\psi$). For $\lambda\ne0$, we choose linear coordinates in which
$\lambda$ is the first coordinate and apply the Künneth isomorphism of
[sk-lem-wft-compact-kunneth]. For $\lambda=0$, we apply that
isomorphism $r$ times, and it identifies the top class with the
product of the traces. All these calculations take place with
coefficients in $E$ and commute with extension of scalars
([det-prop-sfb-finite-extension-compatibility]).
