Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p$, for a
linear form $\lambda$ on $\mathbf A^r$ one has

$$
 R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^r,\lambda^*\mathcal L_\psi)
 \simeq
 \begin{cases}
 E(-r)[-2r],&\lambda=0,\\
 0,&\lambda\ne0.
 \end{cases}
$$

In the zero case the isomorphism is the compact-support trace.
