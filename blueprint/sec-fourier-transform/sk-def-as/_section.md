*Definition (sketch).*

For $X_0/k_0$ and $f\in\mathcal O_{X_0}$, the Artin–Schreier covering is
$\pi:Y=\Spec\mathcal O_{X_0}[T]/(T^p-T-f)\to X_0$ with its $\Fp$-action
$T\mapsto T+a$; the *Artin–Schreier sheaf* $\mathcal L_{\psi,f}$ is the
$\psi$-isotypic component of $\pi_*\Qlb$ for the idempotent
$e_\psi=\frac1p\sum_{a\in\Fp}\psi(-a)\sigma_a$. On $\Aone$ one writes
$\mathcal L_\psi=\mathcal L_{\psi,x}$. (Blueprint the Fourier–Deligne chapter,
def:fd-artin-schreier-sheaf.)
