In the notation of [def-fd-artin-schreier-sheaf], $\mathcal L_{\psi,f}$ is
one of the $p$ isotypic summands of the lisse rank-$p$ sheaf
$\pi_*\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ (lemmas below); the summand on which
$\mathbf F_p$ acts trivially (the $\chi=0$ component) is the constant sheaf,
and for $f=0$ the covering is the trivial torsor
$X_0\times\mathbf F_p$, so that $\mathcal L_{\psi,0}\cong\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$
as well.
The sign in $e_\Psi$ is the one for which the geometric Frobenius at a
closed point $u$ acts on $\mathcal L_{\psi,f}$ by
$\psi(\operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(u)/k_0}f(u))$, as proved in the trace lemma
below.
