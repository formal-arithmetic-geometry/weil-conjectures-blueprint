$\mathcal K_\psi$ is lisse of rank one on $V_0\times_{S_0}V_0^\vee$ and of
finite Tor amplitude, and

$$
\mathcal K_\psi\otimes\mathcal K_{\psi'}\cong\mathcal K_{\psi\psi'},
\qquad
\mathcal K_\psi^{-1}\cong\mathcal K_{\psi^{-1}} .
$$
