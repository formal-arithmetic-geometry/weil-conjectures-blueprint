$\sum_{x\in k_0}c\,\psi_a(x)=cq$ if $\psi_a=1$, and $0$ otherwise; and
$\sum_{y\in\mathbf F_{q^n}}\psi_n((x-x')y)=q^n$ if $x=x'$, and $0$ otherwise.
