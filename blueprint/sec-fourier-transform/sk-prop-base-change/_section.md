For a morphism $g:S'\to S$ of separated finite-type $k_0$-schemes and
$V'=V\times_SS'$, there is a canonical identification
$\FT_\psi(g^*K)\cong g^*\FT_\psi(K)$ for $K\in\Dbc(V,E)$.
