For $k$ finite or algebraically closed,

$$
H^q(\mathbf A^1_{\bar k},\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)=\begin{cases}\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell,&q=0,\\0,&q>0.\end{cases}
$$
