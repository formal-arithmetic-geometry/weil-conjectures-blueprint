For a finite morphism $\pi:Y\to X_0$ and a torsion etale sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $Y$,

$$
H^q(X_0,\pi_*\mathcal F)\cong H^q(Y,\mathcal F)\qquad(q\geq0).
$$
