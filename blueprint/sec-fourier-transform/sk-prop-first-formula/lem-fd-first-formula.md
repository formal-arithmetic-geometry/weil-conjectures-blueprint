Let $S=\operatorname{Spec}k$ with $k$ finite or algebraically closed and $V=\mathbf A^1_k$. Let $\underline{E}$ be the constant sheaf with value $E$ (the unit of $D^b_c(-,E)$) and $\delta_0=i_{0*}\underline{E}$ the skyscraper with value $E$ at the origin $i_0:\{0\}\hookrightarrow V^\vee$ (resp. $V$), placed in degree $0$. Then, in $D^b_c(V^\vee,E)$,

$$
\operatorname{FT}_\psi(\underline{E})\cong\delta_0(-1)[-1],\qquad
\operatorname{FT}_\psi(\delta_0)\cong\underline{E}[1],
$$

where $\delta_0(-1)$ is the Tate twist $(-1)$ of $\delta_0$. (The transform of [def-fd-transform] is $E$-linear; the same formulas for the constant sheaf $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ and the $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-skyscraper follow by the extension of scalars $-\otimes_E\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.)

## Proof

All sheaves are $E$-sheaves: $\mu_p\subset E$, so the exponential sheaves and the kernel are lisse $E$-sheaves of rank one, and the cohomology computations quoted below, stated with $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-coefficients, hold verbatim at the $E$-stage, because $-\otimes_E\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ is exact, commutes with $R\Gamma$ and $R\Gamma_c$ on $D^b_c$, and detects vanishing and dimension. For $K=\underline{E}$ and a geometric point $\bar\xi:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to V^\vee$, [lem-fd-stalk-formula] identifies the stalk of the transform with $H^*_c(V_{\bar\xi},\mathcal L_{\psi(x,\xi)})[1]$, $V_{\bar\xi}\cong\mathbf A^1_\Omega$ (for $\Omega\supsetneq\bar k$ the computation is the same over $\Omega$, the cohomology being invariant under extension of algebraically closed fields). We compute this compactly supported cohomology by the degreewise Poincare duality of [thm-pod-degreewise-perfectness-over-a-field] for the smooth, separated (not proper) curve $X=\mathbf A^1$ of dimension $d=1$ at the field stage $\Lambda=E$: for a lisse rank-one sheaf $M$ on $\mathbf A^1_{\bar k}$, perfect over $\Lambda_X$ in the sense of [def-pod-perfect-complex-on-x], it gives $H^i_c(\mathbf A^1,M)^\vee\cong H^{2-i}(\mathbf A^1,M^\vee(1))$. If $\xi=0$ the integrand is the constant sheaf, $M^\vee$ is again constant, and $H^j(\mathbf A^1,\underline{E})$ is $E$ for $j=0$ and zero otherwise by [lem-fd-affine-line-constant-cohomology]; hence $H^i_c(\mathbf A^1,\underline{E})$ vanishes for $i\neq2$ and is one-dimensional for $i=2$. For the Frobenius action, the similitude of [cor-pod-untwisted-similitude] for the constant sheaf reads $\langle Fx,Fy\rangle_2=q\langle x,y\rangle_2$ with $x\in H^2_c$, $y\in H^0$; the global Frobenius fixes the unit section $y=1\in H^0(\mathbf A^1,\underline{E})$, so $F$ acts on $H^2_c$ by $q$, i.e. $R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,\underline{E})\cong E(-1)[-2]$. For $\xi\neq0$ the integrand is $\mathcal L_{\psi(x,\xi)}$, whose coefficient dual is its inverse $\mathcal L_{\psi(-x,\xi)}$ by [lem-fd-as-lisse], again an exponential sheaf of a nonzero linear function; its ordinary cohomology vanishes by [lem-fd-cohomology-affine-line-character], hence so does the compactly supported cohomology of $\mathcal L_{\psi(x,\xi)}$ by the same duality. Hence the transform has a single nonzero stalk, at the origin, one-dimensional in degree $+1$ (the class of $H^2_c$, in degree $2$, shifted by the built-in $[1]$) and twisted by $(-1)$; this is the skyscraper $\delta_0(-1)[-1]$, whose only cohomology sheaf sits in degree $1$. For $K=\delta_0$, the support of $p_1^*\delta_0$ is $\{0\}\times V^\vee$, on which $p_2$ restricts to the canonical isomorphism onto $V^\vee$; the kernel restricted to the support is $\mathcal L_{\psi(0,\xi)}=\mathcal L_{\psi,0}$, the component $\chi=0$ of [lem-fd-as-characters], hence the constant sheaf $\underline{E}$. The compact-support direct image along $p_2$ of the constant sheaf on the support is therefore the constant sheaf $\underline{E}$ on $V^\vee$, and the shift gives $\underline{E}[1]$, which is the claimed second identity.
