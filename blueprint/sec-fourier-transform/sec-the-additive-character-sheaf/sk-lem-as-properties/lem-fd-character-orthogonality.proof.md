If $\psi_a\neq1$, choose $y\in k_0$ with $\psi_a(y)\neq1$; the
translation $x\mapsto x+y$ is a bijection of $k_0$, so

$$
S:=\sum_x c\,\psi_a(x)=\sum_x c\,\psi_a(x+y)=\psi_a(y)S,
$$

hence $(\psi_a(y)-1)S=0$ and $S=0$. If $\psi_a=1$, the sum is
$c\cdot|k_0|=cq$.
