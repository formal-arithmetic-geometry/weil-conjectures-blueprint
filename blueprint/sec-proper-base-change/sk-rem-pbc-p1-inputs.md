In the proof of [sk-thm-p1] the henselian comparison
[sk-prop-pbc-p1-henselian] rests on affine effacement and the lifting of
finite torsors, and the passage between geometric stalks rests on the
cohomology of curves [sk-thm-pbc-curve-input]. The explicit cohomology of
$\mathbf P^1$ over a base, its trace, and smooth purity are treated later,
using [sk-thm-p1].
