In the cartesian square of [det-pbc-complete-exchange-map], assume $p$ proper,
and let $K\in D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ be such that each reduction
$K_n=K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}(\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)_X$, regarded as
an object of $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, lies in
$D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$. Then
$\operatorname{Ex}^{\operatorname{Tow}}_{\operatorname{red}(K)}$ is an isomorphism in
$D(\operatorname{Tow}(Y'))$, and the completed exchange map

$$
\operatorname{Ex}^{\mathrm{comp}}_K\colon g^*_{\mathrm{comp}}Rp_*K
\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Rp'_*g'^*_{\mathrm{comp}}K
$$

is an isomorphism in $D(Y'_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$.
