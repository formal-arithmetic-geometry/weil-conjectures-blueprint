Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $I=(\varpi)$ an ideal generated by a
nonzerodivisor (the case of interest is $R=\mathcal O_E$, $\varpi=\pi_E$). For a
scheme $T$ write $D(T)=D(T_{\mathrm{proét}},R)$ for the derived category of
pro-étale sheaves of $R$-modules, and for $M\in D(T)$ write
$M_n=M\otimes^{\mathbf L}_R R/I^n$. An object $M$ is *derived complete* if the
canonical map $M\to R\varprojlim_nM_n$ to the derived limit
[def-weil-derived-limit] of its reductions is an isomorphism; equivalently,
every morphism to $M$ from an object on which $\varpi$ acts invertibly is zero.

The inclusion of the full subcategory of derived complete objects of $D(T)$ has a
left adjoint, the *derived completion* $M\mapsto\widehat M$, with
$\widehat M\simeq R\varprojlim_nM_n$. Its unit $\kappa_M:M\to\widehat M$ sits in a
distinguished triangle $M\xrightarrow{\kappa_M}\widehat M\to Z\to M[1]$ in which
$\varpi$ acts invertibly on $Z$; consequently $\kappa_M\otimes^{\mathbf L}R/I^n$ is
an isomorphism $M_n\xrightarrow{\sim}\widehat M_n$ for every $n\geq1$, and
$\kappa_M$ is an isomorphism if and only if $M$ is derived complete.
