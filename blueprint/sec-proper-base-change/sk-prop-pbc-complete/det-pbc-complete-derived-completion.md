For every $M\in D(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$, the derived completion
$\widehat M=R\varprojlim\operatorname{red}(M)$ of [det-pbc-complete-towers] is
derived $\pi_E$-complete, and the completion map
$\kappa_M\colon M\to\widehat M$ induces an isomorphism
$\operatorname{red}(\kappa_M)\colon\operatorname{red}(M)\xrightarrow{\sim}
\operatorname{red}(\widehat M)$ in $D(\operatorname{Tow}(T))$; in particular
$M_m\simeq\widehat M_m$ for every $m\geq1$.
