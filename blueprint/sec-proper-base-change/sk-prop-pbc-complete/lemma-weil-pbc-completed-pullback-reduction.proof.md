(1) Derived completeness of $\widehat{g^*M}$ and the isomorphisms
$\widehat{g^*M}\otimes^{\mathbf L}R/I^n\simeq(g^*M)\otimes^{\mathbf L}R/I^n$ are
[det-pbc-complete-derived-completion], applied to $g^*M$ on $T'$. The derived
inverse image is $R$-linear and strong monoidal, and $g^*$ of the constant
coefficient ring is the constant coefficient ring, so
$(g^*M)\otimes^{\mathbf L}R/I^n\simeq g^*(M\otimes^{\mathbf L}R/I^n)=g^*(M_n)$,
naturally in $M$ and compatibly with the transitions.

(2) This is the last assertion of [det-pbc-complete-derived-completion] for $g^*M$.
