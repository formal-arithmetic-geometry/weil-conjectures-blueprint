Let $R\to S$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings, where $S$ is an Artinian local ring (a local ring in which every descending chain of ideals is eventually constant) with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$. If the composite $R\to S\to S/\mathfrak m$ is essentially of finite type, then $R\to S$ is essentially of finite type.
