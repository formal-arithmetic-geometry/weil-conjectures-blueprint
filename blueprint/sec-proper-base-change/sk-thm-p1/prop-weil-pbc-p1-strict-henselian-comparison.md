In the setup of [def-weil-pbc-strict-henselian-setup], for every torsion
etale sheaf $F$ on $X$, restriction gives natural isomorphisms

$$
 H^q(X,F)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^q(X_0,F_0)
 \qquad(q\geq0).
$$
