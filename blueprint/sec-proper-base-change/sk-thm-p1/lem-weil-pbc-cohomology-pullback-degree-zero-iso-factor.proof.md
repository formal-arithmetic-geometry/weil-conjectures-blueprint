The category of abelian etale sheaves is Grothendieck abelian, hence has enough injectives. Choose its ordinary natural-number-indexed injective resolution R of G and extend by zero to integers. The incoming differential at degree zero vanishes in both G[0] and R. Global sections are additive and preserve finite limits; Functor.preservesLeftHomology_of_zero_f and ShortComplex.quasiIso_map_of_preservesLeftHomology therefore show that H^0(Gamma(R.ι')) is an isomorphism. This implements the left-exact degree-zero comparison without computing an explicit kernel comparison. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isGrothendieckAbelian_sheaf_smallEtaleTopology` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/AffineEtale.lean)), [lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives], `CategoryTheory.instIsIsoToRightDerivedZero'` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Abelian/RightDerived.lean))).

R is bounded below and termwise injective, hence K-injective. By the bijection from homotopy-category maps to derived-category maps into I, lift Q(s) Q(R.ι')^{-1} to a chain map a : R -> I. Naturality of quotientCompQhIso and injectivity of that bijection give a homotopy R.ι' ≫ a ∼ s. Its image Q(a) is invertible, so a is a quasi-isomorphism; between the two K-injective complexes it is a homotopy equivalence. (`DerivedCategory.Plus.exists_injective_nonempty_iso` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/DerivedCategory/DerivabilityStructureInjectives.lean)), `CochainComplex.IsKInjective.Qh_map_bijective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/DerivedCategory/KInjective.lean))).

Apply Gamma to this homotopy equivalence and the augmentation homotopy. The homotopy equivalence induces an isomorphism H^0(Gamma(a)); homotopy invariance and composition of homology maps identify H^0(Gamma(s)) with H^0(Gamma(R.ι')) ≫ H^0(Gamma(a)), hence it is an isomorphism. (`Homotopy.homologyMap_eq; CategoryTheory.Functor.mapHomotopy` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/Homotopy.lean))).

In the Gamma-image of G[0], the cycle inclusion and homology projection in degree zero are isomorphisms because both adjacent differentials are zero. Set c = Gamma(singleObjXSelf.inv) ≫ iCycles^{-1}. Cancel the monic cycle inclusion using hz to identify z with c ≫ cyclesMap(Gamma(s)). Naturality of homologyπ then gives z ≫ homologyπ = c ≫ homologyπ ≫ H^0(Gamma(s)), a composite of isomorphisms.
