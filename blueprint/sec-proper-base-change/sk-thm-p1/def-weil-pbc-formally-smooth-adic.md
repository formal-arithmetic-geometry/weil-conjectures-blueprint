Let $\varphi:R\to S$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings, making $S$ an $R$-algebra, and $\mathfrak n\subseteq S$ an ideal. $S$ is *formally smooth over $R$ for the $\mathfrak n$-adic topology* (also: $\varphi$ is formally smooth for the $\mathfrak n$-adic topology) if the following lifting property holds: for every commutative ring $D$ with a ring map $R\to D$, every ideal $J\subseteq D$ with $J^2=0$, with quotient map $q:D\to D/J$ `AlgHom, Ideal.Quotient.algebra, Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ, Ideal (IdemCommSemiring: I * J, I ^ k)` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Hom.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Operations.lean)), and every homomorphism of $R$-algebras $g:S\to D/J$ with $g(\mathfrak n^k)=0$ for some $k\ge1$, there is a homomorphism of $R$-algebras $f:S\to D$ with $q\circ f=g$. Remarks (proved in the steps below; not part of the condition): (a) such an $f$ satisfies $f(\mathfrak n^{2k})=0$; (b) for a homomorphism $g:S\to E$ into any ring $E$, '$g(\mathfrak n^k)=0$ for some $k\ge1$' says exactly that $g$ is continuous for the $\mathfrak n$-adic topology on $S$ and the discrete topology on $E$ (Stacks, Lemma 07E9), so the condition is Stacks More on Algebra, Definition 07EB for $R$ with the discrete topology, $S$ with the $\mathfrak n$-adic topology and $D$ discrete, by (a) with $f$ continuous. Explanatory note (not used in the DAG, not proved here): by Stacks Lemma 07EC(2) the same condition is obtained when $R$ carries the $\mathfrak m$-adic topology for an ideal $\mathfrak m\subseteq R$ with $\varphi(\mathfrak m^e)\subseteq\mathfrak n$ for some $e\ge1$; Stacks Definition 07NI calls these equivalent conditions 'formally smooth for the $\mathfrak n$-adic topology'. The DAG uses the condition as written above (the case $\mathfrak m=0$ of 07NI).
