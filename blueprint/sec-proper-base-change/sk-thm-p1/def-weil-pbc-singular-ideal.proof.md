(Well-formedness.) For $g\in A$ the localization $A_g$ is a commutative ring with the ring map $R\to A\to A_g$, so '$A_g$ is a smooth $R$-algebra' is the condition of `Algebra.Smooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean)) for this ring map (finite presentation, $H_1(L_{A_g/R})=0$, $\Omega_{A_g/R}$ finite projective); hence $G\subset A$ is a well-defined subset and 'smooth at $\mathfrak q$' is a well-defined property of a prime $\mathfrak q$. Both depend only on the $R$-algebra $A$ (an $R$-algebra isomorphism $A\cong A'$ induces $R$-algebra isomorphisms $A_g\cong A'_{g'}$ and so carries $G$ onto $G'$), not on a presentation. $(G)$ is the ideal of finite sums $\sum a_ig_i$, and its radical $\sqrt{(G)}=\{x: x^n\in(G)\text{ for some }n\ge1\}$ is an ideal (if $x^a,y^b\in I$ then $(x+y)^{a+b-1}\in I$ by the binomial formula, and $(\lambda x)^a=\lambda^ax^a\in I$) and radical ($x^n\in\sqrt I$ gives $x^{nm}\in I$ for some $m$). So $H_{A/R}$ is a well-defined radical ideal of $A$.

(The smooth locus is open and equals the complement of $V(H_{A/R})$.) Let $\mathfrak q\subset A$ be prime. By the definition, $R\to A$ is smooth at $\mathfrak q$ iff some $g\in G$ has $g\notin\mathfrak q$, i.e. iff $\mathfrak q\in\bigcup_{g\in G}D(g)$, where $D(g)=\{\mathfrak q: g\notin\mathfrak q\}$ is a basic open of $\operatorname{Spec}A$; a union of opens is open, which is Stacks' remark after Definition 07C5 that 'the set of primes where $R\to A$ is smooth is open'. Conversely $R\to A$ is not smooth at $\mathfrak q$ iff $G\subset\mathfrak q$ iff $(G)\subset\mathfrak q$ ($\mathfrak q$ is an ideal) iff $\sqrt{(G)}\subset\mathfrak q$ (if $x^n\in(G)\subset\mathfrak q$ then $x\in\mathfrak q$ as $\mathfrak q$ is prime; and $(G)\subset\sqrt{(G)}$). Hence $V(H_{A/R})=\bigcap_{g\in G}V(g)$ is exactly the set of primes at which $R\to A$ is not smooth.

(Uniqueness; agreement with Stacks' definition.) Let $J\subset A$ be a radical ideal with $V(J)=\{\mathfrak q: R\to A\text{ not smooth at }\mathfrak q\}$. By step 2 this set is $V(H_{A/R})$, so $V(J)=V(H_{A/R})$, hence $\sqrt J=\sqrt{H_{A/R}}$ `PrimeSpectrum.zeroLocus_eq_iff` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/Topology.lean)); as both are radical (step 1), $J=\sqrt J=\sqrt{H_{A/R}}=H_{A/R}$. So $H_{A/R}=\sqrt{(G)}$ is the unique radical ideal with $V(H_{A/R})$ equal to the non-smooth locus, which is Stacks' Definition 07C5; existence is step 2.

(What the definition does not contain.) The other results of Section 07C4 are statements about $H_{A/R}$ for $R\to A$ of finite presentation and depend on this definition, so they are not part of it and are left to the nodes that use them: Lemma Tag 07C6 (lemma-find-strictly-standard), Definition Tag 07C7 (definition-strictly-standard, elementary and strictly standard elements), Lemma Tag 07ET (lemma-parse-equation-strictly-standard-one), Lemma Tag 07CA (lemma-elkik: $H_{A/R}$ is the radical of the ideal generated by strictly, resp. elementary, standard elements), Example Tag 07CB (the smooth locus need not be quasi-compact), Lemma Tag 07CC (lemma-strictly-standard-base-change), and Lemma Tag 07EU (lemma-final-solve, node lem-weil-pbc-popescu-final-solve). In particular the fact used in the proof of 07EU that $A_f$ is smooth over $R$ for every $f\in H_{A/R}$ (not only for $f\in G$; it needs that $D(f)\subset\bigcup_{g\in G}D(g)$ is covered by finitely many $D(fg_j)$ and that smoothness is local, Algebra Lemma Tag 00TC) is a statement about $H_{A/R}$, to be created in the split of lem-weil-pbc-popescu-final-solve. Stacks gives no lemma on localization or base change of $H_{A/R}$ in this section.
