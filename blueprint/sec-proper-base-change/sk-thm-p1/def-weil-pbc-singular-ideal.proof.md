(Well-formedness.) For $g\in A$ the localization $A_g$ is a commutative ring with the ring map $R\to A\to A_g$, so '$A_g$ is a smooth $R$-algebra' is a condition on this ring map (finite presentation and formal smoothness); hence $G\subset A$ is a well-defined subset and 'smooth at $\mathfrak q$' is a well-defined property of a prime $\mathfrak q$. Both depend only on the $R$-algebra $A$ (an $R$-algebra isomorphism $A\cong A'$ induces $R$-algebra isomorphisms $A_g\cong A'_{g'}$ and so carries $G$ onto $G'$), not on a presentation. $(G)$ is the ideal of finite sums $\sum a_ig_i$, and its radical $\sqrt{(G)}=\{x: x^n\in(G)\text{ for some }n\ge1\}$ is an ideal (if $x^a,y^b\in I$ then $(x+y)^{a+b-1}\in I$ by the binomial formula, and $(\lambda x)^a=\lambda^ax^a\in I$) and radical ($x^n\in\sqrt I$ gives $x^{nm}\in I$ for some $m$). So $H_{A/R}$ is a well-defined radical ideal of $A$.

(The smooth locus is open and equals the complement of $V(H_{A/R})$.) Let $\mathfrak q\subset A$ be prime. By the definition, $R\to A$ is smooth at $\mathfrak q$ iff some $g\in G$ has $g\notin\mathfrak q$, i.e. iff $\mathfrak q\in\bigcup_{g\in G}D(g)$, where $D(g)=\{\mathfrak q: g\notin\mathfrak q\}$ is a basic open of $\operatorname{Spec}A$; a union of opens is open, which is Stacks' remark after Definition 07C5 that 'the set of primes where $R\to A$ is smooth is open'. Conversely $R\to A$ is not smooth at $\mathfrak q$ iff $G\subset\mathfrak q$ iff $(G)\subset\mathfrak q$ ($\mathfrak q$ is an ideal) iff $\sqrt{(G)}\subset\mathfrak q$ (if $x^n\in(G)\subset\mathfrak q$ then $x\in\mathfrak q$ as $\mathfrak q$ is prime; and $(G)\subset\sqrt{(G)}$). Hence $V(H_{A/R})=\bigcap_{g\in G}V(g)$ is exactly the set of primes at which $R\to A$ is not smooth.

(Uniqueness; agreement with Stacks' definition.) Let $J\subset A$ be a radical ideal with $V(J)=\{\mathfrak q: R\to A\text{ not smooth at }\mathfrak q\}$. By step 2 this set is $V(H_{A/R})$, so $V(J)=V(H_{A/R})$, hence $\sqrt J=\sqrt{H_{A/R}}$; as both are radical (step 1), $J=\sqrt J=\sqrt{H_{A/R}}=H_{A/R}$. So $H_{A/R}=\sqrt{(G)}$ is the unique radical ideal with $V(H_{A/R})$ equal to the non-smooth locus, which is Stacks' Definition 07C5; existence is step 2.
