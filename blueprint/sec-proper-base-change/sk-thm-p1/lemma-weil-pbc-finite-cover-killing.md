For a fixed $c\in H^q(T_0,C_0)$ with $q>0$ and constructible $C$, there is a
finite finitely presented cover $h:V\to T$ whose closed-fibre pullback
$h_0^*c$ is zero; the induced map $C\hookrightarrow h_*h^*C$ is monic.
