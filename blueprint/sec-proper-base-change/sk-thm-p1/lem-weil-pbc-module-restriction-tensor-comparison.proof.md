Construct the comparison by the sheafification universal property: restriction of the sectionwise tensor presheaf is the tensor presheaf of the restrictions, and restriction commutes with sheafification by the restriction/direct-image adjunction. The universal maps agree on pure tensors, giving the displayed formula. ([def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]).
