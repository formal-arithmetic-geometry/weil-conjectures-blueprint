Let k be an algebraically closed field `AlgebraicClosure` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/IsAlgClosed/AlgebraicClosure.lean)), f:X → Spec k a separated finite-type curve of dimension at most one [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting], X reduced `AlgebraicGeometry.IsReduced` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Properties.lean)), j:U → X an open immersion `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)), and r a prime. Put R=ULift(Z/rZ) and let R_U/(r) denote the constant R-module sheaf with value R/(r) `CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean)). Define ReducedConstantLowerShriekReductionData(f,j,r) to mean the following existential proposition. There exist a scheme Y, a finite surjective morphism ν:Y → X `AlgebraicGeometry.IsFinite, AlgebraicGeometry.IsIntegralHom` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Finite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Integral.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Surjective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/UnderlyingMap.lean)), a dense open V of X, a scheme U′, morphisms j′:U′ → Y and ν′:U′ → U, such that ν followed by f is smooth [def-sbc-smooth-morphisms], ν restricted over V is an isomorphism, j′ is an open immersion, the square (j′,ν′,ν,j) is cartesian [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions], and j′_! R_U′/(r) [def-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero] satisfies the eight assertions for Y → Spec k [def-weil-pbc-eight-curve-assertions].
