Let $u:X_0\to X$ be universally injective and surjective (for instance a
universal homeomorphism), and let $U$ be an etale $X$-scheme. A sieve $S$ on
$U$ in the small etale site of $X$ is a covering sieve (the etale morphisms
$U_i\to U$ in $S$ are jointly surjective) if and only if the sieve on
$U\times_XX_0$ generated by the base changes $U_i\times_XX_0\to U\times_XX_0$
is a covering sieve in the small etale site of $X_0$.

## Proof

Put $U_0=U\times_XX_0$ and let $\pi\colon U_0\to U$ be the projection. It is
a base change of $u$, hence surjective and universally injective, so it is a
bijection on underlying points. For an étale $X$-morphism $V\to U$, the image
of its base change $V\times_XX_0=V\times_UU_0\to U_0$ is
$\pi^{-1}(\operatorname{im}(V\to U))$: a point of $U_0$ lies in the image of
the fibre product if and only if its image in $U$ lies in the image of $V$,
since points of a fibre product exist over any pair of points with the same
image.

A sieve on $U$ is covering if and only if the images of its members cover
$|U|$, and the sieve generated by the base changes of the members of $S$ is
covering if and only if the images of these base changes cover $|U_0|$ (every
member of the generated sieve factors through one of them, so they have the
same union of images). By the previous paragraph the latter union is
$\pi^{-1}\bigl(\bigcup_{V\in S}\operatorname{im}(V\to U)\bigr)$. As $\pi$ is
surjective, this is all of $|U_0|$ if and only if
$\bigcup_{V\in S}\operatorname{im}(V\to U)=|U|$.
