Let $X$ be a reduced scheme. Consider the empty family (indexed by the empty set) of schemes $C_i$ with morphisms $\iota_i:C_i\to X$ satisfying the component-cover hypotheses: each $C_i$ is integral, each $\iota_i$ is a closed immersion whose image is an irreducible component of $X$, distinct indices have distinct images, and every irreducible component of $X$ is the image of some $\iota_i$; and the empty family of morphisms $\nu_i:Y_i\to C_i$, each restricting to an isomorphism over a dense open subset of $C_i$. Then there is a dense open subset $U\subseteq X$ over which the morphism $\coprod_iY_i\to X$ induced by the composites $\iota_i\circ\nu_i$ (a morphism out of the empty scheme) restricts to an isomorphism.
