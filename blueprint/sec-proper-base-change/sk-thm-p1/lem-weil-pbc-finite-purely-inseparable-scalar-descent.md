title = "A finite purely inseparable extension descends along a field extension"
lean = ["Algebra.TensorProduct.finite_purelyInseparable_of_tensorProduct"]
order = 1772

Let $K\subseteq L$ be fields ($L$ a field extension of $K$) and let $B$ be a commutative $K$-algebra. Regard $L\otimes_KB$ as an $L$-algebra via $l\mapsto l\otimes1$. If $L\otimes_KB$ is a field and, via this map, a finite purely inseparable field extension of $L$, then $B$ is a field, the structure map $K\to B$ makes $B$ a finite purely inseparable field extension of $K$, and $\dim_KB=\dim_L(L\otimes_KB)$.
