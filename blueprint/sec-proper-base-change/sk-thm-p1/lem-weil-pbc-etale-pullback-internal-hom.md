Let $f:Y\to X$ be an étale morphism of schemes, $\Lambda$ a commutative ring, and $F,G$ sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on $X_{\Et}$. Then there is an isomorphism $f^{-1}\mathcal{H}om(F,G)\cong\mathcal{H}om(f^{-1}F,f^{-1}G)$ of sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on $Y_{\Et}$, where $\mathcal{H}om$ is the internal Hom right adjoint to the tensor product (the sheafification of the objectwise tensor product). No finiteness hypothesis is imposed on $F$, $G$ or $\Lambda$.
