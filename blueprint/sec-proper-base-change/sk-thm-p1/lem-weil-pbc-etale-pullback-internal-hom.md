Let f : Y → X be an etale morphism of schemes, Lambda a commutative ring, and F,G sheaves of Lambda-modules on X_et. There is an isomorphism f^* internalHom(F,G) ≅ internalHom(f^*F,f^*G), with internal Hom for the sheafified objectwise tensor product. No finiteness hypothesis is imposed on F,G or Lambda.
