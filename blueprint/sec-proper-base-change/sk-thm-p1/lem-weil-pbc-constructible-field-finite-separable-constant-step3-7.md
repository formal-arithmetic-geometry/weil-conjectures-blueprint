Given a field $k$, finite commutative ring $\\Lambda$, sheaf $G$, a $LocallyConstantDatum\\ d$ for $G$, and finite constant module values on every member of the datum cover, there exist a finite separable field extension $K/k$, a finite $\\Lambda$-module $M$, and an isomorphism of the pullback of $G$ to $\\operatorname{Spec}K$ with the constant sheaf of value $M$.
