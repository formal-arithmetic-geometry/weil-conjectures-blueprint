Let $k$ be a field, $\Lambda$ a finite commutative ring and $G$ a sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules on the small etale site of $\operatorname{Spec}k$. Suppose given an etale covering $\{U_i\to\operatorname{Spec}k\}_{i\in I}$ trivializing $G$ [def-constructible-locally-constant] such that, for every $i$, the restriction of $G$ to $(U_i)_{\Et}$ is isomorphic to the constant sheaf with value a finite $\Lambda$-module. Then there exist a finite separable field extension $K/k$, a finite $\Lambda$-module $M$, and an isomorphism of the pullback of $G$ to $\operatorname{Spec}K$ with the constant sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules with value $M$.
