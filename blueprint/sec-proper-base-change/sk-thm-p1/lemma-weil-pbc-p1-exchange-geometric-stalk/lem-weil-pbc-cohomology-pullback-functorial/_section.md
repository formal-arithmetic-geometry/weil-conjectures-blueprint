For a morphism of schemes $u:Y\to X$ [def-pc-scheme] and an abelian sheaf $G$ on $X_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves], let $u^*:H^q(X_{\Et},G)\to H^q(Y_{\Et},u^{-1}G)$ be the pullback map of [def-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback], built from a K-injective resolution $s:G\to I^\bullet$ and a K-injective resolution $r:u^{-1}I^\bullet\to J^\bullet$ [def-weil-pbc-kinjective-resolutions], as $\mathcal H^q$ of $a\mapsto r(\eta_u(a))$, $\eta_u$ the unit of $u^{-1}\dashv u_*$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. For a morphism $\varphi:G\to G'$ of abelian sheaves on a scheme $T$ and K-injective resolutions $G\to I^\bullet$, $G'\to I'^\bullet$, write $H^q(\varphi):H^q(T_{\Et},G)\to H^q(T_{\Et},G')$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-cohomology] for $\mathcal H^q(\Gamma(T,\tilde\varphi))$, where $\tilde\varphi:I^\bullet\to I'^\bullet$ is any morphism of complexes with $\tilde\varphi\circ(G\to I^\bullet)$ homotopic to $(G'\to I'^\bullet)\circ\varphi$; such $\tilde\varphi$ exist and $H^q(\varphi)$ does not depend on $\tilde\varphi$ (step 1), and for $\varphi=\mathrm{id}_G$ these are the identifications of the groups computed from different resolutions [prop-weil-pbc-cohomology-resolution-independent]. Then:

1. (Independence.) $u^*$ does not depend on the choice of $I^\bullet$ and $J^\bullet$: for a second choice $(I'^\bullet,J'^\bullet)$ the two maps $u^*$ agree after the identifications $H^q(\mathrm{id}_G)$ and $H^q(\mathrm{id}_{u^{-1}G})$. The same holds for $u^*:\RGamma(X_{\Et},K)\to\RGamma(Y_{\Et},u^{-1}K)$, $K\in\Dplus(X_{\Et})$ [def-weil-pbc-derived-categories], [def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image], as a morphism of $\Dplus(\Ab)$ [def-weil-abstract-derived-category] up to the canonical isomorphisms between the complexes computed from different resolutions.
2. (Any comparison complex.) Let $t:u^{-1}G\to M^\bullet$ be a quasi-isomorphism to a bounded below complex of abelian sheaves on $Y_{\Et}$ and $\beta:u^{-1}I^\bullet\to M^\bullet$ a morphism of complexes with $\beta\circ u^{-1}(s)=t$. Let $\gamma:M^\bullet\to J^\bullet$ be a morphism of complexes with $\gamma\circ t$ homotopic to $r\circ u^{-1}(s)$ (it exists, unique up to homotopy). Then $u^*=\mathcal H^q(\Gamma(Y,\gamma))\circ\mathcal H^q\bigl(a\mapsto\beta(\eta_u(a))\bigr)$, the second map being $\mathcal H^q$ of $\Gamma(X,I^\bullet)\to\Gamma(Y,M^\bullet)$. If $M^\bullet$ is K-injective, or a complex of $\Gamma(Y,-)$-acyclic sheaves, then $\mathcal H^q(\Gamma(Y,\gamma))$ is an isomorphism, so that $u^*$ is computed on $\Gamma(Y,M^\bullet)$ by $a\mapsto\beta(\eta_u(a))$.
3. (Naturality.) For a morphism $\varphi:G\to G'$ of abelian sheaves on $X_{\Et}$, $u^*\circ H^q(\varphi)=H^q(u^{-1}\varphi)\circ u^*$.
4. (Composition.) For a morphism $v:Z\to Y$, let $c_{u,v}:v^{-1}u^{-1}\xrightarrow{\sim}(u\circ v)^{-1}$ be the isomorphism of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (compatible with the units). Then $(u\circ v)^*=H^q(c_{u,v,G})\circ v^*\circ u^*:H^q(X_{\Et},G)\to H^q(Z_{\Et},(u\circ v)^{-1}G)$, and, with $e_X:\mathrm{id}_X^{-1}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{id}$ of the same lemma, $H^q(e_{X,G})\circ\mathrm{id}_X^*=\mathrm{id}$.
5. (Etale maps.) If $u$ is etale [def-weil-etale-morphism], let $\rho_G:u^{-1}G\xrightarrow{\sim}G|_Y$ be the identification of the inverse image with the restriction, $G|_Y(V)=G(V)$ for $V$ etale over $Y$, of [lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction]. If $G\to I^\bullet$ is a resolution by a bounded below complex of injective abelian sheaves, then $u^{-1}G\to u^{-1}I^\bullet$ is a resolution of $u^{-1}G$ by a bounded below complex of injective abelian sheaves, one may take $J^\bullet=u^{-1}I^\bullet$, $r=\mathrm{id}$, and then $u^*$ is $\mathcal H^q$ of the restriction map $\Gamma(X,I^\bullet)=I^\bullet(X)\to I^\bullet(Y)\cong\Gamma(Y,u^{-1}I^\bullet)$ (through $\rho_{I^\bullet}(Y)$) along $u:Y\to X$.
