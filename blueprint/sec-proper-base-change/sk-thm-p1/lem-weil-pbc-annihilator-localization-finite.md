Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $f\in A$, and $M$ a finitely generated $A$-module `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)), with annihilator $\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)=\{a\in A: am=0\text{ for all }m\in M\}$. Let $A_f$ be the localization of $A$ at $f$ `Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean)) and $M_f$ the localization of $M$ at $f$, an $A_f$-module `LocalizedModule.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)). Then $\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)=\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)A_f$, the ideal of $A_f$ generated by the elements $a/1$, $a\in\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)$.
