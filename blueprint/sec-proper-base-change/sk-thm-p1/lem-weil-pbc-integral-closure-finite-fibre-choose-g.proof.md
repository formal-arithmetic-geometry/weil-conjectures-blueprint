Let R be the integral closure and let q : R → C₁′ × C₂′ be the surjective quotient map followed by eB′. The composite f = fst ∘ q is surjective. Let K = ker f. By the quotient-prime correspondence V(K) = range(Spec f), and hU says this is contained in U. Let J be the vanishing ideal of the closed complement of U, so V(J) is that complement. The disjointness gives V(J ⊔ K) = ∅ and hence J ⊔ K = R. Choose j ∈ J and k ∈ K with j + k = 1. Choose g₀ with q(g₀) = (1,0). Then q(j).1 = 1 and q(g₀j) = (1,0), so g = g₀j has the prescribed quotient image. Because g ∈ J, D(g) ⊆ U. Write φ = Spec(algebraMap R B). Restrict the isomorphism φ|U further to the inverse image under U.ι of D(g). Its image in Spec R is U ∩ D(g) = D(g). The Arrow isomorphism morphismRestrictRestrict shows φ|D(g) is an isomorphism. Mathlib SpecMapRestrictBasicOpenIso identifies this arrow with Spec of Localization.awayMap (algebraMap R B) g.
