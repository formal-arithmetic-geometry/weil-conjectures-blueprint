Let $R$ be a normal domain whose fraction field is separably closed [def-pc-separably-closed]. Let $I\subseteq R$ be an ideal and $h\in R/I$. Then there exist a normal domain $S$ with separably closed fraction field and a closed immersion from the open subscheme $D(h)$ of $\operatorname{Spec}(R/I)$ into $\operatorname{Spec}S$. This includes the cases $D(h)=\emptyset$ and $I=R$.
