**Step 1 (clause 1: the isomorphism $d_{u,v}$).** Let $W\to X$ be an object of $X_{\Et}$. In the diagram with rows $(W\times_XY)\times_YZ\to W\times_XY\to W$ over $Z\xrightarrow{v}Y\xrightarrow{u}X$ `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)), both squares are cartesian (each is the chosen fibre product), so by the pasting lemma `CategoryTheory.IsPullback.paste_vert_iff` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/IsPullback/Basic.lean)) the outer rectangle is cartesian: $(W\times_XY)\times_YZ$ with its projections to $W$ and to $Z$ is a fibre product of $W\to X$ and $u\circ v:Z\to X$. By the uniqueness of fibre products `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)) there is a unique isomorphism $\phi_W:(W\times_XY)\times_YZ\xrightarrow{\sim}W\times_XZ$ compatible with the projections to $W$ and to $Z$; in particular $\phi_W$ is a $Z$-morphism, i.e. an isomorphism in $Z_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site] (both sides are etale over $Z$ as base changes of the etale $W\to X$, as in [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]). For a morphism $g:W'\to W$ of $X_{\Et}$, the two $Z$-morphisms $\phi_W\circ((g\times_XY)\times_YZ)$ and $(g\times_XZ)\circ\phi_{W'}$ from $(W'\times_XY)\times_YZ$ to $W\times_XZ$ have the same compositions with both projections (each projection is $g$ composed with the projection to $W'$, resp. the projection to $Z$), so they are equal by the uniqueness clause of the universal property `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)): $\phi$ is natural in $W$. For an abelian sheaf $F$ on $Z_{\Et}$ put $d_{u,v,F}(W)=F(\phi_W):F(W\times_XZ)\to F((W\times_XY)\times_YZ)$; by the formula $(s_*F)(W)=F(W\times_ST)$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image] applied to $u\circ v$, to $v$ and then to $u$, its source is $((u\circ v)_*F)(W)$ and its target $(v_*F)(W\times_XY)=(u_*v_*F)(W)$. It is bijective with inverse $F(\phi_W^{-1})$; compatibility with the restriction maps along $g$ is $F$ applied to the naturality square of $\phi$ (the restriction maps of $(u\circ v)_*F$ and $u_*v_*F$ along $g$ being $F(g\times_XZ)$ and $F((g\times_XY)\times_YZ)$); naturality in $F$ holds because a morphism of sheaves $F\to F'$ is a natural transformation and so commutes with $F(\phi_W)$, $F'(\phi_W)$. Hence the $d_{u,v,F}$ are isomorphisms of sheaves, natural in $F$, i.e. an isomorphism of functors $d_{u,v}:(u\circ v)_*\xrightarrow{\sim}u_*\circ v_*$. This is the identity $(g\circ f)_{small,*}=g_{small,*}\circ f_{small,*}$ of Stacks 021J, read with the canonical isomorphism of iterated fibre products written out.

**Step 2 (clause 3, first part: $d_X$).** For $u=\mathrm{id}_X$ and an object $W\to X$ of $X_{\Et}$, $(W,\mathrm{id}_W,W\to X)$ is a fibre product of $W\to X$ and $\mathrm{id}_X$ (a pair $a:T\to W$, $b:T\to X$ with $(W\to X)\circ a=b$ is determined by $a$), so by uniqueness of fibre products `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)) the first projection $\psi_W:W\times_XX\to W$ is an isomorphism of $X$-schemes, with inverse $(\mathrm{id}_W,W\to X)$, and as in step 1 it is natural in $W$ (for $g:W'\to W$, $g\circ\psi_{W'}=\psi_W\circ(g\times_XX)$ is the definition of $g\times_XX$). Put $d_{X,G}(W)=G(\psi_W^{-1}):G(W\times_XX)=((\mathrm{id}_X)_*G)(W)\to G(W)$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]; exactly as in step 1 these are isomorphisms compatible with restrictions and natural in $G$, giving an isomorphism of functors $d_X:(\mathrm{id}_X)_*\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{id}_{\Ab(X_{\Et})}$. ([def-weil-pbc-etale-site]).

**Step 3 (clause 2: $c_{u,v}$).** By [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] there are adjunctions $u^{-1}\dashv u_*$, $v^{-1}\dashv v_*$ and $(u\circ v)^{-1}\dashv(u\circ v)_*$ with units $\eta_u,\eta_v,\eta_{u\circ v}$. Composing the first two `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Adjunction/Basic.lean)) (with $F=u^{-1}$, $G=u_*$, $H=v^{-1}$, $I=v_*$) gives an adjunction $L_1=v^{-1}\circ u^{-1}\dashv R_1=u_*\circ v_*$ whose unit is $\eta_{1,G}=u_*(\eta_{v,u^{-1}G})\circ\eta_{u,G}$. Let $L_2=(u\circ v)^{-1}\dashv R_2=(u\circ v)_*$ with $\eta_2=\eta_{u\circ v}$, and $\delta=d_{u,v}:R_2\xrightarrow{\sim}R_1$ the isomorphism of step 1. By `CategoryTheory.unit_conjugateEquiv_symm` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Adjunction/Mates.lean)) there is a unique natural transformation $c_{u,v}:v^{-1}u^{-1}\to(u\circ v)^{-1}$ with $R_1(c_{u,v,G})\circ\eta_{1,G}=\delta_{L_2G}\circ\eta_{2,G}$ for every $G$, i.e. $u_*v_*(c_{u,v,G})\circ u_*(\eta_{v,u^{-1}G})\circ\eta_{u,G}=d_{u,v,(u\circ v)^{-1}G}\circ\eta_{u\circ v,G}$, and it is an isomorphism. Uniqueness among isomorphisms (as stated) follows from uniqueness among all natural transformations. This is the argument of Stacks 03CB ($(u\circ v)_s$ and $u_s\circ v_s$ are both left adjoints of $(u\circ v)^s$), with the isomorphism of right adjoints $d_{u,v}$ in place of the equality $(u\circ v)^s=v^s\circ u^s$, and with the unit compatibility that makes the isomorphism unique (Stacks 0B65).

**Step 4 (clause 3, second part: $e_X$).** Apply `CategoryTheory.unit_conjugateEquiv_symm` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Adjunction/Mates.lean)) to $L_1=\mathrm{id}_X^{-1}\dashv R_1=(\mathrm{id}_X)_*$ (unit $\eta_{\mathrm{id}_X}$, [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image]), to the identity adjunction $L_2=\mathrm{id}\dashv R_2=\mathrm{id}$ on $\Ab(X_{\Et})$ with identity unit `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Adjunction/Basic.lean)), and to $\delta=d_X^{-1}:\mathrm{id}\xrightarrow{\sim}(\mathrm{id}_X)_*$ of step 2: there is a unique natural transformation $e_X:\mathrm{id}_X^{-1}\to\mathrm{id}$ with $(\mathrm{id}_X)_*(e_{X,G})\circ\eta_{\mathrm{id}_X,G}=d_{X,G}^{-1}$ for every $G$ $(\ast)$, and it is an isomorphism. By naturality of $d_X$ applied to the morphism $e_{X,G}:\mathrm{id}_X^{-1}G\to G$, $(\mathrm{id}_X)_*(e_{X,G})=d_{X,G}^{-1}\circ e_{X,G}\circ d_{X,\mathrm{id}_X^{-1}G}$; hence for any natural transformation $e':\mathrm{id}_X^{-1}\to\mathrm{id}$, $(\ast)$ for $e'$ is equivalent (compose with the isomorphism $d_{X,G}$ on the left) to $e'_G\circ d_{X,\mathrm{id}_X^{-1}G}\circ\eta_{\mathrm{id}_X,G}=\mathrm{id}_G$, the equation of clause (3). So $e_X$ is the unique isomorphism with $e_{X,G}\circ d_{X,\mathrm{id}_X^{-1}G}\circ\eta_{\mathrm{id}_X,G}=\mathrm{id}_G$.
