The tensor bicomplex is the image of $(P,Q)$ under the functor induced on chain complexes by the bifunctor $-\otimes_R-$ on $R$-modules. The differentials $d_P\otimes1$ and $1\otimes d_Q$ square to zero because $d_P^2=0$ and $d_Q^2=0$ and the tensor product is functorial in each variable, and they commute since $(d_P\otimes1)(1\otimes d_Q)=d_P\otimes d_Q=(1\otimes d_Q)(d_P\otimes1)$. For chain maps $a,b$ the termwise maps $a_p\otimes b_q$ commute with both differentials by functoriality of $\otimes$, so they form a morphism of bicomplexes.
