Flatness is Zariski local on the target, stable under base change, and descends along surjective flat quasi-compact morphisms (on affine pieces this is descent of flatness of modules along faithfully flat ring maps). Hence the fpqc family descent lemma [lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-family-descent-of-single-morphism-descent], applied to the covering sieve generated by $\{g_i\}$ and the cartesian squares formed by the fibre products $X\times_SS_i$, shows that $f$ is flat.
