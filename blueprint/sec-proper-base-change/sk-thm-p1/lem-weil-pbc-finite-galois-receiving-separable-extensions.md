title = "Finitely many finite separable extensions embed into one finite Galois extension"

Let $k$ be a field, $n\ge0$, and let $L_1,\dots,L_n$ be field extensions of $k$ which are finite ($\dim_kL_i<\infty$) and separable algebraic. Then there are a field extension $L/k$ and homomorphisms of $k$-algebras $\tau_i:L_i\to L$ ($i=1,\dots,n$) such that $L/k$ is finite ($\dim_kL<\infty$), separable algebraic, and normal: for every $\beta\in L$ the minimal polynomial of $\beta$ over $k$ is a product of polynomials of degree one in $L[T]$. (That is, $L/k$ is a finite Galois extension, Stacks, Fields, Definition 09I0, receiving every $L_i$.)
