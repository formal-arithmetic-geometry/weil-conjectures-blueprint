Let $u:Y\to X$ and $v:Z\to Y$ be morphisms of schemes [def-pc-scheme], $\Lambda$ a commutative ring and, for a scheme $T$, $\Mod(T_{\Et},\Lambda)$ the category of sheaves of $\Lambda_T$-modules on the small etale site $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], `CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean)), `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)). For $w\in\{u,v,u\circ v\}$ let $w^{-1}$ and $w_*$ be the inverse and direct images of sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules of [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (on underlying abelian sheaves the inverse image [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] and the direct image [def-weil-pbc-direct-image] of abelian sheaves). Let $d_{u,v}:(u\circ v)_*\xrightarrow{\sim}u_*\circ v_*$ and $c_{u,v}:v^{-1}\circ u^{-1}\xrightarrow{\sim}(u\circ v)^{-1}$ be the isomorphisms of functors on abelian sheaves of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (1), (2). Then: (1) for every $F\in\Mod(Z_{\Et},\Lambda)$ the morphism $d_{u,v,F}:(u\circ v)_*F\to u_*v_*F$ is $\Lambda_X$-linear for the module structures of [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact]; (2) for every $G\in\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$ the morphism $c_{u,v,G}:v^{-1}u^{-1}G\to(u\circ v)^{-1}G$ is $\Lambda_Z$-linear for the module structures of [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (that of $v^{-1}u^{-1}G$ obtained by applying the module inverse image twice). Hence $d_{u,v}$ and $c_{u,v}$ are isomorphisms of functors $\Mod(Z_{\Et},\Lambda)\to\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$ and $\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)\to\Mod(Z_{\Et},\Lambda)$ whose underlying morphisms of abelian sheaves are those of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition], and $c_{u,v}$ is the unique isomorphism of functors on modules carrying the unit $u_*\eta_vu^{-1}\circ\eta_u$ of the composite adjunction $v^{-1}u^{-1}\dashv u_*v_*$ to the unit $\eta_{u\circ v}$ transported along $d_{u,v}$ (the module form of Stacks Project, Modules on Sites, Lemma 03D8 (lemma-push-pull-composition-modules), for the morphisms of ringed sites $(w,\,w^{-1}\Lambda\cong\Lambda)$).
