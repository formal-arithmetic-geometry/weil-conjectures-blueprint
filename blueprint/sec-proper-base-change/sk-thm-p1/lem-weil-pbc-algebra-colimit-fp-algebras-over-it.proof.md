Smallness and nonemptiness. Mathlib's CostructuredArrow smallness instance applies because P.FullSubcategory is small and algebra homomorphisms lie in Type (max u v). Use the finitely presented algebra ULift R and its structure map to Lambda, transported to a representative in P, to obtain an object. (`Algebra.FinitePresentation` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean)), `CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean))).

Common successors. For objects (A, alpha) and (B, beta), A tensor_R B is finitely presented by base change and transitivity. Its two inclusions and the multiplication map induced by alpha and beta give a common successor after transport to its representative in P. (`Algebra.FinitePresentation` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean)), `Algebra.FinitePresentation.baseChange, Algebra.FinitePresentation.trans` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FiniteStability.lean))).

Equalize parallel arrows. A finite polynomial presentation of their source gives finitely many generators. In the target form the ideal generated by the differences of the images of these generators. It is finitely generated and killed by the target map to Lambda. The quotient is finitely presented and its quotient map equalizes the two maps: precompose with the surjective polynomial presentation and use MvPolynomial.algHom_ext. Transport the quotient to P and form a costructured-arrow morphism. Together with the preceding two steps this constructs IsFiltered. (`Algebra.FinitePresentation` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean)), `Algebra.FinitePresentation.quotient` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean)), `CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean))).

Every element of Lambda is represented in a stage. Evaluate the finitely presented polynomial algebra R[X] at the element, using a lifted singleton variable type to stay in the required universe. Transport this algebra to a representative in P. The image of its variable is the required element. (`Algebra.FinitePresentation` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean))).

Equality is witnessed at a later stage. For elements a,b in a stage A with equal image in Lambda, quotient A by the principal ideal (a-b). This finitely generated ideal is killed by the stage map, which factors through the finitely presented quotient. Transport to P; the resulting arrow sends a and b to equal elements. (`Algebra.FinitePresentation` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean)), `Algebra.FinitePresentation.quotient` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean))).

Colimit universal property. Types.FilteredColimit.isColimitOf' applied to the canonical underlying set cocone and the preceding representation and equality conditions gives a colimit in sets. Any two elements of Lambda can be represented in one common stage by filteredness. For an algebra cocone, its unique underlying set desc map therefore preserves addition and multiplication by computing both elements at this common stage. A fixed stage verifies zero, one and compatibility with R. Bundle this function as an algebra homomorphism. The set-colimit fac equations give its factorization, and representation of every element proves uniqueness. This constructs IsColimit of the canonical algebra cocone directly, without a separate algebra colimit. (`CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean))).
