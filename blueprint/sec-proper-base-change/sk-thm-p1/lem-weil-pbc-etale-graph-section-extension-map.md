Let $\pi:U\to X$ be a finite etale morphism of schemes `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over, CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/MorphismProperty/Comma.lean)), [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions], let $\Lambda$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a sheaf of $\Lambda_U$-modules on the small etale site $U_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves], `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)). For an object $V$ of $X_{\Et}$ write $V_U=V\times_XU$ `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)) with projections $p_V:V_U\to V$ and $q_V:V_U\to U$; $(V_U,q_V)$ is an object of $U_{\Et}$ and $(V_U,\,V_U\to V\to X)$ one of $X_{\Et}$ `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.instPreservesFiniteLimitsTopPullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/MorphismProperty.lean)). For an $X$-morphism $\varphi:V\to U$ write $(V,\varphi)$ for $V$ regarded as an etale $U$-scheme and $\Gamma_\varphi=(\mathrm{id}_V,\varphi):V\to V_U$ for its graph. Then:

1. $(V,\varphi)$ is an object of $U_{\Et}$; $\Gamma_\varphi$ is a morphism $(V,\varphi)\to(V_U,q_V)$ of $U_{\Et}$; its image $\Gamma_\varphi(V)$ is open and closed in $V_U$, and $\Gamma_\varphi$ is an isomorphism of $V$ onto the open subscheme $\Gamma_\varphi(V)$.
2. For every $s\in M(V,\varphi)$ there is exactly one $e_\varphi(s)\in M(V_U,q_V)$ such that $M(\Gamma_\varphi)(e_\varphi(s))=s$ and the restriction of $e_\varphi(s)$ to the open subscheme $V_U\setminus\Gamma_\varphi(V)$ is $0$; the map $e_\varphi:M(V,\varphi)\to M(V_U,q_V)$ is $\Lambda$-linear.
3. Let $P_M$ be the presheaf of $\Lambda$-modules on $X_{\Et}$ of [def-weil-pbc-etale-sum-extension] for $v=\pi$: $P_M(V)=\bigoplus_{\varphi\in\operatorname{Hom}_X(V,U)}M(V,\varphi)$, the restriction along a morphism $a:V'\to V$ of $X_{\Et}$ sending the summand of $\varphi$ to the summand of $\varphi a$ by $M(a:(V',\varphi a)\to(V,\varphi))$. Then the maps
$$
c^p_{M,V}:P_M(V)\longrightarrow M(V_U,q_V)=(\pi_*M)(V),\qquad (s_\varphi)_\varphi\longmapsto\sum_\varphi e_\varphi(s_\varphi),
$$
with $\pi_*$ the direct image [def-weil-pbc-direct-image], form a morphism of presheaves of $\Lambda$-modules $c^p_M:P_M\to\pi_*M$ on $X_{\Et}$, and $c^p_M$ is natural in $M$: $\pi_*\alpha\circ c^p_M=c^p_{M'}\circ P_\alpha$ for every morphism $\alpha:M\to M'$ of sheaves of $\Lambda_U$-modules (with $P_\alpha=\bigoplus_\varphi\alpha_{(V,\varphi)}$). The key point for compatibility with restriction is that for $a:V'\to V$ in $X_{\Et}$ the preimage of $\Gamma_\varphi(V)$ under $a\times_X\mathrm{id}_U:V'_U\to V_U$ is $\Gamma_{\varphi a}(V')$.
