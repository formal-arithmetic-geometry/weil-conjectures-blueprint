The identity B-algebra is an object. Spec sends its identity to the identity, which is etale. The induced quotient map is injective by Ideal.quotientMap_injective' and surjective by Ideal.quotientMap_surjective. This gives nonemptiness of the full subcategory. For objects C and D, take C tensor_B D and its two factor maps. The existing proved Algebra.Etale.tensorProduct instance is base change followed by composition. To verify the quotient condition, turn the canonical quotient maps B/J to C/JC and D/JD into algebra equivalences. Compose their inverses with the quotient projections and use the tensor universal property to obtain a map C tensor_B D to B/J killing J. Descend it to the quotient and check the two inverse identities using quotient and tensor extensionality. This fully proved private helper quotient_tensor_bijective shows that the tensor product is an object. Package the factor maps as morphisms of the under category and then of its full subcategory. For parallel maps f,g:C to D, put S=C tensor_B C, m:S to C the multiplication, k:S to D the map c tensor c' to f(c)g(c'), and T=D tensor_S C. This is the diagonal-base-change presentation of the same coequalizer as the source. The multiplication map is etale by cancellation because S and C are etale over B; its base change D to T is etale, so B to T is etale by composition. The quotient of S by J is B/J by quotient_tensor_bijective. In a tower B to S to A, extension of J composes (Ideal.map_map), and the quotient maps compose. Cancel the bijective map B/J to S/JS: the fully proved private helper quotient_tower_bijective_iff yields that the quotients of D and C by JS are both S/JS. Apply quotient_tensor_bijective over S to T, then compose back to obtain B/J to T/JT bijective. Finally, tensor balance for c tensor 1 and 1 tensor c in S gives f(c) tensor 1 = 1 tensor c = g(c) tensor 1 in T. Extensionality in rings, the under category, and the full subcategory shows f followed by D to T equals g followed by D to T. The three constructions give IsFiltered.
