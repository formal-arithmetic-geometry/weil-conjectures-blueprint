Map Q(R) into the fraction field K of R_p. Nonzerodivisors remain nonzero in R_p by the localization equality criterion, hence become units in K. Map the integral equation and use integral closedness to write the image of x as an element z of R_p. (`LocalizedModule` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)), `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)), `integralClosure, IsIntegral, IsIntegrallyClosedIn` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/Algebra/Basic.lean)), `IsIntegrallyClosed, IsDomain, Localization.AtPrime` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IntegrallyClosed.lean))).

Write x=a/b and z=c/t, with b regular and t outside p. Equality in K implies ta=bc in R_p, since R_p embeds in K. A multiplier u outside p clears this equality in R. Cancel the unit image of b in Q(R) to get (ut)x=uc.
