Extract a nonzero class of y modulo xR with annihilator m from the associated-prime hypothesis, using the Noetherian associated-prime characterization. The quotient QuotSMulTop x R is the module R/xR. Thus y is outside xR and y m is contained in xR. (`IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean))).

*Subcase $y\mathfrak m\subseteq x\mathfrak m$: property (4) with $R'=R[y/x]\subseteq R_x$.* For $f\in\mathfrak m$ write $yf=xg$ with $g\in\mathfrak m$; $g$ is unique because $x$ is a nonzerodivisor, so $\psi(f)=g$ defines a map $\psi:\mathfrak m\to\mathfrak m$, which is $R$-linear by the uniqueness. $\mathfrak m$ is a finite $R$-module since $R$ is Noetherian `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)), so by `LinearMap.exists_monic_and_aeval_eq_zero` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Charpoly/LinearMap.lean)) there is a monic $P=T^d+a_{d-1}T^{d-1}+\cdots+a_0\in R[T]$ `Polynomial` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Polynomial/Basic.lean)) with $P(\psi)=0$. Let $R_x$ be the localization of $R$ at $x$ `Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean)). The map $R\to R_x$, $r\mapsto r/1$, is injective: $r/1=0$ means $x^kr=0$ for some $k$, so $r=0$. Put $s=y/x\in R_x$. For $f\in\mathfrak m$, $x\psi(f)=yf$ gives $\psi(f)/1=s\cdot(f/1)$, hence $\psi^i(f)/1=s^i(f/1)$ for all $i\ge0$ by induction, and $0=P(\psi)(x)/1=P(s)\cdot(x/1)$; since $x/1$ is a unit of $R_x$, $P(s)=0$. So $s$ is integral over $R$, and the subring $R'=R[s]\subseteq R_x$ generated by the image of $R$ and $s$ is a finitely generated $R$-module by `Algebra.finite_adjoin_of_finite_of_isIntegral` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegral/Basic.lean)) (one generator $b_1=s$). Let $\varphi:R\to R'$ be the inclusion. Then: $R'\ne0$ ($R\ne0$ embeds); $\ker\varphi=0$; $\varphi$ is not surjective, since $s=r/1$ with $r\in R$ would give $x^k(y-xr)=0$, so $y=xr\in xR$, contradicting y not in xR; $\mathfrak mR'\subseteq\varphi(R)$: $\mathfrak m s\subseteq\mathfrak m$ (as $fs=\psi(f)/1$), hence $\mathfrak m s^i\subseteq\mathfrak m$ for all $i$ by induction, and $R'=\sum_iRs^i$; so (4) holds with $n=1$ (and $\mathfrak m^1\ker\varphi=0$). Finally $\mathfrak m$ is not an associated prime of $R'$: $x$ is a unit of $R_x$, so $xz\ne0$ for $0\ne z\in R'$, i.e. $x\notin\operatorname{Ann}(z)$ while $x\in\mathfrak m$; and $\operatorname{Ann}(0)=R\ne\mathfrak m$.

*Subcase $y\mathfrak m\not\subseteq x\mathfrak m$: property (2).* Pick $t_0\in\mathfrak m$ with $yt_0\notin x\mathfrak m$; as $yt_0\in xR$, $yt_0=xu$ with $u\in R$, and $u\notin\mathfrak m$, so $u$ is a unit of the local ring $R$. With $t=u^{-1}t_0\in\mathfrak m$ we get $yt=x$. Then $y$ is a nonzerodivisor: $yz=0$ gives $xz=tyz=0$, so $z=0$. For $t'\in\mathfrak m$ write $yt'=xs$ with $s\in R$; then $y(t'-st)=xs-sx=0$, so $t'=st$. Hence $\mathfrak m=tR$ is generated by one element, and $\dim R\le1$ by `ringKrullDim_le_spanFinrank_maximalIdeal` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/KrullsHeightTheorem.lean)). Also $\dim R\ge1$ `ringKrullDim` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/Basic.lean)): $R\ne0$ has the prime $\mathfrak m$; if $\dim R=0$, every prime ideal $\mathfrak p$ satisfies $\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak m$ hence $\mathfrak p=\mathfrak m$, so $\mathfrak m$ is a minimal prime of $R$ and by `Module.associatedPrimes.minimalPrimes_annihilator_subset_associatedPrimes` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/AssociatedPrime/Localization.lean)) there is $z\in R$ with $\operatorname{Ann}_R(z)=\mathfrak m$, which the regular element x in m excludes. So $\dim R=1$ and $\mathfrak m$ is generated by $\dim R=1$ element: $R$ is a regular local ring `IsRegularLocalRing, IsRegularRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean)) of dimension $1$, property (2). This is the regular alternative in the stated disjunction.
