Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a local ring and $R\to B$ a ring map making $B$ a finitely generated $R$-module. Then every maximal ideal $\mathfrak n$ of $B$ has preimage $\mathfrak m$ in $R$, hence contains $\mathfrak mB$; consequently $\mathfrak mB$ is contained in the Jacobson radical of $B$, i.e. $1+x$ is a unit of $B$ for every $x\in\mathfrak mB$.
