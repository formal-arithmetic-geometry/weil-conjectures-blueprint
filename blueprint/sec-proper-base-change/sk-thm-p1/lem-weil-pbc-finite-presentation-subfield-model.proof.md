Choose a finite presentation E ≅ k[X₁,...,Xₙ]/J with J finitely generated, using Algebra.FinitePresentation.iff. Choose a finite set s of ideal generators. (`Algebra.FinitePresentation` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean))).

The union of the finite sets of nonzero coefficients of the polynomials in s is finite. Induction on this set, using hUnion, hdir and Nonempty I in the empty case, gives i with every coefficient in K i. MvPolynomial.mem_range_map_iff_coeffs_subset then lifts every relation to a polynomial over K i. Let J₀ be the ideal generated by these lifts and Q the quotient polynomial algebra over K i.

Polynomial scalar extension MvPolynomial.algebraTensorAlgEquiv identifies k ⊗[K i] (K i)[X₁,...,Xₙ] with k[X₁,...,Xₙ]. Tensoring the quotient uses Algebra.TensorProduct.tensorQuotientEquiv, the quotient-base-change identity in its direct tensor formulation. Under the polynomial equivalence the extended ideal maps to J: Ideal.map_map and Ideal.map_span reduce this to the fact that each lifted relation maps to its original generator. Ideal.quotientEquivAlg thus gives k ⊗[K i] Q ≅ E. (`MvPolynomial.algebraTensorAlgEquiv` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/TensorProduct/MvPolynomial.lean)), `Algebra.TensorProduct.quotIdealMapEquivTensorQuot` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/TensorProduct/Quotient.lean))).

Take B = ULift Q in Type (max u w). Tensor congruence with ULift.algEquiv transports the preceding equivalence and gives the claimed model in the required universe. This also covers zero algebras and presentations with no relations or no variables.
