Let $\ell$ be a prime, $P$ a group and $M$ a representation of $P$ on a finite-dimensional $\mathbf Z/\ell\mathbf Z$-vector space; put $n=\dim M$. Let $(F_i)_{i\in\mathbf N}$ be subspaces of $M$ with $F_0=0$ and $F_n=M$, such that $F_i$ is $P$-stable for every $i\le n$ and, for every $i<n$, $F_i\subseteq F_{i+1}$, $\dim F_{i+1}=i+1$ and $gx-x\in F_i$ for all $g\in P$ and $x\in F_{i+1}$. Then there exist $m\in\mathbf N$, representations $(W_i)_{i\in\mathbf N}$ of $P$ over $\mathbf Z/\ell\mathbf Z$ and morphisms of representations $\iota_i:W_i\to W_{i+1}$ such that $W_0=0$, $W_m\cong M$, and for every $i<m$ the morphism $\iota_i$ is a monomorphism whose cokernel is isomorphic to the trivial one-dimensional representation $\mathbf Z/\ell\mathbf Z$. (A representation is a vector space with a linear action of $P$; morphisms are equivariant linear maps.)
