Let ℓ be prime, P any group, and M a finite-dimensional representation over ℤ/ℓℤ. Let F_i be vector subspaces, n=dim M, F_0=0, F_n=M, all F_i stable for i≤n, and for each i<n assume F_i⊆F_(i+1), dim F_(i+1)=i+1, and g*x-x∈F_i for g∈P and x∈F_(i+1). Then there are representations W_i and morphisms ι_i:W_i→W_(i+1), with W_0 zero, W_n≅M and, for i<n, ι_i monic and its cokernel isomorphic to the trivial representation ULift(ℤ/ℓℤ). A representation is a vector space with a linear group action; morphisms are equivariant linear maps. All indices range over natural numbers; the quotient conditions stop at n. This statement contains no scheme or cover.
