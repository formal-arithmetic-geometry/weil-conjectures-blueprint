Let $\varphi:A\to B$ be the given homomorphism, $T=\varphi(A\setminus\mathfrak p)$, $B_{\mathfrak p}=T^{-1}B$ and $\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak p}$ the ideal of $B_{\mathfrak p}$ generated by $\varphi(\mathfrak p)$. By `Ideal.Fiber.algEquivAux₁, Ideal.Fiber.algEquivQuotient, Algebra.TensorProduct.comm` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/ResidueField/Fiber.lean)) there is an isomorphism of $B$-algebras (in particular of rings) $\theta:B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)\xrightarrow{\sim}B_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak p}$, $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ as in `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)).

$T$ is a multiplicative subset of $B$ ($1=\varphi(1)\in T$ and $\varphi(s)\varphi(s')=\varphi(ss')$ with $ss'\in A\setminus\mathfrak p$ since $\mathfrak p$ is prime), and $B\to B_{\mathfrak p}$, $b\mapsto b/1$, is the localization of $B$ at $T$. Since $B$ is Noetherian `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)), $B_{\mathfrak p}$ is Noetherian by `IsLocalization.isNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Submodule.lean)).

The composite $\theta^{-1}\circ\pi:B_{\mathfrak p}\to B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ of the quotient map $\pi:B_{\mathfrak p}\to B_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak p}$ with the inverse of the isomorphism $\theta$ of step 1 is a surjective ring homomorphism from the Noetherian ring $B_{\mathfrak p}$ (step 2). Hence $B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ is Noetherian by `isNoetherianRing_of_surjective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Basic.lean)).
