Choose a finite polynomial presentation of C. Clear the finitely many denominators of the generator images with a product s₀ in S. Each relation evaluated at these images vanishes after localization at S; choose its annihilating denominator using the localization equality criterion. Multiply these witnesses with s₀. At this principal localization all generators are defined and all relations vanish, giving f. The localization universal property gives k, and equality on the generators proves k.comp f = g. Empty products are 1 and 0 in S is allowed.
