title = "Ind-smoothness is compatible with successive quotients"
lean = ["Algebra.ind_smooth_quotient_compatibility"]

Let R → Λ be a homomorphism of commutative rings, and let K ≤ I be ideals of R. If Λ/IΛ is a filtered colimit of smooth R/I-algebras `CategoryTheory.ObjectProperty.ind` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/ObjectProperty/Ind.lean)), then (Λ/KΛ)/(I/K)(Λ/KΛ) is a filtered colimit of smooth (R/K)/(I/K)-algebras.
