Let $R\to\Lambda$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings, and let $K\subseteq I$ be ideals of $R$. If $\Lambda/I\Lambda$ is a filtered colimit, in the category of commutative $R/I$-algebras, of smooth $R/I$-algebras, then $(\Lambda/K\Lambda)/(I/K)(\Lambda/K\Lambda)$ is a filtered colimit, in the category of commutative $(R/K)/(I/K)$-algebras, of smooth $(R/K)/(I/K)$-algebras.
