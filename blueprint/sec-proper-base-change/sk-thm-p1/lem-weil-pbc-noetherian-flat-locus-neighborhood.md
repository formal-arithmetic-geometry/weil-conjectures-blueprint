title = "A flat point of a finite module over a Noetherian base has a flat basic neighborhood"
lean = ["Module.Flat.exists_notMem_forall_flat_localizedModule"]

Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring, $R\to S$ a finite-type homomorphism of commutative rings, and $M$ a finitely generated $S$-module. Let $\mathfrak q\subset S$ be prime. If $M_{\mathfrak q}$, the localization at $S\setminus\mathfrak q$, is flat as an $R$-module, then there exists $g\in S\setminus\mathfrak q$ such that for every prime $\mathfrak q'\subset S$ with $g\notin\mathfrak q'$, the $R$-module $M_{\mathfrak q'}$ is flat. Thus the basic Zariski open $D(g)=\{\mathfrak q':g\notin\mathfrak q'\}$ is contained in the flat locus.
