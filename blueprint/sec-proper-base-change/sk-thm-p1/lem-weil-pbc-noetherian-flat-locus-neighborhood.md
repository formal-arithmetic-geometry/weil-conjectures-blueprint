title = "A flat point of a finite module over a Noetherian base has a flat basic neighborhood"
lean = ["Module.Flat.exists_notMem_forall_flat_localizedModule"]

Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)), $R\to S$ a finite-type homomorphism of commutative rings `Algebra.FiniteType` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FiniteType.lean)), and $M$ a finitely generated $S$-module `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)). Let $\mathfrak q\subset S$ be prime. If $M_{\mathfrak q}$, the localization at $S\setminus\mathfrak q$ `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)), is flat as an $R$-module `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)), then there exists $g\in S\setminus\mathfrak q$ such that for every prime $\mathfrak q'\subset S$ with $g\notin\mathfrak q'$, the $R$-module $M_{\mathfrak q'}$ is flat. Thus the basic Zariski open $D(g)=\{\mathfrak q':g\notin\mathfrak q'\}$ `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)) is contained in the flat locus.
