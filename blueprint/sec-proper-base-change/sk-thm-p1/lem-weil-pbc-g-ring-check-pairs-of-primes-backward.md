Assume all quotient formal fibres for pairs q ≤ p are Noetherian and geometrically regular. The same quotient formal-fibre identification, together with flatness and the characterization of regular maps by geometrically regular fibres, shows each localization-to-completion map is regular, hence R is a G-ring.
