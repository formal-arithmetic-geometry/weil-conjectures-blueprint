The reverse direction of the proved private helper charpoly_standardCover_comparison proves the universal base-change property from the local identities. For A → A′ and a basis over A′, use Ideal.map_span and Ideal.map_top to show the images of f generate the unit ideal. At T = A′[1/ρ(f_i)], factor A → T through A[1/f_i] using IsLocalization.Away.lift. Apply charpoly_tower to this factorization and to A → A′ → T. The images of χ and the given matrix characteristic polynomial agree at every localization. Localization.algebraMap_injective_of_span_eq_top applied coefficientwise proves their equality.
