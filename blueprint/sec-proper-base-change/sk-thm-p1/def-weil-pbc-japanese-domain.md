Let $R$ be an integral domain with field of fractions $K$. (1) $R$ is *N-1* if the integral closure of $R$ in $K$ is a finitely generated $R$-module. (2) $R$ is *N-2*, or *Japanese*, if for every finite field extension $L/K$ (i.e. $\dim_KL<\infty$) the integral closure of $R$ in $L$, where $L$ is an $R$-algebra through the composite $R\subseteq K\to L$, is a finitely generated $R$-module. Taking $L=K$ shows that an N-2 domain is N-1.
