This is Stacks, Algebra, Lemma 00OF. Since $S$ is regular, every finite $S$-module has finite projective dimension. Let $\kappa=R/\mathfrak m_R$ and take a minimal free resolution $F_\bullet\to\kappa$ over $R$. Since $\varphi$ is flat, $F_\bullet\otimes_RS$ is a free resolution of $\kappa\otimes_RS=S/\mathfrak m_RS$ over $S$, which is minimal because $\varphi$ is local. As $S/\mathfrak m_RS$ has finite projective dimension over $S$, the minimal resolution $F_\bullet\otimes_RS$ is finite, hence so is $F_\bullet$. Thus $\kappa$ has finite projective dimension over $R$, and the Noetherian local ring $R$ is regular by Serre's criterion.
