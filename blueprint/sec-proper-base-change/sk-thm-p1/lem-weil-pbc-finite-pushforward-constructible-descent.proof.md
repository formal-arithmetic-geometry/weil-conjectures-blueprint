**Step 1.** *Notation; $X$ and $Y$ are Noetherian.* Write $G=f_*F$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image], a torsion sheaf on $Y_{\Et}$ by hypothesis, and $F'=f^{-1}G=f^{-1}f_*F$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], with counit $\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{f,F}:F'\to F$ of the adjunction $f^{-1}\dashv f_*$. $X$ and $Y$ are of finite type over the field $k$, hence Noetherian schemes, and their underlying spaces are Noetherian topological spaces; so every locally closed subset of $X$ is constructible [def-weil-constructible-subset] (Stacks Topology 005L, recorded there). Since $X$ is separated of finite type over the algebraically closed $k$ with $\dim X\le1$, a torsion abelian sheaf on $X_{\Et}$ makes a curve coefficient pair with $X$ [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting].

**Step 2.** *The counit is surjective on every geometric stalk.* Let $\bar x:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to X$ be a geometric point [def-weil-geometric-point] and $\bar y=f\circ\bar x$. By [lem-weil-pbc-counit-stalk-germ-restriction], $\varepsilon_{\bar x}=r_{f,\bar x,F}\circ c_{f,\bar x,G}$, where $c_{f,\bar x,G}:F'_{\bar x}\to G_{\bar y}$ is the isomorphism of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-stalk-germ] and $r_{f,\bar x,F}:G_{\bar y}=(f_*F)_{\bar y}\to F_{\bar x}$ is the restriction of germs. As $f$ is finite, $\bar x$ belongs to the finite set $P(\bar y)$ of geometric points of $X$ over $\bar y$ with field $\Omega$, and by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-stalk-formula-restriction] the map $(r_{f,\bar x',F})_{\bar x'\in P(\bar y)}:(f_*F)_{\bar y}\to\prod_{\bar x'\in P(\bar y)}F_{\bar x'}$ is an isomorphism (the finite stalk formula [lemma-weil-pbc-finite-stalk-formula] with its components named). So $r_{f,\bar x,F}$ is this isomorphism followed by the projection onto the factor $\bar x$, which is surjective; composed with the isomorphism $c_{f,\bar x,G}$, $\varepsilon_{\bar x}$ is surjective.

**Step 3.** *The counit is an epimorphism.* Let $Q=\operatorname{Coker}\varepsilon$ in $\Ab(X_{\Et})$ [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves]. For every point $x\in X$ choose a geometric point $\bar x$ over it (Spec of an algebraic closure of $\kappa(x)$ [def-weil-geometric-point]); these geometric points cover $X$. Stalk functors are exact [prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact], so $Q_{\bar x}=\operatorname{Coker}(\varepsilon_{\bar x})=0$ by step 2. Hence the morphism $Q\to0$ is injective on the stalks of a covering family of geometric points, so it is a monomorphism [lem-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-detect-mono], i.e. $Q=0$; in the abelian category $\Ab(X_{\Et})$ a morphism with zero cokernel is an epimorphism. With $K=\operatorname{Ker}\varepsilon\subseteq F'$ and $\kappa:K\to F'$ the inclusion, $0\to K\xrightarrow{\kappa}F'\xrightarrow{\varepsilon}F\to0$ is a short exact sequence in $\Ab(X_{\Et})$.

**Step 4.** *$F'=f^{-1}f_*F$ is constructible (Stacks 095G for abelian sheaves).* By [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion] for the curve coefficient pair $(Y,G)$ there are a finite partition $Y=\coprod_{i=1}^rY_i$ into locally closed constructible subsets and immersions $u_i:Z_i\to Y$ with image $Y_i$ such that $u_i^{-1}G$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] is locally constant [def-constructible-locally-constant] with finite geometric stalks. Let $Z_i'=Z_i\times_YX$ with projections $u_i':Z_i'\to X$ and $f_i:Z_i'\to Z_i$, so $f\circ u_i'=u_i\circ f_i$. (a) *The strata.* By [lemma-constructible-pullback-stratification] the $u_i'$ are immersions forming a stratification of $X$ [def-constructible-stratification] with strata $X_i=f^{-1}(Y_i)$: pairwise disjoint locally closed subsets covering $X$. Each $X_i$ is constructible, $X$ being Noetherian (step 1). So $(u_i':Z_i'\to X)_i$ is a finite partition of $X$ into locally closed constructible subschemes. (b) *Local constancy.* By [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (2), applied to $(f,u_i')$ and to $(u_i,f_i)$, there are isomorphisms $u_i'^{-1}F'=u_i'^{-1}f^{-1}G\cong(f\circ u_i')^{-1}G=(u_i\circ f_i)^{-1}G\cong f_i^{-1}(u_i^{-1}G)$. The sheaf $f_i^{-1}(u_i^{-1}G)$ is locally constant by [lemma-constructible-pullback-locally-constant] (coefficients: abelian groups), hence so is $u_i'^{-1}F'$ by [lemma-constructible-iso-invariance]. (c) *Finite stalks.* For a geometric point $\bar z$ of $Z_i'$, $(u_i'^{-1}F')_{\bar z}\cong(f_i^{-1}u_i^{-1}G)_{\bar z}\cong(u_i^{-1}G)_{f_i\circ\bar z}$ [lemma-openclosed-stalk-upperstar], a stalk of $u_i^{-1}G$ at the geometric point $f_i\circ\bar z$ of $Z_i$ (same algebraically closed field), which is finite. (d) $F'$ is torsion: for every geometric point $\bar x$ of $X$, $F'_{\bar x}\cong G_{f\circ\bar x}$ [lemma-openclosed-stalk-upperstar] is a torsion group since $G$ is torsion ((1)$\Rightarrow$(3) of [lem-weil-pbc-torsion-sheaf-characterizations]), so $F'$ is torsion by (3)$\Rightarrow$(1),(2) there, and $(X,F')$ is a curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting]. By (a)-(c), $F'$ is constructible in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion].

**Step 5.** *$K$ is torsion.* $\kappa$ is a monomorphism, so for every geometric point $\bar x$ of $X$ the map $\kappa_{\bar x}:K_{\bar x}\to F'_{\bar x}$ is injective [prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact]; $F'_{\bar x}$ is torsion (step 4 (d)), hence so is its subgroup $K_{\bar x}$, and $K$ is torsion by (3)$\Rightarrow$(1),(2) of [lem-weil-pbc-torsion-sheaf-characterizations]. So $(X,K)$, $(X,F')$, $(X,F)$ are curve coefficient pairs [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting] ($F$ is torsion by hypothesis).

**Step 6.** *Conclusion.* Apply [lem-weil-pbc-curve-constructible-sub-quotient] on $C=X$ to the short exact sequence $0\to K\to F'\to F\to0$ of step 3, whose three terms are torsion (step 5) and whose middle term $F'$ is constructible in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion] (step 4): the quotient $F$ is constructible in the same sense.
