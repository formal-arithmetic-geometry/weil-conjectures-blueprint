Let $\varphi:R\to S$ be a flat ring map of commutative rings `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)) and $x\in R$. Write $\mathrm{Ann}_R(x)=\{r\in R:rx=0\}$ and $\mathrm{Ann}_S(\varphi(x))=\{s\in S:s\varphi(x)=0\}$. Then $\mathrm{Ann}_S(\varphi(x))=\mathrm{Ann}_R(x)S$, the ideal of $S$ generated by $\varphi(\mathrm{Ann}_R(x))$.
