$M$ is finitely presented: since $M$ is finitely generated and projective, a surjection $A^{\oplus n}\to M$ splits, its kernel $K$ is a direct summand of $A^{\oplus n}$, hence finitely generated, and $A^{\oplus m}\to A^{\oplus n}\to M\to0$ is exact for a surjection $A^{\oplus m}\to K$ `Module.finitePresentation_of_projective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/FinitePresentation.lean)). In Lean, install Module.FinitePresentation A M using Module.finitePresentation_of_projective A M.

Fix the prime $\mathfrak p$. Since $M$ is finite projective, its localization $M_{\mathfrak p}$ `LocalizedModule` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)) is a finite free module over the local ring $A_{\mathfrak p}$ `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)) `Module.isLocallyConstant_rankAtStalk` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/FreeLocus.lean)), first clause; say $M_{\mathfrak p}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}^{\oplus r}$. In Lean, localization preserves finiteness and flatness by instances; Module.free_of_flat_of_isLocalRing gives freeness, and Module.finBasis chooses a basis indexed by Fin (Module.finrank (Localization.AtPrime p) (LocalizedModule p.primeCompl M)). Only the stalk-freeness clause of the cited rank lemma is used. (`Module.isLocallyConstant_rankAtStalk` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/FreeLocus.lean))).

Apply the basis-lifting form of `Module.FinitePresentation.exists_free_localizedModule_powers` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Free.lean)), namely Module.FinitePresentation.exists_basis_localizedModule_powers, with S = p.primeCompl, the map LocalizedModule.mkLinearMap p.primeCompl M, the ring Localization.AtPrime p, and the finite basis from step 2. It returns f in p.primeCompl and a basis of LocalizedModule.Away f M over Localization.Away f with the same finite index set. Membership in p.primeCompl is definitionally f not in p. The coordinate equivalence b'.repr of this lifted basis proves M_f is isomorphic to the direct sum of r copies of A_f `LocalizedModule.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)).
