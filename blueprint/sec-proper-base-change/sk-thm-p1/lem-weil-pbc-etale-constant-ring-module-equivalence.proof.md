Form R as the sheafification of the constant ring presheaf. View a module-valued sheaf as a presheaf of modules over that constant presheaf. PresheafOfModules.sheafify applied to the identity of its underlying abelian sheaf extends the scalar action to R. PresheafOfModules.sheafifyMap gives the functor on morphisms; identity and composition hold sectionwise. The underlying abelian presheaf is definitionally unchanged. ([def-weil-pbc-etale-site], `CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean)), `CategoryTheory.presheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean)), `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)), `CategoryTheory.Sheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheaf.lean))).

Restrict scalars along the constant ring presheaf sheafification map. Reinterpret the resulting constant-ring module presheaf as a ModuleCat-valued presheaf. Its abelian presheaf is the original one, hence the forgetful functor which reflects the sheaf condition shows this is a sheaf. Restrict each morphism to define the inverse functor. (`CategoryTheory.presheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean)), `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)), `CategoryTheory.Sheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheaf.lean))).

The canonical map PresheafOfModules.toSheafify has sectionwise identity maps when the abelian sheafification map is the identity. Each component is therefore a bijective linear map, giving a natural unit isomorphism. For the counit, PresheafOfModules.sheafifyHomEquiv extends the identity of the scalar-restricted presheaf. The equality comp_toPresheaf_map_sheafifyHomEquiv'_symm_hom proves that its underlying map is the identity. Its bijective linear components define an isomorphism of module presheaves via PresheafOfModules.isoMk; the fully faithful inclusion of module sheaves lifts this isomorphism. Naturality is equality on sections. Functor.IsEquivalence.mk' applied to the two natural isomorphisms proves the equivalence. The constructions cover the zero ring and the empty scheme.
