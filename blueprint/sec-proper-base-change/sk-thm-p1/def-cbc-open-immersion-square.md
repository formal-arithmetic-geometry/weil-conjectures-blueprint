An *open-immersion square* is a pair consisting of a
cartesian square

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
U'&\xrightarrow{g_U}&U\\
j'\downarrow&&\downarrow j\\
X'&\xrightarrow{g}&X
\end{array}
$$

in which $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ and $j':U'\hookrightarrow X'$ are
open immersions, so that
$U'=U\times_XX'$; no hypothesis on $g$ is made.
