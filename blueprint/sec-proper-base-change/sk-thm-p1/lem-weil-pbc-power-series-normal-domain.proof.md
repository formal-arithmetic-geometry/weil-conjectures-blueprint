$R[[x]]$ is Noetherian by `MvPowerSeries.isNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/Equiv.lean)) (case $n=1$), and it is an integral domain by `MvPowerSeries.instNoZeroDivisors` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/NoZeroDivisors.lean)) (case $d=1$), since $R$ is a domain. In particular its field of fractions $\operatorname{Frac}(R[[x]])$ is defined, and the normality to be shown is: every $w\in\operatorname{Frac}(R[[x]])$ integral over $R[[x]]$ lies in $R[[x]]$ `IsIntegrallyClosed, IsDomain, Localization.AtPrime` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IntegrallyClosed.lean)).

Let $K=\operatorname{Frac}(R)$. The coefficientwise inclusion $R[[x]]\to K[[x]]$ followed by the inclusion $K[[x]]\to K((x))$ of `LaurentSeries, HahnSeries.ofPowerSeries, HahnSeries.ofPowerSeries_injective, HahnSeries.order` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LaurentSeries.lean)) is an injective ring homomorphism $\iota\colon R[[x]]\to K((x))$ (both maps keep all coefficients and $R\to K$ is injective); its image is the set of Laurent series $\sum_ia_ix^i$ with $a_i\in R$ for all $i$ and $a_i=0$ for $i<0$. By `HahnSeries.instField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/HahnSeries/Summable.lean)), $K((x))$ is a field, so by `IsFractionRing.lift, IsFractionRing.lift_algebraMap, IsFractionRing.lift_unique, RingHom.injective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/FractionRing.lean)) applied to the domain $A=R[[x]]$ (step 1) and $g=\iota$, $\iota$ extends to an injective ring homomorphism $\tilde\iota\colon\operatorname{Frac}(R[[x]])\to K((x))$. For $u\in\operatorname{Frac}(R[[x]])$ we write $\tilde\iota(u)=\sum_ia_ix^i$ with $a_i\in K$, and for $u\ne0$ this is $a_nx^n+a_{n+1}x^{n+1}+\cdots$ with $n\in\mathbf Z$ its order and $a_n\neq0$. (`MvPowerSeries.instNoZeroDivisors` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/NoZeroDivisors.lean))).

Claim: if $v\in\operatorname{Frac}(R[[x]])$ is nonzero and integral over $R[[x]]$, and $\tilde\iota(v)=c_nx^n+c_{n+1}x^{n+1}+\cdots$ with $c_n\ne0$, then $n\ge0$ and $c_n\in R$. Proof. Since $R[[x]]$ is a domain (step 1), `IsIntegral.isAlmostIntegral, IsAlmostIntegral.isIntegral` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegral/AlmostIntegral.lean)) (1) applied to $R[[x]]$ shows that $v$ is almost integral over $R[[x]]$ `IsAlmostIntegral` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegral/AlmostIntegral.lean)): there is a nonzero $h\in R[[x]]$ with $hv^e\in R[[x]]$ for all $e\ge0$. Write $\iota(h)=b_mx^m+b_{m+1}x^{m+1}+\cdots$ with $m\ge0$, $b_i\in R$, $b_m\ne0$. By the product formula of `LaurentSeries, HahnSeries.ofPowerSeries, HahnSeries.ofPowerSeries_injective, HahnSeries.order` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LaurentSeries.lean)), the coefficient of $\tilde\iota(hv^e)=\iota(h)\tilde\iota(v)^e$ in degree $k<m+ne$ is $0$ and in degree $m+ne$ is $b_mc_n^e$, which is nonzero because $K$ is a field; so $\tilde\iota(hv^e)$ has order $m+ne$ and lowest coefficient $b_mc_n^e$. As $hv^e\in R[[x]]$, $\tilde\iota(hv^e)=\iota(hv^e)$ lies in the image of $\iota$ (step 2), so $m+ne\ge0$ and $b_mc_n^e\in R$ for every $e\ge0$. If $n<0$ then $m+ne<0$ for $e=m+1$, a contradiction; hence $n\ge0$. The relation $b_mc_n^e\in R$ for all $e\ge0$ with $b_m\in R\setminus\{0\}$ says that $c_n\in K$ is almost integral over $R$ `IsAlmostIntegral` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegral/AlmostIntegral.lean)); since $R$ is Noetherian `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)), `IsIntegral.isAlmostIntegral, IsAlmostIntegral.isIntegral` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegral/AlmostIntegral.lean)) (2) shows that $c_n$ is integral over $R$, and since $R$ is a normal domain `IsIntegrallyClosed, IsDomain, Localization.AtPrime` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IntegrallyClosed.lean)), $c_n\in R$. (`MvPowerSeries.instNoZeroDivisors` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/NoZeroDivisors.lean))).

Let $w\in\operatorname{Frac}(R[[x]])$ be integral over $R[[x]]$ and write $\tilde\iota(w)=\sum_ia_ix^i$ ($a_i\in K$). We show by induction on $N\ge0$ the statement $P(N)$: $a_i=0$ for all $i<0$ and $a_i\in R$ for $0\le i<N$. $P(0)$: if $w=0$ there is nothing to show; otherwise the claim of step 3 applied to $v=w$ gives that the order of $\tilde\iota(w)$ is $\ge0$. $P(N)\Rightarrow P(N+1)$: by $P(N)$, $p=\sum_{0\le i<N}a_ix^i$ is an element of $R[[x]]$ (a polynomial with coefficients in $R$), and $\tilde\iota(p)=\iota(p)=\sum_{0\le i<N}a_ix^i$. Every element of $R[[x]]$ is integral over $R[[x]]$ and the elements of $\operatorname{Frac}(R[[x]])$ integral over $R[[x]]$ form a subring `integralClosure, IsIntegral, IsIntegrallyClosedIn` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/Algebra/Basic.lean)), so $w'=w-p$ is integral over $R[[x]]$, and $\tilde\iota(w')=\sum_{i\ge N}a_ix^i$. If $a_N=0$, then $a_N\in R$. If $a_N\ne0$, then $w'\neq0$ ($\tilde\iota$ is injective) and $\tilde\iota(w')=a_Nx^N+a_{N+1}x^{N+1}+\cdots$ has order $N$ and lowest coefficient $a_N$, so step 3 applied to $v=w'$ gives $a_N\in R$. In both cases $P(N+1)$ holds. (`LaurentSeries, HahnSeries.ofPowerSeries, HahnSeries.ofPowerSeries_injective, HahnSeries.order` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LaurentSeries.lean))).

By step 4, $a_i=0$ for $i<0$ and $a_i\in R$ for all $i\ge0$, so $f=\sum_{i\ge0}a_ix^i\in R[[x]]$ and $\iota(f)=\tilde\iota(w)$, i.e. $\tilde\iota(f)=\tilde\iota(w)$. Since $\tilde\iota$ is injective (step 2), $w=f\in R[[x]]$. With step 1, $R[[x]]$ is a Noetherian integral domain integrally closed in its field of fractions, i.e. a Noetherian normal domain `IsIntegrallyClosed, IsDomain, Localization.AtPrime` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IntegrallyClosed.lean)). (`IsFractionRing.lift, IsFractionRing.lift_algebraMap, IsFractionRing.lift_unique, RingHom.injective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/FractionRing.lean))).
