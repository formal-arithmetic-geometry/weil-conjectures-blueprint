For a commutative ring B and ideal J, let C be the full category of ring maps B to D with Spec(D) to Spec(B) etale `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)) and the canonical map B/J to D/JD bijective, with all B-algebra maps as morphisms. Then C is essentially small in the universe of B.
