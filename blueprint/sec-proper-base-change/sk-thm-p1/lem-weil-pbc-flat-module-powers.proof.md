For every ideal J of R, let x be in the kernel of M tensor_R J -> M. For k >= 1, apply Module.Flat.lTensor_subtype_injective_of_smul_eq_zero to the image of J -> R/I^k, using the assumed flatness of M/I^k M. Its inclusion in R/I^k stays injective after tensoring with M. The image of x there is zero because its image in M tensor_R R is zero. Right exactness for J intersection I^k -> J -> image(J -> R/I^k) puts x in the image of M tensor_R (J intersection I^k). (`Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)), `TensorProduct.quotTensorEquivQuotSMul, TensorProduct.AlgebraTensorModule.cancelBaseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Quotient.lean)), `rTensor_exact, lTensor_exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/RightExactness.lean))).

Apply Ideal.exists_pow_inf_eq_pow_smul to J inside R, obtaining c. For n > 0 use k = n+c, so k >= 1 and J intersection I^k is contained in I^n J. Factor its tensor inclusion through I^n J; range_lTensor_smul_top_subtype_le shows x lies in I^n(M tensor_R J). For n=0 the claim is automatic. (`Ideal.exists_pow_inf_eq_pow_smul` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Filtration.lean))).

J is finite over R by Noetherianity. Tensor base-change cancellation makes M tensor_R J finite over S. Move scalars across the tensor product and use Ideal.map_pow to transfer the preceding membership to every power of IS. Mathlib Ideal.mem_iInf_smul_pow_eq_bot_iff, the elementwise Artin-Rees intersection theorem, gives r in IS with r*x=x. Thus (1-r)*x=0. If IS is contained in q then 1-r is outside q, since otherwise 1 belongs to q. (`IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)), `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)), `TensorProduct.AlgebraTensorModule.cancelBaseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Tower.lean)), `Ideal.exists_pow_inf_eq_pow_smul` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Filtration.lean))).

Use IsLocalizedModule.rTensor for M -> M_q. Naturality with J -> R is proved on pure tensors. IsLocalizedModule.injective_of_map_zero reduces injectivity of M_q tensor_R J -> M_q tensor_R R to elements coming from M tensor_R J. If the localized image of x is zero, the localization kernel criterion gives s outside q killing its unlocalized image. Apply the previous steps to s*x; a further factor 1-r outside q kills s*x. Therefore x has zero localized image. Module.Flat.iff_lTensor_injective′ gives flatness of M_q over R.
