title = "Flatness of an algebra descends through a faithfully flat algebra"
lean = ["Module.Flat.of_flat_of_faithfullyFlat"]
order = 1777

Let $A\to B\to C$ be compatible homomorphisms of commutative rings. If $C$ is faithfully flat over $B$ and flat over $A$, then $B$ is flat over $A$.
