Let $A\to B\to C$ be compatible homomorphisms of commutative rings. If $C$ is faithfully flat over $B$ `Module.FaithfullyFlat, RingHom.FaithfullyFlat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/FaithfullyFlat/Basic.lean)) and flat over $A$ `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)), then $B$ is flat over $A$ `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)).
