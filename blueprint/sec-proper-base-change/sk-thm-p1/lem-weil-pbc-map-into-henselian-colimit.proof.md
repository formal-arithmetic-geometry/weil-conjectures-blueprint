For a single etale R-algebra A, first allow any field K with an S-algebra structure whose algebra map is surjective and has kernel m_S. Put B=S tensor_R A and let phi:B -> K be the S-algebra map given by AlgHom.liftEquiv from chi:A -> K. Base change makes B etale over S. This is the private helper etale_lift_field. (`Algebra.Etale.baseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Etale/Basic.lean)), [def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology]).

Put q_prime=ker(phi). It is prime since K is a field, and its pullback to S is m_S since phi commutes with S. The residue-field lifts eta:kappa(m_S) -> K and psi:kappa(q_prime) -> K satisfy psi composed with theta=eta, where theta is the canonical residue-field map. The map eta is surjective because S -> K is, and psi is injective because its domain is a field. Given y in kappa(q_prime), choose x with eta(x)=psi(y); injectivity of psi gives theta(x)=y. Thus theta is bijective. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean))).

Translate Algebra.Etale S B to scheme etaleness with HasRingHomProperty.Spec_iff and RingHom.etale_algebraMap. Apply the existing henselian etale retraction theorem to obtain the unique sigma:B -> S with sigma inverse image of m_S equal to q_prime. Identify the algebra structure (algebraMap S B).toAlgebra with the tensor-product left algebra structure. ([lem-weil-pbc-henselian-etale-retraction]).

The reduction of sigma is phi. For b in B choose s in S with residue(s)=phi(b). Then b-algebraMap(s) lies in q_prime, so sigma(b)-s lies in m_S, proving equality of their residues. Restrict sigma to A to lift chi. Conversely, lift any f:A -> S through AlgHom.liftEquiv. Its reduction agrees with phi on the A tensor factor and on S; tensor-product extensionality gives equality on B and hence the same maximal-ideal preimage. Retraction uniqueness and the inverse lift equivalence give uniqueness of f. ([lem-weil-pbc-henselian-etale-retraction], `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean))).

Apply the preceding helper after replacing S and A by ULift S and ULift A in their common universe. Transport henselianity by HenselianLocalRing.of_ringEquiv and etaleness by Algebra.Etale.of_equiv. Use K=kappa(m_S); its algebra map from ULift S is residue composed with the down equivalence, is surjective, and has kernel the maximal ideal because the down equivalence preserves units. Transport the unique lift back to A -> S. This proves the private helper existsUnique_etale_residue_lift for all three original universes. ([def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology], `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean))).

Using h_tau on the images of R in kappa(p), form chi:A -> kappa(m_S) as tau composed with the canonical residue map; compatibility makes it an R-algebra map. For any f:A -> S the original prime-preimage and residue-field conditions are equivalent to residue composed with f equal to chi: field-map injectivity identifies kernels, and Ideal.ResidueField.ringHom_ext identifies the induced residue maps. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean))).

Transport the colimit cocone across the chosen isomorphism to A. At every stage the single etale helper uniquely lifts chi composed with the coprojection. Along a transition t:i -> j, f_j composed with F(t) has the same reduction as f_i by cocone naturality, so single-etale uniqueness gives compatibility.
