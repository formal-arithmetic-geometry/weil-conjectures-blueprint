Let $X$ and $Y$ be schemes [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions] with $X$ Noetherian, and let $f:Y\to X$ be étale. Let $\eta$ be the generic point of an irreducible component of $X$ and $y\in Y$ a point with $f(y)=\eta$. Then there is an open subscheme $W\subseteq Y$ containing $y$ which is irreducible and has $y$ as its generic point. No quasi-compactness of $Y$ and no reducedness of $X$ or $Y$ are assumed.
