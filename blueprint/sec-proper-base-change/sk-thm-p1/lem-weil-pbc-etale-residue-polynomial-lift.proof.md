Write $\kappa=\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ and $\pi:R\to\kappa$ for the canonical map $r\mapsto r/1\bmod\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}$; for $r\in R$ write $r\alpha$ for $\pi(r)\alpha\in L$. Since $L/\kappa$ is separable algebraic, $\alpha$ is algebraic over $\kappa$ `Algebra.IsSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)), so it has a minimal polynomial $g=P_\alpha=T^d+a_{d-1}T^{d-1}+\dots+a_0\in\kappa[T]$, monic of degree $d\geq1$, with $g(\alpha)=0$, and every nonzero $q\in\kappa[T]$ with $q(\alpha)=0$ has $\deg q\geq d$ (definition of $P_\alpha$ as the monic polynomial of least degree vanishing at $\alpha$). (`Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean))).

Common denominator. Every element of $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is a fraction $r/t$ with $r\in R$, $t\in R\setminus\mathfrak p$, and $R_{\mathfrak p}\to\kappa$ is surjective `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)); so for each $i=0,\dots,d-1$ there are $b_i\in R$, $t_i\in R\setminus\mathfrak p$ with $a_i=\pi(b_i)\pi(t_i)^{-1}$ ($t_i/1$ is a unit of $R_{\mathfrak p}$, so $\pi(t_i)$ is a unit of $\kappa$; equivalently $\pi(t_i)\neq0$ since $\ker\pi=\mathfrak p$ `Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.ker_algebraMap_residueField, Ideal.injective_algebraMap_quotient_residueField, instIsFractionRingQuotientIdealResidueField, Ideal.ResidueField.ringHom_ext, Ideal.ResidueField.lift` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/ResidueField/Ideal.lean))(a)). Put $c=t_0t_1\cdots t_{d-1}\in R$. As $\mathfrak p$ is prime and no $t_i$ lies in $\mathfrak p$, $c\notin\mathfrak p$, so $\gamma:=\pi(c)\neq0$ in $\kappa$. For $0\le i\le d-1$ put $r_i=b_i\,c^{d-i-1}\prod_{j\neq i}t_j\in R$; since $d-i\geq1$, $\pi(r_i)=\pi(b_i)\pi(t_i)^{-1}\pi(c)\,\pi(c)^{d-i-1}=\gamma^{d-i}a_i$.

The minimal polynomial of $\beta=c\alpha=\gamma\alpha$. Let $h=T^d+\gamma a_{d-1}T^{d-1}+\gamma^2a_{d-2}T^{d-2}+\dots+\gamma^da_0=\gamma^dg(T/\gamma)\in\kappa[T]$. It is monic of degree $d$, and $h(\beta)=\gamma^dg(\alpha)=0$. If $q\in\kappa[T]$ is nonzero with $q(\beta)=0$, then $q(\gamma T)$ has the same degree as $q$ (its leading coefficient is that of $q$ times $\gamma^{\deg q}\neq0$, $\kappa$ a field) and vanishes at $\alpha$, so $\deg q\geq d$ by step 1. Hence $h$ is a monic polynomial of least degree vanishing at $\beta$; it is the only one, since the difference of two such is zero or a nonzero polynomial of smaller degree vanishing at $\beta$. So $h=P_\beta$ is the minimal polynomial of $\beta$ over $\kappa$ `Algebra.IsSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)). (As $\gamma\in\kappa^\times$, also $\kappa(\beta)=\kappa(\alpha)=L$, which the citing proof uses.)

Lift. Let $f=T^d+r_{d-1}T^{d-1}+\dots+r_0\in R[T]$ with the $r_i$ of step 2; $f$ is monic, and applying $\pi$ to its coefficients gives $T^d+\sum_{i<d}\gamma^{d-i}a_iT^i=h$ by step 2. By step 3 the image of $f$ in $\kappa(\mathfrak p)[T]$ is the minimal polynomial of the image of $c\alpha$, with $c\in R\setminus\mathfrak p$.
