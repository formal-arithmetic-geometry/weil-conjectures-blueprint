Let $A\to B$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings and $\mathfrak b\subseteq B$ an ideal. Assume that for every prime ideal $\mathfrak q\subset B$ with $\mathfrak b\subseteq\mathfrak q$ there is $h\in B\setminus\mathfrak q$ such that $\operatorname{Spec}B_h\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is etale. Then there is $g\in B$ whose image in $B/\mathfrak b$ is a unit such that $\operatorname{Spec}B_g\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is etale.
