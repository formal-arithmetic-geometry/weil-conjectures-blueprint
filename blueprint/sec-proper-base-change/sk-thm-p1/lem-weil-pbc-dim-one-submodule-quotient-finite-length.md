Let $R$ be a Noetherian local integral domain of Krull dimension one, let $K$ be its fraction field, let $r$ be a nonnegative integer, and let $M\subseteq K^{\oplus r}$ be any $R$-submodule, without a finite-generation assumption. For every nonzero $x\in R$, the $R$-module $R/xR$ has finite length, and $M/xM$ has finite length with $\mathrm{length}_R(M/xM)\le r\,\mathrm{length}_R(R/xR)$.
