First show that an image section on an etale W with a point w over eta vanishes after a nonempty etale refinement. Compose the algebraic-closure point of W at w with W to X. The stalk map is zero by the hypothesis. Naturality of the germ maps makes the germ of the image section zero; equality in the filtered colimit gives a pointed refinement W prime where that section is zero. The retained geometric point makes W prime nonempty. The Lean proof uses Scheme.algClosurePoint, Point.toPresheafFiber_naturality and Point.toPresheafFiber_eq_iff'. ([def-weil-pbc-etale-site], [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks]).

A constant element n whose associated section is zero on a nonempty etale W is zero. Local injectivity of sheafification says that the equalizer sieve of n and zero covers W. If n differs from zero that sieve is empty, whereas a covering sieve on a nonempty W has a member through each point. In Lean these are W_toSheafify.isLocallyInjective, Presheaf.equalizerSieve_mem and mem_smallEtaleTopology_iff. This step is proved directly inside the theorem. ([def-constructible-constant], [def-weil-pbc-etale-site]).

For any section a of F on V and any ordinary point v of V, choose a trivializing cover member Ui of G through the image of v. The scheme pullback V times_X Ui has a point w1 over v. The isomorphism G.over Ui with a constant sheaf, compared with the restriction of the constant sheaf on X by constantSheafOverIso, sends the pulled-back image b = phi(a) to a section c of N_X. Local surjectivity of sheafification gives a further etale neighbourhood W2 of w1 on which c is the constant section of some n in N. This uses Presheaf.imageSieve_mem and mem_smallEtaleTopology_iff, and requires no finite generation. ([def-constructible-locally-constant], [def-constructible-constant], [def-weil-pbc-etale-site]).

W2 is nonempty and etale over X, hence has nonempty open image. The generic-point lemma IsGenericPoint.mem_open_set_iff puts eta in this image. At a point w of W2 over eta, apply the first step to the restriction of a. On the resulting nonempty W3 the image section is zero. Naturality of the constant-sheaf isomorphism and of sheafification implies that the constant section of n is zero on W3. The second step gives n = 0. Consequently the image section b is already zero on W2, since the trivializing isomorphism is injective on sections. ([lem-weil-pbc-flat-lfp-open], `IsGenericPoint` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Sober.lean)), [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks], [def-constructible-constant]).

The sieve of restrictions of b that vanish is thus covering: the preceding construction supplies a member over every v of V. Sheaf separatedness gives b = 0. Extensionality on sections proves phi = 0, and additivity of each geometric stalk functor gives its map equal to zero. This proves the stated conclusion without stalk conservativity or the constant-sheaf stalk formula. ([def-weil-pbc-etale-site], [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks]).
