title = "Flat quotients of a completion algebra whose restriction to the original ring is flat"
lean = ["AdicCompletion.flat_quotients_of_flat_restriction"]
order = 1700

Let A and S be commutative rings, I a finitely generated ideal of A, C its I-adic completion, and iota: A -> C the canonical map. Let f: C -> S be a ring homomorphism such that f composed with iota is flat. Put J = I C. Then for every natural number n, including zero, the C/J^n-module S/J^n S with action induced by f is flat. No Noetherianity or locality of A or S is assumed.
