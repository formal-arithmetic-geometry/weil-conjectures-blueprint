Let $R\to S$ be a ring map such that $\operatorname{Spec}S\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is etale [def-weil-etale-morphism], let $\mathfrak q\subset S$ be a prime ideal and $\mathfrak p=\mathfrak q\cap R$ its preimage in $R$. Then the ideal of the localization $S_{\mathfrak q}$ generated by the image of $\mathfrak p$ is the maximal ideal: $\mathfrak pS_{\mathfrak q}=\mathfrak qS_{\mathfrak q}$.
