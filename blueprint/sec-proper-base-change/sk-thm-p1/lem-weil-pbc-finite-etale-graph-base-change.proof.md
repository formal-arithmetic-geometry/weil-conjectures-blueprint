Prove the product square cartesian directly by its universal property: given a:T→P and b:T→U0 with matching U projections, lift the V projection of a and Y0 projection of b to V0, then lift that map and b to P0. Projection identities verify the two required equations. Uniqueness is checked by successive pullback.hom_ext on P0 and V0. This is IsPullback.mk' in the delivered proof. (`CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean))).

The graph sections s and s0 obey s0≫j=t≫s by their two product projections. Compose this square on the right with the cartesian product square. Its outer square has both horizontal maps identity, hence is cartesian; cancellation of the right square (IsPullback.of_right) proves the graph square cartesian. (`CategoryTheory.IsPullback.paste_vert_iff` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/IsPullback/Basic.lean))).

The graph s is an open immersion by `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.of_flat_of_mono, AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion.of_comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FlatMono.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Separated.lean)). Its cartesian base change is the open subscheme given by the inverse image of its image `AlgebraicGeometry.isPullback_morphismRestrict` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Restrict.lean)). Transport the second projection across the canonical pullback isomorphism. Its range is unchanged by that isomorphism, and IsOpenImmersion.range_pullbackSnd (OpenImmersion.lean:636) gives exactly the desired equality.
