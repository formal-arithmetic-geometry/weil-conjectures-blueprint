Let $X$ be a topological space and let $E\subseteq X$ be a constructible subset of $X$. Then $E$ is retrocompact in $X$: for every quasi-compact open subset $W\subseteq X$ the intersection $E\cap W$ is quasi-compact (Stacks Topology, Lemma 09YH).
