Let $X$ be a topological space and let $E\subseteq X$ be a constructible subset of $X$ `Topology.IsConstructible` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Constructible.lean)). Then $E$ is retrocompact in $X$: for every quasi-compact open subset $W\subseteq X$ the intersection $E\cap W$ is quasi-compact (Stacks Topology, Lemma 09YH).
