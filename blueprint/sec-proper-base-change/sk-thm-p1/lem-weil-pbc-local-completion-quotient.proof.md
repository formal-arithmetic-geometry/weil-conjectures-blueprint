Construct q_k with Ideal.quotientMap using Ideal.map_pow. Check transition compatibility on representatives; check factorPow applied to completion evaluations using Cauchy representatives. Apply AdicCompletion.liftRingHom to q_k composed with evalₐ to construct θ. Its evaluation formula proves the coordinate squares and θ c_A = c_B φ. Each q_k is surjective by Ideal.quotientMap_surjective. (`AdicCompletion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Basic.lean))).

Give B its A-algebra structure via φ. Ideal.smul_top_eq_map and Ideal.map_pow identify m^k times B with the restriction of n^k. Thus a ring-adic Cauchy sequence in B is also an m-adic A-module Cauchy sequence. AdicCompletion.map_surjective applied to the surjective linear map A -> B gives a completed preimage. Represent that preimage by a Cauchy sequence in A and compare quotient coordinates to prove θ is surjective. (`AdicCompletion.map_injective, AdicCompletion.map_exact, AdicCompletion.map_surjective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Exactness.lean)), `AdicCompletion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Basic.lean))).

Set J = ker φ. Apply AdicCompletion.map_exact to the inclusion J -> A and quotient A -> A/J. These modules all have the universe of A, avoiding the universe restriction of map_exact when B has a different universe. For x in ker θ, represent x by a Cauchy sequence s. Its k-th image φ(s_k) lies in n^k = map φ (m^k); choose a_k in m^k with φ(a_k)=φ(s_k). Then s_k-a_k lies in J, so the class of s_k in A/J lies in m^k(A/J). Every coordinate of the completed quotient image of x is therefore zero. Exactness gives x as the completed image of some y in the completion of J. (`AdicCompletion.map_injective, AdicCompletion.map_exact, AdicCompletion.map_surjective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Exactness.lean)), `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)), `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean))).

Noetherianity makes J finite. By AdicCompletion.ofTensorProduct_surjective_of_finite, write y as the image of a tensor t in Ahat tensor_A J. Tensor induction, ofTensorProduct_tmul, linearity of the completed inclusion, and map_of show its image lies in J.map c_A: zero lies there, a pure tensor maps to a*c_A(j), and addition preserves membership. Conversely θ c_A = c_B φ kills J, so Ideal.map_le_iff_le_comap gives J.map c_A contained in ker θ. This proves kernel equality. (`AdicCompletion.ofTensorProductEquivOfFiniteNoetherian, AdicCompletion.ofTensorProduct_tmul, AdicCompletion.ofTensorProduct_naturality` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/AsTensorProduct.lean)), `LinearMap.baseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Tower.lean)), `AdicCompletion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Basic.lean))).

Transport Ahat/(J.map c_A) to Ahat/ker θ using Ideal.quotientEquivAlgOfEq and compose with RingHom.quotientKerEquivOfSurjective. The two apply_mk lemmas show the equivalence sends the class of x to θ(x).
