Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $E$ an $R$-module, $\varphi:E\to R$ an $R$-linear map, and $\wedge(E)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\wedge^n_R(E)$ the exterior algebra `ExteriorAlgebra, ExteriorAlgebra.ι, exteriorPower (⋀[R]^n M), ExteriorAlgebra.ιMulti, ExteriorAlgebra.ι_sq_zero, ExteriorAlgebra.gradedAlgebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/ExteriorAlgebra/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/ExteriorAlgebra/Grading.lean)). (i) There is a unique $R$-linear map $D=D_\varphi:\wedge(E)\to\wedge(E)$ with $D(1)=0$ and $D(v\wedge x)=\varphi(v)\,x-v\wedge D(x)$ for all $v\in E$, $x\in\wedge(E)$. (ii) $D(v)=\varphi(v)\cdot1$ for $v\in E$, and $D(\wedge^n_R(E))\subset\wedge^{n-1}_R(E)$ for all $n\ge0$ (with $\wedge^{-1}_R(E)=0$). (iii) For $n\ge1$ and $v_1,\dots,v_n\in E$, $$D(v_1\wedge\dots\wedge v_n)=\sum_{i=1}^n(-1)^{i+1}\varphi(v_i)\,v_1\wedge\dots\wedge\widehat{v_i}\wedge\dots\wedge v_n.$$ (iv) $D$ is a derivation of degree $-1$: $D(x\wedge y)=D(x)\wedge y+(-1)^m x\wedge D(y)$ for $x\in\wedge^m_R(E)$, $y\in\wedge(E)$; and $D$ is the unique $R$-linear map $\wedge(E)\to\wedge(E)$ with this property and $D(v)=\varphi(v)$ for $v\in E$.
