Let $X$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme], $\mathcal F$ an $\mathcal O_X$-module `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)) and $X=\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i$ a covering by open subschemes; for an open $W\subseteq X$ write $\mathcal F|_W$ for the restriction of $\mathcal F$ to the open subscheme $W$ ($(\mathcal F|_W)(V)=\mathcal F(V)$ for $V\subseteq W$ open, as in `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.restrictFunctor, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.restrictAdjunction, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.instIsLeftAdjointRestrictFunctor, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isQuasicoherent_restrictFunctor` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Sheaf.lean))). (1) If $\mathcal F$ is coherent [pqf-def-coherent], then $\mathcal F|_W$ is a coherent $\mathcal O_W$-module for every open $W\subseteq X$. (2) If $\mathcal F|_{U_i}$ is a coherent $\mathcal O_{U_i}$-module for every $i\in I$, then $\mathcal F$ is a coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module. (Both conditions of [pqf-def-coherent] are local: being of finite type is a condition on neighbourhoods of points, and for the kernel condition over an open $W$ one restricts to the $W\cap U_i$, kernels of $\mathcal O_X$-modules being computed sectionwise.)
