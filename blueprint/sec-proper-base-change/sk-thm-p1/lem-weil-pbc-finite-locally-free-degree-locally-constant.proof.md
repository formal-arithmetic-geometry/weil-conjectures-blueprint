For each s, the fibre morphism is finite by base change. Its map on global sections is finite by Scheme.Hom.finite_appTop. Composing with the residue field spectrum global-sections isomorphism preserves finiteness; RingHom.finite_algebraMap gives the required finite module. For the rank function r(s) = f.finrank s, Mathlib Scheme.Hom.isLocallyConstant_finrank gives local constancy. A local proved rank/fibre comparison inside the theorem identifies r(s) with delta(s), by reducing along an affine chart, expressing the morphism as Spec.map, and using Module.rankAtStalk_eq. Here fiberDegree and finrankGamma of the fibre morphism are definitionally equal.
