By the definition of the constant sheaf F_n, every geometric stalk is the finite group Z/nZ, hence is killed by n; therefore F_n is a torsion etale sheaf. Applying the cohomological-dimension lemma for smooth separated connected curves to F_n gives H^q(C_Et,F_n)=0 for every q>2, which is exactly assertion C_1 in the eight curve assertions.
