title = "Extensions of A/(f_1,...,f_t) by P are described by admissible Koszul 1-cycles"
lean = ["Paper.cyclic_extension_cocycle"]
+++
Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $t\ge1$, $f_1,\dots,f_t\in A$, $J=(f_1,\dots,f_t)\subseteq A$ the ideal they generate, and $P$ an $A$-module. Let $F=A^{\oplus t}$ be the free $A$-module `Module.Free` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/FreeModule/Basic.lean)) with basis $e_1,\dots,e_t$, let $M_f=F/W$ be its quotient `Submodule.hasQuotient, Submodule.Quotient.module, Submodule.mkQ, Submodule.mkQ_surjective, Submodule.ker_mkQ` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Defs.lean)) by the submodule $W$ generated by the elements $f_ie_j-f_je_i$ ($1\le i,j\le t$), write $\bar e_i$ for the class of $e_i$, let $\mu:M_f\to A$ be the $A$-linear map with $\mu(\bar e_i)=f_i$, and put $K_f=\operatorname{Ker}\mu$. Put $Z_f(P)=\{(x_1,\dots,x_t)\in P^{t}:\ f_ix_j=f_jx_i\text{ for all }i,j\}$; for $x\in Z_f(P)$ let $\hat x:M_f\to P$ be the $A$-linear map with $\hat x(\bar e_i)=x_i$ (it exists since $x\in Z_f(P)$), and call $x$ *admissible* if $\hat x(K_f)=0$. For admissible $x$ let $E_f(P,x)=(P\oplus A)/W_x$ `DirectSum, DirectSum.instModule, DirectSum.lof, DirectSum.component, DirectSum.induction_on` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Module.lean)), where $W_x$ is the submodule generated by the elements $(x_i,-f_i)$, $i=1,\dots,t$, with the $A$-linear maps $\iota_x:P\to E_f(P,x)$, $p\mapsto[(p,0)]$, and $\pi_x:E_f(P,x)\to A/J$, $[(p,a)]\mapsto a\bmod J$. Then:
(1) for every admissible $x$, $\pi_x$ is well defined and $0\to P\xrightarrow{\iota_x}E_f(P,x)\xrightarrow{\pi_x}A/J\to0$ is a short exact sequence `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean));
(2) if $0\to P\xrightarrow{j}E\xrightarrow{q}A/J\to0$ is a short exact sequence of $A$-modules and $e\in E$ satisfies $q(e)=1\bmod J$, then there are unique $x_1,\dots,x_t\in P$ with $j(x_i)=f_ie$; the tuple $x=(x_i)$ lies in $Z_f(P)$ and is admissible, and the $A$-linear map $P\oplus A\to E$, $(p,a)\mapsto j(p)+ae$, induces an isomorphism $\theta:E_f(P,x)\to E$ with $\theta\circ\iota_x=j$ and $q\circ\theta=\pi_x$;
(3) if $x$ is admissible, $m\in P$ and $x'_i=x_i-f_im$ for all $i$, then $x'$ is admissible, and $(p,a)\mapsto(p+am,a)$ induces an isomorphism $E_f(P,x)\to E_f(P,x')$ commuting with the maps $\iota$ from $P$ and $\pi$ to $A/J$.
