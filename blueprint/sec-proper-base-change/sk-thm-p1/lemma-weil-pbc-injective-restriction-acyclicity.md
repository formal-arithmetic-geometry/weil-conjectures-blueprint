Let $(A,J)$ be an affine henselian pair, let $Y$ be separated of finite type
over $\operatorname{Spec}A$ with affine diagonal, covered by $n+1$ affine
opens, and let $Y_0=Y\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\operatorname{Spec}(A/J)$.
If $I$ is an injective sheaf of $\mathbf Z/n$-modules on $Y_{\Et}$ for some
integer $n\geq1$ (for instance an injective sheaf of $\mathbf F_r$-modules),
then

$$
 H^q(Y_0,I|_{Y_0})=0\qquad(q>n).
$$
