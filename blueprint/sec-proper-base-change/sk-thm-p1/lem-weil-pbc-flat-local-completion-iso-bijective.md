Let $\varphi:(A,\mathfrak m_A)\to(B,\mathfrak m_B)$ be a local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings such that $\varphi(\mathfrak m_A)B=\mathfrak m_B$ and the induced map of residue fields $A/\mathfrak m_A\to B/\mathfrak m_B$ is bijective. Let $\hat A$ and $\hat B$ be the $\mathfrak m_A$-adic and $\mathfrak m_B$-adic completions, and let $\hat\varphi:\hat A\to\hat B$ be a ring homomorphism compatible with $\varphi$ (that is, $\hat\varphi(\iota_A(a))=\iota_B(\varphi(a))$ for the canonical maps $\iota_A:A\to\hat A$, $\iota_B:B\to\hat B$). If $\hat\varphi$ is flat, then $\hat\varphi$ is bijective.
