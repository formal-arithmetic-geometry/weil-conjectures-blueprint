title = "Bijectivity of the induced completion map"
lean = ["RingHom.completion_map_bijective"]

Under the same Noetherian local hypotheses, maximal-ideal equality and residue-field bijectivity, any flat ring homomorphism between the adic completions compatible with the original local map is bijective.
