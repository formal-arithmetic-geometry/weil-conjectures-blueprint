Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $A=(A_{p,q},d,\delta)_{p,q\ge0}$ be a homological double complex of $R$-modules `HomologicalComplex₂` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/HomologicalBicomplex.lean)): $R$-modules $A_{p,q}$ ($p,q\ge0$) and $R$-linear maps $d_{p,q}:A_{p,q}\to A_{p-1,q}$ ($p\ge1$) and $\delta_{p,q}:A_{p,q}\to A_{p,q-1}$ ($q\ge1$) with $d_{p-1,q}\circ d_{p,q}=0$, $\delta_{p,q-1}\circ\delta_{p,q}=0$ and $\delta_{p-1,q}\circ d_{p,q}=d_{p,q-1}\circ\delta_{p,q}$ whenever defined; put $A_{p,q}=0$ if $p<0$ or $q<0$ and let $d_{0,q}$, $\delta_{p,0}$ and all maps out of or into a zero module be $0$. A morphism $\Phi:A\to B$ of such double complexes `HomologicalComplex₂` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/HomologicalBicomplex.lean)) is a family of $R$-linear maps $\Phi_{p,q}:A_{p,q}\to B_{p,q}$ with $\Phi d=d\Phi$ and $\Phi\delta=\delta\Phi$. For $n\ge0$ let $T_n(A)=\bigoplus_{p+q=n,\ p,q\ge0}A_{p,q}$, whose elements are tuples $a=(a_{n,0},a_{n-1,1},\dots,a_{0,n})$ with $a_{p,q}\in A_{p,q}$. The $R$-module of *zig-zags* of total degree $n$ is the submodule $$Z_n(A)=\{a\in T_n(A)\mid d_{p,q}(a_{p,q})=\delta_{p-1,q+1}(a_{p-1,q+1})\ \text{for all }p\ge1,\ q\ge0,\ p+q=n\}$$ (so $Z_0(A)=A_{0,0}$). The $R$-module of *trivial zig-zags* of total degree $n$ is the image $B_n(A)$ of the $R$-linear map $D_{n+1}:T_{n+1}(A)\to T_n(A)$, $D_{n+1}(b)_{p,q}=d_{p+1,q}(b_{p+1,q})+\delta_{p,q+1}(b_{p,q+1})$ ($p+q=n$); equivalently, $B_n(A)$ is generated by the tuples which, for one $b\in A_{p,q}$ with $p+q=n+1$, have $\delta_{p,q}(b)$ at the place $(p,q-1)$, $d_{p,q}(b)$ at the place $(p-1,q)$ (each only when that place has non-negative indices) and $0$ elsewhere. The *zig-zag module* of total degree $n$ is the $R$-module $$\mathbf H_n(A)=Z_n(A)/(Z_n(A)\cap B_n(A)).$$ A morphism $\Phi:A\to B$ acts on $T_n$ componentwise, $\Phi(a)_{p,q}=\Phi_{p,q}(a_{p,q})$. The *map induced by $\Phi$* on zig-zag modules is the $R$-linear map $\mathbf H_n(\Phi):\mathbf H_n(A)\to\mathbf H_n(B)$, $[a]\mapsto[\Phi(a)]$ ($a\in Z_n(A)$). That it is well defined (that $\Phi$ maps $Z_n(A)$ into $Z_n(B)$ and $B_n(A)$ into $B_n(B)$), that $\mathbf H_n(\Psi\circ\Phi)=\mathbf H_n(\Psi)\circ\mathbf H_n(\Phi)$ and $\mathbf H_n(\mathrm{id})=\mathrm{id}$, and that $B_n(A)\subseteq Z_n(A)$ (so $\mathbf H_n(A)=Z_n(A)/B_n(A)$), is the statement node lem-weil-pbc-double-complex-zigzag-module-functorial, named here and not cited, since it is stated in terms of this definition.
