For an affine henselian pair $(T,T_0)$ and a torsion sheaf $F$ on $T_{\mathrm{et}}$,
every class $c\in H^q(T_0,F_0)$ with $q>0$ admits a monomorphism
$F\hookrightarrow F'$ such that the image of $c$ in $H^q(T_0,F'_0)$ is zero.
