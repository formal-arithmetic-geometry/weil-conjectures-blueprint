Lift h to f in R using Ideal.Quotient.mk_surjective. If f = 0, the open subscheme D(h) is empty. Its unique morphism to Spec R is a closed immersion, so choose S = R. The Lean proof constructs its IsEmpty instance and uses isInitialOfIsEmpty. (`AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/ClosedImmersion.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean))).

If f is nonzero, let S = Localization.Away f. powers f lies in nonZeroDivisors R, giving an integral domain and integral closedness. Construct S to FractionRing R by IsLocalization.map and use IsFractionRing.isFractionRing_of_isLocalization. Transfer separable closedness to FractionRing S along FractionRing.algEquiv. (`isIntegrallyClosed_of_isLocalization, IsFractionRing.isFractionRing_of_isLocalization, IsLocalization.isDomain_of_le_nonZeroDivisors` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IntegrallyClosed.lean)), [def-pc-separably-closed]).

The map Localization.awayMap (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) f is surjective: in awayMap_surjective_iff choose a lift b of a in R/I and exponent zero. Therefore its spectrum morphism is a closed immersion by IsClosedImmersion.spec_of_surjective. Compose it with basicOpenIsoSpecAway.hom for h to obtain the required closed immersion. Localization-quotient commutation is not needed. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isOpenImmersion_SpecMap_localizationAway` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/ClosedImmersion.lean))).
