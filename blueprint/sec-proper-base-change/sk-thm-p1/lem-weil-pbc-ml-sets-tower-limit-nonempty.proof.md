For each $i\ge1$ put $B_i=\varphi_{c(i),i}(E_{c(i)})\subseteq E_i$. Every $B_i$ is nonempty because $E_{c(i)}$ is nonempty. The images $\varphi_{k,i}(E_k)$ decrease as $k$ increases, since $\varphi_{k,i}=\varphi_{j,i}\circ\varphi_{k,j}$ for $k\ge j\ge i$ `CategoryTheory.Functor.ofOpSequence, CategoryTheory.NatTrans.ofOpSequence` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Functor/OfSequence.lean)). By the Mittag-Leffler hypothesis they equal $B_i$ for all $k\ge c(i)$, so $B_i=\bigcap_{k\ge i}\varphi_{k,i}(E_k)$; the terms with $i\le k<c(i)$ contain $B_i$ by the same composite identity.

The restricted transition $f_i:B_{i+1}\to B_i$ is well defined: if $x\in B_{i+1}$, then $x=\varphi_{k,i+1}(z)$ for some $k\ge\max(c(i),c(i+1),i+1)$ and $z\in E_k$, by stabilization of the image at level $i+1$; hence $f_i(x)=\varphi_{k,i}(z)\in B_i$. It is surjective: for $y\in B_i$ choose the same kind of $k$; stabilization at $i$ gives $z\in E_k$ with $\varphi_{k,i}(z)=y$. Put $x=\varphi_{k,i+1}(z)$. Since $k\ge c(i+1)$, stabilization at $i+1$ gives $x\in B_{i+1}$, and $f_i(x)=\varphi_{k,i}(z)=y$. Thus $(B_i,f_i|_{B_{i+1}})$ is a tower of nonempty sets with surjective transitions, and its compatible sequences are exactly those of $(E_i)$: any compatible $(e_i)$ satisfies $e_i=\varphi_{k,i}(e_k)\in B_i$ for $k\ge\max(i,c(i))$, while a compatible sequence in the $B_i$ is already one in the $E_i$. (`CategoryTheory.Functor.ofOpSequence, CategoryTheory.NatTrans.ofOpSequence` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Functor/OfSequence.lean))).

Choose $b_1\in B_1$. Given $b_i\in B_i$, surjectivity in Step 2 gives $b_{i+1}\in B_{i+1}$ with $f_i(b_{i+1})=b_i$. Dependent choice makes these successive choices into one sequence $(b_i)_{i\ge1}$; it is compatible at every level, hence belongs to $\varprojlim_iB_i$. By Step 2 the same sequence belongs to $\varprojlim_iE_i$, proving the latter nonempty.
