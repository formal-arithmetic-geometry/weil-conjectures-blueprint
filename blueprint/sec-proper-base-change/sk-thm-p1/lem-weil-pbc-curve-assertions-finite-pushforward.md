Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field [def-weil-algebraic-closure], let $X$ and $Y$ be separated schemes of finite type over $k$ of dimension at most one, and let $f:X\to Y$ be a finite morphism of $k$-schemes [def-weil-finite-integral-morphism]. Let $F$ be a torsion abelian sheaf on $X_{\Et}$, so that $(X,F)$ is a curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting]. Then: (a) the direct image $f_*F$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image] is a torsion abelian sheaf on $Y_{\Et}$, so that $(Y,f_*F)$ is a curve coefficient pair; (b) for each $j\in\{1,\dots,8\}$, if the assertion $\mathcal C_j(X,F)$ holds, then $\mathcal C_j(Y,f_*F)$ holds, where $\mathcal C_1,\dots,\mathcal C_8$ are [def-weil-pbc-C1], [def-weil-pbc-C2], [def-weil-pbc-C3], [def-weil-pbc-C4], [def-weil-pbc-C5], [def-weil-pbc-C6], [def-weil-pbc-C7], [def-weil-pbc-C8], each read, as in Stacks, as the implication 'if its hypotheses (affine, $p$-primary, constructible, prime to $p$, proper) hold for the pair, then its conclusion holds'. Explicitly: $H^q(Y_{\Et},f_*F)=0$ for $q>2$ ($\mathcal C_1$); $=0$ for $q>1$ if $Y$ is affine ($\mathcal C_2$); $=0$ for $q>1$ if $\operatorname{char}k=p>0$ and $f_*F$ is $p$-primary ($\mathcal C_3$); $H^q(Y_{\Et},f_*F)$ is finite for all $q$ if $f_*F$ is constructible and prime to $\operatorname{char}k$ ($\mathcal C_4$), or if $Y$ is proper over $k$ and $f_*F$ is constructible ($\mathcal C_5$); the pullback map $\rho^q_{k'/k}(Y,f_*F)$ is an isomorphism for every algebraically closed extension $k'/k$ and every $q$ if $f_*F$ is prime to $\operatorname{char}k$ ($\mathcal C_6$) or if $Y$ is proper over $k$ ($\mathcal C_7$); and $H^2(Y_{\Et},f_*F)\to H^2(U_{\Et},a^{-1}f_*F)$ is surjective for every open immersion $a:U\hookrightarrow Y$ ($\mathcal C_8$). In particular, if the eight assertions $\mathcal C(X,F)$ of [def-weil-pbc-eight-curve-assertions] hold, then $\mathcal C(Y,f_*F)$ hold.
