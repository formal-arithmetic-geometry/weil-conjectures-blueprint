In an abelian category (for instance abelian sheaves, or sheaves of
$\Lambda$-modules, on a site), given a monomorphism $i_B:A\hookrightarrow B$
and an arbitrary morphism $i_C:A\to C$, the canonical map
$C\to B\amalg_A C$ is a monomorphism.
