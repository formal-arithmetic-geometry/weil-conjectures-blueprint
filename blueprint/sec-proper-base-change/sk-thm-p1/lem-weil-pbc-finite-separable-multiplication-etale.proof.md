Since $k'/k$ is finite and separable algebraic, the morphism $\pi:\operatorname{Spec}k'\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ induced by $k\to k'$ is etale `Algebra.FormallyEtale.of_isSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Etale/Field.lean)).

Let $i_1:k'\to k'\otimes_kk'$, $a\mapsto a\otimes1$. Applying `Algebra.Etale.baseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Etale/Basic.lean)) with $R=k$, $A=k'$ (so that $\operatorname{Spec}A\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is etale by step 1) and $S=k'$, the morphism $g:=\operatorname{Spec}i_1:\operatorname{Spec}(k'\otimes_kk')\to\operatorname{Spec}k'$ is etale.

The multiplication map satisfies $\mu\circ i_1=\mathrm{id}_{k'}$, since $\mu(a\otimes1)=a\cdot1=a$ for $a\in k'$. As $\operatorname{Spec}$ is a contravariant functor `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)), $f:=\operatorname{Spec}\mu:\operatorname{Spec}k'\to\operatorname{Spec}(k'\otimes_kk')$ satisfies $g\circ f=\operatorname{Spec}(\mu\circ i_1)=\operatorname{Spec}(\mathrm{id}_{k'})=\mathrm{id}_{\operatorname{Spec}k'}$.

The identity $\mathrm{id}_{\operatorname{Spec}k'}$ is an open immersion `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)) (an isomorphism onto the open subscheme $\operatorname{Spec}k'$ of itself), hence etale by `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.etale_comp, instance [IsOpenImmersion f] : Etale f` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)) (1). So in $\operatorname{Spec}k'\xrightarrow{f}\operatorname{Spec}(k'\otimes_kk')\xrightarrow{g}\operatorname{Spec}k'$ both $g\circ f$ (step 3) and $g$ (step 2) are etale, and `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.etale_comp, instance [IsOpenImmersion f] : Etale f` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)) (3) gives that $f=\operatorname{Spec}\mu$ is etale `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)). (This is the scheme form of Algebra, Lemma 00U7: $\mu$ is a $k'$-algebra map between the etale $k'$-algebras $k'\otimes_kk'$ and $k'$.)

By `AlgebraicGeometry.HasRingHomProperty.Spec_iff` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RingHomProperties.lean)), since $\operatorname{Spec}\mu$ is etale, $\mu$ is an etale ring map: $k'$ is a finitely presented $(k'\otimes_kk')$-algebra, $H_1(L_{k'/k'\otimes_kk'})=0$ and $\Omega_{k'/k'\otimes_kk'}=0$ `KaehlerDifferential` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Kaehler/Basic.lean)). The zero module is a finite projective $k'$-module (a direct summand of the finite free module $0$), so conditions (i), (ii), (iii) of `Algebra.Smooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean)) hold and $\mu$ is a smooth ring map.
