Let $A$ be a Noetherian integral domain, $K$ a field together with a ring map $A\to K$ making $K$ a fraction field of $A$, and $\mathfrak p\subset A$ a prime ideal. Let $C$ be the completion of the local ring $A_{\mathfrak p}$ with respect to its maximal ideal $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$, an $A$-algebra via $A\to A_{\mathfrak p}\to C$. Suppose that the generic formal fibre of $A$ at $\mathfrak p$, i.e. the fibre $C\otimes_A\kappa((0))$ of $A\to C$ over the zero ideal, is a geometrically regular $\kappa((0))$-algebra, where $\kappa((0))$ is the residue field of $A$ at $(0)$. Then $K\otimes_AC$ is a geometrically regular $K$-algebra.
