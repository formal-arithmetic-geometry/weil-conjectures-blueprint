Base degree. Exactness of the target at $N$ `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)) makes $\eta:N_0\to N$ surjective. Since $F_0$ is free, `Module.Projective.of_free` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Projective.lean)) makes it projective. Apply the lifting characterization in `Module.Projective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Projective.lean)) to the surjection $\eta$ and the linear map $\varphi\pi:F_0\to N$. This gives $\alpha_0:F_0\to N_0$ with $\eta\alpha_0=\varphi\pi$.

First differential. Put $g_1=\alpha_0\delta_1:F_1\to N_0$. The calculation $\eta g_1=\varphi\pi\delta_1=0$ shows that $g_1$ factors as a linear map $\bar g_1:F_1\to\ker\eta$, explicitly $x\mapsto g_1(x)$ with its kernel-membership just checked. Exactness at $N_0$ `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)) says $\operatorname{im}\partial_1=\ker\eta$, so $q_1:N_1\to\ker\eta$, $y\mapsto\partial_1(y)$, is surjective. The projectivity of $F_1$ from `Module.Projective.of_free` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Projective.lean)) and its lifting characterization `Module.Projective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Projective.lean)) give $\alpha_1$ with $q_1\alpha_1=\bar g_1$. Composing with the inclusion $\ker\eta\hookrightarrow N_0$ gives $\partial_1\alpha_1=\alpha_0\delta_1$.

Inductive extension. Suppose $n\ge1$ and maps $\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_n$ have been chosen satisfying the augmentation identity and $\partial_i\alpha_i=\alpha_{i-1}\delta_i$ for $1\le i\le n$. Set $g_{n+1}=\alpha_n\delta_{n+1}$. Then $\partial_n g_{n+1}=\partial_n\alpha_n\delta_{n+1}=\alpha_{n-1}\delta_n\delta_{n+1}=0$. Thus $g_{n+1}$ defines a linear map $\bar g_{n+1}:F_{n+1}\to\ker\partial_n$ by the same elementwise formula. Exactness at $N_n$ `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)) makes $q_{n+1}:N_{n+1}\to\ker\partial_n$, $y\mapsto\partial_{n+1}(y)$, a surjective linear map. Apply `Module.Projective.of_free` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Projective.lean)) and the lifting characterization `Module.Projective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Projective.lean)) to obtain $\alpha_{n+1}$ with $q_{n+1}\alpha_{n+1}=\bar g_{n+1}$. Composing with the kernel inclusion gives precisely $\partial_{n+1}\alpha_{n+1}=\alpha_n\delta_{n+1}$; the earlier equations are preserved because the earlier maps are retained.

For each admissible finite tuple of maps choose one extension furnished above, and recursively start with the base map and apply these choices. The first-differential step starts the recursion, and the inductive extension supplies every subsequent degree. The resulting family $(\alpha_i)_{i\ge0}$ has the stated augmentation identity and all chain identities. Only the target was required to be exact; for the source the two zero-composite hypotheses were exactly those used in the displayed calculations.
