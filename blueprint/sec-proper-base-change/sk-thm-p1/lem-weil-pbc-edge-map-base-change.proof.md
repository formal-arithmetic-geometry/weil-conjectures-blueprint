Work pointwise by extensionality and write the two edge maps as the cohomology pullback followed by H^q of the counit. Use the existing naturality cohomologyMap_map twice: pullback along g' carries the counit map to H^q(g'^* epsilon_f), and pullback along f' carries H^q(Ex^0) to H^q(f'^* Ex^0). This uses the established cohomology maps directly, without choosing new K-injective resolutions. ([def-weil-pbc-direct-image], [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], [def-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback], [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback-functorial]).

Set A=f_*F and h=g' followed by f. The commutative square identifies h with f' followed by g. Clause 4 of cohomologyPullbackFunctorial identifies the successive pullbacks f'^* g^* with H^q(c_1) h^*, and g'^* f^* with H^q(c_2) h^*, where c_1 and c_2 are the hom components of the two upperStarComp isomorphisms. To use the specified equality in the first comparison, first state the comparison for an arbitrary h with h=f' followed by g, substitute h, and apply the clause. ([def-weil-pbc-cartesian-square], [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition], [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback-functorial]).

By definition of squareUpperStarIso, its hom component theta is c_1 inverse followed by c_2. Hence c_1 followed by theta equals c_2, by the isomorphism inverse law. Functoriality Sheaf.H.map_comp_apply then gives H^q(theta) f'^* g^*=g'^* f^* on each cohomology class. ([def-weil-pbc-cartesian-square], [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition]).

Apply the already proved upperStar_map_baseChangeMap_app_counit, attached to def-weil-pbc-underived-exchange: f'^*(Ex^0) followed by epsilon_f' equals theta followed by g'^*(epsilon_f). Apply H^q to this equality and use Sheaf.H.map_comp_apply on both sides. Substitute the equality of successive pullbacks from the previous step and combine with the two naturality equalities. This proves the desired edge-map equality without any hypothesis on f. ([def-weil-pbc-underived-exchange]).

If f is finite, MorphismProperty.of_isPullback and stability of IsFinite under base change give IsFinite f'. The existing bijective_edgeMap_of_isFinite supplies bijectivity of the two edge maps. Compose its bijectivity with that of the f' edge map; associativity of additive-map composition identifies this with the displayed second comparison map.
