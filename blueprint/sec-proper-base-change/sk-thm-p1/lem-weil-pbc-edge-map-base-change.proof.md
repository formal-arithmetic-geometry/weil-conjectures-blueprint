**Step 1.** *Step 1 (unwinding the definitions; choices of resolutions).* Put $h=f\circ g'=g\circ f':X'\to Y$ (one morphism, since the square commutes [def-weil-pbc-cartesian-square]) and $A=f_*F$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. The composition isomorphisms of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (2), for $(u,v)=(f,g')$ and $(u,v)=(g,f')$, are $c_{f,g'}:g'^{-1}f^{-1}\xrightarrow{\sim}h^{-1}$ and $c_{g,f'}:f'^{-1}g^{-1}\xrightarrow{\sim}h^{-1}$, so $\theta_{\square,A}=c_{f,g',A}^{-1}\circ c_{g,f',A}:f'^{-1}g^{-1}A\to h^{-1}A\to g'^{-1}f^{-1}A$. By [def-weil-pbc-underived-exchange], $\Ex^0=\Ex^0_{\square,F}:g^{-1}A\to f'_*g'^{-1}F$ is the adjoint under $f'^{-1}\dashv f'_*$ (unit $\eta_{f'}$, counit $\varepsilon_{f'}$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image]) of $\varphi=g'^{-1}(\varepsilon_{f,F})\circ\theta_{\square,A}:f'^{-1}(g^{-1}A)\to g'^{-1}F$, i.e. $\Ex^0=f'_*(\varphi)\circ\eta_{f',g^{-1}A}$ (the adjoint of Stacks 0037 (2)). The edge maps are $e^q_{f,F}=H^q(\varepsilon_{f,F})\circ f^*$ and $e^q_{f',g'^{-1}F}=H^q(\varepsilon_{f',g'^{-1}F})\circ f'^*$, with $f^*$ on $H^q(Y,A)$ and $f'^*$ on $H^q(Y',f'_*g'^{-1}F)$ the pullback maps of [def-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback]. Fix once and for all a K-injective resolution [def-weil-pbc-kinjective-resolutions] of every sheaf occurring below ($A$; $F$, $f^{-1}A$; $g^{-1}A$, $f'_*g'^{-1}F$; $g'^{-1}F$, $g'^{-1}f^{-1}A$, $f'^{-1}g^{-1}A$, $h^{-1}A$, $f'^{-1}f'_*g'^{-1}F$) and read every group $H^q(T_{\Et},G)$ below as computed from the fixed resolution of $G$. A pullback map $u^*:H^q(T,G)\to H^q(S,u^{-1}G)$ ($f^*$, $g^*$, $h^*$ on $A$; $g'^*$ on $F$ and on $f^{-1}A$; $f'^*$ on $g^{-1}A$ and on $f'_*g'^{-1}F$) is computed by [def-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback] from the fixed resolution $s$ of $G$ and some K-injective resolution $r$ of $u^{-1}I^\bullet$, and lands in the group computed from $r\circ u^{-1}(s)$; it is read followed by the identification $H^q(\mathrm{id}_{u^{-1}G})$ with the group of the fixed resolution of $u^{-1}G$, which by clause 1 of [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback-functorial] does not depend on $r$. All maps $u^*$, $H^q(\psi)$ are taken between these groups. By clause 1 of [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback-functorial] and the independence of $H^q(\psi)$ from the lift (its preamble), any other choices change every map by the identifications $H^q(\mathrm{id})$ on source and target, which are compatible with composition by step 2; so the identity to be proved does not depend on the choices.

**Step 2.** *Step 2 (functoriality of $H^q$ in the sheaf).* For morphisms $\psi:G\to G'$, $\psi':G'\to G''$ of abelian sheaves on a scheme $T$ with the fixed resolutions $s,s',s''$, and lifts $\tilde\psi$, $\tilde\psi'$ as in the preamble of [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback-functorial] ($\tilde\psi s\simeq s'\psi$, $\tilde\psi's'\simeq s''\psi'$), one has $\tilde\psi'\tilde\psi s\simeq\tilde\psi's'\psi\simeq s''\psi'\psi$ (composition preserves homotopies), so $\tilde\psi'\tilde\psi$ is a lift of $\psi'\psi$; and $\mathrm{id}_{I^\bullet}$ lifts $\mathrm{id}_G$. Since $\Gamma(T,-)$ and $\mathcal H^q$ are functors and $H^q(-)$ does not depend on the lift, $H^q(\psi'\circ\psi)=H^q(\psi')\circ H^q(\psi)$ and $H^q(\mathrm{id}_G)=\mathrm{id}$ (the same resolution on both sides). Hence for an isomorphism $\psi$, $H^q(\psi)$ is bijective with inverse $H^q(\psi^{-1})$. (This is step 1(b) of the proof of [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback-functorial], not among its stated clauses.)

**Step 3.** *Step 3 (the adjoint of the exchange map; the triangle identity).* Apply [lem-weil-pbc-adjunct-counit-identity] to the adjunction $f'^{-1}\dashv f'_*$ of [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], the objects $g^{-1}A$ of $\Ab(Y'_{\Et})$ and $g'^{-1}F$ of $\Ab(X'_{\Et})$, and $\varphi$, $\psi=\Ex^0=f'_*(\varphi)\circ\eta_{f',g^{-1}A}$ (step 1, [def-weil-pbc-underived-exchange]): $$\varepsilon_{f',g'^{-1}F}\circ f'^{-1}(\Ex^0)=\varphi=g'^{-1}(\varepsilon_{f,F})\circ c_{f,g',A}^{-1}\circ c_{g,f',A}:\ f'^{-1}g^{-1}A\to g'^{-1}F.$$ (In the source Lean this is `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.upperStar_map_baseChangeMap_app_counit`, the mate identity `mateEquiv_counit`, attached to [def-weil-pbc-underived-exchange].)

**Step 4.** *Step 4 (the left side).* By clause 3 (naturality) of [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback-functorial] for $u=g':X'\to X$ and the morphism $\varepsilon_{f,F}:f^{-1}A\to F$ of $\Ab(X_{\Et})$, $g'^*\circ H^q(\varepsilon_{f,F})=H^q(g'^{-1}\varepsilon_{f,F})\circ g'^*$, so $g'^*\circ e^q_{f,F}=H^q(g'^{-1}\varepsilon_{f,F})\circ g'^*\circ f^*$. By clause 4 (composition) for $u=f$, $v=g'$, $G=A$: $h^*=(f\circ g')^*=H^q(c_{f,g',A})\circ g'^*\circ f^*$ as maps $H^q(Y,A)\to H^q(X',h^{-1}A)$; by step 2, $H^q(c_{f,g',A})$ is invertible with inverse $H^q(c_{f,g',A}^{-1})$, so $g'^*\circ f^*=H^q(c^{-1}_{f,g',A})\circ h^*$. Hence, by step 2, $$g'^*\circ e^q_{f,F}=H^q\bigl(g'^{-1}(\varepsilon_{f,F})\circ c^{-1}_{f,g',A}\bigr)\circ h^*.$$

**Step 5.** *Step 5 (the right side).* By clause 3 of [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback-functorial] for $u=f':X'\to Y'$ and the morphism $\Ex^0:g^{-1}A\to f'_*g'^{-1}F$ of $\Ab(Y'_{\Et})$, $f'^*\circ H^q(\Ex^0)=H^q(f'^{-1}\Ex^0)\circ f'^*$; so $e^q_{f',g'^{-1}F}\circ H^q(\Ex^0)\circ g^*=H^q(\varepsilon_{f',g'^{-1}F})\circ H^q(f'^{-1}\Ex^0)\circ f'^*\circ g^*$. By clause 4 for $u=g$, $v=f'$, $G=A$: $h^*=(g\circ f')^*=H^q(c_{g,f',A})\circ f'^*\circ g^*$, so (step 2) $f'^*\circ g^*=H^q(c^{-1}_{g,f',A})\circ h^*$; here $(g\circ f')^*$ and $(f\circ g')^*$ are the one map $h^*$, computed from the same fixed resolutions (step 1). Hence, by step 2, $$e^q_{f',g'^{-1}F}\circ H^q(\Ex^0)\circ g^*=H^q\bigl(\varepsilon_{f',g'^{-1}F}\circ f'^{-1}(\Ex^0)\circ c^{-1}_{g,f',A}\bigr)\circ h^*.$$

**Step 6.** *Step 6 (conclusion).* By step 3, $\varepsilon_{f',g'^{-1}F}\circ f'^{-1}(\Ex^0)\circ c^{-1}_{g,f',A}=g'^{-1}(\varepsilon_{f,F})\circ c^{-1}_{f,g',A}\circ c_{g,f',A}\circ c^{-1}_{g,f',A}=g'^{-1}(\varepsilon_{f,F})\circ c^{-1}_{f,g',A}$ as morphisms $h^{-1}A\to g'^{-1}F$. So the right sides of steps 4 and 5 are equal: $g'^*\circ e^q_{f,F}=e^q_{f',g'^{-1}F}\circ H^q(\Ex^0_{\square,F})\circ g^*$ for every $q\ge0$. No hypothesis on $f$ was used.

**Step 7.** *Step 7 (the 'in particular').* Let $f$ be finite; then $f'$ is finite as a base change of $f$ [lem-weil-pbc-finite-morphism-permanence] (a). By [lem-weil-pbc-finite-pushforward-cohomology] for $f$ and $F$, and for $f'$ and $g'^{-1}F$, the edge maps $e^q_{f,F}$ and $e^q_{f',g'^{-1}F}$ are isomorphisms; if $\Ex^0_{\square,F}$ is an isomorphism, so is $H^q(\Ex^0_{\square,F})$ (step 2). Then step 6 reads $g'^*=\Psi\circ g^*\circ(e^q_{f,F})^{-1}$ with $\Psi=e^q_{f',g'^{-1}F}\circ H^q(\Ex^0_{\square,F})$ an isomorphism: $g^*$ on $H^q(Y,f_*F)$ and $g'^*$ on $H^q(X,F)$ are identified through the isomorphisms $e^q_{f,F}$ and $\Psi$.
