*The definition is well formed.* The condition quantifies over $R$-algebras $D$, ideals $J$ and maps $g$ and asks for the existence of $f$; no choice is made, so it is a property of the pair $(\varphi,\mathfrak n)$. It depends only on the isomorphism class of $S$ as an $R$-algebra together with $\mathfrak n$: transport $g$ and $f$ along an $R$-algebra isomorphism $\alpha:S'\to S$ with $\alpha(\mathfrak n')=\mathfrak n$, which carries $\mathfrak n'^k$ onto $\mathfrak n^k$.
