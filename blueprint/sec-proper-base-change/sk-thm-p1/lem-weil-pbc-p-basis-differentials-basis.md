Let $p$ be a prime number and $K/k$ a field extension `Algebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Defs.lean)) of fields of characteristic $p$. Let $K^p=\{b^p:b\in K\}$ and let $kK^p$ be the subfield of $K$ generated by $k$ and $K^p$. Let $(t_i)_{i\in I}$ be a family of elements of $K$ such that the monomials $t^E=\prod_{i\in I}t_i^{e_i}$, where $E=(e_i)_{i\in I}$ runs over the families of integers with $0\le e_i<p$ for all $i$ and $e_i=0$ for all but finitely many $i$, form a basis of $K$ as a vector space over $kK^p$. Let $\mathrm d:K\to\Omega_{K/k}$ be the module of Kähler differentials of $K$ over $k$ `KaehlerDifferential` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Kaehler/Basic.lean)). Then the family $(\mathrm dt_i)_{i\in I}$ is a basis of the $K$-vector space $\Omega_{K/k}$.
