title = "Power series over a complete Noetherian ring are complete for the combined ideal"
lean = ["power_series_complete_over_complete_base"]
order = 1920

Let $\Lambda$ be a Noetherian ring and $J\subseteq\Lambda$ an ideal such that $\Lambda$ is $J$-adically complete. Let $d\ge0$, $R=\Lambda[[X_1,\ldots,X_d]]$ the formal power series ring, and $\mathfrak a=JR+(X_1,\ldots,X_d)$. Then $R$ is $\mathfrak a$-adically complete.
