($\dim B\le\dim A$.) Write $\varphi:A\to B$ for the ring map. Let $\mathfrak q_0\subsetneq\mathfrak q_1\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq\mathfrak q_n$ be a chain of prime ideals of $B$ of length $n$ `ringKrullDim` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/Basic.lean)), and put $\mathfrak p_i=\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q_i)$. Each $\mathfrak p_i$ is a prime ideal of $A$: $1\notin\mathfrak p_i$ since $\varphi(1)=1\notin\mathfrak q_i$, and $ab\in\mathfrak p_i$ gives $\varphi(a)\varphi(b)\in\mathfrak q_i$, so $\varphi(a)\in\mathfrak q_i$ or $\varphi(b)\in\mathfrak q_i$. Since $B$ is integral over $A$ and $\mathfrak q_{i-1}$ is a prime with $\mathfrak q_{i-1}\subsetneq\mathfrak q_i$, incomparability `Ideal.IsIntegral.comap_lt_comap` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/GoingUp.lean)) gives $\mathfrak p_{i-1}\subsetneq\mathfrak p_i$ for $i=1,\dots,n$. So $\mathfrak p_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq\mathfrak p_n$ is a chain of prime ideals of $A$ of length $n$, and $n\le\dim A$. Taking the supremum over all chains in $B$ gives $\dim B\le\dim A$ (if $B=0$ there is no chain and $\dim B=-\infty$). (`integralClosure, IsIntegral, IsIntegrallyClosedIn` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/Algebra/Basic.lean))).

(Maximal ideals lie over maximal ideals.) Let $\mathfrak n\subset B$ be a maximal ideal. Since $B$ is integral over $A$, $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak n)$ is a maximal ideal of $A$ `Ideal.isMaximal_comap_of_isIntegral_of_isMaximal'` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/GoingUp.lean)); that is, $\mathfrak n$ lies over the maximal ideal $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak n)$ of $A$.
