The diagonal $\Delta_{f'}$ is a closed immersion, hence universally closed. Forming the diagonal commutes with base change: $\Delta_{f'}$ is the base change of $\Delta_f$ along $X\times_SS'\to X$, up to the canonical isomorphisms of fibre products, and that morphism is in turn a base change of $g$, hence flat, surjective and quasi-compact. Universal closedness descends along surjective flat quasi-compact morphisms, so $\Delta_f$ is universally closed. The diagonal is an immersion, and an immersion with closed image is a closed immersion; hence $\Delta_f$ is a closed immersion and $f$ is separated.
