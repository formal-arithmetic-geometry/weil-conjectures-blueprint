Convert the covering sieve to joint surjectivity with Scheme.ofArrows_mem_smallEtaleTopology_iff [def-weil-pbc-etale-site]. The over-category commutativity identity identifies each (f a).left with (T a).hom. Construct the etale scheme cover C with Scheme.Cover.mkOfCovers.

Use the Mathlib declaration recorded for `AlgebraicGeometry.QuasiCompactCover.exists_hom` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Cover/QuasiCompact.lean)), QuasiCompactCover.exists_hom, in its actual generality: it assumes only CompactSpace of the base, not affineness. Etale structure maps are open by Scheme.Hom.isOpenMap (Morphisms/UniversallyOpen.lean:48 and the flat, locally finitely presented instance), so QuasiCompactCover.of_isOpenMap supplies its remaining hypothesis. Obtain a finite affine etale cover D and a refinement g : D.cover ⟶ C. This is a direct application of the recorded Lean declaration, rather than an application of the narrower natural-language affine-base specialization.

Reindex the finite type D.I₀ by B = ULift (Fin (Fintype.card D.I₀)), in the required universe w, using Equiv.ulift and Fintype.equivFin. Put V b = Scheme.Etale.mk (D.f (e b)). Each member is a spectrum and is affine. The refinement component g.h₀ (e b), with g.w₀ (e b), defines its factorization through T (g.s₀ (e b)); equality of over-category morphisms is equality of their left components. Joint surjectivity follows from D.cover.iUnion_range and the surjective reindexing identity Function.Surjective.iUnion_comp. Convert back to the small etale topology using [def-weil-pbc-etale-site].

Each spectrum V b is compact by `PrimeSpectrum.iSup_basicOpen_eq_top_iff, Ideal.span_singleton_eq_top, PrimeSpectrum.compactSpace` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/Topology.lean))(iii). Since U is quasi-separated, each structure map V b → U is quasi-compact by `AlgebraicGeometry.quasiCompact_of_compactSpace, AlgebraicGeometry.QuasiSeparated.of_quasiSeparatedSpace, AlgebraicGeometry.QuasiCompact.compactSpace_of_compactSpace, AlgebraicGeometry.quasiSeparatedSpace_of_quasiSeparated, quasiCompact_isStableUnderBaseChange, quasiSeparated_isStableUnderBaseChange, locallyOfFinitePresentation_isStableUnderBaseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/QuasiSeparated.lean))(1); its base change to V b′ is quasi-compact by (3), and the pullback is compact by (2). Lean infers these instances using quasiCompact_of_compactSpace (Morphisms/QuasiSeparated.lean:255) and the CompactSpace pullback instance (Morphisms/QuasiCompact.lean:190). This proves the final clause and hence the cofinal refinement assertion.
