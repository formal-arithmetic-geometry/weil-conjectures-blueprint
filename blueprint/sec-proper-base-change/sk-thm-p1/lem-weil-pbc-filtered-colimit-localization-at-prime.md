Let $I$ be a small filtered category and $i\mapsto R_i$ a functor from $I$ to the category of commutative rings, with colimit $R=\varinjlim_IR_i$ in that category `CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean)) and coprojections $\iota_i:R_i\to R$. Let $\mathfrak p\subset R$ be a prime ideal and put $\mathfrak p_i=\iota_i^{-1}(\mathfrak p)$, a prime ideal of $R_i$. Then: (1) for every morphism $a:i\to j$ of $I$ one has $R(a)^{-1}(\mathfrak p_j)=\mathfrak p_i$, so $R(a)$ induces a ring map $(R_i)_{\mathfrak p_i}\to(R_j)_{\mathfrak p_j}$, $x/s\mapsto R(a)(x)/R(a)(s)$, between the localizations `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)), and these maps form a functor $i\mapsto(R_i)_{\mathfrak p_i}$ from $I$ to commutative rings; (2) the ring maps $\kappa_i:(R_i)_{\mathfrak p_i}\to R_{\mathfrak p}$, $x/s\mapsto\iota_i(x)/\iota_i(s)$, are well defined and make $R_{\mathfrak p}$ a colimit of this functor in the category of commutative rings: $R_{\mathfrak p}=\varinjlim_I(R_i)_{\mathfrak p_i}$.
