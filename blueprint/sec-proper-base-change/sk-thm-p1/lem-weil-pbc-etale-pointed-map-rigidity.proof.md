The base equality defines d:Spec B -> X times_S X. The diagonal of an etale morphism is an open immersion (Mathlib FormallyUnramified.isOpenImmersion_diagonal; Stacks 02GE). By the pointed equality and the pullback universal property, Spec beta followed by d equals Spec beta followed by u and the diagonal. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)), `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean))).

Since beta is local, Spec beta sends the closed point of Spec Omega to the closed point of Spec B. Evaluate the preceding equality there: d sends this closed point into the open range of the diagonal. Every point of Spec B generalizes its closed point, so every image under d lies in that range. This uses IsLocalRing.specializes_closedPoint and stability of opens under generization. (`AlgebraicGeometry.LocallyRingedSpace.Hom` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Geometry/RingedSpace/LocallyRingedSpace.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean))).

IsOpenImmersion.lift factors d through the diagonal, whose two projections coincide. Composing lift_fac with the two projections gives l=u and l=v, hence u=v. (`AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)), `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean))).
