Let $A,B$ be commutative rings with $B$ an $A$-algebra and $I\subset A$ an ideal. Let $D\subset B$ be an $A$-subalgebra finite as an $A$-module. Let $C$ be an $(A/I)$-algebra and $\Phi:D/ID\cong (A/I)\times C$ an isomorphism respecting the $A/I$ structure maps. Suppose $b,d\in D$, $d^2=d$, $\alpha\in A$ is a unit, $bd=\alpha d$, $D[1/b]\to B[1/b]$ is bijective, and $\Phi(\bar d)=(1,0)$. For $e$ the image of $d$ in $B$, $A\to B[1/e]$ is integral and the structure map $A/I\to B[1/e]/IB[1/e]$ is bijective.
