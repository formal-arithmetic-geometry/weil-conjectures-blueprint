Let $T$ be a scheme and $C$ an abelian sheaf on its small etale site $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], i.e. an object of $\Ab(T_{\Et})$ `CategoryTheory.Sheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheaf.lean)). The sheaf $C$ is a *constructible abelian sheaf* if there is a stratification $(\iota_k\colon Z_k\to T)_{k<r}$ of the scheme $T$ [def-constructible-stratification] (a finite partition $T=\coprod_{k<r}T_k$ of the underlying space into pairwise disjoint locally closed subsets, with immersions of schemes $\iota_k$ with set-theoretic image $T_k$) such that
(a) every stratum $T_k$ is a constructible subset of the underlying space of $T$ `Topology.IsConstructible` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Constructible.lean)), and
(b) for every $k$ the abelian inverse image $\iota_k^{-1}C$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] on $(Z_k)_{\Et}$ is *finite locally constant* (Stacks 03RU): there are an etale covering $\{V_i\to Z_k\}_{i\in I}$ and finite abelian groups $A_i$ with $(\iota_k^{-1}C)|_{V_i}\cong\underline{A_i}$, the constant abelian sheaf with value $A_i$ on $(V_i)_{\Et}$ `CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean)), for every $i$.
This is the global-partition form of Stacks Definition 03RW (2) (there: a partition of every affine open); for quasi-compact quasi-separated $T$ the two agree by Stacks Lemma 095E. It is the abelian counterpart of [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf] (no coefficient ring), the notion called 'constructible torsion' in the blueprint (such a sheaf has finite geometric stalks, and on a quasi-compact $T$ it is killed by a positive integer). The schemes $Z_k$ are part of the data; one may take $Z_k=T_k$ with its reduced induced structure.
