(A copy of $R/\mathfrak p$ in $M$.) Since $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Ass}_R(M)$ there is $z\in M$ with $\operatorname{Ann}_R(z)=\mathfrak p$. Let $N=Rz\subseteq M$. The $R$-linear map $R\to N$, $a\mapsto az$, is surjective with kernel $\operatorname{Ann}_R(z)=\mathfrak p$, so it induces an isomorphism $R/\mathfrak p\cong N$, $\bar a\mapsto az$. In particular $\mathfrak pN=0$, and under this isomorphism $xN=Rxz$ corresponds to $(\mathfrak p+(x))/\mathfrak p$, so $N/xN\cong R/(\mathfrak p+(x))$.

(Choice of $n$ by Artin–Rees.) Apply `Ideal.exists_pow_inf_eq_pow_smul` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Filtration.lean)) to the Noetherian ring $R$, the ideal $I=(x)$, the finite $R$-module $M$ and its submodule $N$: there is an integer $c\ge0$ with $I^kM\cap N\subseteq I^{k-c}N$ for all $k\ge c$. Put $n=c+1\ge1$. Since $I^n=(x^n)$ we have $I^nM=x^nM$, and $I^{n-c}N=xN$; hence $N\cap x^nM\subseteq xN$.

(Reduction to $\overline N$.) Let $\pi:M\to M/x^nM$ be the quotient map and $\overline N=\pi(N)\subseteq M/x^nM$, an $R$-submodule, generated by $\bar z=\pi(z)$, hence a finite $R$-module; $\pi$ restricts to a surjection $N\to\overline N$ with kernel $N\cap x^nM$, so $\overline N\cong N/(N\cap x^nM)$. By `associatedPrimes.subset_of_injective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/AssociatedPrime/Basic.lean)) applied to the Noetherian ring $R$ and the submodule $\overline N$ of $M/x^nM$, $\operatorname{Ass}_R(\overline N)\subseteq\operatorname{Ass}_R(M/x^nM)$; so it suffices to show $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Ass}_R(\overline N)$, i.e. some $w\in\overline N$ has $\operatorname{Ann}_R(w)=\mathfrak q$ (the same $w$ then lies in $M/x^nM$ with the same annihilator).

($\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Supp}(\overline N)$.) Since $N\cap x^nM\subseteq xN$ (Step 2), the surjection $N\to N/xN$ factors through $N/(N\cap x^nM)\cong\overline N$, giving a surjection $\overline N\to N/xN\cong R/(\mathfrak p+(x))$ (Step 1). An element of $R$ killing $\overline N$ kills its quotient $R/(\mathfrak p+(x))$, hence lies in $\mathfrak p+(x)$ (it kills the class of $1$); so $\operatorname{Ann}_R(\overline N)\subseteq\mathfrak p+(x)\subseteq\mathfrak q$. As $\overline N$ is finite (Step 3), `Module.mem_support_iff_of_finite, Module.support_eq_zeroLocus` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Support.lean)) gives $\overline N_{\mathfrak q}\neq0$, i.e. $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Supp}(\overline N)$.

($\mathfrak q$ is minimal in $\operatorname{Supp}(\overline N)$.) $\overline N$ is annihilated by $\mathfrak p$ (since $\mathfrak pN=0$, Step 1, and $\overline N=\pi(N)$) and by $x^n$ (since $x^n(M/x^nM)=0$); so $\mathfrak p+(x^n)\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}_R(\overline N)$. Let $\mathfrak q'\in\operatorname{Supp}(\overline N)$ with $\mathfrak q'\subseteq\mathfrak q$. By `Module.mem_support_iff_of_finite, Module.support_eq_zeroLocus` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Support.lean)) ($\overline N$ finite), $\operatorname{Ann}_R(\overline N)\subseteq\mathfrak q'$, so $\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak q'$ and $x^n\in\mathfrak q'$, hence $x\in\mathfrak q'$ as $\mathfrak q'$ is prime. Thus $\mathfrak p+(x)\subseteq\mathfrak q'\subseteq\mathfrak q$, and $\mathfrak q'=\mathfrak q$ by the minimality of $\mathfrak q$ over $\mathfrak p+(x)$. So $\mathfrak q$ is a minimal element of $\operatorname{Supp}(\overline N)$.

(Conclusion.) By `Module.associatedPrimes.minimalPrimes_annihilator_subset_associatedPrimes` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/AssociatedPrime/Localization.lean)) applied to the Noetherian ring $R$ and the finite $R$-module $\overline N$ (Step 3), with $\mathfrak q$ minimal in $\operatorname{Supp}(\overline N)$ (Steps 4 and 5), there is $w\in\overline N$ with $\operatorname{Ann}_R(w)=\mathfrak q$. By Step 3, $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Ass}_R(M/x^nM)$ with $n=c+1\ge1$. (`associatedPrimes.subset_of_injective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/AssociatedPrime/Basic.lean))).
