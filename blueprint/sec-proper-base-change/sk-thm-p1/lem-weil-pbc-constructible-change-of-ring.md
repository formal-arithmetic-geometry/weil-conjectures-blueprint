title = "Constructibility is unchanged under restriction of scalars along a surjection of finite rings"
uses = ["def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf", "def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves", "def-weil-pbc-etale-site"]

Let $T$ be a scheme, $\varphi:\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ a surjective homomorphism of finite commutative rings (for instance $\mathbf Z/n\to\mathbf Z/m$ for $m\mid n$), and $\mathcal G$ a sheaf of $\Lambda'_T$-modules on $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves]; write $\varphi^*\mathcal G$ for $\mathcal G$ regarded as a sheaf of $\Lambda_T$-modules via $\varphi$. Then $\mathcal G$ is a constructible sheaf of $\Lambda'$-modules if and only if $\varphi^*\mathcal G$ is a constructible sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf], with the same stratifications serving for both. In particular a sheaf of $\mathbf Z/n$-modules killed by a divisor $m$ of $n$ is constructible as a sheaf of $\mathbf Z/n$-modules iff it is as a sheaf of $\mathbf Z/m$-modules. (Ingredients: inverse image along an immersion commutes with restriction of scalars; the constant sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules on $\varphi^*M'$ is $\varphi^*$ of the constant sheaf of $\Lambda'$-modules on $M'$, both being the sheafification of the same presheaf of abelian groups; a $\Lambda_U$-module isomorphism between sheaves on which $\Lambda$ acts through $\Lambda'$ is $\Lambda'_U$-linear, $\varphi$ being surjective; a finite $\Lambda$-module killed by $\ker\varphi$ is a finite $\Lambda'$-module.)
