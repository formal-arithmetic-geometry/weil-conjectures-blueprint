**Step 1.** *$C$ is Noetherian and $F$ is killed by one integer $N\ge1$.* $C$ is of finite type over the field $k$, hence a Noetherian scheme; its underlying space is a Noetherian topological space, and so is every subspace of it (Mathlib: TopologicalSpace.NoetherianSpace.set), which is therefore quasi-compact (TopologicalSpace.NoetherianSpace.isCompact). By [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion] there are a finite partition $C=\coprod_{i=1}^rC_i$ into locally closed constructible subsets and immersions $u_i:Z_i\to C$ with image $C_i$ such that $G_i=u_i^{-1}F$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] is locally constant with finite stalks: there is a jointly surjective family of etale morphisms $(v_{ij}:U_{ij}\to Z_i)_{j\in J_i}$ [def-weil-etale-morphism] such that the restriction of $G_i$ to $(U_{ij})_{\Et}$ is isomorphic to a constant sheaf $\underline{A_{ij}}$ [def-constructible-locally-constant], and every geometric stalk of $G_i$ is finite. Discard the empty $U_{ij}$. Etale morphisms are flat and locally of finite presentation, hence open (Mathlib: UniversallyOpen.of_flat, Scheme.Hom.isOpenMap), so the images $v_{ij}(U_{ij})$ form an open cover of the quasi-compact space $|Z_i|\cong C_i$; choose a finite subfamily $J_i^0\subseteq J_i$ that is still jointly surjective. (a) *Each $A_{ij}$ ($j\in J_i^0$) is finite.* Pick a point of $U_{ij}$ and a geometric point $\bar w$ of $U_{ij}$ over it (Spec of an algebraic closure of its residue field [def-weil-geometric-point]). The stalk of the constant sheaf $\underline{A_{ij}}$ at $\bar w$ is $A_{ij}$ [lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-geometric-stalk]. The stalk at $\bar w$ of the restriction $G_i|_{U_{ij}}$ equals the stalk $(G_i)_{v_{ij}\circ\bar w}$ [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks]: a pointed etale neighbourhood of $\bar w$ in $U_{ij}$ is, by composition with $v_{ij}$, a pointed etale neighbourhood of $v_{ij}\circ\bar w$ in $Z_i$ with the same sections of $G_i$, and these are cofinal, since for a pointed neighbourhood $(W,\bar x)$ of $v_{ij}\circ\bar w$ the pair $(W\times_{Z_i}U_{ij},(\bar x,\bar w))$ is a pointed neighbourhood of $\bar w$ mapping to it. So $A_{ij}\cong(G_i)_{v_{ij}\circ\bar w}$ is finite by hypothesis; let $e_{ij}\ge1$ be its exponent and $N=\operatorname{lcm}_{i,\,j\in J_i^0}e_{ij}$ (a finite lcm; $N=1$ if $C=\emptyset$). (b) *Every geometric stalk of $G_i$ is killed by $N$.* Let $\bar z$ be a geometric point of $Z_i$; since $(v_{ij})_{j\in J_i^0}$ is jointly surjective, $\bar z=v_{ij}\circ\bar w$ for some $j\in J_i^0$ and a geometric point $\bar w$ of $U_{ij}$ [lem-weil-pbc-etale-cover-geometric-point-lift], so as in (a) $(G_i)_{\bar z}\cong A_{ij}$ is killed by $e_{ij}\mid N$. (c) *A covering family of geometric points of $C$.* For every point $c\in C$, with $c\in C_i$, choose $z\in Z_i$ over $c$ and a geometric point $\bar z$ of $Z_i$ at $z$; then $\bar c=u_i\circ\bar z$ is a geometric point of $C$ at $c$, and $F_{\bar c}\cong(u_i^{-1}F)_{\bar z}=(G_i)_{\bar z}$ [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-stalk-germ], killed by $N$ by (b). (d) *$NF=0$.* Let $\psi=N\cdot\mathrm{id}_F$ and $I=\operatorname{Im}\psi\subseteq F$. Stalk functors are exact [prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact], so $I_{\bar c}=N\cdot F_{\bar c}=0$ for each $\bar c$ of the family (c). Hence the morphism $I\to0$ is injective on the stalks of a family of geometric points whose images cover $C$, so it is a monomorphism [lem-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-detect-mono]; thus $I=0$ and $\psi=0$, i.e. $NF=0$. (This is why one integer works for all three sheaves: Stacks' Noetherian setting has finitely many strata, each with finitely many trivializing pieces.)

**Step 2.** *$F_1$ and $F_2$ are killed by $N$, and the sequence is one of sheaves of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules.* $(N\cdot\mathrm{id}_F)\circ\alpha=\alpha\circ(N\cdot\mathrm{id}_{F_1})$ and the left side is $0$ by step 1; $\alpha$ is a monomorphism, so $N\cdot\mathrm{id}_{F_1}=0$. Likewise $\beta\circ(N\cdot\mathrm{id}_F)=(N\cdot\mathrm{id}_{F_2})\circ\beta$ with $\beta$ an epimorphism gives $N\cdot\mathrm{id}_{F_2}=0$. An abelian sheaf killed by $N$ is the same as a sheaf of $(\mathbf Z/N)_C$-modules [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves] (the module structure $\mathbf Z/N\times F(U)\to F(U)$ is the unique one induced by $\mathbf Z$), and every morphism of abelian sheaves between two such is $\mathbf Z/N$-linear; so $\operatorname{Mod}(C_{\Et},\mathbf Z/N)$ is the full subcategory of $\Ab(C_{\Et})$ [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves] of sheaves killed by $N$. It is closed under subobjects and quotients in $\Ab(C_{\Et})$, so kernels, images and cokernels of its morphisms are the same in both categories, and $0\to F_1\to F\to F_2\to0$ is a short exact sequence in $\operatorname{Mod}(C_{\Et},\mathbf Z/N)$.

**Step 3.** *$F$ is a constructible sheaf of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules.* By steps 1 and 2, $NF=0$ and $(C,F)$ is a curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting]; by [lem-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion-modules-agree] with $n=N$, $F$ being constructible in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion] is constructible as a sheaf of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf] ($\Lambda=\mathbf Z/N$, a finite commutative ring). On the Noetherian scheme $C$ every locally closed subset is constructible [def-weil-constructible-subset] (Stacks Topology, Lemma 005L, recorded in that definition), so condition (a) of that definition is automatic on $C$ and the notion agrees with the one without condition (a) (def-constructible-constructible, used by thm-constructible-serre).

**Step 4.** *$F_1$ is a constructible sheaf of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules (Stacks 09BH (3), submodules).* The monomorphism $\alpha$ induces an isomorphism of $F_1$ onto the subsheaf $\alpha(F_1)=\operatorname{Im}\alpha\subseteq F$, a subsheaf of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules (step 2). $C$ is Noetherian (step 1) and $\mathbf Z/N$ is a finite commutative ring, so $\operatorname{Im}\alpha$ is a constructible sheaf of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules by [lem-weil-pbc-constructible-subobjects-noetherian] applied to $F$ (step 3). Constructibility in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf] is invariant under isomorphism: the same stratification serves, since the inverse image along each stratum immersion carries the isomorphism $F_1\cong\operatorname{Im}\alpha$ to an isomorphism, and composing local trivializations with it shows finite local constancy is transported. So $F_1$ is constructible as a sheaf of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules.

**Step 5.** *$F_2$ is a constructible sheaf of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules (Stacks 09BH (3), quotients, via the weak Serre property 03RZ).* By exactness in $\operatorname{Mod}(C_{\Et},\mathbf Z/N)$ (step 2), $\beta$ induces an isomorphism $\operatorname{Coker}\alpha\cong F_2$. $\alpha:F_1\to F$ is a morphism between constructible sheaves of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules (steps 3, 4), so its cokernel is constructible [thm-constructible-serre] (closed under cokernels; on the Noetherian $C$ its notion of constructible agrees with [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf], step 3). By invariance under isomorphism (as in step 4) $F_2$ is a constructible sheaf of $\mathbf Z/N$-modules.

**Step 6.** *Back to the curve notion.* $F_1$ and $F_2$ are torsion abelian sheaves with $NF_1=0=NF_2$ (step 2), so $(C,F_1)$, $(C,F_2)$ are curve coefficient pairs [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting], and by [lem-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion-modules-agree] with $n=N$ and steps 4, 5 they are constructible in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion].
