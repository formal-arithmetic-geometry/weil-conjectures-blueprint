Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field, $C$ a separated scheme of finite type over $k$ with $\dim C\le1$, $n\ge1$ an integer and $F$ an abelian sheaf on $C_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves] with $n\cdot\mathrm{id}_F=0$. Then $F$ is torsion [def-weil-pbc-torsion-etale-sheaf], so $(C,F)$ is a curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting], and $F$ carries exactly one structure of sheaf of $(\mathbf Z/n)_C$-modules [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves] extending its abelian structure. Moreover the following are equivalent, and the same finite partitions and immersions serve for both: (i) $F$ is constructible in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion]: there are a finite partition $C=\coprod_kC_k$ into pairwise disjoint locally closed constructible subsets [def-weil-constructible-subset] and immersions $\iota_k:Z_k\to C$ with image $C_k$ such that each abelian inverse image $\iota_k^{-1}F$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] is locally constant as an abelian sheaf [def-constructible-locally-constant] (an etale covering $\{U_i\to Z_k\}$ and abelian groups $A_i$ with $(\iota_k^{-1}F)|_{U_i}\cong\underline{A_i}$ as abelian sheaves) and has finite stalks at all geometric points [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks]; (ii) $F$, with this module structure, is a constructible sheaf of $\mathbf Z/n$-modules in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf] with $\Lambda=\mathbf Z/n$ (on each stratum the module inverse image is locally isomorphic to constant sheaves of $(\mathbf Z/n)$-modules $\underline{M_i}$ with $M_i$ finite). Both are also equivalent, with the same partitions and immersions, to (iii) the underlying sheaf of sets of $F$ being constructible in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves] (on each stratum the inverse image of the underlying sheaf of sets is lisse, i.e. finite locally constant). (Ingredients: a constant abelian sheaf $\underline A$ on a nonempty $U$ with $n\underline A=0$ has $nA=0$, $A$ being its stalk at a geometric point, and is then the underlying abelian sheaf of the constant sheaf of the $\mathbf Z/n$-module $A$; an abelian-sheaf isomorphism between sheaves of $\mathbf Z/n$-modules is $\mathbf Z/n$-linear; both notions use finite partitions into locally closed constructible pieces with immersions. The hypotheses on $C$ and $k$ only serve to make $(C,F)$ a curve coefficient pair; the equivalence holds on any scheme.)
