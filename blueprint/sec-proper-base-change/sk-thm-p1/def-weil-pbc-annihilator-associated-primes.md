Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M$ an $R$-module. Define $\operatorname{Ass}_R(M)=\{\mathfrak q\subset R\mid \mathfrak q\text{ is a prime ideal and }\exists z\in M,\ \mathfrak q=\{a\in R\mid az=0\}\}$. No Noetherian hypothesis is imposed.
