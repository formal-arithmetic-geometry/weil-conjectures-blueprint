Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $I\subseteq A$ an ideal, $n\geq0$, and $\bar p$ an idempotent endomorphism of $(A/I)^n$. There exist a commutative integral $A$-algebra $B$, an $A$-algebra map $\psi:B\to\operatorname{Mat}_n(A)$, and $y\in B$ such that $y\bmod IB$ is idempotent and the entrywise reduction of $\psi(y)$ is the standard matrix of $\bar p$.
