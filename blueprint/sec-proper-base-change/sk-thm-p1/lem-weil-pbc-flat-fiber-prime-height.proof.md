Put q = Q.comap(includeRight). It lies over p. Equip S_q with the canonical R_p-algebra from the induced local map; flatness localizes and implies going down. (`RingHom.Flat.localRingHom` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RingHom/Flat.lean)), `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean))).

Apply Mathlib Ideal.height_eq_height_add_of_liesOver_of_hasGoingDown to the maximal ideals of R_p and S_q. The heights of these ideals equal the respective Krull dimensions, hence height(p) and height(q). (`Ideal.height_eq_height_add_of_liesOver_of_hasGoingDown` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/KrullsHeightTheorem.lean)), `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean))).

The image I of the source maximal ideal is pS_q by map composition. This ideal is proper since the map is local. Thus the quotient is nontrivial and local; the surjective quotient map carries the target maximal ideal to the quotient maximal ideal, whose height is its Krull dimension. (`Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean))).

Identify C_Q with S_q/I by Ideal.Fiber.algEquivAux₂ and quotient equivalences for equal ideals. A ring isomorphism preserves Krull dimension. The dimension of C_Q equals height(Q). Substitute in step 2 and remove the injective coercion from extended naturals into WithBot extended naturals.
