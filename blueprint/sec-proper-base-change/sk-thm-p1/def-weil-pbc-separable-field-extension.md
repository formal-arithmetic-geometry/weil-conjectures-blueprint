Let $K/k$ be a field extension. For a family $(x_i)_{i\in I}$ of elements of $K$ let $k(x_i;i\in I)\subseteq K$ be the subfield generated by $k$ and the $x_i$. (1) $K$ is *separably generated over $k$* if there is a transcendence basis $(x_i)_{i\in I}$ of $K/k$ such that the extension $K/k(x_i;i\in I)$ is separable algebraic, i.e. the minimal polynomial over $k(x_i;i\in I)$ of every element of $K$ is separable (coprime to its derivative). (2) $K$ is *separable over $k$* if for every subextension $k\subset K'\subset K$ with $K'$ finitely generated as a field over $k$ (that is, $K'=k(a_1,\dots,a_n)$ for finitely many $a_1,\dots,a_n\in K$), the extension $K'/k$ is separably generated in the sense of (1).
