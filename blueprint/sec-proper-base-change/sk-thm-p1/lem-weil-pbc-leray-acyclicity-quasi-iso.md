Let $\mathcal A$ and $\mathcal B$ be abelian categories [def-sp-abelian], where $\mathcal A$ has enough injectives (every object of $\mathcal A$ admits a monomorphism into an injective object [def-sp-injective-resolutions]), and let $F:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ be a left exact additive functor [def-sp-left-exact-additive], with right derived functors $R^qF$ computed by injective resolutions. Let $K^\bullet$ and $L^\bullet$ be cochain complexes of $\mathcal A$ [def-weil-abstract-derived-category] and $a\in\mathbf Z$ an integer with $K^n=0$ and $L^n=0$ for all $n<a$, such that every term $K^n$ and every term $L^n$ is $F$-acyclic [def-sp-acyclic], i.e. $R^qF(K^n)=0$ and $R^qF(L^n)=0$ for all $q>0$ and all $n$. Let $\varphi:K^\bullet\to L^\bullet$ be a quasi-isomorphism (a morphism of complexes with $H^r(\varphi)$ an isomorphism for every $r\in\mathbf Z$). Then the morphism of complexes $F(\varphi):F(K^\bullet)\to F(L^\bullet)$ of $\mathcal B$, obtained by applying $F$ termwise ($F(K^\bullet)^n=F(K^n)$ with differentials $F(d_K^n)$), is a quasi-isomorphism: $H^r(F(\varphi)):H^r(F(K^\bullet))\to H^r(F(L^\bullet))$ is an isomorphism of $\mathcal B$ for every $r\in\mathbf Z$.
