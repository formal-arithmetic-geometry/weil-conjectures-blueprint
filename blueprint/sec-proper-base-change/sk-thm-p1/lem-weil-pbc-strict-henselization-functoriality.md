Let $g:T\to S$ be a morphism of schemes [def-pc-scheme], let $\bar t:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to T$ be a geometric point [def-weil-geometric-point] lying over $t\in T$, and let $\bar s=g\circ\bar t$, a geometric point of $S$ lying over $s=g(t)$. For a geometric point $\bar x:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$ of a scheme $Y$ lying over $y$, write $\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{Y,\bar x}=\varinjlim_{(V,\bar v)}A_V$ and $Y_{(\bar x)}=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{Y,\bar x}$ as in [def-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-limit] (colimit over the affine pointed etale neighbourhoods $(V=\operatorname{Spec}A_V,\bar v)$ of $\bar x$ [def-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods]), with its canonical maps $c_V:Y_{(\bar x)}\to V$ and $j_Y:Y_{(\bar x)}\to Y$ (the composite of $c_V$ with $V\to Y$, for any $V$), and write $\kappa(\bar x)\subset\Omega$ for the separable algebraic closure [def-weil-pbc-relative-separable-closure] of the residue field $\kappa(y)$ [def-weil-residue-field] inside $\Omega$, which is a $\kappa(y)$-extension through $\bar x$. Then:

1. $\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{Y,\bar x}$ is a strictly henselian local ring [def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology]; $j_Y$ maps the closed point to $y$; and there is a unique isomorphism of $\kappa(y)$-algebras from the residue field of $\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{Y,\bar x}$ onto $\kappa(\bar x)$ such that, for every affine pointed etale neighbourhood $(V,\bar v)$ of $\bar x$, $\bar v$ factors as $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(\bar x)\to Y_{(\bar x)}\xrightarrow{c_V}V$ (the first map from the inclusion $\kappa(\bar x)\subset\Omega$, the second the closed point); in particular $\bar x$ factors as $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(\bar x)\to Y_{(\bar x)}\xrightarrow{j_Y}Y$. Moreover $\kappa(\bar x)$ is separably closed.
2. Inside $\Omega$ one has $\kappa(\bar s)\subset\kappa(\bar t)$, where $\kappa(s)$ and $\kappa(t)$ are embedded in $\Omega$ through $\bar s$ and $\bar t$ (so that $\kappa(s)\to\kappa(t)\to\Omega$ is the embedding of $\kappa(s)$).
3. There is a unique local ring homomorphism [def-weil-locally-ringed-space-morphism] $h:\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{S,\bar s}\to\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{T,\bar t}$ such that $j_S\circ\operatorname{Spec}h=g\circ j_T$ and the map induced by $h$ on residue fields is, under the identifications of (1), the inclusion $\kappa(\bar s)\subset\kappa(\bar t)$ of (2).
4. Compatibility with neighbourhoods: let $(V,\bar v)$ be an affine pointed etale neighbourhood of $\bar s$ and $(W,\bar w)$ an affine pointed etale neighbourhood of $\bar t$ together with a morphism $w:W\to V$ with $(V\to S)\circ w=g\circ(W\to T)$ and $w\circ\bar w=\bar v$ (for instance $W$ an affine open of the fibre product $V\times_ST$ [def-pc-fibre-product] containing the image of $\bar w=(\bar v,\bar t)$). Then the two morphisms $T_{(\bar t)}\to V$, namely $T_{(\bar t)}\xrightarrow{c_W}W\xrightarrow{w}V$ and $T_{(\bar t)}\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Spec}h}S_{(\bar s)}\xrightarrow{c_V}V$, are equal.
