*Setup and decomposition upstairs.* Let $B$ be a finite $R$-algebra. Apply `Algebra.exists_etale_completeOrthogonalIdempotents_forall_liesOver_eq` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Etale/QuasiFinite.lean)) to $R\to B$ and the prime $\mathfrak m$: there are a ring map $R\to R'$ with $\operatorname{Spec}R'\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ etale, a prime $\mathfrak m'\subset R'$ with preimage $\mathfrak m$ in $R$ and $\kappa=\kappa(\mathfrak m)\to\kappa(\mathfrak m')$ bijective (for the maximal ideal of the local ring $R$, $R_{\mathfrak m}=R$ and $\kappa(\mathfrak m)=R/\mathfrak m=\kappa$), an integer $n\ge0$, complete orthogonal idempotents $e_1,\dots,e_n,e_\infty$ of $C=R'\otimes_RB$, and primes $Q_1,\dots,Q_n$ of $C$ over $\mathfrak m'$, such that $e_i\notin Q_i$, and every prime $Q$ of $C$ over $\mathfrak m'$ contains $e_\infty$ and equals $Q_i$ whenever $e_i\notin Q$. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean))).

*The retraction.* $(R',\mathfrak m')$ satisfies the hypothesis of the statement ($\operatorname{Spec}R'\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ etale, $\mathfrak m'$ over $\mathfrak m$, $\kappa\to\kappa(\mathfrak m')$ an isomorphism, by step 1), so there is an $R$-algebra homomorphism $\tau:R'\to R$ with $\tau^{-1}(\mathfrak m)=\mathfrak m'$. In Lean the neighborhood is first replaced by its ULift to Type (max u v). The induced residue-field map is an isomorphism: the maps induced by the lift and its inverse compose to the identity, checked on elements of the ring. Compose its map with the original residue-field map, apply the hypothesis upstairs, and compose the resulting retraction with the lift to recover a retraction of the original neighborhood. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean))).

*Base change back along $\tau$.* Let $\varphi:C=R'\otimes_RB\to B$ be the ring homomorphism $r'\otimes b\mapsto\tau(r')b$ (well defined by the universal property of the tensor product of commutative $R$-algebras, applied to the $R$-algebra maps $R'\xrightarrow{\tau}R\to B$ and $\mathrm{id}_B$). It is surjective, since $\varphi(1\otimes b)=b$; this is the identification $B=(B\otimes_RR')\otimes_{R',\tau}R$ of the source, i.e. $B=C/\ker\varphi$. Its restriction to $R'$ (via $r'\mapsto r'\otimes1$) is $r'\mapsto\tau(r')\cdot1_B$. Hence for every prime $\mathfrak q$ of $B$ whose preimage in $R$ is $\mathfrak m$, the prime $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$ of $C$ has preimage $\tau^{-1}(\mathfrak m)=\mathfrak m'$ in $R'$ (step 2), i.e. lies over $\mathfrak m'$; and $\mathfrak q=\varphi(\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q))$ by surjectivity.

*Idempotents of $B$ and the primes over $\mathfrak m$.* Put $f_i=\varphi(e_i)$ ($1\le i\le n$) and $f_\infty=\varphi(e_\infty)$; as $\varphi$ is a ring homomorphism these are complete orthogonal idempotents of $B$. Let $\mathfrak q$ be a prime of $B$ over $\mathfrak m$ and $Q=\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$, a prime of $C$ over $\mathfrak m'$ (step 3). By step 1, $e_\infty\in Q$, so $f_\infty\in\mathfrak q$. If $f_i\notin\mathfrak q$, then $e_i\notin Q$, so $Q=Q_i$ (step 1) and $\mathfrak q=\varphi(Q_i)$ (step 3). Hence: every prime of $B$ over $\mathfrak m$ contains $f_\infty$, and for each $i$ there is at most one prime of $B$ over $\mathfrak m$ not containing $f_i$, namely $\varphi(Q_i)$.

*Maximal-ideal separation and local quotient factors.* Every maximal ideal of the finite R-algebra B lies over the maximal ideal of R, by `Ideal.isMaximal_comap_of_isIntegral_of_isMaximal` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/GoingUp.lean)). Thus, by step 4, for each idempotent f_i at most one maximal ideal misses f_i; no maximal ideal misses f_infinity. For any finite complete orthogonal family (f_j) with this maximal-ideal separation property, retain J = {j | f_j != 0}. Put K_j = (1-f_j)B. The idempotency identity proves f_j is not in K_j: if f_j=(1-f_j)b, multiplication by f_j gives f_j=0. Hence B/K_j is nonzero. A maximal ideal of B/K_j pulls back to a maximal ideal of B containing 1-f_j and therefore missing f_j. Surjectivity of the quotient map makes this pullback injective, so B/K_j has exactly one maximal ideal and is local. The last idempotent cannot be nonzero, since its quotient would have a maximal ideal whose pullback misses f_infinity. This is implemented by the proved private helper product_local_of_separating_idempotents, applied to the entire family including the last idempotent.

*Product isomorphism and finite indexing.* Removing the zero terms preserves orthogonality and the sum 1. Mathlib's CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents.bijective_pi gives a bijective ring map B -> product_{j in J} B/(1-f_j)B. Its components are R-algebra quotient maps, so AlgEquiv.ofBijective upgrades this map to an R-algebra isomorphism. Reindex the finite subtype J by Fin (Fintype.card J) using AlgEquiv.piCongrLeft'. The factors are the local quotient rings from step 5 and all lie in the universe of B. For B=0, J is empty and the same construction gives the empty product.
