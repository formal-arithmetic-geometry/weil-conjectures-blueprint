The inverse image of U is affine since f is finite. Use IsOpenImmersion.isPullback and IsAffineOpen.SpecMap_appLE_fromSpec to identify the square of the two affine charts as a pullback. The induced fibre square and hU.fromSpec_primeIdealOf transport the closed-immersion hypothesis to the fibre of Spec.map (f.app U). Spec.fiberToSpecResidueFieldIso, which combines the affine pullback and residue-field identifications, transports it to Spec of the scalar map κ(p) → κ(p) ⊗[A] B. The closed-immersion affine criterion gives surjectivity on global sections, and arrowIsoΓSpecOfIsAffine transports this to surjectivity of the scalar ring map. Let φ be the A-linear scalar map A → B and q the quotient map B → B/range φ. The element 1 belongs to range φ. Every element of κ(p) ⊗[A] B is k ⊗ 1 by scalar-map surjectivity, so q.lTensor κ(p) is zero. This tensor map is surjective by LinearMap.lTensor_surjective and Submodule.mkQ_surjective. Thus every element of κ(p) ⊗[A] (B/range φ) is zero, which gives Subsingleton.
