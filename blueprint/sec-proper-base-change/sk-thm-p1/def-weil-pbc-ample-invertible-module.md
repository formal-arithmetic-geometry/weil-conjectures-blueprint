Let $X$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme] and $\mathcal L$ an invertible $\mathcal O_X$-module [def-sc-invertible-module]. For $n\ge0$ write $\mathcal L^{\otimes n}$ for the $n$-fold tensor product $\mathcal L\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\dots\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal L$ [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] (with $\mathcal L^{\otimes0}=\mathcal O_X$); it is an invertible $\mathcal O_X$-module. For a global section $s\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal L^{\otimes n})$ put $X_s=\{x\in X:\ s_x\notin\mathfrak m_x(\mathcal L^{\otimes n})_x\}$, where $s_x$ is the germ of $s$ in the stalk $(\mathcal L^{\otimes n})_x$ `TopCat.Presheaf.stalk, PresheafOfModules.germ_ringCat_smul (instance Module (R.stalk x) (TopCat.Presheaf.stalk M.presheaf x))` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Stalk.lean)) and $\mathfrak m_x$ the maximal ideal of the local ring $\mathcal O_{X,x}$; equivalently, $X_s$ is the set of points where the value of $s$ in $(\mathcal L^{\otimes n})_x\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,x}}\kappa(x)$ is nonzero ($\kappa(x)$ the residue field `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean))). The set $X_s$ is open in $X$ (Stacks Modules Lemma 01CY; the invertibility of $\mathcal L^{\otimes n}$ and the openness of $X_s$ are the statements recorded in `pointers`), and $X_s$ is viewed as an open subscheme of $X$. $\mathcal L$ is *ample* if (1) $X$ is quasi-compact `CompactSpace` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Defs/Filter.lean)), and (2) for every $x\in X$ there exist $n\ge1$ and $s\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal L^{\otimes n})$ such that $x\in X_s$ and $X_s$ is an affine open of $X$ [def-pc-scheme]. This is the absolute notion of Stacks Properties Definition 01PS (EGA II 4.5.3).
