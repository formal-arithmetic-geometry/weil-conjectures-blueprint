Let $\varphi:A\to B$ be a flat ring map of commutative rings and $I\subseteq A$ an ideal; write $I^nB$ for the ideal of $B$ generated by $\varphi(I^n)$. Assume that the ring map $A/I\to B/IB$ induced by $\varphi$ is an isomorphism. Then for every $n\ge1$ the ring map $A/I^n\to B/I^nB$ induced by $\varphi$ is an isomorphism.
