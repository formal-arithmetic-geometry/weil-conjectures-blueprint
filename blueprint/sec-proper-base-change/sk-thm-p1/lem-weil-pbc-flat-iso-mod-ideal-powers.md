title = "Flat maps that are isomorphisms modulo I are isomorphisms modulo all powers of I"
lean = ["RingHom.bijective_quotientMap_pow_of_flat"]

Let $\varphi:A\to B$ be a flat ring map of commutative rings `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)) and $I\subseteq A$ an ideal; write $I^nB$ for the ideal of $B$ generated by $\varphi(I^n)$. Assume that the ring map $A/I\to B/IB$ induced by $\varphi$ is an isomorphism. Then for every $n\ge1$ the ring map $A/I^n\to B/I^nB$ induced by $\varphi$ is an isomorphism.
