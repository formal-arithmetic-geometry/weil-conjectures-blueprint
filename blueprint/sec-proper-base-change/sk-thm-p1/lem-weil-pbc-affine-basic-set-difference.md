Let $A$ be a commutative ring and write $D(f)=\{\mathfrak p:f\notin\mathfrak p\}$ and $V(g_1,\dots,g_n)=\{\mathfrak p:g_1,\dots,g_n\in\mathfrak p\}$ in $\operatorname{Spec}A$. If $B=D(f)\cap V(g_1,\dots,g_r)$ and $C=D(h)\cap V(k_1,\dots,k_s)$ are basic subsets of $\operatorname{Spec}A$, then $B\setminus C$ is a finite pairwise-disjoint union of basic subsets (the empty union is allowed).
