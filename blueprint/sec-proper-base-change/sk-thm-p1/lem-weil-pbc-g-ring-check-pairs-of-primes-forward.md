Assume IsGRing R. For every pair q ≤ p, identify the quotient completion formal fibre with the corresponding fibre of the regular completion map R → completion(R_p), using localization at a prime quotient, completion of a quotient, and tensor base change. Regularity and Noetherianity transport across the resulting κ(q)-algebra isomorphism.
