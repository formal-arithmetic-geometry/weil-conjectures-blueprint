**Step 1 (the ring maps).** For $w\in\{u,v,u\circ v\}$ with source $S$ and target $T$ let $\gamma_w:\Lambda_T\to w_*\Lambda_S$ be the morphism of sheaves of rings of [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (1): for an object $W$ of $T_{\Et}$, $\gamma_{w,W}$ sends the image in $\Lambda_T(W)$ of the constant section $r\in\Lambda$ to the image of the constant section $r$ in $\Lambda_S(W\times_TS)$; by [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (3) the $\Lambda_T$-module structure of the direct image $w_*F$ of a sheaf of $\Lambda_S$-modules $F$ is: $a\in\Lambda_T(W)$ acts on $(w_*F)(W)=F(W\times_TS)$ as $\gamma_{w,W}(a)$. We claim $d_{u,v,\Lambda_Z}\circ\gamma_{u\circ v}=u_*(\gamma_v)\circ\gamma_u$ as morphisms of sheaves of rings $\Lambda_X\to u_*v_*\Lambda_Z$, where $d_{u,v,\Lambda_Z}(W)=\Lambda_Z(\phi_W)$ is restriction along the $Z$-isomorphism $\phi_W:(W\times_XY)\times_YZ\xrightarrow{\sim}W\times_XZ$ of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (1) (a ring map, $\Lambda_Z$ being a sheaf of rings `CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean))), and $u_*(\gamma_v)(W)=\gamma_{v,W\times_XY}$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. Both sides are morphisms of sheaves of rings out of the constant sheaf $\Lambda_X$ on $X_{\Et}$, whose final object is $\mathrm{id}_X$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site]; by `CategoryTheory.constantSheafAdj` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean)) (for rings) they are equal once they agree on the images $c_\Lambda(r)\in\Lambda_X(X)$ of the constant sections. The left side sends $c_\Lambda(r)$ to the constant section $r$ of $\Lambda_Z(X\times_XZ)$ and then, by restriction along $\phi_X$, to the constant section $r$ of $\Lambda_Z((X\times_XY)\times_YZ)$ (restriction maps of the sheafification commute with the maps from the constant presheaf, whose restriction maps are the identity `CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean))); the right side sends it to the constant section $r$ of $\Lambda_Y(X\times_XY)$ and then, by $\gamma_{v,X\times_XY}$, to the constant section $r$ of $\Lambda_Z((X\times_XY)\times_YZ)$. So the two agree and the claim holds.

**Step 2 (clause (1): $d_{u,v,F}$ is $\Lambda_X$-linear).** Let $F\in\Mod(Z_{\Et},\Lambda)$, $W$ an object of $X_{\Et}$, $a\in\Lambda_X(W)$ and $x\in((u\circ v)_*F)(W)=F(W\times_XZ)$. By [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (3) (step 1), $a$ acts on $((u\circ v)_*F)(W)$ as $\gamma_{u\circ v,W}(a)\in\Lambda_Z(W\times_XZ)$, and, applying the module direct image twice, on $(u_*v_*F)(W)=(v_*F)(W\times_XY)=F((W\times_XY)\times_YZ)$ as $\gamma_{v,W\times_XY}(\gamma_{u,W}(a))$. The component $d_{u,v,F}(W)=F(\phi_W)$ of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (1) is the restriction map of $F$ along the morphism $\phi_W$ of $Z_{\Et}$, hence semilinear over $\Lambda_Z(\phi_W)$ `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)): $d_{u,v,F}(W)(\gamma_{u\circ v,W}(a)\,x)=\Lambda_Z(\phi_W)(\gamma_{u\circ v,W}(a))\,d_{u,v,F}(W)(x)=\gamma_{v,W\times_XY}(\gamma_{u,W}(a))\,d_{u,v,F}(W)(x)$ by step 1. So $d_{u,v,F}(W)(ax)=a\,d_{u,v,F}(W)(x)$: $d_{u,v,F}$ is a morphism of $\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$.

**Step 3 (the adjoint of a linear map under the composite adjunction is linear).** Let $G\in\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$, $M\in\Mod(Z_{\Et},\Lambda)$ and $\varphi:G\to u_*v_*M$ a $\Lambda_X$-linear morphism. Under the composite adjunction $v^{-1}u^{-1}\dashv u_*v_*$ of abelian sheaves `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Adjunction/Basic.lean)) (of $u^{-1}\dashv u_*$ and $v^{-1}\dashv v_*$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image]), with unit $\eta_{1,G}=u_*(\eta_{v,u^{-1}G})\circ\eta_{u,G}$, the hom bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_{\Ab}(v^{-1}u^{-1}G,M)\to\operatorname{Hom}_{\Ab}(G,u_*v_*M)$, $\chi\mapsto u_*v_*(\chi)\circ\eta_{1,G}$, is the composite of the bijections $\chi\mapsto v_*(\chi)\circ\eta_{v,u^{-1}G}$ and $\psi\mapsto u_*(\psi)\circ\eta_{u,G}$. By [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (3) for $u$ (with the $\Lambda_Y$-module $v_*M$) and for $v$, each of these two bijections restricts to a bijection between linear morphisms (for the module structures of [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (2), (3): $u^{-1}G$ a $\Lambda_Y$-module, $v^{-1}u^{-1}G$ a $\Lambda_Z$-module, $v_*M$ a $\Lambda_Y$-module, $u_*v_*M$ a $\Lambda_X$-module). Hence the unique abelian morphism $\chi:v^{-1}u^{-1}G\to M$ with $u_*v_*(\chi)\circ\eta_{1,G}=\varphi$ is $\Lambda_Z$-linear.

**Step 4 (clause (2): $c_{u,v,G}$ is $\Lambda_Z$-linear).** Let $G\in\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$ and $M=(u\circ v)^{-1}G$ with its $\Lambda_Z$-module structure of [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (2). The unit $\eta_{u\circ v,G}:G\to(u\circ v)_*M$ is $\Lambda_X$-linear, the unit of the module adjunction being the abelian one [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (3), and $d_{u,v,M}:(u\circ v)_*M\to u_*v_*M$ is $\Lambda_X$-linear by step 2; so $\varphi=d_{u,v,M}\circ\eta_{u\circ v,G}$ is $\Lambda_X$-linear. By [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (2), $u_*v_*(c_{u,v,G})\circ\eta_{1,G}=\varphi$, so $c_{u,v,G}$ is the unique abelian morphism $\chi$ of step 3 for this $\varphi$, hence $\Lambda_Z$-linear. (`CategoryTheory.Adjunction.comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Adjunction/Basic.lean))).

**Step 5 (isomorphisms of functors on modules, and uniqueness).** By steps 2 and 4 the components $d_{u,v,F}$ ($F\in\Mod(Z_{\Et},\Lambda)$) and $c_{u,v,G}$ ($G\in\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$) are morphisms of sheaves of modules; they are isomorphisms of abelian sheaves [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (1), (2), and the inverse of a bijective $\Lambda_T(W)$-linear map is linear, so they are isomorphisms in $\Mod(-,\Lambda)$ `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)). The module functors $w_*$, $w^{-1}$ act on morphisms as the abelian ones [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (2), (3), so the naturality squares of $d_{u,v}$ and $c_{u,v}$ for module morphisms are among those of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (1), (2): $d_{u,v}$ and $c_{u,v}$ are isomorphisms of functors $\Mod(Z_{\Et},\Lambda)\to\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$ and $\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)\to\Mod(Z_{\Et},\Lambda)$ with the abelian ones as underlying morphisms. The unit of the composite module adjunction $v^{-1}u^{-1}\dashv u_*v_*$ is $u_*\eta_vu^{-1}\circ\eta_u$ `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Adjunction/Basic.lean)) with the module units equal to the abelian ones [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (3), so $c_{u,v}$ carries it to $d_{u,v}\circ\eta_{u\circ v}$ by [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition] (2). Uniqueness: if $c'$ is an isomorphism of functors on modules with $u_*v_*(c'_G)\circ\eta_{1,G}=d_{u,v,(u\circ v)^{-1}G}\circ\eta_{u\circ v,G}$ for every $G$, then $c'_G$ and $c_{u,v,G}$ have the same image under the bijection $\chi\mapsto u_*v_*(\chi)\circ\eta_{1,G}$ of step 3, so $c'_G=c_{u,v,G}$ for every $G$.
