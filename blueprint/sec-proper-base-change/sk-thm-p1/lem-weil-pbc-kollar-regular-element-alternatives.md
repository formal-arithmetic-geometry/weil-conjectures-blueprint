title = "Kollar alternatives when the maximal ideal is associated modulo a regular element"

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a Noetherian local commutative ring. Let $x\in\mathfrak m$ act injectively on $R$, and suppose $\mathfrak m$ is an associated prime of $R/xR$, meaning that some nonzero class has annihilator exactly $\mathfrak m$. Then either $R$ is a regular local ring of Krull dimension one, or there exists a nonzero commutative ring $S$ and a ring map $\varphi:R\to S$, making $S$ a finite $R$-module, not bijective, such that for some integer $n\ge1$ we have $\mathfrak m^n\ker\varphi=0$ and $\mathfrak m^n S\subseteq\varphi(R)$, and there is no $z\in S$ with $\{a\in R:\varphi(a)z=0\}=\mathfrak m$.
