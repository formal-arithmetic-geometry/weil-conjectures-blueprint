Write $g=f\circ\iota:A\to S$. For every $n$, the projection $C\to A/I^n$ is surjective, has kernel $I^nC=J^n$ because $I$ is finitely generated, and sends $\iota(a)$ to $a\bmod I^n$; so it induces an isomorphism $e:C/J^n\cong A/I^n$.

Put $K=g(I^n)S$, the ideal of $S$ generated by $g(I^n)$. Since $f(J^n)S=f(\iota(I^n)C)S=K$, $f$ induces $\bar f:C/J^n\to S/K$. As $g$ is flat, flat base change makes $A/I^n\to S/K\cong S\otimes_A A/I^n$ flat.

By the formula for $e$, $\bar f\circ e^{-1}$ is the canonical map $A/I^n\to S/K$; flatness is invariant under precomposition with a ring isomorphism, so $\bar f$ is flat.

Finally $J^nS=K$ as $C$-submodules of $S$, so the module quotient $S/J^nS$ is the ring quotient $S/K$, and this identification is $C/J^n$-linear. Hence $S/J^nS$ is a flat $C/J^n$-module. For $n=0$ both quotients are zero and the same construction applies.
