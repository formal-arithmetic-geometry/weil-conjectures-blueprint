Let $Y$ be a scheme, $\Lambda$ a commutative ring and $\mathcal G$ a sheaf of $\Lambda_Y$-modules on the small etale site $Y_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves] which is of finite type `SheafOfModules.IsFiniteType` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/Generators.lean)). Let $\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$ be a geometric point `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.SpecToEquivOfField, IsAlgClosed` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean)) and $\mathcal G_{\bar y}$ the stalk of the underlying abelian sheaf of $\mathcal G$ [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks], a $\Lambda$-module by $\lambda\cdot(\text{germ of }s)=\text{germ of }\lambda s$. Then $\mathcal G_{\bar y}$ is a finitely generated $\Lambda$-module `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)). More precisely: if $u:U\to Y$ is etale, $\mathcal G|_U$ is generated by the global sections $s_1,\dots,s_n\in\mathcal G(U)$ and $\bar u$ is a geometric point of $U$ with $u\circ\bar u=\bar y$, then $\mathcal G_{\bar y}$ is generated by the germs of $s_1,\dots,s_n$ at the pointed etale neighbourhood $(U,\bar u)$.
