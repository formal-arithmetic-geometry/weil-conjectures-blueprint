Let $Y$ be a scheme, $\Lambda$ a commutative ring and $\mathcal G$ a sheaf of $\Lambda_Y$-modules on the small etale site $Y_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves] which is of finite type. Let $\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$ be a geometric point and $\mathcal G_{\bar y}$ the stalk of the underlying abelian sheaf of $\mathcal G$ [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks], a $\Lambda$-module by $\lambda\cdot(\text{germ of }s)=\text{germ of }\lambda s$. Then $\mathcal G_{\bar y}$ is a finitely generated $\Lambda$-module. More precisely: if $u:U\to Y$ is etale, $\mathcal G|_U$ is generated by the global sections $s_1,\dots,s_n\in\mathcal G(U)$ and $\bar u$ is a geometric point of $U$ with $u\circ\bar u=\bar y$, then $\mathcal G_{\bar y}$ is generated by the germs of $s_1,\dots,s_n$ at the pointed etale neighbourhood $(U,\bar u)$.
