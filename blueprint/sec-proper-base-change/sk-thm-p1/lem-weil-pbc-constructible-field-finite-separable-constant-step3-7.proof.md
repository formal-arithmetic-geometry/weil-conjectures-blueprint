Choose i and y in the etale cover above the unique point of Spec k using Cover.exists_eq. Set U = d.cover.X i and f = d.cover.f i; its etaleness is d.cover.map_prop i. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean))).

Set K = U.residueField y with k-algebra structure (f.residueFieldAlgebraMap y).toAlgebra. The algebra map k to the affine residue field at f(y) is bijective by Ideal.algEquivResidueFieldOfField, and the inverse of Scheme.Spec.residueFieldIso is bijective. Compose these two etale isomorphisms with f.etale_residueFieldMap y (the already proved library theorem in WeilConjectures.Mathlib.AlgebraicGeometry.Morphisms.FormallyUnramified). By RingHom.Etale.stableUnderComposition the structure map k to K is etale. Mathlib Algebra.FormallyUnramified.finite_of_free and Algebra.FormallyEtale.iff_isSeparable give finite dimensionality and separability. By the proved library theorem f.specMap_residueFieldAlgebraMap y, Spec(algebraMap k K) equals U.fromSpecResidueField y followed by f. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean))).

Choose the finite module M and localized trivialization e from hd i. Transport e through Etale.overSheafEquiv. Compose overSheafEquivFunctorObjOverIso.symm, its image of e, and Equivalence.constantSheafSheafCongrIso at M to obtain an isomorphism f^*G with the constant sheaf of the same value M on U. These are already implemented comparisons of the localized and small etale sites; no change of module value is needed. ([lemma-constructible-pullback-locally-constant]).

Write g = U.fromSpecResidueField y. Rewrite Spec(algebraMap k K) as g followed by f using the equality above. Compose Etale.upperStarComp at G, the image of the isomorphism in the preceding step under g^*, and upperStarConstantSheafIso at M. This is the required isomorphism on Spec K. Return K, its field and algebra structures, finite dimensionality, separability, and the original M with its unchanged finiteness proof. ([lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition], [lemma-constructible-pullback-constant]).
