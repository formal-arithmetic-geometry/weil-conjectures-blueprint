Let $\varphi:(R,\mathfrak m)\to(S,\mathfrak m_S)$ be a local homomorphism of local rings (commutative rings with exactly one maximal ideal; $\varphi(\mathfrak m)\subseteq\mathfrak m_S$) with $S$ Noetherian. Let $M$ be an $R$-module which is flat, let $N$ be a finite $S$-module, regarded as an $R$-module via $\varphi$, and let $u:N\to M$ be an $R$-linear map. If the induced map $\bar u:N/\mathfrak mN\to M/\mathfrak mM$ is injective, then $u$ is injective and the $R$-module $M/u(N)$ is flat.
