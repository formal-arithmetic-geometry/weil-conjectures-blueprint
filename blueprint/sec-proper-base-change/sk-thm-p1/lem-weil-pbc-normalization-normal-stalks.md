Let $f:T\to X$ be a quasi-compact and quasi-separated morphism of schemes [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions] with $T$ an integral scheme all of whose local rings $\mathcal O_{T,t}$ are integrally closed domains, and let $\nu:X'\to X$ be the normalization of $X$ in $T$ (the relative spectrum over $X$ of the integral closure of $\mathcal O_X$ in $f_*\mathcal O_T$). Then every local ring $\mathcal O_{X',y}$, $y\in X'$, is an integrally closed domain.
