Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a finite projective $A$-module, i.e. $M$ is finitely generated and projective. For every prime ideal $\mathfrak p\subset A$ there exists an element $f\in A\setminus\mathfrak p$ such that the localization $M_f$ is a finite free $A_f$-module, i.e. $M_f\cong A_f^{\oplus r}$ as $A_f$-modules for some integer $r\ge0$.
