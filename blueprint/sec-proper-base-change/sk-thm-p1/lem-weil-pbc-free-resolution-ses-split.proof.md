Lift Q, Q′, Q″ to the common universe max(u,v₁,v₂,v₃) with ULift.moduleEquiv and conjugate u and v. Injectivity, exactness and surjectivity are preserved by these linear equivalences. Package the resulting sequence as ResolutionSES. (`CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean))).

For a short exact sequence E, construct P=E.A →₀ R and T=E.C →₀ R with evaluation maps η and θ, and a linear lift of θ obtained by choosing preimages of all elements of E.C under the proved surjection E.p. Define μ(x,z)=E.i(η(x))+lift(z). The endpoint evaluation maps are surjective, and an explicit subtraction and lift argument proves μ surjective. These modules and P×T are free by the Finsupp and product free-module instances. (`Module.Free` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/FreeModule/Basic.lean))).

Define ki:ker η→ker μ by x↦(x,0) and kp:ker μ→ker θ by (x,z)↦z. Injectivity of ki follows from first projection. If kp(y)=0, injectivity of E.i forces η(y.1)=0, giving exactness. For z∈ker θ, exactness of E gives a with E.i(a)=−lift(z); choose x with η(x)=a, so (x,z) lies in ker μ and maps to z. This proves kp surjective directly, without the general snake lemma. (`CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean))).

Recursively define stage(0)=E and stage(n+1) as the short exact sequence of the three kernels just constructed. In degree n use stage(n).P, stage(n).P×stage(n).T and stage(n).T. Define differentials as the next evaluation map followed by kernel inclusion. A surjection onto ker f followed by inclusion has range ker f, and its kernel equals the kernel of the surjection because inclusion is injective. This proves all three augmented exactness assertions. (`CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean))).

The inclusion and second projection are chain maps: for the first use the definition of μ at the next stage and the induced kernel inclusion ki; for the second use p(μ(z))=θ(z.2) at the next stage and the induced kernel projection kp. Transport the degree-zero augmentations back through ULift.moduleEquiv. Their kernels are unchanged by these injective equivalences and their surjectivity is preserved. The augmentation squares follow from μ(x,0)=i(η(x)) and p(μ(x,z))=θ(z.2). First projection and second inclusion give the remaining splitting maps. All four splitting identities are the product identities checked by extensionality. Package these explicit witnesses into the original dependent existential.
