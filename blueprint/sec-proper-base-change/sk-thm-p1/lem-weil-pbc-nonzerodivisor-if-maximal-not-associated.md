title = "If the maximal ideal is not an associated prime of a finite module, it contains a nonzerodivisor on it"

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a Noetherian local ring and $M$ a finite $R$-module such that $\mathfrak m$ is not an associated prime of $M$, i.e. there is no $z\in M$ with $\{a\in R: az=0\}=\mathfrak m$. Then there is an $x\in\mathfrak m$ which is a nonzerodivisor on $M$ (multiplication by $x$ on $M$ is injective).
