Let $T$ be a scheme, $T_{\Et}$ its small etale site [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], $n\ge1$ an integer, $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$ the constant sheaf of rings with value $\mathbf Z/n$ on $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], $\Mod(T_{\Et},\mathbf Z/n)$ the category of sheaves of $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$-modules [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves], and $\mathrm{forget}:\Mod(T_{\Et},\mathbf Z/n)\to\Ab(T_{\Et})$ the functor to abelian sheaves [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves] forgetting the module structure. Then: (1) for every sheaf $G$ of $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$-modules, $n\cdot\mathrm{id}=0$ on the abelian sheaf $\mathrm{forget}(G)$; (2) for every abelian sheaf $F$ with $n\cdot\mathrm{id}_F=0$ there is exactly one structure of sheaf of $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$-modules on $F$ extending its structure of abelian sheaf, and in it the image $\iota_U(\bar a)\in(\mathbf Z/n)_T(U)$ of the constant $\bar a\in\mathbf Z/n$ (with $\iota$ the canonical map from the constant presheaf to its sheafification) acts on $F(U)$ as multiplication by any integer $a$ lifting $\bar a$; (3) for sheaves $G,G'$ of $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$-modules, every morphism of abelian sheaves $\mathrm{forget}(G)\to\mathrm{forget}(G')$ is $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$-linear. Hence $\mathrm{forget}$ is fully faithful, and its image is the full subcategory of abelian sheaves killed by $n$; in particular an isomorphism of abelian sheaves between two sheaves of $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$-modules is an isomorphism of sheaves of $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$-modules. (Ingredients: every section of $(\mathbf Z/n)_T$ over $U$ is, on some etale covering of $U$, the image of constants $\bar a_i$; a unital additive action of $\bar a$ is multiplication by $a$; separation of $F$. Existence in (2): the action of the constant presheaf $\mathbf Z/n$ on $F$ by $\bar a\cdot s=as$ is well defined because $nF=0$ and extends along the sheafification of the constant presheaf of rings, sheafification preserving finite products.)
