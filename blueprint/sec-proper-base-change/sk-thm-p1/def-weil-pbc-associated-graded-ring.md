Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $J\subseteq A$ an ideal; $J^0=A$, $J^{k+1}=J^kJ$, and $A/J$ is the quotient ring `AlgHom, Ideal.Quotient.algebra, Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ, Ideal (IdemCommSemiring: I * J, I ^ k)` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Hom.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Operations.lean)). For $n\ge0$ let $G_n=J^n/J^{n+1}$, the quotient of the $A$-module $J^n$ by its submodule $J^{n+1}$ `Submodule.hasQuotient, Submodule.Quotient.module, Submodule.mkQ, Submodule.mkQ_surjective, Submodule.ker_mkQ` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Defs.lean)), whose elements are the classes $a\bmod J^{n+1}$, $a\in J^n$; so $G_0=A/J$ as an abelian group. The *associated graded ring* of $A$ with respect to $J$ is the abelian group $\operatorname{gr}_J(A)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}G_n$ `DirectSum, DirectSum.instModule, DirectSum.lof, DirectSum.component, DirectSum.induction_on` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Module.lean)), with canonical inclusions $\iota_n:G_n\to\operatorname{gr}_J(A)$; an element $\xi=(\xi_n)_n$ is the finite sum $\sum_n\iota_n(\xi_n)$, $\xi_n$ is its *homogeneous component of degree $n$*, and the elements of $\iota_n(G_n)$ are the *homogeneous elements of degree $n$* (written $\xi_n$ for $\iota_n(\xi_n)$). Its multiplication is $(\xi\eta)_k=\sum_{n+m=k}\mu_{n,m}(\xi_n,\eta_m)$, where $\mu_{n,m}:G_n\times G_m\to G_{n+m}$ is the map $(a\bmod J^{n+1},\,b\bmod J^{m+1})\mapsto ab\bmod J^{n+m+1}$ ($a\in J^n$, $b\in J^m$); so $(a\bmod J^{n+1})\cdot(b\bmod J^{m+1})=ab\bmod J^{n+m+1}$ on homogeneous elements, and the product is biadditive. That $\mu_{n,m}$ is well defined and biadditive, that this multiplication makes $\operatorname{gr}_J(A)$ a commutative ring with unit $1\bmod J$ in degree $0$, and that $\iota_0:A/J\to\operatorname{gr}_J(A)$ is an injective ring homomorphism, is the statement recorded in `pointers`; $\operatorname{gr}_J(A)$ is regarded as an $A/J$-algebra `AlgHom, Ideal.Quotient.algebra, Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ, Ideal (IdemCommSemiring: I * J, I ^ k)` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Hom.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Operations.lean)) through $\iota_0$, so that $(a\bmod J)\cdot(x\bmod J^{n+1})=ax\bmod J^{n+1}$ for $a\in A$, $x\in J^n$. An ideal $\mathfrak b\subseteq\operatorname{gr}_J(A)$ is *homogeneous* if for every $\xi\in\mathfrak b$ and every $n\ge0$ the homogeneous component $\iota_n(\xi_n)$ lies in $\mathfrak b$.
