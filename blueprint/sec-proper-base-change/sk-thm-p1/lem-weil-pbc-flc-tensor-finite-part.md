Let $X$ be a scheme and $\Lambda$ a commutative ring, and let $F,G$ be sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on the small etale site of $X$ which are finite, i.e. whose stalks at all geometric points are finitely generated $\Lambda$-modules. Then $F\otimes_\Lambda G$ is finite.
