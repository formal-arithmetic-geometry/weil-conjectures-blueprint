title = "Flatness descends through a faithfully flat module"
lean = ["RingHom.flat_of_flat_module_of_faithfullyFlat_module"]

Let $R\to S$ be a ring map and let $M$ be an $S$-module. If $M$ is flat as an $R$-module `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)) and faithfully flat as an $S$-module `Module.FaithfullyFlat, RingHom.FaithfullyFlat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/FaithfullyFlat/Basic.lean)), then $R\to S$ is flat `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)).
