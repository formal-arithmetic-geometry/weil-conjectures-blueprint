Coefficients of a product with $g_jx_j$. For $p\in P$ and a multi-index $F$ write $\mathrm{coeff}_F(p)$ for the coefficient of $x^F$ in $p$ `MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean)), and let $\varepsilon_j$ be the $j$-th unit multi-index. Since $x_j\cdot x^F=x^{F+\varepsilon_j}$ and $F+\varepsilon_j=E$ holds iff $e_j>0$ and $F=E-\varepsilon_j$, for every $q=\sum_Fb_Fx^F\in P$ we get $x_jq=\sum_Fb_Fx^{F+\varepsilon_j}$, hence $\mathrm{coeff}_E(g_jx_jq)=g_j\,\mathrm{coeff}_{E-\varepsilon_j}(q)$ if $e_j>0$ and $\mathrm{coeff}_E(g_jx_jq)=0$ if $e_j=0$. Also $\mathrm{coeff}_E$ is $R$-linear (addition and scalar multiplication are coefficientwise).

($\Rightarrow$) Since $P$ is commutative, the ideal $J$ generated by $g_1x_1,\dots,g_ix_i$ is the set of combinations $p=\sum_{j=1}^iq_j\,g_jx_j$ with $q_j\in P$ (for $i=0$: $J=0$). For such $p$ and every $E$, by the previous step and $R$-linearity of $\mathrm{coeff}_E$, $c_E=\mathrm{coeff}_E(p)=\sum_{j=1}^i\mathrm{coeff}_E(g_jx_jq_j)=\sum_{1\le j\le i,\ e_j>0}g_j\,\mathrm{coeff}_{E-\varepsilon_j}(q_j)$, a combination with coefficients in $R$ of the $g_j$ with $j\le i$ and $e_j>0$; so $c_E\in I_E$ (if there is no such $j$ the sum is empty and $c_E=0\in I_E=0$). (`MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean))).

($\Leftarrow$) Let $p=\sum_Ec_Ex^E$ (finitely many $c_E\ne0$) with $c_E\in I_E$ for all $E$. For each $E$ with $c_E\ne0$ write $c_E=\sum_{1\le j\le i,\ e_j>0}a_{E,j}\,g_j$ with $a_{E,j}\in R$ (definition of the ideal generated by these $g_j$). For each such $j$ we have $e_j>0$, so $E-\varepsilon_j$ is a multi-index and $x^{E-\varepsilon_j}x_j=x^E$; hence $c_Ex^E=\sum_ja_{E,j}g_jx^E=\sum_j(a_{E,j}x^{E-\varepsilon_j})\,(g_jx_j)\in J$. Summing over the finitely many $E$ with $c_E\ne0$ gives $p\in J$. (`MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean))).

The direct-sum description. The map $\Phi:P\to\bigoplus_ER/I_E$, $p\mapsto(\mathrm{coeff}_E(p)\bmod I_E)_E$, lands in the direct sum (only finitely many coefficients are nonzero) and is $R$-linear (first step). It is surjective: given $(\bar a_E)_E$ with finitely many $\bar a_E\ne0$, choose lifts $a_E\in R$ with $a_E=0$ whenever $\bar a_E=0$; then $p=\sum_Ea_Ex^E\in P$ and $\Phi(p)=(\bar a_E)_E$. Its kernel is $\{p:\mathrm{coeff}_E(p)\in I_E\text{ for all }E\}$, which is $J$ by the two previous steps. Hence $\Phi$ induces an $R$-module isomorphism $P/J\cong\bigoplus_ER/I_E$, under which the class of $c\,x^E$ goes to $c\bmod I_E$ in the summand of index $E$; that is, $P/J=\bigoplus_E(R/I_E)\cdot x^E$. (`MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean))).
