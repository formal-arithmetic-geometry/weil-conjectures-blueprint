For the canonical presentation P, H_1(L_{B/A}) is the kernel of the conormal differential delta. The zero-H_1 hypothesis therefore makes delta injective (Mathlib Algebra.Extension.subsingleton_h1Cotangent). The sequence N_1 -> N_0 -> Omega_{B/A} -> 0 is exact and its last arrow is surjective by `Algebra.Extension.exact_cotangentComplex_toKaehler` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Extension/Cotangent/Basic.lean)). Consequently 0 -> N_1 -> N_0 -> Omega_{B/A} -> 0 is a short exact sequence. (`Algebra.Smooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean))).

Projectivity of Omega_{B/A} gives its flatness by `Module.Flat.of_projective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)). Apply `LinearMap.lTensor_injective_of_exact_of_flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Equalizer.lean)) directly to the short exact sequence in the preceding step and the module C = B/a. It follows that id_C tensor delta is injective. Tensor symmetry (Mathlib LinearMap.lTensor_inj_iff_rTensor_inj) gives injectivity of delta tensor id_C. Its kernel is therefore zero. Because H_1 was already zero, there is no need to factor delta through its image, to prove that image flat, or to use freeness of N_0.

The tensor H_1(L_{B/A}) tensor_B C is a zero module (Mathlib TensorProduct.uniqueLeft). The tensored kernel inclusion has subsingleton source, hence is injective and is the zero map; its image is the zero submodule, equal to the kernel of the tensored differential by the preceding step. The latter kernel is also subsingleton. Mathlib LinearEquiv.ofSubsingleton supplies the linear equivalence between these two zero modules, establishing every conjunct in the conclusion.
