Apply CategoryTheory.moduleSheaves_colimits_forgetful to O and I. Its preservation clause says that toSheaf O preserves I-colimits, so the given module colimit has underlying abelian sheaf the colimit of the abelian-sheaf diagram H = F ⋙ toSheaf O. This keeps the existing Modules 01AH/03DB/03CY tool, without constructing another presheaf of modules. ([lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful]).

Let J be the opens topology, U the forgetful functor from abelian groups to sets, L the abelian presheaf sheafification functor, S = sheafCompose J U, and G = H ⋙ sheafToPresheaf J AddCommGrpCat. The category I is filtered since it is a nonempty directed preorder. The forgetful instance in the cited Mathlib leaf and the pointwise whiskeringRight preservation instance show that forgetting G to a diagram of set presheaves preserves its colimit. (`AddCommGrpCat.FilteredColimits.forget_preservesFilteredColimits` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/Grp/FilteredColimits.lean)), `CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean))).

Sheafification of set presheaves preserves colimits by its left-adjoint universal property. The canonical sheafComposeNatIso J U, which commutes abelian sheafification with forgetting to sets, identifies whiskeringRight U followed by set sheafification with L ⋙ S. Thus L ⋙ S preserves the colimit of G. This uses the abelian-to-sets comparison part of the module-sheafification underlying-sets Mathlib leaf. (`PresheafOfModules.sheafificationCompForgetCompToPresheaf, CategoryTheory.sheafifyComposeIso, CategoryTheory.PreservesSheafification` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Presheaf/Sheafification.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/PreservesSheafification.lean)), `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.sheafifyCocone, CategoryTheory.Sheaf.isColimitSheafifyCocone` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Limits.lean))).

The counit of the abelian sheafification adjunction is an isomorphism, giving e : G ⋙ L ≅ H. For hG = colimit.isColimit G, the mapped cocone L.mapCocone hG.cocone is colimiting, and its image under S is colimiting because L ⋙ S preserves hG. The lemma preservesColimit_of_preserves_colimit_cocone therefore shows that S preserves the colimit of G ⋙ L; preservesColimit_of_iso_diagram transports this along e to H. Composing with step 1 and applying isColimitOfPreserves to the supplied witness proves the required universal property with exactly the mapped coprojections. (`CategoryTheory.presheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean))).
