title = "Residue fields of a localization agree with those of the base"
lean = ["IsLocalization.prime_localRing_residueField_iso"]

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $W\subseteq A$ a multiplicative subset and $\lambda:A\to A'$ a homomorphism of commutative rings which is a localization of $A$ at $W$: (a) $\lambda(w)$ is a unit for every $w\in W$; (b) every element of $A'$ is $\lambda(a)\lambda(w)^{-1}$ with $a\in A$, $w\in W$; (c) $\lambda(a)=0$ implies $wa=0$ for some $w\in W$ (for instance $A'=W^{-1}A$ with $a\mapsto a/1$). Let $\mathfrak q'\subset A'$ be a prime ideal and $\mathfrak q=\lambda^{-1}(\mathfrak q')$. Then: (1) $\mathfrak q$ is a prime ideal of $A$ with $\mathfrak q\cap W=\emptyset$; conversely, for every prime ideal $\mathfrak p\subset A$ with $\mathfrak p\cap W=\emptyset$ the ideal $\mathfrak pA'$ generated by $\lambda(\mathfrak p)$ is a prime ideal of $A'$ with $\lambda^{-1}(\mathfrak pA')=\mathfrak p$, and $\lambda(\lambda^{-1}(\mathfrak q'))A'=\mathfrak q'$, so $\mathfrak q'\mapsto\lambda^{-1}(\mathfrak q')$ is a bijection from the primes of $A'$ onto the primes of $A$ disjoint from $W$, with inverse $\mathfrak p\mapsto\mathfrak pA'$; (2) the composite $A\xrightarrow{\lambda}A'\to A'_{\mathfrak q'}$ is a localization of $A$ at $A\setminus\mathfrak q$ (in the sense of (a)-(c) with $W$ replaced by $A\setminus\mathfrak q$), so the ring map $A_{\mathfrak q}\to A'_{\mathfrak q'}$, $a/s\mapsto\lambda(a)/\lambda(s)$, is an isomorphism of $A$-algebras `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)); (3) this isomorphism carries $\mathfrak qA_{\mathfrak q}$ onto $\mathfrak q'A'_{\mathfrak q'}$ and induces an isomorphism of fields and of $A$-algebras $\sigma:\kappa(\mathfrak q)=A_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak qA_{\mathfrak q}\xrightarrow{\sim}\kappa(\mathfrak q')=A'_{\mathfrak q'}/\mathfrak q'A'_{\mathfrak q'}$, where $\kappa(\mathfrak q')$ is an $A$-algebra via $A\to A'\to\kappa(\mathfrak q')$.
