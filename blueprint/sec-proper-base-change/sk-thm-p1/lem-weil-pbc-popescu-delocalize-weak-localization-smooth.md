title = "Smoothness of the localized auxiliary algebra in the weak delocalization"

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $n,m,c,t\ge0$ integers with $c\le n+m$, $f_1,\dots,f_c\in R[x_1,\dots,x_{n+m}]$ and $g_1,\dots,g_t\in R[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ (polynomial rings `MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean))), and $s_0,s_1,\dots,s_t\in R$, such that (i) $s_0-h\Delta\in(f_1,\dots,f_c)R[x_1,\dots,x_{n+m}]$ for some $h\in R[x_1,\dots,x_{n+m}]$, where $\Delta=\det(\partial f_j/\partial x_i)_{i,j=1,\dots,c}$ is the determinant of the $c\times c$ matrix of formal partial derivatives `MvPolynomial.pderiv` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/PDeriv.lean)) of $f_1,\dots,f_c$ with respect to $x_1,\dots,x_c$ ($\Delta=1$ if $c=0$); (ii) $s_jg_j\in(f_1,\dots,f_c)R[x_1,\dots,x_{n+m}]$ for $j=1,\dots,t$. For $j=1,\dots,c$ write $f_j=\sum_{e\in\mathbf N^m}a_{j,e}\,x_{n+1}^{e_1}\cdots x_{n+m}^{e_m}$ with $a_{j,e}\in R[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ (finitely many nonzero), choose an integer $d_j\ge e_1+\dots+e_m$ for every $e$ with $a_{j,e}\ne0$, and put $F_j=\sum_ea_{j,e}\,X_{n+1}^{e_1}\cdots X_{n+m}^{e_m}X_{n+m+1}^{d_j-e_1-\dots-e_m}\in R[x_1,\dots,x_n,X_{n+1},\dots,X_{n+m+1}]$. Let $B=R[x_0,x_1,\dots,x_n,X_{n+1},\dots,X_{n+m+1}]/(g_1,\dots,g_t,x_0F_1,\dots,x_0F_c)$, let $b\in B$ be the class of $x_0X_{n+m+1}s_0s_1\cdots s_t$, and let $B_b$ be the localization of $B$ at $\{1,b,b^2,\dots\}$ `Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean)), an $R$-algebra via $R\to B\to B_b$. Then $B_b$ is a smooth $R$-algebra `Algebra.Smooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean)).
