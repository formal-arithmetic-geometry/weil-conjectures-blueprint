Part (1). Take e to be the inverse of Mathlib PrimeSpectrum.preimageOrderIsoFiber A B p, the correspondence of `PrimeSpectrum.preimageOrderIsoFiber, PrimeSpectrum.preimageEquivFiber_apply_asIdeal, PrimeSpectrum.preimageEquivFiber_symm_apply_coe, Algebra.TensorProduct.comm` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/ResidueField/Fiber.lean)). Both descriptions, contraction along includeRight and the inverse kernel of the specified tensor evaluation map, hold definitionally in Lean.

For q in the fibre, put Q = e.symm q. The equality q = contraction of Q follows from e.apply_symm_apply. Let iota = Ideal.ResidueField.map q Q includeRight with this equality. By `RingHom.SurjectiveOnStalks.residueFieldMap_bijective, RingHom.SurjectiveOnStalks.baseChange, Ideal.surjectiveOnStalks_residueField` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/ResidueField/Ideal.lean)), implemented by p.surjectiveOnStalks_residueField.baseChange′.residueFieldMap_bijective, iota is bijective. Take theta to be the inverse of RingEquiv.ofBijective iota. (`PrimeSpectrum.preimageOrderIsoFiber, PrimeSpectrum.preimageEquivFiber_apply_asIdeal, PrimeSpectrum.preimageEquivFiber_symm_apply_coe, Algebra.TensorProduct.comm` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/ResidueField/Fiber.lean)), `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean))).

For b in B, the canonical residue-field map satisfies iota(pi_q(b)) = pi_Q(j(b)) by Ideal.ResidueField.map_algebraMap `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)). Applying injectivity of the equivalence associated to iota and its inverse identities proves theta(pi_Q(j(b))) = pi_q(b). The tensor evaluation map has this same value on j(b).

To compare the two homomorphisms on the kappa(p) factor, use Ideal.ResidueField.ringHom_ext `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)): it suffices to compare after composition with A -> kappa(p). Tensor balancing identifies this image in the fibre with j(algebraMap A B a). Step 3, the scalar-tower algebra-map identities, and the tensor lift computation give equality on this image. The extensionality lemma encapsulates the fraction argument because kappa(p) is the fraction field of A/p.

Apply Algebra.TensorProduct.ringHom_ext to the two homomorphisms from kappa(p) tensor_A B. Its left-factor equality is Step 4; its right-factor equality is Step 3 and the computation of the tensor lift on 1 tensor b. This proves theta.toRingHom composed with the canonical residue map equals psi_q.
