Transfer the section-lifting hypothesis to etale A-algebras C in Type u using ULift C and ULift.algEquiv. Transport etaleness by Algebra.Etale.of_equiv, lift the map composed with the equivalence, then compose back. This is the private proved helper lift_small_etale_section.

For x in I and y in A put f = xy + 1. Its image in A/I is 1, hence is a unit; the universal property gives an A-algebra map A_f to A/I. Principal localization is etale (in Lean the Algebra.Etale instance for Localization.Away). Lift this map to tau : A_f to A. Since f is a unit in A_f, tau sends it to the unit f in A. Ideal.mem_jacobson_bot gives I contained in the Jacobson radical. (`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isOpenImmersion_SpecMap_localizationAway` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.etale_comp, instance [IsOpenImmersion f] : Etale f` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean))).

For a monic f and a simple root a0 modulo I use the standard etale pair (f, f'). The root and derivative-unit hypotheses give a map P.Ring to A/I sending P.X to a0. Lift it to tau : P.Ring to A. Then tau(P.X) is a root of f, and evaluating the equality of quotient maps at P.X proves tau(P.X) - a0 belongs to I. Together with the radical assertion this constructs HenselianRing A I directly, without an idempotent-to-root characterization.
