Let $g:S'\to S$ be a morphism of schemes [def-pc-scheme] which is flat, surjective and quasi-compact [def-weil-finite-type-morphism] (the preimage of every affine open of $S$ is quasi-compact). Let $f:X\to S$ be a morphism of schemes and let $f':X\times_SS'\to S'$ be the second projection of the fibre product. If $f'$ is quasi-compact, then $f$ is quasi-compact.
