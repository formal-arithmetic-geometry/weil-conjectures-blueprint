(Notation and the dehomogenization identity.) Put $s=s_0s_1\cdots s_t\in R$ and $X:=X_{n+m+1}$. Claim (H): for every commutative $R$-algebra $T$ and elements $u_1,\dots,u_n,v_1,\dots,v_m,w\in T$, evaluating (by the universal property of `MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean))) gives $F_j(u_1,\dots,u_n,v_1w,\dots,v_mw,w)=w^{d_j}\,f_j(u_1,\dots,u_n,v_1,\dots,v_m)$ for $j=1,\dots,c$. Indeed both sides are sums over the finitely many $e$ with $a_{j,e}\ne0$, and the summand of the left side is $a_{j,e}(u)\,(v_1w)^{e_1}\cdots(v_mw)^{e_m}\,w^{d_j-e_1-\dots-e_m}=w^{d_j}\,a_{j,e}(u)\,v_1^{e_1}\cdots v_m^{e_m}$, the summand of the right side (here $d_j-|e|\ge0$ by the choice of $d_j$). Claim (H'): if moreover $w$ is a unit of $T$ and $V_1,\dots,V_m\in T$, then $F_j(u,V_1,\dots,V_m,w)=w^{d_j}f_j(u,V_1w^{-1},\dots,V_mw^{-1})$: this is (H) with $v_i=V_iw^{-1}$.

(The algebra $C$ and its units.) Let $Q=R[x_0,x_1,\dots,x_{n+m},x_{n+m+1},y,z]$ (`MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean)); $f_j$, $g_j$, $h$, $\Delta$ are elements of $Q$ through $R[x_1,\dots,x_{n+m}]\subset Q$), let $C=Q/(f_1,\dots,f_c,\;x_0x_{n+m+1}y-1,\;sz-1)$ and $\pi:Q\to C$ the quotient map, an $R$-algebra map. In $C$: (a) $x_0x_{n+m+1}$ is a unit with inverse $y$, hence $x_0$ and $x_{n+m+1}$ are units (a factor of a unit is a unit); (b) $s$ is a unit with inverse $z$, hence each $s_j$ ($0\le j\le t$) is a unit, with inverse $z\prod_{k\ne j}s_k$; (c) every element of the ideal $(f_1,\dots,f_c)R[x_1,\dots,x_{n+m}]$ maps to $0$ in $C$, so by hypothesis (ii) $s_jg_j=0$ in $C$, and as $s_j$ is a unit, $g_j=0$ in $C$ for $j=1,\dots,t$; (d) by hypothesis (i), $s_0=h\Delta$ in $C$, and $s_0$ is a unit, so $\Delta$ is a unit of $C$ with inverse $h s_0^{-1}$.

($C$ is standard smooth over $R$.) Number the $N=n+m+4$ variables of $Q$ as $w_1,\dots,w_N$ with $(w_1,\dots,w_c)=(x_1,\dots,x_c)$, $w_{c+1}=y$, $w_{c+2}=z$, and $w_{c+3},\dots,w_N$ the remaining variables $x_{c+1},\dots,x_{n+m},x_0,x_{n+m+1}$ in any order; renaming variables is an isomorphism of $R$-algebras of polynomial rings (`MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean))). Take the $c+2\le N$ relations $(f_1,\dots,f_c,\;x_0x_{n+m+1}y-1,\;sz-1)$. Their matrix of formal partial derivatives (`MvPolynomial.pderiv` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/PDeriv.lean))) with respect to $w_1,\dots,w_{c+2}$: for $i,j\le c$, $\partial f_j/\partial x_i$ computed in $Q$ is the same polynomial as computed in $R[x_1,\dots,x_{n+m}]$ (monomial formula), and $\partial f_j/\partial y=\partial f_j/\partial z=0$ since $f_j$ does not involve $y,z$; $\partial(x_0x_{n+m+1}y-1)/\partial x_i=0$ for $1\le i\le c$ because $c\le n+m$, so $x_i\notin\{x_0,x_{n+m+1},y\}$, while $\partial(x_0x_{n+m+1}y-1)/\partial y=x_0x_{n+m+1}$ and $\partial/\partial z=0$; $\partial(sz-1)/\partial x_i=\partial(sz-1)/\partial y=0$ and $\partial(sz-1)/\partial z=s$. So the matrix is block diagonal with blocks $(\partial f_j/\partial x_i)_{i,j\le c}$, $(x_0x_{n+m+1})$, $(s)$, and its determinant is $\Delta\cdot x_0x_{n+m+1}\cdot s$ (for $c=0$ it is $x_0x_{n+m+1}s$, consistent with $\Delta=1$). By Step 2 (a), (b), (d) its image in $C$ is a product of units, hence a unit. Thus $\mathrm{id}:Q/(\dots)\to C$ is a standard smooth presentation of $C$ over $R$ in the sense of `Algebra.IsStandardSmooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/StandardSmooth.lean)) (integers $N\ge c+2\ge0$).

(The map $\Phi:B_b\to C$.) Let $P=R[x_0,x_1,\dots,x_n,X_{n+1},\dots,X_{n+m+1}]$ and $\rho:P\to B$ the quotient map. By `MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean)) let $\tilde\varphi:P\to C$ be the $R$-algebra map with $x_0\mapsto x_0$, $x_i\mapsto x_i$ ($1\le i\le n$), $X_{n+i}\mapsto x_{n+i}x_{n+m+1}$ ($1\le i\le m$), $X_{n+m+1}\mapsto x_{n+m+1}$ (images in $C$). It kills the generators of the ideal defining $B$: $\tilde\varphi(g_j)=g_j(x_1,\dots,x_n)=0$ in $C$ by Step 2 (c); and by (H) of Step 1 with $T=C$, $u_k=x_k$, $v_i=x_{n+i}$, $w=x_{n+m+1}$, $\tilde\varphi(x_0F_j)=x_0\,F_j(x_1,\dots,x_n,x_{n+1}x_{n+m+1},\dots,x_{n+m}x_{n+m+1},x_{n+m+1})=x_0\,x_{n+m+1}^{d_j}f_j(x_1,\dots,x_{n+m})=0$ in $C$. Hence $\tilde\varphi$ kills the ideal they generate and factors as $\tilde\varphi=\varphi\circ\rho$ with $\varphi:B\to C$ an $R$-algebra map. Now $\varphi(b)=x_0x_{n+m+1}s$, a unit of $C$ with inverse $yz$ (Step 2 (a), (b)). By `IsLocalization.Away.lift, IsLocalization.Away.lift_eq, IsLocalization.ringHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Away/Basic.lean)) there is a unique ring map $\Phi:B_b\to C$ with $\Phi(\beta/1)=\varphi(\beta)$ for $\beta\in B$; it is an $R$-algebra map, since the structure map of $B_b$ is $R\to B\to B_b$ and $\varphi$ is one. (`Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean))).

(The map $\psi:C\to B_b$.) Write $\bar\beta$ for the image in $B_b$ of $\beta\in P$ (through $P\to B\to B_b$). In $B_b$ the element $b/1=\bar x_0\,\bar X_{n+m+1}\,\bar s$ is a unit (`Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean))), hence so are its factors $\bar x_0$, $\bar X_{n+m+1}$, $\bar s$ and $\bar x_0\bar X_{n+m+1}$. By `MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean)) let $\tilde\psi:Q\to B_b$ be the $R$-algebra map with $x_0\mapsto\bar x_0$, $x_i\mapsto\bar x_i$ ($1\le i\le n$), $x_{n+i}\mapsto\bar X_{n+i}\bar X_{n+m+1}^{-1}$ ($1\le i\le m$), $x_{n+m+1}\mapsto\bar X_{n+m+1}$, $y\mapsto(\bar x_0\bar X_{n+m+1})^{-1}$, $z\mapsto\bar s^{-1}$. It kills the relations of $C$: $\tilde\psi(x_0x_{n+m+1}y-1)=0$ and $\tilde\psi(sz-1)=0$ by construction; and by (H') of Step 1 with $T=B_b$, $u_k=\bar x_k$, $V_i=\bar X_{n+i}$, $w=\bar X_{n+m+1}$: $\tilde\psi(f_j)=f_j(\bar x_1,\dots,\bar x_n,\bar X_{n+1}\bar X_{n+m+1}^{-1},\dots,\bar X_{n+m}\bar X_{n+m+1}^{-1})=\bar X_{n+m+1}^{-d_j}\,\overline{F_j}=\bar X_{n+m+1}^{-d_j}\,\bar x_0^{-1}\,\overline{x_0F_j}=0$, because $x_0F_j$ is one of the relations of $B$ (this is where $x_0$ invertible turns $x_0F_j=0$ into $F_j=0$). Hence $\tilde\psi$ kills the ideal defining $C$ and factors as $\tilde\psi=\psi\circ\pi$ with $\psi:C\to B_b$ an $R$-algebra map.

($\Phi$ and $\psi$ are inverse.) $\Phi\circ\psi\circ\pi$ and $\pi$ are $R$-algebra maps $Q\to C$ agreeing on the variables: $x_0,x_i,x_{n+m+1}$ go to themselves; $x_{n+i}\mapsto\Phi(\bar X_{n+i})\Phi(\bar X_{n+m+1})^{-1}=x_{n+i}x_{n+m+1}x_{n+m+1}^{-1}=x_{n+i}$; $y\mapsto\Phi(\bar x_0\bar X_{n+m+1})^{-1}=(x_0x_{n+m+1})^{-1}=y$; $z\mapsto\Phi(\bar s)^{-1}=s^{-1}=z$ (ring maps send inverses to inverses; Step 2 (a), (b)). By the uniqueness in `MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean)) they are equal, and as $\pi$ is surjective, $\Phi\circ\psi=\mathrm{id}_C$. Conversely, $\psi\circ\Phi\circ\lambda\circ\rho$ and $\lambda\circ\rho$ ($\lambda:B\to B_b$, $\beta\mapsto\beta/1$) are $R$-algebra maps $P\to B_b$ agreeing on the variables: $x_0\mapsto\bar x_0$, $x_i\mapsto\bar x_i$, $X_{n+m+1}\mapsto\bar X_{n+m+1}$, and $X_{n+i}\mapsto\psi(x_{n+i}x_{n+m+1})=\bar X_{n+i}\bar X_{n+m+1}^{-1}\bar X_{n+m+1}=\bar X_{n+i}$; so they are equal, and as $\rho$ is surjective, $\psi\circ\Phi\circ\lambda=\lambda$. By the uniqueness clause of `IsLocalization.Away.lift, IsLocalization.Away.lift_eq, IsLocalization.ringHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Away/Basic.lean)) (applied to $\lambda:B\to B_b$, for which $\lambda(b)$ is a unit), $\psi\circ\Phi=\mathrm{id}_{B_b}$. Hence $\Phi:B_b\to C$ is an isomorphism of $R$-algebras.

(Conclusion.) Composing the standard smooth presentation $Q/(f_1,\dots,f_c,x_0x_{n+m+1}y-1,sz-1)\xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}}C$ of Step 3 with the $R$-algebra isomorphism $\Phi^{-1}=\psi:C\to B_b$ of Step 6 gives an isomorphism of $R$-algebras $Q/(\dots)\to B_b$ under which the determinant maps to $\psi(\Delta x_0x_{n+m+1}s)$, a unit of $B_b$ (image of a unit under a ring map). So $B_b$ is a standard smooth $R$-algebra `Algebra.IsStandardSmooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/StandardSmooth.lean)), and by `Algebra.instSmoothOfIsStandardSmooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/StandardSmoothCotangent.lean)) $B_b$ is a smooth $R$-algebra `Algebra.Smooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean)).
