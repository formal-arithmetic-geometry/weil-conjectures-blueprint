*The data are well formed.* $E$ is a set, so the coproduct $\coprod_{e\in E}T$ exists with coprojections $\iota_e$ `AlgebraicGeometry.instHasColimitsOfShapeDiscreteSchemeOfSmall, AlgebraicGeometry.sigmaOpenCover, AlgebraicGeometry.disjoint_opensRange_sigmaι` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Limits.lean)), and its universal property applied to the family $(\mathrm{id}_T)_{e\in E}$ gives the unique $\pi$ with $\pi\circ\iota_e=\mathrm{id}_T$; so $(T_E,\pi)$ is a $T$-scheme `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Over` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Over.lean)). The fibre product $P$ of $\pi$ with itself exists `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)) and $\pi\circ p=\pi\circ q$ by its commutative square, so $\rho$ is well defined. For $T$-morphisms $f,g:U\to T_E$ we have $\pi\circ f=s=\pi\circ g$, so the universal property of $P$ gives the unique $(f,g)$ with $p\circ(f,g)=f$, $q\circ(f,g)=g$. $c_e=\iota_e\circ s$ is a $T$-morphism: $\pi\circ\iota_e\circ s=s$. The morphism $m$ is not constructed here: its existence and uniqueness, and the facts about the $W_{e,e'}$ it rests on, are the statement node lem-weil-pbc-constant-group-scheme-multiplication (a pointer, not a dependency).
