For a commutative ring B and ideal J, let C be the full category of ring maps B to D with Spec(D) to Spec(B) etale and the canonical map B/J to D/JD bijective, with all B-algebra maps as morphisms. Then C is filtered: it is nonempty, any two objects admit a common successor, and every pair of parallel maps is coequalized.
