Let $R\to S$ be a flat homomorphism of Noetherian commutative rings. Let $\mathfrak p$ be a prime ideal of $R$, let $C=\kappa(\mathfrak p)\otimes_RS$ be the fibre ring over $\mathfrak p$, let $Q$ be a prime ideal of $C$, and let $\mathfrak q\subseteq S$ be its inverse image under $S\to C$, $s\mapsto1\otimes s$. Then $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak q)=\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)+\operatorname{ht}(Q)$.
