**Step 1.** *Triples are $R$-algebra maps to $K$.* Regard $K$ as an $R$-algebra through $R\to\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to K$. For a triple $t=(S,\mathfrak q,\phi)$ put $\psi_t:S\to S/\mathfrak q\to\kappa(\mathfrak q)\xrightarrow{\phi}K$ ($\kappa(\mathfrak q)$ the fraction field of $S/\mathfrak q$). Since $\mathfrak q$ lies over $\mathfrak p$ and $\phi$ is a $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-map, $\psi_t$ is an $R$-algebra map, and $\ker\psi_t=\mathfrak q$ ($\phi$ is a map of fields, hence injective). Conversely, for an etale $R$-algebra $S$ and an $R$-algebra map $\chi:S\to K$, $\ker\chi$ is a prime (the image of $S$ is a subring of a field), it lies over $\ker(R\to K)=\mathfrak p$, and the injection $S/\ker\chi\to K$ of a domain into a field extends uniquely to $\bar\chi:\kappa(\ker\chi)\to K$, a $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-map; the two constructions are mutually inverse. An $R$-algebra map $\beta:S\to S'$ is a morphism $t\to t'$ iff $\psi_{t'}\circ\beta=\psi_t$: the kernel of $\psi_{t'}\circ\beta$ is $\beta^{-1}(\mathfrak q')$, and its factorization through the residue field is $\phi'\circ\kappa(\beta)$ (uniqueness of the extension to the fraction field). So $\mathcal T$ is the category of pairs ($S$ etale over $R$, $R$-algebra map $S\to K$) with morphisms the $R$-algebra maps over $K$.

**Step 2.** *$\mathcal T$ is essentially small, and $C$ exists.* An etale $R$-algebra $S$ is of finite presentation [def-weil-etale-morphism] (locally of finite presentation with affine source and target), so it is isomorphic to a quotient $R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/I$; equivalently, by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-etale-essentially-small-cover] applied to $T=\operatorname{Spec}R$, the affine etale $R$-schemes form an essentially small category. Hence the triples whose ring is such a quotient form a set, and their full subcategory $\mathcal T_0$ is equivalent to $\mathcal T$ (transport $\mathfrak q,\phi$ along an isomorphism); colimits over $\mathcal T$ are computed over $\mathcal T_0$ [lem-weil-pbc-colimit-initial-full-subcategory] (2). Filtered colimits of commutative $R$-algebras over a small filtered category exist: take the filtered colimit of the underlying sets (disjoint union modulo $x_i\sim x_j$ iff they agree in some later stage) with the operations computed at a common stage [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit], [lem-weil-pbc-filtered-colimit-algebras-elementwise]. Once $\mathcal T$ is filtered (steps 3-4), $C=\varinjlim_{\mathcal T}S$ exists, with coprojections $\iota_t:S\to C$, and it is by construction a filtered colimit of $R$-algebras etale over $R$: this is the last clause of (1).

**Step 3.** *(1) $\mathcal T$ is nonempty and any two objects map to a common one.* $(R,\mathfrak p,\kappa(\mathfrak p)\subset K)$ is a triple: $\operatorname{Spec}R\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is the identity, an open immersion, hence etale [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (1). Let $t=(S,\mathfrak q,\phi)$, $t'=(S',\mathfrak q',\phi')$ be triples and $S''=S\otimes_RS'$. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-ring-base-change] (base change of $R\to S'$ along $R\to S$) $\operatorname{Spec}S''\to\operatorname{Spec}S$ is etale, so $\operatorname{Spec}S''\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is etale [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (2). The $R$-algebra maps $\psi_t,\psi_{t'}$ induce an $R$-algebra map $\chi''=\psi_t\otimes\psi_{t'}:S''\to K$, $s\otimes s'\mapsto\psi_t(s)\psi_{t'}(s')$ (the tensor product is the coproduct of commutative $R$-algebras). By step 1, $\chi''$ is a triple $t''=(S'',\mathfrak q'',\phi'')$ ($\mathfrak q''=\ker\chi''$ lies over $\mathfrak q$ and $\mathfrak q'$), and the two coprojections $S\to S''$, $S'\to S''$ are morphisms $t\to t''$, $t'\to t''$ since $\chi''$ restricts to $\psi_t$ and $\psi_{t'}$.

**Step 4.** *(1) Parallel morphisms are equalized; $\mathcal T$ is filtered.* Let $\beta_1,\beta_2:t\to t'$ be morphisms, $t=(S,\mathfrak q,\phi)$, $t'=(S',\mathfrak q',\phi')$, so $\psi_{t'}\beta_1=\psi_t=\psi_{t'}\beta_2$ (step 1). $\operatorname{Spec}S'$ and $\operatorname{Spec}S$ are etale over $\operatorname{Spec}R$, so $\operatorname{Spec}\beta_1$ is etale, and so is the multiplication $\mu:S'\otimes_RS'\to S'$ (a map between $R$-algebras etale over $R$, $S'\otimes_RS'$ being etale by step 3) [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (3). Let $E=S'\otimes_{\beta_1,S,\beta_2}S'$; the right factor map $S'\to E$ is the base change of $\beta_1$ along $\beta_2$, hence etale [lem-weil-pbc-etale-ring-base-change], so $E$ is etale over $R$ (composition). Let $S''=E\otimes_{S'\otimes_RS'}S'$ (via the natural surjection $S'\otimes_RS'\to E$ and $\mu$); $E\to S''$ is the base change of $\mu$, hence etale, and $S''$ is etale over $R$ [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (2). The map $\psi_{t'}\otimes\psi_{t'}:E\to K$ is well defined because $\psi_{t'}\beta_1=\psi_{t'}\beta_2$, and it agrees with $\psi_{t'}:S'\to K$ on $S'\otimes_RS'$ (both send $a\otimes b$ to $\psi_{t'}(a)\psi_{t'}(b)$), so they induce an $R$-algebra map $\chi'':S''\to K$, i.e. a triple $t''$ (step 1). The right-most factor map $c:S'\to S''$ satisfies $\chi''c=\psi_{t'}$, so it is a morphism $t'\to t''$; and $c\beta_1=c\beta_2$: in $S''$, $c(a)=1\otimes a$ equals the image of $a\otimes1\in S'\otimes_RS'$ and of $1\otimes a$, so $c(\beta_1(s))=(\beta_1(s)\otimes1)\otimes1=(1\otimes\beta_2(s))\otimes1=c(\beta_2(s))$ (the relation $\beta_1(s)\otimes1=1\otimes\beta_2(s)$ holds in $E$). With step 3, $\mathcal T$ is filtered.

**Step 5.** *(2) The map $\psi$; $C$ is local with $\mathfrak m_C=\ker\psi$.* By step 1 the $\psi_t$ satisfy $\psi_{t'}\circ\beta=\psi_t$ for every morphism $\beta:t\to t'$, so they form a cocone and induce the canonical $R$-algebra map $\psi:C\to K$ with $\psi\iota_t=\psi_t$ [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit]; $\psi(1)=1$, so $C\neq0$ and $\ker\psi$ is a proper ideal. Let $x\in C$ with $\psi(x)\neq0$. Write $x=\iota_t(f)$ with $t=(S,\mathfrak q,\phi)$, $f\in S$ [lem-weil-pbc-filtered-colimit-algebras-elementwise]; then $\psi_t(f)=\psi(x)\neq0$, i.e. $f\notin\mathfrak q$. $\operatorname{Spec}S_f\to\operatorname{Spec}S$ is an open immersion, so $S_f$ is etale over $R$ [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (1),(2), and $\psi_t$ extends to $S_f\to K$ ($\psi_t(f)$ is invertible in $K$): a triple $t_f$ with the localization map a morphism $t\to t_f$ (step 1). Then $\iota_{t_f}(1/f)\cdot x=\iota_{t_f}(f\cdot1/f)=1$, so $x$ is a unit. Elements of $\ker\psi$ are non-units ($\psi(xy)=0\neq1$). So the non-units of $C$ are exactly $\ker\psi$, an ideal: $C$ is local with $\mathfrak m_C=\ker\psi$, and $\psi(C)\cong C/\mathfrak m_C$ is a subfield of $K$.

**Step 6.** *(2) $\mathfrak m_C=\mathfrak pC$.* $\psi$ restricted to $R$ is $R\to\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to K$, which kills $\mathfrak p$; so $\mathfrak pC\subset\ker\psi$. Conversely let $x\in\ker\psi$, $x=\iota_t(f)$ with $t=(S,\mathfrak q,\phi)$, so $f\in\ker\psi_t=\mathfrak q$. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-prime-generates-maximal] $f/1\in\mathfrak pS_{\mathfrak q}$: $f/1=y/g$ with $y\in\mathfrak pS$, $g\in S\setminus\mathfrak q$, so $h(gf-y)=0$ for some $h\in S\setminus\mathfrak q$. Put $u=hg\notin\mathfrak q$; then $uf=hy\in\mathfrak pS$. As in step 5, $(S_u,\mathfrak qS_u,\cdot)$ is a triple $t_u$ with a morphism $t\to t_u$, and in $S_u$ we have $f=hy/u\in\mathfrak pS_u$; since $\iota_{t_u}$ is an $R$-algebra map, $x=\iota_{t_u}(f)\in\mathfrak pC$.

**Step 7.** *Key step: simple roots in $K$ lift to $C$.* Let $P\in C[T ]$ be monic and $a_0\in K$ with $P^\psi(a_0)=0\neq(P^\psi)'(a_0)$, where $P^\psi\in K[T ]$ is the image of $P$ under $\psi$. Claim: there is $a\in C$ with $P(a)=0$ and $\psi(a)=a_0$. The finitely many coefficients of $P$ are $\iota_{t_j}(s_j)$ [lem-weil-pbc-filtered-colimit-algebras-elementwise]; as $\mathcal T$ is filtered (steps 3-4) there is one triple $t=(S,\mathfrak q,\phi)$ receiving morphisms from all $t_j$, so $P$ is the image under $\iota_t$ of a monic $Q\in S[T ]$ (same degree), whose image under $\psi_t$ is $P^\psi$. Let $S'=S[T ]_{Q'}/(Q)$ with $x$ the class of $T$. By [lem-weil-pbc-standard-etale-monic-derivative] (1) $\operatorname{Spec}S'\to\operatorname{Spec}S$ is etale, hence $S'$ is etale over $R$ [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (2). Regard $K$ as an $S$-algebra via $\psi_t$; then $Q(a_0)=P^\psi(a_0)=0$ and $Q'(a_0)=(P^\psi)'(a_0)\neq0$ (derivatives commute with ring maps), a unit of the field $K$; by [lem-weil-pbc-standard-etale-monic-derivative] (2) there is an $S$-algebra map $\chi:S'\to K$ with $\chi(x)=a_0$. It is an $R$-algebra map, so (step 1) a triple $t'$, and the structure map $S\to S'$ is a morphism $t\to t'$ since $\chi$ restricts to $\psi_t$. Put $a=\iota_{t'}(x)$. Since $\iota_{t'}$ restricted to $S$ is $\iota_t$, $P(a)=\iota_{t'}(Q(x))=0$, and $\psi(a)=\psi_{t'}(x)=\chi(x)=a_0$.

**Step 8.** *(2) $\psi$ is surjective; $C/\mathfrak m_C\cong K$; the preimage of $\mathfrak m_C$ in $R$ is $\mathfrak p$.* By step 5, $k'=\psi(C)$ is a subfield of $K$; it contains the image of $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak p)$, since for $r,s\in R$, $s\notin\mathfrak p$, the image of $r/s$ in $K$ is $\psi(r)\psi(s)^{-1}$ with $\psi(s)\neq0$. Let $\alpha\in K$. By hypothesis $\alpha$ is separable algebraic over $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$: its minimal polynomial $\bar g\in\kappa(\mathfrak p)[T ]$ is monic with $\bar g(\alpha)=0\neq\bar g'(\alpha)$ (a separable polynomial is coprime to its derivative). Its coefficients lie in $k'=\psi(C)$; lifting each to $C$ (the leading one to $1$) gives a monic $P\in C[T ]$ with $P^\psi$ the image of $\bar g$ in $K[T ]$, and step 7 gives $a\in C$ with $\psi(a)=\alpha$. So $\psi$ is surjective, with kernel $\mathfrak m_C$ (step 5), and induces a ring isomorphism $C/\mathfrak m_C\to K$, which is a $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebra map because $\psi$ is an $R$-algebra map. Finally the preimage of $\mathfrak m_C=\ker\psi$ in $R$ is $\ker(R\to\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to K)=\mathfrak p$.

**Step 9.** *(3) $C$ is strictly henselian.* $C$ is local (step 5) and $\psi$ induces an isomorphism $\bar\psi:C/\mathfrak m_C\to K$ (step 8). Let $P\in C[T ]$ be monic and $\bar a_0\in C/\mathfrak m_C$ a root of $\overline P$ with $\overline{P'}(\bar a_0)\neq0$. Applying $\bar\psi$, $a_0=\bar\psi(\bar a_0)$ is a root of $P^\psi$ with $(P^\psi)'(a_0)\neq0$, and step 7 gives $a\in C$ with $P(a)=0$ and $\psi(a)=a_0$, i.e. $a\bmod\mathfrak m_C=\bar a_0$. By [lem-weil-pbc-henselian-local-root-lifting] ((ii)$\Rightarrow$(i)) $C$ is henselian [def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology]. Its residue field is isomorphic to $K$, which is separably closed [def-pc-separably-closed]; so $C$ is strictly henselian.
