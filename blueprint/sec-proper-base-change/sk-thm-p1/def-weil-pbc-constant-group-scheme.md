Let $T$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme] and $E$ an abelian group. The *constant group scheme* $T_E$ with value $E$ over $T$ is the coproduct $T_E=\coprod_{e\in E}T$ of copies of $T$ indexed by $E$, with coprojections $\iota_e:T\to T_E$ (the coproduct exists by `AlgebraicGeometry.instHasColimitsOfShapeDiscreteSchemeOfSmall, AlgebraicGeometry.sigmaOpenCover, AlgebraicGeometry.disjoint_opensRange_sigmaι` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Limits.lean))), made a $T$-scheme `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Over` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Over.lean)) by the structure morphism $\pi:T_E\to T$ with $\pi\circ\iota_e=\mathrm{id}_T$ for all $e$ (the codiagonal, given by the universal property of the coproduct). Write $V_e=\iota_e(T)\subseteq T_E$ for the *components* (open and closed, pairwise disjoint and covering $T_E$, by `AlgebraicGeometry.instHasColimitsOfShapeDiscreteSchemeOfSmall, AlgebraicGeometry.sigmaOpenCover, AlgebraicGeometry.disjoint_opensRange_sigmaι` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Limits.lean))). Let $P=T_E\times_TT_E$ be the fibre product `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)) of $\pi$ with itself, with projections $p,q$ and structure morphism $\rho=\pi\circ p=\pi\circ q:P\to T$, and put $W_{e,e'}=p^{-1}(V_e)\cap q^{-1}(V_{e'})$ for $e,e'\in E$. The *multiplication* $m:P\to T_E$ is the morphism of schemes whose restriction to $W_{e,e'}$, regarded as an open subscheme of $P$ `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)), is $\iota_{e+e'}\circ\rho|_{W_{e,e'}}$ for all $(e,e')\in E\times E$. That the $W_{e,e'}$ are open and closed, pairwise disjoint and cover $P$, that such an $m$ exists and is unique (gluing along this cover, `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Cover.glueMorphisms` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Gluing.lean)), the overlaps being empty), and that $\pi\circ m=\rho$ and $m(W_{e,e'})\subseteq V_{e+e'}$, is the statement node lem-weil-pbc-constant-group-scheme-multiplication (recorded in `pointers`), from which this definition takes $m$. For a $T$-scheme $s:U\to T$ and $T$-morphisms $f,g:U\to T_E$, the *sum* is $f+g:=m\circ(f,g):U\to T_E$, where $(f,g):U\to P$ is the morphism with $p\circ(f,g)=f$ and $q\circ(f,g)=g$ (it exists since $\pi\circ f=s=\pi\circ g$); the *constant point* attached to $e\in E$ is $c_e:=\iota_e\circ s:U\to T_E$. The map $E\to\operatorname{Mor}_T(U,T_E)$, $e\mapsto c_e$, is the *canonical map*. (The name follows Stacks Tag 03YW, where $(T_E,m)$ is a group scheme over $T$; the group-scheme axioms for $m$ are not part of this definition and are not claimed here.)
