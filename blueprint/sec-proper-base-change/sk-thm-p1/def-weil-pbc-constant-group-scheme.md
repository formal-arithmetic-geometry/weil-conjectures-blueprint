Let $T$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme] and $E$ an abelian group. The *constant group scheme* $T_E$ with value $E$ over $T$ is the coproduct $T_E=\coprod_{e\in E}T$ of copies of $T$ indexed by $E$, with coprojections $\iota_e:T\to T_E$, made a $T$-scheme by the structure morphism $\pi:T_E\to T$ with $\pi\circ\iota_e=\mathrm{id}_T$ for all $e$ (the codiagonal). Write $V_e=\iota_e(T)\subseteq T_E$ for the *components*; they are open and closed, pairwise disjoint and cover $T_E$. Let $P=T_E\times_TT_E$ be the fibre product of $\pi$ with itself, with projections $p,q$ and structure morphism $\rho=\pi\circ p=\pi\circ q:P\to T$, and put $W_{e,e'}=p^{-1}(V_e)\cap q^{-1}(V_{e'})$ for $e,e'\in E$; the $W_{e,e'}$ form an open cover of $P$ by pairwise disjoint opens, $W_{e,e'}\cong T\times_TT=T$. The *multiplication* $m:P\to T_E$ is a morphism of schemes whose restriction to each $W_{e,e'}$ is $\iota_{e+e'}\circ\rho|_{W_{e,e'}}$ and which satisfies $\pi\circ m=\rho$. For a $T$-scheme $s:U\to T$ and $T$-morphisms $f,g:U\to T_E$, the *sum* is $f+g:=m\circ(f,g):U\to T_E$, where $(f,g):U\to P$ is the morphism with $p\circ(f,g)=f$ and $q\circ(f,g)=g$ (it exists since $\pi\circ f=s=\pi\circ g$); the *constant point* attached to $e\in E$ is $c_e:=\iota_e\circ s:U\to T_E$. The map $E\to\operatorname{Mor}_T(U,T_E)$, $e\mapsto c_e$, is the *canonical map*. (The name follows Stacks Project, Tag 03YW, where $(T_E,m)$ is a group scheme over $T$; the group-scheme axioms for $m$ are not part of this definition.)
