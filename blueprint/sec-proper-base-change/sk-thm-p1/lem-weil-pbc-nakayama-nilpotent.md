Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $I\subseteq R$ a nilpotent ideal ($I^k=0$ for some $k\ge1$), and $u:N\to M$ a homomorphism of $R$-modules (no finiteness assumed on $N$ or $M$). If the induced map $N/IN\to M/IM$ is surjective, i.e. $M=u(N)+IM$, then $u$ is surjective. (Consequently: if $M=IM$ then $M=0$; if $N,N'\subseteq M$ are submodules with $M=N+IN'$, then $M=N$.)
