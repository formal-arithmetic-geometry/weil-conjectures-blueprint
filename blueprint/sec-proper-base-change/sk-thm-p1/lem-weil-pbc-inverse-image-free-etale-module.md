Let $h:Y\to T$ be a morphism of schemes [def-pc-scheme], $\Lambda$ a commutative ring, and for a scheme $S$ let $\Mod(S_{\Et},\Lambda)$ be the category of sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on the small etale site $S_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves]. For an object $W$ of $S_{\Et}$ let $j_{W!}\Lambda_W$ be the sheafification of the presheaf of $\Lambda$-modules $\Lambda[h_W]:V\mapsto\bigoplus_{\varphi\in\mathrm{Mor}_{S_{\Et}}(V,W)}\Lambda$ (the free presheaf of $\Lambda$-modules on the functor represented by $W$), and let $\epsilon_{\mathcal H}:\mathrm{Hom}(j_{W!}\Lambda_W,\mathcal H)\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal H(W)$ ($\mathcal H\in\Mod(S_{\Et},\Lambda)$) be the composite of the bijection given by the universal property of sheafification and the bijection $\beta\mapsto\beta_W(e_{\mathrm{id}_W})$ given by the universal property of the free presheaf; it is natural in $\mathcal H$. Let $W$ be an object of $T_{\Et}$ and $W'=W\times_TY$, an object of $Y_{\Et}$ (a base change of an etale morphism is etale). Let $h^{-1}:\Mod(T_{\Et},\Lambda)\to\Mod(Y_{\Et},\Lambda)$ be the inverse image of sheaves of modules [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact], left adjoint to the direct image $h_*$, $(h_*\mathcal K)(W)=\mathcal K(W\times_TY)$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image], with unit $\eta$. Then there is a unique isomorphism $\theta:h^{-1}(j_{W!}\Lambda_W)\xrightarrow{\sim}j_{W'!}\Lambda_{W'}$ in $\Mod(Y_{\Et},\Lambda)$ such that for every $\mathcal H\in\Mod(T_{\Et},\Lambda)$ and every $s\in\mathcal H(W)$, if $\varphi_s:j_{W!}\Lambda_W\to\mathcal H$ is the morphism with $\epsilon_{\mathcal H}(\varphi_s)=s$, then
$$\epsilon_{h^{-1}\mathcal H}\bigl(h^{-1}(\varphi_s)\circ\theta^{-1}\bigr)=\eta_{\mathcal H,W}(s)\in(h_*h^{-1}\mathcal H)(W)=(h^{-1}\mathcal H)(W').$$
