Let S,T,X,X',S0,T0,X0,X1 be schemes [def-pc-scheme]. Let g:T->S, f:X->S, g':X'->X, f':X'->T, jA:S0->S, jB:T0->T, g0:T0->S0, p0:X0->X, f0:X0->S0, p1:X1->X0, f1:X1->T0, b:X1->X' make all four squares (g',f',f,g), (p0,f0,f,jA), (p1,f1,f0,g0), (b,f1,f',jB) commute, and suppose jB g=g0 jA and b g'=p1 p0. For an abelian etale sheaf G `CategoryTheory.Sheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheaf.lean)) set K=G[0], K0=(p0^*G)[0], M1=g^*Rf_*K, M2=Rf'_*g'^*K, N1=g0^*Rf0_*K0, N2=Rf1_*p1^*K0 [def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image], [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. Let u1:jB^*M1->N1 be inverse-image composition along jB g=g0 jA followed by g0^* of the exchange for (p0,f0,f,jA), followed by the inverse-image single-complex comparison. Let u2:jB^*M2->N2 be the exchange for (b,f1,f',jB) followed by inverse-image composition along b g'=p1 p0 and the inverse-image single-complex comparison. For a separably closed field k, points t:Spec k->T0, s:Spec k->T with t jB=s, and q in Z, form r_i:(H^q M_i)_s -> (H^q N_i)_t by the inverse-image stalk comparison followed by H^q(u_i) [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks]. If e and e0 are H^q of the original and local exchange maps [def-weil-pbc-derived-exchange], then r1 followed by (e0)_t equals e_s followed by r2. No isomorphism of r_i or e is asserted.
