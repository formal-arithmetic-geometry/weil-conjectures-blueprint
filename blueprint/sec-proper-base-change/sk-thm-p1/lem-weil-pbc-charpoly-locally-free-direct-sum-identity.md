title = "Local characteristic polynomial identity on a product cover"
lean = ["Algebra.locallyFreeCharpoly_prodIdentity"]

Given the two locally free covers and any product cover with the expected local bases, the locally free characteristic polynomial of the product endomorphism equals the product of the two characteristic polynomials.
