(The notions used in (1).) For the field extension $K/k$, a transcendence basis $(x_i)_{i\in I}$ is defined in `IsTranscendenceBasis` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AlgebraicIndependent/Defs.lean)) (Stacks 030E (4): algebraically independent over $k$, with $K/k(x_i;i\in I)$ algebraic), and $k(x_i;i\in I)$ is a subfield of $K$ containing $k$, so $K$ is a field extension of $k(x_i;i\in I)$ `Algebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Defs.lean)) and the condition 'separable algebraic' of `Algebra.IsSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)) applies to it. Its algebraicity half holds already by the definition of a transcendence basis, so (1) only adds that every $\alpha\in K$ is separable over $k(x_i;i\in I)$. Stacks writes $k(x_i;i\in I)$ for the fraction field of the polynomial ring (030E (2)); for an algebraically independent family $k[X_i]\to K$ is injective, so it extends to an injective map of the fraction field onto the subfield generated by the $x_i$, and the two readings agree. Clause (1) asks for the existence of one such basis; it does not need that transcendence bases exist (Stacks Fields Lemma 030F, Mathlib `exists_isTranscendenceBasis`), so no further statement is used.

(Agreement of 'separable' with the other notions of the DAG.) Stacks Fields 09H1 (1) calls an irreducible polynomial separable if it is relatively prime to its derivative; `Polynomial.Separable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)) says the same for any polynomial, and the minimal polynomial is irreducible, so 'the minimal polynomial is separable' means the same under both. Some nodes (def-weil-pbc-relative-separable-closure, lem-weil-pbc-etale-fibre-discrete-separable-points) call $\alpha$ separable over $F$ when it is a root of some separable $f\in F[T]$; this is the same: if $f(\alpha)=0$ with $Uf+Vf'=1$, the minimal polynomial $P$ divides $f$, say $f=PG$, so $f'=P'G+PG'$ and $(UG+VG')P+(VG)P'=1$, hence $P$ is separable; conversely take $f=P$. The Fields chapter's restatement 030J (2) ('an algebraic extension $k'/k$ is separable if every element is separable') is the same definition. So the notion 'separable algebraic' used in (1) is unambiguous. (`Algebra.IsSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean))).

(The subextensions in (2).) For finitely many $a_1,\dots,a_n\in K$, $K'=k(a_1,\dots,a_n)$ is the smallest subfield of $K$ containing $k$ and the $a_j$; it contains $k$, so $K'/k$ is a field extension `Algebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Defs.lean)) and (1) applies to it, with transcendence bases taken inside $K'$. 'Finitely generated over $k$' in Stacks means generated as a field by finitely many elements, which is this. Hence (2) is a well-formed condition on $K/k$.

(What the definition does not contain.) Stacks remarks right after 030O that it is not clear from the definition that a separably generated extension is separable; this is Algebra Lemma 030X (lemma-separably-generated-separable, line 10410), and Lemma 030P (lemma-subextensions-are-separable) says subextensions of separable extensions are separable. Neither is part of the definition or used in steps 1-3; they are statements about this node and are not created here (no node cites them yet). Note the clash of words: for an algebraic extension, 'separable over $k$' in the sense of (2) is meant to agree with 'separable algebraic' (Fields 09H1 footnote), but that agreement is also a lemma and not used here; nodes citing this node mean (2). The characteristic-zero case is lem-weil-pbc-char-zero-field-extension-separable, which depends on this node.
