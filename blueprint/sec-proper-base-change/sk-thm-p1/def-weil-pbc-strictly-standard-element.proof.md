(The ingredients are defined in $A$.) Since $R\to A$ is of finite presentation, presentations $\pi:R[x_1,\dots,x_n]\to R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(f_1,\dots,f_m)\cong A$ exist `Algebra.FinitePresentation` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FinitePresentation.lean)), so the existential conditions range over a nonempty set of data. Fix one, with $P=R[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ and $\pi:P\to A$ the surjective $R$-algebra map with kernel $I=(f_1,\dots,f_m)$. Each $\partial f_j/\partial x_i\in P$ is defined `MvPolynomial.pderiv` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/PDeriv.lean)), so its image $\pi(\partial f_j/\partial x_i)\in A$ is defined. As $0\le c\le\min(n,m)$, the indices $j\le c$ are among $1,\dots,m$ and $i\le c$ among $1,\dots,n$, so the $c\times c$ matrix $(\partial f_j/\partial x_i)_{i,j=1,\dots,c}$ is defined, and for every subset $I\subset\{1,\dots,n\}$ with $|I|=c$ (there are $\binom nc\ge1$ of them) the $c\times c$ matrix with rows $j=1,\dots,c$ and columns $i\in I$ (in increasing order) is defined. The determinant is a polynomial with integer coefficients in the entries and $\pi$ is a ring homomorphism, so the determinant of the matrix of images in $A$ equals $\pi$ of the determinant computed in $P$: it does not matter whether the determinants in (07C7 (1)) and (07C7 (2)) are formed in $P$ and then mapped to $A$, or formed from the images. Hence $a=a'\det(\dots)$ and $a=\sum_I a_I\det(\dots)$ are equations between elements of $A$, with $a',a_I\in A$. For $c=0$ the empty determinant is $1$, the only $I$ is $\emptyset$, and the first conditions hold with $a'=a$, resp. $a_\emptyset=a$; for $c=m$ the second conditions are empty.

(The second condition does not depend on the lift of $a$.) With $P$, $\pi$, $I=(f_1,\dots,f_m)$ as in step 1 and $J=(f_1,\dots,f_c)\subset P$, the condition $a f_{c+j}\in J+I^2$ means $h f_{c+j}\in J+I^2$ for a lift $h\in P$ of $a$ ($\pi(h)=a$; one exists since $\pi$ is surjective). If $h'$ is another lift, then $h-h'\in\ker\pi=I$ and $f_{c+j}\in I$, so $(h-h')f_{c+j}\in I^2\subset J+I^2$; hence $h f_{c+j}\in J+I^2$ iff $h' f_{c+j}\in J+I^2$. So the condition is a property of $a\in A$ and the presentation, as the statement says. Equivalently, it says that $a$ times the class of $f_{c+j}$ in the $A$-module $I/I^2$ lies in the submodule generated by the classes of $f_1,\dots,f_c$ ($I/I^2$ is an $A=P/I$-module since $I\cdot I\subset I^2$).

(The notions are properties of $a$ in the $R$-algebra $A$.) Both definitions are existential: over the data $(n,m,f_1,\dots,f_m,\text{an }R\text{-algebra isomorphism }P/(f_1,\dots,f_m)\cong A)$, the integer $c$ and the elements $a'$, resp. $a_I$; no choice is made, and by steps 1 and 2 the conditions are well defined once these data are given. The conditions for a fixed presentation are not claimed to be independent of the presentation; the definition asks for one presentation (the ordering of the $f_j$ and $x_i$ is part of it). If $\phi:A\to A'$ is an isomorphism of $R$-algebras, composing a presentation of $A$ with $\phi$ gives a presentation of $A'$ with the same $f_j$, and $\phi$ carries the images of $\partial f_j/\partial x_i$, $a'$, $a_I$ to those for $A'$; hence $a$ is elementary (resp. strictly) standard in $A$ over $R$ iff $\phi(a)$ is in $A'$ over $R$.

(Elementary standard implies strictly standard; Lemma Tag 07EZ (b), 'clear from Definition 07C7'.) If $a$ is elementary standard, with presentation $A=R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(f_1,\dots,f_m)$, integer $c$ and $a'$ with $a=a'\det(\partial f_j/\partial x_i)_{i,j=1,\dots,c}$, take the same presentation and $c$, $a_{\{1,\dots,c\}}=a'$ and $a_I=0$ for the other $I$: the matrix for $I=\{1,\dots,c\}$ in (2) is the matrix of (1), so the sum in (2) equals $a'\det(\partial f_j/\partial x_i)_{i,j=1,\dots,c}=a$; the second condition of (2) is literally the second condition of (1). So $a$ is strictly standard in $A$ over $R$. (Stacks records this as Lemma 07EZ (b) together with the comparison of the notions with smoothness of $A_a$; the other parts of 07EZ are statements about the definition, used by the nodes that need them.)
