Let $\varphi:R\to S$ be a ring map of commutative rings, $I\subseteq R$ an ideal and $M$ an $S$-module (an $R$-module via $\varphi$). Assume (1) $R$ is a Noetherian ring; (2) $S$ is a Noetherian ring; (3) $M$ is a finitely generated $S$-module; (4) for every $n\ge1$ the module $M/I^nM$ is flat over $R/I^n$. Then for every prime $\mathfrak q\subseteq S$ containing $IS$ (the ideal of $S$ generated by $\varphi(I)$) the localization $M_{\mathfrak q}$ is a flat $R$-module. In particular, if $S$ is a local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_S$ and $IS\subseteq\mathfrak m_S$, then $M$ is a flat $R$-module.
