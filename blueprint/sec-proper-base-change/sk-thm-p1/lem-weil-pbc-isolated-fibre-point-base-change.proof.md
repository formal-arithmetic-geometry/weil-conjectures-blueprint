Fibre of the base change. Let $r:\kappa(s)\to\kappa(s')$ be the map induced by $g$ on residue fields. By `AlgebraicGeometry.isPullback_fiberToSpecResidueField_of_isPullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Fiber.lean)) there is a morphism $h:X'_{s'}\to X_s$ with $\iota\circ h=g'\circ\iota'$ (where $\iota:X_s\to X$, $\iota':X'_{s'}\to X'$ are the first projections) which, together with the projection $X'_{s'}\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')$, makes $X'_{s'}$ a fibre product $X_s\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)}\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')$, the map $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ being $\operatorname{Spec}(r)$. Since $f'(x')=s'$, by `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Fiber.lean)) there is a unique point $\xi'\in X'_{s'}$ with $\iota'(\xi')=x'$; this is the point of the fibre meant by '$x'$ is isolated in $X'_{s'}$'. Put $\xi=h(\xi')$. Then $\iota(\xi)=g'(\iota'(\xi'))=g'(x')=x$, and since $\iota$ is injective (a homeomorphism onto $f^{-1}(s)$ `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Fiber.lean))), $\xi$ is the point of $X_s$ corresponding to $x$; by hypothesis $\{\xi\}$ is open in $X_s$. Lean realization: isPullback_fiberToSpecResidueField_of_isPullback gives the square; pullback.lift_fst identifies the point; fiberHomeo and Homeomorph.setCongr transport the open singleton across the equality of base points.

The open point as a scheme over $\kappa(s)$. Let $W\subseteq X_s$ be the open subscheme with underlying set $\{\xi\}$ and $w:W\to X_s$ its inclusion, an open immersion `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)). The morphism $w$ is locally of finite type by `AlgebraicGeometry.IsImmersion.isStableUnderBaseChange; AlgebraicGeometry.IsImmersion.instLocallyOfFiniteType (instance at line 190); AlgebraicGeometry.isImmersion_iff_exists` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Immersion.lean)) (2); the projection $\pi:X_s\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ is the base change of the morphism $f$, locally of finite type, along $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)\to S$, hence locally of finite type by `AlgebraicGeometry.locallyOfFiniteType_isStableUnderBaseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FiniteType.lean)); so $\pi\circ w:W\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ is locally of finite type by `AlgebraicGeometry.IsProper (instances), AlgebraicGeometry.locallyOfFinitePresentation_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.quasiCompact_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.quasiSeparated_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.locallyOfFiniteType_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.locallyOfFiniteType_of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.IsImmersion (IsMultiplicative), AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion (pullback instances)` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/ClosedImmersion.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Finite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FinitePresentation.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FiniteType.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Immersion.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Proper.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/QuasiCompact.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/QuasiSeparated.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Separated.lean)) (e). The underlying space of $W$ is one point, hence discrete. Lean realization: U is the actual open singleton; U.toScheme is subsingleton. LocallyQuasiFinite.of_injective applied to U.ι ≫ p packages its discrete finite fibres and local finite type.

Base change of the open point is discrete and open. By `AlgebraicGeometry.locallyQuasiFinite_iff_isDiscrete_preimage_singleton, AlgebraicGeometry.IsLocallyArtinian.of_locallyQuasiFinite, AlgebraicGeometry.IsLocallyArtinian.discreteTopology, topologicalKrullDim_zero_of_discreteTopology` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Artinian.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/QuasiFinite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/KrullDimension.lean)) (with $k=\kappa(s)$, $K=\kappa(s')$ via $r$, and the scheme $W$ of the previous step), the base change $W_{\kappa(s')}=W\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)}\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')$ has discrete underlying space. By `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.pullback_map_isOpenImmersion, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Pullback.range_map` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Pullbacks.lean)), applied to the fibre product $X'_{s'}=X_s\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)}\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')$ of the first step with the opens $W\subseteq X_s$, $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ and $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')$, the canonical morphism $W_{\kappa(s')}\to X'_{s'}$ is an open immersion `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)) with image $h^{-1}(W)$. Hence $h^{-1}(W)$ is an open subset of $X'_{s'}$ and, being homeomorphic to $W_{\kappa(s')}$, is discrete in the subspace topology. Lean realization: (isPullback_morphismRestrict h U).paste_vert hp identifies the inverse-image open subscheme directly as the pullback of U over the extension field. MorphismProperty.of_isPullback transfers local quasi-finiteness, and IsLocallyArtinian.of_locallyQuasiFinite gives its discrete topology. This is the recorded Mathlib realization of the discrete-field-base-change leaf, using injectivity for its singleton case; no quasi-finiteness of the original morphism is assumed.

Conclusion. Since $h(\xi')=\xi\in W$, $\xi'\in h^{-1}(W)$. As $h^{-1}(W)$ is discrete, $\{\xi'\}$ is open in $h^{-1}(W)$, and as $h^{-1}(W)$ is open in $X'_{s'}$, $\{\xi'\}$ is open in $X'_{s'}$: $x'$ is an isolated point of $X'_{s'}$. Lean realization: construct zU in the inverse-image open and apply its inclusion’s isOpenEmbedding.isOpenMap to the discrete singleton; Set.image_singleton identifies its image.
