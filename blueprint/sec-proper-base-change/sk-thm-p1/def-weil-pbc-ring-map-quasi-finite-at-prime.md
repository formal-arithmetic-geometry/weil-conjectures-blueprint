Let $\varphi:R\to S$ be a ring map of finite type, i.e. $S$ is finitely generated as an $R$-algebra: there are $n\ge0$ and $s_1,\dots,s_n\in S$ such that the $R$-algebra homomorphism $R[x_1,\dots,x_n]\to S$, $x_i\mapsto s_i$ `MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean)), is surjective. Let $\mathfrak q\subset S$ be a prime ideal and $\mathfrak p=\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$, with residue field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=R_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}$, and let $F=S\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ be the fibre ring of $S$ over $\mathfrak p$ `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)), with $j:S\to F$, $s\mapsto s\otimes1$. Let $\overline{\mathfrak q}\subset F$ be the prime ideal of $F$ corresponding to $\mathfrak q$, i.e. the unique prime ideal of $F$ with $j^{-1}(\overline{\mathfrak q})=\mathfrak q$ (it exists and is unique by `PrimeSpectrum.preimageOrderIsoFiber, PrimeSpectrum.preimageEquivFiber_apply_asIdeal, PrimeSpectrum.preimageEquivFiber_symm_apply_coe, Algebra.TensorProduct.comm` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/ResidueField/Fiber.lean)), since $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)=\mathfrak p$). We say that $R\to S$ is *quasi-finite at $\mathfrak q$* if $\overline{\mathfrak q}$ is an isolated point of $\operatorname{Spec}(F)$ `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)), that is, the singleton $\{\overline{\mathfrak q}\}$ is open in the Zariski topology of $\operatorname{Spec}(S\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak p))$.
