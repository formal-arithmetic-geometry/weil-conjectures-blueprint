*Step 1 ($\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)A_f\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)$).* $\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)$ is an ideal of $A_f$: if $x,y$ kill every element of $M_f$ and $z\in A_f$, so do $x+y$ and $zx$, by the module axioms of $M_f$. Let $a\in\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)$. Every element of $M_f$ is $m/f^e$ with $m\in M$, $e\ge0$ `LocalizedModule.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)), and by the rule $(x/f^d)(m/f^e)=xm/f^{d+e}$ of that definition, $(a/1)(m/f^e)=am/f^e=0/f^e=0$. So $a/1\in\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)$ for every $a\in\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)$, and the ideal $\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)A_f$ of $A_f$ `Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean)) generated by these elements is contained in the ideal $\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)$.

*Step 2 (reduction to generators).* Let $m_1,\dots,m_r$ generate $M$ `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)). Then $\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)=\bigcap_k\operatorname{Ann}_A(m_k)$: an $a$ with $am_k=0$ for all $k$ kills every $\sum_ja_jm_j$. The elements $m_k/1$ generate the $A_f$-module $M_f$: an element of $M_f$ is $m/f^e=(1/f^e)(m/1)$, and writing $m=\sum_ja_jm_j$, $m/1=\sum_j(a_j/1)(m_j/1)$ since $M\to M_f$, $m\mapsto m/1$, is $A$-linear and $a\cdot(m/1)=(a/1)(m/1)$ `LocalizedModule.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)). Hence likewise $\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)=\bigcap_k\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(m_k/1)$.

*Step 3 ($\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)A_f$).* Let $x\in\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)$ and write $x=a/f^e$ with $a\in A$, $e\ge0$ `Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean)). For each $k$, $0=x\,(m_k/1)=am_k/f^e$, hence $am_k/1=(f^e/1)(am_k/f^e)=0$ in $M_f$, and by the equality criterion of `LocalizedModule.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)) ($m/1=0$ iff $f^nm=0$ for some $n$) there is $n_k\ge0$ with $f^{n_k}am_k=0$. Put $n=\max_kn_k$ (finitely many $k$; $n=0$ if $r=0$). Then $(f^na)m_k=0$ for all $k$, so $f^na\in\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)$ by Step 2, and $x=a/f^e=(1/f^{n+e})\cdot(f^na/1)$ in $A_f$ (the two fractions agree by the definition of equality in $A_f$), an $A_f$-multiple of the image of an element of $\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)$. So $x\in\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)A_f$. (This is the localization case of the two flatness arguments of the source: the first assertion of 07T8, $\operatorname{Ann}_A(m)A_f=\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(m/1)$, is the existence of $n_k$, and Lemma 0BBY, $\bigcap_k(I_kA_f)=(\bigcap_kI_k)A_f$, is the passage to the common exponent $n$.)

*Step 4 (conclusion).* Steps 1 and 3 give $\operatorname{Ann}_{A_f}(M_f)=\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)A_f$.
