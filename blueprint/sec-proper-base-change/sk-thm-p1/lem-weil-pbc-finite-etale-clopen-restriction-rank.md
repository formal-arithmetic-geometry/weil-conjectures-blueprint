title = "Rank compatibility for clopen restrictions of finite etale morphisms"

Let $q:X\to S$ be finite etale and let $(j:X_0\to X,q_0:X_0\to S_0,q,i:S_0\to S)$ be a cartesian square of schemes. Let $W\subseteq X$ be open and closed. Then both $q|_W:W\to S$ and $q_0|_{j^{-1}W}:j^{-1}W\to S_0$ are finite etale. For each $x\in S_0$, the rank of the second morphism at $x$ (the dimension over $\kappa(x)$ of the ring of global sections of its fibre over $x$) equals the rank of the first at $i(x)$.
