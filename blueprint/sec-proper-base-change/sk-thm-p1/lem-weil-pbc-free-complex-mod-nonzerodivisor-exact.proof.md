(The complex as a cochain complex of $R$-modules.) The category $\Mod(R)$ of $R$-modules is abelian, with the usual kernels, cokernels and images `ModuleCat.enoughInjectives` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/EnoughInjectives.lean)). Put $F_i=R^{\oplus n_i}$ ($0\le i\le e$) and reindex the given complex as a cochain complex $K$ of $R$-modules indexed by $\mathbf N$: $K^j=F_{e-j}$ for $0\le j\le e$, $K^j=0$ for $j>e$, with $d^j=\varphi_{e-j}:K^j\to K^{j+1}$ for $0\le j\le e-1$ and $d^j=0$ for $j\ge e$; $d^{j+1}\circ d^j=\varphi_{e-j-1}\circ\varphi_{e-j}=0$ for $0\le j\le e-2$ by hypothesis, and the other composites have a zero factor. Its cohomology is $H^j(K)=\ker d^j/\operatorname{im}d^{j-1}$ ($d^{-1}=0$), i.e. $H^{e-i}(K)=\ker\varphi_i/\operatorname{im}\varphi_{i+1}=:H_i(F_\bullet)$ for $1\le i\le e$, with $\varphi_{e+1}=0$. By the definition of exactness `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)) (in $\Mod(R)$: $\operatorname{im}=\ker$ as submodules), the complex $0\to F_e\to\cdots\to F_0$ is exact at $F_i$ iff $\ker\varphi_i=\operatorname{im}\varphi_{i+1}$ (for $i=e$: iff $\varphi_e$ is injective, the incoming arrow being $0\to F_e$), i.e. iff $H_i(F_\bullet)=0$. So the hypothesis says $H_i(F_\bullet)=0$ for $1\le i\le e$.

(The short exact sequence $0\to F_\bullet\xrightarrow{x}F_\bullet\to F_\bullet/xF_\bullet\to0$.) Let $\mu_i:F_i\to F_i$ be multiplication by $x$. Since each $\varphi_i$ is $R$-linear, $\varphi_i\circ\mu_i=\mu_{i-1}\circ\varphi_i$, so $\mu$ is a morphism of complexes $K\to K$. It is injective in each degree: if $x(a_1,\dots,a_{n_i})=0$ in the free module $F_i=R^{\oplus n_i}$ `Module.Free` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/FreeModule/Basic.lean)) then $xa_k=0$ for each coordinate, so $a_k=0$ as $x$ is a nonzerodivisor of $R$. Let $\pi_i:F_i\to\bar R^{\oplus n_i}$ reduce each coordinate modulo $x$; it is $R$-linear and surjective, and its kernel is the set of vectors with all coordinates in $xR$, which is $xF_i=\operatorname{im}\mu_i$. If $A_i=(a^{(i)}_{kl})$ is the matrix `Matrix` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Defs.lean)) of $\varphi_i$ (so $\varphi_i(e_l)=\sum_k a^{(i)}_{kl}f_k$ for the standard bases), then $\pi_{i-1}(\varphi_i(e_l))=\sum_k\overline{a^{(i)}_{kl}}\,\bar f_k=\bar\varphi_i(\pi_i(e_l))$, hence $\pi_{i-1}\circ\varphi_i=\bar\varphi_i\circ\pi_i$ by linearity. Consequently $\bar\varphi_i\circ\bar\varphi_{i+1}\circ\pi_{i+1}=\pi_{i-1}\circ\varphi_i\circ\varphi_{i+1}=0$, and $\pi_{i+1}$ being surjective, $\bar\varphi_i\circ\bar\varphi_{i+1}=0$: the reduced sequence is a complex. Regard it as a cochain complex $\bar K$ of $R$-modules, $\bar K^j=\bar R^{\oplus n_{e-j}}$ ($0\le j\le e$), $\bar K^j=0$ ($j>e$), $\bar d^j=\bar\varphi_{e-j}$ ($0\le j\le e-1$), $\bar d^j=0$ otherwise. Then $\pi:K\to\bar K$ is a morphism of complexes, $\pi\circ\mu=0$, and in every degree $0\to K^j\xrightarrow{\mu}K^j\xrightarrow{\pi}\bar K^j\to0$ is a short exact sequence of $R$-modules `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)) ($\mu$ injective, $\ker\pi=\operatorname{im}\mu$, $\pi$ surjective; trivially in degrees $j>e$).

(Long exact sequence.) Apply the long exact cohomology sequence `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.ShortExact.homology_exact₂` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/HomologySequence.lean)) in the abelian category $\Mod(R)$ `ModuleCat.enoughInjectives` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/EnoughInjectives.lean)) to the degreewise short exact sequence $0\to K\xrightarrow{\mu}K\xrightarrow{\pi}\bar K\to0$ of Step 2: for every $j\ge0$ the sequence $H^j(K)\xrightarrow{H^j(\pi)}H^j(\bar K)\xrightarrow{\delta^j}H^{j+1}(K)$ is exact. Let $2\le i\le e$ and $j=e-i$, so $0\le j\le e-2$. Then $H^j(K)=H_i(F_\bullet)=0$ and $H^{j+1}(K)=H_{i-1}(F_\bullet)=0$ by Step 1, since $i\ge1$ and $i-1\ge1$. Exactness at $H^j(\bar K)$ gives $H^j(\bar K)=\ker\delta^j=\operatorname{im}H^j(\pi)=0$.

(Conclusion.) For $2\le i\le e$, Step 3 gives $0=H^{e-i}(\bar K)=\ker\bar\varphi_i/\operatorname{im}\bar\varphi_{i+1}$ (with $\bar\varphi_{e+1}=0$), so $\ker\bar\varphi_i=\operatorname{im}\bar\varphi_{i+1}$ as subsets of $\bar R^{\oplus n_i}$; kernels and images of the $\bar R$-linear maps $\bar\varphi_i$ are the same sets whether these maps are viewed as $R$- or $\bar R$-linear. By the definition `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)), the complex $0\to\bar R^{\oplus n_e}\xrightarrow{\bar\varphi_e}\cdots\xrightarrow{\bar\varphi_2}\bar R^{\oplus n_1}$ is exact at $\bar R^{\oplus n_i}$ for $i=e,\dots,2$ (at $\bar R^{\oplus n_e}$ this says $\bar\varphi_e$ is injective). The hypotheses that $R$ is local and $x\in\mathfrak m$ are not used.
