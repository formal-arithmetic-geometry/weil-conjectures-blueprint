Let $X$ be a scheme and $\Lambda$ a commutative ring, and let $F,G$ be locally constant sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on the small etale site of $X$ (each is constant on the members of some etale covering of $X$). Then $F\otimes_\Lambda G$ is locally constant.
