title = "A finite module over a Noetherian ring complete along an ideal is complete"
lean = ["finite_module_complete_over_complete_noetherian"]
order = 1784

Let $R$ be a Noetherian ring and $J\subseteq R$ an ideal such that $R$ is $J$-adically complete. Let $N$ be a finitely generated $R$-module. Then $N$ is $J$-adically complete: the canonical $R$-linear map $N\to\varprojlim_nN/J^nN$, $x\mapsto(x\bmod J^nN)_n$, into the module of compatible sequences is bijective.
