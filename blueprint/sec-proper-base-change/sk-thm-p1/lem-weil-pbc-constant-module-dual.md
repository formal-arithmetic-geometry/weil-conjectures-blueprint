Let $X$ be a scheme and $\Lambda$ a commutative noetherian ring. Let $M$ be a finitely generated $\Lambda$-module. Write $M_X$ for its constant coefficient sheaf on the small étale site of $X$ [def-constructible-constant], and $\Lambda_X$ for the monoidal unit. Then $\mathcal Hom_\Lambda(M_X,\Lambda_X)\cong (\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(M,\Lambda))_X$ as sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules.
