($\Rightarrow$) Let $M$ be generated over $R$ by $m_1,\dots,m_r$ `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)). In $M\otimes_RS$ `LinearMap.baseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Tower.lean)) every element is a sum of pure tensors $m\otimes s=s\cdot(m\otimes1)$, and writing $m=\sum_ka_km_k$ gives $m\otimes1=\sum_k\varphi(a_k)(m_k\otimes1)$; so $m_1\otimes1,\dots,m_r\otimes1$ generate $M\otimes_RS$ over $S$. In $M_{f_i}$ `LocalizedModule.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)) every element is $m/f_i^e=(1/f_i^e)\cdot(m/1)=\sum_k(a_k/f_i^e)\cdot(m_k/1)$; so $m_1/1,\dots,m_r/1$ generate $M_{f_i}$ over $R_{f_i}$.

($\Leftarrow$; the ring $T$ and its flatness.) Put $T_0=S$, $T_i=R_{f_i}$ for $1\le i\le t$ `Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean)), $T=T_0\times T_1\times\cdots\times T_t$ with componentwise ring operations, and $\psi:R\to T$, $\psi(r)=(\varphi(r),r/1,\dots,r/1)$, a ring homomorphism. As an $R$-module via $\psi$, $T$ is the product of the $R$-modules $T_0$ (via $\varphi$) and $T_i$ (via $r\mapsto r/1$), with componentwise operations. $T_0=S$ is flat over $R$ by hypothesis `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)); each $R_{f_i}$ is flat over $R$, being the localization of $R$ at the multiplicative set $\{1,f_i,f_i^2,\dots\}$ `IsLocalization.flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Localization.lean)). Hence $\psi$ is flat `Module.Flat.directSum, Module.Flat.of_linearEquiv, DirectSum.linearEquivFunOnFintype` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)).

($\operatorname{Spec}T\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is surjective.) For $0\le j\le t$ let $\pi_j:T\to T_j$ be the projection, a ring homomorphism; if $\mathfrak q_j\subset T_j$ is a prime ideal then $\pi_j^{-1}(\mathfrak q_j)$ is a prime ideal of $T$ and $\psi^{-1}(\pi_j^{-1}(\mathfrak q_j))=(\pi_j\psi)^{-1}(\mathfrak q_j)$, where $\pi_0\psi=\varphi$ and $\pi_i\psi$ is the localization map $R\to R_{f_i}$. So it suffices to show that every prime $\mathfrak p\subset R$ `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)) is $(\pi_j\psi)^{-1}(\mathfrak q_j)$ for some $j$ and some prime $\mathfrak q_j$ of $T_j$. (a) If $I\not\subseteq\mathfrak p$, then $f_i\notin\mathfrak p$ for some $i$, since otherwise $I=(f_1,\dots,f_t)\subseteq\mathfrak p$. By `AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion.spec_of_quotient_mk; AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isOpenImmersion_SpecMap_localizationAway; AlgebraicGeometry.IsImmersion.comp; PrimeSpectrum.localization_away_comap_range; PrimeSpectrum.range_comap_of_surjective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Immersion.lean)) with $A=R$, $f=f_i$ and $n=0$ (so $J=0$, $A/J=R$ and $B=R_{f_i}$, the map $A\to A/J\to B$ being the localization map), the image of $\operatorname{Spec}R_{f_i}\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is $D(f_i)=\{\mathfrak p: f_i\notin\mathfrak p\}$, which contains $\mathfrak p$: there is a prime $\mathfrak q$ of $R_{f_i}$ whose inverse image in $R$ is $\mathfrak p$. (b) If $I\subseteq\mathfrak p$, let $\theta:R/I\to S/IS$, $r+I\mapsto\varphi(r)+IS$, be the isomorphism of the hypothesis and $\rho:S\to S/IS$ the quotient map, so $\rho\varphi=\theta\circ(r\mapsto r+I)$. The ideal $\bar{\mathfrak p}=\mathfrak p/I$ of $R/I$ is prime, hence so is its image $\theta(\bar{\mathfrak p})$ under the ring isomorphism $\theta$, and $\mathfrak q=\rho^{-1}(\theta(\bar{\mathfrak p}))$ is a prime ideal of $S$. For $r\in R$: $\varphi(r)\in\mathfrak q\iff\theta(r+I)\in\theta(\bar{\mathfrak p})\iff r+I\in\bar{\mathfrak p}\iff r\in\mathfrak p$, the last because $I\subseteq\mathfrak p$. So $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)=\mathfrak p$.

($\psi$ is faithfully flat.) By the two previous steps $\psi:R\to T$ is flat and $\operatorname{Spec}T\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is surjective, so $\psi$ is faithfully flat `Module.FaithfullyFlat.of_comap_surjective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/FaithfullyFlat/Algebra.lean)), `Module.FaithfullyFlat, RingHom.FaithfullyFlat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/FaithfullyFlat/Basic.lean)).

($T\otimes_RM$ as a product.) Regard $T\otimes_RM$ as a $T$-module by $t\cdot(u\otimes m)=tu\otimes m$, and $N=(T_0\otimes_RM)\times\cdots\times(T_t\otimes_RM)$ as a $T$-module componentwise, $(t_j)_j\cdot(z_j)_j=(t_jz_j)_j$, with $T_j$ acting on $T_j\otimes_RM$ through the left factor. By `TensorProduct.piLeft` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Pi.lean)) (with the $t+1$ $R$-modules $P_k=T_{k-1}$, $1\le k\le t+1$, and $N=M$) the map $\Phi:T\otimes_RM\to N$, $(u_j)_j\otimes m\mapsto(u_j\otimes m)_j$, is an isomorphism of $R$-modules. It is $T$-linear: for $t=(t_j)_j\in T$, $\Phi(t\cdot((u_j)_j\otimes m))=\Phi((t_ju_j)_j\otimes m)=(t_ju_j\otimes m)_j=t\cdot\Phi((u_j)_j\otimes m)$, and both $x\mapsto\Phi(tx)$ and $x\mapsto t\Phi(x)$ are additive, so they agree on the pure tensors, which generate $T\otimes_RM$ as an abelian group. Moreover $S\otimes_RM\to M\otimes_RS$, $s\otimes m\mapsto m\otimes s$, is an isomorphism of $S$-modules (the symmetry of the tensor product; $S$ acting on $M\otimes_RS$ as in `LinearMap.baseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Tower.lean))), and $R_{f_i}\otimes_RM\to M_{f_i}$, $(a/f_i^e)\otimes m\mapsto am/f_i^e$, is an isomorphism of $R_{f_i}$-modules `IsLocalization.Away.tensorEquiv, IsLocalization.Away.tensorRightEquiv, LocalizedModule.equivTensorProduct` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/BaseChange.lean)), `LocalizedModule.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)). Hence $T_0\otimes_RM$ is a finitely generated $S$-module iff $M\otimes_RS$ is, and $T_i\otimes_RM$ is a finitely generated $R_{f_i}$-module iff $M_{f_i}$ is (an isomorphism carries generators to generators). (`Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean))).

($T\otimes_RM$ is finitely generated over $T$.) Assume $M\otimes_RS$ is finitely generated over $S$ and each $M_{f_i}$ over $R_{f_i}$. By the previous step each $N_j=T_j\otimes_RM$ is generated over $T_j$ by finitely many elements $z_{j,1},\dots,z_{j,b_j}$ `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)). For $z\in N_j$ let $\iota_j(z)\in N$ have $z$ in place $j$ and $0$ elsewhere, and for $c\in T_j$ let $\tau_j(c)\in T$ have $c$ in place $j$ and $0$ elsewhere. For $n=(n_j)_j\in N$ write $n_j=\sum_lc_{j,l}z_{j,l}$ with $c_{j,l}\in T_j$; then $n=\sum_j\iota_j(n_j)=\sum_j\sum_l\tau_j(c_{j,l})\cdot\iota_j(z_{j,l})$ by the componentwise action. So the finitely many $\iota_j(z_{j,l})$ generate $N$ over $T$, and their images under the $T$-linear isomorphism $\Phi^{-1}$ generate $T\otimes_RM$ over $T$.

(Descent.) $\psi:R\to T$ is faithfully flat (step 4) and $T\otimes_RM$ is a finitely generated $T$-module (step 6), so $M$ is a finitely generated $R$-module `Module.Finite.of_finite_tensorProduct_of_faithfullyFlat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Descent.lean)).
