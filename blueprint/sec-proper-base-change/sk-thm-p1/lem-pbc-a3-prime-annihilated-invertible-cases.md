Let $k$ be a field, $\Lambda$ a commutative ring, $X$ a scheme and $r$ a prime. Let $F$ be a sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules on $X_{\mathrm{et}}$ with $r\cdot\mathrm{id}_F=0$. If $r$ is a unit in $k$, then $F$ has torsion of invertible order relative to $\operatorname{Spec}k$, i.e. satisfies the coefficient condition of [def-weil-pbc-eight-curve-assertions]. If $r$ is not a unit in $k$ and $F$ has torsion of invertible order relative to $\operatorname{Spec}k$, then $F=0$.
