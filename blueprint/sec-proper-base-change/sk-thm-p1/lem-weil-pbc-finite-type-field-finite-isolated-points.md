Let $k$ be a field and let $A$ be a finitely generated $k$-algebra `Algebra.FiniteType` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FiniteType.lean)). Then the set of prime ideals $\mathfrak q\subset A$ for which $\{\mathfrak q\}$ is open in $\operatorname{Spec}A$ `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)) is finite.
