Let $(R,\mathfrak m_R)$ and $(S,\mathfrak m_S)$ be Noetherian local rings and $\varphi:R\to S$ a local homomorphism ($\varphi(\mathfrak m_R)\subseteq\mathfrak m_S$). If $\varphi$ is flat and $S$ is a regular local ring, then $R$ is a regular local ring.
