Let $R$ be a commutative Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ and Krull dimension $d$. An ordered finite list $t=(t_1,\ldots,t_r)$ of elements of $R$ is a *regular system of parameters* if $(t_1,\ldots,t_r)=\mathfrak m$ and $r=d$. The empty list is permitted when $d=0$.
