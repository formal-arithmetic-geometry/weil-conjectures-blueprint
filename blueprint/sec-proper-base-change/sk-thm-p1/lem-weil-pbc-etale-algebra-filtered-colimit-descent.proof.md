*A model over a finitely generated subring (Stacks: 'Follows from (8)').* By `Algebra.Etale.exists_subalgebra_fg` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/NoetherianDescent.lean)) applied to $A\to B$ there are a subring $A_0\subseteq A$ generated by finitely many elements, with inclusion $j:A_0\to A$, a ring map $A_0\to B_0$ with $\operatorname{Spec}B_0\to\operatorname{Spec}A_0$ etale, and an isomorphism of $A$-algebras $\varphi:B\xrightarrow{\sim}A\otimes_{A_0}B_0$, where $A$ is an $A_0$-algebra via $j$.

*The model ring factors through a stage (Stacks: '$R_0\to A$ will factor through $A_i$ for some $i$ by Lemma 00QO').* $A_0$ is of finite type over $\mathbf Z$ (step 1). By `RingHom.EssFiniteType.exists_eq_comp_ι_app_of_isColimit, RingHom.EssFiniteType.exists_comp_map_eq_of_isColimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/Ring/FinitePresentation.lean)) (1), applied to the filtered colimit $A=\varinjlim_IA_i$ and the ring map $j:A_0\to A$, there are $i\in I$ and a ring map $g:A_0\to A_i$ with $\iota_i\circ g=j$. (In the cited lemma the factorization is obtained elementwise: the finitely many generators of $A_0$ are images of elements of a common $A_\mu$, and the finitely many defining relations of $A_0=\mathbf Z[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(f_1,\dots,f_m)$, which hold in $A$, hold already in some later $A_i$.)

*The etale algebra over the stage.* Regard $A_i$ as an $A_0$-algebra via $g$ and put $B_i=A_i\otimes_{A_0}B_0$, an $A_i$-algebra via $a\mapsto a\otimes1$. By `Algebra.Etale.baseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Etale/Basic.lean)) (with $R=A_0$, $A=B_0$, $S=A_i$), $\operatorname{Spec}B_i\to\operatorname{Spec}A_i$ is etale.

*The isomorphism $B\cong A\otimes_{A_i}B_i$ (Stacks: implicit).* Regard $A$ as an $A_i$-algebra via $\iota_i$. Its $A_0$-algebra structure through $A_i$ is $\iota_i\circ g=j$ (step 2), the one used in step 1. Hence `Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/TensorProduct/Maps.lean)) (with $R=A_0$, $S=A_i$, the $S$-algebra $A$ and the $R$-algebra $B_0$) gives an isomorphism of $A$-algebras $\psi:A\otimes_{A_i}B_i=A\otimes_{A_i}(A_i\otimes_{A_0}B_0)\xrightarrow{\sim}A\otimes_{A_0}B_0$, $a\otimes(s\otimes b)\mapsto\iota_i(s)a\otimes b$. Then $\psi^{-1}\circ\varphi:B\xrightarrow{\sim}A\otimes_{A_i}B_i$ is an isomorphism of $A$-algebras, and $i$, $A_i\to B_i$ (etale by step 3) satisfy the statement.
