For a commutative coefficient ring Λ, a scheme X and a prime r, let F be an etale sheaf of Λ-modules with r id_F = 0. Then F is r-primary [def-weil-pbc-primary-torsion]. For any prime p different from r, if F is p-primary, F is a zero object.
