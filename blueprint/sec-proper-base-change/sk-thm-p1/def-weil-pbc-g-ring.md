Let $k$ be a field. A Noetherian `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)) $k$-algebra $A$ is *geometrically regular over $k$* if for every finite purely inseparable field extension $k'/k$ `IsPurelyInseparable, Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/PurelyInseparable/Basic.lean)) the ring $k'\otimes_kA$ is regular, i.e. Noetherian with every localization at a prime ideal a regular local ring `IsRegularLocalRing, IsRegularRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean)) (Stacks Algebra, Definition 0382, by condition (2) of Lemma 0381, as recalled in More on Algebra, Section 07BY). A ring map $\varphi:R\to\Lambda$ of commutative rings is *regular* if it is flat `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)) and for every prime ideal $\mathfrak p\subset R$ the fibre ring $\Lambda\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)) is Noetherian and geometrically regular over the residue field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ (More on Algebra, Definition 07BZ). A commutative ring $R$ is a *G-ring* if $R$ is Noetherian and for every prime ideal $\mathfrak p\subset R$ the ring map $R_{\mathfrak p}\to(R_{\mathfrak p})^\wedge$ is regular, where $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is the localization of $R$ at $\mathfrak p$, a local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}$ `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)), and $(R_{\mathfrak p})^\wedge=\varprojlim_nR_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak p^nR_{\mathfrak p}$ is its $\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}$-adic completion with its canonical ring map $R_{\mathfrak p}\to(R_{\mathfrak p})^\wedge$ `AdicCompletion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Basic.lean)) (More on Algebra, Definition 07GH).
