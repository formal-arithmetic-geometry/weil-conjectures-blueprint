For a module X annihilated by the maximal ideal, identify N tensor X and M tensor X with their residue quotients tensor X. Tensoring the injective residue map over the residue field is injective. This is the proved private helper rTensor_injective_of_maximal_annihilates. (`Module.Flat.of_free, Module.Free.of_divisionRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)), `TensorProduct.quotTensorEquivQuotSMul, TensorProduct.AlgebraTensorModule.cancelBaseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Quotient.lean))).

Induct on n for modules annihilated by the nth power. Use the exact sequence mX → X → X/mX → 0, tensor right exactness for N, and injectivity of mX tensor M → X tensor M by flatness. The two tensor maps on mX and X/mX are injective by induction and the preceding step. A diagram chase proves injectivity on X. (`Module.Flat.rTensor_exact, Module.Flat.lTensor_exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)), `rTensor_exact, lTensor_exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/RightExactness.lean))).

For finite X over R, a kernel element in N tensor X maps to zero after tensoring X → X/m^n X, so lies in m^n(N tensor X) for every n. N tensor X is finite over S, via the base-change tensor equivalence. Transfer membership to (mS)^n(N tensor X) and use Krull intersection because mS lies in the Jacobson radical. (`rTensor_exact, lTensor_exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/RightExactness.lean)), `Ideal.iInf_pow_smul_eq_bot_of_le_jacobson` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Filtration.lean))).

Apply the preceding tensor injectivity to X=R/J (a cyclic R-module) for every ideal J. The tensor-quotient equivalences imply u(x) in JM only if x is in JN. At J=0 this proves injectivity of u. (`TensorProduct.quotTensorEquivQuotSMul, TensorProduct.AlgebraTensorModule.cancelBaseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Quotient.lean))).

Prove flatness of C=M/range u by the ideal tensor criterion, adapting Module.Flat.quotient_range from WeilConjectures.Mathlib.RingTheory.Filtration to distinct domain N and codomain M. Lift z in C tensor J to y in M tensor J. Its multiplication image lies in range u, say u(x); purity gives x in JN, so lift x to yprime in N tensor J. Injectivity of the multiplication map for flat M gives y=u tensor J(yprime). The quotient map kills this image, hence z=0. All these steps are written in the proved private helper flat_quotient_range_of_ideal_preimage. (`Module.Flat.rTensor_exact, Module.Flat.lTensor_exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)), `rTensor_exact, lTensor_exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/RightExactness.lean))).
