Let $R$ be a commutative ring. For a commutative $R$-algebra $P$ and a multiplicative subset $S\subseteq P$ ($1\in S$, $st\in S$ for $s,t\in S$) let $S^{-1}P$ be the ring of fractions $a/s$ ($a\in P$, $s\in S$; $a/s=a'/s'$ iff $u(as'-a's)=0$ for some $u\in S$; $a/s+a'/s'=(as'+a's)/ss'$, $(a/s)(a'/s')=aa'/ss'$), an $R$-algebra through $R\to P\to S^{-1}P$, $a\mapsto a/1$. A homomorphism of commutative rings $R\to D$ (equivalently, the $R$-algebra $D$) is *essentially smooth* if there are a smooth $R$-algebra $P$ `Algebra.Smooth` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean)), a multiplicative subset $S\subseteq P$ and an isomorphism of $R$-algebras $D\cong S^{-1}P$. Remarks (proved in the steps below, not part of the definition): (a) every smooth ring map $R\to D$ is essentially smooth, with $P=D$ and $S=\{1\}$; in particular every étale ring map is, i.e. every $R\to D$ with $\operatorname{Spec}D\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)) étale `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)) (equivalently, étale in the sense of Stacks 00U1: finitely presented with $H_1(L_{D/R})=0$ and $\Omega_{D/R}=0$); (b) if $D$ is essentially smooth and local, with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and $D\cong S^{-1}P$ as above, then $\mathfrak P=\iota^{-1}(\mathfrak m)$, $\iota:P\to S^{-1}P\cong D$, is a prime of $P$ with $S\subseteq P\setminus\mathfrak P$, and $D\cong P_{\mathfrak P}$ `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)) as $R$-algebras; so one may take $S=P\setminus\mathfrak P$.
