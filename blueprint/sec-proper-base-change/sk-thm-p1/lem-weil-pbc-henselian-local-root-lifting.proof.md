**Step 1.** *Uniqueness of lifts holds for every local ring.* Let $B$ be a finite $R$-algebra and $e,e'\in B$ idempotents with $e\equiv e'\bmod\mathfrak mB$. Put $d=e-e'\in\mathfrak mB$. Expanding with $e^2=e$, $e'^2=e'$: $d^2=e-2ee'+e'$ and $d^3=d\cdot d^2=(e-2ee'+ee')-(ee'-2ee'+e')=e-e'=d$, so $d(1-d^2)=0$. Since $d^2\in\mathfrak mB$ and $\mathfrak mB$ lies in the Jacobson radical of $B$ [lem-weil-pbc-finite-algebra-local-radical], $1-d^2$ is a unit of $B$; hence $d=0$, i.e. $e=e'$. So for every finite $R$-algebra $B$ the reduction map from idempotents of $B$ to idempotents of $B/\mathfrak mB$ is injective, and (i) amounts to its surjectivity for all finite $B$.

**Step 2.** *(i)$\Rightarrow$(ii).* Assume (i). Let $f\in R[T ]$ be monic of degree $n$ and $a_0\in\kappa$ with $\overline f(a_0)=0\neq\overline{f'}(a_0)$. In $\kappa[T ]$ write $\overline f=(T-a_0)h_0$; then $\overline{f}'=h_0+(T-a_0)h_0'$ gives $h_0(a_0)=\overline{f'}(a_0)\neq0$, so $T-a_0\nmid h_0$ and the ideals $(T-a_0)$, $(h_0)$ of $\kappa[T ]$ are comaximal ($T-a_0$ is irreducible); by the Chinese remainder theorem $\kappa[T ]/(\overline f)\cong\kappa[T ]/(T-a_0)\times\kappa[T ]/(h_0)$. Let $S=R[T ]/(f)$: it is a free $R$-module with basis $1,T,\dots,T^{n-1}$, hence a finite $R$-algebra, and $S/\mathfrak mS\cong\kappa[T ]/(\overline f)$ [lem-weil-pbc-monic-quotient-free]. Let $\bar e\in S/\mathfrak mS$ be the idempotent corresponding to $(1,0)$, so that $\bar e(S/\mathfrak mS)\cong\kappa[T ]/(T-a_0)\cong\kappa$ as quotients of $\kappa[T ]$. By (i) [def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology] there is an idempotent $e\in S$ lifting $\bar e$. Then $S=eS\times(1-e)S$, and $A=eS$ is a ring (unit $e$) and an $R$-algebra, a quotient of $S$ via $s\mapsto es$, and an $R$-module direct summand of the finite free $R$-module $S$; so $A$ is finitely generated and flat (a direct summand of a flat module is flat), hence free [lem-weil-pbc-finite-flat-local-free]. Moreover $A/\mathfrak mA=\bar e(S/\mathfrak mS)\cong\kappa$, so a basis of $A$ has exactly one element (its image is a $\kappa$-basis of $A/\mathfrak mA$): $A=Rb$ freely. Write $e=ub$ with $u\in R$; $e$ is the unit of $A$ and $A/\mathfrak mA\neq0$, so $e\notin\mathfrak mA$, i.e. $u\notin\mathfrak m$, and $u$ is a unit; hence $e$ is also a basis and the structure map $R\to A$, $r\mapsto re$, is an isomorphism of $R$-algebras. Let $a\in R$ be the image of $T$ under the $R$-algebra map $\varphi:R[T ]\to S\to A\cong R$; then $\varphi$ is evaluation at $a$, and $f(a)=\varphi(f)=0$ because $f=0$ in $S$. Reducing modulo $\mathfrak m$, $\varphi$ followed by $R\to\kappa$ equals $R[T ]\to S/\mathfrak mS\to\bar e(S/\mathfrak mS)\cong\kappa[T ]/(T-a_0)\cong\kappa$, which sends $T$ to $a_0$; hence $a\bmod\mathfrak m=a_0$. This is (ii).

**Step 3.** *(ii)$\Rightarrow$ etale retractions (Stacks' condition (8)).* Assume (ii). Then for every ring map $R\to R'$ with $\operatorname{Spec}R'\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ etale and every prime $\mathfrak m'\subset R'$ over $\mathfrak m$ with $\kappa\to\kappa(\mathfrak m')$ an isomorphism there is a (unique) $R$-algebra map $\tau:R'\to R$ with $\tau^{-1}(\mathfrak m)=\mathfrak m'$ [lem-weil-pbc-root-lifting-etale-retraction].

**Step 4.** *Etale retractions $\Rightarrow$ finite algebras split (Stacks (8)$\Rightarrow$(10)).* By step 3 the hypothesis of [lem-weil-pbc-etale-retraction-finite-product-local] holds for $R$, so every finite $R$-algebra $B$ is isomorphic as an $R$-algebra to a finite product $B_1\times\cdots\times B_r$ of local rings.

**Step 5.** *(ii)$\Rightarrow$(i): products of local rings lift idempotents.* Assume (ii) and let $B$ be a finite $R$-algebra; by step 4 we may take $B=B_1\times\cdots\times B_r$ with each $B_i$ local, with maximal ideal $\mathfrak n_i$. Each $B_i$ is a quotient of $B$ (projection), hence a finite $R$-algebra, so $\mathfrak mB_i\subset\mathfrak n_i$ [lem-weil-pbc-finite-algebra-local-radical]; thus $\bar B_i=B_i/\mathfrak mB_i$ is a nonzero local ring. Since $\mathfrak mB=\prod_i\mathfrak mB_i$ (an element $\sum_k x_k(b_{k,i})_i$, $x_k\in\mathfrak m$, has components $\sum_kx_kb_{k,i}\in\mathfrak mB_i$, and conversely $(y_i)_i=\sum_i\epsilon_iy_i$ with $\epsilon_i$ the $i$-th unit vector), $B/\mathfrak mB\cong\prod_i\bar B_i$ componentwise. A local ring has no idempotents besides $0,1$ (if $\epsilon^2=\epsilon$, one of $\epsilon$, $1-\epsilon$ is a unit and $\epsilon(1-\epsilon)=0$). Idempotents of a product are computed componentwise, so every idempotent of $B/\mathfrak mB$ is $(\epsilon_i)_i$ with $\epsilon_i\in\{0,1\}\subset\bar B_i$; the element $(\epsilon_i)_i\in\prod_iB_i=B$ (same $0$s and $1$s) is an idempotent of $B$ reducing to it. With the uniqueness of step 1, every idempotent of $B/\mathfrak mB$ lifts uniquely to an idempotent of $B$: $R$ is henselian in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology]. Together with step 2 this proves (i)$\Leftrightarrow$(ii).
