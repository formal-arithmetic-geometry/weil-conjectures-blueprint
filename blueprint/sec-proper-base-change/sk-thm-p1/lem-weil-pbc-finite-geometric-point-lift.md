id = "lem-weil-pbc-finite-geometric-point-lift"
kind = "lemma"
title = "Geometric points lift along a finite morphism (over every point of the fibre)"
lean = ["AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.GeometricFiber.exists_apply_default_eq"]
+++
Let $\pi:Y'\to Y$ be a finite morphism of schemes `AlgebraicGeometry.IsFinite, AlgebraicGeometry.IsIntegralHom` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Finite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Integral.lean)), let $\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$ be a geometric point `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.SpecToEquivOfField, IsAlgClosed` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ResidueField.lean)) ($\Omega$ an algebraically closed field `AlgebraicClosure` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/IsAlgClosed/AlgebraicClosure.lean))) lying over $y\in Y$, and let $y'\in Y'$ be a point with $\pi(y')=y$. Then there is a geometric point $\bar y':\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y'$ (with the same field $\Omega$) lying over $y'$ such that $\pi\circ\bar y'=\bar y$. In particular, if $\pi$ is moreover surjective `AlgebraicGeometry.Surjective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/UnderlyingMap.lean)), then every geometric point of $Y$ lifts along $\pi$ to a geometric point of $Y'$ with the same field.
