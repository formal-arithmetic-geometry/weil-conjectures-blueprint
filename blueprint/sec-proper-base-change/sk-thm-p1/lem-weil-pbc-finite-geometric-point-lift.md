kind = "lemma"
title = "Geometric points lift along a finite morphism (over every point of the fibre)"
lean = ["AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.GeometricFiber.exists_apply_default_eq"]
order = 1780

Let $\pi:Y'\to Y$ be a finite morphism of schemes, let $\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$ be a geometric point ($\Omega$ an algebraically closed field) lying over $y\in Y$, and let $y'\in Y'$ be a point with $\pi(y')=y$. Then there is a geometric point $\bar y':\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y'$ (with the same field $\Omega$) lying over $y'$ such that $\pi\circ\bar y'=\bar y$. In particular, if $\pi$ is moreover surjective, then every geometric point of $Y$ lifts along $\pi$ to a geometric point of $Y'$ with the same field.
