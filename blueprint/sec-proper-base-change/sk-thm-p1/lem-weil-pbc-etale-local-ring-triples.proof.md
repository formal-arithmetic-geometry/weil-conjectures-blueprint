**Step 1.** *Setting up the affine chart.* Write $\iota:U=\operatorname{Spec}A\to Y$ for the open immersion of the chosen affine open. Since $\bar x$ has image $y\in U$, it factors uniquely as $\bar x=\iota\circ\bar x_U$ with $\bar x_U:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to U$ (universal property of the open immersion $\iota$); by [def-weil-affine-spectrum] $\bar x_U=\operatorname{Spec}\chi$ for a unique ring map $\chi:A\to\Omega$. The image point of $\bar x_U$ is the prime $\ker\chi$, so $\ker\chi=\mathfrak p$, and $\chi$ factors as $A\to\kappa(\mathfrak p)\xrightarrow{\bar\chi}\Omega$; under the identification $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=\kappa(y)$ (the open immersion $\iota$ induces an isomorphism of local rings $A_{\mathfrak p}=\mathcal O_{U,y}\cong\mathcal O_{Y,y}$, [def-weil-open-immersion], [def-weil-residue-field]) $\bar\chi$ is the field map $\kappa(y)\to\Omega$ of $\bar x$ [def-weil-geometric-point]. Hence $\kappa(\bar x)$ is the separable algebraic closure of $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ in $\Omega$ via $\bar\chi$ [def-weil-pbc-relative-separable-closure]. Throughout, a morphism $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to\operatorname{Spec}B$ is $\operatorname{Spec}\psi$ for a unique ring map $\psi:B\to\Omega$, its image point is the prime $\ker\psi$, and $\psi$ factors uniquely as $B\to\kappa(\ker\psi)\xrightarrow{\bar\psi}\Omega$ ($B/\ker\psi\to\Omega$ is an injective map of a domain into a field, which extends uniquely to its fraction field); composition with $\operatorname{Spec}\beta$ corresponds to $\psi\mapsto\psi\circ\beta$.

**Step 2.** *Shrinking.* Let $\mathcal N^{\mathrm{all}}$ be the category of all pointed etale neighbourhoods $(V,\bar v)$ of $\bar x$ [def-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods] ($V\to Y$ etale, $\bar v$ over $\bar x$, morphisms the $Y$-morphisms respecting the points); $\mathcal N$ is its full subcategory of affine objects. For every $(V,\bar v)$ in $\mathcal N^{\mathrm{all}}$ with image point $v$ and every open $W\subset V$ containing $v$, [lem-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhood-affine-shrink] gives an affine $(V_0=\operatorname{Spec}R,\bar v_0)$ in $\mathcal N$ and a morphism $j:(V_0,\bar v_0)\to(V,\bar v)$ with $j$ an open immersion and $j(V_0)\subset W$. With $W=V$: every object of $\mathcal N^{\mathrm{all}}$ receives a morphism from an object of $\mathcal N$.

**Step 3.** *Part (1): $\mathcal N$ is cofiltered.* By [lem-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods-cofiltered] (Stacks 03PQ: the fibre product $V_1\times_YV_2$ with the point $(\bar v_1,\bar v_2)$, and for $h_1,h_2:(V,\bar v)\rightrightarrows(V',\bar v')$ the etale neighbourhood $V''=V'\times_{\Delta,V'\times_YV',(h_1,h_2)}V$) the category $\mathcal N^{\mathrm{all}}$ is cofiltered. $\mathcal N$ is a full subcategory of $\mathcal N^{\mathrm{all}}$ and by the previous step every object of $\mathcal N^{\mathrm{all}}$ receives a morphism from an object of $\mathcal N$; so by [lem-weil-pbc-colimit-initial-full-subcategory] (1) $\mathcal N$ is cofiltered. Concretely: $\mathcal N$ is nonempty (shrink $(Y,\bar x)$); for two objects of $\mathcal N$ shrink $(V_1\times_YV_2,(\bar v_1,\bar v_2))$, which need not be affine when $Y$ is not separated, to an affine object and compose with the projections; for two parallel morphisms of $\mathcal N$ shrink the equalizing $(V'',\bar v'')$ of 03PQ (2) and compose; morphisms of $\mathcal N^{\mathrm{all}}$ between objects of $\mathcal N$ are morphisms of $\mathcal N$ (fullness).

**Step 4.** *Part (2): $\mathcal N_U$ is initial, and the colimit restricts.* Let $(V,\bar v)$ be in $\mathcal N$ with structure map $f:V\to Y$ and image point $v$; then $f(v)=y\in U$, so $W=f^{-1}(U)$ is an open of $V$ containing $v$. The shrinking step gives $j:(V_0,\bar v_0)\to(V,\bar v)$ with $V_0$ affine and $j(V_0)\subset W$, so $f\circ j$ has image in $\iota(U)$ and factors through the open immersion $\iota$ [def-weil-open-immersion]: $(V_0,\bar v_0)$ lies in $\mathcal N_U$. So every object of $\mathcal N$ receives a morphism from an object of the full subcategory $\mathcal N_U$. As $\mathcal N$ is cofiltered (part (1)), [lem-weil-pbc-colimit-initial-full-subcategory] (1) says that $\mathcal N_U$ is cofiltered and initial in $\mathcal N$, and that for the diagram $(V,\bar v)\mapsto A_V=\Gamma(V,\mathcal O_V)$ on $\mathcal N^{\mathrm{op}}$ (with the pullback maps $\Gamma(h):A_V\to A_{V'}$) the canonical map $\varinjlim_{\mathcal N_U}A_V\to\varinjlim_{\mathcal N}A_V$ [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit] is an isomorphism, either colimit existing with the other (both exist: filtered colimits of commutative rings exist).

**Step 5.** *Part (3), objects: the triple of an object of $\mathcal N_U$.* Let $(V=\operatorname{Spec}B,\bar v)$ be in $\mathcal N_U$, with $f=\iota\circ g$, $g:V\to U$. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (1) $\iota$ is etale, so by (3) $g$ is etale; by [def-weil-affine-spectrum] $g=\operatorname{Spec}\alpha$ for a unique ring map $\alpha:A\to B$, which makes $B$ an $A$-algebra with $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ etale. Write $\bar v=\operatorname{Spec}\psi_v$ with $\psi_v:B\to\Omega$, $\mathfrak q_v=\ker\psi_v$ (the prime of the image point $v$) and $\phi_v=\bar\psi_v:\kappa(\mathfrak q_v)\to\Omega$ (step 1; this is the field map of $\bar v$ of [def-weil-geometric-point] under $\kappa(v)=\kappa(\mathfrak q_v)$). The condition $f\circ\bar v=\bar x$ is $\iota\circ g\circ\bar v=\iota\circ\bar x_U$, i.e. ($\iota$ is a monomorphism) $g\circ\bar v=\bar x_U$, i.e. $\psi_v\circ\alpha=\chi$. Taking kernels, $\alpha^{-1}(\mathfrak q_v)=\ker\chi=\mathfrak p$, so $\mathfrak q_v$ lies over $\mathfrak p$; passing to residue fields, $\phi_v\circ\kappa(\alpha)=\bar\chi$, i.e. $\phi_v$ is a $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebra map. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-algebra-residue-field-separable] every $z\in\kappa(\mathfrak q_v)$ is a root of a separable polynomial $P$ over $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, so $\phi_v(z)$ is a root of the separable polynomial $\bar\chi(P)$ over $\bar\chi(\kappa(\mathfrak p))$: $\phi_v(z)\in\kappa(\bar x)$ (step 1, [def-weil-pbc-relative-separable-closure]). So $(B,\mathfrak q_v,\phi_v)$, with $\phi_v$ viewed as a map to $\kappa(\bar x)$, is an object of $\mathcal T$; conversely the condition $\psi_v\circ\alpha=\chi$ holds for any $\psi:B\to\Omega$ whose kernel lies over $\mathfrak p$ and whose residue map is a $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebra map, because $\chi$ and $\psi\circ\alpha$ both factor through $A\to\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ and agree after it.

**Step 6.** *Part (3), morphisms: full faithfulness.* Let $(V'=\operatorname{Spec}B',\bar v')$, $(V=\operatorname{Spec}B,\bar v)$ be in $\mathcal N_U$ with $f'=\iota\circ g'$, $f=\iota\circ g$. A $Y$-morphism $h:V'\to V$ satisfies $\iota\circ g\circ h=f\circ h=f'=\iota\circ g'$, hence $g\circ h=g'$ since $\iota$ is a monomorphism (an open immersion, [def-weil-open-immersion]); conversely every $U$-morphism is a $Y$-morphism. By [def-weil-affine-spectrum] the $U$-morphisms $h$ are the $\operatorname{Spec}\beta$ for the $A$-algebra maps $\beta:B\to B'$, bijectively and compatibly with composition. The condition $h\circ\bar v'=\bar v$ reads $\psi_{v'}\circ\beta=\psi_v$ (step 1), which holds iff $\beta^{-1}(\mathfrak q_{v'})=\mathfrak q_v$ and $\phi_{v'}\circ\kappa(\beta)=\phi_v$: the kernel of $\psi_{v'}\circ\beta$ is $\beta^{-1}(\mathfrak q_{v'})$, and the factorization of $\psi_{v'}\circ\beta$ through its residue field is $\phi_{v'}\circ\kappa(\beta)$ (uniqueness of the factorization in step 1). Hence $(V,\bar v)\mapsto(B,\mathfrak q_v,\phi_v)$, $\operatorname{Spec}\beta\mapsto\beta$ is a contravariant functor $\mathcal N_U\to\mathcal T$ (a functor $\mathcal N_U\to\mathcal T^{\mathrm{op}}$) which is bijective on morphism sets.

**Step 7.** *Part (3), essential surjectivity.* Let $(B,\mathfrak q,\phi)$ be in $\mathcal T$, with structure map $\alpha:A\to B$. Put $V=\operatorname{Spec}B$, $g=\operatorname{Spec}\alpha$ (etale by hypothesis) and $f=\iota\circ g$, etale by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (1),(2). Let $\psi:B\to B/\mathfrak q\to\kappa(\mathfrak q)\xrightarrow{\phi}\kappa(\bar x)\subset\Omega$ and $\bar v=\operatorname{Spec}\psi$; then $\ker\psi=\mathfrak q$ ($\phi$ is injective, a map of fields) and $\bar\psi=\phi$. Since $\mathfrak q$ lies over $\mathfrak p$ and $\phi$ is a $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebra map, $\psi\circ\alpha=\chi$ (end of the objects step), i.e. $f\circ\bar v=\bar x$. So $(V,\bar v)$ is an object of $\mathcal N_U$ and its triple is exactly $(B,\mathfrak q,\phi)$. Together with full faithfulness, the functor $\mathcal N_U\to\mathcal T^{\mathrm{op}}$ is an equivalence (indeed surjective on objects).

**Step 8.** *The colimits.* For $(V=\operatorname{Spec}B,\bar v)$ in $\mathcal N_U$ one has $A_V=\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}B,\mathcal O)=B$, and for $h=\operatorname{Spec}\beta$ the map $\Gamma(h)$ is $\beta$ [def-weil-affine-spectrum]. So the diagram $A_\bullet$ on $\mathcal N_U^{\mathrm{op}}$ is, through the equivalence $\mathcal N_U^{\mathrm{op}}\simeq\mathcal T$ of the previous steps, isomorphic to the diagram $(B,\mathfrak q,\phi)\mapsto B$ on $\mathcal T$. By [lem-weil-pbc-colimit-initial-full-subcategory] (2) $\varinjlim_{\mathcal N_U}A_V\cong\varinjlim_{\mathcal T}B$, the coprojection of $(V,\bar v)$ going to that of its triple; composing with part (2), $\varinjlim_{\mathcal N}A_V=\varinjlim_{(B,\mathfrak q,\phi)\in\mathcal T}B$ canonically, compatibly with the maps from each $A_V$, $(V,\bar v)\in\mathcal N_U$ [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit].
