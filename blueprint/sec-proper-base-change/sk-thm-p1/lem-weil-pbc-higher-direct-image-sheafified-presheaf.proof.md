Choose the Mathlib injective resolution R of F. Abelian sheaves on the small etale site form a Grothendieck abelian category `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isGrothendieckAbelian_sheaf_smallEtaleTopology` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/AffineEtale.lean)), hence have enough injectives [lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives]. Put f=p_*, K=f(R.cocomplex) and L=iota(K), where iota is inclusion into presheaves. InjectiveResolution.isoRightDerivedObj computes the classical derived functor using R [def-sp-injective-resolutions]; [def-sp-left-exact-additive]; [lem-sp-derived-well-defined]. Functor.homologyRightDerivedFunctorPlusObjSingleFunctorIso identifies the total bounded-below derived value on F[0] with the classical right-derived value [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. Thus no separate choice or comparison of arbitrary K-injective resolutions is needed. ([def-sp-injective-resolutions], [def-sp-left-exact-additive], [lem-sp-derived-well-defined]).

For V in S_et, evaluation of L at V is definitionally evaluation of R.cocomplex at the base-change object X times_S V, since p_* is precomposition by the base-change functor [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]; `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.instPreservesFiniteLimitsTopPullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/MorphismProperty.lean)). The same definitional identification holds for differentials and restriction maps. ([def-weil-pbc-direct-image], `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.instPreservesFiniteLimitsTopPullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/MorphismProperty.lean))).

Construct an isomorphism eP from the specified presheaf P_q to the presheaf homology H^q(L). Its component at V is R.isoRightDerivedObj for evaluation at X times_S V followed by ShortComplex.mapHomologyIso for evaluation at V. Evaluation preserves limits and colimits pointwise in a functor category, hence preserves homology `CategoryTheory.Functor.homologyFunctorIso` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/PreservesHomology.lean)). For a restriction g, rewrite its derived map by InjectiveResolution.rightDerived_app_eq [lem-sp-derived-well-defined], cancel the resolution comparison isomorphism, and apply ShortComplex.homologyMap_mapNatTrans to the evaluation natural transformation. This proves naturality of eP, including the specified derived restriction maps.

Sheafification a is exact `CategoryTheory.presheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean)); `CategoryTheory.instPreservesFiniteLimitsFunctorOppositeSheafPresheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean)). The inverse of sheafificationNatIso gives a termwise natural isomorphism a(iota(K)) to K; lift it to complexes with Functor.mapHomologicalComplexCompIso and Functor.mapHomologicalComplexIdIso, obtaining eK. ShortComplex.mapHomologyIso for a identifies a(H^q(L)) with H^q(a(L)) `CategoryTheory.Functor.homologyFunctorIso` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/PreservesHomology.lean)). Mapping eK under homology then identifies this with H^q(K). (`CategoryTheory.presheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean)), `CategoryTheory.instPreservesFiniteLimitsFunctorOppositeSheafPresheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean))).

Compose a.mapIso eP, the inverse homology comparison for a, homology applied to eK, the inverse of R.isoRightDerivedObj f q, and the inverse of f.homologyRightDerivedFunctorPlusObjSingleFunctorIso F q. This is the required isomorphism from the sheafification of P_q to H^q(Rp_*(F[0])). All components are the canonical resolution or homology comparisons; the restriction naturality was verified in step 3.
