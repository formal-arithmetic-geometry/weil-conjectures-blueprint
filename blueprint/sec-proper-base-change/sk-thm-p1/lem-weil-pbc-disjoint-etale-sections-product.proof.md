A family of maps from an arbitrary test object E to G(W_i) is compatible on overlaps. For identical indices compatibility follows by the monomorphism cancellation property. For distinct indices the overlap has empty underlying space by disjointness; G has terminal sections there, by the empty-cover sheaf condition.

The sheaf condition existsUnique_amalgamation_ofArrows gives a unique section map E -> G(V) with these restrictions. Hence the restriction map induces a bijection on morphisms out of every E. Apply isIso_of_yoneda_map_bijective, giving the asserted isomorphism.
