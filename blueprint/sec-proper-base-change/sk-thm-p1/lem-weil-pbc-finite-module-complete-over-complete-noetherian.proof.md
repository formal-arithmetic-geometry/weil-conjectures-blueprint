Write $R^\wedge=\varprojlim_nR/J^n$ and $\iota_R:R\to R^\wedge$, $a\mapsto(a\bmod J^n)_n$. By hypothesis ($R$ is $J$-adically complete) $\iota_R$ is bijective; it is a ring map, hence $R$-linear, and so is its inverse $\iota_R^{-1}$. Base change along the $R$-linear maps $\iota_R$ and $\iota_R^{-1}$ gives $R$-linear maps $\iota_R\otimes\mathrm{id}_N:R\otimes_RN\to R^\wedge\otimes_RN$ and $\iota_R^{-1}\otimes\mathrm{id}_N$ in the other direction, inverse to each other by functoriality of $-\otimes_RN$ ($(\iota_R^{-1}\otimes\mathrm{id})\circ(\iota_R\otimes\mathrm{id})=(\iota_R^{-1}\iota_R)\otimes\mathrm{id}=\mathrm{id}$ and symmetrically). Hence $\beta:N\to R^\wedge\otimes_RN$, $x\mapsto1\otimes x$, the composite of the isomorphism $N\cong R\otimes_RN$, $x\mapsto1\otimes x$, with $\iota_R\otimes\mathrm{id}_N$ (note $\iota_R(1)=1$), is bijective. This is the identification $M=M\otimes_RR=M\otimes_RR^\wedge$ in the proof of Stacks 031B.

Let $N^\wedge=\varprojlim_nN/J^nN$ with $\iota_N:N\to N^\wedge$, $x\mapsto(x\bmod J^nN)_n$, and let $c_N:R^\wedge\otimes_RN\to N^\wedge$ be the map $a\otimes x\mapsto a\cdot\iota_N(x)$. Since $R$ is Noetherian and $N$ is a finitely generated $R$-module, $c_N$ is bijective by (2) (Stacks 00MA (3), with $A=R$, $I=J$, $P=N$).

For $x\in N$, $c_N(\beta(x))=c_N(1\otimes x)=1\cdot\iota_N(x)=\iota_N(x)$, so $\iota_N=c_N\circ\beta$ is a composite of two bijections (steps 1 and 2) and is bijective: the canonical map $N\to\varprojlim_nN/J^nN$ is bijective, i.e. $N$ is $J$-adically complete. (In Stacks 031B surjectivity of this map comes from Lemma 0315 and injectivity from $\bigcap_nJ^nN=0$; over a Noetherian $R$ both are contained in the bijectivity of $c_N$, so neither Lemma 0315 nor the Krull intersection theorem with Lemma 05GI is needed.).
