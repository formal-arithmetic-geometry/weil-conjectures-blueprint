Let I be a nonempty directed preorder, D:I^op -> Sch an arbitrary inverse diagram of schemes [def-pc-scheme], and c a cone with projections f_i:c.pt -> D_i `CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean)). Let F_i be abelian etale sheaves `CategoryTheory.Sheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheaf.lean)) and phi_f:D(f^op)^*F_i -> F_j for f:i -> j, satisfying the canonical inverse-image identity and composition equations [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image]. Let G:I -> Ab(c.pt_et) have specified isomorphisms eG_i:G_i ≅ f_i^*F_i, with G(f) equal to conjugation by eG of the inverse-image composition isomorphism followed by f_j^*(phi_f). Let L be ANY cocone of G, not necessarily a colimit. For each n>=0 there exist a functor H:I -> Ab with isomorphisms eH_i:H_i ≅ H^n(D_i,F_i) [def-weil-pbc-etale-cohomology] whose arrows are conjugate to H^n(phi_f) after cohomology pullback D(f^op)^* [def-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback]. There exist a cocone B of H and an isomorphism eB:B.pt ≅ H^n(c.pt,L.pt) such that B_i followed by eB is eH_i followed by f_i^* and H^n((eG_i)^-1 followed by L_i). No scheme limit, qcqs condition, affine transitions, exactness or connecting morphism is assumed or asserted.
