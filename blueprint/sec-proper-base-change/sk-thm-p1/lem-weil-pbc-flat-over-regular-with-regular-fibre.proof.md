The regular closed-fibre hypothesis supplies its Noetherian and local instances `IsRegularLocalRing, IsRegularRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean)). Write I = m_R S. Locality gives comap(f,m_S) = m_R and I ≤ m_S. The surjective quotient map q : S → S/I sends m_S to the unique maximal ideal of the closed fibre. This uses the standard local-ring map and quotient APIs, not a separate Noetherian-image theorem. (`IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean))).

Equip S with the R-algebra structure from f. Flatness `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)) supplies going down. The comap equality makes m_S lie over m_R. Apply `Ideal.height_eq_height_add_of_liesOver_of_hasGoingDown` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/KrullsHeightTheorem.lean)), Mathlib Ideal.height_eq_height_add_of_liesOver_of_hasGoingDown, to these two maximal ideals. Rewrite the quotient image using IsLocalRing.map_maximalIdeal_of_surjective, and rewrite all maximal-ideal heights as Krull dimensions using IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal_height_eq_ringKrullDim. This yields dim S = dim R + dim(S/I), directly without localizing. (`IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean))).

Choose finite sets x and y generating m_R and m_(S/I), of cardinalities their respective spanFinranks, using Noetherianity `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)). Lift y along q to a finite subset z of m_S, with q injective on z and image y. Set t = f(x) ∪ z. Its span is I ∨ span(z), so it contains I. Its image under q is span(y) = q(m_S). Equality of ideal images under a surjection gives span(t) ∨ ker(q) = m_S ∨ ker(q); ker(q) = I is contained in both, so span(t) = m_S. Finset cardinal bounds and injectivity on z give spanFinrank(m_S) ≤ card(t) ≤ spanFinrank(m_R) + spanFinrank(m_(S/I)).

Regularity of R and S/I identifies their maximal-ideal spanFinranks with their Krull dimensions `IsRegularLocalRing, IsRegularRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean)). Cast the finite generator bound into WithBot ENat, and use the dimension formula from step 2 to obtain spanFinrank(m_S) ≤ dim S. Apply Mathlib IsRegularLocalRing.of_spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_le, whose reverse inequality is Krull's height theorem, to obtain IsRegularLocalRing S. The finite-set construction handles empty generating sets without a separate positive-dimension assumption.
