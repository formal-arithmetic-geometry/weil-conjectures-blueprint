Construct the base-changed polynomial map by the tensor-product map and the canonical polynomial/tensor-product ring equivalence. Right exactness gives surjectivity and identifies its kernel with the extension of the original kernel. The square commutes on coefficients and variables. Use Algebra.TensorProduct.map_surjective, MvPolynomial.algebraTensorAlgEquiv, and Algebra.TensorProduct.rTensor_ker directly; Algebra.Presentation.baseChange is not invoked. (`MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean))).

The two quotient rings by the extended prime ideals are isomorphic, carrying the induced primes to one another, so their heights agree by RingEquiv.height_map. Both vertical maps are flat and all rings are Noetherian; apply Ideal.height_eq_height_add_of_liesOver_of_hasGoingDown to both maps and reassociate addition. Flatness is inferred from Module.Flat tensor-product stability over a field, via RingHom.flat_algebraMap_iff; faithful flatness is not needed. Noetherianity uses polynomial-ring instances, Algebra.FiniteType.isNoetherianRing, and isNoetherianRing_of_surjective. (`MvPolynomial.isRegularRing_of_isRegularRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Polynomial.lean)), `Ideal.height_eq_height_add_of_liesOver_of_hasGoingDown` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/KrullsHeightTheorem.lean)), `Algebra.FiniteType.isNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/FiniteType.lean))).
