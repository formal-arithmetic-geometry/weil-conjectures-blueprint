id = "lem-weil-pbc-finite-subalgebra-fraction-field"
kind = "lemma"
title = "A finite field extension admits a finite algebra model over the power-series polynomial ring"
lean = ["MvPolynomial.exists_finite_subalgebra_fraction_field"]
+++
Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$, let $n,m\ge0$ and let $A=k[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]][y_1,\ldots,y_m]$ be the polynomial ring `Polynomial` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Polynomial/Basic.lean)) in $y_1,\ldots,y_m$ over the formal power series ring $k[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]]$ `MvPowerSeries` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/Basic.lean)). Then $A$ is an integral domain; let $K=A_{(0)}$ be its fraction field `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)), and let $M/K$ be a finite field extension `Algebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Defs.lean)), i.e. $M$ is a finitely generated $K$-module `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)). Then there is a subring $B\subseteq M$ containing $A$ which is a finitely generated $A$-module `Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)) and whose fraction field is $M$ (every element of $M$ is $b/s$ with $b,s\in B$, $s\neq0$).
