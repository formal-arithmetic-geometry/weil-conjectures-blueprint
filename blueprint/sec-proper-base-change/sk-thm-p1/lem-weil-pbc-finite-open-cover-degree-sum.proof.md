Put B_i = Γ(W_i, O), with its k-algebra structure induced by t_i. The Mathlib declarations realizing `AlgebraicGeometry.isAffine_of_isAffineHom, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isoSpec, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.finite_appTop, RingHom.finite_algebraMap` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineScheme.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Affine.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Finite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Defs.lean)) give IsAffine W_i and finiteness of t_i.appTop. Precomposing with the inverse of ΓSpecIso k preserves finiteness, so Module.Finite k B_i. For the field k, its prime spectrum is discrete. Algebra.QuasiFinite.discreteTopology_primeSpectrum specializes the Mathlib leaf `Algebra.QuasiFinite.discreteTopology_primeSpectrum` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/QuasiFinite/Basic.lean)) to B_i; compactness of Spec B_i then gives finitely many points. Transport both properties along W_i.isoSpec.hom.homeomorph. In Lean these facts are established before constructing the global restriction map.

The open-cover equation yields the set equation union_i W_i = T. Set.finite_iUnion applied to the finite underlying sets of step 1 gives Finite T. For x in T, choose a covering W_i containing x. Its singleton is open by discreteness of W_i, and its image under the open embedding W_i -> T is the singleton {x}; hence DiscreteTopology T. With the empty index set the cover equation forces T empty, and the same union argument applies. ([def-pc-scheme]).

*$T$ is affine.* By step 2 and `AlgebraicGeometry.instIsAffineOfFiniteOfDiscreteTopologyCarrierCarrierCommRingCat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Limits.lean)), $T$ is affine: with $A=\Gamma(T,\mathcal O_T)$ the canonical morphism $T\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is an isomorphism. Make $A$ a $k$-algebra through the ring map $t^\sharp:k=\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}k,\mathcal O)\to A$ induced by $t$ `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)).

Equip A = Γ(T,O) with t.ΓAlgebra and each B_i with t_i.ΓAlgebra. Define the algebra homomorphism rho_i using the ring homomorphism (W_i).ι.appTop. Its scalar compatibility is exactly Scheme.Hom.comp_appTop. LinearMap.pi packages these into a k-linear restriction map f : A -> product_i B_i. If f(a)=f(b), congrFun gives equality of restrictions on every W_i. Apply TopCat.Sheaf.eq_of_locally_eq' to T.sheaf and the cover to conclude a=b. The product is a finite k-module by step 1, so Module.Finite.of_injective gives Module.Finite k A. LinearMap.finrank_le_finrank_of_injective and Module.finrank_pi_fintype give dim_k A <= sum_i dim_k B_i <= sum_i n_i. Sheaf locality and the empty product also cover m=0. ([def-pc-scheme]).

Apply the affine-target specialization of `AlgebraicGeometry.HasAffineProperty.iff_of_iSup_eq_top, AlgebraicGeometry.IsZariskiLocalAtTarget.iff_of_iSup_eq_top, AlgebraicGeometry.IsZariskiLocalAtTarget.restrict` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Basic.lean)), namely HasAffineProperty.iff_of_isAffine with P = IsFinite. Its first requirement is IsAffine T from step 3. Its second is finiteness of t.appTop. RingHom.finite_algebraMap translates Module.Finite k A from step 4 into finiteness of ((ΓSpecIso k).inv >> t.appTop).hom. Cancel the ring isomorphism ΓSpecIso k on the left using RingHom.finite_respectsIso.cancel_left_isIso to obtain finiteness of t.appTop. Pair the resulting IsFinite t with the dimension estimate of step 4.
