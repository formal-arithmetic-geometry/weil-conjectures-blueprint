Use HasRingHomProperty.Spec_iff to translate the hypothesis and conclusion to RingHom.Etale. Let G be the set of s in B for which A -> B_s is etale, and J = Ideal.span G. If a prime P contains both b and J, the hypothesis supplies s in G outside P, contradicting Ideal.subset_span and J <= P. Thus b and J are coprime by Ideal.coprime_of_no_prime_ge, the ideal-theoretic version of `PrimeSpectrum.zeroLocus_sup, PrimeSpectrum.zeroLocus_empty_iff_eq_top (element form: Ideal.isCoprime_tfae)` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/Basic.lean)).

IsCoprime.exists gives f in b and g in J with f + g = 1. Applying the quotient map shows that g maps to 1, hence to a unit.

Set T = B_g. The image of g is a unit and belongs to Ideal.map (algebraMap B T) J. Therefore this mapped ideal is top. Ideal.map_span identifies it with the ideal spanned by the images in T of G. This is the unit-ideal condition for the principal cover in `PrimeSpectrum.iSup_basicOpen_eq_top_iff, Ideal.span_singleton_eq_top, PrimeSpectrum.compactSpace` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/Topology.lean)); the Lean proof uses its ring-side form directly rather than constructing a scheme cover. The argument also applies when T is the zero ring.

For s in G, put C = T_(s/1). Give C its B_s-algebra structure using IsLocalization.Away.map. The localization map identity supplies IsScalarTower B B_s C. IsLocalization.Away.commutes then proves that C is the localization of B_s away from the image of g. Consequently A -> C is etale: principal localization is etale `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isOpenImmersion_SpecMap_localizationAway` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)) and etale maps compose `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.etale_comp, instance [IsOpenImmersion f] : Etale f` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)). Mathlib packages these two facts in RingHom.Etale.propertyIsLocal.StableUnderCompositionWithLocalizationAwayTarget. Rewrite the ring map identity to obtain the etale localization of A -> T at s/1.

Apply RingHom.Etale.ofLocalizationSpanTarget to the spanning set of step 3 and the etale localized maps of step 4. This is the affine ring-side form of `AlgebraicGeometry.IsZariskiLocalAtSource.iff_of_iSup_eq_top` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Basic.lean)). Translate back with HasRingHomProperty.Spec_iff, and combine with the quotient-unit assertion of step 2.
