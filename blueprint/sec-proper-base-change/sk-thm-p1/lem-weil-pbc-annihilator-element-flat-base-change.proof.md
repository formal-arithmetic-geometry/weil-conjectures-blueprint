Put $I=\mathrm{Ann}_R(x)$ and let $\mu_x:R\to R$, $r\mapsto rx$, be multiplication by $x$, an $R$-linear map. By definition of the annihilator, $\ker\mu_x=I$, so with $\iota:I\hookrightarrow R$ the inclusion the sequence $0\to I\xrightarrow{\iota}R\xrightarrow{\mu_x}R$ of $R$-modules is exact.

Since $\varphi$ is flat, $S$ is a flat $R$-module `Module.Flat, RingHom.Flat` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)), so tensoring the exact sequence of Step 1 with $S$ gives an exact sequence $0\to I\otimes_RS\xrightarrow{\iota\otimes1}R\otimes_RS\xrightarrow{\mu_x\otimes1}R\otimes_RS$ `Module.Flat.rTensor_exact, Module.Flat.lTensor_exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean)). Identify $R\otimes_RS=S$ by the $S$-linear isomorphism $r\otimes s\mapsto\varphi(r)s$. Under it $\mu_x\otimes1$ becomes $s\mapsto\varphi(x)s$ (as $rx\otimes s\mapsto\varphi(r)\varphi(x)s$), i.e. multiplication by $\varphi(x)$ on $S$, and $\iota\otimes1$ becomes the map $\alpha:I\otimes_RS\to S$, $a\otimes s\mapsto\varphi(a)s$. Exactness gives $\operatorname{im}\alpha=\ker(\cdot\varphi(x):S\to S)=\mathrm{Ann}_S(\varphi(x))$.

The image of $\alpha$ consists of the finite sums $\sum_i\varphi(a_i)s_i$ with $a_i\in I$, $s_i\in S$, which is exactly the ideal $IS$ of $S$ generated by $\varphi(I)$. Combined with Step 2, $\mathrm{Ann}_S(\varphi(x))=\mathrm{Ann}_R(x)S$.
