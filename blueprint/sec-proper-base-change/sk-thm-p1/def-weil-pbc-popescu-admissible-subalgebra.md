Let $k$ be a field, $B$ a commutative $k$-algebra, and $\mathfrak m\subset B$ an ideal. A $k$-subalgebra $A\subset B$ is *admissible relative to $\mathfrak m$* if it is an Artinian local ring, essentially of finite type over $k$, its inclusion $A\to B$ is flat, and $\mathfrak m_A B=\mathfrak m$, where $\mathfrak m_A$ is the maximal ideal of $A$.
