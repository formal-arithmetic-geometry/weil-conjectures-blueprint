*The objects and the morphism $\Gamma_\varphi$.* Since $V\to X$ is etale and $\pi:U\to X$ is etale (it is finite etale `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over, CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/MorphismProperty/Comma.lean))), `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.instPreservesFiniteLimitsTopPullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/MorphismProperty.lean)) (1) applied to $u=\pi$ and the object $V\to X$ of $X_{\Et}$ shows that $q_V:V_U=V\times_XU\to U$ is etale, so $(V_U,q_V)$ is an object of $U_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site]; applied to $u=(V\to X)$ and the object $\pi$ of $X_{\Et}$ it shows that $p_V:V_U\to V$ is etale. The $X$-morphism $\varphi:V\to U$ is a morphism between two schemes etale over $X$ commuting with the structure morphisms, hence etale by `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.etale_comp, instance [IsOpenImmersion f] : Etale f` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)) (3); so $(V,\varphi)$ is an object of $U_{\Et}$. The graph $\Gamma_\varphi=(\mathrm{id}_V,\varphi)$ exists by the universal property of $V\times_XU$ `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)), since $(V\to X)\circ\mathrm{id}_V=\pi\circ\varphi$ ($\varphi$ is an $X$-morphism); it satisfies $p_V\circ\Gamma_\varphi=\mathrm{id}_V$ and $q_V\circ\Gamma_\varphi=\varphi$. The second identity says that $\Gamma_\varphi$ is a $U$-morphism $(V,\varphi)\to(V_U,q_V)$, i.e. a morphism of $U_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site]. This is the setting of the proof of Stacks 0F4L ('Evaluating both sides on $V\to X$ etale ... $\Gamma_\varphi=(1,\varphi):V\to V\times_XU$ over $U$').

*$\Gamma_\varphi$ is an open and closed immersion (clause 1).* The projection $p_V:V_U\to V$ is etale (step 1) and separated: it is the base change of $\pi$ along $V\to X$, and $\pi$ is finite, so $p_V$ is finite by `AlgebraicGeometry.IsFinite (instance IsStableUnderBaseChange @IsFinite), AlgebraicGeometry.isAffine_of_isAffineHom, AlgebraicGeometry.IsProper (instances for IsFinite f and f ≫ g)` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Affine.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Finite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Proper.lean)) (a); a finite morphism is affine by definition `AlgebraicGeometry.IsFinite, AlgebraicGeometry.IsIntegralHom` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Finite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Integral.lean)), and an affine morphism is separated by `AlgebraicGeometry.isAffineHom_isStableUnderBaseChange, AlgebraicGeometry.isAffine_of_isAffineHom, AlgebraicGeometry.instQuasiCompactOfIsAffineHom, AlgebraicGeometry.IsSeparated.of_isAffineHom, instance IsAffine (∐ g) for finite σ (Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Limits.lean:651)` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Affine.lean)) (3). By step 1, $\Gamma_\varphi$ is a section of $p_V$: $p_V\circ\Gamma_\varphi=\mathrm{id}_V$. Hence `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.of_flat_of_mono, AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion.of_comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FlatMono.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Separated.lean)) (for the etale separated $p_V:V_U\to V$ and its section $\Gamma_\varphi$) gives: $\Gamma_\varphi$ is an open immersion `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)) and a closed immersion `AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/ClosedImmersion.lean)), so $O:=\Gamma_\varphi(V)$ is open and closed in $V_U$ and $\Gamma_\varphi=j_O\circ\gamma$ with $j_O:O\to V_U$ the inclusion of the open subscheme and $\gamma:V\to O$ an isomorphism of schemes, whose inverse is $p_V\circ j_O$ (from $p_V\circ j_O\circ\gamma=\mathrm{id}_V$). In particular $\Gamma_\varphi\circ p_V\circ j_O=j_O\circ\gamma\circ\gamma^{-1}=j_O$. This is Stacks 0F4L ('It is open as it is a morphism between schemes etale over $U$ and it is closed as it is a section of a scheme separated over $V$'), with closedness from Schemes Lemma 01KT (schemes-lemma-section-immersion: a section of a separated morphism is a closed immersion), which is clause (3) of `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.of_flat_of_mono, AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion.of_comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FlatMono.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Separated.lean)); openness is clause (2) there (the section is etale, flat and a monomorphism), equivalent to Stacks' argument ($\Gamma_\varphi$ is a $U$-morphism between etale $U$-schemes, hence etale, hence open).

*Sections over $V_U$ split along $O$ and its complement.* Let $W=V_U\setminus O$, open (as $O$ is closed), with inclusion $j_W:W\to V_U$ of the open subscheme; $j_W$ and $\Gamma_\varphi$ are open immersions, hence etale `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.etale_comp, instance [IsOpenImmersion f] : Etale f` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean)) (1), and $(W,q_V\circ j_W)$ is an object of $U_{\Et}$ (composite of etale morphisms, ibid. (2)). As $O\cup W=V_U$, the family $\{\Gamma_\varphi:(V,\varphi)\to(V_U,q_V),\ j_W:(W,q_Vj_W)\to(V_U,q_V)\}$ is a covering of $U_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], whose fibre products are scheme fibre products (ibid.). (i) An empty scheme $\varnothing$ with its unique morphism to $U$ (an open immersion onto the empty open, hence etale) is an object of $U_{\Et}$ covered by the empty family; the sheaf condition `CategoryTheory.Sheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheaf.lean)) for it says $M(\varnothing)\to$ (empty product) is injective, so $M(\varnothing)=0$. (ii) If $T$ is a scheme with $a:T\to V$, $b:T\to W$ and $\Gamma_\varphi a=j_Wb$, every point of $T$ maps into $O\cap W=\varnothing$, so $T$ is empty; hence $V\times_{V_U}W$ and $W\times_{V_U}V$ are empty schemes `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)) and $M$ of them is $0$ by (i). (iii) $\Gamma_\varphi$ and $j_W$ are monomorphisms `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.mono` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)), so the two projections $V\times_{V_U}V\rightrightarrows V$ are equal (they become equal after composing with $\Gamma_\varphi$), and likewise for $W\times_{V_U}W$. The underlying presheaf of abelian groups of $M$ is an abelian sheaf on $U_{\Et}$ `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)), [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves], and its sheaf condition for this covering says that $\rho:M(V_U,q_V)\to M(V,\varphi)\times M(W),\ t\mapsto(M(\Gamma_\varphi)t,\,M(j_W)t)$ is injective with image the pairs whose two restrictions to each of the four fibre products agree; by (ii) and (iii) this holds for every pair, so $\rho$ is bijective. (`SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)), [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves]).

*The $\Lambda$-module structures.* For an object $T$ of $U_{\Et}$ let $\iota_T:\Lambda\to\Lambda_U(T)$ be the ring map sending $\lambda$ to the image of the constant section $\lambda$ under the canonical map from the constant presheaf to its sheafification $\Lambda_U$ `CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean)), `CategoryTheory.presheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean)); since the constant presheaf has identity restriction maps and the canonical map is a morphism of presheaves, every restriction map of $\Lambda_U$ sends $\iota_T(\lambda)$ to $\iota_{T'}(\lambda)$. $M(T)$ is a $\Lambda$-module through $\iota_T$, and by the semilinearity of the restriction maps of the $\Lambda_U$-module $M$ `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)), every $M(g)$, $g$ a morphism of $U_{\Et}$, is $\Lambda$-linear; every component $\alpha_T$ of a morphism $\alpha$ of sheaves of $\Lambda_U$-modules is $\Lambda$-linear. These are the $\Lambda$-module structures on $M(V,\varphi)$, on $(\pi_*M)(V)=M(V_U,q_V)$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image] and, summandwise, on $P_M(V)$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-sum-extension]; the restriction maps of $P_M$ and of $\pi_*M$ (the maps $M(a_U)$, step 6) are $\Lambda$-linear, so both are presheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on $X_{\Et}$. (`CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean)), `CategoryTheory.presheafToSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheafification.lean))).

*Clause 2.* By step 3, $\rho$ is a bijection, and it is $\Lambda$-linear because $M(\Gamma_\varphi)$ and $M(j_W)$ are (step 4); so $\rho^{-1}$ is $\Lambda$-linear. For $s\in M(V,\varphi)$ put $e_\varphi(s)=\rho^{-1}(s,0)$. The conditions '$M(\Gamma_\varphi)(e)=s$ and the restriction $M(j_W)(e)$ of $e$ to the open subscheme $W=V_U\setminus\Gamma_\varphi(V)$ is $0$' say exactly $\rho(e)=(s,0)$, so $e_\varphi(s)$ is the unique element satisfying them, and $e_\varphi=\rho^{-1}\circ(s\mapsto(s,0))$ is $\Lambda$-linear.

*Graphs and base change along $a$.* Let $a:V'\to V$ be a morphism of $X_{\Et}$, $\varphi:V\to U$ an $X$-morphism, $\varphi'=\varphi a$, and $a_U=a\times_X\mathrm{id}_U:V'_U\to V_U$, the morphism with $p_V a_U=a\,p_{V'}$ and $q_Va_U=q_{V'}$ `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)); it is a morphism $(V'_U,q_{V'})\to(V_U,q_V)$ of $U_{\Et}$, and $(\pi_*M)(a)=M(a_U)$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. Also $a$ is a morphism $(V',\varphi')\to(V,\varphi)$ of $U_{\Et}$. (i) $a_U\circ\Gamma_{\varphi'}=\Gamma_\varphi\circ a$: both have $p_V$-component $a$ and $q_V$-component $\varphi a$, so they agree by the uniqueness in `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)). Hence $\Gamma_{\varphi'}(V')\subseteq a_U^{-1}(O)$. (ii) Conversely let $O'=a_U^{-1}(O)$, open in $V'_U$, with inclusion $\iota:O'\to V'_U$. Since $a_U\iota$ maps into $O$ it factors as $j_O\circ h$ `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift_fac, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift_uniq` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)); by step 2, $a_U\iota=\Gamma_\varphi p_Vj_Oh=\Gamma_\varphi p_Va_U\iota=\Gamma_\varphi\,a\,p_{V'}\iota$. Therefore $q_{V'}\iota=q_Va_U\iota=q_V\Gamma_\varphi a p_{V'}\iota=\varphi a\,p_{V'}\iota=q_{V'}\Gamma_{\varphi'}p_{V'}\iota$ and $p_{V'}\iota=p_{V'}\Gamma_{\varphi'}p_{V'}\iota$, so $\iota=\Gamma_{\varphi'}\circ p_{V'}\circ\iota$ `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)), and $O'=\iota(O')\subseteq\Gamma_{\varphi'}(V')$. So $a_U^{-1}(\Gamma_\varphi(V))=\Gamma_{\varphi a}(V')$, the key point named in the statement; equivalently $a_U$ maps $W'=V'_U\setminus\Gamma_{\varphi'}(V')$ into $W=V_U\setminus\Gamma_\varphi(V)$, and $a_U\circ j_{W'}=j_W\circ b$ for a unique $b:W'\to W$ `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift_fac, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift_uniq` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)), a $U$-morphism ($q_Vj_Wb=q_Va_Uj_{W'}=q_{V'}j_{W'}$), hence a morphism of $U_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site].

*Extension by zero commutes with restriction.* With the notation of step 6 and $s\in M(V,\varphi)$, put $t=M(a_U)(e_\varphi(s))\in M(V'_U,q_{V'})$. By step 6 (i), $M(\Gamma_{\varphi'})(t)=M(a_U\Gamma_{\varphi'})(e_\varphi s)=M(\Gamma_\varphi a)(e_\varphi s)=M(a)(M(\Gamma_\varphi)e_\varphi s)=M(a)(s)$, and by step 6 (ii), $M(j_{W'})(t)=M(a_Uj_{W'})(e_\varphi s)=M(b)(M(j_W)e_\varphi s)=M(b)(0)=0$ ($M$ is a contravariant functor on $U_{\Et}$). By the uniqueness in clause 2 (step 5) for $(V',\varphi')$: $M(a_U)(e_\varphi(s))=e_{\varphi a}(M(a)(s))$.

*Clause 3: $c^p_M$ is a morphism of presheaves of $\Lambda$-modules.* $c^p_{M,V}((s_\varphi)_\varphi)=\sum_\varphi e_\varphi(s_\varphi)$ is a finite sum (an element of the direct sum has finitely many nonzero components) of $\Lambda$-linear maps (step 5), hence well defined and $\Lambda$-linear. For $a:V'\to V$ in $X_{\Et}$ the restriction of $P_M$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-sum-extension] sends $(s_\varphi)_\varphi$ to the family whose $\psi$-component is $\sum_{\varphi:\varphi a=\psi}M(a)(s_\varphi)$, and $(\pi_*M)(a)=M(a_U)$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. By step 7, $M(a_U)\big(\sum_\varphi e_\varphi(s_\varphi)\big)=\sum_\varphi e_{\varphi a}(M(a)s_\varphi)=\sum_\psi e_\psi\big(\sum_{\varphi a=\psi}M(a)s_\varphi\big)$, i.e. $(\pi_*M)(a)\circ c^p_{M,V}=c^p_{M,V'}\circ P_M(a)$. So $c^p_M:P_M\to\pi_*M$ is a morphism of presheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on $X_{\Et}$. This is the map $j_{p!}\mathcal F\to j_*\mathcal F$ of Stacks 0F4L for $j=\pi$.

*Naturality in $M$.* Let $\alpha:M\to M'$ be a morphism of sheaves of $\Lambda_U$-modules and $s\in M(V,\varphi)$. The components of $\alpha$ commute with the maps $M(g)$, $M'(g)$ `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)), so $M'(\Gamma_\varphi)(\alpha_{V_U}e_\varphi s)=\alpha_{(V,\varphi)}(M(\Gamma_\varphi)e_\varphi s)=\alpha_{(V,\varphi)}(s)$ and $M'(j_W)(\alpha_{V_U}e_\varphi s)=\alpha_W(0)=0$; by the uniqueness in clause 2 for $M'$, $\alpha_{V_U}(e_\varphi(s))=e'_\varphi(\alpha_{(V,\varphi)}(s))$. Summing over $\varphi$, and since $(\pi_*\alpha)_V=\alpha_{V_U}$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image] and $(P_\alpha)_V=\bigoplus_\varphi\alpha_{(V,\varphi)}$: $\pi_*\alpha\circ c^p_M=c^p_{M'}\circ P_\alpha$.
