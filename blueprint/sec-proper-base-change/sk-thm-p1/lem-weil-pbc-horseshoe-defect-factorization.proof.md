The differential defect D annihilates alpha and is annihilated by beta', by the two outer squares and the chain-map identities. Descend along the source epic beta and lift through the target monic alpha', using exactness; this gives E. ([def-sp-abelian]).

Expand the differential of D using square-zero differentials. Precompose by beta and postcompose by alpha'; cancel the epic and monic maps. This gives the signed cocycle identity for E.

The augmentation defect w annihilates S.f and is annihilated by beta' in degree zero, using the augmentation squares and the two squares of F. Descend along S.g and lift through alpha' to obtain e. ([def-sp-abelian]).

Compute the augmentation followed by D in degree zero both using the E factorization and using w. The differentials kill the augmentation maps. Cancel S.g and alpha' in degree one to prove the augmentation identity for E and e.
