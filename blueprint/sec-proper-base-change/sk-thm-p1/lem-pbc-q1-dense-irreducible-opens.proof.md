Finite type over the field and quasi-compactness make X Noetherian. Index its finitely many irreducible components by I. For each component C_a let O_a be the complement of the union of all other components. This is open, and its closure is C_a; these are NoetherianSpace.finite_irreducibleComponents and closure_sUnion_irreducibleComponents_sdiff_singleton. Set T_a = V intersect O_a. Density of V makes T_a nonempty. It is an open subset of C_a, hence irreducible, and closure T_a = C_a. The sets T_a are pairwise disjoint. (`AlgebraicGeometry.LocallyOfFiniteType.isLocallyNoetherian; AlgebraicGeometry.QuasiCompact.compactSpace_of_compactSpace; AlgebraicGeometry.IsNoetherian (class); instance IsNoetherian (Spec R) for IsNoetherianRing R` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/QuasiCompact.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Noetherian.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.finite_irreducibleComponents_of_isNoetherian` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Noetherian.lean))).

Every point lies in some component C_a = closure T_a, so the union of the T_a is dense. Every point w of the closed complement specializes from the generic point z of a component containing w. Choose y in T_a. Since z specializes to y and T_a is open, z lies in T_a and outside the complement. Apply the proved source-library theorem topologicalKrullDim_lt_of_forall_exists_specializes to the complement inside X with d = 1 and dim X < 2. The result dim complement < 1 is dim complement at most zero by ENat.WithBot.lt_add_one_iff. This implements the dimension argument in Stacks 03SC without an additional postponed lemma. (`topologicalKrullDim` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/KrullDimension.lean))).

For x in T_a, the connected component of x in the union is contained in T_a: T_a and the union of the other opens are disjoint open parts, and a connected subset meeting T_a stays in it. Conversely, irreducibility makes T_a connected, so it lies in that component. Their equality proves irreducibility of each connected component. Reindex I by Fin(card I). The empty case has no components and n = 0.
