For commutative rings R,C,P with R-algebra structures on C and P, C finitely presented over R, a submonoid S of P, and an R-algebra map g : C → Localization S, there exist s in S and R-algebra maps f : C → Localization.Away s and k : Localization.Away s → Localization S with k.comp f = g. No smoothness of P is assumed.
