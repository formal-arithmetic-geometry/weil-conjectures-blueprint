The graph is a section of the finite etale projection. A section is an open and closed immersion by `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.of_comp, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.of_flat_of_mono, AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion.of_comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FlatMono.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Separated.lean)). Its inverse on its open image is the restricted projection, so it gives the stated clopen.

An open immersion is isomorphic to the open subscheme of its image (Scheme.Hom.isoOpensRange, Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Restrict.lean:423). This identifies the inverse of that isomorphism with the restricted projection by composing with the graph and using its section identity. Conversely the inverse of r|_W followed by W inclusion is a section; compose it with the first product projection to obtain a over Y. The pullback identities verify the required triangle. This is the explicit inverse construction. (`CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean))).

To check uniqueness, equal graph images give an isomorphism between the two graph domains (IsOpenImmersion.isoOfRangeEq, OpenImmersion.lean:727). Composing its factorization equality with r identifies the isomorphism with the identity, so the two graphs and then their first projections coincide. To check surjectivity, the graph constructed from W is the inverse of its projection followed by W inclusion; its image is W because that inverse is surjective. These are the hinj and hsurj computations in the delivered proof.
