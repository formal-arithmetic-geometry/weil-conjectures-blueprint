title = "A finitely presented algebra has a model over one subfield"
lean = ["Algebra.exists_subfield_model"]

Let $k$ be a field, let $(K_i)_{i\in I}$ be a nonempty directed family of subfields of $k$ whose union is $k$, and let $E$ be a finitely presented commutative $k$-algebra. Then for some $i\in I$ there is a commutative $K_i$-algebra $B$ and a $k$-algebra isomorphism $k\otimes_{K_i}B\cong E$.
