Any generization z of y maps to a generization of eta. Maximality of the irreducible component closure{eta} forces f(z)=eta, by comparing the two singleton closures and using sobriety. The source-library theorem Scheme.Hom.eq_of_specializes_of_apply_eq applies because an etale map is locally quasi-finite and has discrete fibres, so z=y. This is the hmin subproof in the delivered Lean. (`IsGenericPoint` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Sober.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Etale.lean))).

Y is locally Noetherian by LocallyOfFiniteType.isLocallyNoetherian f. Choose an affine open A containing y; its coordinate ring is Noetherian and noetherianSpace_of_isAffineOpen makes A a Noetherian space. Each component Z of A containing y has a generic point z; z specializes to y, so the first step gives z=y. Therefore Z=closure{y}, and y belongs to exactly one component. ([pqf-lem-locnoeth-lft], `IsGenericPoint` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Sober.lean))).

Use TopologicalSpace.NoetherianSpace.exists_isOpen_isPreirreducible on A: its proof takes the complement of all the other, finitely many closed irreducible components. This gives a preirreducible open B containing y. B is nonempty and sober, hence has a generic point; that point specializes to y and the first step again forces equality. Push B forward under the open immersion A to Y, obtaining W. Specialization transports under the inclusions, proving that y is generic in W; its singleton closure is all of W, which proves irreducibility. (`AlgebraicGeometry.finite_irreducibleComponents_of_isNoetherian` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Noetherian.lean)), `IsGenericPoint` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Sober.lean))).
