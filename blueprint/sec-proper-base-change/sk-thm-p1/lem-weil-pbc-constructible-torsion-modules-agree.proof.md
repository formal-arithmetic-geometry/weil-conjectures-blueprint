**Step 1.** *$F$ is torsion and a sheaf of $\mathbf Z/n$-modules.* Every section $s\in F(U)$ satisfies $ns=(n\cdot\mathrm{id}_F)(U)(s)=0$, so condition (2) of [lem-weil-pbc-torsion-sheaf-characterizations] holds (trivial covering, $n_i=n$) and $F$ is torsion in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-torsion-etale-sheaf]; with the hypotheses on $k$ and $C$ this makes $(C,F)$ a curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting] (the torsion of [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves] is condition (2) of that lemma). By [lem-weil-pbc-sheaves-killed-by-n-zmod-modules] (2) $F$ carries exactly one structure of sheaf of $(\mathbf Z/n)_C$-modules extending its abelian structure; in what follows $F$ denotes this module sheaf in (ii) and its underlying abelian sheaf in (i). By (3) of that lemma, on any scheme an abelian-sheaf isomorphism between the underlying abelian sheaves of two sheaves of $(\mathbf Z/n)$-modules is an isomorphism of module sheaves, its inverse being linear too.

**Step 2.** *The partitions are the same.* In (i), [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion] asks for a finite partition of $C$ by locally closed constructible subschemes: a finite family of pairwise disjoint locally closed subsets $C_k$ covering $C$, each constructible [def-weil-constructible-subset], with a subscheme structure, i.e. an immersion $\iota_k:Z_k\to C$ with image $C_k$, the restriction of $F$ to the piece being the inverse image $\iota_k^{-1}F$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image]. In (ii), [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf] asks for a stratification of the scheme $C$ [def-constructible-stratification] (the same data: finite partition into pairwise disjoint locally closed subsets with immersions $\iota_k:Z_k\to C$ of image $C_k$) whose strata are constructible (its condition (a)). So the data and the conditions on them coincide, and the same family $(\iota_k)$ is used on both sides below; no Noetherian argument is needed since both notions require the strata to be constructible (on the Noetherian $C$ this condition is moreover automatic, [def-weil-constructible-subset], Stacks 005L).

**Step 3.** *Restriction to strata and to etale pieces commutes with forgetting the module structure.* Fix a stratum $\iota=\iota_k:Z=Z_k\to C$. By [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (2) (with $R=\mathbf Z/n$), the module inverse image $\iota^{-1}F$ of (ii), a sheaf of $(\mathbf Z/n)_Z$-modules, has as underlying abelian sheaf the abelian inverse image $\iota^{-1}F$ of (i), up to a canonical isomorphism of abelian sheaves; write $G$ for either. $G$ is killed by $n$: $\iota^{-1}$ is additive [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact] (3), so $n\cdot\mathrm{id}_G=\iota^{-1}(n\cdot\mathrm{id}_F)=0$. For an etale $v:U\to Z$ the restriction $G|_U$ to $U_{\Et}$ [def-constructible-locally-constant] is the sheaf $U'\mapsto G(U')$ on etale $U$-schemes $U'$ (viewed as etale $Z$-schemes through $v$), defined sectionwise; so the underlying abelian sheaf of the module restriction is the restriction of the underlying abelian sheaf, and $G|_U$ is again killed by $n$.

**Step 4.** *Constant pieces.* Let $v:U\to Z$ be etale with $U\ne\emptyset$ and $\varphi:G|_U\xrightarrow{\sim}\underline A$ an isomorphism of abelian sheaves with a constant abelian sheaf [def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf] on $U_{\Et}$. Pick a point of $U$ and a geometric point $\bar w$ of $U$ over it [def-weil-geometric-point]. (a) $A\cong\underline A_{\bar w}$ by [lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-geometric-stalk]. (b) $(G|_U)_{\bar w}=G_{v\circ\bar w}$ [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks]: a pointed etale neighbourhood of $\bar w$ in $U$ is, by composition with $v$, a pointed etale neighbourhood of $v\circ\bar w$ in $Z$ with the same sections of $G$, and these are cofinal, since a pointed neighbourhood $(W,\bar x)$ of $v\circ\bar w$ receives $(W\times_ZU,(\bar x,\bar w))$. So $A\cong G_{v\circ\bar w}$ as abelian groups. (c) Hence $nA=0$: the stalk is a colimit of the groups $G(W)$, all killed by $n$ (step 3). So $A$ is a $\mathbf Z/n$-module, and by [lem-weil-pbc-constant-module-sheaf-underlying] ($R=\mathbf Z/n$, $M=A$) $\underline A$ is the underlying abelian sheaf of the constant sheaf $\underline A^{\mathbf Z/n}$ of $(\mathbf Z/n)_U$-modules. By step 1 (applied on $U$), $\varphi$ is an isomorphism $G|_U\cong\underline A^{\mathbf Z/n}$ of sheaves of $(\mathbf Z/n)_U$-modules, the module structure on $G|_U$ being the one of step 3. Conversely an isomorphism $G|_U\cong\underline M^{\mathbf Z/n}$ of module sheaves, $M$ a $\mathbf Z/n$-module, is by [lem-weil-pbc-constant-module-sheaf-underlying] and step 3 an isomorphism of abelian sheaves $G|_U\cong\underline M$, and (a), (b) give $M\cong G_{v\circ\bar w}$ for every geometric point $\bar w$ of $U$.

**Step 5.** *(i) implies (ii).* Take the partition and immersions of (i) (step 2). On a stratum $Z$ we have an etale covering $\{v_i:U_i\to Z\}_{i\in I}$ and abelian groups $A_i$ with $G|_{U_i}\cong\underline{A_i}$ as abelian sheaves [def-constructible-locally-constant], and every geometric stalk of $G$ is finite. Discard the empty $U_i$; the remaining family is still jointly surjective, hence still an etale covering. For each remaining $i$, step 4 gives $nA_i=0$, an isomorphism $G|_{U_i}\cong\underline{A_i}^{\mathbf Z/n}$ of sheaves of $(\mathbf Z/n)_{U_i}$-modules, and $A_i\cong G_{v_i\circ\bar w}$ for a geometric point $\bar w$ of $U_i$, a finite set by (i). So $M_i=A_i$ is a finite $\mathbf Z/n$-module and the module inverse image $\iota_k^{-1}F$ is finite locally constant as a sheaf of $\mathbf Z/n$-modules in the sense of condition (b) of [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf]; with step 2 for condition (a), $F$ is a constructible sheaf of $\mathbf Z/n$-modules. (The empty pieces must be discarded: on $U=\emptyset$ every abelian group $A$ gives $\underline A=0$, so $A$ need not be finite nor killed by $n$.)

**Step 6.** *(ii) implies (i).* Take the stratification of (ii) (step 2). On a stratum $Z$ we have an etale covering $\{v_i:U_i\to Z\}$ and finite $\mathbf Z/n$-modules $M_i$ with $G|_{U_i}\cong\underline{M_i}^{\mathbf Z/n}$ as module sheaves. By step 4 (converse part) $G|_{U_i}\cong\underline{M_i}$ as abelian sheaves, so the abelian inverse image $\iota_k^{-1}F$ is locally constant as an abelian sheaf [def-constructible-locally-constant] (constant = isomorphic to a constant sheaf, [def-constructible-constant]). Finite stalks: a geometric point $\bar z$ of $Z$ lifts along some $v_i$ to a geometric point $\bar w$ of $U_i$ [lem-weil-pbc-etale-cover-geometric-point-lift] (necessarily $U_i\ne\emptyset$), and step 4 gives $G_{\bar z}=G_{v_i\circ\bar w}\cong M_i$, a finite set. Hence $\iota_k^{-1}F$ is locally constant with finite stalks on every stratum, and with step 2 $F$ is constructible in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-constructible-torsion] ($(C,F)$ being a curve coefficient pair by step 1).
