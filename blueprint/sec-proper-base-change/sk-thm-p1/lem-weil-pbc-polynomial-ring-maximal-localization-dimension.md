Let $k$ be a field, $n\ge0$ an integer, $P=k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ the polynomial ring and $\mathfrak M\subset P$ a maximal ideal. Then the localization $P_{\mathfrak M}$ has Krull dimension $\dim(P_{\mathfrak M})=n$.
