The basic opens have localized section rings. The universal property identifies localization of the A-linear section map with the ring map on the localized rings; under this identification the localized section map is surjective.

The affine criterion turns surjectivity of the map on basic-open sections into a closed immersion of the restriction, using that the sections of the restriction over the basic open are the localized sections.
