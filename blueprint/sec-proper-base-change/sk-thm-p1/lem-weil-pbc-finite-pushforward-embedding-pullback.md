Let $\nu:X\to X'$ be a morphism of schemes [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions], $\Lambda$ a commutative ring and $F'$ a sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules on $X'_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves], `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)). Let $n\ge0$ and, for $i=1,\dots,n$, let $f_i:Y_i\to X'$ be a finite morphism `AlgebraicGeometry.IsFinite, AlgebraicGeometry.IsIntegralHom` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Finite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Integral.lean)) which is locally of finite presentation `AlgebraicGeometry.LocallyOfFinitePresentation` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FinitePresentation.lean)) and $M_i$ a $\Lambda$-module, and let $\varphi:F'\hookrightarrow\bigoplus_{i=1}^nf_{i,*}\underline{M_i}$ be a monomorphism into the finite direct sum (biproduct `CategoryTheory.Limits.HasFiniteBiproducts` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Biproducts.lean))) in $\Mod(X'_{\Et},\Lambda)$, $\underline{M_i}$ the constant sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules with value $M_i$ on $Y_{i,\Et}$ `CategoryTheory.constantSheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/ConstantSheaf.lean)) and $f_{i,*}$ the direct image [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. Then there are, for $i=1,\dots,n$, a finite morphism $g_i:Y'_i\to X$ locally of finite presentation (one may take the base change $Y'_i=Y_i\times_{X'}X$, $g_i$ the second projection) and a monomorphism of sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules $\nu^{-1}F'\hookrightarrow\bigoplus_{i=1}^ng_{i,*}\underline{M_i}$ in $\Mod(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$, with the same $M_i$, where $\nu^{-1}$ is the inverse image [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image].
