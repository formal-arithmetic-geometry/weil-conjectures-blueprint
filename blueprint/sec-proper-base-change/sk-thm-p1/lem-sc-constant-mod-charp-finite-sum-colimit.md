Let u be a universe, Λ : Type (u+1) any commutative ring, C : Scheme.{u} any scheme [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions], and p a prime natural number. Let M = Λ/(p), where (p) is the ideal generated by p in Λ, and F the underlying abelian sheaf of the constant Λ-module sheaf M on the small etale site [def-sc-constant-sheaves], `CategoryTheory.Sheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheaf.lean)). There exist a small filtered category I with objects and morphisms in Type (u+1), a diagram D : I → Ab(C_et), and a colimit cocone with point exactly F `CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean)). Each D(i) is isomorphic to a finite biproduct indexed by Fin n of the existing sheaf C.constantZMod p with coefficients ULift (ZMod p). No dimension, smoothness, compactness, connectedness, or characteristic hypothesis on C or Λ is imposed.
