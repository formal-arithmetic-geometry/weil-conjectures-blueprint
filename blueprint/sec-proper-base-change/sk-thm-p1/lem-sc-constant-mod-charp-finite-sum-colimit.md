Let $\Lambda$ be any commutative ring (in the coefficient universe one above that of the schemes), $C$ any scheme [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions], and $p$ a prime number. Let $M=\Lambda/(p)$, where $(p)$ is the ideal generated by $p$ in $\Lambda$, and let $F$ be the underlying abelian sheaf of the constant sheaf of $\Lambda$-modules with value $M$ on the small etale site of $C$ [def-sc-constant-sheaves]. There exist a small filtered category $I$, a diagram $D:I\to\Ab(C_{\Et})$ and a colimit cocone of $D$ with vertex exactly $F$, such that each $D(i)$ is isomorphic to a finite direct sum $(\underline{\mathbf Z/p})^{\oplus n}$ of copies of the constant abelian sheaf $\underline{\mathbf Z/p}$ on $C_{\Et}$. No dimension, smoothness, compactness, connectedness, or characteristic hypothesis on $C$ or $\Lambda$ is imposed.
