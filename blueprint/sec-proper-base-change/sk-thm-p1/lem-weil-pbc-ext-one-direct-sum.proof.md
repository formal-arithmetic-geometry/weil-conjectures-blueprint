For an extension $0\to N\xrightarrow{u}E\xrightarrow{v}P\to0$ and an inclusion $\iota_j:C_j\to P$, the pullback is $E\times_P C_j=\{(e,c)\mid v(e)=\iota_j(c)\}$ with its evident maps from $N$ and to $C_j$. An equivalence of extensions induces an equivalence of these pullbacks, so $\Phi_A$ is well-defined. (`DirectSum, DirectSum.instModule, DirectSum.lof, DirectSum.component, DirectSum.induction_on` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Module.lean)), `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean))).

To prove surjectivity, choose representatives $0\to N\xrightarrow{u_j}E_j\xrightarrow{v_j}C_j\to0$ of a tuple of classes. The direct-sum map $p:\bigoplus_jE_j\to P$ is surjective, and its kernel is exactly $\bigoplus_j u_j(N)$: an element has finite support, and each component lies in $\ker(v_j)=\operatorname{im}(u_j)$ precisely when it lies in the kernel. Let $\Sigma:\bigoplus_jN\to N$ be the finite-sum map. (`DirectSum, DirectSum.instModule, DirectSum.lof, DirectSum.component, DirectSum.induction_on` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Module.lean)), `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean))).

Form $E=(N\oplus\bigoplus_jE_j)/R$, where $R$ is the image of $\bigoplus_jN\to N\oplus\bigoplus_jE_j$, $n\mapsto(\Sigma(n),-\bigoplus_j u_j(n_j))$. The maps $N\to E$, $a\mapsto[(a,0)]$, and $E\to P$, $[(a,e)]\mapsto p(e)$ are well-defined. The first is injective, the second is surjective, and if $p(e)=0$ then $e=\bigoplus_j u_j(n_j)$, so $[(0,e)]=[(\Sigma(n),0)]$; hence their image and kernel agree. Thus $0\to N\to E\to P\to0$ is short exact by the cited definition. (`DirectSum, DirectSum.instModule, DirectSum.lof, DirectSum.component, DirectSum.induction_on` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Module.lean)), `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)), `Submodule.hasQuotient, Submodule.Quotient.module, Submodule.mkQ, Submodule.mkQ_surjective, Submodule.ker_mkQ` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Defs.lean))).

For each $j$, the map $E_j\to E\times_P C_j$, $e\mapsto([0,\iota_j(e)],v_j(e))$, is an isomorphism: surjectivity follows by subtracting the finitely supported components away from $j$ and absorbing their kernel parts through the relation defining $R$, and injectivity follows from the same relation with only the $j$-component present. Under this map the copy of $N$ is fixed because $[(0,\iota_j u_j(n))]=[(n,0)]$, and the map to $C_j$ is fixed. Therefore the constructed extension maps to the prescribed tuple, proving surjectivity of $\Phi_A$. (`DirectSum, DirectSum.instModule, DirectSum.lof, DirectSum.component, DirectSum.induction_on` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Module.lean)), `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)), `Submodule.hasQuotient, Submodule.Quotient.module, Submodule.mkQ, Submodule.mkQ_surjective, Submodule.ker_mkQ` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Defs.lean))).

For injectivity, let $0\to N\xrightarrow{u}E\xrightarrow{v}P\to0$ and $0\to N\xrightarrow{u'}E'\xrightarrow{v'}P\to0$ have equivalent pullbacks, with fixed-end-term isomorphisms $h_j:E\times_P C_j\to E'\times_P C_j$. Every $e\in E$ is in $u(N)+\sum_j\operatorname{im}(E\times_P C_j\to E)$: write $v(e)$ as the finite sum $\sum_j\iota_j(c_j)$, lift each $c_j$ to a pair in the corresponding pullback, and the remaining difference lies in $u(N)$. (`DirectSum, DirectSum.instModule, DirectSum.lof, DirectSum.component, DirectSum.induction_on` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Module.lean)), `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean))).

Define $q:N\oplus\bigoplus_j(E\times_P C_j)\to E$ by the endpoint inclusion on $N$ and the sum of the pullback inclusions, and define $q'$ analogously using $u'$ and the maps $h_j$. If an element is in $\ker(q)$, applying $v$ shows that every $C_j$-component is zero because the direct sum has unique components; hence every pullback summand lies in the common copy of $N$, where each $h_j$ is the identity. The relation $q=0$ then gives $q'=0$, so $q'$ factors uniquely through a map $h:E\to E'$. Applying the same construction to the inverses $h_j^{-1}$ gives an inverse map. Thus $h$ is an isomorphism, fixes $N$, and commutes with the maps to $P$, proving injectivity of $\Phi_A$. (`DirectSum, DirectSum.instModule, DirectSum.lof, DirectSum.component, DirectSum.induction_on` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Module.lean)), `CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.Exact` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Exact.lean)), `Submodule.hasQuotient, Submodule.Quotient.module, Submodule.mkQ, Submodule.mkQ_surjective, Submodule.ker_mkQ` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Basic.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Defs.lean))).

A well-defined map that is both surjective and injective is a bijection, so $\Phi_A$ has the asserted bijectivity.
