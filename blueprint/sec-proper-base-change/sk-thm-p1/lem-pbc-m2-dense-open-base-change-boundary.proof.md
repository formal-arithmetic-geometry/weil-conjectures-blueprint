The curve is Noetherian and has finitely many irreducible components. For each component Z take the open O which avoids all the other components. The generic point of Z lies in O: otherwise maximality of the components would identify Z with another component. Density makes U intersect O, and every point of O belongs only to Z. Thus U restricts to a nonempty open of every component. The proved private helper dense_open_boundary applies IsOpen.finite_compl_of_topologicalKrullDim_le_one and IsOpen.isClosed_singleton_of_mem_compl from WeilConjectures.Mathlib.Topology.KrullDimension to these irreducible subspaces. Their closed embeddings transport the closed singletons to X; the finite union of their finite complements contains X minus U. This gives a finite boundary of closed points. For each boundary point x, residueFieldIsoBase identifies its residue field with k and SpecMap_residueFieldIsoBase_inv makes Spec κ(x) to X to Spec k an isomorphism. Paste the flipped defining square of the fiber of a at x with the given field-extension square, using IsPullback.paste_vert. IsPullback.isIso_snd_of_isIso, which is the universal property of pullback of an isomorphism, shows that the fiber maps isomorphically to Spec k'. Scheme.Hom.fiberHomeo identifies this singleton fiber with the set-theoretic inverse image of x. The singleton-fiber assertion makes a injective on the inverse image of the finite boundary. Set.Finite.preimage proves that this inverse image is finite. For any of its points y, the inverse image of {a(y)} is exactly {y}; continuity therefore makes {y} closed.

The morphism f' is locally of finite type by base change. Apply residueFieldIsoBase again, now at each closed boundary point of X', to identify its residue field with k'. Transport IsAlgClosed along this ring equivalence and use IsSepClosed.of_isAlgClosed. This proves all three clauses for the preimage open, including an empty boundary.
