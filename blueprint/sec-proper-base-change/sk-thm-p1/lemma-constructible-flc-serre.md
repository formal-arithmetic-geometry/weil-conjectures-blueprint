Let $\mathcal A=\mathrm{Mod}_\Lambda$ with $\Lambda$ a ring. The cokernel
of a morphism between finite locally constant sheaves is finite locally
constant; if $\Lambda$ is Noetherian, so is the kernel. If $\Lambda$ is
finite, the middle term of a short exact sequence whose outer terms are
finite locally constant is finite locally constant.
