Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative ring, let $X$ be a scheme, let
$j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be an
open immersion, and let $F$
be a constructible
$\Lambda$-sheaf on $U_{{\mathrm{et}}}$. Then $j_!F$ of
[def-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero] is constructible on
$X_{{\mathrm{et}}}$.
