Let $p$ be a prime number and $K/k$ a field extension `Algebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Defs.lean)) of fields of characteristic $p$. Let $K^p=\{b^p:b\in K\}$ and let $kK^p$ be the subfield of $K$ generated by $k$ and $K^p$. For a family $(z_l)_{l\in\Lambda}$ of elements of $K$ and $E=(e_l)_{l\in\Lambda}$ a family of integers with $0\le e_l<p$ for all $l$ and $e_l=0$ for all but finitely many $l$, write $z^E=\prod_{l\in\Lambda}z_l^{e_l}$. Let $(x_i)_{i\in I}$ be a family of elements of $K$ which is $p$-independent over $k$: the monomials $x^E$ (as a family indexed by $E$) are linearly independent over $kK^p$. Then there are a set $J$ and a family $(y_j)_{j\in J}$ of elements of $K$ such that the combined family $(z_l)_{l\in I\sqcup J}$, with $z_i=x_i$ for $i\in I$ and $z_j=y_j$ for $j\in J$, is a $p$-basis of $K$ over $k$: the monomials $z^E$ form a basis of the $kK^p$-vector space $K$.
