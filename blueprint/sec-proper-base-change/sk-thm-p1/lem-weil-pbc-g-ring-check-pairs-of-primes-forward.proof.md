Assume $R$ is a G-ring and let $\mathfrak q\subseteq\mathfrak p$ be primes. Using that localization at the prime $\bar{\mathfrak p}$ of $R/\mathfrak q$ is $R_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak qR_{\mathfrak p}$, that completion commutes with this quotient of the Noetherian local ring $R_{\mathfrak p}$, and base change of tensor products, identify $F_{\mathfrak q,\mathfrak p}$ as a $\kappa(\mathfrak q)$-algebra with the fibre over $\mathfrak qR_{\mathfrak p}$ of the regular completion map $R_{\mathfrak p}\to(R_{\mathfrak p})^\wedge$. That fibre is Noetherian and geometrically regular over $\kappa(\mathfrak q)$ by regularity of the map, and both properties transport across the $\kappa(\mathfrak q)$-algebra isomorphism.
