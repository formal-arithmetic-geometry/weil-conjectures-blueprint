Let $Y$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme], let $\bar x:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$ be a geometric point [def-weil-geometric-point] lying over $y\in Y$, and let $\kappa(\bar x)\subset\Omega$ be the separable algebraic closure [def-weil-pbc-relative-separable-closure] of the residue field $\kappa(y)$ [def-weil-residue-field] in $\Omega$ (with $\kappa(y)\to\Omega$ given by $\bar x$). Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subset Y$ be an affine open containing $y$ and $\mathfrak p\subset A$ the prime ideal of $y$, so that $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=\kappa(y)$. Let $\mathcal N$ be the category of affine pointed etale neighbourhoods $(V=\operatorname{Spec}A_V,\bar v)$ of $\bar x$ [def-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods] (a morphism $(V',\bar v')\to(V,\bar v)$ is a $Y$-morphism $V'\to V$ carrying $\bar v'$ to $\bar v$), and let $\mathcal T$ be the category of triples $(B,\mathfrak q,\phi)$ where $A\to B$ is a ring map with $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ etale [def-weil-etale-morphism], $\mathfrak q\subset B$ is a prime lying over $\mathfrak p$, and $\phi:\kappa(\mathfrak q)\to\kappa(\bar x)$ is a homomorphism of $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebras; a morphism $(B,\mathfrak q,\phi)\to(B',\mathfrak q',\phi')$ is an $A$-algebra map $\beta:B\to B'$ with $\beta^{-1}(\mathfrak q')=\mathfrak q$ and $\phi'\circ\kappa(\beta)=\phi$. Then:

1. $\mathcal N$ is cofiltered: it is nonempty, any two objects receive morphisms from a common object, and any two parallel morphisms $V'\rightrightarrows V$ are equalized by a morphism $V''\to V'$.
2. The full subcategory $\mathcal N_U\subset\mathcal N$ of those $(V,\bar v)$ for which $V\to Y$ factors through $U$ is initial: every object of $\mathcal N$ receives a morphism from an object of $\mathcal N_U$. Hence the colimit $\varinjlim_{(V,\bar v)\in\mathcal N}A_V$ [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit] is canonically the colimit over $\mathcal N_U$.
3. For $(V=\operatorname{Spec}B,\bar v)$ in $\mathcal N_U$ let $v\in V$ be the image of $\bar v$, $\mathfrak q_v\subset B$ its prime, and $\phi_v:\kappa(\mathfrak q_v)\to\Omega$ the field homomorphism corresponding to $\bar v$ [def-weil-geometric-point]. Then $\phi_v$ is a $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebra map with image in $\kappa(\bar x)$, and $(V,\bar v)\mapsto(B,\mathfrak q_v,\phi_v)$ is an equivalence of $\mathcal N_U$ with the opposite of $\mathcal T$. Consequently $\varinjlim_{\mathcal N}A_V=\varinjlim_{(B,\mathfrak q,\phi)\in\mathcal T}B$ canonically, compatibly with the maps from each $A_V$, $(V,\bar v)\in\mathcal N_U$.
