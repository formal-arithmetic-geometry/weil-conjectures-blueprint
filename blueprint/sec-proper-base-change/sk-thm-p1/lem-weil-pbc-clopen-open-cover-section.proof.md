*The images are open, closed and cover $Z$ (Stacks: 'As $V\to Z$ is finite we see that $V_i\to Z$ is also closed. Let $U_i\subset Z$ be the image').* For $i=1,\dots,n$ let $k_i:V_i\to V$ be the inclusion of the open subscheme $V_i$, so that $p_i=p\circ k_i$, and let $U_i=p(V_i)=p_i(V_i)\subseteq Z$. Since $p_i$ is an open immersion, $U_i$ is open in $Z$ `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)). Since $p$ is finite it is a closed map (part (1) of `AlgebraicGeometry.IsProper (instance [IsFinite f] : IsProper f), AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isClosedMap, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.finite_preimage_singleton` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Proper.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/QuasiFinite.lean), [Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/UniversallyClosed.lean))), and $V_i$ is closed in $V$, so $U_i=p(V_i)$ is closed in $Z$. Since $p$ is surjective `AlgebraicGeometry.Surjective` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/UnderlyingMap.lean)), every $z\in Z$ is $p(v)$ for some $v\in V=\bigcup_iV_i$, say $v\in V_i$, and then $z\in U_i$; so $Z=U_1\cup\dots\cup U_n$.

*The strata (Stacks: the decomposition $Z=\coprod_{(A,B)}\bigcap_{i\in A}U_i\cap\bigcap_{i\in B}U_i^c$ over $\{1,\dots,n\}=A\amalg B$ with $A\neq\emptyset$; 'each of the strata is contained in a single $U_i$').* For a nonempty subset $A\subseteq\{1,\dots,n\}$ put $Z_A=\bigcap_{i\in A}U_i\cap\bigcap_{i\notin A}(Z\setminus U_i)$. Each $U_i$ and each $Z\setminus U_i$ is open and closed in $Z$ (step 1), so $Z_A$, a finite intersection of open and closed subsets, is open and closed. For $z\in Z$ let $A(z)=\{i: z\in U_i\}$, which is nonempty by step 1; by definition $z\in Z_A$ if and only if $A=A(z)$. Hence the $Z_A$ ($A\neq\emptyset$) are pairwise disjoint and cover $Z$. Put $i(A)=\min A$; then $Z_A\subseteq U_{i(A)}$. (Some $Z_A$ may be empty; nothing below needs them nonempty.)

*The section on one stratum (Stacks: 'we find our section').* Fix $A\neq\emptyset$, write $i=i(A)$, and let $j_{Z_A}:Z_A\to Z$ and $j_{U_i}:U_i\to Z$ be the inclusions of the open subschemes on $Z_A$ and $U_i$ (step 1, step 2) [def-pc-scheme]. By `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isoOpensRange, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isoOpensRange_hom_ι, AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isoOpensRange_inv_comp` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Restrict.lean)) applied to the open immersion $p_i:V_i\to Z$, whose image is $U_i$, there is an isomorphism $\varphi_i:V_i\to U_i$ with $j_{U_i}\circ\varphi_i=p_i$. Since $j_{Z_A}(Z_A)=Z_A\subseteq U_i$, `AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift_fac, AlgebraicGeometry.IsOpenImmersion.lift_uniq` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/OpenImmersion.lean)) gives a morphism $\iota_A:Z_A\to U_i$ with $j_{U_i}\circ\iota_A=j_{Z_A}$. Put $s_A=k_i\circ\varphi_i^{-1}\circ\iota_A:Z_A\to V$. Then $p\circ s_A=p_i\circ\varphi_i^{-1}\circ\iota_A=j_{U_i}\circ\varphi_i\circ\varphi_i^{-1}\circ\iota_A=j_{U_i}\circ\iota_A=j_{Z_A}$.

*Gluing the local sections.* By step 2 the open subschemes $Z_A$, $A$ running over the nonempty subsets of $\{1,\dots,n\}$, form an open covering of $Z$. The morphisms $s_A:Z_A\to V$ of step 3 agree on overlaps: for $A\neq B$ the overlap $Z_A\cap Z_B$ is empty (step 2), and a scheme with empty underlying space has exactly one morphism to $V$ (the empty map of spaces, and on structure sheaves the map to $\mathcal O_{\emptyset}(\emptyset)=0$, the zero ring), so $s_A$ and $s_B$ restrict to the same morphism there; for $A=B$ there is nothing to check. By `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Cover.glueMorphisms` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Gluing.lean)) there is a morphism $s:Z\to V$ with $s\circ j_{Z_A}=s_A$ for every $A$. Then for every $A$, $(p\circ s)\circ j_{Z_A}=p\circ s_A=j_{Z_A}=\mathrm{id}_Z\circ j_{Z_A}$ (step 3), so $p\circ s$ and $\mathrm{id}_Z$ have the same restriction to every member of the covering, and by the uniqueness clause of `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Cover.glueMorphisms` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Gluing.lean)) $p\circ s=\mathrm{id}_Z$: $s$ is a section of $p$.
