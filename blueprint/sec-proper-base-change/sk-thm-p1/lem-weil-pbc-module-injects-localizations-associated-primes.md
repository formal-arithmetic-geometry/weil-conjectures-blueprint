Let $R$ be a Noetherian ring `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)) and $M$ an $R$-module. Call a prime ideal $\mathfrak p\subset R$ an associated prime of $M$ if $\mathfrak p=\{a\in R:az=0\}$ for some $z\in M$, and write $\operatorname{Ass}(M)$ for the set of them. For a prime $\mathfrak p$ let $M_{\mathfrak p}$ be the localization of $M$ at $R\setminus\mathfrak p$ `LocalizedModule` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean)) `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)). Then the $R$-linear map $M\to\prod_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Ass}(M)}M_{\mathfrak p}$, $m\mapsto(m/1)_{\mathfrak p}$, is injective (the empty product being $0$). Equivalently: if $m\in M$ and for every $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Ass}(M)$ there is $s\in R\setminus\mathfrak p$ with $sm=0$, then $m=0$.
