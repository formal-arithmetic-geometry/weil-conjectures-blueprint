Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $r\ge1$, $0\le i\le r$, $g_1,\dots,g_i\in R$, $P=R[x_1,\dots,x_r]$ the polynomial ring and $J=(g_1x_1,\dots,g_ix_i)\subset P$ (so $J=0$ if $i=0$). For a multi-index $E=(e_1,\dots,e_r)\in\mathbf Z_{\ge0}^r$ let $I_E\subset R$ be the ideal generated by the $g_j$ with $1\le j\le i$ and $e_j>0$. Then a polynomial $\sum_Ec_Ex^E\in P$ ($x^E=x_1^{e_1}\cdots x_r^{e_r}$) lies in $J$ if and only if $c_E\in I_E$ for every $E$. Equivalently, the $R$-linear map $P\to\bigoplus_E R/I_E$, $\sum_Ec_Ex^E\mapsto(c_E\bmod I_E)_E$, is surjective with kernel $J$, so $P/J\cong\bigoplus_E (R/I_E)\cdot x^E$ as $R$-modules.
