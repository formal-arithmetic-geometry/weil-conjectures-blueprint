*Step 1 (the two functors and their adjunction).* By `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.mapPullbackAdj` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/MorphismProperty/OverAdjunction.lean)) (1), since $j$ is etale, $\iota_j(V\xrightarrow{v}Y)=(V\xrightarrow{j\circ v}X)$ is a functor $Y_{\Et}\to X_{\Et}$; it is the identity on morphisms, a $Y$-morphism being in particular an $X$-morphism. By clause (2) of the same lemma, $b_j(W)=(W\times_XY\xrightarrow{q_W}Y)$ is a functor $X_{\Et}\to Y_{\Et}$, and by clause (3) $\iota_j$ is left adjoint to $b_j$ ($\operatorname{Hom}_Y(V,W\times_XY)=\operatorname{Hom}_X(V,W)$), with counit $\varepsilon_W=p_W:W\times_XY\to W$, the projection, regarded as a morphism $\iota_jb_jW\to W$ of $X_{\Et}$. By [def-weil-pbc-direct-image], $(j_*F)(W)=F(W\times_XY)=F(b_jW)$ for an abelian sheaf $F$ on $Y_{\Et}$, i.e. $j_*F=F\circ b_j$, with the restriction maps $F(b_j(g))$.

*Step 2 (precomposition with $\iota_j$ and with $b_j$ preserves sheaves; $G|_Y$ is a sheaf).* (a) Let $H$ be a sheaf of sets on $X_{\Et}$ and $\{V_i\to V\}_i$ a covering of $Y_{\Et}$: a jointly surjective family of etale morphisms of schemes etale over $Y$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site]. The same morphisms form a jointly surjective family of etale morphisms between the objects $\iota_jV_i$, $\iota_jV$ of $X_{\Et}$, i.e. a covering $\{\iota_jV_i\to\iota_jV\}$ of $X_{\Et}$. The fibre products $V_i\times_VV_k$ of $Y_{\Et}$ and $\iota_jV_i\times_{\iota_jV}\iota_jV_k$ of $X_{\Et}$ are both the scheme-theoretic fibre product [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)), so $\iota_j(V_i\times_VV_k)=\iota_jV_i\times_{\iota_jV}\iota_jV_k$. Hence the diagram $H(\iota_jV)\to\prod_iH(\iota_jV_i)\rightrightarrows\prod_{i,k}H(\iota_j(V_i\times_VV_k))$ expressing the sheaf condition of $H\circ\iota_j$ for $\{V_i\to V\}$ is the diagram expressing the sheaf condition of $H$ for the covering $\{\iota_jV_i\to\iota_jV\}$, which is an equalizer; so $H\circ\iota_j$ is a sheaf. Applied to the underlying sheaf of sets of an abelian sheaf $G$ (whose sheaf condition is that of the underlying presheaf of sets, `CategoryTheory.Sheaf` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Sheaf.lean))), $G|_Y=G\circ\iota_j$ is an abelian sheaf on $Y_{\Et}$, the first assertion of clause 1. (b) Let $F$ be a sheaf of sets on $Y_{\Et}$ and $\{W_i\to W\}_i$ a covering of $X_{\Et}$. By `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.instIsStableUnderBaseChangePrecoverageOfIsJointlySurjectivePreservingOfIsStableUnderBaseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/MorphismProperty.lean)), applied to the jointly surjective etale family $\{W_i\to W\}$ and the projection $W\times_XY\to W$, the family $\{(W\times_XY)\times_WW_i\to W\times_XY\}$ is etale and jointly surjective; composing with the canonical isomorphisms $(W\times_XY)\times_WW_i\cong W_i\times_XY$ over $W\times_XY$ `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)), $\{b_jW_i\to b_jW\}$ is a covering of $Y_{\Et}$. By `CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.instPreservesFiniteLimitsTopPullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/MorphismProperty.lean)) (3), applied to $u=j$ (its base change $Y\times_X-$ being $b_j$ up to the symmetry of the fibre product), $b_j$ carries the fibre products $W_i\times_WW_k$ to fibre products: $b_j(W_i\times_WW_k)\cong b_jW_i\times_{b_jW}b_jW_k$ compatibly with the projections. So the sheaf-condition diagram of $F\circ b_j$ for $\{W_i\to W\}$ is isomorphic to that of $F$ for the covering $\{b_jW_i\to b_jW\}$, an equalizer; $F\circ b_j$ is a sheaf. In particular $j_*F=F\circ b_j$ of step 1 is an abelian sheaf, as [def-weil-pbc-direct-image] takes for granted.

*Step 3 (restriction is left adjoint to $j_*$, with unit the restriction along $p_W$).* By steps 1 and 2, the adjoint functors $\lambda=\iota_j:Y_{\Et}\to X_{\Et}$, $\beta=b_j:X_{\Et}\to Y_{\Et}$ satisfy the hypotheses of `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.sheafPushforwardContinuous` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Continuous.lean)) with $\mathcal C=Y_{\Et}$, $\mathcal D=X_{\Et}$; there $\lambda^sG=G|_Y$ and $\beta^sF=F\circ b_j=j_*F$. By its clause 1, $G\mapsto G|_Y$ is left adjoint to $j_*:\Ab(Y_{\Et})\to\Ab(X_{\Et})$, with unit $u_G:G\to j_*(G|_Y)$ given at $W\in X_{\Et}$ by $G(\varepsilon_W)=G(p_W):G(W)\to G(W\times_XY)=G|_Y(b_jW)=j_*(G|_Y)(W)$, the restriction map of $G$ along the projection $p_W:W\times_XY\to W$ (a morphism of $X_{\Et}$). (`CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Over.mapPullbackAdj` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/MorphismProperty/OverAdjunction.lean)), [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]).

*Step 4 (the isomorphism $\rho$; clause 1).* By [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], $j^{-1}:\Ab(X_{\Et})\to\Ab(Y_{\Et})$ is left adjoint to $j_*$, with unit $\eta_j$. By step 3, $G\mapsto G|_Y$ is also left adjoint to $j_*$. By `CategoryTheory.Adjunction.sheafPushforwardContinuous` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Continuous.lean)) (2), with $L=j^{-1}$ and $\eta=\eta_j$, there is a unique natural isomorphism $\rho:j^{-1}\xrightarrow{\sim}(-)|_Y$ of functors $\Ab(X_{\Et})\to\Ab(Y_{\Et})$ with $j_*(\rho_G)\circ\eta_{j,G}=u_G$ for all $G$. Its component $\rho_G:j^{-1}G\to G|_Y$ is an isomorphism of abelian sheaves on $Y_{\Et}$, natural in $G$. This is clause 1.

*Step 5 (the unit is restriction; clause 2).* Evaluate $j_*(\rho_G)\circ\eta_{j,G}=u_G$ (step 4) at $W\in X_{\Et}$. Since $j_*F=F\circ b_j$ on objects and on morphisms (step 1), $j_*(\rho_G)(W)=\rho_G(b_jW)=\rho_G(W\times_XY)$; and $u_G(W)=G(p_W)$ by step 3. Hence $\rho_G(W\times_XY)\circ\eta_{j,G}(W)=G(p_W):G(W)\to G(W\times_XY)$, the restriction of $G$ along the projection $p_W$, which is the first statement of clause 2. Global sections: take $W=(X,\mathrm{id}_X)$. The fibre product $X\times_XY$ has $q_X:X\times_XY\to Y$ an isomorphism and $p_X=j\circ q_X$ `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean)), so $q_X$ is an isomorphism $(X\times_XY,p_X)\cong(Y,j)=\iota_j(Y,\mathrm{id}_Y)$ in $X_{\Et}$ and in $Y_{\Et}$; identifying $G(X\times_XY)$ with $G(Y)$ through $G(q_X)$ (and $\Gamma(Y,j^{-1}G)=(j^{-1}G)(Y)$ with $(j^{-1}G)(X\times_XY)$ through $(j^{-1}G)(q_X)$, compatibly with $\rho_G$ by its naturality in the object), the identity reads $\rho_G(Y)\circ\eta_{j,G}(X)=G(j):G(X)\to G(Y)$, since $G(p_X)=G(q_X)\circ G(j)$. Finally $\rho_G(Y):\Gamma(Y,j^{-1}G)\to G(Y)$ is an isomorphism, being a component of the isomorphism of sheaves $\rho_G$. ([def-weil-pbc-direct-image]).
