Equip R' with the algebra structure induced by φ. Convert (a)-(c) into IsLocalization M R' using Mathlib's isLocalization_iff. For surjectivity, write y = φ(r)u and multiply by φ(s), using φ(s)u = 1. For the equality criterion, apply (c) to x-y and rewrite s(x-y)=0 as sx=sy. The characterization and uniqueness of localization are the opening assertion of `IsLocalization.localization_localization_isLocalization` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/LocalizationLocalization.lean)).

The canonical localization Localization M is Noetherian by `IsLocalization.isNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Submodule.lean)), using the Noetherianity contained in regularity `IsRegularLocalRing, IsRegularRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean)). Mathlib supplies this as an instance. (`IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean))).

For a prime p of Localization M, transitivity and uniqueness of localization `IsLocalization.localization_localization_isLocalization` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/LocalizationLocalization.lean)) identify (Localization M)_p with R_q, where q is the contraction of p and is prime. In the transitivity submonoid the elements are exactly those outside q: all elements of M map to units, hence lie outside p, and use denominator 1 for the converse. Mathlib packages this specialization in IsLocalization.localizationLocalizationAtPrimeIsoLocalization. The localization at q `Localization.AtPrime, Ideal.ResidueField, Ideal.Fiber` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean)) is regular local by regularity of R `IsRegularLocalRing, IsRegularRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean)). Apply IsRegularLocalRing.of_ringEquiv to obtain regular localness of (Localization M)_p, and isRegularRing_iff to conclude that Localization M is regular.

Uniqueness of localization `IsLocalization.localization_localization_isLocalization` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/LocalizationLocalization.lean)) gives Localization M ≃ₐ[R] R', implemented by IsLocalization.algEquiv. Apply IsRegularRing.of_ringEquiv to its underlying ring equivalence. This transports Noetherianity and the regular-local condition at every prime, giving regularity of R' `IsRegularLocalRing, IsRegularRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean)).
