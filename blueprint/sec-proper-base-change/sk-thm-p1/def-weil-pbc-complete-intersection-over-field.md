Let $k$ be a field. For a commutative ring $T$ and $g\in T$ let $T_g=T[1/g]$ be the localization of $T$ at the element $g$, i.e. at the multiplicative subset $\{1,g,g^2,\dots\}$ `Localization.Away` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/Away.lean)), a $k$-algebra when $T$ is; let $\dim T$ be the Krull dimension `topologicalKrullDim` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/KrullDimension.lean)) of the topological space $\operatorname{Spec}T$ `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme.lean)) (so $\dim T=-\infty$ iff $T=0$), and $D(g)=\{\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}T:g\notin\mathfrak q\}$. (1) A commutative $k$-algebra $T$ is a *global complete intersection over $k$* if $T=0$, or if there are integers $n,c\ge0$, polynomials $f_1,\dots,f_c\in k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ `MvPolynomial, MvPolynomial.aeval, MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Basic.lean)) and an isomorphism of $k$-algebras $k[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(f_1,\dots,f_c)\cong T$ such that $\dim T=n-c$. (Such a $T$ is in particular a finitely generated $k$-algebra; the convention that the zero ring is a global complete intersection is part of the definition, Stacks 00S9.) (2) Let $S$ be a finitely generated $k$-algebra. $S$ is a *local complete intersection over $k$* if there is a family $(g_i)_{i\in J}$ of elements of $S$ such that $\operatorname{Spec}S=\bigcup_{i\in J}D(g_i)$ and each $S_{g_i}$ is a global complete intersection over $k$ in the sense of (1).
