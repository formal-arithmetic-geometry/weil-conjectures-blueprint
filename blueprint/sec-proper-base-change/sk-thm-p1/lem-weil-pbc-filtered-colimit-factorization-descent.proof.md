Use Concrete.colimit_exists_rep for every coefficient in a finite set and Finset.exists_le to move its representatives to a common stage. Apply Polynomial.lifts_iff_coeff_lifts and Polynomial.lifts_and_natDegree_eq_and_monic to lift a finite family of polynomials simultaneously, preserving monicity whenever required. The Mathlib monic-lifting theorem includes the zero-ring case. (`CategoryTheory.Limits.Concrete.isColimit_exists_rep, CategoryTheory.Under.preservesColimitsOfShape_forget_of_isConnected, CommAlgCat.commAlgCatEquivUnder, CommRingCat.FilteredColimits.forget_preservesFilteredColimits` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/ConcreteCategory/Basic.lean))).

For a stage element whose image belongs to I, use hI to choose an ideal representative. Concrete.colimit_exists_of_rep_eq makes the representatives equal at a common later stage, where ideal compatibility gives membership. For a finite family of polynomials vanishing modulo I, apply this to their finitely many nonzero coefficients, take one common upper bound also dominating the original stage, and use compatibility again. Every coefficient then vanishes modulo the ideal at that stage. (`CategoryTheory.Limits.Concrete.isColimit_rep_eq_iff_exists` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/ConcreteCategory/Basic.lean))).

Lift the prescribed monic quotient factors to monic polynomials over A using Polynomial.lifts_and_natDegree_eq_and_monic and quotient-map surjectivity. Lift the two Bezout witnesses using Polynomial.map_surjective. Apply step 1 to the five polynomials f,g,h,a,b. Apply step 2 to the two polynomials F-GH and AG+BH-1. At the resulting stage their zero reductions give the factorization and the Bezout identity; transition maps preserve monicity, and cocone naturality gives the prescribed images. This uniform argument also handles zero colimit or quotient rings without assuming nontriviality.
