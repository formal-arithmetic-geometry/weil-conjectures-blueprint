On an affine open of T, refine the covering sieve by finitely many flat maps from affine schemes. Their disjoint union is affine and its map p to the affine base is flat, surjective and quasi-compact. This is the affine refinement of an fpqc cover (022E). ([def-pc-scheme], `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.fpqcPrecoverage` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Fpqc.lean))).

Every arrow h in the sieve generated by the g_i factors as k followed by some g_i. Given a cartesian square over h, paste and cancel cartesian squares to exhibit its base-changed morphism as a base change of b_i. Stability under arbitrary base change gives P for this morphism. (`CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean))).

Apply the preceding observation to the pullback of f along each component of p. Zariski locality on the target, applied to the open covering by components of the disjoint union, gives P for the pullback along p. Single-morphism descent along the surjective flat quasi-compact p gives P for f over this affine base. Zariski locality on the affine open covering of T then gives P(f). The finite empty union is allowed. These steps are the complete existing source proof; no new Lean statement or unproved dependency is introduced. ([def-pc-scheme], `CategoryTheory.Limits.pullback` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/HasPullback.lean))).
