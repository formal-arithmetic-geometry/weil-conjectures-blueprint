Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $n,m\geq0$, $P=R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, and $f_1,\ldots,f_m\in P$. Put $J=(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$. A *polynomial inverse certificate* for $f$ is a family $c_{jk}\in P$ ($1\le j\le m$, $1\le k\le n$) such that $\sum_j c_{jk}\,\partial f_j/\partial x_l-\delta_{kl}\in J$ for all $k,l$, and $\sum_k(\partial f_j/\partial x_k)\sum_a c_{ak}f_a-f_j\in J^2$ for every $j$. Here $\delta_{kl}=1$ when $k=l$ and $0$ otherwise; $J^2$ is generated by products of two elements of $J$.
