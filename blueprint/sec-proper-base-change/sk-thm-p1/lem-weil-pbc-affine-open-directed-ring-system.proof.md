Set J={i | i₀ ≤ i} and o=(i₀, refl). The functor K:Jᵒᵖ → Over(op i₀) sends i to its order arrow; it is initial by Functor.initial_of_exists_of_isCofiltered, since every over-object is represented and the base preorder has subsingleton hom sets. Set E=K ⋙ opensDiagram D (op i₀) W and F=E.rightOp ⋙ Scheme.Γ. Every E(i) is affine by IsAffineOpen.preimage. Identify E(o) with W by Scheme.isoOfEq applied to D.map(id)⁻¹(W)=W. Compose its inverse with E(o).isoSpec to define e0. Scheme.isoSpec_hom_naturality gives every stage compatibility; the restrictions agree after composition with W.ι, which is mono. Whisker opensCone D c (op i₀) W by K. Initiality and isLimitOpensCone prove this cone cE limiting. Scheme.isAffine_of_isLimit makes its vertex affine. Taking the right-opposite cone and using preservesColimit_Γ gives a colimit cocone on F with vertex Γ(cE.pt). Let eΓ be its coconePointUniqueUpToIso with the chosen colimit. Define eL=cE.pt.isoSpec followed by Spec.mapIso eΓ.symm.op. The comparison identity comp_coconePointUniqueUpToIso_inv at o identifies the composite of the coprojection and eΓ.inv with the global-sections map of cE.π(o). Applying Spec and Scheme.isoSpec_hom_naturality gives the limit compatibility; composition with the mono W.ι identifies its restricted leg with the required resLE. No nonemptiness of W is used.
