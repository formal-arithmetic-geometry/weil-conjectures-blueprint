Let $R$ and $S$ be commutative rings, $S$ an $R$-algebra, and $s\colon S\to R$ a homomorphism
of $R$-algebras. Let $e\in S$ be an idempotent with $s(e)=1$ and $\ker(s)=(1-e)S$. Then there
is an isomorphism of $R$-algebras $S[1/e]\cong R$ sending $x/1$ to $s(x)$ for every $x\in S$.
