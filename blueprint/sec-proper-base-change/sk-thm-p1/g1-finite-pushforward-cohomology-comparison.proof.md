Induced maps on cohomology and their inverses form additive equivalences. ([def-weil-pbc-etale-cohomology]).

Compose these coefficient isomorphisms with the additive equivalence obtained from the existing bijective finite edge map. ([lem-weil-pbc-finite-pushforward-cohomology]).
