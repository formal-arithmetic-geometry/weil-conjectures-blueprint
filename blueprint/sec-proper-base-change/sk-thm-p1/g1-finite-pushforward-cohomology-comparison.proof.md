Regarding an abelian sheaf as a sheaf of $\mathbb Z$-modules and forgetting again gives back the original sheaf up to a natural isomorphism, so $A_X\cong F$ and $A_Y\cong f_*F$, and these isomorphisms induce isomorphisms on cohomology [def-weil-pbc-etale-cohomology]. Compose them with the isomorphism $H^q(Y,f_*F)\simeq H^q(X,F)$ given by the bijective edge map of the finite morphism $f$ [lem-weil-pbc-finite-pushforward-cohomology].
