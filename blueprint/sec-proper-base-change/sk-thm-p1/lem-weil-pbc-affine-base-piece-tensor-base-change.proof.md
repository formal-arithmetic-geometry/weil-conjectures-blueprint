Let Y_T=Y₀×_S T and X_T=X₀×_S T. Write V_T=p_Y⁻¹(V) and U_T=f_T⁻¹(V_T). The equation for q factors the target chart through W_T=d⁻¹(W); the restricted f_T factors the source chart through the same open. For t:L→T, paste the original pullback squares and open restriction squares to identify the target chart over L with V_T×_{W_T}t⁻¹(W_T) and the source with U_T×_{W_T}t⁻¹(W_T). Both identifications commute with the chart map.

Transport the fibre products along e0,eB,eC and e. The target compatibility hB identifies its structure map with Spec(aB); source compatibility follows from hφ and φ commuting with R. Apply pullbackSpecIso R A B and pullbackSpecIso R A C to identify target and source with Spec(A tensor_R B) and Spec(A tensor_R C). The map is Spec(id tensor φ), because its composites with the A and B projections are the prescribed identity and φ maps. Pullback uniqueness proves equality, giving the arrow isomorphism. This includes zero rings.
