Let $R\to A$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings. For $g\in A$ let $A_g=S^{-1}A$, $S=\{1,g,g^2,\dots\}$, be the localization at $g$, an $R$-algebra via $R\to A\to A_g$. For a prime ideal $\mathfrak q\subset A$, the ring map $R\to A$ is *smooth at $\mathfrak q$* if there is $g\in A\setminus\mathfrak q$ such that $A_g$ is a smooth $R$-algebra (Stacks Project, Algebra, Definition 00TB). Let $G=\{g\in A: A_g\text{ is a smooth }R\text{-algebra}\}$. The *singular ideal of $A$ over $R$* is $H_{A/R}=\sqrt{(G)}\subset A$, the radical of the ideal generated by $G$. The set of primes at which $R\to A$ is smooth is $\bigcup_{g\in G}D(g)$, an open subset of $\operatorname{Spec}A$; its complement is $V(H_{A/R})=\bigcap_{g\in G}V(g)$, the set of primes $\mathfrak q\subset A$ at which $R\to A$ is not smooth; and $H_{A/R}$ is the unique radical ideal of $A$ with this zero locus, which is how Stacks defines it (Definition 07C5).
