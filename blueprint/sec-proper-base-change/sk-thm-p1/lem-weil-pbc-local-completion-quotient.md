Let $(A,\mathfrak m)$ be a Noetherian `IsNoetherianRing` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Noetherian/Defs.lean)) local ring, $\lambda:A\to B$ a surjective homomorphism of commutative rings with kernel $J$, and $\mathfrak n=\lambda(\mathfrak m)$, an ideal of $B$. Let $A^\wedge=\varprojlim_nA/\mathfrak m^n$ be the $\mathfrak m$-adic completion of $A$ and $B^\wedge=\varprojlim_nB/\mathfrak n^n$ the $\mathfrak n$-adic completion of $B$, with canonical maps $c_A:A\to A^\wedge$ and $c_B:B\to B^\wedge$ `AdicCompletion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Basic.lean)). Since $\lambda(\mathfrak m^n)=\mathfrak n^n$, $\lambda$ induces surjections $A/\mathfrak m^n\to B/\mathfrak n^n$ and hence a ring map $\theta:A^\wedge\to B^\wedge$, $(x_n)_n\mapsto(\lambda(x_n))_n$, with $\theta\circ c_A=c_B\circ\lambda$. Then $\theta$ is surjective and its kernel is $JA^\wedge$, the ideal of $A^\wedge$ generated by $c_A(J)$; hence $\theta$ induces a ring isomorphism $A^\wedge/JA^\wedge\xrightarrow{\sim}B^\wedge$.
