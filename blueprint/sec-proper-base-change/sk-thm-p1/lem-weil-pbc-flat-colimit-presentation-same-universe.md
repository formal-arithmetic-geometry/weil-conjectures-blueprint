title = "Flatness from a same-universe filtered colimit presentation"

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $M$ be an $R$-module which is the colimit of a diagram $j\mapsto M_j$ of $R$-modules indexed by a small filtered category $J$ (that is, $M$ is the vertex of a colimit cocone over the diagram), where $J$, $R$, $M$ and the $M_j$ lie in one universe. If every $M_j$ is a flat $R$-module, then $M$ is a flat $R$-module.
