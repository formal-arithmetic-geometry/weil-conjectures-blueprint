Step 1: Module.finrank_baseChange gives dim_K B = dim_L (L tensor_K B). The latter is positive since the tensor product is a nontrivial finite-dimensional L-vector space. Module.finite_of_finrank_pos then gives Module.Finite K B. This uses Mathlib directly and does not choose a basis of B through 1. (`LinearMap.baseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Tower.lean)), `TensorProduct` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Basic.lean)), `Module.finrank` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Dimension/Finrank.lean)), [def-weil-finite-dimensional-subspace]).

Step 2: Algebra.TensorProduct.includeRight_injective, using flatness over the field K and injectivity of K to L, embeds B in L tensor_K B. Transfer IsDomain along this injection; fieldOfFiniteDimensional K B then gives IsField B. (`Algebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Defs.lean)), `fieldOfFiniteDimensional, FiniteDimensional.exists_mul_eq_one` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/FiniteDimensional/Basic.lean))).

Step 3: Choose a K-linear functional f on L with f(1)=1, by Module.Projective.exists_dual_eq_one (vector spaces are free and hence projective). Tensor f with the identity on B and compose with TensorProduct.lid K B. The resulting K-linear map pi sends l tensor b to f(l) times b. This is a fully proved Mathlib existence theorem, not data chosen from an unproved theorem. (`LinearMap.baseChange` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Tower.lean))).

Step 4: Let q be the exponential characteristic of K. Injectivity of K to L gives ExpChar L q. For each b in B, IsPurelyInseparable.pow_mem gives n and l such that (1 tensor b)^(q^n)=l tensor 1. The ring homomorphism includeRight identifies the left side with 1 tensor b^(q^n). Applying pi from step 3 gives b^(q^n)=f(l) times 1, hence membership in the image of K. Apply isPurelyInseparable_iff_pow_mem K q. This simultaneously treats positive characteristic and characteristic zero (q=1). Combine with steps 1 and 2. (`IsPurelyInseparable, Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/PurelyInseparable/Basic.lean)), `Algebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Defs.lean))).
