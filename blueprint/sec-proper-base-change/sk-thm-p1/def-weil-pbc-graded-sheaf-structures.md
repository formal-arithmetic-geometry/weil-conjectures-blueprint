Let $X$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme] and $B=(B_n)_{n\ge0}$, $K=(K_n)_{n\ge0}$ families of sheaves of $\mathcal O_X$-modules `SheafOfModules` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf.lean)). A commutative unital graded sheaf algebra structure on $B$ consists of $\mathcal O_X(U)$-bilinear maps $B_m(U)\times B_n(U)\to B_{m+n}(U)$ for every open $U$, compatible with restriction, associative and commutative, with an $\mathcal O_X$-linear unit $\mathcal O_X\to B_0$ satisfying the two unit laws. A graded sheaf module structure on $K$ over this algebra consists of restriction-compatible $\mathcal O_X(U)$-bilinear maps $B_m(U)\times K_n(U)\to K_{m+n}(U)$ satisfying associativity and the unit law. The underlying sheaves are the direct sums in sheaves of modules; the homogeneous operations extend by bilinearity and sheafification to these sums. Such a module is locally generated by finitely many degree-zero sections if each point has an open neighbourhood $U$ and $s_1,\ldots,s_t\in K_0(U)$ such that, for each $V\subseteq U$, $n\ge0$, $y\in K_n(V)$ and $q\in V$, there is a neighbourhood $q\in W\subseteq V$ and $a_i\in B_n(W)$ with $y|_W=\sum_i a_i(s_i|_W)$. The empty generating family is allowed. This is the local surjectivity criterion for a map from a finite sum of unshifted copies of the graded algebra.
