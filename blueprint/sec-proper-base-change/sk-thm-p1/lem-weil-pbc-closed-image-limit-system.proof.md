Reconstruct ker(i) as the supremum K of I_j.comap(πY_j). The inequality K≤ker(i) is the map/comap Galois connection and map_ker. For the reverse inequality, fix a stage j0 and the affine open cover of D_j0. Projections are affine, so its pullback covers Y by affine opens W. Over an affine open U of D_j0, use the cofinal over-category diagram opensDiagram and its limit opensCone. All of its stages are affine; the section-surjectivity clause of the coordinate-ring colimit theorem, in Mathlib exists_appTop_π_eq_of_isAffine_of_isLimit, lifts any section s of ker(i) on W to a section t on an affine inverse image V in some D_j. Naturality identifies πY_j inverse-image V with W. Since i.app(W)(s)=0, compatibility of appLE with composition and the invertible restriction for this equality of opens show (i followed by πY_j).app(V)(t)=0. Quasi-compactness gives ker_apply, so t belongs to I_j. The Galois connection puts I_j inside K.map(πY_j); ideal_map_of_isAffineHom and restriction of ideals put s inside K(W). IdealSheafData.le_of_iSup_eq_top glues these inclusions. Hence ker(i)=K. For a test cone s on Z, compose with k and use hY.lift to obtain m:s.pt→Y. Each I_j is contained in ker(s.π_j followed by k_j) by le_ker_comp and ker_subschemeι. The Galois connection, the cone equations hY.fac, and ker(i)=K therefore give ker(i)≤ker(m). IsClosedImmersion.lift factors m through i. For a transition a:j→l, its composite with k_l is k_j followed by D(a), which is affine as a composite of affine morphisms. The closed immersion k_l is separated, so Mathlib IsAffineHom.of_comp makes the transition affine. This is the standard separated-target cancellation argument: factor through the graph (a base change of the closed diagonal) followed by the affine base-change projection. The k_j are closed immersions by the subschemeι instance. Together with the constructed limit and factorization equations this proves the stated existential.
