Write $G$ for the group of $F$-algebra automorphisms of $E$ `Algebra` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Algebra/Defs.lean)), so $E_{insep}=\{x\in E:\sigma(x)=x\ \forall\sigma\in G\}$. Each $\sigma\in G$ is a ring automorphism fixing (the image of) $F$, so $F\subseteq E_{insep}$, and if $x,y\in E_{insep}$ then $\sigma(x\pm y)=x\pm y$, $\sigma(xy)=xy$ and, for $x\neq0$, $\sigma(x^{-1})=\sigma(x)^{-1}=x^{-1}$. Hence $E_{insep}$ is a subfield of $E$ containing $F$.

Orbits are root sets. Let $\alpha\in E$, let $P\in F[T]$ be its minimal polynomial over $F$ `Algebra.IsSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)) ($\alpha$ is algebraic over $F$ `IsAlgebraic` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/RingTheory/Algebraic/Defs.lean)) since $E/F$ is algebraic), and let $S_\alpha=\{\beta\in E:P(\beta)=0\}$. Then $\alpha\in S_\alpha$, and $S_\alpha$ is finite, with at most $\deg P$ elements, as $P\neq0$ in $E[T]$. For $\sigma\in G$ and $\beta\in S_\alpha$, applying $\sigma$ to $P(\beta)=0$ (the coefficients of $P$ lie in $F$ and are fixed) gives $P(\sigma(\beta))=0$, so $\sigma$ maps $S_\alpha$ injectively into itself, hence bijectively since $S_\alpha$ is finite. Conversely, for every $\beta\in S_\alpha$ there is $\sigma\in G$ with $\sigma(\alpha)=\beta$ by `minpoly.exists_algEquiv_of_root'` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Normal/Basic.lean)) ($E/F$ is algebraic and normal). Hence $S_\alpha=\{\sigma(\alpha):\sigma\in G\}$, and every $\sigma\in G$ permutes $S_\alpha$.

$E_{insep}/F$ is purely inseparable. Let $\alpha\in E_{insep}$ with minimal polynomial $P$ over $F$, of degree $d\geq1$. By normality $P=\prod_{i=1}^d(T-\alpha_i)$ in $E[T]$ with $\alpha_i\in S_\alpha$; by step 2 each $\alpha_i=\sigma_i(\alpha)$ for some $\sigma_i\in G$, and $\sigma_i(\alpha)=\alpha$ as $\alpha\in E_{insep}$. So $P=(T-\alpha)^d$ in $E[T]$. If $F$ has characteristic $0$: the coefficient of $T^{d-1}$ in $P$ is $-d\alpha\in F$ and $d\neq0$ in $F$, so $\alpha\in F$; hence $E_{insep}=F$. If $F$ has characteristic $p>0$: write $d=p^em$ with $p\nmid m$. In $E[T]$ we have $(T-\alpha)^{p^e}=T^{p^e}-\alpha^{p^e}$ (the binomial coefficients $\binom{p^e}{k}$, $0<k<p^e$, are divisible by $p$), so $P=(T^{p^e}-\alpha^{p^e})^m$, whose coefficient of $T^{p^e(m-1)}$ is $-m\alpha^{p^e}$. This lies in $F$ and $m$ is invertible in $F$, so $\alpha^{p^e}\in F$. In both cases $E_{insep}/F$ is purely inseparable in the sense of `IsPurelyInseparable, Module.Finite` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/PurelyInseparable/Basic.lean)) (in characteristic $0$: $E_{insep}=F$; in characteristic $p$: every element has a $p^e$-th power in $F$).

$E/E_{insep}$ is algebraic and normal: apply `Normal.tower_top_of_normal` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Normal/Defs.lean)) to the tower $F\subseteq E_{insep}\subseteq E$ (step 1), $E/F$ being algebraic and normal.

$E/E_{insep}$ is separable. Let $\alpha\in E$; it is algebraic over $E_{insep}$ by step 4. Let $S_\alpha=\{\beta_1,\dots,\beta_r\}$ be as in step 2, the $\beta_j$ pairwise distinct, and $Q=\prod_{j=1}^r(T-\beta_j)\in E[T]$, monic. For $\sigma\in G$ let $Q^\sigma$ be the polynomial obtained by applying $\sigma$ to the coefficients; $f\mapsto f^\sigma$ is a ring automorphism of $E[T]$ fixing $T$, so $Q^\sigma=\prod_j(T-\sigma(\beta_j))=Q$, because $\sigma$ permutes $S_\alpha$ (step 2). Thus every coefficient of $Q$ is fixed by $G$, i.e. $Q\in E_{insep}[T]$ `Polynomial` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Algebra/Polynomial/Basic.lean)). Since $Q(\alpha)=0$ ($\alpha\in S_\alpha$), the minimal polynomial $R$ of $\alpha$ over $E_{insep}$ `Algebra.IsSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)), the monic generator of the kernel of $E_{insep}[T]\to E$, $T\mapsto\alpha$, divides $Q$ in $E_{insep}[T]$. By `Polynomial.separable_prod_X_sub_C_iff', Polynomial.separable_map, Polynomial.Separable.of_dvd` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)) with $K=E_{insep}$, $L=E$, $Q$ and hence $R$ is separable `Polynomial.Separable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)). So $\alpha$ is separable over $E_{insep}$, and $E/E_{insep}$ is separable algebraic `Algebra.IsSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean)).

By steps 4 and 5, $E/E_{insep}$ is algebraic, separable and normal, i.e. Galois (Definition 09I0). Together with steps 1 and 3 this is the statement. (`Algebra.IsSeparable` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Separable.lean))).
