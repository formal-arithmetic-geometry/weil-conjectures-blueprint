Reduce $\varphi(f)=g'h'$ modulo $IA'$: the image of $\varphi(f)$ is $\alpha(\bar f)=\alpha(g_0)\alpha(h_0)$, and the image of $g'$ is $\alpha(g_0)$, so $\alpha(g_0)\cdot(\bar h'-\alpha(h_0))=0$ in $(A'/IA')[X]$, where $\bar h'$ is the image of $h'$. Since $g_0$ is monic, $\alpha(g_0)$ is monic, hence a nonzerodivisor in $(A'/IA')[X]$; therefore $\bar h'=\alpha(h_0)$.
