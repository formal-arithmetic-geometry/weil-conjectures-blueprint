Set Z_i = range(ι_i) and W_i = complement of the union of the other irreducible components. The cover by Fin(n) makes the set of components finite, hence W_i is open. Mathlib closure_sUnion_irreducibleComponents_sdiff_singleton gives closure(W_i)=Z_i, so W_i is nonempty and contained in Z_i. (`irreducibleComponents` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Irreducible.lean))).

The restriction ι_i over W_i is a surjective closed immersion into a reduced scheme, hence an isomorphism by Mathlib isIso_of_isClosedImmersion_of_surjective. Transfer V_i intersected with the inverse image of W_i through this isomorphism to an open O_i of X. Density of V_i and nonemptiness of the open inverse image of W_i show O_i is nonempty. Since O_i is open, nonempty, and contained in the irreducible Z_i, closure(O_i)=Z_i. Further restriction shows ι_i restricted over O_i is an isomorphism. (`AlgebraicGeometry.IsReduced` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Properties.lean)), `AlgebraicGeometry.IsClosedImmersion` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/ClosedImmersion.lean)), `irreducibleComponents` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Topology/Irreducible.lean))).

Take U = union of O_i. The O_i are disjoint since W_i excludes every other component. Their closures are the components, which cover X, hence U is dense. The inverse image of U in C_i equals the inverse image of O_i and lies in V_i. FinitaryPreExtensive.isUniversal_finiteCoproducts identifies the coproduct of the restricted C_i with the restricted source of Sigma.desc ι. The restricted maps to U are open immersions with disjoint ranges covering U, so Mathlib nonempty_isColimit_cofanMk_of shows their coproduct is an isomorphism. The comparison equation and cancellation give the required isomorphism. The empty family works without a separate branch: hcover forces X empty and U is empty. ([def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions]).
