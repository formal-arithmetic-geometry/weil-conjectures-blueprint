Let $R$ be a reduced commutative ring with finitely many minimal prime ideals. Suppose $R$ is integrally closed in its total ring of fractions $Q(R)$ (the localization of $R$ at its nonzerodivisors). Then for some integer $n\ge 0$ there are commutative integral domains $A_1,\dots,A_n$ and a ring isomorphism $R\simeq\prod_{i=1}^nA_i$. No integral closedness of the factors is asserted.
