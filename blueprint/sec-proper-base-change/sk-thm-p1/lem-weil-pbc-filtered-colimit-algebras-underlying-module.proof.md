Step 1 (underlying ring and type colimits). Prove clause (1) of `CategoryTheory.Limits.Concrete.isColimit_exists_rep, CategoryTheory.Under.preservesColimitsOfShape_forget_of_isConnected, CommAlgCat.commAlgCatEquivUnder, CommRingCat.FilteredColimits.forget_preservesFilteredColimits` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/ConcreteCategory/Basic.lean)) directly, including independent universes: equip each cocone of rings with the R-algebra structure induced by one stage; filteredness makes all stage structure maps agree at a common successor. Apply the algebra colimit universal property `CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean)) to obtain the unique ring map. Mathlib CommRingCat.FilteredColimits.forget_preservesFilteredColimits then gives an IsColimit of the underlying type cocone.

Step 2 (descent of the compatible functions). Regard the linear maps f_i as functions, and obtain their compatibility by taking underlying functions in the hypothesis hf. Apply Functor.CoconeTypes.IsColimit.desc to the type colimit from step 1; this gives g into the arbitrary-universe module P with g(ι_i(a)) = f_i(a). The type colimit universal property encapsulates well-definedness on representatives, without a separate ring equality criterion. (`CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean))).

Step 3 (linearity). By the representability in `CategoryTheory.Limits.Concrete.isColimit_exists_rep, CategoryTheory.Under.preservesColimitsOfShape_forget_of_isConnected, CommAlgCat.commAlgCatEquivUnder, CommRingCat.FilteredColimits.forget_preservesFilteredColimits` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/ConcreteCategory/Basic.lean)) and filteredness, Mathlib Types.FilteredColimit.jointly_surjective_of_isColimit₂ represents any two elements at one stage. Rewrite their sum using additivity of the coprojection, the identities of step 2, and additivity of f_i. For a scalar multiple, represent the element at one stage and use scalar compatibility of the coprojection and f_i. Package g as an R-linear map. (`CategoryTheory.Limits.colimit` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/HasLimits.lean))).

Step 4 (factorization and uniqueness). LinearMap.ext turns the identities of step 2 into equality of composites with every coprojection. Any other linear map with these composites agrees on every representative; every element is a representative by `CategoryTheory.Limits.Concrete.isColimit_exists_rep, CategoryTheory.Under.preservesColimitsOfShape_forget_of_isConnected, CommAlgCat.commAlgCatEquivUnder, CommRingCat.FilteredColimits.forget_preservesFilteredColimits` ([Mathlib source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/ConcreteCategory/Basic.lean)). Hence the descended map is unique.
