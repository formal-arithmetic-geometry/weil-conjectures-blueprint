Fix $i$ and generators $x_{i1},\dots,x_{in}$ of $M_i$ (with $n=n_i$). The $\Lambda$-linear map $\Lambda^n\to M_i$, $(c_j)_j\mapsto\sum_jc_jx_{ij}$, is surjective because the $x_{ij}$ generate $M_i$ (this includes the case $n=0$, $M_i=0$), so it is an epimorphism of $\Lambda$-modules.

The constant presheaf functor preserves epimorphisms (it is computed sectionwise), and sheafification preserves epimorphisms, being a left adjoint. Hence the constant sheaf functor carries this map to an epimorphism $\underline{\Lambda}^n\to\underline{M_i}$ of sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on $(U_i)_{\Et}$. Composing with the inverse of the given isomorphism $\mathcal G|_{U_i}\cong\underline{M_i}$ gives an epimorphism $\Lambda_Y|_{U_i}^{\,n}\to\mathcal G|_{U_i}$ sending the $j$-th basis section to the section corresponding to $x_{ij}$; this is the more precise assertion (compare Stacks 093J).

Doing this for every $i$, the covering $\{U_i\to Y\}$ together with these finite free presentations shows that $\mathcal G$ is locally generated by finitely many sections, i.e. of finite type.
