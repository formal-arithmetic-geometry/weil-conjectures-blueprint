Let X be a scheme, Lambda a commutative ring, and F a constant sheaf of Lambda-modules on the small etale site of X [def-constructible-constant]. If F has finitely generated geometric stalks [def-constructible-finite], there is a finitely generated Lambda-module M and an isomorphism F ≅ M_X. This includes the empty scheme.
