**Step 1.** *Notation and the action as a morphism of sheaves of sets.* Write $\mathcal O=(\mathbf Z/n)_T$, $P$ for the constant presheaf of rings $U\mapsto\mathbf Z/n$ (all restriction maps the identity) and $\eta:P\to\mathcal O$ for the canonical map, a morphism of presheaves of rings, $\mathcal O$ being the sheafification of $P$ in sheaves of rings [def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], [def-weil-pbc-sheafification]; $\iota_U(\bar a)=\eta_U(\bar a)$. For a presheaf $Q$ write $|Q|$ for its underlying presheaf of sets. (a) If $G$ is an abelian sheaf, $|G|$ is a sheaf of sets: the sheaf condition of [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves] says that $G(U)\to\prod_iG(U_i)\rightrightarrows\prod_{i,j}G(U_i\times_UU_j)$ is an equalizer of abelian groups, and products and equalizers of abelian groups are the products and equalizers of the underlying sets; likewise $|\mathcal O|$ is a sheaf of sets. (b) Sectionwise finite products of sheaves of sets, such as $|\mathcal O|^m\times|G_1|\times\dots\times|G_k|$, are sheaves of sets: the inclusion of sheaves into presheaves preserves finite limits and finite limits of presheaves are computed objectwise [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise] (1), (2). (c) By [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category] (with [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves]: $\Mod(T_{\Et},\mathbf Z/n)$ is the category of sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules), a sheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules $G$ is an abelian sheaf with $\mathcal O(U)$-module structures on the $G(U)$ extending the addition and compatible with restriction, $(rx)|_V=r|_Vx|_V$; this compatibility says exactly that the action maps $\mu_{G,U}:\mathcal O(U)\times G(U)\to G(U)$ form a morphism of presheaves of sets $\mu_G:|\mathcal O|\times|G|\to|G|$. Conversely an abelian sheaf $F$ together with a morphism of presheaves of sets $\mu:|\mathcal O|\times|F|\to|F|$ such that each $\mu_U$ is an $\mathcal O(U)$-module structure on $F(U)$ extending its addition is a sheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules; and a morphism of abelian sheaves between two sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules is a morphism of sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules iff each component is $\mathcal O(U)$-linear [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category].

**Step 2.** *Extension and uniqueness along $\eta$.* Let $m\ge0$, $G_1,\dots,G_k$ sheaves of sets on $T_{\Et}$, and $\eta^m\times\mathrm{id}:P^m\times G_1\times\dots\times G_k\to|\mathcal O|^m\times G_1\times\dots\times G_k$ (products sectionwise; here $P$ stands for $|P|$). Claim: this map is a sheafification of the presheaf of sets $P^m\times\prod_jG_j$; hence for every sheaf of sets $H$, (a) two morphisms of presheaves $\alpha,\beta:|\mathcal O|^m\times\prod_jG_j\to H$ with $\alpha\circ(\eta^m\times\mathrm{id})=\beta\circ(\eta^m\times\mathrm{id})$ are equal, and (b) every morphism of presheaves $P^m\times\prod_jG_j\to H$ is $\alpha\circ(\eta^m\times\mathrm{id})$ for exactly one $\alpha$. Proof: $|\eta|:|P|\to|\mathcal O|$ is a sheafification of the presheaf of sets $|P|$ [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheafification-underlying-sets]; for a sheaf of sets $G$, $\mathrm{id}_G$ is a sheafification of $G$ (every morphism $G\to H$ to a sheaf factors uniquely through $\mathrm{id}_G$, which is the universal property of [def-weil-pbc-sheafification]). Sheafification of presheaves of sets commutes with finite products via the projections [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-sets-exact]: for presheaves $Q_1,\dots,Q_r$ with sheafifications $u_i:Q_i\to Q_i^\#$, the map $(\mathrm{pr}_1^\#,\dots,\mathrm{pr}_r^\#):(\prod Q_i)^\#\to\prod Q_i^\#$ is an isomorphism, and composed with the unit $u:\prod Q_i\to(\prod Q_i)^\#$ it equals $\prod u_i$, since $\mathrm{pr}_i^\#\circ u=u_i\circ\mathrm{pr}_i$ (naturality of the unit). Hence $\prod u_i:\prod Q_i\to\prod Q_i^\#$ is itself a sheafification. Apply this to $Q_i=|P|$ ($m$ times) and $Q_i=G_j$; the target is a sheaf by step 1 (b). (a) and (b) are the uniqueness and existence in the universal property [def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. (The small etale site is essentially small and sheafification exists on it, as recorded in the Mathlib matches of the cited nodes.)

**Step 3.** *Constants act by integers in every module sheaf.* Let $G$ be a sheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules, $U$ an object, $a\in\mathbf Z$ and $s\in G(U)$. Since $\eta_U:\mathbf Z/n\to\mathcal O(U)$ is a ring homomorphism, $\eta_U(\bar a)=a\cdot1_{\mathcal O(U)}$, and in the $\mathcal O(U)$-module $G(U)$ we have $(a\cdot1)s=as$ [lem-weil-pbc-zmod-module-structure-killed-by-n] (3). So $\mu_G\circ(\eta\times\mathrm{id})=\mu^0_G$, where $\mu^0_G:P\times|G|\to|G|$, $(\bar a,s)\mapsto as$. This proves the last assertion of (2) for any module structure.

**Step 4.** *(1).* Let $G$ be a sheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules and $s\in G(U)$. By step 3 with $a=n$: $ns=\eta_U(\bar n)s=\eta_U(0)s=0\cdot s=0$. So $n\cdot\mathrm{id}=0$ on $\mathrm{forget}(G)$, the endomorphism $n\cdot\mathrm{id}$ being sectionwise multiplication by $n$.

**Step 5.** *(2), existence.* Let $F$ be an abelian sheaf with $n\cdot\mathrm{id}_F=0$, i.e. $ns=0$ for every section. By [lem-weil-pbc-zmod-module-structure-killed-by-n] (1) each $F(U)$ is a $\mathbf Z/n$-module by $\bar a\cdot s=as$, and restriction maps, being additive, commute with integer multiples; so $\mu^0:P\times|F|\to|F|$, $(\bar a,s)\mapsto as$, is a morphism of presheaves of sets. By step 2 (b) ($m=1$, $G_1=H=|F|$) there is a unique morphism $\mu:|\mathcal O|\times|F|\to|F|$ with $\mu\circ(\eta\times\mathrm{id})=\mu^0$. The module axioms hold, each by step 2 (a), comparing two morphisms of presheaves of sets into the sheaf $|F|$ (all are morphisms since the sectionwise addition of $F$ and the sectionwise ring operations of $\mathcal O$ commute with restriction) after composition with $\eta^m\times\mathrm{id}$, using that $\eta_U$ is a ring homomorphism and $\mu(\eta_U(\bar a),x)=ax$ for all $x\in F(U)$: (i) $1_{\mathcal O(U)}=\eta_U(\bar1)$, so $\mu(1,s)=1s=s$ directly; (ii) $|\mathcal O|\times|F|^2\to|F|$, $(r,s,t)\mapsto\mu(r,s+t)$ and $\mu(r,s)+\mu(r,t)$: on $(\bar a,s,t)$ both give $a(s+t)=as+at$; (iii) $|\mathcal O|^2\times|F|\to|F|$, $(r,r',s)\mapsto\mu(r+r',s)$ and $\mu(r,s)+\mu(r',s)$: on $(\bar a,\bar b,s)$, $\mu(\eta(\bar a+\bar b),s)=(a+b)s=as+bs$; (iv) $(r,r',s)\mapsto\mu(rr',s)$ and $\mu(r,\mu(r',s))$: on $(\bar a,\bar b,s)$, $\mu(\eta(\bar a\bar b),s)=(ab)s=a(bs)=\mu(\eta(\bar a),\mu(\eta(\bar b),s))$. Hence each $\mu_U$ is an $\mathcal O(U)$-module structure on $F(U)$ extending its addition, compatible with restriction ($\mu$ is a morphism of presheaves), and by step 1 (c) $F$ with $\mu$ is a sheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules $\tilde F$ with $\mathrm{forget}(\tilde F)=F$, in which $\iota_U(\bar a)$ acts as $a$ ($\mu\circ(\eta\times\mathrm{id})=\mu^0$).

**Step 6.** *(2), uniqueness.* If $\mu'$ is the action of any structure of sheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules on $F$ extending its abelian structure, step 3 gives $\mu'\circ(\eta\times\mathrm{id})=\mu^0=\mu\circ(\eta\times\mathrm{id})$, both being morphisms $|\mathcal O|\times|F|\to|F|$ of presheaves of sets (step 1 (c)) into the sheaf $|F|$; by step 2 (a) $\mu'=\mu$.

**Step 7.** *(3).* Let $G,G'$ be sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules and $\varphi:\mathrm{forget}(G)\to\mathrm{forget}(G')$ a morphism of abelian sheaves. The maps $\alpha(r,s)=\varphi_U(\mu_G(r,s))$ and $\beta(r,s)=\mu_{G'}(r,\varphi_U(s))$ are morphisms of presheaves of sets $|\mathcal O|\times|G|\to|G'|$ (naturality of $\varphi$, $\mu_G$, $\mu_{G'}$), and $|G'|$ is a sheaf (step 1 (a)). By step 3, $\alpha(\eta_U(\bar a),s)=\varphi_U(as)$ and $\beta(\eta_U(\bar a),s)=a\varphi_U(s)$, which are equal since $\varphi_U$ is additive (it is $\mathbf Z/n$-linear for the module structures of step 3 by [lem-weil-pbc-zmod-module-structure-killed-by-n] (4)). By step 2 (a), $\alpha=\beta$: every $\varphi_U$ is $\mathcal O(U)$-linear, so $\varphi$ is a morphism of sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules (step 1 (c)).

**Step 8.** *Consequences.* $\mathrm{forget}$ is faithful: a morphism of sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules is a morphism of abelian sheaves with a property [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], and $\mathrm{forget}$ is the identity on morphisms. It is full by (3). Its image: $\mathrm{forget}(G)$ is killed by $n$ by (1); conversely an abelian sheaf $F$ killed by $n$ equals $\mathrm{forget}(\tilde F)$ by (2); and being killed by $n$ is invariant under isomorphism of abelian sheaves ($\varphi\circ n\,\mathrm{id}=n\,\mathrm{id}\circ\varphi$, $\varphi$ additive), so the essential image is the full subcategory of abelian sheaves killed by $n$. Finally an isomorphism $\varphi$ of abelian sheaves between $\mathrm{forget}(G)$ and $\mathrm{forget}(G')$ and its inverse are both $\mathcal O$-linear by (3), so $\varphi$ is an isomorphism of sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules.
