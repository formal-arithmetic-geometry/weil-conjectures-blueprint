For a scheme $S$ [def-pc-scheme], define $C(S)$ to be the following proposition. For every nonempty directed preordered set $I$ in the fixed universe, inverse diagram $D:I^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathrm{Sch}$ of quasi-compact quasi-separated schemes with affine transition morphisms [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions], limiting cone $(X,f_i)$, and system of abelian etale sheaves $F_i$ with maps $\varphi_f:D(f^{\mathrm{op}})^{-1}F_i\to F_j$ satisfying the canonical inverse-image identity and composition laws [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], quantify over the following supplied data. The functor $G:I\to\mathrm{Ab}(X_{\acute et})$ has isomorphisms $e_{G,i}:G_i\cong f_i^{-1}F_i$, and its arrows are conjugates of inverse-image composition followed by inverse image of $\varphi_f$. Let $L$ be a colimit cocone on $G$. For each degree $p\geq0$, a functor $H:I\to\mathrm{Ab}$ has isomorphisms $e_{H,i}:H_i\cong H^p(D_i,F_i)$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-cohomology] with arrows conjugate to pullback followed by $H^p(\varphi_f)$ [def-weil-pbc-cohomology-pullback]. Let $B$ be a cocone on $H$ with $e_B:B_{\mathrm{pt}}\cong H^p(X,L_{\mathrm{pt}})$ whose leg at $i$, composed with $e_B$, is $e_{H,i}$ followed by $f_i^*$ and $H^p(e_{G,i}^{-1}\circ\iota_i)$. Whenever a specified stage $i_0$ admits an isomorphism $D_{i_0}\cong S$, $B$ is a colimit cocone. The proposition quantifies over all these data and does not choose any diagram, sheaf, witness or colimit.
