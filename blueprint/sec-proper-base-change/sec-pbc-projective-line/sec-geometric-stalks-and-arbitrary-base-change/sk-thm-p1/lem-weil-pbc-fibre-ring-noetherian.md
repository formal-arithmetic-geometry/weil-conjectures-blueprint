Let $A\to B$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings with $B$ Noetherian, and let $\mathfrak p\subset A$ be a prime ideal with residue field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$. Then the fibre ring $B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ is Noetherian.
