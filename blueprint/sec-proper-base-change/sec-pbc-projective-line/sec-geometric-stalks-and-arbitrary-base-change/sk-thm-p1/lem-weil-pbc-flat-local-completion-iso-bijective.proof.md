*Step 1 ($\hat\varphi$ is local).* The completions of the Noetherian local rings $A$ and $B$ are local with maximal ideals $\mathfrak m_A\hat A$ and $\mathfrak m_B\hat B$. Compatibility of $\hat\varphi$ with $\varphi$ and $\varphi(\mathfrak m_A)B=\mathfrak m_B$ give $\hat\varphi(\mathfrak m_A\hat A)\hat B=\mathfrak m_B\hat B$; in particular $\hat\varphi$ is a local homomorphism.

*Step 2 (injectivity).* A flat local homomorphism of local rings is faithfully flat, and a faithfully flat ring map is injective.

*Step 3 (surjectivity modulo the maximal ideal).* Let $s\in\hat B$. Its image in $\hat B/\mathfrak m_B\hat B=B/\mathfrak m_B$ is represented by some $b\in B$; by bijectivity of the residue field map there is $a\in A$ with $\varphi(a)\equiv b$ modulo $\mathfrak m_B$. Compatibility gives $s-\hat\varphi(\iota_A(a))\in\mathfrak m_B\hat B=\hat\varphi(\mathfrak m_A\hat A)\hat B$. Hence $\hat A\to\hat B/\hat\varphi(\mathfrak m_A\hat A)\hat B$ is surjective.

*Step 4 (surjectivity).* $\hat A$ is $\mathfrak m_A\hat A$-adically complete and $\hat B$ is $\mathfrak m_B\hat B$-adically separated, where by Step 1 $\mathfrak m_B\hat B$ is the ideal generated by the image of $\mathfrak m_A\hat A$. By successive approximation, a ring map from a complete ring to a separated ring which is surjective modulo the extended ideal is surjective. With Step 2, $\hat\varphi$ is bijective. No finiteness of $\hat B$ over $\hat A$ is used.
