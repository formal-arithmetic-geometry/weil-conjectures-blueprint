title = "A normal ring is integrally closed in its total ring of fractions"

Let $R$ be a commutative normal ring. Let $S$ be the set of nonzerodivisors of $R$ and $Q(R)=S^{-1}R$ its total ring of fractions. Then every element of $Q(R)$ integral over $R$ lies in the image of the localization map $R\to Q(R)$; equivalently, $R$ is integrally closed in $Q(R)$.
