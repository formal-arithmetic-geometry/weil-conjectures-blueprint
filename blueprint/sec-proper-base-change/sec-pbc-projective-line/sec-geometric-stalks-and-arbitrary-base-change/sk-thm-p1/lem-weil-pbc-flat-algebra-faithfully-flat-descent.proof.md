For an injective A-linear map u: N → P, flatness of C over A makes C ⊗_A u injective. The flatness criterion reduces flatness of B over A to injectivity of B ⊗_A u.

The natural tensor-cancellation equivalences C ⊗_B (B ⊗_A N) ≃ C ⊗_A N and the corresponding equivalence for P intertwine C ⊗_B (B ⊗_A u) with C ⊗_A u. Hence the former is injective.

Faithful flatness of C over B reflects injectivity after tensoring, so B ⊗_A u is injective. Since u was arbitrary, B is flat over A.
