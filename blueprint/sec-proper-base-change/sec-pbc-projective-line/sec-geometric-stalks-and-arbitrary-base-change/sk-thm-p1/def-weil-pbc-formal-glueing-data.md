Let $\varphi:R\to S$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings and $f_1,\dots,f_t\in R$. Notation: for an $R$-module $P$ and $f\in R$, $P_f$ is the localization of $P$ at $f$ and $h_f:P_f\to Q_f$, $p/f^e\mapsto h(p)/f^e$, the localization of an $R$-linear map $h:P\to Q$ (for the multiplicative set $\{1,f,f^2,\dots\}$); $R_f$ is the localized ring. For an $S$-module $P$, $P_f$ is its localization as an $R$-module, made an $S$-module by the localizations $(s\cdot)_f$ of the multiplications by the elements $s\in S$. $P\otimes_RS$ and $h\otimes_RS$ are the base changes. For an $R$-module $P$ and $f,g\in R$ two canonical $R$-linear maps are used: $\sigma^P_{f,g}:(P_f)_g\to(P_g)_f$, $(p/f^a)/g^b\mapsto(p/g^b)/f^a$, and $c^P_f:(P\otimes_RS)_f\to P_f\otimes_RS$, $(p\otimes s)/f^e\mapsto(p/f^e)\otimes s$ (an $S$-linear map); $\sigma^P_{f,g}$ is the $R$-linear map with $(p/1)/1\mapsto(p/1)/1$ and $c^P_f$ the $R$-linear map with $(p\otimes s)/1\mapsto(p/1)\otimes s$.

The category $\mathrm{Glue}(R\to S,f_1,\dots,f_t)$ of *glueing data* has as objects the systems $\mathbf M=(M',(M_i)_{i},(\alpha_i)_{i},(\alpha_{ij})_{i,j})$, $1\le i,j\le t$, where $M'$ is an $S$-module, $M_i$ is an $R_{f_i}$-module (regarded as an $R$-module through $R\to R_{f_i}$), $\alpha_i:M'_{f_i}\to M_i\otimes_RS$ is an $S$-linear isomorphism and $\alpha_{ij}:(M_i)_{f_j}\to(M_j)_{f_i}$ is an $R$-linear isomorphism, such that
(a) $(\alpha_{ij}\otimes_RS)\circ c^{M_i}_{f_j}\circ(\alpha_i)_{f_j}=c^{M_j}_{f_i}\circ(\alpha_j)_{f_i}\circ\sigma^{M'}_{f_i,f_j}$ as maps $(M'_{f_i})_{f_j}\to(M_j)_{f_i}\otimes_RS$, for all $i,j$; and
(b) (cocycle condition) $(\alpha_{jk})_{f_i}\circ\sigma^{M_j}_{f_i,f_k}\circ(\alpha_{ij})_{f_k}=\sigma^{M_k}_{f_i,f_j}\circ(\alpha_{ik})_{f_j}\circ\sigma^{M_i}_{f_j,f_k}$ as maps $((M_i)_{f_j})_{f_k}\to((M_k)_{f_j})_{f_i}$, for all $i,j,k$.
A morphism $\mathbf M\to\mathbf N=(N',(N_i),(\beta_i),(\beta_{ij}))$ is a family $(u',(u_i)_i)$ of an $S$-linear map $u':M'\to N'$ and $R$-linear maps $u_i:M_i\to N_i$ such that $\beta_i\circ u'_{f_i}=(u_i\otimes_RS)\circ\alpha_i$ and $\beta_{ij}\circ(u_i)_{f_j}=(u_j)_{f_i}\circ\alpha_{ij}$ for all $i,j$. Composition and identities are componentwise: $(v',(v_i))\circ(u',(u_i))=(v'\circ u',(v_i\circ u_i))$ and $\mathrm{id}_{\mathbf M}=(\mathrm{id}_{M'},(\mathrm{id}_{M_i}))$; $\mathrm{Glue}(R\to S,f_1,\dots,f_t)$ is a category. (An $R$-linear map between modules on which $f$ acts bijectively is $R_f$-linear, so the $u_i$, $\alpha_i$, $\alpha_{ij}$ are linear over $R_{f_i}$, $S_{f_i}$, $R_{f_if_j}$ respectively, as in the source.) (Stacks writes conditions (a) and (b) as $\alpha_{ij}\circ\alpha_i=\alpha_j$ and $\alpha_{jk}\circ\alpha_{ij}=\alpha_{ik}$, leaving the maps $c$ and $\sigma$ implicit.)
