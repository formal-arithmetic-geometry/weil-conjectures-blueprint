As in the domain case, $R$ is a domain: it is nonzero (a filtered colimit of nonzero rings) and a relation $xy=0$ is realized at some stage, where it forces $x$ or $y$ to vanish. Let $z$ in the fraction field of $R$ be integral over $R$; write $z=a/b$ with $b\neq0$ and $z^n+c_1z^{n-1}+\dots+c_n=0$, i.e. $a^n+c_1a^{n-1}b+\dots+c_nb^n=0$ in $R$. By filteredness, $a,b,c_1,\dots,c_n$ lift to elements $a_i,b_i,c_{k,i}$ of one $D_i$ satisfying the same relation, and $b_i\neq0$ since $b\neq0$. Then $a_i/b_i$ is integral over $D_i$, so $a_i=d\,b_i$ for some $d\in D_i$ by integral closedness of $D_i$; mapping to $R$ gives $a=\bar d\,b$, i.e. $z=\bar d\in R$.
