Choose finitely many affine opens $U_1,\ldots,U_n$ of $X$ whose base changes $U_{i,A}=U_i\times_{\operatorname{Spec}k}\operatorname{Spec}A$, identified with the open subschemes $\mathrm{pr}_X^{-1}(U_i)$ of $X_A$ (the base change of an open immersion is an open immersion, with image the preimage of the open), cover $V$: affine opens form a basis of a scheme, and the resulting cover of the quasi-compact space $V$ has a finite subcover. Write $U_i=\operatorname{Spec}(R_i)$.

For each $i$, the principal opens in the affine scheme $U_{i,A}\cong\operatorname{Spec}(R_i\otimes_k A)$ (a fibre product of affine schemes is $\operatorname{Spec}$ of the tensor product) form a basis. Applying this basis to the open $V$ and using quasi-compactness of $V$, choose finitely many elements $f_{ij}\in R_i\otimes_k A$ such that $V=\bigcup_{i,j}D(f_{ij})\subseteq\bigcup_iU_{i,A}$. This does not assert that $V\cap U_{i,A}$ is quasi-compact; it only uses a finite subcover of the basis of $V$.

Each of the finitely many tensors $f_{ij}$ involves only finitely many coefficients of $A$. Let $A'\subseteq A$ be the $k$-subalgebra generated by all those coefficients. Then $A'$ is finitely generated over $k$, and every $f_{ij}$ is the image of an element $f'_{ij}\in R_i\otimes_k A'$. Define $V'=\bigcup_{i,j}D(f'_{ij})\subseteq X_{A'}$. It is a quasi-compact open because it is a finite union of affine principal opens.

Let $\pi:X_A\to X_{A'}$ be induced by $A'\hookrightarrow A$. Under the identifications of steps 1 and 2 (for $A$ and for $A'$), $\pi^{-1}(U_{i,A'})=U_{i,A}$ and $\pi|_{U_{i,A}}$ is $\operatorname{Spec}$ of $R_i\otimes_kA'\to R_i\otimes_kA$, so base change carries each $D(f_{ij})$ into $D(f'_{ij})$, so $V\subseteq\pi^{-1}(V')$. Conversely, if $x\in X_A$ has $\pi(x)\in V'$, then $\pi(x)\in D(f'_{ij})$ for some $i,j$; the image of $f'_{ij}$ is $f_{ij}$, hence $x\in D(f_{ij})\subseteq V$. Thus $V=\pi^{-1}(V')$.
