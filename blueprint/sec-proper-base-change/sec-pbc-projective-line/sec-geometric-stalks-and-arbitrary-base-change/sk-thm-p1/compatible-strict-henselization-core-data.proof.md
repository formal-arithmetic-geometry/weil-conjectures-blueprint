Let $A$ and $B$ be the colimits of the coordinate rings of the affine pointed étale neighbourhoods of $\bar s$ and
$\bar t$; their spectra are the strict localizations, which gives $e_A$ and $e_B$. Each is a strictly henselian
local ring whose residue field is the separable closure of the residue field of the image point inside $\Omega$
(Stacks Project, Tags 04HX and 03PS). Since $k$ and $k'$ are separably closed, these separable closures lie in the
images of $k$ and $k'$, which gives $\rho_A$, $\rho_B$ and the equalities (c). The integrality hypotheses make $k$
and $k'$ algebraic over the residue fields of $s_0$ and $t_0$, so $k$ and $k'$ are purely inseparable over
$\kappa_A$ and $\kappa_B$, and $\rho_A$, $\rho_B$ are universal homeomorphisms (Tag 04DY). Functoriality of strict
henselization (Tag 04GU), applied to $\sigma\iota\nu=g\tau\nu$, gives the local homomorphism $\varphi$ and the
square (b).
