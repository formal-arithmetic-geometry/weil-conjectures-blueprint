Map the affine fibre product $\operatorname{Spec}B\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}A$ to $Z\times_ST$ using the specified presentations and the chart inclusions. The two compatibility equations make this a map of diagrams. It is an open immersion with image $p^{-1}(V)\cap q^{-1}(t^{-1}(W))$, by the open fibre-product lemma. The containment $V\subseteq z^{-1}(W)$ and $z\circ p=t\circ q$ imply $p^{-1}(V)\subseteq q^{-1}(t^{-1}(W))$; hence the image is $p^{-1}(V)$. The two open immersions with this same image give an isomorphism with the inverse-image open subscheme.

Compose the inverse of the open-subscheme isomorphism with the symmetry of the fibre product and the identification $\operatorname{Spec}A\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}B\cong\operatorname{Spec}(A\otimes_RB)$. The projection equations of the induced map of fibre products and of this identification show that its inverse projects to $\iota_V\circ e_B^{-1}\circ\operatorname{Spec}(b\mapsto1\otimes b)$ and $\iota_{t^{-1}(W)}\circ e^{-1}\circ\operatorname{Spec}(a\mapsto a\otimes1)$, respectively. No nonzero-ring or nonempty-chart hypothesis is used.
