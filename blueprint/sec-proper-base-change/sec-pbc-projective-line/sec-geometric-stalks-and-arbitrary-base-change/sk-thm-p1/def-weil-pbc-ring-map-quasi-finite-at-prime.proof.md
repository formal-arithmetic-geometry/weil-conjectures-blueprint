*The definition is well formed.* $\mathfrak p=\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$ is a prime ideal of $R$ (the preimage of a prime ideal under a ring map), so $R_{\mathfrak p}$, the field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ and the fibre ring $F=S\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ with $j$ are defined. The map $\mathfrak Q\mapsto j^{-1}(\mathfrak Q)$ is a homeomorphism from $\operatorname{Spec}F$ onto the subspace of primes of $S$ lying over $\mathfrak p$; since $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)=\mathfrak p$, there is exactly one prime $\overline{\mathfrak q}$ of $F$ with $j^{-1}(\overline{\mathfrak q})=\mathfrak q$, and no choice is made. So 'quasi-finite at $\mathfrak q$' is a property of the pair $(\varphi,\mathfrak q)$.
