Put $h=p\circ g'=g\circ p'$. By clause (2) of [lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction], under $\rho$ the unit of $p^{-1}\dashv p_*$ is restriction of sections; restricting this description along the diagonal $W\to W\times_XW$ (an open immersion, $p$ being étale) expresses $\rho_F(W)^{-1}$ as the unit $\eta_{p,F}$ at $W$ followed by restriction along the diagonal, and likewise $\rho_{g^{-1}F}(W')^{-1}\circ(\text{restriction along }\iota)$ through the unit of $p'^{-1}\dashv p'_*$ restricted along $(\iota,\mathrm{id})$. By clause (2) of [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-composition], the composition isomorphism $g'^{-1}p^{-1}\cong h^{-1}$ is compatible with the units: the unit of $h^{-1}\dashv h_*$ is the composite of the units for $p$ and for $g'$, followed by the composition isomorphism. Evaluating at $W$ and restricting along $W'\to W\times_XW'$, $w'\mapsto(g'(w'),w')$, identifies the homomorphism in 1, followed by the inverse of $p'^{-1}g^{-1}F\cong h^{-1}F$, with the unit of $h$ restricted to $W'$ and precomposed with $\rho_F(W)^{-1}$. The same computation for the factorization $h=g\circ p'$ identifies the homomorphism in 2, followed by the same isomorphism, with the same map. Cancelling the composition isomorphism gives the equality. Étaleness of $p'$ follows from stability of étale morphisms under base change, as $\iota$ is an isomorphism.
