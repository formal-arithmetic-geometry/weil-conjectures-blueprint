Let $x\in X$. The closure of the irreducible set $\{x\}$ is contained in an irreducible component $Z$ of $X$. By the cover hypothesis $Z$ is the image of some $\iota_i$, but the index set is empty, a contradiction. Hence $X$ has no points. Take $U=\varnothing$: it is dense in the empty space $X$, and the restriction of $\coprod_iY_i\to X$ over $U$ is a morphism between empty schemes, hence an isomorphism. No gluing of the components is needed.
