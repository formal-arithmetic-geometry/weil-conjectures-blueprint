Every nonzero integer has nonzero residue, hence is a unit in R. The localization universal property for Q as the fraction ring of Z gives R a Q-algebra structure.

Choose a transcendence basis of the residue field over Q. Its intermediate rational function field has characteristic zero and is perfect, and the residue field is algebraic and thus separable over it. A separably generated extension of a field of characteristic zero is formally smooth, so the residue field is formally smooth over $\mathbf Q$.

Apply formal smoothness to the identity of R/m, a Q-algebra map by uniqueness of ring maps out of Q: lifting successively through the square-zero extensions R/m^(n+1) -> R/m^n and passing to the inverse limit, which is R by completeness, gives a ring map R/m -> R whose reduction is the identity.
