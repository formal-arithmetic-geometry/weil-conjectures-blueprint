title = "A power series ring over a Noetherian normal domain is a Noetherian normal domain"
lean = ["PowerSeries.noetherian_normal_domain"]
order = 2022

Let $R$ be a Noetherian normal domain, and let $R[[x]]$ be the ring of formal power series in one variable $x$ over $R$. Then $R[[x]]$ is a Noetherian normal domain: it is Noetherian, an integral domain, and every element of its fraction field which is a root of a monic polynomial with coefficients in $R[[x]]$ lies in $R[[x]]$.
