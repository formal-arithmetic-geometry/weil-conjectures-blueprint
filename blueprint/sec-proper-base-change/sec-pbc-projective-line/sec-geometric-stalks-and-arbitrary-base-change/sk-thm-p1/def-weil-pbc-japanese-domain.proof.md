Specialize the N-2 condition to $L=K$: $K$ is a finite extension of itself, and its $R$-algebra structure is the composite $R\subseteq K\xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}}K$. The N-2 condition then says that the integral closure of $R$ in $K$ is a finitely generated $R$-module, which is the N-1 condition.
