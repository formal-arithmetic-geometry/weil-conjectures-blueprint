Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $Q(R)$ its localization at the set of nonzerodivisors (total ring of fractions), $x\in Q(R)$ integral over $R$, and $\mathfrak p$ a prime ideal of $R$ such that $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is an integrally closed domain. Then there are $s\in R\setminus\mathfrak p$ and $a\in R$ such that $sx=a$ in $Q(R)$.
