Let $F$ be a field and $E/F$ a field extension which is algebraic and normal: every $\alpha\in E$ is algebraic over $F$ and its minimal polynomial over $F$ is a product of polynomials of degree one in $E[T]$. Let $E_{insep}=\{x\in E:\sigma(x)=x$ for every $F$-algebra automorphism $\sigma$ of $E\}$. Then $E_{insep}$ is a subfield of $E$ containing $F$, the extension $E_{insep}/F$ is purely inseparable, and the extension $E/E_{insep}$ is separable algebraic and normal (i.e. Galois).
