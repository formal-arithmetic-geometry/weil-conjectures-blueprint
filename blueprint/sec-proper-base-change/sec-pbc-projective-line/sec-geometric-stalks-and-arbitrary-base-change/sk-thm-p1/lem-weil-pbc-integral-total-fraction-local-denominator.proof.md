Map Q(R) into the fraction field K of R_p. Nonzerodivisors remain nonzero in R_p by the localization equality criterion, hence become units in K. Map the integral equation and use integral closedness to write the image of x as an element z of R_p.

Write x=a/b and z=c/t, with b regular and t outside p. Equality in K implies ta=bc in R_p, since R_p embeds in K. A multiplier u outside p clears this equality in R. Cancel the unit image of b in Q(R) to get (ut)x=uc, so s=ut and a=uc work.
