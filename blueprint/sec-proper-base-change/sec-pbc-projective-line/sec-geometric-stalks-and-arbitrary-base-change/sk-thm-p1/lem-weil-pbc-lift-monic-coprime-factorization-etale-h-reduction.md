Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $I\subseteq A$ an ideal, $f\in A[X]$, and $g_0,h_0\in(A/I)[X]$ monic with $\bar f=g_0h_0$ in $(A/I)[X]$. Let $\varphi:A\to A'$ be a ring map, $\alpha:A/I\to A'/IA'$ the induced map, and $g',h'\in A'[X]$ with $\varphi(f)=g'h'$ such that the image of $g'$ in $(A'/IA')[X]$ is $\alpha(g_0)$. Then the image of $h'$ in $(A'/IA')[X]$ is $\alpha(h_0)$.
