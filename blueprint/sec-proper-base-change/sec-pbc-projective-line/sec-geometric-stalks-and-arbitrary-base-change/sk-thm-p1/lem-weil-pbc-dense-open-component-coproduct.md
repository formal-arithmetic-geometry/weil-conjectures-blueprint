Let X be a reduced scheme, and let ι_i:C_i→X, indexed by Fin(n), be closed immersions from integral schemes whose images enumerate without repetitions the irreducible components. For dense opens V_i⊆C_i, there is a dense open U⊆X such that the component coproduct morphism [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions] restricted to U is an isomorphism and ι_i^{-1}(U)⊆V_i for every i.
