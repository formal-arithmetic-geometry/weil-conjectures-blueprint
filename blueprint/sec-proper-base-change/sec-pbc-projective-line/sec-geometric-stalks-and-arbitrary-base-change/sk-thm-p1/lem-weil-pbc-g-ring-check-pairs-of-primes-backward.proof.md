Let $\mathfrak p$ be a prime of $R$. The completion map $R_{\mathfrak p}\to(R_{\mathfrak p})^\wedge$ of the Noetherian local ring $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is flat. Its fibre over a prime $\mathfrak qR_{\mathfrak p}$, $\mathfrak q\subseteq\mathfrak p$, is identified, by the same quotient formal-fibre identification as in the forward implication, with $F_{\mathfrak q,\mathfrak p}$ as a $\kappa(\mathfrak q)$-algebra, hence is Noetherian and geometrically regular over $\kappa(\mathfrak q)$ by hypothesis. A flat map whose fibres are Noetherian and geometrically regular is regular, so each localization-to-completion map is regular and $R$ is a G-ring.
