Proof: The section is surjective: r is the image of the structure-map value of r.
Its kernel is finitely generated by finite presentation of both R-algebras. Give R
its S-algebra structure through s. The algebra-homomorphism scalar tower and formal
étale cancellation show that R is formally étale over S. For a surjective formally
étale map, the kernel is an idempotent ideal. The finitely generated idempotent ideal
criterion supplies f with f*f=f and ker(s)=(f). Set e=1-f. Then e is idempotent,
s(f)=0, s(e)=1, and ker(s)=(1-e).

Put C=f.Corner and q(x)=f*x. Since f is a central idempotent, q is a unital ring
homomorphism into C, whose unit is f. Give C its R-algebra structure by q composed
with the structure map R to S. Thus its algebra map is r ↦ f*algebraMap R S r.
The R-algebra map Φ(x)=(s(x),q(x)) is injective: a difference z in its kernel lies
in (f), hence f*z=z, whereas its second coordinate gives f*z=0. It is surjective:
for (r,y) take e*algebraMap R S r+y. Corner membership gives f*y=y, so s(y)=0;
f*e=0 and s(e)=1 give precisely the two required coordinates. Taking the algebra
equivalence of this bijective map proves the claim, with first projection s.
