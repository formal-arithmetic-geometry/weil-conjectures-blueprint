On complexes the identity functor preserves the defining kernel and cokernel diagrams identically, so its homology comparison is the homology of the complex identity comparison.

Use the localization factorization equations and natTrans_ext to extend the complex equality to derived objects, then restrict to D⁺. The compatibility isomorphisms cancel.
