Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $S$ an $R$-algebra. Let $\alpha:P\to S$ and $\alpha':P'\to S$ be surjective homomorphisms of $R$-algebras, where $P=R[x_t,\ t\in T]$ and $P'=R[x'_u,\ u\in U]$ are polynomial rings over $R$ on arbitrary sets of variables; let $I=\ker\alpha$, $I'=\ker\alpha'$. Then $I/I^2$ is an $S=P/I$-module and $I'/I'^2$ an $S=P'/I'$-module, and the maps $\mathrm d_\alpha:I/I^2\to S\otimes_P\Omega_{P/R}$, $f\bmod I^2\mapsto1\otimes\mathrm df$, and $\mathrm d_{\alpha'}:I'/I'^2\to S\otimes_{P'}\Omega_{P'/R}$, $f'\bmod I'^2\mapsto1\otimes\mathrm df'$, are well defined and $S$-linear. There exist $S$-linear maps $$\varphi_1:I/I^2\to I'/I'^2,\quad\varphi_0:S\otimes_P\Omega_{P/R}\to S\otimes_{P'}\Omega_{P'/R},\quad\psi_1:I'/I'^2\to I/I^2,\quad\psi_0:S\otimes_{P'}\Omega_{P'/R}\to S\otimes_P\Omega_{P/R},$$ $$h:S\otimes_P\Omega_{P/R}\to I/I^2,\qquad h':S\otimes_{P'}\Omega_{P'/R}\to I'/I'^2$$ such that $\mathrm d_{\alpha'}\circ\varphi_1=\varphi_0\circ\mathrm d_\alpha$, $\mathrm d_\alpha\circ\psi_1=\psi_0\circ\mathrm d_{\alpha'}$, $$\mathrm{id}_{I/I^2}-\psi_1\circ\varphi_1=h\circ\mathrm d_\alpha,\qquad\mathrm{id}_{S\otimes_P\Omega_{P/R}}-\psi_0\circ\varphi_0=\mathrm d_\alpha\circ h,$$ $$\mathrm{id}_{I'/I'^2}-\varphi_1\circ\psi_1=h'\circ\mathrm d_{\alpha'},\qquad\mathrm{id}_{S\otimes_{P'}\Omega_{P'/R}}-\varphi_0\circ\psi_0=\mathrm d_{\alpha'}\circ h'.$$ Moreover $\varphi_1,\varphi_0$ may be taken to be the maps induced by any homomorphism of $R$-algebras $\varphi:P\to P'$ with $\alpha'\circ\varphi=\alpha$ (one exists), namely $\varphi_1(f\bmod I^2)=\varphi(f)\bmod I'^2$ and $\varphi_0(s\otimes\mathrm dp)=s\otimes\mathrm d\varphi(p)$, and likewise $\psi_1,\psi_0$ for any $R$-algebra map $\psi:P'\to P$ with $\alpha\circ\psi=\alpha'$.
