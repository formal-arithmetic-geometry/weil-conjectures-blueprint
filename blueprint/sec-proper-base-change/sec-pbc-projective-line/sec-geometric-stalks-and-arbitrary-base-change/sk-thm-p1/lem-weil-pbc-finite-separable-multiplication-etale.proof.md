Since $k'/k$ is separable algebraic, $k'$ is a formally etale, in particular formally unramified, $k$-algebra; it is of finite type over $k$ because $\dim_kk'<\infty$. For a formally unramified $k$-algebra $k'$ essentially of finite type, the module of Kähler differentials $\Omega_{k'/k}=I/I^2$, with $I=\ker\mu$, vanishes, and $I$ is finitely generated; hence $I$ is generated by an idempotent and the closed immersion $\operatorname{Spec}\mu:\operatorname{Spec}k'\to\operatorname{Spec}(k'\otimes_kk')$ is also an open immersion. Open immersions are etale, so $\operatorname{Spec}\mu$ is etale.

A morphism of affine schemes $\operatorname{Spec}\mu$ is etale exactly when the ring map $\mu$ is etale, and etale ring maps are smooth (of relative dimension zero). Hence $\mu$ is a smooth ring map.
