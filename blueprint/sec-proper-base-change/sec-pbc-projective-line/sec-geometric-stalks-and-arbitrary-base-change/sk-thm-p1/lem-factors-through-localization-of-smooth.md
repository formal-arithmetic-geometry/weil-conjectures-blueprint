title = "Finitely presented maps into localizations factor through a principal localization"

Let $R,C,P$ be commutative rings, with $C$ and $P$ algebras over $R$ and $C$ finitely presented over $R$. Let $S\subseteq P$ be a multiplicative subset and $g:C\to S^{-1}P$ a homomorphism of $R$-algebras. Then there exist $s\in S$ and $R$-algebra homomorphisms $f:C\to P_s=P[1/s]$ and $h:P_s\to S^{-1}P$ with $h\circ f=g$. No smoothness of $P$ is assumed.
