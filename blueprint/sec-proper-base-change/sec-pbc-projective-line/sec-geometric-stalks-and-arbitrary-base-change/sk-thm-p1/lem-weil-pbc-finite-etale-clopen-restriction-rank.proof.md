The inclusion $W\to X$ of the clopen subset is an open immersion with closed image, hence also a closed immersion; so it is both etale and finite. The same holds for the inclusion of $j^{-1}W$, which is clopen in $X_0$ by continuity of $j$. The base change $q_0$ of the finite etale morphism $q$ is finite etale, and composites of finite etale morphisms are finite etale. This gives the four finiteness and etaleness claims.

The square formed by $j^{-1}W\to W$, the two inclusions and $j$ is cartesian, since a morphism to $X_0$ factors through $j^{-1}W$ exactly when its composite with $j$ factors through $W$. Pasting it with the given cartesian square gives a cartesian square whose vertical maps are $q_0|_{j^{-1}W}$ and $q|_W$. The rank of a finite flat morphism of finite presentation is invariant under base change in a cartesian square, which gives the asserted equality of ranks. No surjectivity of $i$ is used.
