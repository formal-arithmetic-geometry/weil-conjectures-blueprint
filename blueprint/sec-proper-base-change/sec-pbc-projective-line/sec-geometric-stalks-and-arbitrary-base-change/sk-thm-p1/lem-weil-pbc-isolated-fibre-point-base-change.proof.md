Fibre of the base change. Let $r:\kappa(s)\to\kappa(s')$ be the map induced by $g$ on residue fields. Since the fibre of a base change is the base change of the fibre, there is a morphism $h:X'_{s'}\to X_s$ with $\iota\circ h=g'\circ\iota'$ (where $\iota:X_s\to X$, $\iota':X'_{s'}\to X'$ are the first projections) which, together with the projection $X'_{s'}\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')$, makes $X'_{s'}$ a fibre product $X_s\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)}\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')$, the map $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ being $\operatorname{Spec}(r)$. Since $f'(x')=s'$ and the projection $X'_{s'}\to X'$ is a homeomorphism onto $f'^{-1}(s')$, there is a unique point $\xi'\in X'_{s'}$ with $\iota'(\xi')=x'$; this is the point of the fibre meant by '$x'$ is isolated in $X'_{s'}$'. Put $\xi=h(\xi')$. Then $\iota(\xi)=g'(\iota'(\xi'))=g'(x')=x$, and since $\iota$ is injective (a homeomorphism onto $f^{-1}(s)$), $\xi$ is the point of $X_s$ corresponding to $x$; by hypothesis $\{\xi\}$ is open in $X_s$.

The open point as a scheme over $\kappa(s)$. Let $W\subseteq X_s$ be the open subscheme with underlying set $\{\xi\}$ and $w:W\to X_s$ its inclusion, an open immersion. The morphism $w$ is locally of finite type, as every immersion is; the projection $\pi:X_s\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ is the base change of the morphism $f$, locally of finite type, along $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)\to S$, hence locally of finite type (stability under base change); so $\pi\circ w:W\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ is locally of finite type (stability under composition). The underlying space of $W$ is one point, hence discrete; so $\pi\circ w$ is locally of finite type with finite discrete fibres, i.e. locally quasi-finite.

Base change of the open point is discrete and open. The base change $W_{\kappa(s')}=W\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)}\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s')$ (via $r$) is locally quasi-finite over the field $\kappa(s')$, since local quasi-finiteness is stable under base change. A scheme locally quasi-finite over a field is locally Artinian, hence has discrete underlying space; so $W_{\kappa(s')}$ is discrete. Applying the restriction of the fibre-product square of the first step to the open $W\subseteq X_s$ (an open subscheme of a fibre product is the fibre product of the corresponding open), the canonical morphism $W_{\kappa(s')}\to X'_{s'}$ is an open immersion with image $h^{-1}(W)$. Hence $h^{-1}(W)$ is an open subset of $X'_{s'}$ and, being homeomorphic to $W_{\kappa(s')}$, is discrete in the subspace topology. No quasi-finiteness of the original morphism is assumed.

Conclusion. Since $h(\xi')=\xi\in W$, $\xi'\in h^{-1}(W)$. As $h^{-1}(W)$ is discrete, $\{\xi'\}$ is open in $h^{-1}(W)$, and as $h^{-1}(W)$ is open in $X'_{s'}$, $\{\xi'\}$ is open in $X'_{s'}$: $x'$ is an isolated point of $X'_{s'}$.
