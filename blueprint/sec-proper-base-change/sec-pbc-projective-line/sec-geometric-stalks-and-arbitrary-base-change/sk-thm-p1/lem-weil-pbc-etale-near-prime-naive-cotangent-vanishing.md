Let $A\to C$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings making $C$ a finitely presented $A$-algebra, and $\mathfrak q\subset C$ a prime ideal such that the localizations at $\mathfrak q$ of the $C$-modules $H_1(\mathrm{NL}_{C/A})$ (the first homology of the naive cotangent complex) and $\Omega_{C/A}$ vanish: $H_1(\mathrm{NL}_{C/A})_{\mathfrak q}=0$ and $(\Omega_{C/A})_{\mathfrak q}=0$. Then there is $h\in C\setminus\mathfrak q$ such that $\operatorname{Spec}C_h\to\operatorname{Spec}A$, induced by $A\to C\to C_h$, is étale.
