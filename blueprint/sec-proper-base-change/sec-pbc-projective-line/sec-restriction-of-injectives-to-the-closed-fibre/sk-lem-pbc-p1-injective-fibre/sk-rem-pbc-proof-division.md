[sk-thm-iff] reduces higher proper base change to a vanishing statement, and
the only geometric fact used in its proof is the degree-zero comparison of
[sk-prop-pbc-degree-zero]. It remains to prove $\mathrm{IA}_\Lambda(f)$ for every
proper $f$ and every ring $\Lambda$ which is torsion as an abelian group. We prove it first for $\mathbf P^1_S\to S$
and then reduce arbitrary proper morphisms to that case. The curve
calculations for primes invertible on the base and for the residue
characteristic are different, but both are used only through this criterion.
