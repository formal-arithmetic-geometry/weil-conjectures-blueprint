The sheaves on the right of the formula in [sk-lem-pbc-p1-injective-fibre]
are direct images along finite morphisms, and (3) says that such direct
images have no higher cohomology and commute with restriction to the
closed fibre. Both assertions of (3) follow from the decomposition of a finite
scheme over a strict localization into strictly henselian local schemes
([Stacks, Tag 03QP](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QP), [Stacks, Tag 0959](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0959)), and they are part of
Lemma [sk-lem-pbc-finite] below.
