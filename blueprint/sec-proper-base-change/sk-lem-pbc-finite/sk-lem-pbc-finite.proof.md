After passage to the strict henselization of $W$ at $\bar w$, the finite
scheme $V$ is a finite disjoint union of strictly henselian local schemes. On
each such local scheme, sections of an étale sheaf are its stalk at the
closed geometric point. Indeed, every étale neighbourhood of that point admits
a section, so a germ extends to a global section, and two extensions agreeing
as germs agree after such a section. This also makes sections exact on
abelian sheaves. The strict-local description of direct image therefore gives
the displayed formula; see Stacks Project, Tag 03QP. Geometric stalks are
conservative and finite products of abelian groups are exact, so $u_*$ is
exact.

In a cartesian base-change square, the two geometric fibres are canonically
the same after using the same geometric field. The underived exchange is the
identity on the corresponding factors of the displayed formula. It is
therefore an isomorphism of sheaves on $W'_{\Et}$. Both direct images in the
square are exact, so their derived functors and the exchange can be computed
term by term. The same argument applies to sheaves of modules.
