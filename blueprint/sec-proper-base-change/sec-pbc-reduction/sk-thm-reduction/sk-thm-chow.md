Let $f:X\to Y$ be proper with $Y$ affine. There are a proper surjection
$\pi:\widetilde X\to X$ and closed immersions
$i_Y:\widetilde X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_Y$ and
$i_X=(\pi,i_Y):\widetilde X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_X$ with $f\pi$ equal to
$i_Y$ followed by the projection. Thus the two maps $\pi$ and $f\pi$ factor as
the closed immersions $i_X$ and $i_Y$ followed by projections of projective
spaces.
