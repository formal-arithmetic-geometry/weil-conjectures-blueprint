title = "Reduction of proper morphisms to the projective line"

We now deduce proper base change for every proper morphism, with a
coefficient ring $\Lambda$ which is torsion as an abelian group, from the case
of the projective line (Proposition [sk-thm-p1]). Finite morphisms satisfy proper base change
directly, and the property is stable under composition and descends along
proper surjections (Lemma [sk-lem-pbc-composition-cancellation]). Descent
along a proper surjection $u:X\to Y$ holds because every injective
sheaf on $Y$ is a direct summand of $u_*J$ for an injective sheaf $J$ on
$X$, and the hypotheses on $u$ and on the composite show that $u_*J$
stays acyclic after base change. Hence proper base change holds for the
projection of $(\mathbf P^1)^n$, then, by descent along the finite
surjection $(\mathbf P^1)^n\to\mathbf P^n$, for the projection of
$\mathbf P^n$, and so for every projective morphism. By Chow's lemma
every proper morphism is dominated by a projective one through a proper
surjection, and descent once more gives proper base change for it.
