Fact (i) extends Theorem [sk-thm-pbc-henselian-h0] to all degrees, but only
for affine schemes, while fact (ii) controls degree one for proper
schemes. In the comparison of $\mathbf P^1_R$ with its closed fibre we use (i) on the
affine charts and (ii) for the classes in degree one.

The facts about curves over an algebraically closed field that we need
here and later are collected in the following theorem. For torsion divisible by the
characteristic, proper and affine curves behave differently, which is
why some of the assertions require $C$ to be proper.
