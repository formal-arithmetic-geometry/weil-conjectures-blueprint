For a proper morphism, the underived base change map is an isomorphism
for every étale sheaf of sets. The reason is that, for a proper scheme
over a henselian pair, restriction of sections to the closed fibre is
bijective (Theorem [sk-thm-pbc-henselian-h0]).

Recall that a pair $(R,I)$, with $I$
an ideal of the ring $R$, is *henselian* if $I$ is contained in the Jacobson radical of $R$
and Hensel's lemma holds for it: every factorization modulo $I$ of a
monic polynomial into coprime monic factors lifts to a factorization in
$R$ ([Stacks, Tag 09XE](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09XE)). A local ring with its maximal ideal
is a henselian pair exactly when it is henselian, and a complete local
ring is henselian.
