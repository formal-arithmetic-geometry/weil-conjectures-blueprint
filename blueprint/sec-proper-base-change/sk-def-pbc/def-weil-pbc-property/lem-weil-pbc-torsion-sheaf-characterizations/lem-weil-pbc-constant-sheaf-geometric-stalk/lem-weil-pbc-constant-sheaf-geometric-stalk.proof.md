**Step 1.** *The stalk of $\underline E$ is the stalk of the constant presheaf.* Let $P$ be the constant presheaf $U\mapsto E$ on $T_{\Et}$ (all restriction maps $\mathrm{id}_E$), so that $\underline E=P^\#$ with canonical map $\iota:P\to\underline E$ [def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], [def-weil-pbc-sheafification]. Define the stalk of the presheaf $P$ at $\bar t$ as $P_{\bar t}=\varinjlim_{(U,\bar u)\in\mathcal N_{\bar t}^{\mathrm{op}}}P(U)$, with coprojections $c_{(U,\bar u)}:E=P(U)\to P_{\bar t}$, the same colimit formula as for the stalk of a sheaf [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks] over the pointed etale neighbourhoods [def-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods]. By [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-geometric-stalk] (Stacks 03PT (2)) the map $\iota_{\bar t}:P_{\bar t}\to\underline E_{\bar t}$ induced by $\iota$, which sends $c_{(U,\bar u)}(e)$ to the class of $\iota_U(e)$ at $(U,\bar u)$, is an isomorphism of abelian groups.

**Step 2.** *The stalk of the constant presheaf is $E$.* The diagram $(U,\bar u)\mapsto P(U)=E$ on $\mathcal N_{\bar t}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is the constant diagram with value $E$, every transition map being a restriction map of $P$, i.e. $\mathrm{id}_E$. The category $\mathcal N_{\bar t}$ is cofiltered, with the object $(T,\bar t)$ [lem-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods-cofiltered] (Stacks 03PQ), so $\mathcal N_{\bar t}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is nonempty and filtered, hence connected, and by [lem-weil-pbc-colimit-constant-connected-diagram] every coprojection $c_{(U,\bar u)}:E\to P_{\bar t}$ of the colimit [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit] is an isomorphism of abelian groups; in particular so is $c_{(T,\bar t)}:E\to P_{\bar t}$, $e\mapsto$ class of $e$ at $(T,\bar t)$. (Elementwise, as in the remark after Definition 040R: every element of $P_{\bar t}$ is a class $(U,\bar u,e)$, and $(U,\bar u,e)=(T,\bar t,e)$ via the morphism $(U,\bar u)\to(T,\bar t)$ given by the structure map; $(T,\bar t,e)=(T,\bar t,e')$ forces $e=e'$ since the restrictions are identities.)

**Step 3.** *Assembly.* For $e\in E$, $\iota_{\bar t}(c_{(T,\bar t)}(e))$ is by step 1 the class of $\iota_T(e)$ at $(T,\bar t)$. So the map of the statement is the composite $\iota_{\bar t}\circ c_{(T,\bar t)}$ of the isomorphisms of steps 2 and 1, hence an isomorphism of abelian groups.

*Source: Stacks project, etale-cohomology.tex, Lemma 03PT (2) and Definition 040R with Lemma 03PQ; the identification of the stalk of a constant presheaf (step 2) is the elementary description of colimits of constant diagrams, written out.*
