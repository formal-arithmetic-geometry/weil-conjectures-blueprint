Since $\Lambda$ is torsion as an additive group, every sheaf of
$\Lambda$-modules is torsion, so every object of $\Dplus(\,\cdot_{\Et},\Lambda)$
has torsion cohomology sheaves. Hence part 1 of the projective-line
proposition [prop-weil-pbc-projective-line] says that $\mathbf P^1_S\to S$
satisfies $\mathrm{PBC}$ in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-property], with
coefficients in $\Lambda$-modules, for every scheme $S$. This is the
hypothesis of the reduction proposition
[prop-weil-pbc-reduction-to-projective-line] for the torsion coefficient ring
$\Lambda$, which therefore yields $\mathrm{PBC}(f)$ for every proper $f$: the
derived exchange morphism of every cartesian square over $f$ is an
isomorphism on all of $\Dplus(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$. For $\Lambda=\mathbf F_r$ and
$K=F[0]$ this is the last assertion.
