For a proper $p:X\to Y$ and a geometric point $\bar y\to Y$, the torsion
theorem [sk-thm-properbasechange] gives

$$
(R^qp_*F)_{\bar y}\simeq H^q(X_{\bar y},F|_{X_{\bar y}}).
$$

The same formula for constructible $\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\Qlb$
coefficients follows from the comparisons of [sk-prop-pbc-complete] and
[sk-thm-sfb-proper-base-change]. Properness is essential: if
$j:\mathbf G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$ and $i:\{0\}\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$
over an algebraically closed field, then $i^*j_*A$ is nonzero for a nonzero
finite constant ring $A$, whereas the base change of $\mathbf G_m$ to $\{0\}$
is empty. The degree-zero exchange map $i^{-1}j_*A\to0$ of sheaves on
$\{0\}_{\Et}$ already fails to be an isomorphism in this nonproper example.
