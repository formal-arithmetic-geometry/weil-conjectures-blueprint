On the displayed chart the ring extension is generated by the $z_i$, each of
which is integral over the elementary-symmetric subring because it is a root
of the monic polynomial with coefficients $e_j=F_j/F_0$; the same argument
with $x_i$ and $y_i$ interchanged covers the charts at infinity. These charts
cover $(\mathbf P^1_S)^n$, so the affine criterion shows that the morphism
built in [lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-product-properties] is finite.
