With the notation of [lemma-weil-pbc-chow-factorization], the map

$$
 i_X=(\pi,i_Y):\widetilde X\longrightarrow
 X\times_Y\mathbf P^n_Y\simeq\mathbf P^n_X
$$

is a closed immersion over $X$.
