Let $X\xrightarrow{f}Y\xrightarrow{h}Z$ be proper. If $\mathrm{IA}(f)$ and
$\mathrm{IA}(h)$ hold, then $\mathrm{IA}(hf)$ and hence $\mathrm{PBC}(hf)$
hold.
