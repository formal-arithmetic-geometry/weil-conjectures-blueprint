**Step 1.** *The easy direction.* If $\varphi$ is an isomorphism with inverse $\psi$, then $t_i^{-1}$, being a functor $\Ab(T_{\Et})\to\Ab(T_{i,\Et})$ [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], sends $\psi\circ\varphi=\mathrm{id}_G$ and $\varphi\circ\psi=\mathrm{id}_H$ to $t_i^{-1}\psi\circ t_i^{-1}\varphi=\mathrm{id}$ and $t_i^{-1}\varphi\circ t_i^{-1}\psi=\mathrm{id}$; so every $t_i^{-1}\varphi$ is an isomorphism.

**Step 2.** *Lifting a geometric point.* Assume every $t_i^{-1}\varphi$ is an isomorphism, and let $\bar t:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to T$ be any geometric point of $T$ [def-weil-geometric-point], lying over $t\in T$. Since the etale family $(t_i)$ is jointly surjective there are $i\in I$ and $s\in T_i$ with $t_i(s)=t$, and [lem-weil-pbc-etale-cover-geometric-point-lift] gives a geometric point $\bar s:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to T_i$ over $s$ with $t_i\circ\bar s=\bar t$. (If $I=\emptyset$ then $T=\emptyset$, there is no geometric point, and this step and the next are vacuous.)

**Step 3.** *The stalk of the pullback is the stalk at the image.* For the geometric point $\bar s$ of $T_i$ of step 2 there is an isomorphism $c_K:(t_i^{-1}K)_{\bar s}\xrightarrow{\sim}K_{t_i\circ\bar s}=K_{\bar t}$ of stalks [def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks], natural in the abelian sheaf $K$ on $T_{\Et}$ [lemma-openclosed-stalk-upperstar] (its $f^*$ is the inverse image $f^{-1}$ of [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image]: in the Lean, `Etale.upperStar` is the sheaf pullback along the base-change functor $T_{\Et}\to T_{i,\Et}$, i.e. the sheafified presheaf inverse image; Stacks 03Q1 (3) is the same identification). Naturality in $K$ applied to $\varphi:G\to H$ gives $\varphi_{\bar t}\circ c_G=c_H\circ(t_i^{-1}\varphi)_{\bar s}$. The stalk at $\bar s$ is a functor (a colimit of sections), so it sends the isomorphism $t_i^{-1}\varphi$ to an isomorphism $(t_i^{-1}\varphi)_{\bar s}$; hence $\varphi_{\bar t}=c_H\circ(t_i^{-1}\varphi)_{\bar s}\circ c_G^{-1}$ is an isomorphism of abelian groups.

**Step 4.** *Geometric stalks detect isomorphisms.* By steps 2 and 3, $\varphi_{\bar t}:G_{\bar t}\to H_{\bar t}$ is an isomorphism for every geometric point $\bar t$ of $T$. A morphism of abelian sheaves on $T_{\Et}$ that is an isomorphism at every geometric stalk is an isomorphism [lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative]; so $\varphi$ is an isomorphism. (In the Lean, `Scheme.isIso_of_isIso_sheafFiber_map` asks this only at one universe-lifted geometric point over each point of $T$, which is among the geometric points just treated.) This proves the converse direction.
