**Step 1.** *Abelian.* Write $\mathcal O=\Lambda_T$, a sheaf of rings on $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf]. Sheafification of presheaves of abelian groups exists on $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves] (in Mathlib: HasSheafify and WEqualsLocallyBijective for T.smallEtaleTopology, AffineEtale.lean:112,124). Hence the category $\Mod(\mathcal O)$ of sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules is abelian [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], [def-sp-abelian]: kernels are computed sectionwise and cokernels are the sheafifications of the sectionwise cokernels. By [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful] (1) the forgetful functor $\mathrm{forget}:\Mod(\mathcal O)\to\Ab(T_{\Et})$ is faithful and exact.

**Step 2.** *Small colimits.* By [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful] (2), applied to the site $T_{\Et}$ (on which abelian sheafification exists [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves], [def-weil-pbc-sheafification]) and $\mathcal O=\Lambda_T$, every diagram $\mathcal I\to\Mod(\mathcal O)$ indexed by a small category has a colimit, the sheafification of the sectionwise colimit; by (3) of the same lemma $\mathrm{forget}$ carries it to the colimit in $\Ab(T_{\Et})$. In particular arbitrary direct sums $\bigoplus_{k\in K}\mathcal F_k$ over a set $K$ exist in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$, with coprojections $\iota_k$, and for morphisms $h_k:\mathcal F_k\to\mathcal G$ there is a unique $h:\bigoplus_k\mathcal F_k\to\mathcal G$ with $h\iota_k=h_k$.

**Step 3.** *Filtered colimits are exact.* Let $\mathcal I$ be a small filtered category and $0\to\mathcal F_i\xrightarrow{a_i}\mathcal G_i\xrightarrow{b_i}\mathcal H_i\to0$ a diagram ($i\in\mathcal I$) of short exact sequences in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$. Colimits commute with cokernels in any category (both are colimits), so $\colim\mathcal F_i\to\colim\mathcal G_i\to\colim\mathcal H_i\to0$ is exact; it remains to show that $a=\colim a_i$ is a monomorphism. Apply $\mathrm{forget}$: by step 2 (part (3) of [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful]) $\mathrm{forget}(a)$ is, up to the canonical isomorphisms, the colimit in $\Ab(T_{\Et})$ of the morphisms $\mathrm{forget}(a_i)$, which are monomorphisms because $\mathrm{forget}$ is exact (step 1). Filtered colimits in $\Ab(T_{\Et})$ are exact [lem-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves-grothendieck], so $\mathrm{forget}(a)$ is a monomorphism. A faithful functor reflects monomorphisms: if $a x=a y$ then $\mathrm{forget}(a)\mathrm{forget}(x)=\mathrm{forget}(a)\mathrm{forget}(y)$, so $\mathrm{forget}(x)=\mathrm{forget}(y)$ and $x=y$. Hence $a$ is a monomorphism and $0\to\colim\mathcal F_i\to\colim\mathcal G_i\to\colim\mathcal H_i\to0$ is exact.

**Step 4.** *The objects $j_{U!}\Lambda_U$.* By [lem-weil-pbc-affine-etale-essentially-small-cover] choose a set $\mathcal S$ of affine objects of $T_{\Et}$ such that every object $V$ of $T_{\Et}$ has a covering $\{U_k\to V\}_{k\in K}$ with all $U_k\in\mathcal S$. For $U\in\mathcal S$ let $\mathcal O[h_U ]$ be the presheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules $V\mapsto\bigoplus_{\varphi\in\mathrm{Mor}(V,U)}\mathcal O(V)$ of [lem-weil-pbc-free-yoneda-presheaf-module] and $j_{U!}\Lambda_U=\mathcal O[h_U ]^\#$ its sheafification as a presheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification]. For every $\mathcal F\in\Mod(\mathcal O)$ we get bijections, natural in $\mathcal F$, $\mathrm{Hom}_{\Mod(\mathcal O)}(j_{U!}\Lambda_U,\mathcal F)\cong\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{PMod}(\mathcal O)}(\mathcal O[h_U ],\mathcal F)\cong\mathcal F(U)$: the first by the universal property of sheafification [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification], the second by [lem-weil-pbc-free-yoneda-presheaf-module]. Write $h_s:j_{U!}\Lambda_U\to\mathcal F$ for the morphism attached to $s\in\mathcal F(U)$; naturality says that for $w:\mathcal F\to\mathcal G$ the morphism $w\circ h_s$ is attached to $w_U(s)\in\mathcal G(U)$, i.e. $w\circ h_s=h_{w_U(s)}$. Since the bijections are additive maps of groups (the sheafification bijection is a homomorphism of abelian groups, and so is $\beta\mapsto\beta_U(e_{\mathrm{id}_U})$), $h_s=0$ iff $s=0$.

**Step 5.** *A generator.* Let $G=\bigoplus_{U\in\mathcal S}j_{U!}\Lambda_U$, which exists by step 2 since $\mathcal S$ is a set. $G$ is a separator: let $u\neq v:\mathcal F\to\mathcal G$ in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$ and $w=u-v\neq0$. A morphism of sheaves is determined by its components [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], so there are an object $V$ and $s\in\mathcal F(V)$ with $w_V(s)\neq0$. Choose a covering $\{U_k\to V\}_{k}$ with $U_k\in\mathcal S$ (step 4). Since $\mathcal G$ is a sheaf, a section whose restrictions to all members of a covering vanish is zero [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves]; so for some $k$, with $U=U_k$, $w_U(s|_U)=w_V(s)|_U\neq0$. Then $w\circ h_{s|_U}=h_{w_U(s|_U)}\neq0$ by step 4. Let $h:G\to\mathcal F$ be the morphism with $h\iota_U=h_{s|_U}$ and $h\iota_{U'}=0$ for $U'\neq U$ (step 2). Then $w h\iota_U\neq0$, so $wh\neq0$, i.e. $uh\neq vh$. $G$ is also a generator in the sense of subobjects: if $N'\subsetneq N$ is a proper subobject, the quotient map $q:N\to N/N'$ is nonzero (the cokernel of a non-isomorphic monomorphism is nonzero in an abelian category), so by the above with $u=q$, $v=0$ there is $h:G\to N$ with $qh\neq0$, i.e. $h$ does not factor through $N'$.

**Step 6.** *Conclusion.* By steps 1, 2, 3 and 5, $\Mod(T_{\Et},\Lambda)=\Mod(\Lambda_T)$ is abelian, has all small colimits, has exact filtered colimits and has the generator $G$; it is a Grothendieck abelian category [def-sp-grothendieck-abelian]. For $\Lambda=\mathbf Z$ the category $\Ab(T_{\Et})$ is Grothendieck abelian by [lem-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves-grothendieck] (equivalently, by the above for $\Lambda=\mathbf Z$, since every abelian sheaf carries exactly one $\mathbf Z_T$-module structure and every morphism of abelian sheaves is $\mathbf Z_T$-linear, so that $\mathrm{forget}:\Mod(\mathbf Z_T)\to\Ab(T_{\Et})$ is an isomorphism of categories).
