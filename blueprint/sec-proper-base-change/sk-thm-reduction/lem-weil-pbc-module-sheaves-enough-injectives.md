Let $T$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme], $T_{\Et}$ its small etale site [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], $R$ a commutative ring, $R_T$ the constant sheaf of rings with value $R$ on $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], and $\Mod(T_{\Et},R)=\Mod(R_T)$ the abelian category of sheaves of $R_T$-modules on $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category]. Then $\Mod(T_{\Et},R)$ has enough injectives: for every object $F$ there are an injective object $J$ of $\Mod(T_{\Et},R)$ [def-sp-injective-resolutions] and a monomorphism $F\hookrightarrow J$ in $\Mod(T_{\Et},R)$. In particular the category $\Ab(T_{\Et})$ of abelian sheaves on $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves] has enough injectives.
