Let $X\xrightarrow{f}Y\xrightarrow{h}Z$ be proper, with $f$ surjective. If
$\mathrm{PBC}(f)$ and $\mathrm{PBC}(hf)$ hold, then $\mathrm{PBC}(h)$ holds.
