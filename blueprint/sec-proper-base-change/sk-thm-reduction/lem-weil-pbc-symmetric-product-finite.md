The morphism $\sigma_n$ of [lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-product-properties] is
finite. More precisely, on the chart where all $y_i$ are nonzero, the induced
affine ring extension is integral: each $z_i=x_i/y_i$ satisfies

$$
 T^n-e_1T^{n-1}+\cdots+(-1)^ne_n=0.
$$

The corresponding reversed equations give integral affine ring extensions on
the charts at infinity, and these charts cover $(\mathbf P^1_S)^n$.
