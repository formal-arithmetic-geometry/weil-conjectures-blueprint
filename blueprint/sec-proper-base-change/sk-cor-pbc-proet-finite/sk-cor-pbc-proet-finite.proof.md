This is the finite-coefficient instance of [sk-thm-properbasechange]: every
sheaf of $A$-modules is killed by the order of $A$, so the torsion hypothesis
is automatic. By [sk-thm-pe-constructible-comparison], its classicality
hypothesis is automatic for bounded constructible finite-coefficient
complexes on qcqs schemes, in particular in our standing finite-type range,
and hence for every $K\in\Dctf(X,A)$ in that range.
