For a morphism [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions] $u:U\to V$ of schemes, the
inverse image [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image]

$$
 u^{-1}:\Ab(V_{\Et})\longrightarrow\Ab(U_{\Et})
$$

of [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] is an exact functor.
