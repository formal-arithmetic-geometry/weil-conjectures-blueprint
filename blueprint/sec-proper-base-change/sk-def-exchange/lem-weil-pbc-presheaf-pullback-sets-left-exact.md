Let $\mathcal C,\mathcal D$ be categories whose objects and morphisms form sets and $b:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ a functor. Let $\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal C)$, $\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal D)$ be the categories of presheaves of sets (contravariant functors to sets, natural transformations as morphisms), and $b^p:\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal D)\to\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal C)$, $\mathcal G\mapsto\mathcal G\circ b$. For $Y\in\mathcal D$ let $\mathcal I_Y$ be the category of pairs $(X,\phi)$, $X\in\mathcal C$, $\phi:Y\to b(X)$, a morphism $(X,\phi)\to(X',\phi')$ being $f:X\to X'$ with $b(f)\circ\phi=\phi'$, and for $\mathcal F\in\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal C)$ put $b_p\mathcal F(Y)=\varinjlim_{(X,\phi)\in\mathcal I_Y^{\mathrm{op}}}\mathcal F(X)$ (colimit of sets), with restriction maps induced by $(X,\phi)\mapsto(X,\phi\circ g)$ for $g:Y'\to Y$. Then: (1) $b_p$ is a functor $\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal C)\to\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal D)$ left adjoint to $b^p$, $\operatorname{Hom}(b_p\mathcal F,\mathcal G)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal F,b^p\mathcal G)$ naturally in $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$, the unit $\mathcal F(X)\to b_p\mathcal F(b(X))$ being the coprojection at $(X,\mathrm{id}_{b(X)})$; (2) if $\mathcal C$ has finite limits (a final object and fibre products) and $b$ commutes with them, then every $\mathcal I_Y^{\mathrm{op}}$ is filtered and $b_p$ commutes with finite limits (finite limits of presheaves of sets being computed sectionwise); in particular $b_p(*)=*$ and $b_p(\mathcal F_1\times\mathcal F_2)=b_p\mathcal F_1\times b_p\mathcal F_2$ via the projections.
