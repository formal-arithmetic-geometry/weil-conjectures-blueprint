Let $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$ be categories with finite products [def-weil-category-product], with final objects $*$; for $f:Z\to X$, $g:Z\to Y$ write $\langle f,g\rangle:Z\to X\times Y$ for the morphism with projections $f,g$, $f\times g=\langle f\circ\mathrm{pr}_1,g\circ\mathrm{pr}_2\rangle$, $!_Z:Z\to*$, and $p_1,p_2,p_3$ for the three coordinate projections of $X^3=(X\times X)\times X$. An *abelian group object* of $\mathcal A$ is $(X,+,0,i)$ with morphisms $+:X\times X\to X$, $0:*\to X$, $i:X\to X$ satisfying (G1) $+\circ\langle+\circ\langle p_1,p_2\rangle,p_3\rangle=+\circ\langle p_1,+\circ\langle p_2,p_3\rangle\rangle$, (G2) $+\circ\langle\mathrm{pr}_2,\mathrm{pr}_1\rangle=+$, (G3) $+\circ\langle0\circ!_X,\mathrm{id}_X\rangle=\mathrm{id}_X$, (G4) $+\circ\langle\mathrm{id}_X,i\rangle=0\circ!_X$; a *commutative ring object* is a sextuple $(X,+,0,i,\cdot,1)$ with $(X,+,0,i)$ an abelian group object and $\cdot:X\times X\to X$, $1:*\to X$ satisfying (R1)-(R3), the axioms (G1)-(G3) with $+,0$ replaced by $\cdot,1$, and (R4) $\cdot\circ\langle p_1,+\circ\langle p_2,p_3\rangle\rangle=+\circ\langle\cdot\circ\langle p_1,p_2\rangle,\cdot\circ\langle p_1,p_3\rangle\rangle$. A morphism of such objects is a morphism $f:X\to X'$ of $\mathcal A$ commuting with the structure maps: $f\circ+=+'\circ(f\times f)$, $f\circ0=0'$, $f\circ i=i'\circ f$ (and $f\circ\cdot=\cdot'\circ(f\times f)$, $f\circ1=1'$). Let $L:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ be left adjoint to $R:\mathcal B\to\mathcal A$, with unit $\eta$ and counit $\varepsilon$, and assume $L$ preserves finite products: $L(*)$ is final and $\lambda_{X,Y}=\langle L\mathrm{pr}_1,L\mathrm{pr}_2\rangle:L(X\times Y)\to LX\times LY$ is an isomorphism; let $\lambda_*:L(*)\to*$ be the unique map. ($R$, a right adjoint, preserves all limits; $\rho_{Y,Y'}=\langle R\mathrm{pr}_1,R\mathrm{pr}_2\rangle$ and $\rho_*:R(*)\to*$ are isomorphisms.) For an abelian group object $(X,+,0,i)$ of $\mathcal A$ let $L(X)$ carry $L(+)\circ\lambda_{X,X}^{-1}$, $L(0)\circ\lambda_*^{-1}$, $L(i)$, and for a commutative ring object also $L(\cdot)\circ\lambda_{X,X}^{-1}$, $L(1)\circ\lambda_*^{-1}$; likewise $R$ with $\rho$. Then: (1) these are abelian group (resp. commutative ring) objects, and $L$, $R$ so extended, $f\mapsto Lf$, $g\mapsto Rg$ on morphisms, are functors $L^{\mathrm{gr}}$, $R^{\mathrm{gr}}$ between the categories of abelian group objects (resp. $L^{\mathrm{rg}}$, $R^{\mathrm{rg}}$ between those of commutative ring objects), commuting with the forgetful functors to $\mathcal A$, $\mathcal B$; (2) for an abelian group (ring) object $X$ of $\mathcal A$ and $Y$ of $\mathcal B$, a morphism $\alpha:LX\to Y$ commutes with the structure maps if and only if its adjoint $R(\alpha)\circ\eta_X:X\to RY$ does; hence $L^{\mathrm{gr}}\dashv R^{\mathrm{gr}}$ (resp. $L^{\mathrm{rg}}\dashv R^{\mathrm{rg}}$), with adjunction bijection the restriction of that of $L\dashv R$, and unit and counit those of $L\dashv R$ (the morphisms $\eta_X$, $\varepsilon_Y$ are morphisms of group, resp. ring, objects).
