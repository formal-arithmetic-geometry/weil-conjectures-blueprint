Let $u:U\to V$ be a morphism of schemes [def-pc-scheme], $R$ a commutative ring, $R_U$ and $R_V$ the constant sheaves of rings with value $R$ on the small etale sites $U_{\Et}$, $V_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], [def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf], and $\Mod(T_{\Et},R)=\Mod(R_T)$ the abelian category of sheaves of $R_T$-modules [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category]. Let $u^{-1}:\Ab(V_{\Et})\to\Ab(U_{\Et})$ be the inverse image of abelian sheaves [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image], [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves], and $u^{-1}$ also the inverse image of sheaves of sets and of rings [lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-algebraic-structures]. Then: (1) the inverse image of the sheaf of rings $R_V$ is canonically isomorphic to $R_U$: the morphism of sheaves of rings $c:u^{-1}R_V\to R_U$ adjoint to the map $\gamma:R_V\to u_*R_U$ induced, by the universal property of sheafification, by the constant sections $R\to R_U(U\times_VW)$, is an isomorphism; (2) for a sheaf $G$ of $R_V$-modules, $u^{-1}G$ carries a unique structure of sheaf of $u^{-1}R_V$-modules, hence of $R_U$-modules through (1), such that $u^{-1}$ of the action map is the action map; this defines a functor $u^{-1}:\Mod(V_{\Et},R)\to\Mod(U_{\Et},R)$ compatible with $u^{-1}$ on abelian sheaves under the forgetful functors, equal (up to a canonical isomorphism natural in $G$) to the module pullback $u^*G=u^{-1}G\otimes_{u^{-1}R_V}R_U$ [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]; (3) this functor is additive and exact (it preserves finite limits and finite colimits), and it is left adjoint to the direct image $u_*:\Mod(U_{\Et},R)\to\Mod(V_{\Et},R)$, $(u_*F)(W)=F(U\times_VW)$ with $R_V$ acting through $\gamma$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image], the unit and counit being those of the adjunction $u^{-1}\dashv u_*$ on abelian sheaves.
