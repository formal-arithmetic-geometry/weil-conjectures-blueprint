Let $F:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ be an exact functor (additive, preserving finite limits and finite colimits) between abelian categories, and let $Q_{\mathcal A}:\mathrm{CoCh}(\mathcal A)\to D(\mathcal A)$, $Q_{\mathcal B}:\mathrm{CoCh}(\mathcal B)\to D(\mathcal B)$ be the localization functors of the cochain complexes at the quasi-isomorphisms, with their shift isomorphisms $Q(K[n ])\cong(QK)[n ]$ [def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. Let $C(F):\mathrm{CoCh}(\mathcal A)\to\mathrm{CoCh}(\mathcal B)$, $K\mapsto(F(K^r),F(d^r))_r$, be the functor applying $F$ termwise, and for $n\in\mathbf Z$ let $\tau_{K,n}:C(F)(K[n ])\to C(F)(K)[n ]$ be the identity in each degree (both complexes have $F(K^{r+n})$ in degree $r$ and differential $(-1)^nF(d^{r+n})$, $F$ being additive); $\tau_{K,n}$ is an isomorphism of complexes, natural in $K$. Then $C(F)$ carries quasi-isomorphisms to quasi-isomorphisms, and there are a functor $F_D:D(\mathcal A)\to D(\mathcal B)$, natural isomorphisms $\sigma_{X,n}:F_D(X[n ])\xrightarrow{\sim}F_D(X)[n ]$ ($X\in D(\mathcal A)$, $n\in\mathbf Z$) and a natural isomorphism\n\n$$\n \varphi_K:F_D(Q_{\mathcal A}K)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Q_{\mathcal B}(C(F)K)\qquad(K\in\mathrm{CoCh}(\mathcal A))\n$$\n\nsuch that: (1) $(F_D,\sigma)$ is a triangulated functor [def-weil-triangulated-category]; (2) $\varphi$ is compatible with the shifts: for every complex $K$ and $n\in\mathbf Z$, the composite $F_D(Q_{\mathcal A}(K[n ]))\cong F_D((Q_{\mathcal A}K)[n ])\xrightarrow{\sigma}F_D(Q_{\mathcal A}K)[n ]\xrightarrow{\varphi_K[n ]}Q_{\mathcal B}(C(F)K)[n ]$ equals $F_D(Q_{\mathcal A}(K[n ]))\xrightarrow{\varphi_{K[n ]}}Q_{\mathcal B}(C(F)(K[n ]))\xrightarrow{Q_{\mathcal B}(\tau_{K,n})}Q_{\mathcal B}(C(F)(K)[n ])\cong Q_{\mathcal B}(C(F)K)[n ]$, the unnamed isomorphisms being the shift isomorphisms of $Q_{\mathcal A}$, $Q_{\mathcal B}$. In particular, for every complex $K$ the complex $C(F)K$ obtained termwise represents $F_D(Q_{\mathcal A}K)$, no resolution being needed.
