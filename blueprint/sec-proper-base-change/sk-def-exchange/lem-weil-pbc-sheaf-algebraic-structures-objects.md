Let $\mathcal C$ be a site (Stacks Sites, Definition 00VH). A *sheaf of abelian groups* (resp. *of commutative rings*) on $\mathcal C$ is a functor $\mathcal A:\mathcal C^{op}\to$ abelian groups (resp. commutative rings) such that for every covering $\{U_i\to U\}_{i\in I}$ the diagram $\mathcal A(U)\to\prod_i\mathcal A(U_i)\rightrightarrows\prod_{i,j}\mathcal A(U_i\times_UU_j)$ is an equalizer of abelian groups (resp. commutative rings) (Sites, Definition 00VR); morphisms are natural transformations, i.e. families of homomorphisms $\mathcal A(X)\to\mathcal B(X)$ commuting with the restriction maps. Let $\Sh(\mathcal C)$ the category of sheaves of sets on $\mathcal C$; it has finite products, computed sectionwise ($(\mathcal F_1\times\mathcal F_2)(X)=\mathcal F_1(X)\times\mathcal F_2(X)$, and the final sheaf $*$ with $*(X)$ a singleton). An *abelian group object* of $\Sh(\mathcal C)$ is a sheaf of sets $\mathcal F$ with morphisms $+:\mathcal F\times\mathcal F\to\mathcal F$, $0:*\to\mathcal F$, $i:\mathcal F\to\mathcal F$ satisfying associativity, commutativity, the unit law and $+\circ(\mathrm{id},i)=0\circ(\mathcal F\to*)$ as equalities of morphisms of $\Sh(\mathcal C)$; a *commutative ring object* has in addition $\cdot:\mathcal F\times\mathcal F\to\mathcal F$, $1:*\to\mathcal F$ satisfying the axioms of a commutative ring (associativity, commutativity and unit of $\cdot$, distributivity) as such equalities; morphisms of such objects are morphisms of $\Sh(\mathcal C)$ commuting with the structure maps. Then sending a sheaf of abelian groups (resp. commutative rings) $\mathcal A$ on $\mathcal C$ to its underlying sheaf of sets with the sectionwise addition, zero and negation (resp. also multiplication and one) as structure maps is an isomorphism of categories from sheaves of abelian groups (resp. commutative rings) on $\mathcal C$ to abelian group objects (resp. commutative ring objects) of $\Sh(\mathcal C)$, commuting with the forgetful functors to $\Sh(\mathcal C)$; its inverse gives $\mathcal F(X)$ the operations $+_X$, $0_X$, $i_X$ (and $\cdot_X$, $1_X$). In particular a morphism of sheaves of sets between the underlying sheaves of two sheaves of abelian groups (rings) is a morphism of sheaves of abelian groups (rings) if and only if it commutes with the structure maps.
