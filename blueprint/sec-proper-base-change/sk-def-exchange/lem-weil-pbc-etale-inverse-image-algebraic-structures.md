Let $u:U\to V$ be a morphism of schemes [def-pc-scheme], $U_{\Et}$, $V_{\Et}$ the small etale sites [def-weil-pbc-etale-site] and $b:V_{\Et}\to U_{\Et}$, $W\mapsto U\times_VW$, the base-change functor. For a sheaf of sets $\mathcal F$ on $U_{\Et}$ put $(u_*\mathcal F)(W)=\mathcal F(U\times_VW)$, and for a sheaf of sets $\mathcal G$ on $V_{\Et}$ let $u^{-1}\mathcal G$ be the sheafification [def-weil-pbc-sheafification] of the presheaf $U'\mapsto\varinjlim\mathcal G(W)$, the colimit over the pairs $(W/V,\ U'\to U\times_VW$ over $U)$. Then: (1) $u^{-1}$ is a functor from sheaves of sets on $V_{\Et}$ to sheaves of sets on $U_{\Et}$, left adjoint to $u_*$, and it commutes with finite limits; in particular $u^{-1}(*)=*$ for the final (singleton) sheaf and $u^{-1}(\mathcal G_1\times\mathcal G_2)=u^{-1}\mathcal G_1\times u^{-1}\mathcal G_2$ via the projections. (2) If $\mathcal G$ is a sheaf of abelian groups (resp. of commutative rings) on $V_{\Et}$, then $u^{-1}\mathcal G$ with the images under $u^{-1}$ of the structure maps ($+$, $0$, $-$; resp. also $\cdot$ and $1$), via (1), is a sheaf of abelian groups (resp. commutative rings) on $U_{\Et}$; this gives functors $u^{-1}$ on sheaves of abelian groups and of commutative rings, compatible with the forgetful functors to sheaves of sets, left adjoint to $u_*$ on sheaves of abelian groups (resp. rings), whose adjunction bijections and units $\mathcal G\to u_*u^{-1}\mathcal G$ are those of (1) on underlying sheaves of sets. (3) On abelian sheaves the functor of (2) is canonically isomorphic, compatibly with the adjunctions, to the inverse image $u^{-1}:\Ab(V_{\Et})\to\Ab(U_{\Et})$ of [def-weil-pbc-inverse-image] [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves], which is left adjoint to the direct image $u_*$ of [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]; in particular the underlying sheaf of sets of the abelian inverse image $u^{-1}\mathcal G$ is the set-theoretic inverse image of the underlying sheaf of sets of $\mathcal G$, and the unit $\eta_u$ of the abelian adjunction is, on underlying sheaves of sets, the unit of (1).
