Under the hypotheses of [sk-thm-properbasechange], for every geometric point
$\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}(\Omega)\to Y$ there is a canonical isomorphism in
the derived category of $\Lambda$-modules

$$
(Rf_*K)_{\bar y}\simeq R\Gamma\bigl(X_{\bar y,\mathrm{proét}},K|_{X_{\bar y}}\bigr).
$$

For a classical torsion sheaf $F$ of $\Lambda$-modules this says

$$
(R^qf_*F)_{\bar y}\simeq H^q_{\mathrm{proét}}(X_{\bar y},F|_{X_{\bar y}})
\qquad(q\geq0).
$$
