A finite ring is torsion as an abelian group, so
[prop-weil-proet-finite-proper-base-change], specialized to the cartesian square
[def-weil-pbc-cartesian-square] defined by the geometric point $\bar y$, whose base
change is $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to Y$, gives the first isomorphism. For the
second, place $F$ in degree zero and take $q$-th cohomology: since $\Omega$ is
separably closed, sections over $\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$ are exact, the stalk
of $R^qf_*F$ at $\bar y$ is $H^q$ of $\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}\Omega,\bar y^{-1}Rf_*F)$,
and $\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}\Omega,Ra_{\bar y,*}i_{\bar y}^{-1}F)$ computes
$R\Gamma(X_{\bar y},F|_{X_{\bar y}})$.
