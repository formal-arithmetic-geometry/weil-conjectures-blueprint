By Propositions [sk-thm-p1] and [sk-thm-reduction], $\mathrm{PBC}_\Lambda(f)$
holds on the étale site for every proper $f$ and every $\Lambda$ which is
torsion as an abelian group. Since the six operations
are defined on the pro-étale site, we need the statement there. By the
comparison theorems of Section “The pro-étale site and its comparison theorems”, it holds
for classical complexes with torsion cohomology sheaves on the pro-étale
site, and with an arbitrary coefficient ring.
