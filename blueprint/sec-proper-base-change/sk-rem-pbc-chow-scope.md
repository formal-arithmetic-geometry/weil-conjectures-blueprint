For the standing schemes of finite type over $k$, the Noetherian case of the
appendix on compactification suffices for [sk-thm-chow]. The qcqs version of
[sk-thm-chow-projective] gives the statement over the arbitrary bases allowed
in this section, for proper morphisms of finite type.
