For a ring $\Lambda$, write $\mathrm{IA}_{\Lambda}(f)$ if for every
cartesian square (4.1) and every injective sheaf $I$ of
$\Lambda$-modules on $X_{\acute{e}t}$ which is torsion, one has

$$
R^qf'_*g'^*I=0\qquad(q>0).
$$
