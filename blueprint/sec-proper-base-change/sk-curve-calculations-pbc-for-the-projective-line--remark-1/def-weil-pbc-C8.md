For every curve coefficient pair [def-weil-pbc-curve-setting] $(C,F)$ and
every open immersion $a:U\hookrightarrow C$, the restriction map

$$
 a^*:H^2(C_{\Et},F)\longrightarrow H^2(U_{\Et},a^{-1}F)
$$

is surjective.
