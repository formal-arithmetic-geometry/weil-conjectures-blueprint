**Step 1.** *Limits (clauses (2) and (3) for limits).* Let $i\mapsto\mathcal F_i$ be a diagram $\mathcal I\to\Mod(\mathcal O)$ with $\mathcal I$ small. By [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-limits-sectionwise] it has a limit $\lim_i\mathcal F_i$ in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$, computed sectionwise: $(\lim_i\mathcal F_i)(V)=\lim_i\mathcal F_i(V)$ with componentwise operations and restrictions, the projections being the coordinate maps, and the underlying abelian group of $(\lim_i\mathcal F_i)(V)$ is the limit of the abelian groups $\mathcal F_i(V)$. Hence the underlying abelian sheaf $\mathrm{forget}(\lim_i\mathcal F_i)$, with the projections, is the sectionwise limit of the presheaves of abelian groups $\mathrm{forget}(\mathcal F_i)$ [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category]. By [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact] (1) the limit of the diagram $i\mapsto\mathrm{forget}(\mathcal F_i)$ in $\Ab(\mathcal C)$ is its limit as presheaves, computed sectionwise; so $\mathrm{forget}(\lim_i\mathcal F_i)$ with $\mathrm{forget}$ of the projections is a limit of $i\mapsto\mathrm{forget}(\mathcal F_i)$ in $\Ab(\mathcal C)$: $\mathrm{forget}$ commutes with small limits.

**Step 2.** *Colimits exist and are the sheafified sectionwise colimits (clause (2) for colimits).* Let $i\mapsto\mathcal F_i$ be a diagram $\mathcal I\to\Mod(\mathcal O)$ with $\mathcal I$ small, regarded as a diagram of presheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules. By [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-modules-colimits-sectionwise] the sectionwise colimit $\mathcal P:V\mapsto\colim_i\mathcal F_i(V)$ is a presheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules and, with the induced maps $\iota_i:\mathcal F_i\to\mathcal P$, a colimit of the diagram in $\mathrm{PMod}(\mathcal O)$. Let $\eta:\mathcal P\to\mathcal P^\#$ be its sheafification as a presheaf of $\mathcal O$-modules [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification], and put $\iota^\#_i=\eta\circ\iota_i:\mathcal F_i\to\mathcal P^\#$, a cocone in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$. It is a colimit [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit]: let $(\mathcal G,(g_i)_i)$ be a cocone in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$. Morphisms of sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules are the morphisms of their underlying presheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], so $(\mathcal G,(g_i))$ is a cocone in $\mathrm{PMod}(\mathcal O)$, and there is exactly one $g:\mathcal P\to\mathcal G$ in $\mathrm{PMod}(\mathcal O)$ with $g\iota_i=g_i$ for all $i$; by the universal property of $\eta$ [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification] there is exactly one $h:\mathcal P^\#\to\mathcal G$ in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$ with $h\eta=g$. Then $h\iota^\#_i=g_i$ for all $i$; and if $h'$ also satisfies $h'\iota^\#_i=g_i$ for all $i$, then $h'\eta$ satisfies $(h'\eta)\iota_i=g_i$, so $h'\eta=g$ and $h'=h$. Hence $\colim_i\mathcal F_i=\mathcal P^\#$ exists in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$, with coprojections $\iota^\#_i$.

**Step 3.** *$\mathrm{forget}$ commutes with colimits (clause (3) for colimits).* Keep the notation of step 2 and let $\mathcal G_i=\mathrm{forget}(\mathcal F_i)\in\Ab(\mathcal C)$. By [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-modules-colimits-sectionwise] the underlying presheaf of abelian groups $\mathcal Q$ of $\mathcal P$, with the maps $\iota_i$, is the colimit of the $\mathcal G_i$ in the category $\mathrm{PAb}(\mathcal C)$ of presheaves of abelian groups. By the construction of [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification], the underlying abelian sheaf of $\mathcal P^\#$, with the map $\eta$, is the sheafification $\mathcal Q\to\mathcal Q^\#$ of $\mathcal Q$ as a presheaf of abelian groups [def-weil-pbc-sheafification], the $\mathcal O$-module structure being added on top of it. So $\mathrm{forget}(\colim_i\mathcal F_i)=\mathcal Q^\#$ and $\mathrm{forget}(\iota^\#_i)=\eta\circ\iota_i$. Now $(\mathcal Q^\#,(\eta\iota_i)_i)$ is a colimit of $i\mapsto\mathcal G_i$ in $\Ab(\mathcal C)$ (this is Stacks 03CO (4), the colimit of abelian sheaves is the sheafification of the presheaf colimit), by the argument of step 2 with $\mathbf Z$ in place of $\mathcal O$: for a cocone $(\mathcal H,(g_i))$ in $\Ab(\mathcal C)$, which is a cocone in $\mathrm{PAb}(\mathcal C)$ since $\Ab(\mathcal C)$ is a full subcategory, the colimit property of $\mathcal Q$ gives exactly one $g:\mathcal Q\to\mathcal H$ with $g\iota_i=g_i$, and the universal property of sheafification [def-weil-pbc-sheafification] exactly one $h:\mathcal Q^\#\to\mathcal H$ with $h\eta=g$; uniqueness of $h$ with $h\eta\iota_i=g_i$ for all $i$ follows as in step 2. Hence $\mathrm{forget}$ carries the colimit cocone of step 2 to a colimit cocone in $\Ab(\mathcal C)$; equivalently the canonical morphism $\colim_i\mathrm{forget}(\mathcal F_i)\to\mathrm{forget}(\colim_i\mathcal F_i)$ is an isomorphism, two colimits of the same diagram being related by a unique isomorphism compatible with the coprojections [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit].

**Step 4.** *Faithfulness, kernels, cokernels, exactness (clause (1)).* $\mathrm{forget}$ is the identity on morphisms, a morphism of sheaves of $\mathcal O$-modules being a morphism of the underlying abelian sheaves with $\mathcal O(U)$-linear components [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category]; so it is injective on each set of morphisms, i.e. faithful, and it is additive, addition of morphisms in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$ being the sectionwise addition of morphisms of abelian sheaves. Let $\varphi:\mathcal F\to\mathcal G$ in $\Mod(\mathcal O)$. Its kernel is the limit of the diagram $\mathcal F\rightrightarrows\mathcal G$ formed by $\varphi$ and $0$; by step 1 it is the sectionwise kernel $V\mapsto\ker(\varphi_V)$ and $\mathrm{forget}$ carries it to the kernel of $\mathrm{forget}(\varphi)$ in $\Ab(\mathcal C)$, which is also the sectionwise kernel [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact] (1). Its cokernel is the colimit of the same diagram; by step 2 it is the sheafification of the sectionwise colimit of that diagram, which is the sectionwise cokernel $V\mapsto\mathcal G(V)/\varphi_V(\mathcal F(V))$; by step 3 $\mathrm{forget}$ carries it to the cokernel of $\mathrm{forget}(\varphi)$ in $\Ab(\mathcal C)$, the sheafification of the same sectionwise cokernel of abelian groups. Both categories are abelian ([def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category]; [def-pur-abelian-sheaf-kernel] for $\Ab(\mathcal C)$). An additive functor between abelian categories that preserves kernels and cokernels preserves images $\operatorname{im}\psi=\ker(\operatorname{coker}\psi)$, hence carries a sequence $A\xrightarrow{\alpha}B\xrightarrow{\beta}C$ with $\operatorname{im}\alpha=\ker\beta$ to one with the same property: $\mathrm{forget}$ is exact. (Equivalently it preserves finite limits and finite colimits, by steps 1 and 3.)
