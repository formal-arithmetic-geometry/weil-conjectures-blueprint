For a proper map $f:X\to Y$ of locally compact Hausdorff spaces, the
stalk of $R^qf_*F$ at $y$ is the cohomology $H^q(f^{-1}(y),F)$ of the
fibre. The reason is that the sets $f^{-1}(V)$, for $V$ a neighbourhood
of $y$, form a basis of neighbourhoods of the compact fibre, and the
cohomology of a compact subset is the colimit of the cohomology of its
neighbourhoods. In the étale topology the stalk of $R^qf_*F$ at a geometric point
$\bar y$ is the cohomology of $X\times_YY_{(\bar y)}$, where
$Y_{(\bar y)}$ is the spectrum of the strict henselization of $Y$ at
$\bar y$, and this scheme is much larger than the fibre $X_{\bar y}$. Yet
the dictionary of Section [sec-n-dictionary] needs the trace function of
$Rf_!K$ to be a sum over the fibres, and this requires the stalks of
$Rf_!K$ to be the cohomology of the fibres.
