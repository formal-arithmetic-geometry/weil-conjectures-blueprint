*Remark (sketch).*

Only the six theorems of §§ *The six theorems* are statements; every other fact used
in the proofs is argued inline (finite morphisms, composition,
cancellation, the symmetric product, étale locality, the
degree-zero exchange). The curve-estimate theorem [sk-thm-p1] is
the single step carrying the split marker: it alone sits on the
critical path (Step 1 of the final theorem and the hypothesis of the
reduction), and its own decomposition is by far the largest one.
The target theorem is last, as the readme's decomposition order
prescribes. The blueprint labels:
`thm:weil-pbc-chow`,
`prop:weil-pbc-property-iff-acyclicity`,
`lem:weil-pbc-leray-acyclicity` (the acyclicity criterion
[sk-thm-acyclicity]),
`thm:weil-grothendieck-spectral-sequence` (deferred to its
own blueprint),
`prop:weil-pbc-projective-line`,
`prop:weil-pbc-reduction-to-projective-line`.
