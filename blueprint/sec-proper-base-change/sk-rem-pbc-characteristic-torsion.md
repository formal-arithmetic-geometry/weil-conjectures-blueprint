Properness in $(C_7)$ of [sk-thm-pbc-curve-input] is essential when the
torsion includes the characteristic. For example, in characteristic $p$,

$$
H^1(\mathbf A^1_k,\mathbf F_p)=k[t]/\{h^p-h:h\in k[t]\},
$$

and extending an algebraically closed ground field can add new classes. This
is why the field-extension step [sk-lem-pbc-curve-field-extension] needs
properness: its coefficients are arbitrary torsion sheaves.
