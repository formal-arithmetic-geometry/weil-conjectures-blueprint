**Step 1.** **Step 1 (the cone of $c$ has no cohomology in degrees $\leq n$).** $D^+(\mathcal A)$ is a full triangulated subcategory of $D(\mathcal A)$, closed under isomorphisms and containing the third vertex of a distinguished triangle of $D(\mathcal A)$ whose other two vertices lie in it [def-weil-pbc-bounded-derived-subcategories]; by axiom TR1 of the triangulated category $D(\mathcal A)$ [def-weil-triangulated-category] complete $c$ to a distinguished triangle $L\xrightarrow{c}M\xrightarrow{p}C\xrightarrow{w}L[1 ]$, so $C\in D^+(\mathcal A)$. Its long exact cohomology sequence [lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact] contains, for each $j$, the exact piece $\mathcal H^j(L)\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^j(c)}\mathcal H^j(M)\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^j(p)}\mathcal H^j(C)\xrightarrow{\delta^j}\mathcal H^{j+1}(L)\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^{j+1}(c)}\mathcal H^{j+1}(M)$. Let $j\leq n$. Since $\mathcal H^j(c)$ is an isomorphism, in particular an epimorphism, exactness at $\mathcal H^j(M)$ gives $\Ker\mathcal H^j(p)=\Im\mathcal H^j(c)=\mathcal H^j(M)$, so $\mathcal H^j(p)=0$; exactness at $\mathcal H^j(C)$ then gives $\Ker\delta^j=\Im\mathcal H^j(p)=0$, so $\delta^j$ is a monomorphism. Since $j+1\leq n+1$, $\mathcal H^{j+1}(c)$ is a monomorphism (an isomorphism if $j+1\leq n$, a monomorphism by hypothesis if $j+1=n+1$), so exactness at $\mathcal H^{j+1}(L)$ gives $\Im\delta^j=\Ker\mathcal H^{j+1}(c)=0$, i.e. $\delta^j=0$. A monomorphism that is zero has zero source: $\mathcal H^j(C)=0$ for all $j\leq n$, i.e. $\mathcal H^i(C)=0$ for all $i<n+1$. (This is where both hypotheses are used: surjectivity of $\mathcal H^j(c)$ for $j\leq n$ and injectivity of $\mathcal H^{j+1}(c)$ for $j+1\leq n+1$; in the source's case $C=\tau_{\geq n+1}K$ this vanishing holds by construction.)

**Step 2.** **Step 2 ($Rv$ of the triangle is distinguished).** The right derived functor $Rv:D^+(\mathcal A)\to D^+(\mathcal B)$, $RvK=v(I(K))$ for a K-injective resolution $K\to I(K)$ [def-sp-kinjective-bridge] (a Grothendieck abelian category has enough injectives and K-injective resolutions of bounded below complexes [def-sp-grothendieck-abelian]; any two K-injective resolutions, in particular a bounded below complex of injectives, are homotopy equivalent over $K$, so this is the functor of the cited lemma), is a triangulated functor with its shift isomorphism $\sigma$ [lem-weil-pbc-derived-direct-image-triangulated]. Hence $RvL\xrightarrow{Rv(c)}RvM\xrightarrow{Rv(p)}RvC\xrightarrow{\sigma_L\circ Rv(w)}(RvL)[1 ]$ is a distinguished triangle of $D^+(\mathcal B)$, hence of $D(\mathcal B)$ ($D^+(\mathcal B)$ being a full triangulated subcategory [def-weil-pbc-bounded-derived-subcategories]).

**Step 3.** **Step 3 ($Rv C$ has no cohomology in degrees $\leq n$).** By Step 1, $C\in D^+(\mathcal A)$ satisfies $\mathcal H^i(C)=0$ for all $i<n+1$. By [lem-weil-pbc-derived-functor-negative-vanishing] with $b=n+1$, $\mathcal H^j(RvC)=0$ for all $j<n+1$, i.e. for all $j\leq n$.

**Step 4.** **Step 4 (the long exact sequence of $Rv$ of the triangle).** Apply [lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact] to the distinguished triangle of Step 2 in $D(\mathcal B)$: for every $j$ the sequence $\mathcal H^{j-1}(RvC)\xrightarrow{\delta^{j-1}}\mathcal H^j(RvL)\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^j(Rv(c))}\mathcal H^j(RvM)\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^j(Rv(p))}\mathcal H^j(RvC)$ is exact. Let $j\leq n$. Exactness at $\mathcal H^j(RvL)$ gives $\Ker\mathcal H^j(Rv(c))=\Im\delta^{j-1}$, which is $0$ because $\mathcal H^{j-1}(RvC)=0$ by Step 3 ($j-1<n$); so $\mathcal H^j(Rv(c))$ is a monomorphism. Exactness at $\mathcal H^j(RvM)$ gives $\Im\mathcal H^j(Rv(c))=\Ker\mathcal H^j(Rv(p))=\mathcal H^j(RvM)$, because $\mathcal H^j(RvC)=0$ by Step 3 ($j\leq n$); so $\mathcal H^j(Rv(c))$ is an epimorphism. A monomorphism and epimorphism in the abelian category $\mathcal B$ is an isomorphism: $\mathcal H^j(Rv(c))$ is an isomorphism for every $j\leq n$.

**Step 5.** **Step 5 (the truncation).** Let $K\in D^+(\mathcal A)$, represented by a bounded below complex $K$, and $c_n:\tau_{\leq n}K\to K$ the inclusion of the canonical truncation. By [lem-weil-pbc-truncation-cohomology], $\tau_{\leq n}K$ is bounded below (so $c_n$ is a morphism of $D^+(\mathcal A)$ [def-weil-pbc-bounded-derived-subcategories]), $\mathcal H^i(c_n)$ is an isomorphism for every $i\leq n$, and $\mathcal H^{n+1}(\tau_{\leq n}K)=0$, so $\mathcal H^{n+1}(c_n)$, a morphism out of a zero object, is a monomorphism. Steps 1-4 applied to $c=c_n$ give that $\mathcal H^j(Rv(c_n))$ is an isomorphism for $j\leq n$, in particular $\mathcal H^n(Rv\,\tau_{\leq n}K)\to\mathcal H^n(RvK)$ for $j=n$.
