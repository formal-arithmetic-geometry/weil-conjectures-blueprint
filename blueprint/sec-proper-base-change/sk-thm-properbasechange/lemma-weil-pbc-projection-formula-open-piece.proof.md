Let $i:Z\to\overline X$ be the closed immersion of the complement of $X$.
A morphism of the derived category of sheaves on $\overline X_{\Et}$ is an
isomorphism as soon as its pullbacks along $j$ and along $i$ are. After
$Lj^*=j^*$, the map becomes, under $j^*j_!\cong1$, the identity of
$E\otimes^LLj^*F$, because the unit of $j_!\dashv j^*$ is an isomorphism.
After $Li^*$, both sides vanish: $i^*j_!=0$, and $Li^*$ is compatible with
$\otimes^L$, so $Li^*((j_!E)\otimes^LF)\cong i^*j_!E\otimes^LLi^*F=0$.
Hence the map is an isomorphism; naturality holds because it is built from the
unit and the tensor compatibility, both natural.
