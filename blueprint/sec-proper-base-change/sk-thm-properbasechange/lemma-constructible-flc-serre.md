Let $\mathcal A=\mathrm{Mod}_\Lambda$ with $\Lambda$ a ring. The kernel
and the cokernel of a morphism between finite locally constant sheaves are
finite locally constant. If $\Lambda$ is finite, the middle term of a short
exact sequence whose outer terms are finite locally constant is finite
locally constant.
