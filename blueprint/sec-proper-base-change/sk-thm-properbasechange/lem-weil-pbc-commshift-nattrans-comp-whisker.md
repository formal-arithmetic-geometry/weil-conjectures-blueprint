Let $\mathcal C,\mathcal D,\mathcal E$ be categories with shift functors $[1 ]$ (automorphisms or auto-equivalences, as for triangles [def-weil-pbc-triangles-with-shift]). A *functor commuting with the shift* is a pair $(F,\varphi)$ of a functor $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ and a natural isomorphism $\varphi_X:F(X[1 ])\xrightarrow{\sim}F(X)[1 ]$; the composite of $(F,\varphi)$ and $(G,\gamma):\mathcal D\to\mathcal E$ is $(GF,\chi)$ with $\chi_X=\gamma_{FX}\circ G(\varphi_X)$, and the identity functor carries the identity isomorphism. A natural transformation $\tau:F_1\to F_2$ between functors $(F_1,\varphi_1),(F_2,\varphi_2)$ commuting with the shift is *compatible with the shift* if $\varphi_{2,X}\circ\tau_{X[1 ]}=\tau_X[1 ]\circ\varphi_{1,X}$ for all $X$. Then: (i) the identity transformation is compatible with the shift, and if $\tau:F_1\to F_2$ and $\tau':F_2\to F_3$ are, so is $\tau'\circ\tau$; (ii) if $\tau:F_1\to F_2$ ($\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$) is compatible with the shift and $(G,\gamma):\mathcal D\to\mathcal E$ commutes with the shift, then $G\tau:GF_1\to GF_2$, $(G\tau)_X=G(\tau_X)$, is compatible with the shift for the composite isomorphisms; (iii) if $\tau:G_1\to G_2$ ($\mathcal D\to\mathcal E$) is compatible with the shift and $(F,\varphi):\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ commutes with the shift, then $\tau F:G_1F\to G_2F$, $(\tau F)_X=\tau_{FX}$, is compatible with the shift for the composite isomorphisms; (iv) the composite isomorphisms of $H\circ(G\circ F)$ and $(H\circ G)\circ F$ agree.
