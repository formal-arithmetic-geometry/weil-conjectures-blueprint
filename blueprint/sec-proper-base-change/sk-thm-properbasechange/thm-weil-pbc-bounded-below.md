For a cartesian square [def-weil-pbc-cartesian-square] with $f$ proper and
every $K\in\Dplus(X_{\Et})$ with torsion cohomology,

$$
 \Ex_{\square,K}:g^{-1}Rf_*K
 \xrightarrow{\sim}Rf'_*g'^{-1}K
$$

is an isomorphism in $\Dplus(Y'_{\Et})$.
