For every $n$, the diagram
$$
 \begin{array}{ccc}
  A_n&\xrightarrow{h_n}&B_n\\
  v_n\downarrow&&\downarrow v'_n\\
  C_n&\xrightarrow{h'_n}&D_n
 \end{array}
$$
commutes. Equivalently, $v'_nh_n=h'_nv_n$.
