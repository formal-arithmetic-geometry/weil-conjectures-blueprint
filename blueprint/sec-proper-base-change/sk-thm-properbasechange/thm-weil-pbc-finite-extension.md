For every finite $E/\Ql$, every cartesian square
[def-weil-pbc-cartesian-square] with $f$ proper, and every
$K\in\Dbc(X,E)$,
$$
 Lg^*Rf_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf'_*Lg'^*K
 \quad\text{in }\Dbc(Y',E).
$$
