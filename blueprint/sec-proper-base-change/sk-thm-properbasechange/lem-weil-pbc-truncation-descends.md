Let $\mathcal A$ be an abelian category with derived category $Q:\mathrm{CoCh}(\mathcal A)\to D(\mathcal A)$ [def-weil-abstract-derived-category], and for $q\in\mathbf Z$ let $\tau_{\leq q}$ be the canonical truncation of complexes ($(\tau_{\leq q}K)^r=K^r$ for $r<q$, $\Ker d^q_K$ for $r=q$, $0$ for $r>q$), a functor on cochain complexes, $H^q(-)[-q ]$ the functor $K\mapsto$ (the complex with $H^q(K)$ in degree $q$, $0$ elsewhere), $i_q:\tau_{\leq q-1}\to\tau_{\leq q}$ the inclusion and $j_q:\tau_{\leq q}\to H^q(-)[-q ]$ the projection $\Ker d^q\to H^q$ in degree $q$, natural transformations of functors on complexes. Then: (a) if $s:K\to L$ is a quasi-isomorphism, so are $\tau_{\leq q}s$ (indeed $H^r(\tau_{\leq q}K)=H^r(K)$ for $r\le q$ and $0$ for $r>q$, naturally) and $H^q(s)[-q ]$; (b) hence there are functors $\tau_{\leq q},\ H^q(-)[-q ]:D(\mathcal A)\to D(\mathcal A)$, unique up to unique isomorphism, with specified natural isomorphisms $\tau_{\leq q}\circ Q\cong Q\circ\tau_{\leq q}$ and $H^q(-)[-q ]\circ Q\cong Q\circ H^q(-)[-q ]$, and unique natural transformations $i_q:\tau_{\leq q-1}\to\tau_{\leq q}$, $j_q:\tau_{\leq q}\to H^q(-)[-q ]$ of functors on $D(\mathcal A)$ whose composites with $Q$ are $Q(i_q)$, $Q(j_q)$ (up to these isomorphisms). In particular a morphism $f:K\to L$ of $D(\mathcal A)$ between complexes $K,L$ gives morphisms $\tau_{\leq q}f$, $\tau_{\leq q-1}f$, $H^q(f)[-q ]$ with $\tau_{\leq q}f\circ i_q=i_q\circ\tau_{\leq q-1}f$ and $H^q(f)[-q ]\circ j_q=j_q\circ\tau_{\leq q}f$, and when $f=Q(\phi)$ for a morphism of complexes $\phi$ these are, up to these isomorphisms, the images under $Q$ of $\tau_{\leq q}\phi$, $\tau_{\leq q-1}\phi$, $H^q(\phi)[-q ]$.
