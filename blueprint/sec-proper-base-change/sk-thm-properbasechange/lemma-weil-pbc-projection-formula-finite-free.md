For $F=\Lambda^N[a]$, the maps $\pi_{h,E,F}$ and $\rho_{h,E,F}$ are inverse:

$$
\rho_{h,E,F}\pi_{h,E,F}=1,\qquad
\pi_{h,E,F}\rho_{h,E,F}=1.
$$
