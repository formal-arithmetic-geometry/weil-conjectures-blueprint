If $X$ is affine and $I$ is injective in etale module sheaves on $X$, then

$$
 H^q(U_{\Proet},(\nu_X^*I)|_U)=0
 \quad(U\in\mathcal B_X,\ q>0).
$$

Here the left side is derived evaluation on the basis object $U$.
