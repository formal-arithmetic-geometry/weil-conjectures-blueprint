Let $\mathcal C$ be a category, $W$ a class of morphisms of $\mathcal C$, and $L:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ a localization of $\mathcal C$ at $W$: $L(w)$ is an isomorphism for every $w\in W$, and for every category $\mathcal E$ the functor $G\mapsto G\circ L$ from functors $\mathcal D\to\mathcal E$ to functors $\mathcal C\to\mathcal E$ sending every $w\in W$ to an isomorphism is an equivalence of categories (for instance the category of fractions $S^{-1}\mathcal C$ of a multiplicative system $S$ with its functor $Q$, or the derived category $Q:\mathrm{CoCh}(\mathcal A)\to D(\mathcal A)$ at the quasi-isomorphisms). Let $\mathcal E$ be a category. Then: (1) for every functor $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal E$ with $F(w)$ an isomorphism for all $w\in W$ there are a functor $F':\mathcal D\to\mathcal E$ and a natural isomorphism $\phi_F:F'\circ L\xrightarrow{\sim}F$; (2) if $F_1,F_2:\mathcal C\to\mathcal E$ invert $W$, $F_1',F_2':\mathcal D\to\mathcal E$ are functors with natural isomorphisms $\phi_i:F_i'\circ L\xrightarrow{\sim}F_i$, and $\tau:F_1\to F_2$ is a natural transformation, there is a unique natural transformation $\tau':F_1'\to F_2'$ with $\tau'_{L(X)}=\phi_{2,X}^{-1}\circ\tau_X\circ\phi_{1,X}$ for all objects $X$ of $\mathcal C$; $\tau\mapsto\tau'$ respects identities and composition, so a natural isomorphism lifts to a natural isomorphism; in particular $F'$ in (1) is unique up to a unique isomorphism compatible with the $\phi$'s; (3) two natural transformations $\sigma,\sigma':G_1\to G_2$ of functors $\mathcal D\to\mathcal E$ with $\sigma_{L(X)}=\sigma'_{L(X)}$ for all objects $X$ of $\mathcal C$ are equal. For the localization $Q_W:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C[W^{-1}]$ constructed by formally inverting the morphisms of $W$ (generators and relations), (1) holds with $F'\circ Q_W=F$ (an equality of functors), and two functors $G_1,G_2:\mathcal C[W^{-1}]\to\mathcal E$ with $G_1\circ Q_W=G_2\circ Q_W$ are equal, so $F'$ is unique with this property.
