For fixed $f$ and $K$, the full subcategory of objects $P$ for which
$\pi_{f,K,P}$ of [def-weil-pbc-projection-formula-map] is an isomorphism is a
thick triangulated subcategory containing every finite free $A$-module. In
particular, it contains every perfect complex, and for every $n$ there is a
natural isomorphism

$$
(Rf_*K)\otimes^L_\Lambda\Lambda/I^n
\xrightarrow[\pi_n]{\sim}Rf_*K_n
$$

whose inverse is $\rho_n$. The families $(\pi_n)$ and $(\rho_n)$ commute
with the reduction tower, compatible coefficient endomorphisms, and base
change.

## Proof

For $P=A$ the map $\pi_{f,K,A}$ of
[def-weil-pbc-projection-formula-map] is the canonical tensor identity, an
isomorphism; for a finite free $P=A^m$ it is the direct sum of finitely many
of these, so every finite free $A$-module lies in the full subcategory. The
subcategory is triangulated-thick: it is closed under shifts ($Lf^*$, $Rf_*$
and tensor are exact triangulated by
[def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations] and commute with the shift comparisons),
under cones (applying $u:P_1\to P_2$ to the projection-formula maps makes the
cones of the $\pi$'s fit into a distinguished triangle, and the
two-out-of-three property gives the claim), and under direct summands (an
idempotent splitting of $P$ gives one of the isomorphism). Hence it contains
every perfect complex, i.e. every object of the thick triangulated
subcategory generated by the finite free modules, and in particular every
$P_n$'s finite free resolution in the sense of
[def-weil-pbc-adic-coefficient-datum], giving the displayed isomorphism
$(Rf_*K)\otimes_A^LP_n\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf_*K_n$.

Choose a strict-perfect package $F_n^\bullet\to P_n$. Signed totalization
gives inverse arrows

$$
(Rf_*K)\otimes^LF_n^\bullet
\mathrel{\substack{\xrightarrow{\ \pi_n\ }\\[-2pt]
\xleftarrow[\ \rho_n\ ]{}}}
Rf_*(K\otimes^LLf^*F_n^\bullet).
$$

Because $P_n$ is constant, $Lf^*P_n=P_n$, and the target is $Rf_*K_n$.
The identity-compactification lemma shows that this is also the open/proper
compactification composite for proper $f$. The totalization lemma gives
$\rho_n\pi_n=1$ and $\pi_n\rho_n=1$. Uniqueness of an inverse makes the map
independent of the strict-perfect package. Tower, operator, and base-change
compatibility are exactly the preceding two lemmas. This expands the
finite-projective argument located at Tag `0944`.
