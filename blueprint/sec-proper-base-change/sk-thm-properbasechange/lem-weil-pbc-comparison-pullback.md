For $u:U\to V$ there is a natural isomorphism

$$
 \nu_U^*u_{\Et}^{-1}\xrightarrow{\sim}
 u_{\Proet}^{-1}\nu_V^*.
$$

It is compatible with identity maps, composites, and the cartesian
identification in [def-weil-pbc-cartesian-square].
