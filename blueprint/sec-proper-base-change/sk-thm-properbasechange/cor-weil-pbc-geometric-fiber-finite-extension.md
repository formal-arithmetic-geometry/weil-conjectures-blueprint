In the setup of the finite-level geometric-fiber corollary, let $E/\Ql$ be
finite and let $K\in\Dbc(X,E)$. The $E$-coefficient exchange morphism is an
isomorphism
$$
 L\bar y^*Rf_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}
 Ra_{\bar y,*}Li_{\bar y}^*K
 \quad\text{in }\Dbc(\operatorname{Spec}\Omega,E).
$$

## Proof

Specialize [thm-weil-pbc-finite-extension] to the geometric-point base
change.
