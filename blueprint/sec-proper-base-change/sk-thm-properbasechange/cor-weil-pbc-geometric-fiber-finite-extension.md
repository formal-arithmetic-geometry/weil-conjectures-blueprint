In the setup of the finite-level geometric-fiber corollary
[cor-weil-pbc-geometric-fiber-finite] (with $f:X\to Y$ proper between
schemes of finite type over $k$), let $E/\Ql$ be finite and let
$K\in\Dbc(X,E)$ be an object of the condensed bounded constructible category
[sk-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]. The base change morphism is an
isomorphism
$$
 L\bar y^*Rf_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}
 Ra_{\bar y,*}Li_{\bar y}^*K
 \quad\text{in }\Dbc(\operatorname{Spec}\Omega,E).
$$
