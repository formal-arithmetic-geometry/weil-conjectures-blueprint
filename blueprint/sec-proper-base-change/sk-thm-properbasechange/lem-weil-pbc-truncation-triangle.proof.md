**Step 1.** **Step 1 (the short exact sequence of truncations).** By [lem-weil-pbc-truncation-step-ses] (a), (b), the inclusion $i_q$ sits in a short exact sequence of complexes $0\to\tau_{\leq q-1}K\xrightarrow{i_q}\tau_{\leq q}K\xrightarrow{p_q}M_q(K)\to0$, where $M_q(K)=\tau_{\leq q}K/\tau_{\leq q-1}K$ is the two-term complex $K^{q-1}/\Ker d^{q-1}\to\Ker d^q$ in degrees $q-1,q$ (it is not $H^q(K)[-q ]$ itself), and $j_q=\bar j_q\circ p_q$ with $\bar j_q:M_q(K)\to H^q(K)[-q ]$ a quasi-isomorphism.

**Step 2.** **Step 2 (the triangle of the short exact sequence).** By [lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-triangle] applied to the sequence of Step 1 there is $\delta:M_q(K)\to(\tau_{\leq q-1}K)[1 ]$ in $D(\mathcal A)$ such that $\tau_{\leq q-1}K\xrightarrow{i_q}\tau_{\leq q}K\xrightarrow{p_q}M_q(K)\xrightarrow{\delta}(\tau_{\leq q-1}K)[1 ]$ is distinguished.

**Step 3.** **Step 3 (existence of $k_q$).** Since $\bar j_q$ is a quasi-isomorphism, its image in the localization $D(\mathcal A)$ is an isomorphism [def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. Put $k_q=\delta\circ\bar j_q^{-1}$. Then $(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{id},\bar j_q)$ is an isomorphism from the triangle of Step 2 to $(\tau_{\leq q-1}K,\tau_{\leq q}K,H^q(K)[-q ],i_q,j_q,k_q)$: the squares read $i_q\circ\mathrm{id}=\mathrm{id}\circ i_q$, $\bar j_q\circ p_q=j_q\circ\mathrm{id}$ (Step 1) and $k_q\circ\bar j_q=\delta=\mathrm{id}[1 ]\circ\delta$. A triangle isomorphic to a distinguished triangle is distinguished (TR1 of [def-weil-triangulated-category]), so $T_q(K)$ is distinguished.

**Step 4.** **Step 4 (a vanishing of morphisms).** For complexes $K,L$: $\Hom_{D(\mathcal A)}\bigl((\tau_{\leq q-1}K)[1 ],H^q(L)[-q ]\bigr)=0$. Indeed $\tau_{\leq q-1}K$ is a complex with zero terms in degrees $>q-1$, so $(\tau_{\leq q-1}K)[1 ]$ has zero terms in degrees $>q-2$ and lies in $D(\mathcal A)^{\leq q-2}$; $H^q(L)[-q ]$ has zero terms in degrees $<q$ and lies in $D(\mathcal A)^{\geq q}\subseteq D(\mathcal A)^{\geq q-1}$ [def-sbc-standard-tstructure-derived-category]. By axiom 2 of a t-structure, shifted by $q-2$ ($\Hom(A,B[-1 ])=0$ for $A\in D^{\leq0}$, $B\in D^{\geq0}$, i.e. $\Hom(D^{\leq n},D^{\geq n+1})=0$) [def-weil-t-structure], every morphism between them is zero.

**Step 5.** **Step 5 (uniqueness of $k_q$).** Let $k'_q$ be another morphism making $(\tau_{\leq q-1}K,\tau_{\leq q}K,H^q(K)[-q ],i_q,j_q,k'_q)$ distinguished. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-triangle-morphism-extension] (2) to the triangles $T=(\ldots,k_q)$, $T'=(\ldots,k'_q)$, $g=\mathrm{id}_{\tau_{\leq q}K}$, $f=\mathrm{id}$, $h=\mathrm{id}$: the squares $i_q\circ\mathrm{id}=\mathrm{id}\circ i_q$ and $\mathrm{id}\circ j_q=j_q\circ\mathrm{id}$ commute and $\Hom((\tau_{\leq q-1}K)[1 ],H^q(K)[-q ])=0$ by Step 4, so $(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{id},\mathrm{id})$ is a morphism of triangles, i.e. $k'_q=k_q$ (this is BBD 1.1.10 (ii), part (3) of the lemma). So the 'unique' of the statement holds: although in general the third arrow completing two given arrows to a distinguished triangle is not unique, here $\Hom(X[1 ],Z)=0$ for the first and third terms.

**Step 6.** **Step 6 (functoriality).** Let $f:K\to L$ be a morphism of $D(\mathcal A)$ between complexes. By [lem-weil-pbc-truncation-descends] there are morphisms $a=\tau_{\leq q-1}f$, $b=\tau_{\leq q}f$, $c=H^q(f)[-q ]$ in $D(\mathcal A)$ with $b\circ i_q=i_q\circ a$ and $c\circ j_q=j_q\circ b$ (naturality of $i_q$, $j_q$ on $D(\mathcal A)$). Apply [lem-weil-pbc-triangle-morphism-extension] (2) to the distinguished triangles $T_q(K)$, $T_q(L)$ (Step 3) and $g=b$: since $\Hom((\tau_{\leq q-1}K)[1 ],H^q(L)[-q ])=0$ (Step 4), $(a,b,c)$ is a morphism of triangles, i.e. also $k_q(L)\circ c=a[1 ]\circ k_q(K)$. (For $f=Q(\phi)$ a morphism of complexes this is also the image of the morphism of short exact sequences of [lem-weil-pbc-truncation-step-ses] (c) under the naturality of [lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-triangle].)

**Step 7.** **Step 7 (bounded below).** If $K$ is a bounded below complex, so are its subcomplexes $\tau_{\leq q}K$, $\tau_{\leq q-1}K$, and $H^q(K)[-q ]$ has one nonzero term. If only $H^n(K)=0$ for $n<n_0$: $H^n(\tau_{\leq q}K)=H^n(K)$ for $n\leq q$ and $=0$ for $n>q$ (the cohomology of the canonical truncation, read off degreewise: in degree $q$, $\Ker d^q/\Im d^{q-1}$), likewise for $q-1$, and $H^n(H^q(K)[-q ])$ is $H^q(K)$ for $n=q$ and $0$ otherwise; so all three have $H^n=0$ for $n<\min(n_0,q)$ and lie in $D^+$; for $\mathcal A=\Ab(T_{\Et})$ this is $\Dplus(T_{\Et})$ [def-weil-pbc-derived-categories].
