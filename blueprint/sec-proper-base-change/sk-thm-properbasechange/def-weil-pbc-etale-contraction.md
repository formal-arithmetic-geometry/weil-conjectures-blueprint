Starting with $R_0=R$, let $R_{n+1}$ be the tensor product over $R_n$ of one
representative of every faithfully flat etale $R_n$-algebra of finite
presentation. An arbitrary tensor product here means the filtered colimit of
its finite tensor products, including the empty tensor product $R_n$. Set
$R^{\mathrm e}=\varinjlim_nR_n$. Representatives are chosen in a fixed
universe throughout.
