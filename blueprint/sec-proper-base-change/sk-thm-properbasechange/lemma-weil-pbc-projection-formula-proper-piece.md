For the proper piece $p$ and every strict-perfect $F^\bullet$ on $Y$,

$$
(Rp_*j_!E)\otimes^LF^\bullet
\xrightarrow[\pi_{p,j_!E,F}]{\sim}
Rp_*(j_!E\otimes^LLp^*F^\bullet),
$$

with inverse $\rho_{p,j_!E,F}$.
