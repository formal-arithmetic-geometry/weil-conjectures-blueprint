**Step 1.** **Step 1 (the image triangles are distinguished).** $T_q$ is a distinguished triangle of $D(X_{\Et})$ whose three terms lie in $\Dplus(X_{\Et})$ [lem-weil-pbc-truncation-triangle] (bounded below because $K$ is), hence a distinguished triangle of the full triangulated subcategory $\Dplus(X_{\Et})$. The inverse images $g^{-1}$, $g'^{-1}$ on $\Dplus$ are the restrictions of the functors induced by the exact sheaf functors $g^{-1}$, $g'^{-1}$ [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-exact] applied termwise [def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image], which are triangulated with the identity $u^{-1}(M[1 ])=(u^{-1}M)[1 ]$ as shift isomorphism [lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated]; $Rf_*$ and $Rf'_*$ are triangulated with shift isomorphism $\sigma$ [lem-weil-pbc-derived-direct-image-triangulated]. A composite of triangulated functors, with the composite shift isomorphism $\psi_{FX}\circ G(\varphi_X)$, is triangulated [lem-weil-pbc-triangulated-functor-composite]; for $\Phi=g^{-1}\circ Rf_*$ the composite isomorphism is $\mathrm{id}\circ g^{-1}(\sigma_L)=g^{-1}(\sigma_L)=\xi_L$, for $\Psi=Rf'_*\circ g'^{-1}$ it is $\sigma_{g'^{-1}L}\circ Rf'_*(\mathrm{id})=\zeta_L$, the isomorphisms of the statement. Hence for $F\in\{\Phi,\Psi\}$ the triangle $F(T_q)=(F\tau_{\leq q-1}K,F\tau_{\leq q}K,F(\mathcal H^q(K)[-q ]),Fi_q,Fj_q,c_{F,\tau_{\leq q-1}K}\circ Fk_q)$ is distinguished in $\Dplus(Y'_{\Et})$, so in $D(Y'_{\Et})$.

**Step 2.** **Step 2 (claim (1): exact couples).** Apply the long exact cohomology sequence [lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact] to the distinguished triangle $F(T_q)$ of Step 1. Its connecting map $\delta^n$ is $\mathcal H^n$ of the third map $c_F\circ Fk_q$ followed by the identification $\mathcal H^n(M[1 ])=\mathcal H^{n+1}(M)$ (same cycles and boundaries; the sign $d_{M[1 ]}=-d_M$ is irrelevant) [lem-weil-pbc-derived-cohomology-ladder] (Stacks 0148: the map $H^0(Z)\to H^1(X)$ is $H(h)$, no signs; Mathlib `DerivedCategory.HomologySequence.δ T n₀ n₁ := (homologyFunctor C 0).shiftMap T.mor₃ n₀ n₁`, whose shift identification is the identity on the middle term of the short complex, `CochainComplex.shiftShortComplexFunctor'`). With $n=p+q$ the sequence reads
$$
 \cdots\to\mathcal H^{n}(F\tau_{\leq q-1}K)\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^n(Fi_q)}\mathcal H^n(F\tau_{\leq q}K)\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^n(Fj_q)}\mathcal H^n(F(\mathcal H^q(K)[-q ]))\xrightarrow{\delta^n}\mathcal H^{n+1}(F\tau_{\leq q-1}K)\xrightarrow{\mathcal H^{n+1}(Fi_q)}\mathcal H^{n+1}(F\tau_{\leq q}K)\to\cdots
$$
In the notation of the statement its terms are $D_F^{p+1,q-1}$, $D_F^{p,q}$, $E_F^{p,q}$, $D_F^{p+2,q-1}$, $D_F^{p+1,q}$ (since $(p+1)+(q-1)=n$, $(p+2)+(q-1)=n+1$), and its maps are $\alpha_F=\mathcal H^{(p+1)+(q-1)}(Fi_{(q-1)+1}):D_F^{p+1,q-1}\to D_F^{p,q}$, $g_F:D_F^{p,q}\to E_F^{p,q}$, $f_F=\delta^n:E_F^{p,q}\to D_F^{p+2,q-1}$ and $\alpha_F=\mathcal H^{(p+2)+(q-1)}(Fi_q):D_F^{p+2,q-1}\to D_F^{p+1,q}$. Exactness at $D_F^{p,q}$ and at $E_F^{p,q}$ is $\ker g=\operatorname{im}\alpha$ and $\ker f=\operatorname{im}g$ of [def-sp-exact-couple] in bidegree $(p,q)$; exactness at $D_F^{p+2,q-1}$ is $\ker\alpha=\operatorname{im}f$ in bidegree $(p+2,q-1)$, so, $(p,q)$ being arbitrary, $\ker(\alpha:D^{p,q}\to D^{p-1,q+1})=\operatorname{im}(f:E^{p-2,q+1}\to D^{p,q})$ holds in every bidegree (read from the sequence of $T_{q+1}$ at $n=p+q$). Exactness gives the vanishing of consecutive composites. Hence both couples are exact couples. (The same argument, with $Rv$ in place of $F$, is Step 3-4 of [lem-sp-truncation-couple-exact], split separately.)

**Step 3.** **Step 3 (the exchange morphisms form a morphism of triangles).** By [lem-weil-pbc-derived-exchange-triangulated] (2), applied to the triangle $T_q$ (whose shift isomorphisms $\xi,\zeta$ are those used here), the triple $(\Ex_{\square,\tau_{\leq q-1}K},\Ex_{\square,\tau_{\leq q}K},\Ex_{\square,\mathcal H^q(K)[-q ]})$ is a morphism of triangles $\Phi(T_q)\to\Psi(T_q)$ [def-weil-pbc-triangles-with-shift]:
$$
 \Psi i_q\circ\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q-1}K}=\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q}K}\circ\Phi i_q,\qquad \Psi j_q\circ\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q}K}=\Ex_{\mathcal H^q(K)[-q ]}\circ\Phi j_q,
$$
$$
 \zeta\circ\Psi k_q\circ\Ex_{\mathcal H^q(K)[-q ]}=\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q-1}K}[1 ]\circ\xi\circ\Phi k_q .
$$
The first two squares are the naturality of $\Ex_\square$ in $K$ [def-weil-pbc-derived-exchange]; the third is naturality for $k_q$ followed by the compatibility of $\Ex_\square$ with the shift, part (1) of that lemma.

**Step 4.** **Step 4 (claim (2): a morphism of exact couples).** By Step 1, $\Phi(T_q)$ and $\Psi(T_q)$ are distinguished, and by Step 3 the exchange maps form a morphism of triangles between them. By [lem-weil-pbc-derived-cohomology-ladder] its $\mathcal H^n$ commute with the maps of the two long exact sequences of Step 2, the connecting maps included: in degree $n=p+q$, with the sequence of $T_{q+1}$ for the first identity, $t_D^{p-1,q+1}\circ\alpha_\Phi=\alpha_\Psi\circ t_D^{p,q}$ on $D^{p,q}$ (from $\Psi i_{q+1}\circ\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q}K}=\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q+1}K}\circ\Phi i_{q+1}$; $t_D^{p-1,q+1}=\mathcal H^{p+q}(\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q+1}K})$), $t_E^{p,q}\circ g_\Phi=g_\Psi\circ t_D^{p,q}$, and $\mathcal H^{n+1}(\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q-1}K})\circ\delta^n_\Phi=\delta^n_\Psi\circ\mathcal H^n(\Ex_{\mathcal H^q(K)[-q ]})$, i.e. $t_D^{p+2,q-1}\circ f_\Phi=f_\Psi\circ t_E^{p,q}$ since $t_D^{p+2,q-1}=\mathcal H^{p+q+1}(\Ex_{\tau_{\leq q-1}K})$. These are the three conditions of a morphism of exact couples (Stacks 011Q (2), read bidegree by bidegree). (Directly: apply $\mathcal H^{p+q}$ to the three squares of Step 3 and use $\mathcal H^n(a[1 ])=\mathcal H^{n+1}(a)$ under the identification of Step 2.)

**Step 5.** **Step 5 (claim (3): the first graded component).** Let $M=\mathcal H^q(K)$, a sheaf placed in degree $0$. By [lem-weil-pbc-derived-exchange-triangulated] (1) with $n=-q$ (whose $\xi^{(n)},\zeta^{(n)}$ are the iterates used in the statement), $\zeta^{(-q)}_M\circ\Ex_{\square,M[-q ]}=\Ex_{\square,M}[-q ]\circ\xi^{(-q)}_M$. Apply $\mathcal H^{p+q}$ and the identification $\mathcal H^{p+q}(L[-q ])=\mathcal H^p(L)$ ($q$-fold iterate of that of Step 2, natural in $L$, so $\mathcal H^{p+q}(a[-q ])=\mathcal H^p(a)$): under the isomorphisms $E_\Phi^{p,q}\cong\mathcal H^p(\Phi M)$ and $E_\Psi^{p,q}\cong\mathcal H^p(\Psi M)$ of the statement, $t_E^{p,q}$ becomes $\mathcal H^p(\Ex_{\square,M}):\mathcal H^p(g^{-1}Rf_*M)\to\mathcal H^p(Rf'_*g'^{-1}M)$. Finally $\mathcal H^p(g^{-1}Rf_*M)\cong g^{-1}\mathcal H^p(Rf_*M)=g^{-1}R^pf_*M$ by the natural isomorphism $H^p(u^{-1}L)\cong u^{-1}H^p(L)$ for the exact functor $g^{-1}$ [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-exact] applied termwise [def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image], [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-bounded-below], and $\mathcal H^p(Rf'_*g'^{-1}M)=R^pf'_*(g'^{-1}M)$ by the definition of $R^pf'_*$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. So $t_E^{p,q}$ is the degree-$p$ exchange map $\mathcal H^p(\Ex_{\square,\mathcal H^q(K)}):g^{-1}R^pf_*\mathcal H^q(K)\to R^pf'_*g'^{-1}\mathcal H^q(K)$, the map of Stacks' $E_2$-pages.
