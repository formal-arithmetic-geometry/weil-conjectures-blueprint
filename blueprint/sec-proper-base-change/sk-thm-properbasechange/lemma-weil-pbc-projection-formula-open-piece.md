For a bounded finite-projective complex $F^\bullet$ on $\overline X$, the
canonical map

$$
(j_!E)\otimes^LF^\bullet
\xrightarrow{\sim}j_!(E\otimes^LLj^*F^\bullet)
$$

is an isomorphism, natural in $E$ and $F^\bullet$.
