Let $\Phi:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ be an exact additive functor between abelian categories [def-sp-abelian], and let $C=(D,E,\alpha,f,g)$ be an exact couple in $\mathcal A$ [def-sp-exact-couple], with iterated couples $C_r=(D_r,E_r,\alpha_r,f_r,g_r)$, $C_2=C$, $C_{r+1}=C_r'$, and spectral sequence $(E_r,d_r)_{r\geq2}$, $d_r=g_r\circ f_r$. Write $\Phi C=(\Phi D,\Phi E,\Phi\alpha,\Phi f,\Phi g)$ for the datum obtained by applying $\Phi$ to every component $D^{p,q}$, $E^{p,q}$ and every structure map. Then:

1. $\Phi C$ is an exact couple in $\mathcal B$.
2. Write $(\Phi C)_r=((\Phi D)_r,(\Phi E)_r,\dots)$ for its iterated couples and $d_r^\Phi$ for its differentials. For every $r\geq2$, $(\Phi D)_r^{p,q}=\Phi(D_r^{p,q})$ as subobjects of $\Phi D^{p,q}$ (via the monomorphism $\Phi(D_r^{p,q}\hookrightarrow D^{p,q})$), and there are isomorphisms $\phi_r^{p,q}:\Phi(E_r^{p,q})\xrightarrow{\sim}(\Phi E)_r^{p,q}$ with $\phi_2=\mathrm{id}$, such that (identity on $D$-terms, $\phi_r$ on $E$-terms) is an isomorphism of exact couples $\Phi(C_r)\to(\Phi C)_r$; in particular $\phi_r\circ\Phi(d_r)=d_r^\Phi\circ\phi_r$, and $\phi_{r+1}$ is the map induced by $\phi_r$ on cohomology composed with the comparison $\Phi(\ker d_r/\operatorname{im}d_r)\cong\ker\Phi(d_r)/\operatorname{im}\Phi(d_r)$ of the exact functor $\Phi$.
3. Suppose $C$ satisfies the hypotheses of [thm-sp-half-plane-convergence] for an integer $a$: $D^{p,q}=0$ for $q<a$, and $E^{p,q}=0$ unless $p\geq0$ and $q\geq a$. Then so does $\Phi C$, for the same $a$. Put $A^n=D^{0,n}$ and $F^pA^n=\operatorname{im}(\alpha^p:D^{p,n-p}\to A^n)$ for $p\geq0$, $F^pA^n=A^n$ for $p\leq0$, the finite filtration [def-sp-filtration] of that theorem; then the abutment of $\Phi C$ is $(\Phi D)^{0,n}=\Phi A^n$ and its filtration is $F^p\Phi A^n=\Phi(F^pA^n)$ as subobjects of $\Phi A^n$, so that $\kappa:\Phi(\operatorname{gr}^pA^n)\cong\operatorname{gr}^p\Phi A^n$ by exactness. For $r>\max(q-a+1,p)$ the isomorphism $\phi_r^{p,q}$ is compatible with the identifications $E_r^{p,q}=E_{r+1}^{p,q}$ and $(\Phi E)_r^{p,q}=(\Phi E)_{r+1}^{p,q}$ of that theorem, hence defines $\phi_\infty^{p,q}:\Phi(E_\infty^{p,q})\cong(\Phi E)_\infty^{p,q}$ [def-sp-convergence]. Finally, for $n=p+q$ and $r\geq\max(p+3,q-a+2)$, put $u=p-r+2$, $v=q+r-2$, let $\lambda_w=\alpha^{-w}:A^n=D^{0,n}\to D^{w,n-w}$ ($w\leq0$), an isomorphism, and let $\psi_r^{p,q}:\operatorname{gr}^pA^n\xrightarrow{\sim}E_r^{p,q}=E_\infty^{p,q}$ be the isomorphism induced by $g_r:D_r^{u,v}\to E_r^{p,q}$ through $D_r^{u,v}=\lambda_u(F^pA^n)$ and $\alpha_r(D_r^{u+1,v-1})=\lambda_u(F^{p+1}A^n)$ (this is the isomorphism $E_\infty\cong\operatorname{gr}$ of [thm-sp-half-plane-convergence] as Step 2 of its proof constructs it); define $\psi_r^{\Phi}$ in the same way for $\Phi C$. Then $\phi_r^{p,q}\circ\Phi(\psi_r^{p,q})=\psi_r^{\Phi,p,q}\circ\kappa$.
