Let $u:U\to V$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated and let
$L\in\Dplus(U_{\Et},\Lambda)$. There is a natural isomorphism

$$
 \nu_V^*Ru_{\Et,*}L
 \xrightarrow{\sim}
 Ru_{\Proet,*}\nu_U^*L.
$$

It is compatible with identities, composites, and morphisms of $L$.
