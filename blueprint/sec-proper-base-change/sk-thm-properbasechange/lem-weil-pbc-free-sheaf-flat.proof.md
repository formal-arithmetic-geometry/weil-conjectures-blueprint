**Step 1.** Step 1 (the functor to be studied). Let $Q$ be the presheaf $V\mapsto\bigoplus_{\varphi\in\mathrm{Mor}_{\mathcal C}(V,X)}P$ of the statement, so $P_X^\#=aQ$, and let $\iota$, $a$, $\otimes_p$ be as in [lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed]. By the definition of the sheaf tensor product in [lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed] (2), $P_X^\#\otimes F=a(\iota aQ\otimes_p\iota F)$, and by the strong monoidality of $a$ in the same clause, $a(Q\otimes_p\iota F)\cong aQ\otimes a(\iota F)$ naturally in the sheaf $F$. The counit $a\iota F\to F$ is an isomorphism, naturally in $F$ ([lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise] (3)), and $aQ\otimes-$ is a functor, so $P_X^\#\otimes F\cong a(Q\otimes_p\iota F)$ naturally in $F$. Hence $P_X^\#\otimes-\cong a\circ(Q\otimes_p-)\circ\iota$ as functors $\mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal C,J;\mathcal A)\to\mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal C,J;\mathcal A)$, and since a functor naturally isomorphic to one preserving finite limits preserves them, it suffices to show that $a\circ(Q\otimes_p-)\circ\iota$ preserves finite limits. (In Stacks this is the identification $j_{U!}\mathcal O_U=(j_{U!}\mathcal O_U)^\#$ together with Lemma 0GMW, $\mathcal F^\#\otimes\mathcal G^\#=(\mathcal F\otimes_p\mathcal G)^\#$; for $\mathcal A$-valued sheaves the strong monoidality of $a$ is the clause of [lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed] that replaces 0GMW.)

**Step 2.** Step 2 (the values of $Q$ are flat in $\mathcal A$). Fix an object $V$ of $\mathcal C$. $\mathrm{Mor}_{\mathcal C}(V,X)$ is a set ($\mathcal C$ is essentially small, in particular locally small), and $\mathcal A$, being Grothendieck abelian, has coproducts indexed by sets, so $Q(V)=\coprod_{\varphi\in\mathrm{Mor}_{\mathcal C}(V,X)}P$ exists. $\mathcal A$ is Grothendieck abelian with a symmetric, hence braided, monoidal closed structure, and each summand $P$ is flat ($P\otimes-$ preserves finite limits, by hypothesis). By [lem-weil-pbc-flat-coproduct] applied to $\mathcal B=\mathcal A$ and the constant family $P$ indexed by $\mathrm{Mor}_{\mathcal C}(V,X)$, $Q(V)$ is flat: $Q(V)\otimes-:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A$ preserves finite limits. (If $\mathrm{Mor}(V,X)=\emptyset$ then $Q(V)=0$ and $0\otimes-\cong0$, which preserves finite limits; this is the empty case of the same lemma.) Concretely, the argument of that lemma is the one asked for here: since $\otimes$ is closed, $-\otimes G$ is a left adjoint and $Q(V)\otimes G\cong\bigoplus_{\varphi}P\otimes G$ naturally in $G$; $P\otimes-$ preserves finite limits; and coproducts in $\mathcal A$ are exact (AB4, implied by AB5), so a coproduct of left exact functors is left exact. This replaces Stacks' remark that $\bigoplus_\varphi\mathcal O(V)$ is a flat $\mathcal O(V)$-module (a direct sum of flat modules is flat): in a general $\mathcal A$ it needs the closed structure and AB4, not only the definition of flatness.

**Step 3.** Step 3 (the presheaf tensor product with $Q$ is left exact). Consider $Q\otimes_p-:\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal C;\mathcal A)\to\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal C;\mathcal A)$, $G\mapsto Q\otimes_pG$, with $(Q\otimes_pG)(V)=Q(V)\otimes G(V)$ and $(Q\otimes_pg)_V=Q(V)\otimes g_V$ for a morphism $g$ of presheaves. For every object $V$, $\mathrm{ev}_V\circ(Q\otimes_p-)=(Q(V)\otimes-)\circ\mathrm{ev}_V$ (by the definition of $\otimes_p$). $\mathcal A$ is abelian, so it has finite limits, and $\mathrm{ev}_V$ preserves finite limits ([lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise] (1)); $Q(V)\otimes-$ preserves finite limits (Step 2); so their composite does. By [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise] (1) (finite limits of presheaves are objectwise), $Q\otimes_p-$ preserves finite limits. (This is Stacks 03ES, 'immediate from the definitions', for $\mathcal A$-valued presheaves.)

**Step 4.** Step 4 (conclusion). $\iota$ preserves finite limits ([lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-finite-limits-objectwise] (2)); $Q\otimes_p-$ preserves finite limits (Step 3); $a$ is exact, in particular preserves finite limits ([lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed] (1), which is stated for $\mathcal A$-valued sheaves on an essentially small site, so the abelian-group node lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact is not needed). A composite of functors preserving finite limits preserves finite limits, so $a\circ(Q\otimes_p-)\circ\iota$ does, and by Step 1 so does $P_X^\#\otimes-$. (This is the hint of Stacks 03ET, 'sheafification is exact', for the sheafification of the flat presheaf of Step 3.)
