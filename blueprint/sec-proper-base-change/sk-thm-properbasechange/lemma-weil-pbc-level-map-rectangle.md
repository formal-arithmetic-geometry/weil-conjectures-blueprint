For every $n$, the diagram

$$
\begin{CD}
A_n @>{h_n}>> B_n\\
@V{v_n}VV @VV{v'_n}V\\
C_n @>{h'_n}>> D_n
\end{CD}
$$

commutes; equivalently, its two composites satisfy

$$
v'_nh_n=h'_nv_n:A_n\longrightarrow D_n.
$$
