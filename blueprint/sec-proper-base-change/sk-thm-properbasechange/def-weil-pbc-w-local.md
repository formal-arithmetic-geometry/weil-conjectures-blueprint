For an affine scheme [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions]
$X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, its *patch topology* is generated by the subsets
$D(f)$ and $V(f)$, for $f\in A$. A subset is *constructible* here if it is a
finite Boolean combination of these subsets. We call $X$ *w-local* if its
closed points form a closed subset and every connected component has exactly
one closed point.
