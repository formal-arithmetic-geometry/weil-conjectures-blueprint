Let $\pi:\mathbf P^1_S\to S$ be the projection. To prove proper base change for
$\pi$ we must show that a geometric stalk of $R^q\pi_*F$ is the cohomology of
the geometric fibre, and that this group does not change under base
change. The stalk is the cohomology of $\mathbf P^1$ over a strictly henselian
local ring, so we need to know that the cohomology of
$\mathbf P^1_R$, for $R$ henselian local, is that of its closed fibre. In
degree zero this is Theorem [sk-thm-pbc-henselian-h0], and we extend it to
higher degrees by means of injective resolutions. Given this comparison,
it remains to show that the cohomology of a proper curve does not change
under extension of separably closed fields
(Lemma [sk-lem-pbc-curve-field-extension], which we deduce from
Theorem [sk-thm-pbc-curve-input]).

Besides the comparison in degree zero of Theorem [sk-thm-pbc-henselian-h0],
the argument uses two cohomological facts about henselian pairs.
