For a henselian local ring $A$ with residue field $k$ and any torsion abelian
sheaf $F$ on $\mathbf P^1_A$, restriction induces natural isomorphisms

$$
H^q(\mathbf P^1_A,F)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H^q(\mathbf P^1_k,F_0)
\qquad(q\geq0).
$$
