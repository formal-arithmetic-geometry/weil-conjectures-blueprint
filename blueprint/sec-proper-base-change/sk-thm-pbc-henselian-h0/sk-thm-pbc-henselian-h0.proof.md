The geometric content is a connectedness statement: every irreducible closed
subset of a scheme proper over a henselian pair meets the closed fibre in a
nonempty connected subset. Stein factorization and henselian connectedness
prove this (Stacks Project, Tag 0A0B), and spectral-space restriction then
gives equality of sections for Zariski sheaves. The connectedness statement
also holds after every finite cover, since that cover remains proper over
$A$. A finite surjection whose Zariski-local pieces factor through the étale
covering, followed by descent on its self-intersection, upgrades the Zariski
comparison to étale sheaves (Stacks Project, Tag 09ZF). Over a general affine
base the integral form of Stein factorization, whose algebra need not be
finite, suffices for this argument.
