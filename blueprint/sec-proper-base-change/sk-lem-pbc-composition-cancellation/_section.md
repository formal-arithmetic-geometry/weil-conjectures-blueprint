Let $X\xrightarrow{u}Y\xrightarrow{v}Z$ be proper morphisms.

1. If $\mathrm{PBC}(u)$ and $\mathrm{PBC}(v)$ hold, then $\mathrm{PBC}(vu)$
   holds.
2. If $u$ is surjective and $\mathrm{PBC}(u)$ and $\mathrm{PBC}(vu)$ hold,
   then $\mathrm{PBC}(v)$ holds.
