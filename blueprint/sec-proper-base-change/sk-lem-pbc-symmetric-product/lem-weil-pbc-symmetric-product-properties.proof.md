Let $\mathfrak a$ be the ideal generated by the dehomogenized $c_m$ on the chart
$D_+(a_i)\times D_+(b_j)$. If $i=0$, put $a_0=1$: then $c_{n+1}=b_n$ and
$c_m=b_{m-1}+a_1b_m$, so by descending induction every $b_k$ lies in
$\mathfrak a$, in particular $b_j=1$. If $i=1$, put $a_1=1$: then $c_0=b_0$ and
$c_m=a_0b_{m-1}+b_m$, so by ascending induction every $b_k$ lies in
$\mathfrak a$, in particular $b_j=1$. Thus $\mathfrak a$ is the unit ideal on
every chart, and the $c_m$ define a morphism to $\mathbf P^{n+1}$, compatibly
with base change.
