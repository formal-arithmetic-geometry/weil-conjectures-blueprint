For $n\geq1$ the formula

$$
((x_1:y_1),\ldots,(x_n:y_n))\longmapsto
[F_0:\cdots:F_n],\qquad
\prod_{i=1}^n(x_it+y_i)=\sum_{j=0}^nF_jt^j,
$$

defines a finite surjective $S_n$-invariant morphism
$\sigma_n:(\mathbf P^1_S)^n\to\mathbf P^n_S$.
