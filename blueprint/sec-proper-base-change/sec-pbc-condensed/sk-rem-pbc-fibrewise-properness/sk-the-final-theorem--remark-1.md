Only the six theorems of §§ *The six theorems* are statements; every other fact used
in the proofs is argued inline (finite morphisms, composition,
cancellation, the symmetric product, étale locality, the
degree-zero exchange). The curve-estimate theorem [sk-thm-p1] alone
sits on the critical path (Step 1 of the final theorem and the hypothesis of the
reduction), and its own decomposition is by far the largest one.
The target theorem is last.
