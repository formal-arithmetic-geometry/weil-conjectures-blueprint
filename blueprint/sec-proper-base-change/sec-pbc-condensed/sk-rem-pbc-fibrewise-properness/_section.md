Once Proposition [sk-thm-sfb-proper-base-change] is proved, the
formula of Corollary [sk-cor-pbc-torsion-stalk] for the stalks of $R^qp_*$
holds for constructible complexes with coefficients in $\mathcal O_E$,
$E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. The properness hypothesis cannot be dropped. For example, let
$j:\mathbf G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$ and $i:\{0\}\hookrightarrow \mathbf A^1$ be the inclusions over an algebraically closed field. Then
$i^*j_*A$ is nonzero for a nonzero finite constant ring $A$, since
its stalk at $0$ contains the constant sections $A$, whereas
the base change of $\mathbf G_m$ to $\{0\}$ is empty. Thus the
degree-zero base change map $i^*j_*A\to0$
is not an isomorphism.
