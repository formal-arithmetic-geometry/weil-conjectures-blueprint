For an identity square the square isomorphism is the identity modulo the unit
isomorphisms of inverse image. Its adjoint is the identity by the triangle
identity for an adjunction.

For pasting, expand each exchange map as unit, square isomorphism, and
counit. The square isomorphism for the outer rectangle is the composite of the
square isomorphisms for the inner squares. The adjacent unit and counit
cancel by the triangle identities; naturality moves the remaining units and
counits past the composition isomorphisms. The result is the
unit–square-isomorphism–counit formula for the outer rectangle. The same
calculation applies in the bounded below derived categories of the étale
sites. In the vertical case the identifications $R(ef)_*\simeq Re_*Rf_*$ and
$R(e'f')_*\simeq Re'_*Rf'_*$ are valid because direct image, being right
adjoint to exact inverse image, preserves injectives. Alternatively, one can
compute the outer exchange on an injective resolution as in the resolution
formula of [sk-def-exchange]; the underived pasting calculation gives the same
map after replacement.
