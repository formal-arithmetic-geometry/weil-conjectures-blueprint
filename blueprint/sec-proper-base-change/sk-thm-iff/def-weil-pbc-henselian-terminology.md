A pair $(A,I)$ consisting of a commutative ring and an ideal is *henselian*
if, for every finite $A$-algebra $B$, every idempotent of $B/IB$ lifts
uniquely to an idempotent of $B$. A local ring is *henselian* when its
maximal-ideal pair is henselian, and *strictly henselian* when it is
henselian and its residue field is separably closed. For
$T=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and an ideal $I$, write
$T_0=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$ for the closed subscheme.
