**Step 1.** *Stalks of $F[n ]$ and of $c_F$.* Fix a geometric point $\bar t$ of $T$. The stalk functor $G\mapsto G_{\bar t}$, $\Ab(T_{\Et})\to\Ab$, is exact [prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact] (Stacks 03PT (3)), hence additive, so it sends the endomorphism $n=n\cdot\mathrm{id}_F$ to $n\cdot\mathrm{id}_{F_{\bar t}}$ and the kernel $F[n ]=\ker(n:F\to F)$ [def-pur-abelian-sheaf-kernel] to the kernel: the inclusion $F[n ]\hookrightarrow F$ induces an isomorphism of $(F[n ])_{\bar t}$ onto the subgroup $F_{\bar t}[n ]=\{x\in F_{\bar t}:nx=0\}$. The stalk functor commutes with colimits [lem-kun-stalks-commute-colimits], so $(\varinjlim_nF[n ])_{\bar t}=\varinjlim_n(F[n ])_{\bar t}=\varinjlim_nF_{\bar t}[n ]$, the colimit taken in $\Ab$ over the positive integers ordered by divisibility with the inclusions as transition maps, and $(c_F)_{\bar t}$ is the map $\varinjlim_nF_{\bar t}[n ]\to F_{\bar t}$ induced by the inclusions (both are induced by the inclusions $F[n ]\hookrightarrow F$, by functoriality of the stalk and the universal property of the colimit [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit]). A filtered colimit in $\Ab$ of subgroups of a fixed group along inclusions is their union: $(c_F)_{\bar t}$ is injective with image $\bigcup_nF_{\bar t}[n ]$, the torsion subgroup of $F_{\bar t}$ (an element of a filtered colimit of abelian groups comes from some term and is zero iff it is zero in some later term; here all transition maps and all maps to $F_{\bar t}$ are injective). Hence $(c_F)_{\bar t}$ is an isomorphism if and only if $F_{\bar t}$ is a torsion group.

**Step 2.** *(1)$\Leftrightarrow$(3).* By step 1, $(c_F)_{\bar t}$ is an isomorphism exactly when $F_{\bar t}$ is torsion. If $c_F$ is an isomorphism, so is every $(c_F)_{\bar t}$ (a functor preserves isomorphisms), hence every $F_{\bar t}$ is torsion. Conversely, if every $F_{\bar t}$ is torsion, then $c_F$ is a morphism of $\Ab(T_{\Et})$ that is an isomorphism on every geometric stalk, hence an isomorphism [lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative] (Stacks Theorem 03PU).

**Step 3.** *(2)$\Rightarrow$(3).* Let $\bar t:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to T$ be a geometric point and $\gamma\in F_{\bar t}$. The stalk is the colimit of the $F(U)$ over the opposite of the category of pointed etale neighbourhoods $(U,\bar u)$ of $\bar t$ [def-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods], which is cofiltered [lem-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods-cofiltered]; so $\gamma$ is the class of some $s\in F(U)$ for a pointed etale neighbourhood $(U,\bar u)$ (Stacks, remark after Definition 040R: elements of the stalk are triples $(U,\bar u,s)$). By (2) there are an etale covering $\{\varphi_i:U_i\to U\}$ and $n_i\ge1$ with $n_is|_{U_i}=0$. A covering of $T_{\Et}$ is a jointly surjective family of etale morphisms [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], so by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-cover-geometric-point-lift] (Stacks Lemma 03PR) there are $i$ and $\bar u_i:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to U_i$ with $\varphi_i\bar u_i=\bar u$. As $U_i\to U\to T$ is etale [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (2), $(U_i,\bar u_i)$ is a pointed etale neighbourhood of $\bar t$ and $\varphi_i$ a morphism $(U_i,\bar u_i)\to(U,\bar u)$, so $\gamma$ is also the class of $s|_{U_i}$. The maps $F(V)\to F_{\bar t}$ are homomorphisms, so $n_i\gamma$ is the class of $n_is|_{U_i}=0$, i.e. $n_i\gamma=0$. Thus $F_{\bar t}$ is torsion.

**Step 4.** *(3)$\Rightarrow$(2).* Let $U\to T$ be etale and $s\in F(U)$. For each point $u\in U$ choose an algebraic closure $\Omega_u$ of the residue field $\kappa(u)$ [def-weil-algebraic-closure]; this gives a geometric point $\bar u:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega_u\to U$ lying over $u$ [def-weil-geometric-point], and $\bar t_u=(U\to T)\circ\bar u$ is a geometric point of $T$ with pointed etale neighbourhood $(U,\bar u)$ [def-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods]. By (3) the class $\gamma_u$ of $s$ in $F_{\bar t_u}$ is killed by some $n_u\ge1$; $n_u\gamma_u$ is the class of $n_us$, and an element $(U,\bar u,\sigma)$ of the colimit over the cofiltered category of pointed neighbourhoods [lem-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods-cofiltered] is zero iff $\sigma$ restricts to zero on some neighbourhood mapping to $(U,\bar u)$ (Stacks, remark after Definition 040R). So there is a morphism of pointed etale neighbourhoods $h_u:(V_u,\bar v_u)\to(U,\bar u)$ with $n_us|_{V_u}=0$. The $T$-morphism $h_u$ between etale $T$-schemes is etale [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation] (3), and $u$ lies in its image since $h_u\bar v_u=\bar u$ lies over $u$. Hence $\{h_u:V_u\to U\}_{u\in U}$ is a jointly surjective family of etale morphisms, a covering of $U$ in $T_{\Et}$ [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], with $n_us|_{V_u}=0$: this is (2). (The same argument as Stacks' proof of Theorem 03PU, surjectivity part.)

**Step 5.** *$\underline{\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z}$ is torsion.* Let $F=\underline{\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z}$ [def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf]. For every geometric point $\bar t$ of $T$, $F_{\bar t}\cong\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z$ [lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-geometric-stalk], a torsion group ($a/b+\mathbf Z$ is killed by $b$). By (3)$\Rightarrow$(1) (steps 1-2), $F$ is torsion.

**Step 6.** *Sections over $U=\coprod_{n\ge1}T$.* By [lem-weil-pbc-scheme-coproduct-copies], $U$ with the codiagonal is an object of $T_{\Et}$, $\{\iota_n:T\to U\}_{n\ge1}$ is a covering in $T_{\Et}$, $T\times_{\iota_n,U,\iota_m}T=\emptyset$ for $n\ne m$ and $T\times_{\iota_n,U,\iota_n}T=T$ (both projections the identity). For any $G\in\Ab(T_{\Et})$ the sheaf condition [def-weil-pbc-abelian-etale-sheaves] for the empty covering of the empty scheme gives $G(\emptyset)=0$ (an equalizer of maps into the empty product); so in the sheaf condition for $\{\iota_n\}$ the two maps $\prod_nG(T)\rightrightarrows\prod_{n,m}G(T\times_UT)$ agree (on the factor $(n,n)$ both are the identity, the other factors are $0$), and $G(U)\to\prod_nG(T)$, $x\mapsto(\iota_n^*x)_n$, is an isomorphism of groups.

**Step 7.** *An element of infinite order.* Assume $T\ne\emptyset$, let $F=\underline{\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z}$ and let $x\in F(U)$ correspond under step 6 to $(\iota_T(1/2^n))_{n\ge1}$. Choose a point $t\in T$ and an algebraic closure $\Omega$ of $\kappa(t)$ [def-weil-algebraic-closure], giving a geometric point $\bar t$ [def-weil-geometric-point]. The composite $\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z\xrightarrow{\iota_T}F(T)\to F_{\bar t}$ is an isomorphism [lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-geometric-stalk], so $\iota_T$ is an injective homomorphism. If $kx=0$ for some $k\ge1$, then by step 6 $k\,\iota_T(1/2^n)=\iota_T(k/2^n)=0$ for all $n$, hence $k/2^n\in\mathbf Z$ for all $n$, false for $2^n>k$. So $x$ has infinite order and $F(U)$ is not a torsion group, although $F$ is a torsion sheaf (step 5).
