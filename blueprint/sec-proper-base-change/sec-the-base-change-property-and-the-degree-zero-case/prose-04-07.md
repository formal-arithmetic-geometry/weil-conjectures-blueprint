This is [Stacks, Tag 0A0C](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A0C). Geometrically it is a statement about
connectedness: every irreducible closed subset of a scheme proper
over a henselian pair meets the closed fibre in a nonempty connected subset.
This follows from Stein factorization, which over a general affine base
only produces an algebra integral over $R$ (this suffices), and the
connectedness properties of henselian pairs [Stacks, Tag 0A0B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A0B). From it one deduces, by a topological
argument on spectral spaces, that restriction is bijective for Zariski
sheaves. The connectedness
statement also holds after every finite cover, since such a cover remains
proper over $R$. To pass from Zariski sheaves to étale sheaves one uses a
finite surjection whose Zariski-local pieces factor through the
étale covering, followed by descent along its self-intersection
[Stacks, Tag 09ZF](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09ZF).
