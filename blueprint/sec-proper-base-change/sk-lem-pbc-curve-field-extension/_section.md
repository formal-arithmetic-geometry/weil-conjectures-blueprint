Let $k\subseteq k'$ be separably closed fields, let $C$ be proper over $k$
with $\dim C\leq1$, and let $F$ be a classical torsion abelian sheaf on
$C_{\mathrm{proét}}$. Then the natural map

$$
H^q(C,F)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H^q(C_{k'},F|)\qquad(q\geq0)
$$

is an isomorphism.
