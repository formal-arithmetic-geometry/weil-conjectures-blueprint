For an injective sheaf $I$ of $\mathbf F_r$-modules on $X=\mathbf P^1_A$ in
the setup of [def-weil-pbc-strict-henselian-setup],

$$
 H^q(X_0,I_0)=0\qquad(q>0).
$$
