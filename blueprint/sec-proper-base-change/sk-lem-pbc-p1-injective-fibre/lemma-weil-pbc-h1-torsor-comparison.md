Let $(A,I)$ be a henselian pair, let $Y\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ be proper,
and let $Y_0=Y\times_AA/I$. For every finite abelian group $M$, restriction
induces a bijection

$$
 H^1(Y,\underline M)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^1(Y_0,\underline M).
$$
