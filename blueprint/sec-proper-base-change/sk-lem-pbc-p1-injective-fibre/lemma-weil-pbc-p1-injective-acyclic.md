For an injective sheaf $I$ of $\mathbf F_\ell$-modules on
$X=\mathbf P^1_A$, the restriction
$I_0$ is $\Gamma(X_0,-)$-acyclic: $H^q(X_0,I_0)=0$ for all $q>0$.
