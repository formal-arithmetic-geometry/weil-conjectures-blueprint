Let $A$ be a henselian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ and
residue field $k$. Set

$$
X=\mathbf P^1_A,\qquad X_0=\mathbf P^1_k,\qquad i:X_0\hookrightarrow X,
$$

and for a sheaf $G$ on $X_{\Et}$ write $G_0=i^{-1}G$; the prime $r$ need not
be invertible on $A$.

If $I$ is an injective sheaf of $\mathbf F_r$-modules on $X_{\Et}$, then
$H^q(X_0,I_0)=0$ for every $q>0$.
