This is [sk-thm-curve-package], proved in the appendix on curves. For a
smooth proper connected curve and $n$ invertible in $k$, the Kummer sequence
and the Jacobian (appendix on the Picard scheme) compute $H^q(C,\mu_n)$
([sk-thm-curveestimate]); for $p$-primary coefficients the Artin–Schreier
sequence is used instead; and general torsion sheaves reduce to these cases by
normalization, removal of finitely many points and a finite étale cover of
degree prime to the torsion order.
