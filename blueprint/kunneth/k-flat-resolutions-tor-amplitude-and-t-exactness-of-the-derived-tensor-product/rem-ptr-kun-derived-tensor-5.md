In the notation of [def-kun-derived-tensor], the following holds. The functoriality of the
construction in both variables, together with its compatibility with
shifts and distinguished triangles in each variable, is
[lem-kun-derived-tensor-triangulated]; its associativity is
[lem-kun-derived-tensor-associative]; and the symmetry and unit
constraints are those of [lem-kun-tensor-sym] and
[lem-kun-tensor-unit].
