Consider a
cartesian square of schemes

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
X&\xleftarrow{\;h\;}&Y\\
\Big\downarrow{\scriptstyle f}&&\Big\downarrow{\scriptstyle e}\\
S&\xleftarrow{\;g\;}&T
\end{array}
$$

where $f$ is flat and locally of finite presentation with
geometrically reduced fibres. Then the canonical base-change
morphism $f^{-1}g_*F\to h_*e^{-1}F$ is an isomorphism for every
abelian sheaf $F$ on $T_{\Et}$, and the statement of the Stacks tag,
which is for sheaves of sets, is used here only in this case.
This is the Stacks tag
[0EYS](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EYS) (Etale
Cohomology, Lemma 59.87.2). In the Stacks Project the proof combines Lemma 59.87.1,
[Tag `0EZX`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EZX)
(base change for a flat morphism which etale-locally factors through a
morphism with geometrically connected fibres, by the description of
sections over such a cover, Lemma 59.39.3,
[Tag `0A3H`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A3H)) with
More on Morphisms, Lemma 37.46.3,
[Tag `0EY5`](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EY5) (the
local factorization for flat finitely presented morphisms with
geometrically reduced fibres).

## Proof

This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Etale Cohomology, Lemma 59.87.2 ([Tag 0EYS](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EYS)): base change holds for a flat morphism which is locally of finite presentation with geometrically reduced fibres, in the sheaf-of-sets statement of that tag, used here only in the abelian case. In the Stacks Project the proof combines Lemma 59.87.1 ([Tag 0EZX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EZX)) with More on Morphisms, Lemma 37.46.3 ([Tag 0EY5](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EY5)). No separate proof is given.
