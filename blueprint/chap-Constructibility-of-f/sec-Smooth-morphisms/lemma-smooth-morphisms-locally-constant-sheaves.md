Let $S$ be integral and let $f:X\to S$ be separated, smooth and of finite type. For a finite locally constant
$\Fell$-sheaf $L$ on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$, there is a nonempty open
$S_0\subset S$ with the following property. For the restriction
$f_{S_0}:X_{S_0}\to S_0$, the complex $Rf_{S_0,*}L$ is bounded with
finite locally constant cohomology sheaves, and its formation commutes
with base change. Namely, for every $k$-scheme $v:T\to S_0$ of finite type, with
base change $f_T:X_T\to T$ and $v_X:X_T\to X_{S_0}$, the canonical
base change map $v^*Rf_{S_0,*}L\to Rf_{T,*}v_X^*L$ is an isomorphism.
