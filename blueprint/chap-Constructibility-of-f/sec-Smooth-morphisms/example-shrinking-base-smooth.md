Let $k$ be algebraically closed, $S=\mathbf A^1$ with coordinate $t$, and
let $X\subset\mathbf A^1_S$ be the complement of the two sections $x=0$
and $x=t$, with $f:X\to S$ the projection and $L=\Fell$. The morphism $f$
is smooth. For $t\neq0$ the fibre is $\mathbf A^1$ minus two points, that
is, a projective line minus three points. Its $H^1$ is
$\Fell(-1)^{\oplus2}$. The fibre over $t=0$ is $\mathbf G_m$, with
$H^1=\Fell(-1)$. On $S_0=\mathbf G_m$ the lemma applies, but no open
containing the origin works. Suppose $R^1f_*L$ were locally constant near
$0$ and commuted with base change to the points of $S$. Then its stalks at
$0$ and at nearby $t$ would be the $H^1$ of the fibres, which have
different ranks. The two missing
points collide at $t=0$, and $Rf_*$ cannot see this from the generic
fibre.
