For $K\in D(X_{\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ put $K^\vee=\cHom_X(K,\Lambda_X)$. If
$K$ is dualizable, with dual $L$ (an exact pairing
$L\otimes^{\mathbf L}K\to\Lambda_X$), then $K^\vee\simeq L$.
