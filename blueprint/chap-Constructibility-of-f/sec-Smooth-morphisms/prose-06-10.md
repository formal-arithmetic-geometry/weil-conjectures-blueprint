Part (2) generalizes $j^!=j^*$ from open immersions to étale maps.
For $d=1$, $k$ algebraically closed and $S=\operatorname{Spec}k$, part (1) says that
$\Hom(R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,F),M)=\Hom(F,M_{\mathbf A^1}(1)[2])$. For
$F=A$ both sides are $\Hom(A,M(1)[2])$, consistent with the example above: the left
side because $R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1_{\bar k},A)\simeq A(-1)[-2]$, and the right side because
$R\Gamma(\mathbf A^1_{\bar k},A)=A$.
