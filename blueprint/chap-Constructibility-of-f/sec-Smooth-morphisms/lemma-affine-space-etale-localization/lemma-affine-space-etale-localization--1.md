For the projection $p_d:\mathbf A^d_S\to S$, the right adjoint
$p_d^!$ of $Rp_{d!}:D_{\mathrm{class}}((\mathbf A^d_S)_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)
\to D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is $p_d^*(d)[2d]$.
