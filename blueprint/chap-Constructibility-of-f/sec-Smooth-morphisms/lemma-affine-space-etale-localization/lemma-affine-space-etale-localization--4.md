Let $f:X\to S$ be separated of finite type, let
$N\in D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, and let $u:U\to X$ be separated, étale
and of finite type. There is a canonical isomorphism

$$
u^*f^!N\simeq(fu)^!N
\tag{6.12}
$$
