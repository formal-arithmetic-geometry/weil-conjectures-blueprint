For the cartesian square in
[proposition-exceptional-inverse-image-smooth-morphism--6], the component
$\beta_{A_S}$, followed by $g^*A_S\simeq A_{S'}$, identifies
$g'^*\omega_f$ with $\omega_{f'}$.

In particular, assume that $S$ is quasi-separated and $f$ is quasi-compact.
For $W=X\times_SX$, with projections $p_1,p_2:W\to X$, the mainline uses
$$
\omega_{p_1}\simeq p_2^*\omega_f.
$$
