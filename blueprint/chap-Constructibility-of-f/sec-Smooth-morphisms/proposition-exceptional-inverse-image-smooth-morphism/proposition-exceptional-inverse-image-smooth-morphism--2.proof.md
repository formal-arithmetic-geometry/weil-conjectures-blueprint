The identification in clause [proposition-exceptional-inverse-image-smooth-morphism--1] is obtained by composing the adjunctions for $v$, $p_d$ and $e$. The counit of a composite adjunction is the composite of their counits. For $e$ it is the sum of the identity maps over the étale fibres, and for $p_d$ it is the composite of the affine-line counits from Lemma [lemma-affine-space-etale-localization]. This is exactly the asserted chart formula.
