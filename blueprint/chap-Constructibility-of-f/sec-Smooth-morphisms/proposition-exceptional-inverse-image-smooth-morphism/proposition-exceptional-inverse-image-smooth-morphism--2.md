The explicit sum-over-fibres description is bypassed by the Lean route here,
which uses the compatibility of composite adjunctions with their counits.

For the Tate-twist chart identification in part (1) of the human statement,
the counit is the sum over the fibres of $e$ followed by the counit of
[lemma-affine-space-etale-localization](1). This description is retained for
comparison with that statement; it is not asserted by a separate declaration
for smooth charts in the mainline.
