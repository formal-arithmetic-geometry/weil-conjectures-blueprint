Let $g:S'\to S$, let $X'=X\times_SS'$, and let $f':X'\to S'$ and $g':X'\to X$ be the projections. The canonical base change map

$$
g'^*_\Lambda f^!_\Lambda N\longrightarrow f'^!_\Lambda g^*_\Lambda N
\tag{6.12}
$$

is an isomorphism.
