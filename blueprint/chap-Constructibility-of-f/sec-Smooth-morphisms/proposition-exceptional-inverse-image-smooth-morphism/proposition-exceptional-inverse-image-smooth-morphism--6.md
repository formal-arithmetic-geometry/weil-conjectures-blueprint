Assume that $S$ is quasi-separated. Let $g:S'\to S$ be any morphism, and let
$X'=X\times_SS'$ with projections $f':X'\to S'$ and $g':X'\to X$.
Assume that $f'$ is quasi-compact and admits a compactification. The canonical
base change map is an isomorphism of functors on $D(S_{\acute{e}t},A)$;
in particular, for every $N$ its component
$$
\beta_N:g'^*f^!N\longrightarrow f'^!g^*N
\tag{6.12}
$$
is an isomorphism.
