There is a locally constant sheaf $L$ of $A$-modules on $X_{\acute{e}t}$,
whose stalk at every geometric point is isomorphic to $A$, and an isomorphism
$$
\omega_f\simeq L[2d].
$$
Thus $\omega_f[-2d]$ is a locally constant sheaf of free $A$-modules of rank
one. Moreover, $\omega_f$ is invertible for $\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}$.
