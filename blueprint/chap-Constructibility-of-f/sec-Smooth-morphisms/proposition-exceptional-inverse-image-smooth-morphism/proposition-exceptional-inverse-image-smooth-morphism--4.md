There is a locally constant sheaf $L$ of $\Lambda$-modules,
whose stalk at every geometric point is isomorphic to $\Lambda$, and an isomorphism

$$
\omega_f\simeq L[2d].
$$

Thus $\omega_f[-2d]$ is a locally constant sheaf of free $\Lambda$-modules of rank
one. Moreover, $\omega_f$ is invertible for $\otimes_{\Lambda_X}^{\mathbf L}$.
