Clause [proposition-exceptional-inverse-image-smooth-morphism--3] identifies the dualizing complex on an open cover with a rank-one locally free sheaf shifted by $2d$. It consequently has a single nonzero cohomology sheaf in degree $-2d$, locally free of rank one, and these local properties hold globally.
