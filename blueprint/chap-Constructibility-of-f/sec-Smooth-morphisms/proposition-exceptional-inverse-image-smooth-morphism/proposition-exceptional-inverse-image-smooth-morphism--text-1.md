*Shared context.* Let $f:X\to S$ be separated, smooth and of finite type, of pure relative dimension $d$, and let $\omega_f=f^!_\Lambda\Lambda_S\in D_{\mathrm{class}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$. Let $N\in D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$. In the local clauses, $U\subset X$ is open with $f|_U=p_d\circ e$, where $e:U\to\mathbf A^d_S$ is separated and étale. The original items (1)–(3) are retained.
