order = 537005

For a morphism $f:X\to S$ of finite-type $k$-schemes, $Rf_*$ has finite cohomological amplitude on classical torsion sheaves, by the étale bound [Stacks, [0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10)] and comparison.
