For a morphism $f:X\to S$ of finite-type $k$-schemes, $Rf_*$ has finite cohomological amplitude on classical torsion sheaves, by the étale bound of Theorem [theorem-proper-base-change-classical-pro], clause [theorem-proper-base-change-classical-pro--3], and comparison.
