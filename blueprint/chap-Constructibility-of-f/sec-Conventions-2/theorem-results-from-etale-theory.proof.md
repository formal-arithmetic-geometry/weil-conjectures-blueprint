For bounded below complexes, (1) is
Theorem [theorem-proper-base-change-classical-pro] together with the
comparison of direct images (Theorem [theorem-comparison-direct-images]);
for unbounded complexes it is [Stacks, [0F0C](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0C)], whose finiteness hypothesis
on $f$ is part (3). Part (2) is [Stacks, [0GL0](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0GL0)]. As for part (3), for $Rf_*$ it is [Stacks, [0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10)], and for $Rf_!$ we write $f=\bar f\circ j$ with $\bar f$ proper
and $j$ an open immersion; then $Rf_!=R\bar f_*j_!$ with $j_!$ exact, and
[Stacks, [0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10)] applies to $\bar f$. Finally, the last assertion follows by the comparison of direct images, since classical sheaves are of the form
$\nu_X^*F$.
