Part (1) is Lemma [lemma-hypotheses-brown-representability]. Its
compactness statement was proved separately for each kind of coefficient
ring: for finite $A$ in
Proposition [proposition-compactness-finite-coefficients], for $E$ and
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ in Theorem [theorem-compactness-rational-coefficients], and
for $\mathcal O_E$ in
Proposition [proposition-compactness-proper-direct-image].

Part (2) is Proposition [proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek].
It rests on the fact that proper direct image preserves direct sums, which
was proved for finite $A$ in
Corollary [corollary-proper-direct-image-finite-coefficients-localizing],
for $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ in
Lemma [lemma-proper-direct-image-unbounded-complexes], and for
$\mathcal O_E$ in
Proposition [proposition-compactness-proper-direct-image]
(see Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-extension-zero]).

Part (3) is Theorem [theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image].

Part (4) is Theorem [theorem-f-shriek-finite-coefficients] for
finite $A$ and Theorem [theorem-f-shriek-ell-adic-coefficients] for $\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. The second theorem includes $\mathbf Z_\ell$ and
$\mathbf Q_\ell$ as the case $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$; recall that $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}=\Dbc$ in the integral and
rational cases. The categories $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ and $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$ are
full subcategories of the localizing categories, and $Rf_!$ and $f^!$
preserve them. Hence the Hom bijections of the adjunction restrict to them.
