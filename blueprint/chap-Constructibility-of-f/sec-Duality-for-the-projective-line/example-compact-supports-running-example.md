We use the open immersion $j_\infty:\mathbf A^1\to\mathbf P^1$ of
Example [example-running-example], base changed to $\bar k$, and we let
$i_\infty:\{\infty\}\to\mathbf P^1_{\bar k}$ be the inclusion of
the complementary point.

By definition, $R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1,\Lambda)=R\Gamma(\mathbf P^1,j_{\infty!}\Lambda)$. The
localization triangle
$j_{\infty!}\Lambda\to\Lambda_{\mathbf P^1}\to i_{\infty*}\Lambda$ and (6.2)
show that

$$
R\Gamma_c(\mathbf A^1_{\bar k},\Lambda)
\simeq\fib\bigl(\Lambda\oplus\Lambda(-1)[-2]\to\Lambda\bigr)
\simeq\Lambda(-1)[-2],
$$

since restriction to $\infty$ is an isomorphism on $H^0$. In other words,
$H^2_c(\mathbf A^1)=\Lambda(-1)$ is the only nonzero compactly supported
cohomology group. The trace on it is the same $\operatorname{Tr}_p$.
