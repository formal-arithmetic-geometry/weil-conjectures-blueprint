**Exercise.** Using the localization sequence of
Example [example-running-example], show that
$H^1_c(\mathbf G_{m,\bar k},\Lambda)\simeq\Lambda$ and
$H^2_c(\mathbf G_{m,\bar k},\Lambda)\simeq\Lambda(-1)$, and that these
are the only nonzero groups.

The adjunction is built from the trace
$\operatorname{Tr}_p$ of (6.3) and the cycle class of the
diagonal provided by Lemma [lemma-cycle-class-cartier-divisor]. As
explained in Section [sec-Conventions-2], we work on the étale sites
of $S$, $\mathbf P^1_S$ and $\mathbf P^1_S\times_S\mathbf P^1_S$, and
transport the results to the pro-étale site at the end. Let
$p:\mathbf P^1_S\to S$ be the projection, let $p_1,p_2:\mathbf P^1_S\times_S\mathbf P^1_S\to\mathbf P^1_S$ be the
two projections and let
$\Delta:\mathbf P^1_S\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^1_S\times_S\mathbf P^1_S$
be the diagonal. For $i=1,2$, the projection $p_i$ is a projective line over
the other factor, with trace $\operatorname{Tr}_{p_i}$ given by
(6.3). Both triangle identities rest on the following
computation.
