Change of finite coefficients for the counit is bypassed by the Lean route
for this adjunction: its counit square concerns a morphism of schemes
with $\Lambda$ fixed.

The retained assertion is that the counit of the projective-line adjunction
is compatible with change of finite coefficients. The base-change equation
in [proposition-adjunction-projective-line--3] does not compare two
coefficient rings, and no declaration of that comparison was found.
