The counit is formed from the trace and the projection formula. The trace is the projection onto the distinguished summand of the projective-line decomposition, and finite coefficient change carries that decomposition and projection to the corresponding ones. The projection-formula map is defined by tensor and adjunction, which commute with this coefficient comparison. Their composite is therefore the new counit.
