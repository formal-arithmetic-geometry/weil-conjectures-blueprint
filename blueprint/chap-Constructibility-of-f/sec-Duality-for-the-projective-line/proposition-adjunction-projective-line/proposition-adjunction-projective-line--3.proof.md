The counit is the projection-formula isomorphism followed by the trace (6.5). The trace commutes with base change by Lemma [lemma-cohomology-projective-line-trace--4], and the projection formula commutes with proper base change by the adjoint-transpose calculation in the proof of clause [proposition-adjunction-projective-line--1]. Thus their composite is the base-changed counit.
