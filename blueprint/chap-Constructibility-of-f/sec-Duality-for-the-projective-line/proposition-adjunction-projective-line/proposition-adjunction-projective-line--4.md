Change of finite coefficients for the unit is bypassed by the Lean route
for this adjunction: the construction and its base-change theorem keep
$\Lambda$ fixed.

The retained assertion is that the unit of the projective-line adjunction
is compatible with change of finite coefficients. No corresponding
comparison of these units for two coefficient rings was found in the
mainline declarations.
