*Shared context.* Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring, equipped with
Tate twists and a normalized Kummer class. The normalization says that the
Kummer boundary of the coordinate of $\mathbf A^1_B$ gives the fundamental
class of the zero section, for $B$ separated of finite type over a field
and with the exponent of $\Lambda$ invertible. Write
$p:\mathbf P^1_S\to S$ for the projection. The Lean statements below use
the full étale derived categories, with $S$ any scheme on which the
exponent of $\Lambda$ is invertible.
