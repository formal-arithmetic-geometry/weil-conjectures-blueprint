Let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring and let $S$ be a scheme on
which some positive integer annihilating $\Lambda$ is invertible. Work
on the étale sites with Tate twists and Kummer classes satisfying the
rank-one normalization: the supported Kummer boundary of the coordinate
of $\mathbf A^1_B$ induces the purity isomorphism for the zero section
whenever $B$ is separated and locally of finite type over a field and
the exponent of $\Lambda$ is invertible on $B$.

Put $P=\mathbf P^1_S$ and $W=\mathbf P^1_P\simeq P\times_S P$, with
projections $p_1,p_2$, diagonal $\Delta:P\hookrightarrow W$ and exchange
of factors $\sigma:W\to W$. Assume that $\Delta$ is a regular immersion
of codimension one and that the complex with supports
$R\Delta_!\Delta^!(\Lambda_W(1)[2])$ is isomorphic to a sheaf placed in
degree zero. Let

$$
\gamma:\Lambda_W\longrightarrow\Lambda_W(1)[2]
$$

be the diagonal's Kummer boundary class with supports forgotten. Here
we use the boundary normalization of the Cartier class: in the usual
Kummer model, $\gamma=c_1(\mathcal O_W(-\Delta))$, the negative of the
class obtained from [lemma-cycle-class-cartier-divisor]. Normalize the
trace by the same convention: if $h$ is the boundary class of the
section at infinity, $\operatorname{Tr}_{p_1}$ is the projection onto
the summand generated by $h$ in the projective-line decomposition of
[lemma-cohomology-projective-line-trace]. In the usual Kummer model,
$h=c_1(\mathcal O(-1))$.

For $\alpha:\Lambda_W\to\Lambda_W(1)[2]$, write $\alpha^\flat$ for its
adjoint $\Lambda_P\to Rp_{1*}(\Lambda_W(1)[2])$, using
$Lp_1^*\Lambda_P\simeq\Lambda_W$. Then

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{p_1}\circ\gamma^\flat=1_{\Lambda_P},
\qquad
\operatorname{Tr}_{p_1}\circ(\sigma^*\gamma)^\flat=1_{\Lambda_P}.
$$

The second equality expresses the integral along $p_2$ after transport
along $\sigma$; the pullback of $\gamma$ is read through the canonical
identifications of constant complexes and Tate twists.
