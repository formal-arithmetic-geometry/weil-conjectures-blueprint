Let $A$ be a finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring and $f$ a separated
morphism of finite type. We show that the right adjoint $f^!$ of
Theorem [theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image] preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,A)$
(Theorem [theorem-f-shriek-finite-coefficients]). By the reduction at the start
of this chapter and by the previous section, we only have to show
that direct image along an open immersion $j$ preserves finite Tor
amplitude. The upper end of the Tor amplitude grows by at most the
$\ell$-cohomological dimension $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(j)$ of
Definition [definition-cohomological-dimension]. This dimension is
finite for morphisms of finite type over $k$.

Finite Tor amplitude cannot be checked on cohomology sheaves, since these
may have infinite Tor dimension over $A$: for $A=\mathbf Z/\ell^2$, already the
constant sheaf $\mathbf F_\ell$ has infinite Tor dimension. We therefore obtain the Tor
bounds below from flat models. By [Stacks, [0F4M](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F4M)], every object of
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)$ is represented by a bounded complex of constructible flat
$A$-sheaves. On the strata of a finite stratification, such a model is
locally a bounded complex of finite projective $A$-modules.
