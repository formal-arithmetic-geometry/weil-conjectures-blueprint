Clause [theorem-f-shriek-finite-coefficients--1] proves that $f^!$ lands in the finite-coefficient constructible finite-Tor-amplitude subcategory. The functor $Rf_!$ lands there by Proposition [proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek--3]. Both subcategories are full, so the Hom bijections, unit and counit of the ambient adjunction restrict unchanged. They give precisely the asserted adjunction.
