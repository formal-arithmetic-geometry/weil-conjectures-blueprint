For $j:\mathbf G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^1$ over $\bar k$ and $K=A$, the
stalk of $Rj_*A$ at the origin is the cohomology of the punctured
strict henselization. It has cohomology $A$ in degree $0$ and $A(-1)$ in
degree $1$, both free. It is therefore isomorphic to $A\oplus A(-1)[-1]$.
The Tor amplitude grows from $[0,0]$ to $[0,1]$, and the bound of the
proposition, with $d=\operatorname{cd}_\ell(j)=1$, is attained.

Combining the proposition with the computation of $p^!$ for affine space,
we obtain the main theorem for finite coefficients.
