Suppose given a dense open $V_{\alpha r}\subset\mathbf A^1_S$ for
each $\alpha,r$, and let

$$
G=\bigcup_{\alpha,r}
t_{\alpha r}^{-1}(V_{\alpha r})\subset Y.
$$

After shrinking $S$, the reduced closed complement $Z=Y\setminus G$
is finite over $S$.
