More generally, let $S$ be an integral scheme with generic point $\eta$,
let $f:X\to S$ be locally of finite type, and let
$t:X\to\mathbf A^1_S$ be an $S$-morphism. If $\eta'$ is the generic
point of $\mathbf A^1_S$, then

$$
\dim t^{-1}(\eta')+1\leq\dim X_{k(S)}.
$$

Here the fibres are scheme-theoretic and the dimensions are topological
Krull dimensions, with values in $\{\bot\}\cup\mathbf N\cup\{\infty\}$;
the empty scheme has dimension $\bot$ and $\bot+1=\bot$.
