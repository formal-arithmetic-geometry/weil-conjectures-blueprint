More generally, let $S$ be an integral scheme with generic point $\eta$,
let $f:X\to S$ be locally of finite type, and let
$t:X\to\mathbf A^1_S$ be an $S$-morphism. If $\eta'$ is the generic
point of $\mathbf A^1_S$, then

$$
\dim t^{-1}(\eta')+1\leq\dim X_{k(S)}.
$$

Here the fibres are scheme-theoretic and the dimensions are Krull
dimensions of the underlying spaces, with $\dim\emptyset=-\infty$.

In particular, for $S$, $Y$ and the $t_{\alpha r}$ of the lemma, if $X\subset Y$ is open and
$\dim X_{k(S)}\leq n$, then for every $\alpha,r$ the generic fibre of
$t_{\alpha r}:X\cap Y_\alpha\to\mathbf A^1_S$ has dimension at most $n-1$.
