If $X\subset Y$ is open and $\dim X_{k(S)}\leq n$, then for
every $\alpha,r$ the generic fibre of
$t_{\alpha r}:X\cap Y_\alpha\to\mathbf A^1_S$ has dimension at most
$n-1$.
