For the two details below, let $A$ be a commutative ring, let $b:Y\to S$
be a morphism of schemes, and let $j:U\hookrightarrow Y$ and
$i:Z\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion and a closed immersion whose
images are complementary. Suppose that $bi:Z\to S$ is finite. We work on
the étale sites; $Z$ may have any closed subscheme structure with this
underlying closed subset. The schemes and the coefficient ring are
arbitrary unless further hypotheses are stated in the detail.
