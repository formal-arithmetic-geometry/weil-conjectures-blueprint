If $\varphi:K\to K'$ is a morphism in $D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Fell)$ such
that $j^*\varphi$ and
$Rb_*\varphi$ are isomorphisms, then $\varphi$ is an
isomorphism.
