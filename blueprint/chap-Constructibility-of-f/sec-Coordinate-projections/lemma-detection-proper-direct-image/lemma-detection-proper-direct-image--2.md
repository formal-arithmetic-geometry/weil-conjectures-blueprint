If $\varphi:K\to K'$ is a morphism in $D(Y_{\acute{e}t},A)$ such that
$j^*\varphi$ and $Rb_*\varphi$ are isomorphisms, then $\varphi$ is an
isomorphism. This holds for arbitrary schemes and any commutative ring
$A$, with $b$ any morphism for which $bi$ is finite.
