Suppose that $S$ is of finite type over a field $k$, that $b$ is proper,
and that $A$ is finite and is annihilated by a positive integer invertible
on $S$. If $K\in D^+(Y_{\acute{e}t},A)$, $j^*K\in\Dbc(U,A)$, and
$Rb_*K\in\Dbc(S,A)$, then $K\in\Dbc(Y,A)$.
