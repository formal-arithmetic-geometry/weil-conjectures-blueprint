If $K\in D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Fell)$, $j^*K\in\Dbc(U,\Fell)$, and
$Rb_*K\in\Dbc(S,\Fell)$, then $K\in\Dbc(Y,\Fell)$.
