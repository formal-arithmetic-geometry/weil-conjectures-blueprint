Let $S$ be integral, let $X$ be an $S$-scheme all of whose irreducible
components dominate $S$, let $n=\dim X_{k(S)}$, and let $F$ be a
constructible $\Fell$-sheaf on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$. After shrinking $S$ to a
nonempty open, there are a finite universal homeomorphism $S'\to S$ with
$S'$ integral and a dense open $V\subset(X_{S'})_{\mathrm{red}}$ such that
$V$ is smooth over $S'$, the pullback of $F$ to $V$ is locally constant,
and the generic fibre of $(X_{S'})_{\mathrm{red}}\setminus V$ over $S'$
has dimension less than $n$.
