Let $S$ be integral, let $j:X\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion of
$S$-schemes, and let $F$ be a constructible $\Fell$-sheaf on $X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t}$.
Let $S'\to S$ be a finite universal homeomorphism with $S'$ integral, let
$X'=(X_{S'})_{\mathrm{red}}$ and $Y'=(Y_{S'})_{\mathrm{red}}$, let
$j':X'\hookrightarrow Y'$ be the open immersion, and let $F'$ be the
pullback of $F$ to $X'$. Suppose that there is a nonempty open
$U'\subset S'$ such that $Rj'_{U',*}(F'|_{X'_{U'}})\in\Dbc(Y'_{U'},\Fell)$
and such that for every $k$-scheme $T'\to U'$ of finite type the base
change map for $j'$ along $T'$ is an isomorphism. Then there is a nonempty
open $U\subset S$ with the same two properties for $j$ and $F$.
