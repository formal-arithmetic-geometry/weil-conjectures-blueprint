That (6.20) is an isomorphism for every noetherian
$T\to S_0$ was shown, with the same $S_0$, in the proof of
clause [theorem-generic-constructibility-base-change-rj--1]. The two formulations agree: $g_Y$ factors as
$Y_T\to Y_{S_0}\subset Y$, the restriction of $Rj_*F$ to the open
$Y_{S_0}$ is $Rj_{S_0,*}F_0$, and by the compatibility of base change maps
with composition of cartesian squares, the base change map over $S$ is the
one over $S_0$ composed with the base change map for the open immersion
$Y_{S_0}\subset Y$, which is an isomorphism.
