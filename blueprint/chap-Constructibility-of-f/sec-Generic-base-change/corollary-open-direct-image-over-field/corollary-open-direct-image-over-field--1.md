Let $k$ be a field, let $A$ be a finite commutative ring killed by a
positive integer invertible in $k$, and let $j:X\hookrightarrow Y$ be an
open immersion with $Y$ of finite type over $k$. On the étale sites,

$$
Rj_*:\Dbc(X,A)\longrightarrow\Dbc(Y,A).
\tag{6.22}
$$
