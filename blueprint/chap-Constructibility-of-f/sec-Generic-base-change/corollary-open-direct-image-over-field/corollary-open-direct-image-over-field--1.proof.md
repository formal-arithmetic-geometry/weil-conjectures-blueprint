Let $n$ be a positive integer killing $A$ and invertible in $k$. The
finite ring $A$ is the product of its $\ell$-primary parts $A_\ell$ for the
primes $\ell$ dividing $n$, cut out by orthogonal idempotents, and each
$\ell$ is invertible in $k$. Every étale sheaf of $A$-modules, and every
complex of such sheaves, is the direct sum of its parts on which $A$ acts
through $A_\ell$. Since $Rj_*$ is additive and a sheaf is constructible if
and only if each of its finitely many parts is, we may assume that $A$ is
killed by a power of a single prime $\ell$ invertible in $k$. The scheme
$X$ is of finite type over $k$, being open in $Y$. For such $A$ the
argument of the proof of Corollary [corollary-open-direct-image-over-field] applies: the case
of constructible $\Fell$-sheaves is
Theorem [theorem-generic-constructibility-base-change-rj] with
$S=\operatorname{Spec}k$, whose only nonempty open is $S$ itself, and one
passes to $\mathbf Z/\ell^r$-sheaves by the filtration by the
$\ell^iF$, to $A$-sheaves by forgetting the $A$-structure [Stacks, [0D6J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0D6J)],
and to bounded complexes by truncation triangles.
