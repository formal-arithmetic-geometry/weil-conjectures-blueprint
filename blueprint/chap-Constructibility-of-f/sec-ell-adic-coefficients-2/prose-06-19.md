For $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we have $\href{@definition-constructible-sheaves}{D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}}=\href{@definition-constructible-sheaves}{\Dbc}$, and we
show that [$f^!$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image) preserves [$\Dbc$](@definition-constructible-sheaves). The argument of
Section [sec-Finite-coefficients-2] uses two geometric facts, namely the finiteness of
$Rj_*$ for an open immersion and the [adjunction for the projective line](@proposition-adjunction-projective-line), and we deduce both from the case of torsion coefficients. This is possible because an integral complex lies in [$\Dbc$](@definition-constructible-sheaves) if it is [derived complete](@definition-derived-adic-completeness) with [constructible](@definition-constructible-sheaves) reduction modulo
$\pi_E$ (Proposition [proposition-integral-constructibility-criterion]), and rational
complexes are built from integral ones (Lemma [lemma-integral-models](4)).

For $Rj_*$ this reduction is immediate, since $Rj_*$ commutes with
$R\varprojlim$ and with cones (Proposition [proposition-direct-image-along-open-immersion]).
For the projective line it is not, because the unit and counit are morphisms, and a compatible family of morphisms modulo $\ell^n$ need not come from a
morphism of the limits. We therefore construct the class of the diagonal
and the trace once, at the level of inverse systems
(Subsection [subsec-The-projective-line]). Besides this, the integral case uses the [structure of integral constructible complexes](@lemma-structure-integral-constructible-complexes) and the fact that proper
direct image preserves direct sums (Subsection [subsec-Integral-coefficients]).
