For $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we have $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}=\Dbc$, and we
show that $f^!$ preserves $\Dbc$. The argument of
Section [sec-Finite-coefficients-2] uses two geometric facts, namely the finiteness of
$Rj_*$ for an open immersion and the adjunction for the projective line.
We deduce both from the case of torsion coefficients. An integral complex lies in
$\Dbc$ if it is derived complete with constructible reduction modulo
$\pi_E$ (Proposition [proposition-integral-constructibility-criterion]), and rational
complexes are built from integral ones (Lemma [lemma-integral-models](4)).

For $Rj_*$ this reduction is immediate, since $Rj_*$ commutes with
$R\varprojlim$ and with cones (Proposition [proposition-direct-image-along-open-immersion]).
For the projective line it is not. The unit and counit are morphisms,
and a compatible family of morphisms modulo $\ell^n$ need not come from a
morphism of the limits. We therefore construct the class of the diagonal
and the trace once, at the level of inverse systems
(Section [subsec-The-projective-line]). The integral case also uses the
structure of integral constructible complexes and the fact that proper
direct image preserves direct sums (Section [subsec-Integral-coefficients]).
