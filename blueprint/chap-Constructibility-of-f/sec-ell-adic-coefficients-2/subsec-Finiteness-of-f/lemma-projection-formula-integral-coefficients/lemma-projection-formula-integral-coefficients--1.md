For proper $p:X\to Y$ the map
$(Rp_*M)\otimes^{\mathbf L}N\to Rp_*(M\otimes^{\mathbf L}p^*N)$ is an
isomorphism for $M\in\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\mathcal O_E))$ and
$N\in\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\mathcal O_E))$.
