By the first three clauses, $f^!_\Lambda$ maps $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ into $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ for every separated $f$ of finite type, and so do $j^*$ and $v^*$. Since $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ is a full subcategory of $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda))$, the isomorphisms of Proposition [proposition-composition-open-restriction-compactifications-f], which are isomorphisms between composites of these functors, restrict to isomorphisms between the restricted functors.
