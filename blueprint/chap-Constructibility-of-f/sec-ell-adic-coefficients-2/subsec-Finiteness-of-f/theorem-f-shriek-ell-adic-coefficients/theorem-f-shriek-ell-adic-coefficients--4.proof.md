The ambient adjunction is $Rf_{!,\Lambda}\dashv f^!_\Lambda$. Proposition [proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek] puts $Rf_{!,\Lambda}M$ in $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$ for $M\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)$, and the corresponding preceding clause does the same for $f^!_\Lambda K$. These subcategories are full. Thus the ambient natural bijection $\Hom(Rf_{!,\Lambda}M,K)\simeq\Hom(M,f^!_\Lambda K)$ is already a bijection between their Hom sets, proving the restricted adjunction.
