Let $\Lambda\in\{\mathcal O_E,E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\}$ and let $f=p\circ j$ be a compactification, with $j$ an open immersion and $p$ proper. By the preceding clauses the right adjoint $p^!_\Lambda$ of $Rp_{*,\Lambda}$ preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$, and we consider the functor

$$
j^*\circ p^!_\Lambda:D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda)\to D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},\Lambda).
$$

By Proposition [proposition-composition-open-restriction-compactifications-f](4) it is the restriction of $f^!_\Lambda$ to $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$.
