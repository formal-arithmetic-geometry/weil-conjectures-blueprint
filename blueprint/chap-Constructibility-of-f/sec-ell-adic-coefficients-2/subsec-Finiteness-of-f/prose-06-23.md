For admissible $\Lambda$ and a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$
with complement $j$, the [local cohomology functor](@definition-local-cohomology) [$R\underline\Gamma_Z$](@definition-local-cohomology)
is right adjoint to $i_*=\href{@proposition-construction-independence-rf-shriek}{Ri_!}$
(Lemma [lemma-sections-support-closed-subscheme](1)). Hence
$\href{@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image}{i^!}\simeq \href{@definition-local-cohomology}{R\underline\Gamma_Z}$, and [$i^!$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image) is computed by the [localization triangle](@lemma-local-cohomology-right-adjoint)

$$
i_*\href{@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image}{i^!K}\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow Rj_*j^*K
\longrightarrow i_*\href{@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image}{i^!K}[1].
$$
