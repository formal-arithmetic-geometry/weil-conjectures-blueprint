For admissible $\Lambda$ and a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$
with complement $j$, the [local cohomology functor](@definition-local-cohomology) [$R\underline\Gamma_Z$](@definition-local-cohomology)
is right adjoint to $i_*=Ri_!$
(Lemma [lemma-sections-support-closed-subscheme](1)). Hence
$i^!\simeq R\underline\Gamma_Z$, and [$i^!$](@theorem-existence-exceptional-inverse-image) is computed by the [localization triangle](@lemma-local-cohomology-right-adjoint)

$$
i_*i^!K\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow Rj_*j^*K
\longrightarrow i_*i^!K[1].
$$
