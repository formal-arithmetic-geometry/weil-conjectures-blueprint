For admissible $\Lambda$ and a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$
with complement $j$, the local cohomology functor $R\underline\Gamma_Z$
is right adjoint to $i_*=Ri_!$
(Lemma [lemma-sections-support-closed-subscheme](1)). Hence
$i^!\simeq R\underline\Gamma_Z$, and $i^!$ is computed by the localization triangle

$$
i_*i^!K\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow Rj_*j^*K
\longrightarrow i_*i^!K[1].
$$
