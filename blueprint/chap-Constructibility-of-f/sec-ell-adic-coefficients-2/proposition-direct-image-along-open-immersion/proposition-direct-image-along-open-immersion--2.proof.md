It suffices to treat the generators of Lemma [lemma-integral-models](4), because $Rj_{*,E}$ is exact and $\Dbc(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},E)$ is triangulated. A generator is obtained from an integral object $K_0$ by inverting $\ell$. The multiplication-by-$\ell$ system has a common lower bound, so Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology] identifies its direct image with $(Rj_{*,\mathcal O_E}K_0)[1/\ell]$. This is bounded constructible by clause [proposition-direct-image-along-open-immersion--1]. Finite cones and shifts give the assertion for all $K$.
