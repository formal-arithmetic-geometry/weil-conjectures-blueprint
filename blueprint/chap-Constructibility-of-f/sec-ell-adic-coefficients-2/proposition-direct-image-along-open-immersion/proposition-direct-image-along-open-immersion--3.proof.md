A generator over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ descends to a finite coefficient field by Lemma [lemma-integral-models](4). Its scalar extension is a filtered colimit of finite coefficient-field extensions with a common lower bound. Finite extensions commute with $Rj_*$ because they are finite direct sums after a basis choice; the filtered colimit commutes with $Rj_*$ by Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology]. Clause [proposition-direct-image-along-open-immersion--2] proves bounded constructibility on these generators. Exactness and finite generation by cones and shifts prove the result in general.
