Apply extension of scalars $-\otimes_{\mathcal O_E}E$ to clause [proposition-ell-adic-cycle-class-diagonal--2]. The continuity comparison of Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology] identifies the transported proper-direct-image maps with those over $E$, proving precisely the asserted construction.
