At each level $\mathbf Z/\ell^n$, use the distinguished-summand projection in the projective-line decomposition defining the trace. The decompositions and their projections commute with reduction, so they form one morphism of systems. Taking its derived inverse limit gives the $\mathbf Z_\ell$-linear trace, and extension of scalars gives the $\mathcal O_E$-linear trace. The levelwise projections and the coefficient comparisons identify all its reductions with the prescribed traces.
