*Shared context.* Let $q:\mathbf P^1_Y\to Y$, and let $\Delta$ be the diagonal with projections $p_1,p_2$. The class and trace at each coefficient stage are chosen together as in the reference proof; all identity and base-change clauses concern those same choices. The integral construction lifts the entire compatible finite system.
