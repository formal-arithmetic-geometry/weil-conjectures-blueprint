The finite-stage identity of clause [proposition-ell-adic-cycle-class-diagonal--7] commutes with extension of the coefficient field because it is constructed from the same Cartier–Kummer system and projection. Pass to the filtered system of finite extensions and use Corollary [corollary-continuity-cohomology]. The resulting identity over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ is independent of the chosen finite stage and is the one asserted.
