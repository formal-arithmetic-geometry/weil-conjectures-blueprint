The trace is the compatible distinguished-summand projection of the projective-line decompositions. Proper base change and naturality of the cycle class identify these decompositions and projections after pullback. Passing to the derived limit and then extending coefficients, as in clauses [proposition-ell-adic-cycle-class-diagonal--2], [proposition-ell-adic-cycle-class-diagonal--6] and [proposition-ell-adic-cycle-class-diagonal--10], preserves this equality.
