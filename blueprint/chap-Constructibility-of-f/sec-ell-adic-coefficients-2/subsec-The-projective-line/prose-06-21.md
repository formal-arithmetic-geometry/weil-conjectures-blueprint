To turn $\delta_\Lambda$ and $\operatorname{Tr}_q$ into an adjunction we
repeat the torsion calculation. It used the projection formula and
proper base change on the categories where $Rq_*$ and $q^*(1)[2]$ act.
Proper base change on $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}})$ is
Proposition [proposition-proper-base-change-localizing-categories], and we extend the projection
formula in the same way.
