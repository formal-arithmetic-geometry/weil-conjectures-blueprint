To turn [$\delta_\Lambda$](@proposition-ell-adic-cycle-class-diagonal) and [$\operatorname{Tr}_q$](@proposition-ell-adic-cycle-class-diagonal) into an adjunction we
repeat the torsion calculation. It used the projection formula and
proper base change on the categories where $Rq_*$ and $q^*(1)[2]$ act.
Proper base change on [$\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}})$](@definition-localizing-subcategory) is
Proposition [proposition-proper-base-change-localizing-categories], and we extend the [projection
formula](@proposition-projection-formula-rational-coefficients) in the same way.
