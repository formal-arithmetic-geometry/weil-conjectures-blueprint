On $\Dbc$, use Corollary [corollary-coefficient-heart-b]: tensor products reduce to $a_!L\otimes^{\mathbf L}b_!M$, which is extension by zero of a lisse sheaf on the intersection of the strata. Thus the constructible generators are closed under tensor. Tensor is exact and preserves direct sums in either variable. Apply the localizing-subcategory criterion of Lemma [lemma-checking-isomorphisms-constructible-complexes] successively to the two variables to obtain closure of the whole localizing category.
