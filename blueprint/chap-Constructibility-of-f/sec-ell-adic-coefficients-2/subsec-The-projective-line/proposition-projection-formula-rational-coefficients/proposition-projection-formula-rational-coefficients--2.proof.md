For constructible $M,N$, the comparison is an isomorphism by Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes]. Proper direct image preserves direct sums by Lemma [lemma-proper-direct-image-unbounded-complexes--3], and tensor and pullback preserve direct sums and cones. For fixed constructible $M$, the objects $N$ for which the map is an isomorphism therefore form the whole localizing category. Keeping such $N$ fixed and repeating in $M$ proves the assertion for both variables.
