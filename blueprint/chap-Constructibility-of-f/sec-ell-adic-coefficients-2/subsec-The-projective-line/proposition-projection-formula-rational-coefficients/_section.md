For $\Lambda=E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, $\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$ is closed under
$\otimes^{\mathbf L}$, and for proper $p:X\to Y$ the map
$(Rp_*M)\otimes^{\mathbf L}N\to Rp_*(M\otimes^{\mathbf L}p^*N)$ is an
isomorphism for $M\in\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda))$ and $N\in\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda))$.
