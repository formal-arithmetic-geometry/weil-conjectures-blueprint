The functor $q^!$ and the functor $q^*(1)[2]$ of clause [proposition-projective-line-rational-coefficients--1] are right adjoints to the same proper direct image $Rq_!=Rq_*$. For every $N$ they represent the same Hom functor. Yoneda gives the unique natural isomorphism identifying these adjunctions, which is the asserted comparison.
