Both claims are first checked on $\Dbc$ and then extended. Tensor
products and both sides of the projection map commute with direct sums
and cones in each variable. Hence, fixing all variables but one, the
objects satisfying a claim form a localizing subcategory. It suffices that
this subcategory contains $\Dbc$
(Lemma [lemma-checking-isomorphisms-constructible-complexes]). By the
dévissage of Corollary [corollary-coefficient-heart-b], $\Dbc$ is closed
under $\otimes^{\mathbf L}$, since $a_!L\otimes^{\mathbf L}b_!M$ is the
extension by zero of a lisse sheaf on the intersection of the two strata. The projection formula on $\Dbc$ is
Proposition [proposition-proper-direct-image-constructible-complexes].
