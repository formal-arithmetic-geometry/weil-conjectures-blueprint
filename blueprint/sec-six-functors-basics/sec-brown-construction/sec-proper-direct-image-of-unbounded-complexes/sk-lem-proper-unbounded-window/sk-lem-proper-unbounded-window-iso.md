Let $\Lambda$, $p$, $d$ be as above and $K\in\Dic(X,\Lambda)$, $q\in\mathbf Z$. The maps
$K\leftarrow\tau_{\leq q}K\to\tau_{\geq q-2d-1}\tau_{\leq q}K$ induce isomorphisms

$$
\mathcal H^q(Rp_*K)\xleftarrow{\sim}\mathcal H^q(Rp_*\tau_{\leq q}K)\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal H^q(Rp_*\tau_{\geq q-2d-1}\tau_{\leq q}K).
$$
