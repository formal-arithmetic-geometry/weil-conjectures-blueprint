Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $p\colon X\to Y$ proper with geometric fibres of dimension $\leq d$, and
$(K_\alpha)$ any family in $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$. Then $\bigoplus_\alpha Rp_*K_\alpha\to Rp_*(\bigoplus_\alpha K_\alpha)$
is an isomorphism.
