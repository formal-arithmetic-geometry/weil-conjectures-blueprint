The proof reduces, by a stratification, to the extension
by zero of a lisse $\mathcal O_E$-lattice on a stratum. Its reductions
modulo $\pi_E^n$ are torsion sheaves, for which the classical bound $2d$ holds, and the
$\varprojlim^1$ term in the passage to the limit over $n$ raises the
bound by one, to $2d+1$. Inverting $\ell$, extending scalars to $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$
and passing to ind-objects are filtered colimits, and by
Lemma [sk-lem-proper-heart-continuity] they preserve the bound.
