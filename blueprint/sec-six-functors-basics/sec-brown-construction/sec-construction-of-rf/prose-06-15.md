If $j:X\to Z$ is an open immersion, then $j_!$ is left adjoint to $j^*$
on the pro-étale derived categories. Hence a map out of
$j_!K$ is determined by its restriction to $X$. The comparisons between
compactifications below are the proper direct images of the unique
maps out of $j_!K$ that restrict to the identity on $X$.
