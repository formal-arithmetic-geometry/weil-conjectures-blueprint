In the situation of [sk-prop-proper-base-change-unbounded], suppose that the base change map
$\beta_K\colon g^*Rp_*K\to Rp'_*g'^*K$ is an isomorphism for every $K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. Then it is an
isomorphism for every $K\in\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$.
