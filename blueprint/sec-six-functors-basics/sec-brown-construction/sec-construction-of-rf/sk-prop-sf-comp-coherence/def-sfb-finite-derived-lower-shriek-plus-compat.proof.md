Both functors are composites of two factors, and it suffices to compare the factors. For the
exact functor $i_!$, $\iota\circ D^+(i_!)=D(i_!)\circ\iota$, since both apply $i_!$ levelwise.
For $g_*$, a bounded below complex of injectives is K-injective, so a bounded below injective
resolution computes the unbounded derived functor as well:
$\iota\circ R^+(g_*)\cong R(g_*)\circ\iota$.
