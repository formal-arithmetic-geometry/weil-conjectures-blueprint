Let $\mathcal S=\{K\in\Loc(\mathcal G):F(K)\in\Loc(\mathcal G')\}$. It contains $\mathcal G$. It is closed under
shifts and cones: $F$ is exact, so $F$ of a cone is a cone of objects of $\Loc(\mathcal G')$, which is
triangulated. It is closed under direct sums: $F(\bigoplus K_s)\cong\bigoplus F(K_s)$, and $\Loc(\mathcal G')$ is
closed under direct sums. It is closed under isomorphisms. Hence $\mathcal S$ is a localizing subcategory of
$\mathcal T$ containing $\mathcal G$, so $\mathcal S\supset\Loc(\mathcal G)$ by minimality
([sk-def-localizing-closure]).

The restricted functor is exact because the triangulated structures on the localizing subcategories are
induced from $\mathcal T$, $\mathcal T'$. Direct sums in $\Loc(\mathcal G)$ and $\Loc(\mathcal G')$ are the ambient ones,
so $F$ preserves them.
