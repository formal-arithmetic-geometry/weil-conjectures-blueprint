Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring and let $j\colon U\to X$ be an open immersion of
the setting of [det-def-sfb-dc-setting]. If $K\in D(\Mod_{\Lambda_U})$ is bounded constructible
([det-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]), then so is the levelwise extension by zero
$D(j_!)K\in D(\Mod_{\Lambda_X})$ ([det-def-sfb-module-lower-shriek],
[det-prop-sfb-module-derived-factorization]).
