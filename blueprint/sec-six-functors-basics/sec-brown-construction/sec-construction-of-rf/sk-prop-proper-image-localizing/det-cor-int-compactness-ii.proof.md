The functor $j_!$ is exact and preserves direct sums, being a left adjoint,
and it preserves $\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E)$ by [sk-lem-int-tensor].
The localizing argument of [det-prop-int-compactness-direct-sums] (3)
gives the second assertion.
