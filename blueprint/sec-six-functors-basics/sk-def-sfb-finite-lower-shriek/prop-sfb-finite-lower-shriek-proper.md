Let $\mathcal A$ be a Grothendieck abelian category and $f\colon X\to Y$ a proper
morphism. Then $f$ admits a compactification (for instance $X\xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}}X\xrightarrow{f}Y$),
and for the chosen one the comparison
$\iota_f\colon f_!\to f_*$ of [def-sfb-finite-lower-shriek-to-lower-star] is an
isomorphism.
