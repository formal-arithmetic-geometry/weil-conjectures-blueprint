Let $X$ be a geometric point and let $S=\mathbf Z_\ell$ be regarded as a
profinite object of $X_{\Proet}$. The completed coefficient sheaf
$\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}$ has sections $\operatorname{Cont}(S,\mathbf Z_\ell)$ on
$S$, among them the identity function. The pullback $\nu_X^*$ from $X_{\Et}$ of
the constant sheaf with value the *discrete* abelian group $\mathbf Z_\ell$ has
only locally constant functions as sections, and these have finite image on the
compact space $S$. So the identity function does not come from any finite stage
of the profinite presentation of $S$, and $\mathbf Z_{\ell,X}$ is not classical
in the sense of [sk-def-pe-classical-definition]. Its reductions
$\mathbf Z/\ell^n$ are classical. This is what allows finite-level étale
arguments to be combined with a single pro-étale definition of coefficients.
