In the situation of [def-sfb-rationalization-morphisms], let $m\in M$ and $X\in\mathcal T$.
Every cone of $m\cdot\mathrm{id}_X$ is killed by $m^2$. In particular
$m\cdot\mathrm{id}_X\in W_M$.
