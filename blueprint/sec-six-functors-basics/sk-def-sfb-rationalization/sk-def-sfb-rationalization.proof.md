The $M$-torsion objects form a triangulated subcategory [def-sfb-torsion-objects],
[lem-sfb-torsion-triangulated]. So the morphisms with $M$-torsion cone
[def-sfb-rationalization-morphisms] form the class inverted by the Verdier quotient, which
is triangulated with a triangulated localization functor
[def-sfb-rationalization-quotient]. Multiplication by $m\in M$ has $M$-torsion cone
[lem-sfb-smul-id-in-w], hence becomes invertible, and this gives the linear structure over
the localizations of $R$ at $M$ [def-sfb-rationalization-linear].
