Let $\mathcal T$ be a preadditive category that is linear over a commutative ring $R$,
and let $M\subseteq R$ be a submonoid. An object $X$ of $\mathcal T$ is *$M$-torsion* if
$m\cdot\mathrm{id}_X=0$ for some $m\in M$. The $M$-torsion objects form a property of
objects closed under isomorphisms.
