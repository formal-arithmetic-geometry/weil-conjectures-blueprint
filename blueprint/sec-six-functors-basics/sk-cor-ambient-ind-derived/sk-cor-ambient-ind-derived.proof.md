In the long exact cohomology sequence of a triangle, each cohomology sheaf of the third term is an
extension of a kernel by a cokernel of maps between cohomology sheaves of the other two; so
[sk-prop-ind-heart] gives closure under cones ([det-ambient-ind-derived-triangulated]). Direct sums of
sheaves are exact, so $H^n$ commutes with direct sums. Truncation only removes cohomology sheaves. Since
$\Dbc(X,\Lambda)\subset\Dic(X,\Lambda)$, the last claim follows from the definition of
$\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$ ([det-ambient-ind-derived-sums]).
