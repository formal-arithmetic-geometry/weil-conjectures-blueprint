The construction of $Rf_!=Rp_*\circ j_!$ in
Section “The compactly supported direct image and Brown representability” is made for every admissible coefficient
ring and starts from proper direct image on $\Dctf$. We therefore need to
know, for every such ring, that $Rp_*$ preserves $\Dctf$ and satisfies proper
base change and the projection formula there.

With finite coefficients these statements are classical, except for the
Tor bound, which we check in Proposition [sk-prop-ctf-finite-proper]. The
integral case reduces to them modulo $\pi_E$, and the cases $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$
follow from the integral one by means of the generators of
Lemma [sk-lem-integral-models] (4).
