Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$.

1. $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is closed under $\otimes^{\mathbf L}$ in
   $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$.
2. For $A,B\in\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$, the derived tensor product
   $A\otimes^{\mathbf L}B$, formed in $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, lies in
   $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$.
