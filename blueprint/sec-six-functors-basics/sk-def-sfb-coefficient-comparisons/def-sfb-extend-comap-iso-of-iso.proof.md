Along an isomorphism of monoid objects, extension of scalars is restriction of scalars along the
inverse ([det-def-sfb-change-of-scalars]): $\operatorname{Ext}_b\cong\operatorname{Res}_{b^{-1}}$,
$\operatorname{Ext}_a\cong\operatorname{Res}_{a^{-1}}$. Restriction of scalars turns composites
into composites in the opposite order, so the left side is
$\operatorname{Res}_{b^{-1}\circ\psi}$ and the right side is
$\operatorname{Res}_{\varphi\circ a^{-1}}$, and $b^{-1}\circ\psi=\varphi\circ a^{-1}$ by the
commutativity of the square.
