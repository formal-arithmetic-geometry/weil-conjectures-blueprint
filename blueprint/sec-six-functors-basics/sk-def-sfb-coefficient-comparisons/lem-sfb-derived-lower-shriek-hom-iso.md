In the situation of [def-sfb-derived-lower-shriek-hom], if (Push) for the proper part $g$ of the
chosen compactification of $f$ ([def-sfb-derived-push-hom]) is an isomorphism, then (Shriek)
$T^Y\circ Rf_!\to Rf_!\circ T^X$ is an isomorphism.
