For each listed coefficient map $\Lambda\to\Lambda'$, the derived extension of scalars
$K\mapsto K_{\Lambda'}=\Lambda'_X\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Lambda_X}K$ maps $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(X,\Lambda')$.
