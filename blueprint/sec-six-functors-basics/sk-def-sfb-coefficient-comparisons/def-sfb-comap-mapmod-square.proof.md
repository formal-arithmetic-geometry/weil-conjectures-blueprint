Restriction of scalars is strictly compatible with composition,
$\operatorname{Res}_{\varphi_Y}\circ\operatorname{Res}_{\eta_S}\cong
\operatorname{Res}_{\eta_S\circ\varphi_Y}$, and along equal morphisms; and
$\mathrm{mapMod}(F)$ commutes with restriction of scalars,
$\mathrm{mapMod}(F)\circ\operatorname{Res}_{\varphi_X}\cong
\operatorname{Res}_{F\varphi_X}\circ\mathrm{mapMod}(F)$ ([sk-def-sfb-transport]). Composing these
isomorphisms with the commutativity of the square gives the asserted one; every step is the
identity on underlying objects.
