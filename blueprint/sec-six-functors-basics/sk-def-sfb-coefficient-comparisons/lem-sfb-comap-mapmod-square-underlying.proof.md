Each constituent isomorphism in the construction of [def-sfb-comap-mapmod-square] (associators,
compatibility of restriction of scalars with composition and with equal morphisms, compatibility
of $\mathrm{mapMod}(F)$ with restriction of scalars) has identity underlying morphisms, and so
does their composite.
