Both functors send $M$ to $PM$. The $B$-action on the left is
$B\otimes PM\xrightarrow{\varphi\otimes1}B'\otimes PM\cong P(SB'\otimes M)\to PM$; the one on the
right is $B\otimes PM\cong P(SB\otimes M)\xrightarrow{P(S\varphi\otimes1)}P(SB'\otimes M)\to PM$.
They agree by the naturality of the projection formula in $L$, so the identity of $PM$ is a
morphism of $B$-modules, natural in $M$.
