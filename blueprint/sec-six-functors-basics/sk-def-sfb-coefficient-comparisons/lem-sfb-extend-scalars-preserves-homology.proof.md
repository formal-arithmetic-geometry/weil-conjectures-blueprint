$\operatorname{Ext}_{a_X}$ preserves finite colimits, being left adjoint to
$\operatorname{Res}_{a_X}$ ([det-def-sfb-change-of-scalars]), and finite limits by assumption;
an exact additive functor between abelian categories preserves homology.
