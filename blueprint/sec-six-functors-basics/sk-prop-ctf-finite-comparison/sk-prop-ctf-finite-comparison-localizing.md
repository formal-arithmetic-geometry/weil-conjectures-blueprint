Let $A$ be a finite admissible ring. The functor $\nu_X^*$ restricts to an exact equivalence
$\Loc(\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A))\xrightarrow{\sim}\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))$ preserving sums, compatible with $g^*$, $Rg_*$,
$j_!$ for open immersions $j$, and hence with $Rf_!=Rp_*j_!$ computed through a compactification.
