The comparison on $\Dctf$ is [sk-cor-pe-finite-tor-comparison]. By [sk-prop-pe-unbounded-classical], $\nu_X^*$
is fully faithful on unbounded complexes, preserves direct sums and is compatible with direct images. An exact
fully faithful functor that preserves direct sums identifies the localizing subcategories generated by
corresponding generators. Using a compactification, we then deduce the comparison for $Rf_!=Rp_*j_!$ from
the comparisons for direct image and extension by zero.

For the equality with the whole étale derived category, let $u\colon U\to X$ range over a basis of
quasi-compact separated étale objects. The free representable module sheaves $u_!A_U$ are flat
constructible, hence belong to $\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$. Since $\RHom_X(u_!A_U,K)=R\Gamma(U,K)$, their shifts
detect zero objects of $\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$, and they are compact in all of $\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ because
$R\Gamma(U,-)$ commutes with direct sums ($\operatorname{cd}_\ell(U)<\infty$), as in the étale part of the proof
of [sk-prop-ctf-finite-compact]. Recall that a set of compact objects whose shifts detect zero objects
generates a triangulated category with direct sums as a localizing subcategory. Therefore the localizing
subcategory generated by these objects is the entire étale derived category.
