For a finite admissible ring $A$, the comparison functor $\nu_X^*\colon\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)\to\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},A)$
identifies

$$
\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)=\Loc(\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A))\simeq\Loc(\Dctf(X,A)).
$$

It preserves arbitrary sums and identifies ordinary direct image, pullback and compactly supported direct
image with their classical calculations. In particular $\nu_Y^*Rg_{\mathrm{ét},*}K\simeq Rg_{\mathrm{proét},*}\nu_X^*K$
for every $K\in\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ and every finite-type $g$ in the standing range.
