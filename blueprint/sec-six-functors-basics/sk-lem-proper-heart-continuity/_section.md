Let $Z$ be a quasi-compact and quasi-separated scheme and let $A$ be a sheaf of rings on
$Z_{\mathrm{proét}}$. For each integer $q$, the functor $H^q(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},-)$ from sheaves of $A$-modules to
abelian groups commutes with filtered colimits and direct sums. If $p\colon X\to Y$ is quasi-compact and
quasi-separated, then each $R^qp_*$ on sheaves of modules on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ has the same two properties.
