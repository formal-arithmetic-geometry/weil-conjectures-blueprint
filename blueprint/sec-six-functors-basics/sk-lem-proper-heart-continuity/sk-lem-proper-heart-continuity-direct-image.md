Let $p\colon X\to Y$ be quasi-compact and quasi-separated and $A$ a sheaf of rings on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$.
For every $q$, $R^qp_*$ on sheaves of $A$-modules commutes with filtered colimits and direct sums.
