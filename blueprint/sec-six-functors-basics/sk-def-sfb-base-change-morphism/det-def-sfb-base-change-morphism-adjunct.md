In the situation of [det-def-sfb-base-change-morphism-bc], write $\varepsilon'$ for the counit of
$Lf'^*\dashv Rf'_*$. Then $\mathrm{BC}_{sq}$ is the unique natural transformation
$\beta\colon Lg^*Rf_*\Rightarrow Rf'_*Lg'^*$ whose adjunct
$$
\varepsilon'_{Lg'^*}\circ Lf'^*\beta\colon Lf'^*Lg^*Rf_*\Rightarrow Lg'^*
$$
equals $Lg'^*\varepsilon\circ(\theta_{sq})_{Rf_*}\colon Lf'^*Lg^*Rf_*\Rightarrow Lg'^*Lf^*Rf_*\Rightarrow Lg'^*$.
In particular $\mathrm{BC}_{sq}$ is the mate of the pullback comparison $\theta_{sq}$ under the
adjunctions $Lf^*\dashv Rf_*$ and $Lf'^*\dashv Rf'_*$.
