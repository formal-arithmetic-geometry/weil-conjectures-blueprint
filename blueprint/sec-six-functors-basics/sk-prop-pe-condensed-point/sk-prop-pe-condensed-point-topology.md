Let $\Omega$ be separably closed. The underlying topological space of
$\Omega[S]$ is $S$, and the morphism $\Omega[T]\to\Omega[S]$ induced by a
continuous $s\colon T\to S$ is $s$ on underlying spaces. A family of morphisms
$\{\Omega[T_a]\to\Omega[S]\}$ is a pro-étale covering if and only if some finite
subfamily is jointly surjective, i.e. $\coprod_{a\in F}T_a\to S$ is surjective for
a finite $F$. Consequently the topology induced on the affine ind-étale basis is
the coherent topology of profinite sets, and restriction along
$S\mapsto\Omega[S]$ is an equivalence between sheaves on
$(\Spec\Omega)_{\Proet}$ and sheaves on $*_{\Proet}$, i.e. condensed sets.
