Let $S$ be an integral Noetherian affine scheme with function field $k(S)$,
and let $Y\to S$ be projective. Choose a finite affine open cover
$Y=\bigcup_\alpha Y_\alpha$ and closed immersions
$Y_\alpha\hookrightarrow\mathbf A^{m_\alpha}_S$ with $m_\alpha\geq1$, and write
$t_{\alpha r}:Y_\alpha\to\mathbf A^1_S$ ($1\le r\le m_\alpha$) for the
coordinate functions.

1. If $X\subset Y$ is open and $\dim X_{k(S)}\leq n$, then for every
   $\alpha,r$ the generic fibre of $t_{\alpha r}:X\cap Y_\alpha\to\mathbf A^1_S$
   has dimension at most $n-1$.
2. Suppose given a dense open $V_{\alpha r}\subset\mathbf A^1_S$ for each
   $\alpha,r$, and let

   $$
   G=\bigcup_{\alpha,r}t_{\alpha r}^{-1}(V_{\alpha r})\subset Y.
   $$

   After shrinking $S$, the reduced closed complement $Z=Y\setminus G$ is
   finite over $S$.
