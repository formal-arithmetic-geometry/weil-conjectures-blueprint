title = "The exceptional inverse image through a compactification"
uses = ["det-sf-mate-open-restriction", "det-sf-existence-brown", "det-sf-comp-coherence-single"]
+++
Let $\Lambda$ be admissible, $f:X\to Y$ separated of finite type, and
$f=p\circ j$ a compactification ($j:X\to\overline X$ open, $p:\overline X\to Y$
proper). Then $j^*\circ p^!$ is right adjoint to $Rf_!=Rp_*\circ j_!$ on the
localizing categories, and there is a unique isomorphism
$f^!\simeq j^*\circ p^!$ compatible with the adjunctions; under it the counit
of $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ is the composite
$Rp_*j_!j^*p^!N\to Rp_*p^!N\to N$ of the two counits.
