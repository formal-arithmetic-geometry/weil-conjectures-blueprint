title = "Targets are filtered colimits of constructible sheaves"

The targets in the compactness statement are direct sums of objects of
$\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$, and their cohomology sheaves are no longer
constructible. They are, however, filtered colimits of constructible
sheaves, and both continuity and the uniform bound for Ext groups extend
to such colimits. The
proposition says that for constructible sheaves the formal and the actual
colimits agree.
