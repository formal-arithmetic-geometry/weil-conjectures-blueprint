In the notation of [def-pur-td-datum], the following holds. The
ambient exceptional right
adjoint datum of [def-weil-ambient-exceptional-adjoint] is not assumed
(it is false in general); the right adjoint of $Rf_!$ occurring below is,
on the coefficients of this chapter, the adjoint relative to the
bounded-below objects of étale origin of [thm-sf-ambient-adjoint-class].
