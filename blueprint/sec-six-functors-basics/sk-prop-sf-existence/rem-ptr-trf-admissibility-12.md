In the notation of [def-trf-admissibility], the following holds. The ambient
exceptional right adjoint datum of
[def-weil-ambient-exceptional-adjoint] — clause (1) of
[def-weil-exceptional-adjoint-admissibility] — is false in general, but
its *class form* is a theorem for a separated finite-type morphism of
finite-type $k$-schemes, by [cor-sf-ambient-adjoint-discharge]: the
class-restricted vanishing, and a right adjoint of $Rf_!$ relative to the
bounded-below objects of étale origin, which include the bounded
constructible and finite-Tor categories.
