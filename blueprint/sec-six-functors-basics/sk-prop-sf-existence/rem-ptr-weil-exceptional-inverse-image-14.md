In the notation of [def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image], the following holds. The
adjunction of the finite-stage functor $Rf_n^!$, relative to the
bounded-below objects of étale origin, is
[thm-sf-ambient-adjoint-class]; it is not the unrestricted ambient datum
of [def-weil-ambient-exceptional-adjoint], which is false in general.
