The étale right adjoint of $Rg_*$ is [thm-sfb-finite-proper-pushforward-left-adjoint], the
exceptional pullback along a compactification and its adjunction are
[def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-compactification] and
[def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction-compactification], and $f^\times$ with $Rf_!\dashv f^\times$ is
[thm-sfb-finite-lower-shriek-left-adjoint]. The pro-étale $Rf^!$ is [def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek];
it preserves bounded below objects and lands in the étale-origin class by
[lem-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-bounded-below], with restriction [def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-plus];
the adjunction on bounded below objects of étale origin is [def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction].
