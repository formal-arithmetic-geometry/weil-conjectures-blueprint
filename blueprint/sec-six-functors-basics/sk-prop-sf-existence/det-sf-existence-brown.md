Let $\Lambda$ be admissible and $f:X\to Y$ separated of finite type. Assume
(as established in [sk-lem-brown-hypotheses]) that $\mathcal T_X=\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$
is a locally small triangulated category with arbitrary direct sums, compactly
generated by a set of objects of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. Then:

1. there is a functor $f^!:\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))\to\mathcal T_X$ and a natural
bijection $\Hom(Rf_!M,N)\simeq\Hom(M,f^!N)$, i.e. $Rf_!\dashv f^!$;
2. $f^!$ is exact (a triangulated functor);
3. any two right adjoints of $Rf_!$ are isomorphic by a unique isomorphism
compatible with the adjunctions; in particular $f^!$ does not depend, up to
unique isomorphism, on the compactification used to define $Rf_!$.
