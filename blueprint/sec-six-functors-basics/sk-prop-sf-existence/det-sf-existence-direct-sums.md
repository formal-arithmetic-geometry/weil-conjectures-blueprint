In the situation of [det-sf-existence-brown], for every family $(N_\alpha)$ in
$\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))$ the canonical map

$$
\bigoplus_\alpha f^!N_\alpha\longrightarrow f^!\Bigl(\bigoplus_\alpha N_\alpha\Bigr)
$$

is an isomorphism.
