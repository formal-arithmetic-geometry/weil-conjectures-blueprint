For every $E\in D(T_{\Et},\Lambda)$, the canonical morphism

$$
\operatorname{hocolim}_{N\to+\infty}\tau_{\le N}E\longrightarrow E
$$

is an isomorphism, where the homotopy colimit is that of
[def-kun-truncations].
