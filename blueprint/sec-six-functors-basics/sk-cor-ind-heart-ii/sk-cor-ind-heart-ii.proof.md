Write $B$ as a filtered colimit of constructible sheaves. By [sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity],
$\Ext^q(C,B)$ is the colimit of the corresponding groups for the constructible terms, which vanish for
$q>d_C$ by [sk-lem-uniform-constructible-ext] ([det-ind-heart-ii-vanishing]). A direct sum is the
filtered colimit of its finite subsums, and $\Ext^q(C,-)$ commutes with finite direct sums; the same
lemma gives the second claim ([det-ind-heart-ii-sums]).
