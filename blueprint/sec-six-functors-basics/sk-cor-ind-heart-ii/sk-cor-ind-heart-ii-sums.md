title = "Ext out of a constructible sheaf commutes with sums of ind-constructible sheaves"
order = 2233
uses = ["sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity"]
+++
Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$, $q\in\mathbf Z$, and let $(B_s)_{s\in S}$ be a family in
$\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$. The natural map

$$
\bigoplus_{s\in S}\Ext^q(C,B_s)\longrightarrow\Ext^q\Bigl(C,\bigoplus_{s\in S}B_s\Bigr)
$$

is an isomorphism.
