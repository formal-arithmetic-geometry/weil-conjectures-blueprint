Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$ and $B\in\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$. For every $q$,
writing $B=\varinjlim_\alpha D_\alpha$ with $D_\alpha$ constructible, the natural map
$\varinjlim_\alpha\Ext^q(C,D_\alpha)\to\Ext^q(C,B)$ is an isomorphism, and $\Ext^q(C,B)=0$ for $q>d_C$.
