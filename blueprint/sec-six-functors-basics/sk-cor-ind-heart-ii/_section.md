Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$. Let $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$ ([sk-def-sfb-constructible]), with $d_C$ as in
[sk-lem-uniform-constructible-ext]. Then $\Ext^q(C,B)=0$ for all $q>d_C$ and all
$B\in\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$ ([sk-prop-ind-heart]), and each functor $\Ext^q(C,-)$ commutes with direct
sums of objects of $\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$.
