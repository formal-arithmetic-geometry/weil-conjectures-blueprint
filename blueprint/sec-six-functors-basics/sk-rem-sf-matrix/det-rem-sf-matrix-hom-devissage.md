Let $k$ be a separably closed field, $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k)$, $A$ a
finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring, and $X$ a scheme of finite type over
$k$. For $K,L\in\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$, the internal Hom
$R\mathcal Hom_A(K,L)$ lies in $\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$.
