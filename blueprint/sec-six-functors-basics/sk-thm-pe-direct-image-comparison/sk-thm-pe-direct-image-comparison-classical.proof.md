By [det-pe-classical-derived-equivalence], $L:=R\nu_{X,*}K$ lies in
$D^+(X_{\Et},\Lambda)$ and the counit $\nu_X^*L\to K$ is an isomorphism. By
[prop-weil-pbc-comparison-direct-image],

$$
Rg_{\Proet,*}K\cong Rg_{\Proet,*}\nu_X^*L\cong\nu_Y^*Rg_{\Et,*}L .
$$

The right side is $\nu_Y^*$ of a bounded-below étale complex, and $\nu_Y^*$ is
exact, so its cohomology sheaves are $\nu_Y^*H^q(Rg_{\Et,*}L)$, which are
classical by [sk-thm-pe-classical-sheaves]. It is bounded below because
$Rg_{\Et,*}$ preserves lower bounds.
