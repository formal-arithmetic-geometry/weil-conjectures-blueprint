For $G$ a sheaf on $T_{\mathrm{proét}}$ both sides evaluate at $V$ to sections of $G$ over
the two iterated fibre products. The pasting isomorphism of fibre products is natural in
$V$, so restriction along it is an isomorphism of sheaves, natural in $G$.
