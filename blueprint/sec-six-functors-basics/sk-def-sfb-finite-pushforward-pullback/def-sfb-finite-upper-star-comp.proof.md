By [def-sfb-finite-upper-lower-star-adjunction], $(h_2\circ h_1)^*$ is left adjoint to
$(h_2\circ h_1)_*$, and $h_1^*\circ h_2^*$ is left adjoint to $(h_2)_*\circ(h_1)_*$.
These two right adjoints are isomorphic by [def-sfb-finite-lower-star-comp]. Left
adjoints are unique up to a unique isomorphism compatible with the adjunctions, and
taking conjugates gives the isomorphism.
