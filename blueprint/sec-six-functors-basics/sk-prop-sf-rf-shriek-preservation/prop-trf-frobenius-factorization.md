For every $m\in\mathbf Z_{\geq1}$, the absolute $q^m$-power Frobenius
$\operatorname{Fr}^{(q^m)}_X:X\to X$, $a\otimes b\mapsto a^{q^m}\otimes b^{q^m}$, factors as

$$
\operatorname{Fr}^{(q^m)}_X=g_\sigma{}^m\Phi^m=\Phi^mg_\sigma{}^m.
$$
