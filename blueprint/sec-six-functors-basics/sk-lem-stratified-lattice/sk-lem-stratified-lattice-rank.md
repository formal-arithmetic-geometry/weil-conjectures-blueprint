title = "The rank of a lisse sheaf is locally constant"
order = 2164
uses = ["det-lem-coefficient-heart-closure"]

Let $X$ be a scheme and $L$ a lisse $E_X$-module. For $r\ge0$ let $X_r\subseteq|X|$ be the set of
points $x$ such that, for some (equivalently every) $U\to X$ in a trivializing covering with
$x$ in the image of $U$, $L|_U$ has rank $r$ near a preimage of $x$. Then the $X_r$ are open
and closed, finitely many are non-empty if $X$ is quasi-compact, and $X=\coprod_rX_r$ with
$L|_{X_r}$ of constant rank $r$.
