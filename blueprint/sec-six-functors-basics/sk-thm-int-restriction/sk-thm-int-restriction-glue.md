1. Let $M\in D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ and let $(U_\alpha)$ be a finite
   open cover of $X$ with $M|_{U_\alpha}\in\Dbc(U_\alpha,\mathcal O_E)$ for all
   $\alpha$. Then $M\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$.
2. For $f:X\to Y$ separated of finite type and $K\in\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E)$,
   $f^!K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, and the adjunction $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ of
   [sk-prop-int-brown] restricts to an adjunction between
   $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ and $\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E)$.
