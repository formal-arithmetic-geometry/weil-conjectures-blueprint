On the localizing categories $\Loc(\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E))$, with $f^!$ the
right adjoints of [sk-prop-int-brown]:

1. for $q:\mathbf P^1_Y\to Y$, $Rq_*\dashv q^*(1)[2]$ and $q^!\simeq q^*(1)[2]$;
2. for $p:\mathbf A^d_Y\to Y$, $p^!\simeq p^*(d)[2d]$;
3. $q^*(1)[2]$ and $p^*(d)[2d]$ map $\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E)$ into
   $\Dbc(\mathbf P^1_Y,\mathcal O_E)$, respectively
   $\Dbc(\mathbf A^d_Y,\mathcal O_E)$.
