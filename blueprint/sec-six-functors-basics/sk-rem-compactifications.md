1. By Nagata's theorem ([sk-thm-nagata-compactification]), every separated morphism of finite type
   $f:X\to Y$ with $Y$ qcqs has a compactification, that is, a factorization $f=p\circ j$ with
   $j:X\to\overline X$ a quasi-compact open immersion and $p:\overline X\to Y$ proper.
2. In comparisons we may replace $\overline X$ by the scheme-theoretic closure of $X$, so that $X$ is
   dense, and by [sk-lem-nagata-density-normalization] this does not change $Rp_*j_!$.
