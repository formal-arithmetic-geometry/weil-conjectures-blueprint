Let $j:X\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion of schemes of finite type over
the standing field $k$, with $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k)$, and let $\Lambda$
be a finite $\ell$-primary ring. Then

$$
Rj_*:\Dbc(X,\Lambda)\longrightarrow\Dbc(Y,\Lambda),
\qquad\text{(G25)}
$$

and for every finite-type $k$-scheme $T\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ and every
$K\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$, the base-change map
$(Rj_*K)|_{Y_T}\to Rj_{T,*}(K|_{X_T})$ in $D((Y_T)_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ is
an isomorphism.
