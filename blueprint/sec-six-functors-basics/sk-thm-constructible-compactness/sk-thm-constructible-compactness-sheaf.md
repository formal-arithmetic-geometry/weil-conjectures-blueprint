Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$ and $(K_\lambda)$ any family in $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$. Then
for every $m$ the natural map $\bigoplus_\lambda\Hom(C,K_\lambda[m])\to\Hom(C,\bigoplus_\lambda K_\lambda[m])$
is an isomorphism.
