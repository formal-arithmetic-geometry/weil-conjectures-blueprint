Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$, $a\leq b$ integers, and $(K_\lambda)$ a family in
$\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ with $H^n(K_\lambda)=0$ for $n\notin[a,b]$ and all $\lambda$. Then for every
$s\in\mathbf Z$ the natural map $\bigoplus_\lambda\Hom(C,K_\lambda[s])\to\Hom(C,\bigoplus_\lambda K_\lambda[s])$
is an isomorphism.
