For $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, every object of $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ ([sk-def-sfb-constructible]) is compact in
$\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$ ([sk-def-localizing-closure]). More precisely, for $C\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ and every
family $(K_\lambda)$ of objects of $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ ([sk-cor-ambient-ind-derived]), the natural map

$$
\bigoplus_\lambda\Hom(C,K_\lambda)\longrightarrow\Hom\Bigl(C,\bigoplus_\lambda K_\lambda\Bigr)
$$

is an isomorphism.
