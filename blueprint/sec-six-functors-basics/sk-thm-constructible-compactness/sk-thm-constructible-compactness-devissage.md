Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and fix a family $(K_\lambda)$ in $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$. The full subcategory of
$C\in\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ for which $\bigoplus_\lambda\Hom(C[n],K_\lambda)\to\Hom(C[n],\bigoplus K_\lambda)$
is an isomorphism for all $n$ is a thick triangulated subcategory. It contains $\Cons(X,\Lambda)$,
hence $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$. Consequently every object of $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is compact in
$\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$.
