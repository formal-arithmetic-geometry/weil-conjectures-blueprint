For a quasi-compact open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, extension by zero
$j_!\colon D(U_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)\to D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$
maps $\Dbc(U,\mathcal O_E)$ into $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, maps
$\Loc(\Dbc(U,\mathcal O_E))$ into $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))$, and preserves
direct sums.
