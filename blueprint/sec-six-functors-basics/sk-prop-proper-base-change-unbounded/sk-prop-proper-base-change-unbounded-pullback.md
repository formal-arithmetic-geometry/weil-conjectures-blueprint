Let $\Lambda$ be admissible and $g\colon Y'\to Y$ a morphism. The inverse image
$g^*\colon\D(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to\D(Y'_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ is exact, preserves direct sums, maps
$\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(Y',\Lambda)$, and hence restricts to an exact sum-preserving functor
$\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(Y',\Lambda))$.
