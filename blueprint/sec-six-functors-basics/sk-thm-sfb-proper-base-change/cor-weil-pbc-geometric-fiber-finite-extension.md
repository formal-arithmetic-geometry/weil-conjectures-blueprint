Let $k$ be algebraically closed, let $f:X\to Y$ be a proper morphism of
separated schemes of finite type over $k$, and let
$\bar y:\operatorname{Spec}k\to Y$ be a $k$-point, i.e. a geometric point over
a closed point of $Y$. Let $i_{\bar y}:X_{\bar y}=X\times_Y\operatorname{Spec}k\to X$
and $a_{\bar y}:X_{\bar y}\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be the projections. Let
$E/\Ql$ be finite and let $K\in\Dbc(X,E)$ be an object of the condensed
bounded constructible category [det-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]. The base
change morphism [sk-def-sfb-base-change-morphism] is an isomorphism
$$
 L\bar y^*Rf_*K\xrightarrow{\sim}
 Ra_{\bar y,*}Li_{\bar y}^*K
 \quad\text{in }\Dbc(\operatorname{Spec}k,E).
$$
