Let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be finite and $p\colon X\to Y$ proper between standing schemes.

1. For $K\in\Dbc(X,\cO_E)$, $Rp_*K$ is derived $\pi_E$-complete, and
   $(Rp_*K)\otimes^{\mathbf L}\cO_{E,Y}/\pi_E\simeq Rp_*(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}\cO_{E,X}/\pi_E)$;
   hence $Rp_*K\in\Dbc(Y,\cO_E)$.
2. (Derived Nakayama.) A derived $\pi_E$-complete $C$ with $C\otimes^{\mathbf L}\cO_E/\pi_E=0$ is
   zero.
3. The proper base change map and the projection formula map are isomorphisms for
   $K\in\Dbc(X,\cO_E)$, $M\in\Dbc(Y,\cO_E)$.
