The functors attached to a compactification are
[def-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-compactification] and
[def-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-compactification-plus], and their compatibility
is [prop-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-compactification-plus-restriction]. The
functors for the chosen compactification are [def-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-chosen]
and [def-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-chosen-plus], with compatibility
[prop-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-chosen-plus-restriction].
