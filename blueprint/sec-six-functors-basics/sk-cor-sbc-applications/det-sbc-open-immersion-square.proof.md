For an open subscheme $U\subseteq Y$ the fibre product $Y'\times_YU$ is the open subscheme
$g^{-1}(U)$ of $Y'$, with the projections $j'$ and $g|_{U'}$; so the square is cartesian. Open
immersions of Noetherian schemes are of finite type, quasi-compact and separated, and $U'$ is
of finite type over $k$ as an open of $Y'$. Smoothness is stable under base change, so $u$ is
smooth.
