title = "The constant family as a smooth base change"

Let $X$ be of finite type over $k$ with structure morphism $a:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, let
$S$ be smooth of finite type over $k$ with structure morphism $b:S\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, and
let $a_S:X\times_kS\to S$ and $v:X\times_kS\to X$ be the projections. Then the square

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
X\times_kS & \xrightarrow{\ v\ } & X\\
{\scriptstyle a_S}\downarrow & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle a}\\
S & \xrightarrow{\ b\ } & \operatorname{Spec}k
\end{array}
$$

is cartesian, all its schemes are of finite type over $k$, $a$ and $a_S$ are quasi-compact and
quasi-separated, and the base change $b$ is smooth. Hence smooth base change for admissible
coefficients applies with $f=a$ and $g=b$, and the exchange map of this square is a morphism
$b^*Ra_*K\to Ra_{S,*}v^*K$ for $K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
