The fibre product of $X$ and $S$ over $\operatorname{Spec}k$ is $X\times_kS$ with the two
projections, so the square is cartesian. A product of schemes of finite type over $k$ is of
finite type over $k$, and morphisms between schemes of finite type over a field are
quasi-compact and quasi-separated. The morphism $b$ is smooth by hypothesis, and $v$, its base
change, is smooth as well.
