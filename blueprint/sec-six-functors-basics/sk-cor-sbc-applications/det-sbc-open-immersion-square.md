id = "det-sbc-open-immersion-square"
kind = "lemma"
title = "Base change of an open immersion along a smooth morphism"
+++
Let $j:U\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion of schemes of finite type over $k$ and let
$g:Y'\to Y$ be smooth. Put $U'=g^{-1}(U)$, let $j':U'\hookrightarrow Y'$ be the inclusion and
$u=g|_{U'}:U'\to U$. Then the square

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
U' & \xrightarrow{\ u\ } & U\\
{\scriptstyle j'}\downarrow & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle j}\\
Y' & \xrightarrow{\ g\ } & Y
\end{array}
$$

is cartesian, all its schemes and morphisms are of finite type over $k$, $j$ and $j'$ are
quasi-compact and quasi-separated, and $u$ is smooth. Hence it is a square to which smooth
base change for admissible coefficients applies with $f=j$, and the exchange map of this
square is a morphism $g^*Rj_*K\to Rj'_*u^*K$ for $K\in\Dctf(U,\Lambda)$.
