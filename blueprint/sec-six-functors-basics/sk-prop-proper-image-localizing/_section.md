For every admissible $\Lambda$, if $p\colon X\to Y$ is proper,
$Rp_*\colon\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to\D(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ restricts to an exact functor
$\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))$ ([sk-def-localizing-closure]) that preserves direct sums.
If $j\colon U\to X$ is an open immersion, $j_!\colon\D(U_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$
restricts to an exact functor $\Loc(\Dctf(U,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ that preserves direct sums
and maps $\Dctf(U,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
