title = "Functors preserving sums and generators preserve the localizing closures"
uses = ["sk-def-localizing-closure"]

Let $\mathcal T,\mathcal T'$ be triangulated categories with arbitrary direct sums, $\mathcal G\subset\mathcal T$ and
$\mathcal G'\subset\mathcal T'$ full subcategories, and $F\colon\mathcal T\to\mathcal T'$ an exact functor preserving
direct sums with $F(\mathcal G)\subset\Loc(\mathcal G')$. Then $F(\Loc(\mathcal G))\subset\Loc(\mathcal G')$, and the
restriction $\Loc(\mathcal G)\to\Loc(\mathcal G')$ is exact and preserves direct sums. In particular this applies
with $\mathcal T=\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, $\mathcal G=\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ ([sk-def-localizing-closure]).
