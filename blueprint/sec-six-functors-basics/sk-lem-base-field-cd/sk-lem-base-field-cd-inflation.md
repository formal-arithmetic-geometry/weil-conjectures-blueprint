title = "Inflation between finite cyclic groups"

For $m\geq1$ let $C_m=\langle\sigma\rangle$ be cyclic of order $m$ and
$N_m=1+\sigma+\dots+\sigma^{m-1}\in\mathbf Z[C_m]$. Let $r\geq1$, let
$\pi\colon C_{rm}\to C_m$ send a generator $\sigma'$ to $\sigma$, and let $M$ be a
$C_m$-module, regarded as a $C_{rm}$-module through $\pi$. Then the inflation map
$H^s(C_m,M)\to H^s(C_{rm},M)$ is multiplication by $r^{\lfloor s/2\rfloor}$ under
the identifications of both sides with the cohomology of the periodic complexes
$M\xrightarrow{\sigma-1}M\xrightarrow{N_m}M\xrightarrow{\sigma-1}\cdots$. In
particular, if $rM=0$, inflation is zero in all degrees $s\geq2$.
