*Reduction to finite modules.* Let $M$ be a torsion discrete
$\widehat{\mathbf Z}$-module. Each $x\in M$ has open stabilizer, hence a finite
orbit, and finite order; the subgroup generated by the orbit is a finitely
generated torsion abelian group, hence finite, and it is stable. So $M$ is the
directed union of its finite stable submodules, and by
[det-base-field-cd-cochains], part (2), it suffices to treat finite $M$.

*Finite modules.* Let $M$ be finite with exponent $e$. The kernel of the action
is open, so it is $m\widehat{\mathbf Z}$ for some $m$, and $M$ is a
$C_m$-module. The open subgroups of $\widehat{\mathbf Z}$ are the
$m'\widehat{\mathbf Z}$, so by [det-base-field-cd-cochains], part (3),
$H^s(\widehat{\mathbf Z},M)=\varinjlim_{m\mid m'}H^s(C_{m'},M)$ along inflation.
Given a class at level $m'$, its image at level $em'$ is its image under
inflation $C_{em'}\to C_{m'}$, which is zero for $s\geq2$ by
[det-base-field-cd-inflation] with $r=e$. Hence every element of the colimit
vanishes.

*Consequences.* By [det-base-field-cd-galois-group], the absolute Galois group of
$K$ is $\widehat{\mathbf Z}$. Étale sheaves on $\Spec K$ correspond to discrete
Galois modules, with sections corresponding to invariants
([lem-exc-field-discrete-modules] for finite coefficient rings; Stacks Project,
Tag 03QQ in general), and torsion sheaves to torsion modules; therefore étale
cohomology of $\Spec K$ is the derived functor of invariants, computed by
[det-base-field-cd-cochains], part (1). So $H^s(K_{\Et},F)=0$ for $s\geq2$ and all
torsion $F$, i.e. $\operatorname{cd}_{\rm tor}(K)\leq1$, and
$\operatorname{cd}_\ell(K)\leq1$ by [det-cd-etale-comparison], part (4).

If $K$ is algebraically closed, [lem-sf-separably-closed-torsion-cd] gives
$\operatorname{cd}_{\rm tor}(K)\leq0$, so $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(K)\leq0$; and
$H^0(K,\mathbf Z/\ell)=\mathbf Z/\ell\neq0$ for the classical sheaf
$\nu^*\mathbf Z/\ell$, so $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(K)=0$.
