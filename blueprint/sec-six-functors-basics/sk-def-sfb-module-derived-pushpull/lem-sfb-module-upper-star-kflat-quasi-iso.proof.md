The functor $f^*$ is exact ([sk-prop-sfb-module-pullback-exact]), and an exact
functor applied termwise commutes with the cohomology of complexes; hence it sends
every quasi-isomorphism, in particular every quasi-isomorphism between K-flat
complexes, to a quasi-isomorphism.
