Let $\mathcal T=D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ for a condensed ring $\Lambda$, and fix a filtered
diagram $(K_i^\bullet)$ of complexes of $\Lambda_X$-modules with colimit $K^\bullet$. Let
$\mathcal C$ be the class of $C\in\mathcal T$ such that
$\varinjlim_i\Hom(C,K_i[q])\to\Hom(C,K[q])$ is bijective for every $q\in\mathbf Z$. Then
$\mathcal C$ is closed under shifts, isomorphisms and extensions: if $C'\to C\to C''\to C'[1]$
is distinguished with $C',C''\in\mathcal C$, then $C\in\mathcal C$. Consequently, if every $a_!L$
lies in $\mathcal C$, so does every object obtained from them by finitely many extensions
and shifts; for $\Lambda=E,\Qlb$ this includes $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$
([det-cor-coefficient-heart-ii-generation]).
