in Section “The exceptional inverse image preserves constructibility” by factoring $f$ locally, we need its
compatibility with composition and with restriction to opens, which we
obtain from the corresponding properties of $Rf_!$.

Base change for $f^!$ does not follow in this way, and we prove it for
smooth morphisms in Proposition [sk-prop-cohsm]. That
$f^!$ preserves $\Dctf$ is proved in
Theorems [sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation] and [sk-thm-restriction].
