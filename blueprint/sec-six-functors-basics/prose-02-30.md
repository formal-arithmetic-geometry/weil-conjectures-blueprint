Proper base change only needs the comparison for bounded-below complexes,
but Brown representability takes place in the unbounded derived category, and
there we need the following extension. Over a field of finite cohomological
dimension, the étale cohomology of schemes of finite type is uniformly
bounded, so every computation in a fixed degree can be carried out on a
bounded-below truncation.
