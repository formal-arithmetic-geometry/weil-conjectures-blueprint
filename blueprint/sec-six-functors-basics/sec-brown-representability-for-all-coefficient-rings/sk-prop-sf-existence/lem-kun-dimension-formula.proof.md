This is the recorded classical input named in the statement, Stacks Project, Morphisms, Lemma 29.29.1 ([Tag 02FX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02FX)), applied to the structure morphism of the scheme of finite type over the field: the dimension of the local ring plus the transcendence degree of the residue field computes the dimension at a point. No separate proof is given.
