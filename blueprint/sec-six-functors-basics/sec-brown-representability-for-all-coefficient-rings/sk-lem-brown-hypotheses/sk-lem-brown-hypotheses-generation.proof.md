*Compactness.* For finite $A$ this is [sk-prop-ctf-finite-compact]. For $\Lambda=E,\Qlb$, $\Dctf=\Dbc$ and it is
[sk-thm-constructible-compactness]; for $\mathcal O_E$, $\Dctf=\Dbc$ and it is [sk-prop-int-compactness]. For
$\Lambda=\mathbf Z_\ell$ and $\mathbf Q_\ell$ these are the cases $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$.

*Generation.* Let $\mathcal S$ be the class of $M\in\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ with $\Hom(M[n],K)=0$ for all $n$. It is
closed under shifts, cones (long exact sequence) and sums ($\Hom(\bigoplus M_s,K)=\prod\Hom(M_s,K)$), and
contains every object isomorphic to one of $G$, i.e. $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. By minimality
([sk-def-localizing-closure]), $\mathcal S=\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))\ni K$, so $\mathrm{id}_K=0$ and $K=0$.
