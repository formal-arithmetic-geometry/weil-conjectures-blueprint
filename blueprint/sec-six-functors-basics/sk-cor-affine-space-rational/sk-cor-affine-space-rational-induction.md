Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and $p:\mathbf A^d_V\to V$ the projection. Then
$p^!\simeq p^*(d)[2d]$ as functors
$\Loc(\Dbc(V,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dbc(\mathbf A^d_V,\Lambda))$, and
$p^*(d)[2d]$ maps $\Dbc(V,\Lambda)$ into $\Dbc(\mathbf A^d_V,\Lambda)$.
