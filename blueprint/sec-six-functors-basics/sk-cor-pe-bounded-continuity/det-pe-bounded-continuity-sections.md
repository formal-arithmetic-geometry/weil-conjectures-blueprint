title = "Cohomology on a qcqs object commutes with bounded-below filtered colimits"
uses = ["det-pe-hypercover-existence", "det-pe-hypercover-complex", "det-pe-sections-limits-colimits"]
+++
Let $U$ be a quasi-compact, quasi-separated object of $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$, let $I$ be a
filtered category and $(K_i^\bullet)_{i\in I}$ a diagram of complexes of
$\mathcal R$-modules with $K_i^b=0$ for all $i$ and all $b<a$. Then for every $q$ the
canonical map $\varinjlim_iH^q(U,K_i^\bullet)\to H^q(U,\varinjlim_iK_i^\bullet)$ is an
isomorphism.
