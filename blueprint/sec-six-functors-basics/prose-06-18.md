Once we know that $f^!$ preserves $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$
(Section “The exceptional inverse image preserves constructibility”), these isomorphisms restrict to the
full subcategories $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$, and the same statements hold for
the adjunction between $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ and $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$.
