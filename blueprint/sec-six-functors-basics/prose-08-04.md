Since [sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit]
and [sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity] are proved before
the Ext bound and Brown representability, [sk-prop-qlbar-two-colimit]
may be used in the proof of [sk-thm-existence]. From now on we may
therefore pass freely between $\Dctf(X,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$ and $\Dctf(X,E)$ for $E$
large enough.
