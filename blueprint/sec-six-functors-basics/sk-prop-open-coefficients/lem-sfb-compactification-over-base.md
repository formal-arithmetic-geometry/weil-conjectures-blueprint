Let $S$ be a quasi-compact and quasi-separated scheme. Let $X$ be an $S$-scheme that is separated
and of finite type over $S$, and let $Y$ be an $S$-scheme that is separated and quasi-compact
over $S$. Then every $S$-morphism $f\colon X\to Y$ has a compactification
([sk-def-nagata-sketch-data]).
