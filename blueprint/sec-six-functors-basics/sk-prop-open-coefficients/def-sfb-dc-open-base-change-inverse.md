For a cartesian square as in [thm-sfb-dc-open-base-change-iso], the *extension-by-zero base
change isomorphism*

$$
Lg^*\circ j_!\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }j'_!\circ Lg'^*
$$

is the inverse of the exchange morphism $\mathrm{Ex}^{j}_{sq}$, which is invertible by
[thm-sfb-dc-open-base-change-iso].
