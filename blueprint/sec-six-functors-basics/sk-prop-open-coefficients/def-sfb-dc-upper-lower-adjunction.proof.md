The ambient derived adjunction $Lf^*\dashv Rf_*$ ([det-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull]) gives
bijections
$\Hom(Lf^*K,L)\cong\Hom(K,Rf_*L)$ in the ambient categories. For $K$ and $L$ bounded
constructible, both sides are Hom-sets of the full subcategories $D_{\mathrm{cons}}$, because the
restricted functors commute with the inclusions. Naturality is inherited.
