The square is cartesian by [det-def-sfb-shriek-base-change-morphism-compactification] and $p$ is
proper. For $\Lambda=\underline E,\underline{\Qlb}$ the category $D_{\mathrm{cons}}$ is $\Dbc=\Dctf$, and
proper base change ([sk-thm-sfb-proper-base-change], in the form
[det-thm-sfb-proper-base-change-rational], part 3) says that the base change map
$Lg^*Rp_*K\to Rp'_*L\bar g^*K$ is an isomorphism for every $K\in D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Z,\Lambda)$. The base
change map there is the one of [det-def-sfb-base-change-morphism], whose detailed construction
[det-def-sfb-base-change-morphism-bc] computes it on $D_{\mathrm{cons}}$ from the restricted functors.
Since the restricted functors and adjunctions are restrictions of the ambient ones along the fully
faithful inclusions, the two maps agree, and an ambient isomorphism between objects of the full
subcategory $D_{\mathrm{cons}}$ is an isomorphism there. Thus every component of $\mathrm{BC}$ is
invertible, so $\mathrm{BC}$ is a natural isomorphism. The components of $\mathrm{BC}_{i_!}$ are the
components $\mathrm{BC}_{i_!K}$, which are invertible.
