Let $\Lambda$ be one of the $\ell$-adic condensed rings $\underline{\cO}_E$, $\underline E$
($E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ finite; [det-def-sfb-condensed-ring]) or $\underline{\Qlb}$
([det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed]), and let $f\colon X\to Y$ be a morphism of the setting of
[det-def-sfb-dc-setting]. If $K\in D(\Mod_{\Lambda_X})$ is bounded constructible
([det-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]), then so is $Rf_*K\in D(\Mod_{\Lambda_Y})$
([det-def-sfb-module-derived-pushpull]).
