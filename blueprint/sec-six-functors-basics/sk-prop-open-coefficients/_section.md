Let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be a quasi-compact open immersion. Then

$$
Rj_*\colon D(U_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)
$$

maps $\Dbc(U,\Lambda)$ into $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ for $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, $E$
and $\Qlb$.
