The points of $\operatorname{Spec}A^w$ are pairs $(x,y)$ of primes of $A$
with $y\subset x$; their local rings are $A_y$. Its connected components are
the fibres over $x$, each isomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec}A_x$. Its closed
points $(x,x)$ form a closed subset identified with $\operatorname{Spec}A$ in
the patch topology. In particular, $A\to A^w$ is faithfully flat and
$\operatorname{Spec}A^w$ is w-local [def-weil-pbc-w-local].
