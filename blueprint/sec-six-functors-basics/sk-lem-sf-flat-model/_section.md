Let $A$ be a finite commutative ring annihilated by a power of $\ell$ and $X$
a scheme of finite type over the standing field. Every $C\in\Dctf(X,A)$ on
$X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ is represented by a bounded complex of flat constructible
$A$-sheaves. A flat constructible sheaf has a finite stratification on which
it is locally constant with finite projective $A$-module local models.
Consequently $\Dctf(X,A)$ is generated, using shifts, finite extensions and
direct summands, by sheaves $a_!L$, where $a:S\hookrightarrow X$ is locally
closed and $L$ is locally constant with finite projective local models.
