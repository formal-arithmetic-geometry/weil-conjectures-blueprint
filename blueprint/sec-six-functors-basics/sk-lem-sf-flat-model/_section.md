Let $A$ be a finite admissible ring, that is, a finite commutative ring
annihilated by a power of $\ell$. Every $C\in\Dctf(X,A)$
([sk-def-sfb-constructible]) on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ is represented by a
bounded complex of flat constructible $A$-sheaves. A flat constructible sheaf
has a finite stratification on whose strata it is locally of the form
$\underline M$ with $M$ a finite projective $A$-module. Consequently,
$\Dctf(X,A)$ is generated, using shifts, finite extensions and direct
summands, by sheaves $a_!L$, where $a:S\hookrightarrow X$ is locally closed
and $L$ is locally of the form $\underline M$ with $M$ a finite projective
$A$-module.
