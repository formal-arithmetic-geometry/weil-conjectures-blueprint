Let $A$ be a finite commutative ring annihilated by a power of $\ell$ and $X$
a scheme of finite type over the standing field. Every $C\in\Dctf(X,A)$ on
$X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ is represented by a bounded complex of flat constructible
$A$-sheaves. A flat constructible sheaf has a finite stratification on which
it is locally constant with finite projective $A$-module local models.
Consequently $\Dctf(X,A)$ is generated, using shifts, finite extensions and
direct summands, by sheaves $a_!L$, where $a:S\hookrightarrow X$ is locally
closed and $L$ is locally constant with finite projective local models.

*Complement (generation over a Noetherian base).* More generally, let $X$ be a
Noetherian scheme and $\Lambda$ a finite commutative ring. Every object of
$\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ lies in the thick subcategory of $D(X,\Lambda)$ generated by
the objects $Rg_*j_!L$, where $U\subset X$ is a locally closed stratum of a
stratification of $X$, $j:U\hookrightarrow\overline U$ is the open immersion
into its closure, $g:\overline U\hookrightarrow X$ is the closed immersion of
the closure, and $L$ is a lisse (finite locally constant) sheaf of
$\Lambda$-modules on $U$ with flat stalks. That is, every property of objects
of $D(X,\Lambda)$ that is triangulated (contains $0$ and is stable under shifts
and under extensions in distinguished triangles), stable under retracts and
under isomorphisms, and holds for all these generators holds for every object
of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
