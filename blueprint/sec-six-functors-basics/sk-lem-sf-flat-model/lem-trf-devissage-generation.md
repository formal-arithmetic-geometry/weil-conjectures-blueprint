Let $X$ be a separated finite-type scheme over $k$ and $A$ a
finite coefficient ring as in [def-trf-coefficient-conventions].
The triangulated category $D^b_{ctf}(X,A)$ is the thick subcategory of
$D(X,A)$ generated by the objects

$$
Rg_*j_!L ,
$$

where $U$ is a locally closed stratum of $X$,
$j:U\hookrightarrow\overline U$ is the open immersion into its closure,
$g:\overline U\hookrightarrow X$ is the closed immersion, and $L$ is a lisse
sheaf of $A$-modules on $U$ with flat stalks. Constant coefficients
$L=A_U$ do not suffice in general.
