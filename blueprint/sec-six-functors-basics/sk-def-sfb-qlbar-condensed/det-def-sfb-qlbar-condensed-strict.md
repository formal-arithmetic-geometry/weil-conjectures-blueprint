title = "$\\underline{\\overline{\\mathbf Q}}_\\ell$ is strictly smaller than $C(-,\\overline{\\mathbf Q}_\\ell)$"
uses = ["det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-definition"]
+++
Let $S=\{0\}\cup\{1/n:n\ge1\}\subseteq\mathbf R$, a convergent sequence with its limit, which is a
profinite set. For each $n\ge1$ choose $\varpi_n\in\Qlb$ with $\varpi_n^n=\ell$, and define
$a\colon S\to\Qlb$ by $a(1/n)=\ell^n\varpi_n$ and $a(0)=0$. Then $a\in C(S,\Qlb)$ but
$a\notin\underline{\Qlb}(S)$. In particular the inclusion
$\underline{\Qlb}\subseteq C(-,\Qlb)$ of [det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-definition] is not an equality.
