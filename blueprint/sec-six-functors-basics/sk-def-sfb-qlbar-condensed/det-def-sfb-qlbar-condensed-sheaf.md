The presheaf of rings $\underline{\Qlb}$ of [det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-definition] is a sheaf on
$*_{\mathrm{proét}}$, i.e. a condensed ring ([sk-def-sfb-condensed-ring]); no sheafification is
needed. Its underlying abelian sheaf is a commutative ring object of $\Sh(*_{\mathrm{proét}},\Ab)$
([det-def-sfb-ring-object-sheaf]), and the inclusion $\underline{\Qlb}\to C(-,\Qlb)$ is a morphism
of condensed rings.
