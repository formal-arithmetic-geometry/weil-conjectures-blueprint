For a compact Hausdorff space $S$, *extremal disconnectedness* means that the closure of
every open subset is open. It is equivalent to projectivity with respect to continuous
surjections of compact Hausdorff spaces. Every profinite space $T$ admits a continuous
surjection $S\to T$ with $S$ compact Hausdorff and extremally disconnected; such $S$ is
also profinite.
