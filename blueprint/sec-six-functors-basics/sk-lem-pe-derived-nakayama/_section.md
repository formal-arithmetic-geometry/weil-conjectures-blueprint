The finite quotient coefficient has the perfect resolution
$[\mathcal O_{E,X}\xrightarrow{\pi_E^n}\mathcal O_{E,X}]$, so $K_n$ is the cone of
$\pi_E^n\colon K\to K$. If a derived-complete complex has $K_1=0$, it is zero.
Consequently a morphism between derived-complete complexes is an isomorphism
whenever its first derived reduction is an isomorphism.
