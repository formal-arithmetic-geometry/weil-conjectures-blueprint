1. $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ is a triangulated subcategory of
   $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$, its objects are bounded, and it is
   stable under the standard truncations. The category $\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E)$
   of constructible $\mathcal O_{E,X}$-modules ([sk-def-sfb-constructible])
   consists of the objects of $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ concentrated in degree
   $0$, and is an essentially small abelian subcategory of
   $\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ closed under extensions.
2. For $C\in\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E)$ there is a finite stratification of $X$
   such that on each stratum $C$ is pro-étale locally the completion of a
   finitely generated $\mathcal O_E$-module, possibly with torsion.
3. On each such stratum, $C$ is dualizable in the derived category, of Tor
   amplitude in $[-1,0]$, and its dual has Tor amplitude in $[0,1]$.
