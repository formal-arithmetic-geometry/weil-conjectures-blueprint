1. $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ is a triangulated subcategory of
   $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ whose objects are bounded, stable under
   the standard truncations.
2. $\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E)$, the objects of $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ concentrated
   in degree $0$, is an abelian subcategory of
   $\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$: kernels and cokernels of morphisms
   in it, computed in $\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$, lie in it. It is
   closed under extensions and essentially small.
