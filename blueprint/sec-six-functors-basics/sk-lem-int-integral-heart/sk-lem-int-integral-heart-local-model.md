Let $K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$.

1. There is a finite stratification of $X$ such that on each stratum $S$,
   pro-étale locally on $S$, $K|_S$ is the completion
   $\widehat{\underline{M^\bullet}}=M^\bullet\otimes_{\mathcal O_E}\mathcal O_{E,S}$
   of a bounded complex $M^\bullet$ of finite free $\mathcal O_E$-modules, with
   differentials given by matrices over $\mathcal O_E$.
2. Each cohomology sheaf $H^q(K)$ is derived complete, lies in
   $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, and on each stratum of (1) is pro-étale locally the
   completion of the finitely generated $\mathcal O_E$-module $H^q(M^\bullet)$.
3. Every truncation $\tau_{\le q}K$, $\tau_{\ge q}K$ lies in
   $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$.
