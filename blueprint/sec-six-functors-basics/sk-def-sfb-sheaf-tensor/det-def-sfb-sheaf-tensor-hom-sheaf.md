title = "The internal hom into a sheaf is a sheaf"
uses = ["det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf"]
+++
In the situation of [det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-presheaf], let $F$ be a presheaf and $G$ a sheaf
on $C$ with values in $\mathcal A$. Then the internal hom $[F,G]$ is a sheaf.
