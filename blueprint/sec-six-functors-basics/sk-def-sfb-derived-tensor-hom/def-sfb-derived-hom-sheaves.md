In the situation of [def-sfb-derived-tensor-sheaves], the *derived internal hom*
$$
\RHom(-,-)\colon D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)^{\mathrm{op}}\times D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)\to D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)
$$
is the internal hom of the closed monoidal structure on
$(D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A),\otimes^{\mathrm L})$ [lem-sfb-derived-monoidal-closed].
