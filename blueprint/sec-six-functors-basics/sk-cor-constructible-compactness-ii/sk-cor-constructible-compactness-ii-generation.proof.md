Let $\mathcal S$ be the full subcategory of objects $A\in\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ with
$\Hom(A[n],K)=0$ for all $n$. It is closed under shifts; it is closed under cones, by the long exact
sequence of $\Hom(-,K)$; and it is closed under direct sums, since
$\Hom(\bigoplus A_s[n],K)=\prod\Hom(A_s[n],K)$. So $\mathcal S$ is localizing. It contains $G$, hence every
object isomorphic to one in $G$, i.e. all of $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$; by minimality of the localizing closure
([sk-def-localizing-closure]), $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))\subset\mathcal S$. Since $K\in\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$,
$\Hom(K,K)=0$, so $\mathrm{id}_K=0$ and $K=0$.

The objects of $G$ are compact in $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$ by [sk-thm-constructible-compactness]; together
with the vanishing just proved, $G$ is a set of compact generators.
