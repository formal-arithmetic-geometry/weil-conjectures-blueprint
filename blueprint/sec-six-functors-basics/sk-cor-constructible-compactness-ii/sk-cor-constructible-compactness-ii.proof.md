Let $G$ be a small skeleton of $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$; its objects are compact by
[sk-thm-constructible-compactness]. It remains to see that they detect zero objects. Suppose
$\Hom(C[n],K)=0$ for all $C\in G$ and all $n$. The objects $A$ with $\Hom(A[n],K)=0$ for all $n$ form a
localizing subcategory containing $G$, hence all of $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$. Taking $A=K$ gives
$\mathrm{id}_K=0$, so $K=0$ ([det-constructible-compactness-ii-generation]).
