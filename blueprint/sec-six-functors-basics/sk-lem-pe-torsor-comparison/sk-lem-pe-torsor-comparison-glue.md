title = "Gluing the étale models of a torsor"
uses = ["det-pe-torsor-comparison-affine", "det-pe-classical-sheaves-unit", "det-pe-classical-sheaves-image"]
+++
Let $X$ be a scheme, $G_0$ a sheaf of groups on $X_{\Et}$ and $T$ a
$\nu_X^*G_0$-torsor on $X_{\Proet}$. Then there is a $G_0$-torsor $T_0$ on $X_{\Et}$
and an isomorphism of torsors $\nu_X^*T_0\cong T$, unique up to unique
isomorphism. In particular $T$ is classical and has a trivializing étale
covering.
