Let $\{S_i\to S\}_{i\in I}$ be a finite jointly surjective family of profinite
sets. Then $\{\Spec LC(S_i,\Z)\to\Spec LC(S,\Z)\}_{i\in I}$ is a covering in
$(\Spec\Z)_{\mathrm{proét}}$ ([def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]). Since the
coherent topology is generated by such families, $u$
([def-sfb-profinite-to-proetale]) is cover preserving.
