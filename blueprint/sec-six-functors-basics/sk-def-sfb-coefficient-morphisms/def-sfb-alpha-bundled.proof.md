Applying monoid objects is compatible with composites of monoidal functors.
A monoidal natural isomorphism of monoidal functors induces a natural
isomorphism of the induced functors on monoid objects. The natural
isomorphism is monoidal by [lem-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-monoidal].
