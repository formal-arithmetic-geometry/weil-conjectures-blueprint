Give $f^*\Lambda_Y$ the ring object structure transported along the strong
monoidal functor $f^*$ ([sk-def-sfb-finite-pushforward-pullback]). Then
$\alpha_f\colon f^*\Lambda_Y\to\Lambda_X$ ([def-sfb-alpha]) is a morphism of
ring objects.
