Give $f_*\Lambda_X$ the ring object structure induced by the lax monoidal
functor $f_*$ ([sk-def-sfb-finite-pushforward-pullback]). Then
$\eta_f\colon\Lambda_Y\to f_*\Lambda_X$ ([def-sfb-eta]) is a morphism of ring
objects.
