The ring structures of $f^*\Lambda_Y=(f^*\circ p_Y^*)\Lambda$ and of
$\Lambda_X=p_X^*\Lambda$ are those of $\Lambda$, transported along the strong
monoidal functors $f^*\circ p_Y^*$ and $p_X^*$. The component at a ring object
of a monoidal natural transformation between monoidal functors is a morphism
of the transported ring objects. The transformation is monoidal by
[lem-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp-monoidal].
