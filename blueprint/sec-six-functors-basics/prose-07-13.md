For $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, $E$ and $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we have $\Dctf=\Dbc$, and we
show that $f^!$ preserves $\Dbc$, and for $\mathcal O_E$ we also check the
compactness hypotheses of [sk-prop-sf-existence]. The argument of
Section “Finite coefficients: finite Tor amplitude and the six operations” needs two geometric facts, the
finiteness of $Rj_*$ for an open immersion and the adjunction for the
projective line, and we deduce both from torsion coefficients. An integral
complex lies in $\Dbc$ if it is derived complete with constructible
reduction modulo $\pi_E$ ([sk-prop-integral-criterion]), and
rational complexes are built from integral ones
([sk-lem-integral-models], part (4)).
