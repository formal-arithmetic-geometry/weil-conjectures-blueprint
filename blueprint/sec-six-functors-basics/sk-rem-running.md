We use the same few schemes throughout:
$$
\{0\}\xrightarrow{\ i\ }\mathbf A^1_k\xleftarrow{\ j\ }\mathbf G_{m,k},
\qquad \mathbf A^1_k\xrightarrow{\ j_\infty\ }\mathbf P^1_k .
$$
The sheaf $j_!\Lambda_{\mathbf G_m}$ is constructible for the stratification
$\mathbf A^1=\mathbf G_m\sqcup\{0\}$. Its stalk is $\Lambda$ at a geometric point of
$\mathbf G_m$ and $0$ at the origin. It is not lisse on $\mathbf A^1$, and the sequence of
part (3) of [sk-lem-coefficient-heart] is the localization sequence
$$
0\to j_!\Lambda_{\mathbf G_m}\to\Lambda_{\mathbf A^1}\to i_*\Lambda\to0 .
$$
We return to this example for compact supports (after [sk-lem-geo-p1-cohomology], in
[sk-rem-compact-supports-running-example]), for $i^!$ ([sk-rem-closed-support]) and for the
trace (in the subsection on base change, composition and the trace of smooth purity).
