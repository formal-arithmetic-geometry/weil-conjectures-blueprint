We use the same few schemes throughout:
$$
\{0\}\xrightarrow{\ i\ }\mathbf A^1_k\xleftarrow{\ u\ }\mathbf G_{m,k},
\qquad \mathbf A^1_k\xrightarrow{\ j\ }\mathbf P^1_k .
$$
The sheaf $u_!\Lambda_{\mathbf G_m}$ is constructible for the stratification
$\mathbf A^1=\mathbf G_m\sqcup\{0\}$. Its stalk is $\Lambda$ at a geometric point of
$\mathbf G_m$ and $0$ at the origin. It is not lisse on $\mathbf A^1$, and the sequence of
part (3) of the lemma on constructible sheaves (the filtration by extensions by zero of
lisse sheaves) is the localization sequence
$$
0\to u_!\Lambda_{\mathbf G_m}\to\Lambda_{\mathbf A^1}\to i_*\Lambda\to0 .
$$
The example returns for compact supports (after the computation of the cohomology of the
projective line and its trace), for $i^!$ (the example of sections with closed support) and
for the trace (in the section on the trace map of smooth purity).
