Both $E$ and $F$ carry the topology restricted from $\Qlb$, so the inclusion
$E\subseteq F$ is a continuous ring homomorphism. Then
[def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring-map] gives a morphism of condensed rings, hence of
ring objects. It also gives the functoriality, since the inclusion
$E\subseteq E$ is $\mathrm{id}_E$ and $(F\subseteq H)\circ(E\subseteq F)=(E\subseteq H)$.
