The exceptional inverse image $f^!$ is the right adjoint of $Rf_!$. It is
the étale analogue of the twisted inverse image of Grothendieck duality
for coherent sheaves, and for a smooth $f$ of relative dimension $d$ it will
turn out to be $f^*(d)[2d]$. We need it for Poincaré duality, where
the dualizing functor is $\cHom(-,a^!\Lambda)$ for the structure map $a$,
and for the local terms of the trace formula. In this section we construct
$f^!:\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ by Brown
representability, which proves parts (1)–(3) of Theorem [sk-thm-existence].
The next section, Section “The exceptional inverse image preserves constructibility”, shows by a geometric
argument that $f^!$ preserves $\Dctf$. Recall that Brown representability gives a right adjoint to every
triangulated functor that preserves direct sums, out of a category
generated by a set of compact objects. We therefore have to show that the
objects of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ are compact in $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$, and
that $Rf_!=Rp_*\circ j_!$, for a compactification $f=p\circ j$, preserves
direct sums and $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$.

The proofs of the two finiteness statements depend on the coefficients. For
$\Lambda=E,\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ we have $\Dctf=\Dbc$, so we can argue by dévissage along
cohomology sheaves. This case is treated in the subsections below, up to
Lemma [sk-lem-proper-unbounded-window]. For finite rings, the
estimates are proved in Section “Compactness and proper direct image for torsion coefficients” using flat
constructible models, and for $\mathcal O_E$ they are
Proposition [sk-prop-int-compactness]. The
construction of $Rf_!$ and of $f^!$ that follows is then the same for
every admissible $\Lambda$ (Theorem [sk-prop-sf-existence]).

We begin with the first hypothesis of Brown representability, compactness,
for $\Lambda=E$ or $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. This $\Lambda$ is fixed until the end of
Section “Compactness of constructible complexes”. Since $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)=\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$, the
bounded constructible complexes are the proposed compact generators, and
we may use truncations and the constructible heart, which is abelian by
Lemma [sk-lem-coefficient-heart].
Without decoration, $\Hom$ and $\operatorname{Ext}$ are taken in $D(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\Lambda)$, also when both
arguments are constructible sheaves. A subscript indicates the coefficient
ring where it changes, as in the lattice arguments over $\mathcal O_E$.

For a constructible sheaf $C$ and a family $(K_\lambda)$ of objects of
$\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$, we have to show that
$\bigoplus_\lambda\Hom(C,K_\lambda)\to\Hom(C,\bigoplus_\lambda K_\lambda)$
is bijective. The case of bounded constructible complexes then follows by
dévissage. We use two facts. First, $\operatorname{Ext}^q(C,-)$ commutes with filtered colimits of
complexes that vanish below a fixed degree, in particular of sheaves
(Lemma [sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity], proved in
Section “Continuity of cohomology and of Ext”). Second, $\operatorname{Ext}^q(C,N)=0$ for
$q>d_C$, with $d_C$ independent of the constructible sheaf $N$
(Lemma [sk-lem-uniform-constructible-ext] below). The second fact is needed
for unbounded targets, whose summands $K_\lambda$ may have cohomology in
unboundedly many degrees.

The bound $d_C$ on $\operatorname{Ext}^q(C,-)$ is a sum of the cohomological
dimensions $\operatorname{cd}_\ell$ of Definition [sk-def-cd]. In the
proof we compute $R\operatorname{Hom}(a_!L_0,K)$, for a derived complete
$K\in D(X_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}},\mathcal O_E)$, as a derived inverse limit over the
reductions modulo $\pi_E^n$. For this, the functors in
the formula in [sk-lem-formal-closed-support] have to commute with derived inverse limits, and
we use the next lemma with $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$.
