Let $\Lambda$ be admissible and $f:X\to Y$ separated of finite type. On the
full subcategories $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$:

1. $f^*\dashv Rf_*$, as the restriction of the adjunction on
   $D(-_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$;
2. $-\otimes^{\mathbf L}K\dashv\cHom(K,-)$ for $K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$, as the
   restriction of the tensor–Hom adjunction on $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$;
3. $Rf_!\dashv f^!$, as the restriction of the adjunction on
   $\Loc(\Dctf(-,\Lambda))$ of [sk-prop-sf-existence].
