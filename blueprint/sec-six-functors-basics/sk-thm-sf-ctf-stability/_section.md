For every admissible $\Lambda\in\{A,\Zl,\mathcal O_E,\Ql,E,\Qlb\}$, the
operations $f^*$, $Rf_*$, $Rf_!$, $f^!$, $\otimes^{\mathbf L}$ and $\cHom$
preserve $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$ for morphisms of the standing schemes. Their
adjunctions are the restrictions of the adjunctions $f^*\dashv Rf_*$ and
$-\otimes^{\mathbf L}K\dashv\cHom(K,-)$ on $D(-_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ or of
the adjunction $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ on $\Loc(\Dctf(-,\Lambda))$
([sk-prop-sf-existence]).

For integral and rational coefficients these categories are the full
categories $\Dbc$, so the proposition concerns all bounded constructible
complexes in those cases.
