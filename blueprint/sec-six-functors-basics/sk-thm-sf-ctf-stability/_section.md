For every admissible $\Lambda\in\{A,\Zl,\mathcal O_E,\Ql,E,\Qlb\}$, the
operations $f^*$, $Rf_*$, $Rf_!$, $f^!$, $\otimes^{\mathbf L}$ and $\cHom$
preserve $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$ ([sk-def-sfb-constructible]) for morphisms of
schemes of finite type over $k$. Their adjunctions are the restrictions of the
adjunctions $f^*\dashv Rf_*$ and $-\otimes^{\mathbf L}K\dashv\cHom(K,-)$ on
$D(-_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ or of the adjunction $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ on
$\Loc(\Dctf(-,\Lambda))$ ([sk-prop-sf-existence]).
