Let $\Lambda\in\{A,\Zl,\mathcal O_E,\Ql,E,\Qlb\}$ be admissible and let
$f:X\to Y$ be a separated morphism of finite type of the standing schemes.

1. $f^*$ maps $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$, and
   $\otimes^{\mathbf L}_\Lambda$ maps $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\times\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$
   into $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
2. For a locally closed immersion $a$ (in particular a quasi-compact open
   immersion), $a_!$ preserves $\Dctf$.
3. $Rf_*$ and $Rf_!$ map $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$.
4. $f^!$ maps $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$.
