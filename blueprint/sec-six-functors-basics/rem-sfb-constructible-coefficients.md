The definitions of [sk-def-sfb-constructible] and [sk-def-sfb-bounded-constructible] are
made for an arbitrary ring object $\mathcal R$, so they apply directly to the coefficient
rings $\Lambda_X=p_X^*\Lambda$ of [sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients]. For
$\mathcal R=\cO_{E,X}$ resp. $E_X$ they give the constructible $\cO_E$- resp. $E$-sheaves
and the categories $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\cO_E)$ resp.
$D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X_{\mathrm{proét}},E)$. These play the role of the constructible
$\ell$-adic sheaves of Bhatt–Scholze, §6.8, which are lisse on the strata.

Restriction to a stratum $g\colon X_a\to X$ produces $g^*\Lambda_X$-modules rather than
$\Lambda_{X_a}$-modules. The two are identified by the isomorphism
$\alpha_g\colon g^*\Lambda_X\cong\Lambda_{X_a}$ of [sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms], which
holds for every morphism $g$ and every commutative condensed ring $\Lambda$
[sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients].
