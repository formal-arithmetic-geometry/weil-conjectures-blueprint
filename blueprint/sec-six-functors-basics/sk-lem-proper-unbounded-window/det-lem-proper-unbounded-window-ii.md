Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$. For $p$ proper, $Rp_*\colon\Dic(X,\Lambda)\to\Dic(Y,\Lambda)$
([det-proper-unbounded-window-arbitrary]) preserves arbitrary direct sums.
