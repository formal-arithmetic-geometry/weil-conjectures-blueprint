Let $\Lambda$, $p$, $d$ be as in [det-proper-unbounded-window-bounded-below] and $K\in\Dic(X,\Lambda)$
arbitrary. For each $q$ there is $N$ such that for $n\geq N$ the map
$\mathcal H^q(Rp_*K)\to\mathcal H^q(Rp_*\tau_{\geq-n}K)$ is an isomorphism. Consequently
$Rp_*K\in\Dic(Y,\Lambda)$, $Rp_*\Dic^{\leq b}(X,\Lambda)\subset\Dic^{\leq b+2d+1}(Y,\Lambda)$ and
$Rp_*\Dic^{\geq a}(X,\Lambda)\subset\Dic^{\geq a}(Y,\Lambda)$.
