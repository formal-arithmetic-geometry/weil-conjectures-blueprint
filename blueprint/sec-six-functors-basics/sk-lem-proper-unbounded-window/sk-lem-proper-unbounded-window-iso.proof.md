*First map.* Apply $Rp_*$ to $\tau_{\leq q}K\to K\to\tau_{\geq q+1}K\to$. By
[det-proper-unbounded-window-arbitrary], $Rp_*\tau_{\geq q+1}K\in\Dic^{\geq q+1}(Y,\Lambda)$, so
$\mathcal H^{q-1}$ and $\mathcal H^q$ of it vanish, and the long exact sequence gives
$\mathcal H^q(Rp_*\tau_{\leq q}K)\cong\mathcal H^q(Rp_*K)$.

*Second map.* Apply $Rp_*$ to $\tau_{\leq q-2d-2}K\to\tau_{\leq q}K\to\tau_{\geq q-2d-1}\tau_{\leq q}K\to$.
By [det-proper-unbounded-window-arbitrary], $Rp_*\tau_{\leq q-2d-2}K\in\Dic^{\leq q-1}(Y,\Lambda)$, so its
$\mathcal H^q$ and $\mathcal H^{q+1}$ vanish, and the long exact sequence gives
$\mathcal H^q(Rp_*\tau_{\leq q}K)\cong\mathcal H^q(Rp_*\tau_{\geq q-2d-1}\tau_{\leq q}K)$.
