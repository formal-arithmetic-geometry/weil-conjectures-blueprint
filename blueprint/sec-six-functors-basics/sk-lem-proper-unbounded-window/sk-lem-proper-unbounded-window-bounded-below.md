Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $p\colon X\to Y$ proper with geometric fibres of dimension $\leq d$, and
$K\in\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ bounded below. Then $\mathcal H^n(Rp_*K)\in\IndCons(Y,\Lambda)$ for all $n$; if $K$
has cohomology in degrees $\geq a$ then so does $Rp_*K$; and if $K$ has cohomology in degrees $\leq b$
then $Rp_*K$ has cohomology in degrees $\leq b+2d+1$.
