$\Sh(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)$ is Grothendieck abelian, so K-injective resolutions
exist and $Rg_*$, $R^+g_*$ are defined. Under the monoidal hypotheses the sheaf categories
have functorial K-flat resolutions, so $Lg^*$ is defined. Item 1 is
[def-sfb-finite-derived-pullback-iso]. Item 2 is [def-sfb-finite-derived-adjunction] and
[def-sfb-finite-derived-adjunction-plus]. Item 3 is
[def-sfb-finite-derived-extension-by-zero-adjunction] and
[def-sfb-finite-derived-extension-by-zero-adjunction-plus].
