Let $S$ be integral, and let $j:X\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion of
$S$-schemes. For a constructible $\Fell$-sheaf $F$ on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$,
there is a nonempty open $S_0\subset S$ such that

$$
Rj_{S_0,*}(F|_{X_{S_0}})\in\Dbc(Y_{S_0},\Fell),
\qquad\text{(G21)}
$$

and such that for every $k$-scheme $T\to S_0$ of finite type, with base change
$j_T:X_T\hookrightarrow Y_T$, the canonical base change map

$$
(Rj_{S_0,*}F)|_{Y_T}\longrightarrow Rj_{T,*}(F|_{X_T})
\qquad\text{(G22)}
$$

is an isomorphism.
