The restricted $i_!$ and $Rp_*$ commute with the inclusions
([sk-def-sfb-dc-restricted-functors]): $\iota\circ i_!\cong D(i_!)\circ\iota$ and
$\iota\circ Rp_*\cong R(p_*)\circ\iota$. Pasting the two isomorphisms gives

$$
\iota\circ Rp_*\circ i_!\cong R(p_*)\circ\iota\circ i_!\cong R(p_*)\circ D(i_!)\circ\iota ,
$$

which is the asserted compatibility with the ambient functor of
[sk-def-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek]. The statement for $Rf_!$ is the case $c=c_f$.
