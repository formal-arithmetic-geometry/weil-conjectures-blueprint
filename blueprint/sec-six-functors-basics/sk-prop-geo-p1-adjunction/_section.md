Let $S$ be of finite type over $k$, and let $p:\mathbf P^1_S\to S$ be the
projection. The functors
$Rp_*:D_{\mathrm{class}}((\mathbf P^1_S)_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$
and
$p^*(1)[2]:D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to D_{\mathrm{class}}((\mathbf P^1_S)_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$
form an adjunction

$$
Rp_* \ \dashv\ p^*(1)[2],
\qquad\text{(G4)}
$$

with unit (G6) and counit (G7) below. For every morphism $g:S'\to S$ with $S'$
of finite type over $k$, the unit and counit are compatible with base change
along $g$. They are also compatible with change of finite coefficients.
