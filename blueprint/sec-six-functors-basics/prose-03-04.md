Proposition [sk-thm-sfb-rationalization-comparison] fails without a finiteness hypothesis
on $X$: on $\bigsqcup_{n\ge1}\operatorname{Spec}k$ the sheaf equal to
$\mathcal O_E/\ell^n$ on the $n$-th point is killed by inverting $\ell$
but by no power of $\ell$.
