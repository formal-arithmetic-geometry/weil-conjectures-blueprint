By [lem-sfb-coefficient-map-comp] the composite is the coefficient change of the composite
$\underline E\to\underline F\to\underline{\Qlb}$, which is the inclusion
$\underline E\to\underline{\Qlb}$: both send a continuous map $h\colon S\to E$ to $h$ regarded as
a map to $\Qlb$ ([sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-point]).
