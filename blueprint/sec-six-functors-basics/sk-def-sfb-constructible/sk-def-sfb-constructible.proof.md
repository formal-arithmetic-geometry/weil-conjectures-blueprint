The three notions are [def-sfb-finitely-presented-module],
[def-sfb-locally-finitely-presented] and [def-sfb-constructible-module]. Restriction along
$g$ is defined because $g^*$ is lax monoidal (indeed strong monoidal)
[sk-def-sfb-finite-pushforward-pullback], so $\operatorname{mapMod}(g^*)$ of
[sk-def-sfb-transport] applies. Cokernels and finite direct sums of modules exist because
module categories over ring objects of $\Sh(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)$ are abelian
[sk-prop-sfb-module-category].
