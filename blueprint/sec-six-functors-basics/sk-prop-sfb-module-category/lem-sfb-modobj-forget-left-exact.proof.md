Both categories are abelian ([lem-sfb-modobj-abelian]). The forgetful functor is
additive, and it preserves kernels by [lem-sfb-modobj-kernels]. An additive functor between
abelian categories that preserves kernels preserves finite limits, since finite limits are
built from finite biproducts and kernels.
