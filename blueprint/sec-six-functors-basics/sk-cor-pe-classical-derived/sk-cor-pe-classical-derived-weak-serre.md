+++
id = "det-pe-classical-derived-weak-serre"
kind = "lemma"
title = "Classical module sheaves form a weak Serre subcategory"
uses = ["det-pe-classical-sheaves-modules", "sk-prop-sf-comparison"]
+++
Let $X$ be a scheme and $\Lambda$ a discrete commutative ring. The full
subcategory of classical sheaves in $\Mod(X_{\Proet},\Lambda_X)$ contains $0$
and is closed under kernels, cokernels and extensions: if $f\colon G\to G'$ is a
map of classical module sheaves, then $\ker f$ and $\operatorname{coker}f$ are
classical, and if $0\to G'\to E\to G\to0$ is exact with $G,G'$ classical, then
$E$ is classical.
