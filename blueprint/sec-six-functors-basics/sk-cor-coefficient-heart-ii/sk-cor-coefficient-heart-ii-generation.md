Let $X$ be a Noetherian scheme and $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$. Let $\mathcal G$ be the class of
objects $a_!L[n]$ of $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, with $a\colon S\hookrightarrow X$ a locally
closed immersion, $L$ a lisse $\Lambda_S$-module and $n\in\mathbf Z$. Every
$K\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is obtained from objects of $\mathcal G$ by finitely many cones: there is
a finite sequence $K_1,\dots,K_N=K$ such that each $K_r$ is in $\mathcal G$ or is isomorphic to
the cone of a morphism between earlier members (up to shift). Conversely every such object
lies in $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$.
