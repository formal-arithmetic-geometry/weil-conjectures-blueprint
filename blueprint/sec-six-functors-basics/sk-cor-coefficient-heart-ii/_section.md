Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$. Then $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ ([sk-def-sfb-constructible]) is a
triangulated subcategory of $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, and every object of
$\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is obtained by finitely many cones from objects $a_!L[n]$
([sk-lem-coefficient-heart]), where $a\colon S\hookrightarrow X$ is a locally closed
immersion, $L$ is a lisse $\Lambda_S$-module and $n\in\mathbf Z$.
