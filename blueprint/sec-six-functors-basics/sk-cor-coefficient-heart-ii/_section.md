Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$. Then $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is a triangulated subcategory of
$D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$. Every object of $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is obtained by finitely
many cones from objects $a_!L[n]$, with $a$ and $L$ as in [sk-lem-coefficient-heart] (3)
and $n\in\mathbf Z$.
