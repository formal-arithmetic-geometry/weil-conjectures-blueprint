Let $X$ be a Noetherian scheme and $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$. The full subcategory
$\Dbc(X,\Lambda)\subseteq D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ contains $0$, is closed under
isomorphisms and under the shifts $[\pm1]$, and if two vertices of a distinguished triangle
lie in $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ then so does the third. Hence it is a triangulated subcategory, and
it is thick.
