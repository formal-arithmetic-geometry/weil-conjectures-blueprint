Let $b:Y\to S$ be proper, let $u:G\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion with
reduced closed complement $i:Z\hookrightarrow Y$, and suppose that $bi:Z\to S$
is finite.

1. If $K\in D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Fell)$,
   $u^*K\in\Dbc(G,\Fell)$, and $Rb_*K\in\Dbc(S,\Fell)$, then
   $K\in\Dbc(Y,\Fell)$.
2. If $c:K\to K'$ is a morphism in
   $D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Fell)$ such that $u^*c$ is an
   isomorphism in $D^+(G_{\mathrm{proét}},\Fell)$ and $Rb_*c$ is an isomorphism
   in $D^+(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Fell)$, then $c$ is an isomorphism.

The hypotheses are preserved by every base change in the standing range, since
$bi$ stays finite and $b$ stays proper; this is how the lemma is applied to
base-change maps.
