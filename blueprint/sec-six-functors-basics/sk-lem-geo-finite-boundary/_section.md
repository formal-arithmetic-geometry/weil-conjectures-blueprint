Let $b:Y\to S$ be proper, let $j:U\hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion with
reduced closed complement $i:Z\hookrightarrow Y$, and suppose that $bi:Z\to S$
is finite.

1. If $K\in D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Fell)$,
   $j^*K\in\Dbc(U,\Fell)$, and $Rb_*K\in\Dbc(S,\Fell)$, then
   $K\in\Dbc(Y,\Fell)$.
2. If $\varphi:K\to K'$ is a morphism in
   $D^+_{\mathrm{class}}(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Fell)$ such that $j^*\varphi$ and
   $Rb_*\varphi$ are isomorphisms, then $\varphi$ is an isomorphism.
