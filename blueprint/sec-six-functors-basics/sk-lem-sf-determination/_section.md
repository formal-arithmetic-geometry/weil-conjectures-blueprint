Let $\Lambda$ be admissible, let
$F_1,F_2:\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))$
([sk-def-localizing-closure]) be exact functors preserving direct sums, and let $\eta:F_1\to F_2$ be a natural transformation
of exact functors. If $\eta_K$ is an isomorphism for every
$K\in\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$, then it is an isomorphism for every
$K\in\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$.
