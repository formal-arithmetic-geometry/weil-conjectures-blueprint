Let $X$ be a Noetherian scheme and $\Lambda$ a finite commutative ring. Every
object of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ lies in the thick subcategory of $D(X,\Lambda)$
generated by the objects $Rg_*j_!L$, where $U\subset X$ is a locally closed
stratum of a stratification of $X$, $j:U\hookrightarrow\overline U$ is the open
immersion into its closure, $g:\overline U\hookrightarrow X$ is the closed
immersion of the closure, and $L$ is a lisse (finite locally constant) sheaf of
$\Lambda$-modules on $U$ with flat stalks. That is, every property of objects
of $D(X,\Lambda)$ that is triangulated (contains $0$ and is stable under shifts
and under extensions in distinguished triangles), stable under retracts and
under isomorphisms, and holds for all these generators holds for every object
of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. Consequently, a natural transformation of exact functors
on $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ is invertible as soon as it is invertible on these
generators.
