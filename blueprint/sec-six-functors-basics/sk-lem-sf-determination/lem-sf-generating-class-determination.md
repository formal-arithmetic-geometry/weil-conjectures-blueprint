Let $\alpha$ be a natural transformation between two exact functors of
triangulated categories. If $\alpha$ is an isomorphism on a class of objects,
then it is an isomorphism on the thick subcategory generated by that class; in
particular, if the class generates, $\alpha$ is an isomorphism.

This propagates invertibility only. A natural transformation is *not*
determined by its values on a generating class: for
$R=\mathbf F_2[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ the derivation $\partial\epsilon=1$
gives $\eta:\mathrm{Id}\to[1]$ on $\operatorname{Perf}(R)$ vanishing on $R$
but nonzero on $P=[R\xrightarrow{\epsilon}R]$, so $\mathrm{id}$ and
$\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\ \eta&1\end{pmatrix}$ are natural automorphisms of
$\mathrm{Id}\oplus[1]$ agreeing on $R$ and differing on $P$. Equalities of
comparison maps must be proved from their constructions.
