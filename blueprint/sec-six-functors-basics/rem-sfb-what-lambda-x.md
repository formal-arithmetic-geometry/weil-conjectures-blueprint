Let $T\in\{\mathbb Z_\ell,\mathbb Q_\ell,\cO_E,E\}$, and let $\underline T_X$
be the pullback of $\underline T$ from the point
([sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients]). One expects that
$$
\underline T_X(U)=C(|U|,T)\qquad\text{for all }U\in X_{\mathrm{proét}} .
$$
Here is the heuristic. On a w-contractible $U$ one expects
$(p_X^*F)(U)=F(\pi_0U)$, where $\pi_0U$ is the profinite set of connected
components. A continuous map from $|U|$ to the totally disconnected space $T$
factors through $\pi_0U$. If this description holds, then $\underline T_X$ is
the Bhatt–Scholze sheaf $U\mapsto C(|U|,T)$ attached to the topological
ring $T$.

For $\Qlb$, the identity
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}=\colim_E E_X$ holds
([sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x]). It is the image of
$\underline{\Qlb}=\colim_E\underline E$ under the left adjoint $p_X^*$. So,
given the description above for each $E$, $\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}$ is
the Bhatt–Scholze sheaf $\Qlb=\colim_E E$ on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$.

**Reference.** B. Bhatt, P. Scholze, *The pro-étale topology for schemes*,
Astérisque 369 (2015), §4.2 and §6.8.
