Let $p:X\to Y$ be proper, with fibres of dimension $\le d$.

1. For $N\in\Cons(X,\mathcal O_E)$, $Rp_*N\in\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E)$ has
   cohomology in degrees $[0,2d+1]$; for $N\in\IndCons(X,\mathcal O_E)$,
   $H^qRp_*N\in\IndCons(Y,\mathcal O_E)$ and vanishes outside $[0,2d+1]$.
2. For $K\in\Dic(X,\mathcal O_E)$, $H^qRp_*K$ depends only on the degrees
   $[q-2d-1,q]$ of $K$, and $Rp_*$ commutes with arbitrary direct sums of
   objects of $\Dic(X,\mathcal O_E)$.
3. $Rp_*$ maps $\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))$ into $\Loc(\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E))$ and
   preserves direct sums there.
