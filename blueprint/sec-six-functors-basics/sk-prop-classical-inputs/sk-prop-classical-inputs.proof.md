*Proper base change.* On the étale site this is [sk-thm-properbasechange] for
bounded below torsion complexes and
[Stacks, Tag 0F0C](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0C) for unbounded
complexes with finite coefficients, and by comparison it holds on the
pro-étale site.

*Finiteness.* This is
[Stacks, Tag 0GL0](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0GL0), after
comparison with the étale site.

*Cohomological dimension.* By
[Stacks, Tag 0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10), the étale
direct images along morphisms of schemes of finite type over $k$ have finite
cohomological amplitude on torsion sheaves. By comparison, the same bound holds
on classical pro-étale coefficient sheaves.
