Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring and $f\colon X\to Y$ a morphism of
schemes admitting a compactification. With $\iota\colon D^+\hookrightarrow D$ the
inclusions of the bounded below derived categories,

$$
\iota\circ R^+f_!\cong Rf_!\circ\iota
$$

for the functors of [def-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-chosen-plus] and
[def-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-chosen].
