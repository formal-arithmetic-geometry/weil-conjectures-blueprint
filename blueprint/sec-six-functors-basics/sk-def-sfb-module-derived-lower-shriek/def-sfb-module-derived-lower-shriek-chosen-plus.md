Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring and $f\colon X\to Y$ a morphism of
schemes admitting a compactification. The *bounded below derived lower shriek*

$$
R^+f_!\colon D^+(\Mod_{\Lambda_X})\to D^+(\Mod_{\Lambda_Y})
$$

is the functor $R^+f^c_!=R^+g_*\circ D^+(i_!)$ of
[def-sfb-module-derived-lower-shriek-compactification-plus] for the chosen
compactification $c$ of $f$.
