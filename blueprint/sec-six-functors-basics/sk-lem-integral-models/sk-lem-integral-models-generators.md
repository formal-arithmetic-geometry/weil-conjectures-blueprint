Let $X$ be a standing scheme and $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ finite. Let $\mathcal G_E$ be the class of
objects $(a_!L_0)[1/\ell]=E_X\otimes_{\cO_{E,X}}a_!L_0$ of $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},E)$, where
$a\colon S\hookrightarrow X$ is a locally closed immersion and $L_0$ a lisse finite free
$\cO_{E,S}$-module (locally on $S$), and $\mathcal G_{\Qlb}$ the class of
$\QbarX\otimes_{\cO_{E,X}}a_!L_0$ for all finite $E$ and such $a$, $L_0$.

1. Every object of $\Dbc(X,E)$ is obtained from $\mathcal G_E$ by finitely many cones and shifts.
2. Every object of $\Dbc(X,\Qlb)$ is obtained from $\mathcal G_{\Qlb}$ by finitely many cones and
   shifts (with one finite $E$ for the given object).
3. $a_!L_0\in\Dbc(X,\cO_E)$, and its reductions $a_!L_0\otimes^{\mathbf L}\cO_E/\pi_E^n=a_!(L_0/\pi_E^n)$
   lie in $\Dbc(X,\cO_E/\pi_E^n)$ and come from $X_{\mathrm{ét}}$.
