title = "Checking natural transformations on constructible complexes"
order = 2489

The proper base change map and the comparison maps between compactifications
are natural transformations between exact functors on $\Loc(\Dctf(-,\Lambda))$
that preserve direct sums. To show that such a transformation is an
isomorphism, it suffices to check this on $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$.
