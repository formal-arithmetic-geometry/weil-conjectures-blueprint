For a prime $\ell$, a scheme $X$, a finite extension $E$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ inside $\Qlb$ and a
bounded constructible complex $K$ of $E_X$-modules ([det-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]), the
complex $T_{E,\Qlb}K$ ([def-sfb-derived-extend-to-qlbar]) is a bounded constructible complex of
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}$-modules.
