As for [lem-sfb-extend-subfield-constructible], with [lem-sfb-qlbar-flat] in place of
flatness of $F_X$: $T_{E,\Qlb}$ is computed termwise, so it preserves boundedness and
$H^n(T_{E,\Qlb}K)=\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}\otimes_{E_X}H^n(K)$, and tensoring local finite presentations on
the strata of a finite stratification ([sk-def-sfb-constructible]) gives local finite
presentations.
