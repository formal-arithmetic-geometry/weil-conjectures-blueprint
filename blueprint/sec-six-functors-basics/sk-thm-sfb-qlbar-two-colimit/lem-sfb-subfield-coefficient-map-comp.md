For finite extensions $E\le F$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ inside $\Qlb$ and every scheme $X$, the
inclusion $E_X\to F_X$ ([def-sfb-subfield-coefficient-map]) followed by $F_X\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}$ is
the inclusion $E_X\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}$ ([def-sfb-subfield-to-qlbar-coefficient-map]).
