Let $\ell$ be a prime and $X$ a scheme whose underlying topological space is Noetherian. The
constructible cocone ([def-sfb-constructible-cocone]) satisfies (Obj) of
[sk-def-sfb-filtered-two-colimit]: every $K\in D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Qlb)$ is isomorphic to
$T_{E,\Qlb}K_E$ for some finite extension $E$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ in $\Qlb$ and some
$K_E\in D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,E)$.
