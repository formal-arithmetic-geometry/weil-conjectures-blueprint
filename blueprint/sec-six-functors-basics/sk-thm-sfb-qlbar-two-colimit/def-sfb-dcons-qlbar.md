For a prime $\ell$ and a scheme $X$, $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Qlb)$ is the bounded constructible
derived category ([sk-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]) of modules over the coefficient ring
$\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}=p_X^*\underline{\Qlb}$: the full subcategory of $D(\Mod_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}})$ of
complexes that are bounded and have constructible cohomology sheaves.
