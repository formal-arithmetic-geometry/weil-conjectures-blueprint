Let $\ell$ be a prime and $X$ a scheme whose underlying topological space is Noetherian. The
constructible cocone $E\mapsto D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,E)\to D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Qlb)$
([def-sfb-constructible-cocone]) is a filtered 2-colimit in the sense of
[sk-def-sfb-filtered-two-colimit].
