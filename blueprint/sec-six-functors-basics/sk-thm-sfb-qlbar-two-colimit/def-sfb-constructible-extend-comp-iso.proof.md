Both functors, followed by the inclusion of $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Qlb)$ into
$D(\Mod_{\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell,X}})$, are the restrictions to $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,E)$ of
$T_{F,\Qlb}\circ T_{E,F}$ resp. $T_{E,\Qlb}$, which are isomorphic by
[def-sfb-derived-extend-comp-iso]. The inclusion of a full subcategory is fully faithful, so the
isomorphism lifts uniquely to $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Qlb)$.
