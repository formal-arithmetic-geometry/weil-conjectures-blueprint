For a prime $\ell$, a scheme $X$ and a finite extension $E$ of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ inside $\Qlb$,
$D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,E)$ is the bounded constructible derived category
([sk-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]) of modules over the coefficient ring
$E_X=p_X^*\underline E$: the full subcategory of $D(\Mod_{E_X})$ of complexes that are bounded
and have constructible cohomology sheaves.
