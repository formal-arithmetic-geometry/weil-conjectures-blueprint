Let $X$ be a standing scheme. For every $K\in\Dbc(X,\Qlb)$ there are a finite $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ and
$K_E\in\Dbc(X,E)$ with $(K_E)_{\Qlb}\cong K$. If $K_E$ is a model over $E$ and $F\supseteq E$ is
finite, then $(K_E)_F$ is a model over $F$.
