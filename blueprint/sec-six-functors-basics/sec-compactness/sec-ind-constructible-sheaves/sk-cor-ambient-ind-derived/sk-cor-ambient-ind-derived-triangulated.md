Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$. $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ contains $0$, is closed under shifts in both directions and
under isomorphisms, and if $K\to L\to M\to K[1]$ is a distinguished triangle in $\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$
with two of $K,L,M$ in $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$, then so is the third. Hence $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ is a full
triangulated subcategory.
