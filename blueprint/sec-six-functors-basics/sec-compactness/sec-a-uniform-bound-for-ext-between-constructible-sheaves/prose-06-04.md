If $C=a_!L$, with $a$ factored as $S\hookrightarrow Z\hookrightarrow X$
and $j$ the open complement of $Z$, and $L$ contains a lisse
$\mathcal O_E$-lattice (which holds after refining $S$, by
Lemma [sk-lem-stratified-lattice]), the proof gives
$d_C=\operatorname{cd}_\ell(j)+\operatorname{cd}_\ell(S)+2$. One of the two extra
degrees comes from the cone defining $R\underline\Gamma_Z$, and the other from
a derived inverse limit over the reductions modulo $\pi_E^n$.
