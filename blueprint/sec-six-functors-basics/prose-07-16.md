For $\mathcal O_E$ the rational compactness argument needs one change.
Constructible sheaves are no longer locally free on strata
($\mathcal O_{E,X}/\pi_E$ is constructible), but since $\mathcal O_E$ is a
discrete valuation ring they have free resolutions of length one, and the
resulting Tor amplitude $[-1,0]$ is all the estimates use.
