Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and $f:X\to Y$ separated of finite type.

1. Let $M\in D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ and let $X=\bigcup_{\alpha}U_\alpha$
   be a finite open cover with $M|_{U_\alpha}\in\Dbc(U_\alpha,\Lambda)$ for all
   $\alpha$. Then $M\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$.
2. For $K\in\Dbc(Y,\Lambda)$, $f^!K\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$, and for
   $A\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ the adjunction bijection restricts to
   $\Hom_{\Dbc(Y,\Lambda)}(Rf_!A,K)\simeq\Hom_{\Dbc(X,\Lambda)}(A,f^!K)$.
