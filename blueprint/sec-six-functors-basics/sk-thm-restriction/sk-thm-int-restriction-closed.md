Let $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion. For
$K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$, $R\underline\Gamma_ZK\in\Dbc(Z,\mathcal O_E)$, and
there is an isomorphism $i^!K\simeq R\underline\Gamma_ZK$, natural in $K$,
where $i^!$ is the right adjoint of [sk-prop-sf-existence].
