For $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and every separated morphism of finite type
$f:X\to Y$, the right adjoint $f^!$ of [sk-prop-sf-existence] on
$\Loc(\Dctf(-,\Lambda))$ satisfies

$$
f^!\bigl(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)\bigr)\subset\Dctf(X,\Lambda).
$$

Its restriction is right adjoint to $Rf_!:\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$.
