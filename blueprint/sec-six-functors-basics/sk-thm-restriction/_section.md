Let $\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$, $E$ or $\Qlb$, and let $f:X\to Y$ be separated of finite type.
Then $f^!$ ([sk-prop-sf-existence]) maps $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$
([sk-def-sfb-constructible]), and this restriction is right adjoint to
$Rf_!:\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$.
