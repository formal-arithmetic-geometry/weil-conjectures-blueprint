Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ a closed immersion with open
complement $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, and $K\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$. Then the
sequence

$$
i_*R\underline\Gamma_ZK\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow Rj_*j^*K
\longrightarrow i_*R\underline\Gamma_ZK[1]
$$

(counit of $i_*\dashv R\underline\Gamma_Z$, unit of $j^*\dashv Rj_*$) is a
distinguished triangle in $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, all of whose terms
lie in $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$, and $R\underline\Gamma_ZK\in\Dbc(Z,\Lambda)$.
