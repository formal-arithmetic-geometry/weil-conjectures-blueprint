Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ a closed immersion and $i^!$
the right adjoint of $Ri_!=i_*$ on the localizing categories
$\Loc(\Dbc(-,\Lambda))$ ([sk-prop-sf-existence]). For $K\in\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$
there is an isomorphism $i^!K\simeq R\underline\Gamma_ZK$ in
$\Loc(\Dbc(Z,\Lambda))$, natural in $K$ and compatible with the counits
$i_*i^!K\to K$ and $i_*R\underline\Gamma_ZK\to K$.
