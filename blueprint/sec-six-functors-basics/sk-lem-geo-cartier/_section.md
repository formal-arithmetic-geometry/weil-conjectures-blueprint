Let $i:D\hookrightarrow Z$ be an effective Cartier divisor on a scheme $Z$ on
which $\ell$ is invertible. There is a morphism

$$
c_D:i_*\Lambda_D\longrightarrow\Lambda_Z(1)[2]\quad\text{in }D(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)
$$

whose composite with $\Lambda_Z\to i_*\Lambda_D$ is the Kummer first Chern
class $c_1(\mathcal O_Z(D))$. It is compatible with change of coefficients and
with every base change along which the pulled-back divisor is still an
effective Cartier divisor.
