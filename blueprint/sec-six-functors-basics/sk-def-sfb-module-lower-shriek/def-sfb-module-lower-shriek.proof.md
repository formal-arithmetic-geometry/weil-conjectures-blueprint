The underlying sheaf of $i_!M$ is $i^{\Ab}_!M$ ([def-sfb-module-open-lower-shriek]),
and that of $g_*N$ is $g^{\Ab}_*N$ ([def-sfb-module-lower-star]); this gives the
underlying sheaf functor. The extension by zero $i_!$ is exact and the pushforward
$g_*$ is left exact, so their composite is left exact; both are additive.
