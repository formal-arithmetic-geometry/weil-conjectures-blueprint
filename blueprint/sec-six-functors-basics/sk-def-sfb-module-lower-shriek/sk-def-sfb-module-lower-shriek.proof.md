The projection formula is [lem-sfb-open-projection-formula]. The linear extension by
zero and its adjunction are [def-sfb-module-linear-open-lower-shriek]. The extension
by zero of module objects and its exactness are [def-sfb-module-open-lower-shriek],
and the adjunction $j_!\dashv j^*$, which uses that $\alpha_j$ is an isomorphism
([sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients]), is
[def-sfb-module-open-lower-shriek-adjunction]. The lower shriek attached to a
compactification is [def-sfb-module-compactification-lower-shriek], and the lower
shriek for the chosen compactification, with its left exactness, is
[def-sfb-module-lower-shriek].
