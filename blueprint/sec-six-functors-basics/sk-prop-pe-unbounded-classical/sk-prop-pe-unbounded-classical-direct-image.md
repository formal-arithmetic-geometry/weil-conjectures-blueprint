Let $X,Y$ be of finite type over a finite or algebraically closed field $k$, let
$g\colon X\to Y$ be of finite type, and let $A$ be a finite commutative
$\ell$-primary ring, $\ell$ invertible in $k$. For every $K\in D(X_{\Et},A)$ the
comparison map
$\alpha_K\colon\nu_Y^*Rg_{\Et,*}K\to Rg_{\Proet,*}\nu_X^*K$, defined as the mate of
$g_{\Proet}^*\nu_Y^*Rg_{\Et,*}K\cong\nu_X^*g_{\Et}^*Rg_{\Et,*}K\to\nu_X^*K$, is an
isomorphism.
