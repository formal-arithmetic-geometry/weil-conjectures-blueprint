Let $X$ be of finite type over a finite or algebraically closed field $k$ and $A$
a finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring, $\ell$ invertible in $k$. For every
$K\in D(X_{\Et},A)$ the unit $K\to R\nu_{X,*}\nu_X^*K$ is an isomorphism.
Consequently $\nu_X^*\colon D(X_{\Et},A)\to D(X_{\Proet},A)$ is fully faithful, and
it preserves arbitrary direct sums.
