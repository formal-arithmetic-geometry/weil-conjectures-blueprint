Let $X$ and $Y$ be of finite type over the base field $k$, let $A$ be a
finite commutative $\ell$-primary ring, and let $g\colon X\to Y$ be of finite
type. Then $\nu_X^*\colon D(X_{\Et},A)\to D(X_{\Proet},A)$ is fully faithful and
preserves arbitrary direct sums. The map
$\nu_Y^*Rg_{\Et,*}K\to Rg_{\Proet,*}\nu_X^*K$ of
[sk-thm-pe-direct-image-comparison], now a natural transformation of functors
$D(X_{\Et},A)\to D(Y_{\Proet},A)$, is an isomorphism for every
$K\in D(X_{\Et},A)$.
