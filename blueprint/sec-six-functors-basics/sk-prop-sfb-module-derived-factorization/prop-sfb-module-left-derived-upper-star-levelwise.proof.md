An exact functor $F$ preserves quasi-isomorphisms, so the functor it induces on
homotopy categories descends to $D(F)$ on derived categories, and $D(F)$ together with
the identity comparison is a left derived functor of $F$: the counit is an
isomorphism on every complex, in particular on K-flat ones
([det-def-sfb-derived-categories]). By uniqueness of left derived functors
$LF\cong D(F)$. Apply this to $F=f^*$, which is exact by
[det-prop-sfb-module-pullback-exact-main].
