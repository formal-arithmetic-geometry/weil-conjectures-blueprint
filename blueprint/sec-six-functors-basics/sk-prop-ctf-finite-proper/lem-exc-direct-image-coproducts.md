Let $u:U\to T$ be
quasi-compact and
quasi-separated, and let $A$ be a finite ring. Suppose that
$R^pu_*M=0$ for all etale $A$-module sheaves $M$ and
$p>c$, where $c\geq0$. Then
$Ru_*$ preserves arbitrary
coproducts on the unbounded
ordinary derived categories.
