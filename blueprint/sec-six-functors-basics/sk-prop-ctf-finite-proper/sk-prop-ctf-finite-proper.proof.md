All statements are classical on the étale site, and [sk-prop-ctf-finite-comparison] transports them to the
pro-étale site. Only the finite Tor amplitude of $Rp_*C$ needs an argument.

We first work on the étale site. Let $C\in\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$. The proper direct image of a constructible
torsion sheaf is constructible ([Stacks, Tag 0GL0](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0GL0)), and
$\operatorname{cd}_\ell(p)<\infty$ ([Stacks, Tag 0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10)). It follows
that $Rp_*C\in\Dbc(Y_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$. To check finite Tor amplitude, suppose that $C$ has Tor amplitude
$[a,b]$, and let $d$ be an integer with $\operatorname{cd}_\ell(X_{\bar y})\leq d$ for all geometric points
$\bar y$ of $Y$. Derived tensor products are computed stalkwise, so it suffices to show that each stalk
$(Rp_*C)_{\bar y}$ has Tor amplitude in $[a,b+d]$ as a complex of $A$-modules, that is, that
$(Rp_*C)_{\bar y}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_AN=((Rp_*C)\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}N_Y)_{\bar y}$ has cohomology in $[a,b+d]$
for every $A$-module $N$. By proper base change and the projection formula for constant modules
([Stacks, Tag 0F12](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F12)), this stalk is

$$
R\Gamma\bigl(X_{\bar y},C|_{X_{\bar y}}\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}N_{X_{\bar y}}\bigr).
$$

The complex $C|_{X_{\bar y}}\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}N_{X_{\bar y}}$ has cohomology in $[a,b]$, and
$R\Gamma(X_{\bar y},-)$ raises the upper bound by at most $d$. Thus $Rp_*C\in\Dctf(Y_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$.

On unbounded étale derived categories, proper direct image preserves all direct sums by
[Stacks, Tag 0F0D](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F0D). We obtain the $A$-linear statements by
forgetting coefficients, and by [sk-prop-ctf-finite-comparison] they hold for the pro-étale localizing
categories as well. Since $Rp_*$ preserves $\Dctf(-,A)$ and direct sums, it preserves the localizing
subcategories generated by $\Dctf(-,A)$.
