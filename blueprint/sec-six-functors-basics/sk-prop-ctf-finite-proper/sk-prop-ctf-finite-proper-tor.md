Let $A$ be a finite $\ell$-primary ring, $p\colon X\to Y$ proper, and $d$ a uniform bound for the
$\ell$-cohomological dimensions of the geometric fibres of $p$. If $C\in\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ has Tor amplitude
in $[a,b]$, then $Rp_*C\in\Dctf(Y_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ with Tor amplitude in $[a,b+d]$.
