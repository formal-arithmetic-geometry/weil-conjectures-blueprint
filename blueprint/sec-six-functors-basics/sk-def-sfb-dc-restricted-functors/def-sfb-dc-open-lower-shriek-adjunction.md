For a commutative condensed ring $\Lambda$ and an open immersion $j\colon U\to X$ of the setting,
the adjunction

$$
j_!\dashv Lj^*\qquad\text{between }D_{\mathrm{cons}}(U,\Lambda)\text{ and }D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Lambda)
$$

of the restricted functors of [def-sfb-dc-open-lower-shriek] and [def-sfb-dc-upper-star] is the
restriction along the fully faithful inclusions of the ambient adjunction
$D(j_!)\dashv D(j^*)$, transported along the identification $D(j^*)\cong Lj^*$.
