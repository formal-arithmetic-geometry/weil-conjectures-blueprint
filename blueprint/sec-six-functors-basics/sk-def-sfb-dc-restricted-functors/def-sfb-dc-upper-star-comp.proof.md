The restricted functors commute with the inclusions ([def-sfb-dc-upper-star]). So the ambient
composition isomorphism $L(g\circ f)^*\cong Lf^*\circ Lg^*$
([sk-prop-sfb-module-derived-factorization]), restricted to bounded constructible complexes,
becomes an isomorphism between $\iota\circ L(g\circ f)^*$ and $\iota\circ Lf^*\circ Lg^*$. Since the
inclusion $\iota$ is fully faithful, this isomorphism has a unique preimage.
