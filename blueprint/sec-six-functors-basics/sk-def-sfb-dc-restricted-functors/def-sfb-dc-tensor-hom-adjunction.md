Let $X$ and $\Lambda$ be as in [def-sfb-dc-tensor] and [def-sfb-dc-hom]. For
$K\in D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Lambda)$, the adjunction

$$
K\otimes^{\mathrm L}-\dashv\RHom(K,-)\qquad\text{on }D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Lambda)
$$

is the restriction along the fully faithful inclusion of the ambient tensor–hom adjunction for
$K$.
