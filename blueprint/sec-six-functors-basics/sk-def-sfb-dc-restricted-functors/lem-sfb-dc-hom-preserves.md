Let $\Lambda$ be one of $\underline{\cO}_E$, $\underline E$ ($E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ finite;
[sk-def-sfb-condensed-ring]) or $\underline{\Qlb}$ ([sk-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed]), and let $X$
be a scheme of the setting of [sk-def-sfb-dc-setting]. If $K,L\in D(\Mod_{\Lambda_X})$ are bounded
constructible ([sk-def-sfb-bounded-constructible]), then so is $\RHom_{\Lambda_X}(K,L)$
([sk-def-sfb-derived-tensor-hom]).
