For morphisms $f\colon X\to Y$ and $g\colon Y\to Z$ of the setting and a commutative condensed
ring $\Lambda$, the *composition isomorphism*

$$
L(g\circ f)^*\cong Lf^*\circ Lg^*\colon D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Z,\Lambda)\to D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Lambda)
$$

of the restricted pullbacks of [def-sfb-dc-upper-star] is the unique natural isomorphism whose
image under the inclusion into $D(\Mod_{\Lambda_X})$ is the ambient composition isomorphism.
