For $f\colon X\to Y$ and $\Lambda$ as in [def-sfb-dc-lower-star], the adjunction

$$
Lf^*\dashv Rf_*\qquad\text{between }D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Y,\Lambda)\text{ and }D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Lambda)
$$

of the restricted functors of [def-sfb-dc-upper-star] and [def-sfb-dc-lower-star] is the
restriction of the ambient derived adjunction along the fully faithful inclusions: its
Hom-bijections are those of the ambient adjunction.
