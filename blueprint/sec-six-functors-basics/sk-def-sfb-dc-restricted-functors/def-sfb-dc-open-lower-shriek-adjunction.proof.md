On the ambient categories, $D(j_!)\dashv D(j^*)$, and the derived pullback is levelwise,
$Lj^*\cong D(j^*)$ ([sk-prop-sfb-module-derived-factorization]). Transporting the right adjoint
along this isomorphism gives $D(j_!)\dashv Lj^*$. Both functors preserve bounded constructible
complexes and their restrictions commute with the inclusions
([def-sfb-dc-open-lower-shriek], [def-sfb-dc-upper-star]). So the Hom-bijections restrict to the
full subcategories $D_{\mathrm{cons}}$.
