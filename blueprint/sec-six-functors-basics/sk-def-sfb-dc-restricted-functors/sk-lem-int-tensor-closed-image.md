Let $j\colon U\to X$ be a quasi-compact open immersion and $i\colon Z\to X$ a closed immersion (of Noetherian
schemes of the setting). Then $j_!\colon\D(U_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)\to\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ and
$i_*\colon\D(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)\to\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ preserve $\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E)$: they preserve derived
$\pi_E$-completeness, commute with reduction modulo $\pi_E^n$, and preserve boundedness. Consequently every
$K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ is a finite iterated extension of objects $a_!M$ with $a\colon S\hookrightarrow X$ the
inclusion of a stratum and $M\in\Dbc(S,\mathcal O_E)$ pro-étale locally the completion of a bounded complex of
finite free $\mathcal O_E$-modules.
