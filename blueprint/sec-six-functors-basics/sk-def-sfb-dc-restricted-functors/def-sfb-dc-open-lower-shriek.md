Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative condensed ring and let $j\colon U\to X$ be an open immersion of
the setting. The extension by zero

$$
j_!\colon D_{\mathrm{cons}}(U,\Lambda)\to D_{\mathrm{cons}}(X,\Lambda)
$$

is the restriction of the levelwise functor $D(j_!)$ of the exact extension by zero of
[sk-def-sfb-module-lower-shriek]. It preserves bounded constructible complexes by
[lem-sfb-dc-open-lower-shriek-preserves], and it commutes with the inclusions.
