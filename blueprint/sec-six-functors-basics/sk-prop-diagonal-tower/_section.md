Let $q:\mathbf P^1_Y\to Y$ be the projection, $\Delta$ the diagonal of
$\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y$ and $p_1,p_2$ its projections. There are a
morphism $\delta_{\mathcal O_E}:\Delta_*\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_E(1)[2]$ reducing
to the cycle classes of [sk-lem-geo-cartier] and a trace
$\operatorname{Tr}_q:Rq_*\mathcal O_E(1)[2]\to\mathcal O_E$ lifting those of
[sk-lem-geo-p1-cohomology], with
$\operatorname{Tr}_{p_i}\circ Rp_{i*}(\delta_{\mathcal O_E})=1$ for $i=1,2$.
Extending scalars gives $\delta_\Lambda$ and $\operatorname{Tr}_q$ with the same
identities for $\Lambda=E,\Qlb$. All are compatible with base change along
separated morphisms of finite type $Y'\to Y$.
