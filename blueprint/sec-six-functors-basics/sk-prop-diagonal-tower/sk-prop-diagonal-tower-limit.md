With the notation of [det-prop-diagonal-tower-system]:

1. $R\varprojlim_n\Delta_*\mathbf Z/\ell^n=\Delta_*\Zl$ and
   $R\varprojlim_n\mathbf Z/\ell^n(1)=\Zl(1)$, and more generally derived
   direct image along any morphism of the pro-étale sites considered commutes
   with $R\varprojlim$ and is computed levelwise on inverse systems.
2. The Tate twist $(1)$, defined as tensor product over the completed
   coefficient sheaf with the invertible sheaf $\Zl(1)$ (and $(-1)$ with its
   dual), is exact, preserves products and commutes with $R\varprojlim$.
3. $R\varprojlim c_\bullet$ is a morphism
   $\delta_{\Zl}:\Delta_*\Zl\to\Zl(1)[2]$ of derived $\ell$-complete objects; it
   is the derived $\ell$-completion of $\partial$, and it is in a unique way a
   morphism in $D(W_{\mathrm{proét}},\Zl)$ over the completed coefficient sheaf
   $\Zl$ of $W$. Its reductions modulo $\ell^n$ are the torsion cycle classes.
