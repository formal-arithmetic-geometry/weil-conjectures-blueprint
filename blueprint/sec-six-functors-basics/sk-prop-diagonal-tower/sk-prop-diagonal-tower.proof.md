All products are over $Y$. We build the torsion cycle classes for all $n$ at
once as one morphism of inverse systems, apply $R\varprojlim$, and prove the
identities already for inverse systems, where they can be checked levelwise
because the objects involved sit in degree $0$.

*The extension defining the cycle class.* The diagonal is an effective
Cartier divisor, with equation $x_0y_1-x_1y_0$. The proof of
[sk-lem-geo-cartier] gives the extension of étale sheaves

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbf G_m\longrightarrow\mathcal M_\Delta
\longrightarrow\Delta_*\mathbf Z\longrightarrow0,
$$

where $\mathcal M_\Delta$ is generated by the units and the inverse of a local
equation $t$ of $\Delta$, and the map to $\Delta_*\mathbf Z$ sends $t^{-1}$ to
$+1$. We keep this sign convention; with it the class of a section has degree
$+1$, and both integration identities hold with sign $+1$. Pull the extension
back to the pro-étale site, keeping the names $\mathbf G_m$ and
$\mathcal M_\Delta$. As a morphism in
$D((\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y)_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathbf Z)$, the
extension is the diagram

$$
\Delta_*\mathbf Z\ \xleftarrow{\ \sim\ }\
[\mathbf G_m\longrightarrow\mathcal M_\Delta]\ \longrightarrow\mathbf G_m[1],
$$

with the two-term complex in degrees $-1$ and $0$. The left arrow is a
quasi-isomorphism by exactness of the extension (on the pro-étale site,
$\Delta_*$ of the pulled-back sheaf is the pullback of $\Delta_*\mathbf Z$ by
closed base change).

*Functorial derived completion.* We reduce modulo all $\ell^n$ at once. On
$(\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y)_{\mathrm{proét}}$, the derived tensor
product with the inverse system of quotient rings,

$$
K\longmapsto K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathbf Z}(\underline{\mathbf Z/\ell^n})_{n\geq1},
$$

is a functor from
$D((\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y)_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathbf Z)$ to the
derived category of inverse systems of $\underline{\mathbf Z_\ell}$-modules on
the same site. It is computed by one K-flat resolution of $K$ tensored
termwise with that system, and $\mathbf Z_\ell$ acts on the $n$-th term
through $\mathbf Z/\ell^n$. Evaluation of systems is exact and detects
quasi-isomorphisms, so this is a derived functor, and it sends the boundary
morphism of the Cartier extension to a single morphism of inverse systems.
Composed with $R\varprojlim$, which takes values in
$D((\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y)_{\mathrm{proét}},\underline{\mathbf Z_\ell})$,
it is derived $\ell$-completion $K\mapsto\widehat K$ (Stacks, Tag 099M), so
the limit morphism is the completion of an existing morphism.

Its source is the system $\Delta_*\mathbf Z/\ell^\bullet$: the formula
$(\Delta_*\mathbf Z)\otimes F\simeq\Delta_*\Delta^{-1}F$ holds because both
sides agree after restriction to the diagonal and to its open complement, and
these restrictions are jointly conservative (Stacks, Tag 09AD); the right side
is exact in $F$. Its target is computed by the Kummer sequence,
$\mathbf G_m\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathbf Z}\mathbf Z/\ell^n\simeq\mu_{\ell^n}[1]$.
To identify the transition maps, use the resolution
$[\mathbf Z\xrightarrow{\ell^n}\mathbf Z]$ of $\mathbf Z/\ell^n$, with
transition $\ell$ in degree $-1$ and the identity in degree $0$; it shows that
the transition maps of the target are $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^\ell$ on
$\mu_{\ell^n}$. The morphism is the image of the boundary morphism, and
canonical truncations identify its target with the system
$\mathbf Z/\ell^\bullet(1)[2]$ as linear objects. We have constructed one
morphism

$$
\Delta_*\mathbf Z/\ell^\bullet\longrightarrow\mathbf Z/\ell^\bullet(1)[2]
$$

in the derived category of inverse systems.

*Passage to the limit.* Apply $R\varprojlim$ to this morphism. Since the
pro-étale site is replete and products of sheaves are exact
([sk-prop-pe-replete], [sk-prop-pe-products]),

$$
R\varprojlim_n\Delta_*\mathbf Z/\ell^n=\Delta_*\Zl,\qquad
R\varprojlim_n\mathbf Z/\ell^n(1)=\Zl(1).
$$

For the coefficient systems this can be seen directly: on a weakly
contractible affine, sections are exact and the transition maps are
surjective, so the Milnor complex computing $R\varprojlim$ has no cohomology
in degree one. The functor $\Delta_*$ is exact and preserves limits. More
generally, derived direct image commutes with $R\varprojlim$ (Stacks, Tag
099L): both are right adjoints of exact functors (inverse image and the
constant-system functor), so both preserve K-injectives and their derived
functors commute. Evaluation of an inverse system at one level also preserves
injectives, since its left adjoint is exact, so derived direct image of a
system is computed levelwise.

The limit morphism is linear over the constant sheaf
$\underline{\mathbf Z_\ell}$, and we need it over the completed coefficient
sheaf $\mathbf Z_\ell$ of $\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y$. Its source and
target are derived $\ell$-complete, and the map from
$\underline{\mathbf Z_\ell}$ to it is flat and an isomorphism modulo every
$\ell^n$. Hence restriction of scalars is an equivalence on derived complete
objects (Stacks, Tag 099I, as in [sk-lem-integral-models](1)), and the
morphism is, in a unique way, a $\Zl$-linear morphism
$\Delta_*\Zl\to\Zl(1)[2]$ over the completed coefficient sheaf.

Here the Tate twist $(1)$ is the tensor product, termwise and over the
completed coefficient ring, with the invertible sheaf $\Zl(1)$; the twist
$(-1)$ uses its dual. The sheaf $\Zl(1)$ is free of rank one on the
pro-étale cover given by the system of primitive $\ell^n$-th roots of unity,
and its reduction modulo $\ell^n$ is $\mu_{\ell^n}$ with the transition maps
computed above. Hence the twist is exact, preserves products, and commutes
with $R\varprojlim$.

*The trace and the integration identities.* Form the decomposition of
[sk-lem-geo-p1-cohomology] in the derived category of inverse systems on
$Y_{\mathrm{proét}}$, from the unit and the class of a section:

$$
\mathbf Z/\ell^\bullet\oplus\mathbf Z/\ell^\bullet(-1)[-2]
\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Rq_*\mathbf Z/\ell^\bullet.
$$

It is an isomorphism because its cone is acyclic at every level, and
cohomology of systems is computed levelwise. Twisting and projecting to the
second summand through its inverse defines the trace on systems, and
$R\varprojlim$ gives $\operatorname{Tr}_q$. Derived direct image preserves
completeness, so its source and target are complete, and the same equivalence
makes it linear over the completed coefficient sheaf on $Y$.

Each composite $\operatorname{Tr}_{p_i}\circ Rp_{i*}(\delta)$ is now an
endomorphism of the system $\mathbf Z/\ell^\bullet$ in the derived category
of inverse systems on $(\mathbf P^1_Y)_{\mathrm{proét}}$, which is
concentrated in degree $0$. Endomorphisms of an object in degree $0$ in a
derived category are the endomorphisms in the abelian category, here
endomorphisms of inverse systems of sheaves, that is, compatible families of
endomorphisms of the levels $\mathbf Z/\ell^n$. At each level the
endomorphism is the identity by [sk-prop-geo-p1-adjunction]. Hence the
identities hold in the derived category of inverse systems, and applying
$R\varprojlim$ gives them over $\Zl$. For morphisms between arbitrary objects
of the derived category of inverse systems, equality at every level would not
imply equality; this is why the identities are checked on objects in degree
$0$.

*Coefficients and base change.* The ring $\mathcal O_E$ is finite free over
$\Zl$, and extension of scalars along it commutes with products and
$R\varprojlim$. Since $\ell^n\mathcal O_E=\pi_E^{en}\mathcal O_E$ up to a
unit, where $e$ is the ramification index, the systems of reductions modulo
$\ell^n$ and modulo $\pi_E^n$ are cofinal. This gives $\delta_{\mathcal O_E}$,
its trace and its identities. Inverting $\ell$ and the filtered colimit over
finite extensions of $E$ give $\delta_E$, $\delta_{\Qlb}$ and their traces;
direct image commutes with these colimits of bounded below objects
([sk-lem-bounded-continuity]), so the identities are preserved.

For a morphism $g:Y'\to Y$, the diagonal and the sections remain Cartier
divisors after base change, and the Cartier extension and the Kummer maps are
natural under pullback. The adjunction between the constant-system functor
and $R\varprojlim$ gives, for an inverse system $K_\bullet$ of sheaves on
$T_{\mathrm{proét}}$ with $T$ one of $Y$, $\mathbf P^1_Y$,
$\mathbf P^1_Y\times_Y\mathbf P^1_Y$, and with $g$ also denoting its base
change $T\times_YY'\to T$, a natural comparison

$$
g^{-1}R\varprojlim K_\bullet\longrightarrow R\varprojlim g^{-1}K_\bullet
$$

in $D((T\times_YY')_{\mathrm{proét}},\underline{\mathbf Z_\ell})$, which is an
isomorphism for the systems used here, by
[sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients], closed base change, and the
compatibility of the Tate twist with pullback. Naturality of this comparison
gives the compatibility of $\delta_\Lambda$ with base change, and the
decomposition of $Rq_*$ gives that of the trace.
