The six operations $f^*$, $Rf_*$, $Rf_!$, $f^!$, $\otimes^{\mathbf L}$
and $\cHom$ preserve $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$ for every admissible $\Lambda$
([sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability]), and they are compatible with change of
coefficients ([sk-prop-scalar-six]). This is the $\ell$-adic
analogue of the classical finiteness theorems for torsion sheaves, and it
is what allows us to work with $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-sheaves in the trace formula and in
the proof of the Weil conjectures: from now on we work in $\Dctf$ without
further comment and pass between $\mathcal O_E$, $E$, $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ and finite
rings whenever a statement is easier for one of them. For $Rf_!$ and $f^!$
the results were proved in
Section “Constructible sheaves and reduction to finite coefficients”–Section “The exceptional inverse image preserves constructibility”. For the
other operations the difficulty is that a complex of finite Tor amplitude
may have cohomology sheaves of infinite Tor dimension, so we cannot argue
sheaf by sheaf; instead we use that on the strata of a finite
stratification such a complex is locally $\Lambda_S\otimes P$ with $P$ a
perfect complex.

We now show that the operations on module sheaves, together with the adjunction
$Rf_!\dashv f^!$ of [sk-prop-sf-existence], restrict to the
categories $\Dctf(-,\Lambda)$.
