For $Rj_*\colon D^+(X_{\mathrm{ét}},\Lambda)\to D^+(Y_{\mathrm{ét}},\Lambda)$
this is the bound of Stacks, Tag
[0F10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F10): the étale direct images
along morphisms of finite type $k$-schemes have finite cohomological amplitude
on torsion sheaves, which holds over every field $k$.
[sk-prop-pe-unbounded-classical] transfers it to classical pro-étale sheaves.
