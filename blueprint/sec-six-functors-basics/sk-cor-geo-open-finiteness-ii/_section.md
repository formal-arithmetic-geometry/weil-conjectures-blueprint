In the situation of [sk-cor-geo-open-finiteness],
$\operatorname{cd}_\ell(j)<\infty$ (in the sense of [sk-def-cd]); that is,
$Rj_*\colon D^+(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to D^+(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$
has finite cohomological amplitude on classical $\ell$-power torsion sheaves.
