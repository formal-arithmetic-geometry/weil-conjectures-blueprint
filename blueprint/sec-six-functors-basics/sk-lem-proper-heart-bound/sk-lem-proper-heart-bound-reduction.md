Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $p\colon X\to Y$ proper and $c\in\mathbf Z$. Suppose that $R^qp_*(a_!L_0)=0$ for
$q>c$ for every locally closed immersion $a\colon S\hookrightarrow X$, every finite extension $E'$ of $E$
and every lisse finite free $\mathcal O_{E',S}$-module $L_0$, and that this vanishing passes from $a_!L_0$
to $(a_!L_0)[1/\ell]$ and to its scalar extension to $\Qlb$. Then $R^qp_*M=0$ for $q>c$ and every
$M\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$.
