Let $p\colon X\to Y$ be proper with geometric fibres of dimension $\leq d$, let $V\to Y$ be affine weakly
contractible, $p_V\colon X_V\to V$ the base change, $a\colon S\hookrightarrow X$ locally closed, and $L_0$ a
lisse finite free $\mathcal O_{E,S}$-module. Then for every $n\geq1$, $a_!L_0\otimes^{\mathbf L}\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n=a_!(L_0/\pi_E^n)$
is a classical constructible sheaf, and

$$
H^q\bigl(X_V,a_!(L_0/\pi_E^n)\bigr)=0\qquad(q>2d).
$$
