Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and $p\colon X\to Y$ proper with geometric fibres of dimension $\leq d$. For
$M\in\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$ and $q\geq0$, $R^qp_*M\in\IndCons(Y,\Lambda)$, $R^qp_*M=0$ for $q>2d+1$, and
$R^qp_*$ commutes with arbitrary direct sums of objects of $\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$.
