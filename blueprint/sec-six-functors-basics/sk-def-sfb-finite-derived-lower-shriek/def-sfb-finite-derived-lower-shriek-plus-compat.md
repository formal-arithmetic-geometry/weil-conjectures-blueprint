With $\iota\colon D^+\to D$ the inclusions of the bounded below derived categories, there
is a natural isomorphism

$$
\iota\circ Rf_!^+\cong Rf_!\circ\iota
$$

between the functors of [def-sfb-finite-derived-lower-shriek-plus] and
[def-sfb-finite-derived-lower-shriek].
