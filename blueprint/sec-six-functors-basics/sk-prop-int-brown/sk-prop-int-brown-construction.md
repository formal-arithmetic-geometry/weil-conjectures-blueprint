For a separated morphism of finite type $f:X\to Y$ with compactification
$f=p\circ j$ ($j$ an open immersion, $p$ proper), put
$Rf_!=Rp_*\circ j_!:\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))\to\Loc(\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E))$.
Then $Rf_!$ is a well-defined exact functor preserving direct sums, it maps
$\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ into $\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E)$, and up to canonical
isomorphism it is independent of the compactification, compatible with
composition and with base change.
