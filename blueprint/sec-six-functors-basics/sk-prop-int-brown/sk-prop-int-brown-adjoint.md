For $f:X\to Y$ separated of finite type, the functor
$Rf_!:\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))\to\Loc(\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E))$ of
[det-prop-int-brown-construction] has an exact right adjoint $f^!$, unique up
to unique isomorphism; it is the right adjoint of [sk-prop-sf-existence] for
$\Lambda=\mathcal O_E$.
