title = "Constructible sheaves are finitely presented; full faithfulness"
uses = ["sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity"]
+++
Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$.

1. For $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$ the functor $\Hom_{\Lambda}(C,-)$ on $\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ commutes
   with filtered colimits; i.e. $C$ is finitely presented.
2. The functor $\Phi\colon\operatorname{Ind}(\Cons(X,\Lambda))\to\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$,
   $\text{“}\varinjlim\text{”}_\alpha C_\alpha\mapsto\varinjlim_\alpha C_\alpha$, is fully faithful, and its
   essential image is by definition $\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$.
