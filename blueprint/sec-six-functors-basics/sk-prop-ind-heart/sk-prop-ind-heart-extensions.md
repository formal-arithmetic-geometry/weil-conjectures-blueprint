Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and let $0\to N\to M\to B\to0$ be exact in $\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ with
$N,B\in\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$. Then $M\in\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$.
