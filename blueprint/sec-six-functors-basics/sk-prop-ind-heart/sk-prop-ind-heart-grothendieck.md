Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$. $\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$ is an abelian category, its inclusion into
$\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ is exact and preserves all colimits, filtered colimits in it are exact,
and the direct sum of a set of representatives of the isomorphism classes of $\Cons(X,\Lambda)$ is a
generator. Hence $\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$ is a Grothendieck abelian category (Stacks, Tag 079A).
