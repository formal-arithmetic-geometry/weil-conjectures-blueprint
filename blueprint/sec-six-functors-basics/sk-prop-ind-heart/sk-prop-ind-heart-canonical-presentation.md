Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and $B\in\IndCons(X,\Lambda)$. Let $J(B)$ be the category whose objects are pairs
$(C,c)$ with $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$ and $c\colon C\to B$, and whose morphisms are maps over $B$. Then
$J(B)$ is essentially small and filtered, and $B=\varinjlim_{(C,c)\in J(B)}C$. If
$B=\varinjlim_\alpha C_\alpha$ for some filtered diagram of constructible sheaves, the induced functor
from the index category into $J(B)$ is cofinal.
