Let $p:X\to Y$ be proper.

1. For $K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ and $L\in\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E)$ the
   projection formula map
   $Rp_*K\otimes^{\mathbf L}L\to Rp_*(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}p^*L)$ (underived
   completion, tensor over $\mathcal O_{E,Y}$) is an isomorphism in
   $D(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$.
2. The categories $\Loc(\Dbc(-,\mathcal O_E))$ are closed under
   $\otimes^{\mathbf L}$, and the projection formula map is an isomorphism for
   $K\in\Loc(\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E))$ and $L\in\Loc(\Dbc(Y,\mathcal O_E))$.
