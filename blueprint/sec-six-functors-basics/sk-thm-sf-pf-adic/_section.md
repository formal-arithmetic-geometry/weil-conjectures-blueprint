For proper $p:X\to Y$ the map
$(Rp_*M)\otimes^{\mathbf L}N\to Rp_*(M\otimes^{\mathbf L}p^*N)$ is an isomorphism for
$M\in\Loc(\Dctf(X,\mathcal O_E))$ and $N\in\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\mathcal O_E))$
([sk-def-localizing-closure], [sk-def-sfb-constructible]).
