Let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be a quasi-compact open immersion and
$K\in\Dbc(U,\mathcal O_E)$. Then $Rj_*K$ is derived complete,
$(Rj_*K)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}\mathbf F_{E,X}\simeq Rj_*(K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,U}}\mathbf F_{E,U})$
with $\mathbf F_E=\mathcal O_E/\pi_E$, and $Rj_*K\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)=\Dctf(X,\mathcal O_E)$.
