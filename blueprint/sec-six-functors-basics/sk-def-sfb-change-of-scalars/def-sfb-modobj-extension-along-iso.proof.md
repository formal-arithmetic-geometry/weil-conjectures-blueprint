$\operatorname{Res}_{\varphi^{-1}}$ is a quasi-inverse of $\operatorname{Res}_\varphi$
([def-sfb-modobj-restriction-equivalence]), so it is left adjoint to
$\operatorname{Res}_\varphi$. So is $\operatorname{Ext}_\varphi$, by
[def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction]. Left adjoints are unique up to a unique
natural isomorphism compatible with the adjunctions.
