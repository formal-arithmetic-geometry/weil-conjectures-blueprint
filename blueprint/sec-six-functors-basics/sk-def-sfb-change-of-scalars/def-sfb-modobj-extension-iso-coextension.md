In the situation of [def-sfb-modobj-restriction-equivalence], there is a natural
isomorphism
$$
\operatorname{Ext}_\varphi\cong\operatorname{Coext}_\varphi
$$
of functors $\Mod_{\mathcal R}(\mathcal C)\to\Mod_{\mathcal S}(\mathcal C)$.
