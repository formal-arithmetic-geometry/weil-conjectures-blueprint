Let $A$ be a finite commutative ring annihilated by a power of $\ell$. Every object of $\Dctf(X,A)$ is
compact in $\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))$, and a small skeleton of $\Dctf(X,A)$ is a set of compact generators.
Moreover $\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))^c=\Dctf(X,A)$.
