Let $A$ be a finite admissible ring. Every object of $\Dctf(X,A)$ is compact in $\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))$
([sk-def-localizing-closure]), and a small skeleton of $\Dctf(X,A)$ is a set of compact generators.
Moreover, $\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))^c=\Dctf(X,A)$.
