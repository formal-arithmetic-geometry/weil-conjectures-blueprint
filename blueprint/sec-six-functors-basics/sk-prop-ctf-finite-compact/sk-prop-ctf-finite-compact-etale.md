title = "Finite-Tor constructible complexes are compact in the étale derived category"

Let $A$ be a finite $\ell$-primary ring and $X$ a scheme of the standing setting (finite type over the base
field, so all $\operatorname{cd}_\ell$ are finite). Every $C\in\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ is compact in
$\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$: for every family $(K_s)$ in $\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$,
$\bigoplus_s\Hom(C,K_s)\to\Hom(C,\bigoplus_sK_s)$ is an isomorphism.
