We first prove compactness on the étale site against arbitrary objects of $\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$, where the
commutation of cohomology with direct sums that we need is classical, and then transfer the result to
$X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ by $\nu_X^*$.

Recall that compact objects are closed under shifts, finite extensions and direct summands. Hence, by
[sk-lem-sf-flat-model], it suffices to treat $a_!L$. Factor $a=i\circ u$ with $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ closed
and $u\colon S\hookrightarrow Z$ open, and let $j\colon X\setminus Z\hookrightarrow X$ be the complementary open
immersion. Since $L$ has finite projective stalks, it is dualizable with dual
$L^\vee=\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(L,A_S)$, and the étale form of [sk-lem-formal-closed-support](2) shows
that for $K\in\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ we have

$$
\RHom_X(a_!L,K)=R\Gamma\bigl(S,L^\vee\otimes_Au^*R\underline\Gamma_ZK\bigr),
\qquad R\underline\Gamma_ZK=i^*\fib\bigl(K\to Rj_*j^*K\bigr),
$$

where $R\underline\Gamma_Z$ denotes sections with support in $Z$. We claim that every functor on the right,
viewed as a functor between étale derived categories, preserves arbitrary direct sums. For $Rj_*$ and
$R\Gamma(S,-)$, this is [Stacks, Tag 0F11](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F11), which says that a derived
direct image of finite cohomological dimension commutes with direct sums of unbounded complexes. It applies
to $A$-modules because derived direct image and global sections commute with forgetting the coefficient ring
([Stacks, Tag 0D6J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0D6J)). The remaining functors are inverse images,
tensor products with finite projective sheaves, and a fibre. Inverse images are left adjoints, tensor products
commute with direct sums, and the fibre of a map between functors that preserve direct sums again preserves
them. Taking $H^0$, we see that $a_!L$ is compact, and by [sk-lem-sf-flat-model] it follows that every object
of $\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ is compact.

To pass to the pro-étale site, we use [sk-cor-pe-finite-tor-comparison] and [sk-prop-pe-unbounded-classical],
which show that the inverse image $\nu_X^*\colon\D(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)\to\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},A)$ is exact, fully
faithful and preserves direct sums. Hence it identifies the localizing subcategories generated by $\Dctf$ on
the two sites, and compactness against all étale complexes implies compactness in $\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))$. The
next proposition ([sk-prop-ctf-finite-comparison]) identifies $\Loc(\Dctf(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A))$ with the whole
étale derived category.

Since constructibility and finite Tor amplitude are preserved by direct summands, the category $\Dctf(X,A)$ is
thick. It is essentially small, because it is a full subcategory of the essentially small bounded
constructible category, and by definition a skeleton generates $\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))$. In a triangulated category
with direct sums generated by a set of compact objects, the compact objects form the thick closure of that set
([Stacks, Tag 09SM](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SM),
[Stacks, Tag 09SN](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09SN)). In our case this closure is $\Dctf(X,A)$.
