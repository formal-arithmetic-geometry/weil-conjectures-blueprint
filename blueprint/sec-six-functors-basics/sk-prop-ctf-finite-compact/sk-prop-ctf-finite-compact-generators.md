Let $A$ be a finite $\ell$-primary ring. $\Dctf(X,A)$ is a thick, essentially small subcategory of
$\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},A)$; a small skeleton of it is a set of compact generators of $\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))$; and
$\Loc(\Dctf(X,A))^c=\Dctf(X,A)$.
