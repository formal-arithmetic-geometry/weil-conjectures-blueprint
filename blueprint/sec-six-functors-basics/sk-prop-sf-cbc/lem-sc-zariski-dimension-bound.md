Let $X$ be a Noetherian
scheme of finite dimension $d$.
For every abelian sheaf $F$ on [def-sc-zariski-site],

$$
H^q(X_{\rm Zar},F)=0\quad(q>d),
$$

with cohomology as in [def-sc-zariski-cohomology].
