A proper morphism is separated and of finite type, so
[thm-chow-lemma] applies to $f$ and gives $Y$, the proper surjective
morphism $\pi:Y\to X$, the dense open $U\subset X$ over which $\pi$ is an
isomorphism, the finite set $N$ and an immersion $e:Y\to\mathbf P^N_S$
with $e$ followed by $\mathbf P^N_S\to S$ equal to $f\circ\pi$. Since $f$
is proper, $e$ is a closed immersion by [cor-chow-lemma-proper]. Finally
$f\circ\pi$ is proper, hence quasi-compact, so $Y\to S$ is projective.
