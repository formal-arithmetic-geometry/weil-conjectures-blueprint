The category $\Dbc(T,\Qlb)=\mathcal P_T$ of
[def-weil-pbc-qbar-category] is idempotent complete, and the canonical fully
faithful functor $\Dbc(T,\Qlb)\to\Kar(\Dbc(T,\Qlb))$ is an exact
equivalence.
