title = "Compactness and proper direct image for torsion coefficients (2)"

We need flat models because the cohomology sheaves of an object of
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ can have infinite Tor amplitude. For example, the complex of
constant sheaves

$$
[A\xrightarrow{\ell}A],\qquad A=\mathbf Z/\ell^2\mathbf Z,
$$

is perfect, while its nonzero cohomology sheaves are
copies of $\mathbf F_\ell$, which have infinite projective dimension
over $A$. In other words, finite Tor amplitude is a property of the complex,
which can be read off from a flat model, and the thick triangulated category
$D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ is not the bounded derived category of an abelian category of
sheaves of finite Tor amplitude.
