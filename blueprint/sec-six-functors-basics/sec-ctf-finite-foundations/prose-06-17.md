In this subsection the coefficient ring is a finite admissible ring $A$.
The construction of $Rf_!$ above used, for finite rings,
Proposition [sk-prop-ctf-finite-proper], which we now prove together with
compactness. These are the two facts Brown representability needs: the
constructible complexes of finite Tor amplitude are compact
generators of the localizing subcategory they generate, and $Rf_!$ preserves
this subcategory and its direct sums. For torsion
coefficients both facts reduce to the classical étale theory, because
every object of $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}$ has a bounded flat constructible model and the
étale cohomology of a finite type scheme commutes with direct sums of
unbounded complexes.
