This is the general derived adjunction for an adjoint pair whose left adjoint is
left derivable and right adjoint right derivable. The unit is
$K\to g_*g^*K$ on a K-flat representative followed by $g_*$ of a K-injective resolution of
$g^*K$; the counit is dual. The required absoluteness conditions hold because the
comparison maps of the derived functors are isomorphisms on K-flat, respectively
K-injective, complexes, and the triangle identities follow from those of $g^*\dashv g_*$.
