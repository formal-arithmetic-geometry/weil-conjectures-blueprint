$\Sh(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)$ is Grothendieck abelian, hence has enough injectives,
so $R^+g_*$ is computed on bounded below injective resolutions. Since $g^*$ is exact,
$D^+(g^*)$ is its own left derived functor. The adjunction is then the derived adjunction
of $g^*\dashv g_*$, as in [def-sfb-finite-derived-adjunction], on the bounded below
categories.
