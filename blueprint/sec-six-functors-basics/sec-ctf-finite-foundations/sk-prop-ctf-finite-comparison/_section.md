For a finite admissible ring $A$, the comparison functor
$\nu_X^*:D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)\to D(X_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t},A)$ identifies

$$
D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)=\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\acute{e}t},A))
\simeq\Loc(D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,A)).
$$

It preserves arbitrary direct sums and identifies ordinary direct image,
pullback and compactly supported direct image with their classical
calculations. In particular,
$\nu_Y^*Rg_{\acute{e}t,*}K\simeq Rg_{\mathrm{pro}\acute{e}t,*}\nu_X^*K$ for every
$K\in D(X_{\acute{e}t},A)$ and every morphism $g:X\to Y$.
