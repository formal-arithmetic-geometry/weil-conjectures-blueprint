The first assertion is [sk-cor-pe-finite-tor-comparison] and
[sk-thm-pe-direct-image-comparison], together with the commutation of
extension by zero with comparison, $\nu_X^*j_{\mathrm{ét},!}\simeq j_{\mathrm{proét},!}\nu_U^*$
as functors $\Mod(U_{\mathrm{ét}},\Lambda)\to\Mod(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ for an open
immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ (both functors are left adjoint to restriction
followed by $\nu_{U,*}$). The localizing extension is
[sk-prop-ctf-finite-comparison].
