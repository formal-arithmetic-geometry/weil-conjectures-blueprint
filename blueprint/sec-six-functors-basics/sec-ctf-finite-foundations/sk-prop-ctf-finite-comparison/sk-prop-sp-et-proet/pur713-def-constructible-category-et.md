The full subcategory $D^b_c(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)\subseteq D(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$ consists of the objects with only finitely many nonzero cohomology sheaves, all constructible in the sense of Definition [pur713-def-finite-constructible] for $\tau=\Et$. Morphisms are those of $D(X_{\Et},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$.
