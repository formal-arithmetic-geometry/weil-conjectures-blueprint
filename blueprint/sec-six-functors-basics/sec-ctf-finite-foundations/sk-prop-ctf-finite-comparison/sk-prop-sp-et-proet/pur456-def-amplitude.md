The adjunction of Definition [pur456-def-adjoint-datum] has uniform constructible cohomological amplitude with bounds $c_f^-\leq c_f^+$, integers, if for all integers $a\leq b$,

$$
Rf^!_{\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n}
\bigl(D_c^{[a,b]}(Y_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)\bigr)
\subseteq D_c^{[a+c_f^-,b+c_f^+]}(X_{\Proet},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n),
$$

with the subcategories of Definition [pur456-def-intervals]. The same two bounds must work for every interval and every object in it; they may depend on $f$ and the specified adjunction. Uniformity over $n$, over fields $E$, or over base changes is an additional assertion.
