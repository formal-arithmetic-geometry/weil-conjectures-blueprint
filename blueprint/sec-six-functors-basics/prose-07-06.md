Zariski locally on $X$ and $S$, a smooth morphism $f:X\to S$
factors as an étale morphism to some $\mathbf A^d_S$ followed by the
projection to $S$. To compute $f^!$ for smooth $f$, we therefore
first treat these two kinds of morphisms.
