Let $a=i\circ u\colon S\hookrightarrow Z\hookrightarrow X$ with $i$ closed, $u$ open, and let
$j\colon X\setminus Z\hookrightarrow X$ be the open complement of $Z$. Let $L_0$ be a lisse
$\mathcal O_{E,S}$-module (finite free locally), $b\colon T\hookrightarrow X$ locally closed and $M_0$ a
lisse $\mathcal O_{E,T}$-lattice. Then for every $n\geq1$:

1. $R\underline\Gamma_Z\,b_!(M_0/\pi_E^n)$ lies in
   $\D^{[0,\operatorname{cd}_\ell(j)+1]}(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E/\pi_E^n)$, with cohomology sheaves
   pulled back from $Z_{\mathrm{ét}}$;
2. $H^qR\Gamma\bigl(S,(L_0^\vee/\pi_E^n)\otimes u^*R\underline\Gamma_Z\,b_!(M_0/\pi_E^n)\bigr)=0$ for
   $q>\operatorname{cd}_\ell(j)+\operatorname{cd}_\ell(S)+1$.
