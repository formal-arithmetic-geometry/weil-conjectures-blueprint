Let $S$ be of finite type over the standing field $k$.

1. For the projection $a:\mathbf A^d_S\to S$, the right adjoint $a^!$ of
   $Ra_!\colon D_{\mathrm{class}}((\mathbf A^d_S)_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$
   is $a^*(d)[2d]$, with unit and counit compatible with base change.
2. Let $f:X\to S$ be separated of finite type, let
   $N\in D_{\mathrm{class}}(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, and let $u:U\to X$ be
   separated, étale and of finite type. There is a canonical identification

   $$
   u^*f^!N=(fu)^!N\quad\text{in }D_{\mathrm{class}}(U_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda).
   \qquad\text{(G10)}
   $$

   It is compatible with units, counits, composition, and further étale
   localization.
