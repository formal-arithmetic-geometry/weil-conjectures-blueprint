title = "$Rp_*$ on $D_{\\mathrm{cons}}(-,\\Qlb)$ is a left adjoint for proper $p$"

Let $\Lambda=\underline{\Qlb}$ ([det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed]) and let $p\colon Z\to Y$ be a proper
morphism of the setting. Then the derived pushforward
$Rp_*\colon D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Z,\underline{\Qlb})\to D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Y,\underline{\Qlb})$
of [def-sfb-dc-lower-star] has a right adjoint $Rp^!$.
