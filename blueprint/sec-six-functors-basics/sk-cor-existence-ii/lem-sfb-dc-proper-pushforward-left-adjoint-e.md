kind = "lemma"
title = "$Rp_*$ on $D_{\\mathrm{cons}}(-,E)$ is a left adjoint for proper $p$"
order = 1794
uses = ["def-sfb-dc-lower-star", "sk-def-sfb-condensed-ring"]
+++
Let $E$ be a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$, let $\Lambda=\underline E$
([sk-def-sfb-condensed-ring]), and let $p\colon Z\to Y$ be a proper morphism of the setting. Then
the derived pushforward $Rp_*\colon D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Z,\underline E)\to D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Y,\underline E)$
of [def-sfb-dc-lower-star] has a right adjoint $Rp^!$.
