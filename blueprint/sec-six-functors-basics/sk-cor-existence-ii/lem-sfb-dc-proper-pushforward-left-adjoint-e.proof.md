This is the existence of the exceptional inverse image for $\ell$-adic sheaves
(Bhatt–Scholze, §6.8). On each torsion level $\cO_E/\varpi^n$, in which $p$ is invertible, the
étale $Rp_*=Rp_!$ has a right adjoint $Rp^!$ (SGA 4, XVIII). It preserves bounded
constructible complexes by Deligne's finiteness theorem (SGA 4½, *Th. finitude*), and it is
compatible with reduction modulo $\varpi^n$. Passing to the derived limit over $n$ gives a right
adjoint of $Rp_*$ on $D_{\mathrm{cons}}(-,\underline{\cO}_E)$. Inverting $\ell$ gives one on
$D_{\mathrm{cons}}(-,\underline E)$.
