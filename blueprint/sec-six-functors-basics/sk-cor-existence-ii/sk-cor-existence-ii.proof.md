For proper $p$ the identity is an admissible compactification, so $Rp_!=Rp_*$ on
$\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$, and parts (3) and (4) of [sk-def-sfb-dc-upper-shriek] give the right
adjoint $p^!$ on $\Dctf$. Details: [det-cor-existence-ii-restriction]. For the three
$\ell$-adic rings the same conclusion is stated, with a proof by reduction to torsion
levels, in [lem-sfb-dc-proper-pushforward-left-adjoint-e],
[lem-sfb-dc-proper-pushforward-left-adjoint-oe] and
[lem-sfb-dc-proper-pushforward-left-adjoint-qlbar].
