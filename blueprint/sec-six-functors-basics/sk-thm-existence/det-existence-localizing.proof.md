The cases $\Lambda=\mathbf Z_\ell$ and $\Lambda=\mathbf Q_\ell$ are the cases $\cO_E$ and $E$
with $E=\mathbf Q_\ell$, so it suffices to treat $A$, $\cO_E$, $E$ and $\Qlb$.

(1) *Compactness.* For a finite ring $A$ this is [sk-prop-ctf-finite-compact]. For
$\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ one has $\Dctf=\Dbc$, and every object of $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is compact in
$\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$ by [sk-thm-constructible-compactness]. For $\Lambda=\cO_E$ this is the
first assertion of [sk-prop-int-compactness].

*Generation.* By definition ([sk-def-localizing-closure]) $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ is the
smallest localizing subcategory of $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ containing
$\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. The category $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ is essentially small
([sk-lem-brown-hypotheses]); choose a small skeleton $\mathcal S$. The full subcategory of
objects $K$ of $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ with $\Hom(S[n],K)=0$ for all $S\in\mathcal S$ and
$n\in\mathbf Z$ is zero. Indeed, call this subcategory $\mathcal K$. The objects $M$ with
$\Hom(M,K)=0$ for all $K\in\mathcal K$ form a localizing subcategory (it is closed under shifts,
cones and direct sums, since $\Hom(\bigoplus_i M_i,K)=\prod_i\Hom(M_i,K)$). It contains
$\mathcal S$, hence every object of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$, each being isomorphic to an object of
$\mathcal S$; so it contains $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))\supseteq\mathcal K$. Every $K\in\mathcal K$
therefore satisfies $\Hom(K,K)=0$, i.e. $K=0$. Hence $\mathcal S$ is a set of compact
generators; for the field coefficients this is also [sk-cor-constructible-compactness-ii].

(2) This is [sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence]: $j_!$ and $Rp_*$ restrict to exact, sum-preserving
functors between the localizing categories by [sk-prop-proper-image-localizing], they preserve
$\Dctf$, and two compactifications give canonically isomorphic functors.

(3) By [sk-lem-brown-hypotheses], $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ is a locally small triangulated
category with arbitrary direct sums and split idempotents, compactly generated by a set. An
exact functor out of such a category that preserves direct sums has an exact right adjoint
(Brown representability, Neeman's form). Applied to $Rf_!$ from (2), this is
[sk-prop-sf-existence].
