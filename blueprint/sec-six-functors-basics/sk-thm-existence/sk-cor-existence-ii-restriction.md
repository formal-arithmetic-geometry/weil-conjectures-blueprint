Let $\Lambda$ be admissible and $p\colon X\to Y$ a proper morphism of standing schemes. Then:

1. the functor $Rp_!\colon\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))$ of
   [sk-thm-existence] (2) is isomorphic to the restriction of
   $Rp_*\colon D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to D(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$;
2. $Rp_*$ maps $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$, and the restriction of the right
   adjoint $p^!$ of [sk-thm-existence] (3) to $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ is a right adjoint
   of $Rp_*\colon\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$.
