Let $\Lambda\in\{A,\mathbf Z_\ell,\cO_E,\mathbf Q_\ell,E,\Qlb\}$ be admissible and let
$f\colon X\to Y$ be a separated morphism of finite type between standing schemes.

1. Every object of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ is compact in $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$, and a small
   skeleton of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ is a set of compact generators of $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$.
2. For a compactification $f=p\circ j$, the functor $Rf_!=Rp_*\circ j_!$ is an exact functor
   $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))$ that preserves direct sums and sends
   $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$; up to canonical isomorphism it does not depend
   on the compactification.
3. $Rf_!$ has an exact right adjoint
   $f^!\colon\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$.
