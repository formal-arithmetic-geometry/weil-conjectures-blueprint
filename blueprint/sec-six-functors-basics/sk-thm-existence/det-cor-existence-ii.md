Since $Rp_!=Rp_*$ as functors $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))$ for proper $p$,
[sk-thm-existence] gives in particular:

For proper $p\colon X\to Y$ and admissible $\Lambda$, the functor
$Rp_*\colon\Dctf(X,\Lambda)\to\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$ has a right adjoint $p^!$.
