For every condensed abelian group $\Lambda$ (in particular every condensed ring) and every scheme
$X$, the coefficient change of the identity is the identity:
$(\mathrm{id}_\Lambda)_X=\mathrm{id}_{\Lambda_X}$ ([def-sfb-coefficient-map]).
