For morphisms $a\colon\Lambda\to\Lambda'$ and $b\colon\Lambda'\to\Lambda''$ of condensed
abelian groups (in particular of condensed rings) and every scheme $X$, the coefficient changes
of [def-sfb-coefficient-map] satisfy $(b\circ a)_X=b_X\circ a_X$.
