By [sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms], $\alpha_f$ and $\alpha'_f$ are the components at
$\Lambda$ and at $\Lambda'$ of the natural isomorphism $f^*\circ p_Y^*\cong p_X^*$ of
[sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp]. The asserted identity is the naturality square of this
isomorphism at the morphism $a$, since $f^*p_Y^*a=f^*a_Y$ and $p_X^*a=a_X$.
