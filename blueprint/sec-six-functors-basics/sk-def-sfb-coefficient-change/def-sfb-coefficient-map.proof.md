The inverse image $p_X^*$ is a strong monoidal functor, and the ring structures of $\Lambda_X$,
$\Lambda'_X$ are the images of those of $\Lambda$, $\Lambda'$
([sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients]). A monoidal functor sends a morphism of monoid objects
to a morphism of monoid objects between the image monoids, so $p_X^*a$ is a morphism of ring
objects.
