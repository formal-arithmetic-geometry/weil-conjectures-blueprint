Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $C\in\Cons(X,\Lambda)$, and $L\in\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ with $H^q(L)=0$ for
$q>-d_C-1$ (no lower bound). Then $\Hom(C,L)=0$.
