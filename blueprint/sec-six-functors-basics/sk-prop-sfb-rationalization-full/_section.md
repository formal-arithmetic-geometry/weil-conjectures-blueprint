Extension of scalars $E_X\otimes_{\cO_{E,X}}-:
D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\cO_E)\to D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},E)$ ([sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x]) identifies
$D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},E)$ with the Verdier quotient of $D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\cO_E)$
by the complexes $K$ with $H^q(K)[1/\ell]=0$ for all $q$.
