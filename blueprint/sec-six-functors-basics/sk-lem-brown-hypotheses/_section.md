For every admissible $\Lambda$, the category $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ ([sk-def-localizing-closure]) is
locally small and triangulated, has arbitrary direct sums, and has split idempotents. The thick category
$\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ is essentially small, and a small skeleton is a set of compact generators of
$\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$.
