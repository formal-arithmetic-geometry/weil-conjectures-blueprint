For every admissible $\Lambda$, the category $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ is locally small and triangulated, has
arbitrary direct sums, and has split idempotents. The thick category $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ is essentially small,
and a small skeleton is a set of compact generators of $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$.
