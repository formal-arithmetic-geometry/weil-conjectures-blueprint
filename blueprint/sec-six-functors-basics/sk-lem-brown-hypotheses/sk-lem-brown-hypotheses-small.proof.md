*Thick.* Bounded constructible complexes are closed under shifts, cones (long exact cohomology sequence
and closure of constructible sheaves under kernels, cokernels and extensions, on a common refinement of
stratifications) and direct summands (cohomology sheaves of a summand are summands, hence images of
idempotents of constructible sheaves). Finite Tor amplitude is preserved by shifts, cones (long exact Tor
sequence, with the union of the intervals) and summands.

*Essentially small.* The admissible constructible hearts are essentially small: a constructible sheaf is
described by a finite stratification of the Noetherian scheme $X$, local models given by finite
presentations of $\Lambda$-modules, sheaves on a fixed small site, and gluing morphisms, which form a set
([sk-def-sfb-constructible]). A bounded complex with constructible cohomology in $[a,b]$ is obtained from its
cohomology sheaves by the finitely many truncation triangles; at each step the extension is classified by
an element of an ambient Hom set $\Hom(H^n(K)[-n],\tau_{<n}K[1])$, which is a set. By induction on $b-a$,
the isomorphism classes of objects of $\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ form a set, and $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ is a full subcategory of
it. Truncations here are those of $\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$.
