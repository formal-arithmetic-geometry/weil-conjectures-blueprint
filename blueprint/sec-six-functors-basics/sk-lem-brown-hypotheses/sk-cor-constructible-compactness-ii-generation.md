Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$ and let $G$ be a set of representatives of the isomorphism classes of
$\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ (which is essentially small by part (2) of [sk-lem-coefficient-heart] and
finiteness of the Postnikov data). If $K\in\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$ satisfies $\Hom(C[n],K)=0$ for all
$C\in G$ and $n\in\mathbf Z$, then $K=0$. Hence $G$ is a set of compact generators of
$\Loc(\Dbc(X,\Lambda))$.
