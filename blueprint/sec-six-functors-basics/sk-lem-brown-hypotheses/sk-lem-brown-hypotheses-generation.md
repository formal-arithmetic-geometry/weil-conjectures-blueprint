Let $\Lambda$ be admissible and $G$ a small skeleton of $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$. Every object of $G$ is compact in
$\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$, and if $K\in\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$ has $\Hom(C[n],K)=0$ for all $C\in G$ and $n$, then
$K=0$.
