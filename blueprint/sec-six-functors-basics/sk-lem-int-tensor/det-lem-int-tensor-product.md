Let $X$ be a standing scheme and $K,L\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$. Then the derived
tensor product $K\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_{E,X}}L\in\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$, formed
without any completion, lies in $\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$.

Moreover, if $M\in\D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$ is pro-étale locally of the form
$P\otimes_{\mathcal O_E}\mathcal O_{E,S}$ with $P$ a bounded complex of finite free
$\mathcal O_E$-modules, then $M$ is dualizable in $\D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)$, and
$M\otimes^{\mathbf L}-$ preserves products, derived inverse limits and derived
complete objects.
