This follows from [sk-lem-integral-models] (3), since closed direct image is exact and
preserves limits ([Stacks, Tag 09AA](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09AA),
[Stacks, Tag 09BM](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09BM)). The comparison holds on
objects that are pro-étale locally of the form $\mathcal O_{E,S}\otimes P$ with $P$ finitely
generated, hence on $\Dbc$.
