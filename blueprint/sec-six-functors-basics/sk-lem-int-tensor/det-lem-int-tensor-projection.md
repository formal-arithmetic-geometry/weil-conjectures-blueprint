Let $\Lambda$ be a condensed ring, $X$ a standing scheme, and
$a=i\circ u\colon S\hookrightarrow X$ a locally closed immersion, with
$u\colon S\hookrightarrow\overline S$ open and $i\colon\overline S\hookrightarrow X$ closed, and
$a_!=i_*u_!$. For $M\in\D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ and $L\in\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$
there is a natural isomorphism
$$
(a_!M)\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Lambda_X}L\simeq a_!\bigl(M\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Lambda_S}a^*L\bigr)
\quad\text{in }\D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda).
$$
