Let $f\colon X\to Y$ admit a compactification [sk-def-nagata-sketch-data], with chosen
compactification $c(f)$. The *exceptional pullback* of $f$ is

$$
Rf^!:=Rf^!_{c(f)}\colon D(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)\to D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)
$$

in the sense of [def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-compactification].
