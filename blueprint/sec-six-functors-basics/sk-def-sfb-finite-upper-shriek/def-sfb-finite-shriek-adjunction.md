For $f$ admitting a compactification and $\mathcal A$ as in
[def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction-compactification], the adjunction

$$
Rf_!\dashv Rf^!
$$

on the unbounded derived categories, between the functors of
[def-sfb-finite-derived-lower-shriek] and [def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek], is the adjunction
of [def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction-compactification] for the chosen compactification.
