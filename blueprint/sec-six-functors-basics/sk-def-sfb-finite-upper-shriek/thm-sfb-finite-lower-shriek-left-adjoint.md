Let $\mathcal A$ be as in [def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction] and let $f$ admit a
compactification. The derived lower shriek
$Rf_!\colon D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)\to D(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal A)$ of
[def-sfb-finite-derived-lower-shriek] is a left adjoint.
