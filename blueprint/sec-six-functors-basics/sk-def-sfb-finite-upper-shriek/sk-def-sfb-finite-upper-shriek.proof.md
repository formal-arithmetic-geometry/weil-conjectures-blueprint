The right adjoint of $Rg_*$ for proper $g$ is [thm-sfb-finite-proper-pushforward-left-adjoint].
$Rf^!$ is [def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek], formed along the chosen compactification as in
[def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-compactification]. For 1., the adjunction is
[def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction], built from
[def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction-compactification], and $Rf_!$ is a left adjoint by
[thm-sfb-finite-lower-shriek-left-adjoint]. For 2., $Rf^!$ preserves bounded below objects by
[lem-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-bounded-below]; the restricted functor and adjunction are
[def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-plus] and [def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction-plus].

The same construction, with the étale site in place of the pro-étale site, defines the
exceptional pullback for étale sheaves.
