Under the hypotheses of [def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction] and
[def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-plus], the functor $Rf_!^+$ of
[def-sfb-finite-derived-lower-shriek-plus] is left adjoint to $Rf^{!,+}$:

$$
Rf_!^+\dashv Rf^{!,+},
$$

the restriction of the adjunction $Rf_!\dashv Rf^!$ of [def-sfb-finite-shriek-adjunction].
