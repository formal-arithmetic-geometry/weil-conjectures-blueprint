The inclusions $\iota\colon D^+\subseteq D$ are fully faithful, and both functors are
restrictions: $\iota\circ Rf_!^+\cong Rf_!\circ\iota$
([def-sfb-finite-derived-lower-shriek-plus-compat]) and $\iota\circ Rf^{!,+}=Rf^!\circ\iota$
([def-sfb-finite-upper-shriek-plus]). An adjunction between functors that restrict along
fully faithful functors restricts:
$\Hom(Rf_!^+K,L)\cong\Hom(Rf_!\iota K,\iota L)\cong\Hom(\iota K,Rf^!\iota L)
\cong\Hom(K,Rf^{!,+}L)$.
