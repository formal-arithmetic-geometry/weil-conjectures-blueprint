Let $S$ be integral of finite type over the standing field $k$, let
$\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k)$, and let $f:X\to S$ be separated, smooth and of
finite type. For a finite locally constant $\Fell$-sheaf $L$ on
$X_{\mathrm{proét}}$, there is a nonempty open $S_0\subset S$ such that, for the
restriction $f_{S_0}:X_{S_0}\to S_0$, the complex $Rf_{S_0,*}L$ is bounded with
finite locally constant cohomology sheaves, and its formation commutes with
base change: for every finite-type $k$-scheme $v:T\to S_0$, with base change
$f_T:X_T\to T$ and $v_X:X_T\to X_{S_0}$, the canonical base-change map
$v^*Rf_{S_0,*}L\to Rf_{T,*}v_X^*L$ in $D(T_{\mathrm{proét}},\Fell)$ is an
isomorphism.
