For $C\in\Dbc(X,\mathcal O_E)$ and every $q$, the functor
$\Hom_{D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\mathcal O_E)}(C,-[q])$ commutes with filtered
colimits $\operatorname{colim}_iK_i$ of complexes with a common lower bound.
