Let $\Lambda$ be admissible and $f:X\to Y$ separated of finite type with $Y$
qcqs. For compactifications $c_a=(j_a:X\to Z_a,\ p_a:Z_a\to Y)$ write
$Rf_!^{c_a}=Rp_{a*}j_{a!}$ on $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))$. For any two
compactifications $c_1,c_2$ there is an isomorphism
$\phi_{c_2,c_1}:Rf_!^{c_1}\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf_!^{c_2}$, obtained from the
refinement isomorphisms through any common dense refinement, which is
independent of the common refinement chosen and satisfies
$\phi_{c,c}=\mathrm{id}$ and $\phi_{c_3,c_2}\circ\phi_{c_2,c_1}=\phi_{c_3,c_1}$.
