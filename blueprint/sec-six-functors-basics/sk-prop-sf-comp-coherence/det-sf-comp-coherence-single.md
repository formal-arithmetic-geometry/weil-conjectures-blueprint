Let $\Lambda$ be admissible, $f:X\to Y$ separated of finite type with $Y$
qcqs, and $f=p\circ j$ a compactification, $j:X\to\overline X$ a quasi-compact
open immersion and $p:\overline X\to Y$ proper. Then
$Rp_*\circ j_!:D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to D(Y_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ restricts
to an exact functor $\Loc(\Dctf(X,\Lambda))\to\Loc(\Dctf(Y,\Lambda))$ which
preserves direct sums and carries $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)$ into $\Dctf(Y,\Lambda)$.
