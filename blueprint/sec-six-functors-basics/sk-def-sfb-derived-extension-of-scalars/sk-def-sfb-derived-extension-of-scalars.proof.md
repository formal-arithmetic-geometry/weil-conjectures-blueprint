$\Mod_{\mathcal R}$ and $\Mod_{\mathcal S}$ are closed symmetric monoidal Grothendieck
abelian categories with functorial flat resolutions [sk-prop-sfb-module-category]. So
left derived functors exist for functors preserving K-flat quasi-isomorphisms, and right
derived functors exist for all additive functors [sk-def-sfb-derived-categories].
Extension of scalars preserves K-flat quasi-isomorphisms [lem-sfb-extend-scalars-kflat],
so $L(\operatorname{Ext}_\varphi)$ is defined. The adjunction with the right derived
restriction is [def-sfb-derived-extend-restrict-adjunction]. That right derived functor
is the termwise one [def-sfb-derived-restrict-levelwise], which gives the adjunction
[def-sfb-derived-extend-levelwise-adjunction].
