Let $E/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be finite and let $L$ be a lisse $E_X$-module
([sk-def-sfb-constructible]). A *lattice* in $L$ is a lisse $\cO_{E,X}$-submodule $M\subset L$
with $M[1/\ell]=L$. The sheaf on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ of lattices in $L$ is represented by a
separated surjective étale $X$-scheme, with a universal lattice. In particular, $L$ admits a
lattice étale locally on $X$.
