In the situation of [sk-lem-formal-closed-support] ($\Lambda$ admissible, $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ a closed
immersion with open complement $j\colon V\hookrightarrow X$, and $u\colon S\hookrightarrow Z$ an open
immersion), the functors

$$
R\underline\Gamma_Z\colon \D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to\D(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda),
\qquad
u^*R\underline\Gamma_Z\colon \D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to\D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)
$$

are left $t$-exact and commute with derived inverse limits of sequences. So does tensoring with a
finite locally free sheaf, as a functor $\D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to\D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$.
