Let $\Lambda$ be admissible, let $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion with open complement
$j\colon V\hookrightarrow X$, and let $u\colon S\hookrightarrow Z$ be an open immersion. The functors
$R\underline\Gamma_Z$ ([sk-lem-formal-closed-support]) and $u^*R\underline\Gamma_Z$,

$$
R\underline\Gamma_Z\colon \D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to\D(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda),
\qquad
u^*R\underline\Gamma_Z\colon \D(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)\to\D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda),
$$

are left $t$-exact and commute with derived inverse limits of sequences, and so does tensoring with a
finite locally free $\Lambda_S$-module.
