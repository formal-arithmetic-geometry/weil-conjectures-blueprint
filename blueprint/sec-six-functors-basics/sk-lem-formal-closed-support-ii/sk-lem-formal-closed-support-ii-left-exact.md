title = "Left $t$-exactness of sections with closed support"
order = 2198

Let $\Lambda$ be admissible, $i\colon Z\hookrightarrow X$ a closed immersion and $u\colon S\hookrightarrow Z$ an
open immersion. Then $R\underline\Gamma_Z$ carries $\D^{\geq0}(X_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ into
$\D^{\geq0}(Z_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$, and so does $u^*R\underline\Gamma_Z$ into
$\D^{\geq0}(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$.
