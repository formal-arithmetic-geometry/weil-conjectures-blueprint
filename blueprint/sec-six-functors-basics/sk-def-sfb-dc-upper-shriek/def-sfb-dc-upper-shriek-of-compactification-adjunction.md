In the situation of [def-sfb-dc-upper-shriek-of-compactification], the adjunction

$$
Rf_!^c\dashv Rf^!_c
$$

between $Rf_!^c=Rp_*\circ i_!$ of [sk-def-sfb-dc-lower-shriek] and $Rf^!_c=Li^*\circ Rp^!$ is the
composite of the adjunction $i_!\dashv Li^*$ of [def-sfb-dc-open-lower-shriek-adjunction] with the
adjunction $Rp_*\dashv Rp^!$.
