title = "$Rp_*$ on $D_{\\mathrm{cons}}(-,\\cO_E)$ is a left adjoint for proper $p$"

Let $E\subseteq\Qlb$ be a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ with ring of integers $\cO_E$, let
$\Lambda=\underline{\cO}_E$ ([sk-def-sfb-condensed-ring]), and let $p\colon Z\to Y$ be a proper
morphism of the setting. Then the derived pushforward
$Rp_*\colon D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Z,\underline{\cO}_E)\to D_{\mathrm{cons}}(Y,\underline{\cO}_E)$
of [def-sfb-dc-lower-star] has a right adjoint $Rp^!$.
