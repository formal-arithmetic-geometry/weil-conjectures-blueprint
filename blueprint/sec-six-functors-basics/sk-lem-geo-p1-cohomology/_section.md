Let $S$ be a Noetherian scheme on which $\ell$ is invertible and
$p:\mathbf P^1_S\to S$ the projection. The unit
$\Lambda_S\to Rp_*\Lambda_{\mathbf P^1_S}$ and the class
$c_1(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1_S}(1))$ define an isomorphism

$$
\Lambda_S\oplus\Lambda_S(-1)[-2]
      \ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ Rp_*\Lambda_{\mathbf P^1_S}
      \quad\text{in }D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda),   \qquad\text{(G2)}
$$

compatible with base change: for $g:S'\to S$ with $S'$ as in the lemma, the
base-change map $g^*Rp_*\Lambda\to Rp'_*\Lambda$ in
$D(S'_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ carries the pullback of (G2) to (G2) for $S'$.
Let

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_p:Rp_*\Lambda_{\mathbf P^1_S}(1)[2]\longrightarrow\Lambda_S
\qquad\text{(G3)}
$$

be the morphism in $D(S_{\mathrm{proét}},\Lambda)$ given by the projection onto
the summand $\Lambda_S$ of the twist of (G2). Then $\operatorname{Tr}_p$ is
compatible with every Noetherian base change.
