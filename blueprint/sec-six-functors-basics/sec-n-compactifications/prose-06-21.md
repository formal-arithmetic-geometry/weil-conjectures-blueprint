Once we know that $f^!$ preserves $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda)$
(Section [sec-constructibility-upper-shriek]), these isomorphisms restrict to the
full subcategories $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(-,\Lambda)$, and the same statements hold for
the adjunction between $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X,\Lambda)$ and $D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(Y,\Lambda)$.
