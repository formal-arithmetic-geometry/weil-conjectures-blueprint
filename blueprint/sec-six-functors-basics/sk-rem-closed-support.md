Let $X=\mathbf A^1_k$, $k$ algebraically closed, $Z$ the origin and
$U=\mathbf G_{m,k}$. Then $Rj_*\Lambda_U$ has $\mathcal H^0=\Lambda_X$ and
$\mathcal H^1=i_*\Lambda(-1)$, from the Kummer covers $t\mapsto t^{\ell^n}$. The triangle

$$
i_*R\underline\Gamma_ZK\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow Rj_*j^*K
\longrightarrow i_*R\underline\Gamma_ZK[1]
$$

with $K=\Lambda_X$ gives $i^!\Lambda_X\simeq\Lambda(-1)[-2]$, as [sk-thm-purity-transport],
applied to the structure maps of $Z$ and $X$, predicts.
