Let $X=\mathbf A^1_k$ with $k$ algebraically closed, let $Z$ be the origin and
$U=\mathbf G_{m,k}$ its complement. Then $Rj_*\Lambda_U$ has
$\mathcal H^0=\Lambda_X$ and $\mathcal H^1=i_*\Lambda(-1)$; the latter comes
from the Kummer covers $t\mapsto t^{\ell^n}$ of $\mathbf G_m$ near $0$. The
unit $\Lambda_X\to Rj_*\Lambda_U$ is an isomorphism on $\mathcal H^0$, so the
closed-support triangle

$$
i_*R\underline\Gamma_ZK\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow Rj_*j^*K
\longrightarrow i_*R\underline\Gamma_ZK[1]
$$

with $K=\Lambda_X$ gives
$i_*R\underline\Gamma_Z\Lambda_X\simeq\mathcal H^1(Rj_*\Lambda_U)[-2]$, that is
$i^!\Lambda_X\simeq\Lambda(-1)[-2]$ (by [sk-prop-closed-adjoint-constructible]).
This is the answer one expects for a point of codimension one, and it agrees
with smooth purity ([sk-prop-a1]).
