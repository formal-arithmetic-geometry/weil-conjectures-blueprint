On a weakly contractible $W$, a product of short exact sequences evaluates to a product of
short exact sequences of $\mathcal R(W)$-modules, which is exact: by the axiom of choice one
lifts a tuple coordinatewise. [sk-cor-pe-sections] detects exactness of sheaves on such
$W$ ([det-pe-products-exact]). (Repleteness alone gives the countable case, as in
Bhatt–Scholze, §3.1; arbitrary products use the weakly contractible basis.)

Exact products preserve quasi-isomorphisms. The derived product is the termwise product of
K-injective representatives, and by exactness any quasi-isomorphic representatives may be
used instead. Termwise products therefore compute the product and commute with cohomology
([det-pe-products-derived]; compare the abstract statement
[lem-weil-derived-limit-existence], part 2). See Stacks, Tags 0947 and 08TB.
