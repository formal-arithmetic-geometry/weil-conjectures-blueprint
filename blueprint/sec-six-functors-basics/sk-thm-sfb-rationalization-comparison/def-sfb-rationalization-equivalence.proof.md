Both $Q$ and $E\otimes^{\mathrm L}_{\cO_E}-$ are localization functors for the same class
$W_{\ell^{\mathbf N}}$: $Q$ by definition [det-def-sfb-rationalization], and
$E\otimes^{\mathrm L}_{\cO_E}-$ by [thm-sfb-extend-scalars-localization]. Two localizations
at the same class are related by a unique equivalence compatible with the localization
functors.
