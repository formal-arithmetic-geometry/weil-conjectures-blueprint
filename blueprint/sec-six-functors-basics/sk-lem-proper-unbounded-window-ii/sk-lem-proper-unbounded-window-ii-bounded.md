Let $\Lambda=E$ or $\Qlb$, $p\colon X\to Y$ proper, and $(M_\alpha)$ a family in $\Dic(X,\Lambda)$ with
cohomology in a common interval $[a,b]$. Then
$\bigoplus_\alpha Rp_*M_\alpha\to Rp_*(\bigoplus_\alpha M_\alpha)$ is an isomorphism.
