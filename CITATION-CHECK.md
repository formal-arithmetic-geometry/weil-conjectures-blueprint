# Citation check of the declaration arrows

The arrows between declarations follow the natural-language notes, `DerivedCategoryAndSixFunctorsChoice_readable.tex`: `A uses B` when the statement or a proof of A cites B (`\ref`, `\eqref`) and uses it. Arrows with a definition at either end are left out for now. This file records how they were made and what the check found.

## Method

1. `scripts/tex_citations.py` reads the tex on its own and lists, for every theorem-like environment, the declarations its statement and each of its proofs cite. A label names the declaration it stands in (its own label, an item, or an equation inside its statement or proof); a label in running text names none. A proof proves the statement before it, or the one its title names ("Proof of Theorem~\ref{x}").
2. Every citation between two non-definitions was read in context and classified: **use** (the step rests on it, or the statement needs it), **argument** ("as in the proof of B"), **setting** (only B's hypotheses or notation, "in the setting of B"), **mention** (forward pointer, comparison, example, "does not depend on B"). Only uses are arrows: a statement's on the statement, a proof's on that proof.
3. The same reading looked for uses without a citation (below). These are reported, not drawn.
4. The arrows were checked for cycles at every level and together with the section and chapter arrows.

## Numbers

- 538 declarations, 400 proofs. Between non-definitions the notes make 913 citations (counted per statement or proof): 817 uses, 41 mentions, 32 settings, 23 arguments.
- Three more uses come from proof sketches written as running text after a statement that has no proof environment (Cartan's criterion → Engel's theorem; the extension of partial sections → the relative Zariski main theorem and the lemma on products and composites of admissible blow-ups).
- That gives 802 arrows between 430 declarations. There is no cycle. Two arrows go to a declaration stated later and not in an appendix: `example-weak-contractibility-point` → `proposition-pro-etale-site-separably-closed` and `example-running-example-duality` → `proposition-kummer-duality-integral-tate-module`; both examples compute with the later result.
